# 39.말라기 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** The burden of the Word of Jehovah to Israel by the hand of Malachi:  
**1:2** I have loved you, says Jehovah. But you say, In what <i>way</i> have You loved us? <i>Was</i> not Esau brother to Jacob, and I loved Jacob? A statement of Jehovah.  
**1:3** And I hated Esau and have made his mountains a desolation, and his inheritance <i>to be</i> for the jackals of the wilderness.  
**1:4** If Edom says, We are beaten down, but we will return and build the ruined places; thus says Jehovah of Hosts: They shall build, but I will tear down; And they shall call them the region of wickedness, and the people <i>with</i> whom Jehovah <i>is</i> angrily indignant until forever.  
**1:5** And your eyes shall see, and you shall say, Jehovah will be magnified from on the border of Israel.  
**1:6** A son honors <i>his</i> father, and a servant his master. and if I <i>am</i> a father, where is My honor? And if I <i>am</i> a master, where is My fear? says Jehovah of Hosts to you, O priests <i>who</i> despise My name. But you say, In what have we despised Your name?  
**1:7** You <i>are</i> offering defiled food on My altar, and you say, In what have we defiled You? <i>It is</i> in your saying, The table of Jehovah, it <i>is</i> to be despised.  
**1:8** And if you offer the blind for sacrifice, <i>is it</i> not evil? And if you offer the lame and the sick, <i>is it</i> not evil? Bring it now to your governor. Will he accept you <i>or</i> lift up your face? says Jehovah of Hosts.  
**1:9** And now please entreat the face of God, that He favor us. This has been by your hands; will He lift up your faces? says Jehovah of Hosts.  
**1:10** Who <i>is</i> even among you and would shut the doors, and you not kindle <i>fire on</i> My altar in vain? There is no delight to Me in you, says Jehovah of Hosts. I will not be pleased <i>with</i> a food offering from your hand.  
**1:11** For from the rising of the sun until its going in, My name shall be great among the nations, and every place where incense shall be offered to My name; and a pure food offering; for My name <i>shall be</i> great among the nations, says Jehovah of Hosts.  
**1:12** But you are polluting it when you say, The table of the Lord, it <i>is</i> defiled. And His food, its fruit, is to be despised.  
**1:13** And you have said, Behold, what weariness! And you have snuffed at it, says Jehovah of Hosts. And you bring what <i>is</i> torn, and lame <i>ones</i>, and what <i>is</i> sick; and you bring the food offering. Should I be pleased <i>with</i> it from your hand? says Jehovah.  
**1:14** But cursed <i>be one</i> deceiving and there is a male in his flock, and he vows <i>it</i>, but <i>he</i> sacrifices a blemished <i>one</i> to the Lord. For I <i>am</i> a great King, says Jehovah of Hosts, and My name <i>is</i> feared among the nations.  

## 2장

**2:1** And now, O priests, this command is to you.  
**2:2** If you will not hear, and if you will not set <i>it</i> on <i>your</i> heart to give glory to My name, says Jehovah of Hosts, even I will send the curse on you, and I will curse your blessings. And indeed, I have cursed it, because you <i>are</i> not setting <i>it</i> on <i>your</i> heart.  
**2:3** Behold, I <i>am</i> rebuking your seed, and I will spread dung on your faces, the dung of your solemn feasts; and one will lift you up to it.  
**2:4** And you shall know that I have sent this command to you, to be My covenant with Levi, says Jehovah of Hosts.  
**2:5** My covenant with him was life and peace, and I gave them to him <i>for</i> fear; and he feared Me, and from before My name he is put in awe.  
**2:6** The Law of truth was in his mouth, and iniquity was not found in his lips. In peace and in uprightness he walked with Me, and he turned many from iniquity.  
**2:7** For the lips of the priest should guard knowledge, and they should seek the Law from his mouth; for he <i>is</i> the messenger of Jehovah of Hosts.  
**2:8** But you have turned out of the way; you have caused many to stumble at the Law. You have corrupted the covenant of Levi, says Jehovah of Hosts.  
**2:9** And I have also made you despised and low to all the people, in <i>your</i> mouth <i>by</i> which you <i>are</i> not keeping My ways, but <i>are</i> lifting up faces in the Law.  
**2:10** Is there not one father to us all? <i>Has</i> not one God created us? Why do we act deceitfully, <i>each</i> man with his brother, to pollute the covenant of our fathers?  
**2:11** Judah has acted deceitfully, and has done an abomination in Israel and in Jerusalem; for Judah has polluted the holy <i>place</i> of Jehovah, which He loves and <i>has</i> married a daughter of a foreign god.  
**2:12** Jehovah will cut off from the tents of Jacob the man who does it, <i>he</i> awakening and answering, or offering a food offering to Jehovah of Hosts.  
**2:13** And this <i>is</i> a second <i>thing</i> you do: covering the altar of Jehovah <i>with</i> tears, <i>with</i> weeping and groaning, yet not facing toward the food offering, and taking it <i>with</i> delight from your hand.  
**2:14** Yet you say, On what <i>cause</i>? On that Jehovah has been witness between you and the wife of your youth, against whom you have acted deceitfully; and she <i>is</i> your companion and your covenant wife.  
**2:15** And did He not make <i>you</i> one? And the vestige of the Spirit <i>is</i> to him. And what <i>of</i> the one? He was seeking a seed of God; even guard your spirit, and do not act deceitfully with the wife of your youth.  
**2:16** Jehovah, the God of Israel, says, He hates sending away, and to cover <i>with</i> violence on his garment, says Jehovah of Hosts. Even guard your spirit and do not act deceitfully.  
**2:17** You have wearied Jehovah with your words. Yet you say, In what <i>way</i> have we wearied Him? When you say, Every doer of evil <i>is</i> good in the eyes of Jehovah, and in them He delights; or, Where <i>is</i> the God of justice?  

## 3장

**3:1** Behold, I <i>am</i> sending My messenger, and He will clear the way before Me. And the Lord whom you <i>are</i> seeking shall suddenly come to His temple, even the Angel of the Covenant, in whom you delight. Behold, He comes, says Jehovah of Hosts.  
**3:2** But who can endure the day of His coming? And who <i>will be</i> standing at His appearing? For He <i>is</i> like a refiner's fire and like fuller's soap.  
**3:3** And He shall sit <i>as</i> a refiner and purifier of silver; and He shall purify the sons of Levi and purge them like gold and like silver, that they may be to Jehovah, to bring near an offering in righteousness to Jehovah.  
**3:4** And the offering of Judah and Jerusalem shall be pleasing to Jehovah, as <i>in</i> the days of old, and as <i>in</i> former years.  
**3:5** And I will draw near to you for judgment; and I will be a swift witness against those divining, and against <i>ones</i> doing adultery, and against those swearing to a lie; and against those who extort from the hired laborer's pay; and turning away the widow, and the fatherless, and the sojourner, and the <i>one</i> not fearing Me, says Jehovah of Hosts.  
**3:6** For I, Jehovah, change not. Because of this, you sons of Jacob do not come to an end.  
**3:7** From the days of your fathers, you have turned aside from My statutes and have not kept <i>them</i>. Turn back to Me, and I will turn back to you, says Jehovah of Hosts. But you say, In what <i>way</i> shall we turn back?  
**3:8** Will a man rob God? Yet you are robbing Me. But you say, In what have we robbed You? <i>In</i> the tithe and the offering!  
**3:9** You <i>are</i> cursed with a curse, for you <i>are</i> robbing Me, the nation, all of it.  
**3:10** Bring all the tithe into the storehouse, so that there may be food in My house. And test Me now in this, says Jehovah of Hosts, whether I will not open the windows of the heavens for you and pour out a blessing until there is no sufficiency.  
**3:11** And I will rebuke for you the <i>one</i> devouring, and he shall not cause to decay for you the fruit of your ground against you; and not will your vine miscarry for you in the field, says Jehovah of Hosts.  
**3:12** And all nations shall call you blessed, for you shall be a land of delight, says Jehovah of Hosts.  
**3:13** Your words have been strong against Me, says Jehovah. But you say, What have we spoken against you?  
**3:14** You have said, <i>It is</i> vanity to serve God. And, What unjust profit <i>is it</i> that we have kept His charge, and that we have walked as mourners before Jehovah of Hosts?  
**3:15** And now we <i>are</i> calling the arrogant blessed. Not only are the doers of wickedness built up, they also test God, and escape.  
**3:16** Then those fearing Jehovah spoke together, <i>each</i> man to his neighbor. And Jehovah gave attention and heard. And a Book of Remembrance was written before Him for those fearing Jehovah, and for those esteeming His name.  
**3:17** And they shall be Mine, says Jehovah of Hosts, for the day that I will make up <i>My</i> treasure. And I will pity them as a man has pity on his son who serves him.  
**3:18** Then you shall again see the <i>difference</i> between the righteous <i>one</i> and the wicked <i>one,</i> between him who serves God and him who does not serve Him.  

## 4장

**4:1** For, behold, the day is coming, burning like a fire pot; and all the arrogant and every doer of wickedness will be chaff. And the coming day will set them ablaze, says Jehovah of Hosts, which will not leave root or branches to them.  
**4:2** But to you who fear My name, the Sun of Righteousness shall rise up, and healing will be in His wings. And you shall go out and frisk like calves of a stall.  
**4:3** And you shall tread under the wicked <i>ones</i>, for they shall be ashes under the soles of your feet in the day which I <i>am</i> preparing, says Jehovah of Hosts.  
**4:4** Remember the Law of My servant Moses which I commanded him in Horeb for all Israel, the statutes and judgments.  
**4:5** Behold, I <i>am</i> sending you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of Jehovah.  
**4:6** And he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the sons, and the heart of the sons to their fathers, lest I come and strike the earth <i>with</i> utter destruction.
