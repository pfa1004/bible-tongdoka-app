# 26.에스겔 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** And it happened in the thirtieth year, in the fourth <i>month</i>, in the fifth of the month, as I was among the captives by the river Chebar, the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God.  
**1:2** On the fifth of the month, the fifth year of the captivity of Jehoiachin the king,  
**1:3** coming the Word of Jehovah became <i>known</i> to Ezekiel, the son of Buzi, the priest, in the land of the Chaldeans, by the river Chebar. And there the hand of Jehovah was on him.  
**1:4** And I looked, and behold, a windstorm came out of the north, a great cloud, and a fire flashing itself, and a brightness to it round about. And out of its midst, like the color of polished metal it came, out of the midst of the fire.  
**1:5** And from its midst was the likeness of four living creatures. And this <i>was</i> how they looked: the likeness of a man was to them,  
**1:6** and four faces <i>were</i> to each, and four wings to each of them,  
**1:7** and their feet <i>were</i> feet of straightness, and the sole of their feet like the sole of a calf's foot. And they sparkled like the color of burnished copper.  
**1:8** And the hands of a man <i>extended</i> from under their wings on their four sides. And their faces and their wings <i>were</i> to the four of them,  
**1:9** joining each <i>one</i> to the other <i>by</i> their wings. They did not turn in their going, each <i>one</i> went toward the front of their face.  
**1:10** And the likeness of their faces: the face of a man, and the face of a lion, on the right <i>side</i> to the four of them; and the face of an ox on the left to the four of them, and the face of an eagle to the four of them.  
**1:11** So their faces <i>were</i>. And their wings <i>were</i> spread upward, to <i>each</i> one the two <i>wings</i> were joined, and two <i>wings of</i> each covering their bodies.  
**1:12** And each <i>one</i> went toward the front of their faces. To where the spirit was to go, there they went; they did not turn in their going.  
**1:13** And the likeness of the living creatures: they continually going among the living creatures. And the fire <i>was</i> bright, and out of the fire went forth lightning.  
**1:14** And the living creatures kept running and returning, like the appearance of a flash of lightning.  
**1:15** And I looked at the living creatures, and, behold, one wheel <i>was</i> on the earth beside the living creatures, with the four of its faces.  
**1:16** The appearance of the wheels and their workmanship <i>was</i> like the color of beryl; and the one likeness <i>was</i> to the four of them. And their appearance and their workmanship <i>was</i> like the wheel in the middle of the wheel.  
**1:17** They went on the four of their sides in their going, they did not turn in their going.  
**1:18** And also their rims <i>were</i> high to them, even awesome to them. And their rims <i>were</i> full of eyes round about the four of them.  
**1:19** And in the going of the living creatures the wheels went beside them. And in the lifting up of the living creatures from on the earth, the wheels were lifted up.  
**1:20** There where the spirit was to go, there they went; there their spirit <i>was</i> to go, and the wheels were lifted up along with them. For the spirit of the living creature <i>was</i> in the wheels.  
**1:21** In their going, <i>these</i> went; and in their standing still, these stood still. And in their being lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up along with them. For the spirit of the living creature <i>was</i> in the wheels.  
**1:22** And a likeness <i>was</i> over the heads of the living creature, an expanse like the color of awesome crystal, stretched out over their heads from above.  
**1:23** And under the expanse their wings <i>were</i> straight, the one toward the other, to each <i>one,</i> two <i>wings</i> covering on this <i>side</i>, and to each <i>one</i> two covering on that <i>side of</i> their bodies.  
**1:24** And I heard the sound of their wings like the sound of great waters, like the voice of the Almighty. In their going <i>was</i> the sound of tumult, like the sound of an army. In their standing still, they let down their wings.  
**1:25** And there was a voice from the expanse that <i>was</i> over their heads. In their standing still, they let down their wings.  
**1:26** And from above the expanse that <i>was</i> over their heads <i>was</i> an appearance like a stone of lapis lazuli, <i>azure blue</i>, the likeness of a throne. And on the likeness of the throne <i>was</i> a likeness in appearance like a man on it from above.  
**1:27** And I saw <i>Him</i>, like the color of polished metal, looking like fire within it round about. From the appearance of His loins and upward, and from the appearance of His loins and downward, I saw <i>Him</i> looking like fire; and brightness to it round about.  
**1:28** As the appearance of the bow that is in the cloud in the day of the rain, so appeared the brightness round about. This <i>was</i> the appearance of the likeness of the glory of Jehovah. And I saw, and I fell on my face, and I heard a voice of <i>One</i> speaking.  

## 2장

**2:1** And He said to me, Son of man, stand on your feet, and I will speak to you.  
**2:2** And the Spirit entered into me as He spoke to me, and He made me stand on my feet, and I heard Him speaking to me.  
**2:3** And He said to me, Son of man, I am sending you to the sons of Israel, to the nations, the ones rebelling who have rebelled against Me. They and their fathers have transgressed against Me to this day.  
**2:4** And the sons <i>are</i> stiff of face and hard of heart. I am sending you to them. And you shall say to them, So says the Lord Jehovah.  
**2:5** And they, whether they will hear or whether they will stop; for they are a rebellious house; yea, they shall know that a prophet has been among them.  
**2:6** And you, son of man, do not be afraid of them and of their words. Do not be afraid, though briers and thorns <i>are</i> with you, and <i>though</i> you are living among scorpions. Do not be afraid of their words, and do not be frightened by their faces, though they <i>are</i> a house of rebellion.  
**2:7** And you shall speak My words to them, whether they will hear, or whether they will stop, for they <i>are</i> rebellious.  
**2:8** But you, son of man, hear what I am saying to you. Do not be like that rebellious house of rebellion. Open your mouth and eat what I am giving to you.  
**2:9** And I looked, and behold, a hand <i>was</i> extended to me. And behold, a roll of a book <i>was</i> in it.  
**2:10** And He spread it before me, and it <i>was</i> written on the face and the back. And written on it <i>were</i> weepings, and mourning, and woe.  

## 3장

**3:1** And He said to me, Son of man, eat what you find. Eat this roll, and go speak to the house of Israel.  
**3:2** So I opened my mouth, and He made me eat that roll.  
**3:3** And He said to me, Son of man, cause your belly to eat, and fill your bowels with the roll, this that I give to you. And I ate, and it was in my mouth like honey for sweetness.  
**3:4** And He said to me, Son of man, Go! Come to the house of Israel and speak with My words to them.  
**3:5** For you <i>are</i> not sent to a people of deep lip and difficult of language, <i>but</i> to the house of Israel;  
**3:6** not to many peoples of deep lip and of a difficult language, whose words you cannot hear. If I had sent you to them, they would have listened to you.  
**3:7** But the house of Israel is not willing to listen to you, for they are not willing to listen to Me, for all the house of Israel <i>are</i> strong of forehead; and they <i>are</i> hard of heart.  
**3:8** Behold, I have given your face <i>to be</i> strong over against their faces, and your forehead strong over against their foreheads.  
**3:9** I have given your forehead <i>to be</i> as an adamant harder than flint. Do not fear them, and do not be frightened by their faces, though they are a house of rebellion.  
**3:10** And He said to me, Son of man, all My words which I speak to you, receive into your heart, and hear with your ears.  
**3:11** And go! Come to the exiles, to the sons of your people, and speak to them, and say to them, So says the Lord Jehovah, whether they will hear, or whether they will stop.  
**3:12** And the Spirit lifted me up, and I heard behind me a sound of a great quaking. <i>He said</i>, Blessed <i>be</i> the glory of Jehovah from His place!  
**3:13** And <i>there was</i> the sound of the wings of the living creatures kissing each one to the other, and the sound of the wheels along with them, and the sound of a great quaking.  
**3:14** So the Spirit lifted me up and took me, and I went bitterly in the heat of my spirit, and the hand of Jehovah was strong upon me.  
**3:15** And I came to the exiles at Tel-abib, those dwelling by the river Chebar. And I sat there <i>where</i> they were dwelling. I also dwelt there seven days, being stunned among them.  
**3:16** And it happened at the end of the seven days, even it happened <i>that</i> the Word of Jehovah <i>came</i> to me, saying,  
**3:17** Son of man, I have given you <i>to be</i> a watchman for the house of Israel. And hear the Word of My mouth, and warn them from Me.  
**3:18** In My saying to the wicked <i>one</i>, Dying you shall die; and you do not warn him, and you do not speak to warn the wicked <i>one</i> from his wicked <i>wa</i>y, to <i>save</i> his life, he, the wicked <i>one</i>, shall die in his iniquity, but I will require his blood at your hand.  
**3:19** And you, because you have warned the wicked <i>one</i>, and he does not turn from his wickedness or from his way, he, the wicked <i>one</i>, shall die in his iniquity. But you have delivered your soul.  
**3:20** And when the righteous <i>one</i> turns from his righteousness and does injustice, and I lay a stumbling-block before him, he shall die. Since you have not warned him, he shall die in his sin, and his righteousness which he has done shall not be remembered, but I will require his blood at your hand.  
**3:21** But you, because you warned him, the righteous <i>one</i>, so that the righteous <i>one</i>, should not sin, and he does not sin, living he shall live because he is warned. And you have delivered your soul.  
**3:22** And the hand of Jehovah was on me there. And He said to me, Rise up, go forth into the plain, and I will speak with you there.  
**3:23** And I rose up and went forth into the plain. And, behold, there the glory of Jehovah was standing, like the glory which I saw by the river Chebar, and I fell on my face.  
**3:24** And the Spirit entered into me and stood me on my feet and spoke with me. And He said to me, Come, shut yourself within your house.  
**3:25** But you, son of man, behold, they shall put on you cords and shall bind you with them, and you shall not go out among them.  
**3:26** And I will make your tongue cling to your palate, and you shall be mute. And there shall not be to them a reproving man; for they <i>are</i> a rebellious house.  
**3:27** But in My speaking with you, I will open your mouth, and you shall say to them, So says the Lord Jehovah. He who hears, let him hear; and he who stops, let him stop. For they <i>are</i> a rebellious house.  

## 4장

**4:1** And you, son of man, take a brick to yourself, and put it before you, and engrave on it a city, Jerusalem.  
**4:2** And set a siege on it, and build a fort on it, and pour out a ramp on it. And place a camp on it, and set <i>battering</i> rams round about on it.  
**4:3** And you, take a griddle of iron to yourself, and place it <i>as</i> a wall of iron between you and the city. And place your face against it, and it shall be under siege, and thrust upon it. It is a sign to the house of Israel.  
**4:4** And you, lie down on your left side, and set the iniquity of the house of Israel on it. The number of the days that you shall lie down on it, you shall bear their iniquity.  
**4:5** For I have given <i>to be</i> on you the years of their iniquity, according to the number of days: three hundred and ninety days. And you shall bear the house of Israel's iniquity.  
**4:6** And when you complete them, even lie on your right side, the second. And you shall bear the house of Judah's iniquity forty days; a day for a year. I have given it for you, a day for a year.  
**4:7** And you shall set your face toward the siege of Jerusalem, and your arm bared; and you shall prophesy over it.  
**4:8** And, behold, I will give cords <i>to be</i> on you, and you cannot turn from your side to your <i>other</i> side until you have completed the days of your siege.  
**4:9** And you, take to yourself wheat, and barley, and beans, and lentils, and millet, and spelt, and place them in a single vessel; and make them for yourself into bread, to the number of days that you are lying on your side. You shall eat it three hundred and ninety days.  
**4:10** And your food which you shall eat <i>shall be</i> by weight: twenty shekels a day. From time to time you shall eat it.  
**4:11** And you shall drink water by measure: the sixth part of a hin. From time to time you shall drink.  
**4:12** And you shall eat cakes of barley, and you shall bake it with dung of the dung of man, in their sight.  
**4:13** And Jehovah said, Even so the sons of Israel shall eat their defiled bread among the nations there where I will drive them.  
**4:14** And I said, Ah, Lord Jehovah! Behold, my soul has not been defiled. I have not even eaten a carcass or a torn animal, from my youth, and until now, And unclean flesh has not come into my mouth.  
**4:15** And He said to me, See, I have given to you the dung of cattle in place of the dung of man. And you shall make bread over it.  
**4:16** And He said to me, Son of man, behold, I am breaking the staff of bread in Jerusalem. And they shall eat bread by weight, and with anxiety. And they shall drink water by measure, and in horror,  
**4:17** because they will lack bread and water, and each <i>one</i> be stunned with his brother. And <i>they</i> will waste away in their iniquity.  

## 5장

**5:1** And you, son of man, take to yourself a sharp sword, the razor of a barber. Take it to yourself and make it pass over your head and over your beard. And take to yourself scales to weigh, and to divide them out.  
**5:2** You shall burn a third part in the fire, in the midst of the city, when the days of the siege are fulfilled. And you shall take the third part <i>and</i> beat with a sword round about it. And you shall scatter the third part into the wind, and I will draw out a sword after them.  
**5:3** Also you shall take from there a few in number and bind them in your skirts.  
**5:4** And take from them again, and throw them into the middle of the fire and burn them in the fire. From it shall come forth a fire into all the house of Israel.  
**5:5** So says the Lord Jehovah: This <i>is</i> Jerusalem. I have set her in the midst of the nations, and round about her <i>are</i> the lands.  
**5:6** And she has changed My judgments for wickedness more than the nations, and My laws more than the lands that <i>are</i> round about her. For they have rejected My judgments and My statutes; they have not walked in them.  
**5:7** Therefore, so says the Lord Jehovah: Because you multiplied more than the nations that <i>are</i> round about you, not having walked in My statutes, and not having done My judgments, and you have not done according to the judgments of the nations round about you;  
**5:8** therefore, so says the Lord Jehovah: Behold! I, even I <i>am</i> against you, and will execute judgments in your midst in the sight of the nations.  
**5:9** And I will do in you that which I have not done, and which I will not do the like again, for all your disgusting things.  
**5:10** Therefore, the fathers shall eat the sons in your midst, and the sons shall eat their fathers. And I will execute judgments against you, and I will scatter the whole remnant of you into every wind.  
**5:11** Therefore, <i>as</i> I live, a statement of the Lord Jehovah, If not because you have defiled My sanctuary with all your detestable things and with all your disgusting things, even I also will withdraw. And My eye shall not spare, and I will not have pity.  
**5:12** A third part of you shall die by the plague and shall be consumed by the famine in your midst. And a third part shall fall by the sword round about you. And a third part I will scatter into every wind, and I will draw out a sword after them.  
**5:13** And My anger shall be spent, and I will make My fury rest on them, and I will be eased. And they shall know that I, Jehovah, have spoken in My zeal, in My completing My fury among them.  
**5:14** And I will give you <i>to be</i> a waste and a reproach among the nations that <i>are</i> round about you, in the eyes of all who pass by.  
**5:15** And it shall be a reproach and a taunt, a chastening and a horror, to the nations which <i>are</i> round about you, when I shall execute against you judgments in anger and in fury and in chastisements <i>among</i> them. I, Jehovah, have spoken it.  
**5:16** When I shall send the arrows of evil famine among them, which shall be for ruin, for which I will send them to destroy you, even I will add the famine on you and break the staff of bread to you.  
**5:17** Yea, I will send famine and evil beasts on you, and you will be bereaved. And pestilence and blood shall pass among you, and I shall bring a sword on you. I, Jehovah, have spoken.  

## 6장

**6:1** And it happened, the Word of Jehovah was to me, saying,  
**6:2** Son of man, set your face towards the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them.  
**6:3** And say, Mountains of Israel, hear the Word of the Lord Jehovah: So says the Lord Jehovah to the mountains, and to the hills, to the ravines, and to the valleys, Behold, I, <i>even</i> I will bring a sword on you, and I will destroy your high places.  
**6:4** And your altars shall be ruined, and your pillars shall be broken. And I will cast down your slain before your idols.  
**6:5** And I will put the corpses of the sons of Israel before their idols, and I will scatter your bones round about your altars.  
**6:6** In all your dwelling places the cities shall be laid waste, and the high places shall be deserted, so that your altars may be laid waste and become guilty, and <i>that</i> your idols may be broken and brought to an end, and your pillars may be cut down, and your works wiped out.  
**6:7** And the slain shall fall in your midst, and you shall know that I <i>am</i> Jehovah.  
**6:8** Yet I will leave a remainder, so that may be <i>left</i> to you refugees of the sword among the nations, in your scattering among the lands.  
**6:9** And the refugees shall remember Me among the nations where they will be exiled, because I was broken <i>by</i> their fornicating heart which has turned away from Me, and by their fornicating eyes which go after their idols. And they shall loathe against their faces for the evils which they have done in all their disgusting things.  
**6:10** And they shall know that I <i>am</i> Jehovah; not in vain have I said to do this evil to them.  
**6:11** So says the Lord Jehovah: Strike with your hand, and stamp with your foot, and say, Alas, for all the evil abominations of the house of Israel! For they shall fall by the sword, by the famine, and by the plague.  
**6:12** He who is far off shall die by the plague, and he who is near shall fall by the sword, and he who remains and is besieged shall die by the famine; so I will complete My fury on them.  
**6:13** And you shall know that I <i>am</i> Jehovah when their slain shall be in the midst of their idols round about their altars, on every high hill, in all the tops of the mountains, and under every green tree, and under every leafy oak, the place where they gave there a soothing aroma to all their idols.  
**6:14** And I will stretch out My hand on them and give the land a <i>to be</i> a desolation even more desolate than the desert toward Diblath, in all their dwelling places. And they shall know that I <i>am</i> Jehovah.  

## 7장

**7:1** And it happened, the Word of Jehovah was to me, saying,  
**7:2** And you, son of man, so says the Lord Jehovah to the land of Israel: An end! The end has come on the four corners of the land.  
**7:3** The end <i>is</i> now on you, and I will send My anger on you and will judge you according to your ways and will put on you all your disgusting things.  
**7:4** And My eye shall not spare you, and I will not have pity. But I will put your ways on you, and your disgusting things shall be in your midst, and you shall know that I <i>am</i> Jehovah.  
**7:5** So says the Lord Jehovah: An evil! An only evil! Behold, it has come!  
**7:6** An end has come, the end has come! It has awakened against you, behold, it has come!  
**7:7** The crown has come to you, <i>you</i> living in the land. The time has come, the day of tumult <i>is</i> near, and not a shout of the hills.  
**7:8** Now I will soon pour out My fury on you and fulfill My anger on you. And I will judge you according to your ways and will put on you all your disgusting things.  
**7:9** And My eye shall not spare, and I will not have pity; I will put on you according to your ways and your disgusting things that are in your midst. And you shall know that I <i>am</i> Jehovah who strikes.  
**7:10** Behold the day! Behold, it has come, the crown has gone out, the rod has blossomed, and pride has budded.  
**7:11** Violence has risen for a rod of wickedness. None of them <i>shall remain</i>, even none of their crowds, and none of their <i>possessions</i>, and none of distinction among them.  
**7:12** The time has come, the day has arrived. Do not let the buyer rejoice, and do not let the seller mourn, for wrath <i>is</i> on all her multitude.  
**7:13** For the seller shall not return to that which is sold, although they still are among the living (for the vision is to all her multitude, and it shall not return); and a man shall not hold his life strong in his iniquity.  
**7:14** They have blown the trumpet, and to make all ready, but no one goes to the battle, for My wrath is on all her crowds.  
**7:15** The sword <i>is</i> outside, and the plague, and the famine <i>are</i> inside. He who <i>is</i> in the field shall die with the sword. And he who <i>is</i> in the city, famine and plague shall devour him.  
**7:16** But <i>if</i> their fugitives shall escape, then they shall be on the mountains like doves of the valleys, all of them mourning, <i>each</i> man for his iniquity.  
**7:17** All hands shall be feeble, and all knees shall go <i>as</i> water.  
**7:18** They shall also gird <i>on</i> sackcloth, and trembling shall cover them. And shame <i>shall be</i> on all faces, and baldness on all heads.  
**7:19** They shall throw their silver in the broad places, and their gold shall be an impure thing. Their silver and their gold shall not be able to deliver them in the day of the wrath of Jehovah. They shall not satisfy their soul, and they shall not fill their bowels, for their iniquity has become a stumbling block for <i>them</i>.  
**7:20** And the beauty of His ornament, He set it in majesty. But they made it the images of their disgusting things, <i>and</i> of their detestable idols. Therefore, I have given it to them as an impurity.  
**7:21** And I will give it into the hand of the aliens for a prize, and to the wicked <i>one</i> of the earth for a prey. And they shall defile it.  
**7:22** I also will turn My face from them, and they shall defile My hiding <i>place</i>. And breakers shall enter into it and defile it.  
**7:23** Make the chain, for the land is full of bloody judgments, and the city is full of violence.  
**7:24** And I will make come the most evil of the nations, and they shall possess their houses. And I will make cease the pomp of the strong <i>ones</i>, and their holy places shall be defiled.  
**7:25** Anguish comes! And they shall seek peace, but none <i>shall be</i>.  
**7:26** Disaster on disaster shall come, and rumor to rumor shall be. And they shall seek a vision from the prophet, but the Law shall perish from the priest, and counsel from the elders.  
**7:27** The king shall mourn, and the ruler shall be clothed with despair. And the hands of the people of the land shall be anxious. According to their way, I will do to them, and according to their judgments, I will judge them. And they shall know that I <i>am</i> Jehovah.  

## 8장

**8:1** And it was in the sixth year, in the sixth <i>month</i>, on the fifth of the month, I was sitting in my house. And the elders of Judah were sitting before me. And the hand of the Lord Jehovah fell on me there.  
**8:2** And I looked, and behold, a likeness as the look of fire, from the appearance of His loins and downward, <i>like</i> fire, and from His loins and upward, as the look of brightness, like the color of polished metal.  
**8:3** And He put forth the form of a hand and took me by a lock of my head; and the Spirit lifted me up between the earth and the heavens and brought me to Jerusalem, in the visions of God, to the opening of the inner gate facing north, where there <i>was</i> a seat of the image of jealousy, which causes jealousy.  
**8:4** And, behold! The glory of the God of Israel <i>was</i> there, like the appearance which I saw in the plain.  
**8:5** And He said to me, Son of man, lift up your eyes now northward. And I lifted up my eyes the way of the north, and, behold, from the north, at the gate of the altar, <i>was</i> this image of jealousy at the entrance.  
**8:6** And He said to me, Son of man, do you see what they are doing, the great abominations which the house of Israel is doing here, that I should be far from My sanctuary? But you turn back <i>and</i> you shall see greater abominations.  
**8:7** And He brought me to the opening of the court. And I looked, and, behold, a single hole in the wall.  
**8:8** And He said to me, Son of man, dig now in the wall. And I dug in the wall; and, behold, an opening!  
**8:9** And He said to me, Go in and see the evil abominations that they are doing here.  
**8:10** And I went in and looked. And, behold, every form of creeping thing, and detested beast, and all the idols of the house of Israel were carved on the wall round <i>and</i> round.  
**8:11** And seventy men of the elders of the house of Israel, and Jaazaniah, the son of Shaphan, <i>were</i> standing among them. <i>These</i> were standing among them, and <i>each</i> man with his firepan in his hand, and the odor of the cloud of incense going up.  
**8:12** And He said to me, Son of man, have you seen what the elders of the house of Israel are doing in the dark, <i>each</i> man in the rooms of his image? For they are saying, Jehovah does not see us, Jehovah has forsaken the earth.  
**8:13** And He said to me, Yet you turn back, you shall yet see greater abominations which they are doing.  
**8:14** And He brought me to the opening of the gate of the house of Jehovah on the north. And, behold, women were sitting there weeping for Tammuz.
