# 64.요한3서 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** The elder to Gaius the beloved, whom I love in truth.  
**1:2** Beloved <i>one</i>, in regard to all things, I pray <i>for</i> you to prosper and to be in health, even as your soul prospers.  
**1:3** For I rejoiced greatly <i>at the</i> coming of <i>the</i> brothers, also bearing witness of you in the truth, as you walk in truth.  
**1:4** I have no greater joy than these things, that I hear my children are walking in truth.  
**1:5** Beloved <i>one</i>, you do a faithful <i>thing</i> whatever you work for the brothers and for the strangers,  
**1:6** who in love bore witness of you before <i>the</i> assembly, whom you will do well to send forward worthily of God.  
**1:7** For on behalf of His name they went out, taking nothing from the nations.  
**1:8** Therefore, we ought to entertain such <i>men</i>, that we may become co-workers with the truth.  
**1:9** I wrote to the assembly, but the <i>one</i> loving to be first of them, Diotrephes, does not receive us.  
**1:10** Because of this, if I come, I will recall his works which he does, slandering us with evil words. And not being satisfied <i>with</i> these, neither does he receive the brothers; and the <i>ones</i> purposing <i>it</i> he stops, and thrusts <i>them</i> out from the assembly.  
**1:11** Beloved <i>one</i>, do not imitate the bad, but the good. The <i>one</i> doing good is of God; but the <i>one</i> doing evil has not seen God.  
**1:12** Witness has been borne to Demetrius by all, and by the truth itself. And we also bear witness, and you know that our witness is true.  
**1:13** I had many things to write, but I do not desire to write by means of pen and ink.  
**1:14** But I am hoping to see you at once, and we will speak mouth to mouth. Peace to you. The friends greet you. Greet the friends by name.
