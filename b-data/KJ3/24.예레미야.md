# 24.예레미야 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** The words of Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah, <i>one</i> of the priests who <i>resided</i> in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin,  
**1:2** <i>to</i> whom the Word of Jehovah came in the days of Josiah the son of Amon, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.  
**1:3** And it happened in the days of Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, king of Judah, to the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah, the son of Josiah, king of Judah, to those gone into exile of Jerusalem in the fifth month.  
**1:4** And the Word of Jehovah was to me, saying,  
**1:5** I knew you before I formed you in the belly, even before you came out of the womb, I consecrated you. I gave you <i>to be</i> a prophet to the nations.  
**1:6** And I said, Alas, Lord Jehovah! Behold, I do not know <i>how</i> to speak. for I <i>am</i> a boy.  
**1:7** But Jehovah said to me, Do not say, I <i>am</i> a boy; for you shall go to all that I shall send you. And whatever I command you, you shall speak.  
**1:8** Do not be afraid of their faces, for I <i>am</i> with you to deliver you, a statement of Jehovah.  
**1:9** And Jehovah put out His hand and touched my mouth. And Jehovah said to me, Behold, I have put My words in your mouth.  
**1:10** Behold, I have today appointed you over the nations and over the kingdoms, to root out, and to tear down, and to destroy, and to cast down, to build, and to plant.  
**1:11** And the Word of Jehovah was to me, saying, Jeremiah, what do you see? And I said, I see an almond rod.  
**1:12** And Jehovah said to me, You have seen well; for I will watch over My Word to perform it.  
**1:13** And the Word of Jehovah was to me the second time, saying, What do you see? And I said, I see a pot being blown; and its face <i>is</i> from the face of the north.  
**1:14** And Jehovah said to me, Out of the north, evil will be set loose on all those living in the land.  
**1:15** For, behold, I will call all the families of the kingdoms of the north, a statement of Jehovah. And they shall come, and they will give to <i>each</i> man his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, and against its walls round about, and against the cities of Judah.  
**1:16** And I will speak My judgments against them on all their evil, <i>those</i> who have forsaken Me and have burned incense to other gods, and have worshiped the works of their hands.  
**1:17** And you must gird up your loins, and rise up and speak to them all that I command you. Do not be dismayed before their faces, that I not shatter you before them.  
**1:18** For, behold, today I have given you <i>as</i> a fortified city, and an iron pillar, and bronze walls against the whole land, against the kings of Judah, against her rulers, against her priests, and to the people of the land.  
**1:19** And they shall fight against you; but they shall not overcome you. For I <i>am</i> with you to deliver you, a statement of Jehovah.  

## 2장

**2:1** And was the Word of Jehovah to me, saying,  
**2:2** Go and cry in the ears of Jerusalem, saying, So says Jehovah, I remember you, the kindness of your youth, the love of your betrothals, your going after Me in the wilderness, in a land not sown.  
**2:3** Israel <i>was</i> holy to Jehovah, the firstfruits of His increase. All that devour him become guilty; evil shall come on them, a statement of Jehovah.  
**2:4** Hear the Word of Jehovah, O house of Jacob and all the families of the house of Israel.  
**2:5** So says Jehovah, What iniquity have your fathers found in Me, that they went far from Me and have walked after vanity, and have become vain?  
**2:6** And they did not say, Where <i>is</i> Jehovah who brought us up out of the land of Egypt, who led us in the wilderness, in a land of deserts and of pits, in a dry land and death shade, in a land that not a man has passed through, and not a man has lived there?  
**2:7** And I brought you into a garden land to eat its fruit and its goodness. But when you went in, you defiled My land and made My inheritance a disgusting thing.  
**2:8** The priests did not say, Where <i>is</i> Jehovah? And they who handle the Law did not know Me. And the shepherds transgressed against Me; and the prophets prophesied by Baal, and went after <i>things</i> not being profitable.  
**2:9** Therefore, I will contend with you, a statement of Jehovah, and I will contend with your sons' sons.  
**2:10** For go to the isles of Kittim and see, and send to Kedar, and carefully consider and see if there is any like this.  
**2:11** Has a nation changed its gods, and they were not gods? But My people have changed their Glory without it being profitable.  
**2:12** Be amazed at this, O heavens, and be horrified; be completely desolated, a statement of Jehovah.  
**2:13** For My people have done two evils: they have forsaken Me, the Fountain of living waters, to hew out cisterns for themselves, broken cisterns that can hold no water.  
**2:14** <i>Is</i> Israel a servant? Or <i>is</i> he a servant of the house? Why has he become a prey?  
**2:15** The young lions roared against him; they gave their voice. And they laid waste his land; his cities are burned, without one living <i>in it</i>.  
**2:16** Also the sons of Noph and Tahpanhes have fed on your crown.  
**2:17** Have you not done this to yourself by your forsaking Jehovah your God when He led you by the way?  
**2:18** And now what <i>is</i> for you toward the way of Egypt, to drink the waters of Sihor? Or what for you toward Assyria, to drink the waters of the <i>Euphrates</i> River?  
**2:19** Your own evil shall instruct you, and your apostasies shall reprove you. Therefore know and see that your forsaking Jehovah your God <i>is</i> evil and bitter; and My fear <i>is</i> not in you, a statement of the Lord Jehovah of Hosts.  
**2:20** For long ago you broke your yoke <i>and</i> tore up your bonds. And you said, I will not pass by, when on every high hill and under every green tree you lay down <i>like one</i> doing adultery.  
**2:21** Yet I planted you a choice vine, wholly a true seed. And how have you turned into shoots of an alien vine to Me?  
**2:22** Though you wash yourself with potash, and multiply soap for yourself, your iniquity is marked before Me, a statement of the Lord Jehovah.  
**2:23** How can you say, I am not defiled; I have not gone after Baals? See your way in the valley; know what you have done! <i>You are</i> a swift camel criss-crossing her ways;  
**2:24** a wild ass used to the wilderness. In the passion of her desire she snuffs up the wind <i>in</i> her time. Who can turn her away? All those who seek her will not tire themselves; in her month they will find her.  
**2:25** Withhold your foot from <i>being</i> bare and your throat from thirst. But you said, It is hopeless! No! For I love strangers, and after them I will go.  
**2:26** As the thief is ashamed when he is found, so is the house of Israel ashamed; they, their kings, their rulers, and their priests, and their prophets,  
**2:27** saying to a tree, You are my father; and to a stone, You gave us birth. For they turned <i>their</i> back to Me, and not the face. But in the time of their evil, they will say, Arise, and save us!  
**2:28** But where <i>are</i> your gods that you have made for yourselves? Let them arise, if they can save you in the time of your evil; for <i>according to</i> the number of your cities are your gods, O Judah.  
**2:29** Why do you contend with Me? All of you, you have transgressed against Me, a statement of Jehovah.  
**2:30** In vain I have stricken your sons; they took no correction. Your own sword has devoured your prophets, like a destroying lion.  
**2:31** O generation, see the Word of Jehovah. Have I been a wilderness to Israel, or a land of darkness? Why do My people say, We roam; we will come no more to You.  
**2:32** Can a virgin forget her ornaments, a bride her headband? Yet My people have forgotten Me days without number.  
**2:33** What? Do you trim your ways to seek love? Therefore you have even taught the evil <i>ones</i> your ways.  
**2:34** Also on your skirts is found the blood of the souls of the poor innocents; I have not found them breaking in, but on all these.  
**2:35** Yet you say, Because I am free from guilt, surely His anger shall turn from me. Behold, I will judge with you, because you say, I have not sinned.  
**2:36** Why do you go about so much to change your way? You also shall be ashamed of Egypt, as you were ashamed of Assyria.  
**2:37** Yes, you shall go out from this <i>place</i>, and your hands on your head. For Jehovah has rejected those in whom you trust, and you will not prosper by them.  

## 3장

**3:1** <i>They</i> say, If a man sends away his wife, and she goes from him and will be for another man, will he return to her again? Would not that land be greatly defiled? But you fornicate with many lovers; yet <i>would</i> you come back to Me, a statement of Jehovah.  
**3:2** Lift up your eyes on the bare heights and see. Where have you not been lain with? By the highways you have sat for them, like an Arab in the desert. And you have defiled the land with your fornications and with your evil.  
**3:3** And the showers are withheld, and there has been no latter rain. And the forehead of a woman, <i>one</i> doing adultery, was to you; you refused to be ashamed.  
**3:4** Have you not just now called to Me, <i>saying</i>, My father, <i>You are</i> the friend of my youth?  
**3:5** Will He keep <i>His anger</i> forever? Or will He guard it to the end? Behold, you have spoken, and you have done <i>all</i> the evil that you could.  
**3:6** And Jehovah said to me in the days of Josiah the king, Have you seen what the apostate Israel has done? She has gone up on every high hill and under every green tree, and has fornicated there.  
**3:7** And after she had done all these, I said, She will return to Me; but she did not return. And her treacherous sister Judah saw.  
**3:8** And I watched when for all the causes <i>for</i> which the apostate Israel committed adultery, I sent her away and I gave the writ of her divorce to her. Yet her treacherous sister Judah did not fear, but she also went and fornicated.  
**3:9** And it happened, from the vileness of her fornication she defiled the land, and fornicated with stones and with trees.  
**3:10** And yet for all this her treacherous sister Judah has not turned to Me with her whole heart, but with falsehood, a statement of Jehovah.  
**3:11** And Jehovah said to me, The apostate Israel has justified herself more than treacherous Judah.  
**3:12** Go and cry these words toward the north, and say, Return, O apostate Israel, a statement of Jehovah. I will not cause My face to fall on you, for I <i>am</i> holy, a statement of Jehovah; I will not keep <i>anger</i> forever.  
**3:13** Only know your iniquity, that you have transgressed against Jehovah your God and have scattered your ways to strangers under every green tree, and you have not obeyed My voice, a statement of Jehovah.  
**3:14** Turn back, O apostate sons, a statement of Jehovah; for I am Lord over you. And I will take you, one from a city, and two from a family, and I will bring you to Zion.  
**3:15** And I will give you shepherds according to My heart, who will feed you <i>with</i> knowledge and understanding.  
**3:16** And it will be, when you multiply and increase in the land in those days, a statement of Jehovah, they will no longer say, The ark of the covenant of Jehovah! And it shall not come on the heart, and not shall they remember it, and not they shall miss <i>it</i>, and it shall not be made any more.  
**3:17** At that time they shall call Jerusalem the throne of Jehovah. And all nations shall be gathered to it, to the name of Jehovah, to Jerusalem. And they shall not walk any more after the stubbornness of their evil heart.  
**3:18** In those days the house of Judah shall walk with the house of Israel, and they shall come together out of the land of the north, to the land that I have caused your fathers to inherit.  
**3:19** But I said, How <i>gladly</i> would I put you among the sons, and give you a pleasant land, a beautiful inheritance <i>among</i> the multitudes of nations! And I said, You shall call Me, My Father; and you shall not turn away from following Me.  
**3:20** Surely <i>as</i> a wife acts treacherously from her companion, so you have acted treacherously with Me, O house of Israel, a statement of Jehovah.  
**3:21** A voice was heard weeping on the bare heights, pleadings of the sons of Israel; for they have perverted their way; they have forgotten Jehovah their God.  
**3:22** Return, O apostate sons; I will heal your apostasies. Behold, we come to You, for You <i>are</i> Jehovah our God.  
**3:23** Truly, for delusion <i>comes</i> from the hills, a murmur <i>on</i> the mountains. Truly, in Jehovah our God is the salvation of Israel.  
**3:24** For the <i>things</i> of shame have eaten up the labor of our fathers from our youth, their flocks and their herds, their sons and their daughters.  
**3:25** We lie down in our shame, and our confusion covers us. For we have sinned against Jehovah our God, we and our fathers, from our youth even to this day. And we have not obeyed the voice of Jehovah our God.  

## 4장

**4:1** If you will return, O Israel, a statement of Jehovah, return to Me. And if you will turn away from your detestable things out of My face and will not wander ,  
**4:2** and you will swear, <i>As</i> Jehovah lives, in truth, in judgment, and in righteousness; and the nations shall bless themselves in Him, and in Him they will glory.  
**4:3** For so says Jehovah to the men of Judah and to Jerusalem, Break up your fallow ground, and do not sow to the thorns.  
**4:4** Circumcise yourselves to Jehovah, and take away the foreskin of your heart, O men of Judah and those living <i>in</i> Jerusalem, lest My fury go forth like fire and burn, so that no one can quench <i>it</i>, because of the evil of your doings.  
**4:5** Declare in Judah, and make <i>it</i> heard in Jerusalem, and say, Blow the rams' horn in the land. Cry, and say, It will be ended. Gather yourselves and go into the fortified cities.  
**4:6** Lift up a banner toward Zion. Flee, seek refuge, and do not stand. For I will bring evil from the north and a great shattering.  
**4:7** The lion has come up from his thicket, and a destroyer of nations has set out. He has left his place to make your land a waste. Your cities will fall into ruins without <i>one</i> living <i>in them</i>.  
**4:8** Put on sackcloth for this, wail and howl. For the the anger of Jehovah has not turned back from us.  
**4:9** And it will be on that day, a statement of Jehovah, the heart of the king, and the heart of the rulers shall perish. And the priests shall be amazed; and the prophets shall be astounded.  
**4:10** Then I said, Ah, Lord Jehovah! Surely deceiving You have deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, You shall have peace, but the sword reaches to the soul.  
**4:11** At that time it shall be said to this people and to Jerusalem, A hot wind <i>from</i> the bare hills in the desert toward the daughter of My people, not to winnow and not to cleanse!  
**4:12** A wind more full than these shall come for Me. Now I also will speak judgments against them.  
**4:13** Behold, he shall come up like clouds, and his chariots like a windstorm. His horses are swifter than eagles. Woe to us, for we are plundered!  
**4:14** O Jerusalem, cleanse your heart from evil that you may be saved. Until when will your thoughts of iniquity lodge within you?  
**4:15** For a voice announces from Dan, and proclaims iniquity from Mount Ephraim.  
**4:16** Recall <i>it</i> to the nations: Behold, proclaim against Jerusalem, Besiegers are coming from a distant land and give forth their voice against the cities of Judah.  
**4:17** Like the men watching a field, they are against her round about because she has rebelled against Me, a statement of Jehovah.  
**4:18** Your way and your doings have done these <i>things</i> to you; <i>in</i> this you have done evil, because it is bitter, because it reaches to your heart.  
**4:19** My bowels! My bowels! I convulse in pain. O walls of my heart! My heart roars within me. I cannot be silent, for I have heard the sound of the rams' horn. O my soul, the alarm of war!  
**4:20** Ruin on ruin has been called; for the whole land is devastated. Suddenly my tents are devastated, my curtains <i>in</i> a moment.  
**4:21** Until when must I see the banner <i>and</i> hear the sound of the rams' horn?  
**4:22** For My people are foolish. They do not know Me. They are stupid children, and they have no understanding. They <i>are</i> wise to do evil, but they do not know to do good.  
**4:23** I looked on the earth, and, behold! <i>It was</i> formlessness and void; and to the heavens, and they <i>had</i> no light.  
**4:24** I looked on the mountains, and, behold! They quaked, and all the hills were shaken.  
**4:25** I looked, and, behold! There <i>was</i> no man, and all the birds of the heavens had fled.  
**4:26** I looked, and, behold! The fruitful place <i>was</i> a wilderness, and all its cities were broken down before the face of Jehovah, before the anger of His nostrils.  
**4:27** For so Jehovah has said, The whole land shall be a desolation; but <i>it</i> will not be a full ending <i>that</i> I make.  
**4:28** The earth shall mourn for this, and the heavens above shall grow black, because I have seen, I have purposed and I will not repent, and I will not turn back from it.  
**4:29** Every city shall flee from the noise of the horsemen and shooters of the bow. They shall go into thickets and go up in the rocks. Every city shall be forsaken, and not a man shall live in them.  
**4:30** And you, one being desolated, what will you do? Though you clothe yourself with crimson, though you adorn yourself with ornaments of gold, though you make large your eyes with paint, you beautify yourself in vain. Lovers despise you; they will seek your life.  
**4:31** For I have heard a voice as of one weakened, the anguish as one bearing her first child, the voice of Zion's daughter gasping and spreading her palms, <i>saying</i>, Woe <i>is</i> to me now, for my soul faints because of murderers!

**5:20** Declare this in the house of Jacob and make it heard in Judah, saying,

**5:29** Shall I not call to account for these <i>things</i>, a statement of Jehovah? Or shall not My soul be avenged on a nation that is like this?

**5:31** the prophets prophesy falsely, and the priests rule by their hands; and My people love <i>it</i> so, and what will they do at the end of it?  

## 6장

**6:1** O sons of Benjamin take refuge from the midst of Jerusalem. And blow the rams' horn in Tekoa; and set up a signal over Beth-haccerem. For evil looks down out of the north, and a great shattering.  
**6:2** I will destroy the daughter of Zion, the beautiful and delicate one.  
**6:3** Those feeding shall come to her with their flocks; they shall pitch tents on her all around. They shall <i>each</i> man feed <i>in</i> his hand.  
**6:4** Consecrate war against her. Rise up and let us go up at noon. Woe to us, for the day turns; for the shadows of the evening are stretched out!  
**6:5** Rise up and let us go up by night and destroy her fortresses.  
**6:6** For so Jehovah of Hosts has said, Cut down her trees and pour out a <i>siege</i>-mound against Jerusalem. She <i>is</i> the city to be called to account; in her midst <i>is</i> oppression, all of her.  
**6:7** As a cistern keeps fresh its waters, so she keeps fresh her evil. Violence and destruction are heard in her. Sickness and wounds <i>are</i> continually before My face.  
**6:8** O Jerusalem, be taught lest My soul be alienated from you; lest I make you a desolation, a land with not <i>one</i> living <i>in it</i>.  
**6:9** So says Jehovah of Hosts, Gleaning they shall glean the remnant of Israel like a vine <i>and</i> turn back your hand as a grape gatherer over the tendrils.  
**6:10** To whom shall I speak and give warning and they may hear? Behold, their ears <i>are</i> not circumcised, and they are not able to pay attention-. Behold, the Word of Jehovah is a reproach to them; they have no delight in it.  
**6:11** And I am full of the fury of Jehovah; I am weary with holding in. Pour it out on the child in the street and on the circle of the young men together. For even the husband with the wife shall be taken, the elder with fullness of days.  
**6:12** And their houses shall be turned to others, fields and wives together. For I will stretch out My hand on those living <i>in</i> the land, a statement of Jehovah.  
**6:13** For everyone from the least of them even to the greatest of them cuts off a profit. And from the prophet even to the priest, everyone deals a lie.  
**6:14** They have also healed the break of My people slightly, saying, Peace, peace, when there is no peace.  
**6:15** Were they ashamed when they made a disgusting thing? Yea, they were not at all ashamed, nor did they know to blush. So they shall fall among those falling. At the time I call them to account, says Jehovah.  
**6:16** So says Jehovah, Stand by the ways and see, and ask for the old paths, where the good way <i>is</i>, and walk in it; and you shall find rest for your souls. But they said, We will not walk <i>in it</i>.  
**6:17** And I raised watchmen over you, <i>saying</i>, Attend to the sound of the rams' horn; but they said, We will not listen.  
**6:18** So hear, O nations, and know, O assembly, that which is <i>coming</i> on them.  
**6:19** Hear, O earth, behold, I will bring evil on this people, the fruit of their thoughts; for they have not paid attention to My words; and My Law, they also rejected it.  
**6:20** Why <i>is</i> this to Me; frankincense comes from Sheba, and the good cane from a land afar off? Your burnt offerings <i>are</i> not for acceptance, and your sacrifices <i>are</i> not sweet to Me.
