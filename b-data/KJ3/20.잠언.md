# 20.잠언 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** The Proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel:  
**1:2** To know wisdom and instruction; to discern the words of understanding;  
**1:3** to receive instruction in prudence, righteousness, and judgment, and uprightness;  
**1:4** to give sense to the <i>one of</i> simplicity, knowledge and discretion to the young man.  
**1:5** The wise <i>ones</i> hear and increase learning, and he understanding gets wise counsel,  
**1:6** to understand a proverb and an enigma; the words of the wise <i>ones</i>, and their riddles.  
**1:7** The fear of Jehovah <i>is</i> the beginning of knowledge; foolish <i>ones</i> despise wisdom and instruction.  
**1:8** My son, hear your father's instruction, and do not forsake the law of your mother;  
**1:9** for they <i>shall be</i> an ornament of grace to your head, and chains for your neck.  
**1:10** My son, if sinners lure you, do not be willing.  
**1:11** If they say, Walk with us, let us lie in wait for blood, let us secretly lurk for the innocent <i>ones</i> without cause,  
**1:12** let us swallow them up alive as Sheol; and whole, as those going down <i>into</i> the Pit,  
**1:13** we shall find all precious wealth; we shall fill our houses <i>with</i> plunder;  
**1:14** cast in your lot among us; one purse shall be to all of us.  
**1:15** My son, do not walk in the way with them; hold back your foot from their path;  
**1:16** for their feet run to evil and they haste to shed blood.  
**1:17** For in vain the net is spread in the sight of every lord of the wing.  
**1:18** And they lie in wait for their <i>own</i> blood, they are hiding for their own souls.  
**1:19** So <i>are</i> the ways of everyone who gains <i>unjust</i> gain; it takes away its owner's soul.  
**1:20** Wisdom cries aloud outside; she gives her voice in the square;  
**1:21** she calls at the head of roaring <i>places,</i> at the opening of the gates, in the city she speaks her words:  
**1:22** Until when, <i>ones of</i> simplicity, will you love simplicity? And will scorning <i>ones</i> desire scorning for themselves? And will fools hate knowledge?  
**1:23** Turn back at My warning; behold, I will pour out My Spirit to you; I will make My words known to you.  
**1:24** Because I called, and you refused; I stretch out a hand, and not <i>one</i> pays attention,  
**1:25** but you have ignored all My counsel, and you did not desire My warning.  
**1:26** I also will laugh in your calamity, I will mock when your dread comes;  
**1:27** when your dread comes like a storm, and your calamity arrives like a tempest, when distress and anguish come on you,  
**1:28** then they shall call on Me, and I will not answer; they shall seek Me early, but they shall not find Me.  
**1:29** Instead, they hated knowledge and chose not the fear of Jehovah.  
**1:30** They did not desire My counsel; they despised all My reproof,  
**1:31** and they shall eat of the fruit of their own way and be filled with their own devices.  
**1:32** For the going astray of the <i>ones of</i> simplicity kills them, and the ease of fools destroys them.  
**1:33** But he who listens to Me shall live securely and shall be at ease from the dread of evil.  

## 2장

**2:1** My son, if you will receive My words, and treasure up My commandments with you,  
**2:2** so that your ear attends to wisdom, you shall stretch out your heart to understanding.  
**2:3** For if you cry for discernment, giving forth your voice for understanding;  
**2:4** if you seek her as silver, and search <i>for</i> her as hidden treasures,  
**2:5** then you shall understand the fear of Jehovah and find knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For Jehovah gives wisdom; out of His mouth <i>are</i> knowledge and understanding.  
**2:7** He lays up sound wisdom for the upright <i>ones</i>; <i>He is</i> a shield to those who walk in integrity,  
**2:8** to guard the paths of judgment, and He guards the way of His godly <i>ones</i>.  
**2:9** Then you shall understand righteousness and judgment and uprightness; <i>yea</i>, every good path.  
**2:10** When wisdom enters into your heart, and knowledge is pleasant to your soul,  
**2:11** discretion shall keep you, understanding shall watch over you;  
**2:12** to deliver you from the evil way, from the man speaking perversity;  
**2:13** those who leave the paths of uprightness to walk in the ways of darkness,  
**2:14** rejoicing to do evil; they delight in the perversity of the evil <i>one,  
**2:15** whose paths <i>are</i> crooked and being devious in their tracks,  
**2:16** <i>these</i> will deliver you from the strange woman, from the foreign <i>woman</i> flattering <i>by</i> her words,  
**2:17** who forsakes the guide of her youth, and forgets the covenant of her God.  
**2:18** For her house leads down to death, and her tracks to the departed spirits.  
**2:19** All going in to her do not return, and not do they reach the paths of life.  
**2:20** Then you may walk in the ways of the good and keep the ways of the righteous <i>ones</i>.  
**2:21** For the upright <i>ones</i> shall live <i>in</i> the land, and the blameless shall remain in it;  
**2:22** but the wicked <i>ones</i> shall be cut off from the earth, and <i>ones</i> acting deceitfully shall be rooted out of it.  

## 3장

**3:1** My son, do not forget My law, but let your heart guard My commands,  
**3:2** for they shall add length of days and years of life and peace to you.  
**3:3** Mercy and truth will not forsake you; tie them on your neck; write them on the tablet of your heart;  
**3:4** and you shall find favor and good understanding in the sight of God and man.  
**3:5** Trust in Jehovah with all your heart, and lean not to your <i>own</i> understanding;  
**3:6** in all your ways acknowledge Him, and He shall direct your paths.  
**3:7** Do not be wise in your own eyes; fear Jehovah and turn from evil.  
**3:8** Healing shall be to your navel and marrow to your bones.  
**3:9** Honor Jehovah with your substance, and with the firstfruits of all your increase,  
**3:10** and your storerooms shall be filled <i>with</i> plenty, and your wine-vats shall burst <i>with</i> new wine.  
**3:11** My son, do not reject the chastening of Jehovah, and do not loathe His chastisement;  
**3:12** for whom Jehovah loves He corrects, even as a father <i>corrects</i> the son with whom he is pleased.  
**3:13** Happy <i>is</i> the man who finds wisdom, and the man who gets understanding.  
**3:14** For better <i>is</i> its profit than the gain of silver, and its produce more than fine gold;  
**3:15** she <i>is</i> more precious than jewels, and all the things you can desire are not to be compared with her.  
**3:16** Length of days <i>is</i> in her right hand; riches and honor in her left hand.  
**3:17** Her ways <i>are</i> ways of pleasantness, and all her paths peace.  
**3:18** She <i>is</i> a tree of life to the ones who lay hold on her, and blessed <i>are</i> the ones holding her fast.  
**3:19** Jehovah founded the earth by wisdom; He founded the heavens by understanding;  
**3:20** the depths were broken up by His knowledge, and the clouds dropped down the dew.  
**3:21** My son, do not let them depart from your eyes; keep sound wisdom and judgment,  
**3:22** and they shall be life to your soul, and grace to your neck;  
**3:23** then you shall walk in your way safely, and your foot shall not stumble.  
**3:24** When you lie down, you shall not dread; yea, you shall lie down and your sleep shall be sweet.  
**3:25** Do not be afraid of sudden dread, and of the ruin of the wicked <i>ones</i> when it comes.  
**3:26** For Jehovah shall be at your loins, and He shall keep your foot from capture.  
**3:27** Do not withhold good from its owners, when it is in the power of your hand to do <i>it</i>.  
**3:28** Do not say to your neighbor, Go and return, or, Tomorrow I will give, when it is with you.  
**3:29** Do not plot evil against your neighbor, and he lives securely by you.  
**3:30** Do not strive with a man without cause, if he has dealt you no evil.  
**3:31** Do not be envious of the man of violence, and choose not among all his ways.  
**3:32** For <i>one</i> being devious <i>is</i> a disgusting thing to Jehovah, but His counsel <i>is</i> with the upright <i>ones</i>.  
**3:33** The curse of Jehovah <i>is</i> in the house of the wicked <i>one</i>, but He blesses the abode of the righteous <i>ones</i>.  
**3:34** If He will scorn at those scorning, yet He will give grace to the humble <i>one</i>.  
**3:35** The wise <i>ones</i> inherit honor, but fools exalt shame.  

## 4장

**4:1** Sons, hear the instruction of a father, pay attention to know understanding.  
**4:2** For I give you good teaching, do not forsake my Law.  
**4:3** For I was my father's son, tender and an only <i>one</i> in the sight of my mother.  
**4:4** And he taught me and said to me, May my words hold to your heart; keep my commands and live.  
**4:5** Obtain wisdom, and with all your obtaining, obtain understanding; do not stretch out, and do not stretch away from the words of my mouth.  
**4:6** Do not forsake her, and she will preserve you; love her, and she will keep you.  
**4:7** The first thing <i>is</i> wisdom; obtain wisdom, and with all your obtaining, obtain understanding.  
**4:8** Exalt her, and she will lift you up; she shall bring you to honor when you embrace her.  
**4:9** She shall give a wreath of grace to your head; she shall shield you with a crown of glory.  
**4:10** Oh my son, hear and receive my sayings, and the years of your life <i>shall be</i> many.  
**4:11** I have taught you in the way of wisdom; I have made you walk in the right tracks;  
**4:12** when you go, your tracks shall not be narrowed, and if you run, you shall not stumble.  
**4:13** Take fast hold of instruction, do not let go; keep her, for she <i>is</i> your life.  
**4:14** Do not enter into the path of the wicked <i>ones</i>, and do not go straight in the way of evil <i>ones  
**4:15** Avoid it, do not pass by it; turn from it and pass on.  
**4:16** For they do not sleep if they have done no evil, and their sleep is taken away if not they cause <i>some</i> to stumble.  
**4:17** For they eat the bread of wickedness, and drink the wine of violence.  
**4:18** But the path of the righteous <i>ones is</i> as a light of brightness going on and shining till the day is established.  
**4:19** The way of the wicked <i>ones is</i> as darkness; they do not know at what they stumble.  
**4:20** My son, pay attention to my words; stretch your ear to what I say;  
**4:21** let them not depart from your eyes; keep them in the center of your heart;  
**4:22** for they <i>are</i> life to those who find them, and healing to all his flesh.  
**4:23** Keep your heart with all diligence, for out of it <i>are</i> the issues of life.  
**4:24** Turn away from you the crooked mouth, and put devious lips far from you.  
**4:25** Let your eyes look straight to the front, and let your eyelids look straight before you.  
**4:26** Ponder the track of your feet, then all your ways <i>will be</i> established.  
**4:27** Do not stretch to the right or to the left; turn your foot aside from evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** My son, pay attention to my wisdom; stretch out your ear to my understanding;  
**5:2** to keep discretion, and your lips may keep knowledge.  
**5:3** For the lips of a strange woman drip honey, and her palate <i>is</i> smoother than oil,  
**5:4** but afterwards she <i>is</i> bitter <i>as</i> wormwood, sharp as a sword of mouths;  
**5:5** her feet go down to death; her steps take hold on Sheol,  
**5:6** lest you ponder on the path of life, her tracks are unstable, you cannot know <i>them</i>.  
**5:7** And hear me now, O sons, and do not depart from the words of my mouth.  
**5:8** Remove your way far from her, and do not come near to the door of her house,  
**5:9** lest you give your vigor to others, and your years to the cruel <i>one</i>;  
**5:10** that strangers not be filled <i>with</i> your strength, and your labors <i>be</i> in the house of a foreigner,  
**5:11** and you groan when your end <i>comes</i>, when your flesh and muscle are used up,  
**5:12** and say, How I have hated instruction, and my heart despised correction;  
**5:13** and I have not heard the voice of my teachers, nor stretched out my ear to those teaching me.  
**5:14** In a little I was in every evil <i>thing</i> in the midst of the congregation and assembly.  
**5:15** Drink waters out of your own cistern, and running waters out of your own well.  
**5:16** Should your overflowing springs be dispersed outside, <i>like</i> rivers of waters in the streets?  
**5:17** Let them be for you alone, and not to strangers with you;  
**5:18** let your fountain be blessed, and rejoice with the wife of your youth;  
**5:19** <i>she is</i> a loving deer, a graceful doe; let her breasts satisfy you at every time, always be ravished in her love.  
**5:20** And my son, why will you be ravished with a strange woman, and embrace the bosom of a foreigner?  
**5:21** For the ways of man <i>are</i> before the eyes of Jehovah, and He ponders all his tracks.  
**5:22** His own iniquities shall capture him, the wicked <i>one</i>, and he shall be held with the cords of his sin.  
**5:23** He shall die without instruction, and in the greatness of his foolishness he shall go astray.  

## 6장

**6:1** My son, <i>if</i> you are surety for your friend, <i>if</i> you struck your palms with an alien,  
**6:2** you are snared with the words of your mouth; you are captured with the words of your own mouth.  
**6:3** My son, do this then, and save yourself when you come into the palm of your friend: go, humble yourself and be bold <i>to</i> your friend.  
**6:4** Do not give sleep to your eyes, or slumber to your eyelids.  
**6:5** Deliver yourself like a gazelle from a hand, and as a bird from the fowler's hand.  
**6:6** Go to the ant, lazy man; see her ways and be wise;  
**6:7** who, having no leader, overseer or ruler,  
**6:8** provides her bread in the summer, <i>and</i> gathers her food in the harvest.  
**6:9** Until when will you lie down, O lazy man? When will you arise out of your sleep?  
**6:10** A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to lie down;  
**6:11** so shall your poverty come as one walking about, and your need like a man of a shield.  
**6:12** A worthless man, an evil man, walks with crookedness of mouth,  
**6:13** winking with his eyes, speaking with his feet, teaching with his fingers.  
**6:14** Perversity <i>is</i> in his heart; he <i>is</i> plotting evil <i>things</i> at every time, he sends out strife.  
**6:15** On account of this, calamity shall come suddenly; he is broken quickly and there is no healing.  
**6:16** These six <i>things</i> Jehovah hates; yea, seven <i>are</i> disgusting things to his soul;  
**6:17** a proud look, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood,  
**6:18** a heart that devises iniquity, feet hurrying to run to evil <i>things</i>,  
**6:19** a lying witness who breathes lies, and he who causes strife among brothers.  
**6:20** My son, keep your father's commands, and do not forsake the law of your mother.  
**6:21** Bind them to your heart continually; tie them around your neck.  
**6:22** When you go, it shall lead you; when you sleep, it shall watch over you; and <i>when</i> you awaken, it will muse <i>with</i> you.  
**6:23** For the commandment <i>is</i> a lamp, and the law a light; and reproofs of instruction <i>are</i> a way of life,  
**6:24** to keep you from the evil woman, from tongue of flattery of the strange woman.  
**6:25** Do not lust after her beauty in your heart, and do not let her take you with her eyelids,  
**6:26** For through a woman committing adultery, <i>a man comes</i> to the <i>last</i> loaf of bread, and <i>another</i> man's wife hunts for the precious soul.  
**6:27** Can a man take fire into his bosom and his clothes not be burned?  
**6:28** Or can a man walk on hot coals and his feet not be burned?  
**6:29** So is he who goes in to his neighbor's wife; everyone touching her shall not be free <i>from guilt</i>.  
**6:30** They do not despise a thief, if he steals to fill his appetite when he is hungry.  
**6:31** But <i>if</i> he is found, he shall restore sevenfold; he shall give all the goods of his house.  
**6:32** He who commits adultery <i>with</i> a woman lacks heart; he <i>who</i> does it <i>is</i> a destroyer of his own soul.  
**6:33** He shall find a wound and dishonor, and his reproach shall not be wiped away.  
**6:34** For jealousy <i>is</i> the rage of a man, and he will not spare in the day of vengeance.  
**6:35** He will not lift up the face of every ransom, nor will he consent when you multiply the bribes.  

## 7장

**7:1** My son, keep my words and treasure up My commands within you.  
**7:2** Keep My commands and live, and <i>keep</i> My Law as the pupil of your eye.  
**7:3** Bind them on your fingers; write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to wisdom, You <i>are</i> my sister, and call understanding <i>your</i> kinswoman,  
**7:5** to keep you from the strange woman, from the foreign <i>one</i> with her flattering words.  
**7:6** For at the window of my house I looked through my lattice,  
**7:7** and I saw in the <i>one</i>s of simplicity, I observed among the sons a young man lacking heart,  
**7:8** passing through the street near her corner; and he stepped the way to her house,  
**7:9** in the twilight, in the evening, in the black and darkness of night.  
**7:10** And, behold, a woman to meet him, <i>in</i> the dress of <i>one</i> doing adultery, and a guarded heart;  
**7:11** she <i>is</i> loud and stubborn; her feet do not abide in her own house.  
**7:12** <i>At this</i> time <i>she is</i> outside, now in the streets, and <i>she</i> lies in wait at every corner;  
**7:13** and she seizes him and kisses him; she hardens her face and says to him,  
**7:14** Sacrifices of peace offerings are on me, today I have completed my vows;  
**7:15** so I came out to meet you, early to seek your face, and I have found you.  
**7:16** I have spread my couch <i>with</i> coverings, <i>with</i> striped cloths of Egyptian linen.  
**7:17** I have sprinkled my bed with myrrh, aloes and cinnamon.  
**7:18** Come, let us take our fill of love until the morning, let us delight ourselves with caresses.  
**7:19** For my husband <i>is</i> not in his house; he is going in the way to a distant <i>place</i>.  
**7:20** He has taken a bag of silver in his hand; at the day of the full moon he will enter his house.  
**7:21** With her much persuasiveness she turned him aside; with the flattering of her lips she forced him,  
**7:22** going after her at once, as an ox goes to the slaughter; or as <i>one</i> in fetters goes to the correction of a foolish <i>one</i>,  
**7:23** until an arrow strikes through his liver, like a bird hastening to the snare, and not knowing that it <i>is</i> for his soul.  
**7:24** And now listen to Me, O sons, and attend to the words of My mouth:  
**7:25** Do not let your heart turn aside to her ways; do not go astray in her paths.  
**7:26** For many <i>are</i> the wounded she has caused to fall, and numerous all the ones being slain by her.  
**7:27** The ways of Sheol <i>are</i> in her house, leading down to the rooms of death.  

## 8장

**8:1** Does not Wisdom call? And does not understanding speak?  
**8:2** She stands at the head of high places, on the way between the paths,  
**8:3** at the side of the gates; at the mouth of the city, <i>at</i> the doors, she cries.  
**8:4** I call to you, O men, and My voice <i>is</i> to the sons of men.  
**8:5** Understand prudence, <i>one</i>s of simplicity; and fools, be of an understanding heart.  
**8:6** Hear, for I will speak of excellent things, and from the opening of My lips <i>shall be</i> right things.  
**8:7** For My mouth shall speak of truth, and a disgusting thing <i>is</i> wickedness to My lips.  
**8:8** In righteousness are all the sayings of My mouth there <i>is</i> not a distorted or perverse <i>thing</i> in them;  
**8:9** they <i>are</i> all straight to those discerning and <i>to</i> the upright <i>ones,</i> to those who find knowledge.  
**8:10** Receive My instruction, and not silver; and knowledge, rather than choice gold.  
**8:11** For wisdom <i>is</i> better than jewels, and all delights cannot be compared to it.  
**8:12** I, Wisdom, dwell with prudence, and I find knowledge of discretions.  
**8:13** The fear of Jehovah <i>is</i> to hate evil; I hate pride and loftiness, and the evil way, and the perverse mouth.  
**8:14** To Me <i>is</i> counsel and sound wisdom; I <i>am</i> understanding; to Me is strength.  
**8:15** By Me kings reign, and leaders decree righteousness.  
**8:16** Rulers and nobles rule by Me, and all the judges of the earth.  
**8:17** I love those who love Me, and those who seek Me early find Me.  
**8:18** Riches and honor <i>are</i> with Me; enduring wealth and righteousness.  
**8:19** My fruit <i>is</i> better than gold, yea, than fine gold; and My increase <i>is better than</i> the best silver.  
**8:20** I walk in the path of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of justice;  
**8:21** to cause those who love Me to inherit wealth, and I will fill up their treasuries.  
**8:22** Jehovah possessed Me in the beginning of His way, from then, before His works.  
**8:23** I was anointed from everlasting, from before the earth.  
**8:24** When there were no depths, I was brought forth; when <i>there were</i> no springs heavy <i>with</i> water.  
**8:25** Before the mountains were sunk, before the hills, I was brought forth;  
**8:26** before He had made the earth and the fields, or the head of the dust of the world.  
**8:27** In His preparing the heavens, I <i>was</i> there; in His decreeing a circle on the face of the deep,  
**8:28** in His forming the clouds above, in <i>His</i> making the strong springs of the deep,  
**8:29** in His setting its limit for the sea, that the waters should not pass beyond <i>His</i> command; in His decreeing the foundations of the earth,  
**8:30** then I was at His side, like a master workman; and I was <i>His</i> delights day <i>by</i> day, rejoicing before Him <i>at</i> every time;  
**8:31** rejoicing in the world, His earth; and My delight <i>was</i> with the sons of men.  
**8:32** And now listen to Me, O sons, for blessed <i>are those who</i> keep My ways.  
**8:33** Hear instruction, and be wise, and do not let go.  
**8:34** Blessed <i>is</i> the man listening to Me, watching day <i>by</i> day at My gates, guarding at the posts of My doors.  
**8:35** For the one finding Me finds life, and he shall draw out favor from Jehovah.  
**8:36** But he who sins against Me wrongs his own soul; all who hate Me love death.  

## 9장

**9:1** Wisdom has built her house; she has carved out her seven pillars;  
**9:2** she has slaughtered her slaughter; she has mixed her wine; she has also set her table.  
**9:3** She has sent out her maidens; she cries on the heights of the city;  
**9:4** <i>One</i> who is of simplicity, turn in here; <i>one</i> lacking heart, she says to him,  
**9:5** Come, eat of my bread, and drink of the wine I have mixed.  
**9:6** Forsake simplicity, and live, and go straight in the way of understanding.  
**9:7** He rebuking one scorning takes shame for himself, and one reproving a wicked <i>one has</i> his own blemish.  
**9:8** Do not reprove one scorning, lest he hate you; reprove a wise <i>one</i>, and he will love you.  
**9:9** Give to a wise <i>one</i>, and he will be more wise; cause a just <i>one</i> to know and he will add <i>to</i> learning.  
**9:10** The fear of Jehovah <i>is</i> the beginning of wisdom, and the knowledge of the Holy Ones <i>is</i> understanding.  
**9:11** For by Me your days shall increase and shall be added to you the years of life.  
**9:12** If you are wise, you shall be wise for yourself, but <i>if</i> you scorn, you alone shall bear <i>it</i>.  
**9:13** A woman of foolishness <i>is</i> noisy; <i>one of</i> simplicity, and she knows not what.  
**9:14** And she sits at the door of her house, in a seat in the high places of the city,  
**9:15** to call those passing in the way, who are going straight <i>on</i> their paths:  
**9:16** <i>One of</i> simplicity turn aside here. And to the one lacking heart, she says to him:  
**9:17** Stolen waters are sweet, and bread <i>eaten</i> in secret is pleasant.  
**9:18** But he does not know that the departed spirits <i>are</i> there; In the depths of Sheol <i>are</i> her called <i>ones</i>.  

## 10장

**10:1** The proverbs of Solomon: A wise son makes a father rejoice, but a son who <i>is</i> a fool <i>is</i> a mother's sorrow.  
**10:2** Treasures of wickedness do not profit, but righteousness delivers from death.

**31:19** She has sent forth her hands on the distaff, and her palms have held the spindle.  
**31:20** She spreads out her palms to afflicted <i>ones</i>, yea, she stretches out her hands to the needy <i>ones</i>.  
**31:21** She does not fear for her household from the snow, for all her household <i>are</i> clothed <i>with</i> scarlet.  
**31:22** She makes herself coverlets; her clothing <i>is</i> bleached <i>linen</i> and purple.  
**31:23** Her husband is known in the gates when he sits with the elders of the land.  
**31:24** She makes fine linen garments, and sells, and she delivers girdles to the merchant.  
**31:25** Strength and honor <i>are</i> her clothing, and she laughs at the day to come.  
**31:26** She opens her mouth in wisdom, and the law of kindness <i>is</i> on her tongue.  
**31:27** She watches the ways of her household, and does not eat the bread of idleness.  
**31:28** Her children rise up and call her blessed, her husband <i>also</i>, for he praises her:  
**31:29** Many <i>are</i> the daughters who act with virtue, but you rise above them all!  
**31:30** Charm <i>may be</i> deceitful, and beauty vanity, <i>but</i> a woman who fears Jehovah, she shall be praised.  
**31:31** Give her of the fruit of her hands, and let her works praise her in the gates.
