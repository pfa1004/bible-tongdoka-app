# 46.고린도전서 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul, a called apostle of Jesus Christ, by <i>the</i> will of God, and Sosthenes the brother,  
**1:2** to the assembly of God which is in Corinth, those having been sanctified in Christ Jesus, called saints, with all the <i>ones</i> calling on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ in every place, both theirs and ours:  
**1:3** Grace to you, and peace, from God our Father and <i>the</i> Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:4** I give thanks to my God always concerning you <i>for</i> the grace of God given to you in Christ Jesus,  
**1:5** that in everything you were enriched in Him, <i>in</i> all discourse and all knowledge,  
**1:6** even as the testimony of Christ was confirmed in you,  
**1:7** so that you <i>are</i> not lacking in any gift, awaiting the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ,  
**1:8** who also will confirm you until <i>the</i> end, blameless in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:9** God <i>is</i> faithful, through whom you were called into <i>the</i> fellowship of His Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord.  
**1:10** Now I exhort you, brothers, through the name of our Lord Jesus Christ that you all say the same, and there be no schisms among you, but you be restored to harmony, in the same mind and in the same judgment.  
**1:11** For about you, my brothers, it was shown to me by the <i>ones</i> of Chloe that there are contentions among you.  
**1:12** But I say this, that each of you says, I am of Paul, and I of Apollos, and I of Cephas, and I of Christ.  
**1:13** Has Christ been divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Or were you baptized into the name of Paul?  
**1:14** I thank God that I did not baptize one of you, except Crispus and Gaius,  
**1:15** that not anyone should say that you were baptized in my name.  
**1:16** And I also baptized the house of Stephanas. For the rest, I do not know if I baptized any other.  
**1:17** For Christ did not send me to baptize, but to preach the gospel, not in wisdom of words, lest the cross of Christ be nullified.  
**1:18** For the Word of the cross is foolishness to the <i>ones</i> perishing, but to us being saved it is <i>the</i> power of God.  
**1:19** For it has been written, "I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and I will set aside the understanding of the understanding <i>ones</i>." <n>Isa 29:14</n>  
**1:20** Where <i>is the</i> wise <i>one</i>? Where <i>is the</i> scribe? Where <i>is the</i> disputer of this world? Did God not make the wisdom of this world to be foolish?  
**1:21** For since in the wisdom of God the world <i>by</i> wisdom did not know God, God was pleased through the foolishness of preaching to save the <i>ones</i> believing.  
**1:22** And since Jews ask for a sign, and Greeks seek wisdom,  
**1:23** we, on the other hand, preach Christ crucified (truly a stumbling block to Jews, and a pitfall to Greeks),  
**1:24** but to them, the <i>ones</i> called, both to Jews and to Greeks, Christ <i>is</i> the power of God and the wisdom of God;  
**1:25** because the foolish <i>thing</i> of God is wiser <i>than</i> men, and the weak <i>thing</i> of God is stronger <i>than</i> men.  
**1:26** For you see your calling, brothers, that <i>there are</i> not many wise <i>ones</i> according to flesh, not many powerful <i>ones</i>, not many wellborn <i>ones</i>.  
**1:27** But God chose the foolish <i>things</i> of the world that the wise might be put to shame, and God chose the weak <i>things</i> of the world so that He might put to shame the strong <i>things</i>.  
**1:28** And God chose the low-born <i>ones</i> of the world, and the things beng despised, and the things not being, so that He might nullify the things being,  
**1:29** so that no flesh might glory in His presence.  
**1:30** But of Him, you are in Christ Jesus, who was made to us wisdom from God, both righteousness and sanctification and redemption,  
**1:31** so that even as it has been written, "He that glories, let him glory in" <i>the</i> "Lord." <n>Jer 9:24</n>  

## 2장

**2:1** And when I came to you, brothers, I did not come with excellency of word or wisdom, declaring to you the testimony of God.  
**2:2** For I decided not to know anything among you except Jesus Christ, and Him being crucified.  
**2:3** And I was with you in weakness, and in fear, and in much trembling.  
**2:4** And my word and my preaching <i>was</i> not in persuasive words of human wisdom, but in proof of <i>the</i> Spirit and of power,  
**2:5** that your faith might not be in <i>the</i> wisdom of men, but in <i>the</i> power of God.  
**2:6** But we speak wisdom among the perfect, but not the wisdom of this age, nor of the rulers of this age, the <i>one<e>s </e></i>being caused to cease.  
**2:7** But we speak <i>the</i> wisdom of God having been hidden in a mystery, which God predetermined before the ages for our glory,  
**2:8** which none of the rulers of this age has known. For if they had known, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory;  
**2:9** but even as it is written, "The things <i>which</i> an eye did not see, and an ear did not hear," and did not come up on <i>the</i> heart of man, the <i>things</i> which God has prepared for the <i>ones</i> loving Him. <n>Isa 64:4</n>  
**2:10** But God revealed <i>them</i> to us through His Spirit, for the Spirit searches all things, even the depths of God.  
**2:11** For who among men knows the things of a man, except the spirit of a man within him? So also no one has known the things of God if not the Spirit of God.  
**2:12** But we have not received the spirit of the world, but the Spirit from God, so that we might know the things freely given to us by God.  
**2:13** Which things we also speak, not in words taught in human wisdom, but in <i>words</i> taught of <i>the</i> Holy Spirit, comparing spiritual <i>things</i> with spiritual <i>things</i>.  
**2:14** But a natural man does not receive the things of <i>the</i> Spirit of God, for they are foolishness to him, and he is not able to know <i>them</i>, because they are spiritually discerned.  
**2:15** But the spiritual <i>one</i> discerns all things, but he is discerned by no one.  
**2:16** For "who has known <i>the</i> mind of <i>the</i> Lord?" "Who will instruct Him?" But we have <i>the</i> mind of Christ. <n>Isa 40:13</n>  

## 3장

**3:1** And, brothers, I was not able to speak to you as to spiritual <i>ones</i>, but as to fleshly <i>ones</i>, as to babes in Christ.  
**3:2** I gave you milk to drink, and not <i>solid</i> food, for you were not then able, for neither are you yet able now.  
**3:3** For you are yet fleshly <i>ones</i>. For where among you <i>is</i> jealousy, and strife, and divisions, are you not fleshly <i>ones</i> and walking according to man?  
**3:4** For when one says, Truly I am of Paul, and another, I of Apollos; are you not fleshly <i>ones</i>?  
**3:5** Who then is Paul? And who <i>is</i> Apollos, but ministers through whom you believed, and to each <i>one</i> as the Lord gave?  
**3:6** I planted, Apollos watered, but God the <i>One</i> made to grow.  
**3:7** So as neither the <i>one</i> planting is anything, nor the <i>one</i> watering, but God the <i>One</i> making to grow.  
**3:8** So the <i>one</i> planting and the <i>one</i> watering are one, and each <i>one</i> will receive <i>his</i> own reward according to <i>his</i> own labor.  
**3:9** For of God we are fellow-workers, a field of God, <i>and</i> you are a building of God.  
**3:10** According to God's grace given to me, as a wise master builder, I laid a foundation, but another builds on <i>it</i>. But let each <i>one</i> look how he builds on <i>it</i>.  
**3:11** For no one is able to lay another foundation beside the <i>one</i> having been laid, which is Jesus, the Christ.  
**3:12** And if anyone builds on this foundation gold, silver, precious stones, wood, grass, straw,  
**3:13** the work of each <i>one</i> will be revealed; for the Day will make <i>it</i> known, because it is revealed in fire; and the fire will prove the work of each <i>one</i>, what sort it is.  
**3:14** If the work of anyone which he built remains, he will receive a reward.  
**3:15** If the work of anyone shall be burned down, he shall suffer loss; but he will be saved, but so as through fire.  
**3:16** Do you not know that you are a temple of God, and the Spirit of God dwells in you?  
**3:17** If anyone corrupts the temple of God, God will corrupt this <i>one</i>; for the temple of God is holy, which you are.  
**3:18** Let no one deceive himself. If anyone thinks to be a wise <i>one</i> among you in this age, let him become foolish, that he may become a wise <i>one</i>.  
**3:19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God; for it is written, "He is taking the wise <i>ones</i> in their <i>own</i> craftiness." <i>Job 5:13  
**3:20** And again, "<i>The</i> Lord knows the reasonings of the wise <i>ones</i>, that they are useless." <i>LXX-Psa. 93:11; MT-Psa. 94:11  
**3:21** So then let no one glory in men; for all things are yours,  
**3:22** whether Paul, or Apollos, or Cephas, or <i>the</i> world, or life, or death, or things present, or things to come; all are yours,  
**3:23** and you are Christ's, and Christ <i>is</i> God's.  

## 4장

**4:1** Let a man count us as under-servants of Christ and stewards of <i>the</i> mysteries of God.  
**4:2** For the rest, it is sought among stewards that one be found faithful.  
**4:3** But to me it is a small thing that I should be judged by you, or by a man's day. But I do not even judge myself.  
**4:4** For I know nothing of myself, but I have not been justified by this; but He judging me is <i>the</i> Lord.  
**4:5** So then do not judge anything before <i>the</i> time, until the Lord comes, who will both shed light on the hidden things of darkness and will reveal the purposes of the hearts. And then praise will be to each <i>one</i> from God.  
**4:6** And, brothers, I transferred these things to myself and Apollos because of you, that in us you may learn not to think above what is written, that you not <i>be</i> puffed up one against the other.  
**4:7** For who makes you to differ? And what do you have that you did not receive? And <i>if</i> you received <i>it</i>, why do you boast as not receiving <i>it</i>?  
**4:8** You are already being satiated; you already became rich; you reigned without us (and I would that you really did reign, so that we also might reign together with you!)  
**4:9** For I think that God pointed us out <i>as</i> last, the apostles, as condemned to death, because we became a spectacle to the world, even to angels and to men.  
**4:10** We <i>are</i> fools for the sake of Christ, but you <i>are</i> wise in Christ. We <i>are</i> weak, but you <i>are</i> strong. You <i>are</i> honored, but we <i>are</i> not honored.  
**4:11** Until the present hour we also hunger and thirst, and are poorly clothed, and are buffeted, and wander homeless,  
**4:12** and we labor, working with our own hands. Being cursed, we bless; persecuted, we bear;  
**4:13** being defamed, we entreat. We have become as offscouring of the world, dirt wiped off by all until now.  
**4:14** I do not write these things shaming you, but admonishing <i>you</i> as my beloved children.  
**4:15** For if you should have myriads of tutors in Christ, yet not many fathers; for I fathered you in Christ Jesus through the gospel.  
**4:16** Therefore I urge you, be imitators of me.  
**4:17** Because of this I sent Timothy to you, who is my beloved child, and faithful in <i>the</i> Lord, who will remind you <i>of</i> my ways in Christ, even as I teach everywhere in every assembly.  
**4:18** As to my not coming to you now, some were puffed up.  
**4:19** But if the Lord wills, I will come to you shortly. And I will know, not the word of the <i>ones</i> who have been puffed up, but the power.  
**4:20** For the kingdom of God <i>is</i> not in word, but in power.  
**4:21** What do you desire? <i>Shall</i> I come to you with a rod, or <i>in</i> love and a spirit of meekness?  

## 5장

**5:1** Everywhere <i>it is</i> heard that fornication <i>is</i> among you, and such fornication which is not named among the heathen, so as <i>for</i> someone to have <i>his</i> father's wife.  
**5:2** And you are being puffed up, and have not rather mourned, that he that did this deed might be taken from your midst.  
**5:3** For as being absent in body, but being present in spirit, I have already judged the <i>one</i> who has perpetrated this deed, as being present:  
**5:4** In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, you being assembled with my spirit also, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ,  
**5:5** to give over such a one to Satan for destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus.  
**5:6** Your boast <i>is</i> not good. Do you not know that a little leaven leavens all the lump?  
**5:7** Therefore clean out the old leaven so that you may be a new lump, even as you are unleavened. For also Christ our Passover was sacrificed for us.  
**5:8** So then let us keep <i>the</i> feast, not with old leaven, nor with leaven of malice and of wickedness, but with unleavened <i>bread</i> of sincerity and truth.  
**5:9** I wrote to you in the letter not to associate with fornicators;  
**5:10** and not altogether with the fornicators of this world, or with the covetous one, or with rapacious <i>ones</i>, or with idolaters, since then you must go out of the world.  
**5:11** But now I wrote to you not to associate intimately; if anyone being named a brother <i>and is</i> either a fornicator, or a covetous one, or an idolater, or a reviler, or a drunkard, or a rapacious <i>one</i>, with such a one not to eat.  
**5:12** For what <i>is it</i> to me also to judge the ones outside? Do you not judge those inside?  
**5:13** But God will judge the ones outside. "And take away from yourselves the wicked <i>one</i>." <n>Deut 17:7</n>  

## 6장

**6:1** Does anyone of you having a matter against another dare to be judged before the unjust, and not before the saints?  
**6:2** Do you not know that the saints will judge the world? And if the world is judged by you, are you unworthy of small judgments?  
**6:3** Do you not know that we shall judge angels, not to speak of this life?  
**6:4** If, then, you truly have judgments of this life, the <i>ones</i> being least esteemed in the assembly, you seat these.  
**6:5** For I say <i>this,</i> Shame to you. So, is there not a wise <i>one</i> among you, not even one in your midst who will be able to judge his brother?  
**6:6** But brother is judged with brother, and this before unbelievers!  
**6:7** Indeed, therefore, there is already actually a defeat with you, that you have lawsuits with yourselves. Why not instead be wronged? Why not instead be defrauded?  
**6:8** But you do wrong, and defraud, and these things to brothers!  
**6:9** Or do you not know that unjust <i>ones</i> will not inherit <i>the</i> kingdom of God? Do not be led astray, neither fornicators, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor boy-abusers, nor homosexuals,  
**6:10** nor thieves, nor covetous ones, nor drunkards, nor revilers, nor rapacious <i>ones</i> shall inherit <i>the</i> kingdom of God.  
**6:11** And some were these things, but you were washed, but you were sanctified, but you were justified in the name of the Lord Jesus, and in the Spirit of our God.  
**6:12** All things are lawful to me, but not all things are profitable. All things are lawful to me, but I will not be subjected by any.  
**6:13** Foods for the belly, and the belly for foods, but God will destroy both this and these. But the body <i>is</i> not for fornication, but for the Lord, and the Lord for the body.  
**6:14** And God both raised up the Lord, and <i>He</i> will raise us up through His power.  
**6:15** Do you not know that your bodies are members of Christ? Then taking the members of Christ, shall I make <i>them</i> members of a prostitute? Let it not be!  
**6:16** Or do you not know that he being joined to a prostitute is one body? For He says, "The two <i>shall be</i> into one flesh." <n>Gen 2:24</n>  
**6:17** But the <i>one</i> being joined to the Lord is one spirit.  
**6:18** Flee fornication. Every sin which a man may do is outside the body, but he doing fornication sins against <i>his</i> own body.  
**6:19** Or do you not know that your body is a temple of <i>the</i> Holy Spirit in you, which you have from God, and you are not of yourselves?  
**6:20** You were bought with a price; then glorify God in your body, and in your spirit, which are of God.  

## 7장

**7:1** But concerning what you wrote to me, <i>it is</i> good for a man not to touch a woman;  
**7:2** but because of fornication, let each <i>one</i> have his <i>own</i> wife, and let each <i>one</i> have her own husband.  
**7:3** Let the husband give due kindness to the wife, and likewise the wife also to the husband.  
**7:4** The wife does not have authority of <i>her</i> own body, but the husband. And likewise also the husband does not have authority <i>over his</i> own body, but the wife.  
**7:5** Do not deprive one another, if not by agreement for a time, that you may devote <i>yourselves</i> to fasting and to prayer. And come together again on the same <i>place</i>, that Satan may not tempt you through your lack of self-control.  
**7:6** But I say this by concession, not by command.  
**7:7** But I desire all men also to be as myself. But each <i>one</i> has his own gift from God, thus and thus.  
**7:8** But I say to the unmarried men, and to the widows, it is good for them if they also remain as I.  
**7:9** But if they do not have self control, let them marry; for it is better to marry than to be inflamed.  
**7:10** But I command the ones being married (not I, but the Lord), a woman not to be separated from her husband;  
**7:11** but if indeed she is separated, remain unmarried, or be reconciled to the husband; and a husband not to let <i>his</i> wife go.  
**7:12** But to the rest I say, not the Lord, if any brother has an unbelieving wife, and she consents to live with him, let him not let her go.  
**7:13** And a woman who has an unbelieving husband, and he consents to live with her, let her not let him go.  
**7:14** For the unbelieving husband has been sanctified by the wife, and the unbelieving wife has been sanctified by the husband; else, then, your children are unclean, but now they are holy.  
**7:15** But if the unbelieving <i>one</i> separates, let <i>them</i> be separated; the brother or the sister is not in bondage in such matters; but God has called us in peace.  
**7:16** For what do you know, wife, whether you will save the husband? Or what do you know, husband, whether you will save the wife?  
**7:17** If not as God has apportioned to each, <i>one,</i> to each <i>one</i> as the Lord has called, so let him walk. So I command in the assemblies.  
**7:18** <i>Was</i> anyone called having been circumcised? Do not be uncircumcised. Was anyone called in uncircumcision? Do not be circumcised.  
**7:19** Circumcision is nothing, and uncircumcision is nothing, but the keeping of God's commands.  
**7:20** Each <i>one</i> in the calling in which he was called, in this remain.  
**7:21** Were you called as a slave? It does not matter to you. But if you are able to be free, rather use <i>it</i>.  
**7:22** For the <i>one</i> having been called as a slave in <i>the</i> Lord is a free man of <i>the</i> Lord. And likewise, the <i>one</i> being called a free man is a slave of Christ.  
**7:23** You were bought with a price; do not become slaves of men.  
**7:24** Each <i>one</i> in which <i>state</i> he was called, brothers, in this remain with God.  
**7:25** But about the virgins, I have no command of <i>the</i> Lord. But I give judgment, as having received mercy by <i>the</i> Lord to be faithful.  
**7:26** Therefore I think this to be good, because of the present necessity: that <i>it is</i> good for a man to be so.  
**7:27** Have you been bound to a wife? Do not seek to be released. Have you been released from a wife? Do not seek a wife.  
**7:28** But if you also marry, you do not sin. And if the virgin marries, she does not sin. But such will have trouble in the flesh; and I am sparing you.  
**7:29** But I say this, brothers, that the time having been cut short, the remaining <i>thing</i> is, that even the <i>ones</i> having wives should be as not having,  
**7:30** and the <i>ones</i> weeping as not weeping, and the <i>ones</i> rejoicing as not rejoicing, and the <i>ones</i> buying as not possessing,  
**7:31** and the <i>ones</i> using this world as not overusing <i>it</i>; for the fashion of this world is passing away.  
**7:32** But I desire you to be without care. The unmarried <i>one</i> cares <i>for</i> the things of <i>the</i> Lord, how to please the Lord;  
**7:33** but the <i>one</i> marrying cares <i>for</i> the things of the world, how to please the wife.  
**7:34** The wife and the virgin <i>are</i> different. The unmarried <i>one</i> cares <i>for</i> the things of <i>the</i> Lord, that she be holy in both body and spirit. But the married one cares <i>for</i> the things of the world, how to please the husband.  
**7:35** And I say this for your advantage, not that I put a noose <i>before</i> you, but for the fitting thing, and waiting on the Lord without distraction.  
**7:36** But if anyone thinks <i>he</i> acts unseemly toward his virgin (if she is beyond <i>her</i> prime, and so it ought to be), let him do what he desires; he does not sin; let them marry.  
**7:37** But <i>he</i> who stands firm in heart, not having necessity, but has authority as to <i>his</i> own will, and has judged this in his heart, to keep his virgin; he does well.  
**7:38** So that he giving in marriage does well, and he not giving in marriage does better.  
**7:39** A wife is bound by law for as long a time as her husband lives; but if her husband sleeps, she is free to be married to whomever she desires, only in <i>the</i> Lord.  
**7:40** But she is happier if she remains so, according to my judgment. And I think I also have <i>the</i> Spirit of God.  

## 8장

**8:1** But concerning the sacrifices to idols, we know that we all have knowledge. Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up.  
**8:2** But if anyone thinks to know anything, he still has known nothing as he ought to know.  
**8:3** But if anyone loves God, this <i>one</i> has been known by Him.  
**8:4** Therefore concerning the eating of idolatrous offerings, we know that an idol <i>is</i> nothing in <i>the</i> world, and that <i>there</i> is no other God except one.  
**8:5** For even if <i>some</i> are called gods, either in <i>the</i> heavens or on the earth; (just as there are many gods, and many lords);  
**8:6** but to us <i>is</i> one God, the Father, of whom <i>are</i> all things, and we to Him, and one Lord Jesus Christ, through whom are all things, and we through Him.  
**8:7** But the knowledge <i>is</i> not in all; but some being aware of the idol eat as an idolatrous offering until now; and their conscience, being weak, is defiled.  
**8:8** But food will not commend us to God. For neither if we eat do we excel, nor if we do not eat are we lacking.  
**8:9** But watch how this authority of yours may not become a cause of stumbling to the <i>ones</i> being weak.  
**8:10** For if anyone sees you, the <i>one</i> having knowledge, reclining in an idol's temple, will not the weak <i>one's</i> conscience be built up so as to eat the idolatrous offering?  
**8:11** And on your knowledge the weak brother shall perish, <i>he</i> for whom Christ died.  
**8:12** But sinning so against <i>your</i> brothers, and wounding their conscience, being weak, you sin against Christ.  
**8:13** On account of this, if food causes my brother to stumble, I will not eat flesh into the age, not <i>ever</i>, so that I do not cause my brother to stumble.  

## 9장

**9:1** Am I not an apostle? Am I not free? Have I not seen our Lord Jesus Christ? Are you not my work in <i>the</i> Lord?  
**9:2** If I am not an apostle to others, yet I am indeed to you; for you are the seal of my apostleship in <i>the</i> Lord.  
**9:3** My defense to those examining me is this:  
**9:4** Have we not authority to eat and to drink?  
**9:5** Have we not authority to lead about a sister, a wife, as <i>do</i> the rest of the apostles also, and Cephas, and the Lord's brothers?  
**9:6** Or <i>is it</i> only Barnabas and I <i>who</i> have no authority not to work?  
**9:7** Who serves as a soldier at <i>his</i> own wages ever? Who plants a vineyard and does not eat of its fruit? Or who shepherds a flock and does not eat of the milk of the flock?  
**9:8** Do I speak these things according to man, or does not the Law say these things also?  
**9:9** For it has been written in the Law of Moses, "You shall not muzzle an ox threshing grain." <n>Deut 25:4</n></i> Not <i>that</i> it matters to God for the oxen, <i>is it?  
**9:10** Or does He certainly say <i>it</i> because of us? It is written because of us, so that the <i>one</i> plowing ought to plow <i>in</i> hope, and the <i>one</i> threshing <i>in</i> hope to partake of hope.  
**9:11** If we sowed spiritual things to you, <i>is it</i> a great thing if we shall reap of your fleshly things?  
**9:12** If others have a share of the authority <i>over</i> you, <i>should</i> not rather we? But we did not use this authority, but we endured all things, so that we might not give a hindrance to the gospel of Christ.  
**9:13** Do you not know that those laboring <i>about</i> the holy things of the temple eat? Those attending on the altar share together with the altar.  
**9:14** So also the Lord ordained the <i>ones</i> proclaiming the gospel to live from the gospel.  
**9:15** But I have not used one of these. And I do not write these things that it be so in me. For <i>it is</i> good to me rather to die than that anyone nullify my glorying.  
**9:16** For if I preach the gospel, no glory is to me; for necessity is laid on me, and <i>it</i> is woe to me if I do not preach the gospel.  
**9:17** For if I do this willingly, I have a reward; but if unwillingly, I am entrusted with a stewardship.  
**9:18** What then is my reward? That preaching the gospel I may make the gospel of Christ free, so as not to use fully my authority in the gospel.  
**9:19** For being free from all <i>men</i> I enslaved myself to all <i>men</i>, that I might gain the more.  
**9:20** And I became as a Jew to the Jews, that I might gain Jews; to the <i>ones</i> under Law as under Law, that I might gain the <i>ones</i> under Law;  
**9:21** to the <i>ones</i> without Law as without Law (not being without Law of God, but under <i>the</i> law of Christ), that I might gain <i>the ones</i> without Law.  
**9:22** I became to the weak <i>ones</i> as weak, that I might gain the weak <i>ones</i>. To all <i>people</i> I have become all things, that in any and every way I might save some.  
**9:23** And I do this for the gospel, that I might become a fellow partaker of it.  
**9:24** Do you not know that those running in a stadium indeed all run, but one receives the prize? So run that you may obtain.  
**9:25** But everyone striving controls himself <i>in</i> all things. Then the <i>ones</i> truly that they may receive a corruptible crown, but we an incorruptible.  
**9:26** Therefore, so I run, as not uncertainly; so I fight, as not beating air;  
**9:27** but I buffet my body and enslave <i>it</i>, lest having preached to others I myself might be disapproved.  

## 10장

**10:1** And I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, that our fathers were all under the cloud, and all passed through the Sea.  
**10:2** And all were baptized into Moses in the cloud, and in the Sea,  
**10:3** and all ate the same spiritual food.  
**10:4** And all drank the same spiritual drink; for they drank of the spiritual rock following, and that Rock was Christ.  
**10:5** But God was not well-pleased with most of them, "for they were scattered in the wilderness " <n>Num 14:16</n>  
**10:6** But these things became examples for us, so that we may not be lusters after bad <i>things</i>, even as those indeed lusted.  
**10:7** Neither be idolaters, even as some of them, as it is written, "The people sat down to eat and drink, and stood up to play." <n>Ex 32:6</n>  
**10:8** Nor should we commit fornication, as some of them fornicated, and twentythree thousand fell in one day.  
**10:9** Neither over-tempt the Christ, as some of them tempted, and were destroyed by serpents.  
**10:10** Neither murmur, as also some of them murmured, and were destroyed by the destroyer.  
**10:11** And all these things happened to those <i>people as</i> examples, and <i>it</i> was written for our admonition, on whom the ends of the ages have come.  
**10:12** So then the <i>one</i> thinking to stand, let him watch <i>that</i> he not fall.  
**10:13** No temptation has taken you if not a human <i>one</i>; but God <i>is</i> faithful, who will not allow you to be tempted above what you are able. But with the temptation, <i>He</i> will also make the way out, <i>so as</i> for you to be able to bear <i>it</i>.  
**10:14** On account of this, my beloved, flee from idolatry.  
**10:15** I speak as to prudent <i>ones</i>; you judge what I say.  
**10:16** The cup of blessing that we bless, is <i>it</i> not a communion of the blood of Christ? The bread which we break, is <i>it</i> not a communion of the body of Christ?  
**10:17** Because we, the many, are one bread, one body, for we all partake of the one bread.  
**10:18** Look at Israel according to flesh; are not those eating the sacrifices sharers of the altar?  
**10:19** What then do I say, that an idol is anything, or that an idolatrous offering is anything?  
**10:20** But the things the nations sacrifice, "<i>they</i> sacrifice to demons, and not to God." <n>Deut 32:17</n></i> But I do not want you to become sharers of demons;  
**10:21** Not you are able to drink <i>the</i> cup of <i>the</i> Lord and a cup of demons; not you are able to partake of <i>the</i> table of <i>the</i> Lord and a table of demons.  
**10:22** Or do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than He?  
**10:23** All <i>things</i> are lawful to me, but not all things are profitable. All things are lawful to me, but not all <i>things</i> build up.  
**10:24** Let no one seek the <i>thing</i> of himself, but each <i>one</i> the <i>thing</i> of the other.  
**10:25** Eat everything being sold in a meat market, examining nothing because of conscience,  
**10:26** for "the earth <i>is</i> the Lord's, and the fullness of it." <n>Psa 24:1</n>  
**10:27** And if any of the unbelieving <i>ones</i> invite you, and you desire to go, eat everything set before you, examining nothing because of conscience.  
**10:28** But if anyone tells you, This is an idolatrous offering, do not eat because of that one disclosing <i>it</i>, and the conscience; for "the earth <i>is</i> the Lord's, and the fullness of it." <n>Psa 24:1</n>  
**10:29** But I say conscience, not the <i>one</i> of himself, but the <i>one</i> of the other. For why is my freedom judged by another's conscience?  
**10:30** But if I partake by grace, why am I evil spoken of because of that <i>for</i> which I give thanks?  
**10:31** Then whether you eat or drink, or anything you do, do all things to the glory of God.  
**10:32** Be not led into sin, both to Jews and Greeks, and to the assembly of God.  
**10:33** Even as I also please all <i>in</i> all <i>things</i>, not seeking <i>my</i> own advantage, but that of the many, that they may be saved.  

## 11장

**11:1** Be imitators of me, as I <i>am</i> also of Christ.  
**11:2** But I praise you, brothers, that in all <i>things</i> you have remembered me, and even as I gave <i>them</i> over to you, you hold fast the traditions.  
**11:3** But I want you to know that Christ is <i>the</i> head of every man, and <i>the</i> man <i>is</i> the head of a woman, and God <i>is the</i> head of Christ.  
**11:4** Every man praying or prophesying, having <i>any covering</i> on his head shames his head.  
**11:5** And every woman praying or prophesying with <i>the</i> head uncovered dishonors her head, for it is one and the same <i>as</i> being shaved.  
**11:6** For if a woman is not covered, let her also be shorn. But <i>if it</i> is shameful for a woman to be shorn, or to be shaved, let her be covered.  
**11:7** For truly a man ought not to have <i>the</i> head covered, being <i>the</i> image and glory of God. But woman is <i>the</i> glory of man;  
**11:8** for man is not of the woman, but woman of man;  
**11:9** for also man was not created for the sake of the woman, but woman for the sake of the man;  
**11:10** because of this, the woman ought to have authority on the head, because of the angels.  
**11:11** But neither is the man apart from woman, nor woman apart from man, in <i>the</i> Lord.  
**11:12** For as the woman <i>is</i> out of the man, so also the man through the woman; but all <i>things</i> from God.  
**11:13** You judge among yourselves: is it fitting <i>for</i> a woman to pray to God uncovered?  
**11:14** Or does not nature herself teach you that if a man indeed wears long hair, it is a dishonor to him?  
**11:15** But if a woman wears <i>her</i> hair long, it is a glory to her; because the long hair has been given to her instead of a covering.  
**11:16** But if anyone thinks to be contentious, we do not have such a custom, nor the assemblies of God.  
**11:17** But enjoining this, I do not praise <i>you</i>, because you come together not for the better, but for the worse.  
**11:18** Indeed, first, I hear divisions to be among you <i>when</i> you come together in the assembly. And I believe <i>it in</i> some part.  
**11:19** For also factions need to be among you, so that the approved <i>ones</i> may become revealed among you.  
**11:20** Therefore you coming together into one place, it is not to eat <i>the</i> Lord's supper.  
**11:21** For each <i>one</i> takes his own supper first in <i>order</i> to eat; and one is hungry, and another drunken.  
**11:22** For do you not have houses <i>in which</i> to eat and to drink? Or do you despise the assembly of God, and shame the <i>ones</i> who have not? What should I say to you? Shall I praise you for this? I do not praise.  
**11:23** For I received from the Lord what I also gave over to you, that the Lord Jesus in the night in which He was given over took bread;  
**11:24** and having given thanks, He broke and said, Take, eat; this is My body, the <i>one</i> being broken on behalf of you; this do in remembrance of Me.  
**11:25** In the same way the cup also, after the <i>order</i> to <i>eat</i> supper, <i>He</i> said, This cup is the New Covenant in My blood; as often as you drink, do this in remembrance of Me.  
**11:26** For as often as you may eat this bread, and drink this cup, you solemnly proclaim the death of the Lord, until He shall come.  
**11:27** So then whoever should eat this bread, or drink the cup of the Lord, unworthily, <i>that one</i> will be guilty of the body and of the blood of the Lord.  
**11:28** But let a man examine himself, and so let him eat from the bread, and let him drink from the cup;  
**11:29** for the <i>one</i> eating and drinking unworthily eats and drinks judgment to himself, not discerning the body of the Lord.  
**11:30** Because of this many among you <i>are</i> weak and infirm, and a considerable number sleep.  
**11:31** For if we judged ourselves, we would not be judged.  
**11:32** But being judged, we are chastened by <i>the</i> Lord, that we not be condemned with the world.  
**11:33** So that, my brothers, coming together to eat, wait for one another.  
**11:34** But if anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, that you may not come together for judgment. And the remaining things I will set in order whenever I come.  

## 12장

**12:1** But concerning the spiritual <i>gifts</i>, brothers, I do not wish you to be ignorant.  
**12:2** You know that <i>when</i> you were nations, how you were led, being carried away to voiceless idols.  
**12:3** Because of this I make known to you that no one speaking by <i>the</i> Spirit of God says, Jesus <i>is</i> a curse. And no one is able to say Jesus <i>is</i> Lord, if not by <i>the</i> Holy Spirit.  
**12:4** And there are varieties of gifts, but the same Spirit;  
**12:5** and there are varieties of ministries, yet the same Lord.  
**12:6** And there are varieties of workings, but the same is God, the <i>One</i> working all things in all.  
**12:7** And to each <i>one</i> is given the showing forth of the Spirit to the profiting <i>of</i> each <i>one</i>.  
**12:8** For through the Spirit is given to one a word of wisdom, and to another a word of knowledge, according to the same Spirit;  
**12:9** and to another, faith by the same Spirit; and to another, gifts of healing by the same Spirit,  
**12:10** and to another; workings of powers; and to another, prophecy; and to another, discerning of spirits; and to another, kinds of languages; and to another, interpretation of languages.  
**12:11** But the one and the same Spirit works all these things, distributing separately to each <i>one</i> as He purposes.  
**12:12** Even as the body is one, and has many members, but all the members of the one body, being many, <i>are</i> one body; so also <i>is</i> Christ.  
**12:13** For also we all were baptized into one Spirit into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, whether slaves or free, even all were given to drink into one Spirit.  
**12:14** For also the body is not one member, but many.  
**12:15** If the foot says, Because I am not a hand, I am not of the body; because of this, is it not of the body?  
**12:16** And if the ear says, Because I am not an eye, I am not of the body, on account of this, is it not of the body?  
**12:17** If all the body <i>were</i> an eye, where <i>would be</i> the hearing? If all hearing, where the smelling?  
**12:18** But now God set the members, each one of them, in the body, even as He willed.  
**12:19** But if all was one member, where <i>would</i> the body <i>be</i>?  
**12:20** But now, indeed, many <i>are</i> the members, but one body.  
**12:21** And the eye is not able to say to the hand, I have no need of you; or again the head to the feet, I have no need of you.  
**12:22** But by much more the members of the body seeming to be weaker are necessary.  
**12:23** And those of the body we think to be less honorable, to these we put more abundant honor around <i>them</i>. And our unpresentable <i>members</i> have more abundant propriety.  
**12:24** But our presentable <i>members</i> have no need. But God tempered the body together, giving more abundant honor to the <i>member</i> lacking <i>it,  
**12:25** that there not be division in the body, but <i>that</i> the members might have the same care for one another.  
**12:26** And if one member suffers, all the members suffer with <i>it</i>. If one member is glorified, all the members rejoice with <i>it</i>.  
**12:27** And you are Christ's body, and members in part.  
**12:28** And God placed some in the assembly, firstly, apostles; secondly, prophets; thirdly, teachers; then works of power; then gifts of healing, helps, governings, kinds of languages.  
**12:29** <i>Are</i> all apostles? All prophets? All teachers? All workers of power?  
**12:30** Do all have gifts of healings? Do all speak languages? Do all interpret?  
**12:31** But zealously strive after the better gifts. And yet I show you a way according to excellence:  

## 13장

**13:1** If I speak with the tongues of men and of angels, but do not have love, I have become <i>as</i> sounding brass or a clanging cymbal.  
**13:2** And if I have prophecies, and know all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have all faith so as to move mountains, but do not have love, I am nothing.  
**13:3** And if I give out all my possesssions, and if I give over my body that I be burned, but I do not have love, I am profited nothing.  
**13:4** Love patiently endures, is kind; love is not envious; love boasts not itself, is not puffed up;  
**13:5** does not behave unbecomingly, does not seek the <i>things of</i> itself, is not <i>easily</i> provoked, does not impute evil;  
**13:6** does not rejoice over unrighteousness, but rejoices in the truth.  
**13:7** <i>Love</i> quietly covers all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things.  
**13:8** Love never fails. But if <i>there are</i> prophecies, they will be caused to cease; if tongues, they shall cease; if knowledge, it will be caused to cease.  
**13:9** For we know in part, and we prophesy in part;  
**13:10** but when the perfect <i>thing</i> comes, then that <i>which is</i> in part will be caused to cease.  
**13:11** When I was a little child, I spoke as a little child, I thought as a little child, I reasoned as a little child. But when I have become a man, I did away with the <i>things</i> of the little child.  
**13:12** For now we see through a mirror in dimness, but then face to face. Now I know by part, but then I will fully know even as I also was fully known.  
**13:13** And now faith, hope, and love, these three things remain; but the greatest of these <i>is</i> love.  

## 14장

**14:1** Pursue love, and seek eagerly the spiritual <i>things</i>, but rather that you may prophesy.  
**14:2** For the <i>one</i> speaking in a language does not speak to men, but to God; for no one hears, but in spirit he speaks mysteries.  
**14:3** But the <i>one</i> prophesying to men speaks <i>for</i> building up, and encouragement, and comfort.  
**14:4** The <i>one</i> speaking in a langusge builds himself up, but he prophesying builds up an assembly.  
**14:5** And I wish all of you to speak in languages, but rather that you may prophesy. For the <i>one</i> prophesying <i>is</i> greater than the <i>one</i> speaking in languages, if not he interpret, that the assembly may receive building up.  
**14:6** But now, brothers, if I come to you speaking in languages, what will I profit you, if not I speak to you either in revelation, or in knowledge, or in prophecy, or in teaching?  
**14:7** Likewise lifeless <i>things</i> giving a sound, whether flute or harp, if they do not give a distinction in the sound, how will it be known what is being fluted or being harped?  
**14:8** For also if a trumpet gives an uncertain sound, who will get himself ready for war?  
**14:9** So also you, if you do not give a clear word through the language, how will the <i>thing</i> being said be known? For you will be speaking into air.  
**14:10** So it may be many kinds of sounds are in <i>the</i> world, and not one <i>is</i> without <i>distinct</i> sound.  
**14:11** If, then, I do not know the power of the sound, I will be a foreigner to the <i>one</i> speaking, and he speaking in me a foreigner.  
**14:12** So also you, since you are zealots of spiritual <i>gifts</i>, seek to build up the assembly that you may abound.  
**14:13** Therefore, the <i>one</i> speaking in a language, let him pray that he may interpret.  
**14:14** For if I pray in a <i>foreign</i> language, my spirit prays, but my mind is unfruitful.  
**14:15** What then is it? I will pray with the spirit, and I will also pray with the mind; I will sing with the spirit, and I will also sing with the mind.  
**14:16** Else, if you bless in the spirit, the one occupying the place of the unlearned, how will he say the amen at your giving of thanks, since he does not know what you say?  
**14:17** For you truly give thanks well, but the other <i>person</i> is not built up.  
**14:18** I thank my God <i>that</i> I speak more languages than all of you.  
**14:19** But in an assembly I desire to speak five words with my mind, that I may also instruct others, than myriads of words in a <i>foreign</i> language.  
**14:20** Brothers, do not be children in your minds, but in malice act like children, and in your minds be mature.  
**14:21** It is written in the Law, "By other languages" and "by other lips" "I will speak to this people," "and even so they will not hear" Me, says <i>the</i> Lord. <n>Isa 28:11</n>, 12  
**14:22** So that languages are not a sign to the <i>ones</i> believing, but to the faithless <i>ones</i>. But prophecy <i>is</i> not to the faithless <i>ones</i>, but to the <i>ones</i> believing.  
**14:23** Then if the whole assembly comes together, and all speak in <i>foreign</i> languages, and uninstructed or faithless <i>ones</i> come in, will they not say that you rave?  
**14:24** But if all prophesy, and some faithless <i>one</i> or an uninstructed <i>one</i> comes in, he is convicted by all, he is judged by all.  
**14:25** And so the secrets of his heart become revealed; and so, falling on <i>his</i> face, he will worship God, declaring that God is truly among you.  
**14:26** Then what is it, brothers? When you come together, each <i>one</i> of you has a psalm, he has a teaching, he has a language, he has a revelation, he has an interpretation. Let all things be for building up.  
**14:27** If one speaks in a language, <i>let it be</i> by two or three <i>at</i> the most, and in turn, also let one interpret.  
**14:28** And if there is no interpreter, let him be silent in <i>the</i> assembly, and let him speak to himself and to God.  
**14:29** And <i>if there are</i> two or three prophets, let them speak, and let the others judge.  
**14:30** But if <i>a revelation</i> is revealed to another sitting <i>by</i>, let the first be silent.  
**14:31** For you can all prophesy one by one, that all may learn, and all may be encouraged.  
**14:32** And the spirits of prophets <i>are</i> subject to prophets.  
**14:33** For God is not <i>God</i> of confusion, but of peace, as in all the assemblies of the saints.  
**14:34** Let your women be silent in the assemblies, for it is not allowed to them to speak, but to be in subjection, as also the Law says.  
**14:35** But if they desire to learn anything, let them question their husbands at home; for it is a disgraceful <i>thing</i> for a woman to speak in an assembly.  
**14:36** Or did the Word of God go out from you? Or did it reach only to you?  
**14:37** If anyone thinks to be a prophet, or a spiritual <i>one</i>, let him recognize the things I write to you, that they are commandments of the Lord.  
**14:38** But if any be ignorant, let him be ignorant.  
**14:39** So then, brothers, seek eagerly to prophesy, and do not forbid to speak in languages.  
**14:40** And let all things come to be <i>done</i> decently and according to order.  

## 15장

**15:1** Now, brothers, I make known to you the gospel which I preached to you, which you also received, in which you also stand,  
**15:2** through which you also are being saved, if you hold fast the Word which I preached to you, except if not you believed in vain.  
**15:3** For I gave over to you among the first <i>things</i> what I also received, that Christ died in behalf of our sins, according to the Scriptures,  
**15:4** and that He was buried, and that He has been raised the third day, according to the Scriptures,  
**15:5** and that He appeared to Cephas, and then to the Twelve.  
**15:6** Then He appeared to more than five hundred brothers at once, of whom the most remain until now, but some also fell asleep.  
**15:7** Then He appeared to James, then to all the apostles;  
**15:8** and last of all, even as if to an untimely birth He also appeared to me.  
**15:9** For I am the least of the apostles, who am not sufficient to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the Assembly of God.  
**15:10** But by the grace of God I am what I am, and His grace which <i>was</i> toward me has not been fruitless, but I labored more abundantly than all of them, yet not I, but the grace of God with me.  
**15:11** Whether therefore I or those, so we preach, and so you believed.  
**15:12** But if Christ is being preached, that He has been raised from <i>the</i> dead <i>ones</i>, how do some among you say <i>there</i> is not a resurrection of the dead <i>ones</i>?  
**15:13** But if there is not a resurrection of <i>the</i> dead <i>ones</i>, neither has Christ been raised.  
**15:14** But if Christ has not been raised, then our preaching <i>is</i> worthless, and your faith is also worthless.  
**15:15** And also we are found <i>to be</i> false witnesses of God, because we witnessed according to God that He raised Christ, whom He did not raise, if then dead <i>ones</i> are not raised.  
**15:16** For if dead <i>ones</i> are not raised, Christ has not been raised.  
**15:17** But if Christ has not been raised, your faith <i>is</i> vain; you are still in your sins.  
**15:18** And then the <i>ones</i> that fell asleep in Christ perished.  
**15:19** If we only have hope in Christ in this life, we are of all men most pitiable.  
**15:20** But now Christ has been raised from the dead <i>ones</i>; He became the firstfruit of the <i>ones</i> having fallen asleep.  
**15:21** For since death <i>is</i> through man, also through a Man <i>is</i> a resurrection of dead <i>ones</i>;  
**15:22** for as in Adam all die, so also in Christ all will be made alive.  
**15:23** But each <i>one</i> in <i>his</i> own order: Christ, the firstfruit; afterward the <i>ones</i> of Christ in His coming.  
**15:24** Then <i>is</i> the end, whenever He gives over the kingdom to God, even the Father, whenever He causes to cease all rule and all authority and power.  
**15:25** For it is right for Him to reign until He puts all the hostile <i>ones</i> under His feet; <n>Psa 110:1</n>  
**15:26** <i>the</i> last hostile <i>thing</i> caused to cease <i>is</i> death.  
**15:27** For "He subjected all things under His feet;" but when He says that all things have been subjected, <i>it is</i> evident that <i>this it</i> except the <i>One</i> having subjected all things to Him. <n>Psa 8:6</n>  
**15:28** But when all things are subjected to Him, then the Son Himself also will be put in subjection to the <i>One</i> who has subjected all things to Him, that God may be all things in all.  
**15:29** Otherwise, what will they do, the <i>ones</i> being baptized on behalf of the dead <i>ones</i>? If <i>the</i> dead <i>ones</i> are not at all raised, why indeed are they baptized on behalf of the dead <i>ones</i>?  
**15:30** Why are we also in danger every hour?  
**15:31** According to a day I die, by your boasting, which I have in Christ Jesus our Lord.  
**15:32** If according to man I fought with beasts in Ephesus, what <i>is</i> the profit to me if <i>the</i> dead <i>ones</i> are not raised? ("Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die"). <n>Isa 22:13</n>.)  
**15:33** Do not be led astray; bad companionships corrupt good habits.  
**15:34** Be sober uprightly, and do not sin; for some have ignorance of God. I speak to your shame.  
**15:35** But someone will say, How are dead <i>ones</i> raised? And with what body do they come?  
**15:36** Foolish <i>ones</i>! What you sow is not made alive if not it dies.  
**15:37** And what you sow, you do not sow the body that <i>is</i> going to be, but a naked grain, (if it may be of wheat, or of some of the rest),  
**15:38** but God gives it a body according as He willed, and to each <i>one</i> of the seeds <i>its</i> own body.  
**15:39** Not every flesh <i>is</i> the same flesh, but, indeed, another flesh of men, and another flesh of beasts, and another of fish, and another of birds.  
**15:40** And <i>there are</i> heavenly bodies, and earthly bodies. But the glory of the heavenly <i>is</i> truly different, and the <i>ones</i> of the earthly <i>one</i> different;  
**15:41** another glory of <i>the</i> sun, and another glory of <i>the</i> moon, and another glory of <i>the</i> stars; for star differs from star in glory.  
**15:42** So also the resurrection of dead <i>ones</i>. It is sown in corruption, it is raised in incorruption.  
**15:43** It is sown in dishonor, it is raised in glory. It is sown in weakness, it is raised in power.  
**15:44** It is sown a natural body, it is raised a spiritual body; <i>there</i> is a natural body, and <i>there</i> is a spiritual body.  
**15:45** So also it has been written, "The" first "man", Adam, "became a living soul;" the last Adam a Spirit giving life. <n>Gen 2:7</n>  
**15:46** But not the spiritual first, but the natural; afterward the spiritual.  
**15:47** The first man <i>was</i> out of earth, an earthy <i>one</i>. The second Man <i>was</i> the Lord out of Heaven. <n>Gen 2:7</n>  
**15:48** Such <i>as is</i> the earthy <i>man</i>, such also <i>are</i> the earthy <i>ones</i>. And such as is the heavenly <i>Man</i>, such also <i>are</i> the heavenly <i>ones</i>.  
**15:49** And as we bore the image of the earthy <i>man</i>, we shall also bear the image of the heavenly <i>Man</i>.  
**15:50** And I say this, brothers, that flesh and blood are not able to inherit <i>the</i> kingdom of God, nor does corruption inherit incorruption.  
**15:51** Behold, I tell a mystery to you: we shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed.  
**15:52** In a moment, in a glance of an eye, at the last trumpet; for a trumpet will sound, and the dead <i>ones</i> will be raised incorruptible, and we shall all be changed.  
**15:53** For this corruptible must put on incorruption, and this mortal <i>one must</i> put on immortality.  
**15:54** But when this corruptible shall put on incorruption, and this mortal <i>one</i> shall put on immortality, then will come to pass the Word, the <i>one</i> having been written, "Death was swallowed up in victory." <n>Isa 25:8</n>  
**15:55** "O death, where <i>is your</i> sting? Hades, where <i>is your</i> victory?" <n>Hos 13:14</n>  
**15:56** Now the sting of death <i>is</i> sin, and the power of sin <i>is</i> the Law;  
**15:57** but thanks <i>be</i> to God, the <i>One</i> giving us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ!  
**15:58** So that, my beloved brothers, <i>you</i> be firm, immovable, abounding in the work of the Lord always, knowing that your labor is not fruitless in <i>the</i> Lord.  

## 16장

**16:1** And about the collection, the <i>one</i> for the saints, as I charged the assemblies of Galatia, so also you do.  
**16:2** On <i>the</i> first of <i>the</i> sabbaths, let each <i>one</i> of you put by himself, storing up whatever he is prospered, that there not be collections then when I come.  
**16:3** And when I arrive, whomever you approve through these epistles, I will send to carry your gift of grace to Jerusalem.  
**16:4** And if it is suitable for me to go also, they shall go with me.  
**16:5** But I will come to you when I go through Macedonia. For I am going through Macedonia.  
**16:6** And it may be I will stay with you, or even spend the winter, that you may send me forward wherever I may go.  
**16:7** For I do not desire to see you now in passage, but I am hoping to remain some time with you, if the Lord permits.  
**16:8** But I will remain in Ephesus until Pentecost.  
**16:9** For a door opened to me, great and effective, and many <i>are</i> opposing.  
**16:10** But if Timothy comes, see that he is with you without fear; for he works the work of the Lord, even as I.  
**16:11** Therefore do not let anyone despise him, but send him forward in peace, that he may come to me; for I am waiting for him with the brothers.  
**16:12** And about the brother Apollos, I much urged him that he come to you with the brothers, but it was not at all <i>his</i> will that he come now. But he will come when he his opportunity.  
**16:13** Watch! Stand fast in the faith! Be men! Be strong!  
**16:14** Let all your things be in love.  
**16:15** But I exhort you, brothers. You know the power of sin <i>is</i> the Law; house of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruit of Achaia, and they appointed themselves to ministry to the saints.  
**16:16** <i>See</i> that you also may submit to such <i>ones</i>, and to everyone working and laboring with <i>us</i>.  
**16:17** And I rejoice at the coming of Stephanas, and of Fortunatus and Achaicus, because these supplied your lack.  
**16:18** For they refreshed my spirit and yours. Then recognize such ones.  
**16:19** The assemblies of Asia greet you. Aquila and Priscilla greet you much in <i>the</i> Lord, with the assembly in their house.  
**16:20** The brothers all greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss.  
**16:21** The greeting with my hand, Paul.  
**16:22** If anyone does not love the Lord Jesus Christ, let him be accursed. The Lord comes!  
**16:23** The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ <i>be</i> with you. My love <i>be</i> with all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen.
