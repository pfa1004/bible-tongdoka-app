# 55.디모데후서 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul an apostle of Jesus Christ by <i>the</i> will of God according to the promise of life which <i>is</i> in Christ Jesus,  
**1:2** to <i>my</i> beloved child Timothy: Grace, mercy, peace from God <i>the</i> Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.  
**1:3** I have thanks to God, whom I worship from <i>my</i> forefathers in a pure conscience, how unceasingly I have remembrance concerning you in my petitions night and day,  
**1:4** longing to see you, being reminded of your tears, that I may be filled with joy,  
**1:5** taking recollection of the unfeigned faith in you, which first dwelt in your grandmother Lois and <i>in</i> your mother Eunice, and I am persuaded that <i>it is</i> also in you.  
**1:6** For which cause I remind you to fan into flame the gift of God which is in you through the laying on of my hands.  
**1:7** For God did not give a spirit of cowardice to us, but of power and of love and of self-control.  
**1:8** Therefore do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor <i>of</i> me, His prisoner. But suffer hardship with the gospel, according to <i>the</i> power of God,  
**1:9** the <i>One</i> having saved us and having called <i>us</i> with a holy calling, not according to our works, but according to <i>His</i> own purpose and grace given to us in Christ Jesus before eternal times,  
**1:10** but now revealed through the appearance of our Savior, Jesus Christ, indeed making death of no effect, bringing life and incorruption to light through the gospel,  
**1:11** to which I was appointed a herald and apostle and a teacher of nations.  
**1:12** For which cause I also suffer these things. But I am not ashamed, for I know whom I have believed, and I am persuaded that He is able to guard my deposit until that Day.  
**1:13** Hold fast a pattern of sound words which you heard from me, in faith and love in Christ Jesus.  
**1:14** Guard the good Deposit <i>given</i> through <i>the</i> Holy Spirit indwelling in us.  
**1:15** You know this, that all the <i>ones</i> in Asia turned away from me, of whom are Phygellus and Hermogenes.  
**1:16** May the Lord give mercy to the house of Onesiphorus, because he often refreshed me, and he was not ashamed <i>of</i> my chain,  
**1:17** but coming to Rome, he more diligently sought and found me.  
**1:18** May the Lord give to him to find mercy from <i>the</i> Lord in that Day, and how much he served in Ephesus, you know very well.  

## 2장

**2:1** You, therefore, my child, be empowered by grace in the Christ Jesus.  
**2:2** And what things you heard from me through many witnesses, commit these things to faithful men, such as will be competent also to teach others.  
**2:3** You, therefore, suffer hardship as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.  
**2:4** No one serving as a soldier entangles <i>himself</i> with the affairs of <i>this</i> life, so that he might please the <i>One</i> having enlisted <i>him</i>.  
**2:5** And also if anyone competes, he is not crowned unless he competes lawfully.  
**2:6** <i>It is</i> right <i>for</i> the laboring farmer to partake first of the fruits.  
**2:7** Consider what I say, for the Lord will give you understanding in all <i>things</i>.  
**2:8** Remember Jesus Christ, having been raised from <i>the</i> dead, of <i>the</i> seed of David, according to my gospel,  
**2:9** in which I suffer hardship as an evildoer, unto bonds; but the Word of God has not been bound.  
**2:10** Because of this, I endure all things on account of the elect, that they also may obtain salvation, the <i>one</i> in Christ Jesus, with everlasting glory.  
**2:11** Faithful <i>is</i> the Word: for if we died with <i>Him</i>, we also shall live with <i>Him</i>;  
**2:12** if we endure, we shall also reign with <i>Him</i>; if we deny <i>Him</i>, that One will deny us;  
**2:13** if we are faithless, that One remains faithful; He is not able to deny Himself.  
**2:14** Remind <i>them of</i> these things, solemnly testifying before the Lord not to dispute about words for nothing useful, to <i>the</i> subversion <i>of</i> those hearing.  
**2:15** Be eager, <i>study</i> to present yourself approved to God, a workman unashamed, rightly dividing the Word of Truth.  
**2:16** But shun profane, empty babblings, for they will go on to more ungodliness,  
**2:17** and their word will have spreading like gangrene, of whom <i>are</i> Hymenaeus and Philetus,  
**2:18** who deviated concerning the truth, saying the resurrection already has come, and overturn the faith of some.  
**2:19** However, the foundation of God stands firm, having this seal, "<i>The</i> Lord knows the <i>ones</i> being His." Also, Let every <i>one</i> naming the name of Christ stand away from unrighteousness.  
**2:20** But in a great house not only are there vessels of gold and silver, but also of wood and of earth, and some to honor and some to dishonor.  
**2:21** Therefore if anyone purifies himself from these, he will be a vessel unto honor, having been sanctified and made useful unto the Master, having been prepared to every good work.  
**2:22** But flee youthful lust and pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace, with the <i>ones</i> calling on the Lord out of a pure heart.  
**2:23** But refuse the foolish and uninstructed questionings, knowing that they generate quarrels.  
**2:24** But a slave of <i>the</i> Lord ought not to quarrel, but to be gentle towards all, apt to teach, forbearing,  
**2:25** in meekness instructing the <i>ones</i> opposing, if perhaps God may give them repentance for a full knowledge of <i>the</i> truth,  
**2:26** and they regained their senses out of the snare of the devil, being captured by him to <i>do</i> the will of that one.  

## 3장

**3:1** But know this, that in <i>the</i> last days grievous times will be <i>upon us</i>.  
**3:2** For men will be lovers of themselves, money-lovers, braggarts, arrogant, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy,  
**3:3** without natural affections, unyielding, slanderers, without self-control, savage, haters of good,  
**3:4** betrayers, reckless, being puffed up, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,  
**3:5** having a form of godliness, but denying the power of it; even turn away from these.  
**3:6** For of these are those creeping into houses and leading silly women captive, <i>the ones</i> having been heaped with sins, being led away by various lusts,  
**3:7** always learning, but never being able to come to a full knowledge of <i>the</i> truth.  
**3:8** But in the way Jannes and Jambres withstood Moses, so also these withstand the truth, men having been corrupted <i>in</i> mind, found worthless concerning the faith.  
**3:9** But they will not go further, for their foolishness will be plain to all, as also that of the <i>one</i> of <i>theirs</i> became.  
**3:10** But you have closely followed my doctrine, conduct, purpose, faith, longsuffering, love, patient endurance,  
**3:11** persecutions, sufferings, such as happened to me in Antioch, in Iconium, in Lystra, what persecutions I bore. And the Lord delivered me out of all.  
**3:12** And, indeed, all the <i>ones</i> desiring to live godly in Christ Jesus will be persecuted.  
**3:13** But evil men and imposters will go forward to worse, leading astray and being led astray.  
**3:14** But you keep on in what you learned and were assured of, knowing from whom you learned,  
**3:15** and that from a babe you know the Holy Scriptures, the <i>ones</i> being able to make you wise to salvation through belief in Christ Jesus.  
**3:16** All Scripture <i>is</i> God-breathed and profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness,  
**3:17** so that the man of God may be complete, having been fully equipped for every good work.  

## 4장

**4:1** Therefore, I solemnly witness before God and the Lord Jesus Christ, He being about to judge <i>the</i> living and dead <i>at</i> His appearance and His kingdom:  
**4:2** preach the Word, stand by seasonably, unseasonably, convict, warn, encourage with all long-suffering and teaching.  
**4:3** For a time will be when they will not endure sound doctrine, but according to their own lusts, they will heap up to themselves teachers tickling the ear;  
**4:4** and <i>they</i> will turn away the ear from the truth and will be turned aside to myths.  
**4:5** But you be clear-minded in all, suffer hardship, do <i>the</i> work of an evangelist, fully carry out your ministry.  
**4:6** For I am already being poured out, and the time of my release is present.  
**4:7** I have fought the good fight. I have finished the course. I have kept the faith.  
**4:8** For the rest, the crown of righteousness is laid up for me, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will give to me in that Day, and not only to me, but also to all the <i>ones</i> loving His appearance.  
**4:9** Make haste to come to me quickly.  
**4:10** For Demas deserted me, loving the present age, and <i>he</i> went to Thessalonica. Crescens <i>went</i> to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.  
**4:11** Only Luke is with me. Taking Mark, bring <i>him</i> with you, for he is useful to me for ministry.  
**4:12** But I sent Tychicus to Ephesus.  
**4:13** When you come, bring the cloak which I left in Troas with Carpus, and the books, especially the parchments.  
**4:14** Alexander the coppersmith showed many evil things to me. The Lord "will give back to him according to his works." <i>LXX-Psa. 61:13; MT-Psa. 62:12</i> ; <n>Prov 24:12</n>  
**4:15** You also <i>be on</i> guard against <i>him</i>, for he greatly stood against our words.  
**4:16** In my first defense no one came to <i>my</i> aid, but all deserted me. May it not be reckoned to them.  
**4:17** But the Lord stood with me and gave me power, that through me the preaching might be fulfilled, and all the nations might hear. And I was delivered out of <i>the</i> mouth of the lion.  
**4:18** And the Lord will deliver me from every wicked work and will save <i>me</i> for His heavenly kingdom; to whom <i>be</i> the glory to the ages of the ages. Amen.  
**4:19** Greet Prisca and Aquila and the house of Onesiphorus.  
**4:20** Erastus remained in Corinth, but I left Trophimus sick in Miletus.  
**4:21** Make haste to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, and Pudens, and Linus, and Claudia, and all the brothers.  
**4:22** The Lord Jesus Christ <i>be</i> with your spirit. Grace <i>be</i> with you. Amen.
