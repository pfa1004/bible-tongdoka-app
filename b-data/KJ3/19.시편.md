# 19.시편 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** Happy is the man who has not walked in the counsel of the wicked <i>ones</i>, and has not stood in the way of sinners, and has not sat in the seat of scorners.  
**1:2** But his delight <i>is</i> only in the Law of Jehovah, and he meditates in His Law day and night.  
**1:3** And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivulets of waters, which will give its fruit in its seasons, and its leaf will not wither, and all which he does will prosper.  
**1:4** Not so <i>are</i> the wicked <i>ones</i>, <i>but they</i> are only as chaff which the wind drives about.  
**1:5** On account of this the wicked <i>one</i> shall not stand in the judgment, and sinners not in the company of the righteous <i>ones</i>.  
**1:6** For Jehovah is knowing the way of the righteous <i>ones</i>, and the way of the wicked <i>ones</i> shall perish.  

## 2장

**2:1** Why have the nations raged and the peoples are meditating vanity?  
**2:2** The kings of the earth set themselves; yea, the rulers have plotted together against Jehovah and His Anointed One, <i>saying</i>,  
**2:3** We will break their bands in two, and throw off their cords from us.  
**2:4** He who sits in the heavens shall laugh; the Lord shall mock at them.  
**2:5** Then He will speak to them in His anger, and He will terrify them in His wrath;  
**2:6** Yea, I have anointed My king on Zion, My holy Mount.  
**2:7** I will declare concerning the statute of Jehovah: He said to Me, You <i>are</i> My Son. Today I have begotten You.  
**2:8** Ask of Me, and I will give the nations <i>as</i> Your inheritance; and the uttermost parts of the earth as Your possession.  
**2:9** You shall break them with a rod of iron; You shall smash them like a potter's vessel.  
**2:10** And now be wise, O kings; be taught, O judges of the earth:  
**2:11** Serve Jehovah with fear; yea, rejoice with trembling.  
**2:12** Kiss the Son, lest He be angry, and you perish <i>from</i> the way, when His wrath is kindled but a little. Blessed <i>are</i> all <i>the ones</i> seeking refuge in Him!  

## 3장

**3:1** <i>A Psalm of David, when he fled from his son Absalom.</i> O Jehovah, how <i>ones</i> pressing me have increased! Many <i>are</i> rising against me.  
**3:2** Many are saying to my soul, <i>There is</i> no salvation for him in God. Selah.  
**3:3** But You, O Jehovah, <i>are</i> a shield around me; my glory, and He who lifts up my head.  
**3:4** I cried to Jehovah with my voice, and He heard me out of His holy mountain. Selah.  
**3:5** I laid down and slept. I awoke, for Jehovah sustained me.  
**3:6** I am not afraid of myriads of people who have been set against me all around.  
**3:7** Arise, O Jehovah! Save me, O my God. For You have struck all my enemies <i>on</i> the cheekbone. You have broken the teeth of the wicked <i>ones</i>.  
**3:8** Salvation <i>belongs</i> to Jehovah. Your blessing <i>is</i> on Your people. Selah.  

## 4장

**4:1** <i>To the chief musician, for stringed instruments, A Psalm of David.</i> Answer me when I call, God of my righteousness. You gave room to me in distress. Have mercy on me and hear my prayer.  
**4:2** O sons of men, until when shall My glory <i>be</i> for a reproach? Will you love vanity? Will you seek a lie? Selah.  
**4:3** But know that Jehovah has set apart the godly <i>ones</i> for Himself. Jehovah hears when I call to Him.  
**4:4** Tremble and do not sin. Speak within your own heart on your bed and be still. Selah.  
**4:5** Offer the sacrifices of righteousness and trust in Jehovah.  
**4:6** Many <i>are</i> saying, Who will make us see <i>any</i> good? O Jehovah, lift up the light of Your face on us.  
**4:7** You have put gladness in my heart, more than in the time their grain and their wine became great.  
**4:8** I will both lie down in peace and sleep; for You alone, O Jehovah, make me dwell in safety.  

## 5장

**5:1** <i>To the chief musician, for flutes. A Psalm of David.</i> Give ear to my words, O Jehovah; consider my meditation.  
**5:2** Attend to the voice of my cry, my King and my God; for I will pray to You.  
**5:3** You will hear my voice in the morning, O Jehovah, I will set myself for You in the morning, and I will look up.  
**5:4** For You <i>are</i> not a God enjoying wickedness; evil shall not live with You.  
**5:5** The boasters shall not set themselves before Your eyes. You hate all workers of iniquity.  
**5:6** You shall destroy those speaking lies; Jehovah will despise the man of blood and deceit.  
**5:7** But I, in the abundance of Your grace, I will come <i>into</i> Your house. I will worship in Your fear toward Your holy temple, O Jehovah.  
**5:8** Lead me in Your righteousness, because of my enemies; make straight Your way before me.  
**5:9** For <i>there is</i> no faithfulness in their mouth; their inward part is engulfing ruin; their throat is an open grave; they flatter with their tongue.  
**5:10** O God, hold them guilty; let them fall from their own counsels. Drive them away in the multitude of their transgressions, for they have rebelled against You.  
**5:11** But all putting their trust in You shall be joyful; they shall shout for joy forever, because You cover them. And those who love Your name shall be joyful in You.  
**5:12** For You, O Jehovah, will bless the righteous <i>one</i>; You will surround him with favor, as <i>with</i> a shield.  

## 6장

**6:1** <i>To the chief musician, for eightstringed instruments. A Psalm of David.</i> O Jehovah, do not rebuke me in Your anger; and not chasten me in the heat of Your fury;  
**6:2** have mercy on me, O Jehovah, for I <i>am</i> weak; heal me, O Jehovah, for my bones are troubled.  
**6:3** My soul also is greatly troubled and You, O Jehovah, until when?  
**6:4** O Jehovah, return, deliver my soul; save me for Your mercy's sake.  
**6:5** For <i>there is</i> no memory of You in death; who shall give thanks to You in Sheol?  
**6:6** I am weary in my groaning; I shall make my bed swim in all the night; I melt my couch with my tears.  
**6:7** My eye is dim because of grief; it wastes away because of all those distressing me.  
**6:8** Turn away from me, all workers of iniquity, for Jehovah has heard the voice of my weeping.  
**6:9** Jehovah has heard my plea; Jehovah will receive my prayer.  
**6:10** Let all my enemies be ashamed and greatly troubled; they shall turn back, ashamed in a moment.  

## 7장

**7:1** <i>A song of David, which he sang to Jehovah on the words of Cush the Benjamite.</i> O Jehovah my God, I seek refuge in You; save me from all who pursue me, and deliver me,  
**7:2** lest he tear my soul like a lion, ripping, and there be no one to deliver.  
**7:3** O Jehovah, my God, if I have done this, if there is iniquity in my palms,  
**7:4** if I have repaid evil to one at peace <i>with</i> me or I have plundered <i>one</i> pressing me without cause,  
**7:5** let the enemy pursue and overtake my soul; yea, trample down my life to earth, and lay my honor in the dust. Selah.  
**7:6** Arise, O Jehovah, in Your anger; be lifted up at the rage of the <i>one</i> distressing me; and awake for me! You have commanded judgment.  
**7:7** And the assembly of the peoples shall surround You, and over it You will return on high;  
**7:8** Jehovah will judge the people. O Jehovah, judge me according to my righteousness, and according to my integrity on me.  
**7:9** I pray, let the evil of the wicked <i>ones</i> come to an end, and You will establish the righteous <i>one,</i> for the righteous God <i>is</i> a trier of hearts and reins.  
**7:10** My defense <i>is</i> on God, who saves the upright of heart.  
**7:11** God is a righteous judge; and God is angry with <i>evildoers</i> every day.  
**7:12** If he will not turn, He will whet His sword; He has trod His bow, He makes it ready;  
**7:13** yea, He has fitted for him instruments of death. He will make His arrows hotly pursue <i>him</i>.  
**7:14** Behold, he is bound <i>with</i> iniquity and he will conceive mischief; and he has fathered falsehood.  
**7:15** He dug a pit and bored it, and has fallen into the pit he made.  
**7:16** His mischief shall turn back on his own head, and his violence shall come down on his own crown.  
**7:17** I will thank Jehovah according to His righteousness and will praise the name of Jehovah most high.  

## 8장

**8:1** <i>To the chief musician, on the harp. A Psalm of David.</i> O Jehovah, our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth; <i>You</i> have given Your glory above the heavens!  
**8:2** Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings, You have founded strength, because of <i>ones</i> restricting You, to cause to cease the enemy and <i>the ones</i> avenging.  
**8:3** When I look upon Your heavens, the work of Your fingers: the moon and the stars which You have fixed;  
**8:4** what is man that You are mindful of him, and the son of man, that You care for him?  
**8:5** For You have made him lack a little from God; and have crowned him with glory and honor.  
**8:6** You made him rule over the works of Your hands; You have put all under his feet:  
**8:7** all flocks and oxen, and also the animals of the field,  
**8:8** the birds of the heavens, and the fish of the sea, <i>all that</i> pass through the sea.  
**8:9** O Jehovah, our Lord, how majestic <i>is</i> Your name in all the earth!  

## 9장

**9:1** <i>To the chief musician, To die for the Son. A Psalm of David.</i> I will give thanks, O Jehovah, with all my heart; I will tell of all Your wonderful acts;  
**9:2** I will be glad and rejoice in You; I will praise Your name, O Most High,  
**9:3** In turning my enemies to turn back, they will stumble and perish before You.  
**9:4** For You have maintained my justice and my cause; You sat on the throne judging right.  
**9:5** You have rebuked the nations; You have destroyed the wicked <i>one;</i> You have put out their name forever and ever.  
**9:6** The desolations of the enemy are ended forever; and You have uprooted the cities; their memory has perished <i>with</i> them.  
**9:7** But Jehovah abides forever; He has established His throne for judgment.  
**9:8** And He shall judge the world in righteousness; He shall judge the peoples in uprightness.  
**9:9** Jehovah also will be a refuge for oppressed <i>ones</i>, a refuge in times of distress.  
**9:10** And those who know Your name will put their trust in You; for You, Jehovah, have not forsaken those who seek You.  
**9:11** Sing praises to Jehovah who dwells in Zion; declare His deeds among the nations.  
**9:12** For He remembers them, the <i>ones</i> seeking bloodshed; He does not forget the cry of the afflicted <i>one</i>.  
**9:13** Be gracious to me, O Jehovah; see my affliction from <i>ones</i> hating me, be lifting me up from the gates of death,  
**9:14** so that I may declare all Your praises in the gates of the daughter of Zion; I will rejoice in Your salvation.  
**9:15** The nations have sunk into the pit they made; their foot is taken in the net they hid.  
**9:16** Jehovah has been known, He has executed judgment; the wicked <i>one</i> is snared in the work of his own palms. A meditation, Selah.  
**9:17** The wicked <i>ones</i> shall be turned to Sheol, all the nations forgetting God.  
**9:18** For the needy <i>one</i> shall not forever be forgotten; the hope of the poor <i>ones</i> shall <i>not</i> perish forever.  
**9:19** Arise, O Jehovah! Do not let man have strength; let the nations be judged before Your face.  
**9:20** O Jehovah, put fear in them; let the nations know they <i>are</i> but men. Selah.  

## 10장

**10:1** Why do You stand far away, O Jehovah? Will You hide in times of distress?  
**10:2** The wicked <i>one</i> in <i>his</i> pride will pursue the afflicted <i>one</i>; they will be caught in the schemes which they have devised.  
**10:3** For the wicked <i>one</i> boasts of his soul's desire, and he has blessed the covetous; scorning Jehovah.  
**10:4** Through pride of his face, the wicked <i>one</i> will not seek; there is no God in all of his schemes.  
**10:5** His ways are perverted at all times. Your judgments <i>are</i> high from his sight; <i>as for</i> all distressing him, he puffs at them.  
**10:6** He has said in his heart, I shall not be moved! From generation to generation never in evil.  
**10:7** His mouth is full of cursing and deceit and oppression; under his tongue <i>are</i> mischief and vanity.  
**10:8** He dwells in the dens of the villages; in the secret places he murders the innocent <i>one</i>; his eyes watch against the poor <i>one</i>.  
**10:9** He lies in wait secretly like a lion in his den; he lies in ambush to catch the poor <i>one</i>; he seizes the humble <i>ones</i> drawing him into his net;  
**10:10** and being crushed, he bows; yea, the poor <i>ones</i> have fallen by his mighty <i>ones</i>.  
**10:11** He has said in his heart, God has forgotten; He hides His face; He will not see forever.  
**10:12** Arise, O Jehovah! O God, lift up your hand; do not forget the lowly <i>ones</i>.  
**10:13** Upon what does the wicked <i>one</i> despise God? He has said in his heart, You will not demand <i>it</i>.  
**10:14** You have seen, for You take note of mischief and provocation, to give back with Your hand. The poor <i>one</i> leaves <i>himself</i> to You; You are the helper of the orphan.  
**10:15** Break the arm of the wicked and evil <i>one</i>; search out his wickedness <i>until</i> You find none.  
**10:16** Jehovah <i>is</i> King forever and ever; the nations are perished out of His land.  
**10:17** O Jehovah, You have heard the desire of the humble <i>one</i>; You will fix their heart; Your ear will hear.  
**10:18** Judge the orphan and the crushed <i>one</i> No more shall the man of the earth continue to terrify.

## 12장

**12:3** Jehovah shall cut off all with flattering lips <i>and</i> the tongue that speaks great things;

**12:7** You shall keep them, O Jehovah; You shall preserve them from this generation forever.  
**12:8** The wicked <i>ones</i> walk around on every side, when vileness is exalted by the sons of men.

## 14장

## 18장

**18:1** To the chief musician, A Psalm of David, the servant of Jehovah, who spoke the words of this song to Jehovah in the day that Jehovah delivered him from the hand of all his enemies, and from the hand of Saul. And he said: I love You, O Jehovah, my strength.  
**18:2** Jehovah <i>is</i> my rock and my fortress and my deliverer; <i>He is</i> my God, my Rock; I seek refuge in Him; <i>He is</i> my shield and the horn of my salvation, my high tower.  
**18:3** I will call on Jehovah, who is to be praised, and I shall be saved from my enemies.  
**18:4** The cords of death hemmed me in; yea, the floods of wicked <i>men</i> overwhelmed me;  
**18:5** cords of Sheol surrounded me; the snares of death confronted me.  
**18:6** In my distress I called on Jehovah and I cried to my God; He heard my voice out of His temple, and my cry went before Him, into His ears.  
**18:7** Then the earth shook and quaked; and the foundations of the mountains quaked and were shaken, because it angered Him.  
**18:8** A smoke went up out of His nostrils, and fire devoured out of His mouth; coals were kindled from it.  
**18:9** He bowed the heavens also and came down, and darkness <i>was</i> under His feet;  
**18:10** and He rode on a cherub, and He flew; yea, He soared on the wings of the wind;  
**18:11** He made darkness His covering, His pavilion all around Him, darkness of waters, thick clouds of the skies.  
**18:12** Out of the brightness before Him, His dark clouds passed through, hail and coals of fire.  
**18:13** Jehovah also thundered in the heavens; and the Highest gave forth His voice, hailstones and coals of fire.  
**18:14** Yea, He sent out His arrows and scattered them; and He shot out lightnings and confused them.  
**18:15** Then the stream beds of waters were seen, and the foundations of the earth were bared at Your rebuke, O Jehovah, at the blast of the breath of Your nostrils.  
**18:16** He sent from on high, He took me; He drew me out of many waters;  
**18:17** He delivered me from my strong enemy, and from <i>ones</i> hating me; for they were stronger than I.  
**18:18** They went before me in my day of calamity, but Jehovah was my support.  
**18:19** And He brought me out into a broad place; He delivered me because He delighted in me;  
**18:20** Jehovah rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands He has repaid me.  
**18:21** For I have kept the ways of Jehovah, and have not done wickedly from before my God.  
**18:22** For all His judgments <i>were</i> before me, and I did not turn away His statutes from me.  
**18:23** For I was upright with Him and kept myself from my iniquity.  
**18:24** And Jehovah has returned to me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands before His eyes.  
**18:25** With the godly <i>ones</i> You reveal Yourself as kind; with an upright man You reveal Yourself as upright;  
**18:26** with the pure You reveal Yourself as pure; and with the perverting <i>ones</i> You appear perverse;  
**18:27** for You will save an afflicted people; but <i>You</i> will bring low haughty eyes.  
**18:28** For You will light my lamp; Jehovah my God will make my darkness light.  
**18:29** For through You I can run <i>through</i> a troop; and through my God I have leaped over a wall.  
**18:30** <i>As for</i> God, His way <i>is</i> perfect; the Word of Jehovah <i>is</i> purified; He <i>is</i> a shield to all who seek refuge in Him.  
**18:31** For who <i>is</i> God besides Jehovah? Or who <i>is</i> a Rock except our God?  
**18:32** <i>It is</i> God who girds me with strength and gives my way <i>to be</i> perfect;  
**18:33** setting my feet like the deer, and making me stand on my high places;  
**18:34** teaching my hands to war, so that a bow of bronze is bent by my arms.
