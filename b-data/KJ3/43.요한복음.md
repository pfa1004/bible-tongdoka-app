# 43.요한복음 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** In <i>the</i> beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.  
**1:2** He was in <i>the</i> beginning with God.  
**1:3** All things came into being through Him, and without Him not even one <i>thing</i> came into being that has come into being.  
**1:4** In Him was life, and the life was the light of men;  
**1:5** and the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not overtake it.  
**1:6** There was a man having been sent from God; his name <i>was</i> John.  
**1:7** He came for a witness, that he might witness concerning the Light, that all might believe through Him.  
**1:8** That <i>man</i> was not the Light, but that <i>he</i> might witness concerning the Light;  
**1:9** He was the true Light which, coming into the world, enlightens every man.  
**1:10** He was in the world, and the world came into being through Him, yet the world did not know Him.  
**1:11** He came to <i>His</i> own, and <i>His</i> own did not receive Him.  
**1:12** But as many as received Him, to them He gave authority to become children of God, to the <i>ones</i> believing into His name,  
**1:13** who were generated not of blood, nor of <i>the</i> will of <i>the</i> flesh, nor of <i>the</i> will of man, but were generated of God.  
**1:14** And the Word became flesh and tabernacled among us. And we beheld His glory, glory as of an only begotten <i>One</i> from <i>the</i> Father, full of grace and of truth.  
**1:15** John witnesses about Him, and has cried out, saying, This <i>One</i> was <i>He</i> of whom I said, The <i>One</i> coming after me has been before me, for He was first of me.  
**1:16** And out of His fullness we all received, and grace for grace.  
**1:17** For the Law was given through Moses, <i>but</i> grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.  
**1:18** No one has seen God at any time; the only begotten Son, the <i>One</i> being into the bosom of the Father, that One declared Him.  
**1:19** And this is the witness of John, when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem that they might ask him, Who are you?  
**1:20** And he confessed and did not deny; and <i>he</i> confessed, I am not the Christ.  
**1:21** And they asked him, What, then? Are you Elijah? And he said, I am not. Are you the Prophet? And he answered, No.  
**1:22** Then they said to him, Who are you, that we may give an answer to the <i>ones</i> sending us? What do you say about yourself?  
**1:23** He said, "I <i>am</i> a voice crying in the wilderness:" "Make straight" "the way of the Lord," as Isaiah the prophet said. <n>Isa 40:3</n>  
**1:24** And the ones having been sent were of the Pharisees.  
**1:25** And they asked him and said to him, Why then do you baptize, if you are not the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the Prophet?  
**1:26** John answered them, saying, I baptize in water, but <i>One</i> has stood in your midst whom you do not know;  
**1:27** He it is the <i>One</i> coming after me, who come to be before me, of whom I am not worthy that I should loose the thong of His sandal.  
**1:28** These things took place in Bethabara beyond the Jordan, where John was baptizing.  
**1:29** <i>On</i> the next day, John sees Jesus coming toward him and said, Behold! The Lamb of God, the <i>One</i> taking away the sin of the world!  
**1:30** It is He about whom I said, After me comes a Man who has come to be before me, for He was first of me.  
**1:31** And I did not know Him; but that He might be revealed to Israel; on account of this I came baptizing in water.  
**1:32** And John witnessed, saying, I have seen the Spirit coming down as a dove out of Heaven, and He abode on Him.  
**1:33** And I did not know Him, but the <i>One</i> having sent me to baptize in water, that One said to me, On whomever you see the Spirit coming down and abiding on Him, this is the <i>One</i> baptizing in the Holy Spirit.  
**1:34** And I have seen and have witnessed that this <i>One</i> is the Son of God.  
**1:35** Again <i>on</i> the next day, John and two from his disciples stood.  
**1:36** And looking at Jesus walking, he says, Behold, the Lamb of God!  
**1:37** And the two disciples heard him speaking, and they followed Jesus.  
**1:38** But turning having seen them following, Jesus said to them, What do you seek? And they said to Him, Rabbi (which being translated is called Teacher), where do You stay?  
**1:39** He said to them, Come and see. They came and saw where He stays, and <i>they</i> stayed with Him that day. And <i>the</i> hour was about the tenth.  
**1:40** Andrew the brother of Simon Peter was one of the two, the <i>ones</i> hearing from John, and following Him.  
**1:41** This <i>one</i> first finds <i>his</i> own brother Simon and says to him, We have found the Messiah (which is, being translated, the Christ).  
**1:42** And he led him to Jesus. And looking at him, Jesus said, You are Simon the son of Jonah; you shall be called Cephas (which is translated Peter).  
**1:43** <i>On</i> the next day, Jesus desired to go out into Galilee. And He finds Philip, and says to him, Follow Me!  
**1:44** And Philip was from Bethsaida, of the city of Andrew and of Peter.  
**1:45** Philip finds Nathanael and said to him, We have found the <i>One of</i> whom Moses wrote in the Law and the Prophets, Jesus the son of Joseph, the <i>One</i> from Nazareth.  
**1:46** And Nathanael said to him, Can any good thing be out of Nazareth? Philip said to him, Come and see.  
**1:47** Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward Him and says concerning him, Behold, truly an Israelite in whom is no guile!  
**1:48** Nathanael said to Him, From where do You know me? Jesus answered and said to him, Before Philip called, you being under the fig tree, I saw you.  
**1:49** Nathanael answered and says to Him, Rabbi, You are the Son of God; You are the King of Israel.  
**1:50** Jesus answered and said to him, Because I said to you I saw you under the fig tree, do you believe? You will see greater <i>things</i> than these.  
**1:51** And He says to him, Truly, truly, I say to you, From now on you will see Heaven opened, and "the angels of God ascending and descending" on the Son of Man. <n>Gen 28:12</n>  

## 2장

**2:1** And the third day a marriage took place in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there.  
**2:2** And Jesus and His disciples also were invited to the marriage.  
**2:3** And <i>they</i> lacking wine, the mother of Jesus says to Him, They have no wine.  
**2:4** Jesus says to her, What is that to Me and to you, woman? My hour has not yet come.  
**2:5** His mother said to the servants, Whatever He says to you, do.  
**2:6** And there were six waterpots of stone lying, according to the purification of the Jews, each containing two or three measures.  
**2:7** Jesus said to them, Fill the waterpots with water. And they filled them to <i>the</i> top.  
**2:8** And He says to them, Now draw out and carry to the master of the feast. And they carried it.  
**2:9** But when the master of the feast tasted the water that had become wine, and did not know from where it was (but the servants drawing the water knew), the master of the feast called the bridegroom,  
**2:10** and <i>he</i> says to him, Every man first sets on the good wine, and when they have drunk freely, then the inferior. You have kept the good wine until now.  
**2:11** This beginning of the <i>miraculous</i> signs Jesus did in Cana of Galilee and revealed His glory, and His disciples believed into Him.  
**2:12** After this He went down to Capernaum, He and His mother and His brothers and His disciples. And He remained there not many days.  
**2:13** And the Passover of the Jews was near. And Jesus went up to Jerusalem.  
**2:14** And He found those selling oxen and sheep and doves in the temple, and the money changers sitting.  
**2:15** And having made a whip out of ropes, He threw all out of the temple, both the sheep, and the oxen, and the money changers, poured out the coin and overturned the tables.  
**2:16** And to the ones selling the doves, He said, Take these things from here! Not do make My Father's house a market-house.  
**2:17** And His disciples remembered that it was written, "The zeal of Your house has consumed Me." <n>Psa 69:9</n>  
**2:18** Then the Jews answered and said to Him, What sign do You show to us, since these things You do?  
**2:19** Jesus answered and said to them, Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.  
**2:20** Then the Jews said, This temple was forty-six years being built, and do You raise it up in three days?  
**2:21** But He spoke about the temple of His body.  
**2:22** Then when He was raised from the dead, His disciples remembered that He said this to them. And they believed the Scripture, even the Word that Jesus said.  
**2:23** And as He was in Jerusalem, at the Passover, at the Feast, many believed into His name, seeing the signs which He did.  
**2:24** But Jesus Himself did not commit Himself to them, because He knew all,  
**2:25** and because He had no need that anyone should witness concerning man, for He knew what was in man.  

## 3장

**3:1** But there was a man from the Pharisees, Nicodemus his name, a ruler of the Jews.  
**3:2** This one came to Jesus by night and said to Him, Rabbi, we know that You have come <i>as</i> a teacher from God. For no one is able to do these <i>miraculous</i> signs which You do, except God be with Him.  
**3:3** Jesus answered and said to him, Truly, truly, I say to you, If one is not generated from above, he is not able to see the kingdom of God.  
**3:4** Nicodemus said to Him, How is a man able to be generated, being old? He is not able to enter into his mother's womb a second <i>time</i> and be generated.  
**3:5** Jesus answered, Truly, truly, I say to you, If one is not generated out of water and Spirit, he is not able to enter into the kingdom of God.  
**3:6** The <i>thing</i> having been generated out of the flesh is flesh, and that having been generated out of the Spirit is spirit.  
**3:7** Do not wonder because I said to you, You must be generated from above.  
**3:8** The Spirit breathes where He desires, and you hear His voice; but you do not know from where He comes, and where He goes; so is everyone having been generated from the Spirit.  
**3:9** Nicodemus answered and said to Him, How are these things able to occur?  
**3:10** Jesus answered and said to him, You are the teacher of Israel, and you do not know these things?  
**3:11** Truly, truly, I say to you, That what we know, we speak; and that which we have seen, we testify. And you do not receive our witness.  
**3:12** If I tell you earthly things, and you do not believe, how will you believe if I told you heavenly things?  
**3:13** And no one has gone up into Heaven, except He having come down out of Heaven, the Son of Man who is in Heaven.  
**3:14** And even as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up,  
**3:15** that everyone believing into Him should not perish, but may have everlasting life.  
**3:16** For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that everyone believing into Him should not perish, but have everlasting life.  
**3:17** For God did not send His Son into the world that He might judge the world, but that the world might be saved through Him.  
**3:18** The <i>one</i> believing into Him is not condemned; but the <i>one</i> not believing has already been condemned, for he has not believed into the name of the only begotten Son of God.  
**3:19** And this is the judgment, that the Light has come into the world, and men loved the darkness more than the Light, for their works were evil.  
**3:20** For everyone practicing wickedness hates the Light, and does not come to the Light, that his works may not be exposed.  
**3:21** But the <i>one</i> doing the truth comes to the Light, that his works may be revealed, that they are being worked in God.  
**3:22** After these things Jesus and His disciples came into the land of Judea. And <i>He</i> stayed there with them and was baptizing.  
**3:23** And John was also baptizing in Aenon, near Salim, for many waters were there. And they were coming and were being baptized.  
**3:24** For John had not yet been thrown into the prison.  
**3:25** Then a questioning from John's disciples arose with the Jews about purification.  
**3:26** And they came to John and said to him, Teacher, <i>He</i> who was with you beyond the Jordan, to whom you have witnessed, behold, this <i>One</i> baptizes, and all are coming to Him.  
**3:27** John answered and said, A man is able to receive nothing if it is not given to him from Heaven.  
**3:28** You yourselves witness to me that I said, I am not the Christ, but that having been sent, I am before that One.  
**3:29** The <i>one</i> having the bride is <i>the</i> bridegroom. But the friend of the bridegroom, standing and hearing him, rejoices with joy because of the bridegroom's voice. Then this my joy has been fulfilled.  
**3:30** That One must increase, but me to decrease.  
**3:31** The <i>One</i> coming from above is above all. The <i>one</i> being of the earth is of the earth, and speaks of the earth. The <i>One</i> coming out of Heaven is above all.  
**3:32** And what He has seen and heard, this He testifies, and no one receives His testimony.  
**3:33** The <i>one</i> receiving His testimony has sealed that God is true.  
**3:34** For <i>the One</i> whom God sent speaks the words of God, for God does not give the Spirit by measure.  
**3:35** The Father loves the Son and has given all things into His hand.  
**3:36** The <i>one</i> believing into the Son has everlasting life; but the <i>one</i> disobeying the Son will not see life, but the wrath of God remains on him.  

## 4장

**4:1** Therefore when the Lord knew that the Pharisees heard that Jesus is making more disciples and baptizing <i>more</i> than John  
**4:2** (though truly Jesus Himself did not baptize, but His disciples),  
**4:3** He left Judea and went away into Galilee again.  
**4:4** And it was needful for Him to pass through Samaria.  
**4:5** And He came to a Samaritan city called Sychar, near the piece of land which Jacob gave to his son Joseph.  
**4:6** And Jacob's well was there. Then being wearied by the journey, Jesus sat thus on the well. <i>The</i> hour was about <i>the</i> sixth.  
**4:7** A woman comes out of Samaria to draw water. Jesus said to her, Give Me to drink.  
**4:8** For His disciples had gone away into the city that they might buy foods.  
**4:9** Then woman said to Him the woman, the Samaritan, How <i>is it that</i> You, being a Jew, ask to drink from me, <i>I</i> being a Samaritan woman? For Jews do not use <i>things</i> together with Samaritans.  
**4:10** Jesus answered and said to her, If you knew the gift of God, and who is the <i>One</i> saying to you, Give Me to drink, you would have asked Him, and He would have given you living water.  
**4:11** The woman said to Him, Sir, You have no thing to draw with. From where, then, do You have living water?  
**4:12** Not You are greater <i>than</i> our father, Jacob, who gave us the well, and he drank out of it, and his sons and his livestock <i>also</i>?  
**4:13** Jesus answered and said to her, Everyone drinking of this water will thirst again;  
**4:14** but whoever may drink of the water which I will give him will not thirst, not <i>ever</i>! But the water which I will give to him will become a fountain of water in him, springing up into everlasting life.  
**4:15** The woman said to Him, Sir, give me this water, that I may not thirst, nor come here to draw.  
**4:16** Jesus said to her, Go, call your husband and come here.  
**4:17** The woman answered and said, I have no husband. Jesus said to her, Well did you say, I have no husband.  
**4:18** For you have had five husbands, and now <i>he</i> whom you have is not your husband. This <i>thing</i> truly you have said.  
**4:19** The woman said to Him, Sir, I perceive that You are a prophet.  
**4:20** Our fathers worshiped in this mountain, and you say that in Jerusalem is the place where it is necessary to worship.  
**4:21** Jesus said to her, Woman, believe Me that an hour is coming when you will worship the Father neither in this mountain nor in Jerusalem.  
**4:22** You worship what you do not know; we worship what we know, because salvation is of the Jews.  
**4:23** But an hour is coming, and now is, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and in truth. For the Father also seeks such, the <i>ones</i> worshiping Him.  
**4:24** God <i>is</i> spirit, and the <i>ones</i> worshiping Him must worship in spirit and truth.  
**4:25** The woman said to Him, I know that Messiah is coming, the <i>One</i> being called Christ. When that One comes, He will announce to us all things.  
**4:26** Jesus said to her, I AM! the <i>One</i> speaking to you.  
**4:27** And upon this, His disciples came and marveled that He was speaking with a woman. However, no one said, What do You seek? Or, Why do You speak with her?  
**4:28** Then the woman left her waterpot and went away into the city and says to the men,  
**4:29** Come, see a Man who told me all things, as many as I did. Is not this the Christ?  
**4:30** Therefore they went out from the city and were coming to Him.  
**4:31** But in the meantime the disciples were as King Him, saying, Rabbi, eat.  
**4:32** But He said to them, I have food to eat which you do not know.  
**4:33** Then the disciples said to one another, Not did someone bring Him <i>food</i> to eat?  
**4:34** Jesus said to them, My food is that I should do the will of <i>Him</i> having sent Me, and <i>that</i> I may finish His work.  
**4:35** Do you not say, It is yet four months and the harvest comes? Behold, I say to you, Lift up your eyes and behold the fields, for they are already white to harvest.  
**4:36** And the <i>one</i> reaping receives wages, and gathers fruit to everlasting life, so that both the <i>one</i> sowing and the <i>one</i> reaping may rejoice together.  
**4:37** For in this the word is true, that another <i>is</i> the <i>one</i> sowing, and another the <i>one</i> reaping.  
**4:38** I sent you to reap what you have not labored over. Others have labored, and you have entered into their labor.  
**4:39** And many of the Samaritans out of that city believed into Him, because of the word of the woman testifying, He told me all things, as many as I did.  
**4:40** Then as the Samaritans came to Him, they asked Him to remain with them. And He remained there two days.  
**4:41** And many more believed through His Word.  
**4:42** And they were saying to the woman, We no longer believe because of your speech; for we ourselves have heard, and we know that this One is truly the Savior of the world, the Christ.  
**4:43** But after the two days, He went out from there, and <i>He</i> went into Galilee.  
**4:44** For Jesus Himself testified that a prophet has no honor in <i>his</i> own fatherland.  
**4:45** Then when He came into Galilee, the Galileans received Him, seeing all things which He did in Jerusalem at the Feast. For they also went to the Feast.  
**4:46** Then Jesus came again to Cana of Galilee where He made the water wine. And there was a certain royal <i>nobleman</i> whose son was sick in Capernaum.  
**4:47** This one, hearing that Jesus has come from Judea into Galilee, went out to Him and asked Him that He would come down and heal his son, for he was about to die.  
**4:48** Then Jesus said to him, Unless you see <i>miraculous</i> signs and wonders, you will not believe, not <i>ever</i>.  
**4:49** The royal <i>nobleman</i> said to Him, Sir, come down before my child dies.  
**4:50** Jesus said to him, Go! Your son lives. And the man believed the Word which Jesus said to him, and went away.  
**4:51** But already, as he was going down, his slaves met him and reported, saying, Your child lives.  
**4:52** He then asked from them the hour in which he had <i>become</i> better. And they said to him, Yesterday, <i>at the</i> seventh hour, the fever left him.  
**4:53** Then the father knew that <i>it was</i> at that hour in which Jesus said to him, Your son lives. And he himself, and his whole household, believed.  
**4:54** This, again, <i>is the</i> second <i>miraculous</i> sign Jesus did, coming from Judea into Galilee.  

## 5장

**5:1** After these things, there was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.  
**5:2** And in Jerusalem is a pool at the Sheep Gate, the <i>one</i> called in Hebrew, Bethesda, having five porches.  
**5:3** In these was a great multitude of the <i>ones</i> being infirm lying, blind <i>ones</i>, lame <i>ones</i>, withered <i>ones</i>, awaiting the stirring of the water.  
**5:4** For an angel at a <i>certain</i> time descended in the pool and agitated the water. Therefore the <i>one</i> first entering after the agitation of the water became well, whatever disease he was held by.  
**5:5** But a certain man was there, having <i>been</i> in infirmity thirty-eight years.  
**5:6** Jesus, seeing this <i>one</i> lying, and knowing that he had already <i>spent</i> much time, <i>He</i> said to him, Do you desire to become well?  
**5:7** The <i>one</i> being infirm answered Him, Lord, I do not have a man, that when the water is agitated he may throw me into the pool; but while I am coming, another goes down before me.  
**5:8** Jesus said to him, Rise up, Take up your pallet and walk!  
**5:9** And instantly the man became well, and took up his pallet and walked. And it was a sabbath that day.  
**5:10** Therefore the Jews said to the <i>one</i> having been healed, It is sabbath. It is not lawful for you to lift up the pallet.  
**5:11** He answered them, The One making me well, that <i>One</i> said to me, Lift up your pallet and walk.  
**5:12** Then they asked him, Who is the man who told you, Lift up your pallet and walk?  
**5:13** But the <i>one</i> having been healed did not know who it is, for a crowd being in that place, Jesus had withdrawn.  
**5:14** After these things, Jesus found him in the temple and said to him, Behold, you have become well, sin no more that a worse thing not happen to you.  
**5:15** The man went away and told the Jews that Jesus is the <i>One</i> making him well.  
**5:16** And because of this the Jews were persecuting Jesus and were seeking to kill Him, because He did these things on a sabbath.  
**5:17** But Jesus answered them, My Father works until now, and I work.  
**5:18** Because of this, therefore, the Jews were seeking the more to kill Him, for not only did He break the sabbath, but also called God His own Father, making Himself equal to God.  
**5:19** Then Jesus answered and said to them, Truly, truly, I say to you, The Son is not able to do anything from Himself, except what He may see the Father doing; for whatever that One does, these things also the Son does likewise.  
**5:20** For the Father loves the Son and shows to Him all things which He does. And He will show Him greater works than these in order that you may wonder.  
**5:21** For even as the Father raises the dead, and makes alive, so also the Son makes alive whom He wills.  
**5:22** For the Father judges no one, but has given all judgment to the Son,  
**5:23** so that all may honor the Son, even as they honor the Father. The <i>one</i> not honoring the Son does not honor the Father who has sent Him.  
**5:24** Truly, truly, I say to you, The <i>one</i> who hears My Word, and believing the <i>One</i> having sent Me, has everlasting life, and does not come into judgment, but has passed out of death into life.  
**5:25** Truly, truly, I say to you that an hour is coming, and now is, when the dead ones will hear the voice of the Son of God, and the <i>ones</i> hearing will live.  
**5:26** For even as the Father has life in Himself, so He gave also to the Son to have life in Himself.  
**5:27** And He also gave authority to Him to execute judgment, for He is <i>the</i> Son of Man.  
**5:28** Marvel not <i>at</i> this, for an hour is coming in which all those in the tombs will hear His voice.  
**5:29** And will come out, the <i>ones</i> having done good <i>things</i>, into a resurrection of life; and the <i>ones</i> having practiced evil things into a resurrection of judgment.  
**5:30** I am not able to do <i>anything</i> of Myself, nothing! Just as I hear, I judge; and My judgment is just, because I do not seek My will, but the will of the Father having sent Me.  
**5:31** If I witness about Myself, My witness is not true;  
**5:32** it is Another that witnesses about Me, and I know that the witness which He witnesses about Me is true.  
**5:33** You have sent to John, and he has testified to the truth.  
**5:34** But I do not receive witness from man, but I say these things that you may be saved.  
**5:35** That one was the burning and shining lamp, and you were willing to exult in his light for an hour.  
**5:36** But I have the witness greater than the <i>one</i> of John, for the works which the Father has given Me, that I should finish them, the works which I do themselves, witness concerning Me, that the Father has sent Me.  
**5:37** And the Father, the <i>One</i> having sent Me, has Himself borne witness about Me. You have neither heard His voice at any time, nor have you seen His form.  
**5:38** And you do not have His Word abiding in you, for <i>the One</i> whom that One sent, this <i>One</i> you do not believe.  
**5:39** You search the Scriptures, for you think in them you have everlasting life. And they are the <i>ones</i> witnessing concerning Me.  
**5:40** And you are not willing to come to Me that you may have life.  
**5:41** I do not receive glory from men;  
**5:42** but I have known you, that you do not have the love of God in yourselves.  
**5:43** I have come in the name of My Father, and you do not receive Me. If another comes in <i>his</i> own name, you will receive that one.  
**5:44** How are you able to believe, <i>you who</i> receive glory from one another, and the glory which <i>is</i> from the only God you do not seek?  
**5:45** Do not think that I will accuse you to the Father; there is <i>one</i> accusing you, Moses, in whom you have hoped.  
**5:46** For if you believed Moses, you would believe Me; for that one wrote about Me.  
**5:47** But if you do not believe his writings, how will you believe My words?  

## 6장

**6:1** After these things, Jesus went away over the Sea of Galilee, the Tiberian <i>Sea</i>.  
**6:2** And a great crowd followed Him, for they saw His <i>miraculous</i> signs which He did on the <i>ones</i> being sick.  
**6:3** And Jesus went up into the mountain and sat there with His disciples.  
**6:4** And the Passover was near, the feast of the Jews.  
**6:5** Then Jesus lifting up <i>His</i> eyes and seeing that a great crowd is coming to Him, He said to Philip, From where may we buy bread that these may eat?  
**6:6** But He said this to test him, for He knew what He was about to do.  
**6:7** Philip answered Him, Bread for two hundred denarii are not enough for them, that each of them may receive a little.  
**6:8** One of His disciples said to Him, Andrew the brother of Simon Peter,  
**6:9** There is one little boy here who has five barley loaves and two small fish; but what are these among so many?  
**6:10** And Jesus said, Make the men to recline. And much grass was in the place. Then the men reclined, the number was about five thousand.  
**6:11** And Jesus took the loaves of bread, and giving thanks distributed to the disciples, and the disciples likewise of the fish, as much as they wanted.  
**6:12** And when they were filled, He said to His disciples, Gather up the fragments left over, that not anything be lost.  
**6:13** Then they gathered and filled twelve baskets of fragments from the five barley loaves of bread which were left over to the <i>ones</i> having eaten.  
**6:14** Then the men, seeing what <i>miraculous</i> sign Jesus did, said, This is truly the Prophet, the <i>One</i> coming into the world.  
**6:15** Therefore knowing that they were about to come and seize Him, that they might make Him king, Jesus withdrew again to the mountain Himself alone.  
**6:16** And when it became evening, His disciples went down on the sea.  
**6:17** And entering into the boat, they were going across the sea to Capernaum. And darkness had already occurred, and Jesus had not come to them.  
**6:18** And the sea was aroused by a great wind blowing.  
**6:19** Then having rowed about twenty five or thirty stadia, they saw Jesus walking on the sea, and becoming near the boat, they were afraid.  
**6:20** But He said to them, I AM! Do not fear.  
**6:21** Then they desired to take Him into the boat. And the boat was instantly at the land to which they were going.  
**6:22** The next day, the crowd, the <i>one</i> standing across the sea had seen that no other little boat was there except one, that one into which His disciples entered, and that Jesus did not go in with His disciples into the small boat, but <i>that</i> the disciples went away alone.  
**6:23** But other small boats came from Tiberias near the place where they ate the bread, the Lord having given thanks.  
**6:24** Therefore, when the crowd saw that Jesus was not there nor His disciples, they themselves also stepped into the boats and came to Capernaum seeking Jesus.  
**6:25** And finding Him across the sea, they said to Him, Rabbi, when did you come here?  
**6:26** Jesus answered them and said, Truly, truly, I say to you, You seek Me not because you saw <i>miraculous</i> signs, but because you ate of the loaves of bread and were satisfied.  
**6:27** Do not work <i>for</i> the food, the <i>one</i> perishing, but for the food, the <i>one</i> enduring to everlasting life, which the Son of Man will give to you; for God the Father sealed Him.  
**6:28** Then they said to Him, What may we do that we may work the works of God?  
**6:29** Jesus answered and said to them, This is the work of God, that you believe into <i>Him</i> whom that One sent.  
**6:30** Therefore they said to Him, Then what <i>miraculous</i> sign do You do that we may see and may believe You? What do You work?  
**6:31** Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, even as it is written "He gave them bread out of the heaven to eat." <i>LXX-Psa. 77:24; MT-Psa. 78:24  
**6:32** Then Jesus said to them, Truly, truly, I say to you, Moses has not given you the bread out of the heaven, but My Father gives you the true bread out of Heaven.  
**6:33** For the bread of God is the <i>One</i> coming down out of the heaven and giving life to the world.  
**6:34** Then they said to Him, Lord, always give us this bread.  
**6:35** And Jesus said to them, I am the Bread of life; the <i>one</i> coming to Me will not hunger, not <i>ever;</i> and the <i>one</i> believing into Me will not thirst, not <i>ever  
**6:36** But I said to you that you also have seen Me and do not believe.  
**6:37** All who the Father gives to Me shall come to Me, and the <i>one</i> coming to Me I will not cast out, not <i>ever</i>,  
**6:38** for I have come down out of Heaven, not that I should do My will, but the will of Him who sent Me.  
**6:39** And this is the will of the Father sending Me, that of all that He has given Me, I shall not lose <i>any</i> of it, but shall raise it up in the last day.  
**6:40** And this is the will of the <i>One</i> having sent Me, that everyone seeing the Son and believing into Him should have everlasting life; and I will raise him up at the last day.  
**6:41** Therefore the Jews murmured about Him, because He said, I am the Bread coming down out of Heaven.  
**6:42** And they said, Is this not Jesus the son of Joseph, of whom we know the father and the mother? How, then, does this <i>One</i> now say, I have come down out of Heaven?  
**6:43** Therefore Jesus answered and said to them, Do not murmur with one another.  
**6:44** No one is able to come to Me if not the Father, the <i>One</i> having sent Me, draws him, and I will raise him up in the last day.  
**6:45** It is being written in the Prophets, They "shall" all "be taught of God." Therefore every <i>one</i> hearing and having learned from the Father comes to Me; <n>Isa 54:13</n>  
**6:46** not that anyone has seen the Father, except He being from God, this One has seen the Father.  
**6:47** Truly, truly, I say to you, The <i>one</i> believing into Me has everlasting life.  
**6:48** I am the Bread of life.  
**6:49** Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness and died.  
**6:50** This is the Bread, the <i>One</i> coming down out of Heaven, that anyone may eat of it and not die.  
**6:51** I am the Living Bread, the <i>One</i> having come down from Heaven. If anyone eats of this Bread, he will live to the age. And indeed the bread which I will give is My flesh, which I will give for the life of the world.  
**6:52** Therefore the Jews argued with one another, saying, How can this <i>One</i> give us <i>His</i> flesh to eat?  
**6:53** Then Jesus said to them, Truly, truly, I say to you, If you do not eat the flesh of the Son of Man, and drink His blood, you do not have life in yourselves.  
**6:54** The <i>one</i> eating My flesh and drinking My blood has everlasting life, and I will raise him up at the last day.  
**6:55** For My flesh is truly food, and My blood is truly drink.  
**6:56** The <i>one</i> eating My flesh and drinking My blood abides in Me, and I in him.  
**6:57** Even as the living Father sent Me, and I live through the Father; also the <i>one</i> eating Me, even that one will live through Me.  
**6:58** This is the Bread which came down out of Heaven, not as your fathers ate the manna and died; the <i>one</i> partaking of this Bread will live to the age.  
**6:59** He said these things teaching in a synagogue in Capernaum.  
**6:60** Then many of His disciples having heard, <i>they</i> said, This Word is hard; who is able to hear it?  
**6:61** But knowing in Himself that His disciples were murmuring about this, He said to them, Does this cause you to stumble ?  
**6:62** Then <i>what</i> if you see the Son of Man going up where He was before?  
**6:63** It is the Spirit, the <i>One</i> making alive. The flesh does not profit, nothing! The words which I speak to you are spirit and are life.  
**6:64** But there are some of you who are not believing. For Jesus knew from <i>the</i> beginning who <i>they</i> are, the <i>ones</i> not believing, and who is the <i>one</i> giving Him over.  
**6:65** And He said, Because of this, I have told you that no one is able to come to Me if it is not given to him from My Father.  
**6:66** From this <i>time</i> many of His disciples went away into the things behind, and no longer walked with Him.  
**6:67** Therefore, Jesus said to the Twelve, Do you not also wish to go?  
**6:68** Then Simon Peter answered Him, Lord, to whom shall we go? You have <i>the</i> words of everlasting life.  
**6:69** And we have believed and have known that You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.  
**6:70** Jesus answered them, Did I not choose you, the Twelve? Yet one <i>of</i> you is a devil!  
**6:71** But He spoke <i>of</i> Judas Iscariot, Simon's son, for this <i>one</i> was going to give Him over, being one of the Twelve.  

## 7장

**7:1** And after these things Jesus was walking in Galilee; for He did not desire to walk in Judea, because the Jews were seeking to kill Him.  
**7:2** And the Feast of the Jews, The <i>Feast of</i> Tabernacles was near.  
**7:3** Therefore His brothers said to Him, Depart from here and go to Judea, that Your disciples will also see Your works which You are doing,  
**7:4** for no one does anything in secret and himself seeks to be in openness. If You do these things, reveal Yourself to the world.  
**7:5** For not even His brothers believed into Him.  
**7:6** Therefore Jesus said to them, My time is not yet present, but your time is always ready.  
**7:7** The world cannot hate you, but it hates Me because I witness about it, that its works are evil.  
**7:8** You go up to this feast. I am not yet going up to this feast, for My time has not yet been fulfilled.  
**7:9** And saying these things to them, He remained in Galilee.  
**7:10** But when His brothers went up, then He also went up to the feast, not openly, but as in secret.  
**7:11** Then the Jews sought Him in the feast, and said, Where is that One?  
**7:12** And much murmuring about Him was in the crowds. Some indeed said, He is a good <i>One</i>; but others said, No, but He deceives the crowd.  
**7:13** However, no one freely spoke about Him, because of the fear of the Jews.  
**7:14** But the feast now being in the middle, Jesus went up to the temple and taught.  
**7:15** And the Jews marveled, saying, How does this One know letters, not having learned?  
**7:16** Jesus answered them and said, My doctrine is not Mine, but of the <i>One</i> who sent Me.  
**7:17** If anyone desires to do His will, he will know concerning the doctrine, whether it is of God, or I speak from Myself.  
**7:18** The <i>one</i> speaking from himself seeks his own glory. But the <i>one</i> seeking the glory of the <i>One</i> who sent Him, this <i>One i</i>s true, and unrighteousness is not in Him.  
**7:19** Has not Moses given you the Law, and not one of you does the Law? Why do you seek to kill Me?  
**7:20** The crowd answered and said, You have a demon. Who seeks to kill You?  
**7:21** Jesus answered and said to them, I did one work, and you all marvel.  
**7:22** Because of this Moses has given you circumcision; (not that it is of Moses, but of the fathers); and <i>on</i> a sabbath, you circumcise a man.  
**7:23** If a man receives circumcision <i>on</i> a sabbath, that the Law of Moses is not broken, are you angry with Me because I made a whole man healthy on a sabbath?  
**7:24** Do not judge according to sight, but judge righteous judgment.  
**7:25** Therefore some of the Jerusalemites said, Is it not this <i>One</i> whom they are seeking to kill?  
**7:26** And, behold, He speaks freely, and they say nothing to Him. Perhaps the rulers truly knew that this is truly the Christ?  
**7:27** But we know this <i>One</i>, from where He is. But when the Christ comes, no one knows from where He is.  
**7:28** Therefore teaching, Jesus cried out in the temple, and saying, You both know Me, and you know from where I am. And I have not come from Myself, but He is true, the <i>One</i> having sent Me, whom you do not know.  
**7:29** But I know Him, because I am from Him; and that <i>one</i> sent Me.  
**7:30** Therefore they sought to seize Him; yet no one laid <i>a</i> hand on Him, because His hour had not yet come.  
**7:31** But many of the crowd believed into Him, and said, The Christ, when He comes will He do more <i>miraculous</i> signs than these which this One did?  
**7:32** The Pharisees heard the crowd murmuring these things about Him, and the Pharisees and the chief priests sent officers, that they might seize Him.  
**7:33** Then Jesus said to them, Yet a little while I am with you, and I go to the <i>One</i> having sent Me.  
**7:34** You will seek Me, and you will not find <i>Me</i>; and where I am, you are not able to come.  
**7:35** Therefore the Jews said amongst themselves, Where is this <i>One</i> about to go that we will not find Him? Is He about to go to the Dispersion of the Greeks, and to teach the Greeks?  
**7:36** What is this word which He said, You will seek Me and will not find <i>Me</i>, and, Where I am, you are not able to come?  
**7:37** And in the last great <i>day</i> of the feast, Jesus stood and cried out, saying, If anyone thirsts, let him come to Me and drink.  
**7:38** The <i>one</i> believing into Me, as the Scripture said, Out of his belly will flow rivers of living water. <i>See John 4:14  
**7:39** But He said this about the Spirit, whom the <i>ones</i> believing into Him were about to receive; for <i>the</i> Holy Spirit was not yet <i>given</i>, because Jesus was not yet glorified.  
**7:40** Then hearing the Word, many of the crowd said, This is truly the Prophet.  
**7:41** Others said, This is the Christ. But others said, For the Christ does not come out of Galilee, <i>does He</i>?  
**7:42** Has not the Scripture said that the Christ comes from the seed of David, and from Bethlehem, the village where David was? <n>Mic 5:2</n>  
**7:43** Therefore a division occurred in the crowd because of Him.  
**7:44** And some of them desired to seize Him, but no one laid hands on Him.  
**7:45** Then the officers came to the chief priests and Pharisees. And those <i>men</i> said to them, Why did you not bring Him?  
**7:46** The officers answered, Never did a man so speak as does this man.  
**7:47** Therefore the Pharisees answered them, Have you also been deceived?  
**7:48** Not any from the rulers or from the Pharisees believed into Him?  
**7:49** But this crowd, not knowing the Law, is cursed.  
**7:50** Nicodemus said to them, the <i>one</i> coming by night to Him, being one of them.  
**7:51** Our Law does not judge the man if not it hear from him first, and know what he does?  
**7:52** They answered and said to him, Are you also from Galilee? Search and see that a prophet has not been raised out of Galilee.  
**7:53** And they each one went to his house.  

## 8장

**8:1** But Jesus went to the Mount of Olives.  
**8:2** And at dawn, He again arrived into the temple; and all the people came to Him. And having sat down, He was teaching them.  
**8:3** And the scribes and the Pharisees bring to Him a woman having been taken in adultery. And standing her in <i>the</i> midst,  
**8:4** they said to Him, Teacher, this woman was taken in the very act, committing adultery.  
**8:5** And in the Law, Moses commanded that such should be stoned. You, therefore, what do You say?  
**8:6** But they said this, testing Him, that they may have <i>reason</i> to accuse Him. But bending down, Jesus was writing with the finger in the earth, not appearing <i>to hear</i>.  
**8:7** But as they continued questioning Him, bending back up, He said to them, The <i>one</i> among you without sin let him cast a stone at her first.  
**8:8** And bending down again, He wrote in the earth.  
**8:9** But they hearing, and being convicted by the conscience, went out one by one, beginning from the older <i>ones</i>, until the last. And Jesus was left alone, and the woman standing in <i>the</i> midst.  
**8:10** And Jesus bending back up, and having seen no one but the woman, He said to her, Woman, where are those, the accusers of you? Did not one give judgment against you?  
**8:11** And she said, No one, Lord. And Jesus said to her, Neither do I give judgment against you. Go, and sin no more.  
**8:12** Then Jesus again spoke to them, saying, I am the Light of the world. The <i>one</i> following Me will not walk in the darkness, not <i>ever,</i> but will have the light of life.  
**8:13** Therefore the Pharisees said to Him, You witness about Yourself; Your witness is not true.  
**8:14** Jesus answered and said to them, Even if I witness about Myself, My witness is true; for I know from where I came, and where I go. But you do not know from where I came, and where I go.  
**8:15** You judge according to the flesh. I judge no one.  
**8:16** But even if I judge, My judgment is true, because I am not alone, but I and <i>the</i> Father sending Me.  
**8:17** And in your Law it has been written that the witness of two men is true. <n>Deut 19:15</n>  
**8:18** I am the One witnessing about Myself, and He sending Me, <i>the</i> Father, witnesses concerning Me.  
**8:19** Then they said to Him, Where is Your father? Jesus answered, You neither know Me, nor My Father. If you had known Me, then you also would have known My Father.  
**8:20** Jesus spoke these words in the treasury, teaching in the temple; and no one seized Him, for His hour had not yet come.  
**8:21** Then Jesus said to them again, I go, and you will seek Me. And you will die in your sin. Where I go, you are not able to come.  
**8:22** Therefore the Jews said, Will He not kill Himself, because He says, Where I go, you are not able to come?  
**8:23** And He said to them, You are from below; I am from above. You are from this world; I am not from this world.  
**8:24** Therefore, I said to you that you will die in your sins. For if you do not believe that I AM, you will die in your sins.  
**8:25** Then they said to Him, Who are You? And Jesus said to them, <i>At</i> the Beginning, what I also said to you.  
**8:26** I have many things to say and to judge concerning you; but the <i>One</i> sending Me is true, and what I heard from Him, these things I say to the world.  
**8:27** They did not know that He spoke to them <i>of</i> the Father.  
**8:28** Therefore Jesus said to them, When you lift up the Son of Man, then you will know that I AM; and from Myself I do nothing; but as My Father taught Me, these things I speak.  
**8:29** And the <i>One</i> having sent Me is with Me. The Father did not leave Me alone, for I do the things pleasing to Him always.  
**8:30** <i>As</i> He spoke these things, many believed into Him.  
**8:31** Then Jesus said to the Jews having believed into Him, If you continue in My Word, you are truly My disciples.  
**8:32** And you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.  
**8:33** They answered Him, We are Abraham's seed, and we have been in slavery to no one, ever! How do You say, You will become free?  
**8:34** Jesus answered them, Truly, truly, I say to you, Everyone practicing sin is a slave of sin.  
**8:35** But the slave does not remain into the house to the age; the son remains to the age.  
**8:36** Therefore, if the Son sets you free, you are free indeed.  
**8:37** I know that you are Abraham's seed, but you seek to kill Me, because My Word is not given place in you.  
**8:38** I speak what I have seen with My Father. And you therefore do what you have seen with your father.  
**8:39** They answered and said to Him, Abraham is our father. Jesus said to them, If you were children of Abraham, you would do the works of Abraham.  
**8:40** But now you seek to kill Me, a man who has spoken the truth to you, which I heard alongside <i>of</i> God. Abraham did not do this.  
**8:41** You do the works of your father. Then they said to Him, We were not generated of fornication; we have one father, God.  
**8:42** Therefore Jesus said to them, If God were your Father, you would love Me, for I went forth and have come from God. For I have not come from Myself, but that One sent Me.  
**8:43** Why do you not know My speech? <i>It is</i> because you are not able to hear My Word.  
**8:44** You are of the devil as father, and the lusts of your father you desire to do. That one was a murderer from <i>the</i> beginning, and he has not stood in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaks <i>a</i> lie, he speaks from <i>his</i> own <i>things</i>, because he is a liar, and the father of it.  
**8:45** And because I speak the truth, you do not believe Me.  
**8:46** Who of you reproves Me concerning sin? But if I speak truth, why do you not believe Me?  
**8:47** The <i>one</i> who is of God hears the words of God; for this reason you do not hear, because you are not of God.  
**8:48** Then the Jews answered and said to Him, Do we not say well that You are a Samaritan and have a demon?  
**8:49** Jesus answered, I do not have a demon; but I honor My Father, and you dishonor Me.  
**8:50** But I do not seek My glory; <i>there</i> is One who seeks and judges.  
**8:51** Truly, truly, I say to you, If anyone keeps My Word, he will not see death, into the age, not <i>ever</i>!  
**8:52** Then the Jews said to Him, Now we know that You have a demon. Abraham died, and the prophets, and You say, If anyone keeps My Word, he will not taste of death into the age, not <i>ever</i>!  
**8:53** Are You greater than our father Abraham who died? And the prophets died! Whom do You make Yourself?  
**8:54** Jesus answered, If I glorify Myself, My glory is nothing; it is My Father who glorifies Me, whom you say is your God.  
**8:55** And you have not known Him; but I know Him, And if I say that I do not know Him, I shall be like you, a liar. But I know Him, and I keep His Word.  
**8:56** Your father Abraham leaped for joy that he should see My day, and he saw and was glad.  
**8:57** Then the Jews said to Him, You do not yet have fifty years, and have You seen Abraham?  
**8:58** Jesus said to them, Truly, truly, I say to you, Before Abraham came to be, I AM!  
**8:59** Because of this, they picked up stones that they might throw <i>them</i> on Him. But Jesus was hidden, and went forth out of the temple, going through <i>the</i> midst of them, and so passed by.  

## 9장

**9:1** And passing by, He saw a man blind from birth.  
**9:2** And His disciples asked Him, saying, Teacher, who sinned, this <i>one</i>, or his parents, that he was born blind?  
**9:3** Jesus answered, Neither this <i>one</i> nor his parents sinned, but that the works of God might be revealed in him.  
**9:4** It is necessary <i>for</i> Me to work the works of the <i>One</i> having sent Me while it is day. Night comes when no one is able to work.  
**9:5** While I am in the world, I am <i>the</i> Light of the world.  
**9:6** Saying these things, He spat on the ground and made clay out of the spittle, and anointed clay on the eyes of the blind <i>one</i>.  
**9:7** And He said to him, Go wash in the pool of Siloam, which translated is Sent. Then he went and washed, and came seeing.  
**9:8** Therefore the neighbors and the <i>ones</i> formerly seeing him, that he was blind, said, Is this <i>one</i> not the <i>one</i> sitting and begging?  
**9:9** Others said, It is he; and others, He is like him. That one said, I am.  
**9:10** Therefore they said to him, How were your eyes opened?  
**9:11** That one answered and said, <i>A</i> man being called Jesus made clay and anointed my eyes, and told me, Go to the pool of Siloam and wash. And having gone and having washed I saw anew.  
**9:12** Then they said to him, Where is that One? He said, I do not know.  
**9:13** They lead him to the Pharisees, the <i>one</i> once blind.  
**9:14** And it was a sabbath when Jesus made the clay and opened his eyes.  
**9:15** Then also the Pharisees again asked him how he saw anew. And he said to them, He put clay on my eyes, and I washed, and I see.  
**9:16** Therefore some of the Pharisees said, This man is not from God, because He does not keep the sabbath. Others said, How can a man, a sinful <i>man</i>, do such <i>miraculous</i> signs? And there was a division among them.  
**9:17** They said to the blind <i>one</i> again, What do you say about Him, because He opened your eyes? And he said, He is a prophet.  
**9:18** Therefore the Jews did not believe about him, that he was blind and saw anew, until they called the parents of him, the <i>one</i> seeing anew.  
**9:19** And they asked them, saying, Is this your son, whom you say that he was born blind? Then how does he now see?  
**9:20** His parents answered them and said, We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind.  
**9:21** But how he now sees, we do not know; or who opened his eyes, we do not know. He is of age, ask him. He will speak about himself.  
**9:22** His parents said these things because they feared the Jews; for the Jews had already agreed that if anyone should confess Him <i>as</i> Christ, he would be expelled from the synagogue.  
**9:23** Because of this, his parents said, He is of age, ask him.  
**9:24** Therefore a second time they called the man who was blind, and <i>they</i> said to him, Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner.  
**9:25** Therefore he answered and said, Whether He is a sinner, I do not know. One <i>thing</i> I do know; that being blind, now I see.  
**9:26** And they said to him again, What did He do to you? How did He open your eyes?  
**9:27** He answered them, I told you already, and you did not hear. Why do you want to hear again? Do you also want to become disciples of Him?  
**9:28** Then they reviled him and said, You are a disciple of that One, but we are disciples of Moses.  
**9:29** We know that God has spoken by Moses, but this One, we do not know from where He is.  
**9:30** The man answered and said to them, For this is a marvelous <i>thing</i>, that you do not know from where He is, and He opened my eyes.  
**9:31** But we know that God does not hear sinful <i>ones</i>, but if anyone is God-fearing, and does His will, He hears that <i>one</i>.

**20:10** Then the disciples went away again to themselves.  
**20:11** But Mary stood outside at the tomb, weeping. Therefore as she wept she stooped to look into the tomb.  
**20:12** And <i>she</i> sees two angels in white, sitting one at the head, and one at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain.  
**20:13** And those <i>ones</i> say to her, Woman, why do you weep? She says to them, Because they took away my Lord, and I do not know where they put Him.  
**20:14** And having said these things, she turned to the things behind and sees Jesus standing, and did not know that it is Jesus.  
**20:15** Jesus says to her, Woman, why do you weep? Whom do you seek? Thinking that it was the gardener, she says to Him, Sir, if You carried Him away, tell me where You put Him, and I will take Him away.  
**20:16** Jesus says to her, Mary! Turning around, she says to Him, Rabboni! (which is to say, Teacher).  
**20:17** Jesus says to her, Do not take hold <i>of Me</i>, for I have not yet ascended to My Father. But go to My brothers and say to them, I am ascending to My Father and your Father, and My God, and your God.  
**20:18** Mary Magdalene comes reporting to the disciples that she had seen the Lord, and that He said to her these things.  
**20:19** Then it being evening on that day, the first of the sabbaths, and the doors having been locked where the disciples were gathered together because of fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood in the midst and says to them, Peace to you.  
**20:20** And saying this, He showed them <i>His</i> hands and side. Then seeing the Lord, the disciples rejoiced.  
**20:21** Therefore Jesus said to them again, Peace to you. As the Father has sent Me, I also send you.  
**20:22** And saying this, He breathed on <i>them</i> and said to them, Receive <i>the</i> Holy Spirit.  
**20:23** Of whomever you may remit the sins, they are remitted to them. Of whomever you hold, they have been held.  
**20:24** But Thomas, one of the Twelve, the <i>one</i> called Twin, was not with them when Jesus came.  
**20:25** Therefore the other disciples said to him, We have seen the Lord. But he said to them, If I do not see the mark of the nails in His hands, and put my finger into the mark of the nails, and put my han<i>d</i> into His side, I will not believe, not <i>at all</i>!  
**20:26** And after eight days, His disciples were inside again, and Thomas was with them. The door having been locked, Jesus came and stood in the midst, and said, Peace to you.  
**20:27** Then He says to Thomas, Bring your finger here and see My hands, and bring your hand and put <i>it</i> into My side, and do not be unbelieving, but believing.  
**20:28** And Thomas answered and said to Him, My Lord and my God!  
**20:29** Jesus says to him, Because you have seen Me, Thomas, you have believed. Blessed <i>are</i> the <i>ones</i> not seeing, and believing.  
**20:30** Then truly Jesus also did many other <i>miraculous</i> signs in the sight of His disciples, which are not written in this book.  
**20:31** But these have been written that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing you may have life in His name.  

## 21장

**21:1** After these things, Jesus revealed Himself again to the disciples at the Sea of Tiberias. And thus He revealed <i>Himself</i>.  
**21:2** <i>There</i> were together Simon Peter, and Thomas, the <i>one</i> being called Twin, and Nathanael from Cana of Galilee, and the <i>sons</i> of Zebedee, and two others of His disciples.  
**21:3** Simon Peter says to them, I am going away to fish. They say to him, We also are coming with you. They went out and went up into the boat at once. And in that night, they caught nothing.  
**21:4** But early morning now having become, Jesus stood onto the shore. However, the disciples did not know that it is Jesus.  
**21:5** Then Jesus says to them, Children, do you not have any thing for eating? They answered Him, No.  
**21:6** And He said to them, Cast the net into the right side of the boat and you will find. Therefore they cast, and they no longer had <i>the</i> strength to draw, from the multitude of the fish.  
**21:7** Therefore the disciple whom Jesus loved says to Peter, It is the Lord. Then Simon Peter hearing that it is the Lord, having girded on <i>his</i> outer garment (for he was unclad) and he threw himself into the sea.  
**21:8** And the other disciples came in the little boat, for they were not far from the land, from about two hundred cubits, dragging the net of the fish.  
**21:9** Then when they came down onto the land, they saw a coal fire lying, and a fish lying on <i>it</i>, and bread.  
**21:10** Jesus says to them, Bring from the little fish which you caught now.  
**21:11** Simon Peter went up and dragged the net onto the land, full of great fish, a hundred and fifty three. And <i>though</i> being so many, the net was not torn  
**21:12** Jesus says to them, Come, break fast. But no one of the disciples dared to ask Him, Who are You? knowing that it is the Lord.  
**21:13** Then Jesus comes and takes the bread, and gives to them, and the little fish likewise.  
**21:14** This <i>was</i> now a third <i>time</i> Jesus was revealed to His disciples, He having been raised from <i>the</i> dead <i>ones</i>.  
**21:15** Therefore when they broke fast, Jesus says to Simon Peter, Simon, <i>son</i> of Jonah, do you love Me more <i>than</i> these? He says to Him, Yes, Lord, You know that I love You. He says to him, Feed My lambs!  
**21:16** Again He says to him a second <i>time</i>, Simon, <i>son</i> of Jonah, do you love Me? He says to Him, Yes, Lord, You know that I love You. He says to him, Feed My sheep!  
**21:17** He says to him a third <i>time</i>, Simon <i>son</i> of Jonah, do you love Me? Peter was grieved that He said to him a third <i>time</i>, Do you love Me? And he said to him, Lord, You perceive all things, You know that I love You! Jesus said to him, Feed My sheep!  
**21:18** Truly, truly, I say to you, When you were younger, you girded yourself, and you walked where you desired. But when you grow old, you will stretch out your hands, and another will gird you, and will carry you where <i>you</i> do not desire.  
**21:19** But He said this signifying by what death he would glorify God. And having said this, He says him, Follow Me.  
**21:20** But turning, Peter sees the disciple wom Jesus loved following <i>them</i>, who also leaned on His breast at the Supper, and said, Lord, who is the <i>one</i> giving You over?  
**21:21** Seeing this <i>one</i>, Peter said to Jesus, Lord, and what <i>of</i> this one--?  
**21:22** Jesus says to him, If I desire him to remain until I come, what <i>is that</i> to you? You follow Me.  
**21:23** Therefore, this word went out to the brothers <i>that</i> that one, that disciple does not die. Yet Jesus did not say to him that he does not die, but, If I desire him to remain until I come, what <i>is that</i> to you?  
**21:24** This is the disciple, the <i>one</i> witnessing about these things, and having written these things. And we know that his witness is true.  
**21:25** And there are many other things, as many as Jesus did, which if they were wtitten by one I suppose the world not even have room for the books being written. Amen.
