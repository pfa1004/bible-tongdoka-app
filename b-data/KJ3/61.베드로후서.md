# 61.베드로후서 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** Simon Peter, a slave and apostle of Jesus Christ, to the <i>ones</i> equally precious with us, having obtained faith in <i>the</i> righteousness of our God and our Savior, Jesus Christ:  
**1:2** Grace to you, and peace be multiplied by a full knowledge of God, and of Jesus our Lord.  
**1:3** As His divine power having given as a gift to us all things <i>pertaining</i> to life and godliness through the full knowledge of the <i>One</i> calling us through glory and virtue,  
**1:4** through which have been given as a gift to us the very great and precious promises, so that through these we might become partakers of <i>the</i> divine nature, having escaped from the corruption in <i>the</i> world by lust.  
**1:5** But also in this very thing having brought in all diligence, fully supply in your faith virtue, and with virtue knowledge,  
**1:6** and with the knowledge self-control, and with the self-control patience, and with the patience godliness,  
**1:7** and with the godliness brotherly love, and with brotherly love, love.  
**1:8** For these things being in you, and abounding, <i>they will</i> place <i>you so as</i> not <i>to be</i> idle, not unfruitful into the full knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:9** For the one in whom these things are not present is blind, being shortsighted, receiving forgetfulness of the cleansing of his sins in time past.  
**1:10** Therefore, brothers, rather be diligent to make sure of your calling and election; for doing these things, you will not stumble into sin, not <i>ever</i>!  
**1:11** For so will be richly furnished to you the entrance into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ.  
**1:12** Therefore I will not neglect to cause you to remember always concerning these <i>things</i>, though <i>you</i> know and have been confirmed in the present truth.  
**1:13** But I deem <i>it</i> right, so long as I am in this tabernacle, to arouse you by a reminder,  
**1:14** knowing that the putting off of my tabernacle is soon, as indeed our Lord Jesus Christ made clear to me.  
**1:15** And I will also be diligent to cause you always to have memory of these things after my departure.  
**1:16** For not following fables having been cleverly devised, but having become eyewitnesses of the majesty of that <i>One</i>, Jesus Christ, we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord.  
**1:17** For receiving honor and glory from God <i>the</i> Father such a voice being borne to Him from the Magnificent Glory, "This is My Son, the Beloved, in whom I was well-pleased," <n>Psa 2:7; Gen 22:2; Isa 42:1; Matt 17:5</n>  
**1:18** even we heard this voice being borne out of Heaven, being with Him in the holy mountain,  
**1:19** and we have the more established prophetic Word, in which you do well to pay attention, as to a lamp shining in a murky place, until day dawns and the lightbearing <i>One</i> rises in your hearts;  
**1:20** knowing this first, that every prophecy of Scripture did not come into being of <i>its</i> own interpretation;  
**1:21** for prophecy was not at any time borne by <i>the</i> will of man, but being borne along by <i>the</i> Holy Spirit, holy men of God spoke.  

## 2장

**2:1** But <i>there</i> also came to be false prophets among the people, as also among you will be false teachers who will secretly bring in destructive heresies, even denying the <i>One</i> having bought them, the Master, bringing swift destruction on themselves.  
**2:2** And many will follow their destructive ways, through whom the way of truth will be evil spoken of.  
**2:3** And by covetousness, with feigned words, they will use you for gain, for whom judgment of old does not linger, and their destruction does not slumber.  
**2:4** For if God did not spare sinning angels, but giving <i>them</i> over to chains of darkness, thrust them down into Tartarus, having been kept to judgment;  
**2:5** and did not spare <i>the</i> ancient world, but protected Noah <i>the</i> eighth, a herald of righteousness, bringing a flood on a world of ungodly <i>ones</i>;  
**2:6** and covering the cities <i>of</i> Sodom and Gomorrah with ashes, <i>He</i> condemned <i>them</i> with an overthrow, setting an example to men intending to live ungodly.  
**2:7** And He delivered righteous Lot, who had been oppressed by the behavior of lawless <i>ones</i> in unbridled lusts.  
**2:8** For that righteous <i>one</i> living among them day after day, in seeing and in hearing, <i>his</i> righteous soul <i>was</i> tormented with <i>their</i> lawless works.  
**2:9** <i>But the</i> Lord knows to deliver the godly out of temptation, and to keep the unjust for a day of judgment, being punished,  
**2:10** and most of all the <i>ones</i> going after flesh in <i>the</i> lust of defilement, and despising rulership, darers, self-pleasing; they do not tremble <i>at</i> glories, blaspheming;  
**2:11** where angels, being greater in strength and power, do not bring against them a blasphemous charge before <i>the</i> Lord.  
**2:12** But these as unreasoning natural beasts, having been born for capture and corruption, blaspheming in that <i>of</i> which they are ignorant, they shall utterly perish in their corruption,  
**2:13** receiving <i>the</i> wages of unrighteousness, deeming luxuriousness in the day <i>to be</i> pleasure; <i>they are</i> spots and blemishes, reveling <i>in</i> their deceptions, feasting together with you,  
**2:14** having eyes full of an adulteress, and never ceasing from sin; alluring unstable souls; having a heart being busied <i>with</i> covetousness; cursed children;  
**2:15** forsaking the straight way, they went astray, following the way of Balaam the <i>son</i> of Beor, who loved <i>the</i> wages of unrighteousness,  
**2:16** but had reproof of <i>his</i> own lawlessness, the voiceless ass speaking in a man's voice restrained the madness of the prophet.  
**2:17** These are springs without water, clouds being driven by tempest, for whom the blackness of darkness has been kept to <i>the</i> ages.  
**2:18** For speaking great swollen-over <i>words</i> of vanity, by <i>the</i> lusts of the flesh, by unbridled lusts, they allure the <i>ones</i> indeed escaping, the <i>ones</i> walking in error,  
**2:19** promising to them freedom, <i>though</i> themselves being slaves of corruption; for by whom anyone has been overcome, even to this <i>one</i> he has been enslaved.  
**2:20** For if by a recognition of the Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, having escaped the defilements of the world, and again being entangled <i>they</i> are overcome by these, <i>then</i> the last things of them have become worse <i>than</i> the first.  
**2:21** For it was better for them not to have fully known the way of righteousness than fully knowing <i>it</i> to turn from the holy commandment having been given over to them.  
**2:22** But <i>the</i> word of the true proverb has happened to them: "<i>The</i> dog turning back to <i>his</i> own vomit;" also, <i>the</i> sow being washed <i>returns</i> to a wallowing <i>in</i> mud. <n>Prov 26:11</n>  

## 3장

**3:1** Beloved <i>ones</i>, I now write to you this second epistle, in which I stir up your pure mind in a reminder  
**3:2** to remember the words having been spoken previously by the holy prophets, and the commandment of the Lord and Savior by us, the apostles;  
**3:3** first, knowing this, that during <i>the</i> last days scoffers will come, going on according to their own lusts,  
**3:4** and saying, Where is the promise of His coming? For from which <i>time the</i> fathers fell asleep, all things remain so from <i>the</i> beginning of creation.  
**3:5** For this escapes their notice <i>by their</i> willing <i>it so</i>, that heavens were of old, and earth by water, and through water, subsisting by the Word of God,  
**3:6** through which the world which then was, being flooded by water, perished.  
**3:7** But the heavens and the earth now, being stored up by the same Word, are being kept for fire to a day of judgment and destruction of ungodly men.  
**3:8** But let not this one <i>thing</i> escape your notice, beloved <i>ones</i>, that one day with <i>the</i> Lord <i>is</i> "as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day." <n>Psa 90:4</n>  
**3:9** The Lord of the promise is not slow, as some deem slowness, but is long-suffering toward us, not purposing any to perish, but all to turn one's self to repentance.  
**3:10** But the day of the Lord will come as a thief in <i>the</i> night, in which the heavens will pass away with a loud noise, and being burning up <i>the</i> elements will be dissolved, and earth and the works in it will be burned up.  
**3:11** Therefore, all these <i>things</i> being <i>about</i> to be dissolved, <i>of</i> what sort ought you to be in holy behavior and godliness,  
**3:12** looking for and hastening the coming of the Day of God, through which <i>the</i> heavens being set on fire will be dissolved; and <i>the</i> elements being burned up?  
**3:13** But according to His promise, we look for "new heavens and a new earth," in which righteousness dwells. <n>Isa 65:17</n>  
**3:14** Therefore, beloved <i>ones</i>, looking for these things, be diligent, spotless, and without blemish, to be found in peace by Him.  
**3:15** And think of the long-suffering of our Lord <i>as</i> salvation, as also our beloved brother Paul wrote to you, according to the wisdom having been given to him;  
**3:16** as also in all his letters, speaking in them concerning these things, in which are some things hard to understand, which the unlearned and unstable pervert, as also <i>they do</i> the rest of the Scriptures, to <i>their</i> own destruction.  
**3:17** Therefore, beloved <i>ones</i>, you knowing beforehand, be on guard lest being led away by the error of the lawless <i>ones</i> you fall from <i>your</i> own firmness.  
**3:18** But grow in grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ. To Him <i>be</i> the glory, both now and to <i>the</i> day of eternity. Amen.
