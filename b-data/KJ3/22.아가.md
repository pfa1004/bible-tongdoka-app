# 22.아가 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** The song of songs which is Solomon's.  
**1:2** Let Him kiss me with the kisses of His mouth; for Your love <i>is</i> better than wine.  
**1:3** For Your ointments <i>are</i> good for a fragrance; Your name is <i>as</i> ointment poured out; on account of this the virgins love You.  
**1:4** Draw me; we will run after You. The King has brought me <i>into</i> His chambers. We will be glad and rejoice in You; we will remember Your love more than wine; the upright <i>ones</i> love You.  
**1:5** I <i>am</i> black, but comely, O daughters of Jerusalem, like the tents of Kedar, like the curtains of Solomon.  
**1:6** Do not look at me, that I <i>am</i> black, that the sun has looked on me. My mother's sons were angry with me; they made me the one keeping the vineyards; <i>but</i> my own vineyard I have not kept.  
**1:7** Tell me, <i>You</i> whom my soul loves, where do You feed; where do You lie down at noon? For should I be as one who is veiled beside the flocks of Your companions?  
**1:8** If you yourself do not know, most beautiful among women, go out yourself in the footsteps of the flock. And feed your kids beside the dwellings of the shepherds.  
**1:9** O My love, I have compared you to My mares in Pharaoh's chariots.  
**1:10** Your cheeks <i>are</i> lovely with ornaments, your neck with strings of beads.  
**1:11** We will make for you ornaments of gold with studs of silver.  
**1:12** While the King is in His circle, my spikenard gives its fragrance.  
**1:13** A bundle of myrrh <i>is</i> my Beloved to me. <i>He</i> shall lie between my breasts.  
**1:14** My Beloved <i>is</i> to me <i>like</i> a cluster of henna in the vineyards of En-gedi.  
**1:15** Behold, you <i>are</i> beautiful, My love. Behold, you are beautiful; your eyes as doves' <i>eyes</i>.  
**1:16** Behold, You <i>are</i> beautiful, my Beloved; yea, pleasant. Also our couch <i>is</i> green.  
**1:17** The beams of our house <i>are</i> cedars <i>and</i> our rafters of firs.  

## 2장

**2:1** I <i>am</i> a rose of Sharon, a lily of the valleys.  
**2:2** As a lily among thorns, so <i>is</i> My love among the daughters.  
**2:3** As the apple among the trees of the forest, so <i>is</i> my Beloved among the sons. I delighted in His shadow, and I sat down; and His fruit <i>was</i> sweet to my palate.  
**2:4** He brought me to the house of wine, and His banner over me <i>was</i> love.  
**2:5** Support me with raisin cakes, refresh me with apples, for I <i>am</i> sick <i>with</i> love.  
**2:6** His left <i>hand is</i> under my head, and His right hand embraces me.  
**2:7** I adjure you by the gazelles, or by the does of the field, O daughters of Jerusalem, that you not stir up, and that you not awaken <i>my</i> Love until it pleases.  
**2:8** The voice of my Beloved! Behold this, He comes leaping on the mountains, skipping on the hills.  
**2:9** My Beloved is likened to a gazelle, or to a young deer, the stag. Behold, <i>He</i> stands behind our wall, peering from the windows, sparkling from the lattice.  
**2:10** My Beloved answered and said to me, rise up yourself, My love, My beautiful <i>one</i>, and yourself come away.  
**2:11** For, behold, the winter has passed, the rain has passed, it goes to itself.  
**2:12** The flowers are seen on the earth; the time of singing has come, and the voice of the turtledove is heard in our land.  
**2:13** The fig tree spices her unripe figs, and the vines give a fragrance <i>by</i> the blossom. Arise, My love; come, My beautiful <i>one</i>, and come yourself.  
**2:14** My dove, in the clefts of the rock, in the secrecy of the steep place, let Me see your form. Let Me hear your voice; for your voice <i>is</i> sweet, and your form <i>is</i> beautiful.  
**2:15** Take for us the foxes, the little foxes spoiling the vineyards, even our vineyards <i>with</i> blossoms.  
**2:16** My Beloved <i>is</i> to me, and I <i>am</i> to Him; He feeds among the lilies.  
**2:17** Until when does the day blow, and the shadows flee away? Turn, my Beloved, and be like a gazelle, or a young deer, the stag, on the cleft mountains.  

## 3장

**3:1** By night on my bed I sought <i>Him</i> whom my soul loves. I sought Him, but I did not find Him.  
**3:2** I will rise now and go about in the city, in the streets and in the broad places. I will seek <i>Him</i> whom my soul loves. I sought Him, but I did not find Him.  
**3:3** The <i>ones</i> watching going about in the city found me. <i>I said</i>, Have you seen <i>Him whom</i> my soul loves?  
**3:4** When I had passed on from them, <i>it was</i> a little while until I found <i>Him</i> whom my soul loves. I seized Him, and I did not let Him go until I had brought Him into my mother's house, and into the room of her who conceived me.  
**3:5** I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem, by the gazelles or by the does of the field, that awakening you do not awaken, even that you not awaken the Beloved until it pleases.  
**3:6** Who <i>is</i> this who comes up out of the wilderness like columns of smoke, sacrificing <i>as incense</i> myrrh and frankincense, from all powders of the merchant?  
**3:7** Behold his bed, Solomon's! Sixty mighty men <i>are</i> around it, of the mighty men of Israel.  
**3:8** They all hold the sword, instructed in war; <i>each</i> man has his sword on his thigh from dread in the night.  
**3:9** King Solomon made himself a litter bed of the trees of Lebanon.  
**3:10** He made its poles <i>of</i> silver; its back <i>of</i> gold; its seat <i>of</i> purple; its middle was paved <i>with</i> love by the daughters of Jerusalem.  
**3:11** Go forth, O daughters of Zion, and see King Solomon with the crown <i>with</i> which his mother crowned him on his wedding day, even on the day of the gladness of his heart.  

## 4장

**4:1** Behold, you <i>are</i> beautiful, My love. Behold, you <i>are</i> beautiful; your eyes <i>are</i> as doves' from behind your veil. Your hair <i>is</i> like a flock of goats which recline from Mount Gilead.  
**4:2** Your teeth <i>are</i> like a flock of shorn <i>sheep</i> which come up from the washing place; of which they all <i>are</i> bearing twins; and bereavement is not among them.  
**4:3** Your lips <i>are</i> like a cord of scarlet, and your speech <i>is</i> comely; your temples <i>are</i> like a piece of pomegranate behind your veil.  
**4:4** Your neck <i>is</i> like the tower of David, built for an armory; a thousand bucklers hang on it, all the shields of the mighty <i>men</i>.  
**4:5** Your two breasts <i>are</i> like two fawns, twins of a gazelle, feeding among the lilies.  
**4:6** Until when the day blows, and the shadows flee away, I myself will go to the mountain of myrrh, and to the hills of frankincense.  
**4:7** You <i>are</i> all beautiful, My love; a blemish is not on you.  
**4:8** <i>Come</i> with Me from Lebanon, <i>My</i> spouse; with Me from Lebanon. Look from the top of Amana, from the top of Shenir and Hermon, from the lions' dens, from the mountains of the leopards.  
**4:9** You have ravished My heart, My sister, <i>My</i> spouse; you have ravished My heart with one of your eyes, with one string of beads from your neck.  
**4:10** How beautiful <i>are</i> your loves, My sister, <i>My</i> spouse! How <i>much</i> better <i>are</i> your loves than wine, and the scent of your ointments than all spices!  
**4:11** Your lips, <i>My</i> spouse, drip <i>like</i> the honeycomb; honey and milk <i>are</i> under your tongue. And the scent of your garments <i>is</i> like the scent of Lebanon.  
**4:12** A locked garden <i>is</i> My sister, <i>My</i> spouse; a spring locked up, a sealed fountain.  
**4:13** Your plants <i>are</i> an orchard of pomegranates with excellent fruits, with henna <i>and</i> spikenard;  
**4:14** spikenard and saffron, calamus and cinnamon; with all trees of frankincense, myrrh and aloes; with all the chief balsam spices;  
**4:15** a fountain of gardens, a well of living waters; even flowings from Lebanon.  
**4:16** Awake, north <i>wind</i>; yea, come, south <i>wind</i>; blow on my garden; let its spices flow out. Let my Beloved come into His garden and eat its excellent fruits.  

## 5장

**5:1** I have come to My garden, My sister, <i>My</i> spouse; I have gathered My myrrh with My spice. I have eaten My honeycomb with My honey; I have drunk My wine with My milk. Eat and drink, O friends; yea, drink fully, beloved ones.  
**5:2** I sleep, but my heart is waking. <i>It is</i> the sound of my Beloved that knocks, <i>saying</i>, Open to Me, My sister, My love, My dove, My undefiled. For My head is filled with dew, My locks with the drops of the night.  
**5:3** I have stripped off My coat; how shall I put it on? I have washed My feet; how shall I make them unclean?  
**5:4** My Beloved sent His hand from the opening, and my bowels sighed for Him.  
**5:5** I rose up to open to my Beloved, and my hands dripped <i>with</i> myrrh; yea, my fingers flowing <i>with</i> myrrh on the handles of the bolt.  
**5:6** I opened to my Beloved, but my Beloved had withdrawn; He passed on. My soul went out when He spoke; I sought Him, but I could not find Him. I called Him, but He did not answer me.  
**5:7** The <i>ones</i> watching going about the city found me <i>and</i> struck me; they wounded me; those keeping the walls lifted my veil from me.  
**5:8** I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem, if you find my Beloved, what do you tell Him? That I <i>am</i> sick <i>with</i> love.  
**5:9** What <i>is</i> your Beloved above <i>any</i> beloved, most beautiful among women? What <i>is</i> your Beloved above <i>any other</i> beloved, that you adjure us so?  
**5:10** My Beloved <i>is</i> bright and ruddy, standing out among ten thousand.  
**5:11** His head <i>is like</i> refined gold; His locks <i>are</i> wavy and black as a raven.  
**5:12** His eyes <i>are</i> as <i>the</i> eyes of doves on the rivers of waters, washed with milk, sitting on a setting.  
**5:13** His cheeks <i>are</i> like a bed of spices, a raised bed of aromatic herbs. His lips <i>are like</i> lilies dropping flowing myrrh.  
**5:14** His hands <i>are like</i> rings of gold filled with jewels; His body an ivory plate overlaid with sapphires.  
**5:15** His legs <i>are like</i> pillars of marble founded on bases of fine gold; His appearance <i>is like</i> Lebanon, being chosen as the cedars.  
**5:16** His palate <i>is</i> most sweet, and He <i>is,</i> all of Him, <i>One of</i> desire. This <i>is</i> my Beloved, and this <i>is</i> my Friend, O daughters of Jerusalem.  

## 6장

**6:1** Where has your Beloved gone, most beautiful among women? Where has your Beloved turned? For we seek Him along with you.  
**6:2** My Beloved has gone down to His garden, to the garden-bed of spices, to feed in the gardens and to gather lilies.  
**6:3** I <i>am</i> to my Beloved, and my Beloved <i>is</i> to me; He feeds among the lilies.  
**6:4** O My love, you <i>are</i> as beautiful as Tirzah, as comely as Jerusalem, awesome as bannered armies.  
**6:5** Turn away your eyes from Me, because they are disturbing to Me. Your hair <i>is</i> like a flock of goats which recline from Gilead.  
**6:6** Your teeth <i>are</i> like a flock of ewes which come up from the washing place, of which they all <i>are</i> giving birth, bearing twins, and bereavement is not among them.  
**6:7** Your temples from behind your veil <i>are</i> like a piece of pomegranate.  
**6:8** Sixty of them <i>are</i> queens, and eighty concubines, and virgins without number.  
**6:9** <i>But</i> My dove, My perfect one is one <i>alone</i>. She <i>is</i> the <i>only</i> one to her mother; she <i>is</i> pure to the one who bore her. The daughters saw <i>her</i> and called her blessed; the queens and the concubines saw her, and they praised her.  
**6:10** Who <i>is</i> she who looks down like the dawn, beautiful as the moon, pure as the sun, awesome as bannered <i>armies</i>?  
**6:11** I went down to the garden of nut trees, to see the greenery of the ravine, to see whether the vine flowered <i>and</i> the pomegranates budded.  
**6:12** I did not know, <i>but</i> My soul set Me <i>on</i> the chariots of My princely people.  
**6:13** Return, return, O Shulamite! Return, return, that we may gaze upon you. What will you see in the Shulamite? As it were the dance of two <i>army</i> camps.  

## 7장

**7:1** How beautiful <i>are</i> your footsteps in sandals, O prince's daughter! The curves of your thighs <i>are</i> like jewels, the work of the hands of an artisan.  
**7:2** Your navel <i>is like</i> a round goblet; it never lacks mixed wine. Your belly <i>is like</i> a heap of wheat hedged about with lilies.  
**7:3** Your two breasts <i>are</i> like two fawns, twins of a gazelle.  
**7:4** Your neck <i>is like</i> an ivory tower; your eyes <i>like</i> the fishpools in Heshbon, by the gate of Bath-rabbim. Your nose <i>is like</i> a tower of Lebanon, peering toward the face of Damascus.  
**7:5** Your head <i>is like</i> Carmel, and the hair of your head like purple <i>cloth</i>; the King <i>is</i> held captive in its flowing.  
**7:6** How beautiful and how pleasant you <i>are</i> in delights, O love!  
**7:7** This your stature <i>is</i> likened to a palm tree, and your breasts to clusters of grapes.  
**7:8** I said, I will go up in the palm tree; I will take hold of its stalk. And please let your breasts be like clusters of the vine, and the scent of your nose like apples,  
**7:9** and your palate like the best wine going down for my Beloved for uprightness, flowing softly <i>over the</i> lips of sleeping <i>ones.  
**7:10** I <i>am</i> my Beloved's, and His desire <i>is</i> toward me.  
**7:11** Come, my Beloved, let us go forth into the field; let us stay in the villages.  
**7:12** Let us rise up early to the vineyards; let us see <i>if</i> the vine flowers and whether the blossom opens, and the pomegranates bud forth. There I will give my loves to You.  
**7:13** The love apples give a scent, and over our doors <i>are</i> all excellent <i>fruits</i>; new, also old, I have laid up for You, my Beloved.  

## 8장

**8:1** Who can give You to me, <i>as my</i> brother, suckling the breasts of my mother? <i>If</i> I find You outside, I would kiss You. They also would not despise me.  
**8:2** I would lead You; I would bring You into my mother's house, <i>that</i> You might teach me; I would cause You to drink the spiced wine from the juice of my pomegranate.  
**8:3** His left hand <i>would be</i> under my head, and His right <i>hand</i> embracing me.  
**8:4** I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem; why do you awaken; yea, why do you awaken <i>my</i> Love until it pleases?  
**8:5** Who <i>is</i> this that comes up from the wilderness, leaning on her Beloved? I awakened you under the apple tree; there your mother travailed with you; there she travailed; she bore you.  
**8:6** Set me as a seal on Your heart, as a seal on Your arm. For love <i>is</i> strong as death; jealousy <i>is</i> cruel as Sheol; its flames <i>are</i> flames of fire, a flame of Jehovah.  
**8:7** Many waters cannot quench love, and the rivers will not overflow it. If <i>a</i> man would give all the wealth of his house for love, despising they would despise him.  
**8:8** A little sister <i>is</i> to us, and no breasts are to her. What shall we do for our sister in the day she shall be spoken for?  
**8:9** If she <i>is</i> a wall, we will build a turret of silver on her. And if she <i>is</i> a door, we will enclose her <i>with</i> boards of cedar.  
**8:10** I <i>was</i> a wall, and my breasts like towers; then I was in His eyes as one finding peace.  
**8:11** A vineyard in Baal-hamon was to Solomon. He gave the vineyard to those keeping <i>it</i>; for its fruit <i>each</i> man was to bring a thousand of silver.  
**8:12** My vineyard which <i>is</i> mine <i>is</i> before me; the thousand <i>is</i> for you, O Solomon, and two hundred for <i>those</i> keeping of its fruit.  
**8:13** You who dwell in the gardens, the companions <i>are</i> listening to your voice; cause me to hear <i>it</i>.  
**8:14** Hurry, my Beloved, and be like a gazelle, or a young deer, the stag, on the mountains of spices.
