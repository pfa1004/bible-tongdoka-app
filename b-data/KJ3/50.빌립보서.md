# 50.빌립보서 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul and Timothy, slaves of Jesus Christ, to all the saints in Christ Jesus who are in Philippi, with the overseers and deacons:  
**1:2** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and <i>the</i> Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:3** I thank my God on all the remembrance of you,  
**1:4** always in my every prayer on your behalf making <i>my</i> prayer with joy  
**1:5** over your fellowship in the gospel, from <i>the</i> first day until now,  
**1:6** being persuaded of this very thing, that the <i>One</i> having begun a good work in you will complete <i>it</i> until <i>the</i> day of Jesus Christ;  
**1:7** as it is righteous for me to think this of you all because you have me in <i>your</i> heart, both in my bonds and in the defense and confirmation of the gospel, you are all sharers of the grace with me.  
**1:8** For God is my witness how I long after you all in <i>the</i> bowels of Jesus Christ.  
**1:9** And this I pray, that your love may yet abound more and more in full knowledge and all perception,  
**1:10** for your examining the differing things, that you may be sincere and without blame for <i>the</i> day of Christ,  
**1:11** being filled <i>with</i> fruits of righteousness, the <i>ones</i> through Jesus Christ, to <i>the</i> glory and praise of God.  
**1:12** Now I want you to know, brothers, that the things concerning me have more fully come to <i>the</i> advancement of the gospel,  
**1:13** so that in all the praetorium, and to all the rest, my bonds have become clearly revealed <i>to be</i> in Christ.  
**1:14** And the most of the brothers in <i>the</i> Lord, having been persuaded in my bonds, more exceedingly dare to speak the Word fearlessly.  
**1:15** Some, indeed, even proclaim Christ because of envy and strife, but some also because of good will.  
**1:16** These, indeed, proclaim Christ out of self-seeking, not sincerely, thinking to bring affliction to my bonds.  
**1:17** But the <i>others</i> out of love, knowing that I am appointed for defense of the gospel.  
**1:18** What then? Yet in every way, whether in pretense or in truth, Christ is proclaimed, and I rejoice in this; yet also I will rejoice.  
**1:19** For I know that this will turn out into deliverance to me through your petition and <i>the</i> supply of <i>the</i> Spirit of Jesus Christ, <i>Job 13:16  
**1:20** according to my earnest expectation and hope, that in nothing I shall be ashamed, but as always in all boldness even now Christ will be magnified in my body, whether through life or through death.  
**1:21** For to me to live <i>is</i> Christ, and to die <i>is</i> gain.  
**1:22** But if <i>I</i> live in <i>the</i> flesh, this to me <i>is</i> fruit of <i>my</i> labor, and what I shall choose I do not know.  
**1:23** For I am pressed together by the two: having a desire to depart and to be with Christ, <i>which is</i> far better,  
**1:24** but to remain in the flesh <i>is</i> more necessary on account of you.  
**1:25** And being persuaded of this, I know that I will remain and will continue with you all for your advancement and joy of faith,  
**1:26** so that your glorying may abound in Christ Jesus in me through my presence with you again.  
**1:27** Only conduct yourself worthily of the gospel of Christ, so that whether coming and seeing you or being absent, I hear the things concerning you, that you stand fast in one spirit and one soul, striving together in the faith of the gospel,  
**1:28** and not being terrified in no <i>thing</i> by the <i>ones</i> opposing, which to them truly is a proof of destruction, but to you of salvation, and this from God;  
**1:29** because it was granted to you on behalf of Christ not only to believe in Him, but also to suffer on His behalf,  
**1:30** having the same struggle which you saw in me and <i>even</i> now you hear <i>to be</i> in me.  

## 2장

**2:1** Therefore, if <i>there is</i> any comfort in Christ, if any consolation of love, if any fellowship of <i>the</i> Spirit, if any <i>tender</i> bowels and mercies,  
**2:2** fulfill my joy, that you think the same, having the same love, one in soul, being mindful of the one thing,  
**2:3** <i>doing</i> nothing according to intrigue or self-glory, but in humility, esteeming one another, as being better than themselves;  
**2:4** each <i>one</i> not looking at the things of themselves, but each <i>one</i> also <i>the</i> things of others.  
**2:5** For think this within you, which <i>mind was</i> also in Christ Jesus,  
**2:6** who subsisting in the form of God thought <i>it</i> not robbery to be equal with God,  
**2:7** but emptied Himself, taking <i>the</i> form of a slave, coming to be in <i>the</i> likeness of men  
**2:8** and coming to be found in figure as a man, He humbled Himself, coming to be obedient until death, even <i>the</i> death of a cross.  
**2:9** Because of this also God highly exalted Him and gave Him a name above every name,  
**2:10** that at the name of Jesus "every knee should bow," of heavenly <i>beings</i>, and earthly <i>beings</i>, and under <i>the</i> earth <i>beings</i>,  
**2:11** and "every tongue should confess" that Jesus Christ <i>is</i> "Lord," to <i>the</i> glory of God <i>the</i> Father. <n>Isa 45:23</n>  
**2:12** So, then, my beloved, even as you always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much rather in my absence, cultivate your salvation with fear and trembling,  
**2:13** for it is God who is working in you both to will and to work for the sake of <i>His</i> good pleasure.  
**2:14** Do all things without murmurings and disputings,  
**2:15** that you may be blameless and guileless, children of God, without fault in <i>the</i> midst of a crooked generation, even being perverted, among whom you shine as luminaries in <i>the</i> world,  
**2:16** holding forth <i>the</i> Word of life, for a boast to me in <i>the</i> day of Christ, that I ran not in vain, nor labored in vain.  
**2:17** But if indeed I am poured out on the sacrifice and service of your faith, I rejoice; yea, I rejoice with you all.  
**2:18** And you also rejoice <i>in</i> the same and rejoice with me.  
**2:19** But I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon, that I may also be of good cheer, knowing the things about you.  
**2:20** For I have no one like-minded, who genuinely will care for the things about you.  
**2:21** For all seek their own things, not the things of Christ Jesus.  
**2:22** But you know the proof of him, that as a child to a father, he served with me for the gospel.  
**2:23** Therefore I hope to send this <i>one</i> at once, when ever I have seen the things about me.  
**2:24** But I am convinced in <i>the</i> Lord that I myself also will come soon.  
**2:25** But I thought it needful to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother and fellowworker, and my fellow soldier, and your messenger and minister of my need,  
**2:26** since he was longing after you all and being distressed because you heard that he was sick.  
**2:27** For indeed he was sick, coming near to death; but God had mercy on him, and not only on him, but also me, lest I should have grief on grief.  
**2:28** Therefore, I sent him more eagerly, that seeing him again you may rejoice, and I may be less grieved.  
**2:29** Therefore receive him in <i>the</i> Lord with all joy and hold such in honor,  
**2:30** because through the work of Christ he drew near as far as <i>to</i> death, <i>his</i> life having been exposed, that he may fill up your lack of service toward me.  

## 3장

**3:1** For the rest, my brothers, rejoice in <i>the</i> Lord. To write the same things to you truly <i>is</i> not troublesome to me, but safe for you.  
**3:2** Watch <i>for</i> the dogs, watch <i>for</i> the evil workers, watch <i>for</i> the cutting off <i>party</i>.  
**3:3** For we are the circumcision, the <i>ones</i> who worship by the Spirit of God, and who glory in Christ Jesus, and who do not trust in flesh.  
**3:4** Even though I <i>might</i> have trust in flesh; if any other thinks to trust in flesh, I more;  
**3:5** in circumcision, <i>the</i> eighth day, of <i>the</i> race of Israel, <i>the</i> tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew of the Hebrews; according to Law, a Pharisee;  
**3:6** according to zeal, persecuting the Assembly; according to righteousness in Law, being blameless.  
**3:7** But what things were gain to me, these I have counted loss because of Christ.  
**3:8** But, nay, rather I also count all things to be loss because of the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord, for whose sake I have suffered the loss of all things and count <i>them to be</i> trash, that I might gain Christ  
**3:9** and be found in Him; not having my own righteousness of Law, but through the faith of Christ, <i>having</i> the righteousness of God on faith,  
**3:10** to know Him and the power of His resurrection, and the fellowship of His sufferings, being conformed to His death,  
**3:11** if somehow I may arrive to the resurrection out of the dead.  
**3:12** Not that I already received or already have been perfected, but I pursue, if I also may lay hold, inasmuch as I also was laid hold of by Christ Jesus.  
**3:13** Brothers, I do not count myself to have laid hold, but one <i>thing I do</i>, forgetting the things behind, and stretching out for the <i>things</i> before,  
**3:14** I am pursuing a goal for the prize of the upward calling of God in Christ Jesus.  
**3:15** Therefore as many as <i>are</i> perfect, think this; and if you think any other thing, God will also reveal this to you.  
**3:16** Yet <i>as</i> to where we have arrived, walk by the same rule, <i>being</i> of the same mind.  
**3:17** Be fellow-imitators of me, brothers, and consider the <i>ones</i> walking this way, even as you have us <i>for</i> a pattern.  
**3:18** For many walk <i>as</i> hostile <i>to</i> the cross of Christ, of whom I often told you, and now even weeping I say it,  
**3:19** whose end <i>is</i> destruction, whose god <i>is</i> the belly, and who glory in their shame, the <i>ones</i> thinking earthly <i>things.  
**3:20** For our citizenship is in Heaven, from where we also wait for a Savior, <i>the</i> Lord Jesus Christ,  
**3:21** who will transform the body of our humiliation, for it to be conformed to His body of glory, according to the working <i>of</i> Him to be able even to subject all things to Himself.  

## 4장

**4:1** So then, my brothers, beloved and longed for, my joy and crown, so stand firm in <i>the</i> Lord, beloved <i>ones</i>.  
**4:2** I entreat Euodias, and I entreat Syntyche, to mind the same thing in <i>the</i> Lord.  
**4:3** And I also ask you, true yoke-fellow, together help with them, <i>women</i> who struggled along with me and with Clement in the gospel, and the rest, fellow-workers with me, whose names <i>are</i> in the Book of Life.  
**4:4** Rejoice in <i>the</i> Lord always. Again I will say, Rejoice!  
**4:5** Let your kind <i>spirit</i> be known to all men. The Lord <i>is</i> near.  
**4:6** Be anxious about nothing, but in everything by prayer and by petition with thanksgivings, let your requests be made known to God;  
**4:7** and the peace of God which surpasses all understanding will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.  
**4:8** For the rest, brothers, as many <i>things</i> as are true, as many as <i>are</i> honorable, as many as <i>are</i> right, as many as <i>are</i> pure, as many as <i>are</i> pleasing, as many as <i>are</i> of good report, if <i>of</i> any virtue, and if <i>of</i> any praise, meditate on these things.  
**4:9** And what <i>things</i> you learned and received and heard and saw in me, practice these things, and the God of peace will be with you.  
**4:10** But I rejoiced in <i>the</i> Lord greatly that now once again you revived <i>your</i> thinking on my behalf, on which you also were thinking, but lacked opportunity.  
**4:11** Not that I speak as to need, for I have learned to be content in whatever state I am.  
**4:12** And I know to be humbled, and I know to abound; in everything, and in all things, I am taught both to be full and to hunger, both to abound, and to be in need.  
**4:13** I can do all things in Christ, the <i>One</i> strengthening me.  
**4:14** Yet you did well in sharing my affliction.  
**4:15** And you know, too, Philippians, that in <i>the</i> beginning of the gospel, when I went out from Macedonia, not one assembly shared with me in <i>the</i> matter of giving and receiving, if not you only.  
**4:16** Because truly in Thessalonica you sent to my need, both once and twice.  
**4:17** Not that I seek a gift, but I seek the fruit, the <i>one</i> multiplying to your account.  
**4:18** But I have all things and more than enough; I have been made full, receiving from Epaphroditus the things <i>from you</i>, a fragrance of sweet smell, an acceptable sacrifice, well-pleasing to God.  
**4:19** And my God will fill your every need according to His riches in glory in Christ Jesus.  
**4:20** Now may glory <i>be</i> to our God and Father to the ages of the ages. Amen.  
**4:21** Greet every saint in Christ Jesus. The brothers with me greet you.  
**4:22** All the saints greet you, most of all the <i>ones</i> of Caesar's house.  
**4:23** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <i>be</i> with all of you. Amen.
