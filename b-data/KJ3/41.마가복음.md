# 41.마가복음 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, <i>the</i> Son of God,  
**1:2** as it has been written in the Prophets, "Behold, I send forth My messenger before Your face, who will prepare Your way before You;  
**1:3** <i>the</i> voice <i>of one</i> crying in the wilderness. Prepare the way of <i>the</i> Lord, make His paths straight." <n>Mal 3:1; Isa 40:3</n>  
**1:4** John came baptizing in the wilderness and proclaiming a baptism of repentance for remission of sins.  
**1:5** And all the Judean country and those of Jerusalem went out to him, and were all baptized by him in the Jordan River, confessing their sins.  
**1:6** And John was clothed in camel's hair, and a leather girdle about his loin, and eating locusts and wild honey.  
**1:7** And he proclaimed, saying, He who comes after me is mightier than I, of whom bending down I am not fit to loose the thong of His sandals.  
**1:8** I indeed baptized you in water, but He will baptize you in <i>the</i> Holy Spirit.  
**1:9** And it happened in those days, Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee and was baptized by John in the Jordan.  
**1:10** And going up from the water, immediately He saw the heavens splitting, and the Spirit coming down as a dove upon Him.  
**1:11** And there was a voice out of the heavens, You are My Son, the Beloved, in whom I am well-pleased. <n>Psa 2:7; Gen 22:2; Isa 42:1</n>  
**1:12** And the Spirit at once thrusts Him out into the deserted <i>place</i>.  
**1:13** And He was there in the deserted <i>place</i> forty days, being tempted by Satan, and was with the wild beasts. And the angels ministered to Him.  
**1:14** And after John was given over, Jesus came into Galilee proclaiming the gospel of the kingdom of God,  
**1:15** and saying, The time has been fulfilled, and the kingdom of God draws near. Repent and believe in the gospel.  
**1:16** And walking along beside the Sea of Galilee, He saw Simon and his brother Andrew casting a small net in the sea; for they were fishers.  
**1:17** And Jesus said to them, Come after Me, and I will make you to become fishers of men.  
**1:18** And at once, having left their nets, they followed Him.  
**1:19** And going forward from there a little, He saw James the <i>son</i> of Zebedee, and his brother John. And they were in the boat mending the nets.  
**1:20** And at once He called them. And leaving their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired servants, they went after Him.  
**1:21** And they are going into Capernaum. And entering into the synagogue, at once He taught on the sabbaths.  
**1:22** And they were astounded at His doctrine, for He was teaching them as having authority, and not as the scribes.  
**1:23** And a man with an unclean spirit was in their synagogue. And he cried out,  
**1:24** saying, What <i>is</i> to us and to You, Jesus, Nazarene? Have You come to destroy us? I know You, who You are, the Holy <i>One</i> of God.  
**1:25** And Jesus rebuked him, saying, Be muzzled, and come out of him.  
**1:26** And the unclean spirit convulsing him, and crying out with a loud voice, he came out of him.  
**1:27** And all were astonished, so as to be questioning to themselves, saying, What is this? What new teaching <i>is</i> this, that He commands even the unclean spirits with authority, and they obey Him?  
**1:28** And His fame went out at once into all the Galilean surrounding region.  
**1:29** And at once going out of the synagogue, they came into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John.  
**1:30** And the mother-in-law of Simon was laid down, fever-stricken. And at once they spoke to Him about her.  
**1:31** And coming near, He raised her up, holding her hand. And the fever left her instantly, and she served them.  
**1:32** And evening coming, when the sun set, they brought to Him all those badly having <i>illness</i> and those having been demonpossessed.  
**1:33** And the whole city was gathered at the door.  
**1:34** And He healed many who badly having <i>illness</i> of various diseases. And he cast out many demons, and <i>He</i> did not allow the demons to speak, because they knew Him.  
**1:35** And rising up quite early in <i>the</i> night, He went out and went away into a deserted place. And <i>He</i> was praying there.  
**1:36** And Simon and those with him searched for Him.  
**1:37** And finding Him, they said to Him, All are seeking You.  
**1:38** And He said to them, Let us go into the neighboring towns, that I may proclaim there also. For it was for this I came forth.  
**1:39** And He was proclaiming in their synagogues in all Galilee, and casting out the demons.  
**1:40** And a leper came to Him, begging Him, and falling on <i>his</i> knees to Him, and saying to Him, If You will, You are able to make me clean.  
**1:41** And being moved with compassion, reaching out the hand, Jesus touched him, and said to him, I will be made clean!  
**1:42** And <i>He</i> having spoken, instantly the leprosy departed from him, and he was made clean.  
**1:43** And strictly warning him, He at once put him out,  
**1:44** and said to him, See, tell no one a thing, but go show yourself to the priest, and offer what Moses directed concerning your cleansing, for a testimony to them.  
**1:45** But going out he began to proclaim much, and to spread about the matter, so that He no longer could openly enter into a city. But He was outside in deserted places. And they came to Him from every quarter.  

## 2장

**2:1** And again He entered into Capernaum after <i>some</i> days. And it was heard that He was in <i>a</i> house.  
**2:2** And at once many were gathered, so as none any longer had room, not even to the door. And He spoke the Word to them.  
**2:3** And they came to Him carrying a paralyzed <i>one</i>, being borne by four.  
**2:4** And not being able to draw near to Him, due to the crowd, they unroofed the roof where He was. And having dug through, they lowered the cot on which the paralyzed <i>one</i> was lying.  
**2:5** And seeing their faith, Jesus said to the paralyzed <i>one</i>, Child, your sins are remitted to you.  
**2:6** But some of the scribes were sitting there and reasoning in their hearts,  
**2:7** Why does this one speak blasphemies this way? Who is able to remit sins, except One, God?  
**2:8** And instantly knowing in His spirit that they reasoned this way within themselves, Jesus said to them, Why do you reason these things in your hearts?  
**2:9** Which is easier? To say to the paralyzed <i>one</i>, <i>Your</i> sins are remitted to you, or to say, Rise up and take your cot and walk?  
**2:10** But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority to remit sins on the earth, He said to the paralyzed <i>one</i>,  
**2:11** I say to you, Rise up and take up your cot and go to your house.  
**2:12** And he rose up at once. And taking his cot, he went out before all, so as all to be astonished and glorified God, saying, Never did we see <i>it</i> this way.  
**2:13** And He went out by the sea again. And all the crowd came to Him, and He taught them.  
**2:14** And passing on, He saw Levi the <i>son</i> of Alpheus sitting at the tax office. And <i>He</i> said to him, Follow Me. And rising up, he followed Him.  
**2:15** And it happened as He reclined in his house, even many tax collectors and sinners reclined with Jesus and His disciples, for they were many. And they followed Him.  
**2:16** And the scribes and Pharisees seeing Him eating with tax collectors and sinners, <i>they</i> said to His disciples, Why <i>is it</i> that He eats and drinks with the tax collectors and sinners?  
**2:17** And hearing, Jesus says to them, Those who are strong have no need of a physician, but those badly having <i>illness</i>. I did not come to call the righteous <i>ones</i> to repentance, but sinners.  
**2:18** And John's disciples, and those of the Pharisees, were fasting. And they came and said to Him, Why do John's disciples and those of the Pharisees fast, but Your disciples do not fast?  
**2:19** And Jesus said to them, Are able the sons of the bridechamber fast in which the groom is with them? As much time they have the groom with them, they cannot fast.  
**2:20** But the days will come when the groom will be taken away from them, and then they will fast in those days.  
**2:21** And no one sews a patch of unmilled cloth on an old garment, else <i>it</i> takes away its fullness, the new <i>from</i> the old, and a worse tear occurs.  
**2:22** And no one puts new wine into old wineskins, else the new wine bursts the wineskins, and the wine pours out, and the wineskins will be destroyed. But new wine is put into fresh wineskins.  
**2:23** And it happened, He went along through the grain fields in the sabbaths. And His disciples began to make way, plucking the heads <i>of grain</i>.  
**2:24** And the Pharisees said to Him, Behold, why do they do that which is not lawful on the sabbaths?  
**2:25** And He said to them, Did you never read what David did when he had need and hungered, he and those with him,  
**2:26** how he entered the house of God <i>in the days of</i> Abiathar the high priest, and ate the Loaves of the Presentation, which it is not lawful to eat, except for the priests, and <i>he</i> even gave to those being with him?  
**2:27** And He said to them, The sabbath came into being for man's sake, not man for the sabbath's sake.  
**2:28** So then the Son of Man is Lord of the sabbath also.  

## 3장

**3:1** And again He entered into the synagogue. And there was a man having the hand withered.  
**3:2** And they watched Him, whether He will heal him on the sabbath, that they might accuse Him.  
**3:3** And He said to the man having the hand withered, Rise up into the middle.  
**3:4** And He said to them, <i>Is it</i> lawful to do good on the sabbath, or to do evil? To save a soul, or to kill? But they were silent.  
**3:5** And having looked around on them with anger, being greatly grieved over the hardness of their heart, He said to the man, Stretch out your hand! And he stretched out, and his hand was restored sound as the other.  
**3:6** And going out, the Pharisees at once took counsel with the Herodians against Him, how they might destroy Him.  
**3:7** And Jesus withdrew to the sea with His disciples; and a great multitude from Galilee and from Judea followed Him,  
**3:8** also from Jerusalem, and from Idumea, and beyond the Jordan, also those around Tyre and Sidon. A great multitude, hearing how much He was doing, came to Him.  
**3:9** And He spoke to His disciples that a small boat should stay near to Him because of the crowd, that they might not press upon Him.  
**3:10** For He healed many, so that they fell on Him, that they might grasp Him, as many as had plagues.  
**3:11** And when the unclean spirits saw Him, <i>they</i> fell down before Him, and cried out, saying, You are the Son of God!  
**3:12** And He warned them very much that they should not make Him known.  
**3:13** And He went up into the mountain, and He called near whom He desired. And they went to Him.  
**3:14** And He made <i>disciples of</i> twelve, that they might be with Him; and that He might send them to proclaim,  
**3:15** and to have authority to heal diseases, and to cast out the demons.  
**3:16** And He put on Simon <i>the</i> name Peter.  
**3:17** And on James the <i>son</i> of Zebedee, and John the brother of James, He put on them <i>the</i> names Boanerges, which is, Sons of Thunder.  
**3:18** Also <i>He appointed</i> Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James the <i>son</i> of Alpheus, and Thaddaeus, and Simon the Canaanite,  
**3:19** and Judas Iscariot, who also gave Him over. And He came into a house.  
**3:20** And again a crowd came together, so as they <i>were</i> not able even to eat bread.  
**3:21** And hearing, those with Him went out to take hold <i>of</i> Him; for they said, He is out of <i>His</i> mind.  
**3:22** And coming down from Jerusalem, the scribes said, He has Beelzebub; and He casts out demons by the ruler of the demons.  
**3:23** And calling them near, He spoke to them in parables, <i>saying</i>, How can Satan cast out Satan?  
**3:24** And if a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand.  
**3:25** And if a house is divided against itself, that house is not able to stand.  
**3:26** And if Satan rises upon himself, and has been divided, he is not able to stand, but he has an end.  
**3:27** No one, not <i>any</i>, having entered into his house, is able to plunder the vessels of the strong <i>one</i>, unless he first tie up the strong <i>one;</i> and then he will plunder his house.  
**3:28** Truly I say to you, All the sins will be forgiven to the sons of men, also blasphemies, as many as they may blaspheme, but whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit has no remission unto the age, but is liable to eternal judgment  
**3:29** - - -  
**3:30** (because they said, He has an unclean spirit).  
**3:31** Then His mother and brothers came. And standing outside, <i>they</i> sent to Him, calling Him.  
**3:32** And a crowd sat around Him. And they said to Him, Behold, Your mother and Your brothers seek You outside.  
**3:33** And He answered them, saying, Who is My mother <i>or</i> My brothers?  
**3:34** And having looked around on those sitting around Him in a circle, He said, Behold, My mother and My brothers!  
**3:35** For whoever does the will of God, this one is My brother, and My sister, and My mother.  

## 4장

**4:1** And again He began to teach beside the sea. And a large crowd was gathered to Him, so that He stepped into the boat in order to sit in the sea. And all the crowd were on the land toward the sea.  
**4:2** And He taught them many things in parables, and said to them in His teaching:  
**4:3** Listen! Behold, the sower went out to sow.  
**4:4** And as he sowed, it happened that one indeed fell beside the road; and the birds of the heaven came and devoured it.  
**4:5** And another fell on the rocky <i>place</i> where it did not have much earth. And it sprang up at once, due to not having depth of earth.  
**4:6** And <i>the</i> sun rising, it was scorched. And through not having root, it was dried out.  
**4:7** And another fell into the thorn-bushes, and the thorn bushes grew up and choked it; and it did not yield fruit.  
**4:8** And another fell into the good ground, and yielded fruit, going up and increasing; and one bore thirty, and one sixty, and one a hundredfold.  
**4:9** And He said to them, The <i>one</i> having ears to hear, let him hear.  
**4:10** And when He was alone, those around Him, with the Twelve, asked Him <i>as to</i> the parable.  
**4:11** And He said to them, To you <i>it</i> has been given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God. But to these, those outside, all things are being <i>given</i> in parables,  
**4:12** that seeing they may see and not perceive; and hearing they may hear, and not understand, that not ever they should convert, and the sins be remitted to them. <n>Isa 6:9</n>, 10  
**4:13** And He said to them, Do you not know this parable? And how will you know all the parables?  
**4:14** The sower sows the Word.  
**4:15** And these are those beside the road where the Word is sown. And when they hear, Satan comes at once and takes away the Word having been sown in their hearts.  
**4:16** And likewise, these are the ones being sown on the rocky <i>places</i>, who, when they hear the Word, <i>they</i> immediately receive it with joy,  
**4:17** yet they have no root in themselves, but are temporary. Then trouble or persecution occurring through the Word, immediately they are stumbled.  
**4:18** These are The <i>ones</i> being sown into the thorn-bushes, The <i>ones</i> hearing the Word,  
**4:19** and the cares of this age, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the lusts about other things entering in, <i>they</i> choke the Word, and it becomes unfruitful.  
**4:20** And these are those being sown on the good ground, who hear and welcome the Word and bring forth fruit, one thirty, and one sixty, and one a hundredfold.  
**4:21** And He said to them, Does the lamp come that it may be put under the grain measure, or under the bed? <i>Is it</i> not also that it may be put on the lampstand?  
**4:22** For not anything is hidden but that it will be revealed, nor <i>anything</i> become covered but that it will come to light.  
**4:23** If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear.  
**4:24** And He said to them, Watch what you hear. With what measure you measure, it will be measured to you; and more will be given to you, the <i>ones</i> hearing.  
**4:25** For whoever may have, <i>more</i> will be given to him; and the <i>one</i> who does not have, even what he has will be taken away from him.  
**4:26** And He said, So is the kingdom of God, as if a man should cast seed on the earth,  
**4:27** and should sleep, and rise night and day; and the seed should sprout and lengthen of itself, as he does not know.  
**4:28** For of itself the earth bears fruit: first greenery, then an ear, then full grain in the ear.  
**4:29** And when the fruit gives over, immediately he "sends forth the sickle, for the harvest has come." <i>Joel 3:13  
**4:30** And He said, To what shall we compare the kingdom of God? Or with what parable shall we compare it?  
**4:31** <i>It is</i> like a grain of mustard, which, when it is sown on the earth, it is smallest than all the seeds of The <i>ones</i> on the earth.  
**4:32** And when it is sown, <i>it</i> comes up and becomes greater than all the plants, and produces great branches, so as to enable the birds of the heaven to dwell.  
**4:33** And many such parables He spoke the Word to them, even as they were able to hear.  
**4:34** But He did not speak to them without a parable. And He explained all things to His disciples privately.  
**4:35** And evening having come, He said to them on that day, let us pass over to the other side.  
**4:36** And leaving the crowd they took Him along in the boat as He was. And other small boats also were with Him.  
**4:37** And a great windstorm occurred, and the waves beat into the boat so that it was filled already.  
**4:38** And He was on the stern, sleeping on the headrest. And they awakened Him, and said to Him, Teacher, does it not matter to You that we are perishing?  
**4:39** And being awakened, He rebuked the wind, and said to the sea, Silence! Be muzzled! And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm.  
**4:40** And He said to them, Why are you so fearful? How do you not have faith?  
**4:41** And they feared a great fear and said to one another, Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey Him?  

## 5장

**5:1** And they came to the other side of the sea, to the country of the Gadarenes.  
**5:2** And He coming out from the boat, immediately out of the tombs a man with an unclean spirit met Him,  
**5:3** who had <i>his</i> abode among the tombs; and no one was able to bind him, not even with chains.  
**5:4** Because he had often been bound with fetters and chains, and the chains had been torn by him, and the fetters had been shattered. And no one was able to subdue him.  
**5:5** And continually night and day in the hills, and in the tombs, he was crying and cutting himself with stones.  
**5:6** And seeing Jesus from afar, he ran and worshiped Him.  
**5:7** And crying with a loud voice, he said, What <i>is</i> to me and to You, Jesus, Son of the most high God? I adjure You by God, do not torment me.  
**5:8** For He said to him, Unclean spirit, come out of the man!  
**5:9** And He asked him, What <i>is</i> your name? And he answered, saying, My name <i>is</i> Legion, because we are many.  
**5:10** And he was imploring Him much that He would not send them outside the country.  
**5:11** And a great herd of pigs was feeding there near the hills.  
**5:12** And all the demons implored Him, saying, Send us into the pigs, that we may enter into them.  
**5:13** And Jesus immediately allowed them. And coming out, the unclean spirits entered into the pigs. And the herd rushed down the precipice into the sea (and they were about two thousand), and <i>they</i> were choked in the sea.  
**5:14** And The <i>ones</i> who fed the pigs fled, and <i>they</i> told <i>it</i> to the city, and to the fields. And they came out to see what is the thing having happened.  
**5:15** And they come to Jesus, and stared at the one being demon-possessed, sitting and being clothed, and being of sound mind, the <i>one</i> having had the legion. And they feared.  
**5:16** And The <i>ones</i> seeing <i>it</i> related to them how it happened to the <i>one</i> being demon possessed, and about the pigs.  
**5:17** And they began to implore Him to go away from their borders.  
**5:18** And He stepping into the boat, the <i>former</i> demoniac was imploring Him, that he be with Him.  
**5:19** But Jesus did not allow him, but says to him, Go to your house, to your <i>own</i>, and report to them as many things as the Lord has done to you and had mercy on you.  
**5:20** And he went and began to proclaim in Decapolis as many things as Jesus did to him. And all marveled.  
**5:21** And Jesus crossing over in the boat again to the other side, a large crowd was gathered upon Him; and He was beside the sea.  
**5:22** And, behold, one of the synagogue rulers ,Jairus by name, comes; and seeing Him, <i>he</i> falls at His feet.  
**5:23** And <i>he</i> much implores Him, saying, My little daughter has finally <i>come to the</i> end. <i>I pray</i> that coming You may lay hands on her, that she may be cured and live.  
**5:24** And He went with him. And a large crowd was following and was pressing on Him.  
**5:25** And a certain woman being with a flow of blood twelve years,  
**5:26** and who had suffered many things by many physicians, and had spent all things that she had, and having gained nothing, but rather coming to worse,  
**5:27** hearing about Jesus, coming in the crowd behind <i>Him</i>, she grasped His garment.  
**5:28** For she said, If I may but grasp His garments, I will be cured.  
**5:29** And instantly the fountain of her blood was dried up, and she knew in <i>her</i> body that she is healed from the plague.  
**5:30** And knowing instantly within Himself that power had gone forth out of Him, turning in the crowd, Jesus said, Who grasped My garments?  
**5:31** And His disciples said to Him, You see the crowd pressing on You, and do You say, Who grasped Me?  
**5:32** And He looked around to see the <i>one</i> who had done this.  
**5:33** And the woman, being afraid and trembling, knowing what had happened on her, she came and fell down before Him and told Him all the truth.  
**5:34** And He said to her, Daughter, your faith has healed you. Go in peace and be well from your plague.  
**5:35** As He was speaking, they came from the synagogue ruler saying, Your daughter has died. Why do you still trouble the Teacher?  
**5:36** But hearing the word spoken, Jesus said to the synagogue ruler at once, Do not fear, only believe.  
**5:37** And He did not allow any<i>one</i> to accompany Him except Peter and James and John, the brother of James.  
**5:38** And He comes into the synagogue ruler's house. And He saw a tumult, and weeping and much wailing.  
**5:39** And going in, He said to them, Why do you make a tumult and weep? The child has not died, but is sleeping.  
**5:40** And they ridiculed Him. But having put all out, He took along the father and the mother of the child, and the <i>ones</i> with Him, and passed on into where the child was lying.  
**5:41** And taking hold of the child's hand, He said to her, Talitha koumi; which is, being translated, Little girl, I say to you, Rise up!  
**5:42** And immediately the little girl rose up and walked. For she was twelve years <i>old</i>. And they were amazed with great amazement.  
**5:43** And very much He charged them that no one should know this. And He said to give her something to eat.  

## 6장

**6:1** And He went out from there and came to His fatherland. And His disciples follow Him.  
**6:2** And a sabbath having come, He began to teach in the synagogue. And hearing, many were astonished, saying, From where <i>came</i> these things to this One? And what <i>is</i> the wisdom given to Him, that even such works of power are occurring through His hands?  
**6:3** Is this One not the carpenter, the son of Mary, and brother of James and Joses and Judas and Simon? And are not His sisters here with us? And they were stumbled in Him.  
**6:4** And Jesus said to them, A prophet is not without honor, except in his fatherland, and among the relatives, and in his own house.  
**6:5** And He could do no work of power there, except He performed healing on a few sick <i>ones</i>, laying on <i>His</i> hands.  
**6:6** And He marveled because of their unbelief. And He went about the villages in a circuit, teaching.  
**6:7** And He called the Twelve to Him and began to send them out two by two. And He gave them authority over the unclean spirits,  
**6:8** and charged them that they take nothing in <i>the</i> way, except only a staff; no bag, no bread, no copper in the belt;  
**6:9** but having tied on sandals, and not putting on two tunics.  
**6:10** And He said to them, Wherever you enter into a house, remain there until you go out from there.  
**6:11** And as many as will not receive you, nor hear you, going out from there, shake off the dust under your feet for a testimony to them. Truly I say to you, It will be more bearable for Sodom or Gomorrah in Judgment Day than for that city.  
**6:12** And going out, they proclaimed that <i>men</i> should repent.  
**6:13** And they cast out many demons, and anointed with oil many sick <i>ones</i> and healed <i>them.</i>.  
**6:14** And Herod the king heard, for His name became well known. And he said, John the Baptist was raised from the dead, and because of this the works of power operate in Him.  
**6:15** Others said, He is Elijah; and others said, He is a prophet or as one of the prophets.  
**6:16** But hearing, Herod said, This one is John whom I beheaded. He has risen from the dead.  
**6:17** For having sent, Herod himself had seized John and bound him in the prison, because of Herodias the wife of his brother Philip, because he had married her.  
**6:18** For John had said to Herod, It is not lawful for you to have the wife of your brother.  
**6:19** And Herodias held it against him, and desired to kill him, but was not able.  
**6:20** For Herod feared John, knowing him <i>to be</i> a holy and just man, and kept him safe. And hearing him, he did many things, and gladly heard from him.  
**6:21** And a well-timed day having come, when Herod made a dinner for his chuef men on his birthday, also the chiliarchs, and the chief ones of Galilee.

**6:26** And having become deeply grieved, <i>but</i> because of the oaths and those reclining together, the king did not wish to reject her.

**6:40** And they sat, ranks <i>by</i> ranks each hundred, and each fifty.  
**6:41** And taking the five loaves and the two fish, looking up to Heaven, He blessed, and broke the loaves, and giving out to His disciples, that they might set before them. And He divided the two fish to all.

**6:43** And they took up twelve baskets full <i>of</i> fragments, also from the fish.

**6:46** And taking leave <i>of</i> them, He went away to the mountain to pray.

**6:48** And He saw them being distressed in the rowing, for the wind was against them. And <i>it was</i> about the fourth watch of the night <i>when</i> He came toward them, walking on the sea. And <i>He</i> willed to go by them.

**6:50** For all saw Him, and were troubled. And immediately He spoke to them and said to them, Have courage. I AM! Do not fear.

**6:53** And crossing over, they came into the land of Gennesaret and drew to shore.  
**6:54** And they, coming out of the boat, at once having recognized Him,

## 7장

**7:2** And seeing some of His disciples eating bread with unclean, that is unwashed hands, they found fault.  
**7:3** For the Pharisees and all the Jews do not eat unless they wash the hands with <i>the</i> fist, holding the tradition of the elders.  
**7:4** And <i>coming</i> from the market, if they <i>do</i> not dip themselves, they do not eat. And there are many other things which they received to hold: immersings of cups, and of utensils, and of copper vessels, and couches.  
**7:5** Then the Pharisees and scribes questioned Him, Why do Your disciples not walk according to the tradition of the elders, but eat bread with unwashed hands?  
**7:6** And answering, He said to them, Well did Isaiah prophesy concerning you, hypocrites; as it has been written: "This people honors Me with the lips, but their heart is far away from Me;  
**7:7** and in vain they worship Me, teaching <i>as</i> doctrines the commandments of men." <n>Isa 29:13</n>  
**7:8** For, forsaking the commandment of God, you hold the tradition of men: immersings of utensils and cups, and many other such like things you do.  
**7:9** And He said to them, Do you do well to set aside the commandment of God so that you may keep your tradition?  
**7:10** For Moses said, "Honor your father and your mother," <n>Ex 20:12; Deut 5:16</n></i> and, "The one speaking evil of father or mother, let him end by death." <n>Ex 21:17</n>  
**7:11** But you say, If a man says to his father or mother, Corban, (which is, A gift!) whatever you may profit by me.  
**7:12** And you no longer allow him to do anything for <i>his</i> father or mother,  
**7:13** making void the Word of God by your tradition which you gave over. And many such like things you do.  
**7:14** And calling all the crowd near, He said to them, Hear Me, all <i>of you</i> and understand.  
**7:15** There is nothing from outside the man, entering into him, which is able to defile <i>him</i>. But the things going out from him, those are the things defiling the man.  
**7:16** If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear.  
**7:17** And when He entered into a house from the crowd, His disciples questioned Him about the parable.  
**7:18** And He said to them, Are you also so without understanding? Do you not perceive that everything entering from the outside into the man is not able to defile him?  
**7:19** <i>This is</i> because it does not enter into his heart, but into the belly, and goes out into the latrine, purging all the foods.  
**7:20** And He said, That out of the man coming out, <i>it is</i> the <i>thing</i> that defiles the man.  
**7:21** For from within, out of the heart of men go out the depraved reasonings, adulteries, fornications, murders,  
**7:22** thefts, greedy desires, depravities, deceit, unbridled luats, an evil eye, blasphemy, pride, recklessness.  
**7:23** All these evil things come out from within and defile the man.  
**7:24** And rising up from there, he went away into the borders of Tyre and Sidon. And entering into the house, He desired no one to know, but He could not be hidden.  
**7:25** For hearing about Him, a woman came up, <i>one</i> whose little daughter had an unclean spirit, coming <i>she</i> fell down at His feet.  
**7:26** And the woman was a Greek, a Syrophoenician by race. And she asked Him, that He would cast out the demon from her daughter.  
**7:27** And Jesus said to her, First, allow the children to be satisfied, for it is not good to take the children's bread and to throw to the little dogs.  
**7:28** But she answered and said to Him, Yes, Lord; for even the little dogs under the table eat from the crumbs of the children.  
**7:29** And He said to her, Because of this word, go. The demon has gone out from your daughter.  
**7:30** And going away to her house, she found the demon had gone out, and her little daughter being laid on the couch.  
**7:31** And again going out from the borders of Tyre and Sidon, He came to the Sea of Galilee, in the midst of the borders of Decapolis.  
**7:32** And they brought a deaf <i>one</i> to Him, hardly speaking. And they begged Him, that He put <i>His</i> hand on him.  
**7:33** And taking him away from the crowd privately, He put His fingers into his ears; and spitting, He touched his tongue;  
**7:34** and looking up into Heaven, He groaned and said to him, Ephphatha! (which is, Be completely opened!)  
**7:35** And instantly his ears were opened completely, and the bond of his tongue was loosed, and he spoke correctly.  
**7:36** And He charged them that they should tell no one. But as much as He charged them, more even more they proclaimed.  
**7:37** And they were most exceedingly amazed, saying, He has done all things well. He makes even the deaf <i>one</i> to hear, and the mute <i>one</i> to speak.  

## 8장

**8:1** The crowd being very great in those days, and not having anything they may eat, Jesus, calling His disciples near, said to them,  
**8:2** I have compassion on the crowd because now three days they remain with Me, and they do not have what they may eat.  
**8:3** And if I send them away fasting to their house, they will faint in the way, for some of them come from afar.  
**8:4** And His disciples answered Him, From where will anyone here be able to satisfy these <i>with</i> loaves on a place of solitude?  
**8:5** And He asked them, How many loaves do you have? And they said, Seven.  
**8:6** And He ordered the crowd to recline on the ground. And taking the seven loaves, having giving thanks, He broke and gave to His disciples, that they may set before <i>them</i>. And they set before the crowd.  
**8:7** And they had a few fish. And blessing, He said for these also to be set before <i>them</i>.  
**8:8** And they ate, and were satisfied. And they took up <i>the</i> surplus, seven baskets of fragments.  
**8:9** And the <i>ones</i> eating were about four thousand. And He sent them away.  
**8:10** And at once stepping into the boat with His disciples, He came into the parts of Dalmanutha.  
**8:11** And the Pharisees went out and began to question Him, seeking from Him a sign from Heaven, tempting Him.  
**8:12** And sighing in His spirit, He said, Why does this generation seek after a sign? Truly I say to you, <i>As</i> if this generation will be given a sign!  
**8:13** And leaving them, again stepping into the boat, He went away to the other side.  
**8:14** And <i>the disciples</i> forgot to take loaves. And they did not have <i>any</i> with them, except one loaf in the boat.  
**8:15** And He charged them, saying, See! Look out <i>for</i> the leaven of the Pharisees, and <i>of</i> the leaven of Herod.  
**8:16** And they reasoned with one another, saying, We have no loaves.  
**8:17** And knowing, Jesus said to them, Why do you reason that you have no loaves? Do you not yet perceive nor understand? Have you still hardened your heart?  
**8:18** "Having eyes, do you not see? And having ears, do you not hear?" And do you not remember? <n>Jer 5:21</n>  
**8:19** When I broke the five loaves to the five thousand, how many baskets full of fragments did you take up? They said to Him, Twelve.  
**8:20** And when the seven to the four thousand, how many baskets did you take up <i>with the</i> fillings of fragments? And they said, Seven.  
**8:21** And He said to them, How do you not understand?  
**8:22** And He came to Bethsaida. And they bring a blind <i>one</i> to Him, and begged Him that He would touch him.  
**8:23** And having taken hold of the blind <i>one's</i> hand, He led him forth outside the village. And having spit into his eyes, having laid <i>His</i> hands on him, He asked him if he saw anything.  
**8:24** And having looked up, he said, I see men as trees walking around.  
**8:25** Then again He placed <i>His</i> hands on his eyes, and made him look up. And he was restored and saw all clearly.  
**8:26** And He sent him to his house, saying, You may not go into the village, nor may tell anyone in the village.  
**8:27** And Jesus and His disciples went out to the villages of Caesarea of Philip. And in the way, He questioned His disciples, saying to them, Whom do men say Me to be?  
**8:28** And they answered, John the Baptist, and others <i>say</i> Elijah; but others, one of the prophets.  
**8:29** And He said to them, And you, whom do you say Me to be? And answering, Peter said to Him, You are the Christ.  
**8:30** And He warned them that they may tell no one about Him.  
**8:31** And He began to teach them that it is necessary for the Son of Man to suffer many things and to be rejected of the elders and chief priests and scribes, and to be killed, and after three days to rise again.  
**8:32** And He spoke the Word plainly. And taking Him aside, Peter began to rebuke Him.  
**8:33** But turning around and seeing His disciples, He rebuked Peter, saying, Go behind me, Satan, because you do not think the things of God, but the things of men.  
**8:34** And calling near the crowd with His disciples, He said to them, Whoever desires to come after Me, let him deny himself and take up his cross, and let him follow Me.  
**8:35** For whoever desires to save his life, <i>he</i> shall lose it. But whoever may lose his life for My sake and the gospel, this one shall save it.  
**8:36** For what shall it profit a man if he gain the whole world, yet forfeit his soul?  
**8:37** Or what shall a man give <i>as</i> an exchange <i>for</i> his soul?  
**8:38** For whoever may be ashamed of Me and My Word in this adulterous and sinful generation, the Son of Man will also be ashamed of him when He comes in the glory of His Father with the holy angels.  

## 9장

**9:1** And He said to them, Truly I say to you, There are some of The <i>ones</i> standing here who not shall taste of death, not until they see the kingdom of God coming in power.

**9:19** And answering them, He said, O unbelieving generation! How long will I be with you? How long shall I endure you? Bring him to Me.

**9:24** And immediately crying out, the father of the child said with tears, Lord, I believe! Help my unbelief!  
**9:25** And seeing that a crowd is running together, Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to it, Mute and deaf spirit, I command you, Come out from him, and you may no more go into him!  
**9:26** And crying out, and convulsing him very much, it came out. And he became as if dead, so as <i>for</i> many to say that he died.  
**9:27** But taking hold of his hand, Jesus raised him up, and he stood up.  
**9:28** And He entering into a house, His disciples questioned Him apart, <i>Why</i> were we not able to cast it out?  
**9:29** And He said to them, This kind can go out by nothing except by prayer and fasting.  
**9:30** And going forth from there, they passed by through Galilee. And He desired that no one know.  
**9:31** For He was teaching His disciples, and said to them, The Son of Man is given over into <i>the</i> hands of men, and they will kill Him. And being killed, He will rise up the third day.

**15:5** But Jesus answered no more, nothing, so as <i>for</i> Pilate to marvel.  
**15:6** And at a feast, he released to them one prisoner, whomever they asked.  
**15:7** And there was <i>one</i> called Barabbas, having been bound with the fellow-insurrectionists, who <i>all</i> in the insurrection had committed murder.  
**15:8** And crying aloud, the crowd began to beg <i>him to do</i> as he always did for them.  
**15:9** But Pilate answered them, saying, Do you desire I should release to you the king of the Jews?  
**15:10** For he knew that the chief priests had given Him over through envy.  
**15:11** But the chief priests stirred up the crowd, that rather he should release Barabbas to them.  
**15:12** But answering again, Pilate said to them, What then do you desire I do to <i>him</i> whom you call king of the Jews?  
**15:13** And again they cried out, Crucify Him!  
**15:14** But Pilate said to them, For what bad <i>thing</i> did He do? But they much more cried out, Crucify Him!  
**15:15** And having decided to do the easiest <i>to</i> the crowd, Pilate released Barabbas to them. And having scourged <i>Him</i>, he gave over Jesus, that He might be crucified.  
**15:16** And the soldiers led Him away inside the courtyard, which is <i>the</i> praetorium. And they called together all the cohort.  
**15:17** And they clothed Him <i>with</i> purple, and having plaited a crown of thorns, they put <i>it</i> around Him.  
**15:18** And they began to salute Him, Hail, king of the Jews!  
**15:19** And they struck His head with a reed, and spat at Him. And placing the knees, <i>they</i> did homage to Him.  
**15:20** And when they had mocked Him, they took the purple off Him, and put His own garments on Him. And they led Him out, that they might crucify Him.  
**15:21** And they compelled one passing by, Simon, a Cyrenian, coming from a field, the father of Alexander and Rufus, that he might bear His cross.  
**15:22** And they brought Him to Golgotha Place, which translated is, Of a Skull Place.  
**15:23** And they gave Him wine spiced with myrrh to drink. But He did not take <i>it</i>.  
**15:24** And having crucified Him, they divided His garments, casting a lot on them, who should take what. <n>Psa 22:18</n>  
**15:25** And it was <i>the</i> third hour, and they crucified Him.  
**15:26** And the inscription of His accusation was written over <i>Him</i>, THE KING OF THE JEWS.  
**15:27** And they crucified two plunderers with Him, one at <i>the</i> right, and one at <i>the</i> left of Him.  
**15:28** And the Scripture was fulfilled which says, "And He was numbered with <i>the</i> lawless." <n>Isa 53:12</n>  
**15:29** And those passing by blasphemed Him, shaking their heads, and saying, Aha! <i>You</i> razing the temple, and in three days building <i>it</i>,  
**15:30** save Yourself and come down from the cross.  
**15:31** And also the chief priests and the scribes mocking to one another said likewise, He saved others; He is not able to save Himself.  
**15:32** Let come down the Christ, the king of Israel now come down from the cross, that we may see and believe Him. And the ones crucified with Him were reviling Him.  
**15:33** And <i>it</i> being <i>the</i> sixth hour, darkness came over all the land until the ninth hour.  
**15:34** And at the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying, Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani? (Which being translated is, "My God, My God, why did You forsake Me?") <n>Psa 22:1</n>  
**15:35** And hearing, some of those standing by said, Behold, He calls Elijah.  
**15:36** And one running up, and filling a sponge with vinegar, and putting <i>it</i> on a reed, was giving Him to drink, saying, Leave alone, let us see if Elijah comes to take Him down. <n>Psa 69:21</n>  
**15:37** And letting out a cry, Jesus breathed out <i>His</i> spirit.  
**15:38** And the veil of the temple was torn into two, from top to bottom.  
**15:39** And the centurion standing near across from Him, seeing that <i>He</i> crying out so He breathed out <i>His</i> spirit, <i>he</i> said, Truly, this Man was Son of God.  
**15:40** And also women were watching from afar, among whom also was Mary Magdalene; also Mary the mother of James the younger, and of Joses, and Salome,  
**15:41** who also followed Him and ministered to Him when He was in Galilee; and many other <i>women</i> who came up to Jerusalem with Him.  
**15:42** And it becoming evening already, since it was <i>the</i> preparation, that is, <i>the</i> day before sabbath,  
**15:43** Joseph from Arimathea came, an honorable councillor, who himself was also waiting for the kingdom of God. <i>And</i> taking courage, he went in to Pilate and begged the body of Jesus.  
**15:44** And Pilat marveled if He had already died. And calling the centurionm he asked him if He died long ago.  
**15:45** And knowing from the centurion, he granted the body to Joseph.  
**15:46** And having bought a linen cloth, and having taken Him down, he wrapped <i>Him</i> in the linen, and laid Him in a tomb which was cut out of rock. And <i>he</i> rolled a stone against the mouth of the tomb.  
**15:47** And Mary Magdalene, and Mary of Joses, beheld where He was laid.  

## 16장

**16:1** And the sabbath passing, Mary Magdalene and Mary the <i>mother</i> of James and Salome, bought spices, so that coming they might anoint Him.  
**16:2** And very early on the first of the sabbaths, the sun having risen, they came upon the tomb.  
**16:3** And they said to themselves, Who will roll away the stone from the door of the tomb for us?  
**16:4** And looking up, they saw that the stone had been rolled back; for it was very great.  
**16:5** And having entered into the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right, having been clothed <i>in</i> a white robe. And they were much amazed.  
**16:6** But he says to them, Do not be muchamazed. You seek Jesus the Nazarene who has been crucified. He was raised. He is not here. Behold! The place where they put Him.  
**16:7** But go, say to the disciples and to Peter, He goes before you into Galilee. You will see Him there, even as He told you.  
**16:8** And going out quickly, they fled from the tomb. And trembling and ecstasy took hold of them. And they told no one, not a thing, for they were afraid.  
**16:9** And having risen early on the first of the sabbath, He first appeared to Mary Magdalene, from whom He had cast out seven demons.  
**16:10** Going, that <i>one</i> reported to those having been with Him, mourning and weeping.  
**16:11** And they having heard that He lives, and was seen by her, they did not believe.  
**16:12** And after these things, He was revealed in a different form to two of them walking <i>and</i> going into the country.  
**16:13** And going, they reported to the rest; not even did they believe those.  
**16:14** Afterward, as they reclined, He was revealed to the Eleven. And <i>He</i> reproached their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they did not believe the <i>ones</i> who had seen Him, having been raised.  
**16:15** And He said to them, Going into all the world, proclaim the gospel to all the creation.  
**16:16** The <i>one</i> believing and being baptized will be saved; but the <i>one</i> not believing will be condemned.  
**16:17** And <i>miraculous</i> signs will follow to those believing these things: they will cast out demons in My name; they will speak new languages;  
**16:18** they will take up snakes; and if they drink anything deadly, it will not hurt them, not <i>any;</i> they will lay hands on <i>ones</i> being infirm, and they will be well.  
**16:19** Then indeed, after speaking to them, the Lord was taken up into Heaven, and sat at <i>the</i> right of God. <n>Psa 110:1</n>  
**16:20** And going out, they preached everyhere, the Lord working with <i>them</i> and confirming the Word through the <i>miraculous</i> signs following after. Amen
