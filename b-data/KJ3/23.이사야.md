# 23.이사야 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision of Isaiah the son of Amoz, which he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz <i>and</i> Hezekiah, kings of Judah.  
**1:2** Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth! For Jehovah has spoken: I have lifted up and reared sons, but they have transgressed against Me.  
**1:3** The ox knows his owner, and the ass his master's manger, <i>but</i> Israel does not know; My people have not understood.  
**1:4** Woe, sinful nation, a people heavy <i>with</i> iniquity, a seed of evildoers, sons who corrupt! They have forsaken Jehovah; they have spurned the Holy One of Israel. They <i>are</i> estranged backward.  
**1:5** Why will you be stricken any more? Will you continue the revolt? The whole head is sick, and the whole heart is faint.  
**1:6** From the sole of the foot even to the head, no soundness is in it; <i>only</i> a wound and a stripe and a fresh blow; they have not been closed, nor bound up; and not was it softened with oil.  
**1:7** Your land <i>is</i> a desolation; your cities burned with fire. Strangers devour your land before you; and <i>behold</i>, <i>it is</i> ruin, as overthrown by strangers.  
**1:8** And the daughter of Zion is left a booth in a vineyard, like a hut in a cucumber field, like a besieged city.  
**1:9** Except Jehovah of Hosts had left a remnant for us, a few, we would be as Sodom; we would be as Gomorrah.  
**1:10** Hear the Word of Jehovah, rulers of Sodom. Listen to the Law of our God, people of Gomorrah.  
**1:11** What <i>good</i> to Me <i>are</i> your many sacrifices, says Jehovah? I am sated <i>with</i> burnt offerings of rams, and the fat of fattened cattle, and <i>the blood of</i> bulls; nor do I delight in the blood of lambs and he goats.  
**1:12** When you come to see My face, who has required this at your hand, to trample My courts?  
**1:13** Do not add to bringing vain sacrifice; <i>its</i> incense is a disgusting thing to Me. I cannot endure the new moon and sabbath, the going to meeting; I cannot endure the evil assembly.  
**1:14** My soul hates your new moons and your set feasts. They are a burden on Me. I am weary of bearing <i>them</i>.  
**1:15** And when you spread out your palms, I will hide My eyes from you; also, when you multiply prayer, I will not hear. Your palms are full of bloods.  
**1:16** Wash yourselves, purify yourselves. Remove the evil of your doings from before My eyes; cease doing evil.  
**1:17** Learn to do good, seek justice, straighten the oppressor, judge the orphan, strive for the widow.  
**1:18** Come now and let us reason <i>together</i>, says Jehovah: Though your sins are as scarlet, they shall be white as snow; though they are red as the crimson, they shall be like wool.  
**1:19** If you are willing and hear, you shall eat the good of the land;  
**1:20** but if you refuse and rebel, you shall be devoured <i>with</i> the sword; for the mouth of Jehovah has spoken.  
**1:21** Oh how the faithful city has become <i>one</i> doing adultery! <i>She was</i> full of justice; righteousness lodged in it; but now murderers!  
**1:22** Your silver has become dross; your liquor is diluted with water.  
**1:23** Your rulers <i>are</i> rebelling and <i>are</i> companions of thieves. Everyone loves a present, and is pursuing bribes. They do not judge the orphan, nor does the cause of the widow come to them.  
**1:24** Therefore, a statement of the Lord, Jehovah of Hosts, the mighty <i>One</i> of Israel, Alas! I will be eased of My adversaries, and avenge Myself of My enemies.  
**1:25** And I will return My hand on you, and refine your dross, as <i>with</i> lye, and turn aside all your alloy.  
**1:26** And I will return your judges as at the first; and one advising you, as at the beginning; then you shall be called the city of righteousness, a faithful city.  
**1:27** Zion shall be redeemed with judgment, and her returning <i>ones</i> with righteousness.  
**1:28** And the breaking of those transgressing and of the sinners shall be together. And those who forsake Jehovah <i>shall be</i> consumed.  
**1:29** For they shall be ashamed of the trees which you lusted after; and you shall be ashamed of the gardens that you have chosen.  
**1:30** For you shall be like a tree whose leaf fades, and like a garden that has no water for it.  
**1:31** And the strong <i>one</i> shall be for tow, and his work for a spark; and they shall burn both together; and no one shall quench <i>them</i>.  

## 2장

**2:1** The Word that Isaiah the son of Amoz saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem:  
**2:2** And it shall be in the last days, the mountain of the house of Jehovah shall be established in the top of the mountains, and shall be exalted above the hills; and all nations shall flow into it.  
**2:3** And many peoples shall go and say, Come and let us go up to the mount of Jehovah, to the house of the God of Jacob. And He will teach us from His ways, and we will walk in His paths. For out of Zion the Law will go forth, and the Word of Jehovah from Jerusalem.  
**2:4** And He shall judge among the nations and shall rebuke many peoples. And they shall beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruning knives. Nation shall not lift up sword against nation, and they shall not learn war any more.  
**2:5** O house of Jacob, come and let us walk in the light of Jehovah.  
**2:6** For You have forsaken Your people, the house of Jacob, because they are filled from the east, and <i>are</i> spirit-knowers like the Philistines. And they clap <i>hands</i> with children of <i>those</i> being foreign.  
**2:7** And his land is filled <i>with</i> silver and gold. <i>There is</i> no end of his treasures. And his land is full of horses; his chariots also do not have an end.  
**2:8** And his land is full of idols; they worship the work of their own hands, that which their own fingers have made.  
**2:9** And man bowed down; and man was humbled, but You do not lift them up.  
**2:10** Enter into the rock and hide in the dust from fear of Jehovah, and from the glory of His majesty.  
**2:11** The haughtiness of eyes of man shall be humbled, and the pride of men shall be bowed down; but Jehovah, He alone, will be exalted in that day.  
**2:12** For the day of Jehovah of Hosts <i>shall be</i> on all the proud <i>ones</i> and those exalted, and on all that is lifted up; and it <i>will be</i> abased;  
**2:13** and against all Lebanon's cedars, high and lifted up; and against all the oaks of Bashan;  
**2:14** and against all the high mountains; and against all the lifted up hills;  
**2:15** and against every tall tower; and against every fortified wall;  
**2:16** and against all the ships of Tarshish; and on all ships of desire .  
**2:17** And the pride of man shall be humbled; and the haughtiness of men shall be abased; and Jehovah alone will be exalted in that day.  
**2:18** And the idols shall completely vanish.  
**2:19** And they shall go into the caves of the rocks, and into the holes of the earth, for dread of Jehovah, and from the glory of His majesty; when He rises up to make the earth tremble.  
**2:20** In that day a man shall cast out his silver, and his golden idols which they made for him to worship to the hole of the burrowers and to the bats;  
**2:21** to go into the crevices of the rocks, and into the clefts of the cliffs, from the dread of Jehovah, and from the glory of His majesty; when He rises up to make the earth tremble.  
**2:22** Cease yourself from man, whose breath <i>is</i> in his nostril, for in what is he to be esteemed?  

## 3장

**3:1** For, behold, the Lord, Jehovah of Hosts, <i>is</i> turning aside from Jerusalem and from Judah the stay and the staff, the whole stay of bread and the whole stay of water;  
**3:2** the mighty <i>one</i>, and the man of war; the judge and the prophet, and the one divining, and the old <i>one</i>;  
**3:3** the commander of fifty and the exalted of face; the counselor and the skilled craftsman; and the discerning charmer.  
**3:4** And I will give young boys <i>to be</i> their rulers, and caprices shall rule over them.  
**3:5** And the people shall be oppressed man against man, and a man against his neighbor. The boy will act insolently against the old <i>one</i>, and the <i>one</i> being despised against the <i>one</i> being honored.  
**3:6** When a man shall take hold of his brother, <i>at</i> his father's house, <i>saying</i>, A cloak! Come, you be a ruler for us; let this ruin be under your hand.  
**3:7** In that day he shall rise up, saying, I will not be one who binds up; for there <i>is</i> no bread and not a cloak in my house; you shall not appoint me a ruler of the peoples.  
**3:8** For Jerusalem has stumbled, and Judah has fallen, because their tongue and their doings toward Jehovah <i>are</i> to rebel against the eyes of His glory.  
**3:9** The look of their faces answers against them; they have declared their sin like Sodom; they do not hide <i>it</i>. Woe to their soul! For they have dealt an evil <i>way</i> to themselves.  
**3:10** Say to the righteous <i>one</i> that <i>it is</i> good; for they shall eat of the fruit of their doings.  
**3:11** Woe to the wicked <i>one</i>! For the evil <i>thing</i>, the doing of his hand will be done to him.  
**3:12** <i>As for</i> My people, children <i>are</i> the ones oppressing, and women rule over him. Oh My people, those leading you cause <i>you</i> to go astray, and they swallow the way of your paths.  
**3:13** Jehovah stands up to contend, and stands up to judge the peoples.  
**3:14** Jehovah will enter into judgment with the old <i>ones</i> of His people, and their rulers. For you have eaten up the vineyard, the plunder of the afflicted <i>one is</i> in your houses.  
**3:15** What <i>is</i> it to you? You crush My people, and <i>you</i> grind the faces of the afflicted <i>ones</i>, a statement of the Lord, Jehovah of Hosts?  
**3:16** And Jehovah says, Because are being proud the daughters of Zion, and walk with stretched out necks and wanton eyes, walking and skipping <i>as</i> they go, and make a tinkling with their feet;  
**3:17** So Jehovah will make the crown of the daughter of Zion scabby; and Jehovah will lay their secret parts bare.  
**3:18** In that day the Lord will turn aside the beauty of the ankle bracelets, and the headbands, and the crescents,  
**3:19** the pendants, and the bracelets, and the veils;  
**3:20** the turbans, and the leg ornaments, and the sashes, and the houses of the soul, and the amulets;  
**3:21** the rings and nose-rings;  
**3:22** the festal apparel, and the outer garments, and the mantles, and the purses;  
**3:23** the mirrors and the fine linen; and the turbans and the veils.  
**3:24** And it shall be, instead of a smell of perfume, there shall be an odor of decay. And instead of a girdle, a rope; and instead of curled work, baldness; and instead of a girding of a rich robe, sackcloth; instead of beauty, a burning  
**3:25** Your men shall fall by the sword, and your mighty <i>ones</i> in the battle.  
**3:26** And her gates shall lament and mourn, and she shall sit on the ground.  

## 4장

**4:1** And in that day seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, We will eat our own bread and wear our own clothing, only let your name be called on us; collect our shame.  
**4:2** In that day the Branch of Jehovah will be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the earth for majesty and for glory for the escaped of Israel.  
**4:3** And it shall be, the remnant in Zion, and the remainder in Jerusalem, it shall be said of him: <i>He is</i> holy, every one who is written among the living in Jerusalem;  
**4:4** when the Lord shall have washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion, and the blood of Jerusalem shall have been rinsed away from its midst by a spirit of judgment, and by a spirit of burning.  
**4:5** Then Jehovah will create a <i>pillar of</i> cloud and smoke by day, and the shining of a flaming fire by night, over all the site of Mount Zion, and over her assemblies; for over all the glory <i>will be</i> a covering.  
**4:6** And there shall be a booth for a shade from the heat, and for a refuge, and for a hiding place from storm and rain.  

## 5장

**5:1** Now I will sing to my Beloved a song of my Beloved concerning His vineyard: There was a vineyard to My Beloved in a horn, a son of fatness.  
**5:2** And He dug about it, and cleared it of stones, and planted it <i>with</i> the choicest vine, and built a tower in its midst, and also hewed out a wine vat in it. And He waited <i>for it</i> to produce grapes, but it produced rotten grapes.  
**5:3** And now, ones living <i>in</i> Jerusalem and men of Judah, I ask you, judge between Me and My vineyard.  
**5:4** What more could have been done to My vineyard that I have not done in it? Who knows? I waited <i>for it</i> to produce grapes, but it produced rotten grapes.  
**5:5** And now, please, I will make known to you what I will do to My vineyard. I will turn aside its hedge, and it will be burned. I will breach its wall, and it will become a trampling place.  
**5:6** And I will lay it waste; it shall not be pruned and not hoed; but briers and thorns shall come up. And on the clouds I will command the rain from raining on it.  
**5:7** For the vineyard of Jehovah of Hosts <i>is</i> the house of Israel, and the man of Judah <i>is</i> His delightful plant. And He waited for justice, but behold, bloodshed; for righteousness, but behold, a cry!  
**5:8** Woe <i>to</i> those touching house to house, bringing near field to field until the end of space! And you are made to dwell alone in the midst of the land!  
**5:9** Jehovah of Hosts <i>has sworn</i> in my ears, If not many houses shall be a waste, large and beautiful <i>ones</i> without <i>one</i> living <i>in them</i>.  
**5:10** Yea, ten acres of vineyard shall produce one bath, and the seed of a homer shall produce an ephah.  
**5:11** Woe to those who rise early in the morninog pursue fermented drink, tarrying in the twilight <i>while</i> wine inflames them!  
**5:12** And the lyre and the harp, the timbrel and flute, and wine are <i>at</i> their feasts, but they do not look upon the work of Jehovah; yea, they do see. Therefore My people will go into exile without knowledge, and his men of glory into famine; and his multitude is parched with thirst.  
**5:13** - - -  
**5:14** Therefore Sheol has enlarged its appetite, and opened its mouth without measure, and her glory and her multitude, and her uproar, he who exults in her, shall go down in it.  
**5:15** And man is bowed down, and man is humbled, and the eyes of the proud <i>ones</i> are humbled.  
**5:16** But Jehovah of Hosts is exalted in judgment, and God the Holy One is <i>seen</i> holy in righteousness.  
**5:17** Then the lambs shall feed as in their pasture, and ones being sojourners shall eat the waste places of the fatlings.  
**5:18** Woe to those who draw iniquity with cords of vanity, and sin as <i>with</i> ropes of a cart;  
**5:19** who say, Let Him hurry <i>and</i> hasten His work, so that we may see; and let the purpose of the Holy One of Israel draw near and come, so that we may know!  
**5:20** Woe to those who say to an evil <i>thing</i>, a good <i>thing</i>; and to a good <i>thing</i>, an evil <i>thing</i>; who put darkness for light, and light for darkness; who put a bitter <i>thing</i> for a sweet <i>thing</i>, and a sweet <i>thing</i> for a bitter <i>thing</i>!  
**5:21** Woe to the wise <i>ones</i> in their own eyes, and discerning in their <i>own</i> faces!  
**5:22** Woe to the mighty <i>ones</i> to drink wine, and men of strength to mix fermented drink;  
**5:23** who justify the wicked <i>one</i> for a bribe, and turn aside the righteousness of the righteous <i>ones</i> from him!  
**5:24** Therefore, as the tongue of fire devours the stubble, and the dry grass falls <i>in</i> the flame; their root shall be like rottenness, and their blossoms shall go up like dust, because they have rejected the Law of Jehovah of Hosts; and despised the Word of the Holy One of Israel.  
**5:25** On account of this the anger of Jehovah is kindled on His people, and He has stretched out His hand against them, and has struck them; and the mountains quaked, and their corpses <i>were</i> as offal in the midst of the streets. In all this His anger does not turn back, but His hand is stretched out still.  
**5:26** And He will lift up a banner to nations from afar, and hiss to them from the ends of the earth; and, behold, it shall come <i>with</i> swift speed!  
**5:27** None <i>shall be</i> weary and none stumble in it; not one will slumber and not <i>one</i> sleep; and not shall the waist cloth of their loins be opened, and not the thong of their sandals broken;  
**5:28** whose arrows <i>are</i> sharp, and all their bows bent; their horses' hooves shall seem like flint, and their wheels like a windstorm.  
**5:29** Its roaring <i>shall be</i> like a lion; they shall roar like young lions. Yea, they roar and seize the prey and bring <i>it</i> away safely; and not <i>one</i> shall deliver.  
**5:30** And in that day they shall roar against them like the roaring of the sea; when <i>one</i> looks to the land, and behold, darkness! Distress! And light shall be darkened by its clouds.  

## 6장

**6:1** In the year that King Uzziah died, then I saw the Lord sitting on a throne, high and lifted up. And His train filled the temple.  
**6:2** Above it stood the seraphs. To <i>each</i> one are six wings; with two he covered his face; and with two he covered his feet; and with two he flew.  
**6:3** And this <i>one</i> cried to this <i>one</i> and said, Holy, holy, holy <i>is</i> Jehovah of Hosts; all the earth <i>is</i> full <i>of</i> His glory!  
**6:4** And the posts of the threshold shook from the voice of the one who cried; and the house was filled with smoke.  
**6:5** Then I said, Woe to me! For I am cut off; for I <i>am</i> a man of unclean lips, and I live amongst a people of unclean lips; for my eyes have seen the King, Jehovah of Hosts.  
**6:6** And one of the seraphs flew to me and in his hand <i>was</i> a burning coal taken with tongs from the altar.  
**6:7** And <i>he</i> touched <i>it</i> on my mouth, and said, Behold, this has touched your lips; and your iniquity is taken away, and your sin is covered.  
**6:8** And I heard the voice of Jehovah, saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go for Us? And I said, Behold me. Send me!  
**6:9** And He said, Go and say to this people, Hearing <i>you</i> hear, but do not understand; and seeing <i>you</i> see, but do not know.  
**6:10** Make the heart of this people fat, and make his ears heavy, and shut his eyes, that he not see with his eyes, and hear with his ears, and understand with his heart, and turn back, and <i>one</i> heals him.  
**6:11** And I said, Until when, O Lord? And He said, Until cities lie desolate without <i>one</i> living <i>in them</i>, and the houses without man, and the land is laid waste, a desolation;  
**6:12** and <i>until</i> Jehovah has sent mankind far away, and the desolation in the midst of the land <i>is</i> great.  
**6:13** But yet a tenth <i>shall be</i> in it, and <i>it</i> shall return and be burned like the terebinth <i>tree</i> and like the oak that in being felled <i>yet has</i> its stump (the holy seed <i>is</i> its stump).  

## 7장

**7:1** And it happened in the days of Ahaz: the son of Jotham, the son of Uzziah, king of Judah, Rezin the king of Syria, and Pekah the son of Remaliah, king of Israel, went up to Jerusalem to war against it, but were not able to do battle against it.  
**7:2** And the house of David was told, saying, Syria is resting upon Ephraim, and his heart and the heart of his people quivering like the the trees of the forest toss about from the wind.  
**7:3** And Jehovah said to Isaiah, Go out to meet Ahaz, you and your son Shear-jashub, at the end of the conduit of the upper pool, to the highway of the Fuller's Field.  
**7:4** And say to him, Be on guard and be quiet. Do not fear and not be timid of heart because of the two tails of these smoking firebrands, because of the fierce anger of Rezin and Syria, and of the son of Remaliah.  
**7:5** Because Syria, Ephraim, and the son of Remaliah have plotted evil <i>things</i> against you, saying,  
**7:6** Let us go up against Judah and make her to be loathed, and break her for ourselves, and set a king in her midst, the son of Tabeal.  
**7:7** So says the Lord Jehovah, It shall not rise, nor shall it happen.  
**7:8** For the head of Syria <i>is</i> Damascus, and the head of Damascus <i>is</i> Rezin; and within sixty five years Ephraim shall be broken from <i>being</i> a people.  
**7:9** And the head of Ephraim <i>is</i> Samaria, and the head of Samaria <i>is</i> Remaliah's son. If you will not stand firm, surely you will not be established.  
**7:10** And Jehovah spoke again to Ahaz, saying,  
**7:11** Ask a sign from Jehovah your God; make deep the asking, or make <i>it</i> high above.  
**7:12** But Ahaz said, I will not ask, nor will I test Jehovah.  
**7:13** And He said, Hear now, O house of David, is <i>it</i> too little for you to weary men, that you will also weary my God?  
**7:14** So, The Lord Himself will give you a sign: Behold! The virgin will conceive and will bear a son; and she shall call His name Immanuel.  
**7:15** He shall eat curds and honey until He knows to refuse the evil <i>thing</i> and choose the good <i>thing</i>.  
**7:16** For before the boy shall know to refuse the evil and choose the good <i>thing</i>, the land that you hate will be forsaken before both her kings.  
**7:17** Jehovah shall bring the king of Assyria on you, and on your people, and on your father's house, <i>by</i> the king of Assyria, days which have not come since the days Ephraim turned aside from Judah.  
**7:18** And it shall be in that day Jehovah shall hiss for the fly that <i>is</i> in the end of the rivers of Egypt, and for the bee in the land of Assyria.  
**7:19** And they shall come, and they shall all rest in the steep ravines, and in the clefts of the rocks, and in all the thorn-bushes, and in all the pastures.  
**7:20** In that day the Lord will shave with a razor that is hired beyond the River, by the king of Assyria, the head, and the hair of the feet, and also it shall sweep away the beard.  
**7:21** And it shall be in that day, a man shall keep alive a heifer of the herd, and two sheep.  
**7:22** And it shall be, he shall eat curds from the plenty of making of milk; for everyone who is left in the land shall eat curds and honey.  
**7:23** And it shall be in that day, every place where there were a thousand vines <i>worth</i> a thousand <i>pieces</i> of silver, <i>it</i> shall be for briers and thorns.  
**7:24** With arrows and with the bow, he shall come there; because all the land shall be briers and thorns.  
**7:25** And all the hills which were hoed with the hoe, you shall not come there <i>for</i> fear of briers and thorn-bushes; but it shall be for the sending out of the ox, and for trampling of sheep.  

## 8장

**8:1** And Jehovah said to me, Take a big tablet and write on it with a man's pen: To Make Haste <i>to</i> Plunder! Hurry to the Spoil!  
**8:2** And I took faithful witnesses to record for me, Uriah the priest, and Zechariah the son of Jeberechiah.  
**8:3** And I drew near the prophetess. And she conceived, and bore a son. Then Jehovah said to me, Call his name, Make Haste <i>to</i> Plunder! Hurry to the Spoil!  
**8:4** For before the boy knows to cry, My father, or, My mother, the riches of Damascus and the plunder of Samaria shall be lifted up before the king of Assyria.  
**8:5** And Jehovah added to speak to me again, saying,  
**8:6** Because this people has refused the waters of Shiloah that flow softly, and rejoices in Rezin and Remaliah's son,  
**8:7** even behold, therefore the Lord also brings up on them the waters of the River, mighty and many, the king of Assyria and all his glory. And he shall come up over all its waters, and go over all its banks.  
**8:8** And he shall pass through Judah. He shall overflow and pass over; he shall reach to the neck; and his wings will be stretching out, filling the breadth of your land, O Immanuel.  
**8:9** O peoples, suffer evil, and be broken! And give ear, all from the far places of the earth; gird yourselves and be broken. Gird yourselves and be broken!  
**8:10** Counsel a plan, and it is broken; speak a word, and it shall not rise; for God <i>is</i> with us.  
**8:11** For so Jehovah spoke to me with a strong hand, and taught me against walking in the way of this people, saying,  
**8:12** Do not say, A conspiracy, to everything of which this people says, A conspiracy! And do not dread its dread, and do not be terrified.  
**8:13** Sanctify Jehovah of Hosts Himself, and He will <i>be</i> your fear, and let Him be terrifying to you.  
**8:14** And He shall be for a sanctuary, and become a Stone-of-Stumbling and <i>shall be</i> a Rock -of-Falling to the two houses of Israel; for a trap and for a snare to the one living <i>in</i> Jerusalem.  
**8:15** And many among them shall stumble and fall, and be broken, and be snared, and be taken.  
**8:16** Bind up the Testimony, seal the Law among My disciples.  
**8:17** And I will wait for Jehovah, who hides His face from the house of Jacob; and I will look for Him.  
**8:18** Behold, I and the children whom Jehovah has given to me <i>are</i> for signs and wonders in Israel from Jehovah of Hosts, who dwells in Mount Zion.  
**8:19** And when they say to you, Seek to the mediums, and to spirit-knowers who peep and mutter; should not a people seek to its God, <i>than</i> for the living to <i>seek</i> the <i>ones</i> who died?
