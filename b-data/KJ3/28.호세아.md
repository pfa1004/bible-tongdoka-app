# 28.호세아 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** The Word of Jehovah that was to Hosea, the son of Beeri, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz <i>and</i> Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam, the son of Joash, king of Israel.  
**1:2** The beginning of the speaking of Jehovah by Hosea. And Jehovah said to Hosea, Go, take to yourself a wife of adultery, and children of adultery. For fornicating the land goes fornicating away from Jehovah.  
**1:3** And he went and took Gomer, the daughter of Diblaim, who conceived and bore him a son.  
**1:4** And Jehovah said to him, Call his name God Will Sow, for yet <i>in</i> a little <i>while</i> I will call to account the blood of Jezreel on the house of Jehu, and will cause the kingdom of the house of Israel to cease.  
**1:5** And it shall be in that day, I will break the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezreel.  
**1:6** And she conceived again and bore a daughter. And He said to him, Call her name No Mercy, for I will not again still have mercy <i>on</i> the house of Israel; but taking away I will take them away.  
**1:7** And I will have mercy on the house of Judah and will save them by Jehovah their God. And I will not save them by bow or by sword, or by battle, by horses, or by horsemen.  
**1:8** And she having weaned No Mercy, she conceived and bore a son.  
**1:9** Then He said, Call his name Not My People, for you <i>are</i> not My people, and I will not be for you.  
**1:10** Yet the number of the sons of Israel shall be as the sand of the sea, which is not measured nor numbered. And it shall be, in the place where it is said to them, You <i>are</i> Not My People, it shall be said to them, Sons of the Living God.  
**1:11** And the sons of Judah and the sons of Israel shall be gathered together, and shall set over themselves one head. And they shall go up out of the land; for great <i>shall be</i> the day of Jezreel.  

## 2장

**2:1** Say to your brothers, A People; and to your sisters, Mercy.  
**2:2** Strive! Strive with your mother, for she <i>is</i> not My wife, and I <i>am</i> not her husband. Therefore, let her put away her fornications from her face, and her adulteries from between her breasts,  
**2:3** lest I strip her naked and set her out as in the day that she was born, and make her as the wilderness, and place her like a dry land, and kill her with thirst.  
**2:4** And I will not have pity for her sons, for they <i>are</i> the sons of fornications.  
**2:5** For their mother has prostituted <i>herself</i>; she who conceived them has acted shamefully. For she said, I will go after my lovers who give my bread and my water, my wool and my flax, my oil and my drink.  
**2:6** Therefore, behold, I will hedge your way with thorns, and I will wall up her wall, that she shall not find her paths.  
**2:7** And she shall run after her lovers, but she shall not overtake them. And she shall look for them, but she shall not find <i>them</i>. Then she shall say, I will go and return to my first husband, for then <i>it was</i> better with me than now.  
**2:8** For she did not know that I gave her grain and new wine and oil, and I multiplied to her silver and the gold they made for Baal.  
**2:9** So I will return and take My grain in its time, and My wine in its season. And I will snatch away My wool and My flax <i>she uses</i> to cover her shamelessness.  
**2:10** And now I will reveal her shamefulness to the eyes of those loving her, and not a man shall deliver her out of My hand.  
**2:11** I will also cause all her joy to cease, her feast days, her new moons, and her sabbaths, and all her solemn feasts.  
**2:12** And I will destroy her vines and her fig trees, of which she has said, They <i>are</i> my rewards that those loving me have given to me. And I will set them for a forest, and the beasts of the field shall eat them.  
**2:13** And I will call to account on her the days of the Baals <i>in</i> which she burned <i>incens</i>e to them. Yea, she put on herself her nose rings, and her jewels, and she has gone after those loving her and forgotten Me, says Jehovah.  
**2:14** Therefore, behold, I will lure her and bring her <i>to</i> the wilderness, and speak to her heart.  
**2:15** And from there I will give her vineyards to her, and the valley of Achor for an opening of hope. And she shall answer there as in the days of her youth, and as in the day when she came up out of the land of Egypt.  
**2:16** And at that day, says Jehovah, you shall call Me, My Husband; and you shall not still call Me, My Baal.  
**2:17** For I will take away the names of the Baals out of her mouth; and they shall not still be remembered by their name.  
**2:18** And in that day I will cut a covenant for them with the beasts of the field, and with the birds of the heavens, and the creepers of the ground. And I will break the bow and the sword, and the battle <i>weapons</i> out of the earth, and I will make them to lie down to be secure.  
**2:19** And I will betroth you to Me forever. Yes, I will betroth you to Me in righteousness, and in judgment, and in mercy, and in compassions.  
**2:20** I will even betroth you to Me in faithfulness, and you shall know Jehovah.  
**2:21** And it shall be in that day, I will answer, says Jehovah. I will answer the heavens, and they shall answer the earth.  
**2:22** And the earth shall answer the grain, and the wine, and the oil; and they shall answer Jezreel.  
**2:23** And I will sow her to Me in the earth. And I will have mercy on No Mercy. And I will say to Not My People, You <i>are</i> My People! And he shall say, My God! (<i>Rom. 2:24)  

## 3장

**3:1** And Jehovah said to me, Go again, love a woman loved by a friend, yet an adulteress, according to the love of Jehovah <i>towar</i>d the sons of Israel, who turn to other gods, and love raisin cakes of grapes.  
**3:2** And I bargained over her for me with fifteen <i>pieces of</i> silver, and <i>for</i> a homer of barley, and a half of barley.  
**3:3** And I said to her, You shall live with me many days. You shall not be an adulteress, and not be to a man, and I also <i>will be</i> for you.  
**3:4** For the sons of Israel shall live many days <i>with</i> no king and no ruler, and with no sacrifice, and no pillars, and no ephod or teraphim.  
**3:5** Afterward the sons of Israel shall turn back and seek Jehovah their God and David their king. And they shall fear Jehovah and His goodness in the ends of the days.  

## 4장

**4:1** Sons of Israel, hear the Word of Jehovah, for Jehovah <i>has</i> a quarrel with those living <i>in</i> the land; for there is no truth, and no mercy, and no knowledge of God in the land.  
**4:2** Swearing, and lying, and killing, and stealing, and the doing of adultery increase. And blood touches against blood.  
**4:3** On account of this the land shall mourn, and every one living in it shall droop, with the beasts of the field and the birds of the heaven; yes, also the fish of the sea shall be removed.  
**4:4** Yet let not a man strive with nor reprove a man. For your people <i>are</i> as those striving with a priest.  
**4:5** And you shall stumble in the day, and the prophet also shall stumble with you <i>at</i> night, and I will cut off your mother.  
**4:6** My people are cut off for lack of knowledge; because you rejected the knowledge, I also rejected you from being priest to Me. Since you have forgotten the Law of your God, I will forget your sons, even I.  
**4:7** As they were increased, so they sinned against Me. I will change their glory into shame.  
**4:8** They eat up the sin of My people, and they lift their soul to their iniquity.  
**4:9** And it will be, Like people, like priest. And I will visit their ways on them and their doings I will return to them.  
**4:10** For they shall eat and not have enough. They shall fornicate and not increase, because they have ceased heeding Jehovah.  
**4:11** Fornication and wine and new wine take away the heart.  
**4:12** My people seek advice by their wood<i>,</i> and their rod declares to them. For the spirit of fornication has led them astray, and they fornicated from under their God.  
**4:13** They sacrifice on the tops of the mountains and burn incense on the hills, under oak and poplar and terebinth <i>trees</i>, because <i>its</i> shade <i>is</i> good. On account of this, your daughters shall be prostitutes, and your brides shall commit adultery.  
**4:14** I will not punish your daughters when they fornicate, and not your brides when they commit adultery. For they themselves go apart with ones who prostitute, and they sacrifice with temple prostitutes. The people not understanding are thrust down.  
**4:15** Israel, though you fornicate, do not let Judah become guilty. And do not come <i>to</i> Gilgal, and not go up to Beth-aven, and not swear, <i>As</i> Jehovah lives.  
**4:16** For as a heifer, rebelling Israel rebels. Now Jehovah will feed them as a lamb in a roomy place.  
**4:17** Ephraim <i>is</i> joined to idols. Let him alone.  
**4:18** Their drink has turned them aside; fornicating they fornicate; her shields dearly love shame.  
**4:19** The wind binds her up in her wings, and they shall be ashamed because of their sacrifices.  

## 5장

**5:1** Hear this, O priests, and attend, house of Israel. And give ear, house of the king. For the judgment <i>is</i> toward you, because you have been a snare to Mizpeh, and a net spread on Tabor.  
**5:2** And revolters have gone deep <i>in</i> slaughtering, and I chasten all of them.  
**5:3** I know Ephraim, and Israel has not hidden from me. For now, O Ephraim, you have fornicated; Israel is defiled.  
**5:4** <i>They</i> will not give <i>up</i> their doings to turn back to their God; for the spirit of fornication <i>is</i> in their midst, and they do not know Jehovah.  
**5:5** And the pride of Israel answers to his face. So Israel and Ephraim shall stumble in their iniquity; Judah shall also stumble with them.  
**5:6** They shall go with their flocks and with their herds to seek Jehovah, but they shall not find <i>Him</i>; He has withdrawn Himself from them.  
**5:7** They have acted deceitfully against Jehovah, for they have borne strange sons. Now a new moon shall devour them with their portions.  
**5:8** Blow a rams' horn in Gibeah, a trumpet in Ramah. Cry aloud, Beth-aven; after you, O Benjamin.  
**5:9** Ephraim shall become a waste in the day of correction. Among the tribes of Israel, I have made known that which is confirmed.  
**5:10** The rulers of Judah were as <i>ones</i> removing a border; I will pour out My wrath on them like water.  
**5:11** Ephraim <i>is</i> oppressed, crushed in judgment; because he was pleased, he walked after the command.  
**5:12** And I <i>am</i> as a moth to Ephraim, and to the house of Judah as rottenness.  
**5:13** When Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah his wound, then Ephraim went to Assyria, and sent to King Jareb. And he was not able to heal you, and not did he cure you of your wound.  
**5:14** For I <i>am</i> to Ephraim as the lion, and as the young lion to the house of Judah; I, <i>even</i> I, tear and go; I bear away, and no one rescues.  
**5:15** I will go; I will return to My place until they <i>confess</i> their guilt and seek My face. In the oppressing <i>one</i> they will seek Me diligently.  

## 6장

**6:1** Come and let us return to Jehovah. For He has torn, and He will heal us. He has stricken, and He will bind us up.  
**6:2** After two days He will bring us to life. In the third day He will raise us up, and we shall live before Him.  
**6:3** And we shall know, we who follow on to know Jehovah. His going forth is prepared as the dawn. And He shall come to us as the rain, as the latter <i>and</i> former rain to the earth.  
**6:4** O Ephraim, what shall I do to you? O Judah, what shall I do to you? For your faithfulness <i>is</i> like a morning cloud, <i>and</i> it goes away like the early dew.  
**6:5** On account of this I have cut <i>them</i> down by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of My mouth; and your judgments <i>have been as</i> the light <i>that</i> goes forth.  
**6:6** For I desired faithfulness, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.  
**6:7** But, like Adam, they have passed over the covenant; they have acted deceitfully against Me there.  
**6:8** Gilead <i>is</i> a city of those who work iniquity, slippery from blood.  
**6:9** And as troops of robbers wait for a man, the company of priests murder <i>in</i> the way to Shechem; for they have done wickedness.  
**6:10** I have seen a horrible thing in the house of Israel: the fornication of Ephraim <i>is</i> there; Israel is defiled.  
**6:11** Also, O Judah, a harvest is appointed to you, when I return the captivity of My people.  

## 7장

**7:1** When I would have healed Israel, then the iniquity of Ephraim was uncovered, and the evil of Samaria. For they have worked falsehood; and a thief comes; a troop of robbers plunders outside.  
**7:2** And they do not say within their hearts <i>that</i> I remember all their evil. Now their own doings have turned about them; they are before My face.  
**7:3** They make the king glad with their evil, and the rulers with their lies.  
**7:4** They <i>are</i> all adulterers, like an oven heated by the baker; he ceases from stirring, from kneading the dough until it is leavened.  
**7:5** <i>In</i> the day of our king, the rulers have sickened themselves <i>with</i> the heat of wine; he draws out his hand with scorners.  
**7:6** For they have brought their heart near like an oven, while they lie in wait. Their baker sleeps all night; in the morning it burns like a fire of flame.  
**7:7** They are all hot as an oven, and devour their judges. All their kings have fallen; not one among them calls to Me.  
**7:8** Ephraim mixes himself among the peoples; Ephraim is a cake not turned.  
**7:9** Strangers have eaten up his strength, and he does not know. Yea, gray hairs are sprinkled on him, and he does not know.  
**7:10** And the pride of Israel testifies to his face, but they do not turn back to Jehovah their God, and do not seek Him in all this.  
**7:11** Ephraim also is like a silly dove without heart; they call <i>to</i> Egypt; they go <i>to</i> Assyria.  
**7:12** When they go, I will spread My net over them; I will bring them down like the birds of the heavens; I will chastise them, as a report to their congregation.  
**7:13** Woe to them! For they have fled from Me. Ruin to them! For they have transgressed against Me. Though I would redeem them, yet they have spoken lies against Me.  
**7:14** And they have not cried to Me with their heart when they howled on their beds. They gather themselves for grain and new wine; they turn against Me.  
**7:15** Though I chastened; I made their arms strong, and they think evil against Me.  
**7:16** They turn not to the <i>Most</i> High. They are like a treacherous bow. Their rulers shall fall by the sword from the rage of the tongue. This <i>shall be</i> their scorn in the land of Egypt.  

## 8장

**8:1** <i>Put</i> a rams' horn to your mouth. <i>He comes</i> like an eagle against the house of Jehovah, because they have passed over My covenant and have transgressed against My Law.  
**8:2** Israel shall cry to Me, My God, we know You.  
**8:3** Israel has cast off good; the enemy shall pursue him.  
**8:4** They have set up kings, but not by Me. They have made rulers, but I did not know. They made idols for themselves <i>with</i> their silver and their gold, so that they may be cut off.  
**8:5** O Samaria, your calf has cast <i>you</i> off. My anger is kindled against them. Until when will they not be able to <i>gain</i> innocency?  
**8:6** For it also <i>came</i> from Israel: The artisan made it, but <i>it is</i> not God. For the calf of Samaria shall be splinters.  
**8:7** For they sow the wind, and they reap the storm wind. A stalk is not to him; the bud shall make no flour. If it does make <i>it</i>, strangers will swallow it up.  
**8:8** Israel is swallowed up. They are now among the nations as a vessel in which is no pleasure.  
**8:9** For they have gone up to Assyria, a wild ass alone by himself. Ephraim has hired lovers.  
**8:10** Also, though they have hired among the nations, now I will gather them. And they began <i>to become</i> few, from the burden of the king of rulers.  
**8:11** Because Ephraim has made many altars to sin, they are altars to him to sin.  
**8:12** I have written the great things of My Law for him, <i>but</i> they were counted as being strange.  
**8:13** And <i>for</i> the sacrifices of My offerings, they sacrifice flesh, and they eat. Jehovah did not accept them. Now He will remember their iniquity and call their sins to account. They shall turn back <i>to</i> Egypt.  
**8:14** For Israel has forgotten his Maker, and builds temples. And Judah has multiplied fortified cities. But I will send a fire on his cities, and it shall burn up her palaces.  

## 9장

**9:1** O Israel, do not rejoice for joy, like the peoples. For you have fornicated away from your God. You have loved hire on all threshing-floors of grain.  
**9:2** The floor and the winepress shall not feed them, and the new wine shall fail in her.  
**9:3** They shall not live in the land of Jehovah, but Ephraim shall turn back to Egypt, and they shall eat unclean things in Assyria.  
**9:4** They shall not offer wine to Jehovah, and not shall be pleasing to Him. Their sacrifices <i>shall be</i> like the bread of sorrows to them; all who eat it shall be defiled, for their bread <i>is</i> for their person; it shall not come into the house of Jehovah.  
**9:5** What will you do in the day of meeting, and in the day of the feast of Jehovah?  
**9:6** For, behold, they have gone from destruction; Egypt shall gather them; Memphis shall bury them. Nettles shall possess the desirable things of their silver; thorns <i>shall be</i> in their tents.  
**9:7** The days of her calling to account have come; Israel shall know the days of her retribution. The prophet is a fool, the man of spirit <i>is</i> mad, for great <i>is</i> your iniquity and your hatred much.  
**9:8** Ephraim <i>is</i> a watchman with my God. The prophet <i>is</i> a snare of a fowler on all his ways; hatred <i>is</i> in the house of his God.  
**9:9** They have deeply corrupted, as <i>in</i> days of Gibeah. He will remember their iniquity; He will call their sins to account.  
**9:10** I found Israel like grapes in the wilderness. I saw your fathers as the firstfruit in the fig tree at its beginning. They came <i>to</i> Baal-peor and separated to shame. And they became detestable things like that which they loved.  
**9:11** As for Ephraim, their glory shall fly away like a bird; and from birth from the womb, and from conception.  
**9:12** Though they multiply their sons, yet I will make them childless, without a man. Yea, woe also to them when I turn away from them!  
**9:13** As when I looked toward Tyre, Ephraim was planted in a meadow. But Ephraim shall bring forth his sons to the slayer.  
**9:14** O Jehovah, give to them; what will you give? Give them a miscarrying womb and dry breasts.  
**9:15** All their evil <i>is</i> in Gilgal, for there I hated them. I will drive them out of My house for the evil of their doings, I will not add to love them; all their rulers <i>are</i> revolters.  
**9:16** Ephraim is stricken; their root is dried up; they shall bear no fruit. Yea, though they bear, yet I will kill the delight of their womb.  
**9:17** My God shall reject them because they did not listen to Him; and they shall be wanderers among the nations.  

## 10장

**10:1** Israel <i>is</i> a luxuriant vine; he bears fruit for himself. According to the plenty of his fruit he has increased the altars; as the goodness of the land they have made goodly pillars.  
**10:2** Their heart is divided. Now they shall be guilty. He will break down their altars; He will devastate their pillars.  
**10:3** For now they shall say, We have no king, because we did not fear Jehovah. What then should a king do for us?  
**10:4** They have spoken words, swearing vanity to cut a covenant. And judgment springs up like hemlock in the furrows of the field.  
**10:5** The people of Samaria shall dread because of the calves of Beth-aven. For its people shall mourn over it; also its priests <i>who</i> rejoiced on it for its glory, because it has departed from it.  
**10:6** It shall also be carried to Assyria, a present to King Jareb. Ephraim shall receive shame, and Israel shall be ashamed of his own counsel.  
**10:7** Samaria is cut off; her king is as a bough on the face of the water.  
**10:8** And the high places of Aven, the sin of Israel, shall be destroyed. The thorn and the thistle shall come up on their altars. And they shall say to the mountains, Cover us! and to the hills, Fall on us!  
**10:9** O Israel, you have sinned from the days of Gibeah. There they stood. The battle against the sons of iniquity did not overtake them in Gibeah.  
**10:10** When I desire, I shall bind them; and the peoples shall be gathered against them, when they bind themselves to their two perversities.  
**10:11** And Ephraim <i>is</i> a trained heifer, loving to tread out. But I passed over on the goodness of her neck; I will make Ephraim to ride; Judah shall plow; Jacob shall harrow for him.  
**10:12** Sow to yourselves in righteousness, reap as kindness. Break up your fallow ground. For <i>it is</i> time to seek Jehovah, until He comes and rains righteousness to you.  
**10:13** You have plowed wickedness; you have reaped iniquity. You have eaten the fruit of lies because you trusted in your way, in <i>the</i> multitude of your mighty <i>men</i>.  
**10:14** And an uproar shall rise up among your peoples. And all your strongholds shall be devastated as the violence by Shalman <i>of</i> Beth-arbel in the day of battle. The mother was dashed in pieces on <i>her</i> sons.  
**10:15** So He does to you, Bethel, because of your great evil. In the dawn, cutting the king of Israel shall be cut off.  

## 11장

**11:1** When Israel <i>was</i> a child, then I loved him, and I called My son out of Egypt.  
**11:2** As they called to them, so they went from their face. They sacrifice to the Baals, and burn incense to graven images.  
**11:3** And I taught Ephraim to walk; He took them on His arms. but they did not know that I healed them.  
**11:4** I drew them with cords of a man, with bands of love. And I was to them as those who lift off the yoke on their jaws; and I stretched out food to him.  
**11:5** He shall not turn back to the land of Egypt, but he of Assyria <i>shall be</i> his king, because they refused to turn back.  
**11:6** And the sword shall whirl in his cities, and shall bring to an end his bars, and shall devour, because of their own counsels.  
**11:7** And My people are bent <i>on</i> backsliding from Me. And they call him to the Most High; <i>they</i> would not at all exalt <i>Him</i>.  
**11:8** How shall I give you up, Ephraim? Shall I deliver you, Israel? How shall I make you like Admah? Shall I set you as Zeboim? My heart has turned within Me; My compassions are kindled together.  
**11:9** I will not execute the heat of My anger; I will not return to destroy Ephraim. For I <i>am</i> God, and not a man, the Holy One in your midst. And I will not enter into the city.  
**11:10** They shall walk after Jehovah; He shall roar as a lion. When He roars, even sons shall tremble from the west.  
**11:11** They tremble like a bird from Egypt, and like a dove from the land of Assyria. And I will make them live in their houses, says Jehovah.  
**11:12** Ephraim turns around Me with deception, and the house of Israel with deceit; but Judah still rules with God and is faithful with the holy <i>one</i>s.  

## 12장

**12:1** Ephraim feeds on wind, and follows after the east wind; all the day he multiplies lies and ruin. And they cut a covenant with Assyria, and oil is carried into Egypt.  
**12:2** And Jehovah has a quarrel with Judah, and will call Jacob to account according to his ways; He will repay to him according to his doings;  
**12:3** he took his brother by the heel in the womb, and by his strength he prevailed with God.  
**12:4** And he contended with the Angel, and overcame. <i>At</i> Beth-el he wept and pleaded to Him. And He finds us, and there He speaks with us,  
**12:5** even Jehovah, the God of Hosts. Jehovah <i>is</i> His memorial.  
**12:6** And therefore you return to your God; keep kindness and judgment, and call on your God continually.  
**12:7** <i>He is</i> a merchant; the scales of deceit <i>are</i> in his hand; he loves to oppress.  
**12:8** And Ephraim said, I am rich; I have found much wealth for myself. <i>In</i> all my labors they shall find in me no iniquity that <i>is</i> sin.  
**12:9** And I <i>am</i> Jehovah, your God from the land of Egypt; yet I will make you live in tents, as in the days of meeting.  
**12:10** And I have spoken by the prophets, and I have multiplied visions; and by the hand of the prophets I use similitudes.  
**12:11** <i>Is</i> Gilead evil? Surely they have been vanity. They sacrificed bulls in Gilgal; yea, their altars <i>are</i> as heaps in the furrows of the field.  
**12:12** And Jacob fled into the field of Syria, and Israel served for a wife; even he kept watch for a wife.  
**12:13** And by a prophet Jehovah brought up Israel out of Egypt, and by a prophet he was guarded.  
**12:14** Ephraim has provoked <i>Me</i> to anger most bitterly. And He shall leave his blood to him, and his Lord shall turn his reproach to him.  

## 13장

**13:1** When Ephraim spoke trembling, he lifted up <i>himself</i> in Israel. But he offended in Baal, and he died.  
**13:2** And now they sin more and more, and <i>they</i> have made themselves a molten image of their silver, idols according to their understanding, all of it the work of the craftsmen. They say of them, Men who sacrifice, let them kiss calves.  
**13:3** So they shall be as the morning cloud, and as the early dew that passes away, as chaff stormed away from the threshingfloor, and as the smoke out of the window.  
**13:4** And I <i>am</i> Jehovah, your God from the land of Egypt; and you shall not know other gods than Me. For there is not a Savior besides Me.  
**13:5** I have known you in the wilderness, in the land of droughts.  
**13:6** According to their pasturage, so they were sated. They were sated, and their heart was lifted up, therefore they have forgotten Me.  
**13:7** Now I am as a lion to them; I will watch by the way like a leopard.  
**13:8** I will meet them like a bereaved bear, and <i>I</i> will tear the lining of their heart; and as a lion, I will devour them there; the beast of the field shall tear them.  
**13:9** O Israel, you are destroyed, but your help <i>is</i> in Me.  
**13:10** Where <i>is</i> your king now, that he may save you in all your cities? And where are your judges of whom you said, Give to me a king and rulers?  
**13:11** I gave to you a king in My anger, and take <i>him</i> away in My fury.  
**13:12** The iniquity of Ephraim is bound up; his sin <i>is</i> hidden.  
**13:13** The pangs of <i>one</i> giving birth shall come to him; he <i>is</i> not a wise son, for he cannot stand in the time of the breaking forth of sons.  
**13:14** I will ransom them from the hand of Sheol; I will redeem them from death. O death, where <i>are</i> your plagues? O Sheol, where <i>is</i> your ruin? Repentance is hidden from My eyes.  
**13:15** Though he is fruitful among brothers, an east wind shall come. The wind of Jehovah comes up from the wilderness, and his spring shall be ashamed, and his fountain shall be dried up. He shall plunder the treasure of all desirable vessels.  
**13:16** Samaria shall be desolate, for she has rebelled against her God. They shall fall by the sword, their infants shall be dashed in pieces, and their pregnant women shall be ripped up.  

## 14장

**14:1** O Israel, return to Jehovah your God, for you have fallen by your iniquity.  
**14:2** Take words with you and return to Jehovah. Say to Him, Lift up all iniquity and take <i>us for</i> good, and we will repay <i>with</i> the calves of our lips.  
**14:3** Assyria shall not save us; we will not ride on horses. We shall not say any more to the work of our hands, Our gods! For in You the fatherless finds mercy.  
**14:4** I will heal their backslidings; I will love them freely; for My anger has turned away from him.  
**14:5** I will be as the dew to Israel; he shall blossom as the lily and cast out his roots like Lebanon.  
**14:6** His branches shall go out, and he shall be like the olive tree in his beauty, and his scent as Lebanon to him.  
**14:7** They who live under his shadow shall return; they shall live <i>like</i> the grain, and blossom like the vine; their memorial <i>shall be</i> as the wine of Lebanon.  
**14:8** Ephraim <i>shall say</i>, What <i>is it</i> to me any more with idols? I answered <i>him</i>, and I regarded him. I <i>am</i> as a green cypress tree; your fruit is found from Me.  
**14:9** Who <i>is</i> wise and discerns these things? <i>Who is</i> discerning and knows them? For the ways of Jehovah are right, and the righteous <i>one</i> shall walk in them; but <i>those</i> transgressing shall stumble in them.
