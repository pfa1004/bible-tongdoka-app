## 1장

**1:1** The words of Amos, who was among the herdsmen of Tekoa, which he saw concerning Israel in the days of Uzziah, king of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam, the son of Joash, king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.  
**1:2** And he said: Jehovah will roar from Zion and give His voice from Jerusalem. And the pastures of the shepherds shall mourn, and the top of Carmel shall wither.  
**1:3** So says Jehovah: For three transgressions of Damascus, and for four, I will not turn back <i>from</i> it, for they have threshed Gilead with threshing sledges of iron.  
**1:4** But I will send a fire against the house of Hazael, and it shall devour the palaces of Ben-hadad.  
**1:5** I will also break the bar of Damascus, and cut off the <i>one</i> living from the Valley of Aven, and him who holds the scepter from Beth-eden. And the people of Syria shall go captive to Kir, says Jehovah.  
**1:6** So says Jehovah: For three transgressions of Gaza, and for four, I will not turn back <i>from</i> it, for they have carried into exile, to deliver up a complete <i>population</i> to Edom.  
**1:7** But I will send a fire against the wall of Gaza, and it shall devour its palaces.  
**1:8** And I will cut off the ones living <i>in</i> Ashdod and him who holds the scepter from Ashkelon; and I will turn My hand against Ekron; and the remnant of the Philistines shall perish, says the Lord Jehovah.  
**1:9** So says Jehovah: For three transgressions of Tyre, and for four, I will not turn back <i>from</i> it, for they shut up <i>into</i> exile a complete <i>population</i> to Edom and did not remember the covenant of brothers.  
**1:10** But I will send a fire against the wall of Tyre, and it shall devour its palaces.  
**1:11** So says Jehovah: For three transgressions of Edom, and for four, I will not turn back <i>from</i> it, for he pursued his brother with the sword, and corrupted his compassions, and his anger has torn perpetually, and he kept it, his wrath, forever.  
**1:12** But I will send a fire against Teman, and it shall devour the palaces of Bozrah.  
**1:13** So says Jehovah: For three transgressions of the sons of Ammon, and for four, I will not turn back <i>from</i> it, for they ripped open the pregnant <i>women</i> of Gilead, to make their border larger.  
**1:14** And I will kindle a fire against the wall of Rabbah, and it shall devour its palaces, with a war cry in the day of battle, with a tempest in the day of the windstorm.  
**1:15** And their king shall go into exile, he and his rulers together, says Jehovah.  

## 2장

**2:2** And I will send a fire against Moab, and it shall devour the palaces of Kerioth. And Moab shall die with uproar, with a call <i>to arms</i>, with the sound of the rams' horn.  
**2:3** And I will cut off he who judges from its midst, and <i>I</i> will kill all his rulers with him, says Jehovah.  
**2:4** So says Jehovah: For three transgressions of Judah, and for four, I will not turn back <i>from</i> it; for they rejected the Law of Jehovah, and they have not kept His statutes. And their lies after which their fathers walked led them astray.  
**2:5** And I shall send a fire against Judah, and it shall devour the palaces of Jerusalem.  
**2:6** So says Jehovah: For three transgressions of Israel, and for four, I will not turn back <i>from</i> it, for they sold the righteous <e><i> </e>one</i> for silver, and the needy <i>one</i> for a pair of sandals,  
**2:7** panting for the dust of the earth <i>to be</i> on the head of the poor <i>one</i>, and stretch out the way of the humble <i>one</i>. And a man and his father will go <i>in</i> to the <i>same</i> girl, in order to profane the name of My holiness.  
**2:8** And they will stretch out beside every altar, and on garments taken in pledge. And they will drink wine of those being fined <i>in</i> the house of God.  
**2:9** Yet I destroyed the Amorite from before them, whose height <i>was</i> like the height of the cedars, and he <i>was</i> strong as the great trees. And I destroyed his fruit from above and his roots from below.  
**2:10** Also I brought you up from the land of Egypt and led you in the wilderness forty years, to possess the land of the Amorite.  
**2:11** And I raised up from your sons for prophets, and for Nazarites from your young men. <i>Is it</i> not even this, O sons of Israel? a statement of Jehovah.  
**2:12** And you gave the Nazarites wine to drink, and you commanded the prophets, saying, Do not prophesy.  
**2:13** Behold, I <i>am</i> pressed under you, as <i>is</i> pressed the cart <i>with</i> the fullness <i>of</i> cut grain <i>in</i> it.  
**2:14** And refuge shall perish from the swift, and the strong <i>one</i> shall not strengthen his power, and not shall the mighty <i>one</i> save his life,  
**2:15** and he who handles the bow shall not stand, and the <i>one</i> swift with his feet shall not save, and he who rides the horse shall not save his life.  
**2:16** And the strong of heart among the mighty <i>ones</i> shall run away naked <i>in</i> that day, a statement of Jehovah.  

## 3장

**3:1** Hear this Word that Jehovah has spoken against you, sons of Israel, against all the family which I brought up from the land of Egypt, saying:  
**3:2** You only have I known of all the families of the earth. On account of this I will call all your iniquities to account.  
**3:3** Will two walk together except they are agreed?  
**3:4** Will a lion roar in the forest when there is no prey for him? Will a young lion cry out of his den except he has caught <i>prey</i>?  
**3:5** Will a bird fall into a trap <i>on</i> the ground, and there is no bait for it? Will a trap spring up from the ground, and it catches nothing at all?  
**3:6** If a rams' horn is blown in a city, will the people not tremble? And if there is a calamity in a city and Jehovah has not done <i>it</i>?  
**3:7** For the Lord Jehovah will do nothing unless He reveals His <i>secret</i> counsel to His servants the prophets.  
**3:8** A lion has roared, who will not fear? The Lord Jehovah has spoken, who will not prophesy?  
**3:9** Make <i>it</i> heard at the palaces in Ashdod, and at the palaces in the land of Egypt, and say, Gather yourselves on the mountains of Samaria and see many tumults in its midst, and oppressions in its midst.  
**3:10** For they do not know to do right, a statement of Jehovah, those storing up violence and destruction in their palaces.  
**3:11** So thus says the Lord Jehovah: An adversary, even round about the land! And he shall bring you down from your fortress, and your palaces shall be plundered.  
**3:12** So says Jehovah, As the shepherd snatches two legs out of the mouth of the lion, or a piece of an ear, so shall the sons of Israel be saved, those who dwell in Samaria in a corner of a bed, and in Damascus <i>on</i> a couch.  
**3:13** Hear and testify in the house of Jacob, a statement of the Lord Jehovah, the God of Hosts.  
**3:14** For in the day I call to account the transgressions of Israel on him, I will also call to account on the altars of Bethel. And the horns of the altar will be cut off and will fall to the ground.  
**3:15** And I will strike the winter house with the summer house; and the houses of ivory shall perish, and the great houses shall be swept away, a statement of Jehovah.

**4:3** And you shall go out <i>at</i> the breaches, <i>each</i> woman before her. And you shall throw down the high place, a statement of Jehovah.  
**4:4** Enter Beth-el and transgress; multiply transgressing <i>at</i> Gilgal. And bring your sacrifices for the morning, your tithes for three days;  
**4:5** and offer a sacrifice of a thanksgiving offering <i>that is</i> leavened. And cry out, call out the voluntary offerings; hear <i>them</i>! For so you love <i>to do</i>, sons of Israel, a statement of the Lord Jehovah.  
**4:6** And I also have given you cleanness of teeth in all your cities, and lack of bread in all your places; and you have not returned to Me, a statement of Jehovah.  
**4:7** And I have also withheld the rain from you when <i>it was</i> yet three months to the harvest. And I caused rain to fall on one city, and caused it not to rain on another city. One portion was rained on, and the portion where it did not rain was withered.  
**4:8** And two <i>or</i> three cities staggered to one city in order to drink water, but they were not satisfied; yet you have not returned to Me, a statement of Jehovah.  
**4:9** I have struck you with scorching and mildew. The multitude of your gardens and your vineyards, and your figs, and your olives <i>are</i> devoured by the creeping locust; yet you have not returned to Me, a statement of Jehovah.  
**4:10** I have sent a plague among you in the way of Egypt; I have killed your young men with the sword and your horses with captivity. And I have made the stench of your camps to come up even into your nostrils; yet you have not returned to Me, a statement of Jehovah.  
**4:11** I have overturned among you, as God overturned Sodom and Gomorrah, and you were like a firebrand snatched out of the burning; yet you have not returned to Me, a statement of Jehovah.  
**4:12** Therefore thus I will do to you, O Israel: Because of this <i>that</i> I will do to you, prepare to meet your God, O Israel.  
**4:13** For, behold, He who forms mountains and creates the wind and declares to man what His thought <i>is</i>, He who makes the dawn darkness, and treads on the high places of the earth; Jehovah, the God of Hosts, <i>is</i> His name.  

## 5장

**5:1** Hear this word which I am lifting up against you, a dirge, O house of Israel.  
**5:2** The virgin of Israel has fallen, and will not rise up again; she lies forsaken on her land; there is no one raising her up.  
**5:3** For so says the Lord Jehovah, The city that goes out <i>by</i> a thousand shall have a hundred left. And that which goes out <i>by</i> a hundred shall have ten left in the house of Israel.  
**5:4** For so says Jehovah to the house of Israel, Seek Me, and live.  
**5:5** But do not seek Beth-el, and do not enter Gilgal, and do not cross over to Beersheba; for going Gilgal shall go into exile, and Beth-el shall be for nothing.  
**5:6** Seek Jehovah, and live, lest He rush like a fire <i>on</i> the house of Joseph, and devour <i>it</i>, and no one is quenching it for Bethel.  
**5:7** He abandoned those who turn judgment into wormwood, and righteousness to the earth.  
**5:8** He who created the Pleiades and Orion, and turns the deep darkness into the morning, and darkens the day <i>into</i> night; who calls for the waters of the sea and pours them out on the face of the earth, Jehovah <i>is</i> His name;  
**5:9** who flashes out with destruction on the strong <i>one</i>, and destruction comes on the fortress.  
**5:10** They hate him who reproves in the gate, and they detest him who speaks uprightly.  
**5:11** So, because of your trampling on the poor <i>ones</i>, and you take tribute of grain from him, you have built houses of hewn stones, but you shall not dwell in them; you have planted desirable vineyards, but you shall not drink their wine.  
**5:12** For I know your transgressions <i>are many</i>, and numerous <i>are</i> your sins: oppressing the righteous <i>ones</i>, taking of a bribe, and turning aside the needy <i>ones</i> in the gate.  
**5:13** So he acting wisely shall keep silent in that time, for it <i>is</i> an evil time.  
**5:14** Seek good and not evil, that you may live; and if it be so Jehovah the God of Hosts <i>will be</i> with you, as you have said.  
**5:15** Hate evil, and love good, and set up judgment in the gate. It may be Jehovah the God of Hosts will be gracious <i>to</i> the remnant of Joseph.  
**5:16** So Jehovah, the God of Hosts, the Lord, says this: Wailing <i>shall be</i> in all open squares, and they shall say in all the outside <i>places</i>, Alas! Alas! And they shall call the farmer to the mourning, and those knowing wailing to lamentation.  
**5:17** And wailing <i>shall be</i> in all vineyards, for I will pass in your midst, says Jehovah.  
**5:18** Woe to those desiring the day of Jehovah! What <i>is</i> this for you? The day of Jehovah <i>is</i> darkness, and not light.  
**5:19** As <i>when</i> a man fled before a lion, and the bear met him. Or he goes into the house and leans his hand against the wall, and a snake bites him.  
**5:20** <i>Is</i> not the day of Jehovah darkness, and not light; even very dark, and not any brightness in it?  
**5:21** I hate, I reject your feast days; and I will not delight in your solemn assemblies.  
**5:22** For though you offer Me burnt offerings and your food offerings, I will not be pleased; and not will I regard the peace offerings of your fat animals.  
**5:23** Take away from Me the noise of your songs; and I will not hear the melody of your stringed instruments.  
**5:24** But let judgment roll down like waters, and righteousness like an ever-flowing torrent.  
**5:25** Did you bring near sacrifices and food offerings to Me forty years in the wilderness, O house of Israel?  
**5:26** Yea, you have lifted up the booth of your king, and Kiyyun, your images, the star of your gods which you made for yourselves!  
**5:27** And I will exile you beyond Damascus, says Jehovah; the God of Hosts <i>is</i> His name.

**7:1** The Lord Jehovah made me see this: And, behold, He is forming locusts at the beginning of the coming up of the after growth; even behold, the after growth after the mowings of the king.  
**7:2** And it happened when it had made an end of eating the green plant of the land, even I said, Lord Jehovah, I pray, forgive. How can Jacob rise up? For he <i>is</i> small.  
**7:3** Jehovah was moved to pity concerning this: It shall not be, says Jehovah.  
**7:4** The Lord Jehovah made me to see this: And, behold, the Lord Jehovah was calling to contend by fire. And it consumed the great deep, and <i>it was</i> devouring part <i>of it</i>.  
**7:5** And I said, Lord Jehovah, I pray, stop. How can Jacob rise up? For he <i>is</i> small.  
**7:6** Jehovah was moved to pity concerning this: Also it shall not be, says the Lord Jehovah.  
**7:7** He made me see this: And, behold, the Lord was standing by the plumb line wall, and a plumb line in His hand.  
**7:8** And Jehovah said to me, Amos, what are you seeing? And I said, A plumb line. And the Lord said, Behold, I will set a plumb line in the midst of My people Israel. I will not again pass over him any more.  
**7:9** And the high places of Isaac shall be desolated, and the holy places of Israel shall be laid waste; and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword.  
**7:10** And Amaziah, the priest of Bethel, sent to Jeroboam, the king of Israel, saying, Amos has conspired against you in the midst of the house of Israel. The land is not able to endure all his words.  
**7:11** For so Amos says: Jeroboam shall die by the sword, and going Israel shall go into exile out of his land.  
**7:12** And Amaziah said to Amos, Seer, go! Flee for yourself into the land of Judah, and eat bread there, and prophesy there.  
**7:13** But do not add to prophesy at Bethel any more, for it is the king's holy place, and it <i>is</i> the royal house.  
**7:14** Then Amos answered and said to Amaziah, I <i>was</i> not a prophet, and not <i>was</i> I a prophet's son, but I <i>was</i> a herdsman and one tending sycamore trees.  
**7:15** And Jehovah took me from behind the flock, and Jehovah said to me, Go, prophesy to My people Israel.  
**7:16** And now hear the Word of Jehovah: You are saying, Do not prophesy against Israel, and do not drop <i>words</i> against the house of Isaac.  
**7:17** So thus says Jehovah, Your wife shall be a prostitute in the city, and your sons and your daughters shall fall by the sword, and your land shall be divided by a line. And you shall die in a defiled land. And going Israel shall go into exile from his land.
