# 42.누가복음 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** Since many have undertaken to draw up in order an account concerning the matters having been borne out among us,  
**1:2** even as the <i>ones</i> from <i>the</i> beginning gave over to us, becoming eye-witnesses and ministers of the Word,  
**1:3** it seemed good also to me, having traced out all things accurately from the first, to write in order to you, most excellent Theophilus,  
**1:4** that you may know the certainty concerning the words which you were taught.  
**1:5** In the days of Herod the king of Judea, there was a certain priest named Zacharias, of <i>the</i> daily course of Abijah. And his wife <i>was</i> of the daughters of Aaron, and her name <i>was</i> Elizabeth.  
**1:6** And they were both righteous before God, walking in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord, blameless .  
**1:7** And no child was <i>born</i> to them, because Elizabeth was barren. And both were advanced in age in their days.  
**1:8** And it happened in his serving as priest in the order of his course before God,  
**1:9** according to the custom of the priests, entering into the Holy Place of the Lord, <i>it was Zacharias'</i> lot to burn incense.  
**1:10** And all the multitude of the people was praying outside at the hour of incense.  
**1:11** And an angel <i>of the</i> Lord appeared to him, standing on <i>the</i> right of the altar of incense.  
**1:12** And seeing <i>this</i>, Zacharias was troubled, and fear fell on him.  
**1:13** But the angel said to him, Do not fear, Zacharias, because your prayer was heard, and your wife Elizabeth will bear a son to you, and you shall call his name John.  
**1:14** And he will be joy and exultation to you, and many will rejoice over his birth.  
**1:15** For he shall be great in the eyes of the Lord, and he shall not drink wine or strong drink. And he will be filled of <i>the</i> Holy Spirit, even from his mother's womb.  
**1:16** And he will turn many of the sons of Israel to <i>the</i> Lord their God.  
**1:17** And he will go ahead before Him in <i>the</i> spirit and power of Elijah "to turn <i>the</i> hearts of fathers to <i>their</i> children," and disobedient ones to <i>the</i> wisdom of <i>the</i> just, to make ready a people having been prepared for <i>the</i> Lord. <n>Mal 4:5</n>, 6  
**1:18** And Zacharias said to the angel, By what shall I know this? For I am an old man and my wife is advanced in her days.  
**1:19** And answering, the angel said to him, I am Gabriel, who stands before God, and I was sent to speak to you and to announce to you <i>the</i> good news of these things.  
**1:20** And behold, you shall be silent and not able to speak until the day these things take place, because you did not believe my words which shall be fulfilled in their season.  
**1:21** And the people were expecting Zacharias, and they wondered at his delay in the temple.  
**1:22** But coming out, he was not able to speak to them, and they recognized that he had seen a vision in the temple. And he was making signs to them and remained mute.  
**1:23** And it happened when the days of his service were fulfilled, he went away to his house.  
**1:24** And after these days his wife Elizabeth conceived. And she hid herself five months, saying,  
**1:25** So has the Lord done to me in <i>the</i> days in which He looked on <i>me</i> to take away my reproach among men.  
**1:26** And in the sixth month, the angel Gabriel was sent by God to a city of Galilee named Nazareth,  
**1:27** to a virgin who had been betrothed to a man whose name was Joseph, of <i>the</i> house of David; and the virgin's name <i>was</i> Mariam <i>(Mary)</i>.  
**1:28** And entering, the angel said to her, Rejoice <i>Mary</i>, <i>one</i> having received grace! The Lord <i>is</i> with you. You <i>are</i> blessed among women!  
**1:29** And seeing <i>this</i>, she was disturbed at his word, and considered what kind of greeting this might be.  
**1:30** And the angel said to her, Fear nor, Mariam (<i>Mary</i>), for you have found grace from God.  
**1:31** And behold! You will conceive in <i>your</i> womb and bear a Son, and you will call His name Jesus.  
**1:32** This One will be great and will be called Son of <i>the</i> Most High. And the Lord God will give Him the throne of His father David.  
**1:33** And He will reign over the house of Jacob to the ages, and of His kingdom there will be no end.  
**1:34** But Mariam (<i>Mary</i>) said to the angel, How will this be since I do not know a man?  
**1:35** And answering, the angel said to her, The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and <i>the</i> power of <i>the</i> Most High will overshadow you, and on account of this the Holy <i>One</i> being born of you will be called Son of God.  
**1:36** And behold, your kinswoman Elizabeth! She also conceived a son in her old age, and this <i>is</i> the sixth month to her who <i>was</i> called barren;  
**1:37** for not will be impossible with God every thing.  
**1:38** And Mariam (<i>Mary</i>) said, Behold, the slave woman of <i>the</i> Lord! May it be to me according to your word. And the angel departed from her.  
**1:39** And rising up in these days, Mariam <i>(Mary)</i> went into the hill country with haste to a city of Judah.  
**1:40** And <i>she</i> entered into the house of Zacharias and greeted Elizabeth.  
**1:41** And it happened, as Elizabeth heard Mariam's (<i>Mary's</i>) greeting, the babe in her womb leaped, and Elizabeth was filled of the Holy Spirit.  
**1:42** And <i>she</i> cried out with a loud voice and said, Blessed <i>are</i> you among women and blessed <i>is</i> the fruit of your womb!  
**1:43** And why <i>is</i> this to me that the mother of my Lord comes to me?  
**1:44** For behold, as the sound of your greeting came to my ears, the babe in my womb leaped in exultation.  
**1:45** And blessed <i>is</i> she believing, because there will be a completion to the things spoken to her from <i>the</i> Lord.  
**1:46** And Mariam (<i>Mary</i>) said, My soul magnifies the Lord,  
**1:47** and my spirit exulted in God my Savior.  
**1:48** For He looked upon the lowliness of His slave woman. For, behold, from now on all generations will count me blessed. <n>1Sam 1:11</n>  
**1:49** For the Mighty One did great things to me, and holy <i>is</i> His name.  
**1:50** And His mercy <i>is</i> to generations of generations to those fearing Him. <n>Psa 103:17</n>  
**1:51** He performed mightily with His arm; He scattered proud ones <i>in the</i> thought of their heart.  
**1:52** He put down powerful ones from thrones, and lifted up lowly ones.  
**1:53** He filled <i>the</i> hungry with good things, and He sent <i>the</i> rich away empty.  
**1:54** He helped His servant Israel in order to remember mercy,  
**1:55** even as He spoke to our fathers, to Abraham, and to his seed to the age.  
**1:56** And Mariam (<i>Mary)</i> remained with her about three months, and returned to her house.  
**1:57** And the time was fulfilled to Elizabeth <i>for</i> her to bear, and she bore a son.  
**1:58** And the neighbors and her relatives heard that <i>the</i> Lord magnified His mercy with her, and they rejoiced with her.  
**1:59** And it happened on the eighth day, they came to circumcise the child and were calling it by his father's name, Zacharias.  
**1:60** And his mother answered, saying, Not so, but he shall be called John.  
**1:61** And they said to her, No one is among your kindred who is called by this name.  
**1:62** And they signaled to his father, what he might desire him to be called.  
**1:63** And asking for a writing tablet, he wrote, saying, John is his name. And all marveled.  
**1:64** And instantly his mouth was opened and his tongue <i>loosed</i>, and he spoke, blessing God.  
**1:65** And fear came on all those living around them. And in all the hill country of Judea all these things were talked about.  
**1:66** And all who heard laid <i>them</i> up in their hearts, saying, What then will this child be? And the hand of <i>the</i> Lord was with him.  
**1:67** And his father Zacharias was filled of <i>the</i> Holy Spirit and prophesied, saying,  
**1:68** Blessed be <i>the</i> Lord, the God of Israel, because He visited and worked redemption for His people.  
**1:69** And <i>He</i> raised up a Horn of salvation for us in the house of His servant David;  
**1:70** even as He spoke through the mouth of His holy prophets from the age <i>before</i>:  
**1:71** Salvation from the hostile <i>ones</i> of us, and from <i>the</i> hand of all the <i>ones</i> hating us,  
**1:72** to execute mercy with our fathers, and to remember His holy covenant,  
**1:73** <i>the</i> oath which He swore to our father Abraham, to give to us,  
**1:74** <i>that we</i> being delivered out of the hand of hostile <i>ones</i> of us , in order to serve Him without fear,  
**1:75** in consecration and righteousness before Him all the days of our life.  
**1:76** And you, child, will be called Prophet of <i>the</i> Most High, for you will go before <i>the</i> face of <i>the</i> Lord to prepare His ways, <n>Mal 3:1</n>  
**1:77** to give a knowledge of salvation to His people by remission of their sins,  
**1:78** through <i>the</i> tender bowels of mercy of our God, in which <i>the</i> Dayspring from on high will visit us,  
**1:79** to shine on those sitting in darkness and <i>in</i> shadow of death, to direct our feet into <i>the</i> way of peace. <n>Isa 9:2</n>  
**1:80** And the child grew, and became strong in spirit. And he was in deserted <i>places</i> until <i>the</i> day of his showing to Israel.  

## 2장

**2:1** And it happened in those days, a decree went out from Caesar Augustus <i>for</i> all the habitable world to be registered.  
**2:2** This registration first occurred <i>under</i> the governing of Syria <i>by</i> Cyrenius.  
**2:3** And all went to be registered, each to <i>his</i> own city.  
**2:4** And Joseph also went from Galilee, out of <i>the</i> city of Nazareth to Judea, to <i>the</i> city of David which is called Bethlehem, because of his being of <i>the</i> house and family of David,  
**2:5** to be registered with Mariam (<i>Mary</i>), the <i>one</i> having been betrothed to him as wife, <i>she</i> being pregnant.  
**2:6** And it happened as they were there, the days were fulfilled <i>for</i> her to bear.  
**2:7** And she bore her son, the First-born. And she wrapped Him and laid Him in the manger, because there was no place for them in the inn.  
**2:8** And shepherds were in the same country living in the fields and keeping guard over their flock <i>by</i> night.  
**2:9** And, behold, an angel of <i>the</i> Lord came on them. And <i>the</i> glory of <i>the</i> Lord shone around them. And they feared a great fear.  
**2:10** And the angel said to them, Do not fear. For, behold, I proclaim good news to you, a great joy, which will be to all people,  
**2:11** because today a Savior, who is Christ <i>the</i> Lord, was born to you in <i>the</i> city of David.  
**2:12** And this <i>is</i> the sign to you: You will find a babe having been wrapped, lying in the manger.  
**2:13** And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of <i>the</i> heavenly host, praising God and saying,  
**2:14** Glory to God in the highest, and peace on earth, good will among men.  
**2:15** And it happened as the angels departed from them into the heaven, even the men, the shepherds, said to one another, Indeed, let us go over to Bethlehem, and let us see this thing occurring, which the Lord made known to us.  
**2:16** And hurrying, they came and sought out both Mariam (<i>Mary</i>) and Joseph, and the Babe lying in the manger.  
**2:17** And seeing, they publicly told about the Word spoken to them about this Child.  
**2:18** And all those hearing marveled about the things spoken to them by the shepherds.  
**2:19** And Mariam (<i>Mary</i>) kept all these words, meditating in her heart.  
**2:20** And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all things which they heard and saw, even as was spoken to them.  
**2:21** And when eight days were fulfilled to circumcise the child His name was called Jesus, <i>the name</i> called by the angel before He was conceived in the womb.  
**2:22** And when were fulfilled the days of her cleansing according to the Law of Moses they took Him up to Jerusalem to present <i>Him</i> to the Lord,  
**2:23** as it has been written in <i>the</i> Law of the Lord: Every male opening a womb shall be called holy to the Lord; <n>Ex 13:2</n>  
**2:24** and to offer a sacrifice according to that said in the Law of <i>the</i> Lord, a pair of turtledoves, or two nestlings of doves. <n>Lev 12:8</n>  
**2:25** And behold, there was a man in Jerusalem whose name <i>was</i> Simeon. And this man <i>was</i> righteous and devout, eagerly expecting <i>the</i> Consolation of Israel. And <i>the</i> Holy Spirit was upon him.  
**2:26** And <i>it</i> was <i>said</i> to him, having been alerted by the Holy Spirit, <i>that he was</i> not to see death before he would see <i>the</i> Christ of <i>the</i> Lord.  
**2:27** And by the Spirit he came into the temple. And <i>as</i> the parents <i>were</i> bringing in the child Jesus <i>for</i> them to do according to the custom of the Law concerning Him,  
**2:28** even <i>Simeon</i> received Him into his arms. And <i>he</i> blessed God and said,  
**2:29** Now, Master, You will let Your slave go in peace according to Your Word;  
**2:30** because my eyes saw Your Salvation,  
**2:31** which You prepared before <i>the</i> face of all the peoples;  
**2:32** a Light for revelation <i>to the</i> nations, and <i>the</i> Glory of Your people Israel.  
**2:33** And Joseph was marveling, also His mother, at the things being said concerning Him.  
**2:34** And Simeon blessed them and said to His mother Mariam (<i>Mary</i>), Behold, this One is set for <i>the</i> fall and rising up of many in Israel, and for a sign spoken against;  
**2:35** yea, a sword also will go through the soul of you, <i>your</i>self, so that <i>the</i> thoughts of many hearts may be revealed.  
**2:36** And there was Anna, a prophetess, a daughter of Phanuel, of <i>the</i> tribe of Asher. She was advanced in many days, having lived seven years with a husband from her virginity;  
**2:37** and she <i>was</i> a widow <i>of</i> eighty four years, who did not depart from the temple, serving night and day with fastings and prayers.  
**2:38** And coming on at the very hour, she gave thanks to the Lord, and spoke concerning Him to all those in Jerusalem eagerly expecting redemption.  
**2:39** And as they finished all things according to <i>the</i> Law of <i>the</i> Lord, they returned to Galilee, to Nazareth their city.  
**2:40** And the Child grew, and became strong in spirit, being filled with wisdom. And the grace of God was upon Him.  
**2:41** And His parents went into Jerusalem year by year at the Feast of the Passover.  
**2:42** And when He was twelve years <i>old</i>, they, going up to Jerusalem according to the custom of the Feast,  
**2:43** and fulfilling the days, in their returning, the boy Jesus stayed in Jerusalem. And Joseph and His mother did not know.  
**2:44** But supposing Him to be in the company, they went a day on the way. And they looked for Him among the relatives and friends.  
**2:45** And not finding Him, they returned to Jerusalem, looking for Him.  
**2:46** And it happened after three days they found Him in the temple sitting in the midst of the teachers, even hearing them and questioning them.  
**2:47** And all the <i>ones</i> hearing Him were amazed at His intelligence and His answers.  
**2:48** And seeing Him, they were astounded. And His mother said to Him, Child, why did You do so to us? Behold, Your father and I were looking for You, greatly distressed.  
**2:49** And He said to them, Why did you look for Me? Did you not know that I must be <i>busy</i> in the <i>affairs</i> of My Father?  
**2:50** And they did not understand the Word which He spoke to them.  
**2:51** And He went with them and came to Nazareth and was being subject to them. And His mother carefully kept all these words in her heart.  
**2:52** And Jesus progressed <i>in</i> wisdom and stature and favor before God and men.  

## 3장

**3:1** And in <i>the</i> fifteenth year of the government of Tiberius Caesar, Pontius Pilate governing Judea, and Herod ruling as tetrarch of Galilee, and his brother Philip ruling as tetrarch of Iturea and <i>the</i> Trachonitis country, and Lysanias ruling as tetrarch of Abilene,  
**3:2** upon <i>the</i> high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas, a word of God came on John the son of Zacharias in the deserted <i>place</i>.  
**3:3** And he came into the region around the Jordan proclaiming a baptism of repentance for remission of sins,  
**3:4** as it has been written in <i>the</i> scroll of the words of Isaiah the prophet, saying: "<i>the</i> voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare the way of <i>the</i> Lord, make His paths straight.  
**3:5** Every valley shall be filled up, and every mountain and hill shall be made low; and the crooked places shall be made into straight, and the rough into smooth ways;"  
**3:6** "and all flesh shall see the salvation of God." <i>Isaiah 40:3-5  
**3:7** Then he said to the crowds going out to be baptized by him, Offspring of vipers! Who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?  
**3:8** Then bring forth fruits worthy of repentance, and do not begin to say within yourselves, We have Abraham as father. For I say to you that God is able to raise up children to Abraham out of these stones.  
**3:9** And also the axe is already laid to the root of the trees; therefore, every tree not producing good fruit is being cut down and being thrown into the fire.  
**3:10** And the crowd asked him, saying, What then shall we do?  
**3:11** And answering, he says to them, The one that has two tunics, let him share with him that has not. And the one that has food, let him do the same.  
**3:12** And tax collectors also came to be baptized. And they said to him, Teacher, what shall we do?  
**3:13** And he said to them, Exact nothing more than that commanded to you.  
**3:14** And also ones serving as soldiers asked him, saying, And we, what shall we do? And he said to them, Do not forcibly extort anyone nor accuse falsely, and be satisfied with your wages.  
**3:15** But the people were expecting, and all reasoning in their hearts about John, lest perhaps he is the Christ,  
**3:16** John answered all, saying, I indeed baptize you with water; but He stronger than I comes, of whom I am not fit to loose the thong of His sandals. He will baptize you in <i>the</i> Holy Spirit and fire,  
**3:17** whose sifting fan <i>is</i> in His hand; and He will fully purge His threshing-floor, and will gather the wheat into His storehouse, but the chaff He will burn up with fire that cannot be put out.  
**3:18** And then indeed exhorting many different things, he preached the gospel to the people.  
**3:19** But Herod the tetrarch, being reproved by him concerning Herodias, the wife of his brother, Philip, and concerning all <i>the</i> evil things Herod did,  
**3:20** he also added this above all, he even shut up John in the prison.  
**3:21** And it happened, in the baptizing of all the people, Jesus also being baptized, and praying, the heaven was opened;  
**3:22** and the Holy Spirit came down in a bodily form as a dove upon Him. And there was a voice out of Heaven, saying, You are My Son, the Beloved; in You I am wellpleased. <n>Psa 2:7</n>; Gen 22:2; <n>Isa 42:1</n>  
**3:23** And Jesus Himself <i>was</i> beginning <i>to be</i> about thirty years <i>of age</i>, being, as was supposed, the son of Joseph, the <i>son</i> of Heli,  
**3:24** the <i>son</i> of Matthat, the <i>son</i> of Levi, the <i>son</i> of Melchi, the <i>son</i> of Janna, the <i>son</i> of Joseph,  
**3:25** the <i>son</i> of Mattathias, the <i>son</i> of Amos, the <i>son</i> of Nahum, the <i>son</i> of Esli, the <i>son</i> of Naggai,  
**3:26** the <i>son</i> of Maath, the <i>son</i> of Mattathias, the <i>son</i> of Semei, the <i>son</i> of Joseph, the <i>son</i> of Judah,  
**3:27** the <i>son</i> of Joannas, the <i>son</i> of Rhesa, the <i>son</i> of Zerubbabel, the <i>son</i> of Shealtiel, the <i>son</i> of Neri,  
**3:28** the <i>son</i> of Melchi, the <i>son</i> of Addi, the <i>son</i> of Cosam, the <i>son</i> of Elmodam, the <i>son</i> of Er,  
**3:29** the <i>son</i> of Joses, the <i>son</i> of Eliezer, the <i>son</i> of Jorim, the <i>son</i> of Matthat, the <i>son</i> of Levi,  
**3:30** the <i>son</i> of Simeon, the <i>son</i> of Judah, the <i>son</i> of Joseph, the <i>son</i> of Jonan, the <i>son</i> of Eliakim,  
**3:31** the <i>son</i> of Melea, the <i>son</i> of Menan, the <i>son</i> of Mattatha, the <i>son</i> of Nathan, the <i>son</i> of David,  
**3:32** the <i>son</i> of Jesse, the <i>son</i> of Obed, the <i>son</i> of Boaz, the <i>son</i> of Salmon, the <i>son</i> of Nahshon,  
**3:33** the <i>son</i> of Amminadab, the <i>son</i> of Aram, the <i>son</i> of Hezron, the <i>son</i> of Pharez, the <i>son</i> of Judah,  
**3:34** the <i>son</i> of Jacob, the <i>son</i> of Isaac, the <i>son</i> of Abraham, the <i>son</i> of Terah, the <i>son</i> of Nahor,  
**3:35** the <i>son</i> of Serug, the <i>son</i> of Reu, the <i>son</i> of Peleg, the <i>son</i> of Eber, the <i>son</i> of Salah,  
**3:36** the <i>son</i> of Cainan, the <i>son</i> of Arphaxad, the <i>son</i> of Shem, the <i>son</i> of Noah, the <i>son</i> of Lamech,  
**3:37** the <i>son</i> of Methuselah, the <i>son</i> of Enoch, the <i>son</i> of Jared, the <i>son</i> of Mahalaleel, the <i>son</i> of Cainan;  
**3:38** the <i>son</i> of Enos, the <i>son</i> of Seth, the <i>son</i> of Adam, the <i>son</i> of God.  

## 4장

**4:1** And full of the Holy Spirit, Jesus returned from the Jordan, and was led by the Spirit into the deserted <i>place</i>, forty days, being tried by the devil. And He ate nothing in those days, and they being ended, He afterwards hungered.  
**4:2** - - -  
**4:3** And the devil said to Him, If You are Son of God, speak to this stone that it become a loaf.  
**4:4** And Jesus answered to him, saying, It has been written: "Man shall not live on bread alone, but on every Word of God." <n>Deut 8:3</n>  
**4:5** And leading Him up into a high mountain, the Devil showed Him all the kingdoms of the world in a moment of time.  
**4:6** And the Devil said to Him, I will give all this authority and their glory to You, because it has been delivered to me, and I give it to whomever I wish.  
**4:7** Therefore if You worship before me, all will be Yours.  
**4:8** And answering, to him, Jesus said, Go behind me, Satan! For it has been written: "You shall worship <i>the</i> Lord your God, and Him only you shall serve." <n>Deut 6:13</n>  
**4:9** And he led Him to Jerusalem, and stood Him on the wing of the temple, and said to Him, If You are the Son of God, throw Yourself down from here;  
**4:10** for it has been written: "He will command His angels about You, even to protect You,"  
**4:11** and, "that on <i>their</i> hands they shall lift You up, that you not strike Your foot against a stone." <i>LXX-Psa. 90:11, 12; MT-Psa. 91:11, 12  
**4:12** And answering, Jesus said to him, It has been said: "You shall not tempt <i>the</i> Lord your God." <n>Deut 6:16</n>  
**4:13** And having finished every temptation, the devil departed from Him until a time.  
**4:14** And Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit to Galilee. And a report went out through all the surrounding region about Him.  
**4:15** And He taught in their synagogues, being glorified by all.  
**4:16** And He came to Nazareth where He was brought up. And as was His custom, He went in on the day of the sabbaths, into the synagogue, and <i>He</i> stood up to read.  
**4:17** And <i>the</i> scroll of Isaiah the prophet was handed to Him. And unrolling the scroll, He found the place where it was written:  
**4:18** "<i>The</i> Spirit of <i>the</i> Lord <i>is</i> upon Me. Because of this He anointed Me to proclaim the gospel to <i>the</i> poor <i>ones</i>; He has sent Me" to heal those being broken <i>in</i> heart, "to proclaim freedom to captives, and to the blind <i>ones</i> to see again," to send away in freedom <i>the ones</i> being crushed,  
**4:19** "to preach an acceptable year of <i>the</i> Lord." <n>Isa 61:1</n>, 2  
**4:20** And rolling up the scroll, returning to the attendant, He sat down. And the eyes of all in the synagogue were looking intently on Him.  
**4:21** And He began to say to them, Today this Scripture has been fulfilled in your ears.  
**4:22** And all bore witness to Him, and marveled at the gracious words coming out of His mouth. And they said, Is this not the son of Joseph?

**8:30** And Jesus asked him, saying, What is your name? And he said, Legion (because many demons entered into him).  
**8:31** And they implored Him that He not order them to go away into the abyss.  
**8:32** And there was a herd of many pigs feeding there in the mountain. And they implored Him that He would allow them to enter into those. And He allowed them.  
**8:33** And coming out from the man, the demons entered into the pigs; and the herd rushed down the precipice into the lake and was choked.  
**8:34** And seeing the thing having been done, the <i>ones</i> feeding <i>the pigs</i> fled. And leaving, <i>they</i> reported to the city and to the farms.  
**8:35** And they went out to see the thing happening, and came to Jesus. And <i>they</i> found the man from whom the demons had gone out, sitting at the feet of Jesus, clothed and of sound mind. And they were afraid.  
**8:36** And also the <i>ones</i> who had seen related to them how the <i>one</i> having been demon-possessed was healed.  
**8:37** And all the multitude of the surrounding region of the Gadarenes were seized with a great fear, and asked Him to depart from them. And entering into the boat, He returned.  
**8:38** And the man from whom the demons had gone out begged Him, <i>desiring</i> to be with Him. But Jesus sent him away, saying,

**8:40** And it happened <i>as</i> Jesus returned, the crowd gladly received Him, for they were all waiting for Him.  
**8:41** And, behold, a man named Jairus came, and this one was a synagogue ruler. And falling at the feet of Jesus, he begged Him to come into his house,  
**8:42** because an only daughter was born to him, about twelve years <i>of age</i>; and she was dying. And in His going, the crowds pressed on Him.  
**8:43** And a woman being in a flow of blood from twelve years, who had spent her whole living on physicians, <i>and</i> could not be cured by anyone,  
**8:44** coming up behind she touched the fringe of His garment. And instantly the flow of her blood stopped.  
**8:45** And Jesus said, Who <i>was</i> touching Me? And all denying <i>it</i>, Peter and the <i>ones</i> with Him said, Master, the crowds are pressing You together and are pressing hard. And do You say, Who <i>was</i> touching Me?  
**8:46** But Jesus said, Someone touched Me, for I knew power <i>was</i> going from Me.  
**8:47** And seeing that she was not hidden, the woman came trembling and falling down before Him she declared to Him before all the people for what reason she touched Him, and how she was instantly cured.  
**8:48** And He said to her, Daughter, be comforted. Your faith has healed you. Go in peace.  
**8:49** As He was yet speaking, someone came from the synagogue ruler's, saying to him, Your daughter has died. Do not trouble the Teacher.  
**8:50** But hearing, Jesus answered him, saying, Do not fear; only believe and she will be healed.  
**8:51** And coming into the house, He did not allow anyone to enter, except Peter and James and John, and the father and mother of the girl.  
**8:52** And all were weeping and bewailing her. But He said, Do not weep. She has not died, but is sleeping.  
**8:53** And they ridiculed Him, knowing that she died.  
**8:54** But putting all outside, and taking hold of her hand, He called out, saying, <i>Little</i> girl, arise!  
**8:55** And her spirit returned, and she rose up immediately. And He ordered that she be given <i>something</i> to eat .  
**8:56** And her parents were amazed. But He charged them to tell no one of that which occurred.  

## 9장

**9:2** And <i>He</i> sent them to proclaim the kingdom of God, and to heal the ones being sick.  
**9:3** And He said to them, Take nothing for the way, neither staffs, nor moneybags, nor bread, nor silver, nor each to have two tunics.  
**9:4** And into whatever house you enter, remain there, and go out from there.  
**9:5** And as many as may not receive you, going out from that city even shake off the dust from your feet, for a testimony against them.  
**9:6** And going out, they passed through the villages, having announced the gospel, and healing everywhere.  
**9:7** And Herod the tetrarch heard all the things happening by Him, and was perplexed, because of the saying by some that John had been raised from <i>the</i> dead,  
**9:8** and by some that Elijah had appeared. And others <i>said</i>, A prophet, one of the ancients rose again.  
**9:9** And Herod said, I beheaded John, but who is this about whom I hear such things? And he sought to see Him.  
**9:10** And having returned, the apostles told Him as many <i>things</i> as they did. And taking them, He went out privately to a deserted place of a city called Bethsaida.  
**9:11** But knowing <i>this</i>, the crowds followed Him. And having received them, He spoke to them about the kingdom of God. And He cured the <i>ones</i> having need of healing.  
**9:12** But the day began to decline. And coming up, the Twelve said to Him, Send away the crowd that going to the surrounding villages and farms they may lodge and find food, because here we are in a deserted place.  
**9:13** But He said to them, You give them to eat. But they said, <i>there</i> is not to us more than five loaves and two fish, unless going we buy food for all this people.  
**9:14** For they were about five thousand men. And He said to His disciples, Cause them to recline in groups, by fifties.  
**9:15** And they did so, and made all recline.  
**9:16** And taking the five loaves and the two fish, looking up to Heaven, He blessed them, and broke, and gave to the disciples to set before the crowd.  
**9:17** And they ate and were all filled. And twelve baskets of fragments of that left over to them were taken up.  
**9:18** And it happened <i>as</i> He was praying alone, the disciples were with Him. And He questioned them, saying, Whom do the crowds say Me to be?  
**9:19** And answering, they said, John the Baptist; and others, Elijah; and others that some prophet of the ancients has risen again.  
**9:20** And He said to them, But whom do you say Me to be? And answering, Peter said, The Christ of God.  
**9:21** And strictly warning them, He ordered no one to tell this,  
**9:22** saying, The Son of Man must suffer many things and be rejected by the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised the third day.  
**9:23** And He said to all, If anyone desires to come after Me, let him deny himself and take up his cross daily. And let him follow Me.  
**9:24** For whoever desires to save his life, he will lose it. But whoever loses his life for My sake, this one will save it.  
**9:25** For what is a man profited gaining the whole world but destroying himself or suffering loss?  
**9:26** For whoever is ashamed of Me and My words, the Son of Man will be ashamed of that one when He comes in His glory, and <i>that</i> of the Father, and of the holy angels.  
**9:27** But truly I say to you, There are some of those standing here who shall not taste of death until they see the kingdom of God, not <i>at all</i>.  
**9:28** And it was about eight days after these sayings, also taking Peter and John and James, He went up into the mountain to pray.  
**9:29** And in His praying, the appearance of His face <i>became</i> different, and His clothing <i>was</i> dazzling white.  
**9:30** And, behold, two men talked with Him, who were Moses and Elijah,  
**9:31** who, appearing in glory, spoke of His exodus, which He was about to filfill in Jerusalem.  
**9:32** But Peter and the <i>ones</i> with him were weighed down with sleep. But fully awakening, they saw His glory, and the two men standing with Him.  
**9:33** And it happened in their parting from Him, Peter said to Jesus, Master, it is good for <i>us</i> to be here. And, Let us make three tents, one for You, and one for Moses, and one for Elijah, not knowing what he said.  
**9:34** And he saying these things, a cloud came and overshadowed them. And they feared <i>as</i> they entered into the cloud.  
**9:35** And a voice came out of the cloud, saying, This is My Son, the Beloved; hear Him! <n>Psa 2:7; Isa 42:1; Deut 18:15</n>  
**9:36** And as the voice occurred, Jesus was found alone. And they were quiet. And <i>they</i> reported to no one in those days, nothing which they had seen.  
**9:37** And it happened on the next day, they coming down from the mountain, a large crowd met Him.  
**9:38** And, behold, a man called aloud from the crowd, saying, Teacher, I beg You, look upon my son, because he is my only born.  
**9:39** And, behold, a spirit takes him, and <i>he</i> suddenly cries out, and <i>it</i> throws him into convulsions, with foaming. And <i>it</i> departs from him with pain, bruising him.  
**9:40** And I begged Your disciples, that they cast it out. And they were not able.  
**9:41** And answering, Jesus said, O unbelieving generation, and being perverted, until when shall I be with you and endure you? Bring your son here.  
**9:42** But as <i>he</i> was yet coming near, the demon tore him and violently convulsed <i>him</i>. But Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit and healed the child, and gave him back to his father.  
**9:43** And all were astonished at the majesty of God. And as all were marveling at all things which He did, Jesus said to His disciples,  
**9:44** You lay into your ears these sayings, for the Son of Man is about to be given over into <i>the</i> hands of men.  
**9:45** But they did not understand this saying, and it was hidden from them so that they might not perceive it. And they feared to ask Him about this word.  
**9:46** But there arose among them a reasoning, who But might be <i>the</i> greater of them.  
**9:47** And seeing the reasoning of their heart, taking a child, Jesus stood it beside Himself,  
**9:48** and said to them, Whoever receives this child on My name receives Me. And whoever receives Me receives Him who sent Me. For the one being least among you all, this one shall be great.  
**9:49** And answering, John said, Master, we saw someone casting out demons on Your name, and we forbade him because he does not follow with us.  
**9:50** And Jesus said to them, Do not forbid <i>them</i>, for whoever is not against us is for us.  
**9:51** And it happened in the fulfilling <i>of</i> the days of His taking up, even He set His face to go to Jerusalem.  
**9:52** And <i>He</i> sent messengers before His face. And going they went into a village of Samaritans, so as to make ready for Him.  
**9:53** And they did not receive Him, because His face was going toward Jerusalem.  
**9:54** And seeing, His disciples, James and John, said Lord, do You desire <i>that</i> we tell fire to come down from Heaven, and to consume them even as Elijah did? <i>2 <n>K 1:10-12</n>  
**9:55** But turning He rebuked them. And He said, You do not know of what spirit you are.  
**9:56** For the Son of Man did not come to destroy men's souls, but to save. And they went to another village.  
**9:57** And it happened as they were going in the way, one said to Him, I will follow You wherever You may go, Lord.  
**9:58** And Jesus said to him, The foxes have holes, and the birds of the heaven nests, but the Son of Man has nowhere He may lay <i>His</i> head.  
**9:59** And He said to another, Follow Me. But he said, Lord, allow me to go first to bury my father.  
**9:60** But Jesus said to him, Leave the dead to bury their dead, but going out, you announce the kingdom of God.  
**9:61** And also another said, I will follow You, Lord, but first allow me to take leave of the <i>ones</i> in my house.  
**9:62** But Jesus said to him, No one putting his hand on the plow, and looking at the things behind, is fit for the kingdom of God.  

## 10장

**10:5** And into whatever house you may enter, first say, Peace to this house.  
**10:6** And if the son of peace is truly there, your peace shall rest on it; but if not so, it shall return to you.

**10:20** But do not rejoicing in this, that the <i>evil</i> spirits are subject to you but, rather rejoice that your names are written in Heaven.

**10:26** And He said to him, Which has been written in the Law? How do you read <i>it</i>?

**10:31** And by a coincidence, a certain priest was going on that road; and seeing him, he passed on the opposite <i>side</i>.  
**10:32** And in the same way, a Levite, also being at the place, coming and seeing <i>him</i>, he passed on the opposite <i>side</i>.  
**10:33** But a certain traveling Samaritan came upon him, and seeing him, he was filled with pity.  
**10:34** And coming near, <i>he</i> bound up his wounds, pouring on oil and putting him on his beast, <i>he</i> brought him to an inn and cared for him.  
**10:35** And going forth on the morrow, taking out two denarii, he gave <i>them</i> to the innkeeper, and said to him, Care for him, and whatever more you spend, on my return I will repay you.

**10:37** And he said, The <i>one</i> doing the <i>deed of</i> mercy with him. Then Jesus said to him, Go, and you do likewise.

**10:39** And to this <i>one</i> was a sister being called Mary, who also was sitting alongside, at the feet of Jesus, <i>and</i> heard His Word.  
**10:40** But Martha was distracted about much serving. And coming up she said, Lord, <i>is it</i> not a care to You that my sister left me alone to serve? Therefore tell her that she should help me.  
**10:41** But answering Jesus said to her, Martha, Martha, you are anxious and troubled about many things,  
**10:42** But there is need of <i>only</i> one; and Mary chose the good part, which shall not be taken from her.  

## 11장

**11:1** And it happened <i>as</i> He was praying in a certain place, when He ceased, one of His disciples said to Him, Lord teach us to pray, as John also taught his disciples.  
**11:2** And He said to them: When you pray, say, Our Father who <i>is</i> in Heaven, hallowed be Your name, let Your kingdom come, let Your will be done on earth as <i>it</i> also <i>is</i> in Heaven.  
**11:3** Give us our needful bread according to a day;  
**11:4** and forgive us our sins, for we ourselves also forgive everyone indebted to us. And bring us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil.  
**11:5** And He said to them, Who out of you shall have a friend, and will come to him at midnight and say to him, Friend, lend me three loaves.  
**11:6** <i>For</i> a friend of mine arrived to me from a journey, and I do not have what I may set before him.  
**11:7** And answering from within that one may say, Do not cause me troubles. The door has already been shut, and my children are in bed with me. I cannot rise up to give to you.  
**11:8** I say to you, Even if rising up he will not give to him because he is a friend, yet because of his shameless insisting, rising up he will give him as many as he needs.  
**11:9** And I say to you, Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you.  
**11:10** For everyone asking receives, and the <i>one</i> seeking finds, and to the one knocking, it will be opened.  
**11:11** And what father of you, <i>if</i> the son asks <i>for</i> bread, will he give him a stone? And if a fish, will he give him a snake instead of a fish?  
**11:12** And if he should ask an egg, will he give him a scorpion?  
**11:13** Then if you being evil know to give good gifts to your children, how much more the Father out of Heaven will give <i>the</i> Holy Spirit to the <i>ones</i> asking Him.  
**11:14** And He was casting out a demon, and it was mute <i>one</i>. And it happened as the demon <i>was</i> going out, the mute <i>one</i> spoke. And the crowds marveled.  
**11:15** But some of them said, He casts out the demons by Beelzebul the chief of the demons.  
**11:16** And tempting <i>Him</i>, others were seeking a <i>miraculous</i> sign from Heaven from Him.  
**11:17** But knowing their thoughts, He said to them, Every kingdom divided against itself is made a desolate <i>one</i>, and a house against a house falls.  
**11:18** And also if Satan is divided against himself, how shall his kingdom stand? Because you say I cast out the demons by Beelzebul.  
**11:19** And if I cast out the demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your sons cast out? Because of this they shall be your judges.  
**11:20** But if I cast out the demons by <i>the</i> finger of God, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.  
**11:21** When the strong <i>one</i>, having been armed, guards the dwelling of himself, in peace are the <i>things</i> belonging to him.  
**11:22** But as soon as the Stronger than he having come, <i>He</i> overcomes him; He takes away all his armor on which he trusted, and gives over his arms.  
**11:23** The <i>one</i> not being with Me is against Me. And the <i>one</i> not gathering with Me scatters.  
**11:24** When the unclean spirit goes out from the man, he goes through waterless places seeking rest. And not finding, <i>he</i> says, I will return to my house from where I came out.  
**11:25** And coming, he finds <i>it</i> having been swept and having been adorned.  
**11:26** Then he goes and takes along seven other spirits more wicked than himself, and entering he dwells there. And the last things of that man become worse than the first.  
**11:27** And it happened in His saying these things, lifting up <i>her</i> voice out of the crowd a certain woman said to Him, Blessed <i>is</i> the womb having borne You and <i>the</i> breasts which You sucked.  
**11:28** But He said, No; rather, blessed <i>are</i> the <i>ones</i> hearing the Word of God, and keeping it.  
**11:29** But the crowds pressing on <i>Him</i>, He began to say, This generation is evil. It seeks a <i>miraculous</i> sign, and a sign will not be given to it, except the sign of Jonah the prophet.  
**11:30** For even as Jonah became a <i>miraculous</i> sign to the Ninevites, so also the Son of Man will be to this generation.  
**11:31** <i>The</i> queen of <i>the</i> south will be raised in the Judgment with the men of this generation and will condemn them because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon. And, behold, a Greater-than-Solomon <i>is</i> here.  
**11:32** Men, Ninevites will rise up in the Judgment with this generation, and will condemn it, because they repented at the preaching of Jonah. And, behold, a Greater-than-Jonah <i>is</i> here.  
**11:33** But no one having lit a lamp places <i>it</i> in secret, nor under the peck-measure, but on the lampstand, that the <i>ones</i> entering may see the light.  
**11:34** The lamp of the body is the eye. Therefore when your eye is sound, also all your body is light. But when it is evil, also your body <i>is</i> dark.  
**11:35** Mark, therefore, that the light in you is not darkness.  
**11:36** If, then, your whole body <i>is</i> light, not having any part dark, all will be light, as when the lamp enlightens you with <i>its</i> shining.  
**11:37** And <i>as He</i> was speaking, a certain Pharisee asked Him that He would dine with him. And going in, He reclined.  
**11:38** But seeing, the Pharisee marveled that He did not first wash before dinner.  
**11:39** But the Lord said to him, Now you Pharisees cleanse the outside of the cup and of the dish, but your inside is full of robbery and evil.  
**11:40** Fools! Did not He who made the outside also make the inside?  
**11:41** But give merciful <i>gifts of</i> the things which are within, and see all things <i>are</i> clean to you.  
**11:42** But woe to you, Pharisees, for you pay tithes of the mint, and the rue, and every plant, and pass by the judgment and the love of God. It was right to do these things, but not to leave aside those.  
**11:43** Woe to you, Pharisees! For you love the chief seat in the synagogues and the greetings in the marketplaces.  
**11:44** Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees! Hypocrites! For you are as the unseen tombs, and the men walking over <i>them</i> do not know.  
**11:45** And answering, one of the lawyers said to Him, Teacher, saying these things You also insult us.  
**11:46** And He said, Woe to you also, lawyers! Because you burden men with burdens hard to bear, and <i>you</i> yourselves do not touch the burdens with one of your fingers.  
**11:47** Woe to you! Because you build the tombs of the prophets, and your fathers killed them.  
**11:48** So you bear witness and consent to the works of your fathers; for they indeed killed them, and you build their tombs.  
**11:49** And because of this, the wisdom of God said, I will send prophets and apostles to them, and they will kill and drive out <i>some</i> of them,  
**11:50** that the blood of all the prophets which has been shed from <i>the</i> foundation of the world may be required from this generation,  
**11:51** from the blood of Abel until the blood of Zechariah <i>who</i> perished between the altar and the House. Yea, I say to you, It will be required from this generation.  
**11:52** Woe to you, lawyers! Because you took the key of knowledge; you yourselves did not enter in, and you hindered the ones entering in.  
**11:53** And as He was saying these things to them, the scribes and the Pharisees began to be terribly angry, and to draw <i>Him</i> out concerning many things,  
**11:54** lying in ambush for Him, and seeking to catch something out of His mouth that they might accuse Him.  

## 12장

**12:1** At which time the myriads of the crowd being gathered together, so as to trample on one another, He began to say to His disciples first, Take heed to yourselves of the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy.  
**12:2** But there is nothing which has been completely concealed which will not be uncovered, nor hidden which will not be known.  
**12:3** Therefore, as many <i>things</i> as you said in the darkness will be heard in the light; and whatever you spoke in the ear in the secret rooms will be proclaimed on the housetops.  
**12:4** But I say to you, My friends, stop being afraid of the ones killing the body, and after these things not having anything more <i>they can</i> do.  
**12:5** But I will warn you whom you should fear; fear the One who after killing has authority to cast into hell ; yea, I say to you, Fear that One!  
**12:6** Are not five sparrows sold for two assaria? And not one of them has been forgotten before God.  
**12:7** But even the hairs of your head have all been numbered. Therefore stop being afraid; you differ from many sparrows.  
**12:8** But I say to you, Everyone who may confess Me before men, the Son of Man will also confess him before the angels of God.  
**12:9** But the <i>one</i> denying Me before man shall be denied before the angels of God.  
**12:10** And everyone who shall say a word against the Son of Man, it shall be forgiven him; but the <i>one</i> blaspheming against the Holy Spirit, <i>it</i> will not be forgiven.  
**12:11** But when they bring you in before synagogues and rulers and the authorities, do not be anxious how or what you shall reply, or what you should say;  
**12:12** for the Holy Spirit will teach you in the same hour what you must say.  
**12:13** And one from the crowd said to Him, Teacher, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me.  
**12:14** But He said to him, Man, who appointed Me a judge or a divider over you?  
**12:15** And He said to them, Beware, and be on guard from covetousness; for one's life is not in the abundance of the things belonging of him.  
**12:16** And He spoke a parable to them, saying of a man, The land of a certain rich man produced well.  
**12:17** And he reasoned within himself, saying, What should I do, for I have nowhere I may gather my fruits?  
**12:18** And he said, I will do this; I will tear down my storehouses and I will build larger; and I will gather there all my produce and my goods.  
**12:19** And I will say to my soul, Soul, you have many goods laid <i>up</i> for many years; take rest, eat, drink, <i>and</i> be glad.  
**12:20** But God said to him, Fool! This night they demand your soul from you; and that which you prepared, to whom will it be?  
**12:21** So <i>is</i> he treasuring up for himself, and not being rich toward God.  
**12:22** And He said to His disciples, Because of this I say to you, Do not be anxious as to your life, what you should eat; nor as to the body, what you should put on.  
**12:23** The life is more than food and the body than clothing.  
**12:24** Consider the ravens, for they do not sow, nor do they reap; to which there is no storeroom nor storehouse, and God feeds them. How much rather you differ from the birds!  
**12:25** And who of you <i>by</i> being anxious is able to add one cubit to his stature?  
**12:26** Then if you are not able <i>to do</i> even <i>the</i> least, why are you anxious about the rest?  
**12:27** Consider the lilies, how they grow: they do not labor, nor do they spin, but I say to you, Not even Solomon in all his glory was clothed as one of these.  
**12:28** But if God so dresses the grass, which today being in the field and tomorrow is thrown into the oven, how much more you, little-faiths?  
**12:29** And you, do not seek what you shall eat, or what you shall drink, and stop being in anxiety.  
**12:30** For all the nations of the world seek after these things, and your Father knows that you need these things.  
**12:31** But seek the kingdom of God and all these things will be added to you.  
**12:32** Do not fear, little flock, because your Father was pleased to give you the kingdom.  
**12:33** Sell the <i>things</i> belonging of you and give merciful <i>gifts</i>. Make for yourselves purses that do not grow old, an unfailing treasure in Heaven, where a thief cannot come near, nor moth can ruin.  
**12:34** For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.  
**12:35** Let your loins be girded about, and the lamps burning,  
**12:36** and you <i>be</i> like men awaiting their lord when he returns from the wedding feast, so that <i>he</i> coming and knocking, they will at once open to him.  
**12:37** Blessed <i>are</i> those slaves whom the lord will find awake when <i>he</i> comes. Truly I say to you that he will gird himself and will make them recline, and coming near <i>he</i> will serve them.  
**12:38** And if he comes in the second watch, or he comes in the third watch, and finds <i>it</i> so, blessed are those slaves.  
**12:39** But know this, that if the housemaster had known <i>in</i> what hour the thief is coming, he would have watched and would not have allowed his house to be dug through.  
**12:40** And therefore you be ready, for <i>in</i> which hour you think not the Son of man comes.  
**12:41** And Peter said to Him, Lord, do You speak this parable to us, or also to all?  
**12:42** And the Lord said, Who then is the faithful and wise steward whom the lord will appoint over his service, to give the portion of food in season?  
**12:43** Blessed <i>is</i> that slave <i>when</i> his lord comes <i>and</i> will find <i>him</i> so doing.  
**12:44** Truly I say to you, He will set him over all things belonging of him . .  
**12:45** But if that slave should say in his heart, My lord delays to come, and should begin to beat the men servants and the female servants, and to eat and to drink and be drunk,  
**12:46** the lord of that slave will come in the day in which he does not expect, and in an hour which he does not know. And He will cut him in two and will put his portion with the unbelievers.  
**12:47** But that slave knowing the will of his lord, and not preparing, nor doing according to his will, will be beaten with many <i>stripes</i>.  
**12:48** But he not knowing, and doing <i>things</i> worthy of stripes, will be beaten with few. And everyone given much, much will be demanded from him. And to whom much was committed, more exceedingly they will ask <i>of</i> him.  
**12:49** I came to hurl fire into the earth, and what will I if it already was kindled?  
**12:50** But I have an immersion to be immersed in, and how am I pressed until it is finished!  
**12:51** Think you that I came to give peace in the earth? No, I say to you, But rather division.  
**12:52** For from now on five in one house will have been divided, three against two, and two against three.  
**12:53** Father will be divided against son, and son against father; mother against the daughter, and daughter against mother; mother-in-law against the bride, "and <i>the</i> bride against her mother-in-law." <n>Mic 7:6</n>

**12:55** And when a south wind <i>is</i> blowing, you say, There will be heat; and it occurs.  
**12:56** Hypocrites! You know to discern the face of the earth and of the heaven, but how <i>is it</i> you do not discern this time?  
**12:57** And why do you not judge the right even of yourselves?  
**12:58** For as you go with your adversary to a magistrate, give pains in the way to be freed from him, that he not drag you to the judge, and the judge deliver you to the officer, and the officer throw you into prison.  
**12:59** I say to you, Not you will go out from there, not until you pay back even the last lepton.  

## 13장

**13:2** And answering, Jesus said to them, Do you think that these Galileans were sinners beyond all the Galileans, because they suffered such things?  
**13:3** No, I say to you, But if you do not repent, you will all perish likewise.  
**13:4** Or those eighteen on whom the tower in Siloam fell, and killed them, do you think that these were sinners above all men living in Jerusalem?  
**13:5** No, I say to you, But if you do not repent, you will all likewise perish.  
**13:6** And He spoke this parable: A certain one had planted a fig tree in his vineyard. And he came looking for fruit on it and did not find <i>any</i>.  
**13:7** And he said to the vinedresser, Behold, three years I come looking for fruit on this fig tree, and not he did find <i>any</i>. Cut it down, and why does it waste the ground?  
**13:8** And the vinedresser said to him, Sir, leave it also this year until I shall dig around it and throw manure;  
**13:9** and <i>see</i> if it indeed makes fruit. But if not, in the coming <i>year</i> you may cut it down.  
**13:10** And He was teaching in one of the synagogues on <i>one of</i> the sabbaths.  
**13:11** And, behold, there was a woman having a spirit of infirmity eighteen years, and was bent together and was not able to be erect to the complete <i>straightness..  
**13:12** And seeing her, Jesus called <i>her</i> near and said to her, Woman, you have been loosed from your infirmity.  
**13:13** And He laid hands on her. And instantly she was made erect and glorified God.  
**13:14** But answering, being angry that Jesus healed on the sabbath, the synagogue ruler said to the crowd, There are six days in which it is right to work. Therefore, coming in these, be healed, and not on the sabbath day.  
**13:15** Then the Lord answered him and said, Hypocrite! Each <i>one</i> of you on the sabbath, does he not loose his ox or ass from the manger, and leading <i>it</i> away, give <i>it</i> drink?  
**13:16** And this one being a daughter of Abraham, whom Satan has bound, behold, eighteen years, ought <i>she</i> not to be loosed from this bond on the sabbath day?  
**13:17** And on His saying these things, all the <i>ones</i> opposing Him were ashamed. And all the crowd rejoiced over all the glorious things being done by Him.  
**13:18** And He said, To what is the kingdom of God like? And to what shall I liken it?  
**13:19** It is like a grain of mustard which a man having taken threw into his garden. And it grew and became a great tree, and the birds of the heaven dwelt in its branches.  
**13:20** And again He said, To what shall I liken the kingdom of God?  
**13:21** It is like leaven, which taking, a woman hid in three measures of meal until all was leavened.  
**13:22** And He went through cities and villages, teaching and making progress toward Jerusalem.  
**13:23** And one said to Him, Lord, <i>are</i> the ones being saved few? But He said to them,  
**13:24** Strive to enter in through the narrow gate, for I say to you that many will seek to enter in and will not have strength.  
**13:25** From the time the Master of the house shall have risen up, and He shuts the door, and you begin to stand outside and to knock at the door, saying, Lord, Lord, open to us. And answering, He will say to you, I do not know you, from where you are.  
**13:26** Then you will begin to say, We ate and drank in Your presence, and You taught in our streets.  
**13:27** And He will say, I tell you I do not know you, from where you are. "Stand back from Me all workers of unrighteousness!" <n>Psa 6:8</n>  
**13:28** There will be weeping and gnashing of the teeth when you see Abraham and Isaac and Jacob and all the prophets in the kingdom of God, but yourselves being thrust outside.  
**13:29** And they will come from east and west, and from north and south, and will recline in the kingdom of God.  
**13:30** And, behold, there are last <i>ones</i> who will be first, and there are first <i>ones</i> who will be last.  
**13:31** In the same day, certain Pharisees came, saying to Him, Go out and go on from here, for Herod desires to kill You.  
**13:32** And He said to them, Going, say to that fox, Behold, today and tomorrow I cast out demons and I complete cures, and the third <i>day</i> I will be finished.  
**13:33** But today and tomorrow and on the following <i>day</i> I must travel on. For it is not accepted <i>for</i> a prophet to perish outside Jerusalem.  
**13:34** Jerusalem! Jerusalem! The <i>one</i> killing the prophets, and stoning the <i>ones</i> having been sent to her, how often I desired to gather your children in the way a hen <i>gathers</i> her brood under the wings, and you did not desire <i>it</i>.  
**13:35** "Behold, your house is left to you desolate. And truly I say to you, You shall not at all see Me until it comes when you say, "Being blessed <i>is</i> the <i>One</i> coming in <i>the</i> name of <i>the</i> Lord."  

## 14장

**14:1** And it occurred, on His going into a house of one of the Pharisee leaders on a sabbath to eat bread, and they were closely observing Him.  
**14:2** And behold, a certain man was dropsical before Him.  
**14:3** And answering, Jesus spoke to the lawyers and Pharisees, saying, Is it lawful to heal on the sabbath?  
**14:4** And they were silent. And taking <i>the man</i>, He healed him and sent him away.  
**14:5** And answering to them He said, Whose ass or ox of yours shall fall into a pit, and he will not at once pull it up on the sabbath day?  
**14:6** And they were not able to reply to Him against these things.  
**14:7** And He spoke a parable to the <i>ones</i> who had been invited, noting how they were choosing the chief seats, saying to them,  
**14:8** When you are invited by anyone to wedding feasts, do not recline at the chief seat lest <i>one</i> more honorable <i>than</i> you be invited by him,  
**14:9** and coming, he who invited you and him will say to you, Give this one place. And then you begin with shame to take the last place.  
**14:10** But when you are invited, going, recline at the last place, so that when he who has invited you comes, he <i>may</i> say to you, Friend, go up higher. Then glory will be to you before those reclining with you.  
**14:11** For everyone exalting himself will be humbled, and the one humbling himself will be exalted.  
**14:12** And He also said to him having invited Him, When you make a dinner or supper, do not call your friends, nor your brothers, nor your relatives, nor rich neighbors, lest they also should invite you in return, and it become a repayment to you.  
**14:13** But when you make a banquet, call the poor <i>ones</i>, the crippled <i>ones</i>, the lame <i>ones</i>, the blind <i>ones</i>;  
**14:14** and you will be blessed, for they have nothing to repay you. For it will be repaid to you in the resurrection of the just.  
**14:15** And one of those reclining with <i>Him</i>, hearing these things, <i>he</i> said to Him, Blessed <i>is he</i> who eats bread in the kingdom of God.  
**14:16** But He said to him, A certain man made a great supper and invited many.  
**14:17** And he sent his slave at the supper hour to say to the <i>ones</i> who had been invited, Come, for now all is ready.  
**14:18** And all with one <i>mind</i> began to excuse themselves. The first said to him, I have bought a field, and I have need to go out and see it; I ask you, have me excused.  
**14:19** And another said, I bought five yoke of oxen and I am going to try them out; I ask you, have me excused.  
**14:20** And another said, I married a wife, and because of this I am not able to come.  
**14:21** And having come near that slave reported these things to his lord. Then being angry, the housemaster said to his slave, Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring in here the poor <i>ones</i> and crippled <i>ones,</i> and lame <i>ones</i> and blind <i>ones</i>.  
**14:22** And the slave said, Sir, <i>it</i> has been done as you ordered, and still there is room.  
**14:23** And the lord said to the slave, Go out into the highways and hedges and compel <i>them</i> to come in, so that my house may be filled.  
**14:24** For I say to you that not one of those men who had been invited shall taste of my supper.  
**14:25** And great crowds came together to Him. And turning, He said to them,  
**14:26** If anyone comes to Me and does not hate his father, and mother, and wife, and children, and brothers and sisters, and besides, even his <i>own</i> life, he cannot be My disciple.  
**14:27** And whoever does not bear his cross and come after Me, he cannot be My disciple.  
**14:28** For who of you desiring to build a tower does not first sit down <i>and</i> count the cost, whether he has the things to finish;  
**14:29** that having laid a foundation, and not having strength to finish, all those seeing begin to mock him,  
**14:30** saying, This man began to build, and did not have strength to finish.  
**14:31** Or what king going to encounter another king in war does not first sit down <i>and</i> take counsel whether he is able with ten thousand to meet those coming upon him with twenty thousand?  
**14:32** But if not, he being yet far off, sending a delegation, he asks the things for peace.  
**14:33** So then every one of you who does not separate from all his possessions is not able to be My disciple.  
**14:34** The salt <i>is</i> good, but if the salt becomes tasteless, with what will it be seasoned?  
**14:35** Neither for soil nor for manure is it fit; they throw it out. The <i>one</i> having ears to hear, let him hear.

**16:7** Then he said to another, And you, how much do you owe? And he said, a hundred cors of wheat. And he said to him, Take your statement and write eighty.

**16:23** And being in torments in hell, lifting up his eyes, he sees Abraham afar off and Lazarus in his bosom.  
**16:24** And calling he said, Father Abraham, have mercy on me and send Lazarus that he may dip the tip of his finger in water and cool my tongue, for I am tormented in this flame.

**16:26** And besides all these things, a great chasm has been fixed between us and you, so that those desiring to pass from here to you are not able, nor can they pass from there to us.  
**16:27** And he said, Therefore I ask you, father, that you send him to my father's house;  
**16:28** (for I have five brothers, so that he may witness to them, that they not also come to this place of torment).

**16:30** But he said, No, Father Abraham, but if one should go from dead <i>ones</i> to them, they will repent.  
**16:31** And he said to him, If they will not hear Moses and the Prophets, they will not be persuaded even if someone from dead <i>ones</i> should rise.

## 23장

**23:5** But they insisted, saying, He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee to here.  
**23:6** And Pilate, hearing Galilee, asked if the man is a Galilean.  
**23:7** And knowing that He is from Herod's jurisdiction, he sent Him up to Herod, <i>he</i> also being in Jerusalem in these days.

**23:10** And the chief priests and the scribes stood vehemently accusing Him.  
**23:11** And Herod having despised Him with his soldiery, and having mocked <i>Him</i> by putting around Him luxurious clothing, <i>he</i> sent Him back to Pilate.

## 24장

**24:28** And they drew near to the village where they were going, and He seemed to be going further.

**24:31** And their eyes were opened, and they knew Him. And He became invisible from them.
