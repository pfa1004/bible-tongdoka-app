# 37.학개 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** In the second year of Darius the king, in the sixth month, in the first day of the month, the Word of Jehovah came by Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah; and to Joshua, the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, saying:  
**1:2** So speaks Jehovah of Hosts, saying, This people says, The time is not come, the time for the house of Jehovah to be built.  
**1:3** And the Word of Jehovah was by Haggai the prophet, saying,  
**1:4** <i>Is it</i> time for you, <i>even</i> you <i>all</i> to live in your covered houses, and this house to lie waste?  
**1:5** And now, thus says Jehovah of Hosts: Set your heart on your ways!  
**1:6** You have sown much, and brought in little; <i>you</i> eat, but not to satiety; <i>you</i> drink, but not to be filled; you dress, but there is no warmth to one. And one hiring out himself hires out himself for a bag of holes.  
**1:7** So says Jehovah of Hosts: Set your heart on your ways.  
**1:8** Go up the mountain and bring wood and build this house. And I will be pleased with it, and I will be glorified, says Jehovah.  
**1:9** <i>You</i> looked for much, and, behold, little! And when you brought <i>it</i> home, then I blew on it. Why, a statement of Jehovah of Hosts? Because of My house that is ruined, and you, <i>each</i> man, run to his <i>own</i> house.  
**1:10** Therefore the heavens above you have held back the dew, and the earth is held back <i>from</i> her produce.  
**1:11** And I called for a drought in the land, and on the mountains, and on the grain, and on the new wine, and on the oil, and on that which the ground brings forth, and on man, and on livestock, and on all the labor of your palms.  
**1:12** And Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel, and Joshua, the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all the remnant of the people, obeyed the voice of Jehovah their God, and the words of Haggai the prophet, as Jehovah their God had sent him. And the people feared before Jehovah.  
**1:13** And Haggai, the messenger of Jehovah, spoke the message of Jehovah to the people, saying, I <i>am</i> with you, a statement of Jehovah.  
**1:14** And Jehovah stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua, the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people and they came and did work on the house of Jehovah of Hosts, their God,  
**1:15** in the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month, in the second year of Darius the king.  

## 2장

**2:1** In the seventh <i>month</i>, on the twentyfirst of the month, the Word of Jehovah was by Haggai the prophet, saying,  
**2:2** Now speak to Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua, the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the remnant of the people, saying,  
**2:3** Who <i>is</i> left among you who saw this house in her former glory? And how do you see it now? <i>Is it</i> not as nothing like it in your eyes?  
**2:4** And now be strong, O Zerubbabel, a statement of Jehovah. And be strong, O Joshua, son of Jehozadak, the high priest. And be strong and work, all people of the land, a statement of Jehovah. For I <i>am</i> with you, a statement of Jehovah of Hosts,  
**2:5** <i>with</i> the Word which I cut with you when you came out of Egypt, and My Spirit stands in your midst; do not fear.  
**2:6** For so says Jehovah of Hosts: Yet once, it <i>is</i> a little while, and I will shake the heavens and the earth and the sea and the dry land.  
**2:7** And I will shake all the nations; and the Desire of all nations shall come. And I will fill this house <i>with</i> glory, says Jehovah of Hosts.  
**2:8** To Me <i>is</i> the silver, and to Me <i>is</i> the gold, a statement of Jehovah of Hosts.  
**2:9** The glory of this latter house shall be greater than that of the former, says Jehovah of Hosts. And in this place I will give peace, a statement of Jehovah of Hosts.  
**2:10** In the twenty-fourth of the ninth <i>month</i>, in the second year of Darius, the Word of Jehovah came to Haggai the prophet, saying,  
**2:11** So says Jehovah of Hosts: Now ask the priests the Law, saying,  
**2:12** Behold, <i>if</i> a man lifts up holy flesh in the <i>skirt</i> of his garment, and touches his <i>skirt</i> to the bread, or boiled food, or wine, or oil, or any food, will it become holy? And the priests answered and said, No.  
**2:13** And Haggai said, If the unclean of body touches these, is it unclean? And the priests answered and said, It is unclean.  
**2:14** And Haggai answered and said, So <i>is</i> this people, and so <i>is</i> this nation before Me, a statement of Jehovah. And so <i>is</i> every work <i>of</i> their hands; and that which they offer there <i>is</i> unclean.  
**2:15** And now set your heart on it. From this day and onward: before the placing of a stone on a stone in the temple of Jehovah;  
**2:16** from then onward, <i>one</i> came to a heap of twenty <i>measures</i>, and there were <i>but</i> ten. <i>One</i> came to the wine vat to draw out fifty from the wine trough, and there were <i>but</i> twenty.  
**2:17** I struck you with blight, and with mildew, and with hail, <i>in</i> all the work <i>of</i> your hands; and you did not <i>turn</i> to Me, a statement of Jehovah.  
**2:18** Now set your heart from this day and forward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth <i>month</i>, from the day that Jehovah's temple was founded; set it <i>to</i> your heart:  
**2:19** <i>Is</i> the seed still in the storehouse? And the vine, and the fig tree, and the pomegranate, and the olive tree have not borne. From this day I will bless <i>them</i>.  
**2:20** And a second time the Word of Jehovah was to Haggai, in the twenty-fourth of the month, saying,  
**2:21** Speak to Zerubbabel, the governor of Judah, saying, I will shake the heavens and the earth.  
**2:22** And I will overturn the throne of the kingdoms, and I will destroy the strength of the kingdoms of the nations. And I will overturn the chariots and those riding <i>in them</i>, and the horses and those riding will come down, <i>each</i> man by the sword of his brother.  
**2:23** In that day, a statement of Jehovah of Hosts, I will take you, O Zerubbabel, My servant, the son of Shealtiel, a statement of Jehovah, and will make you like a signet; for I have chosen you, a statement of Jehovah of Hosts.
