# 48.갈라디아서 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul, an apostle, not from men nor through man, but through Jesus Christ and God <i>the</i> Father, the <i>One</i> raising Him from <i>the</i> dead,  
**1:2** and all the brothers with me, to the assemblies of Galatia.  
**1:3** Grace to you and peace from God <i>the</i> Father and our Lord Jesus Christ,  
**1:4** who gave Himself for our sins, so that He might deliver us out of the evil age being present, according to the will of our God and Father,  
**1:5** to whom <i>be</i> the glory unto the ages of the ages. Amen.  
**1:6** I wonder that you are so quickly turning away from the <i>One</i> having called you in <i>the</i> grace of Christ to another gospel,  
**1:7** which is not another <i>of the same kind</i>; if not there are some troubling you, even determined to pervert the gospel of Christ.  
**1:8** But even if we, or an angel out of Heaven, should preach a gospel to you beside the gospel we preached to you, let him be accursed.  
**1:9** As we have said before, and now I say again, If anyone preaches a gospel beside what you received, let him be accursed.  
**1:10** For do I now persuade men or God? Or do I seek to please men? For if I yet pleased men, I would not be a slave of Christ.  
**1:11** And, brothers, I make known to you the gospel preached by me, that it is not according to man.  
**1:12** For I did not receive it from man, nor was I taught <i>it</i>, but by a revelation of Jesus Christ.  
**1:13** For you heard <i>of</i> my way of life when <i>I was</i> in Judaism, that with surpassing <i>zeal</i> I persecuted the Assembly of God and ravaged it.  
**1:14** And <i>I</i> advanced in Judaism beyond many of my own age; in my race being much more a zealot of the ancestral traditions of my <i>fathers</i>.  
**1:15** But when God was pleased, He having separated me from my mother's womb, and having called <i>me</i> through His grace, <n>Isa 49:1</n>  
**1:16** to reveal His Son in me, that I might preach Him among the nations, immediately I did not confer with flesh and blood,  
**1:17** nor did I go up to Jerusalem to the apostles before me, but I went away into Arabia and returned again to Damascus.  
**1:18** Then after three years, I went up to Jerusalem to know Peter and remained with him fifteen days.  
**1:19** But I saw <i>no</i> other of the apostles if not James, the brother of the Lord.  
**1:20** And what I write to you, behold, before God I do not lie.  
**1:21** Then I went into the regions of Syria and of Cilicia,  
**1:22** but I was being unknown by face to the assemblies of Judea in Christ.  
**1:23** But only they were hearing that he who <i>was</i> persecuting us once, now preaches the gospel, the faith which he once ravaged;  
**1:24** and they glorified God in me.  

## 2장

**2:1** Then through fourteen years, I again went up to Jerusalem with Barnabas, also taking Titus with <i>me</i>.  
**2:2** And I went up according to revelation. And I put before them the gospel which I proclaim in the nations, but privately to the <i>ones</i> thought <i>to be pillars</i>, lest perhaps I run, or I ran, to no purpose.  
**2:3** But not even Titus, the <i>one</i> with me, a Greek, was compelled to be circumcised.  
**2:4** But <i>it was</i> because of the <i>ones</i> brought in stealthily false brothers, who stole in to spy on our freedom which we have in Christ Jesus, they desiring to enslave us,  
**2:5** to whom not even for an hour did we yield in subjection, that the truth of the gospel might continue with you.  
**2:6** But from the <i>ones</i> thought to be something (of what kind they were then does not matter to me; God does not accept the face of man), for the <i>ones</i> thought <i>to be pillars</i> conferred nothing to me;  
**2:7** but on the contrary, seeing that I have been entrusted <i>with</i> the gospel of the uncircumcision, even as Peter to the circumcision,  
**2:8** (for the <i>One</i> working in Peter to an apostleship of the circumcision, also worked in me to the nations),  
**2:9** and knowing the grace being given to me, James and Cephas and John, the <i>ones</i> thought to be pillars, gave right <i>hands</i> of fellowship to Barnabas and to me, that we <i>go</i> to the nations, but they to the circumcision;  
**2:10** only that we might remember the poor, which same thing I was eager to do.  
**2:11** But when Peter came to Antioch, I opposed <i>him</i> to his face, because he was to be blamed.  
**2:12** For before some came from James, he ate with the nations. But when they came, he drew back and separated himself, being afraid of the <i>ones</i> of the circumcision.  
**2:13** And also the rest of the Jews joined in hypocrisy <i>with</i> him, so as even Barnabas was led away with their hypocrisy.  
**2:14** But when I saw that they did not walk uprightly with the truth of the gospel, I said to Peter before all, If you being a Jew, live heathen-like and not Jewishly, why do you compel <i>the</i> nations to Judaize?  
**2:15** We, Jews by nature, and not sinners of the nations,  
**2:16** knowing that a man is not justified by works of Law, if not through faith of Jesus Christ (we also believed into Christ Jesus, that we may be justified by faith of Christ and not by works of Law, because all flesh will not be justified by works of Law). <n>Psa 123:2</n>  
**2:17** But if seeking to be justified in Christ, we ourselves also were found <i>to be</i> sinners, <i>is</i> Christ then a minister of sin? Let it not be!  
**2:18** For what if I build again these things which I destroyed, I myself stand out <i>as</i> a transgressor.  
**2:19** For through Law I died to Law, that I might live to God.  
**2:20** I have been crucified with Christ, and I live; <i>yet</i> no longer I, but Christ lives in me. And the <i>life</i> I now live in the flesh, I live in faith in the Son of God, the <i>One</i> loving me and giving Himself over on my behalf.  
**2:21** I do not set aside the grace of God; for if righteousness <i>is</i> through Law, then Christ died for nothing.  

## 3장

**3:1** O foolish Galatians, who bewitched you not to obey the truth, to whom before <i>your</i> eyes Jesus Christ was portrayed afore among you being crucified?  
**3:2** This only I desire to learn from you: Did you receive the Spirit by works of Law or by hearing of faith?  
**3:3** Are you so foolish? Having begun in <i>the</i> Spirit, are you now being perfected in the flesh?  
**3:4** Did you suffer so much vainly, if indeed <i>it</i> also <i>was</i> vainly?  
**3:5** Therefore the <i>One</i> supplying the Spirit to you and working works of power in you, <i>is it</i> by works of Law or by hearing of faith?  
**3:6** Even "as Abraham believed God, and it was imputed to him for righteousness," <n>Gen 15:6</n>  
**3:7** know, therefore, that the <i>ones</i> of faith, these are sons of Abraham.  
**3:8** And the Scripture foreseeing that God would justify the nations by faith, preached the gospel before to Abraham: "All the nations will be blessed" "in you." <n>Gen 12:3</n>  
**3:9** So that the <i>ones</i> of faith are blessed with the faithful Abraham.  
**3:10** For as many as are out of works of Law, <i>these</i> are under a curse. For it is writ- ten, "Accursed <i>is</i> everyone who does not continue in all the things having been written in the book of the Law, to do them." <n>Deut 27:26</n>  
**3:11** And that no one is justified by Law before God <i>is</i> clear because, "The just <i>one</i> shall live by faith." <n>Hab 2:4</n>  
**3:12** But the Law is not of faith, but, "The man doing these things shall live in them." <n>Lev 18:5</n>  
**3:13** Christ redeemed us from the curse of the Law, having become a curse for us; for it is written, "Accursed <i>is</i> everyone having been hung on a tree;" <n>Deut 21:23</n>  
**3:14** that the blessing of Abraham might be to the nations in Christ Jesus, that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through the faith.  
**3:15** Brothers, I speak according to man, a covenant having been ratified, even <i>among</i> mankind, no one annuls or adds to <i>it</i>.  
**3:16** But the promises were spoken to Abraham and to his Seed (it does not say, And to seeds, as of many, but as of one, "And to your Seed," which is Christ). <n>Gen 3:15</n>; 21:12; 22:18, <n>Rom 9:7; Heb 11:18</n>  
**3:17** And I say this, A covenant having been ratified before to Christ by God, <i>the</i> Law coming into being four hundred and thirty years after, does not annul the promise, so as to abolish <i>it</i>.  
**3:18** For if the inheritance <i>is</i> of Law, <i>it is</i> no more of promise; but God has given <i>it</i> to Abraham through promise.  
**3:19** Why the Law then? It was added for the sake of transgressions, until the Seed should come, to whom it had been promised, being ordained through angels in a mediator's hand.  
**3:20** But the Mediator is not <i>mediator</i> of one, but God is one.  
**3:21** Therefore is the Law against the promises of God? Let it not be! For if a law had been given which had been able to make alive, indeed righteousness would have been out of Law.  
**3:22** But the Scripture shut up together all under sin, that the promise by faith of Jesus Christ might be given to the <i>ones</i> believing.  
**3:23** But before the coming of faith, we were guarded under Law, being shut up to the faith being about to be revealed.  
**3:24** So that the Law has become a tutor of us <i>until</i> Christ, that we might be justified by faith.  
**3:25** But faith coming, we are no longer under a tutor;  
**3:26** for you are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus.  
**3:27** For as many as were baptized into Christ, you put on Christ.  
**3:28** There cannot be Jew nor Greek, there is no slave nor freeman, there is no male and female; for you are all one in Christ Jesus.  
**3:29** And if you <i>are</i> of Christ, then you are a seed of Abraham, and heirs according to promise.  

## 4장

**4:1** But I say, Over so long as time the heir is a little child, he being lord of all, in nothing he differs from a slave,  
**4:2** but is under guardians and housemasters until the <i>term</i> set before by the father.  
**4:3** So we also, when we were little children, we were under the elements of the world, being enslaved.  
**4:4** But when the fullness of the time came, God sent forth His Son, becoming of a woman, becoming under Law,  
**4:5** that He might redeem the <i>ones</i> under Law, that we might receive the adoption of sons.  
**4:6** And because you are sons, God sent forth the Spirit of His Son into your hearts, crying, Abba! Father!  
**4:7** So that you no more are a slave, but a son, and if a son, also an heir of God through Christ.  
**4:8** But then, indeed, not knowing God, you served as slaves <i>to</i> the <i>ones</i> by nature not being gods.  
**4:9** But now, knowing God, but rather being known by God, how do you turn again to the weak and poor elements to which again you desire to be a slave anew?  
**4:10** You observe days and months and seasons and years.  
**4:11** I fear <i>for</i> you, lest somehow I have labored among you in vain.  
**4:12** Be as I <i>am</i>, I beg of you, b<i>rothers</i>, because I <i>am</i> as you. You wronged me <i>in</i> nothing.  
**4:13** But you know that because of weakness of the flesh, I announced the gospel to you before;  
**4:14** and you did not despise my affliction, the <i>one</i> in my flesh, nor disdained <i>it</i>, but you received me as an angel of God, as Christ Jesus.  
**4:15** What then was your blessedness? For I testify to you that if you were able, digging out your eyes, <i>you</i> would have given <i>them</i> to me.  
**4:16** So then have I become a hostile <i>one to</i> you speaking truth to you?  
**4:17** They are zealous for you, <i>but</i> not well; but they <i>only</i> desire to shut you out, that you be zealous to them.  
**4:18** But <i>it is</i> good to be zealous always in <i>a</i> good thing, and not only in my being present with you.  
**4:19** My children, <i>for</i> whom I again travail until Christ should be formed to you,  
**4:20** even now I desired to be present with you and to change my voice, for I am in doubt <i>as</i> to you.  
**4:21** Tell me, the <i>ones</i> desiring to be under Law, do you not hear the Law?  
**4:22** For it is written, Abraham had two sons, one out of the slave woman and one out of the free woman.  
**4:23** But, indeed, the <i>one</i> of the slave woman has been born according to flesh, and the <i>one</i> out of the free <i>woman</i> through the promise,  
**4:24** which things are being allegorized, for these are two covenants, one, indeed, from Mount Sinai bringing forth to slavery (which is Hagar,  
**4:25** for Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia and corresponds to the present Jerusalem, and she slaves with her children),  
**4:26** but the Jerusalem <i>from</i> above is free, who is the mother of us all;  
**4:27** for it is written, "Rejoice, barren <i>one</i> not bearing; break forth and shout, the <i>one</i> not travailing; for more <i>are</i> the children of the desolate rather than she having the husband." <n>Isa 54:1</n>  
**4:28** But, brothers, we are children of promise according to Isaac.  
**4:29** But even as then, the <i>one</i> being generated according to flesh persecuted the <i>one</i> according to Spirit, so <i>it is</i> also now.  
**4:30** But what says the Scripture? "Cast out the slave woman and her son, for not shall the son of the slave <i>woman</i> inherit with the son of the free <i>woman</i>," not <i>ever</i>! ` <n>Gen 21:10</n>  
**4:31** Then, brothers, we are not children of a slave woman but of the free <i>woman</i>.  

## 5장

**5:1** Therefore stand firm in the freedom with which Christ made us free and do not be held again with a yoke of slavery.  
**5:2** Behold, I, Paul, say to you that if you are circumcised, Christ will profit you nothing.  
**5:3** And I testify again to every man being circumcised, that he is a debtor to do all the Law,  
**5:4** <i>you</i>, whoever are justified by Law, you were severed from the Christ, you fell from grace.  
**5:5** For we through <i>the</i> Spirit eagerly wait for <i>the</i> hope of righteousness out of faith.  
**5:6** For in Christ Jesus, neither circumcision nor uncircumcision has any strength, but faith working through love.  
**5:7** You were running well; who beat you back <i>that</i> you do not obey the truth?  
**5:8** The persuasion <i>is</i> not from Him calling you.  
**5:9** A little leaven leavens all the lump.  
**5:10** I trust as to you in <i>the</i> Lord that you will think nothing else, but that the <i>one</i> troubling you shall bear the judgment, whoever he may be.  
**5:11** But I, brothers, if I proclaim circumcision, why am I still persecuted? Then the stumbling-block of the Cross has passed away.  
**5:12** O that also the <i>ones</i> unsettling you will cut themselves off.  
**5:13** For, brothers, you were called to freedom. Only do not <i>use</i> the freedom for <i>an</i> opening to the flesh. But through love be slaves to one another.  
**5:14** For the whole Law is fulfilled in one word, in <i>this</i>: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself." <n>Lev 19:18</n>  
**5:15** But if you bite and devour one another, be careful that you are not consumed by one another.  
**5:16** But I say, Walk in <i>the</i> Spirit, and you will not fulfill <i>the</i> lust of <i>the</i> flesh.  
**5:17** For the flesh lusts against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh; and these are contrary to one another; that not whatever you may will, <i>but</i> these things you do.  
**5:18** But if you are led by <i>the</i> Spirit, you are not under Law.  
**5:19** Now the works of the flesh are clearly revealed, which are: adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lustfulness,  
**5:20** idolatry, sorcery, enmities, fightings, jealousies, angers, intrigues, dissensions, heresies,  
**5:21** envyings, murders, drunkennesses, revelings, and things like these; of which I tell you beforehand, even as I also said before, that the <i>ones</i> practicing such things will not inherit <i>the</i> kingdom of God.  
**5:22** But the fruit of the Spirit is: love, joy, peace, long-suffering, kindness, goodness, faith,  
**5:23** meekness, self-control. Against such things there is not a law.  
**5:24** But the <i>ones</i> belonging to Christ crucified the flesh with <i>its</i> passions and lusts.  
**5:25** If we live in <i>the</i> Spirit, let us also walk in <i>the</i> Spirit.  
**5:26** Let us not become self-conceited, provoking one another, envying one another.  

## 6장

**6:1** Brothers, if a man is taken in some deviation, you, the spiritual ones, restore such a one in the spirit of meekness, considering yourself, that you not also be tempted.  
**6:2** Bear one another's burdens, and so you will fulfill the law of Christ.  
**6:3** For if anyone thinks to be something, he deceives himself, being nothing.  
**6:4** But let each <i>one</i> prove his own work, and then he alone will have a boast in himself and not in the other.  
**6:5** For each <i>one</i> will bear <i>his</i> own load.  
**6:6** But let the <i>one</i> being taught in the Word share with the <i>one</i> teaching, in all good things.  
**6:7** Do not be deceived, God is not mocked. For whatever a man may sow, that he also will reap.  
**6:8** For the <i>one</i> sowing to his own flesh will reap corruption of the flesh. But the <i>one</i> sowing to the Spirit will reap everlasting life of the Spirit.  
**6:9** But let us not be wearied in doing good; for in its own time we shall reap, not growing faint-hearted.  
**6:10** So, then, as we have time, let us work the good toward all, and especially toward the household of the faith.  
**6:11** See in what large letters I write to you with my <i>own</i> hand.  
**6:12** As many as desire to look well in <i>the</i> flesh, these compel you to be circumcised, only that they may not be persecuted for the cross of Christ.  
**6:13** For neither the <i>ones</i> being circumcised themselves do not even keep <i>the</i> Law, but they desire you to be circumcised so that they may boast in your flesh.  
**6:14** But may it never be for me to boast, except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.  
**6:15** For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision <i>has</i> any strength nor uncircumcision, but a new creation.  
**6:16** And as many as shall walk by this rule, peace and mercy <i>be</i> on them and on the Israel of God.  
**6:17** For the rest, let no one give troubles to me, for I bear in my body the brands of the Lord Jesus.  
**6:18** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <i>be</i> with your spirit, brothers. Amen.
