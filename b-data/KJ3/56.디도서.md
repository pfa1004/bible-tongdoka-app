# 56.디도서 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul, a slave of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ according to <i>the</i> faith of <i>the</i> elect of God and full knowledge of <i>the</i> truth according to godliness,  
**1:2** on hope of eternal life which the God who does not lie promised before eternal times,  
**1:3** but revealed in its own times in a proclamation of His Word, with which I was entrusted by <i>the</i> command of our Savior God;  
**1:4** to Titus, a true child according to <i>our</i> common faith: Grace, mercy, peace from God <i>the</i> Father and <i>the</i> Lord Jesus Christ our Savior.  
**1:5** For this cause I left you in Crete, that you might set in order the things lacking and appoint elders in every city, as I prescribed to you:  
**1:6** If anyone is blameless, husband of one wife, having faithful children, not in accusation of unsavedness, or disobedient,  
**1:7** (It is right for the overseer to be blameless as a steward of God) not self-pleasing, not prone to anger, not given to wine, not a quarreler, not greedy of ill gain;  
**1:8** but hospitable, a lover of good, selfcontrolled, just, holy, sensible,  
**1:9** clinging to the faithful Word according to the teaching, that he may be able both to encourage by sound doctrine and to convict the <i>ones</i> contradicting.  
**1:10** For there are indeed many insubordinate men, empty talkers and mind-deluders, especially the <i>ones</i> of <i>the</i> circumcision,  
**1:11** whose mouth <i>you</i> must stop, who overturn whole houses, teaching things which <i>they</i> ought not for the sake of ill gain.  
**1:12** One of them, a prophet of their own, said: Cretans <i>are</i> always liars, evil beasts, lazy gluttons.  
**1:13** This testimony is true; for which cause convict them severely, that they may be sound in the faith,  
**1:14** not listening to Jewish myths and commandments of men, having turned away from the truth.  
**1:15** Truly, all things <i>are</i> pure to the pure, but to the <i>ones</i> being defiled and unbelieving, nothing <i>is</i> pure, but even their mind and conscience has been defiled.  
**1:16** They profess to know God, but by <i>their</i> works they deny <i>Him</i>, being abominable and disobedient, and to every good work disapproved.  

## 2장

**2:1** But you speak things which become sound doctrine:  
**2:2** aged men to be temperate, honorable, sensible, sound in faith, in love, in patience;  
**2:3** aged women likewise in reverent behavior, not slanderers, not having been enslaved by much wine, teachers of good,  
**2:4** that they might train the young women to be lovers of husbands, lovers of children,  
**2:5** sensible, chaste, home-keepers, good, subject to <i>their</i> own husbands, so that the Word of God may not be blasphemed,  
**2:6** the younger men in the same way exhort to be sensible;  
**2:7** holding forth yourself <i>as</i> a pattern of good works about all <i>things</i> in doctrine, in purity, dignity, incorruption,  
**2:8** sound speech, without condemnation, that the hostile <i>ones</i> may he shamed, having nothing bad to say about you.  
**2:9** Let slaves be subject to <i>their</i> own masters, well-pleasing in all <i>things</i>, not contradicting,  
**2:10** not embezzling, but showing all good faith, that they may adorn the doctrine of our Savior God in every <i>thing</i>.  
**2:11** For the saving grace of God has appeared to all men,  
**2:12** instructing us that having denied ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live prudently and righteously and godly in the now age,  
**2:13** looking for the blessed hope and appearance of the glory of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ,  
**2:14** who gave Himself on our behalf, "that He might redeem us from all lawlessness" "and purify" "for Himself a people for Himself for possession," zealous of good works. <n>Psa 130:8; Eze 37:23; Deut 14:2</n>  
**2:15** Speak these things and exhort and convict with all authority. Let no one despise you.  

## 3장

**3:1** Remind them to be subject to rulers and authorities, to be obedient, to be ready in every good work,  
**3:2** to speak evil of no one, not quarrelsome, <i>but</i> gentle, having displayed all meekness to all men.  
**3:3** For we also once were senseless, disobedient, being led astray, slaving for various lusts and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, hating one another.  
**3:4** But when the kindness and love of God our Savior toward man appeared,  
**3:5** not out of works in righteousness which we had done, but according to His mercy, He saved us through <i>the</i> bathing of regeneration and renewal of <i>the</i> Holy Spirit,  
**3:6** whom He poured out on us richly through Jesus Christ, our Savior;  
**3:7** that being justified by the grace of that <i>One</i>, we should become heirs according to <i>the</i> hope of eternal life.  
**3:8** Faithful <i>is</i> the Word, and concerning these things I purpose you to strongly affirm that the <i>ones</i> believing God should take thought to maintain good works. These things are good and profitable to men.  
**3:9** But keep back from foolish questionings and genealogies and arguments and quarrels of law, for they are unprofitable and vain.  
**3:10** Reject a heretical man after a first and second admonition,  
**3:11** knowing that such a one has been perverted and sins, being self-condemned.  
**3:12** When I shall send Artemas to you or Tychicus, hasten to come to me at Nicopolis. For I have decided to winter there.  
**3:13** Diligently set forward Zenas the lawyer and Apollos, that nothing be lacking to them.  
**3:14** And let ours also learn to maintain good works for necessary uses, that they may not be without fruit.  
**3:15** All the <i>ones</i> with me greet you. Greet the <i>ones</i> who love us in <i>the</i> faith. Grace <i>be</i> wiih you all. Amen.
