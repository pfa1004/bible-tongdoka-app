## 1장

**1:2** Gathering I will completely gather away all from on the face of the land, a statement of Jehovah.  
**1:3** Gathering I will gather away man and beast: I will gather away the birds of the heavens and the fish of the sea, and the stumbling-blocks, <i>even</i> the wicked <i>ones</i>. And I will cut off man from the face of the land, a statement of Jehovah.  
**1:4** I will also stretch out My hand on Judah and on all those dwelling in Jerusalem. And I will cut off the remnant of Baal from this place, the name of the idol-priests, with the priests;  
**1:5** and those bowing to the host of the heavens on the housetops; and those bowing, swearing to Jehovah, and swearing by Malcham,  
**1:6** and those drawing back from after Jehovah, and who have not sought Jehovah, and not asked of Him.  
**1:7** Be silent before the face of the Lord Jehovah, for the day of Jehovah <i>is</i> near. For Jehovah has appointed a sacrifice; He has consecrated <i>ones</i> He called.  
**1:8** And it shall be in the day of the sacrifice of Jehovah, I will call to account on the rulers and on the king's sons, and all those clothed <i>in</i> foreign clothing.  
**1:9** And I will call to account all <i>those</i> leaping on the threshold in that day, who <i>are</i> filling the houses of their masters <i>with</i> violence and deceit.  
**1:10** And it shall be in that day, a statement of Jehovah, the sound of a cry from the Fish Gate, and a howling from the Second, and a great breaking from the hills.  
**1:11** Howl, those living in Maktesh, for all the people of Canaan have perished; all those laden <i>with</i> silver are cut off.  
**1:12** And it shall be in that time, I will search Jerusalem with lamps, and call to account the men being settled on their lees; who say in their heart, Jehovah will not do good, and not will He do evil.  
**1:13** And their wealth shall become a prize, and their houses a desolation. And they shall build houses, but not live <i>there</i>. And they shall plant vineyards, but not drink their wine.  
**1:14** The great day of Jehovah is near; <i>it is</i> near and hurrying greatly, the sound of the day of Jehovah. The mighty <i>man</i> shall cry out bitterly there.  
**1:15** That day <i>is</i> a day of wrath, a day of distress and pressure, a day of waste and ruin, a day of darkness and gloom, a day of clouds and thick darkness,

**1:17** And I will bring distress to men, and they shall walk like the blind <i>men</i>, because they have sinned against Jehovah. And their blood shall be poured out like dust, and their flesh like dung.  
**1:18** Also their silver and their gold shall not be able to deliver them in the day of the wrath of Jehovah. But all the earth shall be devoured by the fire of His jealousy. For He shall make a complete, yea, a speedy <i>end</i> of all those living <i>on</i> the land.  

## 2장

**2:1** Gather yourselves, even gather, O nation not being longed for;  
**2:2** before the birth of the decree, the day shall pass like the chaff, yet not before the heat of the anger of Jehovah comes on you, yet not before the day of the anger of Jehovah comes on you.

**2:5** Woe <i>to</i> those living <i>on</i> the seacoast, the nation of the Cherethites! The Word of Jehovah <i>is</i> against you, Canaan, the land of the Philistines; I will cause you to perish, so as not one <i>shall be</i> living <i>in it</i>.  
**2:6** And the seacoast shall be pastures, meadows <i>for</i> feeding, and folds for the flock.  
**2:7** And the coast shall be for the remnant of the house of Judah; they shall feed on them. In the houses of Ashkelon, they shall lie down in the evening, for Jehovah their God shall care for them and turn back their captivity.  
**2:8** I have heard the scorn of Moab, and the revilings of the sons of Ammon, <i>with</i> which they have cursed My people and have magnified <i>themselves</i> on their border.

**2:10** This <i>shall be</i> to them for their pride, because they have cursed and magnified <i>themselves</i> against the people of Jehovah of Hosts.  
**2:11** Jehovah <i>will be</i> frightening to them, for He will <i>make</i> lean all the gods of the earth; <i>each</i> man from his place, and all the isles of the nations will bow to Him.  
**2:12** You also, O Ethiopians, they <i>are</i> pierced by My sword.  
**2:13** And He will stretch out His hand against the north, and make perish Assyria, and will make Nineveh a desolation, dry like the desert.
