# 66.요한계시록 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** A Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave to Him to show to His slaves things which must occur quickly. And He signified <i>by</i> sending through His angel to His slave, John,  
**1:2** who testified of the Word of God and the witness of Jesus Christ, even as many things as he saw.  
**1:3** Blessed <i>is</i> the <i>one</i> reading, and the <i>ones</i> hearing the words of this prophecy, and keeping the things having been written; for the time <i>is</i> near.  
**1:4** John to the seven assemblies in Asia: Grace to you, and peace, from the <i>One</i> being, and the <i>One who</i> was, and the <i>One</i> coming, and from the seven spirits which are before His throne;  
**1:5** even from Jesus Christ the faithful Witness, the First-begotten out of the dead <i>ones</i>, and the Ruler of the kings of the earth. To the <i>One</i> loving us and having washed us from our sins by His blood,  
**1:6** and made us kings and priests to God, even His Father. To Him <i>is</i> the glory and the might forever and ever. Amen.  
**1:7** "Behold, He comes with the clouds," and "every eye will see Him, and those who pierced" Him, and all the tribes of the earth "will wail on account of Him." Yes, Amen. <n>Dan 7:13; Zech 12:10</n>  
**1:8** I am the Alpha and the Omega, <i>the</i> Beginning and <i>the</i> End, says the Lord, the <i>One</i> being, and the <i>One who</i> was, and the <i>One</i> coming, the Almighty.  
**1:9** I, even your brother John, and cosharer in the affliction, and in the kingdom and patience of Jesus Christ, came to be in the island being called Patmos on account of the Word of God, and for the witness of Jesus Christ.  
**1:10** I came to be in <i>the</i> Spirit on <i>the</i> day belonging to the Lord, and I heard behind me a great voice, as of a trumpet,  
**1:11** saying, I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last; also, What you see, write in a book, and send to the seven assemblies of Asia: to Ephesus, and to Smyrna, and to Pergamos, and to Thyatira, and to Sardis, and to Philadelphia, and to Laodicea.  
**1:12** And I turned to see the voice which spoke with me.  
**1:13** And having turned, I saw seven golden lampstands, and in <i>the</i> midst of the seven lampstands <i>One</i> like <i>the</i> Son of Man, having been clothed to <i>the</i> feet, and having been girded with a golden girdle at the breasts.  
**1:14** And the hairs of His head <i>were</i> white as white wool, as snow, and His eyes as a flame of fire;  
**1:15** and His feet like burnished brass having been fired in a furnace; and His voice as a sound of many waters;  
**1:16** and having in His right hand seven stars; and a sharp, two-edged sword proceeding out of His mouth, and His face shining as the sun in its power.  
**1:17** And when I saw Him, I fell at His feet, as dead. And He put His right hand on me, saying to me, Do not fear. I am the First and the Last,  
**1:18** and the <i>One</i> Living; and I became dead; and, behold, I am living to the ages of the ages. Amen. And I have the keys to hell, and of death.  
**1:19** Write the things which you saw, and the things which are, and the things which are about to occur after these things.  
**1:20** The mystery of the seven stars which you saw on My right, and the seven golden lampstands: the seven stars are angels of the seven assemblies, and the seven lampstands you saw are seven assemblies.  

## 2장

**2:1** To the angel of the Ephesian assembly, write: These things says the <i>One</i> holding the seven stars in His right <i>hand</i>, the <i>One</i> walking in <i>the</i> midst of the seven golden lampstands:  
**2:2** I know your works, and your labor, and your patience, and that you cannot bear evil <i>ones</i>; and <i>you</i> tried the <i>ones</i> pretending to be apostles and are not, and found them <i>to be</i> liars.  
**2:3** And <i>I know you</i> bore up and have patience and on account of My name you have labored and have not wearied.  
**2:4** But I have against you that you left your first love.  
**2:5** Therefore, remember from where you have fallen, and repent, and do the first works. And if not, I am coming to you quickly, and will remove your lampstand from its place, unless you repent.  
**2:6** But you have this, that you hate the works of the Nicolaitans, which I also hate.  
**2:7** The <i>one</i> having an ear, hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies. To the <i>one</i> overcoming, I will give to him to eat of the Tree of Life which is in <i>the</i> midst of the Paradise of God.  
**2:8** And to the angel of the assembly <i>in</i> Smyrna, write: These things says the First and the Last, who became a dead <i>one</i>, and lived:  
**2:9** I know your works, and the affliction, and the poverty; but you are rich. And <i>I know</i> the blasphemy of the <i>ones</i> saying themselves to be Jews, and they are not, but <i>are</i> a synagogue of Satan.  
**2:10** Fear nothing <i>of</i> what you are about to suffer. Behold, the devil is about to throw <i>some</i> from you into prison, so that you may be tried; and you will have affliction ten days. Be faithful until death, and I will give you the crown of life.  
**2:11** The <i>one</i> having an ear, hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies. The <i>one</i> overcoming will not be hurt by the second death, not <i>ever!  
**2:12** And to the angel of the assembly <i>in</i> Pergamos, write: These things says the <i>One</i> having the sharp, two-edged sword:  
**2:13** I know your works, and where you dwell, where the throne of Satan <i>is</i>. And you hold My name, and did not deny My faith even in the days in which Antipas <i>was</i> My faithful witness; who was killed alongside you, where Satan dwells.  
**2:14** But I have a few <i>things</i> against you, that you have there <i>some</i> holding the teachings of Balaam, who taught Balak to cast a stumbling block before the sons of Israel, to eat idol sacrifices, and to commit fornication.  
**2:15** So you also have <i>some</i> holding the teaching of the Nicolaitans, which thing I hate.  
**2:16** Repent! But if not, I will come to you quickly, and I will make war with them by the sword of My mouth.  
**2:17** The <i>one</i> having an ear, hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies. To the <i>one</i> overcoming, I will give him to eat from the hidden manna. And I will give to him a white pebble, and on the pebble a new name having been written, which no one knows if not the <i>one</i> receiving <i>it</i>.  
**2:18** And to the angel of the assembly in Thyatira, write: These things says the Son of God, the <i>One</i> having His eyes as a flame of fire, and His feet like burnished brass.  
**2:19** I know your works, and the love, and the ministry, and the faith, and your patience, and your works; and the last more than the first.  
**2:20** But I have a few things against you, that you allow the woman Jezebel to teach, the <i>one</i> saying herself <i>to be</i> a prophetess, and to cause My slaves to go astray, and to commit fornication, and to eat idol sacrifices.  
**2:21** And I gave time to her that she might repent of her fornication. And she did not repent.  
**2:22** Behold, I am throwing her into a bed, and into great affliction the <i>ones</i> committing adultery with her, unless they repent of their works.  
**2:23** And I will kill her children with death; and all the assemblies will know that I am the <i>One</i> searching the kidneys and hearts. And I will give to you, each <i>one,</i> according to your works.  
**2:24** But I say to you and to the rest in Thyatira, as many as do not have this teaching, and who did not know the deep things of Satan, as they say; I am not casting another burden on you;  
**2:25** but what you have, hold until I shall come.  
**2:26** And the <i>one</i> overcoming, and the <i>one</i> keeping My works until <i>the</i> end, "I will give to him authority over the nations,"  
**2:27** and "he will shepherd them with an iron staff" (they are "broken to pieces like clay vessels"), as I also have received from My Father. <n>Psa 2:8</n>, 9  
**2:28** And I will give to him the Morning Star.  
**2:29** The <i>one</i> havings an ear, hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies.  

## 3장

**3:1** And to the angel of the assembly in Sardis, write: These things says the <i>One</i> having the seven spirits of God, and the seven stars: I know your works, that you have the name that you live, and are a lifeless assembly.  
**3:2** Be watching, and establish the things remaining which are about to die. For I have not found your works being fulfilled before God.  
**3:3** Therefore, remember how you received and heard, and keep, and repent. If, therefore, you do not watch, I will come upon you like a thief and you will not know what hour I come upon you, not <i>at all</i>.  
**3:4** You also have a few names in Sardis which did not defile their garments, and they shall walk with Me in white because they are worthy.  
**3:5** The <i>one</i> overcoming, this one shall be clothed in white garments, and I will not blot his name out of the Book of Life, not <i>ever</i>! And I will acknowledge his name before My Father, and before His angels.  
**3:6** The <i>one</i> having an ear, hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies.  
**3:7** And to the angel of the assembly in Philadelphia, write: These things says the Holy <i>One,</i> the True <i>One,</i> the <i>One</i> having "the key of David", "the <i>One</i> opening, and no one shuts; and shuts, and no one opens": <n>Isa 22:22</n>  
**3:8** I know your works. Behold, I have given a door being opened before you, and no one is able to shut it, for you have a little power and have kept My Word, and have not denied My name.  
**3:9** Behold, I give out of the synagogue of Satan the <i>ones</i> saying themselves to be Jews, and they are not, but they lie. Behold, I will make them come and prostrate themselves before your feet, and they shall know that I loved you.  
**3:10** Because you kept the Word of My patience, I also will keep you out of the hour of trial, the <i>one</i> being about to come on the whole inhabited earth in order to try the <i>ones</i> dwelling on the earth.  
**3:11** Behold, I am coming quickly. Hold what you have that no one take your crown.  
**3:12** The <i>one</i> overcoming, I will make him a pillar in the temple of My God, and he shall not go out any more, not <i>ever</i>! And I will write the name of My God on him, and the name of the city of My God, the new Jerusalem which comes down out of Heaven from My God, and My new name.  
**3:13** The <i>one</i> having an ear, hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies.  
**3:14** And to the angel of <i>the</i> assembly <i>in</i> Laodicean, write: These things says the Amen, the faithful and true Witness, the Beginning of the creation of God:  
**3:15** I know your works, that you are neither cold nor hot. I would that you were cold or hot.  
**3:16** So, because you are lukewarm, and neither cold nor hot, I am about to vomit you out of My mouth.  
**3:17** Because you say, I am a rich <i>one</i>, and I am made wealthy, and I have need of nothing, and do not know that you are the wretched <i>one</i> and miserable <i>one</i> and poor <i>one</i> and the blind <i>one</i> and the naked <i>one</i>.  
**3:18** I advise you to buy from Me gold having been fired by fire, that you may be rich; and white garments, that you may be clothed, and your shame and nakedness may not appear. And anoint your eyes with eye salve, that you may see.  
**3:19** I, as many "as I love, I rebuke and I chasten." Be zealous, then, and repent. <n>Prov 3:12</n>  
**3:20** Behold, I stand at the door and am knocking. If anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will enter to him, and I will dine with him, and he with Me.  
**3:21** The <i>one</i> overcoming, I will give to him to sit with Me in My throne, as I also overcame and sat with My Father in His throne.  
**3:22** The <i>one</i> having an ear, hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies.  

## 4장

**4:1** After these things I saw. And behold, a door being opened in Heaven! And I heard the first voice as a trumpet speaking with me, saying, Come up here, and I will show you what needs to happen after these things.  
**4:2** And at once I became in spirit. And, behold, a throne was set in Heaven, and <i>One</i> sitting on the throne.  
**4:3** And the <i>One</i> sitting was in appearance like a jasper stone, and a sardius; and a rainbow was around the throne, in appearance of emeralds.  
**4:4** And around the throne <i>I saw</i> twentyfour thrones, and on the thrones I saw twenty four elders sitting, having been clothed in white garments. And they had golden crowns on their heads.  
**4:5** And out of the throne come forth lightnings and thunders and voices. And seven lamps of fire <i>were</i> burning before the throne, which are the seven Spirits of God;  
**4:6** and a glassy sea before the throne, like crystal. And in <i>the</i> midst of the throne and around the throne <i>were</i> four living creatures, full of eyes before and behind.  
**4:7** And the first living creature <i>was</i> like a lion; and the second living creature like a calf; and the third living creature having a face like a man; and the fourth living creature like an eagle flying.  
**4:8** And the four living creatures one by one had six wings around, and within being full of eyes. And they have no rest day and night, saying, "Holy, holy, holy, Lord God Almighty," the <i>One who</i> was, and the <i>One</i> being, and the <i>One</i> coming! <n>Isa 6:3</n>  
**4:9** And whenever the living creatures shall give glory and honor and thanks to the <i>One</i> sitting on the throne, to the <i>One</i> living to the ages of the ages,  
**4:10** the twenty-four elders fall down before the <i>One</i> sitting on the throne; and they will worship the <i>One</i> living unto the ages of the ages, and will throw their crowns before the throne, saying,  
**4:11** Lord, You are worthy to receive the glory and the honor and the power, because You created all things, and through Your will they exist and were created.  

## 5장

**5:1** And I saw on the right of the <i>One</i> sitting on the throne a scroll having been written within and on the back, having been sealed with seven seals.  
**5:2** And I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a great voice: Who is worthy to open the scroll, and to loosen its seals?  
**5:3** And no one in Heaven was able to open the scroll nor to look at it, neither on the earth, nor underneath the earth.  
**5:4** And I wept much, because no one worthy was found to open and to read the scroll, nor to look at it.  
**5:5** And one of the elders said to me, Do not weep. Behold, the Lion being of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, overcame <i>so as</i> to open the scroll, and to loose its seven seals.  
**5:6** And I saw, and behold, in <i>the</i> midst of the throne, and of the four living creatures, and in <i>the</i> midst of the elders, <i>was</i> a Lamb standing, as having been slaughtered, having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven Spirits of God, having been sent out into all the earth.  
**5:7** And He came and has taken the scroll out of the right <i>hand</i> of the <i>One</i> sitting on the throne.  
**5:8** And when He took the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb, each <i>one</i> having harps, and golden bowls full of incenses, which are the prayers of the saints.  
**5:9** And they sing a new song, saying, Worthy are You to receive the scroll, and to open its seals, because You were slaughtered, and by Your blood purchased us to God out of every tribe and tongue and people and nation,  
**5:10** and made us kings and priests to our God; and we shall reign over the earth.  
**5:11** And I saw, and I heard a sound of many angels around the throne, and the living creatures, and the elders, and their number was myriads of myriads, and thousands of thousands,  
**5:12** saying with a great voice, Worthy is the Lamb having been slaughtered to receive the power and riches and wisdom and strength and honor and glory and blessing.  
**5:13** And every creature which is in Heaven, and in the earth, and underneath the earth, and the things that are on the sea, and the things in all of them, I heard saying: To the <i>One</i> sitting on the throne, and to the Lamb be the blessing and the honor and the glory and the might unto the ages of the ages.  
**5:14** And the four living creatures said, Amen. And the twenty-four elders fell down and worshiped the <i>One</i> living to the ages of <i>the</i> age<i>s</i>.  

## 6장

**6:1** And I saw that the Lamb opened one of the seals. And I heard one of the four living creatures, like a sound of thunder, saying, Come and see.  
**6:2** And I saw, and behold, a white horse! And the <i>One</i> sitting on it had a bow. And a crown was given to him, and He went out conquering, and that He might conquer.  
**6:3** And when He opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature saying, Come and see.  
**6:4** And another horse went out, red, the color of fire. And it was given to the <i>one</i> sitting on <i>it</i> to take peace from the earth, and that they should slay one another. And a great sword was given to him.  
**6:5** And when He opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature saying, Come and see. And I saw, and behold, a black horse, and the <i>one</i> sitting on it having a balance in his hand.  
**6:6** And I heard a voice in <i>the</i> midst of the four living creatures saying, A choenix of wheat <i>for</i> a denarius, and three choenixes of barley <i>for</i> a denarius; and do not harm the oil and the wine.  
**6:7** And when He opened the fourth seal, I heard a voice of the fourth living creature saying, Come and see.  
**6:8** And I saw, and behold, a pale green horse, and the name of the <i>one</i> sitting on it, the name to him <i>was</i> Death; and Hades followed after him. And authority was given to them to kill over the fourth of the earth with sword, and with famine, and with death, and by the wild beasts of the earth.  
**6:9** And when He opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of the <i>ones</i> having been slaughtered for the Word of God, and for the witness which they had.  
**6:10** And they cried with a great voice, saying, Until when, holy and true Master, do You not judge and take vengeance <i>for</i> our blood, from the <i>ones</i> dwelling on the earth?  
**6:11** And there was given to each <i>one</i> a white robe. And it was said to them that they should rest yet a little time, until might be fulfilled also <i>the number</i> of their fellow- slaves and their brothers, the <i>ones</i> being about to be killed, even as they.  
**6:12** And I saw when He opened the sixth seal. And behold, a great earthquake occurred. And the sun became black as sackcloth made of hair; and the moon became as blood;  
**6:13** and the stars of the heaven fell to the earth, as a fig tree being shaken by a great wind casts its unripe figs.  
**6:14** And <i>the</i> heaven was torn away like a scroll being rolled up. And every mountain and island were torn away out of their places.  
**6:15** And the kings of the earth, and the magnates, and the rich <i>ones</i>, and the chiliarchs, and the powerful <i>ones,</i> and every slave, and every free <i>man</i> hid themselves in the caves and in the rocks of the mountains.  
**6:16** And "they said to the mountains" and to the rocks, "Fall on us," and hide us from <i>the</i> face of the <i>One</i> sitting on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb, <n>Hos 10:8</n>  
**6:17** because the great day of His wrath has come; and who is able to stand?  

## 7장

**7:1** And after these things I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth, that wind should not blow on the earth, nor on the sea, nor on every tree.  
**7:2** And I saw another angel coming up from the rising of the sun, having a seal of the living God. And he cried with a great voice to the four angels to whom it was given to them to harm the earth and the sea,  
**7:3** saying, Do not harm the earth, nor the sea, nor the trees, until we seal the slaves of our God on their foreheads.  
**7:4** And I heard the number of the <i>one</i> having been sealed: one hundred forty four thousands, the <i>ones</i> having been sealed out of every tribe of the sons of Israel:  
**7:5** Out of the tribe of Judah, twelve thousand having been sealed. Out of the tribe of Reuben, twelve thousand having been sealed. Out of the tribe of Gad, twelve thousand having been sealed.  
**7:6** Out of the tribe of Asher, twelve thousand having been sealed. Out of the tribe of Naphtali, twelve thousand having been sealed. Out of the tribe of Manasseh, twelve thousand having been sealed.  
**7:7** Out of the tribe of Simeon, twelve thousand having been sealed. Out of the tribe of Levi, twelve thousand having been sealed. Out of the tribe of Issachar, twelve thousand having been sealed.  
**7:8** Out of the tribe of Zebulun, twelve thousand having been sealed. Out of the tribe of Joseph, twelve thousand having been sealed. Out of the tribe of Benjamin, twelve thousand having been sealed.  
**7:9** After these things I saw, and behold, a large multitude which no one was able to number them, out of every nation, even tribes and peoples and languages, standing in front of the throne, and before the Lamb, having been clothed with white robes, and in their hands palm branches.  
**7:10** And they cry with a great voice, saying, Salvation to our God, the <i>One</i> sitting on the throne, and to the Lamb.  
**7:11** And all the angels and of the elders and of the four living creatures stood around the throne. And they fell before the throne on their faces, and worshiped God,  
**7:12** saying, Amen. Blessing and glory and wisdom and thanksgiving and honor and power and strength to our God to the ages of the ages. Amen.  
**7:13** And one of the elders answered, saying to me, These, the <i>ones</i> having been clothed in the white robes, who are they, and from where did they come?  
**7:14** And I said to him, Sir, you know. And he said to me, These are the <i>ones</i> coming out of the great tribulation; and they washed their robes and whitened them in the blood of the Lamb.  
**7:15** Because of this they are before the throne of God, and serve Him day and night in His temple. And the <i>One</i> sitting on the throne tabernacled over them.  
**7:16** And they will not hunger still, nor will they thirst still, nor the sun shall not fall on them, nor any <i>kind of</i> heat, not <i>ever</i>!  
**7:17** Because the Lamb in the midst of the throne will shepherd them, and will lead them on <i>the</i> living springs of waters; and God will wipe off every tear from their eyes.  

## 8장

**8:1** And when He opened the seventh seal, a silence occurred in Heaven, about a half hour.  
**8:2** And I saw the seven angels who stood before God, and seven trumpets were given to them.  
**8:3** And another angel came and stood on the altar, having a golden censer. And many incenses were given to him, that he give them with the prayers of all the saints on the golden altar before the throne.  
**8:4** And the smoke of the incenses went up with the prayers of the saints out of the hand of the angel before God.  
**8:5** And the angel has taken the censer, and has filled it from the fire of the altar, and cast <i>it</i> into the earth; and voices and thunders and lightnings and earthquakes occurred.  
**8:6** And the seven angels, the <i>ones</i> having the seven trumpets, prepared themselves, that they might sound the trumpets.  
**8:7** And the first angel sounded <i>his</i> trumpet. And hail and fire mixed with blood occurred. And it was cast onto the earth; and the third <i>part</i> of the trees was burned down; and all green grass was burned down.  
**8:8** And the second angel sounded <i>his</i> trumpet. And as, <i>it were</i>, a great mountain burning with fire was thrown into the sea. And the third <i>part</i> of the sea became blood;  
**8:9** and the third <i>part</i> of the creatures, the <i>ones</i> having souls, died in the sea; and the third <i>part</i> of the ships was destroyed.  
**8:10** And the third angel trumpeted. And a great burning star, like a lamp, fell out of the heaven. And it fell onto the third <i>part</i> of the rivers, and onto the springs of waters.  
**8:11** And the name of the star is said to be Wormwood. And the third <i>part</i> of the waters became changed into wormwood. And many men died from the waters, because they were made bitter.  
**8:12** And the fourth angel sounded <i>his</i> trumpet. And the third <i>part</i> of the sun, and the third <i>part</i> of the moon, and the third <i>part</i> of the stars, was struck, that the third <i>part</i> of them might be darkened, and the third <i>part</i> of the day might not appear; and likewise the night.  
**8:13** And I saw, and I heard one angel flying in mid-heaven, saying with a great voice, Woe! Woe! Woe to those dwelling on the earth, from the remaining voices of the trumpet of the three angels being about to trumpet!  

## 9장

**9:1** And the fifth angel sounded <i>his</i> trumpet. And I saw a star out of the heaven falling onto the earth. And the key to the pit of the abyss was given to it.  
**9:2** And he opened the pit of the abyss. And smoke went up out of the pit, like smoke of a great furnace. And the sun was darkened, and the air, by the smoke of the pit.  
**9:3** And out of the smoke locusts came forth to the earth. And authority was given to them, as the scorpions of the earth have authority.  
**9:4** And it was said to them that they should not harm the grass of the earth, nor every green thing, nor every tree, if not only the men who do not have the seal of God on their foreheads.  
**9:5** And it was given to them that they should not kill them, but that they be tormented five months. And their torment <i>is</i> as the torment of a scorpion when it strikes a man.  
**9:6** And in those days men will seek death, and they will not find it. And they will long to die, yet death will flee from them.  
**9:7** And the likenesses of the locusts <i>were</i> like horses having been prepared for war; and on their heads as crowns, like gold; and their faces like the faces of men.  
**9:8** And they had hairs like the hairs of women; and their teeth were like those of lions.  
**9:9** And they had breastplates like iron breastplates; and the sound of their wings <i>was</i> like the sound of chariots with many horses running into battle.  
**9:10** And they have tails like scorpions, and their stings were in their tails; and their authority <i>is</i> to harm men five months.  
**9:11** And they have a king over them, the angel of the abyss. In Hebrew his name <i>was</i> Abaddon, and in Greek he <i>has</i> the name Apollyon.  
**9:12** The first woe has gone away; behold, after these things come two woes.  
**9:13** And the sixth angel sounded <i>his</i> trumpet. And I heard one voice out of the four horns of the golden altar before God,

**18:21** And one strong angel lifted a stone like a great millstone, and threw <i>it</i> into the sea, saying, Thus on a violent impulse, Babylon the great city will be thrown down, and it will not still be found, not <i>ever</i>!.  
**18:22** And the sound of harpers, and of musicians, and flutists, and of trumpeters will not still be heard in you, not <i>ever</i>! And every craftsman of every craft will not still be found in you, not <i>ever!</i> And the sound of a mill will not still be heard in you, not <i>ever</i>!  
**18:23** And the light of a lamp will not still shine in you, not <i>ever</i>! And the voice of the bridegroom and bride will not still be heard in you, not <i>ever</i>! For your merchants were the great ones of the earth, for by your sorcery all the nations were led astray.  
**18:24** And in her was found the blood of prophets, and of saints, and of all the <i>ones</i> having been slaughtered on the earth.  

## 19장

**19:3** And secondly they said, Alleluia! Also her smoke goes up to the ages of the ages.  
**19:4** And the twenty-four elders, and the four living creatures fell down and worshiped God sitting on the throne, saying, Amen! Alleluia!  
**19:5** And a voice came out from the throne, saying, Praise our God, all His slaves, and the <i>ones</i> fearing Him, the small <i>ones</i> and the great <i>ones</i>.  
**19:6** And I heard as a sound of a large multitude, and as a sound of many waters, and as a sound of strong thunders, saying, Alleluia! Because the Lord God reigned, the Almighty.  
**19:7** Let us rejoice and let us exult, and we will give glory to Him, because the marriage of the Lamb came, and His wife prepared herself.  
**19:8** And it was given to her that she be clothed in fine linen, pure and bright; for the fine linen is the righteous deeds of the saints.  
**19:9** And he says to me, Write: Blessed <i>are</i> the <i>ones</i> having been called to the supper of the marriage of the Lamb. And he says to me, These are the true words of God.  
**19:10** And I fell before his feet to worship him, but he said to me, See <i>that you</i> do not <i>do it</i>! I am a fellow-slave of yours, and of your brothers, the <i>ones</i> having the testimony of Jesus. Worship God. For the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy.  
**19:11** And I saw Heaven having been opened. And, behold! A white horse, and the <i>One</i> sitting on it being called Faithful and True. And He judges and wars in righteousness.  
**19:12** And His eyes <i>were</i> as a flame of fire, and on His head many diadems, having a name written, which no one knows if not Himself;  
**19:13** and having been clothed in a garment having been dipped in blood. And His name is called The Word of God.  
**19:14** And the armies in Heaven followed Him on white horses, being dressed in fine linen, white and pure.  
**19:15** And out of His mouth goes forth a sharp sword, that with it He might smite the nations. And He will shepherd them with an iron rod. And He treads the winepress of the wine of the anger and of the wrath of God Almighty.  
**19:16** And He has on <i>His</i> garment and on His thigh a name having been written: KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS.  
**19:17** And I saw one angel standing in the sun. And he cried with a great voice, saying to all the birds flying in mid-heaven, Come and gather together to the supper of the great God,  
**19:18** that you may eat the flesh of kings, and <i>the</i> flesh of chiliarchs, and <i>the</i> flesh of strong <i>ones</i>, and <i>the</i> flesh of horses, and of the <i>ones</i> sitting on them, and <i>the</i> flesh of all, both free<i>men</i> and slaves, even of the small <i>ones</i> and great <i>ones</i>.  
**19:19** And I saw the beast and the kings of the earth, and their armies being assembled to make war with the <i>One</i> sitting on the horse, and with His army.  
**19:20** And the beast was seized, and with this <i>one</i> the false prophet, the <i>one</i> doing signs before it, by which he led astray the <i>ones</i> receiving the mark of the beast, and the <i>ones</i> worshiping its image. The two were thrown living into the Lake of Fire burning with brimstone.  
**19:21** And the rest were killed with the sword of the <i>One</i> sitting on the horse, the <i>sword</i> going forth from His mouth. And all the birds were filled from their flesh.  

## 20장

**20:1** And I saw an angel coming down out of Heaven, having the key of the abyss, and a great chain on his hand.  
**20:2** And he laid hold of the dragon, the ancient serpent who is the devil, even Satan, and bound him a thousand years,  
**20:3** and threw him into the abyss, and shut him up, and sealed over him, that he should not mislead the nations any more, until the thousand years are fulfilled. And after these things, he must be set loose a little time.  
**20:4** And I saw thrones, and they sat on them. And judgment was given to them, and the souls of the <i>ones</i> having been beheaded because of the witness of Jesus, and because of the Word of God, and who had not worshiped the beast nor its image, and had not received the mark on their forehead and on their hand. And they lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years.  
**20:5** But the rest of the dead <i>ones</i> did not live again until the thousand years were ended. This <i>is</i> the first resurrection.  
**20:6** Blessed and holy <i>is</i> the <i>one</i> having part in the first resurrection. The second death has no authority over these, but they will be priests of God and of Christ, and will reign with Him a thousand years.  
**20:7** And whenever the thousand years are ended, Satan will be set loose out of his prison,  
**20:8** and he will go to mislead the nations in the four corners of the earth, Gog and Magog, to assemble them into war, whose number <i>is</i> as the sand of the sea.  
**20:9** And they went up over the breadth of the land and encircled the camp of the saints, and the beloved city. And fire from God came down out of Heaven and devoured them.  
**20:10** And the devil leading them astray was thrown into the Lake of Fire and Brimstone, where the beast and the false prophet <i>were</i>. And they were tormented day and night to the ages of the ages.  
**20:11** And I saw a great white throne, and the <i>One</i> sitting on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled; and a place was not found for them.  
**20:12** And I saw the dead <i>ones</i>, the small <i>ones</i> and the great <i>ones</i>, standing before God. And books were opened. And another Book was opened, <i>which is</i> the <i>Book</i> of Life. And the dead <i>ones</i> were judged out of the <i>things</i> written in the books, according to their works.  
**20:13** And the sea gave up the dead <i>ones</i>, the <i>ones</i> in it. And death and hell gave up the dead <i>ones</i>, the <i>ones</i> in them. And they were each <i>one</i> judged according to their works.  
**20:14** And death and hell were thrown into the Lake of Fire. This is the second death.  
**20:15** And if anyone was not found written in the Book of Life, he was thrown into the Lake of Fire.  

## 21장

**21:1** And I saw a new heaven and a new earth, for the first heaven and the first earth passed away, and the sea not still is.  
**21:2** And I, John, saw the holy city, New Jerusalem, coming down out of Heaven from God, having been prepared as a bride, having been adorned for her Husband.  
**21:3** And I heard a great voice out of Heaven, saying, Behold, the tabernacle of God with men! And He will tabernacle with them, and they will be His peoples, and God Himself will be with them as their God.  
**21:4** And God will wipe away every tear from their eyes. And death shall be no longer, nor mourning, nor outcry, nor will <i>there</i> be pain <i>still</i>; for the first things passed away.  
**21:5** And the <i>One</i> sitting on the throne said, Behold! I make all things new. And He says to me, Write, because these words are faithful and true.  
**21:6** And He said to me, It is done! I am the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End. To the <i>one</i> thirsting, I will freely give of the fountain of the water of life.  
**21:7** The <i>one</i> overcoming will inherit all things, and I will be God to him, and he will be the son to Me.  
**21:8** But for the cowardly and faithless <i>ones</i>, and disgusting <i>ones</i>, and murderers, and fornicators, and users of magic, and idolaters, and all liars, their part will be in the Lake burning with fire and brimstone, which is the second death.  
**21:9** And one of the seven angels came to me, the <i>ones</i> having the seven bowls being filled with the seven last plagues, and spoke with me, saying, Come, I will show you the bride, the wife of the Lamb.  
**21:10** And he carried me in spirit on to a great and high mountain, and showed me the great city, holy Jerusalem, coming down out of Heaven from God,  
**21:11** having the glory of God; and its light <i>was</i> like a very precious stone, as a jasper stone, <i>being clear</i> as crystal,  
**21:12** also having a great and high wall, having twelve gates, and twelve angels at the gates and names having been inscribed, which are of the twelve tribes of the sons of Israel.  
**21:13** From the east, three gates; from the north, three gates; from the south, three gates; and from the west, three gates.  
**21:14** And the wall of the city had twelve foundations, and in them the names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.  
**21:15** And he speaking with me had a golden reed, that he may measure the city, and its gates, and its wall.  
**21:16** And the city lies four cornered, even its length as much as the width also. And he measured the city with the reed at twelve thousand stadia; its length and width and height are equal.  
**21:17** And he measured its wall, a hundred <i>and</i> forty four cubits, a measure of a man, which is of an angel.  
**21:18** And the structure of its wall was jasper; and the city <i>was</i> pure gold, pure like glass.  
**21:19** And the foundation of the wall of the city having been adorned with every precious stone: The first foundation, jasper; the second, sapphire; the third, chalcedony; the fourth, emerald;  
**21:20** the fifth, sardonyx; the sixth, sardius; the seventh, chrysolite; the eighth, beryl; the ninth, topaz; the tenth, chrysoprasus; the eleventh, hyacinth; the twelfth, amethyst.  
**21:21** And the twelve gates <i>were</i> twelve pearls; <i>respectively</i> each <i>one</i> of the gates was of one pearl. And the street of the city was pure gold, as transparent glass.  
**21:22** And I saw no temple in it, for the Lord God Almighty is its temple, even the Lamb.  
**21:23** And the city had no need of the sun, nor of the moon, that they might shine in it, for the glory of God illuminated it, and its lamp <i>is</i> the Lamb.  
**21:24** And the nations of the <i>ones</i> saved will walk in its light; and the kings of the earth bring their glory and honor into it.  
**21:25** And its gates may not be shut by day, for no night will be there, not <i>ever</i>!  
**21:26** And they will bring the glory and the honor of the nations into it.  
**21:27** And all profaning may not enter into it, or any making an abomination or a lie; not <i>ever</i>; if not the <i>ones</i> having been written in the Book of Life of the Lamb.
