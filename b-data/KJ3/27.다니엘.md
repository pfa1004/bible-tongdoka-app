# 27.다니엘 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and besieged it.  
**1:2** And the Lord gave Jehoiakim, king of Judah, into his hand, with part of the vessels of the house of God, which he bore into the land of Shinar <i>to</i> the house of his god. And he brought the vessels into the treasure house of his god.  
**1:3** And the king spoke to Ashpenaz, the chief of his eunuchs that he should bring <i>some</i> of the sons of Israel, and of the royal seed, and of the nobles,  
**1:4** young men <i>in</i> whom was no blemish, but who were of good appearance and having understanding in all wisdom, having knowledge and understanding learning, even those <i>with</i> strength in them to stand in the king's palace, and to teach them the writing and the language of the Chaldeans.  
**1:5** And the king set to them the portion of a day in its day, from the king's food, and the wine of his drinking, even to rear them three years, so that at their end they may stand before the king.  
**1:6** And there were among them of the sons of Judah, Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah,  
**1:7** to whom the chief of the eunuchs gave names. For he called Daniel, Belteshazzar; and Hananiah, Shadrach; and Mishael, Meshach; and Azariah, Abed-nego.  
**1:8** And Daniel set on his heart that he would not defile himself with the king's food, or with the wine of his drinking, and he sought of the chief of the eunuchs that he might not defile himself.  
**1:9** And God had given Daniel kindness and compassion before the chief of the eunuchs.  
**1:10** And the chief of the eunuchs said to Daniel, I fear my lord the king, who has chosen your food and your drink. For why should he see your faces worse looking than the boys who <i>are</i> in your circle? And you would endanger my head to the king.  
**1:11** And Daniel said to the guardian, whom the chief of the eunuchs had set over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah,  
**1:12** Please, test your servants ten days. And let <i>some</i> of the vegetables be given to us that we may eat, and water that we may drink.  
**1:13** And let our face be seen before you, our look and the appearance of the boys who eat of the king's food. And as you see, do so with your servants.  
**1:14** And he listened to them in this matter and tested them for ten days.  
**1:15** And at the end of ten days <i>he</i> saw their appearance <i>to be</i> better and fatter of flesh than all the boys who were eating the king's food.  
**1:16** And it happened the guardian took away their food and the wine that they were to drink, and he gave them vegetables.  
**1:17** As for these four boys, God gave them knowledge and skill in all writing and wisdom. And Daniel had understanding in all visions and dreams.  
**1:18** And at the end of the days that the king had said to bring them in, the chief of the eunuchs brought them in before Nebuchadnezzar.  
**1:19** And the king talked with them. And among them all was found none like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah; and they stood before the king.  
**1:20** And <i>in</i> any matter of wisdom <i>and</i> understanding that the king asked from them, he found them ten hands better than all the magicians <i>and</i> the conjurers who <i>were</i> in all his kingdom.  
**1:21** And Daniel continued to the first year of King Cyrus.  

## 2장

**2:1** And in the second year of the reign of Nebuchadnezzar, Nebuchadnezzar was dreaming dreams, and his spirit was troubled, and his sleep was finished <i>from</i> upon him.  
**2:2** And the king said to call to the magicians, and to the conjurers, and to the sorcerers and to the Chaldeans, to declare to the king his dreams. And they came and stood before the king.  
**2:3** And the king said to them, I have dreamed a dream, and my spirit was disturbed to know the dream.  
**2:4** And the Chaldeans spoke to the king <i>in</i> Aramaic: O king, live forever! Tell your servants the dream, and we will reveal the meaning.  
**2:5** The king answered and said to the Chaldeans, The word is gone from me. If you will not make known the dream and its meaning to me, you will be made into <i>mere</i> members and your houses shall be made an outhouse.  
**2:6** But if you make known the dream, you shall receive gifts and a present and great honor from before me. Therefore, reveal the dream and its meaning to me.  
**2:7** They again answered and said, Let the king tell his servants the dream, and we will reveal its meaning.  
**2:8** The king answered and said, For I know surely that you buy time, that on account of all, you see the thing is certainly gone from me.  
**2:9** But if you will not reveal the dream to me it <i>is</i> your edict; for you have agreed upon lying and corrupting words to speak before me until the time has changed. Therefore, tell me the dream, and I shall know that you can reveal the meaning to me.  
**2:10** The Chaldeans replied before the king and said, There is not a man on the earth who can reveal the king's matter, because not any king, lord, or ruler has asked such a thing from any magician, or conjurer, or Chaldean.  
**2:11** And the thing that the king asks <i>is</i> rare. And there is no other who may reveal it before the king, except the gods, whose dwelling is not with flesh.  
**2:12** Accordingly all this was exceedingly enraging and angering <i>to</i> the king. And he commanded to destroy all the wise <i>men</i> of Babylon.  
**2:13** And the decree went out that the wise <i>men</i> should be killed. And they sought Daniel and his companions, to be killed.  
**2:14** Then Daniel answered <i>with</i> counsel and insight to Arioch the king's chief of the executioners, who had gone out to kill the wise <i>men</i> of Babylon.  
**2:15** He answered and said to Arioch, the king's ruling <i>officer</i>, Why <i>is</i> the decree so hasty from before the king? And Arioch made the thing known to Daniel.  
**2:16** And Daniel went in and asked of the king that he would give him time, and he would show the king the meaning.  
**2:17** Then Daniel went to his house and declared the thing to Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, his companions,  
**2:18** that they might pray for the mercies of God in Heaven about this secret, that Daniel and his companions should not perish with the rest of the wise <i>men</i> of Babylon.  
**2:19** Then the secret was revealed to Daniel in a vision of the night, and Daniel blessed the God of Heaven.  
**2:20** Daniel answered and said, Blessed be the name of God forever, even until ever, for wisdom and might <i>are</i> His.  
**2:21** And He changes the times and the seasons; He causes kings to pass away, and sets up kings; He gives wisdom to the wise <i>men</i>, and knowledge to those who know understanding.  
**2:22** He reveals the deep and secret things; He knows what <i>is</i> in the darkness, and the light dwells in Him.  
**2:23** I thank You and praise You, O God of my fathers, who has given me wisdom and might, and has made me know what we ask of You. For You have made known to us the king's matter.  
**2:24** All on account of this Daniel went in to Arioch, whom the king had chosen to destroy the wise <i>men</i> of Babylon. He went and said this to him, Do not destroy the wise <i>men</i> of Babylon. Bring me in before the king, and I will interpret the meaning to the king.  
**2:25** Then Arioch hurried to bring in Daniel before the king and thus he spoke to him, I have found a man of the captives of Judah who will make the meaning known to the king.  
**2:26** The king answered and said to Daniel, whose name <i>was</i> Belteshazzar, Are you able to make known to me the dream which I have seen, and its meaning?  
**2:27** Daniel answered before the king and said, The secret which the king has asked the wise <i>men</i>, the conjurers, the magicians, or those determining, not are able to interpret to the king.  
**2:28** But there is a God in Heaven who reveals secrets, and makes known to King Nebuchadnezzar what shall be in the latter days. Your dream, and the visions of your head on your bed, are these:  
**2:29** As for you, O king, your thoughts came up <i>on you</i> on your bed, what should happen after this. And He who reveals secrets makes known to you what shall come to pass.  
**2:30** But as for me, this secret is not revealed to me for <i>any</i> wisdom that I have more than any living man, but so that the meaning might be made known to the king, and that you might know the thoughts of your heart.  
**2:31** You, O king, were seeing. And, behold, a certain great image! This great image stood before you with a brilliant brightness, and its form <i>was</i> terrible.  
**2:32** The head of this image <i>was</i> of fine gold, its breast and its arms <i>were</i> of silver, its belly and its thighs <i>were</i> of bronze,  
**2:33** its legs <i>were</i> of iron, its feet <i>were</i> partly of iron and partly of clay.  
**2:34** You continued seeing until a stone was cut out without hands, which struck the image on its feet of iron and clay, and shattered them.  
**2:35** Then the iron, the clay, the bronze, the silver, and the gold were together shattered, and <i>they</i> became like the chaff of the summer threshing-floors. And the wind bore them away so that not any place was found for them. And the stone that struck the image became a great mountain and filled the whole earth.  
**2:36** This <i>is</i> the dream, and we will tell the meaning of it before the king.  
**2:37** You O king <i>are</i> the king of kings. For the God of Heaven has given you the kingdom, the power, and the strength, and the honor.  
**2:38** And wherever the sons of men, the animals of the field, and the fowl of the heavens dwell, He has given <i>them</i> into your hand, and has made you ruler over them all. You are that head of gold.  
**2:39** And in your place shall arise another kingdom lower than yours, and another third kingdom of bronze, which shall rule in all the earth.  
**2:40** And the fourth kingdom shall be <i>as</i> strong as iron. Inasmuch as iron crushes and smashes all things, and as the iron that shatters all these, it will crush and shatter.  
**2:41** And as to that you saw, the feet and the toes <i>were</i> partly of potters' clay and partly of iron, <i>the</i> kingdom shall be divided. But there shall be in it the strength of iron, because you saw the iron mixed with clay of the potter.  
**2:42** And <i>as</i> the toes of the feet <i>were</i> partly of iron and partly of clay, <i>so</i> the kingdom shall be partly strong and partly fragile.  
**2:43** And as you saw the iron mixed with the clay of the clay, they shall be mixed with the seed of men. But they shall not cleave to one another, even as iron does not meld with clay.  
**2:44** And in the days of these kings, the God of Heaven shall set up a kingdom which shall never be destroyed. And the kingdom shall not be left to another people. It shall crush and destroy all these kingdoms, and it shall stand forever.  
**2:45** Because you saw that <i>the</i> stone was cut out of the mountain without hands, and that it broke the iron, the bronze, the clay, the silver, and the gold in pieces. The great God has made known to the king what shall occur after this, and the dream <i>is</i> true, and the meaning of it is trustworthy.  
**2:46** Then King Nebuchadnezzar fell on his face and did homage to Daniel. And he commanded an offering, even to offer incense to him.  
**2:47** The king answered Daniel and said, Of truth your God <i>is</i> a God of gods and a Lord of kings, and One revealing secrets, since you were able to reveal this secret.  
**2:48** Then the king made Daniel great and gave many great gifts to him. And he made him ruler over all the province of Babylon, and chief of the prefects over all the wise <i>men</i> of Babylon.  
**2:49** And Daniel asked of the king, and he appointed Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego <i>to be</i> over the affairs of the province of Babylon. But Daniel <i>sat</i> in the gate of the king.  

## 3장

**3:1** Nebuchadnezzar the king made an image of gold whose height <i>was</i> sixty cubits; its breadth, six cubits. He set it up in the plain of Dura, in the province of Babylon.  
**3:2** Then Nebuchadnezzar the king sent to gather the satraps, the prefects, the governors, the counselors, the treasurers, the judges <i>and</i> the magistrates. And all the officials of the provinces <i>were ordered</i> to come to the dedication of the image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.  
**3:3** And were gathered the satraps, the prefects, the governors, the counselors, the treasurers, the judges, the magistrates, and all the officials of the provinces, to the dedication of the image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up. And they stood before the image that Nebuchadnezzar had set up.  
**3:4** And the herald shouted with strength: To you it is commanded O peoples, nations, and languages,  
**3:5** at the time you hear the sound of the horn, the pipe, zither, the lyre, harp, bagpipe, and all kinds of music, you shall fall down and do homage to the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king has set up.  
**3:6** And whoever does not fall down and do homage, at that moment they will be thrown into the midst of a furnace of burning fire.  
**3:7** All accordingly <i>did</i> this, at that time when all the people heard the sound of the horn, the pipe, zither, the lyre, harp, and all kinds of music, all the peoples, the nations, and the languages, fell down, doing homage to the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.  
**3:8** All accordingly at that time men, Chaldeans, came near and ate the piece of the Jews.  
**3:9** They answered and said to Nebuchadnezzar the king, O king, live forever!  
**3:10** You, O king, have made a decree that every man who shall hear the sound of the horn, the pipe, zither, the lyre, harp, and the bagpipe, and all kinds of music, shall fall down and do homage to the golden image.  
**3:11** And whoever does not fall down and do homage, he should be thrown into the middle of a furnace of burning fire.  
**3:12** There are men, Jewish <i>men</i>, whom you have set over the business of the province of Babylon: Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. These men do not fix attention on you, O king, <i>on your</i> decree They do not serve your gods, not doing homage to the golden image which you have set up.  
**3:13** Then Nebuchadnezzar in anger and wrath commanded to bring Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego. Then they brought these men before the king.  
**3:14** Nebuchadnezzar answered and said to them, <i>Is it</i> true, O Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego <i>that</i> you are not serving my gods, <i>and</i> not doing homage to the golden image which I have set up?  
**3:15** Now if you are ready, at the time you hear the sound of the horn, the pipe, zither, the lyre, harp, and bagpipe, and all kinds of music, fall down and do homage to the image which I have made. But if you do not do homage, in that moment you shall be thrown into the middle of a burning furnace of fire. And who is that god who shall deliver you out of my hand?  
**3:16** Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego answered and said to the king, O Nebuchadnezzar, we have no need to return a word to you on this matter.  
**3:17** If it is <i>that</i> our God whom we serve is able to deliver us from the burning, fiery furnace, even He will deliver out of your hand, O king.  
**3:18** And if not, let it be known to you, O king, that we will not serve your gods or do homage to the golden image which you have set up.  
**3:19** Then Nebuchadnezzar was filled with wrath, and the form of his face was changed against Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. He spoke and commanded to heat the furnace seven times more than it was customary to heat it.  
**3:20** And he commanded mighty men of strength in his army to bind Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, <i>and</i> to throw <i>them</i> into the furnace of burning fire.  
**3:21** Then these men were bound in their slippers, their tunics, and their mantles, and their <i>other</i> clothes, and were thrown into the midst of the furnace of burning fire.  
**3:22** All accordingly <i>did</i> this, because the king's command was urgent, and the furnace was heated exceedingly, the flame of the fire killed those men who took up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego.  
**3:23** But those men, the three of them, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, fell down bound into the midst of the furnace of burning fire.  
**3:24** Then Nebuchadnezzar the king was amazed. And he rose up in haste; he spoke and said to his royal officials, Did we not cast three men bound into the midst of the fire? They replied and said to the king, True, O king.  
**3:25** He answered and said, Behold! I see four men loose, walking in the midst of the fire, and there is no injury among them. and the form of the fourth is like a son of <i>the</i> gods.  
**3:26** Then Nebuchadnezzar drew near to the door of the furnace of burning fire. He answered and said, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, servants of the Most High God, come out and come <i>here</i>. Then Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego came out of the midst of the fire.  
**3:27** And the satraps, the prefects, the governors, and the king's officials gathered. And <i>they</i> saw these men <i>on whose</i> bodies the fire had no power, and the hair of their head was not singed, and their slippers were not changed, and the smell of fire did not cling on them.  
**3:28** Nebuchadnezzar answered and said, Blessed <i>be</i> the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, who has sent His Angel and has delivered His servants who trusted in Him, and have changed the king's words, and have given their bodies that they might not serve and not do homage to any god except their own God.  
**3:29** And a decree is set out by me, that every people, nation, and language who speak anything amiss about the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, he will be made into members, and his house shall be made an outhouse. Because there is no other God who is able to deliver like this.  
**3:30** Then the king made Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego prosper in the province of Babylon.  

## 4장

**4:1** Nebuchadnezzar the king to all the peoples, the nations, and the tongues that dwell in all the earth: Your peace be great.  
**4:2** It seemed good before me to declare the signs and wonders that the Most High God has done with me.  
**4:3** How great <i>are</i> His signs! And how mighty <i>are</i> His wonders! His kingdom <i>is</i> an everlasting kingdom, and His rule <i>is</i> from generation and generation.  
**4:4** I, Nebuchadnezzar, was at ease in my house, and flourishing in my palace.  
**4:5** I saw a dream, and it terrified me, and <i>the</i> thoughts on my bed and visions of my head troubled me.  
**4:6** And I made a decree to bring in all the wise <i>men</i> of Babylon before me, that they might make known to me the meaning of the dream.  
**4:7** Then the magicians, the conjurers, the Chaldeans, and <i>those</i> determining came in. And I told the dream before them, but they did not make its meaning known to me.  
**4:8** But at last Daniel came in before me, whose name <i>was</i> Belteshazzar, according to the name of my god, and in whom <i>is</i> the spirit of the holy gods. And I spoke the dream before him, <i>saying</i>,  
**4:9** O Belteshazzar, chief of the magicians, because I know that the spirit of the holy gods <i>is</i> in you, and no secret constrains you, speak <i>to me</i> the visions of my dream that I have seen, and its meaning.  
**4:10** As to the visions of my head on my bed, I was looking, and, behold, a tree <i>was</i> in the midst of the earth. And its height <i>was</i> great.  
**4:11** The tree became great and strong, and its height reached to the heaven, and its appearance to the end of all the earth.  
**4:12** Its leaves <i>were</i> beautiful, and its fruit plentiful, and food for all <i>was</i> in it. The beast of the field sought shade under it, and the birds of the heaven dwelt in its branches, and all flesh was fed from it.  
**4:13** I was looking in the visions of my head on my bed. And, behold, a watcher, even a holy one, came down from the heaven.  
**4:14** He cried with might and thus he said, Cut down the tree and cut off its branches. Shake off its leaves and scatter its fruit. Let the beast flee from under it, and the birds <i>leave</i> its branches.  
**4:15** But leave the stump of its roots in the earth, even with a fetter of iron and bronze, in the grass of the field. And <i>let</i> it <i>be</i> wet with the dew of the heaven, and his lot <i>be</i> with the beast in the grass of the earth.  
**4:16** Let his heart be changed from man's, and let the heart of a beast be given to him. And let seven times pass over him.  
**4:17** The word <i>is</i> by the decree of the watchers, and <i>the</i> command and the affair <i>is</i> of the holy <i>ones</i>, so that the living may know that the Most High <i>is</i> ruling in the kingdom of men and gives it to whomever He will. And <i>He</i> sets up over it the lowliest of men.  
**4:18** And this is the dream I, king Nebuchadnezzar, have seen. And you, O Belteshazzar, speak <i>to me</i> its meaning. All accordingly all this the wise <i>men</i> of my kingdom are not able to make known the meaning to me. But you are able, for the spirit of the holy gods <i>is</i> in you.  
**4:19** Then Daniel, whose name <i>was</i> Belteshazzar, was stunned for one moment, and his thoughts troubled him. The king answered and said, O Belteshazzar, do not let the dream or its meaning trouble you. Belteshazzar answered and said, My lord, the dream <i>is</i> to those who hate you, and its meaning <i>is</i> to your enemies.  
**4:20** The tree that you saw, which became great and strong, whose height reached to the heaven and its appearance to all the earth,  
**4:21** and its leaves <i>being</i> beautiful, and its fruit plentiful, and food for all <i>being</i> in it, under which the beast of the field dwelt, and in its branches the birds of the heaven dwelt:  
**4:22** It <i>was</i> you, O king, for you have become great and strong. For your greatness has grown and reaches to the heaven, and your rule <i>is</i> to the end of the earth.  
**4:23** And as the king saw a watcher and a holy <i>one</i> coming down from the heaven, and saying, Cut the tree down, and destroy it. Yet leave the stump of its roots in the earth, even with a band of iron and bronze, in the grass of the field. And let him be wet with the dew of the heavens, and his lot <i>be</i> with the beast of the field, until seven times pass over him.  
**4:24** This <i>is</i> the meaning, O king, and this the decree of the Most High, which has come on my lord the king:  
**4:25** And you shall be driven from men, and your abode shall be with the beast of the field. And you shall be fed with grass like oxen. And you shall be wet <i>with</i> dew of the heaven; and seven times shall pass over you until you have known that the Most High is ruling in the kingdom of men, and He gives it to whomever He desires.  
**4:26** And in that they commanded to leave the stump of the tree roots, Your kingdom <i>shall be</i> enduring to you after you have known that Heaven <i>is</i> ruling.  
**4:27** So, O king, let my counsel be pleasing to you: Even break off your sins by righteousness, and your iniquities by pitying the humble <i>one</i>, whether there will be duration to your prosperity.  
**4:28** All <i>this</i> came on Nebuchadnezzar the king.  
**4:29** At the end of twelve months, he was walking in the palace of the kingdom of Babylon.  
**4:30** The king answered and said, <i>Is</i> this not great Babylon that I have built for the house of the kingdom, by the might of my power, and for the honor of my majesty?  
**4:31** The word <i>was</i> still in the king's mouth <i>when</i> a voice fell from the heaven, <i>saying</i>, O King Nebuchadnezzar, to you it is declared: The kingdom has been taken from you!  
**4:32** And you shall be driven from men, and your abode <i>will be</i> with the beast of the field. You shall be fed with the grass like oxen, and seven times shall pass by over you until you know that the Most High <i>is</i> ruling in the kingdom of men, and that He gives it to whomever He desires.  
**4:33** In its moment the thing was fulfilled on Nebuchadnezzar. And he was driven from men, and he ate the grass like oxen, and his body was wet with the dew of the heaven, until his hair had grown like eagles' <i>feathers</i>, and his nails like birds' <i>claws</i>.  
**4:34** And at the end of the days, I, Nebuchadnezzar, lifted up my eyes to <i>the</i> heaven, and my understanding returned to me, and I blessed the Most High. And I praised and honored Him who lives forever, whose dominion <i>is</i> an everlasting dominion, and His kingdom from generation and generation.  
**4:35** And all those living in the earth are counted as nothing. And He does according to His will among the army of Heaven, and <i>among</i> those living <i>in</i> the earth. And no one is able to strike His hand or say to Him, What are You doing?  
**4:36** In its moment my reason and the glory of my kingdom returned to me, my majesty, and my brightness returned to me. And my advisers and my nobles sought to me. And I was re-established in my kingdom, and excellent greatness was added to me.  
**4:37** Now I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise and exalt and honor the King of Heaven, for all His works are truth, and His ways are justice. And He is able to humble those who walk in pride.  

## 5장

**5:1** Belshazzar the king made a great feast to a thousand of his nobles. And he drank wine before the thousand.  
**5:2** When tasting the wine, Belshazzar commanded the golden and silver vessels brought, those his father Nebuchadnezzar had taken out of the temple which <i>was</i> in Jerusalem, that the king and his nobles, his wives, and his concubines might drink with them.  
**5:3** Then they brought the vessels of gold that were taken out of the temple of the house of God which <i>was</i> in Jerusalem. And the king, his nobles, his wives, and his concubines drank with them.  
**5:4** They drank wine and praised the gods of gold, and of silver, and of bronze, of iron, wood, and stone.  
**5:5** In that moment fingers of a man's hand came out and wrote on the plaster of the wall of the king's palace before the lampstand. And the king saw the palm of the hand that wrote.  
**5:6** Then the king's brightness was changed, and his thoughts troubled him, and the joints of his loins shook, and his knees knocked one against this <i>other</i>.  
**5:7** The king cried with strength to bring in the conjurers, the Chaldeans, and those determining. The king answered and said to the wise <i>men</i> of Babylon, Any man who can read this writing and reveal its meaning shall be clothed with purple, and <i>have</i> a chain of gold around his neck. And he shall rule third in the kingdom.  
**5:8** Then all the king's wise <i>men</i> came in. But they could not read the writing or make the meaning known to the king.  
**5:9** Then King Belshazzar was greatly troubled, and his face was changing on him, and his nobles were perplexed.  
**5:10** The queen came in to the banquet house because of the words of the king and his nobles. The queen answered and said, O king, live forever, do not let your thoughts terrify you, and do not let your face be changed.  
**5:11** There <i>is</i> a man in your kingdom in whom <i>is</i> the spirit of the holy gods. And in the days of your father, light and understanding and wisdom was found in him, like the wisdom of the gods. The king answered and said, The thing <i>is</i> certain, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which does not pass away.

**10:18** And he added, and touched me, <i>one</i> in form as a man, and made me strong.  
**10:19** And he said, O man greatly beloved, do not fear. Peace to you. Be strong. Yea, be strong! And when he had spoken to me I was made strong. And <i>I</i> said, Speak, my lord, for you have made me strong.  
**10:20** And he said, Do you know why I have come to you? And now I will return to fight with the ruler of Persia. And when I have gone out, even, behold, the ruler of Greece shall come.  
**10:21** But I will tell you what is inscribed in the Writing of Truth. And no one makes himself strong with me in these things except Michael your Ruler.  

## 11장

**11:1** And I, in the first year of Darius the Mede, I was standing for <i>one</i> who strengthens, and for a stronghold for him.  
**11:2** And now I will declare the truth to you: Behold! Three kings shall stand up in Persia; and the fourth <i>shall be</i> rich <i>in</i> all greater riches. And when he is made strong by his riches he shall stir up all the kingdom of Greece.  
**11:3** And a mighty king shall stand up and shall rule with great authority and do according to his will.  
**11:4** And when he stands up his kingdom shall be shattered and shall be divided to the four winds of the heavens, and not to his posterity, and not according to his authority with which he ruled. For his kingdom shall be pulled up and not to others besides these.  
**11:5** And the king of the south shall be strong. And <i>one</i> of his rulers, even he will be strong on him, and he will rule. His rule <i>shall be</i> a great rule.  
**11:6** And at the end of the years they shall join together, and the king of the south's daughter shall come to the king of the north to make <i>full</i> equity, but she shall not keep the power of the arm. And he will not stand, and not his arm. But she and those who brought her <i>shall be</i> given up, also her boy, and <i>he</i> making her strong in <i>these</i> times.  
**11:7** But the shoots of her roots will stand <i>in</i> his place, and he shall come to the army and will enter into the fortress of the king of the north. And he will act against them and will show strength.  
**11:8** And he will also bring their gods with their molten images, with silver and gold vessels of their possessions, into exile <i>to</i> Egypt. And <i>for</i> years he will stand away from the king of the north.  
**11:9** And the king of the south will come into <i>his</i> kingdom and will return to his own land.  
**11:10** But his sons shall be stirred up and shall gather a multitude of great forces. And coming <i>one</i> shall come and overflow and pass through. And he will return to his fortress and be stirred up.  
**11:11** And the king of the south will be bitter and will go out and fight with him, with the king of the north. And he shall raise a great multitude, but the multitude shall be given into his hand.  
**11:12** And having borne away the multitude, his heart will be lifted up. And he will cause myriads to fall, but he shall not be strong.  
**11:13** For the king of the north shall return and raise a greater multitude than the former. And at the end of times, years, coming he shall come with a great army and with much baggage.  
**11:14** And in those times many shall stand up against the king of the south. And the sons of the breakers of your people shall rise up to make stand <i>the</i> vision, but they shall stumble.  
**11:15** And the king of the north shall come and pour out a siege mound and seize a guarded fortification. And the arms of the south shall not stand, and his choice people, for there <i>will be</i> no firmness to stand.  
**11:16** But he who comes against him will do as he wills, and none shall stand before him. And he shall stand in the glorious land, and annihilation <i>shall be</i> in his hand.  
**11:17** And he shall set his face to go in with the might of his whole kingdom, and upright <i>ones</i> with him; and he shall do. And <i>he</i> shall give the daughter of women to him, to destroy it. But she shall not stand, and not be for him.  
**11:18** And he shall turn his face to the coastlands and shall capture many. But a ruler shall make cease his reproach for him, but his reproach shall return to him.  
**11:19** And he shall turn his face toward the fortresses of his own land, but he will stumble and fall and shall not be found.  
**11:20** And <i>one</i> passing over, a <i>tax</i> exacter, shall stand in his place, <i>for</i> the glory of <i>the</i> kingdom. But within one <i>of the</i> days he will be shattered, but not in anger and not in battle.  
**11:21** And a despised one shall stand up on his place, and they shall not give to him the honor of the king. But he shall come in while at ease and make strong <i>the</i> kingdom by intrigues.  
**11:22** And the arms of the overflow shall be swept from before his face, and they will be shattered, and also the ruler of a covenant.  
**11:23** And after they join themselves to him, he will practice deceit; for he shall come and shall be strong with a few people.  
**11:24** He will go in safely, even into the rich places of <i>the</i> province. And he shall do what his fathers have not done, nor his fathers' fathers. He shall plunder and spoil and scatter goods among them. And he shall devise his plots against the strongholds, even for a time.  
**11:25** And he will stir up his power and his heart against the king of the south with a great army. And the king of the south will be stirred up to battle with a great and very mighty army. But he shall not stand, for they will devise plots against him.  
**11:26** Yea, those who eat his food shall shatter him, and his army shall overflow. And many will fall down slain.  
**11:27** And both of them, the kings, shall have in their hearts to do evil, and they will speak lies at one table but will not prosper, for <i>the</i> end still <i>shall be</i> at the appointed time.  
**11:28** And he will return to his land with great wealth. And his heart <i>shall be</i> against <i>the</i> holy covenant. And he will act, and he shall return to his land.  
**11:29** At the appointed time he will return and come against the south, but it will not be as the former or as the latter.  
**11:30** For the ships of Kittim will come against him, and he will be pained and turn back and be furious against <i>the</i> holy covenant. And he will work, and he will return and heed <i>the</i> forsakers of the holy covenant.  
**11:31** And forces will stand away from him, and they will pollute the sanctuary, the stronghold. And <i>they</i> shall turn aside the continual <i>sacrifice</i>, and they will place the abomination that desolates.  
**11:32** And he will defile by flatteries those who do evil against <i>the</i> covenant. But the people who know their God will be strong and will work.  
**11:33** And those who ponder among <i>the</i> people will instruct many, and they will stumble by the sword and by flame, by exile and spoil <i>for</i> days.  
**11:34** And when they shall stumble, they will be helped with a little help, but many will join them with hypocrisy.  
**11:35** And <i>many</i> of those who ponder shall stumble, to refine and to purge them, and <i>to</i> make white to the time of <i>the</i> end; for <i>it is</i> yet for the appointed time.  
**11:36** And the king shall do according to his will. And he shall exalt and magnify <i>himself</i> above every god; <i>he</i> shall even speak marvelous things against the God of gods and shall prosper until the fury is finished. For that which is decreed shall be done.  
**11:37** He shall not consider the God of his fathers, and not the desire of women; yea, he will not come to any god. For he shall magnify himself above all.  
**11:38** But in his place he shall honor the god of strongholds, and he shall honor a god whom his fathers did not know, with gold and silver and with precious stones, and desirable things.  
**11:39** And he will act in the fortifications of <i>the</i> strongholds with an alien god, whom he shall acknowledge. He shall multiply in glory, and he shall cause them to rule over many and shall divide the land for a price.  
**11:40** And at the end time, the king of the south will press against him. And the king of the north shall come against him like a tempest, with chariots and with horsemen and with many ships. And he shall go into the lands and shall overflow and pass over.  
**11:41** And he shall go in to the glorious land, and many will be stumbled. But these shall escape out of his hand: Edom and Moab, and the chief of the sons of Ammon.  
**11:42** And his hand will stretch out against the lands, and the land of Egypt shall not escape.  
**11:43** But he will rule over the treasures of gold and silver, and over all Egypt's desirable things. And the Libyans and the Ethiopians <i>shall be</i> at his steps.  
**11:44** But news shall trouble him from the east and from the north. And he will go out with great fury to destroy and to devote many to destruction.  
**11:45** And he shall plant the tents of his palace between <i>the</i> seas, in the glorious holy mountain. Yet he shall go to his end, and there is not a helper for him.  

## 12장

**12:1** And at that time, Michael shall stand up, the great Ruler who stands for the sons of your people. And there shall be a time of distress, such as has not been from the being of a nation until that time. And at that time, your people shall be delivered, everyone that shall be found written in the Book.  
**12:2** And many of the sleeping <i>ones</i> in the dust of the ground shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to reproaches <i>and</i> to everlasting abhorrence.  
**12:3** And those who act wisely shall shine as the shining of the expanse, and those turning many to righteousness as the stars forever and ever.  
**12:4** But you, O Daniel, shut up the words and seal the book, to the end time. Many shall roam around, and knowledge shall be increased.  
**12:5** And I, Daniel, saw, and, behold, Two others stood there, the one on this <i>side</i>, and one on that <i>side</i> of the river's lip.  
**12:6** And <i>one</i> said to the man clothed in linen, who <i>was</i> on top of the waters of the river, Until when <i>is</i> the end of the wonders?  
**12:7** And I heard the man clothed in linen, who <i>was</i> on top of the waters of the river, and he held up his right and his left <i>hand</i> to the heavens and swore by Him who lives forever, that it <i>shall be</i> for a time, times, and a half. And when they have made an end of dispersing the power of the holy people, all these <i>things</i> shall be finished.  
**12:8** And I heard, but I did not understand. and I said, O my lord, what <i>shall be</i> the end of these <i>things</i>?  
**12:9** And He said, Go, Daniel, for the words <i>are</i> closed up and sealed until the end time.  
**12:10** Many shall be purified and made white and tested. But the wicked <i>ones</i> shall do wickedly. And not one of the wicked <i>ones</i> shall understand, but the ones acting wisely shall understand.  
**12:11** And from the time the continual <i>sacrifice</i> shall be taken away, and the abomination that desolates given up, a thousand, two hundred and ninety days <i>shall occur</i>.  
**12:12** Blessed <i>is</i> he who waits and comes to the thousand, three hundred and thirty-five days.  
**12:13** But you go on to the end, for you shall rest and stand for your lot at the end of the days.
