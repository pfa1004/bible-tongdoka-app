# 53.데살로니가후서 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul and Silvanus and Timothy to the assembly of <i>the</i> Thessalonians in God our Father and <i>the</i> Lord Jesus Christ:  
**1:2** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and <i>the</i> Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:3** Brothers, we ought always to give thanks to God about you, even as it is right, because your faith grows exceedingly, and the love of each <i>one</i> of all of you multiplies toward one another,  
**1:4** so as for us to boast ourselves in you in the assemblies of God for your patient endurance and faith in all your persecutions, and the afflictions which you endure,  
**1:5** a clear token of the just judgment of God, for you to be counted worthy of the kingdom of God, for which you indeed suffer;  
**1:6** since <i>it is</i> a just thing with God to repay affliction to the <i>ones</i> afflicting you,  
**1:7** and to <i>give</i> you, the <i>ones</i> being afflicted, rest with us at the revelation of the Lord Jesus from Heaven with angels of His power,  
**1:8** in flaming fire giving full vengeance to the <i>ones</i> not knowing God, and to the <i>ones</i> not obeying the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ, <n>Isa 66:15; Jer 10:25</n>  
**1:9** who will pay the penalty: everlasting destruction from the face of the Lord, "and from the glory of His strength," <n>Isa 2:19</n>  
**1:10** when He comes to be glorified in His saints, and to be marveled at among all the <i>ones</i> having believed in that Day, because our testimony to you was believed.  
**1:11** For which we also continually pray concerning you, that our God would deem you worthy of the calling, and would fulfill all the good pleasure of <i>His</i> goodness, and work of faith in power,  
**1:12** so that the name of our Lord Jesus Christ may be glorified in you, and you in Him, according to the grace of our God and of <i>the</i> Lord Jesus Christ.  

## 2장

**2:1** And, brothers, we entreat you, by the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, and of our gathering together to Him,  
**2:2** for you not to be quickly shaken <i>in</i> the mind, nor to be disturbed, neither through a spirit, nor through speech, nor through letter, as through us, <i>that</i> the Day of Christ has come.  
**2:3** Do not let anyone deceive you, by no manner, because <i>that Day will not come</i> if not the apostasy comes first and the man of sin is revealed, the son of perdition,  
**2:4** the <i>one</i> opposing and exalting himself over every <i>thing</i> being called God, or object of worship, so as for him "to sit in the temple of God" as God, setting forth himself, that he is God. <n>Dan 11:36; Eze 28:2</n>  
**2:5** Do you not remember that I told you these things, I yet being with you?  
**2:6** And now you know the <i>thing</i> holding back, for him to be revealed in his time.  
**2:7** For the mystery of lawlessness already is working, only He <i>is</i> restraining now, until He comes out of the midst.  
**2:8** And then "the lawless <i>one</i>" will be revealed, "whom" "the Lord" "will consume" "by the spirit of His mouth," and will bring to nought by the appearance of His presence. <n>Isa 11:4</n>  
**2:9** <i>His</i> coming is according to the working of Satan in all power and <i>miraculous</i> signs and lying wonders,  
**2:10** and in all deception of unrighteousness in the <i>ones</i> perishing, because they did not receive the love of the truth in order for them to be saved.  
**2:11** And because of this, God will send to them a working of error, for them to believe the lie,  
**2:12** that all may be judged, the <i>ones</i> not believing the truth, but being well-pleased in unrighteousness.  
**2:13** But we ought to thank God always concerning you, brothers, having been loved by the Lord, because God chose you from the beginning to salvation in sanctification of <i>the</i> Spirit and belief of <i>the</i> truth,  
**2:14** to which He called you through our gospel, to obtain the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**2:15** So, then, brothers, stand firm and strongly hold the teachings you were taught, whether by word or by our letter.  
**2:16** But may our Lord Himself, Jesus Christ, and our God and Father, the <i>One</i> having loved us and having given everlasting comfort and good hope by grace,  
**2:17** encourage your hearts, and may He establish you in every good word and work.  

## 3장

**3:1** For the rest, brothers, pray concerning us, that the Word of the Lord may run and be glorified, even as also <i>it has</i> with you,  
**3:2** and that we may be delivered from harmful and evil men. For the faith <i>is</i> not of all <i>men</i>.  
**3:3** But the Lord is faithful who will establish and will guard <i>you</i> from the evil.  
**3:4** But we are persuaded in <i>the</i> Lord as to you, that whatever things we command to you, you both are doing and you will do.  
**3:5** And the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God and into the patience of Christ.  
**3:6** And we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to draw yourselves back from every brother walking in a disorderly way, and not according to the teaching which you received from us.  
**3:7** For you yourselves know how it is right to act like us, because we were not disorderly among you;  
**3:8** nor did we eat bread from anyone <i>as</i> a gift, but by labor and toil, working night and day in order not to burden any of you.  
**3:9** Not that we do not have authority, but that we give ourselves <i>as</i> an example to you, for you to act like us.  
**3:10** For even when we were with you, we commanded this to you: If anyone wills not to work, neither let him eat.  
**3:11** For we hear some are walking in a disorderly way among you, not working at all, but bustling about uselessly.  
**3:12** And we command to such <i>ones,</i> and exhort through our Lord Jesus Christ that, working with quietness, they may eat their own bread.  
**3:13** And you, brothers, do not lose heart in well doing.  
**3:14** But if anyone does not obey our word through the letter, mark this <i>one</i>, and do not associate with him, that he be shamed.  
**3:15** But do not count <i>him a</i> hostile <i>one</i>, but warn <i>him</i> as a brother.  
**3:16** And may the Lord of peace Himself continually give peace to you in every way. The Lord <i>be</i> with all of you.  
**3:17** The greeting of Paul by my hand is <i>the</i> sign in every letter; so I write.  
**3:18** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <i>be</i> with you all. Amen.
