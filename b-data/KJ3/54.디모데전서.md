# 54.디모데전서 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul an apostle of Jesus Christ according to a command of God our Savior, even <i>the</i> Lord Jesus Christ, <i>our</i> Hope,  
**1:2** to Timothy, a true child in <i>the</i> faith: Grace, mercy, peace from God our Father and our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:3** Even as I encouraged you to remain <i>in</i> Ephesus (I going to Macedonia), that you might charge certain <i>ones</i> not to teach a different doctrine,  
**1:4** nor to pay attention to myths and to endless genealogies, which provide questionings, rather than a stewardship of God, the <i>one being</i> in faith,  
**1:5** but the end of the commandment is love out of a pure heart and a good conscience, and faith without hypocrisy,  
**1:6** from which certain <i>ones</i> deviating turned aside to empty talking,  
**1:7** wishing to be teachers of law, not understanding, neither the things what <i>things</i> they say, nor about what things they confidently affirm.  
**1:8** And we know that the Law <i>is</i> good, if anyone uses it lawfully,  
**1:9** knowing this, that Law <i>is</i> not laid down for a righteous <i>one</i>, but for lawless and insubordinate <i>ones</i>, for ungodly and sinful <i>ones</i>, for unholy and profane <i>ones,</i> for slayers of fathers and slayers of mothers, for murderers,  
**1:10** for fornicators, for homosexuals, for slave-traders, for liars, for perjurers, and if any other thing is opposed to sound doctrine,  
**1:11** according to the gospel of the glory of the blessed God <i>with</i> which I was entrusted.  
**1:12** And I have thanks to Him empowering me, our Lord Jesus Christ, because He counted me faithful, putting <i>me</i> into the service;  
**1:13** the <i>one</i> who before was a blasphemer, and a persecutor, and an insolent man; but I received mercy, because being ignorant I did <i>it</i> in unbelief.  
**1:14** <i>But</i> the grace of our Lord abounded exceedingly with faith and love in Christ Jesus.  
**1:15** Faithful <i>is</i> the Word and worthy of all acceptance, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am chief.  
**1:16** But for this reason I received mercy, that in me first Jesus Christ might show forth all long-suffering, for an example to the <i>ones</i> being about to believe on Him unto everlasting life.  
**1:17** Now to the King eternal, incorruptible, invisible, <i>the</i> only wise God, <i>be</i> honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.  
**1:18** This charge I commit to you, <i>my</i> child Timothy, according to the prophecies going before concerning you, that you might war the good warfare by them,  
**1:19** having faith and a good conscience, which certain <i>ones</i> thrusting away, made shipwreck concerning the faith,  
**1:20** of whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I gave over to Satan, that they may be taught not to blaspheme.  

## 2장

**2:1** First of all therefore, I exhort <i>that</i> petitions, prayers, supplications, <i>and</i> thanksgivings be made on behalf of all men,  
**2:2** for kings and all the <i>ones</i> being in preeminence, that we may lead a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness and dignity.  
**2:3** For this <i>is</i> good and acceptable before God our Savior,  
**2:4** who desires all men to be saved and to come to a full knowledge of truth.  
**2:5** For God <i>is</i> one, also <i>there is</i> one Mediator of God and of men, <i>the</i> Man Christ Jesus,  
**2:6** the <i>One</i> having given Himself a ransom on behalf of all, the testimony <i>to be given</i> in its own time,  
**2:7** to which I was appointed a herald and apostle (I speak the truth in Christ, I do not lie), a teacher of <i>the</i> nations, in faith and truth.  
**2:8** Therefore, I purpose the men to pray in every place, lifting up holy hands without wrath and doubting.  
**2:9** So also the women to adorn themselves in respectable clothing, with modesty and sensibleness, not with plaiting, or gold, or pearls, or expensive garments,  
**2:10** but what becomes women professing fear of God, through good works.  
**2:11** Let a woman learn in silence, in all subjection.  
**2:12** And I do not allow a woman to teach nor to exercise authority <i>over</i> a man, but to be in silence.  
**2:13** For Adam was formed first, then Eve.  
**2:14** And Adam was not deceived, but <i>the</i> woman being deceived has come to be in transgression;  
**2:15** but she will be kept safe through the childbearing, if they continue in faith and love and holiness, with sensibleness.  

## 3장

**3:1** Faithful <i>is</i> the Word: If anyone reaches out to overseership, he desires a good work.  
**3:2** Therefore it is right <i>for</i> the overseer to be blameless, husband of one wife, temperate, sensible, well-ordered, hospitable, apt at teaching;  
**3:3** not given to wine, not a quarreler, not loving silver, but gentle, not contentious, not eager for ill gain;  
**3:4** ruling his own house well, having children in subjection with all honor.  
**3:5** But if anyone does not know <i>how</i> to rule <i>his</i> own house, how will he care for an assembly of God?  
**3:6** <i>He should</i> not <i>be</i> a novice, lest being puffed up he may fall into the devil's judgment.  
**3:7** And it is right <i>for</i> him to also have a good witness from the <i>ones</i> outside, that he not fall into reproach and <i>into</i> a snare of the devil.  
**3:8** Likewise, deacons <i>are to be</i> honorable, not double-tongued, not being given to much wine, not eager for ill gain,  
**3:9** having the mystery of the faith with a pure conscience.  
**3:10** And also let these be tested first, then let them serve, being without reproach.  
**3:11** Likewise, <i>their</i> wives <i>are to be</i> reverent, not slanderers, temperate, faithful in all things.  
**3:12** Let deacons be husbands of one wife, ruling <i>their</i> own houses and children well.  
**3:13** For the <i>ones</i> having served well as <i>a deacon</i> acquire a good standing for themselves and much boldness in faith, the <i>ones</i> in Christ Jesus.  
**3:14** I write these things to you, hoping to come to you shortly;  
**3:15** but if I delay, that you may know how to conduct oneself in the house of God, which is <i>the</i> assembly of the living God, <i>the</i> pillar and foundation of the truth.  
**3:16** And, confessedly, great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifested in flesh, was justified in Spirit, was seen by angels, was proclaimed among nations, <i>was</i> believed on in <i>the</i> world, was taken up in glory.  

## 4장

**4:1** But the Spirit expressly says that in latter times some will depart from the faith, paying attention to deceiving spirits and teachings of demons,  
**4:2** in hypocrisy of liars, being seared in <i>their</i> own conscience,  
**4:3** forbidding to marry, <i>saying</i> to abstain from foods, which God created for partaking with thanksgiving by the believing <i>ones</i> and the <i>ones</i> fully knowing the truth.  
**4:4** Because every creature of God <i>is</i> good, and nothing to be thrust away, but being received with thanksgiving;  
**4:5** for through God's Word and supplication it is sanctified.  
**4:6** Having laid down these things to the brothers, you will be a good servant of Jesus Christ, having been nourished by the words of the faith, and by the good doctrine which you have followed.  
**4:7** But refuse the profane and old-womanish myths. And exercise yourself to godliness.  
**4:8** For bodily exercise is profitable for a little, but godliness is profitable for all things, having promise of the life now, and <i>of that</i> coming.  
**4:9** Faithful <i>is</i> the Word and worthy of all acceptance;  
**4:10** for to this we also labor and <i>are</i> reproached, because we hope on <i>the</i> living God, who is Savior of all men, especially of believing <i>ones</i>.  
**4:11** Enjoin and teach these things.  
**4:12** Let no one despise your youth, but become an example of the believing <i>ones</i> in word, in conduct, in love, in spirit, in faith, in purity.  
**4:13** Until I come, attend to reading, to exhortation, to the doctrine.  
**4:14** Do not neglect the gift in you, which was given to you through prophecy, with laying on of the hands of the elderhood.  
**4:15** Give care to these things; be in these things in order that your progress may be evident among all.  
**4:16** Give attention to yourself and to the doctrine; continue in them, for doing this, you will both deliver yourself and the <i>ones</i> hearing you.  

## 5장

**5:1** Do not sharply chastise an older <i>man</i>, but exhort as a father; <i>and</i> younger ones as brothers,  
**5:2** older women as mothers, younger <i>ones</i> as sisters, in all purity.  
**5:3** Honor widows, the <i>ones</i> being really widows.  
**5:4** But if any widow has children or grandchildren, let them learn first to be godly to their own house and to make a return payment to <i>their</i> forefathers, for this is good and pleasing before God.  
**5:5** But <i>honor</i> the <i>one</i> being really a widow, even having been left alone, <i>who</i> has set <i>her</i> hope on God and continues in petitions and prayers night and day.  
**5:6** But the <i>one</i> living for self-pleasure has died <i>while</i> living.  
**5:7** And enjoin these things that they may be blameless.  
**5:8** But if anyone does not provide for <i>his</i> own, and especially <i>his</i> family, he has denied the faith and is worse than a faithless <i>one</i>.  
**5:9** Let a widow be enrolled having become not less than sixty years, <i>the</i> wife of one man,  
**5:10** being witnessed by good works, if she brought up children, if she was hospitable, if she washed the feet of the saints, if she relieved afflicted <i>ones</i>, if she followed after every good work.  
**5:11** But refuse younger widows; for whenever they grow lustful against Christ, they desire to marry,  
**5:12** having guilt because they set aside the first faith;  
**5:13** and with it all, they also learn <i>to be</i> idle, going around the houses, and not only idle, but also bubbling <i>gossip</i>, and trifling <i>ones</i>, speaking the things not proper.  
**5:14** Therefore, I purpose the younger women to marry, to bear children, to rule the house, giving no occasion to the <i>one</i> opposing, because of reproach.  
**5:15** For some already have turned aside behind Satan.  
**5:16** If any believing man or believing woman has widows, let them relieve <i>them</i>, and do not burden the assembly, that it may relieve the <i>ones</i> being really widows.  
**5:17** Let the elders who take the lead well be counted worthy of double honor, especially the <i>ones</i> laboring in Word and teaching.  
**5:18** For the Scripture says, "You shall not muzzle an ox treading out grain," and, the laborer is worthy of his pay. <n>Deut 25:4</n>  
**5:19** Do not receive an accusation against an elder unless on "<i>the testimony of</i>" "two or three witnesses". <n>Deut 19:15</n>  
**5:20** The ones sinning before all, rebuke, that the rest also may have fear.  
**5:21** I solemnly testify before God and <i>the</i> Lord Jesus Christ and the elect an- gels, that you should guard these things without pre-judgment, doing nothing by way of partiality.  
**5:22** Lay hands quickly on no one, nor share in <i>the</i> sins of others. Keep yourself pure.  
**5:23** No longer drink water, but use a little wine on account of your stomach and your frequent infirmities.  
**5:24** The sins of certain men are plain before all, brought forth before to judgment, but certain <i>ones</i> follow after also.  
**5:25** Likewise also the good works are plain before all, and the <i>ones</i> otherwise are not able to be hidden.  

## 6장

**6:1** Let as many as are slaves under a yoke count their own masters worthy of all honor, that the name and teaching of God may not be blasphemed.  
**6:2** And the <i>ones</i> having believing masters, let them not despise <i>them</i>, because they are brothers, but rather let them serve as slaves, because they are faithful and beloved <i>ones</i>, the <i>ones</i> receiving of the benefit in return. Teach and exhort these things.  
**6:3** If anyone teaches differently, and does not consent to sound words, the <i>ones</i> of our Lord Jesus Christ and the teaching according to godliness,  
**6:4** he has been puffed up, understanding nothing, but <i>is</i> sick concerning questions and disputes, out of which comes envy, strife, evil-speakings, evil suspicions,  
**6:5** idle uselessness of men whose mind has been corrupted and deprived of the truth, supposing gain to be godliness. Withdraw from such <i>persons</i>.  
**6:6** But godliness with contentment is great gain.  
**6:7** For we have brought nothing into the world, <i>and it is</i> plain that neither can we carry anything out.  
**6:8** But having food and coverings, we will be satisfied with these.  
**6:9** But the <i>ones</i> purposing to be rich fall into temptation, and a snare, and many foolish and hurtful lusts, which plunge men into ruin and destruction.  
**6:10** For the love of money is a root of all evils, <i>by means</i> of which some stretching toward <i>it</i> were led astray from the faith, and <i>they</i> themselves pierced through by many pains.  
**6:11** But you, O man of God, flee these things and pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, patience, and meekness.  
**6:12** Fight the good fight of faith. Lay hold on eternal life, to which you were also called and confessed the good confession before many witnesses.  
**6:13** I charge you before God, He making all things alive, and Christ Jesus, the <i>One</i> witnessing the good confession <i>to</i> Pontius Pilate,  
**6:14** that you keep the commandment spotless, beyond reproach, until the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ;  
**6:15** who in His own time will reveal <i>who is</i> the blessed and only Potentate, the King of the <i>ones</i> reigning and Lord of the <i>ones</i> exercising lordship,  
**6:16** the only <i>One</i> having immortality, living in light that cannot be approached, whom no one of men saw, nor can see; to whom be honor and everlasting might. Amen.  
**6:17** Charge the rich in the age now, not to be high-minded, nor to set hope on the uncertainty of riches, but in the living God, the <i>One</i> offering to us richly all things for enjoyment;  
**6:18** to do good, to be rich in good works, to be ready to impart, to be generous <i>ones</i>,  
**6:19** treasuring away for themselves a good foundation for the coming <i>age</i>, that they may lay hold on everlasting life.  
**6:20** O Timothy, guard the Deposit, having turned away from the profane empty babblings and opposing theories of the falsely named knowledge,  
**6:21** which some professing have missed the mark concerning the faith. Grace <i>be</i> with you. Amen.
