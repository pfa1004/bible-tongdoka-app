# 18.욥기 (KJ3)



## 1장

**1:1** There was a man in the land of Uz, his name <i>was</i> Job. And this man was perfect and upright, and fearing God, and turning away from evil.  
**1:2** And seven sons and three daughters were born to him.  
**1:3** And his possessions were seven thousand sheep, and three thousand camels, and five hundred yoke of oxen, and five hundred she-asses, and a very great household, so that this man was greater than all the sons of the east.  
**1:4** And his sons would go and make a feast <i>in</i> the house of a man on his day. And they sent and called their three sisters to eat and to drink with them.  
**1:5** And it happened, when the day of feasting had gone around, Job would send and sanctify them. And he would rise early in the morning and offer burnt sacrifices <i>according</i> to all their number. For Job said, It may be that my sons have sinned, and cursed God in their hearts. This Job did all the days.  
**1:6** And a day came when the sons of God came to present themselves before Jehovah. And Satan also came among them.  
**1:7** And Jehovah said to Satan, From where have you come? And Satan answered Jehovah and said, From going to and fro in the earth, and from walking up and down in it.  
**1:8** And Jehovah said to Satan, Have you set your heart on My servant Job because <i>there is</i> none like him in the earth, a perfect and upright man, fearing God and turning away from evil?  
**1:9** And Satan answered Jehovah and said, Does Job fear God for nothing?  
**1:10** Have You not made a hedge for him, and for his house, and for all that <i>is</i> to him all around? You have blessed the work of his hands, and his livestock have increased in the land.  
**1:11** But put out Your hand now, and touch against all which is to him, <i>and see</i> if he will not curse You to Your face.  
**1:12** And Jehovah said to Satan, Behold, all that <i>is</i> to him <i>is</i> in your hand! Only, do not lay your hand on him. And Satan went out from the face of Jehovah.  
**1:13** And a day was when his sons and his daughters <i>were</i> eating and drinking wine in their brother's house, the first-born.  
**1:14** And a messenger came to Job and said, The oxen were plowing, and the asses were feeding beside them.  
**1:15** And the Sabeans fell <i>on them</i> and took them away. And they killed the young men with the mouth of the sword; and only I, I alone have escaped to tell you.  
**1:16** <i>While</i> this <i>one was</i> still speaking, this <i>other</i> also came and said, The fire of God has fallen from the heavens and has burned up the sheep and the young men. And it has consumed them; and I, I alone have escaped to tell you.  
**1:17** <i>While</i> this <i>one was</i> still speaking, this <i>other</i> also came and said, The Chaldeans made out three bands and made a raid on the camels. And they have taken them away. And they have struck the young men with the mouth of the sword; and only I, I alone have escaped to tell you.  
**1:18** <i>While</i> this <i>one was</i> still speaking, this <i>other</i> came and said, Your sons and your daughters <i>were</i> eating and drinking wine in the house of their brother, the first-born.  
**1:19** And, behold! A great wind came from the wilderness and touched the four corners of the house. And it fell on the young men and they died; and only I, I alone have escaped to tell you.  
**1:20** And Job rose up and tore his robe, and shaved his head. And he fell down on the ground and worshiped.  
**1:21** And he said, I came naked out of my mother's womb, and naked I shall return there. Jehovah gave, and Jehovah has taken away. Blessed <i>be</i> the name of Jehovah.  
**1:22** In all this Job did not sin and did not ascribe unseemliness to God.  

## 2장

**2:1** And it happened that a day came when the sons of God came to present themselves before Jehovah. And Satan also came among them to present himself before Jehovah.  
**2:2** And Jehovah said to Satan, From where have you come? And Satan answered Jehovah and said, From going to and fro in the earth, and walking up and down in it.  
**2:3** And Jehovah said to Satan, Have you set your heart on My servant Job, that <i>there is</i> none like him in the earth, a perfect and upright man, fearing God, and turning away from evil? And he is still holding to his integrity, although you incited Me against him, to swallow him for nothing.  
**2:4** And Satan answered Jehovah and said, Skin for skin. Yea, all that <i>is</i> to a man he will give for his life.  
**2:5** But indeed put out Your hand now and touch to his bone and his flesh, and he will curse You to Your face.  
**2:6** And Jehovah said to Satan, Behold, he <i>is</i> in your hand; but preserve his life.  
**2:7** And Satan went out from before the face of Jehovah. And <i>he</i> struck Job with bad erupting boils from the sole of his foot to the top of his head.  
**2:8** And he took for himself a potsherd to scrape himself with it. And he <i>was</i> sitting among the ashes.  
**2:9** And his wife said to him, <i>Are</i> you still holding fast to your integrity? Bless God and die!  
**2:10** But he said to her, You speak as one of the foolish <i>ones</i> speak. Indeed, shall we receive good at the hand of God, and shall we not receive evil? In all this Job did not sin with his lips.  
**2:11** And three friends of Job heard <i>of</i> all this evil that had come on him. And they came, <i>each</i> man from his own place: Eliphaz the Temanite; Bildad the Shuhite; and Zophar the Naamathite. And they had met together to come to lament with him, and to comfort him.  
**2:12** And they lifted up their eyes from afar and did not recognize him. And they lifted up their voice and wept. And <i>each</i> man tore his robe; and <i>they</i> sprinkled dust on their heads toward the heavens.  
**2:13** And they sat down with him on the ground seven days and seven nights. And no one <i>was</i> speaking a word to him, for they saw that <i>his</i> pain was very great.  

## 3장

**3:1** After this Job opened his mouth and cursed his day.  
**3:2** And Job answered and said:  
**3:3** Let the day perish in <i>which</i> I was born, and the night <i>which</i> said, A man-child has been conceived.  
**3:4** Let that day be darkness! Let not God look on it from above, and not let the light shine on it.  
**3:5** Let darkness and the shadow of death seize upon it. Let a cloud dwell on it; let darkness of day terrify it! Let it not be joined to the days of the year. Let it not come into the number of the months.  
**3:6** - - -  
**3:7** Behold, let that night be barren; let not joyful voice come in it.  
**3:8** Let it be cursed, the day being cursed, those ready to stir up Leviathan.  
**3:9** Let the stars of its twilight be dark; let it look for light, but have none, let it wait for eyelids of the dawn.  
**3:10** For it did not shut up the doors of my <i>mother's</i> womb and did not hide toil from my eyes.  
**3:11** Why did I not die from the womb, come from the womb and expire?  
**3:12** Why did the knees go before me; or why the breasts, that I should suck?  
**3:13** For now I would have lain down and have been quiet; I would have slept. Then I would have been at rest,  
**3:14** with kings and wise men of the earth, who built ruins for themselves;  
**3:15** or with chiefs; they had gold, they filled their houses with silver;  
**3:16** or as a hidden miscarriage, I would not have been, like infants who never saw light.  
**3:17** There the wicked <i>ones</i> cease <i>from</i> turmoil, and there the weary of strength are at rest;  
**3:18** The prisoners are at ease together, they hear not the voice of the slave-driver;  
**3:19** the small and the great, he is there, and the slave free from his master.  
**3:20** Why is light given to the sufferer, and life to him who is bitter of soul;  
**3:21** who is waiting for death, but <i>it</i> is not; and they dig for it more than <i>for</i> treasures?  
**3:22** They are rejoicing to exultation. They are glad when they find the grave.  
**3:23** To a man whose way is hidden, God has made a hedge about him.  
**3:24** For my sighing comes before my food; and my groanings are poured out like the waters.  
**3:25** For the dreadful thing I dreaded has come on me; and that which I feared has come to me.  
**3:26** I am not at ease, and I am not at rest; and I am not quiet; yet turmoil comes.  

## 4장

**4:1** And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said:  
**4:2** Shall one try a word with you, you <i>who</i> are weary? But who can hold back with words?  
**4:3** Behold, you have instructed many; and you have made weak hands strong.  
**4:4** Your words have upheld him who was falling, and you have made feeble knees strong.  
**4:5** But now it has come upon you, and you faint. It touches you, and you are troubled.  
**4:6** <i>Is</i> not your fearing <i>of God</i> your confidence; your hope the integrity of your ways?  
**4:7** Please remember, Who, being innocent, has perished? Or where were the upright <i>ones</i> cut off?  
**4:8** As I have seen, the ones plowing iniquity and sowing trouble; <i>they</i> harvest it.  
**4:9** They perish from the breath of God, and by the wind of His anger they are finished  
**4:10** The roar of the lion. and the voice of the lion, and the teeth of the young lions are broken;  
**4:11** Without prey the lion <i>is</i> perishing, and the offspring of the lioness are scattered.  
**4:12** And a word was stolen to me, and my ear received a whisper of it.  
**4:13** In thoughts from the visions of the night, when deep sleep falls on men,  
**4:14** fear and trembling met me, and caused my many bones to dread.  
**4:15** Then a spirit passed before my face; the hair of my flesh stood up;  
**4:16** it stood still, but I could not discern its appearance; an image <i>was</i> before my eyes; <i>there was</i> a whisper and a voice I heard:  
**4:17** Is man <i>more</i> righteous than God? Or <i>is</i> a man <i>more</i> pure than his Maker?  
**4:18** Behold, He puts no trust in His servants, and He puts error against His angels.  
**4:19** How much more those that dwell in houses of clay; whose foundation <i>is</i> in the dust; they crush them before the moth!  
**4:20** They are beaten from morning till evening; they perish forever with no one regarding.  
**4:21** Is not their <i>tent</i> cord pulled up within them? They die, but not with wisdom.  

## 5장

**5:1** Call now. Is there anyone answering you? And to whom of the holy <i>ones</i> will you turn?  
**5:2** For provocation kills the foolish <i>one</i>, and passion causes the <i>one</i> being simple to die.  
**5:3** I have seen the foolish <i>one</i> taking root, but suddenly I cursed his abode.  
**5:4** His sons are far from safety; yea, they are crushed in the gate; and <i>there is</i> not a deliverer.  
**5:5** The hungry eat his harvest, and they take him to the thorns, and the snare crushes up their strength.  
**5:6** For affliction does not come forth from the dust, and trouble does not spring up out of the ground;  
**5:7** for man is born to trouble, and the sons of the flame rise, flying <i>upward</i>.  
**5:8** Truly, I would seek to God, and to God I would put my suit,  
**5:9** <i>who is</i> doing great things, and there is no searching <i>them</i>; marvelous things to which is no numbering,  
**5:10** the <i>One</i> giving rain on the face of the earth and sending water on the face of the outer <i>places</i>;  
**5:11** to set the lowly on high; yea, the mourners become very high <i>in</i> safety;  
**5:12** frustrating the plots of the crafty, and their hands do not perform the undertaking,  
**5:13** taking the wise <i>ones</i> in their craftiness, and the counsel of the wily <i>ones</i> is hurried.  
**5:14** They meet with darkness by day, and grope in the noonday as in the night.  
**5:15** But He saves the needy <i>one</i> from the sword, from their mouth, and from the hand of the strong <i>one</i>;  
**5:16** and there is hope for the weak <i>one</i>; and injustice shuts her mouth.  
**5:17** Behold, blessed <i>is</i> the man whom God corrects; and despise not the chastening of the Almighty.  
**5:18** For He wounds, and He binds up. He shatters, and His hands heal.  
**5:19** He will deliver you in six troubles; yea, in seven evil shall not touch you.  
**5:20** In famine He will redeem you from death; and in war from the hand of the sword.  
**5:21** You shall be hidden from the whip of the tongue, and you shall not be afraid of violence when it comes.  
**5:22** You shall laugh at violence and at famine; and you shall not be afraid of the beasts of the earth.  
**5:23** For you <i>shall be</i> in covenant with the stones of the field; and the beasts of the field will be at peace with you.  
**5:24** And you shall know that your tent <i>is in</i> peace; and you shall visit your abode and shall miss nothing;  
**5:25** and <i>you</i> shall know that your seed <i>will be</i> many, and your offspring as the grass of the earth.  
**5:26** You shall come to the grave in <i>full</i> vigor, like a stack of grain comes up in its season.  
**5:27** Behold this! We have searched it out; it is so. Hear it, and know <i>it</i> for yourself.  

## 6장

**6:1** And Job answered and said:  
**6:2** Oh that weighing my indignation were weighed, and my ruin lifted in the balances together!  
**6:3** For now it would be heavier than the sand of the seas; on account of this my words have been rash.  
**6:4** For the arrows of the Almighty <i>are</i> within me; their fury <i>is</i> drinking my spirit; the terrors of God are set against me.  
**6:5** Does the wild ass bray over tender grass? Or does the ox low over his fodder?  
**6:6** Are tasteless <i>things</i> eaten without salt? Or is there taste in the slime of an egg?  
**6:7** My soul refuses to touch them; they <i>are</i> sickening food to me.  
**6:8** Who will give <i>it that</i> my desire might come, and God would grant my longing;  
**6:9** and God would be willing and crush me; let loose His hand and cut me off?  
**6:10** And it is yet my comfort; and I would exult in pain not spared; for I have not hidden the words of the Holy <i>One</i>.  
**6:11** What <i>is</i> my strength that I should wait? And what <i>is</i> my end that I should prolong my life?  
**6:12** <i>Is</i> my strength the strength of stones? Or <i>is</i> my flesh of bronze?  
**6:13** <i>Is</i> not my help within me? And is wisdom driven from me?  
**6:14** To the despairing <i>one</i>, pity is <i>due</i> from his friend; for he forsakes the fear of the Almighty.  
**6:15** My brothers have dealt deceitfully, like the torrent, like the streams of torrents, they pass away;  
**6:16** those darkened from ice, in which the snow hides itself.  
**6:17** At the time they are warmed, they are cut off; when it is hot they dry up.  
**6:18** The paths of their way bend; they go to nothing and are lost.  
**6:19** The wayfarers of Tema looked; the traveling companies of Sheba hoped for them;  
**6:20** they were ashamed because they had hoped; they came there and were ashamed.  
**6:21** Surely now you have become like it; you see <i>my</i> dismay, and are afraid.  
**6:22** Did I indeed say, Give to me? Or, Offer a bribe for me from your wealth?  
**6:23** Or, Deliver me from an adversary's hand? Or, Redeem me from the hand of terrifying <i>ones</i>?  
**6:24** Teach me and I will be silent; and cause me to understand <i>in</i> what I have erred.  
**6:25** How powerful are right speeches, but what does arguing from you argue?  
**6:26** Do you intend to argue words, and the speeches <i>that are</i> as wind to cause despair?  
**6:27** Yea, you cause even an orphan to fall; and you bargain over your friend.  
**6:28** And now, be willing <i>to</i> face me, and to your face I will not lie.  
**6:29** Turn back, please, let there be no wrong. Yea, turn back again, for my righteousness <i>is</i> in it.  
**6:30** Is there wrong in my tongue? Or cannot my palate discern desirable things?  

## 7장

**7:1** <i>Is there</i> not a warfare to man on earth, and his days like the days of a hireling?  
**7:2** As a servant pants for the shade, and as a hireling looks for his wages,  
**7:3** so I am caused to inherit months of emptiness; and nights of trouble are appointed to me.  
**7:4** When I lie down, I say, When shall I rise up? But the night is long, and I am full of tossings, until the twilight <i>of dawn</i>.  
**7:5** My flesh is clothed with worms and clods of dust; my skin is broken and is loathed.  
**7:6** My days are swifter than a weaver's shuttle and end without hope.  
**7:7** Remember that my life <i>is</i> a breath; my eyes shall not return to see good.  
**7:8** The eye of him who sees me shall not gaze at me; Your eyes <i>will be</i> on me, and I will not be.  
**7:9** <i>As</i> the cloud fades, it goes, so he who goes down to Sheol shall not come up.  
**7:10** He shall not return again to his house;, and his place shall not know him any more.  
**7:11** Also, I will not restrain my mouth; I will speak in the distress of my spirit; I will complain in the bitterness of my soul.  
**7:12** Am I a sea, or a sea-monster, that You set a watch over me?  
**7:13** When I say, My bed shall comfort me; my couch shall bear with my complaining;  
**7:14** even You scare me with dreams and terrify me with visions;  
**7:15** and my soul chooses strangling <i>and</i> death rather than my bones.  
**7:16** I despise them; I will not live always. Cease from me, for my days <i>are</i> vanity.  
**7:17** What <i>is</i> man that You should magnify him, and that You should set Your heart to him,  
**7:18** and visit him every morning, trying him every moment?  
**7:19** How long will You not look away from me, and not leave me alone until I swallow my spittle?  
**7:20** I have sinned; what do I do to You, O Watcher of man? Why have You set me as a target for You so that I am a burden on myself?  
**7:21** And why do You not lift up my transgression, and make my iniquity pass away? For now I shall lie down in the dust, and You shall seek me; but I <i>will</i> not be.  

## 8장

**8:1** And Bildad the Shuhite answered, saying:  
**8:2** Until when will you speak these things, and the words of your mouth <i>will be like</i> a mighty wind?  
**8:3** Or does God pervert judgment? Or does the Almighty pervert the right?  
**8:4** If your sons have sinned against Him, and <i>if</i> He has sent them away in the hand of their transgression,  
**8:5** if you would diligently seek to God, and make your prayer to the Almighty,  
**8:6** if you <i>were</i> pure and upright, surely now He would rise for you, and make peaceful the abode of your righteousness.  
**8:7** And <i>though</i> your beginning was small, even your end would greatly increase.  
**8:8** For please ask of the former generation, and prepare to the search of their fathers;  
**8:9** for we <i>are but of</i> yesterday, and we do not know, because our days on earth <i>are</i> a shadow.  
**8:10** Shall they not teach you, speak to you, and bring forth words out of their heart?  
**8:11** Can the rush grow up without mire? Can the reed grass grow without water?  
**8:12** While it <i>is</i> yet in its greenness, and not cut down, it dries out before every plant;  
**8:13** so <i>are</i> the paths of all those forgetting God; yea, the hope of the godless <i>one</i> shall perish,  
**8:14** whose hope is cut off, and his trust <i>is as</i> a spider's house.  
**8:15** He shall lean on his house, but it shall not stand; he shall hold fast to it, but it shall not rise up.  
**8:16** He <i>is</i> moist before the sun, and his tender plants shoot forth on his garden.  
**8:17** His roots are wrapped around a heap; he sees a house of stones.  
**8:18** If one swallows him from his place, even it shall deny him, <i>saying,</i> I have not seen you!  
**8:19** Behold this <i>is</i> the joy of His way, and out of the dust others shall grow.  
**8:20** Behold, God will not reject the perfect <i>one</i>, and He will not strengthen the hand of evildoers,  
**8:21** until He fills your mouth with laughter and your lips with rejoicing.  
**8:22** Those who hate you shall be clothed with shame; and the tent of the wicked <i>one shall</i> not <i>be</i>.  

## 9장

**9:1** And Job answered and said:  
**9:2** Truly I know <i>it is</i> so; but how can man be just with God?  
**9:3** If he would delight to argue with Him, he cannot answer Him one of a thousand.  
**9:4** <i>He is</i> wise in heart and mighty in strength; who has been hard against Him and been at peace?  
**9:5** <i>He</i> who moves the mountains, and they do not know when He overturns them in His anger;  
**9:6** who shakes the earth out of its place, and its pillars tremble;  
**9:7** <i>the One</i> speaking to the sun and it does not rise; and for the stars He sets a seal;  
**9:8** stretching out the heavens by Himself, and walking on the high place of the sea;  
**9:9** Who made the Ursa Major, Orion, and Pleiades, and the chambers of the south;  
**9:10** doing great things past finding out; yea, wonderful things until there is no number?  
**9:11** Behold, He goes by me, and I do not see <i>Him</i>; and He passes on, but I do not perceive Him.  
**9:12** Behold, He takes away; who can turn Him back? Who will say to Him, What are You doing?  
**9:13** God will not withdraw His anger; the helpers of pride crouch under Him.  
**9:14** How then shall I answer Him, <i>and</i> choose my words with Him!  
**9:15** Whom, though I were being righteous, I could not answer <i>Him</i>; I seek mercy for my judgment.  
**9:16** If I had called and He had not answered me I would not believe that He had listened to my voice;  
**9:17** <i>He</i> falls on me me with a tempest, and adds to my wounds without cause;  
**9:18** <i>who</i> will not allow me to bring back my breath, but fills me with bitterness.  
**9:19** If <i>I speak</i> of strength, behold, <i>He is</i> mighty; and if of judgment, who can summon me?  
**9:20** If I am being righteous my mouth will condemn me; <i>though</i> I <i>am</i> the perfect <i>one</i>, He shall declare me perverse;  
**9:21** <i>though</i> I <i>were</i> the perfect <i>one</i>, I would not know my own soul; I despise my life.  
**9:22** It <i>is all</i> one; on account of this I said, He is consuming the perfect <i>one</i> and the wicked <i>one</i>.  
**9:23** If the whip kills suddenly, He will mock at the testing of the innocent.  
**9:24** The earth is given into the hand of the wicked <i>one</i>; He covers the faces of its judges. If it is not <i>He</i>, then who <i>is</i> it?  
**9:25** Now my days are swifter than a runner; they flee away; they see no good;  
**9:26** they have passed away like the reed ships, as an eagle swoops on food.  
**9:27** If I say, I will forget my complaint, I will loosen my face and be cheerful,  
**9:28** I am afraid of all my sorrows; I know that You will not hold me innocent;  
**9:29** I have been condemned; why should I labor in vain?  
**9:30** If I wash myself with snow, and make my palms clean with lye <i>soap</i>,  
**9:31** yet You will plunge me into the ditch, and my own clothes would abhor me.  
**9:32** For <i>He is</i> not a man, as I <i>am, that</i> I should answer Him; <i>that</i> we should come together in judgment;  
**9:33** there is no mediator between us, <i>who</i> might lay his hand on both of us.  
**9:34** Let Him take His rod away from me, and let not His dread make me afraid;  
**9:35** <i>then</i> I would speak, and not fear Him; for I am not so in myself.  

## 10장

**10:1** My soul loathes my life; I will leave my complaint on myself; I will speak in the bitterness of my soul.  
**10:2** I will say to God, Do not condemn me; make me know on what <i>ground</i> You contend with me.  
**10:3** Is <i>it</i> good to You that You should press on <i>me</i>, that You should despise the work of Your palms, and You shine <i>on</i> the counsel of the wicked <i>ones</i>?  
**10:4** <i>Have</i> You eyes of flesh; or do You see as a man sees?  
**10:5** Are Your days as the days of man? Or your years like the days of man,  
**10:6** that You seek out my iniquity, and search for my sin?  
**10:7** <i>It is</i> on Your knowing that I am not a wicked <i>one</i>; and <i>there is</i> no one delivering out of Your hand.  
**10:8** Your hands have made me and shaped me together round about, yet You engulf me.  
**10:9** Remember, I pray, that You have formed me as clay; and will You bring me to dust again?  
**10:10** Did You not pour me out like milk; yea, curdled me like cheese?  
**10:11** You clothed me with skin and flesh, and <i>You</i> knitted me with bones and sinews.  
**10:12** You have made me <i>have</i> life and favor, and Your providence has guarded my spirit.  
**10:13** And these You have hidden in Your heart; I know that this <i>was</i> with You.  
**10:14** If I sin, even You watch me; and You will not acquit me from my guilt.  
**10:15** If I am wrong, woe to me! And <i>if</i> I am being righteous, I will not lift up my head, <i>being</i> filled with shame and seeing my pain.  
**10:16** And <i>if</i> if rise, You would hunt me as a lion; and again You show Yourself wonderful in me.

**10:18** And why did You bring me from the womb? I would have died, and no eye seen me.

**10:20** Are not my days few? Then cease and let me alone, that I may take a little comfort  
**10:21** before I go; and I shall not return, to the land of darkness, and the shadow of death;  
**10:22** a land of obscurity, the darkness of the shadow of death, and without any order; even the shining <i>is</i> as utter darkness.

**12:2** For truly you <i>are</i> the people, and wisdom will die with you.  
**12:3** Also to me <i>is a</i> heart as <i>well</i> as to you; I do not fall <i>short</i> of you. And with whom <i>are</i> not things like these?

**12:17** He causes counselors to go barefoot; and He makes judges to be boastful.  
**12:18** He loosens the bonds of kings, and He binds their loins with a girdle;

**12:20** turning aside the lip of the trusted men; and He takes away the reason of the aged,

**12:23** He gives greatness to the nations, and He destroys them; spreading out the nations, and He leads them out;

## 14장

**14:1** Man born of woman <i>is</i> short of days and <i>full</i> of turmoil.

**14:5** If his days are determined, the number of his months is with You, and You have made his bounds, and he cannot pass over;  
**14:6** look away from him, so that he may rest, till he shall enjoy his day as a hireling.

**14:15** You shall call, and I will answer You; You shall long to the work of Your hands.  
**14:16** For now You number my steps; do You not watch over my sin?  
**14:17** My transgression <i>is</i> sealed up in a bag, and You cover over my iniquity.  
**14:18** And surely a falling mountain crumbles away, and the rock moves out of its place.  
**14:19** The waters wear away stones; its outpouring washes the dust of the earth; and You cause the hope of man to perish.  
**14:20** You overpower him forever, and he goes. You change his face and send him away.  
**14:21** His sons <i>are</i> honored, and he does not know; they are insignificant, but he does not discern it.

**16:4** I also could speak as you. If your soul were in the place of my soul, I could bind words against you and shake at you with my head.  
**16:5** I might strengthen you with my mouth, and the moving of my lips could spare you.  
**16:6** If I speak, my pain is not held back; and <i>if</i> I forbear, what part of me goes away?  
**16:7** But now He has made me weary. You have desolated all my company.  
**16:8** And You have plucked me; for it is a witness, and my failure rises up against me; yea, it answers to my face.  
**16:9** His anger has torn, and retained animosity against me; He gnashes at me with His teeth; my Adversary sharpens his eyes at me.  
**16:10** They have gaped at me with their mouth <i>and</i> with scorn struck my cheek; they gather themselves together against me.  
**16:11** God has delivered me to the perverse <i>one</i>; and He has pushed me headlong to the hands of the wicked <i>ones</i>.  
**16:12** I was at ease, but He has shattered me; yea, <i>He has</i> also taken hold on my neck and broken me to bits; and He has set me up as a target for Him.  
**16:13** His archers surround me; He splits my kidneys and has no pity; He pours out my gall on the ground.  
**16:14** He breaks me with break on the face of break; He runs on me like a mighty <i>one</i>.  
**16:15** I have sewed sackcloth on my skin, and I have thrust my horn in the dust.  
**16:16** My face is reddened from weeping; and the shadow of death <i>is</i> on my eyelids,  
**16:17** though no violence <i>is</i> in my palm, and my prayer <i>is</i> pure.  
**16:18** O earth, do not cover my blood, and let not be a place for my cry.  
**16:19** Even now, behold! My witness is in Heaven and my Testifier <i>is</i> on high.  
**16:20** My scorners <i>are</i> my friends; my eye pours out <i>tears</i> to God.  
**16:21** O that one might plead for a man with God, as a man <i>pleads</i> for his friend!  
**16:22** For years of number will come; and I shall not return the way I shall go.

**31:12** For it <i>is</i> a fire that devours to the Place of Ruin, and it would root up all my gain.  
**31:13** If I rejected the cause of my male slave or my slave-girl when they contended with me,  
**31:14** what then shall I do when God rises up? And when He calls me up, what shall I answer Him?  
**31:15** Did not He who made me in the belly make him? And did not One shape us in the womb?  
**31:16** If I have withheld from the desire of the poor <i>one</i>, or I have consumed the widow's eyes,

**31:19** If I have seen any perish for lack of clothing, or <i>there was</i> no covering for the needy <i>one</i>;

**31:23** For calamity <i>from</i> God <i>is</i> a terror to me, and I could not do <i>the thing</i> because of His majesty.  
**31:24** If I have made gold my hope or have said fine gold <i>is</i> my trust;  
**31:25** if I rejoiced because my wealth <i>was</i> great; or because my hand had found much;  
**31:26** if I looked to the light when it shone, or the moon walking in splendor,  
**31:27** and my heart was secretly enticed, or my mouth has kissed my hand;  
**31:28** this also <i>would be</i> an iniquity <i>for</i> the judges; for I would have denied God above.  
**31:29** If I rejoiced at the ruin of one hating me, and I was excited when evil found him;  
**31:30** also I have not given my mouth to sin by asking his life with a curse;  
**31:31** if the men of my tent did not say, Who <i>can</i> give; not being satisfied from his flesh?  
**31:32** The stranger did not lodge outside, <i>for</i> I opened my doors to the traveler;  
**31:33** if I covered my transgressions like Adam, by hiding my iniquity in my bosom,  
**31:34** then let me fear before a great multitude, and let the contempt of families frighten me <i>and</i> I will be silent, <i>and</i> not go out the door.  
**31:35** Oh that to me <i>were one</i> to listen to me! Behold my mark! Let the Almighty answer me, and let the Man of my contention write an indictment.  
**31:36** Surely I would carry it on my shoulder, <i>and</i> bind it <i>like</i> crowns to me.  
**31:37** I would tell Him the number of my steps. Like a prince I would come near Him.  
**31:38** If my land cries out against me, or its furrows weep together;  
**31:39** if I have eaten its strength without silver, or have caused the life of its owner to breathe out,  
**31:40** let thorns come forth instead of wheat, and a weed instead of barley. The words of Job are finished.  

## 32장

**32:3** Also his anger burned against his three friends, because they had not found <i>any</i> answer, yet they had condemned Job.  
**32:4** And Elihu had waited for Job with words, because they <i>were</i> older than he in days.  
**32:5** When Elihu saw that there <i>was</i> no answer in the mouth of the three men, then his anger burned.  
**32:6** And Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said, I <i>am</i> young, and you <i>are</i> very aged; so I feared and was afraid to make my opinion known to you.  
**32:7** I said, Days will speak, and many years will make wisdom known.  
**32:8** But it <i>is</i> a spirit in man giving them perception, even the breath of the Almighty.  
**32:9** <i>It is</i> not the great <i>ones</i> that are being wise, and not the aged <i>ones</i> who understand judgment;  
**32:10** so then I say, Listen to me; I will make known my opinion, I also.  
**32:11** Behold, I waited for your words; I gave ear to your reasons while you searched out words;  
**32:12** yea, I considered, and behold, not one of you proved Job wrong, <i>nor</i> answered his sayings;

**32:14** And he has not ordered words against me, and I will not answer him with your words.

**32:21** I will not now lift up the face of a man, nor eulogize <i>any</i> man.

## 42장

**42:1** And Job answered Jehovah and said:  
**42:2** I know that You can do all <i>things</i>, and not <i>any</i> purpose is withheld from You.  
**42:3** Who <i>is</i> this hiding counsel without knowledge? Therefore I declared, but did not understand <i>things</i> too wonderful for me; yea, I did not know.  
**42:4** I pray, Listen, and I will speak; I will ask You, and You will make me know.  
**42:5** I have heard of You by hearing of the ear, but now my eye has seen You;  
**42:6** Therefore, I despise <i>myself</i>, and I have repented on dust and ashes.  
**42:7** And it happened, after Jehovah spoke these words to Job, Jehovah said to Eliphaz the Temanite, My anger burns against you and your two friends. For You have not spoken <i>what is</i> right about Me, as My servant Job.  
**42:8** And now take for you seven young bulls and seven rams and go to My servant Job, and cause to go up a burnt offering for yourselves. And My servant Job will pray for you. Surely I will lift up his face so as not to do with you <i>according to your</i> folly, in that you have not spoken <i>what is</i> right about Me, as My servant Job.  
**42:9** And Eliphaz the Temanite, and Bildad the Shuhite, <i>and</i> Zophar the Naamathite went and did as Jehovah spoke to them. And Jehovah lifted the face of Job.  
**42:10** And when he prayed for his friends, Jehovah turned the captivity of Job; and Jehovah added to Job all which <i>had been</i> to him, to double.  
**42:11** And all his brothers and all his sisters came to him; and all knowing him before. And they ate bread with him in his house and consoled him and comforted him as to all the evil that Jehovah had brought on him. And they <i>each</i> man gave him one piece of money, and <i>each</i> man a ring of gold.  
**42:12** And Jehovah blessed the latter <i>days</i> of Job more than the earlier <i>days</i>. And to him were fourteen thousand sheep and six thousand camels, and a thousand yoke of oxen, and a thousand she-asses.  
**42:13** And he had seven sons and three daughters.  
**42:14** And he called the name of the first, Jemima; and the name of the second, Keziah; and the name of the third, Kerenhappuch.  
**42:15** And in all the land there <i>were</i> not found women as beautiful as Job's daughters. And their father gave them inheritance among their brothers.  
**42:16** After this Job lived a hundred and forty years. And he saw his sons, and his grandsons, <i>even</i> four generations.  
**42:17** And Job died, <i>being</i> old and full of days.
