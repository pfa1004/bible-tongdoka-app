# 4.민수기 (ICB)



## 1장

**1:1** The People of Israel Are Counted The Lord spoke to Moses in the Meeting Tent. This was in the Desert of Sinai. It was on the first day of the second month. This was in the second year after the people of Israel left Egypt. The Lord said to Moses:  
**1:2** “Count all the people of Israel. List the name of each man with his family and family group.  
**1:3** You and Aaron must count all the men of Israel 20 years old or older. They will serve in the army of Israel. List them by their divisions.  
**1:4** One man from each tribe will help you. He is to be the leader of his family.  
**1:5** These are the names of the men who will help you: from the tribe of Reuben—Elizur son of Shedeur;  
**1:6** from the tribe of Simeon—Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;  
**1:7** from the tribe of Judah—Nahshon son of Amminadab;  
**1:8** from the tribe of Issachar—Nethanel son of Zuar;  
**1:9** from the tribe of Zebulun—Eliab son of Helon;  
**1:10** from the tribe of Ephraim son of Joseph—Elishama son of Ammihud; from the tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph—Gamaliel son of Pedahzur;  
**1:11** from the tribe of Benjamin—Abidan son of Gideoni;  
**1:12** from the tribe of Dan—Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;  
**1:13** from the tribe of Asher—Pagiel son of Ocran;  
**1:14** from the tribe of Gad—Eliasaph son of Deuel;  
**1:15** from the tribe of Naphtali—Ahira son of Enan.”  
**1:16** All these men were chosen by the people to be leaders of their tribes. They were the leaders of their family groups.  
**1:17** Moses and Aaron took these men who had been picked.  
**1:18** And Moses and Aaron called all the people of Israel together. This was the first day of the second month. Then the people were listed by their families and family groups. All the men who were 20 years old or older were listed by name.  
**1:19** Moses did exactly what the Lord had commanded. Moses listed the people while they were in the Desert of Sinai.  
**1:20** The tribe of Reuben was counted. Reuben was the first son born to Israel. All the men 20 years old or older who were able to serve in the army were listed. They were each listed by name with their families and family groups.  
**1:21** The tribe of Reuben totaled 46,500 men.  
**1:22** The tribe of Simeon was counted. All the men 20 years old or older who were able to serve in the army were listed. They were each listed by name with their families and family groups.  
**1:23** The tribe of Simeon totaled 59,300 men.  
**1:24** The tribe of Gad was counted. All the men 20 years old or older who were able to serve in the army were listed. They were listed by name with their families and family groups.  
**1:25** The tribe of Gad totaled 45,650 men.  
**1:26** The tribe of Judah was counted. All the men 20 years old or older who were able to serve in the army were listed. They were listed by name with their families and family groups.  
**1:27** The tribe of Judah totaled 74,600 men.  
**1:28** The tribe of Issachar was counted. All the men 20 years old or older who were able to serve in the army were listed. They were listed by name with their families and family groups.  
**1:29** The tribe of Issachar totaled 54,400 men.  
**1:30** The tribe of Zebulun was counted. All the men 20 years old or older who were able to serve in the army were listed. They were listed by name with their families and family groups.  
**1:31** The tribe of Zebulun totaled 57,400 men.  
**1:32** The tribe of Ephraim was counted. (Ephraim was a son of Joseph.) All the men 20 years old or older who were able to serve in the army were listed. They were listed by name with their families and family groups.  
**1:33** The tribe of Ephraim totaled 40,500 men.  
**1:34** The tribe of Manasseh was counted. (Manasseh was also a son of Joseph.) All the men 20 years old or older who were able to serve in the army were listed. They were listed by name with their families and family groups.  
**1:35** The tribe of Manasseh totaled 32,200 men.  
**1:36** The tribe of Benjamin was counted. All the men 20 years old or older who were able to serve in the army were listed. They were listed by name with their families and family groups.  
**1:37** The tribe of Benjamin totaled 35,400 men.  
**1:38** The tribe of Dan was counted. All the men 20 years old or older who were able to serve in the army were listed. They were listed by name with their families and family groups.  
**1:39** The tribe of Dan totaled 62,700 men.  
**1:40** The tribe of Asher was counted. All the men 20 years old or older who were able to serve in the army were listed. They were listed by name with their families and family groups.  
**1:41** The tribe of Asher totaled 41,500 men.  
**1:42** The tribe of Naphtali was counted. All the men 20 years old or older who were able to serve in the army were listed. They were listed by name with their families and family groups.  
**1:43** The tribe of Naphtali totaled 53,400 men.  
**1:44** Moses, Aaron and the 12 leaders of Israel counted these men. There was 1 leader from each of the families.  
**1:45** Every man of Israel 20 years old or older who was able to serve in the army was counted. Each man was listed with his family.  
**1:46** The total number of men was 603,550.  
**1:47** The families from the tribe of Levi were not listed with the others.  
**1:48** The Lord had told Moses:  
**1:49** “Do not count the tribe of Levi or include them with the other Israelites.  
**1:50** But make the Levites responsible for the Holy Tent of the Agreement. They must take care of it and everything that is with it. They must carry the Holy Tent and everything in it. They must take care of it and make their camp around it.  
**1:51** Any time the Holy Tent is moved, the Levites must take it down. Any time it is set up, the Levites must do it. If anyone else goes near the Holy Tent, he will be put to death.  
**1:52** The Israelites will make their camps in separate divisions, each man near his family flag.  
**1:53** But the Levites must make their camp around the Holy Tent of the Agreement. Then I will not be angry with the Israelites. The Levites will take care of the Holy Tent of the Agreement.”  
**1:54** So the Israelites did everything the Lord commanded Moses.  

## 2장

**2:1** The Camp Arrangement The Lord said to Moses and Aaron:  
**2:2** “The Israelites should make their camps around the Meeting Tent. But they should not camp too close to it. Each person should camp under the flag of his division near the banner of his family group.”  
**2:3** The camp of Judah will be on the east side, where the sun rises. They will camp by divisions there under their flag. The leader of the people of Judah is Nahshon son of Amminadab.  
**2:4** There are 74,600 men in his division.  
**2:5** Next to them the tribe of Issachar will camp. The leader of the people of Issachar is Nethanel son of Zuar.  
**2:6** There are 54,400 men in his division.  
**2:7** Next is the tribe of Zebulun. The leader of the people of Zebulun is Eliab son of Helon.  
**2:8** There are 57,400 men in his division.  
**2:9** There are a total of 186,400 men in the camp of Judah. They are in their divisions. They will be the first to march out of camp.  
**2:10** The divisions of the camp of Reuben will be south of the Holy Tent. They will camp under their flag. The leader of the people of Reuben is Elizur son of Shedeur.  
**2:11** There are 46,500 men in his division.  
**2:12** Next to them the tribe of Simeon will camp. The leader of the people of Simeon is Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.  
**2:13** There are 59,300 men in his division.  
**2:14** Next is the tribe of Gad. The leader of the people of Gad is Eliasaph son of Deuel.  
**2:15** There are 45,650 men in his division.  
**2:16** There are a total of 151,450 men in the camp of Reuben. They are in their divisions. They will be the second group to march out of camp.  
**2:17** When the Levites move the Meeting Tent, they will be in between the other camps. The tribes will move in the same order as they camp. Each person will be with his family flag.  
**2:18** The divisions of the camp of Ephraim will be on the west side. They will camp under their flag. The leader of the people of Ephraim is Elishama son of Ammihud.  
**2:19** There are 40,500 men in his division.  
**2:20** Next to them the tribe of Manasseh will camp. The leader of the people of Manasseh is Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.  
**2:21** There are 32,200 men in his division.  
**2:22** Next to them the tribe of Benjamin will camp. The leader of the people of Benjamin is Abidan son of Gideoni.  
**2:23** There are 35,400 men in his division.  
**2:24** There are a total of 108,100 men in the camp of Ephraim. They are in their divisions. They will be the third group to march out of camp.  
**2:25** The divisions of the camp of Dan will be on the north side. They will camp under their flag. The leader of the people of Dan is Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.  
**2:26** There are 62,700 men in his division.  
**2:27** Next to them the tribe of Asher will camp. The leader of the people of Asher is Pagiel son of Ocran.  
**2:28** There are 41,500 men in his division.  
**2:29** Next is the tribe of Naphtali. The leader of the people of Naphtali is Ahira son of Enan.  
**2:30** There are 53,400 men in his division.  
**2:31** There are 157,600 men in the camp of Dan. They will be the last to march out of camp. They will travel under their own flag.  
**2:32** These are the Israelites who were counted by families. The total number of Israelites in the camps, counted by divisions, is 603,550.  
**2:33** Moses did not count the Levites among the other people of Israel. This is what the Lord had commanded.  
**2:34** So the Israelites obeyed everything the Lord commanded Moses. They camped under their flags. Each person traveled with his family and family group.  

## 3장

**3:1** Aaron’s Family, the Priests This is the family history of Aaron and Moses. This was at the time the Lord talked to Moses on Mount Sinai.  
**3:2** Aaron had four sons. Nadab was the oldest. Then Aaron had Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.  
**3:3** Those were the names of Aaron’s sons. They were appointed to serve as priests.  
**3:4** But Nadab and Abihu died when they sinned in the presence of the Lord. They made an offering to the Lord in the Desert of Sinai. But they used the wrong kind of fire. They had no sons. So Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests during the lifetime of their father Aaron.  
**3:5** The Lord said to Moses,  
**3:6** “Bring the tribe of Levi to Aaron the priest. They must help him.  
**3:7** They will help him and all the Israelites at the Meeting Tent. They will do the work of the Holy Tent.  
**3:8** The Levites must take care of everything in the Meeting Tent. They will serve the people of Israel by doing the work in the Holy Tent.  
**3:9** Give the Levites to Aaron and his sons. Of all the Israelites, the Levites are given completely to him.  
**3:10** Appoint Aaron and his sons to serve as priests. Anyone else who comes near the holy things must be put to death.”  
**3:11** The Lord also said to Moses,  
**3:12** “I am choosing the Levites from all the Israelites. They will take the place of all the firstborn children of Israel. The Levites will be mine.  
**3:13** All the firstborn are mine. When you were in Egypt, I killed all the firstborn children of the Egyptians. I took all the firstborn of Israel to be mine. Firstborn animals and children are mine. I am the Lord.”  
**3:14** The Lord again talked to Moses in the Desert of Sinai. The Lord said,  
**3:15** “Count the Levites by families and family groups. Count every male one month old or older.”  
**3:16** So Moses obeyed the Lord and counted them all.  
**3:17** Levi had three sons. Their names were Gershon, Kohath and Merari.  
**3:18** The Gershonite family groups were Libni and Shimei.  
**3:19** The Kohathite family groups were Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.  
**3:20** The Merarite family groups were Mahli and Mushi. These were the family groups that belonged to the Levites.  
**3:21** The family groups of Libni and Shimei belonged to Gershon. They were all the Gershonite family groups.  
**3:22** They had 7,500 males one month or older.  
**3:23** The Gershonite family groups camped on the west side, behind the Holy Tent.  
**3:24** The leader of the families of Gershon was Eliasaph son of Lael.  
**3:25** In the Meeting Tent the Gershonites were responsible for taking care of these things: the Holy Tent, its covering, the curtain at the entrance to the Meeting Tent,  
**3:26** the curtains in the courtyard, the curtain at the entry to the courtyard around the Holy Tent and the altar, the ropes and all the work connected with these things.  
**3:27** The family groups of Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel belonged to Kohath. They were all the Kohathite family groups.  
**3:28** They had 8,600 males one month old or older. They were responsible for taking care of the Holy Place.  
**3:29** The Kohathite family groups camped south of the Holy Tent.  
**3:30** The leader of the Kohathite families was Elizaphan son of Uzziel.  
**3:31** They were responsible for the Ark of the Covenant, the table, the lampstand, the altars, the tools of the holy place, the curtain and all the work connected with these things.  
**3:32** The main leader of the Levites was Eleazar son of Aaron, the priest. Eleazar was in charge of all those responsible for the Holy Place.  
**3:33** The family groups of Mahli and Mushi belonged to Merari. They were all the Merarite family groups.  
**3:34** They had 6,200 males one month old or older.  
**3:35** The leader of the Merari families was Zuriel son of Abihail. They were to camp north of the Holy Tent.  
**3:36** The Merarites were responsible for the frame of the Holy Tent, the braces, the posts, the bases and all the work connected with these things.  
**3:37** They were also responsible for the posts in the courtyard around the Holy Tent and their bases, tent pegs and ropes.  
**3:38** Moses, Aaron and his sons camped east of the Holy Tent. They were in front of the Meeting Tent. They were responsible for the Holy Place. They did this for the Israelites. Anyone else who came near the Holy Place was to be put to death.  
**3:39** Moses and Aaron counted the Levite men. The Lord commanded this. There were 22, males one month old or older.  
**3:40** Levites Take the Place of the Firstborn Sons The Lord said to Moses, “Count all the firstborn sons in Israel one month old or older. List their names.  
**3:41** Take the Levites for me instead of the firstborn sons of Israel. Take the animals of the Levites instead of the firstborn animals from the rest of Israel. I am the Lord.”  
**3:42** So Moses did what the Lord commanded. Moses counted all the firstborn sons of the Israelites.  
**3:43** He listed all the firstborn sons one month old or older. There were 22,273 names.  
**3:44** The Lord also said to Moses,  
**3:45** “Take the Levites instead of all the firstborn sons of the Israelites. And take the animals of the Levites instead of the animals of the other people. The Levites are mine. I am the Lord.  
**3:46** There are 273 more firstborn sons than Levites.  
**3:47** So collect two ounces of silver for each of the 273 sons. Use the measure as set by the Holy Place. It weighs two-fifths of an ounce.  
**3:48** Give the silver to Aaron and his sons. It is the payment for the 273 Israelites.”  
**3:49** So Moses collected the money for the people the Levites could not replace.  
**3:50** Moses collected the silver from the firstborn of the Israelites. He collected 35 pounds of silver, using the measure set by the Holy Place.  
**3:51** So Moses obeyed the Lord. He gave the silver to Aaron and his sons.  

## 4장

**4:1** The Jobs of the Kohath Family The Lord said to Moses and Aaron,  
**4:2** “Count the Kohathites among the Levites. Count them by family groups and families.  
**4:3** Count the men from 30 to 50 years old. They will serve in the Meeting Tent.  
**4:4** “They are responsible for the most holy things in the Meeting Tent.  
**4:5** When the Israelites move, Aaron and his sons must go into the Holy Tent. They are to take down the curtain. Then they must cover the Ark of the Covenant with it.  
**4:6** Over this they must put a covering made from fine leather. Then they must spread the solid blue cloth over it. And they must put the poles in the rings on the Ark of the Covenant.  
**4:7** “Then they must spread a blue cloth over the table. This is the table for the bread that shows we are in God’s presence. They must put the plates, spoons, bowls and the jars for drink offerings on the table. They must leave the bread on the table.  
**4:8** Then they must put a red cloth over all of these things. They must cover everything with fine leather. Then they must put the poles in place.  
**4:9** “With a blue cloth they must cover the lampstand, its lamps, its wick trimmers and trays. They must cover all the jars for the oil used in the lamps.  
**4:10** Then they must wrap everything in fine leather. They must put all these things on a frame for carrying them.  
**4:11** “They must spread a blue cloth over the gold altar. Then they must cover it with fine leather. They must put its poles in place.  
**4:12** “They must gather everything used for serving in the Holy Place. They must wrap them in a blue cloth. Then they must cover that with fine leather. They must put these things on a frame for carrying them.  
**4:13** “They must clean the ashes off the bronze altar. Then they must spread a purple cloth over it.  
**4:14** They must gather all the things used for serving at the altar. These are the pans for carrying the fire, meat forks, shovels and bowls. They must put them on the bronze altar. Then they must spread a covering of fine leather over it. They must put the carrying poles in the rings on the altar.  
**4:15** “When the camp is ready to move, have Aaron and his sons cover all the holy things. Then the Kohathites may go in and carry away those things. This way they won’t touch the holy things and die. It is the Kohathites’ job to carry the things that are in the Meeting Tent.  
**4:16** “Eleazar son of Aaron, the priest, will be responsible for the Holy Tent. And he is responsible for everything in it: the oil for the lamp, the sweet-smelling incense, the continual grain offering and the oil used to appoint priests and things to the Lord’s service.”  
**4:17** The Lord said to Moses and Aaron,  
**4:18** “Don’t let the Kohathites be destroyed from the Levites.  
**4:19** Do this for the Kohathites. Then they may go near the Most Holy Place and not die. Aaron and his sons must go in and show each Kohathite what to do. And they must show each man what to carry.  
**4:20** The Kohathites must not enter and look at the holy things, even for a second. If they do, they will die.”  
**4:21** The Jobs of the Gershon Family The Lord said to Moses,  
**4:22** “Count the Gershonites by families and family groups.  
**4:23** Count the men from 30 to 50 years old. Everyone has a job to do in the Meeting Tent.  
**4:24** “This is what the Gershonite family groups must do and what they must carry.  
**4:25** They must carry the pieces of cloth of the Holy Tent and Meeting Tent. They must also carry its covering and the covering made from fine leather. And they must carry the curtains for the entrance to the Meeting Tent.  
**4:26** They must carry the curtains of the courtyard that goes around the Holy Tent and the altar. They must carry the curtain for the entry to the courtyard and the ropes and all the things used with the curtains. They must do everything connected with these things.  
**4:27** Aaron and his sons are in charge of what the Gershonites do or carry. You tell them what they are responsible for carrying.  
**4:28** This is the work of the Gershonite family group at the Meeting Tent. Ithamar son of Aaron, the priest, will direct their work.  
**4:29** The Jobs of the Merari Family “Count the Merarite families and family groups.  
**4:30** Count the men from 30 to 50 years old. They will work at the Meeting Tent.  
**4:31** It is their job to carry the frame of the Meeting Tent, the crossbars, the posts and bases.  
**4:32** They must also carry the posts that go around the courtyard. They must carry the bases, tent pegs and ropes. And they must carry everything that is used with the poles around the courtyard. Tell each man exactly what to carry.  
**4:33** This is the work the Merarites will do for the Meeting Tent. Ithamar son of Aaron, the priest, will direct their work.”  
**4:34** The Levite Families Moses, Aaron and the leaders of Israel counted the Kohathites by families and family groups.  
**4:35** The men from 30 to 50 years old were to work at the Meeting Tent.  
**4:36** The family group had 2,750 men.  
**4:37** This was the total of the Kohath family group who worked at the Meeting Tent. Moses and Aaron counted them as the Lord had commanded Moses.  
**4:38** Also, the Gershon family group was counted by families and family groups.  
**4:39** The men from 30 to 50 years old were given work at the Meeting Tent.  
**4:40** The families and family groups had 2,630 men.  
**4:41** This was the total of the Gershon family group who worked at the Meeting Tent. Moses and Aaron counted them as the Lord had commanded.  
**4:42** Also, the men in the families and family groups of the Merari family were counted.  
**4:43** The men from 30 to 50 years old were to work at the Meeting Tent.  
**4:44** The family group had 3,200 men.  
**4:45** This was the total of the Merari family group. Moses and Aaron counted them as the Lord had commanded Moses.  
**4:46** So Moses, Aaron and the leaders of Israel counted all the Levites. They counted them by families and family groups.  
**4:47** The men from 30 to 50 were given work at the Meeting Tent. They also carried the Tent.  
**4:48** The total number of these men was 8,580.  
**4:49** Each man was counted as the Lord had commanded Moses. Each man was given his work and told what to carry. This was as the Lord had commanded Moses.  

## 5장

**5:1** Rules About Cleanliness The Lord said to Moses,  
**5:2** “Command the Israelites to send away from camp anyone with a harmful skin disease. Send away anyone who gives off body fluid. And send away anyone who has touched a dead body.  
**5:3** Send both men and women away from camp. Then they won’t spread the disease there. I am living among you.”  
**5:4** So Israel obeyed God’s command. They sent those people outside the camp as the Lord had told Moses.  
**5:5** Paying for Doing Wrong The Lord said to Moses,  
**5:6** “Tell the Israelites: ‘When a man or woman does something wrong to another person, that is really sinning against God. That person is guilty.  
**5:7** He must tell what he has done wrong. And he must fully pay for the wrong he has done. Then he must add one-fifth to it. And he must give it to the person he wronged.  
**5:8** But that person may be dead. That person may not have any close relatives to receive the payment. Then the one who did wrong owes the Lord. That person must pay the priest. The priest must sacrifice a male sheep to remove the wrong so that person will belong to God.  
**5:9** When an Israelite brings a holy gift, he should give it to the priest.  
**5:10** No one has to give these holy gifts. But if someone does give them, they belong to the priest.’”  
**5:11** Suspicious Husbands Then the Lord said to Moses,  
**5:12** “Tell the Israelites: ‘A man’s wife might be unfaithful to him.  
**5:13** She might commit adultery with another man. Her sin might be kept hidden from her husband. Her husband might not know about the wrong thing she did. Perhaps no one saw it, and she wasn’t caught.  
**5:14** But her husband might have feelings of jealousy whether she has sinned or not.  
**5:15** He then should take her to the priest. The husband must also take an offering for her. This offering will be two quarts of barley flour. He must not pour oil or incense on it. This is a grain offering for jealousy. It is to find out if she is guilty.  
**5:16** “‘The priest will bring in the woman and make her stand before the Lord.  
**5:17** He will take some holy water in a clay jar. Then he will put some dirt from the floor of the Holy Tent into the water.  
**5:18** The priest will make the woman stand before the Lord. Then he will loosen her hair. And he will hand her the grain offering for jealousy. He will hold the bitter water that brings a curse on her.  
**5:19** The priest will tell her what the oath says will happen to her. He will ask her, “Has another man committed adultery with you? Have you been unfaithful to your husband? If you haven’t, then this water that brings a curse won’t hurt you.  
**5:20** But what if you have been unfaithful to your husband? What if you have committed adultery with a man besides your husband?”  
**5:21** The priest will tell her the curse that the oath will bring. He will say, “If you are guilty, the Lord will make the people curse you. Your stomach will get big, and you will not be able to have another baby.  
**5:22** This water that brings a curse will go inside you. Your stomach will get big, and you will not be able to have another baby.” “‘The woman must say, “I agree.”  
**5:23** “‘The priest should write these curses on a scroll. Then he should wash the words off into the water.  
**5:24** Then he will make the woman drink the water that brings a curse. When she does, it may make her sick.  
**5:25** Then the priest will take the grain offering for jealousy from her. He will present it before the Lord. Then he will bring it to the altar.  
**5:26** Then the priest will take a handful of the grain. This is a memorial offering. He will burn it on the altar. After that he will make the woman drink the water.  
**5:27** He will make her drink it to see if she is not pure. He will know if she has sinned against her husband. When it goes into her, her stomach may get big. And she may not be able to have another baby. Her people will turn against her.  
**5:28** But if the woman has not sinned, she is pure. She is not guilty. She will be able to have more babies.  
**5:29** “‘So this is the teaching about jealousy. This is what to do when a woman is unfaithful to her husband.  
**5:30** It also should be done if the man gets jealous because he suspects his wife. The priest will have her stand before the Lord. Then he will do all these things. This is the teaching.  
**5:31** If the husband is right, the woman will suffer.’”  

## 6장

**6:1** The Nazirites The Lord said to Moses,  
**6:2** “Tell the Israelites: ‘A man or a woman might want to promise to belong to the Lord in a special way. This person will be called a Nazirite.  
**6:3** During this time, he must not drink wine or beer. He must not drink vinegar made from wine or beer. He must not even drink grape juice or eat grapes or raisins.  
**6:4** While he is a Nazirite, he must not eat anything that comes from the grapevine. He must not even eat the seeds or the skin.  
**6:5** “‘During the time he promised to belong to the Lord, he must not cut his hair. He must be holy until this special time is over. He must let his hair grow long.  
**6:6** During his special time of belonging to the Lord, a Nazirite must not go near a dead body.  
**6:7** Even if his own father, mother, brother or sister dies, he must not touch them. This would make him unclean. He must still keep his promise to belong to God in a special way.  
**6:8** While he is a Nazirite, he belongs to the Lord.  
**6:9** “‘If he is next to someone who dies suddenly, his hair has been made unclean. It was part of his promise. So he must shave his head seven days later to be clean.  
**6:10** Then on the eighth day, he must bring two doves or two young pigeons to the priest. The priest will be at the entrance to the Holy Tent.  
**6:11** The priest will offer one as a sin offering. He will offer the other as a burnt offering. This removes sin so the man will belong to God. He had sinned because he was near a dead body. That same day he will again promise to let his hair grow for the Lord.  
**6:12** He must give himself to the Lord for another special time. He must bring a male lamb a year old. It will be a penalty offering. The days of the special time before don’t count. This is because he became unclean during his first special time.  
**6:13** “‘This is the teaching for a Nazirite. When the promised time is over, he must go to the entrance of the Meeting Tent.  
**6:14** There he will give his offerings to the Lord. He must offer a year-old male lamb as a burnt offering. It must have nothing wrong with it. He must also offer a year-old female lamb as a sin offering. There must be nothing wrong with it. And he must bring a male sheep for a fellowship offering. There must be nothing wrong with it.  
**6:15** He must also bring the grain offerings and drink offerings that go with them. And he must bring a basket of bread made without yeast. There must be loaves made with fine flour mixed with oil. And there must be wafers made without yeast spread with oil.  
**6:16** “‘The priest will give these offerings to the Lord. He will make the sin offering and the burnt offering.  
**6:17** Then he will kill the male sheep as a fellowship offering to the Lord. Along with it, he will present the basket of bread. He will also make the grain offering and the drink offering along with it.  
**6:18** “‘The Nazirite must go to the entrance of the Meeting Tent. There he must shave off his hair that he grew for his promise. The hair will be put in the fire that is under the sacrifice on the fellowship offering.  
**6:19** “‘After the Nazirite cuts off his hair, the priest will give him a boiled shoulder from the male sheep. From the basket he will also give him a loaf and a wafer. Both are made without yeast.  
**6:20** Then the priest will present them to the Lord. This is an offering presented before the Lord. They are holy and belong to the priest. Also, he is to present the breast and the thigh from the male sheep to the Lord. They also belong to the priest. After that, the Nazirite may drink wine.  
**6:21** “‘This is the teaching for the Nazirite promise. If a person makes the Nazirite promise, he must give all of these gifts to the Lord. If he promised to do more, he must keep his promise. That is also the teaching of the Nazirite promise.’”  
**6:22** The Priests’ Blessings The Lord said to Moses,  
**6:23** “Tell Aaron and his sons, ‘This is how you should bless the Israelites. Say to them:  
**6:24** “May the Lord bless you and keep you.  
**6:25** May the Lord show you his kindness. May he have mercy on you.  
**6:26** May the Lord watch over you and give you peace.”’  
**6:27** “So Aaron and his sons will bless the Israelites with my name. And I will bless them.”

**7:2** Then the leaders of Israel made offerings. These were the heads of the families and leaders of each tribe. They were the men who counted the people.  
**7:3** They brought to the Lord 6 covered carts and 12 oxen. Each leader gave an ox. Every two leaders gave a cart. They gave these to the Holy Tent.  
**7:4** The Lord said to Moses,  
**7:5** “Accept these gifts from the leaders. Use them in the work of the Meeting Tent. Give them to the Levites as they need them.”  
**7:6** So Moses accepted the carts and the oxen. He gave them to the Levites.  
**7:7** He gave 2 carts and 4 oxen to the Gershonites. This is what they needed for their work.  
**7:8** Then Moses gave 4 carts and 8 oxen to the Merarites. This is what they needed for their work. Ithamar son of Aaron, the priest, directed the work of all of them.

These weights were set by the Holy Place measure. Each bowl and plate were filled with fine flour mixed with oil. This was for a grain offering. Each leader also brought a large gold dish that weighed about 4 ounces. It was filled with incense. Each leader also brought 1 young bull, 1 male sheep and 1 male lamb a year old. These were for a burnt offering. Each leader also brought 1 male goat for a sin offering. Each leader brought 2 oxen, 5 male sheep, 5 male goats and 5 male lambs a year old. All of these were sacrificed for a fellowship offering. On the first day Nahshon son of Amminadab brought his gifts. He was the leader of the tribe of Judah. On the seventh day Elishama son of Ammihud brought his gifts. He was the leader of the tribe of Ephraim. On the eighth day Gamaliel son of Pedahzur brought his gifts. On the tenth day Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai brought his gifts. He was the leader of the tribe of Dan. On the eleventh day Pagiel son of Ocran brought his gifts. **7:19** Moses went into the Meeting Tent to speak with the Lord. He heard the Lord speaking to him. The voice was coming from between the two gold creatures with wings. They were above the lid of the Ark of the Covenant. And the Lord spoke with him.  

## 8장

**8:1** The Lampstand The Lord said to Moses,  
**8:2** “Speak to Aaron. Tell him, ‘Put the seven lamps where they can light the area in front of the lampstand.’”  
**8:3** Aaron did this. He put the lamps so they lighted the area in front of the lampstand. He obeyed the command the Lord gave Moses.  
**8:4** The lampstand was made from hammered gold. It was gold from its base to the flowers. It was made exactly the way the Lord had showed Moses.  
**8:5** The Levites The Lord said to Moses,  
**8:6** “Take the Levites away from the other Israelites and make them clean.  
**8:7** This is what you should do to make them clean. Sprinkle the cleansing water on them. Have them shave their bodies and wash their clothes. Then they will be clean.  
**8:8** They must take a young bull and the grain offering that goes with it. The grain offering will be flour mixed with oil. Then take another young bull for a sin offering.  
**8:9** Bring the Levites to the front of the Meeting Tent. And gather all the Israelites around.  
**8:10** Bring the Levites before the Lord. Then the Israelites should put their hands on them.  
**8:11** Aaron will present the Levites before the Lord. They will be like an offering presented from the Israelites. Then the Levites will be ready to do the work of the Lord.  
**8:12** “The Levites will put their hands on the bulls’ heads. One bull will be a sin offering to the Lord. The other will be a burnt offering. This will remove the sins of the Levites so they will belong to God.  
**8:13** Make the Levites stand in front of Aaron and his sons. Then present the Levites as an offering to the Lord.  
**8:14** In this way you must set apart the Levites from the other Israelites. The Levites will be mine.  
**8:15** “So make the Levites pure. And present them as an offering. Then they may come to work at the Meeting Tent.  
**8:16** They will be given to me from the Israelites. I have taken them for myself. They are mine instead of the firstborn son of every Israelite woman.  
**8:17** Every firstborn male in Israel—man or animal—is mine. I killed all the firstborn in Egypt. So now I set them aside for myself.  
**8:18** I have taken the Levites instead of all the firstborn sons in Israel.  
**8:19** I have chosen the Levites from all the Israelites. And I have given them to Aaron and his sons. They will serve all the Israelites at the Meeting Tent. They will help remove the Israelites’ sins so they will belong to God. Then no disaster will strike the Israelites when they approach the Holy Place.”  
**8:20** So Moses, Aaron and all the Israelites obeyed the Lord. They did with the Levites what the Lord commanded Moses.  
**8:21** The Levites made themselves clean and washed their clothes. Then Aaron presented them as an offering to the Lord. Aaron also removed their sins so they would be pure for the Lord.  
**8:22** After that, the Levites came to the Meeting Tent to work. Aaron and his sons told them what to do. They did with the Levites what the Lord commanded Moses.  
**8:23** The Lord said to Moses,  
**8:24** “This command is for the Levites. Every man 25 years old or older must come to the Meeting Tent. They all have a job to do in the work there.  
**8:25** At the age of 50, he must retire from his job. He doesn’t have to work again.  
**8:26** He may help his fellow Levites with their work at the Meeting Tent. But he must not do the work himself. This is the way you are to give the Levites their jobs.”  

## 9장

**9:1** The Passover The Lord spoke to Moses in the Desert of Sinai. This was in the first month of the second year after the Israelites left Egypt. He said,  
**9:2** “Tell the Israelites to celebrate the Passover at the appointed time.  
**9:3** That appointed time is the fourteenth day of this month. They should celebrate it at twilight. They must obey all the rules about it.”  
**9:4** So Moses told the Israelites to celebrate the Passover.  
**9:5** And so they did. It was in the Desert of Sinai at twilight. This was on the fourteenth day of the first month. The Israelites did everything just as the Lord commanded Moses.  
**9:6** But some of the people could not celebrate the Passover on that day. They were unclean because of a dead body. So they went to Moses and Aaron that day.  
**9:7** They said to Moses, “We are unclean because of a dead body. But why should we be kept from offering gifts to the Lord at this appointed time? Why can’t we join the other Israelites?”  
**9:8** Moses said to them, “Wait. I will find out what the Lord says about you.”  
**9:9** Then the Lord said to Moses,  
**9:10** “Tell the Israelites this: ‘You or your descendants might become unclean because of a dead body. Or, you might be away on a trip during the Passover. Still celebrate the Lord’s Passover.  
**9:11** But celebrate it at twilight on the fourteenth day of the second month. Eat the lamb with bitter herbs and bread made without yeast.  
**9:12** Don’t leave any of it until the next morning. Don’t break any of its bones. When you celebrate the Passover, follow all the rules.  
**9:13** Anyone who is clean and is not away on a trip must eat the Passover. If he doesn’t, he must be separated from his people. He did not give an offering to the Lord at the appointed time. He must be punished for his sin.  
**9:14** “‘A foreigner among you may celebrate the Lord’s Passover. But he must follow all the rules. You must have the same rules for foreigners as you have for yourselves.’”  
**9:15** The Cloud Above the Tent On the day the Holy Tent was set up, a cloud covered it. (The Holy Tent was also called the Tent of the Agreement.) From dusk until dawn the cloud above the Tent looked like fire.  
**9:16** The cloud stayed above the Tent. At night it looked like fire.  
**9:17** When the cloud moved from its place over the Tent, the Israelites moved. Wherever the cloud stopped, the Israelites camped.  
**9:18** So the Israelites moved at the Lord’s command. And they camped at his command. While the cloud stayed over the Tent, they stayed in place.  
**9:19** Sometimes the cloud stayed over the Tent for a long time. The Israelites obeyed the Lord and did not move.  
**9:20** Sometimes the cloud was over it only a few days. At the Lord’s command the people camped. And at his command they moved.  
**9:21** Sometimes the cloud stayed only from dusk until dawn. When the cloud lifted the next morning, the people moved. When the cloud lifted, day or night, the people moved.  
**9:22** The cloud might stay over the Tent for two days, a month or a year. As long as it stayed, the people would camp. But when the cloud lifted, they moved.  
**9:23** At the Lord’s command the people camped. And at his command they moved. They obeyed the Lord’s order that he commanded through Moses.  

## 10장

**10:1** The Silver Trumpets The Lord said to Moses,  
**10:2** “Make two trumpets of hammered silver. Use them to call the people together and to march out of camp.  
**10:3** When both trumpets are blown, the people should gather. They should gather before you at the entrance to the Meeting Tent.  
**10:4** If you blow only one trumpet, the leaders should meet before you.  
**10:5** When you blow the trumpets once, the tribes camping on the east should move.  
**10:6** When you blow them again, the tribes camping on the south should move. The sound will tell them to move.  
**10:7** When you want to gather the people, blow the trumpets. But don’t blow them the same way.  
**10:8** “Aaron’s sons, the priests, should blow the trumpets. This is a law for you and your descendants from now on.  
**10:9** You might be fighting an enemy who attacks you in your own land. Blow the trumpets. The Lord your God will remember you. He will save you from your enemies.  
**10:10** Also blow your trumpets at happy times. Blow them during your feasts and at New Moon festivals. Blow them over your burnt offerings and fellowship offerings. They will help you remember your God. I am the Lord your God.”  
**10:11** The Israelites Move Camp The cloud lifted from the Tent of the Agreement. This was on the twentieth day of the second month of the second year.  
**10:12** So the Israelites moved from the Desert of Sinai. They moved until the cloud stopped in the Desert of Paran.  
**10:13** This was their first time to move. They did it as the Lord had commanded Moses.  
**10:14** The divisions from the camp of Judah moved first under their flag. Nahshon son of Amminadab was the commander.  
**10:15** Nethanel son of Zuar was over the division of the tribe of Issachar.  
**10:16** Eliab son of Helon was over the division of the tribe of Zebulun.  
**10:17** Then the Meeting Tent was taken down. The Gershonites and Merarites, who carried it, moved next.  
**10:18** Then came the divisions from the camp of Reuben under their flag. Elizur son of Shedeur was the commander.  
**10:19** Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai was over the division of the tribe of Simeon.  
**10:20** Eliasaph son of Deuel was over the division of the tribe of Gad.  
**10:21** Then came the Kohathites. They carried the holy things. The Holy Tent was to be set up before they arrived.  
**10:22** Next came the divisions from the camp of Ephraim under their flag. Elishama son of Ammihud was the commander.  
**10:23** Gamaliel son of Pedahzur was over the division of the tribe of Manasseh.  
**10:24** Abidan son of Gideoni was over the division of the tribe of Benjamin.  
**10:25** The last ones were the rear guard for all the tribes. These were the divisions from the camp of Dan under their flag. Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai was the commander.  
**10:26** Pagiel son of Ocran was over the division of the tribe of Asher.  
**10:27** Ahira son of Enan was over the division of the tribe of Naphtali.  
**10:28** This was the order the Israelite divisions marched in when they moved.  
**10:29** Hobab was the son of Reuel the Midianite. Reuel, who is also called Jethro, was Moses’ father-in-law. Moses said to Hobab, “We are moving to the land God promised to give us. Come with us. We will be good to you. The Lord has promised good things to Israel.”  
**10:30** But Hobab answered, “No, I will not go. I will go back to my own land where I was born.”  
**10:31** But Moses said, “Please don’t leave us. You know where we can camp in the desert. You can be our guide.  
**10:32** Come with us. We will share with you all the good things the Lord gives us.”  
**10:33** So they left the mountain of the Lord. The Ark of the Lord’s Covenant went in front of the people. For three days they looked for a place to camp.  
**10:34** The Lord’s cloud was over them during the day when they left their camp.  
**10:35** When the Ark of the Covenant left the camp, Moses always said, “Rise up, Lord! Scatter your enemies. Make those who are against you run from you.”  
**10:36** And when the Ark of the Covenant was set down, Moses always said, “Return, Lord, to the thousands of people of Israel.”  

## 11장

**11:1** Fire from the Lord The people complained to the Lord about their troubles. When he heard them, he became angry. Fire from the Lord burned among the people. It burned the edge of the camp.  
**11:2** So the people cried out to Moses. He prayed to the Lord, and the fire stopped burning.  
**11:3** So that place was called Taberah. The people named it that because the Lord’s fire had burned among them.  
**11:4** The 70 Older Leaders Some troublemakers among them wanted better food. Soon all the Israelites began complaining. They said, “We want meat!  
**11:5** We remember the fish we ate for free in Egypt. We also had cucumbers, melons, leeks, onions and garlic.  
**11:6** But now we have lost our appetite. We never see anything but this manna!”  
**11:7** The manna was like small white seeds.  
**11:8** The people would go to gather it. Then they ground it in handmills. Or they crushed it between stones. They cooked it in a pot or made cakes with it. It tasted like bread baked with olive oil.  
**11:9** When the dew fell on the camp each night, so did the manna.  
**11:10** Moses heard every family crying. They stood in the entrances of their tents. The Lord became very angry. And Moses got upset.  
**11:11** He asked the Lord, “Why have you brought me this trouble? I’m your servant. What have I done wrong? Why did you make me responsible for all these people?  
**11:12** I am not the father of all these people. I didn’t give birth to them. Why do you make me carry them to the land you promised to our ancestors? Must I carry them in my arms as a nurse carries a baby?  
**11:13** Where can I get meat for all these people? They keep crying to me, ‘We want meat!’  
**11:14** I can’t take care of all these people alone. It is too much for me.  
**11:15** If you are going to continue doing this to me, then kill me now. If you like me, put me to death. Then I won’t have any more troubles.”  
**11:16** The Lord said to Moses, “Bring me 70 of Israel’s elders. Pick men you know are leaders among the people. Bring them to the Meeting Tent. Have them stand there with you.  
**11:17** I will come down and speak with you there. I will take some of the Spirit that is in you. And I will give it to them. They will help you care for the people. Then you will not have to care for them alone.  
**11:18** “Tell the people this: ‘Make yourselves holy. Tomorrow you will eat meat. The Lord heard you cry, “We want meat! We were better off in Egypt!” So now the Lord will give you meat to eat.  
**11:19** You will not eat it for just 1, 2, 5, 10 or even 20 days.  
**11:20** You will eat that meat for a whole month. You will eat it until it comes out your nose. You will hate it. This is because you have rejected the Lord. He is here with you. But you have cried to him. You said, “Why did we ever leave Egypt?”’”  
**11:21** Moses said, “Lord, here are 600, men standing around me. And you say, ‘I will give them enough meat to eat for a month!’  
**11:22** If we killed all the sheep and cattle, that would not be enough. If we caught all the fish in the sea, that would not be enough.”  
**11:23** But the Lord said to Moses, “Do you think I’m weak? You will see if I can do what I say.”  
**11:24** So Moses went out to the people. He told them what the Lord had said. Moses gathered 70 of the elders together. He had them stand around the Tent.  
**11:25** Then the Lord came down in the cloud and spoke to Moses. The Lord took some of the Spirit Moses had. And he gave it to the 70 leaders. With the Spirit in them, they prophesied, but just that one time.  
**11:26** Two men named Eldad and Medad were also listed as leaders. But they did not go to the Tent. They stayed in the camp. The Spirit was given to them. So they prophesied in the camp.  
**11:27** A young man ran to Moses. He said, “Eldad and Medad are prophesying in the camp.”  
**11:28** Joshua son of Nun said, “Moses, my master, stop them!” (Since he was a young boy, Joshua had been Moses’ assistant.)  
**11:29** But Moses answered, “Are you afraid for me? I wish all the Lord’s people could prophesy. I wish the Lord would give his Spirit to all of them!”  
**11:30** Then Moses and the leaders of Israel went back to the camp.  
**11:31** The Quail Come The Lord sent a strong wind from the sea. It blew quail into the area all around the camp. The quail were about three feet above the ground. There were quail a day’s walk in any direction.  
**11:32** The people went out and gathered quail. They gathered all that day, that night and the next day. Everyone gathered at least 60 bushels. Then they spread them around the camp.  
**11:33** But the Lord became very angry. He gave the people a terrible sickness. This came while the meat was still in their mouths.  
**11:34** So the people named that place Kibroth Hattaavah. They named it that because there they buried those who wanted other food.  
**11:35** From Kibroth Hattaavah the people went to stay at Hazeroth.  

## 12장

**12:1** Miriam and Aaron Speak Against Moses’ Wife Miriam and Aaron began to talk against Moses, who had married a Cushite.  
**12:2** They said to themselves, “Is Moses the only one the Lord speaks through? Doesn’t he speak through us?” And the Lord heard this.  
**12:3** (Now Moses was very humble. He was the least proud person on earth.)  
**12:4** So the Lord suddenly spoke to Moses, Aaron and Miriam. He said, “All three of you come to the Meeting Tent now.” So they went.  
**12:5** The Lord came down in a pillar of cloud. He stood at the entrance to the Tent. He called to Aaron and Miriam, and they both came near.  
**12:6** He said, “Listen to my words: When a prophet is among you, I, the Lord, will show myself to him in visions. I will speak to him in dreams.  
**12:7** But this is not true with my servant Moses. I trust him to lead all my people.  
**12:8** I speak face to face with him. I speak clearly, not with hidden meanings. He has even seen the form of the Lord. You should be afraid to speak against my servant Moses.”  
**12:9** The Lord was very angry with them, but he left.  
**12:10** The cloud lifted from the Tent. Then Aaron turned toward Miriam. She was as white as snow. She had a harmful skin disease.  
**12:11** Aaron said to Moses, “Please, my master, forgive us for our foolish sin.  
**12:12** Don’t let her be like a baby who is born dead. (Sometimes a baby is born with half of its flesh eaten away.)”  
**12:13** So Moses cried out to the Lord, “God, please heal her!”  
**12:14** The Lord answered Moses, “If her father had spit in her face, she would have been shamed for seven days. So put her outside the camp for seven days. After that, she may come back.”  
**12:15** So Miriam was shut outside of the camp for seven days. And the people did not move on until she came back.  
**12:16** After that, the people left Hazeroth. And they camped in the Desert of Paran.  

## 13장

**13:1** The Spies Explore Canaan The Lord said to Moses,  
**13:2** “Send men to explore the land of Canaan. I will give that land to the Israelites. Send one leader from each tribe.”  
**13:3** So Moses obeyed the Lord’s command. He sent the Israelite leaders out from the Desert of Paran.  
**13:4** These are their names: from the tribe of Reuben, Shammua son of Zaccur;  
**13:5** from the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat son of Hori;  
**13:6** from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;  
**13:7** from the tribe of Issachar, Igal son of Joseph;  
**13:8** from the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Nun;  
**13:9** from the tribe of Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu;  
**13:10** from the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel son of Sodi;  
**13:11** from the tribe of Manasseh (a tribe of Joseph), Gaddi son of Susi;  
**13:12** from the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gamalli;  
**13:13** from the tribe of Asher, Sethur son of Michael;  
**13:14** from the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi son of Vophsi;  
**13:15** from the tribe of Gad, Geuel son of Maki.  
**13:16** These are the names of the men Moses sent to explore the land. (Moses gave Hoshea son of Nun the new name Joshua.)  
**13:17** Moses sent them to explore Canaan. He said, “Go through southern Canaan and then into the mountains.  
**13:18** See what the land looks like. Are the people who live there strong or weak? Are there a few or many?  
**13:19** What kind of land do they live in? Is it good or bad? What about the towns they live in—do they have walls, or are they open like camps?  
**13:20** What about the soil? Is it fertile or poor? Are there trees there? Try to bring back some of the fruit from that land.” (It was the season for the first grapes.)  
**13:21** So they went up and explored the land. They went from the Desert of Zin all the way to Rehob by Lebo Hamath.  
**13:22** They went through the southern area to Hebron. That is where Ahiman, Sheshai and Talmai lived. They were the descendants of Anak. (The city of Hebron had been built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.)  
**13:23** In the Valley of Eshcol, they cut off a branch of a grapevine. It had one bunch of grapes on it. They carried that branch on a pole between two of them. They also got some pomegranates and figs.  
**13:24** They call that place the Valley of Eshcol. That is because the Israelites cut off the bunch of grapes there.  
**13:25** After 40 days of exploring the land, the men returned to the camp.  
**13:26** They came back to Moses and Aaron and all the Israelites at Kadesh. This was in the Desert of Paran. The men reported to them and showed everybody the fruit from the land.  
**13:27** They told Moses, “We went to the land where you sent us. It is a land where much food grows! Here is some of its fruit.  
**13:28** But the people who live there are strong. Their cities are walled and large. We even saw some Anakites there.  
**13:29** The Amalekites live in the southern area. The Hittites, Jebusites and Amorites live in the mountains. The Canaanites live near the sea and along the Jordan River.”  
**13:30** Then Caleb told the people near Moses to be quiet. Caleb said, “We should go up and take the land for ourselves. We can do it.”  
**13:31** But the men who had gone with him said, “We can’t attack those people. They are stronger than we are.”  
**13:32** And those men gave the Israelites a bad report about the land they explored. They said, “The land would eat us up. All the people we saw are very tall.  
**13:33** We saw the Nephilim people there. (The Anakites come from the Nephilim people.) We felt like grasshoppers. And we looked like grasshoppers to them.”

When parents sin, he will also punish their children. He will punish their grandchildren, great-grandchildren and great-great-grandchildren.’

**15:1** Rules About Sacrifices The Lord said to Moses,  
**15:2** “Speak to the people of Israel. Say to them, ‘You will enter a land that I am giving you as a home.

**15:5** Each time you offer a lamb as a burnt offering or sacrifice, also prepare a quart of wine. This is a drink offering.  
**15:6** “‘If you are giving a male sheep, also prepare a grain offering. It should be four quarts of fine flour mixed with one and one-fourth quarts of olive oil.  
**15:7** Also prepare one and one-fourth quarts of wine. This is a drink offering. Offer it to the Lord. Its smell is pleasing to him.  
**15:8** “‘You might prepare a young bull as a burnt offering or sacrifice. This might be for a fellowship offering or for a special promise to the Lord.  
**15:9** Bring a grain offering with the bull. It should be six quarts of fine flour mixed with two quarts of olive oil.  
**15:10** Also bring two quarts of wine as a drink offering. This offering is made by fire. And its smell will be pleasing to the Lord.  
**15:11** Prepare each bull or male sheep, lamb or young goat this way.  
**15:12** Do this for every one of the animals you bring.  
**15:13** “‘All citizens must do these things in this way. And the smell of their offerings by fire will be pleasing to the Lord.  
**15:14** From now on foreigners will live among you. They will make offerings by fire so the smell will be pleasing to the Lord. They must offer them the same way you do.

**15:18** “Tell the Israelites: ‘You are going to another land. I am taking you there.  
**15:19** When you eat the food there, offer part of it to the Lord.  
**15:20** Offer a loaf of bread from the first of your grain. That will be your offering from the threshing floor.

**15:22** “‘Now what if you forget to obey any of these commands the Lord gave Moses?

Its smell is pleasing to the Lord. By law you must also give the grain offering and the drink offering with it. And you must bring a male goat as a sin offering.

**15:29** The same teaching is for everyone who sins without meaning to. It is the same for those born Israelites and for foreigners living among you.  
**15:30** “‘But anyone who sins on purpose is against the Lord. That person must be separated from his people. It is the same for someone born among you or a foreigner.

**16:1** Korah, Dathan, Abiram and On Korah, Dathan, Abiram and On turned against Moses. (Korah was the son of Izhar. Izhar was the son of Kohath, and Kohath was the son of Levi. Dathan and Abiram were brothers, the sons of Eliab. And On was the son of Peleth.) Dathan, Abiram and On were from the tribe of Reuben.  
**16:2** These 4 men gathered 250 other Israelite men and challenged Moses. They were well-known leaders chosen by the community.  
**16:3** They came as a group to speak to Moses and Aaron. The men said, “You have gone too far. All the people are holy. Every one of them is holy. And the Lord is with them. So why do you put yourselves above all the people?”  
**16:4** When Moses heard this, he bowed facedown.  
**16:5** Then he said to Korah and all his followers: “Tomorrow morning the Lord will show who belongs to him. The Lord will bring the one who is holy near to him. The Lord will bring to himself the person he chooses.  
**16:6** So Korah, you and all your followers do this: Get some pans for burning incense.  
**16:7** Tomorrow put fire and incense in them. Then take them before the Lord. He will choose the man who is holy. You Levites have gone too far.”  
**16:8** Moses also said to Korah, “Listen, you Levites.  
**16:9** The God of Israel has separated you from the rest of the Israelites. He brought you near to himself. You do the work in the Lord’s Holy Tent. You stand before all the Israelites and serve them. Isn’t that enough?

**16:19** Korah gathered all his followers who were against Moses and Aaron. And they stood at the entrance to the Meeting Tent. Then the glory of the Lord appeared to everyone.

**16:21** “Move away from these men. In a minute I will destroy them.”

**16:24** “Tell everyone to move away from the tents of Korah, Dathan and Abiram.”

**16:26** Moses warned the people, “Move away from the tents of these evil men! Don’t touch anything of theirs. If you do, you will be destroyed because of their sins.”

**16:29** If these men die a normal death—the way men usually die—then the Lord did not really send me.

**16:39** So Eleazar the priest gathered all the bronze pans. These were the pans brought by the men who were burned up. Eleazar had the pans hammered into flat sheets to put on the altar.  
**16:40** This is what the Lord had commanded him through Moses. These sheets were to remind the Israelites that only descendants of Aaron should burn incense before the Lord. Anyone else would die like Korah and his followers.

**16:42** The people gathered to complain against Moses and Aaron. But when they turned toward the Meeting Tent, the cloud covered it. The glory of the Lord appeared.  
**16:43** Then Moses and Aaron went in front of the Meeting Tent.  
**16:44** The Lord said to Moses,  
**16:45** “Move away from these people. In a minute I am going to destroy them.” So Moses and Aaron bowed facedown.  
**16:46** Then Moses said to Aaron, “Get your pan. Put fire from the altar and incense in it. Hurry to the people and remove their sin. The Lord is angry with them. The sickness has already started.”  
**16:47** So Aaron did as Moses said. He ran to the middle of all the people. The sickness had already started among them. So Aaron offered the incense to remove their sin.  
**16:48** He stood between the living and the dead. And the sickness stopped there.  
**16:49** But 14,700 people died from that sickness. There were also those who died because of Korah.  
**16:50** Then Aaron went back to Moses at the entrance to the Meeting Tent. The terrible sickness had been stopped.

## 19장

**19:1** The Offering for Cleansing The Lord said to Moses and Aaron,  
**19:2** “These are the teachings that the Lord commanded. Tell the Israelites to get a young red cow. It must not have anything wrong with it. It must not have been worked.  
**19:3** Give the cow to Eleazar the priest. He will take it outside the camp and kill it.  
**19:4** Then Eleazar the priest must put some of the blood on his finger. He must sprinkle it seven times toward the front of the Meeting Tent.  
**19:5** Then the whole cow must be burned while he watches. The skin, the meat, the blood and the intestines must all be burned.  
**19:6** Then the priest must take a cedar stick, a hyssop branch and a red string. He must throw them onto the burning cow.  
**19:7** Then the priest must wash himself and his clothes with water. After that, he may come back into the camp. But he will be unclean until evening.  
**19:8** The man who burns the cow must wash himself and his clothes in water. He will be unclean until evening.  
**19:9** “Then someone who is clean will collect the ashes from the cow. He will put them in a clean place outside the camp. The Israelites will use these ashes in a special ceremony to cleanse away sin.  
**19:10** The man who collected the cow’s ashes must wash his clothes. He will be unclean until evening. This is a lasting rule. It is for the Israelites and for the foreigners among them.  
**19:11** “Whoever touches a dead body will be unclean for seven days.  
**19:12** He must wash himself with this water. He must do this on the third day and on the seventh day. Then he will be clean. But if he does not wash himself on the third day and the seventh day, he cannot be clean.  
**19:13** Whoever touches a dead body is unclean. If he stays unclean and goes to the Lord’s Holy Tent, it becomes unclean. So he must be separated from Israel. If the cleansing water is not sprinkled on him, he will stay unclean.  
**19:14** “This is the teaching about someone who dies in his tent. Anyone in the tent or who enters it will be unclean for seven days.  
**19:15** And every open jar or pot without a cover becomes unclean.  
**19:16** If anyone touches someone who was killed by a sword or who died a natural death, he is unclean. If he touches a human bone or a grave, he is unclean. He will be unclean for seven days.  
**19:17** “So you must use the ashes from the burnt offering to make that person clean again. Pour fresh water over the ashes into a jar.  
**19:18** A clean person must take a hyssop branch and dip it into the water. Then he must sprinkle it over the tent and its objects. And he must sprinkle the people who were there. He must sprinkle anyone who touched a bone or the body of someone who was killed. He must sprinkle anyone who touched a dead person or a grave.  
**19:19** Then someone who is clean must sprinkle this water on the unclean person. He must do this on the third day and on the seventh day. On the seventh day that person becomes clean. He must wash his clothes and take a bath. He will be clean that evening.  
**19:20** If anyone who is unclean does not become clean, he must be separated from the community. He was not sprinkled with the cleansing water. He stays unclean. He could make the Holy Tent unclean.  
**19:21** This is a lasting rule. Whoever sprinkles the cleansing water must also wash his clothes. Anyone who touches the water will be unclean until evening.  
**19:22** Anyone the unclean person touches becomes unclean. And whoever touches him will be unclean until evening.”

**20:4** Why did you bring the Lord’s people into this desert? Are we and our animals to die here?  
**20:5** Why did you bring us from Egypt to this terrible place? It has no grain, figs or pomegranates. And there’s no water to drink!”  
**20:6** So Moses and Aaron left the people. Then they went to the entrance of the Meeting Tent. They bowed facedown. And the glory of the Lord appeared to them.  
**20:7** The Lord said to Moses,  
**20:8** “You and your brother Aaron should gather the people. Also take your walking stick. Speak to that rock in front of them. Then water will flow from it. Give that water to the people and their animals.”  
**20:9** So Moses took the stick from in front of the Lord. He did as the Lord had said.  
**20:10** He and Aaron gathered the people in front of the rock. Then Moses said, “Now listen to me, you complainers! Do you want us to bring water out of this rock?”  
**20:11** Then Moses lifted his hand and hit the rock twice with his stick. Water began pouring out. And the people and their animals drank it.  
**20:12** But the Lord said to Moses and Aaron, “You did not believe me. You did not honor me as holy before the people. So you will not lead them into the land I will give them.”  
**20:13** These are the waters of Meribah. Here the Israelites argued with the Lord. And the Lord showed them he was holy.  
**20:14** Edom Will Not Let Israel Pass From Kadesh, Moses sent messengers to the king of Edom. He said, “Your brothers, the Israelites, say to you: You know about all the troubles we have had.  
**20:15** Our ancestors went down into Egypt. And we lived there for many years. The people of Egypt were cruel to our ancestors.  
**20:16** But we cried out to the Lord. He heard us and sent us an angel to bring us out of Egypt. “Now we are here at Kadesh, a town on the edge of your land.  
**20:17** Please let us pass through your country. We will not touch any fields of grain or vineyards. We will not drink water from the wells. We will travel only along the king’s road. We will not turn right or left until we have passed through your country.”  
**20:18** But the king of Edom answered: “You may not pass through here. If you try, I will come and meet you with swords.”  
**20:19** The Israelites answered: “We will go along the main road. If our animals drink any of your water, we will pay for it. We only want to walk through. That’s all.”  
**20:20** But he answered: “You may not pass through here.” Then the Edomites went out to meet the Israelites with a large and powerful army.  
**20:21** The Edomites refused to let them pass through their country. So the Israelites turned back.  
**20:22** Aaron Dies All the Israelites moved from Kadesh to Mount Hor.  
**20:23** It was near the border of Edom. The Lord said to Moses and Aaron,  
**20:24** “Aaron will die. He will not enter the land that I’m giving to the Israelites. This is because you both acted against my command at the waters of Meribah.  
**20:25** Take Aaron and his son Eleazar up on Mount Hor.  
**20:26** Take off Aaron’s special clothes and put them on his son Eleazar. Aaron will die there. He will join his ancestors.”  
**20:27** Moses obeyed the Lord’s command. They climbed up Mount Hor. All the people saw them go.  
**20:28** Moses took off Aaron’s clothes. He put them on Aaron’s son Eleazar. Then Aaron died there on top of the mountain. And Moses and Eleazar came back down the mountain.  
**20:29** Then all the people learned that Aaron was dead. So everyone in Israel cried for him for 30 days.  

## 21장

**21:1** War with the Canaanites The Canaanite king of Arad lived in the southern area. He heard that the Israelites were coming on the road to Atharim. So he attacked them and captured some of them.  
**21:2** Then the Israelites made this promise to the Lord: “If you will help us defeat these people, we will completely destroy their cities.”  
**21:3** The Lord listened to the Israelites. And he let them defeat the Canaanites. The Israelites completely destroyed the Canaanites and their cities. So the place was named Hormah.  
**21:4** The Bronze Snake The Israelites left Mount Hor and went on the road toward the Gulf of Aqaba. They did this to go around the country of Edom. But the people became impatient on the way.  
**21:5** They grumbled at God and Moses. They said, “Why did you bring us out of Egypt? We will die in this desert! There is no bread! There is no water! And we hate this terrible food!”  
**21:6** So the Lord sent them poisonous snakes. They bit the people, and many of the Israelites died.  
**21:7** The people came to Moses and said, “We sinned when we grumbled at you and the Lord. Pray that the Lord will take away these snakes.” So Moses prayed for the people.  
**21:8** The Lord said to Moses, “Make a bronze snake. And put it on a pole. If anyone is bitten, he should look at it. Then he will live.”  
**21:9** So Moses made a bronze snake. And he put it on a pole. Then when a snake bit anyone, he looked at the bronze snake and lived.  
**21:10** The Journey to Moab The Israelites went and camped at Oboth.  
**21:11** They went from Oboth to Iye Abarim. This was in the desert east of Moab.  
**21:12** From there they went and camped in the Zered Valley.  
**21:13** From there they went and camped across the Arnon. This was in the desert just inside the Amorite country. The Arnon is the border between the Moabites and the Amorites.  
**21:14** That is why the Book of the Wars of the Lord says: “. . . and Waheb in Suphah, and the ravines, the Arnon,  
**21:15** and the slopes of the ravines that lead to the settlement of Ar. These places are at the border of Moab.”  
**21:16** The Israelites went from there to Beer. A well is there where the Lord said to Moses, “Gather the people. I will give them water.”  
**21:17** Then the Israelites sang this song: “Pour out water, well! Sing about it.  
**21:18** Princes dug this well. Important men made this hole. With their scepters and poles, they dug it.” The people went from the desert to Mattanah.  
**21:19** From Mattanah they went to Nahaliel and on to Bamoth.  
**21:20** From Bamoth they went to the valley of Moab. There the top of Mount Pisgah looks over the desert.  
**21:21** Sihon and Og The people of Israel sent messengers to Sihon, king of the Amorites. They said to him,  
**21:22** “Let us pass through your country. We will not go through any fields of grain or vineyards. We will not drink water from the wells. We will travel only along the king’s road. We will stay on it until we have passed through your country.”  
**21:23** But King Sihon would not let the Israelites pass through his country. He gathered his army together. They marched out to meet Israel in the desert. At Jahaz they fought the Israelites.  
**21:24** Israel killed the king. Then they captured his land from the Arnon River to the Jabbok River. They took the land as far as the Ammonite border. That border was strongly defended.  
**21:25** Israel captured all the Amorite cities and lived in them. They took Heshbon and all the towns around it.  
**21:26** Heshbon was the city where Sihon, the Amorite king, lived. In the past he had fought with the king of Moab. Sihon had taken all the land as far as the Arnon.  
**21:27** That is why the poets say: “Come to Heshbon and rebuild it. Rebuild Sihon’s city.  
**21:28** A fire began in Heshbon. Flames came from Sihon’s city. It destroyed Ar in Moab. It burned the Arnon highlands.  
**21:29** How terrible for you, Moab! The people of Chemosh are ruined. His sons ran away. His daughters were captured by Sihon, king of the Amorites.  
**21:30** But we defeated those Amorites. We ruined their towns from Heshbon to Dibon. We destroyed them as far as Nophah, near Medeba.”  
**21:31** So Israel lived in the land of the Amorites.  
**21:32** Moses sent spies to the town of Jazer. Then they captured the towns around it. They forced out the Amorites who lived there.  
**21:33** Then the Israelites went up the road toward Bashan. Og, the king of Bashan, and his army marched out to meet the Israelites. They fought at Edrei.  
**21:34** The Lord said to Moses, “Don’t be afraid of him. I will hand him, his whole army and his land over to you. Do to him what you did to Sihon, the Amorite king who lived in Heshbon.”  
**21:35** So the Israelites killed Og and his sons and all his army. No one was left alive. And they took his land.  

## 22장

**22:1** Balaam and Balak Then the people of Israel went to the plains of Moab. They camped near the Jordan River across from Jericho.  
**22:2** Balak son of Zippor saw everything the Israelites had done to the Amorites.  
**22:3** And Moab was scared of so many Israelites. Truly, Moab was terrified by them.  
**22:4** The Moabites said to the elders of Midian, “This mob will take everything around us. It will be like an ox eating grass.” Balak son of Zippor was the king of Moab at this time.  
**22:5** He sent messengers to Balaam son of Beor at Pethor. It was near the Euphrates River in the land of Amaw. Balak said, “A nation has come out of Egypt. They cover the land. They have camped next to me.  
**22:6** They are too powerful for me. So come and put a curse on them. Maybe then I can defeat them and make them leave the area. I know that if you bless someone, the blessings happen. And if you put a curse on someone, it happens.”  
**22:7** The elders of Moab and Midian went with payment in their hands. They found Balaam. Then they told him what Balak had said.  
**22:8** Balaam said to them, “Stay here for the night. I will tell you what the Lord tells me.” So the Moabite leaders stayed with him.  
**22:9** God came to Balaam and asked, “Who are these men with you?”  
**22:10** Balaam said to God, “The king of Moab, Balak son of Zippor, sent them. He sent me this message:  
**22:11** ‘A nation has come out of Egypt. They cover the land. So come and put a curse on them. Then maybe I can fight them and force them out of my land.’”  
**22:12** But God said to Balaam, “Do not go with them. Don’t put a curse on those people. I have blessed them.”  
**22:13** The next morning Balaam awoke and said to Balak’s leaders, “Go back to your own country. The Lord will not let me go with you.”  
**22:14** So the Moabite leaders went back to Balak. They said, “Balaam refused to come with us.”  
**22:15** So Balak sent other leaders. He sent more leaders this time. And they were more important.  
**22:16** They went to Balaam and said, “Balak son of Zippor says this: Please don’t let anything stop you from coming to me.  
**22:17** I will pay you well. I will do what you say. Come and put a curse on these people for me.”  
**22:18** But Balaam answered Balak’s servants, “King Balak could give me his palace full of silver and gold. But I cannot disobey the Lord my God in anything, great or small.  
**22:19** You stay here tonight as the other men did. I will find out what more the Lord tells me.”  
**22:20** That night God came to Balaam. He said, “These men have come to ask you to go with them. Go. But only do what I tell you.”  
**22:21** Balaam and His Donkey Balaam got up the next morning. He put a saddle on his donkey. Then he went with the Moabite leaders.  
**22:22** But God became angry because Balaam went. So the angel of the Lord stood in the road to stop Balaam. Balaam was riding his donkey. And he had two servants with him.  
**22:23** The donkey saw the angel of the Lord standing in the road. The angel had a sword in his hand. So the donkey left the road and went into the field. Balaam hit the donkey to force her back on the road.  
**22:24** Later, the angel of the Lord stood on a narrow path between two vineyards. There were walls on both sides.  
**22:25** Again the donkey saw the angel of the Lord. So the donkey walked close to one wall. This crushed Balaam’s foot against the wall. So he hit her again.  
**22:26** The angel of the Lord went ahead again. The angel stood at a narrow place. It was too narrow to turn left or right.  
**22:27** The donkey saw the angel of the Lord. So she lay down under Balaam. Balaam was very angry and hit her with his stick.  
**22:28** Then the Lord made the donkey talk. She said to Balaam, “What have I done to make you hit me three times?”  
**22:29** Balaam answered the donkey, “You have made me look foolish! I wish I had a sword in my hand! I would kill you right now!”  
**22:30** But the donkey said to Balaam, “I am your very own donkey. You have ridden me for years. Have I ever done this to you before?” “No,” Balaam said.  
**22:31** Then the Lord let Balaam see the angel. The angel of the Lord was standing in the road with his sword drawn. Then Balaam bowed facedown on the ground.  
**22:32** The angel of the Lord asked Balaam, “Why have you hit your donkey three times? I have stood here to stop you. What you are doing is wrong.  
**22:33** The donkey saw me. She turned away from me three times. If she had not turned away, I would have killed you by now. But I would let her live.”  
**22:34** Then Balaam said to the angel of the Lord, “I have sinned. I did not know you were standing in the road to stop me. If I am wrong, I will go back.”  
**22:35** The angel of the Lord said to Balaam, “Go with these men. But say only what I tell you.” So Balaam went with Balak’s leaders.  
**22:36** Balak heard that Balaam was coming. So he went out to meet him at Ar in Moab. It was beside the Arnon, at the edge of his country.  
**22:37** Balak said to Balaam, “I had asked you before to come quickly. Why didn’t you come to me? I am able to reward you well.”  
**22:38** But Balaam answered, “I have come to you now. But I can’t say just anything. I can only say what God tells me to say.”  
**22:39** Then Balaam went with Balak to Kiriath Huzoth.  
**22:40** Balak offered cattle and sheep as a sacrifice. He gave some meat to Balaam and the leaders with him.  
**22:41** The next morning Balak took Balaam to Bamoth Baal. From there he could see the edge of the Israelite camp.  

## 23장

**23:1** Balaam’s First Message Balaam said, “Build me seven altars here. And prepare seven bulls and seven male sheep for me.”  
**23:2** Balak did what Balaam asked. Then they offered a male sheep and a bull on each of the altars.  
**23:3** Then Balaam said to Balak, “Stay here beside your burnt offering. I will go. Maybe the Lord will come to me. I will tell you whatever he shows me.” Then Balaam went to a higher place.  
**23:4** God came to Balaam there. Balaam said to him, “I have prepared seven altars. And I have offered a bull and a male sheep on each altar.”  
**23:5** The Lord told Balaam what he should say. Then the Lord said, “Go back to Balak and give him this message.”  
**23:6** So Balaam went back to Balak. Balak and all the leaders of Moab were still standing beside his burnt offering.  
**23:7** Then Balaam gave them this message: “Balak brought me here from Aram. The king of Moab brought me from the eastern mountains. Balak said, ‘Come, put a curse on the people of Jacob for me. Come, wish evil on the people of Israel.’  
**23:8** But God has not cursed them. So I cannot curse them. The Lord has not wished evil on them. So I cannot wish evil on them.  
**23:9** I see them from the mountains. I see them from the hills. I see a people who live alone. They think they are different from other nations.  
**23:10** No one can number the people of Jacob. No one can count a fourth of Israel. Let me die like good men. Let me end up like them!”  
**23:11** Balak said to Balaam, “What have you done to me? I brought you here to curse my enemies. But you have only blessed them!”  
**23:12** But Balaam answered, “I must say what the Lord tells me to say.”  
**23:13** Balaam’s Second Message Then Balak said to him, “Come with me to another place. There you can also see the people. But you can only see part of them, not all of them. Curse them for me from there.”  
**23:14** So Balak took Balaam to the field of Zophim. This was on top of Mount Pisgah. There Balak built seven altars. And he offered a bull and a male sheep on each altar.  
**23:15** So Balaam said to Balak, “Stay here by your burnt offering. I will meet with God over there.”  
**23:16** So the Lord came to Balaam. He told Balaam what to say. Then he said, “Go back to Balak and say such and such.”  
**23:17** So Balaam went to Balak. Balak and the leaders of Moab were standing beside his burnt offering. Balak asked him, “What did the Lord say?”  
**23:18** Then Balaam gave this message: “Stand up, Balak, and listen. Hear me, son of Zippor.  
**23:19** God is not a man. He will not lie. God is not a human being. He does not change his mind. What he says he will do, he does. What he promises, he keeps.  
**23:20** He told me to bless them. So I cannot change the blessing.  
**23:21** He has found no wrong in the people of Jacob. He saw no fault in Israel. The Lord their God is with them. They praise their King.  
**23:22** God brought them out of Egypt. They are as strong as a wild ox.  
**23:23** No tricks will work on the people of Jacob. No magic works against Israel. People now say this about them, ‘Look what God has done!’  
**23:24** The people are strong like a lioness. They get up like a lion. Lions don’t rest until they have eaten. They drink their enemies’ blood.”  
**23:25** Then Balak said to Balaam, “You haven’t cursed these people. So at least don’t bless them!”  
**23:26** Balaam answered Balak, “I told you before. I can only do what the Lord tells me.”  
**23:27** Balaam’s Third Message Then Balak said to Balaam, “Come, I will take you to another place. Maybe God will be pleased. He may let you curse them from there.”  
**23:28** So Balak took Balaam to the top of Peor. This mountain looks over the desert.  
**23:29** Balaam told Balak, “Build me seven altars here. Then prepare for me seven bulls and seven male sheep.”  
**23:30** Balak did what Balaam asked. He offered a bull and a male sheep on each altar.

**24:3** And Balaam gave this message: “This is the message of Balaam son of Beor. This is the message of a man who sees clearly.

**24:25** Then Balaam got up and returned home. Balak also went on his way.  

## 25장

**25:2** The women invited them to their sacrifices to their false gods. The Israelites ate food there and worshiped these gods.  
**25:3** So the Israelites began to worship Baal of Peor. And the Lord was very angry with them.  
**25:4** The Lord said to Moses, “Get all the leaders of the people. Then kill them in open daylight in the presence of the Lord. Then the Lord will not be angry with the people of Israel.”  
**25:5** So Moses said to Israel’s judges, “Each of you must put to death your men who have become worshipers of Baal of Peor.”  
**25:6** Moses and the Israelites were gathered at the entrance to the Meeting Tent. They were crying there. Then an Israelite man brought a Midianite woman to his brothers. This was in plain sight of Moses and all the people.

**25:16** The Lord said to Moses,  
**25:17** “The Midianites are your enemies. You should kill them.  
**25:18** They have already made you their enemies. They tricked you at Peor. Then there was Cozbi, the daughter of a Midianite leader. She was the woman who was killed when the sickness came. It came because the people were worshiping the false god at Peor.”

## 35장

**35:1** The Levites’ Towns The Lord spoke to Moses on the plains of Moab. This was across from Jericho by the Jordan River. He said,  
**35:2** “Command the Israelites to give the Levites cities to live in. These will be cities in the land they receive. Also give the Levites the pastureland around these cities.  
**35:3** Then the Levites will have cities where they may live. And they will have pastureland for their cattle, sheep and other animals.  
**35:4** The pastureland you give the Levites will extend 1,500 feet from the city wall.  
**35:5** Also measure 3, feet in each direction from the city wall. Measure 3, feet east of the city. Measure 3, feet south of the city. Measure 3, feet west of the city. And measure 3, feet north of the city. The city will be in the center. This will be pastureland for the Levites’ cities.  
**35:6** Cities of Safety “Six of the cities you give the Levites will be cities of safety. A person might accidentally kill someone. If he does, he may run to one of those cities for safety. You must also give 42 other cities to the Levites.  
**35:7** Give the Levites a total of 48 cities and their pastures.  
**35:8** The larger tribes of Israel must give more cities. The smaller tribes must give fewer cities. Each tribe must give some of its cities to the Levites. The number of cities they give will depend on the size of their land.”  
**35:9** Then the Lord said to Moses,  
**35:10** “Tell the Israelites these things: ‘You will cross the Jordan River and go into Canaan.  
**35:11** You must choose cities to be cities of safety. If a person accidentally kills someone, he may run to them for safety.  
**35:12** There he will be safe from the dead person’s relative who has the duty of punishing the killer. He will not die before he is judged in court.  
**35:13** The six cities you give will be cities of safety.  
**35:14** Give three cities east of the Jordan River. And give three cities in Canaan as cities of safety.  
**35:15** These six cities will be places of safety for citizens of Israel. And they will be for foreigners and other people living with you. Any of these people may run to one of these cities if he accidentally kills someone.  
**35:16** “‘If a person uses an iron weapon to kill someone, he is a murderer. He must be put to death.  
**35:17** A person might take a rock that could kill someone. If he kills a person with it, he is a murderer. He must be put to death.  
**35:18** A person might pick up a piece of wood that could kill someone. If he kills someone with it, he is a murderer. He must be put to death.  
**35:19** A relative of the dead person must put the murderer to death. When he finds the murderer, he must kill him.  
**35:20** A person might shove someone and kill him. Or he might throw something at someone and kill him.  
**35:21** Or he might hit someone with his hand and kill him. If he did that from hate, he is a murderer. He must be put to death. A relative of the dead person must kill the murderer when he finds him.  
**35:22** “‘But a person might suddenly shove someone. He did not hate the person. Or he might accidentally throw something and hit someone.  
**35:23** Or he might drop a rock that could kill someone. It might fall on someone he did not see. And it might kill that person. He didn’t plan to hurt anyone. He didn’t hate the person he killed.  
**35:24** If that happens, the community must decide what to do. They must decide between the relative of the dead person and the killer. Here are the rules.  
**35:25** They must protect the killer from the dead person’s relative. They must send the killer back to the city of safety that he ran to. He must stay there until the high priest dies. The high priest had the holy oil poured on him.  
**35:26** “‘That man must never go outside the limits of his city of safety.  
**35:27** If a relative of the dead person finds him outside the city, the relative may kill him. He will not be guilty of murder.  
**35:28** The killer must stay in his city of safety until the high priest dies. After the high priest dies, he may go back to his own land.  
**35:29** “‘These laws are for you from now on, wherever you live.  
**35:30** “‘A killer may be put to death only if he is a murderer. And there must be witnesses. No one may be put to death with only one witness.  
**35:31** “‘A murderer should die. Don’t take money to spare his life. He must be put to death.  
**35:32** “‘A person might run to a city of safety. Don’t take money to let him go back home before the high priest dies.  
**35:33** “‘Don’t let murder spoil your land. There is only one way to remove the sin of killing an innocent person. The murderer must be put to death.  
**35:34** I am the Lord. I live among the Israelites. I live in that land with you. So do not spoil it with murder.’”  

## 36장

**36:1** Land for Zelophehad’s Daughters The leaders of Gilead’s family group went to talk to Moses and the leaders of the families of Israel. (Gilead was the son of Makir. Makir was Manasseh’s son, and Manasseh was Joseph’s son.)  
**36:2** They said, “The Lord commanded you, our master, to give the land to the Israelites by throwing lots. And the Lord commanded you to give the land of Zelophehad, our brother, to his daughters.  
**36:3** His daughters may marry men from other tribes of Israel. Then that land will leave our family. The people of the other tribes will get that land. So we will lose some of our land.  
**36:4** The time of Jubilee will come for the Israelites. Then their land will go to other tribes. It will go to the tribes of the people they marry. So their land will be taken away from us. And that was land we received from our fathers.”  
**36:5** Then Moses gave the Israelites this command from the Lord: “These men from the tribe of Joseph are right.  
**36:6** This is the Lord’s command to Zelophehad’s daughters: You may marry anyone you wish from your own tribe.  
**36:7** In this way the Israelites’ land will not pass from tribe to tribe. Each Israelite will keep the land in the tribe that belonged to his ancestors.  
**36:8** A woman who inherits her father’s land may marry. But she must marry someone from her own tribe. In this way every Israelite will keep the land that belonged to his ancestors.  
**36:9** The land must not pass from tribe to tribe. Each Israelite tribe will keep the land it received from its ancestors.”  
**36:10** Zelophehad’s daughters obeyed the Lord’s command to Moses.  
**36:11** So Zelophehad’s daughters—Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah and Noah—married their cousins. These were their father’s relatives.  
**36:12** Their husbands were from the tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph. So their land stayed in their father’s family group and tribe.  
**36:13** These were the laws and commands that the Lord gave to the Israelites. He gave them through Moses. The Israelites were on the plains of Moab by the Jordan River, across from Jericho.
