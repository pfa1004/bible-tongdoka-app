## 1장

**1:1** Book 1 Psalms 1—41 Two Ways to Live Happy is the person who doesn’t listen to the wicked. He doesn’t go where sinners go. He doesn’t do what bad people do.  
**1:2** He loves the Lord’s teachings. He thinks about those teachings day and night.  
**1:3** He is strong, like a tree planted by a river. It produces fruit in season. Its leaves don’t die. Everything he does will succeed.  
**1:4** But wicked people are not like that. They are like useless chaff that the wind blows away.  
**1:5** So the wicked will not escape God’s punishment. Sinners will not worship God with good people.  
**1:6** This is because the Lord protects good people. But the wicked will be destroyed.  

## 2장

**2:1** God’s Chosen King Why are the nations so angry? Why are the people making useless plans?  
**2:2** The kings of the earth prepare to fight. Their leaders make plans together against the Lord and his appointed king.  
**2:3** “Let’s break the chains that hold us prisoners. Let’s throw off the ropes that tie us,” the nations say.  
**2:4** But the Lord in heaven laughs. He makes fun of them.  
**2:5** Then the Lord warns them. He frightens them with his anger.  
**2:6** He says, “I have appointed my own king! He will rule in Jerusalem on my holy mountain.”  
**2:7** Now I will tell you what the Lord has declared: He said to me, “You are my son. Today I have become your father.  
**2:8** If you ask me, I will give you the nations. All the people on earth will be yours.  
**2:9** You will make them obey you by punishing them with an iron rod. You will break them into pieces like pottery.”  
**2:10** So, kings, be wise. Rulers, learn this lesson.  
**2:11** Obey the Lord with great fear. Be happy, but tremble.  
**2:12** Show that you are loyal to his son. Otherwise you will be destroyed. He can quickly become angry. But happy are those who trust him for protection.
