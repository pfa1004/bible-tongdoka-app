## 1장

**1:1** John Tells About This Book This is the revelation of Jesus Christ. God gave this revelation to Jesus, to show his servants what must soon happen. And Jesus sent his angel to show it to his servant John.  
**1:2** John has told everything that he has seen. It is the truth that Jesus Christ told him; it is the message from God.  
**1:3** The one who reads the words of God’s message is happy. And the people who hear this message and do what is written in it are happy. The time is near when all of this will happen.  
**1:4** Jesus’ Message to the Churches From John, To the seven churches in Asia: Grace and peace to you from the One who is and was and is coming, and from the seven spirits before his throne,  
**1:5** and from Jesus Christ. Jesus is the faithful witness. He is first among those raised from death. He is the ruler of the kings of the earth. He is the One who loves us. And he is the One who made us free from our sins with the blood of his death.  
**1:6** He made us to be a kingdom of priests who serve God his Father. To Jesus Christ be glory and power forever and ever! Amen.  
**1:7** Look, Jesus is coming with the clouds! Everyone will see him, even those who stabbed him. And all peoples of the earth will cry loudly because of him. Yes, this will happen! Amen.  
**1:8** The Lord God says, <J>“I am the Alpha and the Omega. I am the One who is and was and is coming. I am the All-Powerful.”</J>  
**1:9** I am John, and I am your brother in Christ. We are together in Jesus, and we share in these things: in suffering, in the kingdom, and in patience. I was on the island of Patmos because I had preached God’s message and the truth about Jesus.  
**1:10** On the Lord’s day the Spirit took control of me. I heard a loud voice behind me that sounded like a trumpet.  
**1:11** The voice said, <J>“Write what you see and send that book to the seven churches: to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea.”</J>  
**1:12** I turned to see who was talking to me. When I turned, I saw seven golden lampstands.  
**1:13** I saw someone among the lampstands who was “like a Son of Man.” He was dressed in a long robe. He had a gold band around his chest.  
**1:14** His head and hair were white like wool, as white as snow. His eyes were like flames of fire.  
**1:15** His feet were like bronze that glows hot in a furnace. His voice was like the noise of flooding water.  
**1:16** He held seven stars in his right hand. A sharp two-edged sword came out of his mouth. He looked like the sun shining at its brightest time.  
**1:17** When I saw him, I fell down at his feet like a dead man. He put his right hand on me and said, <J>“Do not be afraid! I am the First and the Last.</J>  
**1:18** <J>I am the One who lives. I was dead, but look: I am alive forever and ever! And I hold the keys of death and where the dead are.</J>  
**1:19** <J>So write the things you see, what is now and what will happen later.</J>  
**1:20** <J>Here is the hidden meaning of the seven stars that you saw in my right hand and the seven golden lampstands that you saw: The seven lampstands are the seven churches. The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches.</J>

## 4장

**4:1** John Sees Heaven After this I looked, and there before me was an open door in heaven. And I heard the same voice that spoke to me before. It was the voice that sounded like a trumpet. The voice said, <J>“Come up here, and I will show you what must happen after this.”</J>  
**4:2** Then the Spirit took control of me. There before me was a throne in heaven. Someone was sitting on the throne.  
**4:3** The One who sat on the throne looked like precious stones, like jasper and carnelian. All around the throne was a rainbow the color of an emerald.  
**4:4** Around the throne there were 24 other thrones. There were 24 elders sitting on the 24 thrones. The elders were dressed in white, and they had golden crowns on their heads.  
**4:5** Lightning flashes and noises of thunder came from the throne. Before the throne there were seven lamps burning. These lamps are the seven spirits of God.  
**4:6** Also before the throne there was something that looked like a sea of glass. It was clear like crystal. Around the throne, on each side, there were four living things. These living things had eyes all over them, in front and in back.  
**4:7** The first living thing was like a lion. The second was like a calf. The third had a face like a man. The fourth was like a flying eagle.  
**4:8** Each of these four living things had six wings. The living things were covered all over with eyes, inside and out. Day and night they never stop saying: “Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God All-Powerful. He was, he is, and he is coming.”  
**4:9** These living things give glory and honor and thanks to the One who sits on the throne. He is the One who lives forever and ever. And every time the living things do this,  
**4:10** the 24 elders bow down before the One who sits on the throne. The elders worship him who lives forever and ever. They put their crowns down before the throne and say:  
**4:11** “Our Lord and God! You are worthy to receive glory and honor and power. You made all things. Everything existed and was made because you wanted it.”  

## 5장

**5:1** Then I saw a scroll in the right hand of the One sitting on the throne. The scroll had writing on both sides. It was kept closed with seven seals.  
**5:2** And I saw a powerful angel. He called in a loud voice, “Who is worthy to break the seals and open the scroll?”  
**5:3** But there was no one in heaven or on earth or under the earth who could open the scroll or look inside it.  
**5:4** I cried and cried because there was no one who was worthy to open the scroll or look inside.  
**5:5** But one of the elders said to me, “Do not cry! The Lion from the tribe of Judah has won the victory. He is David’s descendant. He is able to open the scroll and its seven seals.”  
**5:6** Then I saw a Lamb standing in the center of the throne with the four living things around it. The elders were also around the Lamb. The Lamb looked as if he had been killed. He had seven horns and seven eyes. These are the seven spirits of God that were sent into all the world.  
**5:7** The Lamb came and took the scroll from the right hand of the One sitting on the throne.  
**5:8** After he took the scroll, the four living things and the 24 elders bowed down before the Lamb. Each one of them had a harp. Also, they were holding golden bowls full of incense. These bowls of incense are the prayers of God’s holy people.  
**5:9** And they all sang a new song to the Lamb: “You are worthy to take the scroll and to open its seals, because you were killed; and with the blood of your death you bought men for God from every tribe, language, people, and nation.  
**5:10** You made them to be a kingdom of priests for our God. And they will rule on the earth.”  
**5:11** Then I looked, and I heard the voices of many angels. The angels were around the throne, the four living things, and the elders. There were thousands and thousands of angels—there were 10, times 10,.  
**5:12** The angels said in a loud voice: “The Lamb who was killed is worthy to receive power, wealth, wisdom and strength, honor, glory, and praise!”  
**5:13** Then I heard every living thing in heaven and on earth and under the earth and in the sea. I heard every thing in all these places, saying: “All praise and honor and glory and power forever and ever to the One who sits on the throne and to the Lamb!”  
**5:14** The four living things said, “Amen!” And the elders bowed down and worshiped.  

## 6장

**6:1** Then I watched while the Lamb opened the first of the seven seals. I heard one of the four living things speak with a voice like thunder. It said, “Come!”  
**6:2** I looked and there before me was a white horse. The rider on the horse held a bow, and he was given a crown. And he rode out, defeating the enemy. He rode out to win the victory.  
**6:3** The Lamb opened the second seal. Then I heard the second living thing say, “Come!”  
**6:4** Then another horse came out. This was a red horse. Its rider was given power to take away peace from the earth. He was given power to make people kill each other. And he was given a big sword.  
**6:5** The Lamb opened the third seal. Then I heard the third living thing say, “Come!” I looked, and there before me was a black horse. Its rider held a pair of scales in his hand.  
**6:6** Then I heard something that sounded like a voice. It came from where the four living things were. The voice said, “A quart of wheat for a day’s pay. And three quarts of barley for a day’s pay. And do not damage the olive oil and wine!”  
**6:7** The Lamb opened the fourth seal. Then I heard the voice of the fourth living thing say, “Come!”  
**6:8** I looked, and there before me was a pale horse. Its rider was named death. Hades was following close behind him. They were given power over a fourth of the earth. They were given power to kill people by war, by starving them, by disease, and by the wild animals of the earth.  
**6:9** The Lamb opened the fifth seal. Then I saw some souls under the altar. They were the souls of those who had been killed because they were faithful to God’s message and to the truth they had received.  
**6:10** These souls shouted in a loud voice, “Holy and true Lord, how long until you judge the people of the earth and punish them for killing us?”  
**6:11** Then each one of these souls was given a white robe. They were told to wait a short time longer. There were still some of their brothers in the service of Christ who must be killed as they were. They were told to wait until all of this killing was finished.  
**6:12** Then I watched while the Lamb opened the sixth seal. There was a great earthquake. The sun became black like rough black cloth. The full moon became red like blood.  
**6:13** The stars in the sky fell to the earth like figs falling from a fig tree when the wind blows.  
**6:14** The sky disappeared. It was rolled up like a scroll. And every mountain and island was moved from its place.  
**6:15** Then all people hid in caves and behind the rocks on the mountains. There were the kings of the earth, the rulers, the generals, the rich people and the powerful people. Everyone, slave and free, hid himself.  
**6:16** They called to the mountains and the rocks, “Fall on us. Hide us from the face of the One who sits on the throne. Hide us from the anger of the Lamb!  
**6:17** The great day for their anger has come. Who can stand against it?”

**7:3** “Do not harm the land or the sea or the trees before we put the sign on the people who serve our God. We must put the sign on their foreheads.”

**7:7** from the tribe of Simeon 12,, from the tribe of Levi 12,, from the tribe of Issachar 12,,  
**7:8** from the tribe of Zebulun 12,, from the tribe of Joseph 12,, from the tribe of Benjamin 12,.

They were from every nation, tribe, people, and language of the earth. They were all standing before the throne and before the Lamb. They wore white robes and had palm branches in their hands.

## 8장

**8:1** The Seventh Seal The Lamb opened the seventh seal. Then there was silence in heaven for about half an hour.  
**8:2** And I saw the seven angels who stand before God. They were given seven trumpets.  
**8:3** Another angel came and stood at the altar. This angel had a golden pan for incense. The angel was given much incense to offer with the prayers of all God’s holy people. The angel put this offering on the golden altar before the throne.  
**8:4** The smoke from the incense went up from the angel’s hand to God. It went up with the prayers of God’s people.  
**8:5** Then the angel filled the incense pan with fire from the altar and threw it on the earth. There were flashes of lightning, thunder and loud noises, and an earthquake.  
**8:6** The Seven Angels and Trumpets Then the seven angels who had the seven trumpets prepared to blow them.  
**8:7** The first angel blew his trumpet. Then hail and fire mixed with blood was poured down on the earth. And a third of the earth and all the green grass and a third of the trees were burned up.  
**8:8** The second angel blew his trumpet. Then something that looked like a big mountain burning with fire was thrown into the sea. And a third of the sea became blood.  
**8:9** And a third of the living things in the sea died, and a third of the ships were destroyed.  
**8:10** The third angel blew his trumpet. Then a large star, burning like a torch, fell from the sky. It fell on a third of the rivers and on the springs of water.  
**8:11** The name of the star is Wormwood. And a third of all the water became bitter. Many people died from drinking the water that was bitter.  
**8:12** The fourth angel blew his trumpet. Then a third of the sun and a third of the moon and a third of the stars were hit. So a third of them became dark. A third of the day was without light.  
**8:13** While I watched, I heard an eagle that was flying high in the air. The eagle said with a loud voice, “Trouble! Trouble! Trouble for those who live on the earth! The trouble will begin with the sounds of the trumpets that the other three angels are about to blow.”  

## 9장

**9:1** Then the fifth angel blew his trumpet. And I saw a star fall from the sky to the earth. The star was given the key to the deep hole that leads down to the bottomless pit.  
**9:2** Then it opened the bottomless pit. Smoke came up from the hole like smoke from a big furnace. The sun and sky became dark because of the smoke from the hole.  
**9:3** Then locusts came down to the earth out of the smoke. They were given the power to sting like scorpions.  
**9:4** They were told not to harm the grass on the earth or any plant or tree. They could harm only the people who did not have the sign of God on their foreheads.  
**9:5** These locusts were given the power to cause pain to the people for five months. But they were not given the power to kill anyone. And the pain they felt was like the pain that a scorpion gives when it stings a person.  
**9:6** During those days people will look for a way to die, but they will not find it. They will want to die, but death will run away from them.  
**9:7** The locusts looked like horses prepared for battle. On their heads they wore things that looked like crowns of gold. Their faces looked like human faces.  
**9:8** Their hair was like women’s hair, and their teeth were like lions’ teeth.  
**9:9** Their chests looked like iron breastplates. The sound their wings made was like the noise of many horses and chariots hurrying into battle.  
**9:10** The locusts had tails with stingers like scorpions. The power they had to hurt people for five months was in their tails.  
**9:11** The locusts had a king who was the angel of the bottomless pit. His name in the Hebrew language is Abaddon. In the Greek language his name is Apollyon.  
**9:12** The first great trouble is past. There are still two other great troubles that will come.  
**9:13** The sixth angel blew his trumpet. Then I heard a voice coming from the horns on the golden altar that is before God.  
**9:14** The voice said to the sixth angel who had the trumpet, “Free the four angels who are tied at the great river Euphrates.”  
**9:15** These four angels had been kept ready for this hour and day and month and year. They were freed to kill a third of all people on the earth.  
**9:16** I heard how many troops on horses were in their army. There were 200,,.  
**9:17** In my vision I saw the horses and their riders. They looked like this: They had breastplates that were fiery red, dark blue, and yellow like sulfur. The heads of the horses looked like heads of lions. The horses had fire, smoke, and sulfur coming out of their mouths.  
**9:18** A third of all the people on earth were killed by these three terrible things coming out of the horses’ mouths: the fire, the smoke, and the sulfur.  
**9:19** The horses’ power was in their mouths and also in their tails. Their tails were like snakes that have heads to bite and hurt people.  
**9:20** The other people on the earth were not killed by these terrible things. But they still did not change their hearts and turn away from what they had made with their own hands. They did not stop worshiping demons and idols made of gold, silver, bronze, stone, and wood—things that cannot see or hear or walk.  
**9:21** These people did not change their hearts and turn away from murder or evil magic, from their sexual immorality or stealing.  

## 10장

**10:1** The Angel and the Little Scroll Then I saw another powerful angel coming down from heaven. He was dressed in a cloud and had a rainbow around his head. His face was like the sun, and his legs were like poles of fire.  
**10:2** The angel was holding a small scroll open in his hand. He put his right foot on the sea and his left foot on the land.  
**10:3** He shouted loudly like the roaring of a lion. When he shouted, the voices of seven thunders spoke.  
**10:4** The seven thunders spoke, and I started to write. But then I heard a voice from heaven. The voice said, “Do not write what the seven thunders said. Keep these things secret.”  
**10:5** Then the angel I saw standing on the sea and on the land raised his right hand to heaven.  
**10:6** He made a promise by the power of the One who lives forever and ever. He is the One who made the skies and all that is in them. He made the earth and all that is in it, and he made the sea and all that is in it. The angel said, “There will be no more waiting!  
**10:7** In the days when the seventh angel is ready to blow his trumpet, God’s secret plan will be finished. This plan is the Good News God told to his servants, the prophets.”

**20:5** (The others that were dead did not live again until the 1, years were ended.) This is the first raising of the dead.  
**20:6** Blessed and holy are those who share in this first raising of the dead. The second death has no power over them. They will be priests for God and for Christ. They will rule with him for 1, years.  
**20:7** When the 1, years are over, Satan will be set free from his prison.  
**20:8** He will go out to trick the nations in all the earth—Gog and Magog. Satan will gather them for battle. There will be so many people that they will be like sand on the seashore.  
**20:9** And Satan’s army marched across the earth and gathered around the camp of God’s people and the city that God loves. But fire came down from heaven and burned them up.  
**20:10** And Satan, who tricked them, was thrown into the lake of burning sulfur with the beast and the false prophet. There they will be punished day and night forever and ever.  
**20:11** People of the World Are Judged Then I saw a great white throne and the One who was sitting on it. Earth and sky ran away from him and disappeared.  
**20:12** And I saw the dead, great and small, standing before the throne. And the book of life was opened. There were also other books opened. The dead were judged by what they had done, which was written in the books.  
**20:13** The sea gave up the dead who were in it. Death and Hades gave up the dead who were in them. Each person was judged by what he had done.  
**20:14** And Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire. This lake of fire is the second death.  
**20:15** And if anyone’s name was not found written in the book of life, he was thrown into the lake of fire.  

## 21장

**21:1** The New Jerusalem Then I saw a new heaven and a new earth. The first heaven and the first earth had disappeared. Now there was no sea.  
**21:2** And I saw the holy city coming down out of heaven from God. This holy city is the new Jerusalem. It was prepared like a bride dressed for her husband.  
**21:3** I heard a loud voice from the throne. The voice said, “Now God’s home is with men. He will live with them, and they will be his people. God himself will be with them and will be their God.  
**21:4** He will wipe away every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death, sadness, crying, or pain. All the old ways are gone.”  
**21:5** The One who was sitting on the throne said, “Look! I am making everything new!” Then he said, “Write this, because these words are true and can be trusted.”  
**21:6** The One on the throne said to me: “It is finished! I am the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End. I will give free water from the spring of the water of life to anyone who is thirsty.  
**21:7** Anyone who wins the victory will receive this. And I will be his God, and he will be my son.  
**21:8** But those who are cowards, who refuse to believe, who do evil things, who kill, who are sexually immoral, who do evil magic, who worship idols, and who tell lies—all these will have a place in the lake of burning sulfur. This is the second death.”  
**21:9** One of the seven angels came to me. This was one of the angels who had the seven bowls full of the seven last troubles. He said, “Come with me. I will show you the bride, the wife of the Lamb.”  
**21:10** The angel carried me away by the Spirit to a very large and high mountain. He showed me the holy city, Jerusalem. It was coming down out of heaven from God.  
**21:11** It was shining with the glory of God. It was shining bright like a very expensive jewel, like a jasper. It was clear as crystal.  
**21:12** The city had a great high wall with 12 gates. There were 12 angels at the gates. On each gate was written the name of 1 of the 12 tribes of Israel.  
**21:13** There were three gates on the east, three on the north, three on the south, and three on the west.  
**21:14** The walls of the city were built on 12 foundation stones. On the stones were written the names of the 12 apostles of the Lamb.  
**21:15** The angel who talked with me had a measuring rod made of gold. He had this rod to measure the city, its gates, and its wall.  
**21:16** The city was built in a square. Its length was equal to its width. The angel measured the city with the rod. The city was 1,500 miles long, 1,500 miles wide, and 1,500 miles high.  
**21:17** The angel also measured the wall. It was 216 feet high, by man’s measurement. That was the measurement the angel was using.  
**21:18** The wall was made of jasper. The city was made of pure gold, as pure as glass.  
**21:19** The foundation stones of the city walls had every kind of jewel in them. The first cornerstone was jasper, the second was sapphire, the third was chalcedony, the fourth was emerald,  
**21:20** the fifth was onyx, the sixth was carnelian, the seventh was chrysolite, the eighth was beryl, the ninth was topaz, the tenth was chrysoprase, the eleventh was jacinth, and the twelfth was amethyst.  
**21:21** The 12 gates were 12 pearls. Each gate was made from a single pearl. The street of the city was made of pure gold. The gold was clear as glass.  
**21:22** I did not see a temple in the city. The Lord God All-Powerful and the Lamb are the city’s temple.  
**21:23** The city does not need the sun or the moon to shine on it. The glory of God is its light, and the Lamb is the city’s lamp.  
**21:24** By its light the people of the world will walk. The kings of the earth will bring their glory into it.  
**21:25** The city’s gates will never be shut on any day, because there is no night there.  
**21:26** The greatness and the honor of the nations will be brought into it.  
**21:27** Nothing unclean will ever enter the city. No one who does shameful things or tells lies will ever go into it. Only those whose names are written in the Lamb’s book of life will enter the city.
