# 54.디모데전서 (MLV)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the commandment of God our Savior and Lord Jesus Christ, our hope;  
**1:2** to Timothy, <i>my</i> genuine child in <i>the</i> faith: grace, mercy, peace, from God our Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.  
**1:3** As I encouraged you to remain in Ephesus, <i>while I was</i> traveling into Macedonia, in order that you might command to some, <i>they are</i> not to teach a different teaching,  
**1:4** nor to take-heed to fables and unending genealogies, which provide <i>more</i> debates, rather than a stewardship of God which <i>is</i> in faith.  
**1:5** But the end of the command is love out of a clean heart and a good conscience and a non-pretended faith;  
**1:6** from which some having deviated, turned aside to empty-talking;  
**1:7** wishing to be teachers of the law, not perceiving neither what they are saying nor <i>the things</i> concerning what they are confidently confirming.  
**1:8** But we know that the law is good, if anyone uses it lawfully,  
**1:9** knowing this: that law is not laid <i>down</i> {Or: destined} for <i>the</i> righteous, but for the lawless and rebellious, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and profane, for killers of their fathers and killers of their mothers, for murderers,  
**1:10** for fornicators, for homosexuals, for kidnappers, for liars, for perjurers and if any other thing which lays in opposition to sound teaching;  
**1:11** according to the good-news of the glory of the blessed God, <i>to</i> which I was entrusted.  
**1:12** And I have gratitude <i>to</i> the one who empowered me, <i>namely,</i> the Christ, Jesus our Lord, because he deemed me faithful, placing me into <i>his</i> service;  
**1:13** <i>even though</i> I was a former blasphemer and a persecutor and an insolent <i>man</i>, but I was shown-mercy, because being ignorant, I did <i>it</i> in unbelief;  
**1:14** and the grace of our Lord overwhelmed <i>me</i> with <i>the</i> faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.  
**1:15** Faithful <i>is</i> the word and worthy of all acceptance, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am foremost.  
**1:16** But I was shown-mercy because of this, in order that Jesus Christ might show all his patience in me <i>as</i> foremost, for a pattern of those who are <i>afterward</i> about to believe on him for everlasting life.  
**1:17** Now to the King of the ages, incorruptible, invisible, <i>the</i> only wise God, <i>is</i> honor and glory forevermore. Amen.  
**1:18** I consign this command to you, <i>my</i> child Timothy, according to the prophecies preceding upon you, in order that you may be warring the good warfare by them;  
**1:19** holding faith and a good conscience, which some having shoved away, are shipwrecked concerning the faith;  
**1:20** of whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander; whom I gave to the Adversary, in order that they might be disciplined not to blaspheme.  

## 2장

**2:1** Therefore I am encouraging first of all that supplications, prayers, petitions, thanksgivings, be made on behalf of all men,  
**2:2** on behalf of kings and all who are in superior-stations; in order that we may lead a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness and honesty.  
**2:3** For this is good and acceptable in God our Savior’s sight;  
**2:4** who wishes all men to be saved and to come to the full knowledge of <i>the</i> truth.  
**2:5** For <i>there is</i> one God, and one intermediary of God and of men, <i>the</i> man, Christ Jesus,  
**2:6** who gave himself <i>as</i> a ransom on behalf of all; the testimony <i>given</i> in its own times;  
**2:7** to which I was appointed a preacher and an apostle, (I am speaking <i>the</i> truth in Christ, I am not lying), <i>and</i> a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth.  
**2:8** Therefore I will that the men pray in every place, lifting up holy hands, without wrath and <i>evil</i> reasoning.  
**2:9** Likewise also, the women to adorn themselves in a respectable array, with modesty and self-discipline; not in <i>hair</i> braids or gold or pearls or costly garments;  
**2:10** but <i>with</i> what is suitable to women professing godliness through good works.  
**2:11** Let a woman learn in quietness in all subjection.  
**2:12** Now I do not permit a woman to teach, nor to dominate a man, but to be in quietness.  
**2:13** For Adam was first molded, thereafter Eve;  
**2:14** and Adam was not deceived, but the woman was deceived; she has become <i>the one</i> in transgression. {Gen 1:17, 2:7-22, 3:6,12}  
**2:15** But she will be saved through <i>her</i> childbearing, if they remain in faith and love and holiness with self-discipline.  

## 3장

**3:1** {1Ti 3:1-7 & Tit 1:5-9 Congregational leaders.}<br>{‘Unimpeachable,’ (below, G423), ‘irreproachable,’ (Tit 1, G410), are both in the qualifications of the Lord’s earthly congregation’s leadership, described in various Bible versions and locations within as: elders, bishops, overseers, pastors, shepherds, presbyters; (all terms are always plural in the Bible). These words do not translate well into English. They are simply a description of a person not under an accusation by an enemy or legal system or better, as under any kind of accusation. This person is blameless, guiltless and has an impeccable reputation. Another note: ‘children’ is plural in Greek.}<br>Faithful is the word: if any <i>man</i> is aspiring to the overseership, he is desiring a good work.  
**3:2** Therefore, it is essential <i>for</i> the overseer to be: unimpeachable, <i>the</i> husband of one wife, temperate, sensible, orderly, hospitable, skilled in teaching;  
**3:3** not a drunkard, not a bully, not covetous, but gentle, not quarrelsome, not a money-lover;  
**3:4** the one who is governing his own house well, having <i>his</i> children in subjection with all honesty;  
**3:5** (but if anyone does not know how to govern his own house, how will he be caring for the congregation of God?;)  
**3:6** not a new-convert, (in order that <i>when</i> he is puffed up, he might not fall into the condemnation of the devil).  
**3:7** But it is also essential for him to have a good testimony from those who are outside <i>the congregation</i>; in order that he may not fall into reproach and <i>the</i> snare of the devil.<br>{Deacons, is a transliteration of the Greek word diakonos G1249. This word and its compounds in all other contexts are translated ‘serve,' ‘servant,' or ‘service’ (see 3:10 & 3:13). Because ‘elder’ (‘overseer’ in Php 1:1) a known office of the congregation is used, this context calls for an office or serving position in the congregation. The actual word in Greek means ‘servant’ who serves in reference to his job. The same word is used by Paul to describe a ‘policeman’ (in modern terms) in Rm. 13:4. Context could also mean ‘those who minister,’ in general. Other Greek synonyms in the MLV are translated as: ‘domestic servant,’ ‘attendant,’ ‘minister’ and ‘bondservant.’}  
**3:8** Deacons likewise <i>must be</i> honorable, not double-tongued, not taking-heed to much wine, not covetous;  
**3:9** holding the mystery of the faith in a clean conscience.  
**3:10** But let these also first be tested; thereafter let them serve, being irreproachable.  
**3:11** Women likewise <i>must be</i> honorable, not slanderers, <i>but</i> temperate, faithful in all things.  
**3:12** Let deacons be husbands of one wife, governing <i>their</i> children and their own houses well.  
**3:13** For those who served well are acquiring for themselves a good rank and much boldness in <i>the</i> faith which is in Christ Jesus.  
**3:14** I am writing these things to you, hoping to come to you shortly;  
**3:15** but if I am slow, <i>these things are</i> in order that you may know how it is essential <i>for men</i> to conduct themselves in the house of God, which is the congregation of the living God, <i>the</i> pillar and base of the truth.  
**3:16** And confessedly great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifested in <i>the</i> flesh, was made righteous in <i>the</i> Spirit, was seen by messengers, was preached among the nations, was believed <i>on</i> in the world, was received up in glory.  

## 4장

**4:1** But the Spirit expressly says, Some will be withdrawing from the faith in later times, taking-heed to misleading spirits and teachings of demons,  
**4:2** through the hypocrisy from <i>the</i> speakers of lies, having been branded in their own conscience;  
**4:3** forbidding to marry <i>and commanding</i> to abstain from foods, which God created for reception with thanksgiving to the believing and those who have fully known the truth.  
**4:4** Because every created thing of God <i>is</i> good, and casting off nothing if it is received with thanksgiving,  
**4:5** for it is made holy through the word of God and <i>through</i> petition.  
**4:6** If you suggest these things to the brethren, you will be a good servant of Jesus Christ, being nourished in the words of the faith and of the good teaching in which you have followed closely.  
**4:7** But renounce the profane and old-wives’ fables and exercise yourself to godliness;  
**4:8** for bodily exercise is profitable for few <i>things</i>, but godliness is profitable for all things, having <i>the</i> promise of life the current <i>one</i> and the future <i>one</i>.  
**4:9** Faithful is the word and worthy of all acceptance.  
**4:10** For we are also laboring and are reproached toward this <i>end</i>, because we have hope upon the living God, who is the Savior of all men, especially of the believing <i>ones</i>.  
**4:11** Command and teach these things.  
**4:12** Let no one despise your youth, but become an example of the believing <i>ones</i>: in word, in conduct, in love, in spirit, in faith, in purity.  
**4:13** Until I come, take-heed to the public-reading, to the encouragement, to the teaching.  
**4:14** Do not neglect the gift <i>which is</i> in you, which was given to you through prophecy, with <i>the</i> laying on of the hands of the eldership.  
**4:15** Meditate on these things; be <i>diligent</i> in them; in order that your progress may be apparent in all things.  
**4:16** Take-heed to yourself and to the teaching. Abide in these things; for <i>while</i> practicing this, you will be saving both yourself and those who hear you.  

## 5장

**5:1** Do not berate an elder <i>man</i>, but encourage him like a father; younger-men like brethren;  
**5:2** elder <i>women</i> like mothers; younger <i>women</i> like sisters, in all purity.  
**5:3** Honor widows <i>who are</i> really widows.  
**5:4** But if any widow has children or grandchildren, let them learn first to be devout toward their own household and to give back reimbursement to their parents; for this is acceptable in God’s sight.  
**5:5** Now she who really is a widow and has been <i>left</i> alone <i>and</i> has hoped upon God and remains in supplications and prayers night and day.  
**5:6** But she who is self-indulgent has died, <i>while still</i> living.  
**5:7** Also command these things, in order that they may be unimpeachable.  
**5:8** But if anyone is not  planning-for his own and especially his own household, he has denied the faith and is worse <i>than</i> an unbeliever.  
**5:9** Do not let a widow be enrolled <i>who is</i> less-than sixty years <i>old</i>, who had become the wife of one man,  
**5:10** being testified of in good works; if she reared children, if she was hospitable to strangers, if she washed the holy-ones’ feet, if she relieved <i>those</i> being afflicted, if she followed after every good work.  
**5:11** But renounce younger widows; for whenever they are promiscuous against the Christ, they wish to marry;  
**5:12** having condemnation, because they rejected <i>their</i> first faith.  
**5:13** Now at the same time, they also are learning <i>to be</i> idle, strolling the houses, and not only idle, but also gossips and busybodies, speaking things which it is not essential <i>to be speaking</i>.  
**5:14** Therefore I will the younger <i>widows</i> to marry, to be childbearing, to manage the house, to not give a starting-point to those who lay in opposition to <i>them</i> because of reviling:  
**5:15** for some <i>have</i> already turned aside after the Adversary.  
**5:16** If any believing <i>man</i> or believing <i>woman</i> has <i>relatives who are</i> widows, they should relieve them and do not let the congregation be burdened; in order that it may relieve those who really are widows.  
**5:17** The elders <i>who are</i> governing well, let them be deemed worthy of double honor, especially those who are laboring in the word and in teaching.  
**5:18** For the Scripture says, ‘You will not muzzle a threshing bull.’ {Deu 25:4} And, ‘The worker is worthy of his wages.’ {Lev 19:13}  
**5:19** Do not accept an accusation against an elder, unless upon <i>the testimony of</i> two or three witnesses.  
**5:20** Reprove those who are sinning in the sight of all, in order that the rest may also have fear.  
**5:21** I am thoroughly testifying in sight of God and the Lord Jesus Christ and the chosen messengers, in order that you should observe these things without prejudice, doing nothing according to bias.  
**5:22** Lay hands on no one quickly, nor share in another’s sins. Keep yourself pure!  
**5:23** No longer be a drinker of water <i>only</i>, but use a small <i>amount of</i> wine because of your stomach and your frequent sicknesses.  
**5:24** Some men’s sins are evident, leading <i>them</i> into judgment, but even for some, they are also following after <i>them later</i>.  
**5:25** Likewise also, the good works are evident, and those having <i>works</i> contrariwise are not able to be hidden.<br>{Footnotes: 1Ti 5:9, or: provide; or: planning in the short term.}  

## 6장

**6:1** As many as are bondservants under the yoke, let them deem their own masters worthy of all honor, in order that the name of God and the teaching may not be blasphemed.  
**6:2** But those who have believing masters, let them not despise them, because they are brethren, but rather let them serve them, because they are believing and beloved who are being helped <i>by your</i> good work. Teach and encourage these things.  
**6:3** If anyone is teaching a different teaching and is not coming near to sound words, <i>even</i> the words of our Lord Jesus Christ and to <i>the</i> teaching which is according to godliness;  
**6:4** he has been puffed up, knowing nothing, but being mentally diseased concerning debates and quarrels over words, out of which become: envy, strife, blasphemies, evil suspicions,  
**6:5** incessant wranglings of men who have been corrupted in the mind and who have been deprived of the truth, supposing that godliness <i>is</i> a method to earnings. Withdraw yourself from such.  
**6:6** But godliness with contentment is a great method to earnings;  
**6:7** for we brought nothing into the world, nor <i>is it</i> evident that we are able to carry anything out; {Job 1:21}  
**6:8** but we will be content with these things, having nourishment and clothes.  
**6:9** But those who are planning to be rich are falling into a temptation and a snare and many foolish and harmful lusts, which are submerging men into utter-destruction and <i>everlasting</i> destruction.  
**6:10** For the love of money is a root of all <i>kinds</i> of evils; of which some aspiring to, were misled from the faith and they themselves are staked-through by many sorrows.  
**6:11** But you, O man of God, flee these things and pursue: righteousness, godliness, faith, love, endurance, meekness.  
**6:12** Fight the good fight of the faith, grab the everlasting life, to which you were called and professed the good confession in the sight of many witnesses.  
**6:13** I am commanding you in God’s sight, who gives-life to all things and of Christ Jesus, who in front of Pontius Pilate testified the good confession;  
**6:14** that you keep the commandment, unstained, unimpeachable, till the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**6:15** Which in its own times, he will show, the Blessed and only Sovereign, the King of those who are reigning, and a Lord of those who have lordship over <i>others</i>;  
**6:16** he who alone has immortality, dwelling in unapproachable light; whom no one from mankind has seen, nor is able to see: to whom <i>is</i> honor and everlasting dominion. Amen.  
**6:17** Command the rich in this current age, not to be cavalier, nor to have hope on <i>the</i> uncertainty of riches, but <i>have hope</i> in the living God, who is richly providing to us all things toward enjoyment;  
**6:18** <i>also command them</i> to work <i>what is</i> good, to be rich in good works, to be liberal in giving, generous,  
**6:19** storing up for themselves a good foundation toward the future, in order that they may grab the everlasting life.  
**6:20** O Timothy, guard the deposit consigned <i>to you</i>, turning away from the profane empty-talks and oppositions of the falsely named knowledge;  
**6:21** which some professing <i>have</i> deviated concerning the faith. Grace <i>is</i> with you. Amen.<br>{NOTES: You should read the 'Definitions' Section and other non-bible sections.}
