# 52.데살로니가전서 (MLV)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul and Silvanus and Timothy to the congregation of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ: grace to you° and peace from God our Father and <i>the</i> Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:2** We are always giving-thanks to God concerning all of you°, making a remembrance of you° in our prayers;  
**1:3** constantly remembering your° work of the faith, and labor of love, and of the endurance of the hope in our Lord Jesus Christ, before our God and Father,  
**1:4** knowing beloved brethren your° choice by God.  
**1:5** You° <i>know</i> because our good-news did not happen to you° in word only, but also in power, and in <i>the</i> Holy Spirit, and in much full assurance; just-as you° know what <i>manner of men</i> we became among you°, because of you°.  
**1:6** And you° became imitators of us and of the Lord, having accepted the word in much affliction with joy of <i>the</i> Holy Spirit;  
**1:7** so-that you° became examples to all who believe in Macedonia and in Achaia.  
**1:8** For the word of the Lord has echoed forth from you°, not only in Macedonia and in Achaia, but also in every place your° faith toward God has gone forth; so-that we do not need to say anything.  
**1:9** For they themselves are reporting concerning us, what sort of entrance we had toward you°, and how you° turned from idols to God, to serve a living and true God,  
**1:10** and to await his Son Jesus from the heavens, whom he raised <i>up</i> from the dead; who rescues us from the coming wrath.  

## 2장

**2:1** For brethren, you° yourselves know our entrance to you°, that it has not become empty.  
**2:2** But having suffered before, and having been abused in Philippi {Acts 16:12-40} , just-as you° know, we were bold in our God to speak to you° the good-news of God, <i>even</i> in <i>our</i> large struggle.  
**2:3** For our encouragement <i>is</i> not from deceit, nor from uncleanness, nor in treachery.  
**2:4** But just-as we have been approved by God to be entrusted <i>with</i> the good-news, so we are speaking; not as pleasing men, but <i>it is</i> God who tests our hearts.  
**2:5** For just-as you° know, (God <i>being</i> witness), we neither previously came in words of flattery, nor in a pretext of greed,  
**2:6** nor seeking glory from men, neither from you° nor from others, <i>while</i> being able to be a burden <i>to</i> you° as apostles of Christ.  
**2:7** But we became mild in your° midst, as a nurse may cherish her own children.  
**2:8** So having kindly-feelings of you°, we were delighted to give to you° not only the good-news of God, but also our own souls, because you° have become beloved to us.  
**2:9** For brethren, you° remember our labor and hardship. For <i>while</i> working night and day, in order to not burden any of you°, we preached to you° the good-news of God.  
**2:10** You° and God <i>are</i> witnesses, as we became holy and righteous and blameless toward you° who believe.  
**2:11** Just-as you° know, as we <i>became with</i> each one of you°, like a father <i>with</i> his own children, encouraging you° and consoling you°  
**2:12** and testifying, that you° should walk worthy of God, who is calling you° into his own kingdom and glory.  
**2:13** We also are constantly giving-thanks to God because of this, that <i>after</i> you° received <i>the</i> word of God from our report, you° accepted <i>it</i> not <i>as</i> the word of men, but just-as it truly is, the word of God, which is also working in you° who believe.  
**2:14** For brethren, you° became imitators of the congregations of God, which are in Judea in Christ Jesus, because you° also suffered the same things by your° own countrymen, just-as they did also by the Jews.  
**2:15** <i>The Jews</i>, who both killed the Lord Jesus and their own prophets, and banished us, and <i>were</i> not pleasing to God, and <i>are</i> adverse to all men;  
**2:16** forbidding us to speak to the Gentiles in order that they might be saved; that they should always fill up their sins, but the wrath <i>has</i> arrived-unexpectedly upon them– to the end.  
**2:17** But brethren, having been orphaned away from you° for <i>the</i> time of an hour, in face {i.e. person} <i>but</i> not in heart, we were even-more diligent to see your° face in much desire.  
**2:18** Hence, we wished to come to you°, indeed, I, Paul, even once and <i>then</i> twice, and <i>yet</i> the Adversary hindered us.  
**2:19** For what <i>is</i> our hope or joy or crown of boasting, or <i>is it</i> not also you°, <i>which is</i> before our Lord Jesus Christ at his presence?  
**2:20** For you° are our glory and <i>our</i> joy.  

## 3장

**3:1** Hence, no longer forbearing <i>it</i>, we were delighted to be left behind in Athens alone.  
**3:2** And we sent Timothy, our brother and God’s servant and our fellow worker in the good-news of the Christ, that he might establish you° and to encourage you° concerning your° faith;  
**3:3** <i>that</i> no one be shook up in these afflictions; for you° yourselves know that we are destined for this thing.  
**3:4** For even when we were with you°, we were telling you° beforehand that we are about to be afflicted; just-as it also happened and you° know.  
**3:5** Because of this, no longer forbearing <i>it</i>, I also sent <i>Timothy</i>, that I might know your° faith, lest the tempter <i>had</i> tempted you°, and our labor might become vain.  
**3:6** But now, <i>after</i> Timothy came to us from you°, and proclaimed to us good-news <i>about</i> your° faith and love, and that you° have a good remembrance of us always, longing to see us, just-as we also <i>long to see</i> you°.  
**3:7** Brethren, because of this, we were comforted on <i>the basis of</i> you° in all our affliction and calamity through your° faith,  
**3:8** because now we live, if you° stand-firm in the Lord.  
**3:9** For what thanksgiving are we able to repay to God concerning you°? Over all the joy in which we are rejoicing before our God <i>is</i> because of you°,  
**3:10** exceptionally beseeching <i>God</i> night and day, that we may see your° face and may mend the things lacking of your° faith.  
**3:11** Now may our God and Father himself and our Lord Jesus Christ, steer our way toward you°.  
**3:12** And may the Lord increase <i>in</i> you° and <i>this increase</i> to abound in love toward one another and toward all men, just-as we also <i>do</i> toward you°;  
**3:13** that he might establish your° hearts, blameless in holiness before our God and Father, in the presence of our Lord Jesus Christ, with all his holy-ones.  

## 4장

**4:1** Therefore furthermore brethren, we request and are encouraging you° in the Lord Jesus, just-as you° received from us how it is essential <i>for you°</i> to walk and to please God, in order that you° might abound <i>even</i> more.  
**4:2** For you° know what commands we gave you° through the Lord Jesus.  
**4:3** For this is <i>the</i> will of God, your° holiness, <i>for</i> you° to abstain from fornication;  
**4:4** <i>for</i> each of you° to know <i>how</i> to procure his own vessel in holiness and honor,  
**4:5** not in the passion of lust, just-as also the Gentiles who do not know God,  
**4:6** <i>and</i> not to go-beyond limits and take-advantage of his brother in the matter, because the Lord <i>is</i> an avenger concerning all these things, just-as we also foretold to you° and thoroughly testified.  
**4:7** For God did not call us in uncleanness, but in holiness.  
**4:8** Therefore, the one who is rejecting <i>this</i> is not rejecting man, but God, who also gave his Holy Spirit to you°.  
**4:9** Now concerning brotherly-love, you° have no need <i>for someone</i> to write to you°; for you° yourselves are taught by God, that you° should love one another;  
**4:10** for you° are also practicing it toward all the brethren who <i>are</i> in the whole <i>of</i> Macedonia. But brethren, we encourage you°, that you° abound <i>even</i> more,  
**4:11** and that you° make it your aim to be <i>living</i> quiet <i>lives</i> and to do your° own things, and to work with your° own hands (just-as we commanded you°),  
**4:12** in order that you° may walk decently toward those <i>who are</i> outside, and may have need of nothing. {1Th 4:13-17 & Mat 24:27, 24:29-31 & Mar 13:24-37 & Luk 21:25-28 & Acts 1:11 & 2Th 1:7-9 & 1Co 15:51-15:58 & Rev 1:7 Second Coming.}  
**4:13** But brethren, we do not wish you° to be ignorant concerning those who have fallen-asleep; in order that you° may not be sorrowful like the rest who have no hope.  
**4:14** For if we believe that Jesus died and rose up again, so those who fell-asleep through Jesus, God will also be bringing together with him.  
**4:15** For this we are saying to you° in <i>the</i> word of <i>the</i> Lord, that we, the living, who are left at the presence of the Lord, should never attain <i>heaven before</i> those who fell-asleep.  
**4:16** Because the Lord himself will be descending from heaven, in a commanding yell, in the voice of the archangel and at the trumpet of God, and the dead in Christ will be rising up first;  
**4:17** thereafter we, the living, who are left, will be seized at the same time together with them in <i>the</i> clouds, to <i>the</i> encounter of the Lord in the air, and so we will always be together with <i>the</i> Lord.  
**4:18** So-then, encourage° one another in these words.  

## 5장

**5:1** Now brethren, you° have no need <i>for anything</i> to be written to you° concerning the times and the seasons.  
**5:2** For you yourselves know accurately that the day of the Lord so comes like a thief in the night.  
**5:3** For whenever they say, Peace and security; then sudden utter-destruction stands by them, just-like the travail <i>a woman</i> has in <i>her</i> womb, and they should never flee away from <i>it</i>.  
**5:4** But brethren, you° are not in darkness, in order that the day might overtake you° like a thief.  
**5:5** You° are all sons of light and sons of <i>the</i> day. We are not of the night, nor of darkness;  
**5:6** Therefore consequently, we should not sleep, like the rest also <i>do</i>, but let us watch and be sober.  
**5:7** For those who are sleeping, sleep in the night, and those who are drunken, are drunken in the night.  
**5:8** But we, being of <i>the</i> day, should be sober; having clothed yourselves with the breastplate of faith and love, and a helmet– <i>the</i> hope of salvation.  
**5:9** Because God appointed us not for wrath, but for the acquisition of salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,  
**5:10** who died on our behalf, in order that, whether we should watch or sleep, we should live together with him at the same time.  
**5:11** Hence, encourage° one another and build° up one <i>by</i> one, just-as you° are also practicing.  
**5:12** But brethren, we request of you° to know those who are laboring among you°, and are governing over you°, and admonishing you° in the Lord;  
**5:13** and to deem them exceptionally in love because of their work. Be° at peace among yourselves.  
**5:14** But brethren, we encourage you°: admonish° the disorderly, console° the fainthearted, hold up the weak, have° patience toward all.  
**5:15** See° that not anyone repays evil in exchange-for evil to anyone, but always pursue° the good, both toward one another and toward all.  
**5:16** Rejoice° always.  
**5:17** Pray° constantly.  
**5:18** Give-thanks° in everything; for this <i>is</i> the will of God in Christ Jesus toward you°.  
**5:19** Do° not quench the Spirit.  
**5:20** Do° not scorn prophecy.  
**5:21** Now test° all things! Hold-onto° the good <i>things</i>!  
**5:22** Abstain° from every form of evil!  
**5:23** Now may the God of peace himself make you° entirely holy, and may your° whole spirit and soul and body be kept blameless in the presence of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**5:24** <i>He is</i> faithful who calls you°, who will also do <i>it</i>.  
**5:25** Brethren, pray° concerning us.  
**5:26** Greet° all the brethren by a holy kiss.  
**5:27** I am imploring you° <i>by</i> the Lord, <i>that this</i> letter be read to all the holy brethren.  
**5:28** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <i>is</i> with you°. Amen.<br>{NOTES: You should read the 'Definitions' Section and other non-bible sections.}
