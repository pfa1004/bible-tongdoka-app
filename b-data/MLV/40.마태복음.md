# 40.마태복음 (MLV)



## 1장

**1:1** <i>The</i> book of lineage of Jesus Christ, <i>the</i> son of David, <i>the</i> son of Abraham:  
**1:2** Abraham fathered Isaac; and Isaac fathered Jacob; and Jacob fathered Judah and his brethren;  
**1:3** and Judah fathered Perez and Zerah from Tamar; and Perez fathered Hezron; and Hezron fathered Ram;  
**1:4** and Ram fathered Amminadab; and Amminadab fathered Nahshon; and Nahshon fathered Salmon;  
**1:5** and Salmon fathered Boaz from Rahab; and Boaz fathered Obed from Ruth; and Obed fathered Jesse;  
**1:6** and Jesse fathered David, the king. And David, the king, fathered Solomon from the <i>widow</i> of Uriah;  
**1:7** and Solomon fathered Rehoboam; and Rehoboam fathered Abijah; and Abijah fathered Asa;  
**1:8** and Asa fathered Jehoshaphat; and Jehoshaphat fathered Jehoram; and Jehoram fathered Uzziah;  
**1:9** and Uzziah fathered Jotham; and Jotham fathered Ahaz; and Ahaz fathered Hezekiah;  
**1:10** and Hezekiah fathered Manasseh; and Manasseh fathered Amon; and Amon fathered Josiah;  
**1:11** and Josiah fathered Jechoniah and his brethren, at <i>the time of</i> the exile of Babylon.  
**1:12** And after the exile of Babylon, Jechoniah fathered Shealtiel; and Shealtiel fathered Zerubbabel;  
**1:13** and Zerubbabel fathered Abiud; and Abiud fathered Eliakim; and Eliakim fathered Azor;  
**1:14** and Azor fathered Sadoc; and Sadoc fathered Achim; and Achim fathered Eliud;  
**1:15** and Eliud fathered Eleazar; and Eleazar fathered Matthan; and Matthan fathered Jacob;  
**1:16** and Jacob fathered Joseph, the husband of Mary, from whom was born Jesus, who is called the Christ.<br>{Mat 1:1-17 & Luk 3:23-28 Mary’s Genealogy.}  
**1:17** Therefore, all the generations from Abraham until David <i>were</i> fourteen generations; and from David until the exile of Babylon, <i>were</i> fourteen generations; and from the exile of Babylon until the Christ, <i>were</i> fourteen generations.<br>{Mat 1:18-25 Nazareth 5 BC; no parallel.}  
**1:18** Now the birth of Jesus Christ was thus: for his mother Mary was engaged to Joseph, before they came together, she was found holding <i>a child</i> in <i>her</i> womb {i.e. pregnant} from <i>the</i> Holy Spirit.  
**1:19** Now Joseph, her husband, being a righteous man and not willing to disgrace her, planned to divorce her secretly.  
**1:20** But <i>when</i> he was contemplating these things, behold, a messenger of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying, Joseph, son of David, do not fear to take Mary to you, <i>as</i> your wife for what was born {Or: conceived} in her is from <i>the</i> Holy Spirit.  
**1:21** Now she will be bearing a son, and you will call his name Jesus, for he will be saving his people from their sins.  
**1:22** Now this whole thing has happened, in order that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled, saying,  
**1:23** ‘Behold, the virgin will be holding <i>a child</i> in <i>her</i> womb {i.e. pregnant} , and will be bearing a son, and they will call his name Immanuel'; which is, <i>after</i> being translated, ‘God with us.’ {Isa 7:14, 8:8, 10}  
**1:24** Now having been awakened from his sleep, Joseph did as the messenger of the Lord commanded him and took his wife to him;  
**1:25** and did not know her <i>intimately</i> until she bore her firstborn son, and he called his name Jesus.  

## 2장

**2:1** {Mat 2:1-12 Jerusalem & Bethlehem 4 BC; no parallel.}<br>Now <i>when</i> Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of Herod the king, behold, wise-men {Persian Magi; v7 & v16} from the east came into Jerusalem,  
**2:2** saying, Where is he who was born <i>the</i> King of the Jews? For we saw his star in the east and came to worship him.  
**2:3** Now <i>after</i> Herod the king heard it, he was disturbed and everyone <i>in</i> Jerusalem with him.  
**2:4** And having gathered together all the high-priests and scribes of the people, he inquired from them where the Christ should be born.  
**2:5** Now they said to him, In Bethlehem of Judea; for thus it has been written through the prophet,  
**2:6** ‘And Bethlehem, a land of Judah, you are by no means least among the governors of Judah; for a leader will be coming forth from you, who will be shepherding my people Israel.’ {Mic 5:2, 2Sam 5:2}  
**2:7** Then Herod, having called the wise-men secretly, ascertained accurately from them the time of the star’s appearing.  
**2:8** And he sent them to Bethlehem and said, <i>While</i> you° <i>have</i> traveled <i>on</i>, probe° out accurately <i>the things</i> concerning the young child. And whenever you° find <i>him</i>, report° to me, that <i>after</i> I <i>have</i> gone <i>there</i>, I also may worship him.  
**2:9** Now having heard the king, they traveled <i>away</i>. And behold, the star, which they saw in the east, was preceding them until it came and stood above where the young child was.  
**2:10** Now having seen the star, they rejoiced <i>with</i> extremely great joy.  
**2:11** And they came into the house and saw the young child with Mary, his mother. They fell <i>down</i> and worshiped him. Having opened their treasures, they brought gifts of gold and frankincense and myrrh to him. {Isa 60:6, Psa 72:10, 15}  
**2:12** And having been divinely-warned in a dream not to revisit Herod, they departed into their own region through another way.<br>{Mat 2:13-18 Leaving for Egypt 4 BC; no parallel.}  
**2:13** Now <i>after</i> they departed, behold, a messenger of <i>the</i> Lord appears to Joseph in a dream, saying, <i>After</i> you <i>have</i> raised yourself <i>up</i>, take the young child and his mother, and flee into Egypt, and be there until I should speak to you <i>again</i>, for Herod is about to seek the young child to destroy him.  
**2:14** Now having raised himself <i>up</i>, he took the young child and his mother by night, and departed into Egypt;  
**2:15** and they were there until the end of Herod; in order that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled, saying, ‘I called my son out of Egypt.’ {Hos. 11:1}  
**2:16** Then Herod, having seen that he was mocked by the wise-men, was exceedingly furious, and sent forth <i>soldiers and</i> assassinated all the boys who <i>were</i> in Bethlehem, and in all her borders, from two years and downward, according to the time which he <i>had</i> ascertained accurately from the wise-men.  
**2:17** Then what was spoken by Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled, saying,  
**2:18** ‘A voice was heard in Ramah, mourning, and weeping, and much anguish, Rachel weeping <i>for</i> her children. And she did not wish to be comforted, because they are not <i>there</i>.’ {Jer 31:15}<br>{Mat 2:19-23 & Luk 2:39b Egypt & Nazareth 4 BC.}  
**2:19** But <i>after</i> Herod died, behold, a messenger of the Lord appears in a dream to Joseph in Egypt,  
**2:20** saying, <i>After</i> you <i>have</i> raised yourself <i>up</i>, take the young child and his mother, and travel into the land of Israel, for those seeking the young child’s life have died.  
**2:21** Now he raised himself <i>up</i> and took the young child and his mother and came into the land of Israel.  
**2:22** But <i>after</i> he heard that Archelaus is reigning over Judea instead of his father Herod, he was afraid to go there, and having been divinely-warned in a dream, he departed into the parts of Galilee,  
**2:23** and he came and dwelt in a city called Nazareth; that what was spoken through the prophets might be fulfilled, that he will be called a Nazarene.  

## 3장

**3:1** {Mar 1:1-8 & Mat 3:1-12 & Luk 3:1-18. Judea near Jordan River 25 or 26 AD.}<br>Now in those days John the Immerser comes, preaching in the wilderness of Judea,  
**3:2** and saying, Repent°; for the kingdom of the heavens has drawn near.  
**3:3** For this one is he who was spoken of by Isaiah the prophet, saying, ‘A voice <i>is</i> crying in the wilderness, prepare° the way of the Lord, make° his paths straight.’ {Isa 40:3}  
**3:4** Now John himself had his <i>outer</i> clothing <i>made</i> from camel’s hair and a leather belt around his loin, and his nourishment was locusts and wild honey.  
**3:5** Then Jerusalem and everyone <i>in</i> Judea and everyone <i>from</i> the region around the Jordan were traveling out to him.  
**3:6** And they were being immersed by him in the Jordan, confessing their sins.  
**3:7** But <i>after</i> he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his immersion, he said to them, Offspring of vipers, who warned you° to flee from the future wrath?  
**3:8** Therefore produce° fruit worthy of repentance.  
**3:9** Do° not think to say among yourselves, We have Abraham for our father; for I say to you°, that God is able to lift up children out of these stones for Abraham.  
**3:10** But even already, the ax is laid to the root of the trees. Therefore every tree which is not producing good fruit is cut down and cast into the fire.  
**3:11** I indeed immerse you° in water toward repentance, but he who is coming after me is mightier <i>than</i> I, whose shoes I am not sufficient to bear. He will immerse you° in <i>the</i> Holy Spirit;  
**3:12** whose winnowing fan <i>is</i> in his hand, and he will be thoroughly cleansing his threshing floor, and he will be gathering his grain into the barn, but the chaff he will be burning up with unquenchable fire.<br>{Mar 1:9-11, Mat 3:13-17, Luk 3:21-22, Joh 1:29-34? Jordan east of Jericho, Spring 27 AD.}  
**3:13** Then Jesus comes from Galilee to John, to be immersed by him in the Jordan.  
**3:14** But John was preventing him, saying, I have need to be immersed by you and you are coming to me?  
**3:15** But Jesus answered and said to him, Allow <i>it</i> now; for thus it is suitable for us to fulfill every righteousness. Then he allows him.  
**3:16** And Jesus, <i>after</i> he was immersed, came-up immediately from the water; and behold, the heavens were opened to him, and he saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove, and coming upon him;  
**3:17** and behold, a voice <i>came</i> out of the heavens, saying, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am delighted.  

## 4장

**4:2** And having fasted forty days and forty nights, later he hungered.  
**4:3** And the Tempter came near and said to him, If you are the Son of God, speak, in order that these stones may become bread.  
**4:4** But he answered and said, It has been written, ‘Man will not be living on bread alone, but upon every declaration that travels through the mouth of God!’ {Deu 8:3}  
**4:5** Then the devil takes him into the holy city. And he stands him upon the pinnacle of the temple,  
**4:6** and says to him, If you are the Son of God, cast yourself downward; for it has been written, ‘He will be commanding the messengers concerning you, and they will be lifting you up upon <i>their</i> hands, lest you hit your foot against a stone.' {Psa 91:11-12}  
**4:7** Jesus said to him, Again it has been written, ‘Do not test the Lord, your God.’ {Deu 6:16}  
**4:8** Again, the devil takes him to an exceedingly high mountain, and shows him all the kingdoms of the world, and the glory of them;  
**4:9** and I will be giving you all these things, if, <i>after</i> you <i>have</i> fallen <i>down</i>, you worship me.  
**4:10** Then Jesus says to him, Go behind me, Adversary! For it has been written, ‘You will worship the Lord your God and you will give-divine service to him only.’ {Deu 6:13}  
**4:11** Then the devil leaves him, and behold, messengers came and were serving him.<br>{Mar 1:14a & Mat 4:12 & Luk 3:19-20 & Joh 4:1-4 Judea to Galilee.}  
**4:12** Now Jesus, having heard that John was given up, departed into Galilee.<br>{Mat 4:13-16 Capernaum.}  
**4:13** And having left Nazareth, he came and dwelt at Capernaum, the <i>area</i> by the sea, in the borders of Zebulun and Naphtali;  
**4:14** in order that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, saying,  
**4:15** ‘The land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, the way of the sea, beyond-that of the Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles,  
**4:16** the people who sit in darkness saw a great light and to those who sit in the region and shadow of death, light rose <i>up</i> for them.’ {Isa 9:1-2}<br>{Mar 1:14b-15 & Mat 4:17 & Luk 4:14b-15.}  
**4:17** From then <i>onward</i>, Jesus began to preach and to say, Repent°; for the kingdom of the heavens has drawn near.<br>{Mar 1:16-20 & Mat 4:18-22 & Luk 5:1-11 Sea of Galilee near Capernaum.}  
**4:18** Now walking beside the sea of Galilee, he saw two brethren, Simon who is called Peter and Andrew, his brother, casting a net into the sea; for they were fishermen.  
**4:19** And he says to them, Come-here° after me and I will make you° fishermen of men.  
**4:20** Now immediately, they left the nets and followed him.  
**4:21** And having advanced from there, he saw two other brothers, James, the <i>son</i> of Zebedee and John, his brother, in the ship with Zebedee, their father, mending their nets. And he called them.  
**4:22** Now immediately they followed him having left the ship and their father.<br>{Mar 1:35-39 & Mat 4:23-25 & Luk 4:42-44 Preaching through Galilee.}  
**4:23** And Jesus was proceeding around the whole <i>of</i> Galilee, teaching in their synagogues and preaching the good-news of the kingdom and healing every disease and every illness among the people.  
**4:24** And the report of him went forth into the whole <i>of</i> Syria and they brought to him all who were ill, held by various diseases and torments and demon-possessed and epileptic and paralytics, and he healed them.  
**4:25** And large crowds followed him from Galilee and Decapolis and Jerusalem and Judea and beyond-that of the Jordan.  

## 5장

**5:1** {Mat 5:1 & Luk 6:17-20. Sermon on the mountain.}<br>Now having seen the crowds, he went-up into the mountain. And having sat down, his disciples came to him.<br>{Mat 5:2-12 & Luk 6:20-26.}  
**5:2** And he opened his mouth and was teaching them, saying:  
**5:3** The poor in spirit <i>are</i> fortunate, because theirs is the kingdom of the heavens.  
**5:4** Those who are mourning <i>are</i> fortunate, because they will be comforted.  
**5:5** The meek <i>are</i> fortunate, because they will inherit the earth.  
**5:6** Those who hunger and are thirsty <i>for</i> righteousness <i>are</i> fortunate, because they will be fully-fed.  
**5:7** The merciful are fortunate, because they will be shown-mercy.  
**5:8** The clean in heart <i>are</i> fortunate, because they will be seeing God.  
**5:9** The peacemakers <i>are</i> fortunate, because they will be called sons of God.  
**5:10** Those who have been persecuted because of righteousness are fortunate, because theirs is the kingdom of the heavens.  
**5:11** You° are fortunate whenever they reproach and persecute you° and say every evil word against you°– lying, because of me.  
**5:12** Rejoice° and be° glad, because your° reward is much in the heavens; for thus, they persecuted the prophets who <i>were</i> before you°.<br>{Mat 5:13-16; no parallel.}  
**5:13** You° are the salt of the earth, but if the salt is flavorless, <i>then</i> with what will it be salted? It is still not strong-enough <i>to be used</i> for anything, except to be cast outside and trampled by men.  
**5:14** You° are the light of the world. A city laying upon a mountain is not able to be hidden.  
**5:15** Nor are they burning a lamp and placing it under a peck-container, but upon the lamp-stand, and it is shining to all who are in the house.  
**5:16** So let your° light shine before men, that they may see your° good works and may glorify your° Father, the <i>one</i> in the heavens.<br>{Mat 5:17-48 & Luk 6:27-36.}  
**5:17** Do° not suppose that I came to tear-down the law or the prophets; I did not come to tear-down, but to fulfill.  
**5:18** For assuredly I am saying to you°, Until heaven and earth should pass away, one iota or one serif should never pass away from the law, until all things should become <i>fulfilled</i>.   
**5:19** Therefore, whoever loosens one of these least commandments and may teach men <i>to do</i> so, will be called least in the kingdom of the heavens, but whoever practices and should teach them, he will be called great in the kingdom of the heavens.  
**5:20** For I am saying to you°, that if your° righteousness is not abounding more-than <i>that</i> of the scribes and Pharisees, you° should never enter into the kingdom of the heavens.  
**5:21** You° <i>have</i> heard that it was said to the ancients, ‘Do not murder,' but ‘whoever murders will be liable to the judgment.’ {Exo 20:13 & Deu 5:17}  
**5:22** But I am saying to you°, that everyone who is vainly angry to<i>ward</i> his brother will be liable to the judgment, and whoever says to his brother, You, empty-head, will be liable to the council, but whoever says, You fool, will be liable to the hell of fire.<br>{Hell (Gehenna) is listed under Definitions section.}<br>{Vainly can be: with emptiness (without cause or reason; i.e. to further one's own vanity)}<br>{The words here in Mat 5:22 don't matter, but the way in which they are expressed or meant. Empty-head is an expression of contempt. The Greek here could be simply ‘fool’ said as an expression of condemnation or could be a transliteration of ‘Moreh’ which is a Hebrew expression of condemnation.}  
**5:23** Therefore, if you are offering your gift at the altar, and <i>while you are</i> there, you should remember that your brother has something against you,  
**5:24** leave your gift there before the altar and go away. First be reconciled to your brother and then <i>when</i> you have come <i>back</i>, offer your gift.  
**5:25** Be agreeable with your opponent quickly, until which <i>time</i> you are with him on the road; lest the opponent should give you to the judge and the judge should give you to the attendant and you will be cast into prison.  
**5:26** Assuredly I am saying to you, You should never come out from there, until you should repaid the last copper-coin.  
**5:27** You° <i>have</i> heard that it was said, ‘Do not commit adultery.’ {Exo 20:14 & Deu 5:18}  
**5:28** But I am saying to you°, that everyone who is looking <i>at</i> a woman {Or wife} in order to lust-after her already committed adultery with her in his heart.  
**5:29** Now if your right eye is offending you, pluck it out and cast it from you°; for it is advantageous for you, that one of your members should perish and not <i>that</i> your whole body should be cast into hell.  
**5:30** And if your right hand is offending you, cut it off and cast <i>it</i> away from you°; for it is advantageous for you, that one of your members should perish and not <i>that</i> your whole body should be cast into hell.   
**5:31** It was also said, ‘Whoever divorces his wife, let him give her a <i>document of</i> divorcement.’ {Deu 24:1, 3}  
**5:32** But I am saying to you°, that whoever divorces his wife, except-for <i>the</i> reason of fornication, is making her commit adultery and whoever marries her who has been divorced is committing adultery.<br>{Mat 19:9, Luk 16:18}<br>{Once betrothed, once the couple leave the parents house, (Mat 1:19), with or without all the pomp and ceremony, you were married.<br>We have a ‘separation’ and ‘a legal separation’ in English, the Jews had no such thing, nor did most of the world before the 20th century. The Jews only had divorce [release, dismiss]. 'Put away' was a kindness to mean divorce, similar to our 'he passed on' vs. 'he is dead'. Once you separated, moved out or was put out, you were divorced.}  
**5:33** Again, you° <i>have</i> heard that it was said to the ancients, ‘You will not perjure yourself, but will be giving your oaths to the Lord.’ {Lev 19:12, Num 30:2, Deu 23:21}  
**5:34** But I am saying to you°, Not to swear in anyway; neither by the heaven, because it is the throne of God;  
**5:35** nor by the earth, because it is the footstool of his feet; nor in Jerusalem, because it is the city of the Great King,  
**5:36** nor should you swear by your head, because you are unable to make one hair white or black.  
**5:37** But let your° word be, a Yes, yes; a No, no; and whatever is more-than these, is from the evil <i>one</i>.  
**5:38** You° <i>have</i> heard that it was said, An eye in exchange-for an eye and a tooth in exchange-for a tooth. {Exo 21:24, Lev 24:20, Deu 19:21}  
**5:39** But I am saying to you°, Not to stand against an evil <i>man</i>, but who<i>ever</i> slaps you upon your right jaw, turn also the other to him.  
**5:40** And to him, who is willing to have a lawsuit against you and <i>wants</i> to take away your tunic, <i>then</i> also allow for him <i>to take your</i>° <i>outer</i> garment.  
**5:41** And who<i>ever</i> compels you <i>to go</i> one mile, go with him two.  
**5:42** Give to the one who asks you and do not turn away from the one who wishes to borrow from you.  
**5:43** You° <i>have</i> heard that it was said, ‘You will love your neighbor and hate your enemy.’ {Lev 19:18}  
**5:44** But I am saying to you°, Love° your° enemies, speak° well of those who curse you°, do° good <i>things</i> to those who hate you° and pray° on behalf of those who are railing at you° and are persecuting you°.  
**5:45** <i>Do this</i> that you° may become sons of your° Father <i>who is</i> in the heavens, because his sun rises upon the evil and the good and <i>it</i> rains upon the righteous and the unrighteous.  
**5:46** For if you° love those who love you°, what reward do you° have? Do not even the tax collectors do the same?  
**5:47** And if you° only greet your° friends, what are you° doing more-than <i>others</i>? Are not even the tax collectors doing so?  
**5:48** Therefore you° will be complete, just-like your° Father is complete, the <i>one</i> in the heavens.  

## 6장

**6:1** {Mat 6:1-34; no parallel.}<br>Take-heed° not to do your° charity before men, in order to be seen by them, otherwise you° have no reward from your° Father, the <i>one</i> in the heavens.  
**6:2** Therefore, whenever you are doing charity, do not sound <i>a trumpet</i> before you just-like the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the lanes, that they may be glorified by men. Assuredly I am saying to you°, They are fully having their reward.  
**6:3** But <i>when</i> you are doing charity, do not let your left <i>hand</i> know what your right <i>hand</i> is doing,  
**6:4** that your charity may be in secret, and your Father who sees in secret will be repaying you openly.  
**6:5** And whenever you° pray, do not be just-like the hypocrites, because they love to pray, standing in the synagogues and at the street corners, that they may appear <i>devout</i> to men. Assuredly I am saying to you°, that they are fully having their reward.  
**6:6** But you, whenever you pray, enter into your storeroom, and <i>after</i> you <i>have</i> locked your door, pray to your Father who is in secret, and your Father who sees in secret will be repaying you openly.  
**6:7** And <i>while</i> praying, do° not be repetitious just-like the Gentile; for they think that they will be heard in their many words.  
**6:8** Therefore, do° not be similar to them, for your° Father knows what things you° have need of before you° ask him.  
**6:9** Therefore pray° thus: our Father, the <i>one</i> in the heavens, let your name be made holy.  
**6:10** Let your kingdom come. Let your will happen as in heaven, also upon the earth.  
**6:11** Give our  needful bread to us today.  
**6:12** And forgive us our debts, as we also forgive our debtors.  
**6:13** And do not bring us into temptation, but rescue us from the evil <i>one</i>, because yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory, forever. Amen.  
**6:14** For if you° forgive men their trespasses, your° heavenly Father will also be forgiving you°.  
**6:15** But if you° do° not forgive men their trespasses, neither will your° Father be forgiving your° trespasses.<br>{Footnotes: Mat 6:11, no one knows for sure what the original word means. It is only in the N.T. twice and no where else.}  
**6:16** Now whenever you° fast, do° not become just-like the hypocrites, <i>with</i> a gloomy face; for they disfigure their faces, that they may appear to men <i>to be</i> fasting. Assuredly I am saying to you°, They are fully having their reward.  
**6:17** But <i>as for</i> you, <i>when</i> you are fasting, anoint your head and wash your face  
**6:18** that you do not appear to be fasting to men, but to your Father <i>who is</i> in secret, and your Father who sees in secret will be repaying you.  
**6:19** Do° not store up treasures for you upon the earth, where moth and rust ingest, and where thieves burrow through <i>your</i>° <i>wall</i> and steal.  
**6:20** But store° up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust ingest, and where thieves do not burrow through nor steal.  
**6:21** For where your° treasure is, your° heart will also be there.  
**6:22** The lamp of the body is the eye, therefore if your eye is clear, your whole body will be radiant.  
**6:23** But if your eye is evil, your whole body will be dark. Therefore, if the light which is in you is darkness, how-much <i>greater</i> is the darkness!  
**6:24** No one is able to serve two lords, for either he will hate the one and will love the other; or he will hold to one and will despise the other. You° are not able to serve God and  money.<br>{Mat 6:24, or: Money as in your ‘God of Money,’ or ‘The Almighty Dollar.’}  
**6:25** Because of this, I am saying to you°, Do° not be anxious for your° life, what you° may eat and what you° may drink; nor for what you° may clothe your° body with. Is not the life <i>worth</i> more-than the nourishment and the body <i>worth more-than</i> the clothing?  
**6:26** Look° at the birds of the heaven, that they do not sow, nor reap, nor gather into barns, and your° heavenly Father is nourishing them. Are you° not rather carrying much more-value-than them?
