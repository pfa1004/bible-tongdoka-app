# 58.히브리서 (MLV)



## 1장

**1:1** God spoke long-ago in many parts and in many manners, in the prophets to the fathers, <i>and</i> spoke to us upon the last of those days in <i>his</i> Son,  
**1:2** whom he appointed heir of all things, through whom also he made the ages.  
**1:3** <i>It is he</i> who, being the brightness of his glory and <i>the</i> exact representation of his essence, and <i>is</i> carrying all things by his declaration of power. Having made a cleansing of our sins through himself, he sat down by <i>the</i> right <i>hand</i> of the Majesty in <i>the</i> high <i>place</i>;  
**1:4** <i>when</i> he became so-much better <i>than</i> the messengers, inasmuch as he has inherited a more excellent name than they.  
**1:5** For to which of the messengers has he previously said, ‘You are my Son; I have fathered you today.’? And again, ‘I will be a Father to him and he will be for a Son to me.’?  
**1:6** And again, whenever he brings in the firstborn into the inhabited-earth, he says, ‘And let all the messengers of God worship him.’  
**1:7** And he indeed says toward the messengers, ‘Who makes his messengers<i>,</i> spirits, and his ministers, a flame of fire.’ {Psa 2:7, 89:26, 27, Psa 104:4}  
**1:8** But <i>he says</i> toward the Son<i>,</i> ‘Your throne, <i>O</i> God, is forevermore; the scepter of uprightness is the scepter of your kingdom.  
**1:9** You loved righteousness and hated lawlessness. Because of this, God, your God, anointed you <i>with the</i> oil of gladness <i>more</i> than your companions.’  
**1:10** And, ‘You, Lord, in <i>the</i> beginnings, founded the earth and the heavens are the works of your hands:  
**1:11** they themselves will be perishing, but you are remaining and they will all become-obsolete like a garment;  
**1:12** and you will coil them up like an <i>outer</i> dressing and they will be changed, but you are the same and your years will not be failing.’  
**1:13** But toward which of the messengers has he previously said, ‘Sit at my right <i>hand</i>, until I should place your enemies <i>as the</i> footstool of your feet? {Psa 45:6-7, 102:25-27, 110:1}  
**1:14** Are they not all ministering spirits, being sent forth into service because of those who are about to inherit salvation?  

## 2장

**2:1** Because of this, it is essential to take-heed even-more to the things which were heard, lest we might drift away.  
**2:2** For if the word that was spoken through <i>the</i> messengers became steadfast and every transgression and disobedience received a just reward;  
**2:3** how will we flee, having neglected so-great a salvation, which having received <i>at the</i> beginning was spoken through the Lord, which was confirmed to us under those who heard <i>it</i>?  
**2:4** God <i>was</i> testifying together with them, both by signs and by wonders and by various powers and by distributions of <i>the</i> Holy Spirit, according to his own will.  
**2:5** For he did not subject the future inhabited-earth to messengers, concerning what we are speaking.  
**2:6** But a some <i>writer</i> thoroughly testified somewhere, saying, ‘What is man, that you remember him? Or the son of man, that you visit him?  
**2:7** You made him a bit <i>of</i> something inferior <i>less</i> than the messengers. You crowned him with glory and honor.  <i>And stood him over the works of your hands.</i>  
**2:8** You subjected all things underneath his feet.’ {Psa 8:4-6} For in that he subjected all things to him, he left nothing which is not subject to him. But we now do not yet see all things having been subjected to him.  
**2:9** But we see Jesus who has been made a bit of something inferior, <i>less</i> than <i>the</i> messengers, having been crowned with glory and honor, because of the suffering of the death <i>he underwent</i>,that in the grace from God, he should taste of death on behalf of everyone.  
**2:10** For it was suitable for him, because of whom <i>are</i> all things and through whom <i>are</i> all things, having led many sons to glory, to complete the author of their salvation through sufferings.  
**2:11** For both he who is making <i>him</i> holy and those who are made holy are all from one. Because of which case, he is not ashamed to call them brethren,  
**2:12** saying, ‘I will be proclaiming your name to my brethren, in the midst of the congregation I will be singing hymns to you.’  
**2:13** And again, ‘I will have confidence in him.’ And again, ‘Behold, <i>here</i> I <i>am</i> and the children whom God gave me.’ {Psa 22:22, Psa 18:2, 2Sam. 22. 3, Isa 8:17-18}  
**2:14** Therefore since the children have shared of flesh and blood, in like-manner he partook of the same; in order that through death, he might do-away-with the one who had the dominion of death, this is the devil;  
**2:15** and he might set-free these, as many as <i>were</i> in fear of death, <i>who were</i> liable to bondage throughout all <i>their</i> life.  
**2:16** For surely he does not help messengers, but he is helping the seed of Abraham.  
**2:17** Hence he was obligated to be similar to his brethren according to all things, in order that he might become a merciful and faithful high-priest in the things pertaining to God, that he should make atonement for the sins of the people.  
**2:18** For in what he has suffered, having been tempted, he is able to help with those who are tempted.  

## 3장

**3:1** Hence, holy brethren, partakers of a heavenly calling, consider° Jesus Christ, the Apostle and High-Priest of our confession;  
**3:2** who was faithful to the one who made him <i>such</i>, as Moses also <i>was</i> in his whole house. {Num 12:7}  
**3:3** For he has been deemed worthy of more glory than Moses, inasmuch as the one who constructed <i>the house</i> has more honor <i>than</i> the house.  
**3:4** For every house is constructed by someone, but the one who constructed all things is God.  
**3:5** And Moses indeed was faithful in his whole house as a bondservant for a testimony of the things which will be spoken <i>later</i>;  
**3:6** but Christ <i>was faithful</i> as a son, over his house; whose house we are, if indeed we hold-onto our boldness and the boasting of our hope steadfast till the end.  
**3:7** Hence, as the Holy Spirit says, ‘Today if you° hear his voice,  
**3:8** do not harden your° hearts, as in the rebellion, according to the day of the testing in the wilderness,  
**3:9** where your° fathers tested me, they proved me<i>,</i> and <i>then</i> saw my works forty years.  
**3:10** Hence I was displeased with that generation and said, They themselves are habitually misled in their heart<i>s</i>, but they did not know my ways.  
**3:11** As I swore in my wrath, they will not be entering into my rest.’ {Num 14:22, Psa 95:7-11}  
**3:12** Beware° brethren, lest there will be an evil heart of unbelief in anyone of you°, in withdrawing away from the living God.  
**3:13** But encourage° yourselves each <i>and</i> every day, till what is called today; in order that not anyone of you° may be hardened by the deception of sin.  
**3:14** For we have become companions of the Christ, if indeed we hold-onto the beginning of our firmness steadfast till the end;  
**3:15** while it was said, ‘If you° hear his voice today, do not harden your° hearts, as in the rebellion.’ {Num 14:29, Psa 95:7-11 }  
**3:16** For some who <i>had</i> heard <i>it</i> rebelled. But was it not all those who came out of Egypt through Moses?  
**3:17** And with whom was he displeased <i>for</i> forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose carcasses fell in the wilderness? {Num 14:35}  
**3:18** Now to whom had he sworn <i>that</i> they will not be entering into his rest, except to those who were disobedient?  
**3:19** And we see that they were not able to enter because of unbelief.  

## 4장

**4:1** Therefore, since a promise was left of entering into his rest, let us fear lest anyone out of you° seem to have come-short <i>of it</i>.  
**4:2** For we also are having the good-news proclaimed to us, just-as they also <i>had</i>. But the word of hearing did not profit them, <i>because</i> it had not been mixed together with the faith in those who heard <i>it</i>.  
**4:3** For we who believed are entering into that rest; just-as he has said, ‘As I swore in my wrath, they will not be entering into my rest,’ although the works <i>have</i> happened from the conception of the world. {Psa 95:7:11, Num 14:22}  
**4:4** For he has thus said somewhere concerning the seventh <i>day</i>, ‘And God rested on the seventh day from all his works’; {Gen 2:2}  
**4:5** and again in this one, ‘They will not be entering into my rest.’ {Psa 95:11}  
**4:6** Therefore, since it is left <i>for</i> some to enter into it, and those to whom the good-news was proclaimed beforehand did not enter into <i>it</i> because of disobedience,  
**4:7** he again determines a certain day <i>as</i> today, saying in David, after so-much time, (just-as it has been said <i>beforehand</i>), ‘Today if you° hear his voice, do not harden your° hearts.’ {Psa 95:7, 8}  
**4:8** For if Joshua had given-rest to them, he would have not been speaking concerning another day after these things.  
**4:9** Consequently, a Sabbath-rest is left for the people of God.  
**4:10** For the one who entered into his rest <i>has</i> also rested from his works, just-as God did from his own.  
**4:11** Therefore let us be diligent to enter into that rest, in order that no one should fall into the same example of disobedience.  
**4:12** For the word of God is living and effective and sharper beyond <i>that of</i> every two edged sword and <i>is</i> penetrating till the division of both soul and spirit, of both joints and marrow and <i>is</i> a judge of <i>the</i> contemplations and deliberations of <i>the</i> heart.  
**4:13** And there is no created thing unapparent in his sight, but all things are naked and have been laid-bare to his eyes to whom <i>is</i> our account.  
**4:14** Therefore having a great high-priest, Jesus the Son of God, who has gone through the heavens, let us hold-fast our confession.  
**4:15** For we do not have a high-priest who is unable to sympathize together with our weaknesses, but one who has been tempted according to all things according to <i>our</i> likeness, <i>yet</i> without sin.  
**4:16** Therefore let us come near to the throne of grace with boldness, in order that we may receive mercy and may find grace for opportune help.  

## 5장

**5:1** For every high-priest, being taken out of men, is designated <i>to act</i> on behalf of men in things pertaining to God, in order that he may offer both gifts and sacrifices on behalf of sins;  
**5:2** he who is able to moderate his emotions with those who are ignorant and are misled, since he himself is also encompassed <i>with</i> weakness;  
**5:3** and because of this <i>weakness,</i> just-as he is obligated to offer <i>sacrifices</i> on behalf of sins concerning the people so also concerning himself.  
**5:4** And not anyone takes the honor for himself, but he who is called by God, just-as <i>was</i> Aaron.  
**5:5** So the Christ also did not glorify himself to become a high-priest, but he who spoke to him, ‘You are my Son; I have fathered you° today.’ {Psa 2:7}  
**5:6** Just-as he also says in a different <i>Scripture,</i> ‘You are a priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek.’ {Psa 110:4}  
**5:7** Who in the days of his flesh, having offered up both prayers and supplications with a strong outcry and tears to the one who was able to save him from death and he was heard from <i>his</i> piety.  
**5:8** Although being a Son, he learned obedience from the things which he suffered;  
**5:9** and having been completed, he became the cause of everlasting salvation to all those who are obeying him;  
**5:10** having been addressed by God, <i>as</i> high-priest according to the order of Melchizedek.  
**5:11** Concerning whom, much for us <i>to say in</i> speech (also hard to translate {i.e. simplify} ), since you° have become sluggish in hearing.  
**5:12** For you° also ought to be teachers <i>by now</i> because of the time, <i>but</i> you° again have need of one to teach you° what <i>are</i> the fundamental principles of the beginning oracles of God, and have become <i>ones</i> having need of milk and not of solid nourishment.  
**5:13** For everyone who partakes of milk <i>is</i> inexperienced of the word of righteousness; for he is an infant.  
**5:14** But solid nourishment is for <i>the</i> mature <i>ones</i>, from those who, through <i>their</i> habit, <i>their</i> senses have been exercised to the discerning of both good and evil.  

## 6장

**6:1** Hence, having left the word of the beginning <i>principles</i> of Christ, we should carry on<i>to our</i> maturity; not putting down again a foundation of repentance from dead works and of faith upon God,  
**6:2** of the teaching of immersions {Or: O.T. ceremonial washings} and of <i>the</i> laying on of hands and of <i>the</i> resurrection of the dead and of everlasting judgment.  
**6:3** And we might do this, if indeed God permits.  
**6:4** For <i>it is</i> impossible, those who were once enlightened and tasted of the heavenly gift and become partakers of <i>the</i> Holy Spirit  
**6:5** and tasted the good word of God and the powers of the future world  
**6:6** and <i>then</i> having fallen away, <i>it is impossible</i> to renew them again to repentance, <i>because they are</i> again crucifying for themselves the Son of God and disgracing <i>him</i>.  
**6:7** For the soil, which drank the rainfall, <i>while</i> coming upon her often, and is bearing <i>forth</i> vegetation fit for those because of whom it is also cultivated, receives blessing from God.  
**6:8** But <i>while</i> it is bringing forth thorns and thistles, <i>it is</i> unapproved and near to a curse; whose end is for burning.  
**6:9** But, beloved, we have confidence <i>of</i> the better things concerning you° and <i>better things</i> holding salvation, even if we speak so.  
**6:10** For God is not unrighteous to forget your° work and the labor of the love which you° showed toward his name, having served the holy-ones and <i>still</i> serving <i>them</i>.  
**6:11** But we are desiring each of you° to show the same diligence to the full assurance of hope till <i>the</i> end;  
**6:12** in order that you° should not become sluggish, but imitators of those who are inheriting the promises through faith and patience.  
**6:13** For <i>after</i> God promised to Abraham, since he was holding no one greater to swear according to, he had sworn according to himself,  
**6:14** saying, Absolutely <i>in</i> blessing, I will bless you and <i>in</i> multiplying, I will multiply you. {Gen 22:17}  
**6:15** And thus <i>Abraham</i>, having <i>had</i> patience, obtained the promise.  
**6:16** For indeed, men swear according to the greater and the oath <i>made</i> for confirmation <i>is</i> an ending in every dispute of theirs.  
**6:17** In which God, planning to show even-more to the heirs of the promise the unchangeable <i>will</i> of his plan, intermediated <i>this</i> with an oath;  
**6:18** in order that through two unchangeable matters, in which <i>it is</i> impossible <i>for</i> God to lie, we may have a strong encouragement, having fled for refuge to take-hold of the hope that lays before us.  
**6:19** <i>This hope</i> which we have as an anchor of the soul, <i>is</i> both secure and steadfast and entering into the inner <i>side</i> of the curtain;  
**6:20** where a forerunner Jesus entered on our behalf, <i>when</i> he became a high-priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek.  

## 7장

**7:1** For this Melchizedek, King of Salem, priest of the Highest God, who met with Abraham returning from the butcher of the kings and blessed him,  
**7:2** to whom Abraham also divided a tenth away from all things. Being first indeed, by translation, King of righteousness and thereafter also King of Salem, which is King of peace;  
**7:3** <i>he had</i> no father, no mother, with no genealogy, having neither beginning of days nor end of life, but having been made similar to the Son of God, remains a continual priest. {Gen 14:18}  
**7:4** Now view° how-great this <i>man was</i>, to whom even Abraham, the patriarch, gave a tenth out of the spoils.  
**7:5** And they indeed from the sons of Levi who receive the priest’s office have <i>the</i> commandment to take-tithes from the people according to the law, that is, of their brethren, although these have come out of the loins of Abraham.  
**7:6** But he whose genealogy is not traced from them has taken-tithes of Abraham and has blessed the one who has the promises.  
**7:7** But without any dispute the inferior is blessed by the better one.  
**7:8** And men indeed receive tithes <i>who</i> die here, but there, it is testified that he is living.  
**7:9** And, <i>so</i> as to speak <i>the</i> declaration, even Levi, who receives tithes, has paid-tithes through Abraham;  
**7:10** for he was still in the loins of his father, when Melchizedek met him.<br>{NOTES: At the Lord’s Supper and in Hebrews at 7:22 and throughout the rest of the Bible: the English words, ‘covenant’ and ‘testament’ used by various translations have always been the same Greek word. The Old or New Testament is exactly the same as The Old or New Covenant. Covenant was adapted throughout the MLV because it also has a verb form. Covenant is a contract, one was God with the Jews; replaced by the one which God made with the Christians. More is in the ‘Definitions’ section. This Old Covenant being replaced by the New Covenant was foretold in Jer 31:31-34.}  
**7:11** Therefore, if there indeed was completion through the Levitical priesthood (for the people had <i>the</i> law instituted upon it), what need <i>was there</i> still <i>for</i> a different priest to rise up according to the order of Melchizedek and not to be called according to the order of Aaron?  
**7:12** For <i>when</i> the priesthood is transferred, there also becomes, out of necessity, a transfer of <i>the</i> law.  
**7:13** For he belongs to a different tribe on whom these things are said, from which no one has taken-heed at the altar.  
**7:14** For it is evident that our Lord has risen up out of Judah; <i>as</i> to which tribe Moses spoke nothing concerning the priesthood.  
**7:15** And <i>what we say</i> is still even-more evident, if there stands up a different priest, according to the likeness of Melchizedek,  
**7:16** who has become, not according to <i>the</i> Law of a fleshly commandment, but according to the power of an indestructible life;  
**7:17** for he testifies<i>,</i> ‘You are a priest forever, according to the order of Melchizedek.’ {Psa 110:4}  
**7:18** For indeed, there becomes a nullifying of the preceding commandment because of its weakness and unprofitableness  
**7:19** (for the law completed nothing) and a bringing in of a better hope, through which we are drawing near to God.  
**7:20** And inasmuch as <i>it is</i> not without the taking of an oath for those who indeed became priests are <i>so</i> without an oath,  
**7:21** but he with an oath through the one who says pertaining to him, ‘<i>The</i> Lord swore and will not regret, you are a priest forever, according to the order of Melchizedek.’ {Psa 110:4}  
**7:22** Jesus has also become the guarantee according to a so-much better covenant.  
**7:23** And they have become indeed many priests <i>in number</i>, because they are prevented by death to remain <i>in office</i>;  
**7:24** but he has his perpetual priesthood because <i>he is</i> to remain forever.  
**7:25** Hence he is also able to completely save those who come to God through him, always living, that he may petition on their behalf.  
**7:26** For such a high-priest was suitable to us, holy, guiltless, undefiled, has been separated away from sinners and became exalted from the heavens;  
**7:27** who has no necessity every day to offer up sacrifices just-like the high-priests, (beforehand on behalf of their own sins), and thereafter for those of the people; for he did this once for all, having offered up himself.  
**7:28** For the law designates men <i>as</i> high-priests, who have weakness, but the word of the oath, which was after the law, <i>appoints</i> a Son, having been completed forever.  

## 8장

**8:1** Now a summation upon the things which we are saying <i>is this</i>: we have such a high-priest, who sat down at <i>the</i> right <i>hand</i> of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens,  
**8:2** a minister of the holy-places and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched and not man.  
**8:3** For every high-priest is designated, that he should offer both gifts and sacrifices; hence <i>it is</i> necessary <i>for</i> this one to also have something that he should offer.  
**8:4** For if he indeed was upon <i>the</i> earth, he would not even be a priest, being <i>that</i> the priests who offer the gifts according to the law;  
**8:5** who gives-divine service to <i>what is</i> a copy and shadow of the heavenly things, just-as Moses had been divinely-warned <i>while</i> being about to complete the tabernacle; for behold, he says, You will be making all things according to the pattern which was shown to you in the mountain. {Exo 25:40}  
**8:6** But now he has obtained a <i>more</i> excellent ministry, inasmuch as he is also the intermediary of a better covenant, which has been instituted upon better promises.  
**8:7** For if that first <i>covenant</i> was blameless, then no place would have been sought for a second.  
**8:8** For finding-fault with them, he says, ‘Behold, the days are coming, says the Lord, That I will complete a new covenant upon the house of Israel and upon the house of Judah;  
**8:9** not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day that I took <i>them</i> by my hand to lead them forth out of <i>the</i> land of Egypt, because they did not remain in my covenant and I neglected them, says <i>the</i> Lord.  
**8:10** Because this one <i>is</i> the covenant which I will covenant with the house of Israel after those days, says <i>the</i> Lord. <i>I</i> will be giving my laws into their mind and I will be writing them upon their hearts. And I will be a God to them and they will be <i>covenanted</i> into a people for me;  
**8:11** and they should never <i>need to</i> teach, saying, Know the Lord; <i>to</i> each <i>one</i> his fellow-citizen and each <i>one</i> his brother, because all will know me, from the little insofar as their great ones.  
**8:12** Because I will be lenient to their unrighteousness; I should never remember their lawlessness and their sins anymore.’ {Jer 31:31-34}  
**8:13** In the saying, a new <i>covenant</i>, the first has become-obsolete. But what becomes-obsolete and is aged, it is near to disappearing.  

## 9장

**9:1** Therefore indeed even <i>the</i> first <i>tabernacle</i> had ordinances of divine service <i>to God</i> and its holy-place, a worldly <i>one</i>.  
**9:2** For the first tabernacle was prepared, which is called <i>the</i> holy-place, in which <i>were</i> the lamp-stand and the table and the loaves of the showbread.  
**9:3** But, the tabernacle which is called the holy of holies <i>was</i> after the second curtain;  
**9:4** holding a golden altar of incense and the ark of the covenant, having been covered <i>all</i> around on every-side with gold, in which <i>was the</i> golden jar holding the manna and Aaron’s scepter which <i>had</i> sprouted and the tablets of the covenant;  
**9:5** and up above it <i>the</i> cherubim of glory overshadowing the mercy-seat; concerning which things it is now not <i>the time</i> to speak <i>of them</i> individually. {Exo 25:1-40, 26:36, 40:3, Num 17:10}  
**9:6** Now these things having thus always been prepared, the priests indeed go-into the first tabernacle, completing the divine services <i>to God</i>;  
**9:7** but the high-priest <i>went</i> alone into the second, once in the year for all, not without blood, which he offers on behalf of himself and <i>on behalf of</i> the sins of ignorance of the people.  
**9:8** The Holy Spirit indicating this, that the way into the holy of holies to have not yet been manifested, <i>while</i> the first tabernacle still has a standing.  
**9:9** <i>This tabernacle,</i> (which <i>is</i> a figure toward the present time); according to which both gifts and sacrifices are offered, is not able to complete he who is giving-divine service to <i>God</i> according to <i>the</i> conscience,  
**9:10** <i>being</i> only ordinances of <i>the</i> flesh, (upon <i>the basis of</i> foods, drinks and assorted ceremonial washings), laid upon <i>us</i> till a time of reformation.  
**9:11** But Christ came <i>as</i> a high-priest of the future good things, through the greater and more complete tabernacle, not made with hands, that is, not of this creation,  
**9:12** nor through the blood of male-goats and calves, but through his own blood, entered in once for all into the holy of holies, having found everlasting redemption.  
**9:13** For if the blood of male-goats and oxen and the ashes of a heifer, sprinkling those who have been desecrated, makes <i>one</i> holy toward the cleanness of the flesh;  
**9:14** how-much more the blood of Christ, (who through the everlasting Spirit offered himself unblemished to God), will be cleansing your° conscience from dead works, that you may give-divine service to the living God.  
**9:15** And because of this, he is the intermediary of a new covenant, that <i>his</i> death having happened for the redemption of the transgressions that were against the first covenant, that those who have been called may receive the promise of the everlasting inheritance.  
**9:16** For where <i>there is</i> a covenant, <i>it is</i> a necessity <i>for</i> the death of the one who covenanted <i>it</i> to be brought <i>about</i>.  
**9:17** For a covenant is steadfast upon <i>the</i> dead; since it is never enforced when he who covenanted <i>it</i> is living.  
**9:18** Hence even the first <i>covenant</i> has not been inaugurated without blood.  
**9:19** For <i>when</i> every commandment was spoken by Moses to all the people according to <i>the</i> law, <i>and when</i> he took the blood of the calves and the male-goats, with water and scarlet wool and hyssop, he sprinkled both the book itself and all the people,  
**9:20** saying, ‘This is the blood of the covenant which God commanded toward you°.’ {Exo 24:8}  
**9:21** Now likewise, he also sprinkled the tabernacle and all the vessels of the ministry in the blood.  
**9:22** And according to the law, I may say, almost all things are cleansed in blood and no forgiveness happens without the shedding of blood.  
**9:23** Therefore indeed, <i>it was</i> a necessity that the copies of the things in the heavens are to be cleansed with these, but the heavenly things themselves with better sacrifices than these.  
**9:24** For the Christ did not enter into <i>the</i> holy-places made with hands, counterparts to the true <i>one</i>, but into heaven itself, now to be manifested before the face of God on our behalf.  
**9:25** Nor <i>did he enter</i> in order that he should offer himself often, just-like the high-priest enters into the holy-places every year with another’s blood;  
**9:26** since often it was essential <i>for</i> him to suffer from the conception of the world, but now once for all upon the end of the ages, he has been manifested for the nullifying of sin through the sacrifice of himself.  
**9:27** And inasmuch as it is laid up for men to die once but after this <i>is the</i> judgment;  
**9:28** so the Christ, (having been offered once for all, that he should carry the sins of many), will also be seen a second-time for salvation, separate from sin, to the same <i>who are</i> waiting for him.  

## 10장

**10:1** For the law, having a shadow of the future good things, (not the same image of the matters), they are never able to complete those who come near with the same continual sacrifices which they are offering every year.  
**10:2** Otherwise, <i>why</i> would they not have ceased being offered? Because those who are giving-divine service to <i>God</i>, once having been cleansed, <i>would then</i> have a conscience of sins no more.  
**10:3** But in those <i>sacrifices</i> there is a remembrance of sins every year.  
**10:4** For <i>it is</i> impossible that the blood of oxen and male-goats should take away sins.  
**10:5** Hence <i>while</i> entering into the world, he says, ‘You did not wish for sacrifice and offering, but you, yourself, completed a body for me.  
**10:6** You were not delighted in whole burnt-offerings and <i>sacrifices</i> concerning sin.  
**10:7** Then I said, (as it has been written concerning me in the volume of the book), Behold, I am coming to do your will, O God.’ {Psa 40:6-8}  
**10:8** <i>After</i> saying <i>this</i> above, You did not wish, nor were you delighted in sacrifice and offering and whole burnt-offerings and <i>sacrifices</i> concerning sin, (which are offered according to the law),  
**10:9** then he has said, ‘Behold, I am coming to do your will, O God.’ {Psa 40:6-8} He assassinates {Or: takes away permanently.} the first <i>covenant</i>, in order that he should establish the second <i>covenant</i>,  
**10:10** in which will, we have been made holy through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.  
**10:11** And indeed every priest stands every day ministering and often offering the same sacrifices, which are never able to take away sins. {Exo 29:38}  
**10:12** But having offered one continual sacrifice on behalf of sins, he sat down at <i>the</i> right <i>hand</i> of God;  
**10:13** furthermore waiting until his enemies should be placed <i>under the</i> footstool of his feet.  
**10:14** For he has completed in one continual offering those who are made holy.  
**10:15** And the Holy Spirit also testifies to us; for after he has said beforehand,  
**10:16** ‘This <i>is</i> the covenant that I will covenant for them after those days, says the Lord, I will be giving my laws upon their hearts and I will write them upon their minds.’  
**10:17** And ‘I should never remember their sins and their lawlessness anymore.’ {Jer 31:33-34}  
**10:18** Now where <i>the</i> forgiveness of these <i>is</i>, there is no more an offering concerning sin.  
**10:19** Therefore brethren, having boldness for the entrance <i>into</i> the holy-places by the blood of Jesus,  
**10:20** a way which he inaugurated for us, a fresh and living way, through the curtain, that is, his flesh;  
**10:21** and <i>having</i> a great priest over the house of God;  
**10:22** let us come near with a true heart in full assurance of faith, <i>our</i> hearts having been sprinkled away from an evil conscience and <i>our</i> body, having been fully-washed in clean {Or: cleansing} water.  
**10:23** We should hold-onto the confession of our hope– unwavering; for he who promised <i>is</i> faithful.  
**10:24** And we should consider one another for motivating <i>each other</i> from love and from good works;  
**10:25** not forsaking our own gathering-together, just-as <i>the</i> custom <i>is</i> with some, but encouraging <i>one another</i>, and so-much more, inasmuch as you° are seeing the day drawing near.  
**10:26** For <i>when</i> we sin willfully after the receiving <i>of</i> the full knowledge of the truth, <i>then</i> a sacrifice is left no more <i>for us</i> concerning sins,  
**10:27** but a certain fearful expectancy of judgment and a zeal of fire which is about to eat the adversaries.  
**10:28** Anyone, having rejected <i>the</i> Law of Moses, dies without mercy upon <i>the word of</i> two or three witnesses. {Deu 17:6}  
**10:29** How-much worse punishment, do you° think he will be deemed worthy of, <i>after</i> having trampled under foot the Son of God, and having deemed the blood of the covenant in which he was made holy <i>as</i> common, and having insulted the Spirit of grace?  
**10:30** For we know the one who said, ‘Vengeance <i>is</i> for me. I will be repaying says <i>the</i> Lord.’ And again, ‘<i>The</i> Lord will judge his people.’ {Deu 32:35-36, Psa 135:14}  
**10:31** <i>It is</i> a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God.  
**10:32** But remind° yourselves of the former days, <i>when</i> you° were enlightened, in which you° endured a large conflict of sufferings;  
**10:33** <i>in</i> this thing indeed, makes you° a spectacle both by reproaches and afflictions, but <i>in</i> this thing, you became partners of those who conducted themselves so.  
**10:34** For you° also sympathized together with <i>me</i> in my bonds, and you° accepted the ravening of your° possessions with joy, knowing that you° have for yourselves a better possession in the heavens and an abiding <i>one</i>.  
**10:35** Therefore do° not cast away your° boldness, which has great reward.  
**10:36** For you° have need of endurance, in order that, having done the will of God, you° may get the promise for yourselves.  
**10:37** ‘For still a little <i>while</i>, <i>O</i> how-much? <i>O</i> how-much? He who is coming will be coming and will not delay.  
**10:38** But my righteous one will be living from faith and if he retreats back, my soul does not delight in him.’ {Isa 26:20, Hab. 2:3-4}  
**10:39** But we are not from this retreating back <i>leading</i> to destruction, but <i>we are</i> from <i>the</i> faith <i>leading</i> to the preservation of <i>the</i> soul.  

## 11장

**11:1** Now faith is <i>the</i> essence of <i>things</i> hoped for, a conviction of matters not seen.  
**11:2** For the elders were witnessed in this;  
**11:3** in faith, we perceive that the ages have been framed by the declaration of God, toward the things seen, and did not become from <i>things</i> which appear.  
**11:4** In faith, Abel offered a more <i>excellent</i> sacrifice to God than Cain, through which he was witnessed to be righteous, God testifying on <i>the basis of</i> his gifts and through it, he still speaks, <i>although</i> having died.  
**11:5** In faith, Enoch was transferred, not to see death, and he was not being found, because God transferred him; for it had been testified <i>of him</i> to have been well pleasing to God before his transfer.  
**11:6** But without faith <i>it is</i> impossible to be well pleasing <i>to God</i>; for it is essential <i>for</i> the one who comes near to God to believe that he is and <i>that</i> he becomes a rewarder to those who are seeking him out.  
**11:7** In faith, Noah, having been divinely-warned concerning things not yet seen, <i>and</i> being scared, prepared an ark for <i>the</i> salvation of his house; through which he condemned the world and he became an heir of righteousness according to faith.  
**11:8** In faith, Abraham obeyed, being called to go out to the place which he was about to receive for an inheritance, and he went out, not knowing where he was going.  
**11:9** In faith, he sojourned in <i>the</i> land of promise, as an alien, having dwelt in tents with Isaac and Jacob, the joint heirs of the same promise.  
**11:10** For he was waiting for the city which has the foundations, whose craftsman and contractor is God.  
**11:11** In faith, even Sarah herself received power for conception of seed and bore <i>a son</i> contrary to <i>the</i> time of <i>her fertile</i> age, since she deemed him faithful who promised <i>it</i>.  
**11:12** And hence these were fathered from one <i>sexually</i> dead <i>man</i>, just-as the stars of heaven in a multitude and <i>as</i> innumerable as the sand beside the sea shore<i>.</i> {Gen 4:4, 5:24, 6:8, 14, 12:1-2, 5, 27:11-12, 14, Gen 18:1-33, 22:17}  
**11:13** These all died according to faith, not having received the promises. But having seen <i>them</i> and having greeted them from farther out and having confessed that they are strangers and travelers upon the earth.  
**11:14** For those who are saying such things are disclosing that they are seeking after a fatherland.  
**11:15** And if they were indeed remembering that <i>region</i> from which they went out, they would have had an opportunity to revisit.  
**11:16** But now they are aspiring for themselves to a better <i>region</i>, this thing <i>is</i> a heavenly one. Hence God is not ashamed of them, to be called their God; for he prepared a city for them.  
**11:17** In faith, Abraham, being tested, has offered up Isaac. And he who accepted the promises was offering up his only begotten <i>son</i>;  
**11:18** to whom it was spoken, Your seed will be called in Isaac.  
**11:19** He reasoned that God <i>is</i> able to raise <i>him up</i> even from the dead; from where he also got him back in a figure.  
**11:20** In faith, Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau, concerning future things.  
**11:21** In faith, Jacob, <i>while</i> dying, blessed each of the sons of Joseph, and worshiped, <i>resting</i> upon the tip of his staff.  
**11:22** In faith, Joseph, <i>while</i> dying, remembered <i>things</i> concerning the exodus of the sons of Israel, and commanded <i>them</i> concerning his bones. {Gen 21:1, 12, 50:24}  
**11:23** In faith, Moses, having been born, was hid three months by the fathers, because they saw he was a handsome child, and they were not afraid of the command of the king.  
**11:24** In faith, Moses became great, and denied to be called the son of Pharaoh’s daughter;  
**11:25** having selected for himself to be mistreated together with the people of God rather than to have temporary enjoyment of sin,  
**11:26** having deemed the reproach of the Christ greater riches <i>than</i> the treasures of Egypt; for he was looking toward the reward.  
**11:27** In faith, he left Egypt, not fearing the fury of the king; for he persevered, as seeing the invisible <i>one</i>.  
**11:28** In faith, he has made the Passover and the splashing of the blood <i>on the door frames</i>, in order that the destroyer of the firstborn should not touch them.  
**11:29** In faith, they crossed over the Red Sea as <i>if</i> through dry <i>land</i>; from which the Egyptians having taken an attempt <i>at it</i>, were swallowed up. {Exo 2:2, 11, 15, 12:11, 18, 14:22}  
**11:30** In faith, the walls of Jericho fell <i>down</i>, having been surrounded for seven days.  
**11:31** In faith, Rahab the prostitute did not perish together with those who were disobedient, having accepted the spies with peace. {Jos 6:17-23, Jos 2:1}  
**11:32** And what more should I say? For the time will fail me if I describe <i>the things</i> concerning Gideon, Barak, and both Samson and Jephthah; both David and Samuel, and the prophets:  
**11:33** who through faith conquered kingdoms, worked righteousness, obtained promises, sealed the mouths of lions,  
**11:34** quenched the power of fire, fled away from the edge of the sword, were empowered away from weakness, became mighty in war, the encampments of aliens bowed <i>down</i>.  
**11:35** Women received <i>back their</i> dead from a resurrection. But others were tortured, having not accepted <i>their</i> redemption; in order that they might obtain a better resurrection,  
**11:36** and others received a trial from mocking and scourges, but still more, from bonds and prison:  
**11:37** they were stoned; they were sawed in <i>two</i>; they were tempted; they died in murder from the sword; they went around in sheepskins, <i>and</i> in goat hides; <i>they were</i> lacking, being afflicted, <i>and</i> being mistreated,  
**11:38** (of whom the world was not worthy), wandering in <i>the</i> wildernesses and mountains and caves and the holes of the earth.  
**11:39** And all these, having been testified of through the faith, {Or: witnessed to through their faith.} did not get the promise,  
**11:40** God having foreseen something better concerning us, in order that they should not be completed separate from us. {Jud 6:4, 11, 15, 1Sa 7:1-17, 2Sa 2:1-32, 2Sa 8:1-18, Jud 14:1-20, Dan 6:1-28}  

## 12장

**12:1** Therefore, <i>since</i> we also have so-great a cloud of witnesses encompassing us, let us run through endurance the contest laying before us, placing away from ourselves every interference, and the  easily restricting sin.  
**12:2** Looking away from <i>things, look</i> toward Jesus, the author and completer of the faith, who endured the cross having despised shame, in exchange-for the joy laying before him, and sat down at the right <i>hand</i> of the throne of God.  
**12:3** For, study° the one who has endured such dispute by sinners to himself, in order that you° might not be weary, fainting in your° souls.  
**12:4** You° have not yet stood up as far as <i>sacrificing</i> blood toward <i>your</i> struggling against sin.  
**12:5** And you° have forgotten the encouragement which reasons with you° as with sons, ‘My son, do not regard-lightly the disciplining of the Lord, nor be fainting <i>when</i> you are reproved by him.  
**12:6** For whom the Lord loves, he disciplines, and scourges every son whom he is accepting.’ {Prov. 3:11-12, Job 5:17}  
**12:7** <i>It is</i> for disciplining <i>that</i> you° endure; God is dealing with you° as with sons; for what is <i>the</i> son whom <i>his</i> father is not disciplining?  
**12:8** But if you° are without discipline, of which all have become partakers, consequently, you° are illegitimate and not sons.  
**12:9** Thereafter, we indeed had the fathers of our flesh <i>as</i> correctors <i>of us</i>, and we were revering <i>them</i>. Will we not much rather be made subject to the Father of spirits and we will live?  
**12:10** For they indeed were disciplining <i>us</i> for a few days according to what <i>did</i> seem <i>right</i> to them, but <i>he does it</i> upon what is advantageous for <i>us</i>, that we may receive of his holiness.  
**12:11** Now indeed all disciplining does not seem to be <i>a thing</i> of joy for the present, but of sorrow; now later it gives peaceful fruit of righteousness to those who have been exercised because of it.  
**12:12** Hence straighten° the drooping hands and the paralyzed knees;  
**12:13** and make° straightened tracks for your° feet, in order that what is lame may not be turned aside, but rather should be healed.  
**12:14** Pursue° peace with all and the holiness without which no one will be seeing the Lord:  
**12:15** exercising the oversight <i>of yourselves</i> lest <i>there is</i> anyone who comes-short, away from the grace of God; lest any root of bitterness springing upward, might trouble you°, and many may be defiled through this;  
**12:16** lest <i>there is</i> any fornicator or profane <i>person</i>, like Esau, who gave <i>away</i> his birthright in exchange-for one dinner. {Gen 25:31}  
**12:17** For you° know thereafter that even wishing to inherit the bounty, he was rejected; for he found no chance for repentance although having sought it out with tears.  
**12:18** For you° have not come to a mountain being touched and having been burned with fire and to blackness and to darkness and to whirlwind,  
**12:19** and to the noise of a trumpet and to the voice of declarations; which <i>voice</i> those who heard renounced, <i>asking the</i> word not to be added to them.  
**12:20** For they were not carrying <i>out</i> what was ordered, ‘Even if a beast might touch the mountain, it will be stoned.’ {Exo 19:12-13, 16, Deu 4:11}  
**12:21** And so fearful was the manifestation, <i>that</i> Moses said, ‘I am fearful and trembling. {Deu 9:19}  
**12:22** But you° have come to Mount Zion and to <i>the</i> city of the living God, to the heavenly Jerusalem, and to tens of thousands of messengers,  
**12:23** to the festal-gathering and to the congregation of the firstborn <i>ones</i>, who have been registered in the heavens and to God, <i>the</i> judge of all, and to the spirits of <i>the</i> righteous who have been completed,  
**12:24** and to Jesus the intermediary of a new covenant, and to the blood of sprinkling which speaks better than <i>that of</i> Abel.  
**12:25** Beware° <i>that</i> you° do not renounce the one who speaks. For if those <i>men</i> did not flee, having renounced the one who divinely-warned <i>them</i> upon the earth, much more we <i>may not flee</i> who are turning away from him, <i>who is</i> from the heavens;  
**12:26** whose voice then shook the earth. But now he has promised, saying, ‘Still once for all, I do not only shake the earth, but also the heaven.’ {Hag. 2:6}  
**12:27** And the <i>saying</i>, ‘still once for all,’ indicates the transfer of those things which are shaken, as of things which have been made, in order that those things which are not shaken may remain.  
**12:28** Hence, receiving an unshakable kingdom, let us have gratitude, through which we give-divine service <i>in ways</i> well pleasing to God, with reverence and piety;  
**12:29** for our God is also a consuming fire. {Deu 4:24}<br>{Footnotes: Heb 12:1, or: popular sin; what stands around us well.}
