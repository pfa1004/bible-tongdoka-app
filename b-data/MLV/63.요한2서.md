# 63.요한2서 (MLV)



## 1장

**1:1** The elder to <i>the</i> chosen  lady and her children, whom I love in <i>the</i> truth, and not only I, but also all those who have known the truth;  
**1:2** because of the truth which abides in us and it will be with us forever.  
**1:3** Grace, mercy, peace will be with us, from God the Father and from <i>the</i> Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.  
**1:4** I rejoice exceedingly that I have found <i>some</i> out of your children walking in truth, just-as we received commandment from the Father.  
**1:5** And now I ask you  lady, not as <i>though</i> I write to you a new commandment, but what we <i>have</i> had from <i>the</i> beginning, in order that we should love one another.  
**1:6** And this is love, in order that we should walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, just-as you° <i>have</i> heard from <i>the</i> beginning, in order that you° should walk in it.  
**1:7** Because many misleaders entered into the world, those who are not confessing Jesus Christ <i>as</i> coming in the flesh. This is the misleader and the antichrist.  
**1:8** Beware° <i>in</i> yourselves, in order that you° should not destroy the things which we worked <i>in</i> you°, but <i>in order that</i> we may receive a full reward.  
**1:9** Everyone who transgresses and is not abiding in the teaching of the Christ, does not have God. He who abides in the teaching of Christ, this <i>one</i> has both the Father and the Son.  
**1:10** If anyone is coming to you° and is not bringing this teaching, do° not receive him into your° house and do° not say hello to him;  
**1:11** for he who says hello to him is sharing in his evil works.  
**1:12** Having many things to write to you°, I did not plan <i>to write them</i> through paper and ink, but I am hoping to come to you° and to speak mouth to mouth {i.e., face to face} , in order that our joy may be fulfilled.  
**1:13** The children of your chosen sister greet you. Amen.<br>{Footnotes: 2Jn 1:1,5, or: Kyria; (a proper name). Lady is most likely a salutation like ‘Sir’ or ‘Lady,’ used figuratively for a congregation which fits the plural you° & singular you in verse 12-13.}
