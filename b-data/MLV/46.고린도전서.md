# 46.고린도전서 (MLV)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul, a called apostle of Jesus Christ through the will of God, and Sosthenes the brother,  
**1:2** to the congregation of God which is in Corinth– those who have been made holy in Christ Jesus, called ones, holy-ones, together with all who are calling upon the name of our Lord Jesus Christ in every place, both theirs and ours:  
**1:3** grace to you° and peace from God our Father and Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:4** I am always giving-thanks to my God concerning you°, on <i>the basis of</i> the grace of God which was given to you° in Christ Jesus;  
**1:5** that you° were enriched in him in everything, in all speech and all knowledge;  
**1:6** just-as the testimony of Christ was confirmed in you°;  
**1:7** so-that you° are lacking in no gift; waiting for the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ,  
**1:8** who will also confirm you° until the end, <i>so that</i> you° <i>are</i> irreproachable in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:9** God is faithful, through whom you° were called into the fellowship of his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.  
**1:10** Now brethren, I am pleading with you° through the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, in order that you° all should speak the same thing and there should be no splits among you°, but you° should be framed <i>together</i> in the same mind and in the same viewpoint.  
**1:11** For it has been indicated to me concerning you°, my brethren, by the <i>household</i> of Chloe, that strifes are among you°.  
**1:12** Now I am saying this, that each of you° says, I am indeed of Paul! I <i>am indeed</i> of Apollos! I <i>am indeed</i> of Cephas! I <i>am indeed</i> of Christ!  
**1:13** Has Christ been divided? Paul was not crucified on your° behalf, was he? Or were you° immersed into the name of Paul?  
**1:14** I give-thanks to God that I immersed none of you°, except Crispus and Gaius; {Act 18:8}  
**1:15** lest anyone might say that I immersed <i>you</i> into my own name.  
**1:16** And I also immersed the household of Stephanas; furthermore, I do not know if I immersed any other.  
**1:17** For Christ sent me not to immerse, but to proclaim the good-news; not in wisdom of words, (that the cross of Christ might not be made void).  
**1:18** For the account of the cross is indeed foolishness to those who are perishing, but to us who are saved– it is the power of God.  
**1:19** For it has been written, ‘I will destroy the wisdom of the wise and I will reject the understanding of the intelligent <i>ones</i>.’ {Isa 29:14, Psa 33:10}  
**1:20** Where <i>is the</i> wise? Where <i>is the</i> scribe? Where is the debater of this age? Did God not make the wisdom of the world foolish?  
**1:21** For since, in the wisdom of God the world through its wisdom did not know God, God is delighted through the foolishness of the preaching to save those who believe.  
**1:22** Since both Jews ask for a sign and Greeks seek wisdom;  
**1:23** but we preach Christ <i>as</i> having been crucified, indeed an offense to <i>the</i> Jews, but foolishness to <i>the</i> Greeks;  
**1:24** but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ <i>is the</i> power of God and <i>the</i> wisdom of God.  
**1:25** Because the foolish things of God are wiser than men, and the weakness of God is mightier than men.  
**1:26** For you° see your calling brethren, that not many are wise according to the flesh, not many mighty, not many noble.  
**1:27** But God chose the foolish things of the world, in order that he might shame the wise, and God chose the weak things of the world, in order that he might shame the strong;  
**1:28** and God chose the ignoble things of the world and the things which have been scorned and the things which are not, in order that he might do-away-with the things which are;  
**1:29** that every flesh should not boast in God’s sight.  
**1:30** But you° are from him, in Christ Jesus, who has become to us wisdom and righteousness and holiness and redemption from God;  
**1:31** that, just-as it has been written, ‘He who boasts, let him boast in the Lord.’ {Jer 9:24}  

## 2장

**2:1** And brethren, <i>after</i> I came to you° proclaiming the testimony of God to you°, I did not come according to superiority of speech or of wisdom.  
**2:2** For I decided not to know anything among you°, except Jesus Christ and this one having been crucified.  
**2:3** And I happened to <i>be with</i> you° in weakness and in fear, and in much trembling.  
**2:4** And my speech and my preaching was not in persuasive words of human wisdom, but in <i>the</i> demonstration of <i>the</i> Spirit and of power,  
**2:5** in order that your° faith should not be in the wisdom of men, but <i>be</i> in the power of God.  
**2:6** Now we are speaking wisdom among the mature <i>ones</i>; yet a wisdom not of this age, nor of the rulers of this age, who are being done-away-with;  
**2:7** but we are speaking God’s wisdom in a mystery, the <i>wisdom</i> which has been hidden, which God determined beforehand, before the ages, for our glory.  
**2:8** <i>It is this mystery</i> which none of the rulers of this age knew; for if they had known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory;  
**2:9** but as it has been written, ‘Things which <i>the</i> eye did not see and <i>the</i> ear did not hear and <i>things which</i> did not come-up in the heart of man, which God prepared for those who love him.’ {Isa 64:4}  
**2:10** But God revealed <i>them</i> to us through his Spirit; for the Spirit is searching all things, even the depths of God.  
**2:11** For who from men knows the things of a man, except the spirit of the man, which is in him? So also, no one knows the things of God, except the Spirit of God.  
**2:12** But we did not receive the spirit of the world, but the  Spirit <i>who is</i> from God; in order that we might know the things which were granted to us by God.  
**2:13** We also speak these things, not in <i>the</i> taught words from human wisdom, but in <i>the</i> taught words from <i>the</i> Holy Spirit; combining together with spiritual <i>words,</i> spiritual things<i>.</i><br>{Footnotes: 1Co 2:12, Spirit who, or: spirit that; (meaning just a simple contrast like ‘things of the world, things from God.’ All punctuation and verse numbering is man-made.)}  
**2:14** Now <i>the</i> physical man is not accepting the things from the Spirit of God; for they are foolishness to him, and he is not able to know <i>them</i>, because they are spiritually examined.  
**2:15** But the spiritual <i>one</i> is indeed examining all things, but he himself is examined by no one.  
**2:16** For who knew the mind of the Lord, who will be lecturing him? But we have the mind of Christ. {Isa 40:13}  

## 3장

**3:1** And brethren, I was not able to speak to you° as to spiritual <i>people</i>, but as to fleshly <i>ones</i>, as to infants in Christ.  
**3:2** I gave you° milk to drink and not food, for you° were not yet able <i>to eat it.</i> But still you° are not able <i>even</i> now,  
**3:3** for you° are still fleshly. For where <i>there is</i> jealousy and strife and dissensions among you°, are you° not fleshly, and are you° not walking according to <i>the manner of</i> man?  
**3:4** For whenever someone says, I am indeed of Paul, but a different <i>one</i>, I <i>am</i> of Apollos, are you° not fleshly?  
**3:5** Therefore, who is Paul? And who is Apollos? But preferably <i>they are</i> servants through whom you° believed, even as the Lord gave to each.  
**3:6** I planted, Apollos watered, but God was causing the growth.  
**3:7** So-then neither is the one who is planting anything, nor the one who is watering, but God who is causing the growth.  
**3:8** Now the one who is planting and the one who is watering are one, but each one will be receiving his own reward according to his own labor.  
**3:9** For we are God’s fellow workers. You° are God’s cultivation, God’s building.  
**3:10** According to the grace of God which was given to me, I have laid a foundation like a wise expert-builder, and another is building upon <i>it</i>. But let each <i>one</i> beware how he is building upon <i>it</i>.  
**3:11** For no one is able to lay another foundation beside what is laid, which is Jesus Christ.  
**3:12** But if anyone is building on this foundation <i>with</i> gold, silver, precious stones, wood, grass, straw,  
**3:13** the work of each <i>one</i> will become apparent. For the day will indicate it, because it is revealed in fire, and the fire will test the work of each <i>one</i>, of what sort it is.  
**3:14** If anyone’s work remains which he built upon <i>it</i>, he will be receiving a reward.  
**3:15** If the work of anyone will be burned, he will be forfeiting <i>that work</i>, but he himself will be saved, but so as through fire.  
**3:16** Do you° not know that you° are a temple of God and the Spirit of God is dwelling in you°?  
**3:17** If anyone corrupts the temple of God, God will have corruption <i>for</i> this one; for the temple of God is holy, which you° are.  
**3:18** Let no one deceive himself. If anyone seems to be wise in this age among you°, let him become foolish, in order that he may become wise.  
**3:19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. For it has been written, ‘<i>It is</i> he who grabs the wise in their craftiness'; {Job 5:13}  
**3:20** and again, ‘The Lord knows the reasonings of the wise, that they are futile.’ {Psa 94:11}  
**3:21** So-then, let no one boast in men. For all things are yours°,  
**3:22** whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas, or the world or life or death, or things which are present, or future things. All things are yours°,  
**3:23** and you° are Christ’s, and Christ is God’s.  

## 4장

**4:1** So let a man count us as attendants of Christ and stewards of the mysteries of God.  
**4:2** But furthermore, it is required in stewards, in order that someone should be found faithful.  
**4:3** But <i>it is</i> at <i>the</i> least <i>concern</i> to me, in order that I might be judged by you° or by a human <i>judgment</i> day. But I am not <i>even</i> judging myself.  
**4:4** For I have been conscious <i>of</i> nothing in myself, but I have not been made righteous in this thing. But he who is judging me is the Lord.  
**4:5** So-then, do° not judge anything before the time, until the Lord should come, who will both be illuminating the hidden things of darkness and will be manifesting the plans of the hearts, and then the praise from God will happen for each.  
**4:6** Now brethren, I fashioned these things to myself and Apollos because of you°; in order that you° may learn in us not to have a mindset <i>to go</i> beyond what has been written; in order that no one should be arrogant on behalf of one against the other.  
**4:7** For who is making <i>you</i> the judge? But what do you have which you did not receive? But if you also received <i>it</i>, why are you boasting as having not received <i>it</i>?  
**4:8** Already you° are satisfied, already you° were enriched, you° reigned <i>as kings</i> without us, and for-sure, I wish-that you° <i>had</i> reigned, in order that we also might reign together with you°.  
**4:9** For I am thinking that God showed us, the apostles, last of all, as men doomed to death, because we became a spectacle to the world, both to messengers and men.  
**4:10** We <i>are</i> foolish because of Christ, but you° <i>are</i> prudent in Christ. We <i>are</i> weak, but you° <i>are</i> strong. You° <i>are</i> glorious, but we <i>are</i> dishonored.  
**4:11** We are both hungry and thirsty, till the current hour, and are naked and are battered and are homeless;  
**4:12** and we are laboring, working with our own hands. Being reviled, we are speaking well of <i>them</i>; being persecuted, we are tolerating <i>it</i>;  
**4:13** being blasphemed, we are encouraging. We became like the scums of the world, the garbage of all things, <i>even</i> until now.  
**4:14** I am not writing these things <i>to be</i> embarrassing you°, but I am admonishing <i>you</i>° as my beloved children.  
**4:15** For <i>even</i> if you° have ten-thousand guardians in Christ, but you° <i>do</i> not <i>have</i> many fathers, for I fathered you° in Christ Jesus through the good-news.  
**4:16** Therefore I am encouraging you°. Become° imitators of me.  
**4:17** Because of this, I sent Timothy to you°, who is my beloved and faithful child in the Lord, who will be reminding you° of my ways, the <i>ones</i> in Christ, just-as I am teaching everywhere in every congregation.  
**4:18** Now some were being arrogant as <i>though</i> I am not coming to you°.  
**4:19** But I will be coming to you° shortly, if the Lord wills, and I will know, not the speech of those who have been arrogant, but the power.  
**4:20** For the kingdom of God is not in speech, but in power.  
**4:21** What do you° wish? Should I come to you° with a rod, or in love and a spirit of meekness?  

## 5장

**5:1** Fornication is heard literally <i>to be</i> among you° (and such fornication which is not even named among the Gentiles), that someone has his father’s wife.  
**5:2** And you° have been arrogant, and did not rather mourn in order that he who did this work might be taken away out of your° midst.  
**5:3** For indeed, as being absent in the body, but being present in the spirit, I have already judged him thus, who worked out this thing, (as <i>though</i> I am present),  
**5:4** in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. <i>When</i> you° and my spirit were gathered together, together with the power of our Lord Jesus,  
**5:5** to give such a one to the Adversary for the utter-destruction of the flesh, in order that the spirit might be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus.  
**5:6** Your° boasting is not good. Do you° not know that a little leaven leavens the whole batch?  
**5:7** Cleanse° out the old leaven, in order that you° may be a new batch, just-as you° are unleavened. For Christ our Passover was also sacrificed on our behalf.  
**5:8** So-then we should celebrate a festival, not in old leaven, nor in the leaven of malice and wickedness, but in the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.  
**5:9** I wrote to you° in my letter not to socialize together with fornicators;  
**5:10** and certainly not <i>meaning</i> with the fornicators of this world or with the greedy ones or ravening ones or idolaters; otherwise, you° are consequently obligated to go forth out of the world.  
**5:11** But now, I wrote to you° not to socialize together with anyone if he is named a brother, <i>who is</i> either a fornicator or greedy one, or an idolater or a reviler, or a drunkard or a ravening one, not to even eat together with such <i>a person.</i>  
**5:12** For what <i>is it</i> to me to judge those who are outside? Are you° not judging those <i>who are</i> inside?  
**5:13** Now God will be judging those who are outside, and you will be taking away the evil man from <i>among</i> yourselves.  

## 6장

**6:1** Does anyone of you°, having a matter <i>of strife</i> toward the other, dare to have a lawsuit in front of the unrighteous and not in front of the holy-ones?  
**6:2** Do you° not know that the holy-ones will be judging the world? And if the world is judged among you°, are you° unworthy of the least of courts?  
**6:3** Do you° not know that we will be judging <i>the</i> messengers? Consequently, why not <i>judge the things of</i> everyday life?  
**6:4** Therefore, if you° indeed have courts <i>concerning things of</i> everyday life, <i>why</i> are you° seating those who have been scorned in the congregation <i>as judges</i>?  
**6:5** I am saying <i>this</i> to shame you°. So is there not one wise <i>person</i> among you°? Not even one who will be able to judge between his brethren?  
**6:6** But brother is in a lawsuit with brother, and that in front of unbelievers!  
**6:7** Therefore, <i>it is</i> indeed already a literal loss to you°, that you° have lawsuits with yourselves. Why not rather be wronged? Why not rather be defrauded?  
**6:8** But <i>instead</i> you° yourselves do wrong and defraud, and <i>you</i>° <i>do</i> these things <i>to</i> brethren.  
**6:9** Or do you° not know that the unrighteous will not be inheriting the kingdom of God? Do° not be misled. Neither fornicators, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor pedophiles, nor homosexuals,  
**6:10** nor the greedy ones, nor thieves, nor drunkards, nor revilers, nor ravening ones, will be inheriting the kingdom of God.  
**6:11** And some of you° were these, but you° were fully-washed, but you° were made holy, but you° were made righteous in the name of the Lord Jesus and in the Spirit of our God.  
**6:12** All things are legal for me, but not all things are advantageous. All things are legal for me, but I will not be brought-under the authority by anything.  
**6:13** Foods <i>are</i> for the belly and the belly <i>is</i> for foods, but God will be doing-away-with both this and these things. But the body is not for fornication, but for the Lord, and the Lord for the body.  
**6:14** Now God both raised up the Lord and will be raising us up through his power.  
**6:15** Do you° not know that your° bodies are members of Christ? Therefore, <i>after</i> I took the members of the Christ, should I make them members of a prostitute? Let it not happen!  
**6:16** Therefore, do you° not know that he who is joined to a prostitute is one body? For he says, The two will become one flesh. {Gen 2:24}  
**6:17** But he who is joined to the Lord is one spirit.  
**6:18** Flee° fornication. Every sin, whatever a man practices, <i>is</i> outside the body, but he who <i>is</i> fornicating, sins at his own body.  
**6:19** Or do you° not know that your° body is a temple of <i>the</i> Holy Spirit <i>who is</i> in you°, whom you° have from God? And you° are not your° own;  
**6:20** for you° were bought with a price. Glorify° God in your° body and in your° spirit which is God’s!  

## 7장

**7:1** Now concerning the things of which you° wrote to me: <i>it is</i> good for a man not to touch a woman <i>sexually.</i>  
**7:2** But, because of fornications, let each <i>man</i> have his own wife and let each woman have her own husband.  
**7:3** Let the husband give to the wife her owed benevolence and likewise also the wife give to the husband.  
**7:4** The wife does not have authority over her own body, but the husband, and likewise also the husband does not have authority over his own body, but the wife.  
**7:5** Do° not deprive one another, except from agreement for some time, in order that you° may occupy yourselves to fasting and to prayer, and you° may come to the same again, in order that the Adversary may not tempt you° because of your° shortage of self-control.  
**7:6** But I am saying this according to concession, not according to commandment.  
**7:7** For I wish that all men were even as I myself <i>am</i>. But each one has his own gift from God, indeed the one <i>is</i> so and the one <i>is</i> thus.  
**7:8** But I am saying to the unmarried and to the widows, it is good for them if they remain even as I <i>am</i>.  
**7:9** But if they do not exercise self-control, let them marry; for it is better to marry than to be on fire.  
**7:10** But I command to those who have married, (not I, but the Lord): the wife is not to be separated from her husband,  
**7:11** (but even if she is separated, let her remain unmarried, or let her be reconciled to her husband), and that the husband <i>is</i> not to leave his wife.  
**7:12** But to the rest I say, (not the Lord); if some brother has an unbelieving wife and she consents to dwell with him, let him not leave her.  
**7:13** And <i>if</i> a wife who has an unbelieving husband, and he consents to dwell with her, let her not leave him.  
**7:14** For the unbelieving husband has been made holy by the wife and the unbelieving wife has been made holy by the husband. Consequently otherwise, your° children were unclean, but now they are holy.  
**7:15** But if the unbelieving <i>spouse</i> separates, let the one separate; the brother or the sister has not been enslaved in such things. Now God has called us in peace.  
**7:16** For what do you know O wife, if you will be saving your husband? Or what do you know O husband, if you will be saving your wife?  
**7:17** Except, as God divided to each <i>one</i>, as the Lord has called each, so let him walk. And so I myself am commanding in all the congregations.  
**7:18** Was anyone called having been circumcised? Let him not be uncircumcised. Was anyone called in uncircumcision? Let him not be circumcised.  
**7:19** Circumcision is nothing and uncircumcision is nothing, but the keeping of the commandments of God.  
**7:20** Let each <i>one</i> remain in that calling in which he was called.  
**7:21** Were you called being a bondservant? Let it not be a care to you, but if you are also able to become free; use <i>it</i> rather.  
**7:22** For he who was called in the Lord, having been a bondservant, is the Lord’s freedman; likewise, the free one who was called, is Christ’s bondservant.  
**7:23** You° were bought with a price; do° not become bondservants of men.  
**7:24** Brethren, let each <i>one</i> remain with God in this thing in which he was called.  
**7:25** Now concerning virgins I have no commandment from the Lord, but I am giving my viewpoint, as one who has been shown-mercy by the Lord to be faithful.  
**7:26** Therefore I suppose this to be good, because of the present calamity; that <i>it is</i> good for a man to be thus.  
**7:27** Have you been bound to a wife? Do not seek divorce. Have you been loosed from a wife? Do not seek a wife.  
**7:28** But even if you marry, you did not sin, and if the virgin marries, she did not sin. But such will have affliction in the flesh, and I <i>would</i> spare you°.  
**7:29** But I am saying this, brethren, the time is limited, furthermore it is that both those who have wives may be as <i>though</i> they did not have <i>one</i>;  
**7:30** and those who weep, as <i>though</i> not weeping, and those who rejoice, as <i>though</i> not rejoicing, and those who buy, as <i>though</i> not having;  
**7:31** and those who use this world, as <i>though</i> not making full use of <i>it</i>; for the fashion of this world is passing away.  
**7:32** But I wish <i>for</i> you° to be carefree. He who is unmarried is anxious for the things of the Lord, how he will be pleasing the Lord;  
**7:33** but he who <i>has</i> married is anxious for the things of the world, how he may please the wife.  
**7:34** He has been divided. And the unmarried woman, (also the virgin), is anxious for the things of the Lord, in order that she may be holy both in body and in spirit, but she who is married is anxious for the things of the world, how she will be pleasing the husband.  
**7:35** Now, I say this thing is advantageous for yourselves, not in order that I may cast a noose upon you°, but a non-distraction for the decent, and devoted in the Lord.  
**7:36** But if anyone supposes he is behaving improperly over his virgin, if she is beyond her prime and thus it ought to happen, let her do what she wills; she is not sinning; let them marry.  
**7:37** But he who stands, grounded in his heart, not having necessity, but has the authority concerning his own will, and he has decided this in his own heart to keep his own virgin {Or: keep of himself virgin} , he does well.  
**7:38** So-then both he who betroths does well, and he who does not betroth is doing better.  
**7:39** A wife has been bound by law inasmuch time as the husband is living, but if the husband falls-asleep {i.e. dead} , she is free to be married to whom she wills<i>, but</i> only in the Lord.  
**7:40** But she is fortunate if she remains so, according to my viewpoint, and I think that I also have <i>the</i> Spirit of God.  

## 8장

**8:1** Now concerning idol-sacrifices: we know that all have knowledge. Knowledge is arrogant, but love builds up.  
**8:2** But if anyone thinks to have known anything, he has known nothing yet like it is essential to know;  
**8:3** but if anyone loves God, this one has been known by him.  
**8:4** Therefore concerning the food of idol-sacrifices, we know that an idol in the world is nothing and that there is no other God except one.  
**8:5** For if indeed there are <i>so</i> called gods, whether in heaven or upon <i>the</i> earth; just-like there are many <i>such</i> gods and many <i>such</i> lords,  
**8:6** but to us, <i>there is</i> one God, the Father, from whom are all things, and we <i>are</i> for him, and one Lord, Jesus Christ, through whom are all things, and we <i>are</i> through him.  
**8:7** But the knowledge <i>is</i> not in all <i>people</i>, but until now some, with the conscience of the idol, eat <i>it</i> like an idol-sacrifice, and their conscience is defiled, being weak.  
**8:8** But food does not present us to God; for neither if we eat, do we abound? Nor, if we do not eat, are we lacking?  
**8:9** But beware°, lest this liberty of yours° might become a stumbling block to the weak.  
**8:10** For if anyone sees you, (the one who has knowledge), reclining <i>at a table</i> in an idol’s sanctuary, will not his conscience, being weak, be built up to be eating what <i>is</i> the idol-sacrifices?  
**8:11** And the weak brother will be perishing on <i>the basis of</i> your knowledge, <i>but</i> Christ died because of him.  
**8:12** Now thus, <i>by</i> sinning <i>directly</i> at the brethren and beating their weak conscience, you° are sinning <i>directly</i> at Christ.  
**8:13** Hence, if food is offending my brother, I should never eat <i>idol’s</i> meat forever, in order that I may not offend my brother.  

## 9장

**9:1** Am I not an apostle? Am I not free? Have I not seen Jesus Christ our Lord? Are you° not my work in the Lord?  
**9:2** If I am not an apostle to others, but yet I am to you°; for you° are the seal of my apostleship in the Lord.  
**9:3** My defense to those who are examining me is this:  
**9:4** have we no authority to eat and to drink?  
**9:5** Have we no authority to lead around a wife, (a sister <i>in the Lord)</i>, even as the rest of the apostles and the brethren of the Lord and Cephas?  
**9:6** Or <i>is it</i> only Barnabas and I <i>who</i> have no authority not to work?  
**9:7** Does anyone ever make war <i>while supplying</i> his own rations? Who plants a vineyard and does not eat from its fruit? Or who shepherds a flock and does not drink from the milk of the flock?  
**9:8** I am not speaking these things according to man. Or is the law not also saying these things?  
**9:9** For it has been written in the Law of Moses, ‘You will not muzzle a threshing bull.’ {Deu 25:4} God does not care <i>only</i> for the bulls, does he?  
**9:10** Or does he certainly say it because of us? For it was written because of us, that the one who is plowing ought to plow in hope, and the one who is threshing <i>ought to thresh</i> in hope to partake of his hope.  
**9:11** If we sowed spiritual things to you°, is it a great thing if we will be reaping your° fleshly things?  
**9:12** If others are partaking of <i>this</i> authority over you°, <i>do</i> we not more? But we did not use this authority, but we are forbearing all things, in order that we should not give any hindrance to the good-news of the Christ.  
**9:13** Do you° not know that those who work <i>at</i> the sacred things, eat <i>the things</i> from the temple, and those who wait-upon the altar have their part together with the altar?  
**9:14** So, the Lord also commanded that those who are proclaiming the good-news, to live out of the good-news.  
**9:15** But I used none of these things, and I do not write these things in order that it may become so with me; for <i>it would be</i> good for me rather to die, than in order that anyone should make my boasting void.  
**9:16** For if I proclaim the good-news, it is not boasting to me; for necessity is laid upon me, but woe is to me, if I, myself, do not proclaim the good-news.  
**9:17** For if I am doing this voluntarily, I have a reward, but if <i>I do so</i> involuntarily, I have been entrusted <i>with</i> a stewardship.  
**9:18** Therefore what is my reward? In order that, <i>in</i> proclaiming the good-news of the Christ without cost, I might place the good-news <i>before</i> you°, <i>and</i> that I should not make full use of my authority in the good-news.  
**9:19** For <i>although</i>  being free from all<i>,</i> I enslaved myself to all, in order that I might gain <i>all</i> the more.  
**9:20** And I became like a Jew to the Jews, in order that I might gain Jews. <i>I became</i> like <i>those</i> under <i>the</i> law to those <i>who are</i> under <i>the</i> law, in order that I might gain those <i>who are</i> under <i>the</i> law.  
**9:21** <i>I became</i> like <i>the</i> lawless to those <i>who are</i> lawless, (not being lawless to God, but lawful to Christ), in order that I might gain <i>the</i> lawless.  
**9:22** I became like <i>the</i> weak to the weak, in order that I might gain the weak. I have become all things to all <i>people</i>, in order that I may certainly save some.  
**9:23** But I am doing this because of the good-news, in order that I might become a fellow partner of it.<br>{Footnotes: 1Co 9:19, or: being a free man from all, I was a bondservant to all.}  
**9:24** Do you° not know that those who run in <i>the</i> stadium, all indeed run, but <i>only</i> one receives the prize? So run° in order that you° may grab <i>it</i>.  
**9:25** But everyone who struggles <i>in the race</i> exercises self-control in all things. Therefore indeed, those <i>men do it</i> in order that they may receive a corruptible crown, but we <i>receive</i> an incorruptible <i>crown.</i>  
**9:26** Now-then, I run so, as not with uncertainty. I am combating so, as not <i>just</i> whipping <i>the</i> air;  
**9:27** but I am buffeting my body and enslaving <i>it</i>; lest <i>somehow after</i> having preached to others, I myself might become unapproved.  

## 10장

**10:2** and were all immersed into Moses in the cloud and in the sea;  
**10:3** and all ate the same spiritual food;  
**10:4** and all were drinking the same spiritual drink; for they were drinking from a spiritual rock following <i>them</i>, and the rock was the Christ. {Exo 13:21, 14:22, 16:15, 17:6, Num 20:11}  
**10:5** Yet God was not delighted in most of them; for they were overthrown in the wilderness.  
**10:6** Now these things became our examples, that we should not be desirers of evil things, just-as they also lusted.  
**10:7** Do° not become idolaters, just-as some of them <i>were</i>; as it has been written, ‘The people sat down to eat and drink, and stood up to play.’ {Exo 32:6}  
**10:8** Neither should we fornicate, like some of them fornicated, and twenty three thousand fell in one day.  
**10:9** Nor should we test the Christ, just-as some of them also tested <i>him</i>, and perished by the serpents.  
**10:10** Nor murmur°, like some of them also murmured, and perished by the destroyer.  
**10:11** Now all these things <i>are</i> examples which were befalling to those <i>Jews</i>, and they were written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the ages <i>have</i> arrived.  
**10:12** So-then, he who thinks he stands, let him beware, lest he might fall!  
**10:13** No temptation has taken you° except <i>common</i> human <i>temptations</i>, but God is faithful, who will not be permitting you° to be tempted beyond what you° are able to undergo, but together with the temptation, he will also make an outlet.  
**10:14** Hence, my beloved, flee° from idolatry.  
**10:15** I speak as to prudent men. You°, judge° what I say.<br>{Tradition, and some translations, used ‘communion’ for the word ‘fellowship’ in the verses below. The latter is the common translation of the underlying Greek word throughout the rest of the New Covenant.}

**10:17** Because we, who are many, are one bread, one body; for we all are partaking from the one bread.  
**10:18** Beware° <i>of</i> Israel, according to the flesh: are not those who eat the sacrifices partners of the altar?  
**10:19** Therefore what am I saying? That an idol is anything, or that an idol-sacrifice is anything?  
**10:20** But <i>I am saying</i>, that the things which the Gentiles sacrifice, they are sacrificing to demons, and not to God, and I do not wish you° to become partners of demons. {Deu 32:17}  
**10:21** You° are not able to drink <i>the</i> cup of the Lord and <i>the</i> cup of demons. You° are not able to partake of the table of the Lord and of the table of demons.  
**10:22** Or are we provoking the Lord to jealousy? We are not mightier than him, are we?  
**10:23** All things are legal for me, but not all things are advantageous. All things are legal for me, but not all things build up.  
**10:24** Let no one seek his own, but each <i>one seek</i> the <i>good</i> of the other.  
**10:25** Eat° everything which is sold in the meat-markets, judging nothing because of conscience,  
**10:26** for ‘The earth is the Lord’s and its fullness.’ {Psa 24:1}  
**10:27** But if anyone of the unbelieving invites you° <i>to a feast</i> and you° wish to travel; eat° everything being placed beside you°, judging nothing because of conscience.  
**10:28** But if anyone says to you°, This is an idol-sacrifice, do° not eat, because of him who divulged <i>it</i> and <i>because of</i> the<i>ir</i> conscience; for ‘The earth is the Lord’s and its fullness.’ {Psa 24:1}  
**10:29** I say, not your own conscience, but <i>the conscience</i> of the other. For why is my freedom judged by another conscience?  
**10:30** If I partake with gratitude, why am I blasphemed on behalf of what I am giving-thanks?  
**10:31** Therefore, whether you° eat or drink, or if you° are doing anything, do° all to the glory of God.  
**10:32** Do° not become a stumbling block, either to Jews or to Greeks, or to the congregation of God;  
**10:33** just-as I am also pleasing all <i>people</i> in all things, not seeking what is advantageous for myself, but what <i>is advantageous</i> for many, in order that they may be saved.  

## 11장

**11:1** Become° imitators of me, just-as I also am of Christ.  
**11:2** Now I praise you° brethren that you° have been reminded of me in all things and are holding-onto the traditions, just-as I gave them to you°.  
**11:3** But I wish you° to know, that the head of every man is Christ, and the head of the woman is the man, and the head of the Christ is God.  
**11:4** Every man praying or prophesying, having <i>something</i> on his head, shames his head.  
**11:5** But every woman praying or prophesying with the head unveiled shames her head; for it is one and the same thing, <i>as if</i> she had been shaved.  
**11:6** For if a woman is not veiling herself, let her <i>hair</i> also be sheared, but if it is shameful to a woman to be sheared or shaved, let her be veiled.  
**11:7** For a man indeed ought not to have his head veiled, being <i>that he is the</i> image and glory of God, but woman is <i>the</i> glory of the man.  
**11:8** For the man is not from the woman, but the woman <i>is</i> from the man;  
**11:9** for neither was the man created because of the woman, but the woman because of the man.  
**11:10** The woman ought to have <i>a sign of</i> authority upon her head because of this <i>and</i> because of the messengers.  
**11:11** However, neither is the man separate from the woman, nor the woman separate from the man, in the Lord.  
**11:12** For just-like the woman is from the man, so the man <i>is</i> also through the woman, but all things are from God.  
**11:13** Judge° among you° yourselves. Is it suitable that a woman pray to God unveiled?  
**11:14** Or is not even nature itself teaching you°, that, if a man indeed has long-hair, it is a dishonor to him?  
**11:15** But if a woman has long-hair, it is a glory to her, because <i>her</i> hair has been given <i>to her</i> instead of a <i>head</i> dressing.  
**11:16** But if anyone seems to be contentious, we have no such custom, nor the congregations of God.  
**11:17** But <i>in</i> commanding this, I am not praising you°, because you° are not coming together for the best but for the worst.  
**11:18** For indeed, first <i>of all</i>, <i>when</i> you° are coming together in <i>the</i> congregation, I am hearing that splits exist in you°, and I believe some part <i>of it.</i>  
**11:19** For it is essential <i>for</i> sects to be also among you°, in order that those who are the approved <i>ones</i> may become apparent among you°.  
**11:20** Therefore, <i>while</i> coming together in the same <i>place</i>, it is not possible to eat the Lord’s supper;  
**11:21** for while you° ate, each <i>one</i> is taking his own supper before <i>others can</i>, and one is hungry and another is drunken.  
**11:22** For do you° not have houses that you° should eat and drink <i>in</i>? Or do you° despise the congregation of God and are shaming those who have nothing? What should I say to you°? Should I praise you°? I should not praise you° in this.<br>{Mar 14:22-25 & Mat 26:26-29 & Luk 22:19-20, 1Co 11:23-26.}  
**11:23** For I received from the Lord what I also gave to you°, that the Lord Jesus in the night in which he was being given up, took bread;  
**11:24** and having given-thanks, he broke it and said, Take°, eat°. This is my body, which is being broken on your° behalf. Practice° this in my remembrance.  
**11:25** Likewise also the cup, after the supper, saying, This cup is the new covenant in my blood. Practice° this in my remembrance whenever you° drink <i>it</i>.  
**11:26** For whenever you° eat this bread and drink this cup, you° are proclaiming the Lord’s death till the <i>time</i> he should come.  
**11:27** So-then whoever eats this bread or drinks the cup of the Lord, unworthily of the Lord, will be liable to the body and of the blood of the Lord.  
**11:28** But let a man test himself and so let him eat from the bread and let him drink out of the cup.  
**11:29** For he who unworthily eats and drinks, is eating and drinking condemnation to himself, if he is not discerning the body of the Lord.  
**11:30** Because of this, many among you° are weak and sick and a considerable <i>number</i> are falling-asleep {i.e. are dead} .  
**11:31** For if we were discerning ourselves, we would not be judged.  
**11:32** But being judged, we are disciplined by the Lord, in order that we should not be condemned together with the world.  
**11:33** So-then my brethren, <i>after</i> you° come together that you should eat, wait° for one another.  
**11:34** But if anyone is hungry, let him eat in <i>his own</i> house; in order that you° may not come together for condemnation. Now I will be commanding <i>accordingly</i> the rest as I may come.  

## 12장

**12:1** Now brethren, I do not wish you° to be ignorant concerning spiritual <i>gifts</i>.  
**12:2** You° know that when you° were Gentiles, you° were being led away to those voiceless idols, as you° would be led <i>now</i>.  
**12:3** Hence I make known to you°, that no one speaking in <i>the</i> Spirit of God says, Jesus is accursed, and no one is able to say, Jesus is Lord, except in <i>the</i> Holy Spirit.  
**12:4** Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit.  
**12:5** And there are diversities of services and the same Lord.  
**12:6** And there are diversities of workings, but it is the same God, who is working all things in all.  
**12:7** But to each <i>one</i> is given the manifestation of the Spirit for what is advantageous:  
**12:8** for the word of wisdom is given to one through the Spirit, and the word of knowledge to another according to the same Spirit;  
**12:9** and <i>miraculous</i> faith to different <i>one</i> in the same Spirit; and gifts of healing to another in the same Spirit;  
**12:10** and workings of miracles to another; and prophecy to another; and <i>the</i> discernings of spirits to another; varieties of languages to a different <i>one</i>; and the translation of <i>foreign</i> languages to another;  
**12:11** but the one and the same Spirit is working all these things, sectioning off to each <i>one in</i> his own <i>way</i> just-as he wills.  
**12:12** For just-as the body is one and has many members and all the members of the one body, being many, are one body; so also is the Christ.  
**12:13** For also, we were all immersed into one body, in one Spirit, whether Jews or Greeks, whether bondservants or free <i>men</i>, and all were made to drink into one Spirit.  
**12:14** For the body is not one member, but many.  
**12:15** If the foot says, Because I am not the hand, I am not from the body; contrary to this, is it not from the body?  
**12:16** And if the ear says, Because I am not the eye, I am not from the body; contrary to this, is it not from the body?  
**12:17** If the whole body <i>were</i> an eye, where <i>is</i> the hearing? If <i>the</i> whole <i>body were</i> hearing, where <i>is</i> the sense of smell?  
**12:18** But now God himself placed the members in the body, each one of them, just-as he willed.  
**12:19** Now if they were all one member, where <i>is</i> the body?  
**12:20** But now <i>they are</i> indeed many members, but one body.  
**12:21** Now the eye is not able to say to the hand, I have no need of you; or again the head to the feet, I have no need of you°.  
**12:22** But much rather <i>now</i>, those members of the body that seem to be weaker are necessary;  
**12:23** and those things of the body which we think to be <i>more</i> dishonored, we place even-more honor upon these, and our indecent <i>parts</i> have even-more decency;  
**12:24** but our decent <i>parts</i> have no need. But God mingled the body together, having given even-more honor to the ones which are lacking;  
**12:25** in order that no splits should be in the body, but <i>in order that</i> the members should have the same anxiety on behalf of one another.  
**12:26** And if one member is suffering, all the members are suffering together with <i>it</i>. If one member is glorified, all the members are rejoicing together with <i>it</i>.  
**12:27** Now you° are <i>the</i> body of Christ and members individually.  
**12:28** And indeed God placed some in the congregation <i>of believers</i>, first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, thereafter miracles, thereafter gifts of healing, helps, those who pilot <i>others</i>, <i>those with</i> varieties of languages.  
**12:29** All <i>are</i> not apostles, are they? All are not prophets, are they? All are not teachers, are they? All are not <i>workers of</i> miracles, are they?  
**12:30** All do not have gifts of healing, do they? All do not speak with <i>foreign</i> languages, do they? All do not translate, do they?  
**12:31** But be° zealous for the better gifts. And I <i>will</i> show to you° still a surpassingly-better way.  

## 13장

**13:1** If I speak with the languages of men, and <i>even</i> of messengers, but do not have love, I have become <i>like</i> noisy brass or a clanging cymbal.  
**13:2** And if I have <i>the gift of</i> prophecy and <i>if</i> I know all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have all faith, so-then to remove mountains, but <i>if</i> I do not have love, I am nothing.  
**13:3** And if I morsel <i>out</i> {i.e. give away little by little} all my possessed things and if I give my body in order that I would be burned, but <i>if</i> I do not have love, it profits me nothing.  
**13:4** Love has patience. It is kind. Love is not jealous. Love does not brag. It is not arrogant.  
**13:5** It does not behave improperly. It does not seek its own. It does not irritate. It does not reason evil.  
**13:6** It does not rejoice in unrighteousness, but rejoices together with the truth.  
**13:7** It is forbearing all things, is believing all things, is hoping for all things, is enduring all things.  
**13:8** Love never falls short. But if <i>there are</i> prophecies, they will be done-away; if <i>there are foreign</i> languages, they will cease; if <i>there is</i> knowledge, it will be done-away.  
**13:9** But we know partially and we prophesy partially;  
**13:10** but whenever the completed thing comes, then <i>what is</i> partially will be done-away.  
**13:11** When I was an infant, I was speaking like an infant, I had the mindset like <i>that of</i> an infant, I was reasoning like an infant. But when I became a man, I have done-away-with the things of the infant.  
**13:12** For now we see through a mirror, dimly, but then face to face. I know now partially, but then I will fully know just-as I was also fully known.  
**13:13** But these three are remaining now: faith, hope, love, and the greater of these is love.  

## 14장

**14:1** Pursue° love; but <i>yet</i> be° zealous for spiritual <i>gifts</i>, but rather in order that you° may prophesy.  
**14:2** For he who speaks in a <i>foreign</i> language does not speak to men, but to God; for no one hears <i>with understanding</i>, but he speaks mysteries in <i>the</i> spirit.  
**14:3** But he who prophesies speaks to men <i>for</i> building up and encouragement and consolation.  
**14:4** He who speaks in a <i>foreign</i> language builds himself up, but he who prophesies builds up the congregation.  
**14:5** Now I wish <i>for</i> all <i>of</i> you° to speak with <i>foreign</i> languages, but more in order that you° may prophesy, for greater is he who prophesies than he who speaks with <i>foreign</i> languages, unless one translates, in order that the congregation may receive building up.  
**14:6** But brethren, now if I come to you° speaking in <i>foreign</i> languages, what will I profit you°, if I do not speak to you° either in revelation or in knowledge or in prophecy or in teaching?  
**14:7** Even the lifeless things give <i>forth</i> a voice, whether flute or harp, if they do not give a distinction in the sounds, how will it be known what is piped or harped?  
**14:8** For if the trumpet also gives an indistinct voice, who will prepare himself for war?  
**14:9** So you° also, if you° do not give distinct speech through the <i>foreign</i> language, how will it be known what is spoken? For you° will be speaking to air.  
**14:10** If the varieties of <i>foreign</i> voices in the world are so-many, <i>as</i> it may be, and <i>yet</i> none are unintelligible.  
**14:11** Therefore if I do not know the power of the voice, I will be a barbarian to the one who speaks <i>with it,</i> and he who speaks will be a barbarian to me.  
**14:12** So you° also, since you° are zealous ones of <i>the</i> spirits, seek° in order that you° may abound to the building up of the congregation.  
**14:13** Hence let he who speaks in a <i>foreign</i> language pray in order that one may translate.  
**14:14** For if I pray in a <i>foreign</i> language, my spirit prays, but my mind is unfruitful.  
**14:15** Therefore what is it? I will pray with the spirit and I will also pray with the mind. I will sing-praise with the spirit and I will also sing-praise with the mind.  
**14:16** Otherwise if you give-thanks with the spirit, how will he who fills the place of the unskilled say the amen upon your thanksgiving, since he does not know what you are saying?  
**14:17** For you indeed give-thanks well, but the other is not built up.  
**14:18** I give-thanks to my God, I speak with <i>foreign</i> languages more than all of you°.  
**14:19** But in the congregation, I wish to speak five words through my mind in order that I might also instruct others, than ten-thousand words in a <i>foreign</i> language.  
**14:20** Brethren, do° not become children in <i>your</i> mindsets, (but be° infants in malice), but become° mature in <i>your</i> mindsets.  
**14:21** It has been written in the law, ‘I will be speaking to this people in different languages and by other lips, and thus they will not even hear me, says the Lord.’ {Isa 28:11-12}  
**14:22** So-then <i>foreign</i> languages are for a sign, not to those who believe, but to the unbelieving. But prophecy <i>is for a sign</i>, not to the unbelieving, but to those who believe.  
**14:23** Therefore, if the whole congregation comes together in the same <i>place</i>, and <i>if</i> all speak with <i>foreign</i> languages, and <i>if</i> unskilled or unbelieving <i>people</i> should enter, will they not be saying that you° are insane?  
**14:24** But if all prophesy and some unbelieving or unskilled <i>person</i> should enter, he is reproved {Or: convicted} by all. He is judged by all.  
**14:25** And thus, the secret things of his heart become apparent, and so, having fallen down upon his face, he will be worshiping God, reporting that God really is among you°.  
**14:26** Therefore brethren, what is it? Whenever you° come together, each of you° has a psalm, has a teaching, has a revelation, has a <i>foreign</i> language, has a translation. Let all things happen toward <i>your</i> building up.  
**14:27** If anyone speaks in a <i>foreign</i> language, <i>let it be</i> according to two or three <i>at</i> the most and individually, and let one translate.  
**14:28** But if there is no translator, let him be silent in the congregation, and let him speak to himself and to God.  
**14:29** But let the prophets speak <i>by</i> two or three and let the others discern.  
**14:30** But if <i>a prophesy</i> is revealed to another sitting by, let the first be silent.  
**14:31** For all of you° are able to prophesy, one by one, in order that all may learn and all might be encouraged.  
**14:32** The spirits of the prophets are subject to the prophets;  
**14:33** for God is not <i>a God</i> of unrest, but of peace. As in all the congregations of the holy-ones:  
**14:34** let your° women {Or: wives} be silent in the congregations; for it has not been permitted for them to speak, but to be subject, just-as the law also says. {Gen 3:16}  
**14:35** And if they wish to learn anything, let them ask their own husbands in <i>their own</i> house; for it is shameful for women {Or: wives} to speak in a congregation.  
**14:36** What? Was it from you° that the word of God went forth? Or did it arrive only for you°?  
**14:37** If anyone thinks <i>himself</i> to be a prophet or spiritual, let him fully know what I write to you°, that they are the commandments from <i>the</i> Lord.  
**14:38** But if anyone is ignorant, let him be ignorant.  
**14:39** So-then brethren, be° zealous to prophesy and do° not forbid <i>people</i> to speak with <i>foreign</i> languages.  
**14:40** But let all things happen decently and according to <i>some</i> order.

**15:5** And that he was seen by Cephas; thereafter by the twelve;

**15:19** If we are those who have hoped in Christ in this life only, we are the most pitiful of all men.  
**15:20** But now Christ has been raised <i>up</i> from the dead, he became the first-fruit of those who have fallen-asleep.  
**15:21** For since death <i>came</i> through a man, the resurrection of the dead also <i>came</i> through a man.  
**15:22** For just-as all die in Adam, so all will also be given-life in Christ.

**15:38** but God gives it a body just-as he willed and to each seed a body of its own.

**15:44** It is sown a physical body; it is raised up a spiritual body. If there is a physical body, there is also a spiritual body.

**15:48** Such-as <i>is</i> the terrestrial, such also <i>are</i> the terrestrial and such-as <i>is</i> the heavenly, such also <i>are</i> the heavenly.  
**15:49** And just-as we have worn the image of the terrestrial, we should also wear the image of the heavenly.  
**15:50** Now brethren, I say this, that flesh and blood is not able to inherit the kingdom of God; neither is corruption inheriting incorruption. {1Co 15:51-15:58 & Mat 24:27, 24:29-31 & Mar 13:24-37 & Luk 21:25-28 & Acts 1:11 & 1Th 4:13-17 & 2Th 1:7-9 & Rev 1:7 Second Coming.}

**15:53** For it is essential this corruptible <i>body</i> to be clothed with incorruption and this mortal <i>body</i> to be clothed with immortality.  
**15:54** But whenever this corruptible <i>body</i> is clothed with incorruption and <i>whenever</i> this mortal is clothed with immortality, then the word that has been written will become <i>so</i>, Death is swallowed up in victory. {Isa 25:8}  
**15:55** <i>O</i> death, where is your sting? <i>O</i> Hades, where is your victory?  
**15:56** The sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the law;  
**15:57** but gratitude to God, who is giving us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**15:58** So-then my beloved brethren, become° grounded, unmovable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, knowing that your° labor is not empty in the Lord.
