# 53.데살로니가후서 (MLV)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul and Silvanus and Timothy, to the congregation of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:  
**1:2** grace to you° and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:3** Brethren, we always ought to give-thanks God concerning you°, just-as it is worthy, because your° faith is growing substantially, and the love of each one of you° all toward one another is increasing;  
**1:4** so-that we ourselves boast in you° in the congregations of God, on behalf of your° endurance and faith in all your° persecutions, and in the afflictions which you° are tolerating.  
**1:5** <i>This is</i> an example of the righteous judgment of God; that you° may be deemed worthy of the kingdom of God, on behalf of which you° are also suffering;  
**1:6** if indeed <i>it is</i> a righteous thing with God to repay affliction to those who are afflicting you°;  
**1:7** and to you° who are afflicted with us, a rest at the revelation of the Lord Jesus from heaven with <i>the</i> messengers of his power,  
**1:8** giving vengeance in a fire of flame {i.e. in flaming fire} to those who do not know God and to those who are not obeying the good-news of our Lord Jesus Christ,  
**1:9** who will be suffering-punishment, the sentence of everlasting utter-destruction away from the face of the Lord, and away from the glory of his strength,  
**1:10** whenever he comes to be glorified in his holy-ones, and to be marveled <i>at</i> in that day in all those who <i>have</i> believed, (because our testimony to you° was believed). {2Th 1:7-9 & Mat 24:27, 24:29-31 & Mar 13:24-37 & Luk 21:25-28 & Acts 1:11 & 1Th 4:13-17 & 1Co 15:51-15:58 & Rev 1:7 Second Coming.}  
**1:11** For which, we are also always praying concerning you°, in order that our God might deem you° worthy of your° calling, and might fulfill every delight of goodness, and <i>every</i> work of faith in power,  
**1:12** that the name of our Lord Jesus Christ might be glorified in you°, and you° in him, according to the grace of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.  

## 2장

**2:1** Now brethren, we request of you°, on behalf of the presence of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering-together to him,  
**2:2** that you° might not be quickly shaken from your° mind, nor be alarmed, (either through spirit, or through word, or through letter, as <i>if it came</i> through us), as that the day of the Christ is present.  
**2:3** Do not let anyone deceive you° in any manner, because <i>that day will not come,</i> if the apostasy does not come first and the man of sin, the son of destruction, should be revealed.  
**2:4** The one who lays in opposition to <i>us</i> and promotes himself against everyone being called a god or worshiped objects; so-that he sits in the temple of God as a god, showing himself– that he is a god.  
**2:5** Do you° not remember that <i>while</i> still being with you°, I was speaking to you° <i>about</i> these things?  
**2:6** And now you° know what is holding <i>him</i> back, that he may be revealed in his own time.  
**2:7** For the mystery of lawlessness is already working; only <i>there is</i> one who is holding <i>it</i> back now, until he should become from the midst <i>of us</i>.  
**2:8** And then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus will be consuming with the breath of his mouth and will be doing-away-with <i>him</i> by the appearing of his presence;  
**2:9** <i>even he</i>, whose presence is according to <i>the</i> working of the Adversary in every power and in signs and in lying wonders,  
**2:10** and in all deception of unrighteousness in those who are perishing, because they did not accept the love of the truth, that they might be saved.  
**2:11** And because of this, God will be sending them a working of error, that they may believe in the lie;  
**2:12** in order that they all might be judged who did not believe the truth, but were delighted in unrighteousness.  
**2:13** But we ought to give-thanks to God always concerning you° brethren, beloved by <i>the</i> Lord, that God himself selected you° from <i>the</i> beginning to salvation in holiness of <i>the</i> Spirit and in faith of <i>the</i> truth;  
**2:14** to which he called you° through our good-news, for the acquisition of the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**2:15** Therefore, consequently, brethren, stand-firm° and hold-fast° the traditions which you° were taught, whether through speech or through our letter.  
**2:16** Now our Lord Jesus Christ himself and God and Father who loved us and gave <i>us</i> everlasting encouragement and good hope in <i>his</i> grace,  
**2:17** may comfort your° hearts and may establish them in all <i>the</i> word and in good work.  

## 3장

**3:1** Furthermore, brethren, pray° concerning us, in order that the word of the Lord may run <i>onward</i> and may be glorified, just-as <i>it is</i> also with you°;  
**3:2** and in order that we may be rescued from the improper and evil men; for the faith <i>is</i> not of all.  
**3:3** But the Lord is faithful, who will be establishing you° and will be guarding you° from the evil <i>one.</i>  
**3:4** Now we have confidence in the Lord on <i>the basis of</i> you°, that you° are both practicing and will be practicing the things which we command you°.  
**3:5** Now may the Lord steer your° hearts into the love of God and into the endurance of the Christ.  
**3:6** Now brethren, we are commanding you° in <i>the</i> name of our Lord Jesus Christ to withdraw yourselves from every brother who is walking disorderly and not according to the tradition which they received from us.  
**3:7** For you° yourselves know how it is essential <i>for</i> you° to imitate us. Because we did not act-disorderly among you°;  
**3:8** nor did we eat bread freely from anyone, but in labor and hardship, working night and day, in order to not burden anyone of you°;  
**3:9** not because we did not have the authority, but in order that we might give ourselves <i>as</i> an example to you°, that you° might imitate us.  
**3:10** For even when we were with you°, we were commanding you° this, If anyone is not willing to work, neither let him eat.  
**3:11** For we hear <i>there are</i> some who are walking disorderly among you°, working <i>at</i> nothing, but are busybodies.  
**3:12** Now we are commanding to such and are encouraging through our Lord Jesus Christ, in order that they work with quietness and should eat their own bread.  
**3:13** But you° brethren, do° not be depressed <i>in</i> doing good.  
**3:14** And if anyone is not obeying our word through this letter, mark° that man and do° not socialize together with him, in order that he may be embarrassed.  
**3:15** And <i>yet</i> do° not deem <i>him</i> as an enemy, but admonish° <i>him</i> like a brother.  
**3:16** Now the Lord of peace himself may give you° peace always in every manner. The Lord <i>is</i> with all of you°.  
**3:17** The greeting from me, Paul, I thus am writing with my own hand, which is the sign in every letter.  
**3:18** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <i>is</i> with all of you°. Amen.<br>{NOTES: You should read the 'Definitions' Section and other non-bible sections.}
