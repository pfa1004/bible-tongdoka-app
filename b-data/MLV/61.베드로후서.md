# 61.베드로후서 (MLV)



## 1장

**1:1** Simon Peter, a bondservant and apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who were allotted an equally precious faith with us, in the righteousness of our God and Savior Jesus Christ:  
**1:2** may grace and peace be multiplied to you° in the full knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord.  
**1:3** As his divine power has been bestowed to us as a gift, (all things <i>pertaining</i> to life and godliness), through the full knowledge of the one who called us through glory and virtue;  
**1:4** through which he has bestowed to us as a gift, the precious and greatest promises; in order that you° might become partners of the divine nature through these things, having fled away from the corruption in the world among <i>its</i> lust.  
**1:5** Now also <i>for</i> this same thing, besides having brought in all diligence, supply° in your° faith, virtue, and in your° virtue, knowledge  
**1:6** and in your° knowledge, self-control, and in your° self-control, endurance, and in your° endurance, godliness;  
**1:7** and in your° godliness, brotherly-love, and in your° brotherly-love, love.  
**1:8** For <i>if</i> these things are existing and increasing in you°, none <i>will</i> designate you° idle nor unfruitful toward the full knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:9** For the one is blind in whom these things are not present, being short-sighted, having taken <i>on himself the</i> forgetfulness of {i.e. deliberately forgot} the cleansing from his old sins.  
**1:10** Hence brethren, be° more diligent to make your° calling and choice steadfast; for, you° should never trip someday <i>while</i> practicing these things;  
**1:11** for so the entrance into the everlasting kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ will be richly supplied to you°.  
**1:12** Hence, I will habitually not be neglecting to remind you° concerning these things, although you° know them and are established in the present truth.  
**1:13** But I am deeming <i>it</i> righteous, inasmuch as I am in this tabernacle, to awaken you° with a reminder;  
**1:14** knowing that the removal of my tabernacle is <i>coming</i> quickly, just-as our Lord Jesus Christ indicated to me.  
**1:15** But I will also be diligent, each-time, to have you° in remembrance of these things, to practice <i>them</i> after my exodus.  
**1:16** For we did not follow wisely devised fables, <i>when</i> we made known to you° the power and presence of our Lord Jesus Christ, but <i>in that</i>, we became eyewitnesses of that majesty.  
**1:17** For having received honor and glory from God the Father, <i>when</i> such a voice was carried to him by the Majestic Glory, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am delighted.  
**1:18** And we heard this voice, having been carried from heaven, being together with him in the holy mountain.  
**1:19** And we have the steadfast prophetic word, (as to a lamp appearing in a dreary place, until the day should dawn and the  morning-star might rise), to which you° are doing well, <i>and</i> taking-heed in your° hearts.  
**1:20** Knowing this first, that every prophecy of Scripture does not come from <i>one’s</i> own interpretation.  
**1:21** For no prophecy was ever brought <i>about</i> by <i>the</i> will of man, but <i>the</i> holy men of God spoke <i>the word</i> being carried by <i>the</i> Holy Spirit.<br>{Footnotes: 2Pe 1:19, or: Venus; literally: ‘light-bearer’; metaphorically Jesus.}  

## 2장

**2:1** But false prophets happened <i>to be</i> also among the people, as false teachers will also be among you°, who will be smuggling in sects of destruction, denying even the Master who bought them, bringing quick destruction upon themselves.  
**2:2** And many will be following their unbridled-lusts, because of whom the way of the truth will be blasphemed<i>.</i>  
**2:3** And they will be merchants of you° in <i>their</i> greed with fabricated words; to whom the condemnation from long-ago is not idle, and their destruction will not be slumbering.  
**2:4** For if God did not spare messengers who sinned, but threw them into Tartarus {i.e. hell} and gave them to restraints of blackness, being kept for judgment;  
**2:5** and he did not spare the ancient world, but guarded Noah, <i>the</i> eighth <i>person spared</i>, a preacher of righteousness, <i>when</i> he brought a flood upon the world of ungodly <i>people</i>; {Gen 7:23, 8:1-22}  
**2:6** and he condemned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah, with a catastrophe, having incinerated <i>them</i>, having placed them <i>as</i> an example <i>for</i> those who are about to be ungodly; {Gen 19:1-38}  
**2:7** and he rescued righteous Lot, <i>who was</i> being subjugated by the conduct of the immoral in <i>their</i> unbridled-lusts  
**2:8** (for that righteous man, <i>who was</i> dwelling among them, was tormenting <i>his</i> righteous soul by seeing and in hearing <i>their</i> lawless works day by day).  
**2:9** The Lord knows how to rescue the devout out of temptation, and to keep the unrighteous to a day of judgment to be punished;  
**2:10** and especially those who are conducting-themselves after the flesh in the lust of defilement and <i>while</i> despising lordship. Daring <i>and</i> self-willed, they are not trembling <i>when</i> blaspheming <i>heavenly</i> glories;  
**2:11** where messengers, (<i>though</i> being greater in strength and power), do not bring a blasphemous judgment against them beside the Lord.  
**2:12** But these <i>unrighteous men are</i> like natural unreasoning creatures, having been born for apprehension and corruption, blaspheming in what they are ignorant of, and they will be utterly corrupted in their corruption.  
**2:13** They will be getting the wages of unrighteousness; <i>those who are</i> deeming <i>the</i> carousing in <i>the</i> day <i>to be</i> a sensual-delight. <i>They are</i> stains and blemishes, carousing in their <i>own acts of</i> deception, <i>while</i> feasting sumptuously alongside you°,  
**2:14** having eyes full of an adulteress and an unceasing sin; enticing unstable souls; having a heart which has been exercised from greed; children of <i>the</i> curse;  
**2:15** having left <i>the</i> straight way. They were misled, having followed in the way of Balaam the <i>son</i> of Beor, who loved the wages of unrighteousness; {Num 22:5}  
**2:16** but he had <i>his</i> reproof from his own violation of the law: a voiceless donkey, having spoken in a human voice, forbade the insanity of the prophet.  
**2:17** These <i>people</i> are waterless springs and clouds driven by a gale <i>of wind</i> to whom the blackness of darkness has been kept forever.  
**2:18** For <i>while</i> speaking flattering-words of futility, they entice (with the lusts of the flesh to unbridled-lusts) those who really <i>had</i> fled away from those who conduct themselves in error.  
**2:19** <i>While</i> promising them freedom, they themselves are bondservants of corruption. For in what anyone has been succumbed, in this, he has also been enslaved.  
**2:20** For if, having fled away from the defilements of the world in the full knowledge of the Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, and having been again entangled, they are succumbed in these things, the last <i>state</i> has become worse to them <i>than</i> the first.  
**2:21** For it was better for them <i>to</i> have not fully known the way of righteousness, than to turn away from the holy commandment which was given to them <i>after</i> having fully known <i>it</i>.  
**2:22** But it has befallen to them <i>as</i> the <i>way</i> of the true proverb, ‘<i>The</i> dog returned to its own vomit,’ and the sow that <i>had</i> fully-washed <i>to</i> a wallowing in <i>the</i> mud. {Prov. 26:11}  

## 3장

**3:1** Beloved, this is already the second letter that I am writing among you°, and I am awakening your° sincere mind in both of them in a reminder;  
**3:2** to be reminded of the declarations which have been spoken before by the holy prophets, and the commandments from your° apostles of the Lord and Savior.  
**3:3** Knowing this first, that mockers will be coming in the last of the days, conducting-themselves according to their own lusts,  
**3:4** and saying, Where is the promise of his presence? For from which <i>time</i> the fathers fell-asleep, thus all things remain as they were from <i>the</i> beginning of the creation.  
**3:5** For this is eluding them, (willing <i>it to be so</i>): that there were heavens from long-ago, and an earth established {Or: standing together} out of water and through water by the word of God;  
**3:6** through which <i>water</i>, the world <i>that was</i> then was destroyed, having been flooded in water. {Gen 7:21}  
**3:7** But the current heavens and the earth have been stored up for fire by the same word, being kept to <i>the</i> day of judgment and destruction of ungodly men.  
**3:8** But beloved, let this one thing not elude you°, that one day with the Lord is like a thousand years, and a thousand years like one day.  
**3:9** The Lord is not slow <i>concerning</i> his promise, as some deem slowness, but has patience toward us, not planning <i>for</i> any to perish, but <i>for</i> all to make room <i>in your° hearts</i> for repentance.  
**3:10** But the day of the Lord will be coming like a thief in the night; in which the heavens will be passing away with a crashing-sound, and the elements will be dissolved, being burnt with heat, and the earth and the works <i>which are</i> in it will be burned up.  
**3:11** Therefore, <i>with</i> all these things being dissolved, <i>see</i> what sort of <i>things</i> it is essential <i>for you°</i> to possess in <i>all</i> holy conduct and godliness,  
**3:12** expecting, and diligently wanting the presence of the day of God, through which, <i>the</i> heavens will be dissolved, being on fire, and the elements <i>will</i> melt, being burnt with heat.  
**3:13** But according to his promise, we are expecting new heavens and a new earth, in which dwells righteousness.  
**3:14** Hence, beloved, <i>while</i> expecting these things, be° diligent that you° may be found in peace, unstained and unblemished in his sight.  
**3:15** And deem° the patience of our Lord <i>to be</i> salvation; just-as our beloved brother Paul also wrote to you° according to the wisdom which was given to him;  
**3:16** as also in all <i>his</i> letters, speaking in them concerning these things; in which some things are hard to perceive, which the unlearned and unstable ones are twisting, as <i>they</i> also <i>twist</i> the rest of the Scriptures to their own destruction.  
**3:17** Therefore beloved, knowing beforehand <i>these things</i>, guard° yourselves, in order that you° might not fall from your° own firmness, having been led away in the error of the immoral.  
**3:18** But grow° in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. Glory <i>is</i> to him, both now and toward the <i>final</i> day of <i>this</i> world. Amen.<br>{NOTES: You should read the 'Definitions' Section and other non-bible sections.}
