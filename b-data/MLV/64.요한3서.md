# 64.요한3서 (MLV)



## 1장

**1:1** The elder to beloved Gaius, whom I love in truth.  
**1:2** Beloved <i>Gaius</i>, I am praying <i>for</i> you to be prospered and to be sound {Or: healthy} concerning all things, just-as your soul is prospering.  
**1:3** For I rejoiced exceedingly of <i>the</i> brethren coming and testifying of <i>your</i> truth, just-as you are walking in truth.  
**1:4** I have no greater joy from these things, in order that I may hear my children <i>are</i> walking in the truth.  
**1:5** Beloved, you practice faithfully whatever you have worked toward the brethren and toward strangers;  
**1:6** who testified to your° love in the sight of <i>the</i> congregation; whom you will do well, having sent onward worthily of God.  
**1:7** For they went forth on behalf of the Name, taking nothing from the Gentiles.  
**1:8** Therefore we ought to receive those <i>who are</i> such, in order that we should become fellow workers for the truth.  
**1:9** I wrote to the congregation, but Diotrephes, who loves to be their foremost <i>one</i>, is not accepting us.  
**1:10** Because of this, if I come, I will be reminding <i>him of</i> his works <i>in</i> which he practices: gossiping against us with evil words and not being content in these, nor does he himself accept the brethren and he forbids those who plan <i>to come</i>, and casts <i>them</i> out of the congregation.  
**1:11** Beloved, do not imitate the evil <i>one</i>, but the good <i>one</i>. He who practices good is from God. He who practices evil has not seen God.  
**1:12** <i>As</i> for Demetrius, he has been testified of by all and by the truth itself. But we also are testifying and you° know that our testimony is true.  
**1:13** I had many things to write <i>to you</i>, but I do not wish to write <i>them</i> to you through ink and reed.  
**1:14** But I hope to see you immediately and we will be speaking mouth to mouth {i.e., face to face} . Peace to you°. The friends greet you°. Greet the friends according to name.<br>{NOTES: You should read the 'Definitions' Section and other non-bible sections.}
