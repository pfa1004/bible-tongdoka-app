# 60.베드로전서 (MLV)



## 1장

**1:1** Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to <i>the</i> chosen who are travelers from <i>the</i> dispersion from Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia and Bithyna,  
**1:2** according to <i>the</i> foreknowledge of God <i>the</i> Father, in <i>the</i> holiness of <i>the</i> Spirit, to obedience and sprinkling of <i>the</i> blood of Jesus Christ: grace to you° and may peace be multiplied.  
**1:3** The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ <i>is</i> gracious, who according to his much mercy has born us again into a living hope through <i>the</i> resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,  
**1:4** into an incorruptible, undefiled and unfading inheritance having been guarded in the heavens for you°;  
**1:5** who are guarded through faith in <i>the</i> power of God, into a salvation ready to be revealed in <i>the</i> last time.  
**1:6** In which you° are glad now, if it is essential <i>for</i> a few <i>moments</i>, you° were made sorrowful in various tests,  
**1:7** in order that the trial of your° faith, (<i>being</i> much <i>more</i> precious <i>than</i> gold, which perishes), <i>even</i> now being tested through fire, may be found for praise and honor and to glory in <i>the</i> revelation of Jesus Christ.  
**1:8** You° love whom you° have not seen {Or: known} ; now you° believe in whom you° do not see; <i>in both</i> you° are glad with unspeakable and glorified joy;  
**1:9** getting for yourselves the end of your° faith, <i>that is</i> the salvation of your° souls.  
**1:10** Concerning which salvation the prophets, who prophesied concerning the favor that <i>would come</i> to you°, sought out and utterly searched out;  
**1:11** searching what <i>time</i> or what <i>sort of</i> time <i>the</i> Spirit of Christ in them was indicating, <i>while</i> testifying beforehand <i>of</i> the sufferings for Christ and the glories <i>following</i> after these things.  
**1:12** To whom it was revealed, that they were serving these things not to themselves, but to you°; which things now were reported to you° through those who proclaimed the good-news to you° in the Holy Spirit, <i>after</i> he was sent forth from heaven; into which things messengers desire to peer <i>into</i>.  
**1:13** Hence <i>when</i> you° girded up the loins of your° mind, <i>while</i> being sober, hope° completely upon the grace being brought to you° at the revelation of Jesus Christ;  
**1:14** like children of obedience not fashioning yourselves in your° former lusts in <i>the time of</i> your° ignorance;  
**1:15** but according to he who called you°, <i>who is</i> holy, you° yourselves also become° holy in all your° conduct;  
**1:16** because it has been written, ‘Become° holy, because I am holy.’ {Lev 11:44-45, 19:2, 20:7,26}  
**1:17** And if you° call upon the Father, who impartially judges according to the work of each <i>one</i>, conduct° yourselves in fear <i>during</i> the time of your° foreign residency <i>here</i>;  
**1:18** knowing that you° were redeemed from your° futile conduct handed down from <i>your°</i> forefathers not with corruptible things <i>like</i> with silver or with gold.  
**1:19** But like <i>that</i> of an unblemished and unstained lamb, <i>you were redeemed</i> by <i>the</i> precious blood of Christ,  
**1:20** who indeed, having been known beforehand, before the conception of the world, but was manifested in <i>the</i> last times because of you°,  
**1:21** who believe through him in God. <i>The God</i> who raised him <i>up</i> from the dead and gave him glory; so-that your° faith and hope might be in God.  
**1:22** Having purified your° souls in the obedience to the truth through <i>the</i> Spirit toward non-pretended brotherly-love, intensely love° one another from a clean heart;  
**1:23** having been born again, not out of corruptible seed, but from <i>the</i> incorruptible, through <i>the</i> word of God, which is living and remaining forever.  
**1:24** Because, ‘All flesh <i>is</i> like grass and all <i>the</i> glory of man <i>is</i> like the flower of grass. The grass dried up and its flower fell away.  
**1:25** But the declaration of the Lord is abiding forever.’ {Isa 40:6-9} And this is the declaration of the good-news which was proclaimed to you°.  

## 2장

**2:1** Therefore having placed away from yourselves all malice and all treachery, and hypocrisies and envies, and all slanders,  
**2:2** as newborn babies, long-for° the logical milk <i>which has</i> no treachery, in order that you° may grow in it;  
**2:3** if indeed, you° tasted <i>it</i>, that the Lord is kind;  
**2:4** coming to whom <i>is</i> a living stone, having indeed been rejected by men, but chosen <i>and</i> precious with God.  
**2:5** You°, yourselves, like living stones, are also built up <i>as</i> a spiritual house, a holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices, acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.  
**2:6** Because it is contained in the Scripture, ‘Behold, I place a stone in Zion, a cornerstone, chosen <i>and</i> precious, and the one who believes on him should never be ashamed.’ {Isa 28:16}  
**2:7** Therefore, to those who believe <i>is</i> the honor, but to <i>the</i> disobedient, ‘<i>The</i> stone which the builders rejected, this became to <i>them the</i> head of <i>the</i> corner'; {Psa 118:22}  
**2:8** and, ‘A stone of stumbling and a rock of offense'; {Isa 8:14} <i>to</i> those who are stumbling in the word, being disobedient, toward which they were also appointed.  
**2:9** But you° <i>are</i> a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for <i>God’s</i> ownership, that you° may proclaim the virtues of the one who called you° out of darkness into his marvelous light;  
**2:10** who previously <i>were</i> not a people, but <i>now are</i> the people of God; who had not been shown-mercy, but now were shown-mercy. {Hos 1:10, 2:23}  
**2:11** Beloved, I am encouraging you°, as foreigners and travelers, to abstain from fleshly lusts, which are warring against the soul;  
**2:12** having your° good conduct <i>seen</i> among the Gentiles; in order that, in what they are speaking against you°, as evildoers, they may glorify God in the day of visitation, having eye-witnessed <i>things</i> from <i>your°</i> good works.  
**2:13** Therefore be° subject to every human creation because of the Lord: whether to the king, as being superior to you°;  
**2:14** or to governors, as they are sent through him for vengeance <i>on</i> evildoers and praise <i>to the</i> doers of good.  
**2:15** Because so is the will of God, doing good <i>things</i> to hush the ignorance of foolish men;  
**2:16** as free and <i>yet</i> not having <i>your°</i> freedom as a cover-up of malice, but as bondservants of God.  
**2:17** Honor° all <i>men</i>. Love° the brotherhood. Fear° God. Honor° the king.  
**2:18** Domestic <i>servants</i>, be subject to <i>your°</i> masters in all fear; not only to the good and gentle, but also to the crooked.  
**2:19** For this is grace, if anyone undergoes sorrows, suffering wrongfully because of <i>his</i> consciousness of God.  
**2:20** For what credit <i>is it</i>, if, <i>when</i> you° are sinning and are battered <i>for it</i>, you° will be enduring <i>it</i>? But if, <i>when</i> you° are doing good and are suffering <i>for it</i>, you° will be enduring <i>it</i>; this <i>is</i> grace with God.  
**2:21** For you° were called into this, because Christ also suffered on our behalf, leaving a model for you°, in order that you° should follow after his footprints.  
**2:22** <i>He</i> who did no sin, nor was treachery found in his mouth;  
**2:23** who, being reviled, was not reviling again <i>in return</i>; <i>when</i> suffering, was not threatening, (but was giving <i>himself</i> to the one who judges righteously),  
**2:24** who, himself, carried our sins in his body upon the tree, in order that we might live to righteousness, having become-dead to <i>our</i> sins; by whose wound<i>s</i> you° were healed.  
**2:25** For you° were being misled like sheep, but now were returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your° souls. {Isa 53}  

## 3장

**3:1** Likewise, wives be subject to your° own husbands; in order that, even if some are disobedient to the word, they might be gained without a word through the conduct of their wives;  
**3:2** having eye-witnessed your° pure conduct in fear;  
**3:3** whose adornment, do not let it be the outward <i>adornment</i> of braiding <i>the</i> hair, and of placing on of gold things or of clothing of <i>expensive</i> garments;  
**3:4** but <i>let it be</i> the hidden human <i>things</i> of the heart, in the incorruptible <i>adornment</i> of a meek and quiet spirit, which is greatly-valued in God’s sight.  
**3:5** For so, the holy women, those also hoping upon God, were previously adorning themselves, being subject to their own husbands;  
**3:6** as Sarah obeyed Abraham, calling him lord; whose children you° became, doing good and not fearing any terror. {Gen 18:12-15}  
**3:7** Likewise the husbands, dwell together with your° <i>wives</i> according to <i>the</i> knowledge, bestowing honor to the wife, as the weaker vessel, as also being joint heirs of <i>the</i> grace of life; that your° prayers may not be hindered.  
**3:8** Now <i>to</i> the end, all <i>be</i> like-minded, sympathetic, loving the brethren, affectionate, friendly;  
**3:9** not repaying evil in exchange-for evil or reviling in exchange-for reviling, but instead, speaking well of <i>others</i>; knowing that you° were called for this, in order that you° should inherit a blessing.  
**3:10** For he who wills to love life and to see good days, let his tongue cease from <i>speaking</i> evil, and <i>also</i> his lips not to speak treachery.  
**3:11** Let him shun away from evil and let him practice good. Let him seek peace and let him pursue it,  
**3:12** because <i>the</i> eyes of the Lord <i>are</i> upon the righteous and his ears <i>are</i> toward their supplication. But the face of the Lord <i>is</i> against those who are practicing evil things. {Psa 34:12-16}  
**3:13** And who <i>is</i> he who will be mistreating you°, if you° become imitators of the good <i>one</i>?  
**3:14** But even if you° should suffer because of righteousness<i>,</i> you° <i>are</i> fortunate. But do° not fear <i>because of</i> their fear, nor should you° be disturbed;  
**3:15** but make° the Lord God holy in your° hearts, and <i>be</i> ready habitually with a defense to everyone who is asking you° for a reason concerning the hope <i>which is</i> in you°, with meekness and fear;  
**3:16** having a good conscience; in order that, in what they are speaking against you° as evildoers, they may be ashamed who are railing at your° good conduct in Christ.  
**3:17** For <i>it is</i> better to suffer doing good than <i>for</i> doing evil, (if the will of God wills <i>it</i>).  
**3:18** Because Christ also suffered once for all on behalf of <i>our</i> sins, the righteous on behalf of the unrighteous, in order that he might lead you° to God; having indeed been slain in the flesh, but was given-life in <i>the</i> spirit;  
**3:19** in which, he also traveled and preached to the spirits in prison {Eph 2:17} ,  
**3:20** <i>while</i> they were previously disobedient, when the patience of God was waiting in <i>the</i> days of Noah, <i>while</i> the ark <i>was</i> being prepared, in which a few, that is, eight souls, were saved through water;  
**3:21** which <i>is our</i> counterpart, immersion, saves us also now, not the removal of <i>the</i> filth of the flesh, but the demand of a good conscience toward God, through the resurrection of Jesus Christ;  
**3:22** who is at <i>the</i> right <i>hand</i> of God, having traveled into heaven; <i>with</i> messengers and authorities and powers having been made subject to him. {Gen 6:3, 12}  

## 4장

**4:1** Therefore, Christ having suffered on our behalf <i>who are</i> in <i>the</i> flesh, (you° also weaponize° yourselves with the same mind), because he who suffered in <i>the</i> flesh has ceased from sin;  
**4:2** that you should no longer have <i>your</i> life, (the rest of your° time in the flesh), in the lusts of men, but in the will of God.  
**4:3** For enough time which has passed away from <i>our</i> life is for us to have worked out the will of the Gentiles, and having conducted-ourselves in: unbridled-lusts, lusts, drunkenness, revelings, drinking-bouts and illegal {immoral} idolatries;  
**4:4** in which they are surprised <i>by</i> you°, <i>that you</i>° <i>are</i> not running together with <i>them</i> into the same outpouring of riotousness, blaspheming;  
**4:5** who will be giving account to the one holding ready to judge <i>the</i> living and <i>the</i> dead.  
**4:6** For the good-news was proclaimed even to the dead toward this <i>end</i>, in order that they might be judged indeed according to men in <i>the</i> flesh, but may live according to God in <i>the</i> Spirit.  
**4:7** But the end of all things has drawn near. Therefore, be° sensible and be° sober for prayers.  
**4:8** But before all things, having the intense love for yourselves, because love will hide a multitude of sins.  
**4:9** <i>Be</i> hospitable to one another without murmurings;  
**4:10** just-as each received a gift, serving it for yourselves, as good stewards of <i>the</i> various favor<i>s</i> of God.  
**4:11** If anyone is speaking, <i>speak</i> as <i>the</i> oracles of God; if anyone is serving, <i>serve</i> as from the strength which God is supplying; in order that God may be glorified in all things through Jesus Christ to whom is the glory and the dominion forevermore. Amen.  
**4:12** Beloved, do° not be surprised at the fiery ordeal among you°, happening to you° for a test, as <i>though</i> a strange thing is befalling to you°.  
**4:13** But accordingly you° are sharing in the sufferings of the Christ <i>with us</i>, rejoice°, in order that you° may also rejoice in the revelation of his glory, being glad.  
**4:14** If you° are reproached in <i>the</i> name of Christ<i>,</i> you° <i>are</i> fortunate, because the Spirit of glory and of God rests upon you°. He is indeed blasphemed according to their <i>manner</i>, but he is glorified according to your <i>manner</i>.  
**4:15** For let not anyone of you° suffer like a murderer, or a thief, or an evildoer, or like a meddler in the affairs of others.  
**4:16** But if <i>anyone suffers</i> as a Christian, let him not be ashamed, but let him glorify God in this respect.  
**4:17** Because the time <i>for</i> the judgment <i>is</i> to begin from the house of God, and if <i>it begins</i> first from us, what will be the end of those who are disobedient to the good-news of God?  
**4:18** And if the righteous one is scarcely saved, where will the ungodly and <i>the</i> sinner appear?  
**4:19** So-then those who are also suffering according to the will of God in good-doing, let them consign themselves, their souls, as to a faithful Creator.  

## 5장

**5:1** I am encouraging <i>the</i> elders the ones among you°, (the fellow elder and witness of the sufferings of the Christ, also the partner of the glory which is about to be revealed).  
**5:2** Shepherd° the flock of God among you°, exercising the oversight, not by compulsion, but willfully, nor covetously, but eagerly;  
**5:3** nor like <i>one</i> lording it over <i>his</i> inheritances {Or: lots} , but <i>by</i> becoming examples to the flock.  
**5:4** And <i>after</i> the Chief-Shepherd has appeared, you° will be getting the unfading crown of glory.  
**5:5** Likewise, <i>the</i> younger <i>ones</i>, be° subject to the elder <i>ones</i>. But all <i>of</i> you° be subject to one another, clothe° yourselves with humility, because ‘God resists <i>the</i> haughty, but is giving grace to <i>the</i> humble.’ {Prov. 3:34}  
**5:6** Therefore humble° yourselves under the powerful hand of God, in order that he may exalt you° in time;  
**5:7** having tossed forth all your° anxiety upon him, because there is care concerning you° with him.  
**5:8** Be° sober! Watch°! Your° opponent, <i>the</i> devil, like a roaring lion, is walking around seeking whom he may swallow,  
**5:9** to whom, stand° against, solid in the faith, knowing that the same sufferings are to be completed in <i>the</i> world by your° brotherhood.  
**5:10** But the God of all grace, who called you° to his everlasting glory in Christ Jesus, <i>after</i> you° <i>have</i> suffered a small <i>time</i>, he himself may frame you° <i>up</i>, will establish, will strengthen <i>and</i> will found <i>you</i>°. {I.e. make a foundation of you.}  
**5:11** The glory and the dominion <i>is</i> to him, forevermore. Amen.  
**5:12** Through Silvanus, our faithful brother, as I count <i>him</i>, I wrote to you° a few <i>words</i>, encouraging and testifying this to be <i>the</i> true grace of God in which you° are standing.  
**5:13** She <i>who is</i> in Babylon, chosen together with you°, and my son Mark greets you°.
