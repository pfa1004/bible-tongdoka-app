# 65.유다서 (MLV)



## 1장

**1:1** {64 AD. Paul’s release from prison and visitation to various congregations. July 64 AD. The Great Fire at Rome, Christians accused of setting it.}<br>Jude, a bondservant of Jesus Christ and brother of James, to the called <i>ones</i>, those who have been made holy in God the Father and have been kept in Jesus Christ:  
**1:2** mercy to you°, and may peace and love be multiplied.  
**1:3** Beloved, making all diligence to write to you° concerning our common salvation, I had a necessity to write to you° encouraging you° to fight earnestly for the faith which was given once for all to the holy-ones.  
**1:4** For some men sneaked in <i>among you</i>°, those who have been written <i>about, from</i> long-ago for this condemnation <i>of the</i> ungodly, those who are transferring the grace of our God into unbridled-lusts and denying <i>our</i> only Master, God <i>the Father</i>, and our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:5** But I plan to remind you°, <i>though</i> you° knowing this once for all, that the Lord, having saved people out of the land of Egypt, destroyed those who did not believe <i>the</i> second-time. {Num 14:32}  
**1:6** And the messengers who did not keep <i>to</i> their own principality, but having left their own house, he has kept to the judgment of <i>the</i> great day in eternal bonds under blackness.  
**1:7** As Sodom and Gomorrah and the cities around them in a similar manner with these, having indulged in fornication and having gone after different flesh, are laying before <i>us</i> as an example, being held under the sentence of everlasting fire. {Gen 19:1-38}  
**1:8** Nevertheless, these also dreaming likewise, indeed are defiling the flesh and rejecting lordship and blaspheming <i>the heavenly</i> glories<i>.</i>  
**1:9** But Michael the archangel, when arguing with the devil, <i>after</i> he was reasoning concerning the body of Moses, he did not dare to bring a judgment of blasphemy against him, but said, The Lord rebuke you. {Deu 34: 5,6}  
**1:10** But these <i>men</i>, they indeed blaspheme as many things as they do not know. But as many things as they know naturally, like the unreasoning creatures <i>that they are</i>, they corrupt themselves in those things.  
**1:11** Woe to them! Because they traveled in the way of Cain and poured themselves into the error of Balaam’s reward and perished in the dispute of Korah. {Gen 4:8; Num 22:1-41; Num 16:1-50}  
**1:12** These <i>people</i> are hidden-reefs in your° love-feasts, sumptuously feasting fearlessly alongside you°, shepherding themselves, <i>they are</i> waterless clouds, being carried away by <i>the</i> winds; unfruitful autumn trees, twice dead, having been uprooted,  
**1:13** wild waves of the sea, foaming out their own shames, misleading stars– for whom the blackness of darkness has been kept forever.  
**1:14** Now Enoch, seventh from Adam, also prophesied <i>about</i> these <i>people</i>, saying, Behold, the Lord  came in <i>among</i> tens of thousands <i>of</i> his holy-ones,  
**1:15** to make a judgment against all, and to convict all the ungodly of them, concerning all their works of ungodliness of which they did <i>in</i> ungodly <i>ways</i> and concerning all the harsh <i>things</i> which ungodly sinners spoke against him.  
**1:16** These <i>men</i> are murmurers, complainers, conducting-themselves according to their lusts and their mouth is speaking flattering-words, admiring faces because of profit.  
**1:17** But you°, beloved, remember° for yourselves the declarations which have been spoken before by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ;  
**1:18** that they were saying to you° that in the last time there will be mockers, conducting-themselves according to their own lusts of ungodlinesses.  
**1:19** These are the ones who are causing-splits, <i>who are</i> physical, not having <i>the</i> Spirit.  
**1:20** But you°, beloved, <i>keep</i> building yourselves up in your° <i>most</i> holy faith, praying in <i>the</i> Holy Spirit.  
**1:21** Keep° yourselves in God’s love, waiting for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ for everlasting life.  
**1:22** And show-mercy° to some, making a distinction;  
**1:23** but save° others in fear, seizing them out of <i>the</i> fire, hating even the tunic which has been stained from the flesh.  
**1:24** Now to the one who is able to guard us from falling and to stand <i>us</i> in the sight of his glory, unblemished in gladness,  
**1:25** to the only wise God our Savior, <i>is the</i> glory and majesty, dominion and authority, both now and all <i>of</i> forever. Amen.<br>{Footnotes: Jude 1:14, or: with tens of thousands of his holy-ones; or: with his holy tens of thousands.}<br>{NOTES: You should read the 'Definitions' Section and other non-bible sections.}
