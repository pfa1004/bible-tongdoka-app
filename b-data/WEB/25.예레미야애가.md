# 25.예레미야애가 (WEB)



## 1장

**1:1** How the city sits solitary,<br>that was full of people!<br>She has become as a widow,<br>who was great among the nations!<br>She who was a princess among the provinces<br>has become tributary!  
**1:2** She weeps bitterly in the night,<br>and her tears are on her cheeks;<br>among all her lovers<br>she has no one to comfort her:<br>All her friends have dealt treacherously with her;<br>they have become her enemies.  
**1:3** Judah has gone into captivity because of affliction,<br>and because of great servitude;<br>she dwells among the nations,<br>she finds no rest:<br>all her persecutors overtook her within the straits.  
**1:4** The ways of Zion mourn,<br>because no one come to the solemn assembly;<br>all her gates are desolate,<br>her priests sigh:<br>her virgins are afflicted,<br>and she herself is in bitterness.  
**1:5** Her adversaries have become the head,<br>her enemies prosper;<br>for Yahweh<sup>①</sup> has afflicted her for the multitude of her transgressions:<br>her young children have gone into captivity before the adversary.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>“Yahweh” is God’s proper Name, sometimes rendered “LORD” (all caps) in other translations.  
**1:6** All majesty has departed from the daughter of Zion:<br>her princes have become like deer that find no pasture,<br>they have gone without strength before the pursuer.  
**1:7** Jerusalem remembers in the days of her affliction and of her miseries<br>all her pleasant things that were from the days of old:<br>when her people fell into the hand of the adversary,<br>and no one helped her,<br>The adversaries saw her,<br>they mocked at her desolations.  
**1:8** Jerusalem has grievously sinned;<br>therefore she has become as an unclean thing;<br>all who honored her despise her,<br>because they have seen her nakedness:<br>yes, she sighs, and turns backward.  
**1:9** Her filthiness was in her skirts;<br>she didn’t remember her latter end;<br>therefore she has come down astoundingly;<br>she has no comforter:<br>“See, Yahweh, my affliction;<br>for the enemy has magnified himself.”  
**1:10** The adversary has spread out his hand on all her pleasant things:<br>for she has seen that the nations are entered into her sanctuary,<br>concerning whom you commanded that they should not enter into your assembly.  
**1:11** All her people sigh, they seek bread;<br>they have given their pleasant things for food to refresh the soul:<br>“Look, Yahweh, and see;<br>for I have become abject.”  
**1:12** “Is it nothing to you, all you who pass by?<br>Look, and see if there is any sorrow like my sorrow,<br>which is brought on me,<br>with which Yahweh has afflicted me in the day of his fierce anger.  
**1:13** “From on high has he sent fire into my bones,<br>and it prevails against them;<br>He has spread a net for my feet.<br>He has turned me back.<br>He has made me desolate and faint all day long.  
**1:14** “The yoke of my transgressions is bound by his hand;<br>They are knit together.<br>They have come up on my neck.<br>He has made my strength to fail.<br>The Lord<sup>①</sup> has delivered me into their hands,<br>against whom I am not able to stand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The word translated “Lord” is “Adonai.”  
**1:15** “The Lord has set at nothing all my mighty men within me.<br>He has called a solemn assembly against me to crush my young men.<br>The Lord has trodden as in a wine press the virgin daughter of Judah.  
**1:16** “For these things I weep.<br>My eye, my eye runs down with water,<br>because the comforter who should refresh my soul is far from me.<br>My children are desolate,<br>because the enemy has prevailed.”  
**1:17** Zion spreads out her hands.<br>There is no one to comfort her;<br>Yahweh has commanded concerning Jacob,<br>that those who are around him should be his adversaries.<br>Jerusalem is among them as an unclean thing.  
**1:18** “Yahweh is righteous;<br>for I have rebelled against his commandment.<br>Please hear all you peoples,<br>and see my sorrow.<br>My virgins and my young men have gone into captivity.  
**1:19** “I called for my lovers,<br>but they deceived me.<br>My priests and my elders gave up the spirit in the city,<br>while they sought food for themselves to refresh their souls.  
**1:20** “See, Yahweh; for I am in distress.<br>My heart is troubled.<br>My heart is turned within me,<br>for I have grievously rebelled.<br>Abroad the sword bereaves.<br>At home it is like death.  
**1:21** “They have heard that I sigh.<br>There is no one to comfort me.<br>All my enemies have heard of my trouble.<br>They are glad that you have done it:<br>You will bring the day that you have proclaimed,<br>and they will be like me.  
**1:22** “Let all their wickedness come before you;<br>Do to them as you have done to me for all my transgressions.<br>For my sighs are many,<br>and my heart is faint.  

## 2장

**2:1** How has the Lord covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud in his anger!<br>He has cast down from heaven to the earth the beauty of Israel,<br>And hasn’t remembered his footstool in the day of his anger.  
**2:2** The Lord has swallowed up all the habitations of Jacob,<br>and has not pitied:<br>He has thrown down in his wrath the strongholds of the daughter of Judah;<br>He has brought them down to the ground;<br>he has profaned the kingdom and its princes.  
**2:3** He has cut off in fierce anger all the horn of Israel;<br>He has drawn back his right hand from before the enemy:<br>He has burned up Jacob like a flaming fire,<br>which devours all around.  
**2:4** He has bent his bow like an enemy,<br>he has stood with his right hand as an adversary,<br>Has killed all that were pleasant to the eye:<br>In the tent of the daughter of Zion he has poured out his wrath like fire.  
**2:5** The Lord has become as an enemy,<br>he has swallowed up Israel;<br>He has swallowed up all her palaces,<br>he has destroyed his strongholds;<br>He has multiplied in the daughter of Judah mourning and lamentation.  
**2:6** He has violently taken away his tabernacle,<br>as if it were of a garden;<br>he has destroyed his place of assembly:<br>Yahweh has caused solemn assembly and Sabbath to be forgotten in Zion,<br>Has despised in the indignation of his anger the king and the priest.  
**2:7** The Lord has cast off his altar,<br>he has abhorred his sanctuary;<br>He has given up into the hand of the enemy the walls of her palaces:<br>They have made a noise in Yahweh’s house,<br>as in the day of a solemn assembly.  
**2:8** Yahweh has purposed to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion;<br>He has stretched out the line,<br>he has not withdrawn his hand from destroying;<br>He has made the rampart and wall to lament;<br>they languish together.  
**2:9** Her gates are sunk into the ground;<br>he has destroyed and broken her bars:<br>Her king and her princes are among the nations where the law is not;<br>Yes, her prophets find no vision from Yahweh.  
**2:10** The elders of the daughter of Zion sit on the ground,<br>they keep silence;<br>They have cast up dust on their heads;<br>they have clothed themselves with sackcloth:<br>The virgins of Jerusalem hang down their heads to the ground.  
**2:11** My eyes fail with tears,<br>my heart is troubled;<br>My liver is poured on the earth,<br>because of the destruction of the daughter of my people,<br>Because the young children and the infants swoon in the streets of the city.  
**2:12** They ask their mothers,<br>“Where is grain and wine?”<br>when they swoon as the wounded in the streets of the city,<br>when their soul is poured out into their mothers’ bosom.  
**2:13** What shall I testify to you?<br>what shall I liken to you, daughter of Jerusalem?<br>What shall I compare to you,<br>that I may comfort you, virgin daughter of Zion?<br>For your breach is great like the sea:<br>who can heal you?  
**2:14** Your prophets have seen for you false and foolish visions;<br>They have not uncovered your iniquity,<br>to bring back your captivity,<br>but have seen for you false revelations and causes of banishment.  
**2:15** All that pass by clap their hands at you.<br>They hiss and wag their head at the daughter of Jerusalem, saying,<br>“Is this the city that men called ‘The perfection of beauty,<br>the joy of the whole earth’?”  
**2:16** All your enemies have opened their mouth wide against you.<br>They hiss and gnash the teeth.<br>They say, “We have swallowed her up.<br>Certainly this is the day that we looked for.<br>We have found it.<br>We have seen it.”  
**2:17** Yahweh has done that which he purposed;<br>he has fulfilled his word that he commanded in the days of old;<br>He has thrown down,<br>and has not pitied:<br>He has caused the enemy to rejoice over you;<br>he has exalted the horn of your adversaries.  
**2:18** Their heart cried to the Lord:<br>wall of the daughter of Zion,<br>let tears run down like a river day and night;<br>Give yourself no respite;<br>don’t let the apple of your eye cease.  
**2:19** Arise, cry out in the night,<br>at the beginning of the watches;<br>Pour out your heart like water before the face of the Lord:<br>Lift up your hands toward him for the life of your young children,<br>that faint for hunger at the head of every street.  
**2:20** “Look, Yahweh, and see to whom you have done thus!<br>Shall the women eat their offspring,<br>the children that are dandled in the hands?<br>Shall the priest and the prophet be killed in the sanctuary of the Lord?  
**2:21** “The youth and the old man lie on the ground in the streets.<br>My virgins and my young men have fallen by the sword.<br>You have killed them in the day of your anger.<br>You have slaughtered, and not pitied.  
**2:22** “You have called, as in the day of a solemn assembly, my terrors on every side.<br>There was no one that escaped or remained in the day of Yahweh’s anger.<br>My enemy has consumed those whom I have dandled and brought up.  

## 3장

**3:1** I am the man that has seen affliction<br>by the rod of his wrath.  
**3:2** He has led me and caused me to walk in darkness,<br>and not in light.  
**3:3** Surely against me he turns his hand<br>again and again all day long.  
**3:4** He has made my flesh and my skin old;<br>he has broken my bones.  
**3:5** He has built against me,<br>and surrounded me with gall and travail.  
**3:6** He has made me to dwell in dark places,<br>as those that have been long dead.  
**3:7** He has walled me about, that I can’t go out;<br>he has made my chain heavy.  
**3:8** Yes, when I cry, and call for help,<br>he shuts out my prayer.  
**3:9** He has walled up my ways with cut stone;<br>he has made my paths crooked.  
**3:10** He is to me as a bear lying in wait,<br>as a lion in secret places.  
**3:11** He has turned aside my ways,<br>and pulled me in pieces;<br>he has made me desolate.  
**3:12** He has bent his bow,<br>and set me as a mark for the arrow.  
**3:13** He has caused the shafts of his quiver to enter into my kidneys.  
**3:14** I have become a derision to all my people,<br>and their song all day long.  
**3:15** He has filled me with bitterness.<br>He has sated me with wormwood.  
**3:16** He has also broken my teeth with gravel stones;<br>he has covered me with ashes.  
**3:17** You have removed my soul far off from peace;<br>I forgot prosperity.  
**3:18** I said, “My strength has perished,<br>along with my expectation from Yahweh.”  
**3:19** Remember my affliction and my misery,<br>the wormwood and the gall.  
**3:20** My soul still remembers them,<br>and is bowed down within me.  
**3:21** This I recall to my mind;<br>therefore I have hope.  
**3:22** It is because of Yahweh’s loving kindnesses that we are not consumed,<br>because his compassion doesn’t fail.  
**3:23** They are new every morning;<br>great is your faithfulness.  
**3:24** “Yahweh is my portion,” says my soul.<br>“Therefore I will hope in him.”  
**3:25** Yahweh is good to those who wait for him,<br>to the soul who seeks him.  
**3:26** It is good that a man should hope<br>and quietly wait for the salvation of Yahweh.  
**3:27** It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth.  
**3:28** Let him sit alone and keep silence,<br>because he has laid it on him.  
**3:29** Let him put his mouth in the dust,<br>if so be there may be hope.  
**3:30** Let him give his cheek to him who strikes him;<br>let him be filled full with reproach.  
**3:31** For the Lord will not cast off forever.  
**3:32** For though he cause grief,<br>yet he will have compassion according to the multitude of his loving kindnesses.  
**3:33** For he does not afflict willingly,<br>nor grieve the children of men.  
**3:34** To crush under foot all the prisoners of the earth,  
**3:35** To turn aside the right of a man before the face of the Most High,  
**3:36** To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord doesn’t approve.  
**3:37** Who is he who says, and it comes to pass,<br>when the Lord doesn’t command it?  
**3:38** Doesn’t evil and good come out of the mouth of the Most High?  
**3:39** Why does a living man complain,<br>a man for the punishment of his sins?  
**3:40** Let us search and try our ways,<br>and turn again to Yahweh.  
**3:41** Let us lift up our heart with our hands to God<sup>①</sup> in the heavens.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Hebrew word rendered “God” is “אֱלֹהִ֑ים” (Elohim).  
**3:42** “We have transgressed and have rebelled.<br>You have not pardoned.  
**3:43** “You have covered us with anger and pursued us.<br>You have killed.<br>You have not pitied.  
**3:44** You have covered yourself with a cloud,<br>so that no prayer can pass through.  
**3:45** You have made us an off-scouring and refuse<br>in the middle of the peoples.  
**3:46** “All our enemies have opened their mouth wide against us.  
**3:47** Terror and the pit have come on us,<br>devastation and destruction.”  
**3:48** My eye runs down with streams of water,<br>for the destruction of the daughter of my people.  
**3:49** My eye pours down,<br>and doesn’t cease,<br>without any intermission,  
**3:50** until Yahweh looks down,<br>and sees from heaven.  
**3:51** My eye affects my soul,<br>because of all the daughters of my city.  
**3:52** They have chased me relentlessly like a bird,<br>those who are my enemies without cause.  
**3:53** They have cut off my life in the dungeon,<br>and have cast a stone on me.  
**3:54** Waters flowed over my head;<br>I said, I am cut off.  
**3:55** I called on your name, Yahweh,<br>out of the lowest dungeon.  
**3:56** You heard my voice:<br>“Don’t hide your ear from my sighing,<br>and my cry.”  
**3:57** You came near in the day that I called on you;<br>you said, “Don’t be afraid.”  
**3:58** Lord, you have pleaded the causes of my soul;<br>you have redeemed my life.  
**3:59** Yahweh, you have seen my wrong.<br>Judge my cause.  
**3:60** You have seen all their vengeance<br>and all their devices against me.  
**3:61** You have heard their reproach, Yahweh,<br>and all their devices against me,  
**3:62** The lips of those that rose up against me,<br>and their device against me all day long.  
**3:63** You see their sitting down and their rising up;<br>I am their song.  
**3:64** You will render to them a recompense, Yahweh,<br>according to the work of their hands.  
**3:65** You will give them hardness of heart,<br>your curse to them.  
**3:66** You will pursue them in anger,<br>and destroy them from under the heavens of Yahweh.  

## 4장

**4:1** How the gold has become dim!<br>The most pure gold has changed!<br>The stones of the sanctuary are poured out<br>at the head of every street.  
**4:2** The precious sons of Zion,<br>comparable to fine gold,<br>How are they esteemed as earthen pitchers,<br>the work of the hands of the potter!  
**4:3** Even the jackals draw out the breast,<br>they nurse their young ones:<br>But the daughter of my people has become cruel,<br>like the ostriches in the wilderness.  
**4:4** The tongue of the nursing child clings to the roof of his mouth for thirst:<br>The young children ask bread,<br>and no man breaks it to them.  
**4:5** Those who fed delicately are desolate in the streets:<br>Those who were brought up in scarlet embrace dunghills.  
**4:6** For the iniquity of the daughter of my people is greater than the sin of Sodom,<br>That was overthrown as in a moment,<br>and no hands were laid on her.  
**4:7** Her nobles were purer than snow,<br>they were whiter than milk;<br>They were more ruddy in body than rubies,<br>their polishing was as of sapphire.  
**4:8** Their appearance is blacker than a coal;<br>they are not known in the streets:<br>Their skin clings to their bones;<br>it is withered,<br>it has become like a stick.  
**4:9** Those who are killed with the sword are better than those who are killed with hunger;<br>For these pine away, stricken through,<br>for want of the fruits of the field.  
**4:10** The hands of the pitiful women have boiled their own children;<br>They were their food in the destruction of the daughter of my people.  
**4:11** Yahweh has accomplished his wrath,<br>he has poured out his fierce anger;<br>He has kindled a fire in Zion,<br>which has devoured its foundations.  
**4:12** The kings of the earth didn’t believe,<br>neither did all the inhabitants of the world,<br>That the adversary and the enemy would enter into the gates of Jerusalem.  
**4:13** It is because of the sins of her prophets,<br>and the iniquities of her priests,<br>That have shed the blood of the just in the middle of her.  
**4:14** They wander as blind men in the streets.<br>They are polluted with blood,<br>So that men can’t touch their garments.  
**4:15** “Go away!” they cried to them,<br>“Unclean! Go away! Go away! Don’t touch!<br>When they fled away and wandered, men said among the nations,<br>“They can’t live here any more.”  
**4:16** Yahweh’s anger has scattered them;<br>he will no more regard them:<br>They didn’t respect the persons of the priests,<br>they didn’t favor the elders.  
**4:17** Our eyes still fail,<br>looking in vain for our help:<br>In our watching we have watched for a nation that could not save.  
**4:18** They hunt our steps,<br>so that we can’t go in our streets:<br>Our end is near,<br>our days are fulfilled;<br>for our end has come.  
**4:19** Our pursuers were swifter than the eagles of the sky:<br>They chased us on the mountains,<br>they laid wait for us in the wilderness.  
**4:20** The breath of our nostrils,<br>the anointed of Yahweh,<br>was taken in their pits;<br>Of whom we said,<br>Under his shadow we shall live among the nations.  
**4:21** Rejoice and be glad, daughter of Edom,<br>that dwell in the land of Uz:<br>The cup shall pass through to you also;<br>you shall be drunken,<br>and shall make yourself naked.  
**4:22** The punishment of your iniquity is accomplished, daughter of Zion;<br>he will no more carry you away into captivity:<br>He will visit your iniquity, daughter of Edom;<br>he will uncover your sins.  

## 5장

**5:1** Remember, Yahweh, what has come on us:<br>Look, and see our reproach.  
**5:2** Our inheritance is turned to strangers,<br>Our houses to aliens.  
**5:3** We are orphans and fatherless;<br>Our mothers are as widows.  
**5:4** We have drunken our water for money;<br>Our wood is sold to us.  
**5:5** Our pursuers are on our necks:<br>We are weary, and have no rest.  
**5:6** We have given the hand to the Egyptians,<br>To the Assyrians, to be satisfied with bread.  
**5:7** Our fathers sinned, and are no more;<br>We have borne their iniquities.  
**5:8** Servants rule over us:<br>There is no one to deliver us out of their hand.  
**5:9** We get our bread at the peril of our lives,<br>Because of the sword of the wilderness.  
**5:10** Our skin is black like an oven,<br>Because of the burning heat of famine.  
**5:11** They ravished the women in Zion,<br>The virgins in the cities of Judah.  
**5:12** Princes were hanged up by their hand:<br>The faces of elders were not honored.  
**5:13** The young men bare the mill;<br>The children stumbled under the wood.  
**5:14** The elders have ceased from the gate,<br>The young men from their music.  
**5:15** The joy of our heart is ceased;<br>Our dance is turned into mourning.  
**5:16** The crown is fallen from our head:<br>Woe to us! for we have sinned.  
**5:17** For this our heart is faint;<br>For these things our eyes are dim;  
**5:18** For the mountain of Zion, which is desolate:<br>The foxes walk on it.  
**5:19** You, Yahweh, remain forever;<br>Your throne is from generation to generation.  
**5:20** Why do you forget us forever,<br>And forsake us so long time?  
**5:21** Turn us to yourself, Yahweh, and we shall be turned.<br>Renew our days as of old.  
**5:22** But you have utterly rejected us;<br>You are very angry against us.
