# 24.예레미야 (MEV)



## 1장

**1:1** The Commission of Jeremiah<br>The words of Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah, of the priests who were in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin,  
**1:2** to whom the word of the Lord came in the days of Josiah the son of Amon, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.  
**1:3** It came also in the days of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, to the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah the son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the captivity of Jerusalem in the fifth month.  
**1:4** Now the word of the Lord came to me, saying,  
**1:5** “Before I formed you in the womb I knew you;<br>and before you were born I sanctified you,<br>and I ordained you a prophet to the nations.”  
**1:6** Then I said, “Ah, Lord God! Truly, I cannot speak, for I am a youth.”  
**1:7** But the Lord said to me, “Do not say, ‘I am a youth.’ For you shall go everywhere that I send you, and whatever I command you, you shall speak.  
**1:8** Do not be afraid of their faces. For I am with you to deliver you,” says the Lord.  
**1:9** Then the Lord put forth His hand and touched my mouth. And the Lord said to me, “Now, I have put My words in your mouth.  
**1:10** See, I have this day set you over the nations and over the kingdoms, to root out and to pull down, to destroy and to throw down, to build and to plant.”  
**1:11** The Almond Rod and Boiling Pot<br>Moreover the word of the Lord came to me, saying, “Jeremiah, what do you see?”<br>And I said, “I see a branch of an almond tree.”  
**1:12** Then the Lord said to me, “You have seen well. For I will hasten My word to perform it.”  
**1:13** The word of the Lord came to me the second time, saying, “What do you see?”<br>And I said, “I see a boiling pot, and it is facing away from the north.”  
**1:14** Then the Lord said to me: Out of the north calamity will break forth on all the inhabitants of the land.  
**1:15** For I will call all the families of the kingdoms of the north, says the Lord.<br>And they will come and they will set each one his throne<br>at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem,<br>and against all the walls all around,<br>and against all the cities of Judah.  
**1:16** I will utter My judgments against them<br>concerning all their wickedness, whereby they have forsaken Me<br>and have burned incense to other gods,<br>and have worshipped the works of their own hands.  
**1:17** You therefore gird up your loins and arise, and speak to them all that I command you. Do not be dismayed at their faces, lest I confound you before them.  
**1:18** For indeed, I have made you this day a fortified city and an iron pillar and bronze walls against the whole land, against the kings of Judah, against the officials, against the priests, and against the people of the land.  
**1:19** They will fight against you, but they will not prevail against you, for I am with you, says the Lord, to deliver you.  

## 2장

**2:1** God Pleads With Israel to Repent<br>Moreover the word of the Lord came to me, saying,  
**2:2** Go and proclaim in the ears of Jerusalem, saying,<br>Thus says the Lord:<br>I remember you, the kindness of your youth,<br>the love of your espousals,<br>when you followed Me in the wilderness,<br>in a land that was not sown.  
**2:3** Israel is holy to the Lord,<br>and the first fruits of His harvest.<br>All who eat of it will become guilty;<br>disaster will come upon them,<br>says the Lord.  
**2:4** Hear the word of the Lord, O house of Jacob<br>and all the families of the house of Israel.  
**2:5** Thus says the Lord:<br>What iniquity have your fathers found in Me,<br>that they have gone far from Me,<br>and have walked after vanity<br>and have become vain?  
**2:6** They did not say, “Where is the Lord<br>who brought us up out of the land of Egypt,<br>who led us through the wilderness,<br>through a land of deserts and of pits,<br>through a land of drought and of the shadow of death,<br>through a land that no man passed through and where no man lived?”  
**2:7** And I brought you into a plentiful country<br>to eat its fruit and its goodness.<br>But when you entered, you defiled My land<br>and made My heritage an abomination.  
**2:8** The priests did not say,<br>“Where is the Lord?”<br>And those who handle the law did not know Me;<br>the shepherds also transgressed against Me,<br>and the prophets prophesied by Baal<br>and walked after things that do not profit.  
**2:9** Therefore I will yet contend with you,<br>says the Lord,<br>and with your sons’ sons I will contend.  
**2:10** For pass over the coastlands of Cyprus and see,<br>and send to Kedar and consider diligently,<br>and see if there is such a thing.  
**2:11** Has a nation changed their gods,<br>though they are not gods?<br>But My people have changed their glory<br>for that which does not profit.  
**2:12** Be astonished at this, O heavens,<br>and be horribly afraid, be very desolate,<br>says the Lord.  
**2:13** For My people have committed two evils.<br>They have forsaken Me,<br>the fountain of living waters,<br>and hewed out for themselves cisterns,<br>broken cisterns, that can hold no water.  
**2:14** Is Israel a servant? Is he a homeborn slave?<br>Why has he been plundered?  
**2:15** The young lions roared at him<br>and roared loudly,<br>and they made his land a waste;<br>his cities have been destroyed, without inhabitant.  
**2:16** Also the men of Memphis and Tahpanhes<br>have shaved the crown of your head.  
**2:17** Have you not brought this on yourself,<br>in that you have forsaken the Lord your God<br>when He led you in the way?  
**2:18** And now what are you doing in the way of Egypt,<br>to drink the waters of the Nile?<br>Or what are you doing in the way of Assyria,<br>to drink the waters of the River?  
**2:19** Your own wickedness will correct you,<br>and your backslidings will reprove you.<br>Know therefore and see<br>that it is an evil thing and bitter for you<br>to have forsaken the Lord your God,<br>and the fear of Me is not in you,<br>says the Lord God of Hosts.  
**2:20** For long ago I have broken your yoke<br>and burst your bonds;<br>and you said, “I will not serve.”<br>For upon every high hill<br>and under every green tree<br>you wander, playing the harlot.  
**2:21** Yet I had planted you a noble vine,<br>a wholly faithful seed.<br>How then have you turned<br>into the degenerate plant of a strange vine to Me?  
**2:22** For though you wash yourself with lye,<br>and take much soap,<br>yet your iniquity is marked before Me,<br>says the Lord God.  
**2:23** How can you say, “I am not polluted,<br>I have not gone after the Baals”?<br>See your way in the valley.<br>Know what you have done.<br>You are a swift young camel<br>entangling her ways,  
**2:24** a wild donkey used to the wilderness<br>that sniffs the wind in her passion.<br>In the time of her heat, who can turn her away?<br>All those who seek her will not weary themselves;<br>in her month they shall find her.  
**2:25** Withhold your foot from being unshod<br>and your throat from thirst.<br>But you said, “There is no hope. No!<br>For I have loved strangers,<br>and after them I will go.”  
**2:26** As the thief is ashamed when he is found,<br>so is the house of Israel ashamed.<br>They, their kings, their officials,<br>and their priests, and their prophets  
**2:27** say to a tree, “You are my father.”<br>And to a stone, “You gave birth to me.”<br>For they have turned their back to Me,<br>and not their face.<br>But in the time of their trouble they will say,<br>“Arise and save us.”  
**2:28** But where are your gods that you have made for yourself?<br>Let them arise, if they can save you<br>in the time of your trouble;<br>for according to the number of your cities<br>are your gods, O Judah.  
**2:29** Why will you plead with Me?<br>You all have transgressed against Me,<br>says the Lord.  
**2:30** In vain I have smitten your children;<br>they received no correction.<br>Your own sword has devoured your prophets<br>like a destroying lion.  
**2:31** O generation, consider the word of the Lord:<br>Have I been a wilderness to Israel?<br>A land of darkness?<br>Why do My people say, “We are free to roam.<br>We will come no more to You”?  
**2:32** Can a virgin forget her ornaments,<br>or a bride her attire?<br>Yet My people have forgotten Me<br>for days without number.  
**2:33** You prepare your way very well to seek love.<br>Therefore you have also taught the wicked women your ways.  
**2:34** Also in your skirts is found<br>the blood of the souls of the innocent poor.<br>I have not found it by breaking in,<br>but in spite of all these things.  
**2:35** Yet you say, “Because I am innocent,<br>surely His anger shall turn away from me.”<br>Now I will plead with you,<br>because you say, “I have not sinned.”  
**2:36** Why do you go about so much<br>to change your way?<br>You also shall be ashamed of Egypt,<br>as you were ashamed of Assyria.  
**2:37** Indeed, you shall go forth from him<br>with your hands upon your head;<br>for the Lord has rejected those in whom you trust,<br>and you shall not prosper in them.  

## 3장

**3:1** Unfaithful Israel<br>If a man divorces his wife,<br>and she goes from him and becomes another man’s,<br>shall he return to her again?<br>Shall not that land be greatly polluted?<br>But you have played the harlot with many lovers;<br>yet return again to Me,<br>says the Lord.  
**3:2** Lift up your eyes to the high places,<br>and see! Where have you not been ravished?<br>In the roads you have sat for them<br>as the Arabian in the wilderness.<br>And you have polluted the land<br>with your harlotry and with your wickedness.  
**3:3** Therefore the showers have been withheld,<br>and there has been no latter rain.<br>And you had a harlot’s forehead;<br>you refused to be ashamed.  
**3:4** Shall you not from this time cry to Me,<br>“My Father, You are the guide of my youth?  
**3:5** Shall He reserve His anger forever?<br>Shall He keep it to the end?”<br>Indeed, you have spoken<br>and done evil things as you could.  
**3:6** A Call to Repentance<br>The Lord said also to me in the days of Josiah the king: Have you seen that which backsliding Israel has done? She has gone up on every high mountain and under every green tree, and there has played the harlot.  
**3:7** I said after she had done all these things, “Turn to Me.” But she did not return. And her treacherous sister Judah saw it.  
**3:8** I saw that for all the adulteries of backsliding Israel, I had put her away and given her a bill of divorce. Yet her treacherous sister Judah did not fear, but went and played the harlot also.  
**3:9** Through the lightness of her harlotry she defiled the land and committed adultery with stones and with trees.  
**3:10** Yet for all this, her treacherous sister Judah has not turned to Me with her whole heart, but feignedly, says the Lord.  
**3:11** God Invites Repentance<br>The Lord said to me: Backsliding Israel has proved herself more righteous than treacherous Judah.  
**3:12** Go and proclaim these words toward the north, and say:<br>Return, backsliding Israel, says the Lord,<br>and I will not cause My anger to fall on you.<br>For I am merciful, says the Lord,<br>and I will not keep anger forever.  
**3:13** Only acknowledge your iniquity,<br>that you have transgressed against the Lord your God<br>and have scattered your ways to the strangers<br>under every green tree,<br>and you have not obeyed My voice,<br>says the Lord.  
**3:14** Return, O backsliding sons, says the Lord. For I am married to you. And I will take you, one from a city and two from a family, and I will bring you to Zion.  
**3:15** And I will give you shepherds according to My heart, who shall feed you with knowledge and understanding.  
**3:16** It shall come to pass when you are multiplied and increased in the land in those days, says the Lord, they will say no more, “The ark of the covenant of the Lord.” And it will not come to mind, nor will they remember it, nor will they call for it, nor will it be made any more.  
**3:17** At that time they will call Jerusalem, the Throne of the Lord, and all the nations will be gathered to it, to Jerusalem, for the name of the Lord; nor will they walk any more after the stubbornness of their evil hearts.  
**3:18** In those days the house of Judah will walk with the house of Israel, and they will come together out of the land of the north to the land that I have given to your fathers for an inheritance.  
**3:19** But I said:<br>How can I put you among My sons<br>and give you a pleasant land,<br>the most beautiful heritage of the nations?<br>And I said: You shall call Me, My Father,<br>and shall not turn away from Me.  
**3:20** Surely as a wife treacherously departs from her husband,<br>so you have dealt treacherously with Me, O house of Israel,<br>says the Lord.  
**3:21** A voice was heard upon the high places,<br>the weeping and the supplications of the sons of Israel,<br>because they have perverted their way,<br>they have forgotten the Lord their God.  
**3:22** Return, O backsliding sons,<br>and I will heal your backslidings.<br>“We come to You;<br>for You are the Lord our God.  
**3:23** Truly in vain is salvation hoped for from the hills,<br>and from the multitude of mountains.<br>Truly, in the Lord our God<br>is the salvation of Israel.  
**3:24** For shame has devoured the labor<br>of our fathers from our youth,<br>their flocks and their herds,<br>their sons and their daughters.  
**3:25** We lie down in our shame,<br>and our humiliation covers us.<br>For we have sinned against the Lord our God,<br>we and our fathers,<br>from our youth even to this day,<br>and have not obeyed the voice of the Lord our God.”  

## 4장

**4:1** Judah Threatened With Invasion<br>If you will return, O Israel,<br>says the Lord,<br>return to Me.<br>And if you will put away your abominations out of My sight,<br>then you shall not be moved.  
**4:2** You shall swear, “As the Lord lives,”<br>in truth, in justice, and in righteousness;<br>then the nations shall bless themselves in Him,<br>and in Him they shall glory.  
**4:3** For thus says the Lord to the men of Judah and Jerusalem:<br>Break up your fallow ground,<br>and do not sow among thorns.  
**4:4** Circumcise yourselves to the Lord,<br>and take away the foreskins of your heart,<br>you men of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem,<br>lest My fury come forth like fire,<br>and burn so that no one can quench it,<br>because of the evil of your deeds.  
**4:5** Declare in Judah and publish in Jerusalem, and say:<br>Blow the trumpet in the land.<br>Cry aloud and say,<br>“Assemble yourselves,<br>and let us go into the fortified cities.”  
**4:6** Set up the standard toward Zion.<br>Seek refuge, do not delay.<br>For I will bring disaster from the north,<br>and a great destruction.  
**4:7** The lion has come up from his thicket,<br>and the destroyer of the nations is on his way.<br>He has gone forth from his place<br>to make your land desolate.<br>And your cities shall be laid waste<br>without an inhabitant.  
**4:8** For this gird yourself with sackcloth,<br>lament and howl.<br>For the fierce anger of the Lord<br>is not turned back from us.  
**4:9** It shall come to pass in that day, says the Lord,<br>that the heart of the king will fail and the heart of the officials;<br>and the priests will be astonished<br>and the prophets will wonder.  
**4:10** Then I said, “Ah, Lord God! Surely You have greatly deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, ‘You shall have peace,’ whereas the sword reaches the throat.”  
**4:11** At that time it will be said to this people and to Jerusalem: A dry wind of the high places in the wilderness toward the daughter of My people, not to fan or to cleanse,  
**4:12** even a full wind from those places, will come to Me. Now also I will pronounce judgments against them.  
**4:13** Look! He shall come up as clouds,<br>and his chariots shall be as a whirlwind.<br>His horses are swifter than eagles.<br>Woe to us, for we are ruined!  
**4:14** O Jerusalem, wash your heart from wickedness, that you may be saved.<br>How long shall your evil thoughts lodge within you?  
**4:15** For a voice declares from Dan,<br>and publishes wickedness from Mount Ephraim:  
**4:16** “Report it to the nations, now!”<br>Indeed, proclaim against Jerusalem:<br>“Besiegers come from a far country,<br>and lift their voices against the cities of Judah.  
**4:17** As watchmen of a field, they are against her all around,<br>because she has been rebellious against Me,<br>says the Lord.  
**4:18** Your way and your deeds<br>have procured these things to you.<br>This is your wickedness.<br>How bitter it is!<br>How it touches your heart!”  
**4:19** Sorrow for a Doomed Nation<br>My soul, my soul!<br>I am pained at my very heart.<br>My heart makes a noise in me.<br>I cannot hold my peace,<br>because you have heard, O my soul,<br>the sound of the trumpet, the alarm of war.  
**4:20** Destruction upon destruction is proclaimed,<br>for the whole land is devastated.<br>Suddenly are my tents devastated,<br>and my curtains in a moment.  
**4:21** How long shall I see the standard<br>and hear the sound of the trumpet?  
**4:22** “For My people are foolish,<br>they have not known Me.<br>They are foolish children,<br>and they have no understanding.<br>They are wise to do evil,<br>but to do good they have no knowledge.”  
**4:23** I looked on the earth,<br>and it was without form and void.<br>And to the heavens,<br>and they had no light.  
**4:24** I looked on the mountains,<br>and they trembled,<br>and all the hills moved to and fro.  
**4:25** I looked, and there was no man,<br>and all the birds of the heavens had fled.  
**4:26** I looked, and the fruitful place was a wilderness,<br>and all the cities were broken down<br>at the presence of the Lord, and by His fierce anger.  
**4:27** For thus says the Lord:<br>The whole land shall be desolate.<br>Yet I will not make a full end.  
**4:28** For this the earth shall mourn,<br>and the heavens above be black,<br>because I have spoken it, I have purposed it,<br>and will not relent, nor will I turn back from it.  
**4:29** The whole city shall flee<br>for the noise of the horsemen and bowmen.<br>They shall go into thickets<br>and climb up upon the rocks.<br>Every city shall be forsaken,<br>and not a man dwell in it.  
**4:30** When you are devastated, what will you do?<br>Though you clothe yourself with crimson,<br>though you deck yourself with ornaments of gold,<br>though you enlarge your eyes with paint,<br>in vain you will make yourself fair.<br>Your lovers will despise you;<br>they will seek your life.  
**4:31** For I have heard a voice as of a woman in labor,<br>and the anguish as of her who brings forth her first child,<br>the voice of the daughter of Zion,<br>that bewails herself, that spreads her hands, saying,<br>“Ah, woe is me,<br>for my soul is wearied because of murderers.”  

## 5장

**5:1** The Justice of God’s Judgment<br>Run to and fro through the streets of Jerusalem,<br>see now and know;<br>seek in the open places<br>if you can find a man,<br>if there is any who executes justice, who seeks the truth,<br>that I may pardon her.  
**5:2** Though they say, “The Lord lives,”<br>surely they swear falsely.  
**5:3** O Lord, are not Your eyes upon the truth?<br>You have stricken them, but they have not grieved.<br>You have consumed them, but they have refused to receive correction.<br>They have made their faces harder than a rock;<br>they have refused to return.  
**5:4** Therefore I said, “Surely these are the poor.<br>They are foolish;<br>for they know not the way of the Lord<br>or the judgment of their God.  
**5:5** I will go to the great men<br>and will speak to them,<br>for they have known the way of the Lord<br>and the judgment of their God.”<br>But these have altogether broken the yoke<br>and burst the bonds.  
**5:6** Therefore a lion out of the forest will slay them,<br>and a wolf from the deserts will destroy them;<br>a leopard will watch over their cities.<br>Everyone who goes out from there will be torn in pieces,<br>because their transgressions are many<br>and their backslidings have increased.  
**5:7** How shall I pardon you for this?<br>Your children have forsaken Me<br>and sworn by those who are not gods.<br>When I had fed them to the full,<br>then they committed adultery<br>and assembled themselves by troops in the harlots’ houses.  
**5:8** They were as fed horses in the morning;<br>everyone neighed after his neighbor’s wife.  
**5:9** Shall I not punish for these things? says the Lord,<br>and shall not My soul be avenged on such a nation as this?  
**5:10** Go up upon her walls and destroy,<br>but make not a full end.<br>Take away her battlements.<br>For they are not the Lord’s.  
**5:11** For the house of Israel and the house of Judah<br>have dealt very treacherously against Me,<br>says the Lord.  
**5:12** They have lied about the Lord,<br>and said, “Not He.<br>Neither shall evil come upon us,<br>nor shall we see sword or famine.”  
**5:13** And the prophets shall become wind,<br>and the word is not in them.<br>Thus it shall be done to them!  
**5:14** Therefore thus says the Lord God of Hosts:<br>Because you speak this word,<br>indeed I will make My words in your mouth fire<br>and this people wood, and it shall devour them.  
**5:15** Truly, I will bring a nation upon you from far,<br>O house of Israel, says the Lord.<br>It is a mighty nation, it is an ancient nation,<br>a nation whose language you do not know,<br>nor do you understand what they say.  
**5:16** Their quiver is as an open sepulcher,<br>they all are mighty men.  
**5:17** They will eat up your harvest and your bread<br>which your sons and your daughters should eat.<br>They will eat up your flocks and your herds;<br>they will eat up your vines and your fig trees;<br>they will impoverish your fenced cities,<br>in which you trusted, with the sword.  
**5:18** Nevertheless in those days, says the Lord, I will not make a full end of you.  
**5:19** It shall come to pass when they say, “Why does the Lord our God do all these things to us?” then you shall answer them, “As you have forsaken Me and served strange gods in your land, so you will serve strangers in a land that is not yours.”  
**5:20** Declare this in the house of Jacob<br>and publish it in Judah, saying:  
**5:21** Hear this now, O foolish people and without understanding,<br>who have eyes but do not see,<br>who have ears but do not hear.  
**5:22** Do you not fear Me? says the Lord.<br>Will you not tremble at My presence?<br>For I have placed the sand for the boundary of the sea<br>by a perpetual decree so that it cannot pass over it.<br>And though the waves toss themselves, yet they cannot prevail;<br>though they roar, yet they cannot pass over it.  
**5:23** But this people has a revolting and a rebellious heart;<br>they have revolted and gone aside.  
**5:24** They do not say in their heart,<br>“Let us now fear the Lord our God,<br>who gives rain, both the former and the latter, in its season.<br>He reserves for us the appointed weeks of the harvest.”  
**5:25** Your iniquities have turned away these things,<br>and your sins have withheld good things from you.  
**5:26** For among My people are found wicked men.<br>They lie in wait, as he who sets snares;<br>they set a trap, they catch men.  
**5:27** As a cage is full of birds,<br>so are their houses full of deceit.<br>Therefore they have become great and rich.  
**5:28** They are fat, they are sleek.<br>Indeed, they excel in deeds of wickedness.<br>They do not judge the cause of the fatherless,<br>so that they may prosper;<br>and the right of the needy they do not defend.  
**5:29** Shall I not punish them for these things?<br>says the Lord.<br>Shall not My soul be avenged<br>on such a nation as this?  
**5:30** An appalling and horrible thing<br>has been committed in the land.  
**5:31** The prophets prophesy falsely,<br>and the priests rule by their own authority;<br>and My people love to have it so.<br>Yet what will you do in the end?  

## 6장

**6:1** Siege of Jerusalem<br>O sons of Benjamin, gather yourselves<br>to flee from the midst of Jerusalem,<br>and blow the trumpet in Tekoa,<br>and set up a sign of fire in Beth Hakkerem;<br>for disaster appears out of the north,<br>and great destruction.  
**6:2** I have likened the daughter of Zion<br>to a comely and delicate woman.  
**6:3** The shepherds with their flocks will come to her.<br>They will pitch their tents against her all around.<br>They will pasture each in his place.  
**6:4** “Prepare war against her;<br>arise, and let us go up at noon.”<br>“Woe to us, for the day declines,<br>for the shadows of the evening are stretched out.”  
**6:5** “Arise, and let us go by night<br>and let us destroy her palaces.”  
**6:6** For thus says the Lord of Hosts:<br>Hew down trees<br>and build a mound against Jerusalem.<br>This is the city to be punished.<br>She is full of oppression in her midst.  
**6:7** As a fountain casts out her waters,<br>so she casts out her wickedness.<br>Violence and devastation are heard in her;<br>before Me continually are grief and wounds.  
**6:8** Be instructed, O Jerusalem,<br>lest My soul depart from you;<br>lest I make you desolate,<br>a land not inhabited.  
**6:9** Thus says the Lord of Hosts:<br>They will thoroughly glean<br>the remnant of Israel as a vine.<br>Turn back your hand<br>as a grape gatherer into the baskets.  
**6:10** To whom shall I speak and give warning<br>that they may hear?<br>See, their ears are closed,<br>and they cannot listen.<br>Indeed, the word of the Lord is a reproach to them;<br>they have no delight in it.  
**6:11** Therefore I am full of the fury of the Lord;<br>I am weary with holding in.<br>I will pour it out upon the children in the street<br>and upon the assembly of young men together;<br>for even the husband with the wife shall be taken,<br>the aged with him who is full of days.  
**6:12** Their houses shall be turned to others,<br>with their fields and wives together;<br>for I will stretch out My hand<br>upon the inhabitants of the land,<br>says the Lord.  
**6:13** For from the least of them even to the greatest of them,<br>everyone is given to covetousness.<br>And from the prophet even to the priest,<br>everyone deals falsely.  
**6:14** They have healed also the brokenness<br>of the daughter of My people superficially,<br>saying, “Peace, peace,”<br>when there is no peace.  
**6:15** Were they ashamed when they had committed abomination?<br>They were not at all ashamed,<br>nor could they blush.<br>Therefore they shall fall among those who fall;<br>at the time that I punish them they shall be cast down,<br>says the Lord.  
**6:16** Thus says the Lord<br>Stand in the ways and see,<br>and ask for the old paths<br>where the good way is and walk in it,<br>and you shall find rest for your souls.<br>But they said, “We will not walk in it.”

**7:4** Do not trust in lying words, saying, “The temple of the Lord, the temple of the Lord, the temple of the Lord.”

**7:6** if you do not oppress the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place, or walk after other gods to your harm,  
**7:7** then I will cause you to dwell in this place, in the land that I gave to your fathers, forever and ever.  
**7:8** Indeed, you trust in lying words that cannot profit.  
**7:9** Shall you steal, murder, and commit adultery, and swear falsely, and burn incense to Baal, and walk after other gods whom you do not know,  
**7:10** and come and stand before Me in this house, which is called by My name, and say, “We are delivered,” so that you may do all these abominations?  
**7:11** Has this house, which is called by My name, become a den of robbers in your eyes? Certainly, even I have seen it, says the Lord.  
**7:12** But go now to My place which was in Shiloh, where I set My name at the first, and see what I did to it for the wickedness of My people Israel.  
**7:13** And now, because you have done all these works, says the Lord, and I spoke to you, rising up early and speaking, but you did not hear, and I called you, but you did not answer,

**7:15** I will cast you out of My sight as I have cast out all your brothers, even the whole seed of Ephraim.  
**7:16** As for you, do not pray for this people, nor lift up a cry nor prayer for them, nor make intercession to Me, for I will not hear you.  
**7:17** Do you not see what they do in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?

**7:20** Therefore thus says the Lord God: My anger and My fury will be poured out upon this place, upon man and upon beast, and upon the trees of the field, and upon the fruit of the ground; and it shall burn, and shall not be quenched.

**7:24** But they did not listen, nor incline their ear, but walked in the counsels and in the imagination of their evil heart, and went backward and not forward.  
**7:25** Since the day that your fathers came out of the land of Egypt to this day, I have even sent to you all My servants the prophets, daily rising up early and sending them.  
**7:26** Yet they did not listen to Me, nor incline their ear, but hardened their neck. They did worse than their fathers.  
**7:27** Therefore you will speak all these words to them. But they will not listen to you. You will also call to them, but they will not answer you.  
**7:28** You shall say to them: This is a nation that does not obey the voice of the Lord their God, nor receive correction. Truth has perished, and has been cut off from their mouth.  
**7:29** Cut off your hair, O Jerusalem, and cast it away, and take up a lamentation on high places; for the Lord has rejected and forsaken the generation of His wrath.  
**7:30** For the sons of Judah have done evil in My sight, says the Lord. They have set their abominations in the house which is called by My name, to pollute it.  
**7:31** They have built the high places of Topheth, which is in the Valley of Ben Hinnom, to burn their sons and their daughters in the fire, which I did not command them, nor did it come into My heart.  
**7:32** Therefore, truly the days are coming, says the Lord, that it will no more be called Topheth, nor the Valley of Ben Hinnom, but the Valley of Slaughter; for they will bury in Topheth because there is no other place.  
**7:33** The corpses of this people shall be food for the fowl of the heaven and for the beasts of the earth; and no one will frighten them away.  
**7:34** Then I will cause to cease from the cities of Judah and from the streets of Jerusalem the voice of laughter and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride; for the land shall become desolate.

**11:18** Jeremiah’s Life Threatened<br>The Lord has made it known to me and I knew it; then You showed me their deeds.  
**11:19** But I was like a gentle lamb that is brought to the slaughter, and I did not know that they had devised plots against me, saying,<br>“Let us destroy the tree with the fruit,<br>and let us cut him off from the land of the living<br>so that his name may be remembered no more.”  
**11:20** But, O Lord of Hosts, who judges righteously,<br>who tries the feelings and the heart,<br>let me see Your vengeance on them,<br>for to You I have revealed my cause.  
**11:21** Therefore thus says the Lord of the men of Anathoth who seek your life, saying, “Do not prophesy in the name of the Lord so that you not die by our hand.”

**11:23** And there shall be no remnant of them, for I will bring calamity upon the men of Anathoth, even the year of their punishment.

**32:16** Jeremiah Prays for Understanding<br>Now when I had delivered the deed of purchase to Baruch the son of Neriah, I prayed to the Lord, saying:  
**32:17** Ah Lord God! Truly, You have made the heavens and the earth by Your great power and outstretched arm, and there is nothing too hard for You.  
**32:18** You show lovingkindness to thousands, and recompense the iniquity of the fathers into the bosom of their children after them, O great and mighty God. The Lord of Hosts is His name,  
**32:19** great in counsel and mighty in deed, whose eyes are open to all the ways of the sons of men, to give to everyone according to his ways and according to the fruit of his deeds.  
**32:20** You have set signs and wonders in the land of Egypt, even to this day, and in Israel, and among other men, and have made Yourself a name, as it is today.  
**32:21** And You have brought your people Israel out of the land of Egypt with signs, and with wonders, and with a strong hand, and with a stretched-out arm, and with great terror.  
**32:22** And You have given them this land, which You swore to their fathers to give them, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**32:24** See, the siege ramps have come to the city to take it. And the city is given into the hand of the Chaldeans who fight against it, because of the sword, and of the famine, and of the pestilence, and what You have spoken has come to pass, as You can see.  
**32:25** O Lord God, You have said to me, “Buy the field for money and call in witnesses,” although the city is given into the hand of the Chaldeans.

**32:27** Behold, I am the Lord, the God of all flesh. Is anything too hard for Me?  
**32:28** Therefore thus says the Lord: I will give this city into the hand of the Chaldeans and into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he shall take it.  
**32:29** And the Chaldeans who fight against this city will come and set this city on fire and burn it with the houses, upon whose roofs they have offered incense to Baal and poured out drink offerings to other gods, to provoke Me to anger.

**32:31** Indeed this city has been to Me a provocation of My anger and My fury from the day that they built it, even to this day, so that I should remove it from before My face,  
**32:32** because of all the evil of the sons of Israel and of the sons of Judah which they have done to provoke Me to anger, they, their kings, their officials, their priests, and their prophets, and the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**32:35** They built the high places of Baal which are in the Valley of Ben Hinnom to cause their sons and their daughters to pass through the fire to Molek, which I had not commanded them, nor did it come into My mind that they should do this abomination, to cause Judah to sin.

**32:38** And they shall be My people, and I will be their God.  
**32:39** And I will give them one heart and one way, that they may fear Me forever, for their good and for their children after them.  
**32:40** And I will make an everlasting covenant with them that I will not turn away from them, to do them good. But I will put My fear in their hearts so that they shall not depart from Me.  
**32:41** Indeed, I will rejoice over them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land assuredly with My whole heart and with My whole soul.  
**32:42** For thus says the Lord: Just as I have brought all this great calamity upon this people, so I will bring upon them all the good that I have promised them.  
**32:43** Fields will be bought in this land of which you say, It is desolate, without man or beast. It is given into the hand of the Chaldeans.  
**32:44** Men will buy fields for money, sign and seal deeds, and call in witnesses in the land of Benjamin, and in the places about Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, and in the cities of the mountains, and in the cities of the valley, and in the cities of the Negev; for I will restore their fortunes, says the Lord.

**33:3** Call to Me, and I will answer you, and show you great and mighty things which you do not know.  
**33:4** For thus says the Lord God of Israel concerning the houses of this city, and concerning the houses of the kings of Judah, which are thrown down to make a defense against the siege mounds and against the sword:

**33:6** I will bring it health and healing, and I will heal them; and I will reveal to them the abundance of peace and truth.  
**33:7** I will restore the fortunes of Judah and the fortunes of Israel, and will build them as at the first.  
**33:8** I will cleanse them from all their iniquity whereby they have sinned against Me. And I will pardon all their iniquities whereby they have sinned and whereby they have transgressed against Me.  
**33:9** It will be to Me a name of joy, praise, and honor before all the nations of the earth which shall hear of all the good that I do to them; and they will fear and tremble for all the goodness and for all the prosperity that I procure for it.

For I will restore the fortunes of the land as at the first, says the Lord.

**33:20** Thus says the Lord: If you can break My covenant of the day and My covenant of the night so that there should not be day and night in their season,

**33:23** Moreover the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, saying:  
**33:24** Have you not considered what this people have spoken, saying, “The two families which the Lord has chosen, He has cast them off”? Thus they have despised My people, that they should be no more a nation before them.  
**33:25** Thus says the Lord: If My covenant for day and night does not stand, and if I have not appointed the ordinances of heaven and earth,  
**33:26** then I would cast away the seed of Jacob and David My servant, so that I would not take any of his seed to be rulers over the seed of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Yet I will restore their fortunes and have mercy on them.  

## 34장

**34:1** Zedekiah to Die in Exile<br>The word which came to Jeremiah from the Lord when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and all his army and all the kingdoms of the earth of his dominion and all the people fought against Jerusalem and against all the cities, saying:  
**34:2** Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: Go and speak to Zedekiah king of Judah and tell him: Thus says the Lord: I will give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire.  
**34:3** And you will not escape out of his hand, but will surely be taken and delivered into his hand. And you will see the king of Babylon eye to eye, and he will speak with you face to face, and you will go to Babylon.  
**34:4** Yet hear the word of the Lord, O Zedekiah king of Judah! Thus says the Lord concerning you: You will not die by the sword.  
**34:5** But you will die in peace; and as spices were burned for your fathers, the former kings who were before you, so they burn spices for you. And they will lament for you, saying, “Alas, lord!” For I have pronounced the word, says the Lord.  
**34:6** Then Jeremiah the prophet spoke all these words to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem,  
**34:7** when the king of Babylon’s army fought against Jerusalem, and against all the cities of Judah that were left, against Lachish and Azekah, for these fortified cities remained of the cities of Judah.  
**34:8** Slaves to be Free<br>This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord after the king Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people who were in Jerusalem to proclaim liberty to them:  
**34:9** that every man should let his male slave and every man his female slave, being a Hebrew man or a Hebrew woman, go free so that no one should keep them, a Jew his brother, in bondage.  
**34:10** Now when all the officials and all the people who had entered into the covenant heard that everyone should let his male slave and everyone his female slave go free, so that no one should keep them any more in bondage, then they obeyed and let them go.  
**34:11** But afterward they turned around and caused the male slaves and the female slaves whom they had set free to return, and brought them into subjection for male slaves and female slaves.  
**34:12** Therefore the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying:  
**34:13** Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: I made a covenant with your fathers in the day that I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, saying:  
**34:14** “At the end of seven years, each of you shall set free his Hebrew brother who has been sold to you; and when he has served you six years, you shall let him go free from you.” But your fathers did not obey Me nor incline their ear.  
**34:15** You recently turned and did what was right in My sight by proclaiming liberty, every man to his neighbor; and you made a covenant before Me in the house that is called by My name.  
**34:16** But then you turned around and profaned My name when every one of you took back his male and female slaves, whom you had set free, at their pleasure, and you brought them into subjection to be your slaves.  
**34:17** Therefore thus says the Lord: You have not obeyed Me in proclaiming liberty, everyone to his brother and every man to his neighbor. I proclaim a liberty to you, says the Lord, to the sword, to the pestilence, and to the famine; and I will make you a terror to all the kingdoms of the earth.  
**34:18** I will give the men who have transgressed My covenant, who have not performed the words of the covenant which they had made before Me, when they cut the calf in two and passed between the parts,  
**34:19** the officials of Judah and the officials of Jerusalem, the court officers, and the priests, and all the people of the land, who passed between the parts of the calf,  
**34:20** I will even give them into the hand of their enemies and into the hand of those who seek their life; and their dead bodies shall be food to the fowl of heaven and to the beasts of the earth.  
**34:21** Zedekiah king of Judah and his officials I will give into the hand of their enemies and into the hand of those who seek their life and into the hand of the king of Babylon’s army, which has gone away from you.  
**34:22** I will command, says the Lord, and cause them to return to this city, and they will fight against it and take it and burn it with fire; and I will make the cities of Judah a desolation without an inhabitant.  

## 35장

**35:1** The Rekabites’ Obedience<br>The word which came to Jeremiah from the Lord in the days of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying:  
**35:2** Go to the house of the Rekabites and speak to them, and bring them into the house of the Lord, into one of the chambers, and give them wine to drink.

**35:4** and I brought them into the house of the Lord, into the chamber of the sons of Hanan, the son of Igdaliah, a man of God, which was by the chamber of the officials, which was above the chamber of Maaseiah the son of Shallum, the keeper of the door.  
**35:5** Then I set before the sons of the house of the Rekabites pots full of wine and cups, and I said to them, “Drink wine.”

**35:18** Jeremiah said to the house of the Rekabites: Thus says the Lord of Hosts, the God of Israel: Because you have obeyed the commandment of Jonadab your father and kept all his precepts and done according to all that he has commanded you,  
**35:19** therefore thus says the Lord of Hosts, the God of Israel, Jonadab the son of Rekab shall not lack a man to stand before Me always.  

## 36장

**36:1** Jeremiah’s Scroll Is Burned<br>In the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying:  
**36:2** Take a scroll of a book, and write in it all the words that I have spoken to you against Israel and against Judah and against all the nations, from the day I spoke to you, from the days of Josiah, even to this day.  
**36:3** It may be that the house of Judah will hear all the disaster which I intend to do to them, so that every man may turn from his evil way; then I will forgive their iniquity and their sin.  
**36:4** Then Jeremiah called Baruch the son of Neriah. And Baruch wrote on a scroll at the dictation of Jeremiah all the words of the Lord which He had spoken to him.  
**36:5** Jeremiah commanded Baruch, saying, “I am shut in. I cannot go into the house of the Lord.  
**36:6** Therefore go and read from the scroll that you have written at my dictation the words of the Lord in the hearing of the people in the house of the Lord on a fast day. And also you shall read them in the ears of all Judah who come out of their cities.  
**36:7** Perhaps their supplication will come before the Lord, and everyone will turn from his evil way, for great is the anger and the fury that the Lord has pronounced against this people.”  
**36:8** Baruch the son of Neriah did according to all that Jeremiah the prophet commanded him, reading in the book the words of the Lord in the house of the Lord.  
**36:9** In the fifth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, in the ninth month, they proclaimed a fast before the Lord to all the people in Jerusalem and to all the people who came from the cities of Judah to Jerusalem.  
**36:10** Then Baruch read from the book the words of Jeremiah in the house of the Lord, in the chamber of Gemariah the son of Shaphan the scribe, in the higher court, at the entry of the New Gate of the house of the Lord, in the ears of all the people.  
**36:11** When Micaiah the son of Gemariah, the son of Shaphan, had heard out of the book all the words of the Lord,  
**36:12** then he went down to the king’s house, into the scribe’s chamber. And all the officials sat there, even Elishama the scribe, and Delaiah the son of Shemaiah, and Elnathan the son of Akbor, and Gemariah the son of Shaphan, and Zedekiah the son of Hananiah, and all the other officials.  
**36:13** Then Micaiah declared to them all the words that he had heard when Baruch read the book in the ears of the people.  
**36:14** Then all the officials sent Jehudi the son of Nethaniah, the son of Shelemiah, the son of Cushi, to Baruch, saying, “Take in your hand the scroll from which you have read in the ears of the people and come.” So Baruch the son of Neriah took the scroll in his hand and came to them.  
**36:15** They said to him, “Sit down now, and read it in our ears.”<br>So Baruch read it in their ears.  
**36:16** When they heard all the words, they turned to one another in fear and said to Baruch, “We will surely tell the king of all these words.”  
**36:17** They also asked Baruch, saying, “Tell us now, how did you write all these words? Was it at his mouth?”  
**36:18** Then Baruch answered them, “He pronounced all these words to me with his mouth, and I wrote them with ink in the book.”  
**36:19** Then the officials said to Baruch, “Go hide, you and Jeremiah, and let no man know where you are.”  
**36:20** Then they went to the king in the court, but they laid up the scroll in the chamber of Elishama the scribe, and told all the words in the ears of the king.  
**36:21** So the king sent Jehudi to fetch the scroll, and he took it out of Elishama the scribe’s chamber. And Jehudi read it in the ears of the king and in the ears of all the officials who stood beside the king.  
**36:22** Now the king sat in the winter house in the ninth month, and there was a fire burning on the hearth before him.  
**36:23** As Jehudi read three or four columns, the king cut it with a scribe’s knife and cast it into the fire that was on the hearth, until all the scroll was consumed in the fire that was on the hearth.  
**36:24** Yet they were not afraid, nor did they tear their garments, neither the king nor any of his servants who heard all these words.  
**36:25** Although Elnathan and Delaiah and Gemariah entreated the king not to burn the scroll, yet he would not listen to them.  
**36:26** But the king commanded Jerahmeel the son of Hammelech, and Seraiah the son of Azriel, and Shelemiah the son of Abdeel to seize Baruch the scribe and Jeremiah the prophet. But the Lord hid them.  
**36:27** Jeremiah and Baruch Rewrite the Scroll<br>Then the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah after the king had burned the book and the words which Baruch had written at the mouth of Jeremiah, saying:  
**36:28** Take again another scroll and write in it all the former words that were in the first book, which Jehoiakim the king of Judah has burned.  
**36:29** You shall say to Jehoiakim king of Judah: Thus says the Lord: You have burned this book, saying, Why have you written in it that the king of Babylon will certainly come and destroy this land, and will cause man and beast to cease from here?  
**36:30** Therefore thus says the Lord concerning Jehoiakim king of Judah: He shall have no one to sit on the throne of David. And his dead body shall be cast out to the heat of the day and the frost of the night.  
**36:31** I will also punish him and his seed and his servants for their iniquity. And I will bring upon them and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem and upon the men of Judah all the disaster that I have pronounced against them; but they did not listen.  
**36:32** Then Jeremiah took another scroll and gave it to Baruch the scribe, the son of Neriah who wrote in it from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the book which Jehoiakim king of Judah had burned in the fire. And there were added to them many like words.  

## 37장

**37:1** Jeremiah Warns Zedekiah<br>And King Zedekiah the son of Josiah reigned instead of Koniah the son of Jehoiakim, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon made king in the land of Judah.  
**37:2** But neither he nor his servants nor the people of the land listened to the words of the Lord, which He spoke through the prophet Jeremiah.  
**37:3** But Zedekiah the king sent Jehukal the son of Shelemiah and Zephaniah the son of Maaseiah, the priest, to the prophet Jeremiah, saying, “Please pray to the Lord our God for us.”  
**37:4** Now Jeremiah was still coming in and going out among the people, for they had not yet put him into prison.  
**37:5** At that time Pharaoh’s army had come out of Egypt, and when the Chaldeans who were besieging Jerusalem heard the report of them, they departed from Jerusalem.  
**37:6** Then the word of the Lord came to the prophet Jeremiah, saying:  
**37:7** Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel: Thus you shall say to the king of Judah who sent you to Me to inquire of Me: Pharaoh’s army which has come out to help you shall return to Egypt, to its own land.  
**37:8** The Chaldeans will come again and fight against this city, and take it, and burn it with fire.  
**37:9** Thus says the Lord: Do not deceive yourselves, saying, “The Chaldeans will surely depart from us,” for they will not depart.  
**37:10** For though you had struck the whole army of the Chaldeans who fight against you and there remained but wounded men among them, yet every man would rise up in his tent and burn this city with fire.  
**37:11** Jeremiah Imprisoned<br>Now when the army of the Chaldeans had withdrawn from Jerusalem at the approach of Pharaoh’s army,  
**37:12** Jeremiah set out from Jerusalem to go into the land of Benjamin to take possession of his property there among the people.  
**37:13** When he was in the Gate of Benjamin, a captain of the guard was there whose name was Irijah the son of Shelemiah, the son of Hananiah. And he seized Jeremiah the prophet, saying, “You are falling away to the Chaldeans!”  
**37:14** But Jeremiah said, “It is false! I am not falling away to the Chaldeans,” but he did not listen to him. So Irijah seized Jeremiah and brought him to the officials.  
**37:15** Therefore the officials were wrathful with Jeremiah, and struck him, and put him in prison in the house of Jonathan the scribe; for they had made that the prison.  
**37:16** For Jeremiah had entered the dungeon, that is, the vaulted cell, and Jeremiah had remained there many days.  
**37:17** Then Zedekiah the king sent and took him out; and the king asked him secretly in his house, and said, “Is there any word from the Lord?”<br>And Jeremiah said, “There is!” Then he said, “You shall be delivered into the hand of the king of Babylon.”  
**37:18** Moreover Jeremiah said to King Zedekiah, “How have I sinned against you or against your servants or against this people that you have put me in prison?  
**37:19** Where now are your prophets who prophesied to you, saying, ‘The king of Babylon will not come against you, nor against this land’?  
**37:20** Therefore now please listen, O my lord the king. Please let my supplication be accepted before you, that you not cause me to return to the house of Jonathan the scribe, lest I die there.”  
**37:21** Then Zedekiah the king commanded that they should commit Jeremiah into the court of the prison and give him daily a piece of bread out of the bakers’ street, until all the bread in the city was finished. Thus Jeremiah remained in the court of the prison.  

## 38장

**38:1** Jeremiah Thrown Into a Cistern<br>Now Shephatiah the son of Mattan, and Gedaliah the son of Pashhur, and Jukal the son of Shelemiah, and Pashhur the son of Malkijah heard the words that Jeremiah had spoken to all the people, saying:  
**38:2** Thus says the Lord: He who remains in this city will die by the sword, by famine, and by pestilence. But he who goes out to the Chaldeans shall live, for he shall have his life as plunder, and shall live.  
**38:3** Thus says the Lord: This city shall surely be given into the hand of the king of Babylon’s army, and he will capture it.  
**38:4** Therefore the officials said to the king, “We beseech you, let this man be put to death, for he thus weakens the hands of the men of war who remain in this city and the hands of all the people, in speaking such words to them; for this man does not seek the welfare of this people, but their harm.”  
**38:5** Then Zedekiah the king said, “Here he is; he is in your hand; for the king cannot do anything against you.”  
**38:6** Then they took Jeremiah and cast him into the cistern of Malkijah the son of Hammelech that was in the court of the prison, and they let Jeremiah down with cords. And in the cistern there was no water, only mud, so Jeremiah sank in the mud.  
**38:7** Now Ebed-Melek the Ethiopian, one of the eunuchs who was in the king’s house, heard that they had put Jeremiah in the cistern. Now the king was sitting in the Gate of Benjamin.  
**38:8** Ebed-Melek went forth out of the king’s house and spoke to the king, saying,  
**38:9** “My lord the king, these men have done evil in all that they have done to Jeremiah the prophet whom they have cast into the cistern. And he will die by hunger in the place where he is, for there is no more bread in the city.”  
**38:10** Then the king commanded Ebed-Melek the Ethiopian, saying, “Take thirty men with you from here and take up Jeremiah the prophet out of the cistern before he dies.”

**52:3** Through the anger of the Lord this came to pass in Jerusalem and Judah until He had cast them out from His presence.<br>Then Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.  
**52:4** In the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, in the tenth day of the month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came, he and all his army, against Jerusalem, and laid siege to it; they built a siege wall all around it.  
**52:5** So the city was besieged until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah.  
**52:6** In the fourth month, in the ninth day of the month, the famine was so severe in the city that there was no bread for the people of the land.  
**52:7** Then the city was breached, and all the men of war fled and went out of the city by night by way of the gate between the two walls, which was by the king’s garden, though the Chaldeans were all around the city, and they went by way of the Arabah.  
**52:8** But the army of the Chaldeans pursued after the king and overtook Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho, and all his army was scattered from him.  
**52:9** Then they took the king and carried him up to the king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath where he passed sentence on him.  
**52:10** The king of Babylon slew the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes; he also slew all the officials of Judah in Riblah.  
**52:11** Then he put out the eyes of Zedekiah, and the king of Babylon bound him in chains and carried him to Babylon and put him in prison until the day of his death.  
**52:12** The Temple Burned<br>Now in the fifth month, in the tenth day of the month, which was the nineteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard who served the king of Babylon came into Jerusalem,  
**52:13** and burned the house of the Lord, and the king’s house, and all the houses of Jerusalem. Even all the large houses, he burned with fire.  
**52:14** And all the army of the Chaldeans who were with the captain of the guard broke down all the walls of Jerusalem all around.  
**52:15** Then Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard carried away captive some of the poorest of the people, and the rest of the people who remained in the city, and those who fell away, who fell to the king of Babylon, and the rest of the multitude.

**52:17** Also the pillars of bronze that were in the house of the Lord, and the stands, and the bronze sea that was in the house of the Lord, the Chaldeans broke and carried all their bronze to Babylon.  
**52:18** The cauldrons also, and the shovels, and the snuffers, and the bowls, and the spoons, and all the vessels of bronze with which they ministered, they took away.  
**52:19** The captain of the guard also took away the basins, and the fire pans, and the bowls, and the cauldrons, and the lampstands, and the spoons, and the cups, what was of fine gold, and what was of fine silver.

**52:21** Concerning the pillars: the height of one pillar was eighteen cubits, and it was twelve cubits in circumference and in thickness it was four fingers, and hollow.  
**52:22** A capital of bronze was upon it, and the height of one capital was five cubits, with network and pomegranates upon the capital all around, all of bronze. The second pillar also and the pomegranates were like these.  
**52:23** There were ninety-six pomegranates on the sides; all the pomegranates were a hundred upon the network all around.

**52:25** He also took out of the city one official who had charge of the men of war, and seven men of the advisers of the king who were found in the city, and the scribe of the commander of the army who mustered the people of the land, and sixty men of the people of the land who were found in the midst of the city.
