# 20.잠언 (MEV)



## 1장

**1:1** The Purpose and Theme<br>The proverbs of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel:  
**1:2** To know wisdom and instruction,<br>to perceive the words of understanding,  
**1:3** to receive the instruction of wisdom,<br>justice, judgment, and equity;  
**1:4** to give subtlety to the simple,<br>to the young man knowledge and discretion—  
**1:5** a wise man will hear and will increase learning,<br>and a man of understanding will attain wise counsel,  
**1:6** to understand a proverb and the interpretation,<br>the words of the wise and their riddles.  
**1:7** The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge,<br>but fools despise wisdom and instruction.  
**1:8** The Prologue: Embrace Wisdom<br>My son, hear the instruction of your father,<br>and do not forsake the teaching of your mother;  
**1:9** for they will be a garland of grace on your head,<br>and chains about your neck.  
**1:10** My son, if sinners entice you,<br>do not consent.  
**1:11** If they say, “Come with us,<br>let us lie in wait for blood;<br>let us lurk secretly for the innocent without cause;  
**1:12** let us swallow them up alive as the grave,<br>and whole, as those who go down into the pit;  
**1:13** we will find all kinds of precious possessions;<br>we will fill our houses with spoil;  
**1:14** cast in your lot among us,<br>let us all have one purse”—  
**1:15** my son, do not walk in the way with them,<br>keep your foot from their path;  
**1:16** for their feet run to evil<br>and make haste to shed blood.  
**1:17** Surely in vain the net is spread<br>in the sight of any bird.  
**1:18** They lie in wait for their own blood;<br>they lurk secretly for their own lives.  
**1:19** So are the ways of everyone who is greedy of gain,<br>which takes away the life of its owners.  
**1:20** The Call of Wisdom<br>Wisdom cries out in the street;<br>she utters her voice in the markets.  
**1:21** She cries at the corner of the streets, in the openings of the gates;<br>she speaks her words in the city, saying:  
**1:22** “How long, you simple ones, will you love simplicity?<br>For the scorners delight in their scorning,<br>and fools hate knowledge.  
**1:23** Turn at my reproof;<br>surely I will pour out my spirit on you;<br>I will make my words known to you.  
**1:24** Because I have called and you refused,<br>I have stretched out my hand and no man regarded,  
**1:25** because you neglected all my counsel,<br>and would have none of my reproof,  
**1:26** I also will laugh at your calamity;<br>I will mock when your fear comes,  
**1:27** when your fear comes as desolation<br>and your destruction comes as a whirlwind,<br>when distress and anguish come upon you.  
**1:28** “Then they will call on me, but I will not answer;<br>they will seek me early, but they will not find me.  
**1:29** Because they hated knowledge<br>and did not choose the fear of the Lord,  
**1:30** they would have none of my counsel<br>and despised all my reproof.  
**1:31** Therefore they will eat of the fruit of their own way,<br>and be filled with their own devices.  
**1:32** For the turning away of the simple will slay them,<br>and the prosperity of fools will destroy them.  
**1:33** But whoever listens to me will dwell safely,<br>and will be secure from fear of evil.”  

## 2장

**2:1** The Value of Wisdom<br>My son, if you will receive my words,<br>and hide my commandments within you,  
**2:2** so that you incline your ear to wisdom,<br>and apply your heart to understanding;  
**2:3** yes, if you cry out for knowledge,<br>and lift up your voice for understanding,  
**2:4** if you seek her as silver,<br>and search for her as for hidden treasures,  
**2:5** then you will understand the fear of the Lord,<br>and find the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For the Lord gives wisdom;<br>out of His mouth come knowledge and understanding.  
**2:7** He lays up sound wisdom for the righteous;<br>He is a shield to those who walk uprightly.  
**2:8** He keeps the paths of justice,<br>and preserves the way of His saints.  
**2:9** Then you will understand righteousness and judgment<br>and equity, and every good path.  
**2:10** When wisdom enters your heart,<br>and knowledge is pleasant to your soul,  
**2:11** discretion will preserve you;<br>understanding will keep you,  
**2:12** to deliver you from the way of the evil man,<br>from the man who speaks perverse things,  
**2:13** from those who leave the paths of uprightness<br>to walk in the ways of darkness;  
**2:14** who rejoice to do evil,<br>and delight in the perversity of the wicked;  
**2:15** whose ways are crooked,<br>and who are devious in their paths;  
**2:16** to deliver you from the immoral woman,<br>even from the seductress who flatters with her words,  
**2:17** who forsakes the guide of her youth,<br>and forgets the covenant of her God.  
**2:18** For her house leads down to death,<br>and her paths to the departed spirits;  
**2:19** none who go to her return again,<br>nor do they take hold of the paths of life.  
**2:20** So you may walk in the way of good men<br>and keep the paths of the righteous.  
**2:21** For the upright will dwell in the land,<br>and the innocent will remain in it;  
**2:22** but the wicked will be cut off from the earth,<br>and the transgressors will be rooted out of it.  

## 3장

**3:1** The Rewards of Wisdom<br>My son, do not forget my teaching,<br>but let your heart keep my commandments;  
**3:2** for length of days and long life<br>and peace will they add to you.  
**3:3** Do not let mercy and truth forsake you;<br>bind them around your neck,<br>write them on the tablet of your heart,  
**3:4** so you will find favor and good understanding<br>in the sight of God and man.  
**3:5** Trust in the Lord with all your heart,<br>and lean not on your own understanding;  
**3:6** in all your ways acknowledge Him,<br>and He will direct your paths.  
**3:7** Do not be wise in your own eyes;<br>fear the Lord and depart from evil.  
**3:8** It will be health to your body,<br>and strength to your bones.  
**3:9** Honor the Lord with your substance,<br>and with the first fruits of all your increase;  
**3:10** so your barns will be filled with plenty,<br>and your presses will burst out with new wine.  
**3:11** My son, do not despise the chastening of the Lord,<br>nor be weary of His correction;  
**3:12** for whom the Lord loves He corrects,<br>even as a father the son in whom he delights.  
**3:13** Happy is the man who finds wisdom,<br>and the man who gets understanding;  
**3:14** for her benefit is more profitable than silver,<br>and her gain than fine gold.  
**3:15** She is more precious than rubies,<br>and all the things you may desire are not to be compared with her.  
**3:16** Length of days is in her right hand,<br>and in her left hand riches and honor.  
**3:17** Her ways are ways of pleasantness,<br>and all her paths are peace.  
**3:18** She is a tree of life to those who take hold of her,<br>and happy is everyone who retains her.  
**3:19** The Lord by wisdom has founded the earth;<br>by understanding He has established the heavens;  
**3:20** by His knowledge the depths are broken up,<br>and the clouds drop down the dew.  
**3:21** My son, let them not depart from your eyes—<br>keep sound wisdom and discretion;  
**3:22** so they will be life to your soul<br>and grace to your neck.  
**3:23** Then you will walk safely in your way,<br>and your foot will not stumble.  
**3:24** When you lie down, you will not be afraid;<br>yes, you will lie down and your sleep will be sweet.  
**3:25** Do not be afraid of sudden terror,<br>nor of trouble from the wicked when it comes;  
**3:26** for the Lord will be your confidence,<br>and will keep your foot from being caught.  
**3:27** Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due,<br>when it is in the power of your hand to do it.  
**3:28** Do not say to your neighbor,<br>“Go, and come again, and tomorrow I will give it,”<br>when you have it with you.  
**3:29** Do not devise evil against your neighbor,<br>seeing he dwells securely by you.  
**3:30** Do not strive with a man without cause,<br>if he has done you no harm.  
**3:31** Do not envy the oppressor,<br>and choose none of his ways;  
**3:32** for the perverse is an abomination to the Lord,<br>but His secret counsel is with the righteous.  
**3:33** The curse of the Lord is on the house of the wicked,<br>but He blesses the habitation of the just.  
**3:34** Surely He scorns the scornful,<br>but He gives favor to the humble.  
**3:35** The wise will inherit glory,<br>but shame will be the legacy of fools.  

## 4장

**4:1** Wisdom Is Supreme<br>Hear, O children, the instruction of a father,<br>and attend to know understanding.  
**4:2** For I give you good precepts;<br>do not forsake my teaching.  
**4:3** For I was my father’s son,<br>tender and the only beloved in the sight of my mother.  
**4:4** He also taught me and said to me,<br>“Let your heart retain my words;<br>keep my commandments, and live.  
**4:5** Get wisdom! Get understanding!<br>Do not forget it, nor turn away from the words of my mouth.  
**4:6** Do not forsake her, and she will preserve you;<br>love her, and she will keep you.  
**4:7** Wisdom is principal; therefore get wisdom.<br>And with all your getting, get understanding.  
**4:8** Exalt her, and she will promote you;<br>she will bring you honor, when you embrace her.  
**4:9** She will place on your head an ornament of grace;<br>a crown of glory she will deliver to you.”  
**4:10** Hear, my son, and receive my sayings,<br>and the years of your life will be many.  
**4:11** I have taught you in the way of wisdom;<br>I have led you in right paths.  
**4:12** When you walk, your steps will not be hindered,<br>and when you run, you will not stumble.  
**4:13** Take firm hold of instruction, do not let her go;<br>keep her, for she is your life.  
**4:14** Do not enter the path of the wicked,<br>and do not go in the way of evil men.  
**4:15** Avoid it, do not travel on it;<br>turn from it and pass on.  
**4:16** For they do not sleep unless they have done mischief;<br>and their sleep is taken away unless they cause some to fall.  
**4:17** For they eat the bread of wickedness<br>and drink the wine of violence.  
**4:18** But the path of the just is as the shining light,<br>that shines more and more unto the perfect day.  
**4:19** The way of the wicked is as darkness;<br>they do not know at what they stumble.  
**4:20** My son, attend to my words;<br>incline your ear to my sayings.  
**4:21** Do not let them depart from your eyes;<br>keep them in the midst of your heart;  
**4:22** for they are life to those who find them,<br>and health to all their body.  
**4:23** Keep your heart with all diligence,<br>for out of it are the issues of life.  
**4:24** Put away from you a deceitful mouth,<br>and put perverse lips far from you.  
**4:25** Let your eyes look right on,<br>and let your eyelids look straight before you.  
**4:26** Ponder the path of your feet,<br>and let all your ways be established.  
**4:27** Do not turn to the right or to the left;<br>remove your foot from evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** Warning Against Adultery<br>My son, attend to my wisdom,<br>and bow your ear to my understanding,  
**5:2** that you may regard discretion,<br>and that your lips may keep knowledge.  
**5:3** For the lips of an immoral woman drip as a honeycomb,<br>and her mouth is smoother than oil.  
**5:4** But her end is bitter as wormwood,<br>sharp as a two-edged sword.  
**5:5** Her feet go down to death,<br>her steps take hold of Sheol.  
**5:6** She does not ponder the path of life;<br>her ways are unstable, and she does not know it.  
**5:7** Hear me now therefore, O children,<br>and do not depart from the words of my mouth.  
**5:8** Remove your way far from her,<br>and do not go near the door of her house,  
**5:9** lest you give your honor to others,<br>and your years to the cruel;  
**5:10** lest strangers be filled with your wealth,<br>and your labors go to the house of a stranger;  
**5:11** and you mourn at the last,<br>when your flesh and your body are consumed,  
**5:12** and say, “How I have hated instruction,<br>and my heart despised reproof!  
**5:13** And I have not obeyed the voice of my teachers,<br>nor inclined my ear to those who instructed me!  
**5:14** I was almost in utter ruin<br>in the midst of the congregation and assembly.”  
**5:15** Drink waters out of your own cistern,<br>and running waters out of your own well.  
**5:16** Should your fountains be dispersed abroad,<br>streams of water in the streets?  
**5:17** Let them be only your own,<br>and not for strangers with you.  
**5:18** Let your fountain be blessed,<br>and rejoice with the wife of your youth.  
**5:19** Let her be as the loving deer and pleasant doe;<br>let her breasts satisfy you at all times;<br>and always be enraptured with her love.  
**5:20** Why should you, my son, be intoxicated by an immoral woman,<br>and embrace the bosom of a seductress?  
**5:21** For the ways of man are before the eyes of the Lord,<br>and He ponders all his goings.  
**5:22** His own iniquities entrap the wicked himself,<br>and he is snared in the cords of his sins.  
**5:23** He will die for lack of instruction,<br>and in the greatness of his folly he will go astray.  

## 6장

**6:1** Warning Against Pledges<br>My son, if you put up a security for your friend,<br>if you have shaken hands with a stranger,  
**6:2** you are snared with the words of your mouth;<br>you are taken with the words of your mouth.  
**6:3** Do this now, my son, and deliver yourself;<br>when you have come into the hand of your friend,<br>go and humble yourself;<br>plead with your friend.  
**6:4** Give no sleep to your eyes,<br>nor slumber to your eyelids.  
**6:5** Deliver yourself as a doe from the hand of the hunter,<br>and as a bird from the hand of the fowler.  
**6:6** The Folly of Idleness<br>Go to the ant, you sluggard!<br>Consider her ways and be wise.  
**6:7** Which, having no guide,<br>overseer, or ruler,  
**6:8** provides her bread in the summer,<br>and gathers her food in the harvest.  
**6:9** How long will you sleep, O sluggard?<br>When will you arise out of your sleep?  
**6:10** Yet a little sleep, a little slumber,<br>a little folding of the hands to sleep—  
**6:11** so will your poverty come upon you like a stalker,<br>and your need as an armed man.  
**6:12** The Wicked Man<br>A wayward person, a wicked man,<br>walks with a perverse mouth.  
**6:13** He winks with his eyes,<br>he signals with his feet,<br>he motions with his fingers;  
**6:14** perversity is in his heart,<br>he devises mischief continually, he sows discord.  
**6:15** Therefore his calamity will come suddenly;<br>in a moment he will be broken without remedy.  
**6:16** These six things the Lord hates,<br>yes, seven are an abomination to him:  
**6:17** a proud look,<br>a lying tongue,<br>and hands that shed innocent blood,  
**6:18** a heart that devises wicked imaginations,<br>feet that are swift in running to mischief,  
**6:19** a false witness who speaks lies,<br>and he who sows discord among brethren.  
**6:20** Warning Against Adultery<br>My son, keep your father’s commandment,<br>and do not forsake the instruction of your mother.  
**6:21** Bind them continually upon your heart,<br>and tie them around your neck.  
**6:22** When you go, they will lead you;<br>when you sleep, they will keep you;<br>and when you awake, they will speak with you.  
**6:23** For the commandment is a lamp, and the law is light;<br>and reproofs of instruction are the way of life,  
**6:24** to keep you from the evil woman,<br>from the flattery of the tongue of a seductress.  
**6:25** Do not lust after her beauty in your heart,<br>nor let her allure you with her eyelids.  
**6:26** For by means of a harlot a man is reduced to a piece of bread,<br>and the adulteress will prey upon his precious life.  
**6:27** Can a man take fire in his bosom,<br>and his clothes not be burned?  
**6:28** Can one walk upon hot coals,<br>and his feet not be burned?  
**6:29** So he who goes in to his neighbor’s wife;<br>whoever touches her will not be innocent.  
**6:30** Men do not despise a thief if he steals<br>to satisfy himself when he is hungry.  
**6:31** But if he is found, he will restore sevenfold;<br>he will give all the substance of his house.  
**6:32** But whoever commits adultery with a woman lacks understanding;<br>he who does it destroys his own soul.  
**6:33** A wound and dishonor will he get,<br>and his reproach will not be wiped away.  
**6:34** For jealousy is the rage of a man;<br>therefore he will not spare in the day of vengeance.  
**6:35** He will not regard any ransom,<br>nor will he rest content, though you give many gifts.  

## 7장

**7:1** Beware of the Adulteress<br>My son, keep my words,<br>and lay up my commandments within you.  
**7:2** Keep my commandments and live,<br>and my teaching as the apple of your eye.  
**7:3** Bind them on your fingers;<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,”<br>and call understanding your kinswoman,  
**7:5** that they may keep you from the immoral woman,<br>from the seductress who flatters with her words.  
**7:6** For at the window of my house<br>I looked through my casement,  
**7:7** and saw among the simple ones,<br>I discerned among the youths,<br>a young man void of understanding,  
**7:8** passing through the street near her corner;<br>and he went the way to her house  
**7:9** in the twilight, in the evening,<br>in the black and dark night.  
**7:10** And there a woman met him,<br>with the attire of a harlot, and subtle of heart.  
**7:11** She is loud and stubborn;<br>her feet do not abide in her house.  
**7:12** Now she is without, now in the streets,<br>and lies in wait at every corner.  
**7:13** So she caught him, and kissed him;<br>and with an impudent face said to him:  
**7:14** “I have peace offerings with me;<br>this day have I paid my vows.  
**7:15** Therefore I came out to meet you,<br>diligently to seek your face, and I have found you.  
**7:16** I have decked my bed with coverings of tapestry,<br>with carved works, with fine linen of Egypt.  
**7:17** I have perfumed my bed<br>with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.  
**7:18** Come, let us take our fill of love until the morning;<br>let us solace ourselves with love.  
**7:19** For my husband is not at home;<br>he has gone on a long journey;  
**7:20** he has taken a bag of money with him,<br>and will come home at the day appointed.”  
**7:21** With her enticing speech she caused him to yield,<br>with the flattering of her lips she seduced him.  
**7:22** He went after her straightway,<br>as an ox goes to the slaughter,<br>or as a fool to the correction of the stocks,  
**7:23** until a dart struck through his liver.<br>As a bird hastens to the snare,<br>he did not know that it would cost him his life.  
**7:24** Listen to me now therefore, O children,<br>and attend to the words of my mouth:  
**7:25** do not let your heart turn aside to her ways,<br>do not go astray in her paths;  
**7:26** for she has cast down many wounded,<br>and many strong men have been slain by her.  
**7:27** Her house is the way to Sheol,<br>going down to the chambers of death.  

## 8장

**8:1** The Virtue of Wisdom<br>Does not wisdom cry out,<br>and understanding lift up her voice?  
**8:2** She stands on the top of high places,<br>by the way in the places of the paths.  
**8:3** She cries out at the gates,<br>at the entry of the city, at the entrance of the doors:  
**8:4** “To you, O men, I call,<br>and my voice is to the sons of men.  
**8:5** O you simple, understand wisdom,<br>and you fools, be of an understanding heart.  
**8:6** Hear, for I will speak of excellent things,<br>and from the opening of my lips will be right things;  
**8:7** for my mouth will speak truth,<br>and wickedness is an abomination to my lips.  
**8:8** All the words of my mouth are in righteousness;<br>there is nothing crooked or perverse in them.  
**8:9** They are all plain to him who understands,<br>and right to those who find knowledge.  
**8:10** Receive my instruction, and not silver,<br>and knowledge rather than choice gold;  
**8:11** for wisdom is better than rubies,<br>and all the things that may be desired are not to be compared to it.  
**8:12** “I, wisdom, dwell with prudence,<br>and find out knowledge and discretion.  
**8:13** The fear of the Lord is to hate evil;<br>pride and arrogance<br>and the evil way and the perverse mouth I hate.  
**8:14** Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom;<br>I am understanding, I have strength.  
**8:15** By me kings reign,<br>and princes decree justice.  
**8:16** By me princes rule,<br>and nobles, even all the judges of the earth.  
**8:17** I love those who love me,<br>and those who seek me early will find me.  
**8:18** Riches and honor are with me,<br>yes, enduring riches and righteousness.  
**8:19** My fruit is better than gold,<br>yes, than fine gold, and my revenue than choice silver.  
**8:20** I lead in the way of righteousness,<br>in the midst of the paths of justice,  
**8:21** that I may cause those who love me to inherit wealth,<br>and I will fill their treasuries.  
**8:22** “The Lord possessed me in the beginning of His way,<br>before His works of old.  
**8:23** I was set up from everlasting,<br>from the beginning, before there was ever an earth.  
**8:24** When there were no depths, I was brought forth,<br>when there were no fountains abounding with water.  
**8:25** Before the mountains were settled,<br>before the hills I was brought forth;  
**8:26** while as yet He had not made the earth or the fields,<br>or the first dust of the world.  
**8:27** When He prepared the heavens, I was there,<br>when He drew a circle on the face of the deep,  
**8:28** when He established the clouds above,<br>when He strengthened the fountains of the deep,  
**8:29** when He gave to the sea His decree,<br>that the waters should not pass His commandment,<br>when He appointed the foundations of the earth,  
**8:30** then I was by Him, as one brought up with Him;<br>and I was daily His delight,<br>rejoicing always before Him,  
**8:31** rejoicing in the habitable part of His earth,<br>and my delights were with the sons of men.  
**8:32** “Now therefore listen to me, O you children,<br>for blessed are those who keep my ways.  
**8:33** Hear instruction, and be wise,<br>and do not refuse it.  
**8:34** Blessed is the man who hears me,<br>watching daily at my gates,<br>waiting at the posts of my doors.  
**8:35** For whoever finds me finds life,<br>and will obtain favor of the Lord;  
**8:36** but he who sins against me wrongs his own soul;<br>all those who hate me love death.”  

## 9장

**9:1** The Way of Wisdom<br>Wisdom has built her house,<br>she has hewn out her seven pillars;  
**9:2** she has killed her beasts, she has mixed her wine,<br>she has also furnished her table.  
**9:3** She has sent out her maidens,<br>she cries out from the highest places of the city,  
**9:4** “Whoever is simple, let him turn in here.”<br>As for him who wants understanding, she says to him,  
**9:5** “Come, eat of my bread,<br>and drink of the wine which I have mixed.  
**9:6** Forsake foolishness and live,<br>and go in the way of understanding.”  
**9:7** He who reproves a scorner gets shame for himself,<br>and he who rebukes a wicked man gets hurt.  
**9:8** Do not reprove a scorner, lest he hate you;<br>rebuke a wise man, and he will love you.  
**9:9** Give instruction to a wise man, and he will be yet wiser;<br>teach a just man, and he will increase in learning.  
**9:10** The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom,<br>and the knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.  
**9:11** For by me your days will be multiplied,<br>and the years of your life will be increased.  
**9:12** If you are wise, you will be wise for yourself,<br>but if you scorn, you alone will bear it.  
**9:13** The Way of Foolishness<br>A foolish woman is clamorous;<br>she is simple, and knows nothing.  
**9:14** For she sits at the door of her house,<br>on a seat in the high places of the city,  
**9:15** to call those who pass by<br>who go right on their way:  
**9:16** “Whoever is simple, let him turn in here.”<br>And as for him who lacks understanding, she says to him,  
**9:17** “Stolen waters are sweet,<br>and bread eaten in secret is pleasant.”  
**9:18** But he does not know that the dead are there,<br>and that her guests are in the depths of the grave.  

## 10장

**10:1** The Proverbs of Solomon<br>The proverbs of Solomon.<br>A wise son makes a glad father,<br>but a foolish son is the grief of his mother.  
**10:2** Treasures of wickedness profit nothing,<br>but righteousness delivers from death.  
**10:3** The Lord will not allow the soul of the righteous to famish,<br>but He casts away the desire of the wicked.  
**10:4** He becomes poor who deals with a slack hand,<br>but the hand of the diligent makes rich.  
**10:5** He who gathers in summer is a wise son,<br>but he who sleeps in harvest is a son who causes shame.  
**10:6** Blessings are on the head of the just,<br>but violence covers the mouth of the wicked.  
**10:7** The memory of the just is blessed,<br>but the name of the wicked will rot.  
**10:8** The wise in heart will receive commandments,<br>but a prating fool will fall.
