# 29.요엘 (MEV)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the Lord, which came to Joel, son of Pethuel.  
**1:2** A Land Laid Waste<br>Hear this, elders,<br>and give ear, all inhabitants of the land!<br>Has anything like this happened in your days,<br>or in the days of your fathers?  
**1:3** Tell it to your children,<br>and let your children tell their children,<br>and let their children tell another generation.  
**1:4** What the fledging locust left,<br>the adult locust has eaten;<br>what the adult locust left,<br>the larval locust has eaten;<br>what the larval locust left,<br>the hopper locust has eaten.  
**1:5** Awaken, drunkards, and weep!<br>Wail, all wine-drinkers,<br>because the sweet wine<br>has been cut off from your lips.  
**1:6** For a nation powerful and innumerable<br>has invaded my land;<br>its teeth are like the teeth of a lion,<br>like the fangs of a lioness.  
**1:7** It has despoiled my vine,<br>and splintered my fig tree;<br>it has stripped off its bark<br>and cast it away,<br>leaving its branches white.  
**1:8** Lament like a virgin wearing sackcloth<br>for the husband of her youth.  
**1:9** The grain offering and the drink offering<br>are cut off from the house of the Lord;<br>the priests mourn, who are<br>ministers to the Lord.  
**1:10** The field is ravaged,<br>the ground mourns;<br>for the grain is ruined,<br>the new wine is dried up,<br>and the oil dwindles.  
**1:11** Despair, fieldworkers;<br>wail, vinedressers,<br>for the wheat and the barley,<br>because the harvest of the field has perished.  
**1:12** The vine is dried up,<br>and the fig tree is withered;<br>pomegranate, palm, and apple—<br>all the trees of the field are dry;<br>surely joy has withered away<br>from the sons of men.  
**1:13** A Call to Repentance<br>Put on sackcloth and lament, O priests;<br>wail, ministers of the altar.<br>Come, spend the night in sackcloth,<br>ministers of my God,<br>because the grain offering and the drink offering<br>are withheld from the house of your God.  
**1:14** Consecrate a fast,<br>call a sacred assembly,<br>assemble the elders<br>and all the inhabitants of the land<br>to the house of the Lord your God,<br>and cry out to the Lord.  
**1:15** Alas, for the day!<br>For the day of the Lord is near,<br>and like devastation from the Almighty it comes.  
**1:16** Has not the food been cut off<br>before our eyes,<br>joy and gladness<br>from the house of our God?  
**1:17** The seeds have shriveled<br>under their shovels,<br>the storehouses have been deserted;<br>the granaries have been torn down,<br>because the grain has dried up.  
**1:18** How the beasts groan!<br>The herds of cattle are confused,<br>because they have no pasture;<br>even the flocks of sheep suffer.  
**1:19** To You, O Lord, I call,<br>because fire has devoured the wild pastures,<br>and flame has burned all the trees of the field.  
**1:20** Even the beasts of the field long for You,<br>because the streams of water have dried up,<br>and fire has devoured the wild pastures.  

## 2장

**2:1** The Day of the Lord<br>Blow the ram’s horn in Zion,<br>sound the alarm on My holy mountain!<br>All the inhabitants of the earth will tremble,<br>because the day of the Lord has come,<br>because it is near—  
**2:2** a day of darkness and gloom,<br>a day of clouds and thick darkness.<br>Like blackness spreading over the mountain,<br>a great and mighty army comes,<br>such as was never before,<br>and will never be again,<br>even through the years of all generations.  
**2:3** Before them fire devours,<br>and behind them a flame blazes;<br>the land is like the garden of Eden before them,<br>but behind them a desolate wasteland,<br>and nothing escapes them.  
**2:4** They have the appearance of horses,<br>and like cavalry they run.  
**2:5** As with the sound of chariots,<br>they leap on the mountaintops,<br>as with the sound of a flame of fire consuming stubble,<br>as a mighty army arrayed for war.  
**2:6** Before them, peoples are tormented;<br>every face turns pale.  
**2:7** Like mighty men they run,<br>like men of war they scale a wall;<br>each marches on his way,<br>they do not swerve from their paths.  
**2:8** They do not jostle one another;<br>each marches in his track.<br>Through the weapons they plunge;<br>they do not break rank.  
**2:9** They rush on the city,<br>they run on the wall;<br>they climb up into the houses,<br>they enter through the windows like thieves.  
**2:10** Before them the earth quakes,<br>the heavens shake;<br>the sun and moon darken,<br>and the stars withdraw their radiance.  
**2:11** The Lord has sounded His voice<br>before His army,<br>for His camp is exceedingly great;<br>mighty is the one who accomplishes His word.<br>For great is the day of the Lord,<br>and very awe-inspiring.<br>Who can endure it?  
**2:12** Return to the Lord<br>Yet even now, declares the Lord,<br>return to Me with all your heart,<br>and with fasting and with weeping and with mourning.  
**2:13** Rend your heart,<br>and not your garments;<br>return to the Lord your God,<br>for He is gracious and merciful,<br>slow to anger, and abounding in steadfast love;<br>and He relents from punishing.  
**2:14** Who knows? He might turn aside and relent,<br>and He might leave behind a blessing—<br>a grain offering and a food offering<br>for the Lord your God.  
**2:15** Blow the ram’s horn in Zion,<br>consecrate a fast,<br>call a sacred assembly.  
**2:16** Gather the people,<br>consecrate the congregation,<br>assemble the elders,<br>gather the children<br>and those nursing at the breast;<br>let the bridegroom leave his room<br>and the bride her chamber.  
**2:17** Between the temple porch and the altar,<br>let the priests, ministers of the Lord, weep and say,<br>“Have pity upon Your people,<br>and do not make Your heritage a disgrace,<br>a mockery among the nations.<br>Why should they say among the peoples,<br>‘Where is their God?’ ”  
**2:18** Rescue and Restitution<br>Then the Lord became jealous for His land<br>and took pity upon His people.  
**2:19** So the Lord answered and said to His people,<br>Here! I am sending you grain, new wine, and oil,<br>and you will be satisfied,<br>and I will never again make you<br>a disgrace among the nations.  
**2:20** I will remove the northerner far from you,<br>and I will banish him to a dry and desolate land,<br>those in front to the eastern sea,<br>and those in back to the western sea,<br>and his stink will rise,<br>and his stench will rise,<br>for he has done great things.  
**2:21** Do not be afraid, land;<br>exult and rejoice,<br>for the Lord has done great things!  
**2:22** Do not be afraid, beasts of the field,<br>because the wild pastures flourish,<br>because the tree bears its fruit;<br>the fig tree and the vine yield their abundance.  
**2:23** And children of Zion, exult<br>and rejoice in the Lord your God,<br>because He has given to you<br>the early rain for vindication.<br>He showers down rains for you,<br>the early rain and the latter rain, as before.  
**2:24** Then the threshing floors will be filled with grain,<br>and the vats will overflow with new wine and oil.  
**2:25** And I will compensate you for the years the locusts have eaten—<br>the larval locust, the hopper locust,<br>and the fledging locust—<br>My great army which I sent against you.  
**2:26** You will eat abundantly and be satisfied,<br>and you will praise the name of the Lord your God,<br>who has worked wonders for you;<br>and My people will never again be shamed.  
**2:27** Then you will know that I am in the midst of Israel,<br>that I am the Lord your God,<br>and that there is no other.<br>And My people will never again be shamed.  
**2:28** The Outpouring of the Spirit<br>And it will be that, afterwards,<br>I will pour out My Spirit on all flesh;<br>then your sons and your daughters will prophesy,<br>your old men will dream dreams,<br>and your young men will see visions.  
**2:29** Even on the menservants and maidservants<br>in those days I will pour out My Spirit.  
**2:30** Then I will work wonders in the heavens<br>and the earth—<br>blood and fire and columns of smoke.  
**2:31** The sun will be turned to darkness,<br>and the moon to blood,<br>before the great and awe-inspiring day of the Lord comes.  
**2:32** And it will be that everyone<br>who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.<br>For on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem<br>there will be deliverance,<br>as the Lord has said,<br>and among the survivors<br>whom the Lord calls.  

## 3장

**3:1** Judging the Nations<br>In those days and at that time,<br>when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem,  
**3:2** I will gather all the nations,<br>and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat.<br>I will enter into judgment with them there<br>regarding My people and My heritage Israel,<br>whom they have scattered among the nations;<br>they have also divided up My land.  
**3:3** They have cast lots for My people,<br>and have traded a boy for a prostitute;<br>they have sold a girl for wine,<br>that they might drink.  
**3:4** Also, what are you to Me, Tyre and Sidon and all the regions of Philistia? Are you repaying Me for something? If you are repaying Me, I will return your payment swiftly and speedily on your head.  
**3:5** For you have taken My silver and My gold, and you have carried off My finest treasures to your temples.  
**3:6** You have sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks in order to remove them far from their border.  
**3:7** I am about to rouse them from the place to which you have sold them, and I will return your payment on your head.  
**3:8** I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hand of the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans—to a distant nation, for the Lord has spoken.  
**3:9** Proclaim this among the nations:<br>Consecrate a war!<br>Stir up the mighty men!<br>Let all the men of war draw near and rise.  
**3:10** Beat your plowshares into swords<br>and your pruning hooks into spears;<br>let the weakling say,<br>“I am a warrior!”  
**3:11** Hurry and come, all you surrounding nations,<br>and gather there.<br>Bring down Your warriors, O Lord.  
**3:12** Let the nations be roused,<br>and go up to the Valley of Jehoshaphat;<br>for there I will sit<br>to judge all the surrounding nations.  
**3:13** Swing the sickle,<br>for the harvest is ripe.<br>Come and tread,<br>for the winepress is full.<br>The wine vats overflow,<br>because their evil is great.  
**3:14** Multitudes, multitudes,<br>in the valley of decision!<br>For the day of the Lord is near<br>in the valley of the decision.  
**3:15** The sun and moon darken,<br>and the stars withdraw their radiance.  
**3:16** The Lord roars from Zion,<br>and sounds His voice from Jerusalem,<br>and heaven and earth quake.<br>But the Lord is a refuge for His people,<br>and a stronghold for the children of Israel.  
**3:17** The Lord Dwells in Zion<br>Then you will know that I am the Lord your God,<br>who dwells in Zion, My holy mountain.<br>And Jerusalem will be holy,<br>and invaders will never again pass through her.  
**3:18** And it will be that in that day the mountains will drip sweet wine,<br>and the hills will flow with milk,<br>and all the streambeds of Judah will flow with water;<br>a spring will proceed from the house of the Lord<br>and will water the Valley of Shittim.  
**3:19** Egypt will become a desolation,<br>and Edom a desolate wasteland,<br>because of the violence done to the people of Judah—<br>they shed innocent blood in their land.  
**3:20** But Judah will be inhabited forever,<br>and Jerusalem for generations and generations.  
**3:21** I will avenge their blood,<br>which I have not yet avenged.<br>The Lord dwells in Zion!
