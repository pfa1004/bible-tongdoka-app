# 31.오바디야 (MEV)



## 1장

**1:1** Judgment on Edom<br>The vision of Obadiah.<br>Thus says the Lord God concerning Edom<br>(We have heard a report from the Lord<br>and a messenger has been sent among the nations, saying:<br>“Rise up! Let us rise up against it for battle!”):  
**1:2** See, I will make you small among the nations;<br>you will be greatly despised.  
**1:3** The pride of your heart has deceived you,<br>you who live in the clefts of the rock,<br>whose dwelling is high;<br>you say in your heart,<br>“Who will bring me down to the ground?”  
**1:4** Though you ascend high like the eagle,<br>and though you set your nest among the stars,<br>I will bring you down from there,<br>says the Lord.  
**1:5** If thieves came to you,<br>if robbers by night—<br>how you have been destroyed!—<br>would they not steal only what they want?<br>If grape gatherers come to you,<br>would they not leave gleanings?  
**1:6** How the things of Esau have been ransacked!<br>How his hidden treasures hunted out!  
**1:7** All your confederates have driven you to the border;<br>your allies have deceived and prevailed against you.<br>Those who eat your bread have set a trap for you.<br>You will not detect it.  
**1:8** On that day, says the Lord,<br>I will destroy the wise out of Edom,<br>and understanding out of Mount Esau.  
**1:9** Your mighty men shall be shattered, O Teman,<br>so that everyone from Mount Esau will be cut off.  
**1:10** Edom’s Violence Against Jacob<br>For the slaughter and the violence done to your brother Jacob,<br>shame shall cover you,<br>and you shall be cut off forever.  
**1:11** On the day that you stood aside,<br>on the day that strangers carried off his wealth,<br>and foreigners entered his gates<br>and cast lots on Jerusalem,<br>you also were like one of them.  
**1:12** But you should not have gloated<br>on the day of your brother,<br>on the day of his misfortune;<br>you should not have rejoiced over the children of Judah<br>on the day of their destruction;<br>you should not have boasted<br>on the day of distress.  
**1:13** You should not have entered the gate of My people<br>on the day of their calamity.<br>You should not have gloated over the disaster of Judah<br>on the day of his calamity;<br>you should not have seized his wealth<br>on the day of his calamity.  
**1:14** You should not have stood at the crossroads<br>to cut off his fugitives;<br>you should not have handed over his survivors,<br>on the day of distress.  
**1:15** The Day of the Lord Is Near<br>For the day of the Lord is near<br>upon all the nations;<br>as you have done, it shall be done to you;<br>your deeds shall return on your own head.  
**1:16** For as you have drunk on My holy mountain,<br>all the nations shall drink continually;<br>they shall drink and swallow<br>and shall be as though they had never been.  
**1:17** But on Mount Zion there shall be deliverance,<br>and it shall be holy;<br>and the house of Jacob shall possess<br>those who dispossessed them.  
**1:18** The house of Jacob shall be a fire,<br>the house of Joseph a flame,<br>but the house of Esau stubble;<br>they shall burn them and consume them,<br>and there shall be no survivors<br>from the house of Esau,<br>for the Lord has spoken.  
**1:19** The Restoration of Israel<br>Those of the Negev shall possess<br>Mount Esau,<br>and those of the Shephelah the land<br>of the Philistines.<br>They shall possess the fields of Ephraim and the fields of Samaria,<br>and Benjamin shall possess Gilead.  
**1:20** The exiles of this army of the sons of Israel,<br>shall inherit the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath,<br>and the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad<br>shall inherit the cities of the Negev.  
**1:21** Saviors shall go up to Mount Zion<br>to rule Mount Esau,<br>and the kingdom shall be the Lord’s.
