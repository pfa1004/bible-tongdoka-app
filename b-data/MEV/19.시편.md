# 19.시편 (MEV)



## 1장

**1:1** Blessed is the man<br>who walks not in the counsel of the ungodly,<br>nor stands in the path of sinners,<br>nor sits in the seat of scoffers;  
**1:2** but his delight is in the law of the Lord,<br>and in His law he meditates day and night.  
**1:3** He will be like a tree planted by the rivers of water,<br>that brings forth its fruit in its season;<br>its leaf will not wither,<br>and whatever he does will prosper.  
**1:4** The ungodly are not so,<br>but are like the chaff<br>which the wind drives away.  
**1:5** Therefore the ungodly will not stand in the judgment,<br>nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.  
**1:6** For the Lord knows the way of the righteous,<br>but the way of the ungodly will perish.  

## 2장

**2:1** Why do the nations rage,<br>and the peoples plot in vain?  
**2:2** The kings of the earth set themselves,<br>and the rulers take counsel together,<br>against the Lord<br>and against His anointed, saying,  
**2:3** “Let us tear off their bonds<br>and cast away their ropes from us.”  
**2:4** He who sits in the heavens laughs;<br>the Lord ridicules them.  
**2:5** Then He will speak to them in His wrath<br>and terrify them in His burning anger:  
**2:6** “I have installed My king<br>on Zion, My holy hill.”  
**2:7** I will declare the decree of the Lord:<br>He said to me, “You are My son;<br>this day have I begotten you.  
**2:8** Ask of Me,<br>and I will give you the nations for your inheritance,<br>and the ends of the earth for your possession.  
**2:9** You will break them with a scepter of iron;<br>you will dash them in pieces like a potter’s vessel.”  
**2:10** Now then, you kings, be wise;<br>be admonished, you judges of the earth.  
**2:11** Serve the Lord with fear;<br>tremble with trepidation!  
**2:12** Kiss the son, lest He become angry,<br>and you perish in the way,<br>for His wrath kindles in a flash.<br>Blessed are all who seek refuge in Him.  

## 3장

**3:1** A Psalm of David, when he fled from Absalom his son.<br>Lord, how my foes have multiplied!<br>Many rise up against me!  
**3:2** Many are saying about my life,<br>“There is no help for him in God.” Selah  
**3:3** But You, O Lord, are a shield for me,<br>my glory and the One who raises up my head.  
**3:4** I cried to the Lord with my voice,<br>and He answered me from His holy hill. Selah  
**3:5** I lay down and slept;<br>I awoke, for the Lord sustained me.  
**3:6** I will not be afraid of multitudes of people<br>who have set themselves against me all around.  
**3:7** Arise, O Lord;<br>save me, O my God!<br>For You have struck all my enemies on the cheek;<br>You have broken the teeth of the wicked.  
**3:8** Salvation belongs to the Lord.<br>Your blessing is on Your people. Selah

**4:5** Offer sacrifices of righteousness,<br>and trust in the Lord.

**4:7** You have placed gladness in my heart<br>that is better than when their corn and their new wine abound.  
**4:8** I will both lie down in peace and sleep;<br>for You, Lord,<br>make me dwell safely and securely.

**7:14** The wicked man writhes in pain of iniquity;<br>he has conceived mischief and brought forth falsehood.  
**7:15** He who digs a hole and hollows it<br>will then fall into his own pit.  
**7:16** His mischief will return on his own head;<br>his violence will descend on the crown of his own head.  
**7:17** I will thank the Lord according to His righteousness,<br>and will sing praise to the name of the Lord Most High.

## 10장

**10:1** Why do You stand far off, O Lord?<br>Why do You hide Yourself in times of trouble?  
**10:2** In arrogance the wicked persecutes the poor;<br>let them be caught in the devices they have planned.  
**10:3** For the wicked boasts of his soul’s desire;<br>he blesses the greedy and despises the Lord.  
**10:4** The wicked, through the pride of his countenance, will not seek God;<br>God is not in all his thoughts.  
**10:5** His ways are always prosperous;<br>Your judgments are high and distant from him;<br>as for all his enemies, they scoff at him.  
**10:6** He says in his heart, “I shall not be moved;<br>for generations I shall not meet adversity.”  
**10:7** His mouth is filled with cursing and deceit and oppression;<br>under his tongue is mischief and iniquity.  
**10:8** He sits in the lurking places of the villages;<br>in the secret places he murders the innocent;<br>his eyes lurk against the unfortunate.  
**10:9** He lies in wait secretly as a lion in his den;<br>he lies in wait to catch the poor;<br>he catches the poor, drawing them into his net.  
**10:10** He crouches; he lies low,<br>so that the unfortunate fall by his strength.  
**10:11** He says in his heart, “God has forgotten;<br>He hides His face; He will never see it.”  
**10:12** Arise, O Lord! O God, lift up Your hand!<br>Do not forget the humble.  
**10:13** Why do the wicked despise God?<br>He says in his heart,<br>“You will require an account.”  
**10:14** You have seen it, for You observe trouble and grief,<br>to repay it with Your hand.<br>The unfortunate one entrusts it to You;<br>You are the helper of the orphan.  
**10:15** Break the arm of the wicked and the evil man;<br>seek out his wickedness<br>until You find none.  
**10:16** The Lord is King forever and ever;<br>the nations perished from His land.
