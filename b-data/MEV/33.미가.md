# 33.미가 (MEV)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the Lord, which came to Micah the Morashite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw regarding Samaria and Jerusalem.  
**1:2** Hear, all you peoples!<br>Listen, earth and everything in it,<br>that the Sovereign Lord may be a witness against you,<br>the Lord from His holy temple.  
**1:3** Judgment Against Samaria and Jerusalem<br>Look! The Lord is coming out from His place,<br>so that He might come down and tread upon the high places of the earth.  
**1:4** The mountains will melt beneath Him,<br>and the valleys split apart<br>like wax before the fire,<br>like waters deluged down a slope.  
**1:5** All this because of the transgression of Jacob<br>and because of the sins of the house of Israel.<br>What is the transgression of Jacob?<br>Is it not Samaria?<br>And what is the high place of Judah?<br>Is it not Jerusalem?  
**1:6** Therefore I will make Samaria a heap in the field<br>for the planting of vineyards.<br>I will dump her stones into the valley<br>and expose her foundations.  
**1:7** All her idols will be shattered,<br>and her gifts burned with fire,<br>and I will annihilate her images,<br>because she gathered them as the wages of a prostitute,<br>and as the wages of a prostitute they will again be used.  
**1:8** The Lament of Micah<br>Because of this I will lament and wail,<br>I will go about barefoot and naked;<br>I will howl like the jackals<br>and moan like owlets.  
**1:9** For her wound is mortal,<br>for it has come to Judah;<br>it has extended to the gate of my people,<br>to Jerusalem.  
**1:10** Do not tell it in Gath,<br>do not weep at all;<br>in Beth Ophrah,<br>roll around in the dust.  
**1:11** Pass on your way,<br>inhabitants of Shaphir, naked and ashamed;<br>the inhabitants of Zaanan<br>have not come out.<br>The mourning of Beth Ezel<br>has taken from you its foothold.  
**1:12** Indeed the inhabitants of Maroth<br>wait anxiously for good,<br>because calamity has come down from the Lord<br>to the gate of Jerusalem.  
**1:13** Harness the chariot to the steeds,<br>inhabitants of Lachish.<br>It was the beginning of sin<br>to the daughter of Zion,<br>because in you were found<br>the transgressions of Israel.  
**1:14** Therefore you will give parting gifts<br>to Moresheth Gath;<br>the houses of Akzib will be deception<br>to the kings of Israel.  
**1:15** I will again bring a conqueror to you,<br>inhabitants of Mareshah;<br>the glory of Israel<br>will come to Adullam.  
**1:16** Make yourself bald and shave your head<br>for the children of your delight;<br>make yourself as bald as the eagle,<br>for they will go from you into exile.  

## 2장

**2:1** Woe to the Wicked<br>Woe to those who conceive wickedness,<br>to those who devise evil on their beds!<br>At morning’s light they execute it,<br>because it is in the power of their hand.  
**2:2** They covet fields and seize them,<br>and houses and take them.<br>They defraud a man of his house,<br>and a fellow man of his inheritance.  
**2:3** Therefore, thus says the Lord:<br>I am devising disaster against this family,<br>from which you cannot remove your necks;<br>and you will not walk haughtily,<br>for it will be a time of calamity.  
**2:4** In that day they will take up a taunt against you,<br>and they will wail a wailing lament, and say:<br>“We are totally ruined!<br>He diminishes the portion of my people;<br>how He removes it from me!<br>To a traitor He reassigns our fields!”  
**2:5** Therefore you will not have anyone to apportion the land by lot<br>in the assembly of the Lord.  
**2:6** Prophets of Deceit<br>“Do not prophesy,” they say.<br>“One should not prophesy about these things.<br>Disgrace will not overtake us.”  
**2:7** Should it be said, O house of Jacob,<br>“Is the Spirit of the Lord impatient?<br>Are these His deeds?”<br>Do not My words benefit<br>him who walks uprightly?  
**2:8** But lately My people rise up<br>like an enemy.<br>You strip off the rich robe<br>from those who pass by trustingly,<br>like men returning from war.  
**2:9** The women of My people you drive out<br>from their delightful homes;<br>from their children<br>you take My adornment forever.  
**2:10** Get up and go,<br>for this is not the resting place,<br>because uncleanness ruins,<br>and ruin sickens.  
**2:11** If a man, going about vapidly and deceitfully, lies,<br>“I will preach for you wine and beer,”<br>he would be just the preacher for this people.  
**2:12** The Restoration of Israel<br>I will indeed assemble Jacob—all of you;<br>I will indeed gather the remnant of Israel.<br>I will place them together like sheep in a fold,<br>like a herd in its pasture—<br>thronging with people.  
**2:13** He who breaks through has gone up before them;<br>they will break through and pass the gate and go out by it.<br>Then their king will pass on before them,<br>the Lord at their head.  

## 3장

**3:1** Leaders and Prophets Judged<br>Then I said:<br>Listen now, heads of Jacob,<br>and rulers of the house of Israel.<br>Should you not know justice?  
**3:2** You who hate good and love evil,<br>who tear the skin from My people<br>and the flesh from their bones;  
**3:3** you who have eaten the flesh of My people,<br>and have flayed their skin from them,<br>and broken their bones in pieces;<br>who have chopped them up like meat in the pot,<br>and like flesh in the cauldron.  
**3:4** Then they will cry out to the Lord,<br>but He will not answer them;<br>He will hide His face from them at that time,<br>because they have wrought evil deeds.  
**3:5** Thus says the Lord:<br>Regarding the prophets<br>who mislead My people—<br>the ones who have something to eat<br>proclaim “Peace,”<br>but if one does not feed them,<br>then they prepare for war against him.  
**3:6** Therefore you will have night without vision,<br>and you will have darkness without divination;<br>the sun will set upon the prophets,<br>and black upon them will be the day.  
**3:7** The seers will be shamed<br>and the diviners disgraced,<br>and they will all cover their beards,<br>for there is no answer from God.  
**3:8** But as for me, I am filled with power,<br>with the Spirit of the Lord,<br>and with justice and might,<br>to declare to Jacob his transgression<br>and to Israel his sin.  
**3:9** Please hear this, heads of the house of Jacob<br>and rulers of the house of Israel,<br>who detest justice<br>and warp all that is straight,  
**3:10** who build Zion with bloodguilt<br>and Jerusalem with wickedness.  
**3:11** Her leaders judge for a bribe,<br>her priests teach for a price,<br>and her prophets practice divination for money.<br>Yet upon the Lord they lean, saying,<br>“Is not the Lord in our midst?<br>Evil will not come upon us.”  
**3:12** Therefore, because of you,<br>Zion will be plowed like a field,<br>Jerusalem will be mounds of ruins,<br>and the mountain of the house will become wooded heights.  

## 4장

**4:1** The Mountain of the Lord<br>Then it will be that in the latter days,<br>the mountain of the house of the Lord will be established<br>as head of the mountains,<br>and will be lifted up above the hills;<br>and people will stream to it.  
**4:2** And many nations will come and say,<br>“Come, that we might go up to the mountain of the Lord,<br>and to the house of the God of Jacob,<br>that He might teach us His ways,<br>and that we might walk in His paths.”<br>For from Zion will go forth the law,<br>and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.  
**4:3** Then He will judge between many peoples<br>and mediate for mighty nations far and wide;<br>they will beat their swords into plowshares,<br>and their spears into pruning hooks.<br>Nation will not take up sword against nation,<br>and they will no longer train for war.  
**4:4** Then each man will sit under his vine<br>and under his fig tree,<br>and no one will make them afraid;<br>for the mouth of the Lord of Hosts has spoken.  
**4:5** For all the peoples may walk,<br>each in the name of his God,<br>but we will walk in the name of the Lord<br>our God forever and ever.  
**4:6** The Promise of Restoration<br>In that day, declares the Lord,<br>I will assemble the lame<br>and gather the banished<br>and those whom I have afflicted;  
**4:7** and I will make the lame into a remnant,<br>and the banished into a mighty nation;<br>and the Lord will reign over them on Mount Zion<br>from this time forth and forevermore.  
**4:8** As for you, watchtower of the flock,<br>citadel of the daughter of Zion,<br>to you it will come, the former dominion will come,<br>kingship for the daughter of Jerusalem.  
**4:9** Now why do you cry loudly?<br>Have you no king?<br>Has your counselor perished,<br>that agony has seized you like the woman in labor?  
**4:10** Writhe and bring forth, daughter of Zion,<br>like the woman in labor,<br>because now you will go forth from the city<br>and reside in the field,<br>and you will come to Babylon.<br>There you will be rescued;<br>there the Lord will redeem you<br>from the hand of your enemies.  
**4:11** But now many nations<br>are gathered against you, saying,<br>“May she be defiled,<br>and may our eyes gaze upon Zion.”  
**4:12** But they do not know<br>the thoughts of the Lord,<br>and they do not understand His plan,<br>that He has gathered them like sheaves to the threshing floor.  
**4:13** Arise and thresh, daughter of Zion,<br>for I will make your horn iron;<br>your hoofs I will make bronze,<br>and you will shatter many peoples.<br>I will devote their pillage to the Lord,<br>their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.  

## 5장

**5:1** The Ruler From Bethlehem<br>Now gather yourself in troops, O daughter of troops;<br>he has laid siege against us.<br>With a rod they will strike<br>the judge of Israel on the cheek.  
**5:2** But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah,<br>although you are small among the tribes of Judah,<br>from you will come forth for Me<br>one who will be ruler over Israel.<br>His origins are from of old,<br>from ancient days.  
**5:3** Therefore He will give them up,<br>until the time when she who is in labor has given birth,<br>and the rest of his brothers will return<br>to the children of Israel.  
**5:4** He will stand and shepherd<br>in the strength of the Lord,<br>in the majesty of the name of the Lord his God;<br>then they will live securely, because now He will be great<br>until the ends of the earth;  
**5:5** and He will be their peace.<br>The Remnant of Jacob Delivered<br>When Assyria enters our land<br>and treads through our palaces,<br>then we will raise up against him seven shepherds<br>and eight commanders of men.  
**5:6** They will shepherd the land of Assyria with the sword,<br>and the land of Nimrod at her gates;<br>He will rescue us from Assyria,<br>when he enters our land<br>and when he treads within our border.  
**5:7** Then the remnant of Jacob will be<br>in the midst of many peoples,<br>like dew from the Lord,<br>like showers upon the grass,<br>which do not wait for a man<br>and do not linger for the sons of men.  
**5:8** The remnant of Jacob will be among the nations,<br>in the midst of many peoples,<br>like a lion among the beasts of the forest,<br>like a young lion among flocks of sheep,<br>which when it passes through, tramples and mauls,<br>without rescuer.  
**5:9** Your hand will be lifted up over your adversaries,<br>and all your enemies will be cut off.  
**5:10** And in that day, declares the Lord,<br>I will cut off your horses from among you,<br>and I will destroy your chariots.  
**5:11** Then I will cut off the cities of your land,<br>and I will overthrow your strongholds;  
**5:12** then I will cut off sorceries from your hand,<br>and you will no longer have fortune-tellers.  
**5:13** Then I will cut off your idols,<br>and your sacred stones from among you,<br>and you will no longer bow down<br>to the work of your hands;  
**5:14** then I will root out your Asherah idols from among you,<br>and I will annihilate your cities.  
**5:15** And in anger and wrath I will take vengeance<br>on the nations that have not listened.  

## 6장

**6:1** The Indictment of the Lord<br>Listen to what the Lord says:<br>Arise, plead your case before the mountains,<br>that the hills may hear your voice.  
**6:2** Hear, mountains, the indictment of the Lord,<br>O enduring foundations of the earth—<br>that the Lord has an indictment against His people,<br>and against Israel He will dispute.  
**6:3** “My people, what have I done to you,<br>and how have I wearied you? Answer Me!  
**6:4** For I have brought you up from the land of Egypt,<br>and from the house of slaves I have redeemed you;<br>and I sent before you Moses,<br>Aaron, and Miriam.  
**6:5** O My people, remember now<br>what Balak king of Moab counseled,<br>and what Balaam son of Beor answered him,<br>and remember what happened from Shittim to Gilgal,<br>so that you might know the righteous acts of the Lord.”  
**6:6** The Requirement of the Lord<br>“With what should I come before the Lord,<br>and bow down before God on high?<br>Shall I come before Him with burnt offerings,<br>with calves a year old?  
**6:7** Will the Lord be pleased with thousands of rams,<br>with ten thousand rivers of oil?<br>Shall I give my firstborn for my transgression,<br>the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?”  
**6:8** He has told you, O man, what is good—<br>and what does the Lord require of you,<br>but to do justice and to love kindness,<br>and to walk humbly with your God?  
**6:9** The Punishment of Israel’s Guilt<br>The voice of the Lord calls to the city—<br>and wisdom will fear Your name:<br>Heed the rod and Him who has appointed it.  
**6:10** Are there still in the house of the wicked<br>treasures of wickedness?<br>And the short ephah which is accursed?  
**6:11** Should I acquit the scales of wickedness,<br>and the sack of dishonest weights?  
**6:12** Her wealthy men are full of violence,<br>and her inhabitants speak deception,<br>and their tongue in their mouth is treachery.  
**6:13** Therefore, I have struck you a dreadful blow,<br>devastating you because of your sins.  
**6:14** You will eat, but not be satisfied,<br>with hunger within you.<br>You will set aside, but not retain;<br>and what you retain I will give over to the sword.  
**6:15** You will sow, but not reap;<br>you will tread olives, but not anoint yourselves with oil;<br>you will crush grapes, but not drink wine.  
**6:16** You observe the statutes of Omri,<br>and every practice of the house of Ahab,<br>and you walk in their counsels.<br>Therefore I will give you over to destruction,<br>and the inhabitants to derision.<br>You will bear the contempt of My people.  

## 7장

**7:1** Wait on the Lord<br>Woe is me!<br>Because I am as the gathering of summer fruit,<br>as the gleaning of grapes;<br>there is no cluster to eat,<br>no early fig that my soul desires.  
**7:2** The godly has perished from the earth,<br>and no one is upright among men.<br>All of them lie in wait for bloodshed;<br>each hunts his brother with a net.  
**7:3** Both hands are upon evil, to do it well.<br>The prince and the judge request the bribe,<br>the powerful asserts the craving of his very soul—<br>they intertwine together.  
**7:4** The best among them is like a brier,<br>the most upright among them a bramble;<br>the day of your watchmen,<br>of your punishment, has come;<br>now their confusion is at hand.  
**7:5** Do not trust in a companion,<br>do not rely on a friend;<br>from her who lies in your embrace,<br>guard the doors of your mouth.  
**7:6** For the son dishonors the father,<br>the daughter rises up against her mother,<br>the daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law—<br>the enemies of a man are members of his own household.  
**7:7** But as for me, I watch for the Lord;<br>I await the God of my salvation;<br>my God will hear me.  
**7:8** The Penitence of Israel<br>Do not rejoice over me, my enemy!<br>Although I have fallen, I will rise;<br>although I dwell in darkness,<br>the Lord is my light.  
**7:9** I will endure the rage of the Lord,<br>because I have sinned against Him,<br>until the time when He pleads my case<br>and executes judgment for me.<br>He will bring me out into the light;<br>I will look upon His vindication.  
**7:10** Then my enemy will see,<br>and shame will cover her<br>who said to me,<br>“Where is the Lord your God?”<br>My eyes will look upon her;<br>now she will be trampled<br>like muck in the streets.  
**7:11** A day for building your walls,<br>in that day the boundary will be extended.  
**7:12** In that day they will come to you<br>from Assyria and the cities of Egypt,<br>and from Egypt to the River,<br>from sea to sea,<br>and from mountain to mountain.  
**7:13** The earth will become desolate because of its inhabitants,<br>from the fruit of their deeds.  
**7:14** The Restoration of Israel<br>Shepherd your people with your staff,<br>the flock of your inheritance,<br>which live alone in a forest,<br>in the midst of a fertile land;<br>let them graze in Bashan and Gilead,<br>as in the days of old.  
**7:15** As in the days when you came out of Egypt,<br>I will display wonders.  
**7:16** Nations will see and be ashamed,<br>despite all their might;<br>they will put their hand on their mouth,<br>and their ears will be deaf.  
**7:17** They will lick dust like the serpent,<br>like crawling creatures of the earth;<br>they will come shuddering from their lairs.<br>They will turn in dread to the Lord our God,<br>and they will be afraid of You.  
**7:18** Who is a God like You,<br>bearing iniquity and passing over transgression<br>for the remnant of His inheritance?<br>He does not remain angry forever,<br>because He delights in benevolence.  
**7:19** He will again have compassion upon us.<br>He will tread down our iniquities,<br>and cast all of our sins into the depths of the sea.  
**7:20** You will give faithfulness to Jacob<br>and benevolence to Abraham,<br>which You swore to our fathers<br>from the days of old.
