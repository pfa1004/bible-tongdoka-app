# 25.예레미야애가 (MEV)



## 1장

**1:1** The Sorrows of Jerusalem<br>How lonely sits the city<br>that was full of people!<br>How she has become like a widow,<br>who was once great among the nations!<br>She who was a princess among the provinces<br>has become a slave!  
**1:2** She weeps sorely in the night,<br>her tears are on her cheeks;<br>among all her lovers<br>she has none to comfort her.<br>All her friends have dealt treacherously with her;<br>they have become her enemies.  
**1:3** Judah has gone into captivity,<br>under affliction and great servitude;<br>she dwells among the nations,<br>she finds no rest;<br>all her persecutors overtook her<br>in the midst of distress.  
**1:4** The roads to Zion mourn<br>because no one comes to the solemn feasts.<br>All her gates are desolate;<br>her priests sigh,<br>her virgins are afflicted,<br>and she herself suffers bitterly.  
**1:5** Her adversaries have become her masters,<br>her enemies prosper;<br>for the Lord has afflicted her<br>because of the multitude of her transgressions.<br>Her children have gone into captivity<br>before the enemy.  
**1:6** From the daughter of Zion<br>all her beauty has departed.<br>Her princes have become like deer<br>that find no pasture;<br>they fled without strength<br>before the pursuer.  
**1:7** In the days of her affliction and misery<br>Jerusalem remembers all her pleasant things<br>that she had in the days of old.<br>When her people fell into the hand of the enemy,<br>there was no one to help her.<br>The adversaries saw her<br>and mocked at her desolation.  
**1:8** Jerusalem has sinned grievously;<br>therefore she has become vile.<br>All who honored her despise her,<br>for they have seen her nakedness;<br>she herself sighs<br>and turns away.  
**1:9** Her uncleanness is in her skirts;<br>she took no thought of her future;<br>therefore her fall is astounding;<br>she has no comforter.<br>“O Lord, look upon my affliction,<br>for the enemy has triumphed!”  
**1:10** The adversary has spread his hand<br>over all her precious things;<br>for she has seen the nations<br>enter her sanctuary,<br>those whom You commanded<br>not to enter Your congregation.  
**1:11** All her people groan,<br>as they search for bread;<br>they trade their treasures for food<br>to restore their strength.<br>Look, O Lord, and consider,<br>for I am despised.  
**1:12** Is it nothing to you, all you who pass by?<br>Look and see<br>if there is any sorrow like my sorrow,<br>which was brought upon me,<br>which the Lord has inflicted<br>on the day of His fierce anger.  
**1:13** From on high He has sent fire into my bones,<br>and it prevailed against them;<br>He has spread a net for my feet;<br>He turned me back;<br>He has made me desolate<br>and faint all the day long.  
**1:14** The yoke of my transgressions<br>was bound; by His hand<br>they were fastened together; they were set upon my neck.<br>He made my strength fail;<br>the Lord delivered me into the hands<br>of those whom I am not able to withstand.  
**1:15** The Lord has trampled underfoot<br>all my mighty men in my midst;<br>He has called an assembly against me<br>to crush my young men;<br>The Lord has trodden the virgin daughter of Judah<br>as in a winepress.  
**1:16** For these things I weep;<br>my eyes flow with tears;<br>for the comforter, who should relieve my soul,<br>is far from me.<br>My children are desolate<br>for the enemy has prevailed.  
**1:17** Zion spreads out her hands,<br>but there is no one to comfort her;<br>the Lord has commanded against Jacob<br>that his neighbors should be his adversaries;<br>Jerusalem has become a filthy thing among them.  
**1:18** The Lord is in the right,<br>for I have rebelled against His commandment.<br>Hear now, all peoples,<br>and see my sorrow;<br>my virgins and my young men<br>have gone into captivity.  
**1:19** I called for my lovers,<br>but they deceived me;<br>my priests and my elders<br>perished in the city,<br>while they sought food<br>to restore their strength.  
**1:20** Look, O Lord, for I am in distress;<br>my soul is greatly troubled;<br>my heart is overturned within me,<br>for I have grievously rebelled.<br>In the street the sword bereaves,<br>at home it is like death.  
**1:21** They have heard my groaning,<br>yet there is no one to comfort me.<br>All my enemies have heard of my trouble;<br>they are glad that You have done it.<br>Bring the day that You have announced,<br>that they become like me.  
**1:22** Let all their wickedness come before You,<br>and do to them<br>as You have done to me<br>for all my transgressions;<br>for my groans are many,<br>and my heart is faint.  

## 2장

**2:1** The Anger of God Over Jerusalem<br>How the Lord has covered the daughter of Zion<br>with a cloud in His anger!<br>He has cast down from heaven to the earth<br>the beauty of Israel;<br>He has not remembered His footstool<br>in the day of His anger.  
**2:2** The Lord has swallowed up without mercy<br>all the habitations of Jacob;<br>In His wrath He has thrown down<br>the strongholds of the daughter of Judah;<br>He has brought them down to the ground;<br>He has profaned the kingdom and its princes.  
**2:3** He has cut off in fierce anger<br>all the might of Israel;<br>He has drawn back His right hand<br>from before the enemy.<br>He has burned against Jacob<br>like a flaming fire, devouring all around.  
**2:4** He has bent His bow like an enemy,<br>with his right hand set like an adversary;<br>He has killed all who were pleasant to His eye;<br>in the tabernacle of the daughter of Zion,<br>He has poured out His fury like fire.  
**2:5** The Lord has become like an enemy;<br>He has swallowed up Israel,<br>He has swallowed up all her palaces;<br>He has destroyed her strongholds,<br>and has increased mourning and lamentation<br>in the daughter of Judah.  
**2:6** He has violently taken away His tabernacle as if it were a garden;<br>He has destroyed His place of assembly;<br>the Lord has caused the solemn feasts and Sabbaths<br>to be forgotten in Zion.<br>In his fierce indignation He has despised the king and the priest.  
**2:7** The Lord has scorned His altar,<br>He has disowned His sanctuary;<br>He has given up the walls of her palaces<br>into the hand of the enemy.<br>They have made a noise in the house of the Lord<br>as on the day of a solemn feast.  
**2:8** The Lord has purposed to destroy<br>the wall of the daughter of Zion.<br>He has stretched out a line;<br>He has not withdrawn His hand from destroying;<br>therefore He caused the rampart and the wall to lament;<br>they languished together.  
**2:9** Her gates have sunk into the ground;<br>He has destroyed and broken her bars.<br>Her king and her princes are among the nations;<br>the Law is no more,<br>and her prophets find<br>no vision from the Lord.  
**2:10** The elders of the daughter of Zion<br>sit on the ground in silence;<br>they throw dust on their heads<br>and gird themselves with sackcloth.<br>The virgins of Jerusalem<br>bow their heads to the ground.  
**2:11** My eyes fail with tears,<br>my spirit is greatly troubled;<br>my bile is poured on the ground<br>because of the destruction of the daughter of my people,<br>because the children and infants faint<br>in the streets of the city.  
**2:12** They say to their mothers,<br>“Where is grain and wine?”<br>when they faint like a wounded man<br>in the streets of the city,<br>as their life is poured out<br>on their mothers’ bosom.  
**2:13** What can I say for you,<br>to what shall I liken you,<br>O daughter of Jerusalem?<br>What shall I compare with you,<br>that I may comfort you,<br>O virgin daughter of Zion?<br>For your devastation is great like the sea;<br>who can heal you?  
**2:14** Your prophets have seen for you<br>false and deceptive visions;<br>they have not revealed your iniquity,<br>to bring back your captives,<br>but have seen for you oracles<br>that are false and misleading.  
**2:15** All who pass by<br>clap their hands at you;<br>they hiss and shake their heads<br>at the daughter of Jerusalem, saying,<br>“Is this the city that men call<br>the perfection of beauty,<br>the joy of the whole earth?”  
**2:16** All your enemies<br>have opened their mouth against you;<br>they hiss and gnash their teeth.<br>They say, “We have swallowed her up!<br>Certainly this is the day that we longed for;<br>we have found it; we have seen it.”  
**2:17** The Lord has done what He planned;<br>He has fulfilled His word<br>that He had commanded in the days of old.<br>He has thrown down and has not pitied,<br>and He has caused the enemy to rejoice over you;<br>He has exalted the power of your adversaries.  
**2:18** Their heart cried to the Lord:<br>O wall of the daughter of Zion,<br>let your tears run down<br>like a river day and night;<br>give yourself no rest,<br>your eyes no respite!  
**2:19** Arise, cry out in the night,<br>at the beginning of the watches;<br>pour out your heart like water<br>before the face of the Lord.<br>Lift your hands to Him<br>for the lives of your young children,<br>who faint for hunger<br>at the head of every street.  
**2:20** Look, O Lord, and consider!<br>To whom have You done this?<br>Should the women eat their offspring,<br>the children who were born healthy?<br>Should the priest and the prophet be killed<br>in the sanctuary of the Lord?  
**2:21** The young and the old lie<br>on the ground in the streets;<br>my virgins and my young men<br>have fallen by the sword;<br>You have killed them in the day of Your anger,<br>You have slaughtered and not pitied.  
**2:22** You have invited as to a feast day the terrors all around;<br>in the day of the Lord’s anger<br>no one escaped or survived;<br>those whom I held and raised<br>my enemy destroyed.  

## 3장

**3:1** The Prophet’s Anguish<br>I am the man who has seen affliction<br>by the rod of His wrath.  
**3:2** He has driven and brought me<br>into darkness without any light.  
**3:3** Surely against me has He turned His hand<br>continually, the whole day long.  
**3:4** My flesh and my skin He has made waste away;<br>He has broken my bones;  
**3:5** He has besieged and enveloped me<br>with gall and travail.  
**3:6** He has set me in dark places,<br>like the dead of long ago.  
**3:7** He has hedged me in so that I cannot get out;<br>He has made my chain heavy.  
**3:8** Even when I cry for help,<br>He shuts out my prayer.  
**3:9** He has blocked my ways with hewn stone;<br>He has made my paths crooked.  
**3:10** He is to me a bear lying in wait,<br>a lion in hiding.  
**3:11** He has turned aside my ways and torn me in pieces;<br>He has made me desolate.  
**3:12** He has bent His bow<br>and set me as a target for the arrow.  
**3:13** He has caused the arrows of His quiver<br>to pierce my inward parts.  
**3:14** I have become the derision of all my people,<br>their mocking song all the day.  
**3:15** He has filled me with bitterness,<br>He has sated me with wormwood.  
**3:16** He has made my teeth grind on gravel,<br>and covered me with ashes.  
**3:17** My soul is bereft of peace;<br>I have forgotten prosperity.  
**3:18** So I say, “My strength and my hope<br>from the Lord have perished.”  
**3:19** Remember my affliction and my misery,<br>the wormwood and the gall.  
**3:20** Surely my soul remembers<br>and is humbled within me.  
**3:21** But this I call to mind,<br>and therefore I have hope:  
**3:22** It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed;<br>His compassions do not fail.  
**3:23** They are new every morning;<br>great is Your faithfulness.  
**3:24** “The Lord is my portion,” says my soul,<br>“therefore I will hope in Him.”  
**3:25** The Lord is good to those who wait for Him,<br>to the soul who seeks Him.  
**3:26** It is good that a man should wait quietly<br>for the salvation of the Lord.  
**3:27** It is good for a man to bear the yoke<br>in his youth.  
**3:28** Let him sit alone in silence<br>when it is laid on him;  
**3:29** let him put his mouth in the dust—<br>there may yet be hope.  
**3:30** Let him give his cheek to the one who strikes,<br>and let him be filled with insults.  
**3:31** For the Lord<br>will not cast off forever.  
**3:32** But though He causes grief, yet He will have compassion<br>according to the abundance of His mercies.  
**3:33** For He does not afflict from His heart,<br>nor grieve the sons of men.  
**3:34** To crush underfoot<br>all the prisoners of the earth,  
**3:35** to turn aside the justice due a man<br>in the presence of the Most High,  
**3:36** to subvert a man in his cause,<br>the Lord does not approve.  
**3:37** Who is he who speaks and it comes to pass,<br>unless the Lord has commanded it?  
**3:38** Is it not from the mouth of the Most High<br>that good and bad proceed?  
**3:39** Why should a living man complain,<br>a man for the punishment of his sins?  
**3:40** Let us search and try our ways,<br>and return to the Lord!  
**3:41** Let us lift up our hearts and hands<br>to God in heaven:  
**3:42** We have transgressed and rebelled;<br>You have not pardoned.  
**3:43** You have covered Yourself with anger and pursued us;<br>You have killed and not pitied.  
**3:44** You have covered Yourself with a cloud,<br>so that no prayer should pass through.  
**3:45** You have made us filthy refuse<br>in the midst of the peoples.  
**3:46** All our enemies have opened their mouths<br>against us.  
**3:47** Panic and snare have come upon us,<br>desolation and destruction.  
**3:48** My eyes flow with rivers of tears<br>for the destruction of the daughter of my people.  
**3:49** My eyes flow and do not cease,<br>without respite,  
**3:50** until the Lord from heaven<br>looks down and sees.  
**3:51** My eyes bring suffering to my soul<br>at the fate of all the daughters of my city.  
**3:52** My enemies chased me like a bird,<br>without cause.  
**3:53** They cut off my life in the pit<br>and cast stones on me.  
**3:54** Waters flowed over my head;<br>I said, “I am cut off!”  
**3:55** I called on Your name, O Lord,<br>from the lowest pit.  
**3:56** You have heard my plea:<br>“Do not close Your ear to my cry for help!”  
**3:57** You drew near on the day I called on You,<br>and You said, “Do not fear!”  
**3:58** O Lord, You have pleaded the case for my soul;<br>You have redeemed my life.  
**3:59** O Lord, You have seen the wrong done to me;<br>judge my case.  
**3:60** You have seen all their vengeance,<br>all their schemes against me.  
**3:61** You have heard their reproach, O Lord,<br>all their schemes against me,  
**3:62** the lips of my enemies and<br>their devices against me all the day.  
**3:63** Look at their sitting down and their rising up;<br>I am their mocking song.  
**3:64** Render to them a recompense, O Lord,<br>according to the work of their hands.  
**3:65** Give them hardness of heart;<br>may Your curse be upon them!  
**3:66** In Your anger pursue and destroy them<br>from under Your heavens, O Lord!  

## 4장

**4:1** The Punishment of Zion<br>How the gold has become dim!<br>How the most fine gold has changed!<br>The stones of the sanctuary lie scattered<br>at the head of every street.  
**4:2** The precious sons of Zion,<br>comparable to fine gold,<br>how they are esteemed as earthen pots,<br>the work of a potter’s hands!  
**4:3** Even the jackals offer the breast;<br>they nurse their young,<br>yet the daughter of my people has become cruel,<br>like ostriches in the wilderness.  
**4:4** The tongue of the infant cleaves<br>to the roof of his mouth for thirst;<br>the children beg for bread,<br>but no one divides it for them.  
**4:5** Those who once ate delicacies<br>are desolate in the streets;<br>those who were brought up in scarlet<br>embrace ash heaps.  
**4:6** For the iniquity of the daughter of my people<br>is greater than the sin of Sodom,<br>which was overthrown in a moment,<br>and no hands were wrung for her.  
**4:7** Her princes were purer than snow,<br>whiter than milk;<br>their bodies were more ruddy than rubies,<br>their appearance like sapphire.  
**4:8** Their form is blacker than coal;<br>they are not recognized in the streets;<br>their skin cleaves to their bones,<br>it has become as dry as wood.  
**4:9** Those killed by the sword<br>are better off than those who die of hunger,<br>for they pine away,<br>stricken for want of the fruits of the field.  
**4:10** The hands of compassionate women<br>have boiled their own children;<br>they became their food in the destruction<br>of the daughter of my people.  
**4:11** The Lord has fulfilled His fury,<br>He has poured out His fierce anger.<br>He kindled a fire in Zion,<br>and it has devoured its foundations.  
**4:12** The kings of the earth, and all the inhabitants of the world,<br>would not have believed<br>that the adversary and the enemy could enter<br>the gates of Jerusalem.  
**4:13** This was for the sins of her prophets<br>and the iniquities of her priests,<br>who poured out in her midst the blood of the righteous.  
**4:14** They wandered,<br>blind, in the streets;<br>they have defiled themselves with blood,<br>so that no one could touch their garments.  
**4:15** They cried out to them, “Depart! Unclean!<br>Depart, depart, do not touch us!”<br>Therefore they fled and wandered;<br>men among the nations said,<br>“They shall live with us no longer.”  
**4:16** The presence of the Lord scattered them;<br>He will regard them no more;<br>they do not respect the priests,<br>nor show favor to the elders.  
**4:17** Our eyes failed us,<br>watching vainly for help;<br>in our watchtowers we watched<br>for a nation that could not save us.  
**4:18** They tracked our steps<br>so that we could not walk in our streets.<br>Our end drew near; our days were numbered,<br>for our end had come.  
**4:19** Our pursuers were swifter<br>than the eagles of the skies.<br>They pursued us on the mountains;<br>they lay in wait for us in the wilderness.  
**4:20** The breath of our life, the anointed king of the Lord,<br>was captured in their traps,<br>of whom we said, “Under his shadow<br>we shall live among the nations.”  
**4:21** Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom,<br>you who dwell in the land of Uz!<br>The cup shall also pass to you;<br>you shall become drunk and strip yourself bare.  
**4:22** The punishment of your iniquity, O daughter of Zion, is accomplished;<br>He shall exile you no longer.<br>He shall punish your iniquity, O daughter of Edom;<br>He will expose your sins!  

## 5장

**5:1** A Prayer for Restoration<br>Remember, O Lord, what has come upon us;<br>look, and see our reproach!  
**5:2** Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers,<br>our homes to foreigners.  
**5:3** We have become orphans and fatherless;<br>our mothers are like widows.  
**5:4** We must pay for the water we drink;<br>our wood is sold to us.  
**5:5** Our pursuers are at our necks;<br>we labor and have no rest.  
**5:6** We have given our hand to Egypt and to Assyria,<br>to be satisfied with bread.  
**5:7** Our fathers sinned and are no more,<br>but we bear their iniquities.  
**5:8** Slaves rule over us;<br>there is no one to deliver us from their hand.  
**5:9** We get our bread at the peril of our lives,<br>because of the sword in the wilderness.  
**5:10** Our skin is hot as an oven,<br>because of the terrible famine.  
**5:11** They ravished the women in Zion,<br>the virgins in the cities of Judah.  
**5:12** Princes were hung up by their hands,<br>the faces of elders were not honored.  
**5:13** Young men ground at the millstones;<br>boys staggered under loads of wood.  
**5:14** The elders have left the city gate,<br>the young men stopped their music.  
**5:15** The joy of our hearts has ceased;<br>our dancing has turned into mourning.  
**5:16** The crown has fallen from our head;<br>woe to us, for we have sinned!  
**5:17** For this our heart is faint;<br>for these things our eyes grow dim;  
**5:18** because of Mount Zion, which is desolate,<br>with foxes walking upon it.  
**5:19** You, O Lord, remain forever;<br>Your throne endures from generation to generation.  
**5:20** Why do You forget us forever,<br>and forsake us for so long a time?  
**5:21** Restore us to Yourself, O Lord, that we may return!<br>Renew our days as of old,  
**5:22** unless You have utterly rejected us,<br>and are very angry with us.
