# 18.욥기 (MEV)



## 1장

**1:1** The Prologue<br>There was a man in the land of Uz whose name was Job. That man was blameless and upright, fearing God and avoiding evil.  
**1:2** Seven sons and three daughters were born to him.  
**1:3** His possessions were seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred yoke of oxen, five hundred female donkeys, and very many servants. This man was the greatest of all the people of the East.  
**1:4** His sons used to go and make a feast in the house of each on his day, and they would send and call for their three sisters to eat and drink with them.  
**1:5** Now when the days of feasting had run their course, Job sent and sanctified them. He would rise up early in the morning, and he would offer burnt offerings according to the number of them all, because Job said: “It may be that my sons have sinned, and cursed God in their hearts.” Thus Job would do always.  
**1:6** Now there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the Lord, and the Adversary also came among them.  
**1:7** And the Lord said to the Adversary, “From where have you come?”<br>Then the Adversary answered the Lord, saying, “From roaming on the earth, and from walking up and down on it.”  
**1:8** And the Lord said to the Adversary, “Have you considered My servant Job, that there is none like him on the earth, a blameless and an upright man, who fears God, and avoids evil?”  
**1:9** Then the Adversary answered the Lord, saying, “Has Job feared God for nothing?  
**1:10** Have You not made a hedge around him, around his household, and around all that he has on every side? You have blessed the work of his hands, and his possessions have increased in the land.  
**1:11** But stretch out Your hand now, and touch all that he has, and he will curse You to Your face.”  
**1:12** The Lord said to the Adversary, “Look, all that he has is in your power; only do not stretch out your hand against his person.”<br>So the Adversary departed from the presence of the Lord.  
**1:13** So a day came when his sons and his daughters were eating and drinking wine in their eldest brother’s house,  
**1:14** and a messenger came to Job and said, “The oxen were plowing, and the donkeys were feeding beside them,  
**1:15** and the Sabeans attacked them, and took them away, and they killed the servants with the edge of the sword, and only I alone have escaped to tell you.”  
**1:16** While he was still speaking, another came and said, “The fire of God fell from heaven and burned up the sheep and the servants and consumed them, and I alone have escaped to tell you.”  
**1:17** While he was still speaking, another came and said, “The Chaldeans formed three companies and made a raid on the camels and have taken them away. They killed the servants with the edge of the sword, and I alone have escaped to tell you.”  
**1:18** While he was still speaking, another came and said, “Your sons and your daughters were eating and drinking wine in their eldest brother’s house,  
**1:19** and suddenly a great wind came from the wilderness and struck the four corners of the house, and it fell on the young people, and they are dead; and I alone have escaped to tell you.”  
**1:20** Then Job stood up, tore his robe, and shaved his head. He fell to the ground and worshipped.  
**1:21** He said,<br>“Naked I came from my mother’s womb,<br>and naked will I return there.<br>The Lord gave, and the Lord has taken away;<br>blessed be the name of the Lord.”  
**1:22** In all this Job did not sin, and he did not accuse God of wrongdoing.  

## 2장

**2:1** Again there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the Lord, and the Adversary came also among them to present himself before the Lord.  
**2:2** The Lord said to the Adversary, “From where do you come?”<br>And the Adversary answered the Lord, saying, “From roaming on the earth, and from walking up and down on it.”  
**2:3** The Lord said to the Adversary, “Have you considered My servant Job, that there is none like him on the earth, a blameless and an upright man, who fears God and avoids evil? He still holds fast his integrity, although you moved Me against him, to destroy him without cause.”  
**2:4** The Adversary answered the Lord, saying, “Skin for skin; yes, all that a man has he will give for his life.  
**2:5** Put forth Your hand now and touch his bone and his flesh, and he will curse You to Your face.”  
**2:6** The Lord said to the Adversary, “Very well, he is in your hand, but spare his life.”  
**2:7** Therefore, the Adversary went out from the presence of the Lord, and he afflicted Job with severe sores from the sole of his foot to the top of his head.  
**2:8** So he took a piece of broken pottery with which to scrape himself while he was sitting among the ashes.  
**2:9** His wife said to him, “Are you still maintaining your integrity? Curse God and die.”  
**2:10** He said to her, “You talk like one of the foolish women talks. Will we indeed accept the good from God but not accept the adversity?”<br>In all this Job did not sin with his lips.  
**2:11** Three friends of Job heard about all this evil that had come upon him, and each one came from his own place: Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite. They had agreed together to come to mourn with him and to comfort him.  
**2:12** They saw him from a distance and did not recognize him, so they wept aloud. Each one tore his robe, and they tossed dust into the air above their heads.  
**2:13** Then they sat down with him on the ground seven days and seven nights. Meanwhile, no one was speaking to him at all because they saw that his pain was severe.  

## 3장

**3:1** Job Laments His Birth<br>After this Job opened his mouth, and cursed the day of his birth.  
**3:2** Job said:  
**3:3** “Let the day perish in which I was born<br>and the night in which it was said, ‘A male child is conceived.’  
**3:4** As for that day, let it be darkness;<br>let God above not regard it;<br>and let not light shine upon it.  
**3:5** Let darkness and the shadow of death claim it;<br>let a cloud settle on it;<br>let the blackness of the day terrify it.  
**3:6** As for that night, let darkness capture it;<br>let it not rejoice among the days of the year;<br>let it not come into the number of the months.  
**3:7** Yes, as for that night, let it be barren!<br>Let no joyful cry come into it!  
**3:8** Let them curse it who curse any day,<br>those who are prepared to rouse Leviathan.  
**3:9** Let its morning stars be dark;<br>let it look for light, but have none;<br>let it not see the rays of dawn,  
**3:10** because it did not shut the doors of my mother’s womb,<br>nor hide trouble from my eyes.  
**3:11** “Why did I not die at birth?<br>Why did I not expire when I came out of the womb?  
**3:12** Why did her knees receive me?<br>And why her breasts that I should nurse?  
**3:13** For now I would be lying down and would be at peace;<br>I would have slept; then there would be rest for me,  
**3:14** with kings and counselors of the earth,<br>who built ruins for themselves,  
**3:15** or with princes who had gold,<br>who filled their houses with silver.  
**3:16** Or why was I not hidden like a stillborn child,<br>like infants who never saw light?  
**3:17** There the wicked will have stopped causing trouble,<br>and there the exhausted will rest.  
**3:18** Captives will relax together;<br>they do not hear the voice of the oppressor.  
**3:19** The small and great, they are there,<br>and the servant is free from his master.  
**3:20** “Why is light given to the miserable,<br>and life unto the bitter in soul,  
**3:21** who look for death, but it is not there;<br>and they search for it more than for hidden treasures;  
**3:22** who rejoice exceedingly,<br>and they are glad when they find the grave?  
**3:23** And why is light given to a man<br>whose way is hidden,<br>whom God has hedged in?  
**3:24** For my sighing comes before I eat,<br>and my groaning pours forth like the waters.  
**3:25** For the thing which I greatly feared has happened to me,<br>and that which I dreaded has come to me.  
**3:26** I am not at peace; I have no quiet,<br>I cannot rest, and turmoil has come.”  

## 4장

**4:1** Eliphaz Speaks: Job Has Sinned<br>Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered:  
**4:2** “If one attempts a word with you, will you be impatient?<br>But who can keep from speaking?  
**4:3** Surely you have instructed many,<br>and you have strengthened the weak hands.  
**4:4** Your words have raised up him who was falling,<br>and you have fortified the feeble knees.  
**4:5** But now it comes upon you, and you are weary;<br>it reaches even you, and you are terrified.  
**4:6** Is not your reverence your confidence?<br>And the integrity of your ways your hope?  
**4:7** “Remember now, who being innocent ever perished?<br>Or where were the upright ever wiped out?  
**4:8** Just like I have seen, those who plow iniquity<br>and sow trouble, reap the same.  
**4:9** By the breath of God they perish,<br>and by the blast of His anger they are destroyed.  
**4:10** The roaring of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion,<br>and the teeth of the young lions are broken.  
**4:11** The old lion perishes for lack of prey,<br>and the cubs of the lioness are scattered.  
**4:12** “Now a word was brought to me secretly,<br>and my ear received a whisper of it.  
**4:13** Amid disquieting thoughts from night visions,<br>when deep sleep falls on mortals,  
**4:14** terror and trembling came to me,<br>which made all my bones shake.  
**4:15** A breath of wind was passing before my face,<br>and the hair on my body was standing up.  
**4:16** It stood still,<br>but I could not recognize its appearance;<br>a form was in front of my eyes,<br>there was stillness, then I heard a voice saying:  
**4:17** ‘Can a mortal be more righteous than God?<br>Can a man be more pure than his Maker?  
**4:18** He does not trust in His servants,<br>and He charges His angels with error;  
**4:19** Even more, those who dwell in houses of clay,<br>whose foundation is in the dust,<br>who are crushed before the moth.  
**4:20** They are broken in pieces from morning to evening;<br>they perish forever without anyone regarding it.  
**4:21** Are not their tent ropes plucked up,<br>so they die, even without wisdom?’  

## 5장

**5:1** Eliphaz: Job Is Chastised by God<br>“Call out now; is anyone answering you?<br>And to whom among the holy ones will you turn?  
**5:2** For anger slays the foolish man,<br>and jealousy kills the gullible.  
**5:3** Yes, I saw the foolish taking root,<br>and quickly I cursed his dwelling.  
**5:4** May his children be far from safety,<br>and may they be crushed in the gate without a deliverer;  
**5:5** whose harvest the hungry eats up,<br>and takes it even out of the thorns,<br>and the thirsty captures his wealth.  
**5:6** For affliction does not come out of the dust,<br>nor does trouble sprout up out of the ground;  
**5:7** but man is born to trouble,<br>as the sparks fly upward.  
**5:8** “Indeed, I would appeal to God,<br>and before God I would set forth my case,  
**5:9** who does the great and the inscrutable,<br>wonders without number.  
**5:10** He gives rain on the surface of the earth<br>and sends water on the outdoor places.  
**5:11** He sets on high those who are lowly,<br>and those who mourn are lifted to safety.  
**5:12** He frustrates the schemes of the crafty,<br>so that their hands cannot perform their plans.  
**5:13** He catches the wise in their own craftiness,<br>and the scheme of the shrewd is swiftly stopped.  
**5:14** In the daytime they encounter darkness,<br>and at noontime they grope as in the night.  
**5:15** But the needy He saves from the sword,<br>from their mouth, and from the hand of the mighty.  
**5:16** So the helpless has hope,<br>and injustice shuts her mouth.  
**5:17** “How happy is the man whom God corrects!<br>Therefore do not despise the discipline of the Almighty,  
**5:18** for He wounds, but He applies the bandage;<br>He injures, but His hands also heal.  
**5:19** In six crises He will deliver you;<br>even in seven, disaster will not touch you.  
**5:20** In famine He will redeem you from death,<br>and in war from the power of the sword.  
**5:21** You will be hidden from the lash of the tongue,<br>and you will not fear violence when it comes.  
**5:22** You will laugh at devastation and famine,<br>and you will not fear wild animals.  
**5:23** For you will have a covenant with the stones of the field,<br>and the wild animals will be at peace with you.  
**5:24** You will know that your tent is peaceful,<br>and you will inspect your property and find nothing missing.  
**5:25** You will know that your offspring will be many<br>and your descendants as the grass of the earth.  
**5:26** You go to the grave in a full age,<br>as stalks of grain are gathered up in season.  
**5:27** “Look! We have investigated all this, and it is so;<br>hear it, and know for yourself.”  

## 6장

**6:1** Job Replies: My Complaint Is Just<br>But Job answered:  
**6:2** “Oh, that my grief were fully weighed,<br>and my calamity laid with it on the scales!  
**6:3** For now it would be heavier than the sand of the sea;<br>therefore my words are stuck in my throat.  
**6:4** For the arrows of the Almighty are within me;<br>my spirit drinks in their poison;<br>the terrors of God are arrayed against me.  
**6:5** Does the wild donkey bray when he has grass?<br>Or the ox bellow over his fodder?  
**6:6** Is tasteless food eaten without salt?<br>Or is there any taste in the white of an egg?  
**6:7** My soul refuses to touch them;<br>they are like loathsome food to me.  
**6:8** “Oh, that I might have my request,<br>and that God would grant me the thing that I long for!  
**6:9** That it would please God to crush me,<br>that He would let loose His hand and cut me off!  
**6:10** Then I would still have comfort;<br>I would revel in pain; it will not subside,<br>for I have not concealed the words of the Holy One.  
**6:11** “What strength do I have, that I should hope?<br>And what is my end, that I should prolong my life?  
**6:12** Is my strength the strength of stones?<br>Or is my flesh made of brass?  
**6:13** Is there no help within me?<br>And is success banished from me?  
**6:14** “A despairing man should be shown kindness from his friend,<br>or he forsakes the fear of the Almighty.  
**6:15** My brothers have acted deceitfully like a riverbed,<br>like the streams of the riverbeds that run dry;  
**6:16** which are dark because of the ice,<br>and into which the snow disappears.  
**6:17** In time they are scorched; they vanish!<br>When it is hot, they disappear from their place.  
**6:18** The caravans of their way turn aside;<br>they go nowhere, and they perish.  
**6:19** The caravans of Tema looked,<br>the travelers of Sheba hoped for them.  
**6:20** They were disappointed because they were confident;<br>they arrived there and were dismayed.  
**6:21** For now you are nothing;<br>you see terror and are afraid.  
**6:22** Did I say, ‘Give to me’?<br>Or, ‘Out of your wealth, bribe me’?  
**6:23** Or, ‘Deliver me from the power of the enemy’?<br>Or, ‘From the power of the oppressors, liberate me’?  
**6:24** “Teach me, and I will hold my tongue,<br>and make me understand how I have erred.  
**6:25** How forceful are right words!<br>But what does your arguing prove?  
**6:26** Do you mean to correct my words,<br>and treat my desperate words as wind?  
**6:27** Yes, you cast lots for the fatherless,<br>and you bargain over your friend.  
**6:28** “And now, please give me your attention,<br>for surely I will not lie to you.  
**6:29** Turn, I pray, let there be no injustice!<br>Yes, turn again, my righteousness still stands!  
**6:30** Is there injustice on my tongue?<br>Cannot my taste discern pernicious things?  

## 7장

**7:1** Job: My Suffering Is Endless<br>“Is there not a time of hard service for a man upon earth?<br>Are not his days also like the days of a hired worker?  
**7:2** Like a servant, he longs for the shade,<br>and like a hired worker, he looks for his wages,  
**7:3** so I have been assigned months of futility,<br>and nights of trouble have been appointed to me.  
**7:4** When I lie down, I say,<br>‘When will I arise and the night be ended?’<br>And I am full of restlessness until the dawn.  
**7:5** My flesh is covered with worms and caked with dirt;<br>my skin is broken, and has become loathsome.  
**7:6** “My days fly more swiftly than a weaver’s shuttle,<br>and are spent without hope.  
**7:7** Oh, remember that my life is a breath!<br>My eye will never again see good.  
**7:8** The eye of him who sees me will behold me no more;<br>your eyes will be on me, but I will be no more.  
**7:9** As the cloud disappears and vanishes away,<br>so he who goes down to Sheol will come up no more.  
**7:10** He will never return to his house,<br>and his place will not recognize him anymore.  
**7:11** “Therefore, I will not restrain my mouth;<br>I will speak in the anguish of my spirit;<br>I will complain in the bitterness of my soul.  
**7:12** Am I the sea, or a sea monster,<br>that You set a guard over me?  
**7:13** When I say, ‘My bed will comfort me,<br>my couch will ease my complaint,’  
**7:14** then You scare me with dreams<br>and terrify me with visions,  
**7:15** so that my soul chooses strangling,<br>even death rather than my life.  
**7:16** I loathe my life; I would not live forever;<br>let me alone, for my days are emptiness.  
**7:17** “What is man, that You should exalt him,<br>and that You should set Your heart on him,  
**7:18** and that You should visit him every morning,<br>and test him every moment?  
**7:19** How long until You look away from me?<br>Will You not let me alone until I swallow my saliva?  
**7:20** Have I sinned? What am I doing to You,<br>O You watcher of men?<br>Why have You set me as Your target,<br>so that I am a burden to myself?  
**7:21** And why do You not pardon my transgression<br>and take away my iniquity?<br>For now I will lie down in the dust;<br>and You will seek me diligently, but I will not be.”  

## 8장

**8:1** Bildad Speaks: Job Should Repent<br>Then Bildad the Shuhite answered:  
**8:2** “How long will you speak these things,<br>and the words of your mouth be like a strong wind?  
**8:3** Does God pervert judgment?<br>Or does the Almighty pervert justice?  
**8:4** If your children sinned against Him,<br>He cast them away for their transgression.  
**8:5** If you yourself would seek God earnestly,<br>and seek favor from the Almighty,  
**8:6** if you were pure and upright,<br>surely now He would rouse Himself on your behalf,<br>and He would prosper your righteous dwelling.  
**8:7** Though your beginning was small,<br>your end will increase greatly.  
**8:8** “Please, ask the former generation,<br>and prepare yourself for what their fathers searched out;  
**8:9** for we were born but yesterday and know nothing,<br>because our days on earth are a shadow.  
**8:10** Will they not teach you, and tell you,<br>and bring forth words out of their heart?  
**8:11** Can the papyrus grow up without a marsh?<br>Can the reed grow without water?  
**8:12** While it is yet green and not cut down,<br>it withers before any other plant.  
**8:13** So are the paths of all who forget God;<br>and the hypocrite’s hope will perish,  
**8:14** whose confidence will be cut off,<br>and whose trust will be a spider’s web.  
**8:15** He will lean upon his house, but it will not stand;<br>he will hold it fast, but it will not endure.  
**8:16** He is green before the sun,<br>and his branch shoots forth in his garden.  
**8:17** His roots are wrapped around the rock heap,<br>and he sees the place of stones.  
**8:18** If he is uprooted from his place,<br>then it will deny him, saying, ‘I have not seen you.’  
**8:19** See, this is the joy of his way,<br>and out of the ground others will grow.  
**8:20** “Surely, God will not cast away a perfect man,<br>nor will He strengthen the evildoers,  
**8:21** until He fills your mouth with laughing,<br>and your lips with rejoicing.  
**8:22** Those who hate you will be clothed with shame,<br>and the dwelling place of the wicked will come to nothing.”  

## 9장

**9:1** Job Replies: There Is No Mediator<br>Then Job answered:  
**9:2** “Truly, I know it is so,<br>but how can a man be righteous with God?  
**9:3** If one would dispute with Him,<br>he cannot answer Him once in a thousand times.  
**9:4** He is wise in heart and mighty in strength.<br>Who has hardened himself against Him and prospered?  
**9:5** He who removes mountains, and they know not,<br>who overturns them in His anger.  
**9:6** He who shakes the earth out of its place,<br>and its pillars tremble.  
**9:7** He who commands the sun, and it does not rise;<br>he seals off the stars.  
**9:8** He who alone spreads out the heavens,<br>and treads on the waves of the sea.  
**9:9** He who makes the Bear, Orion, and Pleiades,<br>and the constellations of the south.  
**9:10** He who does great things, beyond discovery,<br>yes, and wonders beyond number.  
**9:11** Yes, He would cross before me, and I would not see Him;<br>He would pass on by, but I would not perceive Him.  
**9:12** Yes, He takes away; who can hinder Him?<br>Who will say to Him, ‘What are You doing?’  
**9:13** God will not withdraw His anger.<br>The proud helpers bow down beneath Him.  
**9:14** “How, then, can I myself answer Him,<br>and choose my words to reason with Him?  
**9:15** Even if I were righteous I could not answer;<br>I would plead to my Judge for favor.  
**9:16** If I called, and He answered me,<br>I would not believe that He had listened to my voice.  
**9:17** For He crushes me with a storm<br>and multiplies my wounds without cause.  
**9:18** He will not allow me to get my breath,<br>but fills me with bitterness.  
**9:19** If it is a matter of strength, indeed, He is strong;<br>and if of justice, who will set me a time to plead?  
**9:20** Though I were righteous, my own mouth would condemn me;<br>though I were perfect, it would prove me perverse.  
**9:21** “Though I were perfect,<br>I would not know myself;<br>I would despise my life.  
**9:22** It is all one thing; therefore I said,<br>‘He destroys both the perfect and the wicked.’  
**9:23** If the whip kills suddenly,<br>He will laugh at the trial of the innocent.  
**9:24** The earth is given into the hand of the wicked.<br>He covers the faces of its judges.<br>If it is not He, then who is it?  
**9:25** “Now my days are swifter than a runner;<br>they flee away; they see no good.  
**9:26** They pass by like reed skiffs,<br>like an eagle rushing upon its prey.  
**9:27** If I say, ‘I will forget my complaint;<br>I will leave off my sad face and brighten up,’  
**9:28** I am afraid of all my sorrows;<br>I know that You will not hold me innocent.  
**9:29** If I am guilty,<br>why then do I labor in vain?  
**9:30** If I wash myself with snow water<br>and cleanse my hands with soap,  
**9:31** yet You will plunge me into the pit,<br>and my own clothes will abhor me.  
**9:32** “For He is not a man as I am, that I should answer Him,<br>and we should come together in judgment.  
**9:33** Nor is there a mediator between us,<br>who may lay his hand upon us both.  
**9:34** Let Him take His rod away from me,<br>and let not dread of Him terrify me.  
**9:35** Then I would speak and not fear Him,<br>but it is not so with me.  

## 10장

**10:1** Job: I Abhor My Life<br>“My soul loathes my life;<br>I will freely give my complaint,<br>I will speak in the bitterness of my soul.  
**10:2** I will say to God, Do not condemn me;<br>show me why You contend with me.  
**10:3** Is it good for You that You should oppress,<br>that You should despise the work of Your hands<br>and smile on the counsel of the wicked?  
**10:4** Do You have eyes of flesh?<br>Or do You see as man sees?  
**10:5** Are Your days as the days of man?<br>Are Your years as the days of a mortal,  
**10:6** that You inquire after my iniquity<br>and search after my sin?  
**10:7** You know that I am not wicked,<br>and there is none who can deliver out of Your hand.  
**10:8** “Your hands have shaped me and made me completely,<br>yet You destroy me.  
**10:9** Remember, I pray,<br>that You have made me as the clay.<br>And would You return me to dust?  
**10:10** Have You not poured me out as milk<br>and curdled me like cheese?  
**10:11** You have clothed me with skin and flesh,<br>and have knit me together with bones and sinews.  
**10:12** You have granted me life and loyal love,<br>and Your care has preserved my spirit.  
**10:13** “These things You have hid in Your heart.<br>I know that this is with You.  
**10:14** If I have sinned, then You would watch me,<br>and You would not acquit me from my iniquity.  
**10:15** If I am wicked, woe unto me;<br>and if I am righteous, yet will I not lift up my head.<br>I am full of shame;<br>look at my affliction!  
**10:16** For if my head is lifted up, You would hunt me like a lion,<br>and again You show Yourself marvelous to me.  
**10:17** You renew Your witnesses against me<br>and increase Your indignation upon me.<br>Your troops come against me.  
**10:18** “Why then did You bring me forth out of the womb?<br>Oh, that I had died, and no eye had seen me!  
**10:19** I should have been as though I had not been;<br>I should have been carried from the womb to the grave.  
**10:20** Are not my days few? Stop then,<br>and leave me alone that I may cheer up a little,  
**10:21** before I go and do not return,<br>even to the land of darkness and the shadow of death,  
**10:22** a land of darkness, as darkness itself;<br>and of the shadow of death, without any order,<br>and where the light is as thick darkness.”  

## 11장

**11:1** Zophar Speaks: Job Is Guilty<br>Then Zophar the Naamathite answered:

**41:3** Will he make many supplications to you?<br>Will he speak soft words to you?  
**41:4** Will he make a covenant with you?<br>Will you take him for a servant forever?  
**41:5** Will you play with him as with a bird?<br>Or will you put him on a leash for your maidens?  
**41:6** Will your companions make a banquet of him?<br>Will they divide him among the merchants?  
**41:7** Can you fill his skin with harpoons<br>or his head with fishing spears?  
**41:8** Lay your hand on him;<br>remember the battle—you will do it no more.  
**41:9** Notice, any hope of overcoming him is in vain;<br>shall not one be overwhelmed at the sight of him?  
**41:10** No one is so fierce that he dares to stir him up.<br>Who then is able to stand before Me?  
**41:11** Who has preceded Me that I should repay him?<br>Everything under heaven is Mine.  
**41:12** “I will not conceal his limbs,<br>nor his power, nor his graceful proportions.  
**41:13** Who can remove his outer garment?<br>Or who can approach him with a double bridle?  
**41:14** Who can open the jaws of his face?<br>His teeth are terrible all around.  
**41:15** His scales are his pride,<br>shut up tightly as with a seal.  
**41:16** One is so near to another<br>that no air can come between them.  
**41:17** They are joined to each other;<br>they stick together that they cannot be separated.  
**41:18** His sneezing flashes forth light,<br>and his eyes are like the eyelids of the morning.  
**41:19** Out of his mouth go burning lights,<br>and sparks of fire leap out.  
**41:20** Out of his nostrils goes smoke<br>as out of a seething pot or cauldron.  
**41:21** His breath kindles coals,<br>and a flame goes out of his mouth.  
**41:22** In his neck remains strength,<br>and sorrow is turned into joy before him.  
**41:23** The folds of his flesh are joined together;<br>they are firm on him; they cannot be moved.  
**41:24** His heart is as firm as a stone,<br>yes, as hard as a piece of the lower millstone.  
**41:25** When he raises up himself even the gods are afraid;<br>because of his crashings they are beside themselves.  
**41:26** The sword that reaches him cannot avail,<br>nor does the spear, the arrow, or the javelin.  
**41:27** He counts iron as straw,<br>and brass as rotten wood.  
**41:28** The arrow cannot make him flee;<br>slingstones are turned into stubble by him.  
**41:29** Arrows are counted as straw;<br>he laughs at the shaking of a spear.  
**41:30** Sharp stones are his underside;<br>he leaves a mark in the mire like a sharp threshing sledge.  
**41:31** He makes the deep to boil like a pot;<br>he makes the sea like a pot of ointment.  
**41:32** He leaves a shining wake behind him;<br>one would think the deep had white hair.  
**41:33** On earth there is nothing like him,<br>a creature made without fear.  
**41:34** He beholds all high things;<br>he is a king over all the children of pride.”  

## 42장

**42:1** Job’s Repentance<br>Then Job answered the Lord and said:  
**42:2** “I know that You can do everything,<br>and that no thought can be withheld from You.  
**42:3** ‘Who is he who hides counsel without knowledge?’<br>Therefore I have uttered what I did not understand,<br>things too wonderful for me which I did not know.  
**42:4** “ ‘Hear, and I will speak;<br>I will question you,<br>and you declare to Me.’  
**42:5** I have heard of You by the hearing of the ear,<br>but now my eye sees You.  
**42:6** Therefore I abhor myself,<br>and repent in dust and ashes.”  
**42:7** Epilogue<br>And so it was, that after the Lord had spoken these words to Job, the Lord said to Eliphaz the Temanite, “My wrath is kindled against you and against your two friends, for you have not spoken of Me what is right as My servant Job has.  
**42:8** And now, take for yourselves seven bulls and seven rams, and go to My servant Job, and offer up for yourselves a burnt offering; and My servant Job will pray for you. For him I will accept, lest I deal with you according to your folly, in that you have not spoken of Me the thing which is right like My servant Job.”  
**42:9** So Eliphaz the Temanite, and Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite went and did as the Lord had commanded them; the Lord also accepted Job.  
**42:10** And the Lord restored the fortunes of Job when he prayed for his friends, and also the Lord gave Job twice as much as he had before.  
**42:11** Then all of his brothers, and all his sisters, and all of those who had been of acquaintance before came to Job, and they ate bread with him in his house, and they consoled him and comforted him for all the adversity that the Lord had brought upon him. Every man also gave him a piece of money and an earring of gold.  
**42:12** So the Lord blessed the latter days of Job more than his beginning, for he had fourteen thousand sheep, and six thousand camels, and a thousand yoke of oxen, and a thousand female donkeys.  
**42:13** He had also seven sons and three daughters.  
**42:14** He called the name of the first Jemimah; and the name of the second Keziah; and the name of the third Keren-Happuch.  
**42:15** In all the land, there were no women as beautiful as the daughters of Job, and their father gave them an inheritance along with their brothers.  
**42:16** After this, Job lived a hundred and forty years, and saw his sons, and their sons to the fourth generation.  
**42:17** So Job died, being old and full of days.
