# 35.하박국 (MEV)



## 1장

**1:1** The oracle that Habakkuk the prophet saw.  
**1:2** Habakkuk’s Complaint<br>O Lord, how long shall I cry,<br>and You will not hear?<br>Or cry to You, “Violence!”<br>and You will not save?  
**1:3** Why do You make me see wickedness,<br>and cause me to see trouble?<br>Plundering and violence are before me;<br>strife and contention arise.  
**1:4** Therefore the law is powerless,<br>and justice never goes forth.<br>For the wicked surround the righteous;<br>therefore injustice proceeds.  
**1:5** The Lord’s Answer<br>Look among the nations, and watch—<br>wonder and be amazed!<br>For I am doing a work in your days<br>that you would not believe,<br>though it were told you.  
**1:6** For I am raising up the Chaldeans,<br>that bitter and hasty nation<br>which marches through the breadth of the earth,<br>to possess dwelling places that are not theirs.  
**1:7** They are terrible and dreadful;<br>their justice and their dignity<br>proceed from themselves.  
**1:8** Their horses are swifter than leopards,<br>more fierce than evening wolves.<br>Their horsemen charge on;<br>their horsemen come from afar;<br>they fly like the eagle that hastens to eat.  
**1:9** They all come for violence;<br>their forces advance like the east wind.<br>They gather captives like sand.  
**1:10** They scoff at kings,<br>and they scorn rulers.<br>They deride every stronghold,<br>for they build up siege ramps to capture it.  
**1:11** Then their mind changes,<br>and they transgress and commit offense;<br>their own power is their god.  
**1:12** Habakkuk’s Second Complaint<br>Are You not from everlasting,<br>O Lord my God, my Holy One? We will not die.<br>O Lord, You have appointed them for judgment;<br>and You, O Rock, have established them for correction.  
**1:13** Your eyes are too pure to look on evil,<br>and You cannot look on wickedness.<br>Why do You look on those who deal treacherously,<br>and hold Your tongue when the wicked<br>devours the man who is more righteous than he?  
**1:14** You make men like fish of the sea,<br>like crawling things that have no ruler.  
**1:15** They bring all of them up with a hook,<br>they catch them in their net;<br>they gather them in their dragnet.<br>Therefore they rejoice and are glad.  
**1:16** Therefore they sacrifice to their net<br>and burn incense to their dragnet;<br>for by them their portion is extravagant,<br>and their food plentiful.  
**1:17** Shall they continue to empty their net,<br>and continually kill the nations while not sparing anyone?  

## 2장

**2:1** I will stand at my watch<br>and station myself on the watchtower;<br>and I will keep watch to see what He will say to me,<br>and what I will answer when I am reproved.  
**2:2** The Just Shall Live by Faith<br>And the Lord answered me:<br>Write the vision,<br>and make it plain on tablets,<br>that he who reads it may run.  
**2:3** For the vision is yet for an appointed time;<br>but it speaks of the end,<br>and does not lie.<br>If it delays, wait for it;<br>it will surely come, it will not delay.  
**2:4** Look, his soul is lifted up;<br>it is not upright in him;<br>but the just shall live by his faith.  
**2:5** Indeed, wine betrays the proud man,<br>who does not stay at home.<br>He enlarges his appetite as Sheol,<br>and like death he is never satisfied.<br>He gathers to himself all nations<br>and collects for himself all peoples.  
**2:6** Woe to the Wicked<br>Shall not all these take up a taunt against him, with satire and riddles, and say,<br>“Woe to him who increases what is not his—how long?<br>And to him who loads himself with heavy debts!”  
**2:7** Shall not your debtors rise up suddenly,<br>and those awake who oppress you?<br>Then you will be their plunder.  
**2:8** Because you have plundered many nations,<br>all the remnant of the people will plunder you,<br>because of the bloodshed of men and violence of the land,<br>of the cities and all who live in them.  
**2:9** “Woe to him who gets evil gain for his house,<br>to set his nest on high,<br>to be delivered from the power of calamity!”  
**2:10** You have given shameful counsel to your house<br>by cutting off many peoples,<br>and forfeiting your life.  
**2:11** For the stone will cry out from the wall,<br>and the beam of the woodwork will answer it.  
**2:12** “Woe to him who builds a town with bloodshed<br>and establishes a city on iniquity!”  
**2:13** Is it not from the Lord of Hosts<br>that the people labor to feed fire,<br>and the nations weary themselves for nothing?  
**2:14** For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the Lord,<br>as the waters cover the seas.  
**2:15** “Woe to him who makes his neighbor drink,<br>pouring out your poison until they are drunk,<br>that you may look on their nakedness!”  
**2:16** You will be filled with shame instead of glory.<br>You yourself—drink and show your uncircumcision!<br>The cup of the Lord’s right hand will be turned against you,<br>and utter shame will come on your glory!  
**2:17** The violence done to Lebanon will cover you,<br>as will the plunder of beasts that terrified them,<br>because of the bloodshed of men and violence of the land,<br>of the cities and all who live in them.  
**2:18** What profit is a carved image when its maker has carved it,<br>a cast image, and a teacher of lies,<br>that its maker trusts in what he has shaped<br>when he makes mute idols?  
**2:19** Woe to him who says to the wood, “Awake!”<br>To the silent stone, “Arise!” Can it teach?<br>It is overlaid with gold and silver,<br>and there is no breath at all in it.  
**2:20** But the Lord is in His holy temple.<br>Let all the earth keep silence before Him.  

## 3장

**3:1** The Prayer of Habakkuk<br>A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, on Shigionoth.  
**3:2** O Lord, I have heard the report of You,<br>and was afraid;<br>O Lord, revive Your work<br>in the midst of the years!<br>In the midst of these years<br>make them known;<br>in wrath remember mercy.  
**3:3** God came from Teman,<br>and the Holy One from Mount Paran. Selah<br>His glory covered the heavens,<br>and the earth was full of His praise.  
**3:4** His brightness was like the light;<br>rays flashed from His hand,<br>and there His power was hidden.  
**3:5** Pestilence went out before Him,<br>and plague followed His feet.  
**3:6** He stood and measured the earth;<br>He looked and shook the nations.<br>The eternal mountains were scattered,<br>the perpetual hills bowed.<br>His ways are everlasting.  
**3:7** I saw the tents of Cushan in affliction;<br>the curtains of the land of Midian trembled.  
**3:8** Were you displeased with the rivers, O Lord?<br>Was Your anger against the rivers,<br>and was Your wrath against the sea<br>when You rode on Your horses,<br>on Your chariots of salvation?  
**3:9** You made bare Your bow;<br>oaths were the arrows at Your command.     Selah<br>You divided the earth with the rivers.  
**3:10** The mountains saw You and trembled;<br>the overflowing water passed by.<br>The deep lifted its voice,<br>and lifted its hands on high.  
**3:11** The sun and moon stood still in their places;<br>at the light of Your arrows they went,<br>at the flash of Your glittering spear.  
**3:12** You marched throughout the earth in indignation;<br>You threshed the nations in anger.  
**3:13** You went forth to deliver Your people,<br>to deliver Your anointed one.<br>You wounded the head of the house of the wicked,<br>laying him bare from head to foot.     Selah  
**3:14** You pierced with his own arrows<br>the leaders of his villages.<br>They came like a whirlwind to scatter me;<br>their rejoicing was like devouring the poor in secret.  
**3:15** You trampled the sea with Your horses,<br>through the wakes of great waters.  
**3:16** I heard, and my body trembled;<br>my lips quivered at the sound;<br>rottenness entered my bones;<br>my legs tremble beneath me.<br>Yet I will wait quietly for the day<br>when calamity comes on the people invading us.  
**3:17** A Hymn of Faith<br>Though the fig tree does not blossom,<br>nor fruit be on the vines;<br>though the yield of the olive fails,<br>and the fields produce no food;<br>though the flocks are cut off from the fold,<br>and there be no herd in the stalls—  
**3:18** yet I will rejoice in the Lord;<br>I will exult in the God of my salvation.  
**3:19** The Lord God is my strength;<br>He will make my feet like hinds’ feet,<br>and He will make me walk on my high places.<br>To the Music Director: with my stringed instruments.
