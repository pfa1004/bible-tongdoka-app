# 23.이사야 (MEV)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision of Isaiah the son of Amoz, which he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.  
**1:2** The Wickedness of Judah<br>Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth.<br>For the Lord has spoken:<br>I have nourished and brought up children,<br>and they have rebelled against Me;  
**1:3** the ox knows his owner,<br>and the donkey his master’s crib,<br>but Israel does not know;<br>My people do not consider.  
**1:4** Alas, sinful nation,<br>a people laden with iniquity,<br>a brood of evildoers,<br>children who deal corruptly!<br>They have forsaken the Lord,<br>they have provoked the Holy One of Israel to anger,<br>they are estranged and backward.  
**1:5** Why should you be beaten again,<br>that you revolt more and more?<br>The whole head is sick,<br>and the whole heart faint.  
**1:6** From the sole of the foot even to the head<br>there is no soundness in it.<br>Wounds, bruises,<br>and open sores;<br>they have not been closed,<br>nor bandaged, nor soothed with oil.  
**1:7** Your country is desolate,<br>your cities are burned with fire;<br>strangers devour your land<br>in your presence;<br>and it is desolate, as overthrown by strangers.  
**1:8** The daughter of Zion is left<br>like a shelter in a vineyard,<br>like a hut in a field of melons,<br>like a besieged city.  
**1:9** Unless the Lord of Hosts<br>had left to us a very small remnant,<br>we would have been as Sodom;<br>we would have been like Gomorrah.  
**1:10** Hear the word of the Lord,<br>you rulers of Sodom;<br>listen to the law of our God,<br>you people of Gomorrah:  
**1:11** For what purpose is the multitude<br>of your sacrifices to Me? says the Lord.<br>I am full of the burnt offerings of rams,<br>and the fat of fed animals.<br>I do not delight in the blood of bulls,<br>or of lambs, or of male goats.  
**1:12** When you come to appear before Me,<br>who has required this at your hand,<br>to trample My courts?  
**1:13** Bring no more vain offerings;<br>incense is an abomination to Me.<br>New Moons, Sabbaths, and convocations—<br>I cannot bear with evil assemblies.  
**1:14** My soul hates<br>your New Moons and your appointed feasts;<br>they are a burden to Me;<br>I am weary of bearing them.  
**1:15** When you reach out your hands,<br>I will hide My eyes from you;<br>even when you make many prayers,<br>I will not hear.<br>Your hands are full of blood.  
**1:16** Wash yourselves, make yourselves clean;<br>put away the evil from your deeds, from before My eyes.<br>Cease to do evil,  
**1:17** learn to do good; seek justice,<br>relieve the oppressed;<br>judge the fatherless,<br>plead for the widow.  
**1:18** Come now, and let us reason together,<br>says the Lord.<br>Though your sins be as scarlet,<br>they shall be as white as snow;<br>though they be red like crimson,<br>they shall be as wool.  
**1:19** If you are willing and obedient,<br>you shall eat the good of the land;  
**1:20** but if you refuse and rebel,<br>you shall be devoured with the sword;<br>for the mouth of the Lord has spoken it.  
**1:21** The Degenerate City<br>How the faithful city<br>has become a prostitute!<br>She was full of justice;<br>righteousness lodged in her,<br>but now murderers.  
**1:22** Your silver has become dross,<br>your wine mixed with water.  
**1:23** Your princes are rebellious<br>and companions of thieves;<br>everyone loves a bribe<br>and follows after rewards.<br>They do not defend the fatherless,<br>nor does the cause of the widow come before them.  
**1:24** Therefore the Lord, the Lord of Hosts,<br>the Mighty One of Israel, says:<br>Ah, I will get relief from My adversaries,<br>and avenge Myself on My enemies.  
**1:25** And I will turn My hand against you,<br>thoroughly purge away your dross,<br>and take away all your impurities.  
**1:26** I will restore your judges as at the first,<br>and your counselors as at the beginning.<br>Afterward you shall be called<br>the city of righteousness,<br>a faithful town.  
**1:27** Zion shall be redeemed with justice<br>and her converts with righteousness.  
**1:28** But the destruction of the transgressors and sinners shall be together,<br>and those who forsake the Lord shall be consumed.  
**1:29** For they shall be ashamed of the oaks<br>which you have desired,<br>and you shall be humiliated for the gardens<br>that you have chosen.  
**1:30** For you shall be as an oak whose leaf fades,<br>and as a garden that has no water.  
**1:31** The strong shall be as tinder,<br>and his work like a spark;<br>they will both burn together,<br>and no one will quench them.  

## 2장

**2:1** The Mountain of the Lord<br>The word that Isaiah the son of Amoz saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem.  
**2:2** In the last days,<br>the mountain of the Lord’s house shall be established<br>on the top of the mountains,<br>and shall be exalted above the hills,<br>and all nations shall flow to it.  
**2:3** Many people shall go and say,<br>“Come, and let us go up to the mountain of the Lord,<br>to the house of the God of Jacob,<br>and He will teach us of His ways,<br>and we will walk in His paths.”<br>For out of Zion shall go forth the law,<br>and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.  
**2:4** He shall judge among the nations,<br>and shall rebuke many peoples;<br>and they shall beat their swords into plowshares,<br>and their spears into pruning hooks;<br>nation shall not lift up sword against nation,<br>nor shall they learn war any more.  
**2:5** O house of Jacob, come,<br>and let us walk in the light of the Lord.  
**2:6** The Day of the Lord<br>For You have forsaken Your people,<br>the house of Jacob,<br>because they are replenished from the east,<br>and are soothsayers like the Philistines,<br>and they please themselves with the children of strangers.  
**2:7** Their land also is full of silver and gold,<br>and there is no end to their treasures;<br>their land is also full of horses,<br>and there is no end to their chariots.  
**2:8** Their land also is full of idols;<br>they worship the work of their own hands,<br>that which their own fingers have made.  
**2:9** And the common man bows down,<br>and the great man humbles himself;<br>therefore, do not forgive them.  
**2:10** Enter into the rock,<br>and hide in the dust<br>from the fear of the Lord<br>and from the glory of His majesty.  
**2:11** The lofty looks of man shall be humbled,<br>and the haughtiness of men shall be bowed down,<br>and the Lord alone shall be exalted in that day.  
**2:12** For the day of the Lord of Hosts shall be<br>upon everything that is proud and lofty,<br>and upon everything that is lifted up,<br>and it shall be brought low;  
**2:13** and it will be upon all the cedars of Lebanon that are high and lifted up,<br>and upon all the oaks of Bashan,  
**2:14** and upon all the high mountains,<br>and upon all the hills that are lifted up,  
**2:15** and upon all the high towers,<br>and upon every fenced wall,  
**2:16** and upon all the ships of Tarshish,<br>and upon all pleasant sloops.  
**2:17** The loftiness of man shall be humbled,<br>and the haughtiness of men shall be brought low;<br>the Lord alone will be exalted in that day;  
**2:18** the idols He shall utterly abolish.  
**2:19** They shall go into the holes of the rocks,<br>and into the caves of the earth,<br>from the fear of the Lord,<br>and from the glory of His majesty,<br>when He shall arise to shake the earth mightily.  
**2:20** In that day a man shall cast away<br>his idols of silver and his idols of gold,<br>which they made for themselves to worship,<br>to the moles and to the bats,  
**2:21** to enter the caverns of the rocks,<br>and into the clefts of the cliffs,<br>from the terror of the Lord,<br>and from the glory of His majesty,<br>when He arises to shake the earth mightily.  
**2:22** Cease regarding man,<br>whose breath is in his nostrils;<br>for why should he be esteemed?  

## 3장

**3:1** Judgment on Judah and Jerusalem<br>For now the Lord,<br>the Lord of Hosts,<br>takes away from Jerusalem and from Judah<br>the stock and the store,<br>the whole supply of bread, and the whole supply of water;  
**3:2** the mighty man and the man of war,<br>the judge and the prophet,<br>and the prudent and the ancient,  
**3:3** the captain of fifty and the honorable man,<br>the counselor and the skillful artisan, and the eloquent orator.  
**3:4** I will give youths to be their princes,<br>and unweaned children shall rule over them.  
**3:5** The people shall be oppressed,<br>every one by another, and every one by his neighbor;<br>the youth shall behave himself proudly against the elder,<br>and the inferior against the honorable.  
**3:6** When a man shall take hold of his brother<br>in the house of his father saying,<br>“You have clothing; be our ruler,<br>and let this ruin be under your power,”  
**3:7** in that day he shall swear saying,<br>“I will not be a healer.<br>For in my house is neither bread nor clothing;<br>do not make me a ruler of the people.”  
**3:8** For Jerusalem is ruined,<br>and Judah is fallen,<br>because their tongue and their deeds are against the Lord,<br>to provoke the eyes of His glory.  
**3:9** The expression of their countenance witnesses against them,<br>and they declare their sin as Sodom;<br>they hide it not.<br>Woe to them!<br>For they have brought evil on themselves.  
**3:10** Say to the righteous that it shall be well with them,<br>for they shall eat the fruit of their deeds.  
**3:11** Woe to the wicked! It shall be ill with him,<br>for the reward of his hands shall be given him.  
**3:12** As for My people, children are their oppressors,<br>and women rule over them.<br>O My people, those who lead you cause you to err,<br>and destroy the way of your paths.  
**3:13** The Lord stands up to plead<br>and stands to judge the people.  
**3:14** The Lord will enter into judgment<br>with the elders and the princes of His people:<br>For you have eaten up the vineyard;<br>the spoil of the poor is in your houses.  
**3:15** What do you mean that you beat My people to pieces<br>and grind the faces of the poor?<br>says the Lord God of Hosts.  
**3:16** Moreover the Lord says:<br>Because the daughters of Zion are haughty<br>and walk with outstretched necks<br>and wanton eyes,<br>walking and mincing as they go,<br>and making a tinkling with their feet,  
**3:17** therefore, the Lord will strike with a scab the crown of the head of the daughters of Zion,<br>and the Lord will make their foreheads bare.  
**3:18** In that day the Lord will take away the finery of the ankle ornaments, and the headbands, and the crescent ornaments,  
**3:19** the chains, and the bracelets, and the veils,  
**3:20** the bonnets, the leg ornaments, the sashes, perfume boxes, amulets,  
**3:21** the finger rings, nose rings,  
**3:22** festal robes, outer tunics, cloaks, purses,  
**3:23** hand mirrors, and undergarments, hoods, and veils.  
**3:24** Instead of sweet perfume there shall be a stench;<br>and instead of a belt, a rope;<br>and instead of well-set hair, baldness;<br>and instead of fine clothes, a girding of sackcloth;<br>and branding instead of beauty.  
**3:25** Your men shall fall by the sword,<br>and your mighty in the war.  
**3:26** Her gates shall lament and mourn,<br>and she, being desolate, shall sit on the ground.  

## 4장

**4:1** And in that day seven women<br>shall take hold of one man, saying,<br>“We will eat our own bread,<br>and wear our own apparel,<br>only let us be called by your name,<br>to take away our reproach.”  
**4:2** The Branch of the Lord<br>In that day the branch of the Lord shall be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the earth shall be excellent and comely for those of Israel who have escaped.  
**4:3** He who is left in Zion and he who remains in Jerusalem shall be called holy, even everyone who is written among the living in Jerusalem.  
**4:4** When the Lord has washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion and has purged the blood of Jerusalem from the midst by the spirit of justice and by the spirit of burning,  
**4:5** then the Lord will create upon every dwelling place of Mount Zion, and upon her assemblies, a cloud and smoke by day and the shining of a flaming fire by night. For over all the glory shall be a covering.  
**4:6** There shall be a tabernacle for a shadow in the daytime from the heat, and for a place of refuge, and for a shelter from storm and from rain.  

## 5장

**5:1** The Song of the Vineyard<br>Now I will sing to my well-beloved a song of my beloved<br>concerning His vineyard:<br>My well-beloved has a vineyard<br>in a very fruitful hill.  
**5:2** And He fenced it, and removed its stones,<br>and planted it with the choicest vine.<br>And He built a tower in the midst of it,<br>and also made a winepress in it;<br>and He expected it to bring forth good grapes,<br>but it brought forth wild grapes.  
**5:3** Now, O inhabitants of Jerusalem and men of Judah,<br>judge between My vineyard and Me.  
**5:4** What more could have been done to My vineyard<br>that I have not done in it?<br>Why, when I expected it to bring forth good grapes,<br>did it bring forth wild grapes?  
**5:5** So now I will tell you<br>what I will do to My vineyard:<br>I will take away its hedge,<br>and it shall be consumed;<br>I will break down its wall,<br>and it shall be trodden down.  
**5:6** And I will lay it waste:<br>It shall not be pruned or dug,<br>but briers and thorns shall come up.<br>I will also command the clouds<br>that they rain no rain on it.  
**5:7** For the vineyard of the Lord of Hosts<br>is the house of Israel,<br>and the men of Judah<br>His pleasant plant.<br>Thus He looked for justice, but saw oppression;<br>for righteousness, but heard a cry.  
**5:8** Woes and Judgment<br>Woe to those who join house to house,<br>who add field to field,<br>until there is no more space<br>where they may live alone in the midst of the land!  
**5:9** In my ears the Lord of Hosts said:<br>Truly, many houses shall be desolate,<br>even great and beautiful, without inhabitants.  
**5:10** For ten acres of vineyard shall yield one bath,<br>and the homer of seed shall yield an ephah.  
**5:11** Woe to those who rise up early in the morning<br>that they may pursue strong drink;<br>who continue late in the evening<br>until wine inflames them!  
**5:12** The lyre and the harp, the tambourine and pipe,<br>and wine are in their feasts;<br>but they do not regard the deeds of the Lord,<br>or consider the work of His hands.  
**5:13** Therefore My people go into captivity<br>because they have no knowledge;<br>and their honorable men are famished,<br>and their multitude dried up with thirst.  
**5:14** Therefore Sheol has enlarged itself<br>and opened its mouth without measure;<br>so their glory, and their multitude, and their pomp,<br>and he who rejoices shall descend into it.  
**5:15** The common man shall be brought down,<br>and the great man shall be humbled,<br>and the eyes of the lofty shall be humbled.  
**5:16** But the Lord of Hosts shall be exalted in judgment,<br>and God who is holy shall be hallowed in righteousness.  
**5:17** Then the lambs shall feed in their pasture,<br>and strangers shall eat in the waste places of the wealthy.  
**5:18** Woe to those who draw iniquity with cords of falsehood<br>and sin as if with a cart rope,  
**5:19** who say, “Let Him make speed<br>and hasten His work,<br>that we may see it;<br>and let the counsel of the Holy One of Israel<br>draw near and come,<br>that we may know it!”  
**5:20** Woe to those who call evil good,<br>and good evil;<br>who exchange darkness for light,<br>and light for darkness;<br>who exchange bitter for sweet,<br>and sweet for bitter!  
**5:21** Woe to those who are wise in their own eyes<br>and prudent in their own sight!  
**5:22** Woe to those who are mighty to drink wine,<br>and men of strength to mingle strong drink,  
**5:23** who justify the wicked for a reward,<br>and take away the justice of the righteous from him!  
**5:24** Therefore, as the fire devours the stubble<br>and the flame consumes the chaff,<br>so their root shall be as rottenness,<br>and their blossom shall go up as dust;<br>because they have cast away the law of the Lord of Hosts<br>and despised the word of the Holy One of Israel.  
**5:25** Therefore the anger of the Lord burns against His people,<br>and He has stretched out His hand against them, and has stricken them,<br>and the hills trembled.<br>Their corpses were torn in the midst of the streets.<br>For all this His anger is not turned away,<br>and His hand is still stretched out.  
**5:26** He will lift up a banner to the nations from afar<br>and will hiss at them from the ends of the earth;<br>certainly they shall come<br>with speed, swiftly.  
**5:27** No one shall be weary or stumble among them;<br>no one shall slumber or sleep;<br>neither shall the girdle of their loins be loosed,<br>nor the strap of their shoes be broken;  
**5:28** their arrows are sharp<br>and all their bows bent;<br>their horses’ hooves will seem like flint,<br>and their wheels like a whirlwind.  
**5:29** Their roaring shall be like a lion,<br>they shall roar like young lions;<br>they shall roar and lay hold of the prey;<br>and shall carry it away, and no one shall deliver it.  
**5:30** In that day they shall roar against them<br>like the roaring of the sea.<br>And if one looks to the land—<br>only darkness and sorrow,<br>and the light is darkened by the clouds.  

## 6장

**6:1** The Commission of Isaiah<br>In the year that King Uzziah died I saw the Lord sitting on a throne, high and lifted up, and His train filled the temple.  
**6:2** Above it stood the seraphim. Each one had six wings. With two he covered his face, and with two he covered his feet, and with two he flew.  
**6:3** One cried to another and said:<br>“Holy, holy, holy, is the Lord of Hosts;<br>the whole earth is full of His glory.”  
**6:4** The posts of the door moved at the voice of him who cried, and the house was filled with smoke.  
**6:5** And I said: “Woe is me! For I am undone because I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell in the midst of a people of unclean lips. For my eyes have seen the King, the Lord of Hosts.”  
**6:6** Then one of the seraphim flew to me with a live coal which he had taken with the tongs from off the altar in his hand.  
**6:7** And he laid it on my mouth, and said, “This has touched your lips, and your iniquity is taken away, and your sin purged.”  
**6:8** Also I heard the voice of the Lord saying, “Whom shall I send, and who will go for us?”<br>Then I said, “Here am I. Send me.”  
**6:9** He said, “Go, and tell this people:<br>‘Keep on hearing, but do not understand;<br>keep on seeing, but do not perceive.’  
**6:10** Make the heart of this people dull,<br>and their ears heavy,<br>and shut their eyes;<br>lest they see with their eyes,<br>and hear with their ears,<br>and understand with their heart,<br>and turn and be healed.”  
**6:11** Then I said, “Lord, how long?”<br>And He answered:<br>“Until the cities are laid waste<br>without inhabitants,<br>and the houses without man,<br>and the land is utterly desolate,  
**6:12** and the Lord has removed men far away,<br>and there is a great forsaking in the midst of the land.  
**6:13** But yet in it shall be a tenth,<br>and it shall return, and shall be burned,<br>as a terebinth tree or as an oak,<br>whose stump remains when it is cut down,<br>so the holy seed is its stump.”  

## 7장

**7:1** Isaiah Reassures King Ahaz<br>And it came to pass in the days of Ahaz the son of Jotham, the son of Uzziah, king of Judah, that Rezin the king of Aram, and Pekah the son of Remaliah, king of Israel, went up toward Jerusalem to war against it, but could not prevail against it.  
**7:2** It was said to the house of David, “Aram is allied with Ephraim.” Then his heart trembled and the heart of his people, as the trees of the wood tremble with the wind.  
**7:3** Then the Lord said to Isaiah: Go out now to meet Ahaz, you, and Shear-Jashub your son, at the end of the conduit of the upper pool in the highway of the fuller’s field,  
**7:4** and say to him, Take heed, and be quiet. Do not fear nor be fainthearted because of the two tails of these smoking firebrands, because of the fierce anger of Rezin with Aram and of the son of Remaliah,  
**7:5** because Aram, Ephraim, and the son of Remaliah have taken evil counsel against you, saying,  
**7:6** Let us go up against Judah, and trouble it, and let us make a breach there for us, and set a king in the midst of it, even the son of Tabeel.  
**7:7** Thus says the Lord God:<br>It shall not stand,<br>nor shall it come to pass.  
**7:8** For the head of Aram is Damascus,<br>and the head of Damascus is Rezin.<br>Now within sixty-five years<br>Ephraim shall be broken so that it is not a people.  
**7:9** The head of Ephraim is Samaria,<br>and the head of Samaria is the son of Remaliah.<br>If you will not believe,<br>surely you shall not be established.  
**7:10** The Sign of Immanuel<br>Moreover the Lord spoke again to Ahaz, saying:  
**7:11** Ask for a sign from the Lord your God. Make it either as deep as Sheol or as high as heaven.  
**7:12** But Ahaz said, I will not ask, nor will I tempt the Lord.  
**7:13** Then he said, “Hear now, O house of David. Is it a small thing for you to weary men, but will you weary my God also?  
**7:14** Therefore the Lord Himself shall give you a sign: The virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.  
**7:15** Curds and honey he shall eat at the time that he knows enough to refuse the evil and choose the good.  
**7:16** For before the child shall know to refuse the evil and choose the good, the land that you dread shall be forsaken of both her kings.  
**7:17** The Lord shall bring upon you, and upon your people, and upon your father’s house, days that have not come from the day that Ephraim departed from Judah, even the king of Assyria.”  
**7:18** In that day the Lord shall whistle for the fly that is in the uttermost part of the rivers of Egypt, and for the bee that is in the land of Assyria.  
**7:19** They shall come, and all of them shall rest in the desolate valleys, and in the holes of the rocks, and on all thorns, and on all bushes.  
**7:20** In that same day the Lord shall shave with a hired razor, from regions beyond the River, with the king of Assyria, the head, and the hair of the legs, and it shall also remove the beard.  
**7:21** In that day a man shall nourish a young cow and two sheep,  
**7:22** and because of the abundance of milk that they give, he shall eat curds; for everyone who is left in the land shall eat curds and honey.  
**7:23** In that day every place where there were a thousand vines, worth a thousand shekels of silver, shall become briers and thorns.  
**7:24** With arrows and with bows shall men come there because all the land shall become briers and thorns.  
**7:25** On all hills that used to be cultivated with the hoe, you will not go there for fear of briers and thorns. But it shall be for pasturing oxen and for sheep to tread.  

## 8장

**8:1** The Coming Assyrian Invasion<br>Moreover the Lord said to me, Take for yourself a large book and write in it with a man’s pen: “Swift is the booty, speedy is the prey.”  
**8:2** I will take to Myself faithful witnesses for testimony, Uriah the priest and Zechariah the son of Jeberekiah.  
**8:3** So I went in to the prophetess, and she conceived and bore a son. Then the Lord said to me, Call his name Maher-Shalal-Hash-Baz.  
**8:4** For before the child knows how to cry “My father” and “My mother,” the riches of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria shall be taken away before the king of Assyria.  
**8:5** The Lord spoke to me again, saying:  
**8:6** Because this people refuses<br>the gently flowing waters of Shiloah<br>and rejoices in Rezin<br>and the son of Remaliah,  
**8:7** now therefore the Lord certainly is about to bring upon them<br>the strong and plentiful waters of the River,<br>even the king of Assyria and all his glory;<br>and he shall come up over all his channels<br>and go over all his banks.  
**8:8** And he shall pass through Judah, he shall overflow and go over,<br>he shall reach even to the neck,<br>and the stretching out of his wings shall fill the breadth of your land,<br>O Immanuel.  
**8:9** Be broken, O you peoples, and be broken in pieces.<br>And give ear, all you from far countries.<br>Gird yourselves, but be broken in pieces;<br>gird yourselves, but be broken in pieces.  
**8:10** Take counsel together, but it shall come to nothing;<br>speak the word, but it shall not stand<br>for God is with us.  
**8:11** Fear God<br>For the Lord spoke thus to me with a strong hand and instructed me that I should not walk in the way of this people, saying:  
**8:12** You should not say, “It is a conspiracy,”<br>concerning all that this people calls a conspiracy,<br>neither fear their threats<br>nor be afraid of them.  
**8:13** Sanctify the Lord of Hosts Himself,<br>and let Him be your fear,<br>and let Him be your dread.  
**8:14** He shall become a sanctuary,<br>but a stone of stumbling and a rock of offense<br>to both the houses of Israel,<br>and a trap and a snare to the inhabitants of Jerusalem.  
**8:15** Many among them shall stumble<br>and fall and be broken<br>and be snared and be taken.  
**8:16** Bind up the testimony;<br>seal the law among My disciples.  
**8:17** I will wait on the Lord,<br>who hides His face from the house of Jacob,<br>and I will eagerly look for Him.  
**8:18** See, I and the children whom the Lord has given me are for signs and for wonders in Israel from the Lord of Hosts who dwells in Mount Zion.  
**8:19** When they say to you, “Seek after the mediums and the wizards, who whisper and mutter,” should not a people seek after their God? Should they consult the dead for the living?  
**8:20** To the law and to the testimony; if they do not speak according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.  
**8:21** They shall pass through the land hard-pressed and hungry; when they are hungry, they shall be furious and curse their king and their God as they look upward.  
**8:22** Then they shall look to the earth and see trouble and darkness, gloom of anguish. And they shall be driven away into darkness.  

## 9장

**9:1** The Coming King<br>Nevertheless there shall be no more gloom for her who was in anguish. In the former time He contemptuously treated the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, but in the latter time He shall make it glorious, by the way of the sea, beyond the Jordan, in Galilee of the nations.

**42:2** He shall not cry out, nor lift up his voice,<br>nor cause his voice to be heard in the street.  
**42:3** A bruised reed he shall not break,<br>and the smoking flax he shall not quench;<br>he shall bring forth justice faithfully.  
**42:4** He shall not be disheartened nor be discouraged,<br>until he has set justice in the earth;<br>and the coastlands shall wait for his law.  
**42:5** Thus says God the Lord,<br>who created the heavens and stretched them out,<br>who spread forth the earth and that which comes out of it,<br>who gives breath to the people on it,<br>and spirit to those who walk in it:  
**42:6** I the Lord have called You in righteousness,<br>and will hold Your hand,<br>and will keep You and appoint You<br>for a covenant of the people,<br>for a light of the nations,  
**42:7** to open the blind eyes,<br>to bring out the prisoners from the prison,<br>and those who sit in darkness out of the prison house.  
**42:8** I am the Lord, that is My name;<br>and My glory I will not give to another,<br>nor My praise to graven images.  
**42:9** See, the former things have come to pass,<br>and new things I declare;<br>before they spring forth<br>I tell you of them.  
**42:10** A Song of Praise<br>Sing to the Lord a new song,<br>and His praise from the ends of the earth,<br>you who go down to the sea, and all that is in it,<br>the coastlands, and the inhabitants.  
**42:11** Let the wilderness and the cities lift up their voices,<br>the villages that Kedar inhabits.<br>Let the inhabitants of Sela sing,<br>let them shout from the top of the mountains.  
**42:12** Let them give glory to the Lord,<br>and declare His praise in the islands.  
**42:13** The Lord shall go forth like a mighty man;<br>He shall stir up zeal like a man of war.<br>He shall cry out, yes, raise a war cry;<br>He shall prevail against His enemies.  
**42:14** The Lord’s Help Is Promised<br>I have for a long time held My peace;<br>I have been still and refrained Myself.<br>Now I will cry like a travailing woman;<br>I will destroy and devour at once.  
**42:15** I will lay waste mountains and hills<br>and dry up all their vegetation,<br>and I will make the rivers islands,<br>and I will dry up the pools.  
**42:16** I will bring the blind by a way that they did not know;<br>I will lead them in paths that they have not known.<br>I will make darkness light before them<br>and crooked things straight.<br>These things I will do for them<br>and not forsake them.  
**42:17** They shall be turned back, they shall be greatly ashamed,<br>who trust in graven images,<br>who say to the molded images, “You are our gods.”  
**42:18** Israel’s Disobedience<br>Hear, you deaf;<br>look, you blind, that you may see.  
**42:19** Who is blind, but My servant?<br>Or deaf, as My messenger whom I sent?<br>Who is blind as he who is at peace with Me,<br>and blind as the servant of the Lord?  
**42:20** You have seen many things, but you do not observe them;<br>your ears are open, but no one hears.  
**42:21** The Lord is well pleased<br>for His righteousness’ sake<br>to magnify the law and make it honorable.  
**42:22** But this is a people robbed and despoiled;<br>they are all snared in holes,<br>and they are hidden in prison houses;<br>they are for a prey,<br>and no one delivers,<br>for a spoil,<br>and no one says, “Restore them.”  
**42:23** Who among you will give ear to this?<br>Who will listen and hear for the time to come?  
**42:24** Who gave up Jacob as spoil,<br>and Israel to the robbers?<br>Did not the Lord,<br>against whom we have sinned?<br>For they would not walk in His ways,<br>nor were they obedient to His law.  
**42:25** Therefore, He has poured on him the fury of His anger<br>and the strength of battle;<br>and it has set him on fire all around, yet he did not recognize it;<br>and it burned him, yet he laid it not to heart.  

## 43장

**43:1** The Redeemer of Israel<br>But now, thus says the Lord<br>who created you, O Jacob,<br>and He who formed you, O Israel:<br>Do not fear, for I have redeemed you;<br>I have called you by your name; you are Mine.  
**43:2** When you pass through waters,<br>I will be with you.<br>And through the rivers,<br>they shall not overflow you.<br>When you walk through the fire,<br>you shall not be burned,<br>nor shall the flame kindle on you.  
**43:3** For I am the Lord your God,<br>the Holy One of Israel, your Savior;<br>I gave Egypt for your ransom,<br>Ethiopia and Seba in your place.  
**43:4** Since you were precious in My sight, you have been honorable,<br>and I have loved you;<br>therefore, I will give men for you,<br>and people for your life.  
**43:5** Do not fear, for I am with you;<br>I will bring your descendants from the east,<br>and gather you from the west;  
**43:6** I will say to the north, “Give them up,”<br>and to the south, “Do not keep them back.<br>Bring My sons from afar,<br>and My daughters from the ends of the earth,  
**43:7** even everyone who is called by My name,<br>for I have created him for My glory;<br>I have formed him, and I have made him.”  
**43:8** Bring forth the blind people who have eyes,<br>and the deaf who have ears.  
**43:9** Let all the nations be gathered together,<br>and let the people be assembled.<br>Who among them can declare this,<br>and show us former things?<br>Let them bring their witnesses, that they may be justified;<br>or let them hear and say, “It is true.”  
**43:10** You are My witnesses, says the Lord,<br>and My servant whom I have chosen<br>that you may know and believe Me,<br>and understand that I am He.<br>Before Me there was no God formed,<br>nor shall there be after Me.  
**43:11** I, even I, am the Lord,<br>and besides Me there is no savior.  
**43:12** I have declared, and have saved, and I have shown,<br>when there was no strange god among you;<br>therefore, you are My witnesses, says the Lord, that I am God.  
**43:13** Indeed, from eternity I am He;<br>there is no one who can deliver out of My hand;<br>I act, and who can reverse it?  
**43:14** Thus says the Lord,<br>your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel:<br>For your sake I have sent to Babylon,<br>and have brought down all their nobles, and the Chaldeans,<br>into the ships in which they rejoice.  
**43:15** I am the Lord, your Holy One,<br>the Creator of Israel, your King.  
**43:16** Thus says the Lord,<br>who makes a way in the sea<br>and a path in the mighty waters,  
**43:17** who brings forth the chariot and horse,<br>the army and the mighty men<br>(they shall lie down together, they shall not rise;<br>they are extinguished, they are quenched like a wick):  
**43:18** Do not remember the former things<br>nor consider the things of old.  
**43:19** See, I will do a new thing,<br>now it shall spring forth; shall you not be aware of it?<br>I will even make a way in the wilderness,<br>and rivers in the desert.  
**43:20** The beast of the field shall honor Me,<br>the jackals and the owls,<br>because I give waters in the wilderness,<br>and rivers in the desert,<br>to give drink to My people, My chosen ones.  
**43:21** This people I have formed for Myself;<br>they shall declare My praise.  
**43:22** Pleading With Israel<br>But you have not called upon Me, O Jacob;<br>but you have been weary of Me, O Israel.  
**43:23** You have not brought Me the sheep for your burnt offerings,<br>nor have you honored Me with your sacrifices.<br>I have not burdened you with offerings,<br>nor wearied you with incense.  
**43:24** You have bought Me no sweet cane with money,<br>nor have you filled Me with the fat of your sacrifices;<br>but you have made Me burdened with your sins;<br>you have wearied Me with your iniquities.  
**43:25** I, even I, am He who blots out<br>your transgressions for My own sake,<br>and will not remember your sins.  
**43:26** Put Me in remembrance;<br>let us plead together;<br>state your cause, that you may be justified.  
**43:27** Your first father has sinned,<br>and your teachers have transgressed against Me.  
**43:28** Therefore, I have profaned the princes of the sanctuary,<br>and have given Jacob to the curse,<br>and Israel to reproaches.  

## 44장

**44:1** God’s Blessing on Jacob<br>Yet now listen, O Jacob, My servant,<br>and Israel, whom I have chosen.  
**44:2** Thus says the Lord<br>who made you, and formed you from the womb,<br>who will help you:<br>Do not fear, O Jacob, My servant, and you,<br>Jeshurun, whom I have chosen.  
**44:3** For I will pour water on him who is thirsty,<br>and floods on the dry ground;<br>I will pour out My Spirit on your descendants,<br>and My blessing on your offspring;  
**44:4** and they shall spring up as among the grass,<br>as willows by the water courses.  
**44:5** One will say, “I am the Lord’s”;<br>another will call himself by the name of Jacob;<br>and another will write on his hand, “Belonging to the Lord,”<br>and name himself by the name of Israel.  
**44:6** Idolatry Condemned<br>Thus says the Lord the King of Israel,<br>and his Redeemer the Lord of Hosts:<br>I am the first, and I am the last;<br>besides Me there is no God.  
**44:7** Who is like Me? Let him proclaim<br>and declare it, and recount it in order for Me,<br>since I appointed the ancient people.<br>And let them declare to them the things that are coming,<br>and shall come.  
**44:8** Do not fear, nor be afraid;<br>have I not told you from of old, and declared it?<br>You are My witnesses!<br>Is there a God besides Me?<br>There is no Rock; I know not any.  
**44:9** Those who make a graven image are, all of them, vain,<br>and their delectable things shall not profit;<br>and they are their own witnesses;<br>they do not see nor know, that they may be ashamed.  
**44:10** Who has formed a god or molded a graven image<br>that is profitable for nothing?  
**44:11** All his companions shall be ashamed;<br>and the workmen, they are mere men.<br>Let them all be gathered together; let them stand up,<br>yet they shall fear; they shall be ashamed together.  
**44:12** The smith with the tongs<br>both works in the coals<br>and fashions it with hammers,<br>and works it with the strength of his arms.<br>He becomes hungry, and his strength fails;<br>He drinks no water and is faint.  
**44:13** The carpenter stretches out his measuring line;<br>he marks it out with a line;<br>he fits it with planes,<br>and he marks it out with the compass,<br>and makes it after the figure of a man,<br>according to the beauty of a man,<br>that it may remain in the house.  
**44:14** He hews down cedars for himself,<br>and takes the cypress and the oak,<br>which he raises for himself among the trees of the forest.<br>He plants a fir, and the rain makes it grow.  
**44:15** Then it shall be for a man to burn,<br>for he will take some of it and warm himself;<br>he kindles it and bakes bread;<br>he also makes a god and worships it;<br>he makes it a graven image and bows down to it.  
**44:16** He burns half of it in the fire;<br>over this half he eats meat;<br>he roasts it and is satisfied.<br>Also he warms himself and says,<br>“Aha, I am warm, I have seen the fire.”  
**44:17** And the rest he makes into a god, even his carved image.<br>He falls down to it, and worships it,<br>and prays to it, and says,<br>“Deliver me, for you are my god.”  
**44:18** They have not known nor understood;<br>for He has shut their eyes so that they cannot see,<br>and their hearts so that they cannot understand.  
**44:19** No one considers in his heart,<br>nor is there knowledge nor understanding to say,<br>“I have burned part of it in the fire;<br>I also have baked bread on the coals;<br>I have roasted meat and eaten it;<br>and shall I make the rest into an abomination?<br>Shall I fall down to a block of wood?”  
**44:20** He feeds on ashes; a deceived heart has turned him aside<br>so that he cannot deliver his soul, nor say,<br>“Is there not a lie in my right hand?”  
**44:21** Israel Not Forgotten<br>Remember these things, O Jacob,<br>and Israel, for you are My servant;<br>I have formed you; you are My servant;<br>O Israel, you shall not be forgotten by Me.  
**44:22** I have blotted out, as a thick cloud,<br>your transgressions, and your sins, as a cloud.<br>Return to Me,<br>for I have redeemed you.  
**44:23** Sing, O heavens, for the Lord has done it.<br>Shout joyfully, you lower parts of the earth;<br>break forth into singing, you mountains,<br>O forest, and every tree in it.<br>For the Lord has redeemed Jacob,<br>and glorified Himself in Israel.  
**44:24** Jerusalem to Be Inhabited<br>Thus says the Lord,<br>your Redeemer, and He who formed you from the womb:<br>I am the Lord<br>who makes all things,<br>who stretches out the heavens alone,<br>who spreads abroad the earth by Myself,  
**44:25** who frustrates the omens of the boasters<br>and makes fools out of diviners,<br>who turns wise men backward<br>and makes their knowledge foolish,  
**44:26** who confirms the word of His servant<br>and performs the counsel of His messengers,<br>who says to Jerusalem, “You shall be inhabited,”<br>and to the cities of Judah, “You shall be built.”<br>And I will raise up her ruins again;  
**44:27** it is I who says to the deep, “Be dried up!”<br>And I will dry up your rivers;  
**44:28** it is I who says of Cyrus, “He is My shepherd,<br>and shall perform all My desire”;<br>and he declares to Jerusalem, “You shall be built,”<br>and to the temple, “Your foundation shall be laid.”  

## 45장

**45:1** Cyrus, God’s Anointed<br>Thus says the Lord to Cyrus, His anointed,<br>whose right hand I have held—<br>to subdue nations before him<br>and to loosen the loins of kings,<br>to open doors before him<br>so that the gates will not be shut:  
**45:2** I will go before you<br>and make the crooked places straight;<br>I will break in pieces the gates of bronze<br>and shatter the bars of iron.  
**45:3** And I will give you the treasures of darkness<br>and hidden riches of secret places<br>so that you may know that I, the Lord,<br>who calls you by your name, am the God of Israel.  
**45:4** For Jacob My servant’s sake<br>and Israel My chosen one,<br>I have even called you by your name;<br>I have named you,<br>though you have not known Me.  
**45:5** I am the Lord and there is no other;<br>there is no God besides Me.<br>I strengthen you,<br>though you have not known Me,  
**45:6** so that they may know from the rising of the sun<br>and from the west<br>that there is no one besides Me.<br>I am the Lord, and there is no other;  
**45:7** I form the light and create darkness;<br>I make peace and create calamity;<br>I, the Lord, do all these things.  
**45:8** Drip down, O heavens, from above,<br>and let the clouds pour down righteousness;<br>let the earth open up,<br>and let them bring forth salvation,<br>and let righteousness spring up together.<br>I, the Lord, have created it.  
**45:9** Woe to him who strives with his Maker,<br>the potsherd with the potsherds of the earth!<br>Shall the clay say to the potter,<br>“What are you making?”<br>Or the thing you are making say,<br>“He has no hands”?  
**45:10** Woe to him who says to his father,<br>“What are you begetting?”<br>Or to the woman,<br>“To what are you giving birth?”  
**45:11** Thus says the Lord,<br>the Holy One of Israel and his Maker:<br>Ask Me of things to come<br>concerning My sons,<br>and you shall commit to Me the work of My hands.  
**45:12** I have made the earth<br>and created man on it.<br>I, even My hands, have stretched out the heavens,<br>and I have ordained all their host.  
**45:13** I have raised him up in righteousness,<br>and I will direct all his ways;<br>he shall build My city,<br>and he shall let My captives go,<br>neither for price nor reward,<br>says the Lord of Hosts.  
**45:14** The Only Savior<br>Thus says the Lord:<br>The products of Egypt and merchandise of Ethiopia<br>and of the Sabeans—men of stature—<br>shall come over to you,<br>and they shall be yours;<br>they shall come after you;<br>in chains they shall come over,<br>and they shall fall down before you.<br>They shall make supplication to you, saying,<br>“Surely God is with you, and there is no other;<br>there is no other God.”  
**45:15** Truly You are a God who hides Yourself,<br>O God of Israel, the Savior.  
**45:16** They shall be ashamed and also humiliated, all of them;<br>the craftsmen of idols shall go away in confusion together,  
**45:17** but Israel shall be saved by the Lord<br>with an everlasting salvation;<br>you shall not be ashamed nor humiliated<br>to all eternity.  
**45:18** For thus says the Lord,<br>who created the heavens,<br>who is God,<br>who formed the earth and made it,<br>who has established it,<br>who did not create it in vain,<br>who formed it to be inhabited:<br>I am the Lord,<br>and there is no other.  
**45:19** I have not spoken in secret<br>in a dark place of the earth;<br>I did not say to the descendants of Jacob,<br>“Seek Me in a waste place”;<br>I, the Lord, speak righteousness;<br>I declare things that are right.  
**45:20** Assemble yourselves and come;<br>draw near together, you fugitives of the nations.<br>They have no knowledge who set up the wood of their carved image,<br>and pray to a god that cannot save.  
**45:21** Declare and set forth your case;<br>let them take counsel together.<br>Who has declared this from ancient times?<br>Who has told it from that time?<br>Have not I, the Lord?<br>And there is no God besides Me,<br>a righteous God and Savior;<br>there is no other besides Me.  
**45:22** Turn to Me and be saved,<br>all the ends of the earth.<br>For I am God, and there is no other.  
**45:23** I have sworn by Myself,<br>the word has gone out of My mouth in righteousness<br>and shall not return,<br>that to Me every knee shall bow,<br>every tongue shall take an oath.  
**45:24** Surely, one shall say, “Only in the Lord<br>are righteousness and strength.”<br>Men shall come to him,<br>and all who are incensed at Him shall be ashamed.  
**45:25** In the Lord shall all the seed of Israel<br>be justified and shall glory.

**46:2** They stoop, they bow down together;<br>they could not deliver the burden,<br>but themselves have gone into captivity.  
**46:3** Listen to Me, O house of Jacob,<br>and all the remnant of the house of Israel,<br>who are borne by Me from birth<br>and are carried from the womb:  
**46:4** And even to your old age I am He,<br>and even to your graying years I will carry you;<br>I have done it, and I will bear you;<br>even I will carry, and will deliver you.  
**46:5** To whom will you liken Me, and make Me equal,<br>and compare Me that we may be alike?  
**46:6** They lavish gold out of the bag,<br>and weigh silver on the scales;<br>they hire a goldsmith, and he makes it a god;<br>they fall down and worship it.

## 47장

**47:1** The Fall of Babylon<br>Come down, and sit in the dust,<br>O virgin daughter of Babylon;<br>sit on the ground; there is no throne,<br>O daughter of the Chaldeans.<br>For you shall no more be called<br>tender and delicate.  
**47:2** Take the millstones and grind meal.<br>Uncover your veil,<br>strip off the skirt, uncover the leg,<br>pass over the rivers.  
**47:3** Your nakedness shall be uncovered,<br>and your shame shall be seen;<br>I will take vengeance,<br>and I will not meet you as a man.  
**47:4** As for our Redeemer, the Lord of Hosts is His name,<br>the Holy One of Israel.  
**47:5** Sit silently, and go into darkness,<br>O daughter of the Chaldeans;<br>for you shall no more be called<br>the queen of kingdoms.  
**47:6** I was angry with My people;<br>I have polluted My inheritance<br>and given them into your hand.<br>You did not show them mercy;<br>on the aged<br>you have laid your yoke very heavily.  
**47:7** You said, “I shall be a queen forever,”<br>but you did not take these things to heart,<br>nor remember the outcome of them.  
**47:8** Therefore, now hear this, you who are given to pleasures,<br>who dwell carelessly,<br>who say in your heart,<br>“I am, and there is no one besides me;<br>I shall not sit as a widow,<br>nor shall I know the loss of children”;  
**47:9** but these two things shall come to you<br>in a moment, in one day,<br>the loss of children and widowhood.<br>They shall come upon you in their fullness<br>because of the multitude of your sorceries<br>and for the great abundance of your enchantments.  
**47:10** For you have trusted in your wickedness;<br>you have said, “No one sees me”;<br>your wisdom and your knowledge have perverted you;<br>and you have said in your heart,<br>“I am, and there is no one besides me.”  
**47:11** Therefore, evil shall come upon you,<br>which you shall not know from where it rises.<br>And disaster shall fall upon you,<br>for which you shall not be able to atone.<br>And desolation shall come upon you suddenly,<br>which you shall not know.  
**47:12** Stand fast now with your enchantments,<br>and with the multitude of your sorceries<br>in which you have labored from your youth.<br>Perhaps you shall be able to profit,<br>perhaps you may cause trembling.  
**47:13** You are wearied in the multitude of your counsels;<br>let now the astrologers, the stargazers,<br>the monthly prognosticators stand up<br>and save you from these things that shall come upon you.  
**47:14** Surely they shall be as stubble,<br>the fire shall burn them;<br>they shall not deliver themselves<br>from the power of the flame;<br>it shall not be coal to be warmed by<br>nor a fire to sit before.  
**47:15** Thus those shall be to you<br>with whom you have labored,<br>even your merchants, from your youth;<br>they shall wander, everyone to his quarter.<br>No one shall save you.  

## 48장

**48:1** Israel Refined for God’s Glory<br>Hear this, O house of Jacob,<br>who are called by the name of Israel,<br>and have come forth from the waters of Judah;<br>who swear by the name of the Lord,<br>and invoke the God of Israel,<br>but not in truth or in righteousness;  
**48:2** for they call themselves after the holy city,<br>and lean on the God of Israel;<br>the Lord of Hosts is His name:  
**48:3** I have declared the former things from the beginning;<br>and they went forth from My mouth, and I announced them.<br>Suddenly I did them, and they came to pass.  
**48:4** Because I knew that you are obstinate,<br>and your neck is an iron sinew,<br>and your brow bronze;  
**48:5** I have even from the beginning declared it to you;<br>before it came to pass I announced it to you,<br>lest you should say,<br>“My idol has done them,<br>and my carved image and my molded image has commanded them.”  
**48:6** You have heard; see all this.<br>And will you not declare it?<br>I have shown you new things from this time,<br>even hidden things, and you did not know them.  
**48:7** They are created now and not from the beginning;<br>even before the day when you did not hear them,<br>lest you should say,<br>“Yes, I knew them.”  
**48:8** You have not heard, you have not known,<br>indeed, from long ago your ear has not been open.<br>For I knew that you would deal very treacherously,<br>and that from birth you have been called a rebel.  
**48:9** For My name’s sake I will defer My anger,<br>and for My praise I will restrain it for you<br>so that you are not cut off.  
**48:10** See, I have refined you, but not with silver;<br>I have chosen you in the furnace of affliction.  
**48:11** For My own sake, even for My own sake, I will do it;<br>for how can My name be polluted?<br>And I will not give My glory to another.  
**48:12** The Lord’s Call to Israel<br>Listen to Me, O Jacob<br>and Israel, whom I called:<br>I am He;<br>I am the First, and I am the Last.  
**48:13** My hand also has laid the foundation of the earth,<br>and My right hand has spanned the heavens;<br>when I call to them,<br>they stand up together.  
**48:14** Assemble, all of you, and listen.<br>Who among them has declared these things?<br>The Lord has loved him;<br>he will do His pleasure on Babylon,<br>and His arm shall be against the Chaldeans.  
**48:15** I, even I, have spoken,<br>and I have called him;<br>I have brought him,<br>and his way will prosper.  
**48:16** Come near to Me, hear this:<br>I have not spoken in secret from the beginning;<br>from the time that it was, there I am.<br>And now the Lord God has sent me<br>and His Spirit.  
**48:17** Thus says the Lord,<br>your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel:<br>I am the Lord your God,<br>who teaches you to profit,<br>who leads you in the way you should go.

**66:3** He who kills a bull<br>is as if he kills a man;<br>he who sacrifices a lamb,<br>as if he breaks a dog’s neck;<br>he who offers a grain offering,<br>as if he offers swine’s blood;<br>he who burns incense,<br>as if he blesses an idol.<br>They have chosen their own ways,<br>and their soul delights in their abominations;  
**66:4** I also will choose their punishments<br>and will bring their fears on them,<br>because when I called, no one answered;<br>when I spoke, they did not listen;<br>but they did evil before My eyes<br>and chose that in which I did not delight.  
**66:5** The Vindication of Zion<br>Hear the word of the Lord,<br>you who tremble at His word:<br>Your brothers who hate you,<br>who cast you out for My name’s sake, said,<br>“Let the Lord be glorified<br>that we may see your joy.”<br>Yet they shall be ashamed.  
**66:6** A voice of noise from the city,<br>a voice from the temple,<br>the voice of the Lord<br>who renders recompense to His enemies.  
**66:7** Before she was in labor,<br>she gave birth;<br>before her pain came,<br>she delivered a son.  
**66:8** Who has heard such a thing?<br>Who has seen such things?<br>Shall the earth be made to give birth in one day?<br>Shall a nation be born at once?<br>For as soon as Zion was in labor,<br>she brought forth her sons.  
**66:9** Shall I bring to the point of birth<br>and not cause delivery? says the Lord.<br>Shall I who cause delivery<br>shut the womb? says your God.  
**66:10** Rejoice with Jerusalem and be glad for her,<br>all you who love her;<br>rejoice exceedingly with her,<br>all you who mourn for her,  
**66:11** that you may feed and be satisfied<br>with her consoling breasts,<br>that you may feed<br>and be delighted with the abundance of her bosom.  
**66:12** For thus says the Lord:<br>I will extend peace to her like a river<br>and the glory of the nations like a flowing stream.<br>Then you shall nurse, you shall be carried on her sides,<br>and dandled on her knees.  
**66:13** As one whom his mother comforts,<br>so I will comfort you;<br>and you shall be comforted in Jerusalem.  
**66:14** God’s Reign and Final Judgment<br>Then you shall see this, and your heart shall rejoice,<br>and your bones shall flourish like an herb;<br>and the hand of the Lord shall be known toward His servants,<br>and His indignation toward His enemies.  
**66:15** For the Lord shall come with fire<br>and with His chariots like a whirlwind,<br>to render His anger with fury<br>and His rebuke with flames of fire.  
**66:16** For by fire and by His sword on all flesh,<br>the Lord shall execute judgment;<br>and the slain of the Lord shall be many.  
**66:17** Those who sanctify themselves and purify themselves in the gardens behind one tree in the midst, eating swine’s flesh, and abominable things, and mice shall be consumed together, says the Lord.  
**66:18** For I know their works and their thoughts. The time shall come to gather all nations and tongues. And they shall come and see My glory.  
**66:19** I will set a sign among them, and send from them survivors to the nations: to Tarshish, Pul, and Lud—who draw the bow—to Tubal, and Javan, to the coastlands afar off who have not heard My fame nor seen My glory. And they shall declare My glory among the nations.  
**66:20** They shall bring all your brothers out of all nations as an offering to the Lord on horses, and in chariots, and in litters, and on mules, and on swift beasts to My holy mountain Jerusalem, says the Lord, as the sons of Israel bring an offering in a clean vessel into the house of the Lord.  
**66:21** I will also take some of them for priests and for Levites, says the Lord.  
**66:22** For as the new heavens and the new earth which I will make shall remain before Me, says the Lord, so shall your descendants and your name remain.  
**66:23** From one New Moon to another, and from one Sabbath to another, all flesh shall come to worship before Me, says the Lord.  
**66:24** And they shall go forth and look on the corpses of the men who have transgressed against Me. For their worm shall not die, nor shall their fire be quenched. And they shall be an abhorrence to all flesh.
