# 34.나훔 (MEV)



## 1장

**1:1** An oracle for Nineveh, a writing of the vision of Nahum, the Elkoshite.  
**1:2** God’s Anger With Nineveh<br>The Lord is a jealous and avenging God;<br>the Lord avenges and is furious.<br>The Lord takes vengeance on His enemies,<br>and He reserves it for His adversaries;  
**1:3** the Lord is slow to anger and great in power,<br>and the Lord will in no way acquit the guilty.<br>In gale winds and a storm is His way,<br>and clouds are the dust of His feet.  
**1:4** He rebukes and dries up the sea,<br>and He makes waterless all the rivers.<br>Bashan and Carmel wither up,<br>and the sprout of Lebanon wastes away.  
**1:5** The mountains quake before Him,<br>and the hills melt;<br>the land rises up before Him,<br>the earth and everything that dwells on it.  
**1:6** Who can stand before His anger?<br>Who will rise up before His burning wrath?<br>His heat is poured out like fire,<br>and the rocks are broken up before Him.  
**1:7** The Lord is good,<br>a stronghold in the day of distress;<br>and He knows those who take refuge in Him.  
**1:8** As a flood running forth,<br>He will bring to an end the distress,<br>and He will pursue His adversaries into darkness.  
**1:9** Why do you all scheme against the Lord?<br>He will bring it to an end.<br>It will not rise up a second time.  
**1:10** Because they are like interwoven thorns<br>and as drunkards imbibing,<br>they are consumed like completely dry stubble.  
**1:11** Out of you, O Nineveh, comes one,<br>a worthless counselor,<br>who devises evil against the Lord.  
**1:12** So the Lord says:<br>“Even though they are full and many,<br>they will be cut down, and it will pass away.<br>Even though I have afflicted you,<br>I will no longer afflict you, O Judah;  
**1:13** now I will break apart his yoke from over you,<br>and I will tear apart your bonds.”  
**1:14** The Lord has given a command concerning you:<br>“No longer will your name go forth.<br>I will cut off the carved image and metal image<br>from the house of your gods.<br>I will prepare your grave,<br>for you are despised.”  
**1:15** Look, on the mountains<br>come the feet of him who brings good news,<br>who proclaims peace!<br>Make your feasts, O Judah,<br>and complete your vows.<br>For the wicked one will never again pass through your midst;<br>he is completely cut down.  

## 2장

**2:1** The Fall of Nineveh<br>He who scatters has come to you.<br>Guard the fortifications!<br>Watch the road!<br>Prepare yourself,<br>and strengthen yourself!  
**2:2** For the Lord is restoring the prominence of Jacob,<br>even the prominence of Israel,<br>for others have certainly<br>laid waste their vines.  
**2:3** The shields of his mighty men are soaked red,<br>even the mighty men are clad in red.<br>In the day he prepares the chariots,<br>they are like a fire of iron.<br>The cypress spears are ready.  
**2:4** The chariots run wildly through the streets,<br>they rush to and fro in the open areas;<br>their appearance is like torches,<br>they dash to and fro like lightning.  
**2:5** He remembers his officers<br>as they stumble about on the road;<br>they hurry on to the wall<br>as the siege tower is set up.  
**2:6** The holding gates are opened wide,<br>and the palace washes away.  
**2:7** It is decreed:<br>She is uncovered and led away captive;<br>her handmaidens shall lead her as with the voice of doves,<br>beating their chests.  
**2:8** Nineveh is like a pool<br>whose waters run away.<br>“Halt! Halt!” they cry,<br>but no one turns back.  
**2:9** “Plunder the silver!<br>Plunder the gold!<br>There is no limit to the treasure,<br>or to the wealth of every precious thing.”  
**2:10** She is desolate, empty, and waste!<br>Hearts melt away, and knees shake;<br>pain is in all the loins, and all their faces grow pale.  
**2:11** Where is the den of the lions,<br>and the feeding place of the young lions,<br>where the lion and lioness prowl,<br>and the lion’s cub goes, with no one to disturb them?  
**2:12** The lion tore enough food for his cubs,<br>and strangled prey for his lionesses;<br>he has filled his caves with prey,<br>and his dens with flesh.  
**2:13** I am against you,<br>says the Lord of Hosts,<br>and I will burn your chariots in smoke,<br>and the sword will devour your lions.<br>I will cut off your prey from the earth,<br>and the voice of your messengers<br>will be heard no more.  

## 3장

**3:1** Woe to Nineveh<br>Woe to the bloody city!<br>It is full of lies<br>and plunder.<br>The prey never departs.  
**3:2** The noise of the whip<br>and the noise of the rattling of the wheels,<br>galloping horses,<br>and rushing chariots!  
**3:3** Horsemen charging<br>with flashing sword<br>and glittering spear.<br>Multitude of slain,<br>great number of corpses,<br>dead bodies without end—<br>they stumble on the corpses—  
**3:4** because of the countless harlotries of the seductive harlot,<br>the mistress of sorceries,<br>who sells nations through her harlotries<br>and families through her sorceries.  
**3:5** I am against you, says the Lord of Hosts;<br>I will lift your skirts over your face,<br>and I will show the nations your nakedness,<br>and the kingdoms your shame.  
**3:6** I will throw filth on you,<br>and make you vile,<br>and make you a spectacle.  
**3:7** All who look at you will flee from you, and say,<br>“Nineveh is devastated! Who will lament for her?”<br>Where shall I seek comforters for you?  
**3:8** Are you better than Thebes<br>that sat by the Nile,<br>with water around her,<br>whose rampart was the sea,<br>and whose wall was the water?  
**3:9** Ethiopia and Egypt were her strength, and it was without limit;<br>Put and Libya were her helpers.  
**3:10** Yet she went into exile,<br>she went into captivity;<br>her young children were dashed to pieces<br>at the head of every street;<br>they cast lots for her honorable men,<br>and all her great men were bound in chains.  
**3:11** You also will be drunk;<br>you will go into hiding;<br>you will seek refuge from the enemy.  
**3:12** All your fortresses are like fig trees<br>with first-ripe figs:<br>If they are shaken,<br>they fall into the mouth of the eater.  
**3:13** Your troops<br>are women in your midst!<br>The gates of your land<br>are wide open to your enemies;<br>fire has devoured your bars.  
**3:14** Draw water for the siege!<br>Strengthen your forts!<br>Go into the clay and<br>tread the mortar!<br>Take hold of the brick mold!  
**3:15** There the fire will devour you,<br>the sword will cut you off;<br>it will eat you up like the locust.<br>Multiply yourselves—like the locust!<br>Multiply—like the grasshopper!  
**3:16** You have multiplied your merchants<br>more than the stars of heaven.<br>The locust plunders<br>and flies away.  
**3:17** Your leaders are like grasshoppers,<br>your commanders like swarms of locusts,<br>which camp in the hedges on a cold day;<br>when the sun rises they fly away,<br>and the place where they are is not known.  
**3:18** Your shepherds slumber, O king of Assyria;<br>Your nobles lie in the dust.<br>Your people are scattered on the mountains,<br>and no one gathers them.  
**3:19** There is no healing of your injury,<br>your wound is grievous.<br>All who hear news about you<br>clap their hands over you,<br>for upon whom has not your wickedness<br>continually passed?
