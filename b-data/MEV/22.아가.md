# 22.아가 (MEV)



## 1장

**1:1** Introduction<br>The Song of Songs, which is Solomon’s.  
**1:2** The Woman<br>Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth!<br>For your love is better than wine.  
**1:3** Your anointing oils are fragrant,<br>your name is oil poured out;<br>therefore the virgins love you.  
**1:4** Draw me after you, let us run.<br>The king has brought me into his chambers.<br>Friends of the Woman<br>We will exult and rejoice in you;<br>we will remember your love more than wine;<br>rightly do they love you.  
**1:5** The Woman<br>I am dark, but still lovely, O daughters of Jerusalem,<br>like the tents of Kedar,<br>like the curtains of Solomon.  
**1:6** Do not gaze at me, because I am dark,<br>because the sun has looked upon me.<br>My mother’s sons were angry with me;<br>they made me the keeper of the vineyards,<br>but my own vineyard I have not kept.  
**1:7** Tell me, you whom my soul loves,<br>where you pasture your flock,<br>where you make it lie down at noon;<br>for why should I be like one who veils herself by the flocks of your companions?  
**1:8** Friends of the Woman<br>If you do not know, O fairest among women,<br>follow in the tracks of the flock,<br>and pasture your young goats<br>beside the shepherds’ tents.  
**1:9** The Man<br>I compare you, my love, to a mare among Pharaoh’s chariots.  
**1:10** Lovely are your cheeks with ornaments,<br>your neck with chains of gold.  
**1:11** Friends of the Woman<br>We will make you ornaments of gold,<br>with studs of silver.  
**1:12** The Woman<br>While the king was on his couch,<br>my nard gave forth its fragrance.  
**1:13** My beloved is to me a bundle of myrrh<br>that lies all night between my breasts.  
**1:14** My beloved is to me a cluster of henna blossoms<br>in the vineyards of En Gedi.  
**1:15** The Man<br>How fair you are, my love.<br>How fair you are! Your eyes are doves.  
**1:16** The Woman<br>How fair you are, my beloved!<br>Yes, pleasant!<br>Our bed is verdant;  
**1:17** the beams of our house are cedar,<br>and our rafters of fir.  

## 2장

**2:1** I am the rose of Sharon,<br>the lily of the valleys.  
**2:2** The Man<br>As a lily among thorns,<br>so is my love among the maidens.  
**2:3** The Woman<br>As an apple tree among the trees of the forest,<br>so is my beloved among the young men.<br>In his shadow I sat with great delight,<br>and his fruit was sweet to my taste.  
**2:4** He brought me to the banquet house,<br>and his banner over me was love.  
**2:5** Sustain me with raisins,<br>refresh me with apples;<br>for I am faint with love.  
**2:6** His left hand is under my head,<br>and his right hand embraces me.  
**2:7** I charge you, O daughters of Jerusalem,<br>by the gazelles or the does of the field,<br>do not stir up or awaken love<br>until it pleases.  
**2:8** The voice of my beloved!<br>Look, he comes<br>leaping over the mountains,<br>bounding over the hills.  
**2:9** My beloved is like a gazelle or a young stag.<br>Look, he stands behind our wall, gazing through the windows,<br>looking through the lattice.  
**2:10** My beloved speaks and says to me:<br>“Rise up, my love,<br>my fair one, and come away.  
**2:11** For now the winter has past;<br>the rain is over and gone.  
**2:12** The flowers appear on the earth;<br>the time of singing has come,<br>and the voice of the turtledove is heard in our land.  
**2:13** The fig tree puts forth its green figs,<br>and the vines their blossoms;<br>and they give forth fragrance.<br>Rise up, my love, my beautiful one, and come away.  
**2:14** O my dove, in the clefts of the rock,<br>in the secret places of the cliffs,<br>let me see your face,<br>let me hear your voice;<br>for your voice is sweet,<br>and your face is lovely.  
**2:15** Catch the foxes for us,<br>the little foxes<br>that spoil the vineyards,<br>for our vineyards are in blossom.”  
**2:16** My beloved is mine, and I am his;<br>he feeds his flock among the lilies.  
**2:17** Until the day breathes<br>and the shadows flee,<br>turn, my beloved,<br>be like a gazelle<br>or a young stag<br>on the cleft mountains.  

## 3장

**3:1** On my bed by night I sought him<br>whom my soul loves;<br>I sought him, but found him not.  
**3:2** I will rise now and go about the city,<br>in the streets and in the squares;<br>I will seek him whom my soul loves.<br>I sought him, but found him not.  
**3:3** The watchmen found me,<br>as they went about the city.<br>“Have you seen him whom my soul loves?”  
**3:4** Scarcely had I passed them,<br>when I found him whom my soul loves.<br>I held him, and would not let him go<br>until I brought him to my mother’s house,<br>and into the chamber of her who conceived me.  
**3:5** I charge you, O daughters of Jerusalem,<br>by the gazelles or does of the field,<br>do not stir up or awaken love<br>until it pleases.  
**3:6** Who is that coming up from the wilderness,<br>like columns of smoke,<br>perfumed with myrrh and frankincense,<br>with all the fragrant powders of the merchant?  
**3:7** Look, it is the litter of Solomon!<br>Around it are sixty mighty men,<br>of the mighty men of Israel,  
**3:8** all of them holding swords<br>and expert in war,<br>each with his sword at his thigh,<br>because of terrors by night.  
**3:9** King Solomon made himself a palanquin<br>from the wood of Lebanon.  
**3:10** He made its posts of silver,<br>its back of gold,<br>its seat of purple;<br>its interior was inlaid with love<br>by the daughters of Jerusalem.  
**3:11** Go forth, O daughters of Zion,<br>and see King Solomon with the crown<br>with which his mother crowned him<br>on the day of his wedding,<br>on the day of the gladness of his heart.  

## 4장

**4:1** The Man<br>How fair you are, my love!<br>How very fair!<br>Your eyes are doves behind your veil.<br>Your hair is like a flock of goats,<br>streaming down the hills of Gilead.  
**4:2** Your teeth are like a flock of shorn ewes<br>that have come up from the washing,<br>all of which bear twins,<br>and not one among them has lost its young.  
**4:3** Your lips are like a scarlet thread,<br>and your mouth is lovely.<br>Your cheeks are halves of a pomegranate<br>behind your veil.  
**4:4** Your neck is like the tower of David,<br>built in rows of stone;<br>on it hang a thousand shields,<br>all of them shields of mighty men.  
**4:5** Your two breasts are like two fawns,<br>twins of a gazelle,<br>that feed among the lilies.  
**4:6** Until the day breathes<br>and the shadows flee,<br>I will go away to the mountain of myrrh<br>and the hill of frankincense.  
**4:7** You are altogether fair, my love;<br>there is no flaw in you.  
**4:8** Come with me from Lebanon, my bride;<br>come with me from Lebanon.<br>Depart from the peak of Amana,<br>from the peak of Senir and Hermon,<br>from the dens of lions,<br>from the mountains of leopards.  
**4:9** You have ravished my heart, my sister, my bride;<br>you have ravished my heart<br>with one glance of your eyes,<br>with one jewel of your necklace.  
**4:10** How fair is your love, my sister, my bride!<br>How much better than wine is your love,<br>and the fragrance of your oils than any spice!  
**4:11** Your lips drip honey, my bride;<br>honey and milk are under your tongue;<br>and the fragrance of your garments is like the scent of Lebanon.  
**4:12** A garden locked is my sister, my bride,<br>a fountain sealed.  
**4:13** Your plants are an orchard of pomegranates<br>with all choicest fruits,<br>henna with nard,  
**4:14** nard and saffron,<br>calamus and cinnamon,<br>with all trees of frankincense,<br>myrrh and aloes,<br>with all the chief spices—  
**4:15** a garden fountain,<br>a well of living water,<br>and flowing streams from Lebanon.  
**4:16** The Woman<br>Awake, O north wind,<br>and come, O south wind!<br>Blow upon my garden,<br>that its spices may flow out.<br>Let my beloved come to his garden,<br>and eat its choicest fruits.  

## 5장

**5:1** The Man<br>I come to my garden, my sister, my bride;<br>I gather my myrrh with my spice;<br>I eat my honeycomb with my honey;<br>I drink my wine with my milk.<br>Friends of the Man<br>Eat, friends!<br>Drink, and be drunk with love!  
**5:2** The Woman<br>I slept, but my heart was awake.<br>A sound! My beloved is knocking. “Open to me, my sister, and my love,<br>my dove, my perfect one;<br>for my head is wet with dew,<br>my locks with the drops of the night.”  
**5:3** I had taken off my garment;<br>how could I put it on again?<br>I had bathed my feet;<br>how could I soil them?  
**5:4** My beloved put his hand by the latch,<br>and my heart yearned for him.  
**5:5** I rose up to open to my beloved,<br>and my hands dripped with myrrh,<br>my fingers with liquid myrrh<br>on the handles of the bolt.  
**5:6** I opened to my beloved,<br>but my beloved had turned and was gone.<br>My soul failed me when he spoke.<br>I sought him, but found him not;<br>I called him, but he gave no answer.  
**5:7** The watchmen found me<br>as they went about the city;<br>they struck me, they wounded me;<br>they took away my mantle,<br>those watchmen of the walls.  
**5:8** I charge you, O daughters of Jerusalem,<br>if you find my beloved,<br>that you tell him I am faint with love.  
**5:9** Friends of the Woman<br>What is your beloved more than another beloved,<br>O fairest among women?<br>What is your beloved more than another beloved,<br>that you so charge us?  
**5:10** The Woman<br>My beloved is white and ruddy,<br>distinguished among ten thousand.  
**5:11** His head is the finest gold;<br>his locks are wavy,<br>black as a raven.  
**5:12** His eyes are like doves<br>beside rivers of water,<br>bathed in milk,<br>fitly set.  
**5:13** His cheeks are like beds of spices,<br>mounds of scented herbs.<br>His lips are lilies,<br>dripping liquid myrrh.  
**5:14** His arms are rods of gold,<br>set with jewels.<br>His body is bright ivory,<br>inlaid with sapphires.  
**5:15** His legs are alabaster columns,<br>set on bases of gold.<br>His appearance is like Lebanon,<br>choice as the cedars.  
**5:16** His mouth is most sweet,<br>and he is altogether desirable.<br>This is my beloved and this is my friend,<br>O daughters of Jerusalem.  

## 6장

**6:1** Friends of the Woman<br>Where has your beloved gone,<br>O fairest among women?<br>Where has your beloved turned aside,<br>that we may seek him with you?  
**6:2** The Woman<br>My beloved has gone down to his garden,<br>to the beds of spices,<br>to feed his flock in the gardens,<br>and to gather lilies.  
**6:3** I am my beloved’s and my beloved is mine;<br>he feeds his flock among the lilies.  
**6:4** The Man<br>You are beautiful as Tirzah, my love,<br>comely as Jerusalem,<br>awesome as an army with banners!  
**6:5** Turn your eyes away from me,<br>for they overwhelm me!<br>Your hair is like a flock of goats<br>streaming down from Gilead.  
**6:6** your teeth are like a flock of ewes<br>that have come up from the washing;<br>all of them bear twins; not one among them has lost its young;  
**6:7** your cheeks are like halves<br>of a pomegranate behind your veil.  
**6:8** There are sixty queens<br>and eighty concubines,<br>and virgins without number.  
**6:9** My dove, my perfect one, is the only one,<br>the only one of her mother,<br>choice to her who bore her.<br>The maidens saw her and called her blessed;<br>the queens and concubines also, and they praised her.  
**6:10** Who is this who looks forth like the dawn,<br>fair as the moon, radiant as the sun,<br>awesome as an army with banners?  
**6:11** I went down to the nut orchard<br>to look at the blossoms of the valley,<br>to see whether the vines had budded,<br>whether the pomegranates were in bloom.  
**6:12** Before I was aware, my soul set me in a chariot beside my prince.  
**6:13** Friends of the Woman<br>Return, return, O Shulammite!<br>Return, return, that we may look upon you.<br>The Man<br>Why should you look upon the Shulammite,<br>as upon a dance before two armies?  

## 7장

**7:1** How beautiful are your feet in sandals,<br>O prince’s daughter!<br>The curves of your thighs are like jewels,<br>the work of a master hand.  
**7:2** Your navel is a round bowl<br>that never lacks mixed wine.<br>Your belly is a heap of wheat,<br>encircled with lilies.  
**7:3** Your two breasts are like two fawns,<br>twins of a gazelle.  
**7:4** Your neck is like an ivory tower,<br>your eyes pools in Heshbon,<br>by the gate of Bath Rabbim.<br>Your nose is like a tower of Lebanon,<br>overlooking Damascus.  
**7:5** Your head crowns like Carmel,<br>and your flowing hair is like purple;<br>a king is held captive in the tresses.  
**7:6** How fair and pleasant you are,<br>O loved one, with all your delights!  
**7:7** Your stature is like a palm tree,<br>and your breasts are like its clusters.  
**7:8** I say I will climb the palm tree<br>and take hold of its branches.<br>Oh, may your breasts be like clusters of the vine,<br>and the scent of your breath like apples,  
**7:9** and your mouth like the best wine.<br>It goes down smoothly for my beloved,<br>gliding over lips and teeth.  
**7:10** The Woman<br>I am my beloved’s,<br>and his desire is for me.  
**7:11** Come, my beloved, let us go forth into the fields,<br>and lodge in the villages;  
**7:12** let us go out early to the vineyards,<br>and see whether the vines have budded,<br>whether the grape blossoms have opened<br>and the pomegranates are in bloom.<br>There I will give you my love.  
**7:13** The mandrakes give forth fragrance,<br>and at our doors are all choice fruits,<br>new as well as old,<br>which I have laid up for you, my beloved.  

## 8장

**8:1** Oh, that you were like a brother to me,<br>who nursed at my mother’s breasts!<br>If I found you outside,<br>I would kiss you,<br>and no one would despise me.  
**8:2** I would lead you and bring you<br>into the house of my mother,<br>she who used to instruct me.<br>I would give you spiced wine to drink,<br>the juice of my pomegranates.  
**8:3** His left hand is under my head,<br>and his right arm embraces me!  
**8:4** I charge you, O daughters of Jerusalem,<br>do not stir up or awaken love<br>until it pleases.  
**8:5** Friends of the Woman<br>Who is that coming up from the wilderness,<br>leaning upon her beloved?<br>The Woman<br>Under the apple tree I awakened you.<br>There your mother was in labor with you;<br>there she who bore you was in labor.  
**8:6** Set me as a seal upon your heart,<br>as a seal upon your arm;<br>for love is strong as death,<br>passion fierce as the grave.<br>Its fires of desire are as ardent flames,<br>a most intense flame.  
**8:7** Many waters cannot quench love,<br>neither can floods drown it.<br>If a man offered for love<br>all the wealth of his house,<br>it would be utterly condemned.  
**8:8** Brothers of the Woman<br>We have a little sister,<br>and she has no breasts.<br>What will we do for our sister<br>on the day when she is spoken for?  
**8:9** If she is a wall,<br>we will build upon her a battlement of silver;<br>but if she is a door,<br>we will enclose her with boards of cedar.  
**8:10** The Woman<br>I was a wall,<br>and my breasts were like towers;<br>then I was in his eyes<br>as one who finds peace.  
**8:11** Solomon had a vineyard at Baal Hamon;<br>he leased the vineyard to keepers;<br>each man was to bring for its fruit a thousand pieces of silver.  
**8:12** My vineyard, my very own, is before me;<br>you, O Solomon, may have the thousand,<br>and the keepers of the fruit two hundred.  
**8:13** The Man<br>O you who dwell in the gardens,<br>my companions listen for your voice;<br>let me hear it!  
**8:14** The Woman<br>Make haste, my beloved,<br>and be like a gazelle or a young stag<br>on the mountains of spices!
