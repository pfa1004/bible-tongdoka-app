# 35.하박국 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** The pronouncement which Habakkuk the prophet saw:  
**1:2** How long, LORD, have I called for help,<br>And You do not hear?<br>I cry out to You, “Violence!”<br>Yet You do not save.  
**1:3** Why do You make me see disaster,<br>And make <i>me</i> look at destitution?<br>Yes, devastation and violence are before me;<br>Strife exists and contention arises.  
**1:4** Therefore the Law <sup>①</sup>is ignored,<br>And justice <sup>②</sup>is never upheld.<br>For the wicked surround the righteous;<br>Therefore justice comes out confused.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>is ineffective;</i> lit <i>turns cold</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>never goes forth</i>  
**1:5** “Look among the nations! Watch!<br>Be horrified! Be frightened speechless!<br>For <i>I am</i> accomplishing a work in your days—<br>You would not believe <i>it even</i> if <sup>①</sup>you were told!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>it</i>  
**1:6** For behold, I am raising up the Chaldeans,<br>That <sup>①</sup>grim and impetuous people<br>Who march <sup>②</sup>throughout the earth,<br>To take possession of dwelling places that are not theirs.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>bitter</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>the breadth of</i>  
**1:7** They are terrifying and feared;<br>Their justice and <sup>①</sup>authority <sup>②</sup>originate with themselves.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>sovereignty</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>proceed from</i>  
**1:8** Their horses are faster than leopards,<br>And quicker than wolves in the evening.<br>Their <sup>①</sup>horsemen charge along,<br>Their <sup>②</sup>horsemen come from afar;<br>They fly like an eagle <sup>③</sup>swooping down to devour.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>hurrying to</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>horses</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>horses</i>  
**1:9** All of them come for violence.<br> <sup>①</sup>Their horde of faces moves forward.<br>They gather captives like sand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>The totality of</i>  
**1:10** They make fun of kings,<br>And dignitaries are <i>an object of</i> laughter to them.<br>They laugh at every fortress,<br>Then heap up dirt and <sup>①</sup>capture it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>overthrow</i>  
**1:11** Then they fly along <i>like</i> the wind and pass on.<br>But they will be held guilty,<br>They whose strength is their god.”  
**1:12** Are You not from time everlasting,<br>LORD, my God, my Holy One?<br>We will not die.<br>You, LORD, have appointed them to <i>deliver</i> judgment;<br>And You, O Rock, have destined them to punish.  
**1:13** <i>Your</i> eyes are too pure to look at evil,<br>And You cannot look at harm <i>favorably.</i><br>Why do You look <i>favorably</i><br>At those who deal treacherously?<br><i>Why</i> are You silent when the wicked swallow up<br>Those more righteous than they?  
**1:14** <i>Why</i> have You made people like the fish of the sea,<br>Like crawling things that have no ruler over them?  
**1:15** <i>The Chaldeans</i> bring all of them up with a hook,<br>Drag them away with their net,<br>And gather them together in their fishing net.<br>Therefore they rejoice and are joyful.  
**1:16** Therefore they offer a sacrifice to their net<br>And <sup>①</sup>burn incense to their fishing net,<br>Because through these things their <sup>②</sup>catch is <sup>③</sup>large,<br>And their food is <sup>④</sup>plentiful.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sacrifice</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>portion</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>fat;</i> or <i>plentiful</i> <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>fat</i>  
**1:17** Will they therefore empty their net,<br>And continually slay nations without sparing?  

## 2장

**2:1** I will stand at my guard post<br>And station myself on the watchtower;<br>And I will keep watch to see what He will say to me,<br>And how I may reply <sup>①</sup>when I am reprimanded.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>upon my reprimand</i>  
**2:2** Then the LORD answered me and said,<br>“Write down the vision<br>And inscribe <i>it</i> clearly on tablets,<br>So that one who <sup>①</sup>reads it may run.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>is to proclaim;</i> or <i>reads it</i> may read it <i>fluently</i>  
**2:3** For the vision is yet for the appointed time;<br>It <sup>①</sup>hurries toward the goal and it will not <sup>②</sup>fail.<br>Though it delays, wait for it;<br>For it will certainly come, it will not delay <i>long.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>pants</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>lie</i>  
**2:4** “Behold, as for the impudent one,<br>His soul is not right within him;<br>But the righteous one will live by his <sup>①</sup>faith.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>faithfulness</i>  
**2:5** Furthermore, wine betrays an arrogant man,<br>So that he does not achieve his objective.<br>He enlarges his appetite like Sheol,<br>And he is like death, never satisfied.<br>He also gathers to himself all the nations<br>And collects to himself all the peoples.  
**2:6** “Will all of these not take up a song of ridicule against him,<br>Even a saying <i>and</i> insinuations against him<br>And say, ‘Woe to him who increases what is not his—<br>For how long—<br>And makes himself <sup>①</sup>rich with debts!’<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>heavy</i>  
**2:7** Will <sup>①</sup>your creditors not rise up suddenly,<br>And those who <sup>②</sup>collect from you awaken?<br>Indeed, you will become plunder for them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>those who bite you</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>violently shake you</i>  
**2:8** Since you have looted many nations,<br>All the rest of the peoples will loot you—<br>Because of human bloodshed and violence <sup>①</sup>done to the land,<br>To the town and all its inhabitants.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of the land</i>  
**2:9** “Woe to him who makes evil profit for his household,<br>To put his nest on high,<br>To be saved from the hand of catastrophe!  
**2:10** You have planned a shameful thing for your house<br>By bringing many peoples to an end;<br>So you are sinning against yourself.  
**2:11** For the stone will cry out from the wall,<br>And the rafter will answer it from the <sup>①</sup>framework.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>wood</i>  
**2:12** “Woe to him who builds a city with bloodshed,<br>And founds a town with <sup>①</sup>violence!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>injustice</i>  
**2:13** Is it not indeed from the LORD of armies<br>That peoples labor <i>merely</i> for fire,<br>And nations become weary for nothing?  
**2:14** For the earth will be filled<br>With the knowledge of the glory of the LORD,<br>As the waters cover the sea.  
**2:15** “Woe to him who makes his neighbor drink;<br> <sup>①</sup>To you who mix in your venom even to make <i>your neighbors</i> drunk,<br>So as to look at their genitalia!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Another reading is <i>From the cup of your venom</i>  
**2:16** You will be filled with disgrace rather than honor.<br>Drink, you yourself, and <sup>①</sup>expose your <i>own</i> foreskin!<br>The cup in the LORD’S right hand will come around to you,<br>And utter disgrace <i>will come</i> upon your glory.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>show yourself uncircumcised;</i> DSS and ancient versions <i>stagger</i>  
**2:17** For the violence <sup>①</sup>done to Lebanon will <sup>②</sup>overwhelm you,<br>And the devastation of <i>its</i> animals <sup>③</sup>by which you terrified them,<br>Because of human bloodshed and violence <sup>④</sup>done to the land,<br>To the town and all its inhabitants.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of Lebanon</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>cover</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit which <i>shattered them</i> <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>of the land</i>  
**2:18** “What benefit is a carved image when its maker has carved it,<br><i>Or</i> a cast metal image, a teacher of falsehood?<br>For <i>its</i> maker trusts in his <i>own</i> handiwork<br>When he fashions speechless idols.  
**2:19** Woe to him who says to a <i>piece of</i> wood, ‘Awake!’<br>To a mute stone, ‘Arise!’<br>That is <i>your</i> teacher?<br>Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver,<br>Yet there is no breath at all inside it.  
**2:20** But the LORD is in His holy temple.<br> <sup>①</sup>Let all the earth be silent before Him.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Hush before Him, all the earth</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, according to <sup>①</sup>Shigionoth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Perhaps a highly emotional poetic form  
**3:2** LORD, I have heard <sup>①</sup>the report about You, <i>and</i> <sup>②</sup>I was afraid.<br>LORD, revive Your work in the midst of the years,<br>In the midst of the years make <i>it</i> known.<br>In anger remember <sup>③</sup>mercy.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Your report</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>I am in awe, O LORD</i>, of Your work<i>; In the midst of the years revive it,</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>compassion</i>  
**3:3** God comes from Teman,<br>And the Holy One from Mount Paran. <i>Selah</i> His splendor covers the heavens,<br>And the earth is full of His praise.  
**3:4** <i>His</i> radiance is like the sunlight;<br>He has rays <i>flashing</i> from His hand,<br>And the hiding of His might is there.  
**3:5** Before Him goes plague,<br>And plague comes forth <sup>①</sup>after Him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>at His feet</i>  
**3:6** He stood and caused the earth to shudder;<br>He looked and caused the nations to jump.<br>Yes, the everlasting mountains were shattered,<br>The ancient hills <sup>①</sup>collapsed.<br>His paths are everlasting.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>cowered</i>  
**3:7** I saw the tents of Cushan under distress,<br>The tent curtains of the land of Midian were trembling.  
**3:8** Did the LORD rage against the rivers,<br>Or <i>was</i> Your anger against the rivers,<br>Or <i>was</i> Your rage against the sea,<br>That You rode on Your horses,<br>On Your chariots of salvation?  
**3:9** You removed Your bow <i>from its holder,</i><br>The arrows of <i>Your</i> word were <sup>①</sup>sworn. <i>Selah</i> You divided the earth with rivers.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>oaths</i>  
**3:10** The mountains saw You <i>and</i> quaked;<br>The downpour of waters swept by.<br>The deep raised its voice,<br>It lifted high its hands.  
**3:11** Sun <i>and</i> moon stood in their lofty places;<br>They went away at the light of Your arrows,<br>At the radiance of Your flashing spear.  
**3:12** In indignation You marched through the earth;<br>In anger You <sup>①</sup>trampled the nations.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>threshed</i>  
**3:13** You went forth for the salvation of Your people,<br>For the salvation of Your anointed.<br>You smashed the head of the house of evil<br>To uncover <i>him from</i> <sup>①</sup>foot to neck. <i>Selah</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>base</i>  
**3:14** You pierced with his own <sup>①</sup>arrows<br>The head of his <sup>②</sup>leaders.<br>They stormed in to scatter <sup>③</sup>us;<br>Their arrogance <i>was</i> like those<br>Who devour the oppressed in secret.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>shafts</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>villagers</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>me</i>  
**3:15** You trampled on the sea with Your horses,<br>On the foam of many waters.  
**3:16** I heard, and my <sup>①</sup>inner parts trembled;<br>At the sound, my lips quivered.<br>Decay enters my bones,<br>And in my place I tremble;<br>Because I <sup>②</sup>must wait quietly for the day of distress,<br> <sup>③</sup>For the people to arise <i>who</i> will attack us.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>belly</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>sigh for</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>To come upon the people</i> who <i>will</i>  
**3:17** Even if the fig tree does not blossom,<br>And there is no <sup>①</sup>fruit on the vines,<br><i>If</i> the yield of the olive fails,<br>And the fields produce no food,<br><i>Even if</i> the flock disappears from the fold,<br>And there are no cattle in the stalls,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>produce</i>  
**3:18** Yet I will triumph in the LORD,<br>I will rejoice in the God of my salvation.  
**3:19** The Lord <sup>①</sup>GOD is my strength,<br>And He has made my feet like deer’s <i>feet,</i><br>And has me walk on my high places. For the choir director, on my stringed instruments.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>YHWH,</i> usually rendered <i>LORD</i>
