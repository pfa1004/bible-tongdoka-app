## 1장

**1:1** The words of Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah, of the priests who were in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin,  
**1:2** to whom the word of the LORD came in the days of Josiah the son of Amon, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.  
**1:3** It came also in the days of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah the son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the exile of Jerusalem in the fifth month.  
**1:4** Now the word of the LORD came to me, saying,  
**1:5** “Before I formed you in the womb I knew you,<br>And before you were born I consecrated you;<br>I have appointed you as a prophet to the nations.”  
**1:6** Then I said, “Oh, Lord <sup>①</sup>GOD!<br>Behold, I do not know how to speak,<br>Because I am a youth.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>YHWH,</i> usually rendered <i>LORD</i>  
**1:7** But the LORD said to me,<br>“Do not say, ‘I am a youth,’<br>Because everywhere I send you, you shall go,<br>And all that I command you, you shall speak.  
**1:8** Do not be afraid of them,<br>For I am with you to save you,” declares the LORD.  
**1:9** Then the LORD stretched out His hand and touched my mouth, and the LORD said to me,<br>“Behold, I have put My words in your mouth.  
**1:10** See, I have appointed you this day over the nations and over the kingdoms,<br>To root out and to tear down,<br>To destroy and to overthrow,<br>To build and to plant.”  
**1:11** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying, “What do you see, Jeremiah?” And I said, “I see a branch of an <sup>①</sup>almond tree.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>shaqed,</i> see note v 12  
**1:12** Then the LORD said to me, “You have seen well, for I am <sup>①</sup>watching over My word to perform it.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>shoqed,</i> a wordplay on <i>almond</i> in v 11 (see note v 11)  
**1:13** And the word of the LORD came to me a second time, saying, “What do you see?” And I said, “I see a boiling pot, facing away from the north.”  
**1:14** Then the LORD said to me, “Out of the north the evil <sup>①</sup>will be unleashed on all the inhabitants of the land.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>opened</i>  
**1:15** For, behold, I am calling all the families of the kingdoms of the north,” declares the LORD; “and they will come and place, each one <i>of them,</i> his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, and against all its walls around, and against all the cities of Judah.  
**1:16** And I will <sup>①</sup>pronounce My judgments against them concerning all their wickedness, since they have abandoned Me and have <sup>②</sup>offered sacrifices to other gods, and worshiped the works of their own hands.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>speak</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>burned incense</i>  
**1:17** Now, belt <i>your garment</i> around your waist and arise, and speak to them all that I command you. Do not be dismayed before them, or I will make you dismayed before them.  
**1:18** Now behold, I have made you today like a fortified city and like a pillar of iron and walls of bronze against the whole land, to the kings of Judah, to its leaders, to its priests, and to the people of the land.  
**1:19** And they will fight against you but they will not overcome you, for I am with you to save you,” declares the LORD.  

## 2장

**2:29** “Why do you contend with Me?<br>You have all revolted against Me,” declares the LORD.  
**2:30** “In vain I have struck your sons;<br>They did not accept discipline.<br>Your sword has devoured your prophets<br>Like a destroying lion.  
**2:31** You generation, look to the word of the LORD.<br>Have I been a wilderness to Israel,<br>Or a land of thick darkness?<br>Why do My people say, ‘We <i>are free to</i> roam;<br>We will no longer come to You’?

**2:33** How well you prepare your way<br>To seek love!<br>Therefore even to the wicked women<br>You have taught your ways.  
**2:34** Also on your skirts is found<br>The lifeblood of the innocent poor;<br>You did not find them breaking in.<br>But in spite of all these things,  
**2:35** You said, ‘I am innocent;<br>Surely His anger is turned away from me.’<br>Behold, I will enter into judgment with you<br>Because you say, ‘I have not sinned.’  
**2:36** Why do you go around so much<br>Changing your way?<br>Also, you will be put to shame by Egypt,<br>Just as you were put to shame by Assyria.  
**2:37** From this <i>place</i> as well you will go out<br>With your hands on your head;<br>For the LORD has rejected those in whom you trust,<br>And you will not prosper with them.”

**3:3** Therefore the showers have been withheld,<br>And there has been no spring rain.<br>Yet you had a prostitute’s <sup>①</sup>forehead;<br>You refused to be ashamed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., calloused appearance
