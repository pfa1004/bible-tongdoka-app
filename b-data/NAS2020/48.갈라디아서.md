## 1장

## 5장

**5:1** It was for freedom that Christ set us free; therefore keep standing firm and do not be subject again to a yoke of slavery.  
**5:2** Look! I, Paul, tell you that if you have yourselves circumcised, Christ will be of no benefit to you.  
**5:3** And I testify again to every man who has himself circumcised, that he is obligated to keep the whole Law.  
**5:4** You have been severed from Christ, you who <sup>①</sup>are seeking to be justified by the <sup>②</sup>Law; you have fallen from grace.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>law</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>would be</i>  
**5:5** For we, <sup>①</sup>through the Spirit, <sup>②</sup>by faith, are waiting for the hope of righteousness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>by</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>out of</i>  
**5:6** For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision means anything, but faith working through love.  
**5:7** You were running well; who hindered you from obeying the truth?  
**5:8** This persuasion <i>did</i> not <i>come</i> from Him who calls you.  
**5:9** A little <sup>①</sup>leaven leavens the whole lump <i>of dough.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., fermented dough  
**5:10** I have confidence <sup>①</sup>in you in the Lord, that you will adopt no other view; but the one who is disturbing you will bear the punishment, whoever he is.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>toward</i>  
**5:11** But as for me, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> if I still preach circumcision, why am I still persecuted? Then the stumbling block of the cross has been eliminated.  
**5:12** I wish that those who are troubling you would even <sup>①</sup>emasculate themselves.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>cut themselves off</i>  
**5:13** For you were called to freedom, brothers <i>and sisters;</i> only <i>do</i> not <i>turn</i> your freedom into an opportunity for the flesh, but serve one another through love.  
**5:14** For the whole Law is fulfilled in one word, in the <i>statement,</i> “YOU SHALL LOVE YOUR NEIGHBOR AS YOURSELF.”  
**5:15** But if you bite and devour one another, take care that you are not consumed by one another.  
**5:16** But I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will not carry out the desire of the flesh.  
**5:17** For the desire of the flesh is against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh; for these are in opposition to one another, in order to keep you from doing whatever you want.  
**5:18** But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the Law.  
**5:19** Now the deeds of the flesh are evident, which are: sexual immorality, impurity, indecent behavior,  
**5:20** idolatry, witchcraft, hostilities, strife, jealousy, outbursts of anger, selfish ambition, dissensions, factions,  
**5:21** envy, drunkenness, carousing, and things like these, of which I forewarn you, just as I have forewarned you, that those who practice such things will not inherit the kingdom of God.

**5:23** gentleness, self-control; against such things there is no law.

## 6장

**6:1** Brothers <i>and sisters,</i> even if a person is caught in any wrongdoing, you who are spiritual are to restore such a person in a spirit of gentleness; <i>each one</i> looking to yourself, so that you are not tempted as well.  
**6:2** Bear one another’s burdens, and thereby fulfill the law of Christ.  
**6:3** For if anyone thinks that he is something when he is nothing, he deceives himself.  
**6:4** But each one must examine his own work, and then he will have <i>reason for</i> boasting, <i>but</i> <sup>①</sup>to himself alone, and not <sup>②</sup>to another.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in regard to</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>in regard to</i>  
**6:5** For each one will bear his own load.  
**6:6** The one who is taught the word is to share all good things with the one who teaches <i>him.</i>  
**6:7** Do not be deceived, God is not mocked; for whatever a person sows, this he will also reap.  
**6:8** For the one who sows to his own flesh will reap destruction from the flesh, but the one who sows to the Spirit will reap eternal life from the Spirit.  
**6:9** Let’s not become discouraged in doing good, for in due time we will reap, if we do not become weary.  
**6:10** So then, <sup>①</sup>while we have opportunity, let’s do good to all people, and especially to those who are of the household of the faith.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>as</i>  
**6:11** See with what large letters I have written to you with my own hand!  
**6:12** All who want to make a good showing in the flesh try to compel you to be circumcised, simply so that they will not be persecuted for the cross of Christ.  
**6:13** For those who <sup>①</sup>are circumcised do not even keep <sup>②</sup>the Law themselves, but they want to have you circumcised so that they may boast in your flesh.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Two early mss <i>have been</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>law</i>

**6:15** For neither is circumcision anything, nor uncircumcision, but a new <sup>①</sup>creation.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>creature</i>
