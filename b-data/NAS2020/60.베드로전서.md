## 1장

**1:1** Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, <e>T</e>o those who reside as strangers, scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia, who are chosen  
**1:2** according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, by the sanctifying work of the Spirit, <sup>①</sup>to obey Jesus Christ and be sprinkled with His blood: May grace and peace be multiplied to you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>unto obedience and sprinkling</i>

**1:4** to <i>obtain</i> an inheritance <i>which is</i> imperishable, undefiled, and will not fade away, reserved in heaven for you,  
**1:5** who are protected by the power of God through faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in <i>the</i> last time.

**1:16** because it is written: “YOU SHALL BE HOLY, FOR I AM HOLY.”  
**1:17** If you address as Father the One who impartially judges according to each one’s work, conduct yourselves in fear during the time of your stay <i>on earth;</i>  
**1:18** knowing that you were not <sup>①</sup>redeemed with perishable things like silver or gold from your futile way of life inherited from your forefathers,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>ransomed</i>  
**1:19** but with precious blood, as of a lamb unblemished and spotless, <i>the blood</i> of Christ.  
**1:20** For He was foreknown before the foundation of the world, but has appeared <sup>①</sup>in these last times for the sake of you<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>at the end of the times</i>  
**1:21** who through Him are believers in God, who raised Him from the dead and gave Him glory, so that your faith and hope are in God.  
**1:22** Since you have purified your souls in obedience to the truth for a sincere love of the brothers <i>and</i> sisters, fervently love one another from <sup>①</sup>the heart,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Two early mss <i>a pure heart</i>

**1:24** For,<br>“ALL FLESH IS LIKE GRASS,<br>AND ALL ITS GLORY IS LIKE THE FLOWER OF GRASS.<br>THE GRASS WITHERS,<br>AND THE FLOWER FALLS OFF,  
**1:25** BUT THE WORD OF THE LORD ENDURES FOREVER.” And this is the word which was <sup>①</sup>preached to you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>preached as good news to you</i>

**2:22** <i>HE who committed no</i> sin, NOR WAS ANY DECEIT found IN HIS MOUTH;

## 3장

**3:1** In the same way, you wives, be subject to your own husbands so that even if any <i>of them</i> are disobedient to the word, they may be won over without a word by the behavior of their wives,  
**3:2** as they observe your pure <sup>①</sup>and respectful behavior.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>with respect</i>  
**3:3** Your adornment must not be <i>merely</i> the external—braiding the hair, wearing gold <i>jewelry,</i> or putting on apparel;  
**3:4** but <i>it should be</i> the hidden person of the heart, with the imperishable <i>quality</i> of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is precious in the sight of God.  
**3:5** For in this way the holy women of former times, who hoped in God, also used to adorn themselves, being subject to their own husbands,  
**3:6** just as Sarah obeyed Abraham, calling him lord; and you have proved to be her children if you do what is right <sup>①</sup>without being frightened by any fear.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and are not</i>  
**3:7** You husbands in the same way, live with <i>your wives</i> in an understanding way, as with <sup>①</sup>someone weaker, <i>since she is</i> a woman; and show her honor as a fellow heir of the grace of life, so that your prayers will not be hindered.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>a weaker vessel</i>  
**3:8**  <sup>①</sup>To sum up, all <i>of you</i> be harmonious, sympathetic, <sup>②</sup>loving, compassionate, <i>and</i> humble;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Finally</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., as brothers and sisters  
**3:9** not returning evil for evil or insult for insult, but giving a blessing instead; for you were called for the very purpose that you would inherit a blessing.  
**3:10** For,<br>“THE ONE WHO DESIRES LIFE, TO LOVE AND SEE GOOD DAYS,<br>MUST KEEP HIS TONGUE FROM EVIL AND HIS LIPS FROM SPEAKING DECEIT.  
**3:11** HE MUST TURN AWAY FROM EVIL AND DO GOOD;<br>HE MUST SEEK PEACE AND PURSUE IT.  
**3:12** FOR THE EYES OF THE LORD ARE TOWARD THE RIGHTEOUS,<br>AND HIS EARS ATTEND TO THEIR PRAYER,<br>BUT THE FACE OF THE LORD IS AGAINST EVILDOERS.”  
**3:13** And who is <sup>①</sup>there to harm you if you prove zealous for what is good?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the one who will harm you</i>  
**3:14** But even if you should suffer for the sake of righteousness, you <sup>①</sup>are blessed. AND DO NOT FEAR THEIR <sup>②</sup>INTIMIDATION, AND DO NOT BE IN DREAD,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>would be</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>fear</i>  
**3:15** but <sup>①</sup>sanctify Christ as Lord in your hearts, always <i>being</i> ready to make a <sup>②</sup>defense to everyone who asks you to give an account for the hope that is in you, but with gentleness and respect;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., set apart <sup>②</sup>Or <i>argument;</i> or <i>explanation</i>  
**3:16**  <sup>①</sup>and keep a good conscience so that in the thing in which you are slandered, those who disparage your good behavior in Christ will be put to shame.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>having a good</i>  
**3:17** For it is better, if <sup>①</sup>God should will it <i>so,</i> that you suffer for doing what is right rather than for doing what is wrong.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the will of God</i>  
**3:18** For Christ also <sup>①</sup>suffered for sins once for all <i>time, the</i> just for <i>the</i> unjust, so that He might bring us to God, having been put to death in the flesh, but made alive in the <sup>②</sup>spirit;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms <i>died</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>Spirit</i>  
**3:19** in <sup>①</sup>which He also went and made proclamation to the spirits in prison,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>whom</i>  
**3:20** who once were disobedient when the patience of God kept waiting in the days of Noah, during the construction of the ark, in which a few, that is, eight persons, were brought safely through <i>the</i> water.  
**3:21** Corresponding to that, baptism now saves you—not the removal of dirt from the flesh, but an appeal to God <sup>①</sup>for a good conscience—through the resurrection of Jesus Christ,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>from</i>  
**3:22** who is at the right hand of God, having gone into heaven, after angels and authorities and powers had been subjected to Him.
