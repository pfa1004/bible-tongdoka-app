# 57.빌레몬서 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy <sup>①</sup>our brother, <e>T</e>o Philemon our beloved <i>brother</i> and fellow worker,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the</i>  
**1:2** and to Apphia <sup>①</sup>our sister, and to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church in your house:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the</i>  
**1:3** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:4** I thank my God always, making mention of you in my prayers,  
**1:5** because I hear of your love and of the faith which you have toward the Lord Jesus and toward all the <sup>①</sup>saints;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**1:6** <i>and I pray</i> that the fellowship of your faith may become effective <sup>①</sup>through the knowledge of every good thing which is in you <sup>②</sup>for the sake of Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>toward Christ</i>  
**1:7** For I have had great joy and comfort in your love, because the <sup>①</sup>hearts of the <sup>②</sup>saints have been refreshed through you, brother.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>inward parts</i> <sup>②</sup>See note v 5  
**1:8** Therefore, though I have <sup>①</sup>enough confidence in Christ to order you <i>to do</i> what is proper,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>much</i>  
**1:9** <i>yet</i> for love’s sake I rather appeal <i>to you</i>—since I am such a person as Paul, <sup>①</sup>an old man, and now also a prisoner of Christ Jesus—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>an ambassador</i>  
**1:10** I appeal to you for my <sup>①</sup>son <sup>②</sup>Onesimus, whom I <sup>③</sup>fathered in my <sup>④</sup>imprisonment,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>child</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., useful <sup>③</sup>I.e., led to the Lord <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>bonds</i>  
**1:11** who previously was useless to you, but now is useful both to you and to me.  
**1:12** I have sent him back to you in person, that is, <i>sending</i> my very heart,  
**1:13** whom I wanted to keep with me, so that in your behalf he might be at my service in my <sup>①</sup>imprisonment for the gospel;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>bonds</i>  
**1:14** but I did not want to do anything without your consent, so that your goodness would not be, in effect, by compulsion, but of your own free will.  
**1:15** For perhaps <i>it was</i> for this reason <i>that</i> he was separated <i>from you</i> for a while, that you would have him back forever,  
**1:16** no longer as a slave, but more than a slave, a beloved brother, especially to me, but how much more to you, both in the flesh and in the Lord.  
**1:17** If then you regard me <i>as</i> a partner, accept him as <i>you would</i> me.  
**1:18** But if he has wronged you in any way or owes <i>you anything,</i> charge that to my account;  
**1:19** I, Paul, have written <i>this</i> with my own hand, I will repay <i>it</i> (not to <sup>①</sup>mention to you that you owe to me even your own self as well).<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>say</i>  
**1:20** Yes, brother, let me benefit from you in the Lord; refresh my heart in Christ.  
**1:21** Having confidence in your obedience, I write to you, since I know that you will do even more than what I say.  
**1:22** At the same time also prepare me a guest room, for I hope that through your prayers I will be given to you.  
**1:23** Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, greets you,  
**1:24** <i>as do</i> Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, <i>and</i> Luke, my fellow workers.  
**1:25** The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. <sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms adds <i>Amen</i>
