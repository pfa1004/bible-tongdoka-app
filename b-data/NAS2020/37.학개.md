# 37.학개 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** In the second year of Darius the king, on the first day of the sixth month, the word of the LORD came by the prophet Haggai to Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, saying,  
**1:2** “This is what the LORD of armies <sup>①</sup>says: ‘This people says, “The time has not come, the time for the house of the LORD to be rebuilt.” ’ ”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>says, saying</i>  
**1:3** Then the word of the LORD came by Haggai the prophet, saying,  
**1:4** “Is it time for you yourselves to live in your paneled houses while this house <i>remains</i> desolate?”  
**1:5** Now then, the LORD of armies says this: “ <sup>①</sup>Consider your ways!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Set your heart on</i>  
**1:6** You have sown much, <i>only to</i> <sup>①</sup>harvest little; <i>you</i> eat, but there is not <i>enough</i> to be satisfied; <i>you</i> drink, but there is not <i>enough</i> to become drunk; <i>you</i> put on clothing, but there is not <i>enough</i> for <sup>②</sup>anyone to get warm; and the one who earns, earns wages <i>to put</i> into a money bag full of holes.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>bring in</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>him</i>  
**1:7** The LORD of armies says this: “ <sup>①</sup>Consider your ways!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Set your heart on</i>  
**1:8** Go up to the <sup>①</sup>mountains, bring wood, and rebuild the <sup>②</sup>temple, that I may be pleased with it and be honored,” says the LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>mountain</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>house</i>  
**1:9** “<i>You</i> <sup>①</sup>start an ambitious project, but behold, <i>it comes</i> to little; when you bring <i>it</i> home, I blow it <i>away.</i> Why?” declares the LORD of armies. “<i>It is</i> because of My house which <i>remains</i> desolate, while each of you runs to his own house.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>turn to much</i>  
**1:10** Therefore, because of you the sky has withheld <sup>①</sup>its dew, and the earth has withheld its produce.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>from dew</i>  
**1:11** And I called for a drought on the land, on the mountains, on the grain, on the new wine, on the oil, on what the ground produces, on mankind, on cattle, and on all the products of the labor of <sup>①</sup>your hands.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>palms</i>  
**1:12** Then Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, and Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, with all the remnant of the people, obeyed the voice of the LORD their God and the words of Haggai the prophet, just as the LORD their God had sent him. And the people <sup>①</sup>showed reverence for the LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>feared before</i>  
**1:13** Then Haggai, the messenger of the LORD, spoke <sup>①</sup>by the commission of the LORD to the people, saying, “ ‘I am with you,’ declares the LORD.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>with the commissioned message</i>  
**1:14** So the LORD stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people; and they came and worked on the house of the LORD of armies, their God,  
**1:15** on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month in the second year of Darius the king.  

## 2장

**2:1** On the twenty-first of the seventh month, the word of the LORD came by Haggai the prophet, saying,  
**2:2** “Speak now to Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the remnant of the people, saying,  
**2:3** ‘Who is left among you who saw this <sup>①</sup>temple in its former glory? And how do you see it now? <sup>②</sup>Does it not seem to you like nothing <sup>③</sup>in comparison?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>house</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>Is it not in your eyes like</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>like it</i>  
**2:4** But now <sup>①</sup>take courage, Zerubbabel,’ declares the LORD, ‘take courage also, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all you people of the land take courage,’ declares the LORD, ‘and work; for I am with you,’ declares the LORD of armies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>be strong</i>  
**2:5** ‘As for the <sup>①</sup>promise which I <sup>②</sup>made you when you came out of Egypt, <sup>③</sup>My Spirit remains in your midst; do not fear!’<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>word</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>cut with</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>while My Spirit was standing</i>  
**2:6** For this is what the LORD of armies says: ‘Once more <sup>①</sup>in a little while, I am going to shake the heavens and the earth, the sea also and the dry land.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>it is a little</i>  
**2:7** I will shake all the nations; and <sup>①</sup>they will come <i>with</i> the wealth of all nations, and I will fill this house with glory,’ says the LORD of armies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Another reading is <i>the wealth of all nations will come</i>  
**2:8** ‘The silver is Mine and the gold is Mine,’ declares the LORD of armies.  
**2:9** ‘The latter glory of this house will be greater than the former,’ says the LORD of armies, ‘and in this place I will give peace,’ declares the LORD of armies.”  
**2:10** On the twenty-fourth of the ninth <i>month,</i> in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Haggai the prophet, saying,  
**2:11** “The LORD of armies says this: ‘Now ask the priests <i>for</i> a <sup>①</sup>ruling:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>law;</i> or <i>saying</i>  
**2:12** If someone carries holy meat in the <sup>①</sup>fold of his garment, and touches bread with <sup>②</sup>this fold, or <i>touches</i> cooked food, wine, oil, or any <i>other</i> food, will it become holy?’ ” And the priests answered, “No.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>wing</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>his wing</i>  
**2:13** Then Haggai said, “If one who is unclean from a <sup>①</sup>corpse touches any of these <i>things,</i> will <i>the latter</i> become unclean?” And the priests answered, “It will become unclean.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>soul</i>  
**2:14** Then Haggai responded and said, “ ‘So is this people. And so is this nation before Me,’ declares the LORD, ‘and so is every work of their hands; and what they offer there is unclean.  
**2:15** But now, do <sup>①</sup>consider from this day <sup>②</sup>onward: before one stone was placed on another in the temple of the LORD,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>set your heart</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>backward</i>  
**2:16**  <sup>①</sup>from that time <i>when</i> one came to a grain heap of twenty <i>measures,</i> there would be <i>only</i> ten; <i>and when</i> one came to the wine vat to draw fifty <sup>②</sup>measures, there would be <i>only</i> twenty.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>since they were</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>troughs</i>  
**2:17** I struck you <i>and</i> every work of your hands with scorching wind, mildew, and hail; <sup>①</sup>yet you did not come back to Me,’ declares the LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>As in LXX; MT <i>but you were not to Me</i>  
**2:18** ‘Do <sup>①</sup>consider from this day <sup>②</sup>onward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth <i>month;</i> from the day when the temple of the LORD was founded, <sup>③</sup>consider:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>set your heart</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>backward</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>set your heart</i>  
**2:19** Is the seed still in the barn? Even including the vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate, and the olive tree, it has not produced <i>fruit. Yet</i> from this day <i>on</i> I will bless <i>you.</i>’ ”  
**2:20** Then the word of the LORD came a second time to Haggai on the twenty-fourth <i>day</i> of the month, saying,  
**2:21** “Speak to Zerubbabel governor of Judah, saying, ‘I am going to shake the heavens and the earth.  
**2:22** And I will overthrow the thrones of kingdoms and destroy the power of the kingdoms of the <sup>①</sup>nations; and I will overthrow the chariots and their riders, and the horses and their riders will go down, every one by the sword of another.’<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Gentiles</i>  
**2:23** ‘On that day,’ declares the LORD of armies, ‘I will take you, Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, My servant,’ declares the LORD, ‘and I will make you like a <sup>①</sup>signet ring, for I have chosen you,’ ” declares the LORD of armies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>seal</i>
