## 1장

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus <sup>①</sup>by the will of God, <e>T</e>o the <sup>②</sup>saints who are <sup>③</sup>at Ephesus and <i>are</i> faithful in Christ Jesus:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>through</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people <sup>③</sup>Three early mss do not contain <i>at Ephesus</i>  
**1:2** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:3** Blessed <i>be</i> the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly <i>places</i> in Christ,  
**1:4** just as He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world, that we would be holy and blameless before <sup>①</sup>Him. In love<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Him, in love. He</i>  
**1:5**  <sup>①</sup>He predestined us to adoption as sons <i>and daughters</i> through Jesus Christ to Himself, according to the good pleasure of His will,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>having predestined</i>  
**1:6** to the praise of the glory of His grace, with which He favored us in the Beloved.  
**1:7** In <sup>①</sup>Him we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of our wrongdoings, according to the riches of His grace<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>whom</i>  
**1:8** which He <sup>①</sup>lavished on <sup>②</sup>us. In all wisdom and insight<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>made abundant toward</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>us in all wisdom and insight. He</i>  
**1:9** He <sup>①</sup>made known to us the mystery of His will, according to His good pleasure which He set forth in Him,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>making known</i>  
**1:10** regarding <i>His</i> plan of the fullness of the times, to bring all things together in Christ, things <sup>①</sup>in the heavens and things on the earth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>upon</i>  
**1:11**  <sup>①</sup>In Him we also have obtained an inheritance, having been predestined according to the purpose of Him who works all things in accordance with the plan of His will,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>In whom also</i>  
**1:12** to the end that <sup>①</sup>we who were the first to hope in the <sup>②</sup>Christ would be to the praise of His glory.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., Jewish believers <sup>②</sup>I.e., Messiah  
**1:13** In <sup>①</sup>Him, <sup>②</sup>you also, after listening to the message of truth, the gospel of your salvation—having also <sup>③</sup>believed, you were sealed in <sup>④</sup>Him with the Holy Spirit of the promise,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>whom</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., Gentile believers <sup>③</sup>Or <i>believed in Him, you were sealed</i> <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>whom</i>  
**1:14** who is a first installment of our inheritance, in regard to the redemption of <i>God’s own</i> possession, to the praise of His glory.  
**1:15** For this reason I too, having heard of the faith in the Lord Jesus which <i>exists</i> among you and <sup>①</sup>your love for all the <sup>②</sup>saints,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Three early mss do not contain <i>your love</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**1:16** do not cease giving thanks for you, while making mention <i>of you</i> in my prayers;  
**1:17** that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give you a spirit of wisdom and of revelation in the <sup>①</sup>knowledge of Him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>true knowledge</i>  
**1:18** <i>I pray that</i> the eyes of your heart <sup>①</sup>may be enlightened, so that you will know what is the hope of His calling, what are the riches of the glory of His inheritance in the <sup>②</sup>saints,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>having been</i> <sup>②</sup>See note 2 v 15  
**1:19** and what is the <sup>①</sup>boundless greatness of His power toward us who believe. <i>These are</i> in accordance with the working of the strength of His might<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>surpassing</i>  
**1:20** which He brought about in Christ, when He raised Him from the dead and seated Him at His right hand in the heavenly <i>places,</i>  
**1:21** far above all rule and authority and power and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the one to come.  
**1:22** And He put all things in subjection under His feet, and made Him head over all things to the church,  
**1:23** which is His body, the fullness of Him who fills all in all.  

## 2장

**2:1** And <sup>①</sup>you <sup>②</sup>were dead <sup>③</sup>in your offenses and sins,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>being</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., Gentile believers <sup>③</sup>Or <i>by reason of</i>  
**2:2** in which you previously walked according to the <sup>①</sup>course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air, of the spirit that is now working in the <sup>②</sup>sons of disobedience.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>age</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., people opposed to God  
**2:3** Among them <sup>①</sup>we too all previously lived in the lusts of our flesh, <sup>②</sup>indulging the desires of the flesh and of the <sup>③</sup>mind, and were by nature children of wrath, just as the rest.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., Jewish believers <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>doing</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>thoughts</i>  
**2:4** But God, being rich in mercy, because of His great love with which He loved us,  
**2:5** even when we were dead <sup>①</sup>in our wrongdoings, made us alive together <sup>②</sup>with Christ (by grace you have been saved),<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>by reason of</i> <sup>②</sup>Two early mss <i>in Christ</i>  
**2:6** and raised us up with Him, and seated us with Him in the heavenly <i>places</i> in Christ Jesus,  
**2:7** so that in the ages to come He might show the <sup>①</sup>boundless riches of His grace in kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>surpassing</i>  
**2:8** For by grace you have been saved through faith; and <sup>①</sup>this <i>is</i> not of yourselves, <i>it is</i> the gift of God;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., this salvation  
**2:9** not a result of works, so that no one may boast.  
**2:10** For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand so that we would walk in them.  
**2:11** Therefore remember that previously you, the Gentiles in the flesh, who are called “Uncircumcision” by the so-called “Circumcision” <i>which is</i> performed in the flesh by human hands—  
**2:12** <i>remember</i> that you were at that time separate from Christ, <sup>①</sup>excluded from <sup>②</sup>the people of Israel, and strangers to the covenants of the promise, having no hope and without God in the world.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>alienated</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>citizenship in Israel</i>  
**2:13** But now in Christ Jesus you who previously were far away <sup>①</sup>have been brought near <sup>②</sup>by the blood of Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>became;</i> or <i>were made</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>in</i>  
**2:14** For He Himself is our peace, who made both <i>groups into</i> one and broke down the <sup>①</sup>barrier of the dividing wall,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the dividing wall of the barrier</i>  
**2:15**  <sup>①</sup>by abolishing <sup>②</sup>in His flesh the hostility, <i>which is</i> the Law <i>composed</i> of commandments <i>expressed</i> in ordinances, so that in Himself He might <sup>③</sup>make the two one new person, <i>in this way</i> establishing peace;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the hostility, by abolishing in His flesh the Law</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., by His death <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>create</i>  
**2:16** and that He might reconcile them both in one body to God through the cross, <sup>①</sup>by it having put to death the hostility.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in Himself</i>  
**2:17** And He came and preached peace to you who were far away, and peace to those who were near;  
**2:18** for through Him we both have our access in one Spirit to the Father.  
**2:19** So then you are no longer strangers and foreigners, but you are fellow citizens with the <sup>①</sup>saints, and are of God’s household,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**2:20** having been built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus Himself being the cornerstone,  
**2:21** in whom the whole building, being fitted together, is growing into a holy <sup>①</sup>temple in the Lord,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sanctuary</i>  
**2:22** in whom you also are being built together into a dwelling of God in the Spirit.  

## 3장

**3:1** For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus for the sake of you Gentiles—  
**3:2** if indeed you have heard of the administration of God’s grace which was given to me for you;  
**3:3** that by revelation there was made known to me the mystery, as I wrote before briefly.  
**3:4**  <sup>①</sup>By referring to this, when you read you can understand my insight <sup>②</sup>into the mystery of Christ,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>To which, when you read</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>in</i>  
**3:5** which in other generations was not made known to <sup>①</sup>mankind, as it has now been revealed to His holy apostles and prophets <sup>②</sup>in the Spirit;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the sons of man</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>by</i>  
**3:6** <i>to be specific,</i> that the Gentiles are fellow heirs and fellow members of the body, and fellow partakers of the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel,  
**3:7** of which I was made a minister, according to the gift of God’s grace which was given to me according to the working of His power.  
**3:8** To me, the very least of all <sup>①</sup>saints, this grace was given, to preach to the Gentiles the unfathomable riches of Christ,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**3:9** and to <sup>①</sup>enlighten all people as to what the plan of the mystery is which for ages has been hidden in God, who created all things;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms <i>bring to light what</i>  
**3:10** so that the multifaceted wisdom of God might now be made known through the church to the rulers and the authorities in the heavenly <i>places.</i>  
**3:11** <i>This was</i> in accordance with the <sup>①</sup>eternal purpose which He <sup>②</sup>carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>purpose of the ages</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>formed</i>  
**3:12** in whom we have boldness and <sup>①</sup>confident access through faith <sup>②</sup>in Him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>access in confidence</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>of Him</i>  
**3:13** Therefore I ask <sup>①</sup>you not to become discouraged about my tribulations in your behalf, <sup>②</sup>since they are your glory.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>that I may not lose</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>which are</i>  
**3:14** For this reason I bend my knees before the Father,  
**3:15** from whom <sup>①</sup>every family in heaven and on earth derives its name,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the whole</i>  
**3:16** that He would grant you, according to the riches of His glory, to be strengthened with power through His Spirit in the inner <sup>①</sup>self,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>person</i>  
**3:17** so that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith; <i>and</i> that you, being rooted and grounded in love,  
**3:18** may be able to comprehend with all the <sup>①</sup>saints what is the width and length and height and depth,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>See note v 8  
**3:19** and to know the love of Christ which surpasses knowledge, that you may be filled to all the fullness of God.  
**3:20** Now to Him who is able to do far more abundantly beyond all that we ask or think, according to the power that works within us,  
**3:21** to Him <i>be</i> the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus to all generations <sup>①</sup>forever and ever. Amen.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of the age of the ages</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** Therefore I, the prisoner of the Lord, urge you to walk in a manner worthy of the calling with which you have been called,  
**4:2** with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love,  
**4:3** being diligent to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.  
**4:4** <i>There is</i> one body and one Spirit, just as you also were called in one hope of your calling;  
**4:5** one Lord, one faith, one baptism,  
**4:6** one God and Father of all who is over all and through all and in all.  
**4:7** But to each one of us grace was given according to the measure of Christ’s gift.  
**4:8** Therefore <sup>①</sup>it says,<br>“WHEN HE ASCENDED ON HIGH,<br>HE LED CAPTIVE <i>the</i> captives,<br>AND HE GAVE GIFTS TO PEOPLE.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>He</i>  
**4:9** (Now this <i>expression,</i> “He ascended,” what <sup>①</sup>does it mean except that He also <sup>②</sup>had descended into the lower parts of the earth?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is it except</i> <sup>②</sup>One early ms <i>had first descended</i>  
**4:10** He who descended is Himself also He who ascended far above all the heavens, so that He might fill all things.)  
**4:11** And He gave some <i>as</i> apostles, some <i>as</i> prophets, some <i>as</i> evangelists, some <i>as</i> <sup>①</sup>pastors and teachers,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>From Gr for <i>shepherds</i>  
**4:12** for the equipping of the <sup>①</sup>saints for the work of ministry, for the building up of the body of Christ;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**4:13** until we all attain to the unity of the faith, and of the <sup>①</sup>knowledge of the Son of God, to a mature man, to the measure of the stature <sup>②</sup>which belongs to the fullness of Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>true knowledge</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>of the fullness</i>  
**4:14**  <sup>①</sup>As a result, we are no longer to be children, tossed here and there by waves and carried about by every wind of doctrine, by the trickery of people, by craftiness <sup>②</sup>in deceitful scheming;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>So that we will no longer be</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>with regard to the scheming of deceit</i>  
**4:15** but <sup>①</sup>speaking the truth in love, <sup>②</sup>we are to grow up in all <i>aspects</i> into Him who is the head, <i>that is,</i> Christ,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>holding to;</i> or <i>being truthful in</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>let’s grow up</i>  
**4:16** from whom the whole body, being fitted and held together <sup>①</sup>by what every joint supplies, according to the <sup>②</sup>proper working of each individual part, causes the growth of the body for the building up of itself in love.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>through every joint of the supply</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>working in measure</i>  
**4:17** So I say this, and affirm in the Lord, that you are to no longer walk just as the <sup>①</sup>Gentiles also walk, in the futility of their minds,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., unbelieving Gentiles  
**4:18** being darkened in their understanding, <sup>①</sup>excluded from the life of God because of the ignorance that is in them, because of the hardness of their heart;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>alienated</i>  
**4:19** and they, having become callous, have given themselves up to indecent behavior <sup>①</sup>for the practice of every kind of impurity with greediness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>greedy for the practice of every kind of impurity</i>  
**4:20** But you did not learn Christ in this way,  
**4:21** if indeed you have heard Him and have been taught in Him, just as truth is in Jesus,  
**4:22** that, in reference to your former way of life, you are to rid yourselves of the old <sup>①</sup>self, which is being corrupted in accordance with the lusts of deceit,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>person</i>  
**4:23** and that you are to be renewed in the spirit of your minds,  
**4:24** and to put on the new <sup>①</sup>self, which <sup>②</sup>in <i>the likeness of</i> God has been created in righteousness and holiness of the truth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>person</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>according to God</i>  
**4:25** Therefore, ridding yourselves of falsehood, SPEAK TRUTH EACH ONE <i>of you</i> with his neighbor, because we are parts of one another.  
**4:26** BE ANGRY, AND <i>yet</i> do not sin; do not let the sun go down on your anger,  
**4:27** and do not give the devil <sup>①</sup>an opportunity.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>a place</i>  
**4:28** The one who steals must no longer steal; but rather he must labor, producing with his own hands what is good, so that he will have <i>something</i> to share with the one who has need.  
**4:29** Let no <sup>①</sup>unwholesome word come out of your mouth, but if <i>there is</i> any good <i>word</i> for edification <sup>②</sup>according to the need <i>of the moment, say that,</i> so that it will give grace to those who hear.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>rotten</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>of the need</i>  
**4:30** Do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, <sup>①</sup>by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in</i>  
**4:31** All bitterness, wrath, anger, <sup>①</sup>clamor, and slander must be removed from you, along with all malice.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>shouting</i>  
**4:32** Be kind to one another, compassionate, forgiving each other, just as God in Christ also has forgiven <sup>①</sup>you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Two early mss <i>us</i>  

## 5장

**5:1** Therefore be imitators of God, as beloved children;  
**5:2** and walk in love, just as Christ also loved <sup>①</sup>you and gave Himself up for us, an offering and a sacrifice to God <sup>②</sup>as a fragrant aroma.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms <i>us</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>for an odor of fragrance</i>  
**5:3** But sexual immorality <sup>①</sup>or any impurity or greed must not even be mentioned among you, as is proper among <sup>②</sup>saints;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and all</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**5:4** and <i>there must be no</i> filthiness <sup>①</sup>or foolish talk, or vulgar joking, which are not fitting, but rather giving of thanks.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and</i>  
**5:5** For this you know with certainty, that no sexually immoral or <sup>①</sup>impure or greedy person, which <sup>②</sup>amounts to an idolater, has an inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., morally impure <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>is</i>  
**5:6** <i>See that</i> no one deceives you with empty words, for because of these things the wrath of God comes upon the <sup>①</sup>sons of disobedience.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., people opposed to God  
**5:7** Therefore do not become partners with them;  
**5:8** for you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord; walk as children of light  
**5:9** (for the fruit of the light <i>consists</i> in all goodness, righteousness, and truth),  
**5:10**  <sup>①</sup>as you try to learn what is pleasing to the Lord.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>putting to the test what</i>  
**5:11** Do not participate in the useless deeds of darkness, but instead even expose them;  
**5:12** for it is disgraceful even to speak of the things which are done by them in secret.  
**5:13** But all things become visible when they are exposed by the light, for everything that becomes visible is light.  
**5:14** For this reason <sup>①</sup>it says,<br>“Awake, sleeper,<br>And arise from the dead,<br>And Christ will shine on you.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>He</i>  
**5:15** So then, <sup>①</sup>be careful how you walk, not as unwise people but as wise,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>look carefully</i>  
**5:16**  <sup>①</sup>making the most of your time, because the days are evil.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>redeeming the time</i>  
**5:17** Therefore do not be foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord <i>is.</i>  
**5:18** And do not get drunk with wine, <sup>①</sup>in which there is debauchery, but be filled with the Spirit,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>by which</i>  
**5:19** speaking to <sup>①</sup>one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody with your hearts to the Lord;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>yourselves</i>  
**5:20** always giving thanks for all things in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to <i>our</i> God and Father;  
**5:21**  <sup>①</sup>and subject yourselves to one another in the <sup>②</sup>fear of Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>being submissive to</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>reverence for</i>  
**5:22** Wives, <i>subject yourselves</i> to your own husbands, as to the Lord.  
**5:23** For the husband is the head of the wife, as Christ also is the head of the church, He Himself <i>being</i> the Savior of the body.  
**5:24** But as the church is subject to Christ, so also the wives <i>ought to be</i> to their husbands in everything.  
**5:25** Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the church and gave Himself up for her,  
**5:26** so that He might sanctify her, having cleansed her by the washing of water with the word,  
**5:27** that He might present to Himself the church <sup>①</sup>in all her glory, having no spot or wrinkle or any such thing; but that she would be holy and blameless.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>glorious</i>  
**5:28** So husbands also ought to love their own wives as their own bodies. He who loves his own wife loves himself;  
**5:29** for no one ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, just as Christ also <i>does</i> the church,  
**5:30** because we are parts of His body.  
**5:31** FOR THIS REASON A MAN SHALL LEAVE HIS FATHER AND HIS MOTHER AND BE JOINED TO HIS WIFE, AND THE TWO SHALL BECOME ONE FLESH.  
**5:32** This mystery is great; but I am speaking with reference to Christ and the church.  
**5:33** Nevertheless, as for you individually, each <i>husband</i> is to love his own wife the same as himself, and the wife <i>must see to it</i> that she respects her husband.  

## 6장

**6:1** Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.  
**6:2** HONOR YOUR FATHER AND MOTHER (which is the first commandment with a promise),  
**6:3** SO THAT IT MAY TURN OUT WELL FOR YOU, AND THAT YOU MAY LIVE LONG ON THE EARTH.  
**6:4** Fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord.

**6:6** not <sup>①</sup>by way of <sup>②</sup>eye-service, as people-pleasers, but as slaves of Christ, doing the will of God from the <sup>③</sup>heart.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>according to</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., only when the master is watching <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>soul</i>
