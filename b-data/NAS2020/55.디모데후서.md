# 55.디모데후서 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus <sup>①</sup>by the will of God, according to the promise of life in Christ Jesus,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>through</i>  
**1:2** To Timothy, my beloved <sup>①</sup>son: Grace, mercy, <i>and</i> peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>child</i>  
**1:3** I thank God, whom I serve with a clear conscience <sup>①</sup>the way my forefathers did, as I constantly remember you in my <sup>②</sup>prayers night and day,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>from my forefathers</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>petitions</i>  
**1:4** longing to see you, even as I recall your tears, so that I may be filled with joy.  
**1:5**  <sup>①</sup>For I am mindful of the sincere faith within you, which first dwelled in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am sure that <i>it is</i> in you as well.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Receiving remembrance of</i>  
**1:6** For this reason I remind you to kindle afresh the gift of God which is in you through the laying on of my hands.  
**1:7** For God has not given us a spirit of <sup>①</sup>timidity, but of power and love and <sup>②</sup>discipline.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>cowardice</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>sound judgment</i>  
**1:8** Therefore do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord or of me His prisoner, but join with <i>me</i> in suffering for the gospel according to the power of God,  
**1:9** who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works, but according to His own purpose and grace, which was granted to us in Christ Jesus from all eternity,  
**1:10** but has now been revealed by the appearing of our Savior Christ Jesus, who abolished death and brought life and immortality to light through the gospel,  
**1:11** for which I was appointed a preacher, an apostle, and a teacher.  
**1:12** For this reason I also suffer these things; but I am not ashamed, for I know whom I have believed, and I am convinced that He is able to protect what I have entrusted to Him <sup>①</sup>until that day.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>for</i>  
**1:13** Hold on to the example of sound words which you have heard from me, in <i>the</i> faith and love which are in Christ Jesus.  
**1:14** Protect, through the Holy Spirit who dwells in us, the <sup>①</sup>treasure which has been entrusted to <i>you.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>good deposit</i>  
**1:15** You are aware of the fact that all who are in <sup>①</sup>Asia turned away from me, among whom are Phygelus and Hermogenes.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., the province of Asia  
**1:16** The Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, for he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my <sup>①</sup>chains;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>chain</i>  
**1:17** but when he was in Rome, he eagerly searched for me and found me—  
**1:18** the Lord grant to him to find mercy from the Lord on that day—and you know very well what services he rendered at Ephesus.  

## 2장

**2:1** You therefore, my <sup>①</sup>son, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>child</i>  
**2:2** The things which you have heard from me in the presence of many witnesses, entrust these to faithful <sup>①</sup>people who will be able to teach others also.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>men</i>  
**2:3** Suffer hardship with <i>me,</i> as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.  
**2:4** No soldier in active service entangles himself in the affairs of everyday life, so that he may please the one who enlisted <i>him.</i>  
**2:5** And if someone likewise competes as an athlete, he is not <sup>①</sup>crowned <i>as victor</i> unless he competes according to the rules.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., with a wreath  
**2:6** The hard-working farmer ought to be the first to receive his share of the crops.  
**2:7** Consider what I say, for the Lord will give you understanding in everything.  
**2:8** Remember Jesus Christ, risen from the dead, descendant of David, according to my gospel,  
**2:9**  <sup>①</sup>for which I suffer hardship even to imprisonment as a criminal; but the word of God is not imprisoned.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in which</i>  
**2:10** For this reason I endure all things for the sake of <sup>①</sup>those who are chosen, so that they also may obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus <i>and</i> with <i>it</i> eternal glory.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the chosen</i>  
**2:11** The statement is trustworthy:<br>For if we died with Him, we will also live with Him;  
**2:12** If we endure, we will also reign with Him;<br>If we <sup>①</sup>deny Him, He will also deny us;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>will deny</i>  
**2:13** If we are faithless, He remains faithful, for He cannot deny Himself.  
**2:14** Remind <i>them</i> of these things, and solemnly exhort <i>them</i> in the presence of God not to dispute about words, which is useless <i>and leads</i> to the ruin of the listeners.  
**2:15** Be diligent to present yourself approved to God as a worker who <sup>①</sup>does not need to be ashamed, accurately handling the word of truth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>has no reason to</i>  
**2:16** But avoid worldly <i>and</i> empty chatter, for <sup>①</sup>it will lead to further ungodliness,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>they will make further progress in ungodliness</i>  
**2:17** and their <sup>①</sup>talk will spread like <sup>②</sup>gangrene. Among them are Hymenaeus and Philetus,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>word</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>cancer</i>  
**2:18** <i>men</i> who have gone astray from the truth, claiming that the resurrection has already taken place; and they are jeopardizing the faith of some.  
**2:19** Nevertheless, the firm foundation of God stands, having this seal: “The Lord knows those who are His;” and, “Everyone who names the name of the Lord is to keep away from wickedness.”  
**2:20** Now in a large house there are not only gold and silver implements, but also <i>implements</i> of wood and of earthenware, and some <i>are</i> for honor while others <i>are</i> for dishonor.  
**2:21** Therefore, if anyone cleanses himself from these <i>things,</i> he will be an implement for honor, sanctified, useful to the Master, prepared for every good work.  
**2:22** Now flee from youthful lusts and pursue righteousness, faith, love, <i>and</i> peace with those who call on the Lord from a pure heart.  
**2:23** But refuse foolish and ignorant speculations, knowing that they produce <sup>①</sup>quarrels.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>fightings</i>  
**2:24** The Lord’s bond-servant must not be quarrelsome, but be kind to all, skillful in teaching, patient when wronged,  
**2:25** with gentleness correcting those who are in opposition, if perhaps God may grant them repentance leading to the knowledge of the truth,  
**2:26** and they may come to their senses <i>and escape</i> from the snare of the devil, having been held captive <sup>①</sup>by him to do his will.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>by him, to do His will</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** But realize this, that in the last days difficult times will come.  
**3:2** For people will be lovers of self, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, slanderers, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, unholy,  
**3:3** unloving, irreconcilable, malicious gossips, without self-control, brutal, <sup>①</sup>haters of good,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>not loving good</i>  
**3:4** treacherous, reckless, conceited, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,  
**3:5** holding to a form of <sup>①</sup>godliness although they have denied its power; avoid <i>such people as</i> these.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>religion</i>  
**3:6** For among them are those who <sup>①</sup>slip into households and captivate <sup>②</sup>weak women weighed down with sins, led on by various impulses,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>creep into</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>idle</i>  
**3:7** always learning and never able to come to the <sup>①</sup>knowledge of the truth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>recognition</i>  
**3:8** Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these men also oppose the truth, men of depraved mind, <sup>①</sup>worthless in regard to the faith.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>unfit</i>  
**3:9** But they will not make further progress; for their foolishness will be obvious to all, just as was that also of <sup>①</sup>Jannes and Jambres.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>those</i>  
**3:10** Now you followed my teaching, conduct, purpose, faith, patience, love, <sup>①</sup>perseverance,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>steadfastness</i>  
**3:11** persecutions, <i>and</i> sufferings, such as happened to me at Antioch, at Iconium, <i>and</i> at Lystra; what persecutions I endured, and out of them all the Lord rescued me!  
**3:12** Indeed, all who want to live in a godly way in Christ Jesus will be persecuted.  
**3:13** But evil people and impostors will proceed <i>from bad</i> to worse, deceiving and being deceived.  
**3:14** You, however, continue in the things you have learned and become convinced of, knowing from whom you have learned <i>them,</i>  
**3:15** and that from childhood you have known the sacred writings which are able to give you the wisdom that leads to salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus.  
**3:16** All Scripture is <sup>①</sup>inspired by God and beneficial for teaching, for <sup>②</sup>rebuke, for correction, for training in righteousness;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>God-breathed</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>reprimand</i>  
**3:17** so that the man <i>or woman</i> of God may be <sup>①</sup>fully capable, equipped for every good work.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>proficient</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** I solemnly exhort <i>you</i> in the presence of God and of Christ Jesus, who is to judge the living and the dead, and by His appearing and His kingdom:  
**4:2** preach the word; be ready <sup>①</sup>in season <i>and</i> out of season; correct, rebuke, <i>and</i> <sup>②</sup>exhort, with <sup>③</sup>great patience and instruction.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., whether it is convenient or not <sup>②</sup>Or <i>encourage</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>all</i>  
**4:3** For <i>the</i> time will come when they will not tolerate sound doctrine; but <i>wanting</i> to have their ears tickled, they will accumulate for themselves teachers in accordance with their own desires,  
**4:4** and they will turn their ears away from the truth and will turn aside to myths.  
**4:5** But <i>as for</i> you, use self-restraint in all things, endure hardship, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill your ministry.  
**4:6** For I am already being poured out as a drink offering, and the time of my departure has come.  
**4:7** I have fought the good fight, I have finished the course, I have kept the faith;  
**4:8** in the future there is reserved for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will award to me on that day; and not only to me, but also to all who have loved His appearing.  
**4:9** Make every effort to come to me soon;  
**4:10** for Demas, having loved this present <sup>①</sup>world, has deserted me and gone to Thessalonica; Crescens <i>has gone</i> to <sup>②</sup>Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>age</i> <sup>②</sup>One early ms <i>Gaul</i>  
**4:11** Only Luke is with me. Take along Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for service.  
**4:12** But I have sent Tychicus to Ephesus.  
**4:13** When you come, bring the overcoat which I left at Troas with Carpus, and the books, especially the parchments.  
**4:14** Alexander the coppersmith did me great harm; the Lord will repay him according to his deeds.  
**4:15** Be on guard against him yourself too, for he vigorously opposed our <sup>①</sup>teaching.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>words</i>  
**4:16** At my first defense no one supported me, but all deserted me; may it not be counted against them.  
**4:17** But the Lord stood with me and strengthened me, so that through me the proclamation might <sup>①</sup>be fully accomplished, and that all the Gentiles might hear; and I was rescued out of the lion’s mouth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>be fulfilled</i>  
**4:18** The Lord will rescue me from every evil deed, and will <sup>①</sup>bring me safely to His heavenly kingdom; to <sup>②</sup>Him <i>be</i> the glory forever and ever. Amen.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>save me for</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>Whom</i>  
**4:19** Greet Prisca and Aquila, and the household of Onesiphorus.  
**4:20** Erastus remained at Corinth, but I left Trophimus sick at Miletus.  
**4:21** Make every effort to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, also Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers <i>and sisters.</i>  
**4:22** The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you.
