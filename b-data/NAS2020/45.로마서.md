## 1장

**1:1** Paul, a bond-servant of Christ Jesus, <sup>①</sup>called <i>as</i> an apostle, set apart for the gospel of God,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>a called apostle</i>

**1:4** who was declared the Son of God with power according to the <sup>①</sup>Spirit of holiness <sup>②</sup>by the resurrection from the dead, Jesus Christ our Lord,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>spirit</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>as a result of</i>  
**1:5** through whom we have received grace and apostleship <sup>①</sup>to bring about <i>the</i> obedience of faith among all the Gentiles in behalf of His name,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>for obedience</i>  
**1:6** among whom you also are <i>the</i> called of Jesus Christ;  
**1:7** to all who are beloved of God in Rome, called <i>as</i> <sup>①</sup>saints: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**1:8** First, I thank my God through Jesus Christ <sup>①</sup>for you all, because your faith is being proclaimed throughout the world.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>concerning you all, that...</i>  
**1:9** For God, whom I serve in my spirit in the <i>preaching of the</i> gospel of His Son, is my witness <i>as to</i> how unceasingly I make mention of you,  
**1:10** always in my prayers requesting if perhaps now, at last by the will of God, I will succeed in coming to you.  
**1:11** For I long to see you so that I may impart some spiritual gift to you, that you may be <sup>①</sup>established;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>strengthened</i>  
**1:12** that is, that I may be encouraged together with you <i>while</i> among you, each of us by the other’s faith, both yours and mine.  
**1:13** I do not want you to be unaware, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> that often I have planned to come to you (and have been prevented so far) so that I may obtain some fruit among you also just as among the rest of the Gentiles.  
**1:14** I am <sup>①</sup>under obligation both to Greeks and to the <sup>②</sup>uncultured, both to the wise and to the foolish.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., non-Hellenes <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>debtor</i>  
**1:15** So, for my part, I am eager to preach the gospel to you also who are in Rome.  
**1:16** For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes, to the Jew first and also to the Greek.  
**1:17** For in it <i>the</i> righteousness of God is revealed <sup>①</sup>from faith to faith; as it is written: “ <sup>②</sup>BUT THE RIGHTEOUS <i>one</i> will live by faith.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>by</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>But the one who is righteous by faith shall live</i>  
**1:18** For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of people who suppress the truth <sup>①</sup>in unrighteousness,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>by</i>  
**1:19** because that which is known about God is evident <sup>①</sup>within them; for God made it evident to them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>among</i>  
**1:20** For since the creation of the world His invisible <i>attributes, that is,</i> His eternal power and divine nature, have been clearly perceived, being understood by what has been made, so that they are without excuse.  
**1:21** For even though they knew God, they did not <sup>①</sup>honor Him as God or give thanks, but they became futile in their reasonings, and their senseless hearts were darkened.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>glorify</i>  
**1:22** Claiming to be wise, they became fools,  
**1:23** and they exchanged the glory of the incorruptible God for an image in the form of corruptible mankind, of birds, four-footed animals, and <sup>①</sup>crawling creatures.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>reptiles</i>  
**1:24** Therefore God gave them up to vile impurity in the lusts of their hearts, so that their bodies would be dishonored among them.  
**1:25** For they exchanged the truth of God for <sup>①</sup>falsehood, and worshiped and served the creature rather than the Creator, who is blessed <sup>②</sup>forever. Amen.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the lie</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>unto the ages</i>  
**1:26** For this reason God gave them over to degrading passions; for their women exchanged natural relations for that which is contrary to nature,  
**1:27** and likewise the men, too, abandoned natural relations <sup>①</sup>with women and burned in their desire toward one another, males with males committing <sup>②</sup>shameful acts and receiving in <sup>③</sup>their own persons the due penalty of their error.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of the female</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>shamelessness</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>themselves</i>  
**1:28** And just as they did not see fit <sup>①</sup>to acknowledge God, God gave them up to a depraved mind, to do those things that are not proper,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>to have God in knowledge</i>  
**1:29** <i>people</i> having been filled with all unrighteousness, wickedness, greed, <i>and</i> evil; full of envy, murder, strife, deceit, <i>and</i> malice; <i>they are</i> gossips,  
**1:30** slanderers, <sup>①</sup>haters of God, insolent, arrogant, boastful, inventors of evil, disobedient to parents,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>hateful to God</i>  
**1:31** without understanding, untrustworthy, unfeeling, <i>and</i> unmerciful;  
**1:32** and although they know the ordinance of God, that those who practice such things are worthy of death, they not only do the same, but also approve of those who practice them.  

## 2장

**2:2** And we know that the judgment of God <sup>①</sup>rightly falls upon those who practice such things.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is according to truth against</i>  
**2:3** But do you suppose this, <sup>①</sup>you <i>foolish</i> person who passes judgment on those who practice such things, and <i>yet</i> does them <i>as well,</i> that you will escape the judgment of God?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>O</i>

**2:7** to those who by perseverance in doing good seek glory, honor, and immortality, <i>He will give</i> eternal life;  
**2:8** but to those who are self-serving and do not obey the truth, but obey unrighteousness, <i>He will give</i> wrath and indignation.  
**2:9** <i>There will be</i> tribulation and distress <sup>①</sup>for every soul of mankind who does evil, <sup>②</sup>for the Jew first and also <sup>③</sup>for the Greek,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>upon</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>of the</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>of the</i>  
**2:10** but glory, honor, and peace to everyone who does what is good, to the Jew first and also to the Greek.  
**2:11** For there is no partiality with God.  
**2:12** For all who have sinned <sup>①</sup>without the Law will also perish <sup>②</sup>without the Law, and all who have sinned <sup>③</sup>under the Law will be judged <sup>④</sup>by the Law;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>without law</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>without law</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>under law</i> <sup>④</sup>Or <i>by law</i>  
**2:13** for <i>it is</i> not the hearers <sup>①</sup>of the Law <i>who</i> are <sup>②</sup>righteous before God, but the doers <sup>③</sup>of the Law <i>who</i> will be justified.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>of law</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>just</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>of law</i>  
**2:14** For when Gentiles who do not have <sup>①</sup>the Law <sup>②</sup>instinctively perform the <i>requirements</i> of the Law, these, though not having <sup>③</sup>the Law, are a law to themselves,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>law</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>by nature</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>law</i>  
**2:15** in that they show the work of the Law written in their hearts, their conscience testifying and their thoughts alternately accusing or else defending them,  
**2:16** on the day when, according to my gospel, God will judge the secrets of mankind through Christ Jesus.  
**2:17** But if you call yourself a <sup>①</sup>Jew and rely <sup>②</sup>upon the Law and boast in God,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Judean</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>upon law</i>  
**2:18** and know <i>His</i> will and <sup>①</sup>distinguish the things that matter, being instructed from the Law,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>approve</i>  
**2:19** and are confident that you yourself are a guide to people who are blind, a light to those in darkness,  
**2:20** a <sup>①</sup>corrector of the foolish, a teacher of <sup>②</sup>the immature, possessing in the Law the embodiment of knowledge and of the truth—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>instructor</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>infants</i>  
**2:21** <i>you,</i> therefore, who teach someone else, do you not teach yourself? <i>You</i> who <sup>①</sup>preach that one is not to steal, do you steal?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>proclaim</i>  
**2:22** <i>You</i> who say that one is not to commit adultery, do you commit adultery? <i>You</i> who loathe idols, do you rob temples?  
**2:23** You who boast <sup>①</sup>in the Law, through your breaking the Law, do you dishonor God?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in law</i>  
**2:24** For “THE NAME OF GOD IS BLASPHEMED AMONG THE GENTILES BECAUSE OF YOU,” just as it is written.  
**2:25** For indeed circumcision is of value if you practice <sup>①</sup>the Law; but if you are a violator <sup>②</sup>of the Law, your circumcision has turned into uncircumcision.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>law</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>of law</i>  
**2:26** So if the <sup>①</sup>uncircumcised man keeps the requirements of the Law, will his uncircumcision not be regarded as circumcision?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>uncircumcision</i>  
**2:27** And he who is physically uncircumcised, if he keeps the Law, will he not judge you who <sup>①</sup>though having the letter <i>of the Law</i> and circumcision are a violator <sup>②</sup>of the Law?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>through the letter</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>of law</i>  
**2:28** For he is not a Jew who is one outwardly, nor is circumcision that which is outward in the flesh.  
**2:29** But he is a Jew who is one inwardly; and circumcision is of the heart, by the Spirit, not by the letter; and his praise is not from people, but from God.  

## 3장

**3:1** Then what <sup>①</sup>advantage does the Jew have? Or what is the benefit of circumcision?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is the advantage of the Jew</i>  
**3:2** Great in every respect. First, that they were entrusted with the actual words of God.  
**3:3** What then? If some <sup>①</sup>did not believe, their <sup>②</sup>unbelief will not nullify the faithfulness of God, will it?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>were unfaithful</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>unfaithfulness</i>  
**3:4**  <sup>①</sup>Far from it! Rather, God must prove to be true, though every person <i>be found</i> a liar, as it is written:<br>“SO THAT YOU ARE JUSTIFIED IN YOUR WORDS,<br>AND PREVAIL <sup>②</sup>WHEN YOU ARE JUDGED.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>May it never happen!</i> And so throughout the ch <sup>②</sup>Or <i>in Your judging</i>  
**3:5** But if our unrighteousness <sup>①</sup>demonstrates the righteousness of God, what shall we say? The God who inflicts wrath is not unrighteous, is He? (I am speaking from a human viewpoint.)<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., by requiring His judgment  
**3:6** Far from it! For <i>otherwise,</i> how will God judge the world?  
**3:7** But if through my lie the truth of God abounded to His glory, why am I also still being judged as a sinner?  
**3:8** And <i>why</i> not <i>say</i> (just as we are slanderously reported and as some claim that we say), “Let’s do evil that good may come <i>of it</i>”? <sup>①</sup>Their condemnation is deserved.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Whose</i>  
**3:9** What then? <sup>①</sup>Are we better <i>than they?</i> Not at all; for we have already charged that both Jews and Greeks are all under sin;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Are we worse</i>  
**3:10** as it is written:<br>“THERE IS NO RIGHTEOUS PERSON, NOT EVEN ONE;  
**3:11** THERE IS NO ONE WHO UNDERSTANDS,<br>THERE IS NO ONE WHO SEEKS OUT GOD;  
**3:12** THEY HAVE ALL TURNED ASIDE, TOGETHER THEY HAVE BECOME <sup>①</sup>CORRUPT;<br>THERE IS NO ONE WHO DOES GOOD,<br>THERE IS NOT EVEN ONE.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>depraved</i>  
**3:13** “THEIR THROAT IS AN OPEN GRAVE,<br>WITH THEIR TONGUES THEY KEEP DECEIVING,”<br>“THE VENOM OF <sup>①</sup>ASPS IS UNDER THEIR LIPS”;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., venomous snakes  
**3:14** “THEIR MOUTH IS FULL OF CURSING AND BITTERNESS”;  
**3:15** “THEIR FEET ARE SWIFT TO SHED BLOOD,  
**3:16** DESTRUCTION AND MISERY ARE IN THEIR PATHS,  
**3:17** AND THEY HAVE NOT KNOWN THE WAY OF PEACE.”  
**3:18** “THERE IS NO FEAR OF GOD BEFORE THEIR EYES.”  
**3:19** Now we know that whatever the Law says, it speaks to those who are <sup>①</sup>under the Law, so that every mouth may be closed and all the world may become accountable to God;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in</i>  
**3:20** because by the works <sup>①</sup>of the Law <sup>②</sup>none of mankind will be justified in His sight; for <sup>③</sup>through the Law <i>comes</i> <sup>④</sup>knowledge of sin.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>of law</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>no flesh</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>through law</i> <sup>④</sup>Or <i>recognition</i>  
**3:21** But now apart <sup>①</sup>from the Law <i>the</i> righteousness of God has been revealed, being witnessed by the <sup>②</sup>Law and the Prophets,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>from law</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., the Old Testament  
**3:22** but <i>it is the</i> righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ for all those <sup>①</sup>who believe; for there is no distinction,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>who believe. For there is</i>  
**3:23** for all <sup>①</sup>have sinned and fall short of the glory of God,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sinned</i>  
**3:24** being justified as a gift by His grace through the redemption which is in Christ Jesus,  
**3:25** whom God displayed publicly as a <sup>①</sup>propitiation <sup>②</sup>in His blood through faith. <i>This was</i> to demonstrate His righteousness, <sup>③</sup>because in God’s <i>merciful</i> restraint He let the sins previously committed go unpunished;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., a means of reconciliation between God and mankind by paying the penalty for sin <sup>②</sup>Or <i>by</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>because of the passing over of the sins previously committed, in the restraint of God</i>  
**3:26** for the demonstration, <i>that is,</i> of His righteousness at the present time, so that He would be just and the justifier of the one who <sup>①</sup>has faith in Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is of the faith of Jesus</i>  
**3:27** Where then is boasting? It has been excluded. By what kind of law? Of works? No, but by a law of faith.  
**3:28**  <sup>①</sup>For we maintain that a person is justified by faith apart from works <sup>②</sup>of the Law.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms <i>Therefore</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>of law</i>  
**3:29** Or is God <i>the God</i> of Jews only? Is He not <i>the God</i> of Gentiles also? Yes, of Gentiles also,  
**3:30** since indeed God who will justify the <sup>①</sup>circumcised <sup>②</sup>by faith and the <sup>③</sup>uncircumcised through faith is one.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>circumcision</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>out of</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>uncircumcision</i>  
**3:31** Do we then nullify <sup>①</sup>the Law through faith? Far from it! On the contrary, we establish the Law.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>law</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** What then shall we say that Abraham, <sup>①</sup>our forefather according to the flesh, has found?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>our forefather, has found according to the flesh</i>  
**4:2** For if Abraham was justified <sup>①</sup>by works, he has something to boast about; but not <sup>②</sup>before God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>out of</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>toward</i>  
**4:3** For what does the Scripture say? “ABRAHAM BELIEVED GOD, AND IT WAS CREDITED TO HIM AS RIGHTEOUSNESS.”  
**4:4** Now to the one who works, the wages are not credited as a favor, but as what is due.  
**4:5** But to the one who does not work, but believes in Him who justifies the ungodly, his faith is credited as righteousness,  
**4:6** just as David also speaks of the blessing of the person to whom God credits righteousness apart from works:  
**4:7** “BLESSED ARE THOSE WHOSE LAWLESS DEEDS HAVE BEEN FORGIVEN,<br>AND WHOSE SINS HAVE BEEN COVERED.  
**4:8** BLESSED IS THE MAN WHOSE SIN THE LORD WILL NOT TAKE INTO ACCOUNT.”  
**4:9** Is this blessing then on <sup>①</sup>the circumcised, or on <sup>②</sup>the uncircumcised also? For we say, “FAITH WAS CREDITED TO ABRAHAM AS RIGHTEOUSNESS.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>circumcision</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>uncircumcision</i>  
**4:10** How then was it credited? While he was <sup>①</sup>circumcised, or <sup>②</sup>uncircumcised? Not while <sup>③</sup>circumcised, but while <sup>④</sup>uncircumcised;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in circumcision</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>in uncircumcision</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>in circumcision</i> <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>in uncircumcision</i>  
**4:11** and he received the sign of circumcision, a seal of the righteousness of the faith which <sup>①</sup>he had while uncircumcised, so that he might be the father of all who believe without being circumcised, that righteousness might be credited to them,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>was in uncircumcision</i>  
**4:12** and the father of circumcision to those who not only are of the circumcision, but who also follow in the steps of the faith of our father Abraham which <sup>①</sup>he had while uncircumcised.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>was in uncircumcision</i>  
**4:13** For the promise to Abraham or to his <sup>①</sup>descendants that he would be heir of the world was not <sup>②</sup>through the Law, but through the righteousness of faith.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>seed</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>through law</i>  
**4:14** For if those who are <sup>①</sup>of the Law are heirs, then faith is made void and the promise is nullified;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>of law</i>  
**4:15** for the Law brings about wrath, but where there is no law, there also is no violation.  
**4:16** For this reason <i>it is</i> <sup>①</sup>by faith, in order that <i>it may be</i> in accordance with grace, so that the promise will be guaranteed to all the <sup>②</sup>descendants, not only to <sup>③</sup>those who are of the Law, but also to <sup>④</sup>those who are of the faith of Abraham, who is the father of us all,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>that which is</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>that which is</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>out of</i> <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>seed</i>  
**4:17** (as it is written: “I HAVE MADE YOU A FATHER OF MANY NATIONS”) in the presence of Him whom he believed, <i>that is,</i> God, who gives life to the dead and calls into being things that do not exist.  
**4:18** In hope against hope he believed, so that he might become a father of many nations according to that which had been spoken, “SO SHALL YOUR <sup>①</sup>DESCENDANTS BE.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>seed</i>  
**4:19** Without becoming weak in faith he contemplated his own body, now <i>as good as</i> dead since he was about a hundred years old, and the deadness of Sarah’s womb;  
**4:20** yet, with respect to the promise of God, he did not waver in unbelief but grew strong in faith, giving glory to God,  
**4:21** and being fully assured that what <i>God</i> had promised, He was able also to perform.  
**4:22** Therefore IT WAS ALSO CREDITED TO HIM AS RIGHTEOUSNESS.  
**4:23** Now not for his sake only was it written that it was credited to him,  
**4:24** but for our sake also, to whom it will be credited, to <i>us</i> who believe in Him who raised Jesus our Lord from the dead,  
**4:25** <i>He</i> who was delivered over because of our wrongdoings, and was raised because of our justification.  

## 5장

**5:1** Therefore, having been justified by faith, <sup>①</sup>we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Two early mss <i>let’s have</i>  
**5:2** through whom we also have obtained our introduction by faith into this grace in which we stand; and <sup>①</sup>we celebrate in hope of the glory of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>we boast;</i> or <i>let’s</i>  
**5:3** And not only <i>this,</i> but <sup>①</sup>we also celebrate in our tribulations, knowing that tribulation brings about perseverance;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>we also boast;</i> or <i>let’s also boast</i>  
**5:4** and perseverance, proven character; and proven character, hope;  
**5:5** and hope does not disappoint, because the love of God has been poured out within our hearts through the Holy Spirit who was given to us.  
**5:6** For while we were still helpless, at <i>the</i> right time Christ died for the ungodly.  
**5:7** For one will hardly die for a righteous person; <sup>①</sup>though perhaps for the good person someone would even dare to die.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>for</i>

## 15장

**15:1** Now we who are strong ought to bear the weaknesses of those without strength, and not <i>just</i> please ourselves.  
**15:2** Each of us is to please his neighbor <sup>①</sup>for his good, to <i>his</i> edification.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>for what is good to edification</i>  
**15:3** For even Christ did not please Himself, but as it is written: “THE TAUNTS OF THOSE WHO TAUNT YOU HAVE FALLEN ON ME.”  
**15:4** For whatever was written in earlier times was written for our instruction, so that through perseverance and the encouragement of the Scriptures we might have hope.  
**15:5** Now may the God <sup>①</sup>who gives perseverance and encouragement grant you to be of the same mind with one another, according to Christ Jesus,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of perseverance</i>  
**15:6** so that with one purpose <i>and</i> one <sup>①</sup>voice you may glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>mouth</i>  
**15:7** Therefore, <sup>①</sup>accept one another, just as Christ also accepted <sup>②</sup>us, for the glory of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., accept into fellowship <sup>②</sup>One early ms <i>you</i>  
**15:8** For I say that Christ has become a servant to the circumcision in behalf of the truth of God, to confirm the promises <i>given</i> to the fathers,  
**15:9** and for the Gentiles to glorify God for His mercy; as it is written:<br>“THEREFORE I WILL <sup>①</sup>GIVE PRAISE TO YOU AMONG THE GENTILES,<br>AND I WILL SING PRAISES TO YOUR NAME.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>confess</i>  
**15:10** Again he says,<br>“REJOICE, YOU GENTILES, WITH HIS PEOPLE.”  
**15:11** And again,<br>“PRAISE THE LORD ALL YOU GENTILES,<br>AND LET ALL THE PEOPLES PRAISE HIM.”  
**15:12** Again Isaiah says,<br>“THERE SHALL COME THE ROOT OF JESSE,<br>AND HE WHO ARISES TO RULE OVER THE GENTILES,<br>IN HIM WILL THE GENTILES HOPE.”  
**15:13** Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that you will abound in hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.  
**15:14** And concerning you, my brothers <i>and sisters,</i> I myself also am convinced that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge and able also to admonish one another.  
**15:15** But I have written very boldly to you on some points so as to remind you again, because of the grace that was given to me <sup>①</sup>from God,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms <i>by God</i>  
**15:16** to be a minister of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles, ministering as a priest the gospel of God, so that <i>my</i> offering of the Gentiles may become acceptable, sanctified by the Holy Spirit.  
**15:17** Therefore in Christ Jesus I have found reason for boasting in things pertaining to God.  
**15:18** For I will not presume to speak of anything <sup>①</sup>except what Christ has accomplished through me, <sup>②</sup>resulting in the obedience of the Gentiles by word and deed,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>which Christ has not accomplished</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>to the obedience</i>  
**15:19** in the power of <sup>①</sup>signs and wonders, in the power of the Spirit; so that from Jerusalem and all around as far as Illyricum I have <sup>②</sup>fully preached the gospel of Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>confirming miracles</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>fulfilled</i>  
**15:20** And in this way I aspired to preach the gospel, not where Christ was <i>already</i> known by name, so that I would not build on another person’s foundation;  
**15:21** but just as it is written:<br>“THEY WHO HAVE NOT BEEN TOLD ABOUT HIM WILL SEE,<br>AND THEY WHO HAVE NOT HEARD WILL UNDERSTAND.”  
**15:22** For this reason I have often been prevented from coming to you;  
**15:23** but now, with no further place for me in these regions, and since I have had for many years a longing to come to you  
**15:24** whenever I go to Spain—for I hope to see you in passing, and to be helped on my way there by you, when I have first enjoyed your company <sup>①</sup>for a while—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in part</i>  
**15:25** but now, I am going to Jerusalem, serving the <sup>①</sup>saints.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**15:26** For Macedonia and Achaia have been pleased to make a contribution for the poor among the <sup>①</sup>saints in Jerusalem.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>See note v 25  
**15:27** For they were pleased <i>to do so,</i> and they are indebted to them. For if the Gentiles have shared in their spiritual things, they are indebted to do them a service also in material things.  
**15:28** Therefore, when I have finished this, and have <sup>①</sup>put my seal on this fruit of theirs, I will go on by way of you to Spain.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>sealed to them this fruit</i>  
**15:29** I know that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of the blessing of Christ.  
**15:30** Now I urge you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> by our Lord Jesus Christ and by the love of the Spirit, to strive together with me in your prayers to God for me,  
**15:31** that I may be rescued from those who are disobedient in Judea, and <i>that</i> my service for Jerusalem may prove acceptable to the <sup>①</sup>saints;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>See note v 25  
**15:32** so that I may come to you in joy by the will of God and relax in your company.  
**15:33** Now the God of peace <i>be</i> with you all. Amen.
