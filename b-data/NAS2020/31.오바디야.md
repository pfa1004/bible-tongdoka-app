# 31.오바디야 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision of Obadiah. This is what the Lord <sup>①</sup>GOD says concerning Edom—<br>We have heard a report from the LORD,<br>And a messenger has been sent among the nations <i>saying,</i><br>“Arise, and let’s go up against her for battle”—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>YHWH,</i> usually rendered <i>LORD</i>  
**1:2** “Behold, I will make you small among the nations;<br>You are greatly despised.  
**1:3** The arrogance of your heart has deceived you,<br>The one who lives in the clefts of <sup>①</sup>the rock,<br><i>On the</i> height of his dwelling place,<br>Who says in his heart,<br>‘Who will bring me down to earth?’<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Sela</i>  
**1:4** Though you make <i>your home</i> high like the eagle,<br>Though you set your nest among the stars,<br>From there I will bring you down,” declares the LORD.  
**1:5** “If thieves came to you,<br>If <sup>①</sup>robbers by night—<br>Oh how you will be ruined!—<br>Would they not steal <i>only</i> <sup>②</sup>until they had enough?<br>If grape-pickers came to you,<br>Would they not leave <i>some</i> gleanings?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>devastators of</i> the <i>night</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>their sufficiency</i>  
**1:6** Oh how Esau will be searched,<br><i>And</i> his hidden treasures searched out!  
**1:7** All the people <sup>①</sup>allied with you<br>Will send you to the border,<br><i>And</i> the people at peace with you<br>Will deceive you <i>and</i> overpower you.<br><i>They who eat</i> your bread<br>Will set an ambush for you.<br>(There is no understanding <sup>②</sup>in him.)<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of your covenant</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., in Esau; or <i>of it</i>  
**1:8** Will I not on that day,” declares the LORD,<br>“Eliminate wise men from Edom,<br>And understanding from the mountain of Esau?  
**1:9** Then your warriors will be filled with terror, Teman,<br>So that everyone will be eliminated from the mountain of Esau by murder.  
**1:10** “Because of violence to your brother Jacob,<br>Shame will cover you,<br>And you will be eliminated forever.  
**1:11** On the day that you stood aloof,<br>On the day that strangers carried off his wealth,<br>And foreigners entered his gate<br>And cast lots for Jerusalem—<br>You too were as one of them.  
**1:12** Do not <sup>①</sup>gloat over your brother’s day,<br>The day of his misfortune.<br>And do not rejoice over the sons of Judah<br>On the day of their destruction;<br>Yes, do not <sup>②</sup>boast<br>On the day of <i>their</i> distress.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>look on</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>make your mouth large</i>  
**1:13** Do not enter the gate of My people<br>On the day of their disaster.<br>You indeed, do not <sup>①</sup>gloat over their catastrophe<br>On the day of their disaster.<br>And do not lay <i>a hand</i> on their wealth<br>On the day of their disaster.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>look at</i>  
**1:14** Do not stand at the crossroads<br>To eliminate their survivors;<br>And do not hand over their refugees<br>On the day of their distress.  
**1:15** “For the day of the LORD is near for all the nations.<br>Just as you have done, it will be done to you.<br>Your dealings will return on your own head.  
**1:16** For just as you drank on My holy mountain,<br>All the nations will drink continually.<br>They will drink <sup>①</sup>to the last drop,<br>And become as if they had never existed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and slurp</i>  
**1:17** But on Mount Zion there will be those who escape,<br>And it will be holy.<br>And the house of Jacob will <sup>①</sup>possess their <sup>②</sup>property.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>take possession of</i> <sup>②</sup>or <i>inheritance</i>  
**1:18** Then the house of Jacob will be a fire,<br>And the house of Joseph a flame;<br>But the house of Esau <i>will be</i> like stubble.<br>And they will set <sup>①</sup>them on fire and consume <sup>②</sup>them,<br>So that there will be no survivor of the house of Esau,”<br>For the LORD has spoken.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., the people of Esau <sup>②</sup>I.e., the people of Esau  
**1:19** Then <i>those of</i> the <sup>①</sup>Negev will <sup>②</sup>possess the mountain of Esau,<br>And <i>those of</i> the <sup>③</sup>Shephelah the Philistine <i>plain;</i><br>Also, they will <sup>④</sup>possess the territory of Ephraim and the territory of Samaria,<br>And Benjamin <i>the territory of</i> Gilead.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., South country <sup>②</sup>Or <i>take possession of</i> <sup>③</sup>I.e., the foothills <sup>④</sup>Or <i>take possession of</i>  
**1:20** And the exiles of this army of the sons of Israel,<br>Who are <i>among</i> the Canaanites as far as Zarephath,<br>And the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad,<br>Will <sup>①</sup>possess the cities of the Negev.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>take possession of</i>  
**1:21**  <sup>①</sup>The deliverers will ascend Mount Zion<br>To judge the mountain of Esau,<br>And the kingdom will be the LORD’S.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or with ancient versions <i>Those who are rescued</i>
