# 16.느헤미야 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** The words of Nehemiah the son of Hacaliah. <e>N</e>ow it happened in the month Chislev, <i>in</i> the twentieth year, while I was in Susa the <sup>①</sup>capitol,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>acropolis</i>  
**1:2** that Hanani, one of my brothers, <sup>①</sup>and some men from Judah came; and I asked them about the Jews who had escaped <sup>②</sup>and had survived the captivity, and about Jerusalem.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>he and men</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>who survived</i>  
**1:3** And they said to me, “The remnant there in the province who survived the captivity are in great distress and disgrace, and the wall of Jerusalem is broken down and its gates have been burned with fire.”  
**1:4** Now when I heard these words, I sat down and wept and mourned for days; and I was fasting and praying before the God of heaven.  
**1:5** I said, “Please, LORD God of heaven, the great and awesome God, who keeps the covenant and faithfulness for those who love Him and keep His commandments:  
**1:6** let Your ear now be attentive and Your eyes open, to hear the prayer of Your servant which I am praying before You now, day and night, on behalf of the sons of Israel Your servants, confessing the sins of the sons of Israel which we have committed against You; I and my father’s house have sinned.  
**1:7** We have acted very corruptly against You and have not kept the commandments, nor the statutes, nor the ordinances which You commanded Your servant Moses.  
**1:8** Remember, please, the word which You commanded Your servant Moses, saying, ‘<i>If</i> you are unfaithful, I will scatter you among the peoples;  
**1:9** but <i>if</i> you return to Me and keep My commandments and do them, though those of you who have been scattered were in the most remote part of the heavens, I will gather them from there and bring them to the place where I have chosen to have My name dwell.’  
**1:10** They are Your servants and Your people whom You redeemed by Your great power and by Your strong hand.  
**1:11** Please, Lord, may Your ear be attentive to the prayer of Your servant and the prayer of Your servants who delight to <sup>①</sup>revere Your name, and please make Your servant successful today and grant him mercy before this man.” <e>N</e>ow I was the cupbearer to the king.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>fear</i>  

## 2장

**2:1** And it came about in the month Nisan, in the twentieth year of King Artaxerxes, that wine <i>was</i> before him, and I picked up the wine and gave it to the king. Now I had not been sad in his presence.  
**2:2** So the king said to me, “Why is your face sad, though you are not ill? This is nothing but sadness of heart.” Then I was very much afraid.  
**2:3** And I said to the king, “May the king live forever. Why should my face not be sad when the city, the <sup>①</sup>site of my fathers’ tombs, is desolate and its gates have been consumed by fire?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>house</i>  
**2:4** Then the king said to me, “ <sup>①</sup>What would you request?” So I prayed to the God of heaven.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>For what is this you</i>  
**2:5** Then I said to the king, “If it pleases the king, and if your servant has found favor before you, <i>I request</i> that you send me to Judah, to the city of my fathers’ tombs, that I may rebuild it.”  
**2:6** Then the king said to me, with the queen sitting beside him, “How long will your journey be, and when will you return?” So it pleased the king to send me, and I gave him a definite time.  
**2:7** And I said to the king, “If it pleases the king, let letters be given me for the governors <i>of the provinces</i> beyond the River, so that they will allow me to pass through until I come to Judah,  
**2:8** and a letter to Asaph the keeper of the king’s forest, so that he will give me timber to make beams for the gates of the citadel which is by the <sup>①</sup>temple, for the wall of the city, and for the house to which I will go.” And the king granted <i>them</i> to me because the good hand of my God <i>was</i> on me.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>house</i>  
**2:9** Then I came to the governors <i>of the provinces</i> beyond the <i>Euphrates</i> River and gave them the king’s letters. Now the king had sent with me officers of the army and horsemen.  
**2:10** And when Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the Ammonite <sup>①</sup>official heard <i>about it,</i> it was very displeasing to them that someone had come to seek the welfare of the sons of Israel.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>servant</i>  
**2:11** So I came to Jerusalem and was there for three days.  
**2:12** And I got up in the night, I and a few men with me. I did not tell anyone what my God was putting into my <sup>①</sup>mind to do for Jerusalem, and there was no animal with me except the animal on which I was riding.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>heart</i>  
**2:13** So I went out at night by the Valley Gate in the direction of the Dragon’s Spring and <i>on</i> to the Dung Gate, and I was inspecting the walls of Jerusalem which were broken down and its gates which had been consumed by fire.  
**2:14** Then I passed on to the Fountain Gate and the King’s Pool, but there was no place for <sup>①</sup>my mount to pass.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the animal under me</i>  
**2:15** So I was going up at night by the ravine and inspecting the wall. Then I entered the Valley Gate again and returned.  
**2:16** However, the officials did not know where I had gone or what I was doing; nor had I as yet told the Jews, the priests, the nobles, the officials, or the rest who were doing the work.  
**2:17** Then I said to them, “You see the bad situation we are in, that Jerusalem is desolate and its gates have been burned by fire. Come, let’s rebuild the wall of Jerusalem so that we will no longer be a disgrace.”  
**2:18** And I told them how the hand of my God had been favorable to me and also about the king’s words which he had spoken to me. Then they said, “Let’s arise and build.” So they put their hands to the good <i>work.</i>  
**2:19** But when Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the Ammonite <sup>①</sup>official, and Geshem the Arab heard <i>about it,</i> they mocked us and despised us, and said, “What is this thing that you are doing? Are you rebelling against the king?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>servant</i>  
**2:20** So I answered them and said to them, “The God of heaven will make us successful; therefore we His servants will arise and build, but you have no part, right, or memorial in Jerusalem.”

## 5장

**5:1** Now there was a great outcry of the people and of their wives against their Jewish brothers.

**5:3** And there were <i>others</i> who said, “We are mortgaging our fields, our vineyards, and our houses so that we might get grain because of the famine.”  
**5:4** There also were those who said, “We have borrowed money for the king’s tax <i>on</i> our fields and our vineyards.  
**5:5** And now our flesh is like the flesh of our brothers, our children like their children. **5:6** Then I was very angry when I heard their outcry and these words.  
**5:7** So I thought it over and contended with the nobles and the leading people, and said to them, “You are lending at interest, each to his brother!” Therefore, I held a great assembly against them.  
**5:8** And I said to them, “We, according to our ability, have <sup>①</sup>redeemed our Jewish brothers who were sold to the nations; now would you even sell your brothers that they may be sold to us?” Then they were silent and could not find a word <i>to say.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>purchased</i>

**5:13** I also shook out the <sup>①</sup>front of my garment and said, “So may God shake out every person from his house and from his possessions who does not keep this <sup>②</sup>promise; just so may he be shaken out and emptied.” And all the assembly said, “Amen!” And they praised the LORD. Then the people acted in accordance with this <sup>③</sup>promise.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>fold</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>word</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>word</i>

**5:15** But the previous governors who were before me <sup>①</sup>laid burdens on the people and took from them bread and wine besides forty shekels of silver; even their servants domineered the people. But I did not do so because of <i>my</i> fear of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>made heavy</i>

**5:19** Remember me, my God, for good, <i>in return for</i> all that I have done for this people.  

## 6장

**6:1** Now when it was reported to Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab, and to the rest of our enemies that I had rebuilt the wall, and <i>that</i> no breach was left in it, although at that time I had not installed the doors in the gates,
