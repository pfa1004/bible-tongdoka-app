## 1장

**1:3** it seemed fitting to me as well, having <sup>①</sup>investigated everything carefully from the beginning, to write <i>it out</i> for you in an orderly sequence, most excellent Theophilus;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>followed</i>  
**1:4** so that you may know the exact truth about the <sup>①</sup>things you have been <sup>②</sup>taught.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>words</i> <sup>②</sup>Or<i>orally instructed in</i>  
**1:5** In the days of Herod, king of Judea, there was a priest named Zechariah, of the division of <sup>①</sup>Abijah; and he had a wife <sup>②</sup>from the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gr <i>Abia</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., of priestly descent  
**1:6** They were both righteous in the sight of God, walking blamelessly in all the commandments and requirements of the Lord.  
**1:7** And <i>yet</i> they had no child, because Elizabeth was infertile, and they were both advanced in <sup>①</sup>years.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>days</i>  
**1:8** Now it happened <i>that</i> while he was performing his priestly service before God in the appointed order of his division,  
**1:9** according to the custom of the priestly office, he was chosen by lot to enter the temple of the Lord and burn incense.  
**1:10** And the whole multitude of the people were in prayer outside at the hour of the incense offering.  
**1:11** Now an angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing to the right of the altar of incense.  
**1:12** Zechariah was troubled when he saw <i>the angel,</i> and fear <sup>①</sup>gripped him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>fell upon</i>  
**1:13** But the angel said to him, “Do not be afraid, Zechariah, for your prayer has been heard, and your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you shall <sup>①</sup>name him John.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>call his name</i>  
**1:14** You will have joy and gladness, and many will rejoice over his birth.  
**1:15** For he will be great in the sight of the Lord; and he will drink no wine or liquor, and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit <sup>①</sup>while still in his mother’s womb.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>still from his</i>  
**1:16** And he will turn many of the sons of Israel back to the Lord their God.  
**1:17** And <i>it is</i> he <i>who</i> will go <i>as a forerunner</i> before Him in the spirit and power of Elijah, TO TURN THE HEARTS OF FATHERS BACK TO <i>their</i> children, and the disobedient to the attitude of the righteous, to make ready a people prepared for the Lord.”  
**1:18** Zechariah said to the angel, “How will I know this? For I am an old man, and my wife is advanced in her <sup>①</sup>years.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>days</i>  
**1:19** The angel answered and said to him, “I am Gabriel, who <sup>①</sup>stands in the presence of God, and I was sent to speak to you and to bring you this good news.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>stand beside</i>  
**1:20** And behold, you will be silent and unable to speak until the day when these things take place, because you did not believe my words, which will be fulfilled at their proper time.”  
**1:21** And <i>meanwhile</i> the people were waiting for Zechariah, and were wondering at his delay in the temple.  
**1:22** But when he came out, he was unable to speak to them; and they realized that he had seen a vision in the temple, and he <i>repeatedly</i> <sup>①</sup>made signs to them, and remained speechless.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Possibly gesturing or nodding to them  
**1:23** When the days of his priestly service were concluded, he went back home.  
**1:24** Now after these days his wife Elizabeth became pregnant, and she kept herself <sup>①</sup>in seclusion for five months, saying,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>hidden</i>  
**1:25** “This is the way the Lord has dealt with me in the days when He looked <i>with favor</i> upon <i>me,</i> to take away my disgrace among people.”  
**1:26** Now in the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city in Galilee named Nazareth,  
**1:27** to a virgin <sup>①</sup>betrothed to a man whose name was Joseph, of the <sup>②</sup>descendants of David; and the virgin’s name was <sup>③</sup>Mary.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Unlike engagement, a betrothed couple was considered married, but did not yet live together <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>house</i> <sup>③</sup>Gr <i>Mariam,</i> Heb <i>Miriam;</i> so throughout Luke  
**1:28** And coming in, he said to her, “Greetings, favored one! The Lord <sup>①</sup><i>is</i> with you.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or be  
**1:29** But she was very perplexed at <i>this</i> statement, and was pondering what kind of greeting this was.  
**1:30** And the angel said to her, “Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God.  
**1:31** And behold, you will conceive in your womb and give birth to a son, and you shall name Him Jesus.  
**1:32** He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High; and the Lord God will give Him the throne of His father David;  
**1:33** and He will reign over the house of Jacob forever, and His kingdom will have no end.”  
**1:34** But Mary said to the angel, “How will this be, since I <sup>①</sup>am a virgin?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>do not know a man</i>  
**1:35** The angel answered and said to her, “The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you; for that reason also the <sup>①</sup>holy Child will be called the Son of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the holy one fathered</i>  
**1:36** And behold, even your relative Elizabeth herself has conceived a son in her old age, and <sup>①</sup>she who was called infertile is now in her sixth month.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>this is the sixth month for her who</i>  
**1:37** For nothing will be impossible with God.”  
**1:38** And Mary said, “Behold, the Lord’s bond-servant; may it be done to me according to your word.” And the angel departed from her.  
**1:39** Now <sup>①</sup>at this time Mary set out and went in a hurry to the hill country, to a city of Judah,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in these days</i>  
**1:40** and she entered the house of Zechariah and greeted Elizabeth.  
**1:41** When Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, the baby leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.  
**1:42** And she cried out with a loud voice and said, “Blessed <i>are</i> you among women, and blessed <i>is</i> the fruit of your womb!  
**1:43** And <sup>①</sup>how has it happened to me that the mother of my Lord would come to me?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>from where this to me</i>  
**1:44** For behold, when the sound of your greeting reached my ears, the baby leaped in my womb for joy.  
**1:45** And blessed <i>is</i> she who <sup>①</sup>believed that there would be a fulfillment of what had been spoken to her <sup>②</sup>by the Lord.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>believed, because there will be</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>from</i>  
**1:46** And Mary said:<br>“My soul <sup>①</sup>exalts the Lord,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>makes great</i>  
**1:47** And my spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior.  
**1:48** For He has had regard for the humble state of His bond-servant;<br>For behold, from now <i>on</i> all generations will <sup>①</sup>call me blessed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>consider</i>  
**1:49** For the Mighty One has done great things for me;<br>And holy is His name.  
**1:50** And His mercy is to generation <sup>①</sup>after generation<br>Toward those who fear Him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and</i>  
**1:51** He has done <sup>①</sup>mighty deeds with His arm;<br>He has scattered <i>those who were</i> proud in the <sup>②</sup>thoughts of their hearts.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>might</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>thought, attitude</i>  
**1:52** He has brought down rulers from <i>their</i> thrones,<br>And has exalted those who were humble.  
**1:53** He has filled the hungry with good things,<br>And sent the rich away empty-handed.  
**1:54** He has given help to His servant Israel,<br> <sup>①</sup>In remembrance of His mercy,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>So as to remember</i>  
**1:55** Just as He spoke to our fathers,<br>To Abraham and his <sup>①</sup>descendants forever.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>seed</i>  
**1:56** Mary stayed with her about three months, and <i>then</i> returned to her home.  
**1:57** Now the time <sup>①</sup>had come for Elizabeth to give birth, and she gave birth to a son.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>was fulfilled</i>  
**1:58** Her neighbors and her relatives heard that the Lord had <sup>①</sup>displayed His great mercy toward her; and they were rejoicing with her.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>magnified His mercy with her</i>  
**1:59** And it happened that on the eighth day they came to circumcise the child, and they were going to call him Zechariah, <sup>①</sup>after his father.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>after the name of</i>  
**1:60** And <i>yet</i> his mother responded and said, “No indeed; but he shall be called John.”  
**1:61** And they said to her, “There is no one among your relatives who is called by this name.”  
**1:62** And they <sup>①</sup>made signs to his father, as to what he wanted him called.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., gestured or nodded  
**1:63** And he asked for a tablet and wrote <sup>①</sup>as follows, “His name is John.” And they were all amazed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>saying</i>  
**1:64** And at once his mouth was opened and his tongue <i>freed,</i> and he <i>began</i> speaking in praise of God.  
**1:65** And fear came on all those who lived around them; and all these matters were being talked about in the entire hill country of Judea.  
**1:66** All who heard <i>them</i> kept <i>them</i> in mind, saying, “What then will this child <i>turn out to</i> be?” For indeed the hand of the Lord was with him.  
**1:67** And his father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied, saying:  
**1:68** “Blessed <i>be</i> the Lord God of Israel,<br>For He has visited <i>us</i> and accomplished redemption for His people,  
**1:69** And has raised up a horn of salvation for us<br>In the house of His servant David—  
**1:70** Just as He spoke by the mouth of His holy prophets from ancient times—  
**1:71**  <sup>①</sup>Salvation from our enemies,<br>And from the hand of all who hate us;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Deliverance</i>  
**1:72** To show mercy to our fathers,<br>And to remember His holy covenant,  
**1:73** <i>The</i> oath which He swore to our father Abraham,  
**1:74** To grant us that we, being rescued from the hand of <i>our</i> enemies,<br>Would serve Him without fear,  
**1:75** In holiness and righteousness before Him all our days.  
**1:76** And you, child, also will be called the prophet of the Most High;<br>For you will go on before the Lord to prepare His ways;  
**1:77** To give His people <i>the</i> knowledge of salvation<br> <sup>①</sup>By the forgiveness of their sins,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Consisting in</i>  
**1:78** Because of the tender mercy of our God,<br>With which the Sunrise from on high will visit us,  
**1:79** To shine on those who sit in darkness and the shadow of death,<br>To guide our feet into the way of peace.”  
**1:80** Now the child grew and was becoming strong in spirit, and he lived in the deserts until the day of his public appearance to Israel.

**2:2**  <sup>①</sup>This was the first census taken while <sup>②</sup>Quirinius was governor of Syria.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>This took place as a first census</i> <sup>②</sup>Gr <i>Kyrenios</i>

**2:4** Now Joseph also went up from Galilee, from the city of Nazareth, to Judea, to the city of David which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and family of David,  
**2:5** in order to register along with Mary, who was <sup>①</sup>betrothed to him, and was pregnant.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Unlike engagement, a betrothed couple was considered married, but did not yet live together

**2:20** And the shepherds went back, glorifying and praising God for all that they had heard and seen, just as had been told them.

**2:24** and to offer a sacrifice according to what has been stated in the Law of the Lord: “A PAIR OF TURTLEDOVES OR TWO YOUNG DOVES.”

**2:30** For my eyes have seen Your salvation,  
**2:31** Which You have prepared in the presence of all the peoples:  
**2:32** A light for revelation <sup>①</sup>for the Gentiles,<br>And the glory of Your people Israel.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of the Gentiles</i>

## 3장

**3:1** Now in the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar, when Pontius Pilate was governor of Judea, and Herod was tetrarch of Galilee and his brother Philip was tetrarch of the region of Ituraea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias was tetrarch of Abilene,  
**3:2** in the high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas, the word of God came to John, the son of Zechariah, in the wilderness.  
**3:3** And he came into all the region around the Jordan, preaching a baptism of repentance <sup>①</sup>for the forgiveness of sins;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>with reference to</i>  
**3:4** as it is written in the book of the words of Isaiah the prophet:<br>“THE VOICE OF ONE CALLING <sup>①</sup>OUT IN THE WILDERNESS,<br>‘PREPARE THE WAY OF THE LORD,<br>MAKE HIS PATHS STRAIGHT!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>out, Prepare in the wilderness the way</i>  
**3:5** EVERY RAVINE WILL BE FILLED,<br>AND EVERY MOUNTAIN AND HILL WILL BE LOWERED;<br>THE CROOKED WILL BECOME STRAIGHT,<br>AND THE ROUGH ROADS SMOOTH;  
**3:6** AND ALL <sup>①</sup>FLESH WILL SEE THE SALVATION OF GOD!’ ”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., mankind  
**3:7** So he was saying to the crowds who were going out to be baptized by him, “You <sup>①</sup>offspring of vipers, who warned you to flee from the wrath to come?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>brood</i>  
**3:8** Therefore produce fruits that are consistent with repentance, and do not start saying <sup>①</sup>to yourselves, ‘We have Abraham <i>as our</i> father,’ for I say to you that from these stones God is able to raise up children for Abraham.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>among</i>  
**3:9** But indeed the axe is already being laid at the root of the trees; so every tree that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.”  
**3:10** And the crowds were questioning him, saying, “Then what are we to do?”  
**3:11** And he would answer and say to them, “The one who has two <sup>①</sup>tunics is to share with the one who has none; and the one who has food is to do likewise.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>A long shirt worn next to the skin  
**3:12** Now even tax collectors came to be baptized, and they said to him, “Teacher, what are we to do?”  
**3:13** And he said to them, “ <sup>①</sup>Collect no more than what you have been ordered to.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Do</i>  
**3:14** And soldiers also were questioning him, saying, “What are we to do, we as well?” And he said to them, “Do not extort money from anyone, nor <sup>①</sup>harass <i>anyone,</i> and be content with your wages.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>blackmail</i>  
**3:15** Now while the people were in a state of expectation and they all were thinking carefully in their hearts about John, whether he himself perhaps was the <sup>①</sup>Christ,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., Messiah  
**3:16** John responded to them all, saying, “As for me, I baptize you with water; but He is coming who is mightier than I, and I am not fit to untie the straps of His sandals; He will baptize you <sup>①</sup>with the Holy Spirit and fire.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Gr here can be translated <i>in, with,</i> or <i>by</i>  
**3:17** His winnowing fork is in His hand to thoroughly clear His threshing floor, and to gather the wheat into His barn; but He will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.”  
**3:18** So with many other exhortations he preached the gospel to the people.
