# 34.나훔 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** The pronouncement of Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite:  
**1:2** A jealous and avenging God is the LORD;<br>The LORD is avenging and <sup>①</sup>wrathful.<br>The LORD takes vengeance on His adversaries,<br>And He reserves wrath for His enemies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>a possessor of wrath</i>  
**1:3** The LORD is slow to anger and great in power,<br>And the LORD will by no means leave <i>the guilty</i> unpunished.<br>In <i>the</i> gale and <i>the</i> storm is His way,<br>And clouds are the dust <i>beneath</i> His feet.  
**1:4** He rebukes the sea and dries it up;<br>He dries up all the rivers.<br>Bashan and Carmel wither,<br>The blossoms of Lebanon wither.  
**1:5** Mountains quake because of Him,<br>And the hills come apart;<br>Indeed the earth is upheaved by His presence,<br>The world and all the inhabitants in it.  
**1:6** Who can stand before His indignation?<br>Who can endure the burning of His anger?<br>His wrath gushes forth like fire,<br>And the rocks are broken up by Him.  
**1:7** The LORD is good,<br>A stronghold in the day of trouble,<br>And He knows those who take refuge in Him.  
**1:8** But with an overflowing flood<br>He will make a complete end of <sup>①</sup>its site,<br>And will pursue His enemies <i>into</i> darkness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., Nineveh’s  
**1:9** Whatever you devise against the LORD,<br>He will make a complete end of it.<br>Distress will not rise up twice.  
**1:10** Like tangled thorns,<br>And like those who are drunken with their drink,<br>They are consumed<br>Like stubble completely dried up.  
**1:11** From you has gone out<br>One who plotted evil against the LORD,<br>A <sup>①</sup>wicked counselor.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>worthless;</i> Heb <i>Belial</i>  
**1:12** This is what the LORD says:<br>“Though <i>they are at</i> full <i>strength</i> and so <i>they are</i> many,<br>So also they will be cut off and pass away.<br>Though I have afflicted you,<br>I will afflict you no longer.  
**1:13** So now, I will break his yoke from upon you,<br>And I will tear your shackles to pieces.”  
**1:14** The LORD has issued a command concerning <sup>①</sup>you:<br>“ <sup>②</sup>Your name will no longer be perpetuated.<br>I will eliminate the carved image and the cast metal image<br>From the house of your gods.<br>I will prepare your grave,<br>For you are contemptible.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., the king of Nineveh <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>No more of your name will be sown</i>  
**1:15**  <sup>①</sup>Behold, on the mountains, the feet of him who brings good news,<br>Who announces peace!<br>Celebrate your feasts, Judah,<br>Pay your vows.<br>For never again will the <sup>②</sup>wicked one pass through you;<br>He is eliminated completely.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch 2:1 in Heb <sup>②</sup>Or <i>worthless one;</i> Heb <i>Belial</i>  

## 2장

**2:1**  <sup>①</sup>The one who scatters has come up against <sup>②</sup>you.<br>Keep watch over the fortress, watch the road;<br> <sup>③</sup>Bind up your waist, <sup>④</sup>summon all your strength.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Strengthen your loins</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>strengthen power greatly</i> <sup>③</sup>Ch 2:2 in Heb <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>your face</i>  
**2:2** For the LORD will restore the splendor of Jacob<br>Like the splendor of Israel,<br>Even though destroyers have laid waste to them<br>And ruined their vines.  
**2:3** The shields of <sup>①</sup>his warriors are dyed red,<br>The warriors are dressed in scarlet,<br>The chariots are <i>fitted</i> with <sup>②</sup>flashing steel<br> <sup>③</sup>When he is prepared <i>to march,</i><br>And the juniper spears are brandished.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., those attacking Nineveh <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>fire of steel</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>On the day of his preparation</i>  
**2:4** The chariots drive wildly in the streets,<br>They rush around in the <sup>①</sup>public squares;<br>Their appearance is like torches,<br>They drive back and forth like lightning flashes.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>broad places</i>  
**2:5** He remembers his officers;<br>They stumble in their advance,<br>They hurry to her wall,<br>And the <sup>①</sup>mantelet is set up.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., a shield used for stopping projectiles  
**2:6** The gates of the rivers are opened<br>And the palace sways back and forth.  
**2:7** It is set:<br>She is stripped, she is led away,<br>And her slave women are sobbing like the sound of doves,<br>Beating their <sup>①</sup>breasts.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>hearts</i>  
**2:8** Though Nineveh <i>was</i> like a pool of water throughout her days,<br>Yet they are fleeing;<br>“Stop, stop,”<br>But no one turns back.  
**2:9** Plunder the silver,<br>Plunder the gold!<br>For there is no end to the treasure—<br>Wealth from every <i>kind of</i> desirable object.  
**2:10** She is emptied! Yes, she is desolate and waste!<br>Hearts are melting and knees wobbling!<br>Also trembling is in <sup>①</sup>the entire body,<br>And all their faces have become pale!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>all the loins</i>  
**2:11** Where is the den of the lions<br>And the feeding place of the young lions,<br>Where the lion, lioness, <i>and</i> lion’s cub went<br>With nothing to disturb <i>them?</i>  
**2:12** The lion tore enough for his cubs,<br> <sup>①</sup>Killed <i>enough prey</i> for his lionesses,<br>And filled his lairs with prey<br>And his dens with torn flesh.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Strangled</i>  
**2:13** “Behold, I am against you,” declares the LORD of armies. “I will burn up her chariots in smoke, and a sword will devour your young lions; I will eliminate your prey from the land, and no longer will the voice of your messengers be heard.”  

## 3장

**3:1** Woe to the bloody city, completely full of lies <i>and</i> pillage;<br><i>Her</i> prey does not leave.  
**3:2** The sound of the whip,<br>The sound of the roar of the wheel,<br>Galloping horses<br>And <sup>①</sup>bounding chariots!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>leaping</i>  
**3:3** Horsemen charging,<br>Swords flashing, spears gleaming,<br>Many killed, a mass of corpses,<br>And there is no end to <i>the</i> dead bodies—<br>They stumble over <sup>①</sup>the dead bodies!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>their</i>  
**3:4** <i>All</i> because of the many sexual acts of the prostitute,<br>The charming one, the mistress of sorceries,<br>Who sells nations by her sexual acts,<br>And families by her sorceries.  
**3:5** “Behold, I am against you,” declares the LORD of armies;<br>“And I will <sup>①</sup>lift up your skirts over your face,<br>And show the nations your nakedness,<br>And the kingdoms your shame.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>uncover your private parts</i>  
**3:6** I will throw filth on you<br>And declare you worthless,<br>And set you up as a spectacle.  
**3:7** And it will come about that all who see you<br>Will <sup>①</sup>shrink from you and say,<br>‘Nineveh is devastated!<br>Who will have sympathy for her?’<br>Where shall I seek comforters for you?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>flee</i>  
**3:8** Are you better than <sup>①</sup>No-amon,<br>Which was situated by the canals of the Nile,<br>With water surrounding her,<br>Whose rampart <i>was</i> <sup>②</sup>the sea,<br>Whose wall <i>consisted</i> of <sup>③</sup>the sea?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., the city of Amon: Thebes <sup>②</sup>I.e., the Nile <sup>③</sup>I.e., the Nile  
**3:9** Ethiopia was <i>her</i> might,<br>Egypt too, without limits.<br>Put and Lubim were among <sup>①</sup>her helpers.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>your</i>  
**3:10** Yet she became an exile,<br>She went into captivity;<br>Also her small children were smashed to pieces<br>At the head of every street;<br>They cast lots for her honorable men,<br>And all her great men were bound with shackles.  
**3:11** You too will become drunk,<br>You will be hidden.<br>You too will search for a refuge from the enemy.  
**3:12** All your fortifications are fig trees with <sup>①</sup>ripe fruit—<br>When shaken, they fall into the eater’s mouth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>first fruits</i>  
**3:13** Behold, your people are women in your midst!<br>The gates of your land are opened wide to your enemies;<br>Fire consumes your gate bars.

**3:15** There fire will consume you,<br>The sword will cut you down;<br>It will consume you as the creeping locust <i>consumes a crop.</i><br><e>M</e>ultiply yourself like the creeping locust,<br>Multiply yourself like the migratory locust.

**3:17** Your courtiers are like the migratory locust.<br>Your officials are like a swarm of locusts<br>Settling in the stone shelters on a cold day.<br>The sun rises and they flee,<br>And the place where they are is not known.  
**3:18** Your shepherds are sleeping, O king of Assyria;<br>Your officers are lying down.<br>Your people are scattered on the mountains<br>And there is no one to gather <i>them.</i>  
**3:19** There is no relief for your collapse,<br>Your wound is incurable.<br>All who hear <sup>①</sup>about you<br>Will clap <i>their</i> hands over you,<br>For upon whom has your evil not come continually?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>your report</i>
