## 3장

**3:1** Now Peter and John were going up to the temple at the <sup>①</sup>ninth <i>hour,</i> the hour of prayer.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., 3 p.m.  
**3:2** And a man who had been unable to walk from <sup>①</sup>birth was being carried, whom they used to set down every day at the gate of the temple which is called Beautiful, in order <i>for him</i> to beg for charitable gifts from those entering the temple <i>grounds.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>his mother’s womb</i>  
**3:3** When he saw Peter and John about to go into the temple <i>grounds,</i> he <i>began</i> asking to receive a charitable gift.  
**3:4** But Peter, along with John, looked at him intently and said, “Look at us!”  
**3:5** And he gave them his attention, expecting to receive something from them.  
**3:6** But Peter said, “I do not have silver and gold, but what I do have I give to you: In the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene, walk!”  
**3:7** And grasping him by the right hand, he raised him up; and immediately his feet and his ankles were strengthened.  
**3:8** And leaping up, he stood and <i>began</i> to walk; and he entered the temple with them, walking and leaping and praising God.  
**3:9** And all the people saw him walking and praising God;  
**3:10** and they recognized him as being the very one who used to sit at the Beautiful Gate of the temple <i>to beg</i> for charitable gifts, and they were filled with wonder and amazement at what had happened to him.  
**3:11** While he was clinging to Peter and John, all the people ran together to them at the <sup>①</sup>portico named Solomon’s, completely astonished.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>colonnade</i>  
**3:12** But when Peter saw <i>this,</i> he replied to the people, “Men of Israel, why are you amazed at this, or why are you staring at us, as though by our own power or godliness we had made him walk?  
**3:13** The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, the God of our fathers, has glorified His <sup>①</sup>servant Jesus, <i>the one</i> whom you handed over and disowned in the presence of Pilate, when he had decided to release <i>Him.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Son</i>  
**3:14** But you disowned the Holy and Righteous One, and asked for a murderer to be granted to you,  
**3:15** but put to death the <sup>①</sup>Prince of life, whom God raised from the dead, <sup>②</sup><i>a fact</i> to which we are witnesses.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Originator</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>of whom we</i>  
**3:16** And on the basis of faith in His name, <i>it is</i> <sup>①</sup>the name of Jesus which has strengthened this man whom you see and know; and the faith which <i>comes</i> through Him has given him this perfect health in the presence of you all.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>His name</i>  
**3:17** “And now, brothers, I know that you acted in ignorance, just as your rulers also did.  
**3:18** But the things which God previously announced by the mouths of all the prophets, that His <sup>①</sup>Christ would suffer, He has fulfilled in this way.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., Messiah  
**3:19** Therefore repent and return, so that your sins may be wiped away, in order that times of refreshing may come from the presence of the Lord;  
**3:20** and that He may send Jesus, the <sup>①</sup>Christ appointed for you,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., Messiah  
**3:21** whom heaven must receive until <i>the</i> <sup>①</sup>period of restoration of all things, about which God spoke by the mouths of His holy prophets from ancient times.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>periods, times</i>  
**3:22** Moses said, ‘THE LORD GOD WILL RAISE UP FOR YOU A PROPHET LIKE ME FROM YOUR COUNTRYMEN; TO HIM YOU SHALL LISTEN regarding everything He says to you.  
**3:23** And it shall be that every soul that does not listen to that prophet shall be utterly destroyed from among the people.’  
**3:24** And likewise, all the prophets who have spoken from Samuel and <i>his</i> successors <i>onward,</i> have also announced these days.  
**3:25** It is you who are the sons of the prophets and of the covenant which God ordained with your fathers, saying to Abraham, ‘AND IN YOUR SEED ALL THE FAMILIES OF THE EARTH SHALL BE BLESSED.’  
**3:26** God raised up His <sup>①</sup>Servant for you first, and sent Him to bless you by turning every one <i>of you</i> from your wicked ways.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Son</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** As they were speaking to the people, the priests and the captain of the temple <i>guard</i> and the Sadducees came up to them,  
**4:2** being greatly disturbed because they were teaching the people and proclaiming <sup>①</sup>in Jesus the resurrection from the dead.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in the case of</i>  
**4:3** And they laid hands on them and put <i>them</i> in prison until the next day, for it was already evening.  
**4:4** But many of those who had heard the <sup>①</sup>message believed; and the number of the men came to be about five thousand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>word</i>  
**4:5** On the next day, their rulers and elders and scribes were gathered together in Jerusalem;  
**4:6** and Annas the high priest <i>was there,</i> and Caiaphas, John, and Alexander, and all who were of high-priestly descent.  
**4:7** When they had placed them in the center, they <i>began to</i> inquire, “By what power, or in what name, have you done this?”  
**4:8** Then Peter, <sup>①</sup>filled with the Holy Spirit, said to them, “ <sup>②</sup>Rulers and elders of the people,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>having</i> just <i>been filled</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>Rulers of the people and elders</i>  
**4:9** if we are <sup>①</sup>on trial today for a benefit done to a sick man, as to <sup>②</sup>how this man has been made well,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>answering</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>by what;</i> or <i>by whom</i>  
**4:10** let it be known to all of you and to all the people of Israel, that <sup>①</sup>by the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene, whom you crucified, whom God raised from the dead— <sup>②</sup>by <sup>③</sup>this <i>name</i> this man stands here before you in good health.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>in</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>Him</i>  
**4:11**  <sup>①</sup>He is the STONE WHICH WAS REJECTED by you, THE BUILDERS, <i>but</i> WHICH BECAME THE CHIEF CORNERSTONE.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>This One</i>  
**4:12** And there is salvation in no one else; for there is no other name under heaven that has been given among mankind by which we must be saved.”  
**4:13** Now as they observed the confidence of Peter and John and understood that they were uneducated and untrained men, they were amazed, and <i>began</i> to recognize them <sup>①</sup>as having been with Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>that they had been</i>  
**4:14** And seeing the man who had been healed standing with them, they had nothing to say in reply.  
**4:15** But when they had ordered them to leave the <sup>①</sup>Council, they <i>began</i> to confer with one another,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Sanhedrin</i>  
**4:16** saying, “What are we to do with these men? For the fact that a noteworthy <sup>①</sup>miracle has taken place through them is apparent to all who live in Jerusalem, and we cannot deny it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sign</i>  
**4:17** But so that it will not spread any further among the people, let’s warn them not to speak any longer to any person in this name.”  
**4:18** And when they had summoned them, they commanded them not to speak or teach at all <sup>①</sup>in the name of Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>on the basis of</i>  
**4:19** But Peter and John answered and said to them, “Whether it is right in the sight of God to listen to you rather than to God, make your <i>own</i> judgment;  
**4:20** for we cannot stop speaking about what we have seen and heard.”  
**4:21** When they had threatened them further, they let them go (finding no basis on which to punish them) on account of the people, because they were all glorifying God for what had happened;
