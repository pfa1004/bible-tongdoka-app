## 4장

**4:2** they approached Zerubbabel and the heads of fathers’ <i>households,</i> and said to them, “Let us build with you, for like you, we seek your God; and we have been sacrificing to Him since the days of Esarhaddon king of Assyria, who brought us up here.”

**4:4** Then the people of the land <sup>①</sup>discouraged the people of Judah, and frightened them from building,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>slackened the hands of</i>

**4:9**  <sup>①</sup>Rehum the commander, Shimshai the scribe, and the rest of their colleagues, the judges and the lesser governors, the officials, the secretaries, the men of Erech, the Babylonians, the men of Susa, that is, the Elamites,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Uncertain, MT <i>Then Rehum</i>
