## 1장

**1:1** God, after He spoke long ago to the fathers in the prophets in many portions and in many ways,  
**1:2**  <sup>①</sup>in these last days has spoken to us <sup>②</sup>in <i>His</i> Son, whom He appointed heir of all things, through whom He also made the <sup>③</sup>world.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>at the end of these days</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>in</i> the person of a <i>Son;</i> lit <i>in Son</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>ages</i>  
**1:3**  <sup>①</sup>And He is the radiance of His glory and the exact representation of His nature, and <sup>②</sup>upholds all things by <sup>③</sup>the word of His power. When He had made purification of sins, He sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Who being</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>upholding</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>His word of power</i>  
**1:4** having become so much better than the angels, to the extent that He has inherited a more excellent name than they.  
**1:5** For to which of the angels did He ever say,<br>“YOU ARE MY SON,<br>TODAY I HAVE FATHERED YOU”? And again,<br>“I WILL BE A FATHER TO HIM<br>AND HE WILL BE A SON TO ME”?  
**1:6** And <sup>①</sup>when He again brings the firstborn into <sup>②</sup>the world, He says,<br>“AND LET ALL THE ANGELS OF GOD WORSHIP HIM.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>again when He brings</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>the inhabited earth</i>  
**1:7** And regarding the angels He says,<br>“HE MAKES HIS ANGELS WINDS,<br>AND HIS MINISTERS A FLAME OF FIRE.”  
**1:8** But regarding the Son <i>He says,</i><br>“YOUR THRONE, GOD, IS FOREVER AND EVER,<br>AND THE SCEPTER OF RIGHTEOUSNESS IS THE SCEPTER OF <sup>①</sup>HIS KINGDOM.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Late mss <i>Your</i>  
**1:9** YOU HAVE LOVED RIGHTEOUSNESS AND HATED LAWLESSNESS;<br>THEREFORE GOD, YOUR GOD, HAS ANOINTED YOU<br>WITH THE OIL OF JOY ABOVE YOUR COMPANIONS.”  
**1:10** And,<br>“YOU, LORD, IN THE BEGINNING LAID THE FOUNDATION OF THE EARTH,<br>AND THE HEAVENS ARE THE WORKS OF YOUR HANDS;  
**1:11** THEY WILL PERISH, BUT YOU REMAIN;<br>AND THEY ALL WILL <sup>①</sup>WEAR OUT LIKE A GARMENT,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>become old</i>  
**1:12** AND LIKE A ROBE YOU WILL ROLL THEM UP;<br>LIKE A GARMENT THEY WILL ALSO BE CHANGED.<br>BUT YOU ARE THE SAME,<br>AND YOUR YEARS WILL NOT COME TO AN END.”  
**1:13** But to which of the angels has He ever said,<br>“SIT AT MY RIGHT HAND,<br>UNTIL I MAKE YOUR ENEMIES<br>A FOOTSTOOL FOR YOUR FEET”?  
**1:14** Are they not all ministering spirits, sent out to <i>provide</i> service for the sake of those who will inherit salvation?  

## 2장

**2:1** For this reason we must pay much closer attention to <sup>①</sup>what we have heard, so that we do not drift away <i>from it.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the things that have been heard</i>  
**2:2** For if the word spoken through angels proved <sup>①</sup>unalterable, and every violation and act of disobedience received a just <sup>②</sup>punishment,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>steadfast</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>retribution</i>  
**2:3** how will we escape if we neglect so great a salvation? <sup>①</sup>After it was at first spoken through the Lord, it was confirmed to us by those who heard,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Which was</i>  
**2:4** God also testifying with them, both by signs and wonders, and by various <sup>①</sup>miracles and by <sup>②</sup>gifts of the Holy Spirit according to His own will.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>works of power</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>distributions</i>  
**2:5** For He did not subject to angels <sup>①</sup>the world to come, about which we are speaking.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the inhabited earth</i>  
**2:6** But someone has testified somewhere, saying,<br>“WHAT IS MAN, THAT YOU THINK OF HIM?<br>OR A SON OF MAN, THAT YOU ARE CONCERNED ABOUT HIM?  
**2:7** YOU HAVE MADE HIM <sup>①</sup>FOR A LITTLE WHILE LOWER THAN ANGELS;<br>YOU HAVE CROWNED HIM WITH GLORY AND HONOR <sup>②</sup>;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>...him a little lower than...</i> <sup>②</sup>One early ms continues, <i>and have appointed him over the works of Your hands</i>  
**2:8** YOU HAVE PUT EVERYTHING IN SUBJECTION UNDER HIS FEET.” For in subjecting all things to him, He left nothing that is not subject to him. But now we do not yet see all things subjected to him.  
**2:9** But we do see Him who was made <sup>①</sup>for a little while lower than the angels, <i>namely,</i> Jesus, because of His suffering death crowned with glory and honor, so that by the grace of God He might taste death for everyone.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>a little lower</i>  
**2:10** For it was fitting for Him, for whom are all things, and through whom are all things, in bringing many sons to glory, to perfect the <sup>①</sup>originator of their salvation through sufferings.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>leader</i>  
**2:11** For both He who sanctifies and those who are <sup>①</sup>sanctified are all from one <i>Father;</i> for this reason He is not ashamed to call them brothers <i>and sisters,</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>being sanctified</i>  
**2:12** saying,<br>“I WILL PROCLAIM YOUR NAME TO MY BROTHERS,<br>IN THE MIDST OF THE ASSEMBLY I WILL SING YOUR PRAISE.”  
**2:13** And again,<br>“I WILL PUT MY TRUST IN HIM.” And again,<br>“BEHOLD, I AND THE CHILDREN WHOM GOD HAS GIVEN ME.”  
**2:14** Therefore, since the children share in <sup>①</sup>flesh and blood, He Himself likewise also partook of the same, so that through death He might destroy the one who has the power of death, that is, the devil,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>blood and flesh</i>  
**2:15** and free those who through fear of death were subject to slavery all their lives.  
**2:16** For clearly He does not <sup>①</sup>give help to angels, but He gives help to the <sup>②</sup>descendants of Abraham.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>take hold of angels, but He takes hold of</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>seed</i>  
**2:17** Therefore, in all things He <sup>①</sup>had to be made like His brothers so that He might become a merciful and faithful high priest in things pertaining to God, to make <sup>②</sup>propitiation for the sins of the people.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>was obligated to be</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., reconciliation with God by atoning for the sins  
**2:18** For since He Himself was <sup>①</sup>tempted in that which He has suffered, He is able to come to the aid of those who are <sup>②</sup>tempted.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>tested</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>tested</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** Therefore, holy brothers <i>and sisters,</i> partakers of a heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and High Priest of our confession: Jesus;  
**3:2**  <sup>①</sup>He was faithful to Him who appointed Him, as Moses also was in all His house.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>being faithful</i>  
**3:3** For He has been counted worthy of more glory than Moses, by just so much as the builder of the house has more honor than the house.  
**3:4** For every house is built by someone, but the builder of all things is God.  
**3:5** Now Moses was faithful in all <sup>①</sup>God’s house as a servant, for a testimony of those things which were to be spoken <i>later;</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>His</i>  
**3:6** but Christ <i>was faithful</i> as a Son over His house—whose house we are, if we hold firmly to our confidence and the boast of our <sup>①</sup>hope.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms adds <i>firm until the end</i>  
**3:7** Therefore, just as the Holy Spirit says,<br>“TODAY IF YOU HEAR HIS VOICE,  
**3:8** DO NOT HARDEN YOUR HEARTS AS <sup>①</sup>WHEN THEY PROVOKED ME,<br>AS ON THE DAY OF TRIAL IN THE WILDERNESS,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in the provocation</i>  
**3:9** WHERE YOUR FATHERS PUT <i>ME to the test</i>,<br>AND SAW MY WORKS FOR FORTY YEARS.  
**3:10** THEREFORE I WAS ANGRY WITH THIS GENERATION,<br>AND SAID, ‘THEY ALWAYS GO ASTRAY IN THEIR HEART,<br>AND THEY DID NOT KNOW MY WAYS’;  
**3:11** AS I SWORE IN MY ANGER,<br>‘THEY CERTAINLY SHALL NOT ENTER MY REST.’ ”  
**3:12** Take care, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> that there will not be in any one of you an evil, unbelieving heart <sup>①</sup>that falls away from the living God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>that leaves the;</i> lit <i>in falling away from</i>  
**3:13** But encourage one another every day, as long as it is <i>still</i> called “today,” so that none of you will be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin.  
**3:14** For we have become partakers of Christ if we keep the beginning of our <sup>①</sup>commitment firm until the end,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>assurance</i>  
**3:15** while it is said,<br>“TODAY IF YOU HEAR HIS VOICE,<br>DO NOT HARDEN YOUR HEARTS, AS <sup>①</sup>WHEN THEY PROVOKED ME.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in the rebellion</i>  
**3:16** For who provoked <i>Him</i> when they had heard? Indeed, did not all those who came out of Egypt <i>led</i> by Moses?  
**3:17** And with whom was He angry for forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose dead bodies fell in the wilderness?  
**3:18** And to whom did He swear that they would not enter His rest, but to those who were disobedient?  
**3:19** And <i>so</i> we see that they were not able to enter because of unbelief.  

## 4장

**4:1** Therefore, we must fear if, while a promise remains of entering His rest, any one of you may seem to have come short <i>of it.</i>  
**4:2** For indeed we have had good news preached to us, just as they also <i>did;</i> but the word <sup>①</sup>they heard did not benefit them, because <sup>②</sup>they were not united with those who listened with faith.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of hearing</i> <sup>②</sup>One early ms <i>it was not united with faith in those who heard</i>  
**4:3** For we who have believed enter <i>that</i> rest, just as He has said,<br>“AS I SWORE IN MY ANGER,<br>THEY CERTAINLY SHALL NOT ENTER MY REST,” although His works were finished from the foundation of the world.  
**4:4** For He has said somewhere concerning the seventh <i>day:</i> “AND GOD RESTED ON THE SEVENTH DAY FROM ALL HIS WORKS”;  
**4:5** and again in this <i>passage,</i> “THEY CERTAINLY SHALL NOT ENTER MY REST.”  
**4:6** Therefore, since it remains for some to enter it, and those who previously had good news preached to them failed to enter because of disobedience,  
**4:7** He again sets a certain day, “Today,” saying <sup>①</sup>through David after so long a time just as has been said before,<br>“TODAY IF YOU HEAR HIS VOICE,<br>DO NOT HARDEN YOUR HEARTS.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in</i>  
**4:8** For if <sup>①</sup>Joshua had given them rest, He would not have spoken of another day after that.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gr <i>Jesus</i>  
**4:9** Consequently, there remains a Sabbath rest for the people of God.  
**4:10** For the one who has entered His rest has himself also rested from his works, as God did from His.  
**4:11** Therefore let’s make every effort to enter that rest, so that no one will fall by <i>following</i> the same example of disobedience.  
**4:12** For the word of God is living and active, and sharper than any two-edged sword, even penetrating as far as the division of soul and spirit, of both joints and marrow, and able to <sup>①</sup>judge the thoughts and intentions of the heart.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>discern</i>  
**4:13** And there is no creature hidden from His sight, but all things are open and laid bare to the eyes of Him to whom <sup>①</sup>we must answer.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is our word</i>  
**4:14** Therefore, since we have a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let’s hold firmly to our confession.  
**4:15** For we do not have a high priest who cannot sympathize with our weaknesses, but One who has been tempted in all things just as <i>we are, yet</i> without sin.  
**4:16** Therefore let’s approach the throne of grace with confidence, so that we may receive mercy and find grace for help at the time of <i>our</i> need.  

## 5장

**5:1** For every high priest taken from among men is appointed on behalf of people in things pertaining to God, in order to offer both gifts and sacrifices for sins;  
**5:2**  <sup>①</sup>he can deal gently with the ignorant and misguided, since he himself also is <sup>②</sup>clothed in weakness;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>being able to</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., weak by nature  
**5:3** and because of it he is obligated to offer <i>sacrifices</i> for sins for himself, as well as for the people.  
**5:4** And no one takes the honor for himself, but <i>receives it</i> when he is called by God, just as Aaron also was.  
**5:5** So too Christ did not glorify Himself <i>in</i> becoming a high priest, but <i>it was</i> He who said to Him,<br>“YOU ARE MY SON,<br>TODAY I HAVE FATHERED YOU”;  
**5:6** just as He also says in another <i>passage,</i><br>“YOU ARE A PRIEST FOREVER<br>ACCORDING TO THE ORDER OF MELCHIZEDEK.”  
**5:7** In the days of <sup>①</sup>His humanity, <sup>②</sup>He offered up both prayers and pleas with loud crying and tears to the One able to save Him <sup>③</sup>from death, and He <sup>④</sup>was heard because of His devout behavior.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>His flesh</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>who having offered up</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>out of</i> <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>having been heard</i>  
**5:8** Although He was a Son, He learned obedience from the things which He suffered.  
**5:9** And having been perfected, He became the source of eternal salvation for all those who obey Him,  
**5:10** being designated by God as High Priest according to the order of Melchizedek.  
**5:11** Concerning <sup>①</sup>him we have much to say, and <i>it is</i> difficult to explain, since you have become <sup>②</sup>poor listeners.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>whom;</i> or <i>which</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>sluggish in hearing</i>  
**5:12** For though <sup>①</sup>by this time you ought to be teachers, you have need again for someone to teach you the <sup>②</sup>elementary principles of the actual words of God, and you have come to need milk and not solid food.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>because of the time</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>elements of the beginning</i>  
**5:13** For everyone who partakes <i>only</i> of milk is unacquainted with the word of righteousness, for he is an infant.  
**5:14** But solid food is for the mature, who because of practice have their senses trained to distinguish between good and evil.  

## 6장

**6:1** Therefore leaving the <sup>①</sup>elementary teaching about the <sup>②</sup>Christ, let us press on to <sup>③</sup>maturity, not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works and of faith toward God,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>word of the beginning</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., Messiah <sup>③</sup>Or <i>perfection</i>  
**6:2** of instruction about <sup>①</sup>washings and laying on of hands, and about the resurrection of the dead and eternal judgment.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>baptisms</i>  
**6:3** And this we will do, if God permits.  
**6:4** For it is impossible, in the case of those who have once been enlightened and have tasted of the heavenly gift and have been made partakers of the Holy Spirit,  
**6:5** and have tasted the good word of God and the powers of the age to come,  
**6:6** and <i>then</i> have <sup>①</sup>fallen away, to restore them again to repentance, <sup>②</sup>since they again crucify to themselves the Son of God and put Him to open shame.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>committed apostasy;</i> i.e., renounced the faith <sup>②</sup>Or <i>while</i>  
**6:7** For ground that drinks the rain which often <sup>①</sup>falls on it and produces vegetation useful to those for whose sake it is also tilled, receives a blessing from God;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>comes</i>  
**6:8** but if it yields thorns and thistles, it is worthless and <sup>①</sup>close to being cursed, and <sup>②</sup>it ends up being burned.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>near to a curse</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>whose end is for burning</i>  
**6:9** But, beloved, we are convinced of better things regarding you, and things that <sup>①</sup>accompany salvation, even though we are speaking in this way.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>belong to</i>  
**6:10** For God is not unjust so as to forget your work and the love which you have shown toward His name, by having served and by <i>still</i> serving the <sup>①</sup>saints.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**6:11** And we desire that each one of you demonstrate the same diligence <sup>①</sup>so as to realize the full assurance of hope until the end,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>to the full</i>  
**6:12** so that you will not be sluggish, but imitators of those who through faith and endurance inherit the promises.  
**6:13** For when God made the promise to Abraham, since He could swear an oath by no one greater, He swore by Himself,  
**6:14** saying, “INDEED I WILL GREATLY BLESS YOU AND I WILL GREATLY MULTIPLY YOU.”  
**6:15** And so, having patiently waited, he obtained the promise.  
**6:16** For people swear an oath by <sup>①</sup>one greater <i>than themselves,</i> and with them an oath <i>serving</i> as confirmation is an end of every dispute.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Him who is greater</i>  
**6:17**  <sup>①</sup>In the same way God, desiring even more to demonstrate to the heirs of the promise <sup>②</sup>the fact that His purpose is unchangeable, <sup>③</sup>confirmed it with an oath,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>In which</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>the unchangeableness of His</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>mediated</i>  
**6:18** so that by two unchangeable things in which it is impossible for God to lie, we who have taken refuge would have strong encouragement to hold firmly to the hope set before us.  
**6:19**  <sup>①</sup>This hope we have as an anchor of the soul, a <i>hope</i> both sure and reliable and one which enters <sup>②</sup>within the veil,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Which</i> hope <i>we have</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>inside</i>  
**6:20** where Jesus has entered as a forerunner for us, having become a high priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek.  

## 7장

**7:1** For this Melchizedek, king of Salem, priest of the Most High God, who met Abraham as he was returning from the slaughter of the kings and blessed him,  
**7:2** to whom also Abraham apportioned a tenth of all <i>the spoils,</i> was first of all, by the translation <i>of his name,</i> king of righteousness, and then also king of Salem, which is king of peace.  
**7:3** Without father, without mother, without genealogy, having neither beginning of days nor end of life, but made like the Son of God, he remains a priest perpetually.  
**7:4** Now observe how great this man was to whom Abraham, the patriarch, gave a tenth of the choicest spoils.  
**7:5** And those indeed of the sons of Levi who receive the priest’s office have a commandment <sup>①</sup>in the Law to collect <sup>②</sup>a tenth from the people, that is, from their countrymen, although they <sup>③</sup>are descended from Abraham.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>according to</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>tithes</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>have come out of the loins of</i>  
**7:6** But the one whose genealogy is not traced from them collected <sup>①</sup>a tenth from Abraham and <sup>②</sup>blessed the one who had the promises.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>tithes</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>has blessed</i>  
**7:7** But without any dispute the lesser <i>person</i> is blessed by the greater.  
**7:8** In this case mortal men receive tithes, but in that case one <i>receives them,</i> of whom it is witnessed that he lives <i>on.</i>  
**7:9** And, so to speak, through Abraham even Levi, who received tithes, has paid tithes,  
**7:10** for he was still in the loins of his <sup>①</sup>forefather when Melchizedek met him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>father</i>  
**7:11** So if perfection was through the Levitical priesthood (for on the basis of it the people received the Law), what further need <i>was there</i> for another priest to arise according to the order of Melchizedek, and not be designated according to the order of Aaron?  
**7:12** For when the priesthood is changed, of necessity there takes place a change of law also.  
**7:13** For the one about whom these things are said belongs to another tribe, from which no one has officiated at the altar.  
**7:14** For it is evident that our Lord <sup>①</sup>was descended from Judah, a tribe with reference to which Moses said nothing concerning priests.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>has arisen from</i>  
**7:15** And this is clearer still, if another priest arises according to the likeness of Melchizedek,  
**7:16** who has become <i>a priest</i> not on the basis of a law of <sup>①</sup>physical requirement, but according to the power of an indestructible life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>fleshly commandment;</i> i.e., to be a descendant of Levi  
**7:17** For it is attested <i>of Him,</i><br>“YOU ARE A PRIEST FOREVER<br>ACCORDING TO THE ORDER OF MELCHIZEDEK.”  
**7:18** For, on the one hand, there is <i>the</i> nullification of a former commandment because of its weakness and uselessness  
**7:19** (for the Law made nothing perfect); on the other hand, <i>there is the</i> introduction of a better hope, through which we come near to God.  
**7:20** And to the extent that <i>it was</i> not without an oath  
**7:21** (for they indeed became priests without an oath, but He with an oath through the One who said to Him,<br>“THE LORD HAS SWORN<br>AND WILL NOT CHANGE HIS MIND,<br>‘YOU ARE A PRIEST FOREVER’ ”);  
**7:22** by the same extent Jesus also has become the <sup>①</sup>guarantee of a better covenant.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>guarantor</i>  
**7:23**  <sup>①</sup>The <i>former</i> priests, on the one hand, existed in greater numbers because they were prevented by death from continuing;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>The greater number have become priests...</i>  
**7:24**  <sup>①</sup>Jesus, on the other hand, because He continues forever, holds His priesthood permanently.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>He</i>  
**7:25** Therefore He is also able to save <sup>①</sup>forever those who come to God through Him, since He always lives to make intercession for them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>completely</i>  
**7:26** For it was fitting for us to have such a high priest, holy, innocent, undefiled, separated from sinners, and exalted above the heavens;  
**7:27** who has no daily need, like those high priests, to offer up sacrifices, first for His own sins and then for the <i>sins</i> of the people, because He did this once for all <i>time</i> when He offered up Himself.  
**7:28** For the Law appoints men as high priests who are weak, but the word of the oath, which came after the Law, <i>appoints</i> a Son, who has been made perfect forever.  

## 8장

**8:1** Now the main point in what has been said <i>is this:</i> we have such a high priest, who has taken His seat at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens,  
**8:2** a minister <sup>①</sup>in the sanctuary and <sup>②</sup>in the true <sup>③</sup>tabernacle, which the Lord set up, not man.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>of</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>of</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>sacred tent</i>  
**8:3** For every high priest is appointed to offer both gifts and sacrifices; so it is necessary that this <i>high priest</i> also have something to offer.

**12:11** For the moment, all discipline seems not to be pleasant, but painful; yet to those who have been trained by it, afterward it yields the peaceful fruit of righteousness.  
**12:12** Therefore, <sup>①</sup>strengthen the hands that are weak and the knees that are feeble,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>make straight</i>  
**12:13** and make straight paths for your feet, so that <i>the limb</i> which is impaired may not be dislocated, but rather be healed.  
**12:14** Pursue peace with all people, and the holiness without which no one will see the Lord.  
**12:15** See to it that no one comes short of the grace of God; that no root of bitterness springing up causes trouble, and by it many become defiled;  
**12:16** that <i>there be</i> no sexually immoral or godless person like Esau, who sold his own birthright for a <i>single</i> meal.  
**12:17** For you know that even afterward, when he wanted to inherit the blessing, he was rejected, for he found no place for repentance, though he sought for it with tears.  
**12:18** For you have not come to <i>a mountain</i> that can be touched and to a blazing fire, and to darkness and gloom and whirlwind,  
**12:19** and to the blast of a trumpet and the sound of words, which <i>sound was such that</i> those who heard begged that no further word be spoken to them.  
**12:20** For they could not <sup>①</sup>cope with the command, “If even an animal touches the mountain, it shall be stoned.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>bear</i>  
**12:21** And so terrible was the sight, <i>that</i> Moses said, “I am terrified and trembling.”  
**12:22** But you have come to Mount Zion and to the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem, and to myriads of <sup>①</sup>angels,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>angels in festive gathering, and to the church</i>  
**12:23** to the general assembly and church of the firstborn who are enrolled in heaven, and to God, the Judge of all, and to the spirits of <i>the</i> righteous made perfect,  
**12:24** and to Jesus, the mediator of a new covenant, and to the sprinkled blood, which speaks better than <i>the blood</i> of Abel.  
**12:25** See to it that you do not refuse Him who is speaking. For if those did not escape when they refused him who warned <i>them</i> on earth, <sup>①</sup>much less <i>will</i> we <i>escape</i> who turn away from Him who <i>warns us</i> from heaven.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>much rather we</i> will not escape...  
**12:26** And His voice shook the earth then, but now He has promised, saying, “YET ONCE MORE I WILL SHAKE NOT ONLY THE EARTH, BUT ALSO THE HEAVEN.”  
**12:27** This <i>expression,</i> “Yet once more,” denotes the removing of those things which can be shaken, as of created things, so that those things which cannot be shaken may remain.  
**12:28** Therefore, since we receive a kingdom which cannot be shaken, let’s <sup>①</sup>show gratitude, by which we may offer to God an acceptable service with reverence and awe;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>have</i>  
**12:29** for our God is a consuming fire.  

## 13장

**13:1** Let love of the brothers <i>and</i> sisters continue.  
**13:2** Do not neglect hospitality to strangers, for by this some have entertained angels without knowing it.  
**13:3** Remember the prisoners, as though in prison with them, <i>and</i> those who are badly treated, since you yourselves also are in the body.  
**13:4** Marriage <i>is to be held</i> in honor among all, and the <i>marriage</i> bed <i>is to be</i> undefiled; for God will judge the sexually immoral and adulterers.  
**13:5** <i>Make sure that</i> your character is free from the love of money, being content with what you have; for He Himself has said, “I WILL NEVER DESERT YOU, NOR WILL I EVER ABANDON YOU,”  
**13:6** so that we confidently say,<br>“THE LORD IS MY HELPER, I WILL NOT BE AFRAID.<br>WHAT WILL MAN DO TO ME?”  
**13:7** Remember those who led you, who spoke the word of God to you; and considering the <sup>①</sup>result of their way of life, imitate their faith.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>end of their life</i>  
**13:8** Jesus Christ <i>is</i> the same yesterday and today, and forever.  
**13:9** Do not be misled by varied and strange teachings; for it is good for the heart to be strengthened by grace, not by foods, through which those who <sup>①</sup>were so occupied were not benefited.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>walked</i>  
**13:10** We have an altar from which those who serve the <sup>①</sup>tabernacle have no right to eat.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sacred tent</i>  
**13:11** For the bodies of those animals whose blood is brought into the Holy Place by the high priest <i>as an offering</i> for sin are burned outside the camp.  
**13:12** Therefore Jesus also suffered outside the gate, that He might sanctify the people through His own blood.  
**13:13** So then, let us go out to Him outside the camp, bearing His reproach.  
**13:14** For here we do not have a lasting city, but we are seeking <i>the city</i> which is to come.  
**13:15** Through Him then, let’s continually offer up a sacrifice of praise to God, that is, the fruit of lips praising His name.  
**13:16** And do not neglect doing good and sharing, for with such sacrifices God is pleased.  
**13:17** Obey your leaders and submit <i>to them</i>—for they keep watch over your souls as those who will give an account—so that they may do this with joy, not <sup>①</sup>groaning; for this <i>would be</i> unhelpful for you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sighing</i>  
**13:18** Pray for us, for we are sure that we have a good conscience, desiring to conduct ourselves honorably in all things.  
**13:19** And I urge <i>you</i> all the more to do this, so that I may be restored to you more quickly.  
**13:20** Now may the God of peace, who brought up from the dead the great Shepherd of the sheep <sup>①</sup>through the blood of the eternal covenant, <i>that is,</i> Jesus our Lord,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in</i>  
**13:21** equip you in every good thing to do His will, working in us that which is pleasing in His sight, through Jesus Christ, to whom <i>be</i> the glory forever and ever. Amen.  
**13:22** But I urge you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> <sup>①</sup>listen patiently to <sup>②</sup>this word of exhortation, for I have written to you briefly.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>listen to</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>the</i>  
**13:23** Know that our brother Timothy has been released, with whom, if he comes soon, I will see you.  
**13:24** Greet all of your leaders and all the <sup>①</sup>saints. Those from Italy greet you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**13:25** Grace be with you all.
