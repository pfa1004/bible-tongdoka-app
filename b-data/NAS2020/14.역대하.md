# 14.역대하 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** Now Solomon the son of David established himself securely over his kingdom, and the LORD his God <i>was</i> with him and exalted him greatly.  
**1:2** And Solomon spoke to all Israel, to the commanders of thousands, of hundreds, and to the judges and to every leader in all Israel, the heads of the fathers’ <i>households.</i>  
**1:3** Then Solomon and all the assembly with him went to the high place which was at Gibeon, because God’s tent of meeting was there which Moses, the servant of the LORD had made in the wilderness.  
**1:4** However, David had brought up the ark of God from Kiriath-jearim <sup>①</sup>to the place he had prepared for it, for he had pitched a tent for it in Jerusalem.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>where David had prepared for it</i>  
**1:5** Now the bronze altar which Bezalel, the son of Uri, the son of Hur, had made was there before the tabernacle of the LORD, and Solomon and the assembly sought it <i>out.</i>  
**1:6** And Solomon went up there before the LORD to the bronze altar which <i>was</i> at the tent of meeting, and offered a thousand burnt offerings on it.  
**1:7** In that night God appeared to Solomon and said to him, “Ask what I shall give you.”  
**1:8** And Solomon said to God, “You have dealt with my father David with great faithfulness, and have made me king in his place.  
**1:9** Now, LORD God, Your <sup>①</sup>promise to my father David is fulfilled, for You have made me king over a people as numerous as the dust of the earth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>word</i>  
**1:10** Now give me wisdom and knowledge, so that I may go out and come in before this people, for who can rule this great people of Yours?”  
**1:11** Then God said to Solomon, “Because this was <sup>①</sup>in your heart, and you did not ask for riches, wealth, or honor, or the life of those who hate you, nor did you even ask for long life, but you asked for yourself wisdom and knowledge so that you may rule My people over whom I have made you king,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>with</i>  
**1:12** wisdom and knowledge have been granted to you. I will also give you riches, wealth, and honor, <sup>①</sup>such as none of the kings who were before you has possessed, nor <i>will</i> those who will <sup>②</sup>come after you.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>which was not so to the kings who were before you</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>be</i>  
**1:13** So Solomon went <sup>①</sup>from the high place which was at Gibeon, from the tent of meeting, to Jerusalem, and he reigned over Israel.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>As in other ancient versions; MT <i>to</i>  
**1:14** Solomon amassed chariots and horsemen. He had 1,400 chariots and twelve thousand horsemen, and he stationed them in the chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem.  
**1:15** The king made silver and gold as plentiful in Jerusalem as stones, and he made cedars as plentiful as sycamores in the <sup>①</sup>lowland.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>shephelah</i>  
**1:16** Solomon’s horses were imported from Egypt and from Kue; the king’s traders acquired them from Kue for a price.  
**1:17** They <sup>①</sup>imported chariots from Egypt for six hundred <i>shekels</i> of silver apiece, horses for 150 apiece, and <sup>②</sup>by the same means they <sup>③</sup>exported them to all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>brought out</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>brought up and brought out</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>in the same way by their hand</i>  

## 2장

**2:1**  <sup>①</sup>Now Solomon <sup>②</sup>decided to build a house for the name of the LORD, and a <sup>③</sup>royal palace for himself.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch 1:18 in Heb <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>said</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>house for his royalty</i>  
**2:2**  <sup>①</sup>So Solomon <sup>②</sup>assigned seventy thousand men to carry loads, eighty thousand men to quarry <i>stone</i> in the mountains, and 3,600 to supervise them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch 2:1 in Heb <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>counted</i>  
**2:3** Then Solomon sent <i>word</i> to <sup>①</sup>Huram the king of Tyre, saying, “As you dealt with my father David and sent him cedars to build him a house to live in, <i>do it for me.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>In 1 Kin 5:18, <i>Hiram</i>  
**2:4** Behold, I am about to build a house for the name of the LORD my God, dedicating it to Him, to burn fragrant incense before Him and <i>to set out</i> the showbread continually, and to offer burnt offerings morning and evening, on Sabbaths, on new moons, and on the appointed feasts of the LORD our God. This <i>is to be done</i> in Israel forever.  
**2:5** The house which I am about to build <i>will be</i> great, for our God is greater than all the gods.  
**2:6** But who is able to build a house for Him, since the heavens and the highest heavens cannot contain Him? And who am I, that I should build a house for Him, except to <sup>①</sup>burn <i>incense</i> before Him?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>offer up in smoke</i>  
**2:7** Now send me a skilled man to work in gold, silver, brass, iron, and in purple, crimson, and violet <i>fabrics,</i> one who knows how to make engravings, to <i>work</i> with the skilled workers <sup>①</sup>whom I have in Judah and Jerusalem, whom my father David provided.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>who are with me</i>  
**2:8** Send me also cedar, juniper, and algum timber from Lebanon, for I know that your servants know how to cut timber of Lebanon; and indeed my servants <i>will work</i> with your servants,  
**2:9** to prepare timber in abundance for me, for the house which I am about to build <i>will be</i> great and wonderful.  
**2:10** Now behold, I will give your servants, the woodsmen who cut the timber, <sup>①</sup>twenty thousand kors of crushed wheat, twenty thousand kors of barley, <sup>②</sup>twenty thousand baths of wine, and twenty thousand baths of oil.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>About 154,000 cubic feet or 4,360 cubic meters <sup>②</sup>About 120,000 gallons or 454,249 liters  
**2:11** Then Huram, king of Tyre, <sup>①</sup>answered in a letter sent to Solomon: “Because the LORD loves His people, He has made you king over them.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>said...and he sent</i>  
**2:12** Then Huram <sup>①</sup>continued, “Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who made heaven and earth, who has given King David a wise son, <sup>②</sup>endowed with discretion and understanding, who will build a house for the LORD and a <sup>③</sup>royal palace for himself.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>said</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>knowing discretion</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>house for his royalty</i>  
**2:13** “Now then, I am sending Huram-abi, a skilled man, <sup>①</sup>endowed with understanding,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>knowing understanding</i>  
**2:14** the son of a <sup>①</sup>Danite woman and <sup>②</sup>a Tyrian father, who knows how to work in gold, silver, bronze, iron, stone, and wood, <i>and</i> in purple, violet, linen, and crimson fabrics, and <i>who knows how</i> to make all kinds of engravings and to <sup>③</sup>execute any design which is assigned to him, <i>to work</i> with your skilled workers and with <sup>④</sup>those of my lord, your father David.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>a woman of the daughters of Dan</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>whose father is a Tyrian man</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>devise any device</i> <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>skilled men</i>  
**2:15** Now then, let my lord send his servants wheat and barley, oil and wine, of which he has spoken.  
**2:16** We will cut whatever timber you need from Lebanon and bring it to you as rafts by sea to Joppa, so that you may carry it up to Jerusalem.”  
**2:17** Solomon counted all the foreigners who <i>were</i> in the land of Israel, following the <sup>①</sup>census which his father David had <sup>②</sup>taken; and 153,600 were found.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>numbering</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>numbered of them</i>  
**2:18** He appointed seventy thousand of them to carry loads and eighty thousand to quarry <i>stones</i> in the mountains, and 3,600 supervisors to make the people work.  

## 3장

**3:1** Then Solomon began to build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where <i>the LORD</i> had appeared to his father David, at the place that David had prepared on the threshing floor of <sup>①</sup>Ornan the Jebusite.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>In 2 Sam 24:18, <i>Araunah</i>  
**3:2** He began to build on the second <i>day</i> in the second month <sup>①</sup>of the fourth year of his reign.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in</i>  
**3:3** Now these are the <sup>①</sup>foundations which Solomon laid for building the house of God. The length in cubits, according to the old standard, <i>was</i> <sup>②</sup>sixty cubits, and the width, twenty cubits.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>founding of Solomon to build</i> <sup>②</sup>About 90 ft. long and 30 ft. wide or 27 m long and 9 m wide  
**3:4** The porch which was in front <i>of the house</i> was as long as the width of the house, <sup>①</sup>twenty cubits, and the height <sup>②</sup>twenty; and inside he overlaid it with pure gold.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>About 30 ft. or 9 m <sup>②</sup>As in ancient versions; MT <i>120 cubits</i> or about 180 ft. or 55 m  
**3:5** He overlaid the <sup>①</sup>main room with juniper wood and overlaid it with fine gold; and he <sup>②</sup>ornamented it with palm trees and chains.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>great house</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>put on it palm trees</i>  
**3:6** Further, he overlaid the house <sup>①</sup>with precious stones; and the gold was gold from Parvaim.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>for beauty</i>  
**3:7** He also overlaid the house with gold—the beams, the thresholds, and its walls and doors; and he carved cherubim on the walls.  
**3:8** Then he made the <sup>①</sup>room of the Most Holy Place: its length across the width of the house <i>was</i> <sup>②</sup>twenty cubits, and its width <i>was</i> twenty cubits; and he overlaid it with fine gold, <i>amounting</i> to <sup>③</sup>six hundred talents.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>house</i> <sup>②</sup>About 30 ft. or 9 m <sup>③</sup>About 23 tons or 21 metric tons  
**3:9** The weight of the nails was fifty shekels of gold. He also overlaid the upper rooms with gold.  
**3:10** Then he made two <sup>①</sup>sculptured cherubim in the room of the Most Holy Place and overlaid them with gold.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>cherubim of cast work</i>  
**3:11** The wingspan of the <sup>①</sup>cherubim <i>was</i> twenty <sup>②</sup>cubits; the wing of one, of five cubits, touched the wall of the house, and <i>its</i> other wing, of five cubits, touched the wing of the other cherub.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb plural of <i>cherub</i> <sup>②</sup>About 18 in. or 45 cm  
**3:12** The wing of the other cherub, of five cubits, touched the wall of the house; and <i>its</i> other wing, of five cubits, was attached to the wing of the <sup>①</sup>first cherub.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>other</i>  
**3:13** The wings of these cherubim extended twenty cubits, and they stood on their feet <sup>①</sup>facing the <i>main</i> room.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and their faces to</i>  
**3:14** He made the veil of violet, purple, crimson, and fine linen, and he worked cherubim into it.  
**3:15** He also made two pillars for the front of the house, thirty-five cubits <sup>①</sup>high, and the capital on the top of each <i>was</i> five cubits.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>long</i>  
**3:16** He made chains in the inner sanctuary and placed <i>them</i> on the tops of the pillars; and he made a hundred pomegranates and placed <i>them</i> on the chains.  
**3:17** He erected the pillars in front of the temple, one on the right and the other on the left, and named the one on the right Jachin and the one on the left Boaz.  

## 4장

**4:1** Then he made a bronze altar, <sup>①</sup>twenty cubits in length, <sup>②</sup>twenty cubits in width, and <sup>③</sup>ten cubits in height.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>About 30 ft. or 9 m <sup>②</sup>About 30 ft. or 9 m <sup>③</sup>About 15 ft. or 4.6 m

**4:9** Then he made the courtyard of the priests and the great courtyard, and doors for the courtyard, and overlaid their doors with bronze.  
**4:10** He put the Sea on the right side <i>of the house</i> toward the southeast.  
**4:11** Huram also made the pails, the shovels, and the bowls. So Huram finished the work that he did for King Solomon in the house of God:  
**4:12** the two pillars, the bowls and the two capitals on top of the pillars, and the two latticeworks to cover the two bowls of the capitals which were on top of the pillars,  
**4:13** and the four hundred pomegranates for the two latticeworks, two rows of pomegranates for each latticework to cover the two bowls of the capitals which were on the pillars.  
**4:14** He also made the stands and he made the basins on the stands,  
**4:15** <i>and</i> the one Sea with the twelve oxen under it.

**4:18** So Solomon made all these utensils in great quantities, for the weight of the bronze could not be determined.  
**4:19** Solomon also made all the things that <i>were</i> in the house of God: the golden altar, the tables with the bread of the Presence on them,  
**4:20** the lampstands with their lamps of pure gold, to burn in front of the inner sanctuary in the way prescribed;  
**4:21** the flowers, the lamps, and the tongs of gold, that is, of purest gold;  
**4:22** and the snuffers, the bowls, the spoons, and the firepans of pure gold; and the entrance of the house, its inner doors for the Most Holy Place and the doors of the house, <i>that is,</i> of the main room, of gold.  

## 5장

**5:1** So all the work that Solomon performed for the house of the LORD was finished. And Solomon brought in the <sup>①</sup>things that his father David had dedicated, the silver, the gold, and all the utensils, <i>and he</i> put <i>them</i> in the treasuries of the house of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>dedicated things of his</i>  
**5:2** Then Solomon assembled at Jerusalem the elders of Israel, all the heads of the tribes, <i>and</i> the leaders of the fathers’ <i>households</i> of the sons of Israel, to bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD up from the city of David, which is Zion.  
**5:3** All the men of Israel assembled themselves before the king at the feast, that is <i>in</i> the seventh month.  
**5:4** Then all the elders of Israel came, and the Levites picked up the ark.  
**5:5** They brought up the ark, the tent of meeting, and all the holy utensils that <i>were</i> in the tent. The Levitical priests brought them up.  
**5:6** And King Solomon and all the congregation of Israel who had assembled with him before the ark were sacrificing <sup>①</sup>so many sheep and oxen that they could not be counted or numbered.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>sheep...numbered for multitude</i>  
**5:7** Then the priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD to its place, into the inner sanctuary of the house, to the Most Holy Place, under the wings of the cherubim.

## 6장

**6:3** Then the king turned around and blessed all the assembly of Israel, while all the assembly of Israel was standing.

**6:6** but I have chosen Jerusalem so that My name might be there, and I have chosen David to be over My people Israel.’

**6:12** Then he stood before the altar of the LORD in the presence of all the assembly of Israel and spread out his hands.  
**6:13** For Solomon had made a bronze platform, <sup>①</sup>five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high, and had set it in the midst of the courtyard; and he stood on it, knelt on his knees in the presence of all the assembly of Israel and spread out his hands toward heaven.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>About 7.5 ft. long and wide and 4.5 ft. high or 2.3 m and 1.4 m

**6:20** that Your eye will be open toward this house day and night, toward the place of which You have said that <i>You would</i> put Your name there, to listen to the prayer which Your servant shall pray toward this place.  
**6:21** Listen to the pleadings of Your servant and of Your people Israel when they pray toward this place; hear from Your dwelling place, from heaven; hear and forgive.  
**6:22** “If someone sins against his neighbor and is made to take an oath, and he comes <i>and</i> takes an oath before Your altar in this house,
