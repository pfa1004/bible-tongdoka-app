# 50.빌립보서 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul and Timothy, bond-servants of Christ Jesus, <e>T</e>o all the <sup>①</sup>saints in Christ Jesus who are in Philippi, <sup>②</sup>including the overseers and deacons:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>with</i>  
**1:2** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:3** I thank my God in all my remembrance of you,  
**1:4** always offering prayer with joy in my every prayer for you all,  
**1:5** in view of your <sup>①</sup>participation in the gospel from the first day until now.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sharing in the preaching of the gospel</i>  
**1:6** <i>For I am</i> confident of this very thing, that He who began a good work <sup>①</sup>among you will complete it <sup>②</sup>by the day of Christ Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>until</i>  
**1:7**  <sup>①</sup>For it is only right for me to feel this way about you all, because I have you in my heart, since both in my <sup>②</sup>imprisonment and in the defense and confirmation of the gospel, you all are <sup>③</sup>partakers of grace with me.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Just as it is right</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>bonds</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>partners in</i>  
**1:8** For God is my witness, how I long for you all with the <sup>①</sup>affection of Christ Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>inward parts</i>  
**1:9** And this I pray, that your love may overflow still more and more in real knowledge and all discernment,  
**1:10** so that you may <sup>①</sup>discover the things that are excellent, that you may be sincere and blameless for the day of Christ;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>approve the things that matter</i>  
**1:11** having been filled with the fruit of righteousness which <i>comes</i> through Jesus Christ, for the glory and praise of God.  
**1:12** Now I want you to know, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> that my circumstances have turned out for the greater progress of the gospel,  
**1:13** so that my <sup>①</sup>imprisonment in <i>the cause of</i> Christ has become well known throughout the <sup>②</sup>praetorian guard and to everyone else,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>bonds</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>governor’s palace</i>  
**1:14** and that most of the brothers <i>and sisters,</i> <sup>①</sup>trusting in the Lord because of my <sup>②</sup>imprisonment, have far more courage to speak the word of God without fear.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in the Lord, trusting because of</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>bonds</i>  
**1:15** Some, to be sure, are preaching Christ even <sup>①</sup>from envy and strife, but some also <sup>②</sup>from goodwill;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>because of</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>because of</i>  
**1:16** the latter <i>do it</i> out of love, knowing that I am appointed for the defense of the gospel;  
**1:17** the former proclaim Christ out of selfish ambition <sup>①</sup>rather than from pure motives, thinking that they are causing me distress in my <sup>②</sup>imprisonment.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>not sincerely</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>bonds</i>  
**1:18** What then? Only that in every way, whether in pretense or in truth, Christ is proclaimed, and in this I rejoice. <e>B</e>ut <i>not only that,</i> I also will rejoice,  
**1:19** for I know that this will turn out for my <sup>①</sup>deliverance through your <sup>②</sup>prayers and the provision of the Spirit of Jesus Christ,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>salvation</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>supplication</i>  
**1:20** according to my eager expectation and hope, that I will not be put to shame in anything, but <i>that</i> with all boldness, Christ will even now, as always, be exalted in my body, whether by life or by death.  
**1:21** For to me, to live is Christ, and to die is gain.  
**1:22**  <sup>①</sup>But if <i>I am</i> to live <i>on</i> in the flesh, this <i>will mean</i> fruitful labor for me; and I do not know <sup>②</sup>which to choose.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>But if to live in the flesh, this will be fruitful labor for me, then I</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>what I shall choose</i>  
**1:23** But I am hard-pressed from both <i>directions,</i> having the desire to depart and be with Christ, for <i>that</i> is very much better;  
**1:24** yet to remain on in the flesh is more necessary for your sakes.  
**1:25** Convinced of this, I know that I will remain and continue with you all for your progress and joy <sup>①</sup>in the faith,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of</i>  
**1:26** so that your pride in Christ Jesus may be abundant because of me by my coming to you again.  
**1:27** Only conduct yourselves in a manner worthy of the gospel of Christ, so that whether I come and see you or remain absent, I will hear about you that you are standing firm in one spirit, with one <sup>①</sup>mind striving together for the faith of the gospel;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>soul</i>  
**1:28** and in no way alarmed by <i>your</i> opponents—which is a sign of destruction for them, but of salvation for you, and this <i>too,</i> from God.  
**1:29** For to you it has been granted for Christ’s sake, not only to believe in Him, but also to suffer on His behalf,  
**1:30** experiencing the same conflict which you saw in me, and now hear <i>to be</i> in me.  

## 2장

**2:1** Therefore if there is any encouragement in Christ, if any consolation of love, if any fellowship of the Spirit, if any <sup>①</sup>affection and compassion,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>inward parts</i>  
**2:2** make my joy complete <sup>①</sup>by being of the same mind, maintaining the same love, united in spirit, intent on one purpose.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>that you be</i>  
**2:3** Do nothing <sup>①</sup>from <sup>②</sup>selfishness or empty conceit, but with humility consider one another as more important than yourselves;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>according to</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>contentiousness</i>  
**2:4** do not <i>merely</i> look out for your own personal <i>interests,</i> but also for the <i>interests</i> of others.  
**2:5** Have this attitude <sup>①</sup>in yourselves which was also in Christ Jesus,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>among</i>  
**2:6** who, as He <i>already</i> existed in the form of God, did not consider equality with God something to be <sup>①</sup>grasped,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>seized</i>  
**2:7** but <sup>①</sup>emptied Himself <i>by</i> taking the form of a bond-servant <i>and</i> <sup>②</sup>being born in the likeness of men.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., set aside His divine rights <sup>②</sup>Or <i>having come to be</i>  
**2:8** And being found in appearance as a man, He humbled Himself by becoming obedient to the point of death: death <sup>①</sup>on a cross.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of</i>  
**2:9** For this reason also God highly exalted Him, and bestowed on Him the name which is above every name,  
**2:10** so that at the name of Jesus EVERY KNEE WILL BOW, of those who are in heaven and on earth and under the earth,  
**2:11** and <i>that</i> every tongue will confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.  
**2:12** So then, my beloved, just as you have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling;  
**2:13** for it is God who is at work in you, both to <sup>①</sup>desire and to work for <i>His</i> good pleasure.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>be willing</i>  
**2:14** Do all things without complaining or arguments;  
**2:15** so that you will <sup>①</sup>prove yourselves to be blameless and innocent, children of God above reproach in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation, among whom you <sup>②</sup>appear as <sup>③</sup>lights in the world,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>become</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>shine</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>luminaries, stars</i>  
**2:16** holding firmly the word of life, so that on the day of Christ I can take pride because I did not run in vain nor labor in vain.  
**2:17** But even if I am being poured out as a drink offering upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I rejoice and share my joy with you all.  
**2:18** You too, <i>I urge you,</i> rejoice in the same way and share your joy with me.  
**2:19** But I hope, <sup>①</sup>in the Lord Jesus, to send Timothy to you shortly, so that I also may be encouraged when I learn of your condition.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>trusting in</i>  
**2:20** For I have no one <i>else</i> of kindred spirit who will genuinely be concerned for your welfare.  
**2:21** For they all seek after their own <i>interests,</i> not those of Christ Jesus.  
**2:22** But you know of his proven character, that he served with me in the furtherance of the gospel like a child <i>serving</i> his father.  
**2:23** Therefore I hope to send him immediately, as soon as I see how things <i>go</i> with me;  
**2:24** and I trust in the Lord that I myself will also be coming shortly.  
**2:25** But I thought it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother and fellow worker and fellow soldier, who is also your <sup>①</sup>messenger and minister to my need,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>apostle</i>  
**2:26** because he was longing <sup>①</sup>for you all and was distressed because you had heard that he was sick.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms <i>to see you all</i>  
**2:27** For indeed he was sick to the point of death, but God had mercy on him, and not only on him but also on me, so that I would not have sorrow upon sorrow.  
**2:28** Therefore I have sent him all the more eagerly, so that when you see him again you may rejoice and I may be less concerned <i>about you.</i>  
**2:29** Receive him then in the Lord with all joy, and hold people like him in high regard,  
**2:30** because he came close to death <sup>①</sup>for the work of Christ, risking his life to compensate <sup>②</sup>for your absence in your service to me.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>because of</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>your deficiency of service</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** Finally, my brothers <i>and sisters,</i> rejoice in the Lord. To write the same things <i>again</i> is no trouble for me, and it is a safeguard for you.  
**3:2** Beware of the dogs, beware of the evil workers, beware of the <sup>①</sup>false circumcision;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>mutilation;</i> Gr <i>katatome</i>  
**3:3** for we are the <i>true</i> <sup>①</sup>circumcision, who worship in the Spirit of God and take pride in Christ Jesus, and put no confidence in the flesh,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gr <i>peritome</i>  
**3:4** although I myself <i>could boast as</i> having confidence even in the flesh. If anyone else thinks he is confident in the flesh, I <i>have</i> more <i>reason:</i>  
**3:5** circumcised the eighth day, of the nation of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew of Hebrews; as to the Law, a Pharisee;  
**3:6** as to zeal, a persecutor of the church; as to the righteousness which is in the Law, found blameless.  
**3:7** But whatever things were gain to me, these things I have counted as loss because of Christ.  
**3:8** More than that, I count all things to be loss <sup>①</sup>in view of the surpassing value of <sup>②</sup>knowing Christ Jesus my Lord, <sup>③</sup>for whom I have suffered the loss of all things, and count them <i>mere</i> rubbish, so that I may gain Christ,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>because of</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>the knowledge of</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>because of</i>  
**3:9** and may be found in Him, not having a righteousness of my own derived from <i>the</i> Law, but that which is through faith in Christ, the righteousness which <i>comes</i> from God on the basis of faith,  
**3:10** that I may know Him and the power of His resurrection and <sup>①</sup>the fellowship of His sufferings, being conformed to His death;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>participation in</i>  
**3:11** if somehow I may attain to the resurrection from the dead.  
**3:12** Not that I have already <sup>①</sup>grasped <i>it all</i> or have already become perfect, but I press on if I may also take hold of that <sup>②</sup>for which I was even taken hold of by Christ Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>obtained</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>because also</i>  
**3:13** Brothers <i>and sisters,</i> I do not regard myself as having taken hold of <i>it yet;</i> but one thing <i>I do:</i> forgetting what <i>lies</i> behind and reaching forward to what <i>lies</i> ahead,  
**3:14** I press on toward the goal for the prize of the upward call of God in Christ Jesus.  
**3:15** Therefore, all who are <sup>①</sup>mature, let’s have this attitude; and if in anything you have a different attitude, God will reveal that to you as well;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>perfect</i>  
**3:16** however, let’s keep <sup>①</sup>living by that same <i>standard</i> to which we have attained.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>following in line</i>  
**3:17** Brothers <i>and sisters,</i> join in following my example, and observe those who walk according to the pattern you have in us.  
**3:18** For many walk, of whom I often told you, and now tell you even as I weep, <i>that they are</i> the enemies of the cross of Christ,  
**3:19** whose end is destruction, whose god is <i>their</i> <sup>①</sup>appetite, and <i>whose</i> glory is in their shame, who have their minds on earthly things.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>belly</i>  
**3:20** For our <sup>①</sup>citizenship is in heaven, from which we also eagerly wait for a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>commonwealth</i>  
**3:21** who will transform the body of our lowly condition into conformity with <sup>①</sup>His glorious body, by the exertion of the power that He has even to subject all things to Himself.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the body of His glory</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** Therefore, my beloved brothers <i>and sisters,</i> <sup>①</sup>whom I long <i>to see,</i> my joy and crown, stand firm in the Lord in this way, my beloved.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and longed for</i>  
**4:2** I urge Euodia and I urge Syntyche to <sup>①</sup>live in harmony in the Lord.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>be of the same mind</i>  
**4:3** Indeed, true <sup>①</sup>companion, I ask you also, help these women who have shared my struggle in <i>the cause of</i> the gospel, together with Clement as well as the rest of my fellow workers, whose names are in the book of life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>yokefellow</i>  
**4:4** Rejoice in the Lord always; again I will say, rejoice!  
**4:5** Let your gentle <i>spirit</i> be known to all people. The Lord is <sup>①</sup>near.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>at hand</i>  
**4:6** Do not <sup>①</sup>be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer and pleading with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., have anxiety  
**4:7** And the peace of God, which surpasses all <sup>①</sup>comprehension, will guard your hearts and minds in Christ Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>mind</i>  
**4:8** Finally, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is right, whatever is pure, whatever is <sup>①</sup>lovely, whatever is commendable, if there is any excellence and if anything worthy of praise, think about these things.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>lovable and gracious</i>  
**4:9** As for the things you have learned and received and heard and seen in me, practice these things, and the God of peace will be with you.  
**4:10** But I rejoiced in the Lord greatly, that now at last you have revived your concern for me; indeed, you were concerned <i>before,</i> but you lacked an opportunity <i>to act.</i>  
**4:11** Not that I speak <sup>①</sup>from need, for I have learned to be <sup>②</sup>content in whatever <i>circumstances</i> I am.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>according to</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>self-sufficient</i>  
**4:12** I know how to get along with little, and I also know how to live in prosperity; in any and every <i>circumstance</i> I have learned the secret of being filled and going hungry, both of having abundance and suffering need.  
**4:13** I can do all things <sup>①</sup>through Him who strengthens me.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in</i>  
**4:14** Nevertheless, you have done well to share <i>with me</i> in my difficulty.  
**4:15** You yourselves also know, Philippians, that at the <sup>①</sup>first <i>preaching</i> of the gospel, after I left Macedonia, no church shared with me in the matter of giving and receiving except you alone;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>beginning of</i>  
**4:16** for even in Thessalonica you sent <i>a gift</i> more than once for my needs.  
**4:17** Not that I seek the gift <i>itself,</i> but I seek the <sup>①</sup>profit which increases to your account.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>fruit</i>  
**4:18** But I have received everything in full and have an abundance; I am <sup>①</sup>amply supplied, having received from Epaphroditus <sup>②</sup>what you have sent, <sup>③</sup>a fragrant aroma, an acceptable sacrifice, pleasing to God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>made full</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>the things from you</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>an odor of fragrance</i>  
**4:19** And my God will supply <sup>①</sup>all your needs according to His riches in glory in Christ Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>every need of yours</i>  
**4:20** Now to our God and Father <i>be</i> the glory <sup>①</sup>forever and ever. Amen.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>to the ages of the ages</i>  
**4:21** Greet every <sup>①</sup>saint in Christ Jesus. The brothers who are with me greet you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>holy one;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**4:22** All the <sup>①</sup>saints greet you, especially those of Caesar’s household.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>See note v 21  
**4:23** The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
