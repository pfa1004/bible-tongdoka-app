# 36.스바냐 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD which came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah:  
**1:2** “I will completely remove all <i>things</i><br>From the face of the <sup>①</sup>earth,” declares the LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>ground</i>  
**1:3** “I will remove human and animal <i>life;</i><br>I will remove the birds of the sky<br>And the fish of the sea,<br>And the <sup>①</sup>ruins along with the wicked;<br>And I will eliminate mankind from the face of the <sup>②</sup>earth,” declares the LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>stumbling blocks</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>ground</i>  
**1:4** “So I will stretch out My hand against Judah<br>And against all the inhabitants of Jerusalem.<br>And I will eliminate the remnant of Baal from this place,<br><i>And</i> the names of the idolatrous priests along with the <i>other</i> priests.  
**1:5** And those who bow down on the housetops to the heavenly <sup>①</sup>lights,<br>And those who bow down <i>and</i> swear to the LORD, but <i>also</i> swear by <sup>②</sup>Milcom,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>host;</i> i.e., sun, stars, etc. <sup>②</sup>Or <i>their king;</i> MT <i>Malcam,</i> prob. a variant spelling of Milcom, the Ammonite god  
**1:6** And those who have turned back from following the LORD,<br>And those who have not sought the LORD nor inquired of Him.”  
**1:7**  <sup>①</sup>Be silent before the Lord <sup>②</sup>GOD!<br>For the day of the LORD is near,<br>Because the LORD has prepared a sacrifice,<br>He has consecrated His guests.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Hush</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>YHWH,</i> usually rendered <i>LORD</i>  
**1:8** “Then it will come about on the day of the LORD’S sacrifice<br>That I will punish the princes, the king’s sons,<br>And all who clothe themselves with foreign garments.  
**1:9** And on that day I will punish all who leap on the <i>temple</i> threshold,<br>Who fill the house of their <sup>①</sup>lord with violence and deceit.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Lord</i>  
**1:10** And on that day,” declares the LORD,<br>“There will be the sound of a cry from the Fish Gate,<br>Wailing from the <sup>①</sup>Second Quarter,<br>And a loud crash from the hills.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., a district of Jerusalem  
**1:11** Wail, you inhabitants of the <sup>①</sup>Mortar,<br>Because all the <sup>②</sup>people of Canaan will be destroyed;<br>All who weigh out silver will be eliminated.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., a district of Jerusalem <sup>②</sup>Or <i>merchant people will</i>  
**1:12** And it will come about at that time<br>That I will search Jerusalem with lamps,<br>And I will punish the people<br>Who are <sup>①</sup>stagnant in spirit,<br>Who say in their hearts,<br>‘The LORD will not do good nor harm!’<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>thickening on their dregs</i>  
**1:13** Their wealth will become plunder,<br>And their houses desolate;<br>Yes, they will build houses but not inhabit <i>them,</i><br>And plant vineyards but not drink their wine.”  
**1:14** The great day of the LORD is near,<br>Near and coming very quickly;<br>Listen, the day of the LORD!<br> <sup>①</sup>In it the warrior cries out bitterly.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>There</i>  
**1:15** That day is a day of anger,<br>A day of trouble and distress,<br>A day of destruction and desolation,<br>A day of darkness and gloom,<br>A day of clouds and thick darkness,  
**1:16** A day of trumpet and battle cry<br>Against the fortified cities<br>And the high corner towers.  
**1:17** I will bring distress on mankind<br>So that they will walk like those who are blind,<br>Because they have sinned against the LORD;<br>And their blood will be poured out like dust,<br>And their flesh like dung.  
**1:18** Neither their silver nor their gold<br>Will be able to save them<br>On the day of the LORD’S anger;<br>And all the earth will be devoured<br>By the fire of His jealousy,<br>For He will make a complete end,<br>Indeed a horrifying one,<br>Of all the inhabitants of the earth.  

## 2장

**2:1** Gather yourselves together, yes, join together,<br>You nation without <sup>①</sup>shame,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>longing</i>  
**2:2** Before the decree <sup>①</sup>takes effect—<br>The day passes like chaff—<br>Before the burning anger of the LORD comes upon you,<br>Before the day of the LORD’S anger comes upon you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>gives birth</i>  
**2:3** Seek the LORD,<br>All you humble of the <sup>①</sup>earth<br>Who have practiced His <sup>②</sup>ordinances;<br>Seek righteousness, seek humility.<br>Perhaps you will remain hidden<br>On the day of the LORD’S anger.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>land</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>justice</i>  
**2:4** For Gaza will be abandoned,<br>And Ashkelon <i>will become</i> a desolation;<br><i>The inhabitants of</i> Ashdod will be driven out at noon,<br>And Ekron will be uprooted.  
**2:5** Woe to the inhabitants of the seacoast,<br>The nation of the <sup>①</sup>Cherethites!<br>The word of the LORD is against you,<br>Canaan, land of the Philistines;<br>And I will eliminate you<br>So that there will be no inhabitant.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., a segment of the Philistines with roots in Crete  
**2:6** So the seacoast will become grazing places,<br><i>With</i> pastures for shepherds and folds for flocks.  
**2:7** And the coast will be<br>For the remnant of the house of Judah,<br>They will drive <i>sheep</i> to pasture on it.<br>In the houses of Ashkelon they will lie down at evening;<br>For the LORD their God will care for them<br>And restore their fortunes.  
**2:8** “I have heard the taunting of Moab<br>And the abusive speech of the sons of Ammon,<br>With which they have taunted My people<br>And boasted against their territory.  
**2:9** Therefore, as I live,” declares the LORD of armies,<br>The God of Israel,<br>“Moab will assuredly be like Sodom,<br>And the sons of Ammon like Gomorrah—<br>Ground overgrown with weeds and <i>full of</i> salt mines,<br>And a permanent desolation.<br>The remnant of My people will plunder them,<br>And the remainder of My nation will inherit them.”  
**2:10** This they will have in return for their arrogance, because they have taunted and boasted against the people of the LORD of armies.  
**2:11** The LORD will be terrifying to them, for He will <sup>①</sup>starve all the gods of the earth; and all the coastlands of the nations will bow down to Him, everyone from his <i>own</i> place.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>allow to dwindle</i>  
**2:12** “You also, Ethiopians, will be slain by My sword.”  
**2:13** And He will stretch out His hand against the north<br>And eliminate Assyria,<br>And He will make Nineveh a desolation,<br>Parched like the wilderness.  
**2:14** Flocks will lie down in her midst,<br> <sup>①</sup>All animals that range in herds;<br>Both the <sup>②</sup>pelican and the hedgehog<br>Will spend their nights in <sup>③</sup>the tops of her pillars;<br> <sup>④</sup>Birds will sing in the window,<br>Devastation <i>will be</i> on the threshold;<br>For He has uncovered the cedar work.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>All</i> kinds of <i>beasts in crowds;</i> lit <i>Every</i> kind of <i>beast of a nation</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>owl;</i> or <i>jackdaw</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>her capitals</i> <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>A voice</i>  
**2:15** This is the presumptuous city<br>That dwells securely,<br>Who says in her heart,<br>“I am, and there is no one besides me.”<br>How she has become a desolation,<br>A resting place for animals!<br>Everyone who passes by her will <sup>①</sup>hiss<br><i>And</i> wave his hand <i>in contempt.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>whistle</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** Woe to her who is rebellious and defiled,<br>The oppressive city!  
**3:2** She obeyed no voice,<br>She accepted no discipline.<br>She did not trust in the LORD,<br>She did not approach her God.  
**3:3** Her leaders within her are roaring lions,<br>Her judges are wolves at evening;<br>They have no bones to gnaw in the morning.  
**3:4** Her prophets are insolent, treacherous men;<br>Her priests have profaned the sanctuary.<br>They have done violence to the Law.  
**3:5** The LORD is righteous within her;<br>He will do no injustice.<br>Every morning He brings His justice to light;<br>He <sup>①</sup>does not fail.<br>But the <sup>②</sup>criminal knows no shame.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is not missing</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>sinner</i>  
**3:6** “I have eliminated nations;<br>Their corner towers are deserted.<br>I have laid waste their streets,<br>With no one passing by;<br>Their cities have been laid waste,<br>Without a person, without an inhabitant.  
**3:7** I said, ‘You will certainly revere Me,<br>You will accept discipline.’<br>So her dwelling will not be eliminated<br><i>In accordance with</i> everything that I have stipulated for her.<br>Instead, they were eager to corrupt all their deeds.  
**3:8** “Therefore wait for Me,” declares the LORD,<br>“For the day when I rise up as a witness.<br>Indeed, My decision <i>is</i> to gather nations,<br>To assemble kingdoms,<br>To pour out on them My indignation,<br>All My burning anger;<br>For all the earth will be devoured<br>By the fire of My zeal.  
**3:9** For then I will <sup>①</sup>restore to the peoples pure lips,<br>So that all of them may call on the name of the LORD,<br>To serve Him <sup>②</sup>shoulder to shoulder.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>change</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit with <i>one shoulder</i>  
**3:10** From beyond the rivers of Ethiopia<br>My worshipers, <sup>①</sup>My dispersed ones,<br>Will bring My offerings.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the daughter of My dispersed ones</i>  
**3:11** On that day you will feel no shame<br>Because of all your deeds<br>By which you have rebelled against Me;<br>For then I will remove from your midst<br>Your proud, arrogant ones,<br>And you will never again be haughty<br>On My holy mountain.  
**3:12** But I will leave among you<br>A humble and lowly people,<br>And they will take refuge in the name of the LORD.  
**3:13** The remnant of Israel will do no wrong<br>And tell no lies,<br>Nor will a deceitful tongue<br>Be found in their mouths;<br>For they will feed and lie down<br>With no one to frighten <i>them.</i>”  
**3:14** Shout for joy, daughter of Zion!<br>Shout <i>in triumph,</i> Israel!<br>Rejoice and triumph with all <i>your</i> heart,<br>Daughter of Jerusalem!  
**3:15** The LORD has taken away <i>His</i> judgments against you,<br>He has cleared away your enemies.<br>The King of Israel, the LORD, is in your midst;<br>You will no longer fear disaster.  
**3:16** On that day it will be said to Jerusalem:<br>“Do not be afraid, Zion;<br>Do not let your hands fall limp.  
**3:17** The LORD your God is in your midst,<br>A <sup>①</sup>victorious warrior.<br>He will rejoice over you with joy,<br>He will <sup>②</sup>be quiet in His love,<br>He will rejoice over you with shouts of joy.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>A warrior</i> who <i>saves</i> <sup>②</sup>Or with some ancient versions, <i>renew</i> you <i>in</i>  
**3:18** I will gather those who are worried about the appointed feasts—<br>They <sup>①</sup>came from you, <i>Zion;</i><br><i>The</i> disgrace <i>of exile</i> is a burden on <sup>②</sup>them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>were</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>her</i>  
**3:19** Behold, I am going to deal at that time<br>With all your oppressors;<br>I will save those who limp<br>And gather the scattered,<br>And I will turn their shame into praise and fame<br>In all the earth.  
**3:20** At that time I will bring you in,<br>Even at the time when I gather you together;<br>Indeed, I will make you famous and praiseworthy<br>Among all the peoples of the earth,<br>When I restore your fortunes before your eyes,”<br>Says the LORD.
