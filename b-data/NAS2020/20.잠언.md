# 20.잠언 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel:  
**1:2** To know wisdom and instruction,<br>To discern the sayings of understanding,  
**1:3** To receive instruction in wise behavior,<br>Righteousness, justice, and integrity;  
**1:4** To give prudence to the naive,<br>To the youth knowledge and discretion,  
**1:5** A wise person will hear and increase in learning,<br>And a person of understanding will acquire wise counsel,  
**1:6** To understand a proverb and a saying,<br>The words of the wise and their riddles.  
**1:7** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge;<br>Fools despise wisdom and instruction.  
**1:8** Listen, my son, to your father’s instruction,<br>And do not ignore your mother’s teaching;  
**1:9** For they are a graceful wreath for your head<br>And necklaces for your neck.  
**1:10** My son, if sinners entice you,<br>Do not consent.  
**1:11** If they say, “Come with us,<br>Let’s lie in wait for blood,<br>Let’s ambush the innocent without cause;  
**1:12** Let’s swallow them alive like Sheol,<br>Even whole, like those who go down to the pit;  
**1:13** We will find all <i>kinds</i> of precious wealth,<br>We will fill our houses with plunder;  
**1:14** Throw in your lot <sup>①</sup>with us;<br>We will all have one money bag,”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in the midst of us</i>  
**1:15** My son, do not walk on the way with them.<br>Keep your feet from their path,  
**1:16** For their feet run to evil,<br>And they are quick to shed blood.  
**1:17** Indeed, it is <sup>①</sup>useless to spread the <i>baited</i> net<br>In the sight of any <sup>②</sup>bird;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in vain</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>possessor of wing</i>  
**1:18** But they lie in wait for their own blood;<br>They ambush their own lives.  
**1:19** Such are the ways of everyone who makes unjust gain;<br>It takes away the life of its possessors.  
**1:20** Wisdom shouts in the street,<br>She <sup>①</sup>raises her voice in the public square;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>gives</i>  
**1:21** At the head of the noisy <i>streets</i> she cries out;<br>At the entrance of the gates in the city she declares her sayings:  
**1:22** “How long, you naive ones, will you love simplistic thinking?<br>And <i>how long will</i> scoffers delight themselves in scoffing<br>And fools hate knowledge?  
**1:23** Turn to my rebuke,<br>Behold, I will pour out my spirit on you;<br>I will make my words known to you.  
**1:24** Because I called and you refused,<br>I stretched out my hand and no one paid attention;  
**1:25** And you neglected all my advice<br>And did not want my rebuke;  
**1:26** I will also laugh at your disaster;<br>I will mock when your dread comes,  
**1:27** When your dread comes like a storm<br>And your disaster comes like a whirlwind,<br>When distress and anguish come upon you.  
**1:28** Then they will call on me, but I will not answer;<br>They will seek me diligently but will not find me,  
**1:29** Because they hated knowledge<br>And did not choose the fear of the LORD.  
**1:30** They did not accept my advice,<br>They disdainfully rejected every rebuke from me.  
**1:31** So they shall eat of the fruit of their own way,<br>And be filled with their own schemes.  
**1:32** For the faithlessness of the naive will kill them,<br>And the complacency of fools will destroy them.  
**1:33** But whoever listens to me will live securely<br>And will be at ease from the dread of evil.”  

## 2장

**2:1** My son, if you will receive my words<br>And treasure my commandments within you,  
**2:2** Make your ear attentive to wisdom;<br>Incline your heart to understanding.  
**2:3** For if you cry out for insight,<br><i>And</i> <sup>①</sup>raise your voice for understanding;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>give</i>  
**2:4** If you seek her as silver<br>And search for her as for hidden treasures;  
**2:5** Then you will understand the fear of the LORD,<br>And discover the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For the LORD gives wisdom;<br>From His mouth <i>come</i> knowledge and understanding.  
**2:7** He stores up sound wisdom for the upright;<br><i>He is</i> a shield to those who walk in integrity,  
**2:8** Guarding the paths of justice,<br>And He watches over the way of His godly ones.  
**2:9** Then you will discern righteousness, justice,<br>And integrity, <i>and</i> every good path.  
**2:10** For wisdom will enter your heart,<br>And knowledge will be delightful to your soul;  
**2:11** Discretion will watch over you,<br>Understanding will guard you,  
**2:12** To rescue you from the way of evil,<br>From a person who speaks perverse things;  
**2:13** <i>From</i> those who leave the paths of uprightness<br>To walk in the ways of darkness;  
**2:14** Who delight in doing evil<br><i>And</i> rejoice in the perversity of evil;  
**2:15** Whose paths are crooked,<br>And who are devious in their ways;  
**2:16** To rescue you from the strange woman,<br>From the foreign woman who flatters with her words,  
**2:17** Who leaves the companion of her youth<br>And forgets the covenant of her God;  
**2:18** For her house sinks down to death,<br>And her tracks <i>lead</i> to the <sup>①</sup>dead;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>departed spirits</i>  
**2:19** None who go to her return,<br>Nor do they reach the paths of life.  
**2:20** So you will walk in the way of good people<br>And keep to the paths of the righteous.  
**2:21** For the upright will live in the land,<br>And the blameless will remain in it;  
**2:22** But the wicked will be eliminated from the land,<br>And the treacherous will be torn away from it.  

## 3장

**3:1** My son, do not forget my <sup>①</sup>teaching,<br>But have your heart comply with my commandments;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>law</i>  
**3:2** For length of days and years of life<br>And peace they will add to you.  
**3:3** Do not let kindness and truth leave you;<br>Bind them around your neck,<br>Write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**3:4** So you will find favor and a good <sup>①</sup>reputation<br>In the sight of God and man.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>understanding</i>  
**3:5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart<br>And do not lean on your own understanding.  
**3:6** In all your ways acknowledge Him,<br>And He will make your paths straight.  
**3:7** Do not be wise in your own eyes;<br>Fear the LORD and turn away from evil.  
**3:8** It will be healing to your <sup>①</sup>body<br>And refreshment to your bones.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>navel</i>  
**3:9** Honor the LORD from your wealth,<br>And from the first of all your produce;  
**3:10** Then your barns will be filled with plenty,<br>And your vats will overflow with new wine.  
**3:11** My son, do not reject the <sup>①</sup>discipline of the LORD<br>Or loathe His rebuke,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>instruction</i>  
**3:12** For whom the LORD loves He disciplines,<br><i>Just</i> as a father <i>disciplines</i> the son in whom he delights.  
**3:13** Blessed is a person who finds wisdom,<br>And one who obtains understanding.  
**3:14** For <sup>①</sup>her profit is better than the profit of silver,<br>And her produce better than gold.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., the profit from Wisdom (personified)  
**3:15** She is more precious than <sup>①</sup>jewels,<br>And nothing you desire compares with her.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>corals</i>  
**3:16**  <sup>①</sup>Long life is in her right hand;<br>In her left hand are riches and honor.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Length of days</i>  
**3:17** Her ways are pleasant ways,<br>And all her paths are peace.  
**3:18** She is a tree of life to those who take hold of her,<br>And happy are those who hold on to her.  
**3:19** The LORD founded the earth by wisdom,<br>He established the heavens by understanding.  
**3:20** By His knowledge the ocean depths were burst open,<br>And the clouds drip with dew.  
**3:21** My son, <i>see that</i> they do not escape from your sight;<br>Comply with sound wisdom and discretion,  
**3:22** And they will be life to your soul<br>And adornment to your neck.  
**3:23** Then you will walk in your way securely,<br>And your foot will not stumble.  
**3:24** When you lie down, you will not be afraid;<br>When you lie down, your sleep will be sweet.  
**3:25** Do not be afraid of sudden danger,<br>Nor of trouble from the wicked when it comes;  
**3:26** For the LORD will be <sup>①</sup>your confidence,<br>And will keep your foot from being caught.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>at your side</i>  
**3:27** Do not withhold good from <sup>①</sup>those to whom it is due,<br>When it is in your power to do <i>it.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>its owners</i>  
**3:28** Do not say to your neighbor, “Go, and come back,<br>And tomorrow I will give <i>it to you,</i>”<br>When you have it with you.  
**3:29** Do not devise harm against your neighbor,<br>While he lives securely beside you.  
**3:30** Do not contend with a person for no reason,<br>If he has done you no harm.  
**3:31** Do not envy a violent person,<br>And do not choose any of his ways.  
**3:32** For the devious are an abomination to the LORD;<br>But <sup>①</sup>He is intimate with the upright.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>His private counsel is</i>  
**3:33** The curse of the LORD is on the house of the wicked,<br>But He blesses the home of the righteous.  
**3:34** Though He scoffs at the scoffers,<br>Yet He gives grace to the needy.  
**3:35** The wise will inherit honor,<br>But fools <sup>①</sup>increase dishonor.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>raise high</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** Listen, <i>my</i> sons, to the instruction of a father,<br>And pay attention so that you may <sup>①</sup>gain understanding,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>know</i>  
**4:2** For I give you good teaching;<br>Do not abandon my <sup>①</sup>instruction.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>law</i>  
**4:3** When I was a son to my father,<br>Tender and the only son in the sight of my mother,  
**4:4** He taught me and said to me,<br>“Let your heart take hold of my words;<br>Keep my commandments and live;  
**4:5** Acquire wisdom! Acquire understanding!<br>Do not forget nor turn away from the words of my mouth.  
**4:6** Do not abandon her, and she will guard you;<br>Love her, and she will watch over you.  
**4:7** The <sup>①</sup>beginning of wisdom <i>is:</i> Acquire wisdom;<br>And with all your possessions, acquire understanding.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>primary thing is wisdom</i>  
**4:8** Prize her, and she will exalt you;<br>She will honor you if you embrace her.  
**4:9** She will place on your head a garland of grace;<br>She will present you with a crown of beauty.”  
**4:10** Listen, my son, and accept my sayings,<br>And the years of your life will be many.  
**4:11** I have instructed you in the way of wisdom;<br>I have led you in upright paths.  
**4:12** When you walk, your steps will not be hampered;<br>And if you run, you will not stumble.  
**4:13** Take hold of instruction; do not let go.<br>Guard her, for she is your life.  
**4:14** Do not enter the path of the wicked<br>And do not proceed in the way of evil people.  
**4:15** Avoid it, do not pass by it;<br>Turn away from it and pass on.  
**4:16** For they cannot sleep unless they do evil;<br>And <sup>①</sup>they are robbed of sleep unless they make <i>someone</i> stumble.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>their sleep is robbed</i>  
**4:17** For they eat the bread of wickedness,<br>And drink the wine of violence.  
**4:18** But the path of the righteous is like the light of dawn<br>That shines brighter and brighter until the full day.  
**4:19** The way of the wicked is like darkness;<br>They do not know over what they <sup>①</sup>stumble.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>may stumble</i>  
**4:20** My son, pay attention to my words;<br>Incline your ear to my sayings.  
**4:21** They are not to escape from your sight;<br>Keep them in the midst of your heart.  
**4:22** For they are life to those who find them,<br>And healing to all <sup>①</sup>their body.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>his</i>  
**4:23** Watch over your heart with all diligence,<br>For from it <i>flow</i> the springs of life.  
**4:24** Rid yourself of a deceitful mouth<br>And keep devious <sup>①</sup>speech far from you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>lips</i>  
**4:25** Let your eyes look directly ahead<br>And let your <sup>①</sup>gaze be fixed straight in front of you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>eyelids</i>  
**4:26**  <sup>①</sup>Watch the path of your feet,<br>And all your ways will be established.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., prob. stay on the path  
**4:27** Do not turn to the right or to the left;<br>Turn your foot from evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** My son, pay attention to my wisdom,<br>Incline your ear to my understanding,  
**5:2** So that you may maintain discretion<br>And your lips may comply with knowledge.  
**5:3** For the lips of an <sup>①</sup>adulteress drip honey,<br>And her <sup>②</sup>speech is smoother than oil;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>strange woman</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>palate</i>  
**5:4** But in the end she is bitter as wormwood,<br>Sharp as a two-edged sword.  
**5:5** Her feet go down to death,<br>Her steps take hold of Sheol.  
**5:6**  <sup>①</sup>She does not ponder the path of life;<br>Her ways are unstable, she does not know <i>it.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>That she not watch</i>  
**5:7** Now then, <i>my</i> sons, listen to me<br>And do not turn away from the words of my mouth.  
**5:8** Keep your way far from her,<br>And do not go near the door of her house,  
**5:9** Otherwise you will give your vigor to others,<br>And your years to the cruel one;  
**5:10** And strangers will be filled with your strength,<br>And your hard-earned possessions <i>will go</i> to the house of a foreigner;  
**5:11** And you will groan <sup>①</sup>in <sup>②</sup>the end,<br>When your flesh and your body are consumed;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>at</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>your end</i>  
**5:12** And you say, “How I hated instruction!<br>And my heart disdainfully rejected rebuke!  
**5:13** I did not listen to the voice of my teachers,<br>Nor incline my ear to my instructors!  
**5:14** I was almost in total ruin<br>In the midst of the assembly and congregation.”  
**5:15** Drink water from your own cistern,<br>And <sup>①</sup>fresh water from your own well.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>flowing</i>  
**5:16** Should your springs overflow into the street,<br>Streams of water in the public squares?  
**5:17** Let them be yours alone,<br>And not for strangers with you.  
**5:18** Let your fountain be blessed,<br>And rejoice in the wife of your youth.  
**5:19** <i>Like</i> a loving doe and a graceful mountain goat,<br>Let her breasts satisfy you at all times;<br>Be <sup>①</sup>exhilarated always with her love.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>intoxicated</i>  
**5:20** For why should you, my son, be exhilarated with an <sup>①</sup>adulteress,<br>And embrace the breasts of a foreigner?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>strange woman</i>  
**5:21** For the ways of everyone are before the eyes of the LORD,<br>And He observes all his paths.  
**5:22** His own wrongdoings will trap the wicked,<br>And he will be held by the ropes of his sin.  
**5:23** He will die for lack of instruction,<br>And in the greatness of his foolishness he will go astray.  

## 6장

**6:1** My son, if you have become a guarantor for your neighbor,<br><i>Or</i> have given a handshake for a stranger,  
**6:2** <i>If</i> you have been ensnared by the words of your mouth,<br><i>Or</i> caught by the words of your mouth,  
**6:3** Then do this, my son, and save yourself:<br>Since you have come into the <sup>①</sup>hand of your neighbor,<br>Go, humble yourself, and be urgent with your neighbor <i>to free yourself.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>palm</i>  
**6:4** Give no sleep to your eyes,<br>Nor slumber to your eyelids;  
**6:5** Save yourself like a gazelle from <i>the hunter’s</i> hand,<br>And like a bird from the hand of the fowler.  
**6:6** Go to the ant, you <sup>①</sup>lazy one,<br>Observe its ways and be wise,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>idle one</i>  
**6:7** Which, having no chief,<br>Officer, or ruler,  
**6:8** Prepares its food in the summer<br><i>And</i> gathers its provision in the harvest.  
**6:9** How long will you lie down, you <sup>①</sup>lazy one?<br>When will you arise from your sleep?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>idle one</i>  
**6:10** “A little sleep, a little slumber,<br>A little folding of the hands to <sup>①</sup>rest,”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>lie down</i>  
**6:11** Then your poverty will come in like a <sup>①</sup>drifter,<br>And your need like <sup>②</sup>an armed man.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>one who moves about</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>a man with a shield</i>  
**6:12** A worthless person, a wicked man,<br>Is one who walks with a perverse mouth,  
**6:13** Who winks with his eyes, who <sup>①</sup>signals with his feet,<br>Who <sup>②</sup>points with his fingers;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>scrapes</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>instructs with</i>  
**6:14** Who, <i>with</i> perversion in his heart, continually devises evil,<br>Who <sup>①</sup>spreads strife.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>sends out</i>  
**6:15** Therefore his disaster will come suddenly;<br>Instantly he will be broken and there will be no healing.  
**6:16** There are six things that the LORD hates,<br>Seven that are an abomination <sup>①</sup>to Him:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of His soul</i>  
**6:17** Haughty eyes, a lying tongue,<br>And hands that shed innocent blood,  
**6:18** A heart that devises wicked plans,<br>Feet that run rapidly to evil,  
**6:19** A false witness <i>who</i> declares lies,<br>And one who <sup>①</sup>spreads strife among brothers.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>sends out</i>  
**6:20** My son, comply with the commandment of your father,<br>And do not ignore the <sup>①</sup>teaching of your mother;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>law</i>  
**6:21** Bind them continually on your heart;<br>Tie them around your neck.  
**6:22** When you walk, <sup>①</sup>they will guide you;<br>When you sleep, <sup>②</sup>they will watch over you;<br>And when you awake, <sup>③</sup>they will talk to you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>she</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>she</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>she</i>  
**6:23** For the commandment is a lamp and the <sup>①</sup>teaching is light;<br>And rebukes for discipline are the way of life<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>law</i>  
**6:24** To keep you from the evil woman,<br>From the smooth tongue of the foreign woman.  
**6:25** Do not desire her beauty in your heart,<br>Nor let her capture you with her eyelids.  
**6:26** For the price of a prostitute <i>reduces one</i> to a loaf of bread,<br>And <sup>①</sup>an adulteress hunts for a precious life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>a man’s wife</i>  
**6:27** Can anyone take fire in his lap<br>And his clothes not be burned?  
**6:28** Or can a person walk on hot coals<br>And his feet not be scorched?  
**6:29** So is the one who goes in to his neighbor’s wife;<br>Whoever touches her will not <sup>①</sup>go unpunished.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>be innocent</i>  
**6:30** <i>People</i> do not despise a thief if he steals<br>To satisfy <sup>①</sup>himself when he is hungry;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>his soul</i>  
**6:31** But when he is found, he must repay seven times <i>as much;</i><br>He must give up all the property of his house.  
**6:32** One who commits adultery with a woman is lacking <sup>①</sup>sense;<br>He who would destroy <sup>②</sup>himself commits it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>heart</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>his soul</i>  
**6:33** He will find wounds and disgrace,<br>And his shame will not be removed.  
**6:34** For jealousy <sup>①</sup>enrages a man,<br>And he will not have compassion on the day of vengeance.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is the rage of</i>  
**6:35** He will not <sup>①</sup>accept any settlement,<br>Nor will he be <sup>②</sup>satisfied though you make <i>it</i> a large gift.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>lift up the face of any</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>willing</i>  

## 7장

**7:1** My son, keep my words<br>And treasure my commandments within you.  
**7:2** Keep my commandments and live,<br>And my <sup>①</sup>teaching as the <sup>②</sup>apple of your eye.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>law</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>pupil</i>  
**7:3** Bind them on your fingers;<br>Write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,”<br>And call understanding <i>your</i> intimate friend,  
**7:5** So that they may keep you from an <sup>①</sup>adulteress,<br>From the foreigner who flatters with her words.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>strange woman</i>  
**7:6** For at the window of my house<br>I looked out through my lattice,  
**7:7** And I saw among the naive,<br><i>And</i> discerned among the <sup>①</sup>youths<br>A young man lacking <sup>②</sup>sense,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>sons</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>heart</i>  
**7:8** Passing through the street near her corner;<br>And he walks along the way to her house,  
**7:9** In the twilight, in the <sup>①</sup>evening,<br>In the <sup>②</sup>middle of the night and the darkness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>evening of the day</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>pupil</i> (of the eye)  
**7:10** And behold, a woman <i>comes</i> to meet him,<br>Dressed as a prostitute and cunning of heart.  
**7:11** She is boisterous and rebellious,<br>Her feet do not remain at home;  
**7:12** <i>She is</i> now in the streets, now in the public squares,<br>And lurks by every corner.  
**7:13** So she seizes him and kisses him,<br> <sup>①</sup>And with a brazen face she says to him:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>She makes bold her face and says</i>  
**7:14** “ <sup>①</sup>I was due to offer peace offerings;<br>Today I have paid my vows.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Sacrifices of peace offerings are with me</i>  
**7:15** Therefore I have come out to meet you,<br>To seek your presence diligently, and I have found you.  
**7:16** I have spread my couch with coverings,<br>With colored linens of Egypt.  
**7:17** I have sprinkled my bed<br>With myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.
