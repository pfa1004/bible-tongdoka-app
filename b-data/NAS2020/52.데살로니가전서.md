## 1장

**1:1** Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, <e>T</e>o the church of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ: Grace to you and peace.  
**1:2** We always give thanks to God for all of you, making mention <i>of you</i> in our prayers;  
**1:3** constantly keeping in mind your work of faith and labor of love and <sup>①</sup>perseverance of hope <sup>②</sup>in our Lord Jesus Christ in the presence of our God and Father,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>steadfastness</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>of</i>  
**1:4** knowing, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> beloved by God, <i>His</i> choice of you;  
**1:5** for our gospel did not come to you in word only, but also in power and in the Holy Spirit and with full conviction; just as you know what kind of men we proved to be among you for your sakes.  
**1:6** You also became imitators of us and of the Lord, having received the word during great affliction with the joy of the Holy Spirit,  
**1:7** so that you became an example to all the believers in Macedonia and Achaia.  
**1:8** For the word of the Lord has sounded forth from you, not only in Macedonia and Achaia, but in every place <i>the news of</i> your faith toward God has gone out, so that we have no need to say anything.  
**1:9** For they themselves report about us <sup>①</sup>as to the kind of reception we had with you, and how you turned to God from <sup>②</sup>idols to serve <sup>③</sup>a living and true God,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>what kind of</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>the idols</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>the</i>  
**1:10** and to wait for His Son from <sup>①</sup>heaven, whom He raised from the dead, <i>that is,</i> Jesus who rescues us from the wrath to come.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the heavens</i>  

## 2장

**2:1** For you yourselves know, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> that our <sup>①</sup>reception among you was not in vain,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>coming to you</i>  
**2:2** but after we had already suffered and been treated abusively in Philippi, as you know, we had the boldness in our God to speak to you the gospel of God <sup>①</sup>amid much <sup>②</sup>opposition.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>during</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>struggle;</i> or <i>conflict</i>  
**2:3** For our exhortation does not <i>come</i> from error or impurity or <sup>①</sup>by way of deceit;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in deceit</i>  
**2:4** but just as we have been approved by God to be entrusted with the gospel, so we speak, not intending to please people, but <i>to please</i> God, who <sup>①</sup>examines our hearts.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>approves</i>  
**2:5** For we never came <sup>①</sup>with flattering speech, as you know, nor with a pretext <sup>②</sup>for greed—God is <i>our</i> witness—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in a word of flattery</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., to conceal greed  
**2:6** nor did we seek honor from people, either from you or from others, though <sup>①</sup>we could have <sup>②</sup>asserted our authority as apostles of Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>being able to</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>been burdensome as</i>  
**2:7** But we <sup>①</sup>proved to be <sup>②</sup>gentle <sup>③</sup>among you. As a nursing <i>mother</i> <sup>④</sup>tenderly cares for her own children,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>became gentle</i> <sup>②</sup>Three early mss <i>infants</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>in the midst of you</i> <sup>④</sup>Or <i>cherishes</i>  
**2:8** in the same way we had a fond affection for you and were delighted to share with you not only the gospel of God, but also our own <sup>①</sup>lives, because you had become <sup>②</sup>very dear to us.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>souls</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>beloved</i>  
**2:9** For you recall, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> our labor and hardship: <i>it was by</i> working night and day so as not to be a burden to any of you, <i>that</i> we proclaimed to you the gospel of God.  
**2:10** You are witnesses, and <i>so is</i> God, <i>of</i> how devoutly and rightly and blamelessly we <sup>①</sup>behaved toward you <sup>②</sup>believers;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>became</i> <sup>②</sup>Or who <i>believe</i>  
**2:11** just as you know how <i>we were</i> exhorting and encouraging and imploring each one of you as a father <i>would</i> his own children,  
**2:12** so that you would walk in a manner worthy of the God who calls you into His own kingdom and glory.  
**2:13** For this reason we also constantly thank God that when you received the word of God which you heard from us, you accepted <i>it</i> not <i>as</i> the word of <i>mere</i> men, but as what it really is, the word of God, which also is at work in you who believe.  
**2:14** For you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> became imitators of the churches of God in Christ Jesus that are in Judea, for you also endured the same sufferings at the hands of your own countrymen, even as they <i>did</i> from the Jews,  
**2:15** who both killed the Lord Jesus and the prophets, and <sup>①</sup>drove us out. <sup>②</sup>They are not pleasing to God, <sup>③</sup>but hostile to all people,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>persecuted us</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>and</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>and</i>  
**2:16** hindering us from speaking to the Gentiles so that they may be saved; with the result that they always <sup>①</sup>reach the limit of their sins. But wrath has come upon them <sup>②</sup>fully.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>complete their</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>forever;</i> lit <i>to</i> the <i>end</i>  
**2:17** But we, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> having been orphaned from you <i>by absence</i> for a <sup>①</sup>short while—in <sup>②</sup>person, not in <sup>③</sup>spirit—were all the more eager with great desire to see your face.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>occasion of an hour</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>face</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>heart</i>  
**2:18**  <sup>①</sup>For we wanted to come to you—I, Paul, <sup>②</sup>more than once—and Satan hindered us.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Because</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>both once and twice</i>  
**2:19** For who <i>is</i> our hope, or joy or crown of pride, in the presence of our Lord Jesus at His <sup>①</sup>coming? Or <i>is it</i> not indeed you?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>presence</i>  
**2:20** For you are our glory and joy.  

## 3장

**3:1** Therefore, when we could no longer endure <i>it,</i> we thought it best to be left behind, alone at Athens,  
**3:2** and we sent Timothy, our brother and God’s fellow worker in the gospel of Christ, to strengthen and encourage you for the benefit of your faith,  
**3:3** so that no one would be <sup>①</sup>disturbed by these afflictions. For you yourselves know that we have been destined for this.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>deceived</i>  
**3:4** For even when we were with you, we <i>kept</i> telling you in advance that we were going to suffer affliction; <sup>①</sup>and so it happened, <sup>②</sup>as you know.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>just as</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>and</i>  
**3:5** For this reason, when I could no longer endure <i>it,</i> I also sent to <sup>①</sup>find out about your faith, for fear that the tempter might have tempted you, and our labor would be for nothing.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>to know, to ascertain</i>  
**3:6** But now that Timothy has come to us from you, and has brought us good news of your faith and love, and that you always think kindly of us, longing to see us just as we also <i>long to see</i> you,  
**3:7** for this reason, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> in all our distress and affliction we were comforted about you through your faith;  
**3:8** for now we <i>really</i> live, if you stand firm in the Lord.  
**3:9** For what thanks can we give to God for you in return for all the joy with which we rejoice because of you before our God,  
**3:10** as we keep praying most earnestly night and day that we may see your faces, and may complete what is lacking in your faith?  
**3:11** Now may our God and Father Himself, and our Lord Jesus, direct our way to you;  
**3:12** and may the Lord cause you to increase and overflow in love for one another, and for all people, just as we also <i>do</i> for you;  
**3:13** so that He may establish your hearts blameless in holiness before our God and Father at the <sup>①</sup>coming of our Lord Jesus with all His <sup>②</sup>saints.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>presence</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  

## 4장

**4:1** Finally then, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> we request and urge you in the Lord Jesus, that as you received <i>instruction</i> from us as to how you ought to <sup>①</sup>walk and please God (just as you actually do <sup>②</sup>walk), that you excel <i>even</i> more.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>conduct yourselves</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>conduct yourselves</i>  
**4:2** For you know what instructions we gave you <sup>①</sup>by <i>the authority of</i> the Lord Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>through the Lord</i>  
**4:3** For this is the will of God, your sanctification; <i>that is,</i> that you abstain from sexual immorality;  
**4:4** that each of you know how to <sup>①</sup>possess his own <sup>②</sup>vessel in sanctification and honor,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>acquire</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., body; or wife  
**4:5** not in <sup>①</sup>lustful passion, like the Gentiles who do not know God;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>passion of lust</i>  
**4:6** <i>and</i> that no one violate the rights and take advantage of his brother <i>or sister</i> in the matter, because the Lord is <i>the</i> avenger in all these things, just as we also told you previously and solemnly warned <i>you.</i>  
**4:7** For God has not called us for <sup>①</sup>impurity, but in sanctification.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., moral corruption  
**4:8** Therefore, the one who rejects <i>this</i> is not rejecting man, but the God who gives His Holy Spirit to you.  
**4:9** Now as to the love of the brothers <i>and</i> sisters, you have no need for <i>anyone</i> to write to you, for you yourselves are taught by God to love one another;  
**4:10** for indeed you practice it toward all the brothers <i>and sisters</i> who are in all Macedonia. But we urge you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> to excel <i>even</i> more,  
**4:11** and to make it your ambition to lead a quiet life and attend to your own <i>business</i> and work with your hands, just as we instructed you,  
**4:12** so that you will <sup>①</sup>behave properly toward outsiders and <sup>②</sup>not be in any need.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>walk</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>have need of nothing</i>  
**4:13** But we do not want you to be uninformed, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> about those who <sup>①</sup>are asleep, so that you will not grieve as indeed the rest <i>of mankind do,</i> who have no hope.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., have died  
**4:14** For if we believe that Jesus died and rose <i>from the dead,</i> so also God will bring with Him those who have fallen asleep <sup>①</sup>through Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., as believers  
**4:15** For we say this to you by the word of the Lord, that we who are alive <sup>①</sup>and remain until the coming of the Lord will not precede those who have fallen asleep.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>who</i>  
**4:16** For the Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a <sup>①</sup>shout, with the voice of <i>the</i> archangel and with the trumpet of God, and the dead in Christ will rise first.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>cry of command</i>  
**4:17** Then we who are alive, who remain, will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air, and so we will always be with the Lord.  
**4:18** Therefore, <sup>①</sup>comfort one another with these words.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>encourage</i>  

## 5장

**5:1** Now as to the periods and <sup>①</sup>times, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> you have no need <i>of anything</i> to be written to you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., the end times  
**5:2** For you yourselves know full well that the day of the Lord is coming just like a thief in the night.  
**5:3** While they are saying, “Peace and safety!” then sudden destruction <sup>①</sup>will come upon them like labor pains upon a pregnant woman, and they will not escape.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is coming</i>  
**5:4** But you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> are not in darkness, so that the day would overtake you <sup>①</sup>like a thief;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms <i>like thieves</i>  
**5:5** for you are all sons of light and sons of day. We are not of night nor of darkness;  
**5:6** so then, let’s not sleep as <sup>①</sup>others do, but let’s be alert and <sup>②</sup>sober.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the rest</i> (of mankind) <sup>②</sup>Or <i>self-controlled</i>  
**5:7** For those who sleep, sleep at night, and those who are drunk, get drunk at night.  
**5:8** But since we are of <i>the</i> day, let’s be <sup>①</sup>sober, having put on the breastplate of faith and love, and as a helmet, the hope of salvation.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>self-controlled</i>  
**5:9** For God has not destined us for wrath, but for obtaining salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,  
**5:10** who died for us, so that whether we are awake or asleep, we will live together with Him.  
**5:11** Therefore, <sup>①</sup>encourage one another and build one another up, just as you also are doing.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>comfort</i>  
**5:12** But we ask you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> to recognize those who diligently labor among you and <sup>①</sup>are in leadership over you in the Lord, and give you <sup>②</sup>instruction,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>care for you</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>admonition</i>  
**5:13** and that you regard them very highly in love because of their work. Live in peace with one another.  
**5:14** We urge you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> admonish the <sup>①</sup>unruly, encourage the fainthearted, help the weak, be patient with everyone.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>undisciplined</i>  
**5:15** See that no one repays another with evil for evil, but always seek what is good for one another and for all people.  
**5:16** Rejoice always,  
**5:17** pray without ceasing,  
**5:18** in everything give thanks; for this is the will of God for you in Christ Jesus.  
**5:19** Do not quench the Spirit,  
**5:20** do not utterly reject <sup>①</sup>prophecies,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>prophetic gifts</i>  
**5:21** but examine everything; hold firmly to that which is good,  
**5:22** abstain from every <sup>①</sup>form of evil.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>appearance</i>  
**5:23** Now may the God of peace Himself sanctify you entirely; and may your spirit and soul and body be kept complete, without blame at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**5:24** Faithful is He who calls you, and He also will do it.  
**5:25** Brothers <i>and sisters,</i> pray for us <sup>①</sup>.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Two early mss add <i>also</i>  
**5:26** Greet all the brothers <i>and sisters</i> with a holy kiss.  
**5:27** I put you under oath by the Lord to have this letter read to all the brothers <i>and sisters.</i>  
**5:28** <i>May</i> the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <i>be</i> with you.
