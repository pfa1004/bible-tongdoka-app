# 3.레위기 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** Now the LORD called to Moses and spoke to him from the tent of meeting, saying,  
**1:2** “Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, ‘When anyone of you brings an offering to the LORD, you shall bring your offering of livestock from the herd or the flock.  
**1:3** If his offering is a burnt offering from the herd, he shall offer a male without defect; he shall offer it at the doorway of the tent of meeting, so that he may be accepted before the LORD.  
**1:4** And he shall lay his hand on the head of the burnt offering, so that it may be accepted for him to make atonement on his behalf.  
**1:5** Then he shall <sup>①</sup>slaughter the <sup>②</sup>bull before the LORD; and Aaron’s sons the priests shall offer up the blood and sprinkle the blood around on the altar that is at the doorway of the tent of meeting.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., following a prescribed ritual, and so through ch 22 <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>son of the herd</i>  
**1:6** He shall then skin the burnt offering and cut it into its pieces.  
**1:7** And the sons of Aaron the priest shall put fire on the altar and arrange wood on the fire.  
**1:8** Then Aaron’s sons the priests shall arrange the pieces, <i>with</i> the head and the suet, on the wood which is on the fire that is on the altar.  
**1:9** Its entrails, however, and its legs he shall wash with water. And the priest shall offer all of it up in smoke on the altar as a burnt offering, an offering by fire as a soothing aroma to the LORD.  
**1:10** ‘But if his offering is from the flock, <i>either</i> from the sheep or from the goats, as a burnt offering, he shall offer a male without defect.  
**1:11** And he shall slaughter it on the side of the altar northward before the LORD, and Aaron’s sons the priests shall sprinkle its blood around on the altar.  
**1:12** He shall then cut it into its pieces with its head and its suet, and the priest shall arrange them on the wood which is on the fire that is on the altar.  
**1:13** The entrails, however, and the legs he shall wash with water. And the priest shall offer all of it, and offer it up in smoke on the altar; it is a burnt offering, an offering by fire of a soothing aroma to the LORD.  
**1:14** ‘But if his offering to the LORD is a burnt offering of birds, then he shall bring his offering from the turtledoves or from young doves.  
**1:15** The priest shall bring it to the altar, and pinch off its head, and offer it up in smoke on the altar; and its blood is to be drained out on the side of the altar.  
**1:16** He shall also remove its craw with its feathers and throw it beside the altar eastward, to the place of the fatty ashes.  
**1:17** Then he shall tear it by its wings, <i>but</i> shall not sever <i>it.</i> And the priest shall offer it up in smoke on the altar, on the wood which is on the fire; it is a burnt offering, an offering by fire of a soothing aroma to the LORD.  

## 2장

**2:1** ‘Now when anyone presents a grain offering as an offering to the LORD, his offering shall be of fine flour, and he shall pour oil on it and put frankincense on it.  
**2:2** He shall then bring it to Aaron’s sons the priests; and he shall take from it his handful of its fine flour and of its oil, with all of its frankincense. And the priest shall offer <i>it</i> up in smoke <i>as</i> its memorial portion on the altar, an offering by fire of a soothing aroma to the LORD.  
**2:3** The remainder of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons: a most holy part of the offerings to the LORD by fire.  
**2:4** ‘Now when you bring an offering of a grain offering baked in an oven, <i>it shall be</i> unleavened cakes of fine flour mixed with oil, or unleavened wafers <sup>①</sup>spread with oil.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>anointed</i>  
**2:5** And if your offering is a grain offering <i>made</i> on the griddle, <i>it shall be</i> of fine flour, unleavened, mixed with oil;  
**2:6** you shall break it into bits and pour oil on it; it is a grain offering.  
**2:7** Now if your offering is a grain offering <i>made</i> in a <sup>①</sup>pan, it shall be made of fine flour with oil.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>lidded cooking pan</i>  
**2:8** When you bring in the grain offering which is made of these things to the LORD, it shall be presented to the priest, and he shall bring it to the altar.  
**2:9** The priest then shall take up from the grain offering its memorial portion, and shall offer <i>it</i> up in smoke on the altar <i>as</i> an offering by fire of a soothing aroma to the LORD.  
**2:10** The remainder of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons: a most holy part of the offerings to the LORD by fire.  
**2:11** ‘No grain offering, which you bring to the LORD, shall be made with leaven, for you shall not offer <sup>①</sup>up in smoke any leaven or any honey as an offering by fire to the LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>up from it</i>  
**2:12** As an offering of first fruits you shall bring them to the LORD, but they shall not ascend as a soothing aroma on the altar.  
**2:13** Every grain offering of yours, moreover, you shall season with salt, so that the salt of the covenant of your God will not be lacking from your grain offering; with all your offerings you shall offer salt.  
**2:14** ‘Also if you bring a grain offering of early ripened things to the LORD, you shall bring fresh heads of grain roasted in the fire, crushed grain of new growth, for the grain offering of your early ripened produce.  
**2:15** You shall then put oil on it and place incense on it; it is a grain offering.  
**2:16** Then the priest shall offer up in smoke its memorial portion, part of its crushed grain and its oil with all its incense as an offering by fire to the LORD.  

## 3장

**3:1** ‘Now if his offering is a sacrifice of peace offerings, if he is going to offer from the herd, whether male or female, he shall offer it without defect before the LORD.  
**3:2** And he shall lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it at the doorway of the tent of meeting, and Aaron’s sons the priests shall sprinkle the blood around on the altar.  
**3:3** From the sacrifice of the peace offerings he shall then present an offering by fire to the LORD, the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,  
**3:4** and the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, which is on the loins, and the <sup>①</sup>lobe of the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>appendage on</i>  
**3:5** Then Aaron’s sons shall offer <i>it</i> up in smoke on the altar on the burnt offering, which is on the wood that is on the fire; it is an offering by fire of a soothing aroma to the LORD.  
**3:6** But if his offering for a sacrifice of peace offerings to the LORD is from the flock, he shall offer it, male or female, without defect.  
**3:7** If he is going to offer a lamb for his offering, then he shall offer it before the LORD,  
**3:8** and he shall lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it in front of the tent of meeting, and Aaron’s sons shall sprinkle its blood around on the altar.  
**3:9** From the sacrifice of peace offerings he shall then bring as an offering by fire to the LORD, its fat, the entire fat tail which he shall remove close to the backbone, the fat that covers the entrails, and all the fat that is on the entrails,  
**3:10** and the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, which is on the loins, and the <sup>①</sup>lobe of the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>appendage on</i>  
**3:11** Then the priest shall offer <i>it</i> up in smoke on the altar <i>as</i> food, an offering by fire to the LORD.  
**3:12** ‘Now if his offering is a goat, then he shall offer it before the LORD,  
**3:13** and he shall lay his hand on its head and slaughter it in front of the tent of meeting, and the sons of Aaron shall sprinkle its blood around on the altar.  
**3:14** From it he shall present his offering as an offering by fire to the LORD, the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,  
**3:15** and the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, which is on the loins, and the <sup>①</sup>lobe of the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>appendage on</i>  
**3:16** The priest shall offer them up in smoke on the altar <i>as</i> food, an offering by fire as a soothing aroma; all fat is the LORD’S.  
**3:17** It is a permanent statute throughout your generations in all your dwelling places: you shall not eat any fat or any blood.’ ”

**4:4** He shall bring the bull to the doorway of the tent of meeting before the LORD, and he shall lay his hand on the head of the bull and slaughter the bull before the LORD.

**4:6** and the priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle some of the blood seven times before the LORD, in front of the veil of the sanctuary.  
**4:7** The priest shall also put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of fragrant incense which is before the LORD in the tent of meeting; and all <i>the rest of</i> the blood of the bull he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offering, which is at the doorway of the tent of meeting.  
**4:8** And he shall remove from it all the fat of the bull of the sin offering: the fat that covers the entrails, and all the fat which is on the entrails,  
**4:9** and the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, which is on the loins, and the <sup>①</sup>lobe of the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>appendage on</i>  
**4:10** (just as it is removed from the ox of the sacrifice of peace offerings); and the priest is to offer them up in smoke on the altar of burnt offering.  
**4:11** But the hide of the bull and all its flesh, along with its head, its legs, its entrails, and its refuse,  
**4:12**  <sup>①</sup>that is, all <i>the rest of</i> the bull, he is to bring out to a clean place outside the camp where the fatty ashes are poured out, and burn it on wood with fire; where the fatty ashes are poured out it shall be burned.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and</i>

**4:14** when the sin <sup>①</sup>which they have <sup>②</sup>committed becomes known, then the assembly shall offer a <sup>③</sup>bull of the herd as a sin offering and bring it in front of the tent of meeting.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>concerning which</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>sinned</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>son of the herd</i>

**4:16** Then the anointed priest is to bring some of the blood of the bull to the tent of meeting;  
**4:17** and the priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle <i>it</i> seven times before the LORD, in front of the veil.  
**4:18** He shall then put some of the blood on the horns of the altar which is before the LORD <sup>①</sup>in the tent of meeting; and all <i>the rest of</i> the blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offering which is at the doorway of the tent of meeting.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>which is in</i>  
**4:19** And he shall remove all its fat from it and offer it up in smoke on the altar.  
**4:20** He shall also do with the bull just as he did with the bull of the sin offering; he shall do the same with it. So the priest shall make atonement for them, and they will be forgiven.  
**4:21** Then he is to bring the bull out to <i>a place</i> outside the camp and burn it just as he burned the first bull; it is the sin offering for the assembly.  
**4:22** ‘When a leader sins and unintentionally does any of <sup>①</sup>the things which the LORD his God has commanded not to be done, and he becomes guilty,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>all commands of the LORD</i> which are not to be done  
**4:23**  <sup>①</sup>if his sin <sup>②</sup>which he has committed is made known to him, he shall bring as his offering a <sup>③</sup>goat, a male without defect.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>or</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>in which he has sinned</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>buck of the goats</i>

**4:25** Then the priest is to take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering; and <i>the rest of</i> its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offering.  
**4:26** And he shall offer all its fat up in smoke on the altar as <i>in the case of</i> the fat of the sacrifice of peace offerings. So the priest shall make atonement for him regarding his sin, and he will be forgiven.  
**4:27** ‘Now if <sup>①</sup>anyone of the <sup>②</sup>common people sins unintentionally by doing any of the <sup>③</sup>things which the LORD has commanded not to be done, and becomes guilty,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>one soul</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>people of the land</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>commands of the LORD</i> which are not to be done  
**4:28**  <sup>①</sup>if his sin which he has <sup>②</sup>committed is made known to him, then he shall bring as his offering a <sup>③</sup>goat, a female without defect, for his sin which he has <sup>④</sup>committed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>or</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>sinned</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>female goat</i> <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>sinned</i>  
**4:29** And he shall lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter the sin offering at the place of the burnt offering.  
**4:30** The priest shall then take some of its blood with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering; and all <i>the rest of</i> its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar.

**4:32** ‘But if he brings a lamb as his offering for a sin offering, he shall bring a female without defect.

**4:34** And the priest is to take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and all <i>the rest of</i> its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar.

## 5장

**5:1** ‘Now if a person sins after he hears a <sup>①</sup>public order <i>to testify</i> when he is a witness, whether he has seen or <i>otherwise</i> known, if he does not tell <i>it,</i> then he will bear his punishment.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>voice of a curse</i>  
**5:2** Or if a person touches any unclean thing, whether a carcass of an unclean animal, or the carcass of unclean cattle, or a carcass of unclean swarming things, though it is hidden from him and he is unclean, then he will be guilty.  
**5:3** Or if he touches human uncleanness, of whatever <i>sort</i> his uncleanness <i>may</i> be with which he becomes unclean, and it is hidden from him, and then he comes to know <i>it,</i> he will be guilty.  
**5:4** Or if a person swears thoughtlessly with his lips to do evil or to do good, in whatever <i>matter</i> people speak thoughtlessly with an oath, and it is hidden from him, and then he comes to know <i>it,</i> he will be guilty of one of these things.  
**5:5** So it shall be when he becomes guilty of one of these things, that he shall confess that in which he has sinned.  
**5:6** He shall also bring his guilt offering to the LORD for his sin which he has <sup>①</sup>committed, a female from the flock, a lamb or a <sup>②</sup>goat as a sin offering. **5:7** ‘But if <sup>①</sup>he cannot afford a lamb, then he shall bring to the LORD his guilt offering for that in which he has sinned, two turtledoves or two young doves, one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>his hand does not reach enough for</i>

**5:11** ‘But if his <sup>①</sup>means are insufficient for two turtledoves or two young doves, then for his offering for that which he has sinned, he shall bring the tenth of an <sup>②</sup>ephah of fine flour as a sin offering; he shall not put oil on it or place incense on it, for it is a sin offering.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>hand does not reach</i> <sup>②</sup>About 1 cubic foot or 0.03 cubic meters  
**5:12** He shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take his handful of it as its memorial portion and offer <i>it</i> up in smoke on the altar, <sup>①</sup>with the offerings of the LORD by fire: it is a sin offering.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>upon</i>  
**5:13** So the priest shall make atonement for him concerning his sin which he has <sup>①</sup>committed from one of these, and it will be forgiven him; then <i>the rest</i> shall become the priest’s, like the grain offering.’ ”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>sinned</i>  
**5:14** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**5:15** “If a person acts unfaithfully and sins unintentionally against the LORD’S holy things, then he shall bring his guilt offering to the LORD: a ram without defect from the flock, according to your assessment in silver by shekels, in <i>terms of</i> the shekel of the sanctuary, as a guilt offering.  
**5:16** And he shall make restitution for that which he has sinned against the holy thing, and shall add to it a fifth part of it and give it to the priest. The priest shall then make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and it will be forgiven him.  
**5:17** “Now if a person sins and does any of the things <sup>①</sup>which the LORD has commanded not to be done, though he was unaware, he is still guilty and shall bear his punishment.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the commands of the LORD</i> which are

## 7장

## 27장

**27:1** Again, the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**27:2** “Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, ‘When someone makes an explicit vow, he <i>shall be valued</i> according to your assessment of persons belonging to the LORD.  
**27:3** If your assessment is of a male from twenty years even to sixty years old, then your assessment shall be fifty shekels of silver, by the shekel of the sanctuary.  
**27:4** Or if <sup>①</sup>the person is a female, then your assessment shall be thirty shekels.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>she</i>  
**27:5** And if <i>the person</i> is from five years even to twenty years old, then your assessment for a male shall be twenty shekels, and for a female, ten shekels.  
**27:6** But if <i>the person is</i> from a month even up to five years old, then your assessment shall be five shekels of silver for a male, and for a female your assessment shall be three shekels of silver.  
**27:7** If <i>the person is</i> from sixty years old and upward, if a male, then your assessment shall be fifteen shekels, and for a female, ten shekels.  
**27:8** But if he is poorer than your assessment, then he shall be presented before the priest, and the priest shall assess him; according to <sup>①</sup>the means of the one who vowed, the priest shall assess him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>what the hand reaches</i>  
**27:9** ‘Now if it is an animal of the kind that <sup>①</sup>one can present as an offering to the LORD, any such <i>animal</i> that one gives to the LORD shall be holy.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>they</i>  
**27:10** He shall not replace it nor exchange it, a good for a bad, or a bad for a good; yet if he does exchange animal for animal, then both it and its substitute shall become holy.  
**27:11** If, however, it is any unclean animal of the kind which <sup>①</sup>one does not present as an offering to the LORD, then he shall place the animal before the priest.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>they</i>  
**27:12** And the priest shall assess it <sup>①</sup>as either good or bad; as you, the priest, assess it, so shall it be.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>between</i>  
**27:13** But if he should ever <i>want to</i> redeem it, then he shall add a fifth of it to your assessment.  
**27:14** ‘Now if someone consecrates his house as holy to the LORD, then the priest shall assess it <sup>①</sup>as either good or bad; as the priest assesses it, so shall it stand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>between good</i>  
**27:15** Yet if the one who consecrates <i>it</i> should <i>want to</i> redeem his house, then he shall add a fifth of your assessment price to it, so that it may be his.  
**27:16** ‘Again, if someone consecrates to the LORD part of the field of his own property, then your assessment shall be <sup>①</sup>proportionate to the seed needed for it: a <sup>②</sup>homer of barley seed at fifty shekels of silver.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>according to its seed</i> <sup>②</sup>About 7.7 cubic feet or 0.22 cubic meters  
**27:17** If he consecrates his field as of the year of jubilee, according to your assessment it shall stand.  
**27:18** If he consecrates his field after the jubilee, however, then the priest shall calculate the price for <sup>①</sup>him <sup>②</sup>proportionate to the years that are left until the year of jubilee; and it shall be deducted from your assessment.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>it</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>according to the years</i>  
**27:19** If the one who consecrates it should ever <i>want to</i> redeem the field, then he shall add a fifth of your assessment price to it, so that it may belong to him.  
**27:20** Yet if he does not redeem the field, <sup>①</sup>but has sold the field to another person, it may no longer be redeemed;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>if he</i>  
**27:21** and when it <sup>①</sup>reverts in the jubilee, the field shall be holy to the LORD, like a field banned from secular use; it shall be for the priest as his property.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>goes out</i>  
**27:22** Or if he consecrates to the LORD a field which he has bought, which is not a part of the field of his own property,  
**27:23** then the priest shall calculate for <sup>①</sup>him the amount of your assessment up to the year of jubilee; and he shall on that day give your assessment as holy to the LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>it</i>  
**27:24** In the year of jubilee the field shall return to the one from whom he bought it, to whom the possession of the land belongs.  
**27:25** Every assessment of yours, moreover, shall be by the shekel of the sanctuary. The shekel shall be twenty gerahs.  
**27:26** ‘However, a firstborn among animals, which as a firstborn belongs to the LORD, no one may consecrate; whether ox or sheep, it is the LORD’S.  
**27:27** But if <i>it is</i> among the unclean animals, then he shall <sup>①</sup>redeem it according to your assessment and add to it a fifth of it; and if it is not redeemed, then it shall be sold according to your assessment.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>ransom</i>  
**27:28** ‘Nevertheless, <sup>①</sup>anything which someone sets apart to the LORD for <sup>②</sup>destruction out of all that he has, of man or animal or of the field of his own property, shall not be sold nor redeemed. Anything set apart for destruction is most holy to the LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>anything banned</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., as an offering  
**27:29** No <sup>①</sup>one who may have been <sup>②</sup>set apart among mankind shall be ransomed; he must be put to death.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>one devoted;</i> or <i>banned</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>put under the ban</i>

**27:32** For every tenth part of herd or flock, whatever passes under the rod, the tenth one shall be holy to the LORD.  
**27:33** He is not to be concerned whether <i>it is</i> good or bad, nor shall he exchange it; yet if he does exchange it, then both it and its substitute shall become holy. It shall not be redeemed.’ ”
