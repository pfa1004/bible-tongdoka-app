# 47.고린도후서 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, and <i>our</i> brother Timothy, <e>T</e>o the church of God which is at Corinth with all the <sup>①</sup>saints who are throughout Achaia:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**1:2** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:3** Blessed <i>be</i> the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and God of all comfort,  
**1:4** who comforts us in all our affliction so that we will be able to comfort those who are in <sup>①</sup>any affliction with the comfort with which we ourselves are comforted by God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>every</i>  
**1:5** For just as the sufferings of Christ are <sup>①</sup>ours in abundance, so also our comfort is abundant through Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>to us</i>  
**1:6** But if we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation; or if we are comforted, it is for your comfort, which is effective in the patient enduring of the same sufferings which we also suffer;  
**1:7** and our hope for you is firmly grounded, knowing that as you are partners in our sufferings, so also you are in our comfort.  
**1:8** For we do not want you to be unaware, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> of our affliction which occurred in <sup>①</sup>Asia, that we were burdened excessively, beyond our strength, so that we despaired even of life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., west coast province of Asia Minor  
**1:9**  <sup>①</sup>Indeed, we had the sentence of death within ourselves so that we would not trust in ourselves, but in God who raises the dead,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>But we ourselves</i>  
**1:10** who rescued us from so great a <i>danger of</i> death, and will rescue <i>us,</i> He <sup>①</sup>on whom we have set our hope. And He will yet deliver us,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms <i>on whom we have set our hope that He will also</i>  
**1:11** <i>if</i> you also join in helping us through your prayers, so that thanks may be given by many persons in our behalf for the favor <i>granted</i> to us through <i>the prayers of</i> many.  
**1:12** For our <sup>①</sup>proud confidence is this: the testimony of our conscience, that in holiness and godly sincerity, not in fleshly wisdom but in the grace of God, we have conducted ourselves in the world, and especially toward you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>source of pride</i>  
**1:13** For we write nothing else to you than what you read and understand, and I hope you will understand until the end;  
**1:14** just as you also partially did understand us, that we are your reason to be proud as you also are ours, on the day of our Lord Jesus.  
**1:15** In this confidence I intended at first to come to you, so that you might <sup>①</sup>twice receive a <sup>②</sup>blessing;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>have a second grace</i> <sup>②</sup>One early ms <i>joy</i>  
**1:16**  <sup>①</sup>that is, to pass <sup>②</sup>your way into Macedonia, and again from Macedonia to come to you, and by you to be helped on my journey to Judea.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>through you into</i>  
**1:17** Therefore, I was not vacillating when I intended to do this, was I? Or what I decide, do I decide according to the flesh, so that with me there will be <sup>①</sup>yes, yes and no, no <i>at the same time?</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., a strong <i>yes</i> and <i>no</i>  
**1:18** But <i>as</i> God is faithful, our word to you is not yes and no.  
**1:19** For the Son of God, Christ Jesus, who was preached among you by us—by me and Silvanus and Timothy—was not yes and no, but has been yes in Him.  
**1:20** For as many as the promises of God are, in Him they are yes; therefore through Him also is our Amen to the glory of God through us.  
**1:21** Now He who establishes us with you in Christ and anointed us is God,  
**1:22** who also sealed us and gave <i>us</i> the Spirit in our hearts as a <sup>①</sup>pledge.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>first installment</i>  
**1:23** But I call God as witness to my soul, that <i>it was</i> to spare you <i>that</i> I did not come again to Corinth.  
**1:24** Not that we domineer over your faith, but we are workers with you for your joy; for in your faith you are standing firm.  

## 2장

**2:1** But I decided this <sup>①</sup>for my own sake, that I would not come to you in sorrow again.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>as far as I am concerned</i>  
**2:2** For if I cause you sorrow, who then <i>will be</i> the one making me glad but the one who is made sorrowful by me?  
**2:3** This is the very thing I wrote you, so that when I came, I would not have sorrow from those who ought to make me rejoice; having confidence in you all that my joy was <i>the joy</i> of you all.  
**2:4** For out of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote to you with many tears; not so that you would be made sorrowful, but that you might know the love which I have especially for you.  
**2:5** But if anyone has caused sorrow, he has caused sorrow not for me, but in some degree— <sup>①</sup>not to say too much—for all of you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>so that I not be burdensome</i>  
**2:6** Sufficient for such a person is this punishment which <i>was imposed</i> by the majority,  
**2:7** so that on the other hand, you should rather forgive and comfort <i>him,</i> otherwise such a person might be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow.  
**2:8** Therefore I urge you to reaffirm <i>your</i> love for him.  
**2:9** For to this end I also wrote, so that I might <sup>①</sup>put you to the test, whether you are obedient in all things.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>know the proof of you</i>  
**2:10** But one whom you forgive anything, I also <i>forgive;</i> for indeed what I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, <i>I did so</i> for your sakes in the presence of Christ,  
**2:11** so that no advantage would be taken of us by Satan, for we are not ignorant of his schemes.  
**2:12** Now when I came to Troas for the gospel of Christ and when a door was opened for me in the Lord,  
**2:13** I had no rest for my spirit, not finding Titus my brother; but saying goodbye to them, I went on to Macedonia.  
**2:14** But thanks be to God, who always leads us in triumph in Christ, and through us reveals the fragrance of the knowledge of Him in every place.  
**2:15** For we are a fragrance of Christ to God among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing:  
**2:16** to the one an <sup>①</sup>aroma from death to death, to the other an aroma from life to life. And who is adequate for these things?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>odor</i>  
**2:17** For we are not like the many, <sup>①</sup>peddling the word of God, but as from sincerity, but as from God, we speak in Christ in the sight of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>diluting</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** Are we beginning to commend ourselves again? Or do we need, as some, letters of commendation to you or from you?  
**3:2** You are our letter, written in our hearts, known and read by all people,  
**3:3** revealing yourselves, that you are a letter of Christ, <sup>①</sup>delivered by us, written not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on tablets of stone but on tablets of <sup>②</sup>human hearts.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>served</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>hearts of flesh</i>  
**3:4** Such <i>is the</i> confidence we have toward God through Christ.  
**3:5** Not that we are adequate in ourselves <i>so as</i> to consider anything as <i>having come</i> from ourselves, but our adequacy is from God,  
**3:6** who also made us adequate <i>as</i> servants of a new covenant, not of the letter but of the Spirit; for the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life.  
**3:7** But if the ministry of death, engraved in letters on stones, came <sup>①</sup>with glory so that the sons of Israel could not look intently at the face of Moses because of the glory of his face, fading <i>as</i> it was,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in glory</i>  
**3:8** how will the ministry of the Spirit fail to be <i>even</i> more with glory?  
**3:9** For if the ministry of condemnation has glory, much more does the ministry of righteousness excel in glory.  
**3:10** For indeed what had glory in this case has no glory, because of the glory that surpasses <i>it.</i>  
**3:11** For if that which fades away <i>was</i> <sup>①</sup>with glory, much more that which remains <i>is</i> in glory.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>through</i>  
**3:12** Therefore, having such a hope, we use great boldness in <i>our</i> speech,  
**3:13** and <i>we are</i> not like Moses, <i>who</i> used to put a veil over his face so that the sons of Israel would not stare at the end of what was fading away.  
**3:14** But their minds were hardened; for until this very day at the reading of the old covenant the same veil <sup>①</sup>remains unlifted, because it is removed in Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>remains, it not being revealed that it is done away in Christ</i>  
**3:15** But to this day whenever Moses is read, a veil lies over their hearts;  
**3:16** but whenever <i>someone</i> turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away.  
**3:17** Now the Lord is the Spirit, and where the Spirit of the Lord is, <i>there</i> is freedom.  
**3:18** But we all, with unveiled faces, looking as in a mirror at the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from glory to glory, just as from the Lord, the Spirit.  

## 4장

**4:1** Therefore, since we have this ministry, as we received mercy, we do not lose heart,  
**4:2** but we have renounced the things hidden because of shame, not walking in trickery nor distorting the word of God, but by the open proclamation of the truth commending ourselves to every person’s conscience in the sight of God.  
**4:3** And even if our gospel is veiled, it is veiled <sup>①</sup>to those who are perishing,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>among</i>  
**4:4** in whose case the god of this <sup>①</sup>world has blinded the minds of the unbelieving so that <sup>②</sup>they will not see the light of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>age</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>the light...image of God, will not shine</i> upon them  
**4:5** For we do not preach ourselves, but Christ Jesus as Lord, and ourselves as your bond-servants <sup>①</sup>on account of Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Two early mss <i>through Jesus</i>  
**4:6** For God, who said, “Light shall shine out of darkness,” is the One who has shone in our hearts to give the Light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Christ.  
**4:7** But we have this treasure in <sup>①</sup>earthen containers, so that the extraordinary <i>greatness</i> of the power will be of God and not from ourselves;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>earthenware containers</i>  
**4:8** <i>we are</i> afflicted in every way, but not crushed; perplexed, but not despairing;  
**4:9** persecuted, but not abandoned; struck down, but not destroyed;  
**4:10** always carrying around in the body the dying of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our body.  
**4:11** For we who live are constantly being handed over to death because of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our mortal flesh.  
**4:12** So death works in us, but life in you.  
**4:13** But having the same spirit of faith, according to what is written: “I BELIEVED, THEREFORE I SPOKE,” we also believe, therefore we also speak,  
**4:14** knowing that He who raised the Lord Jesus will also raise us with Jesus, and will present <i>us</i> with you.  
**4:15** For all things <i>are</i> for your sakes, so that grace, having <sup>①</sup>spread to more and more people, will cause thanksgiving to overflow to the glory of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>multiplied through the many</i>  
**4:16** Therefore we do not lose heart, but though our outer person is decaying, yet our inner <i>person</i> is being renewed day by day.  
**4:17** For our momentary, light affliction is producing for us an eternal weight of glory far beyond all comparison,  
**4:18** while we look not at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen; for the things which are seen are temporal, but the things which are not seen are eternal.  

## 5장

**5:1** For we know that if our <sup>①</sup>earthly tent which is our house is torn down, we have a building from God, a house not made by hands, eternal in the heavens.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>earthly house of the tent</i>  
**5:2** For indeed, in this <i>tent</i> we groan, longing to be clothed with our <sup>①</sup>dwelling from heaven,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., the resurrected body  
**5:3** since in fact after putting it on, we will not be found naked.  
**5:4** For indeed, we who are in this tent groan, being burdened, because we do not want to be unclothed but to be clothed, so that what is mortal will be swallowed up by life.  
**5:5** Now He who prepared us for this very <i>purpose is</i> God, who gave us the Spirit as a <sup>①</sup>pledge.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>first installment</i>  
**5:6** Therefore, being always of good courage, and knowing that while we are at home in the body we are absent from the Lord—  
**5:7** for we walk by faith, not by <sup>①</sup>sight—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>appearance</i>  
**5:8** but we are of good courage and prefer rather to be absent from the body and to be at home with the Lord.  
**5:9** Therefore we also have as our ambition, whether at home or absent, to be pleasing to Him.  
**5:10** For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each one may receive compensation for <sup>①</sup>his deeds <i>done</i> through the body, in accordance with what he has done, whether good or bad.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the things</i>  
**5:11** Therefore, knowing the fear of the Lord, we persuade people, but we are well known to God; and I hope that we are also well known in your consciences.  
**5:12** We are not commending ourselves to you again, but <i>are</i> giving you an opportunity to be proud of us, so that you will have <i>an answer</i> for those who take pride in appearance and not in heart.  
**5:13** For if we have lost our minds, <i>it is</i> for God; if we are of sound mind, <i>it is</i> for you.  
**5:14** For the love of Christ controls us, having concluded this, that one died for all, therefore all died;  
**5:15** and He died for all, so that those who live would no longer live for themselves, but for Him who died and rose on their behalf.  
**5:16** Therefore from now on we recognize no one <sup>①</sup>by the flesh; even though we have known Christ by the flesh, yet now we know <i>Him in this way</i> no longer.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., by what one is in the flesh  
**5:17** Therefore if anyone is in Christ, <i>this person is</i> a new <sup>①</sup>creation; the old things passed away; behold, new things have come.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>creature</i>  
**5:18** Now all <i>these</i> things are from God, who reconciled us to Himself through Christ and gave us the ministry of reconciliation,  
**5:19** namely, that God was in Christ reconciling the world to Himself, not counting their wrongdoings against them, and <sup>①</sup>He has <sup>②</sup>committed to us the word of reconciliation.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>having</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>placed in us</i>  
**5:20** Therefore, we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were making an appeal through us; we beg you on behalf of Christ, be reconciled to God.  
**5:21** He made Him who knew no sin <i>to be</i> <sup>①</sup>sin in our behalf, so that we might become the righteousness of God in Him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>a sin offering</i>  

## 6장

**6:1** And working together <i>with Him,</i> we also urge you not to receive the grace of God in vain—  
**6:2** for He says,<br>“AT A FAVORABLE TIME I LISTENED TO YOU,<br>AND ON A DAY OF SALVATION I HELPED YOU.” Behold, now is “A FAVORABLE TIME,” behold, now is “A DAY OF SALVATION”—  
**6:3** giving no reason for <i>taking</i> offense in anything, so that the ministry will not be discredited,  
**6:4** but in everything commending ourselves as <sup>①</sup>servants of God, in much endurance, in afflictions, in hardships, in difficulties,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>ministers</i>  
**6:5** in beatings, in imprisonments, in <sup>①</sup>mob attacks, in labors, in sleeplessness, in hunger,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>tumults</i>  
**6:6** in purity, in knowledge, in patience, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in genuine love,  
**6:7** in the word of truth, <i>and</i> in the power of God; by the weapons of righteousness for the right hand and the left,  
**6:8** by glory and dishonor, by evil report and good report; <i>regarded</i> as deceivers and yet true;  
**6:9** as unknown and <i>yet</i> well known, as dying and <i>yet</i> behold, we are alive; as <sup>①</sup>punished and <i>yet</i> not put to death,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>disciplined</i>  
**6:10** as sorrowful yet always rejoicing, as poor yet making many rich, as having nothing and <i>yet</i> possessing all things.  
**6:11** Our mouth <sup>①</sup>has spoken freely to you, you Corinthians, our heart is opened wide.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is open to you</i>  
**6:12** You are not restrained <sup>①</sup>by us, but you are restrained in your own <sup>②</sup>affections.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in us</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>inward parts</i>  
**6:13** Now in the same way in exchange—I am speaking as to children—open wide <i>your hearts to us,</i> you as well.  
**6:14** Do not be <sup>①</sup>mismatched with unbelievers; for what do righteousness and lawlessness share together, or what does light have in common with darkness?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>unevenly yoked</i>  
**6:15** Or what harmony does Christ have with <sup>①</sup>Belial, or <sup>②</sup>what does a believer share with an unbeliever?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gr <i>Beliar;</i> a name of the devil and the antichrist <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>what part has a believer with an unbeliever</i>  
**6:16** Or what agreement does <sup>①</sup>the temple of God have with idols? For we are <sup>②</sup>the temple of the living God; just as God said,<br>“I WILL DWELL AMONG THEM AND WALK AMONG THEM;<br>AND I WILL BE THEIR GOD, AND THEY SHALL BE MY PEOPLE.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>a temple</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>a temple</i>  
**6:17** Therefore, COME OUT FROM THEIR MIDST AND BE SEPARATE,” says the Lord.<br>“AND DO NOT TOUCH WHAT IS UNCLEAN;<br>And I will welcome you.  
**6:18** And I will be a father to you,<br>And you shall be sons and daughters to Me,”<br>Says the Lord Almighty.  

## 7장

**7:1** Therefore, having these promises, beloved, let’s cleanse ourselves from all defilement of flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in the fear of God.  
**7:2** Make room for us <i>in your hearts;</i> we have wronged no one, we corrupted no one, we have taken advantage of no one.  
**7:3** I do not speak to condemn <i>you,</i> for I have said before that you are in our hearts, to die together and to live together.  
**7:4** My confidence <sup>①</sup>in you is great; my boasting in your behalf is great. I am filled with comfort; I am overflowing with joy in all our affliction.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>toward</i>  
**7:5** For even when we came into Macedonia our flesh had no rest, but we were afflicted on every side: conflicts on the outside, fears inside.  
**7:6** But God, who comforts the <sup>①</sup>discouraged, comforted us by the arrival of Titus;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>lowly</i>  
**7:7** and not only by his arrival, but also by the comfort with which he was comforted among you, as he reported to us your longing, your mourning, your zeal for me; so that I rejoiced even more.  
**7:8** For though I caused you sorrow by my letter, I do not regret it; though I did regret it—<i>for</i> I see that that letter caused you sorrow, though only for a while—  
**7:9** I now rejoice, not that you were made sorrowful, but that you were made sorrowful to <i>the point of</i> repentance; for you were made sorrowful according to <i>the will of</i> God, so that you might not suffer loss in anything <sup>①</sup>through us.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>from</i>  
**7:10** For the sorrow that is according to <i>the will of</i> God produces a repentance <sup>①</sup>without regret, <i>leading</i> to salvation, but the sorrow of the world produces death.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or leading <i>to a salvation without regret</i>  
**7:11** For behold what earnestness this very thing, this <sup>①</sup>godly sorrow, has produced in you: what vindication <i>of yourselves,</i> what indignation, what fear, what longing, what zeal, what punishment of wrong! In everything you demonstrated yourselves to be innocent in the matter.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>sorrow according to God</i>  
**7:12** So although I wrote to you, <i>it was</i> not for the sake of the offender nor for the sake of the one offended, but that your earnestness in our behalf might be made known to you in the sight of God.  
**7:13** Because of this, we have been comforted. <e>A</e>nd besides our comfort, we rejoiced even much more for the joy of Titus, because his spirit has been refreshed by you all.  
**7:14** For if I have boasted to him about you regarding anything, I was not put to shame. But as we spoke all things to you in truth, so also our boasting before Titus proved to be <i>the</i> truth.  
**7:15** His <sup>①</sup>affection abounds all the more toward you, as he remembers the obedience of you all, how you received him with fear and trembling.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>inward parts</i>  
**7:16** I rejoice that in everything I have confidence in you.  

## 8장

**8:1** Now, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> we make known to you the grace of God which has been given in the churches of Macedonia,  
**8:2** that in a <sup>①</sup>great ordeal of affliction their abundance of joy and their deep poverty overflowed in the wealth of their liberality.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>severe test of</i>  
**8:3** For I testify that according to their ability, and beyond their ability, <i>they gave</i> voluntarily,  
**8:4** begging us with much urging for the favor <sup>①</sup>of participation in the <sup>②</sup>support of the <sup>③</sup>saints,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>service to the saints</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**8:5** and <i>this,</i> not as we had <sup>①</sup>expected, but they first gave themselves to the Lord and to us by the will of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>hoped</i>  
**8:6** So we urged Titus that as he had previously made a beginning, so he would also complete in you this gracious work as well.  
**8:7** But just as you excel in everything, in faith, speaking, knowledge, and in all earnestness and in the <sup>①</sup>love we inspired in you, <i>see</i> that you also excel in this gracious work.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>love from us in you;</i> one early ms <i>love from you in us</i>  
**8:8** I am not saying <i>this</i> as a command, but as proving, through the earnestness of others, the sincerity of your love as well.  
**8:9** For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though He was rich, yet for your sake He became poor, so that you through His poverty might become rich.  
**8:10** I give <i>my</i> opinion in this matter, for this is to your advantage, who were the first to begin a year ago not only to do <i>this,</i> but also to desire <i>to do it.</i>  
**8:11** But now finish <sup>①</sup>doing it also, so that just as <i>there was</i> the willingness to desire it, so <i>there may be</i> also the completion of it by your ability.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the doing</i>  
**8:12** For if the willingness is present, it is acceptable according to what <i>a person</i> has, not according to what he does not have.  
**8:13** For <i>this</i> is not for the relief of others <i>and</i> for your hardship, but by way of equality—  
**8:14** at this present time your abundance <i>will serve as assistance</i> for their need, so that their abundance also may <sup>①</sup>serve as <i>assistance</i> for your need, so that there may be equality;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>become</i>  
**8:15** as it is written: “THE ONE WHO <i>had gathered</i> much did not have too much, AND THE ONE WHO <i>had gathered</i> little did not have too little.”  
**8:16** But thanks be to God who puts the same earnestness in your behalf in the heart of Titus.  
**8:17** For he not only accepted our appeal, but being himself very earnest, he has gone to you of his own accord.
