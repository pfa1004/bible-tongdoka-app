## 1장

**1:1** Paul, called <i>as</i> an apostle of Jesus Christ <sup>①</sup>by the will of God, and our brother Sosthenes,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>through</i>

**1:4** I thank <sup>①</sup>my God always concerning you for the grace of God which was given you in Christ Jesus,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Two early mss do not contain <i>my</i>

**1:6** just as the testimony concerning Christ was confirmed <sup>①</sup>in you,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>among</i>  
**1:7** so that you are not lacking in any gift, as you eagerly await the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ,  
**1:8** who will also <sup>①</sup>confirm you to the end, blameless on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>strengthen</i>  
**1:9** God is faithful, through whom you were called into fellowship with His Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.  
**1:10** Now I urge you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you all <sup>①</sup>agree and that there be no <sup>②</sup>divisions among you, but that you be <sup>③</sup>made complete in the same mind and in the same judgment.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>say the same thing</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>schisms</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>united</i>

**1:12** Now I mean this, that each one of you is saying, “I am <sup>①</sup>with Paul,” <sup>②</sup>or “I <i>am</i> <sup>③</sup>with Apollos,” <sup>④</sup>or “I <i>am</i> <sup>⑤</sup>with <sup>⑥</sup>Cephas,” <sup>⑦</sup>or “I <i>am</i> <sup>⑧</sup>with Christ.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>of</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>and</i> <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>of</i> <sup>⑤</sup>Peter’s Aramaic name <sup>⑥</sup>Lit <i>and</i> <sup>⑦</sup>Lit <i>of</i> <sup>⑧</sup>Lit <i>of</i>  
**1:13**  <sup>①</sup>Has Christ been divided? Paul was not crucified for you, was he? Or were you baptized <sup>②</sup>in the name of Paul?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Christ has been divided!</i> or <i>Christ is divided!</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>into</i>  
**1:14** I am thankful that I baptized none of you except Crispus and Gaius,  
**1:15** so that no one would say you were baptized <sup>①</sup>in my name!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>into</i>  
**1:16** But I did baptize the household of Stephanas also; beyond that, I do not know if I baptized anyone else.  
**1:17** For Christ did not send me to baptize, but to preach the gospel, not with <sup>①</sup>cleverness of speech, so that the cross of Christ would not be made of no effect.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., persuasive rhetoric  
**1:18** For the word of the cross is foolishness to those who <sup>①</sup>are perishing, but to us who <sup>②</sup>are being saved it is the power of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>perish</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>are saved</i>

**1:20** Where is the wise person? Where is the scribe? Where is the debater of this age? Has God not made foolish the wisdom of the world?  
**1:21** For since in the wisdom of God the world through its wisdom did not <i>come to</i> know God, God <sup>①</sup>was pleased through the foolishness of the <sup>②</sup>message preached to save those who believe.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>resolved</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>preaching</i>

**1:23** but we preach <sup>①</sup>Christ crucified, to Jews a stumbling block, and to Gentiles foolishness,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., the Messiah

**1:25** For the foolishness of God is wiser than mankind, and the weakness of God is stronger than mankind.  
**1:26** For <sup>①</sup>consider your calling, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> that there were not many wise according to <sup>②</sup>the flesh, not many mighty, not many noble;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>see</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., human standards

**1:28** and the <sup>①</sup>insignificant things of the world and the despised God has chosen, the things that are not, so that He may nullify the things that are,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>lowly</i>  
**1:29** so that no <sup>①</sup>human may boast before God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>flesh</i>  
**1:30** But <i>it is</i> <sup>①</sup>due to Him <i>that</i> you are in Christ Jesus, who became to us wisdom from God, <sup>②</sup>and righteousness and sanctification, and redemption,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>from Him</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>both</i>  
**1:31** so that, just as it is written: “LET THE ONE WHO BOASTS, BOAST IN THE LORD.”  

## 2장

**2:1** And when I came to you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> I did not come as <i>someone</i> superior in <sup>①</sup>speaking ability or <sup>②</sup>wisdom, as I proclaimed to you the <sup>③</sup>testimony of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>speech</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>cleverness</i> <sup>③</sup>One early ms <i>mystery</i>  
**2:2** For I determined to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ, and Him crucified.  
**2:3** I also was with you in weakness and fear, and in great trembling,  
**2:4** and my <sup>①</sup>message and my preaching were not in persuasive words of <sup>②</sup>wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>word</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>cleverness</i>  
**2:5** so that your faith would not <sup>①</sup>rest on the <sup>②</sup>wisdom of mankind, but on the power of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>be</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>cleverness</i>  
**2:6** Yet we do speak wisdom among those who are mature; a wisdom, however, not of this age nor of the rulers of this age, who are passing away;  
**2:7** but we speak God’s wisdom in a mystery, the hidden <i>wisdom</i> which God predestined before the ages to our glory;  
**2:8** <i>the wisdom</i> which none of the rulers of this age has understood; for if they had understood it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory;  
**2:9** but just as it is written:<br>“THINGS WHICH EYE HAS NOT SEEN AND EAR HAS NOT HEARD,<br>AND <i>which</i> have not entered the human heart,<br>ALL THAT GOD HAS PREPARED FOR THOSE WHO LOVE HIM.”  
**2:10**  <sup>①</sup>For to us God revealed <i>them</i> through the Spirit; for the Spirit searches all things, even the depths of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms <i>But</i>  
**2:11** For who among people knows the <i>thoughts</i> of a person except the spirit of the person that is in him? So also the <i>thoughts</i> of God no one knows, except the Spirit of God.  
**2:12** Now we have not received the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God, so that we may know the things freely given to us by God.  
**2:13** We also speak these things, not in words taught by human wisdom, but in those taught by the Spirit, <sup>①</sup>combining spiritual <i>thoughts</i> with spiritual <i>words.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>interpreting spiritual</i> things <i>for spiritual</i> people  
**2:14** But <sup>①</sup>a natural person does not accept the things of the Spirit of God, for they are foolishness to him; and he cannot understand them, because they are spiritually discerned.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>an unspiritual</i>  
**2:15** But the one who is spiritual discerns all things, yet he himself is <sup>①</sup>discerned by no one.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>judged</i>  
**2:16** For WHO HAS KNOWN THE MIND OF THE LORD, THAT HE WILL INSTRUCT HIM? But we have the mind of Christ.  

## 3장

**3:1** And I, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> could not speak to you as spiritual people, but <i>only</i> as fleshly, as to infants in Christ.  
**3:2** I gave you milk to drink, not solid food; for you were not yet able <i>to consume it.</i> But even now you are not yet able,  
**3:3** for you are still fleshly. For since there is jealousy and strife among you, are you not fleshly, and are you not walking like <i>ordinary</i> people?  
**3:4** For when one person says, “I am <sup>①</sup>with Paul,” and another, “I <i>am</i> <sup>②</sup>with Apollos,” are you not <i>ordinary</i> people?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>of</i>  
**3:5** What then is Apollos? And what is Paul? Servants through whom you believed, even as the Lord gave <i>opportunity</i> to each one.  
**3:6** I planted, Apollos watered, but God was causing the growth.  
**3:7** So then neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but God who causes the growth.  
**3:8** Now the one who plants and the one who waters are one; but each will receive his own <sup>①</sup>reward according to his own labor.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>wages</i>  
**3:9** For we are God’s fellow workers; you are God’s <sup>①</sup>field, God’s building.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>cultivated land</i>  
**3:10** According to the grace of God which was given to me, like a wise master builder I laid a foundation, and another is building on it. But each person must be careful how he builds on it.  
**3:11** For no one can lay a foundation other than the one which is laid, which is Jesus Christ.  
**3:12** Now if anyone builds on the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, <i>or</i> straw,  
**3:13** each one’s work will become evident; for the day will show it because it is <i>to be</i> revealed with fire, and the fire itself will test <sup>①</sup>the quality of each one’s work.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of what sort each man’s work is</i>  
**3:14** If anyone’s work which he has built on it remains, he will receive a reward.  
**3:15** If anyone’s work is burned up, he will suffer loss; but he himself will be saved, yet <i>only</i> so as through fire.  
**3:16** Do you not know that you are a <sup>①</sup>temple of God and <i>that</i> the Spirit of God dwells in you?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sanctuary</i>  
**3:17** If anyone destroys the <sup>①</sup>temple of God, God will destroy <sup>②</sup>that person; for the <sup>③</sup>temple of God is holy, <sup>④</sup>and that is what you are.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sanctuary</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>this</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>sanctuary</i> <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>who you are</i>  
**3:18** <i>Take care that</i> no one deceives himself. If anyone among you thinks that he is wise in this age, he must become foolish, so that he may become wise.  
**3:19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness in the sight of God. For it is written: “<i>He is</i> THE ONE WHO CATCHES THE WISE BY THEIR CRAFTINESS”;  
**3:20** and again, “THE LORD KNOWS THE THOUGHTS of the wise, THAT THEY ARE useless.”  
**3:21** So then, no one is to be boasting in people. For all things belong to you,  
**3:22** whether Paul or Apollos or <sup>①</sup>Cephas, or the world or life or death, or things present or things to come; all things belong to you,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Peter’s Aramaic name  
**3:23** and you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.  

## 4장

**4:1** This is the way <i>any</i> person is to regard us: as servants of Christ and stewards of the mysteries of God.  
**4:2** In this case, moreover, it is required <sup>①</sup>of stewards that one be found trustworthy.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in</i>  
**4:3** But to me it is an insignificant matter that I would be examined by you, or by <i>any</i> human <sup>①</sup>court; in fact, I do not even examine myself.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>day</i>  
**4:4** For I am not aware of anything against myself; however I am not vindicated by this, but the one who examines me is the Lord.  
**4:5** Therefore do not go on <sup>①</sup>passing judgment before <sup>②</sup><i>the</i> time, <i>but wait</i> until the Lord comes, who will both bring to light the things hidden in the darkness and disclose the motives of <i>human</i> hearts; and then praise will come to each person from God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>judging anything</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., the appointed time of judgment  
**4:6** Now these things, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> I have figuratively applied to myself and Apollos on your account, so that in us you may learn not to exceed what is written, so that no one of you will become <sup>①</sup>arrogant in behalf of one against the other.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>puffed up</i>  
**4:7** For who considers you as superior? What do you have that you did not receive? And if you did receive it, why do you boast as if you had not received it?  
**4:8** You are already filled, you have already become rich, you have become kings without us; and indeed, <i>I</i> wish that you had become kings so that we also might reign with you!  
**4:9** For I think, God has exhibited us, the apostles, last of all as men condemned to death, because we have become a spectacle to the world, both to angels and to mankind.  
**4:10** We are fools on account of Christ, but you are prudent in Christ! We are weak, but you are strong! You are distinguished, but we are without honor!  
**4:11** Up to this present hour we are both hungry and thirsty, and are poorly clothed and roughly treated and homeless;  
**4:12** and we labor, working with our own hands; when we are verbally abused, we bless; when we are persecuted, we endure <i>it;</i>  
**4:13** when we are slandered, we <sup>①</sup>reply as friends; we have become as the scum of the world, the dregs of all things, <i>even</i> until now.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>conciliate</i>  
**4:14** I do not write these things to shame you, but to admonish you as my beloved children.  
**4:15** For if you were to have countless tutors in Christ, yet <i>you would</i> not <i>have</i> many fathers, for in Christ Jesus I became your father through the gospel.  
**4:16** Therefore I urge you, be imitators of me.  
**4:17** For this reason I have sent to you Timothy, who is my beloved and faithful child in the Lord, and he will remind you of my ways which are in Christ, just as I teach everywhere in every church.  
**4:18** Now some have become <sup>①</sup>arrogant, as though I were not coming to you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>puffed up</i>  
**4:19** But I will come to you soon, if the Lord wills, and I shall find out, not the <sup>①</sup>words of those who are arrogant, but their power.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>word</i>  
**4:20** For the kingdom of God is not in <sup>①</sup>words, but in power.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>word</i>  
**4:21** What do you desire? That I come to you with a rod, or with love and a spirit of gentleness?

**5:4** In the name of our Lord Jesus, when you are assembled, and <sup>①</sup>I with you in spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>my spirit, with the power</i>  
**5:5** <i>I have decided</i> <sup>①</sup>to turn such a person over to Satan for the destruction of his <sup>②</sup>body, so that his spirit may be saved on the day of the <sup>③</sup>Lord.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>that you are to turn</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>flesh</i> <sup>③</sup>One early ms <i>Lord Jesus</i>

## 6장

**6:1** Does any one of you, when he has a <sup>①</sup>case against <sup>②</sup>his neighbor, dare to go to law before the unrighteous and not before the <sup>③</sup>saints?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>matter</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>the other</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**6:2** Or do you not know that the <sup>①</sup>saints will judge the world? If the world is judged by you, are you not competent <i>to form</i> the smallest <sup>②</sup>law courts?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>See note 2 v 1 <sup>②</sup>I.e., to try trivial cases  
**6:3** Do you not know that we will judge angels? How much more matters of this life?  
**6:4** So if you have law courts dealing with matters of this life, <sup>①</sup>do you appoint them <i>as judges</i> who are of no account in the church?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>appoint them...church!</i>  
**6:5** I say <i>this</i> to your shame. <i>Is it</i> so, <i>that</i> there is not among you anyone wise who will be able to decide between his brothers <i>and sisters,</i>  
**6:6** but brother goes to law with brother, and that before unbelievers?  
**6:7** Actually, then, it is already a defeat for you, that you have lawsuits with one another. Why not rather suffer the wrong? Why not rather be defrauded?  
**6:8** On the contrary, you yourselves do wrong and defraud. And this to <i>your</i> brothers <i>and sisters!</i>  
**6:9** Or do you not know that the unrighteous will not inherit the kingdom of God? Do not be deceived; neither the sexually immoral, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor <sup>①</sup>homosexuals,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Two Gr words in the text, prob. submissive and dominant male homosexuals  
**6:10** nor thieves, nor <i>the</i> greedy, nor those habitually drunk, nor verbal abusers, nor swindlers, will inherit the kingdom of God.  
**6:11** Such were some of you; but you were washed, but you were sanctified, but you were justified in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and <sup>①</sup>in the Spirit of our God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>by</i>  
**6:12** All things are permitted for me, but not all things are of benefit. All things are permitted for me, but I will not be mastered by anything.  
**6:13** Food is for the stomach and the stomach is for food, however God will do away with both <sup>①</sup>of them. But the body is not for sexual immorality, but for the Lord, and the Lord is for the body.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>this and these</i>  
**6:14** Now God has not only raised the Lord, but will also raise us up through His power.  
**6:15** Do you not know that your bodies are parts of Christ? Shall I then take away the parts of Christ and make them parts of a prostitute? <sup>①</sup>Far from it!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>May it never happen!</i>  
**6:16** Or do you not know that the one who joins himself to a prostitute is one body <i>with her?</i> For He says, “THE TWO SHALL BECOME ONE FLESH.”  
**6:17** But the one who joins himself to the Lord is one spirit <i>with Him.</i>  
**6:18** Flee sexual immorality. Every other sin that a person commits is outside the body, but the <sup>①</sup>sexually immoral person sins against his own body.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>one who practices sexual immorality</i>  
**6:19** Or do you not know that your body is a <sup>①</sup>temple of the Holy Spirit within you, whom you have from <sup>②</sup>God, and <i>that</i> you are not your own?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sanctuary</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>God? And you...own</i>  
**6:20** For you have been bought for a price: therefore glorify God in your body.  

## 7장

**7:1** Now concerning the things about which you wrote, it is good for a man <sup>①</sup>not to touch a woman.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Prob. referring to abstinence  
**7:2** But because of sexual immoralities, each man is to have his own wife, and each woman is to have her own husband.  
**7:3** The husband must fulfill his duty to his wife, and likewise the wife also to her husband.  
**7:4** The wife does not have authority over her own body, but the husband <i>does;</i> and likewise the husband also does not have authority over his own body, but the wife <i>does.</i>  
**7:5**  <sup>①</sup>Stop depriving one another, except by agreement for a time so that you may devote yourselves to prayer, and <sup>②</sup>come together again so that Satan will not tempt you because of your lack of self-control.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>do not deprive</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>be</i>  
**7:6** But this I say by way of concession, not of command.  
**7:7**  <sup>①</sup>Yet I wish that all <sup>②</sup>men were even as I myself am. However, each has his own gift from God, one in this way, and another in that.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms <i>For</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>people</i>  
**7:8** But I say to the unmarried and to widows that it is good for them if they remain even as I.  
**7:9** But if they do not have self-control, let them marry; for it is better to marry than to burn <i>with passion.</i>  
**7:10** But to the married I give instructions, not I, but the Lord, that the wife is not to leave her husband

**7:17** Only, as the Lord has assigned to each one, as God has called each, in this way let him walk. And so I direct in all the churches.  
**7:18** Was any man called <i>when he was already</i> circumcised? He is not to become uncircumcised. Has anyone been called in uncircumcision? He is not to be circumcised.

**7:20** Each <i>person</i> is to remain in that <sup>①</sup>state in which he was called.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>calling</i>  
**7:21** Were you called as a slave? Do not let it concern you. But if you are also able to become free, take advantage of <i>that.</i>  
**7:22** For the one who was called in the Lord as a slave, is the Lord’s freed person; likewise the one who was called as free, is Christ’s slave.  
**7:23** You were bought for a price; do not become slaves of people.  
**7:24** Brothers <i>and sisters,</i> each one is to remain with God in that <i>condition</i> in which he was called.  
**7:25** Now concerning virgins, I have no command of the Lord, but I am offering direction as one who <sup>①</sup>by the mercy of the Lord is trustworthy.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>has been shown mercy by the Lord to be trustworthy</i>  
**7:26** I think, then, that this is good in view of the <sup>①</sup>present distress, that it is good for a man <sup>②</sup>to remain as he is.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>impending</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>so to be</i>  
**7:27** Are you bound to a wife? Do not seek to be released. Are you released from a wife? Do not seek a wife.  
**7:28** But if you marry, you have not sinned; and if a virgin marries, she has not sinned. Yet such people <i>as yourselves</i> will have <sup>①</sup>trouble in this life, and I <i>am trying to</i> spare you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>tribulation in the flesh</i>

**7:30** and those who weep, as though they did not weep; and those who rejoice, as though they did not rejoice; and those who buy, as though they did not possess;  
**7:31** and those who use the world, as though they did not make full use of it; for the <i>present</i> form of this world is passing away.  
**7:32** But I want you to be free from concern. One who is unmarried is concerned about the things of the Lord, how he may please the Lord;  
**7:33** but one who is married is concerned about the things of the world, how he may please his wife,  
**7:34** and <i>his interests</i> are divided. The woman who is unmarried, and the virgin, is concerned about the things of the Lord, that she may be holy both in body and spirit; but one who is married is concerned about the things of the world, how she may please her husband.  
**7:35** I say this for your own benefit, not to put a restraint on you, but <sup>①</sup>to promote what is appropriate and <i>to secure</i> undistracted devotion to the Lord.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>for what is seemly</i>

**7:39** A wife is bound as long as her husband lives; but if her husband <sup>①</sup>dies, she is free to be married to whom she wishes, only in the Lord.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>falls asleep</i>  
**7:40** But in my opinion she is <sup>①</sup>happier if she remains as she is; and I think that I also have the Spirit of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>more fortunate</i>  

## 8장

**8:1** Now concerning food sacrificed to idols, we know that we all have knowledge. Knowledge <sup>①</sup>makes <i>one</i> conceited, but love edifies <i>people.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>puffs up</i>  
**8:2** If anyone thinks that he knows anything, he has not yet known as he ought to know;  
**8:3** but if anyone loves God, he is known by Him.  
**8:4** Therefore, concerning the eating of food sacrificed to idols, we know that an idol is <sup>①</sup>nothing at all in the world, and that there is no God but one.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., what it represents does not exist  
**8:5** For even if there are so-called gods whether in heaven or on earth, as indeed there are many gods and many lords,  
**8:6** yet for us there is <i>only</i> one God, the Father, from whom are all things, and we <i>exist</i> for Him; and one Lord, Jesus Christ, by whom are all things, and we <i>exist</i> through Him.  
**8:7** However, not all people have this knowledge; but some, being accustomed to the idol until now, eat <i>food</i> as if it were sacrificed to an idol; and their conscience, being weak, is defiled.  
**8:8** Now food will not bring us <sup>①</sup>close to God; we are neither <sup>②</sup>the worse if we do not eat, nor <sup>③</sup>the better if we do eat.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>before God</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>lacking</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>abounding</i>

**8:10** For if someone sees you, the one who has knowledge, dining in an idol’s temple, will his conscience, if he is weak, not be strengthened to eat things sacrificed to idols?  
**8:11** For through your knowledge the one who is weak is ruined, the brother <i>or sister</i> for whose sake Christ died.  
**8:12** And so, by sinning against the brothers <i>and sisters</i> and wounding their conscience when it is weak, you sin against Christ.  
**8:13** Therefore, if food causes my brother to sin, I will never eat meat again, so that I will not cause my brother to sin.  

## 9장

**9:1** Am I not free? Am I not an apostle? Have I not seen Jesus our Lord? Are you not my work in the Lord?  
**9:2** If I am not an apostle to others, at least I am to you; for you are the seal of my apostleship in the Lord.  
**9:3** My defense to those who examine me is this:  
**9:4**  <sup>①</sup>Do we not have a right to eat and drink?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>It is not that we have no right to eat and drink, is it?</i>  
**9:5**  <sup>①</sup>Do we not have a right to take along a <sup>②</sup>believing wife, even as the rest of the apostles and the brothers of the Lord, and <sup>③</sup>Cephas?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>It is not that we have no right to take along...Cephas, is it?</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>sister, as a wife</i> <sup>③</sup>Peter’s Aramaic name  
**9:6** Or do only <sup>①</sup>Barnabas and I have no right to refrain from <sup>②</sup>working?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>I and Barnabas</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., to support themselves  
**9:7** Who at any time serves as a soldier at his own expense? Who plants a vineyard and does not eat its fruit? Or who tends a flock and does not <sup>①</sup>consume some of the milk of the flock?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>eat from</i>  
**9:8** I am not <i>just</i> asserting these things according to human judgment, am I? Or does the Law not say these things as well?  
**9:9** For it is written in the Law of Moses: “YOU SHALL NOT MUZZLE THE OX WHILE IT IS THRESHING.” God is not concerned about oxen, is He?  
**9:10** Or is He speaking entirely for our sake? Yes, it was written for our sake, because the plowman ought to plow in hope, and the thresher <i>to thresh</i> in hope of sharing <i>in the crops.</i>  
**9:11** If we sowed spiritual things in you, is it too much if we reap material things from you?  
**9:12** If others share the right over you, do we not more? Nevertheless, we did not use this right, but we endure all things so that we will cause no hindrance to the gospel of Christ.  
**9:13** Do you not know that those who perform sacred services eat <i>the food</i> of the temple, <i>and</i> those who attend regularly to the altar have their share <sup>①</sup>from the altar?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>with</i>  
**9:14** So also the Lord directed those who proclaim the gospel to get their living from the gospel.  
**9:15** But I have used none of these things. And I have not written these things so that it will be done so in my case; for it would be better for me to die than <i>that.</i> No one shall make my boast an empty one!  
**9:16** For if I preach the gospel, I have nothing to boast <i>about,</i> for I am under compulsion; for woe to me if I do not preach the gospel.  
**9:17** For if I do this voluntarily, I have a reward; but if against my will, I have been entrusted with a commission <i>nonetheless.</i>  
**9:18** What, then, is my reward? That, when I preach the gospel, I may offer the gospel without charge, so as not to make full use of my right in the gospel.  
**9:19** For though I am free from all people, I have made myself a slave to all, so that I may gain more.  
**9:20** To the Jews I became as a Jew, so that I might gain Jews; to those who are under the Law, <i>I became</i> as <i>one</i> under the Law, though not being under the Law myself, so that I might gain those who are under the Law;  
**9:21** to those who are without the Law, <i>I became</i> as one without the Law, though not being without the law of God but under the law of Christ, so that I might gain those who are without the Law.  
**9:22** To the weak I became weak, that I might gain the weak; I have become all things to all people, so that I may by all means save some.  
**9:23** I do all things for the sake of the gospel, so that I may become a fellow partaker of it.  
**9:24** Do you not know that those who run in a race all run, but <i>only</i> one receives the prize? Run in such a way that you may win.  
**9:25** Everyone who competes in the games exercises self-control in all things. So they <i>do it</i> to obtain a perishable wreath, but we an imperishable.  
**9:26** Therefore I run in such a way as not <i>to run</i> aimlessly; I box in such a way, as <sup>①</sup>to avoid hitting air;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>not hitting</i>  
**9:27** but I strictly discipline my body and make it my slave, so that, after I have preached to others, I myself will not be disqualified.  

## 10장

**10:1** For I do not want you to be unaware, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> that our fathers were all under the cloud and they all passed through the sea;  
**10:2** and they all were baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea;

**10:7** Do not be idolaters, as some of them were; as it is written: “THE PEOPLE SAT DOWN TO EAT AND TO DRINK, AND ROSE UP TO PLAY.”

**10:9** Nor are we to put the Lord to the test, as some of them <sup>①</sup>did, and were killed by the snakes.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>made trial</i>  
**10:10** Nor grumble, as some of them <sup>①</sup>did, and were killed by the destroyer.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>grumbled</i>  
**10:11** Now these things happened to them as an example, and they were written for our instruction, upon whom the ends of the ages have come.  
**10:12** Therefore let the one who thinks he stands watch out that he does not fall.  
**10:13** No temptation has overtaken you except <i>something</i> common to mankind; and God is faithful, <sup>①</sup>so He will not allow you to be tempted beyond what you are able, but with the temptation will provide the way of escape also, so that you will be able to endure it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>who will not</i>  
**10:14** Therefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry.  
**10:15** I speak as to wise people; you <i>then,</i> judge what I say.  
**10:16** Is the cup of blessing which we bless not a sharing in the blood of Christ? Is the bread which we break not a sharing in the body of Christ?  
**10:17** Since there is one loaf, we who are many are one body; for we all partake of the one loaf.  
**10:18** Look at <sup>①</sup>the people of Israel; are those who eat the sacrifices not partners in the altar?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Israel according to the flesh</i>  
**10:19** What do I mean then? That food sacrificed to idols is anything, or that an idol is <sup>①</sup>anything?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., anything real  
**10:20** <i>No,</i> but <i>I say</i> that things which <i>the Gentiles</i> sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons and not to God; and I do not want you to become partners with demons.  
**10:21** You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons; you cannot partake of the table of the Lord and the table of demons.  
**10:22** Or do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? We are not stronger than He, are we?  
**10:23** All things are permitted, but not all things are of benefit. All things are permitted, but not all things <sup>①</sup>build <i>people</i> up.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>edify</i>  
**10:24** No one is to seek his own <i>advantage,</i> but rather that of his <sup>①</sup>neighbor.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the other</i>  
**10:25** Eat anything that is sold in the meat market without asking questions, for the sake of conscience;  
**10:26** FOR THE EARTH IS THE LORD’S, AND <sup>①</sup>ALL IT CONTAINS.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>its fullness</i>  
**10:27** If one of the unbelievers invites you and you want to go, eat anything that is set before you without asking questions, for the sake of conscience.  
**10:28** But if anyone says to you, “This is meat sacrificed to idols,” do not eat <i>it,</i> for the sake of that one who informed <i>you</i> and for the sake of conscience;  
**10:29** Now <i>by</i> “conscience” I do not mean your own, but the other person’s; for why is my freedom judged by another’s conscience?  
**10:30** If I partake with thankfulness, why am I slandered about that for which I give thanks?  
**10:31** Therefore, whether you eat or drink, or whatever you do, do all things for the glory of God.  
**10:32** Do not offend Jews or Greeks, or the church of God;  
**10:33** just as I also please everyone in all things, not seeking my own benefit but the <i>benefit</i> of the many, so that they may be saved.

**11:15** but if a woman has long hair, it is a glory to her? For her hair is given to her as a covering.  
**11:16** But if anyone is inclined to be contentious, we have no <sup>①</sup>such practice, nor have the churches of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., the one advocated by Paul’s opponents

**11:18** For, in the first place, when you come together <sup>①</sup>as a church, I hear that <sup>②</sup>divisions exist among you; and in part I believe it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in church</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>dissensions</i>

**11:23** For I received from the Lord that which I also delivered to you, that the Lord Jesus, on the night when He was betrayed, took bread;  
**11:24** and when He had given thanks, He broke it and said, “This is My body, which is for you; do this in remembrance of Me.”

**11:27** Therefore whoever eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord in an unworthy way, shall be guilty of the body and the blood of the Lord.  
**11:28** But a person must examine himself, and in so doing he is to eat of the bread and drink of the cup.  
**11:29** For the one who eats and drinks, eats and drinks judgment to himself if he does not <i>properly</i> recognize the <sup>①</sup>body.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., body of the Lord  
**11:30** For this reason many among you are weak and sick, and a number <sup>①</sup>are asleep.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., are dead  
**11:31** But if we judged ourselves rightly, we would not be judged.  
**11:32** But when we are judged, we are disciplined by the Lord so that we will not be condemned along with the world.  
**11:33** So then, my brothers <i>and sisters,</i> when you come together to eat, wait for one another.  
**11:34** If anyone is hungry, have him eat at home, so that you do not come together for judgment. As to the remaining matters, I will give instructions when I come.  

## 12장

**12:2** You know that when you were <sup>①</sup>pagans, <i>you were</i> led <sup>②</sup>astray to the mute idols, however you were led.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Gentiles;</i> i.e., mainly Greeks and Romans, traditionally polytheistic <sup>②</sup>Or <i>away</i>  
**12:3** Therefore I make known to you that no one speaking <sup>①</sup>by the Spirit of God says, “Jesus is <sup>②</sup>accursed”; and no one can say, “Jesus is Lord,” except <sup>③</sup>by the Holy Spirit.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in</i> <sup>②</sup>Gr <i>anathema</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>in</i>

**12:5** And there are varieties of ministries, and the same Lord.  
**12:6** There are varieties of effects, but the same God who works all things in all <i>persons.</i>

**12:8** For to one is given the word of wisdom through the Spirit, and to another the word of knowledge according to the same Spirit;  
**12:9** to another faith <sup>①</sup>by the same Spirit, and to another gifts of <sup>②</sup>healing <sup>③</sup>by the one Spirit,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>healings</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>in</i>

**13:5** It does not act disgracefully, it does not seek its own <i>benefit;</i> it is not provoked, does not keep an account of a wrong <i>suffered,</i>  
**13:6** it does not rejoice in unrighteousness, but rejoices with the truth;  
**13:7** it <sup>①</sup>keeps every confidence, it believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>covers all things</i>

## 14장

**14:1** Pursue love, yet earnestly desire spiritual <i>gifts,</i> but especially that you may prophesy.  
**14:2** For the one who speaks in a tongue does not speak to people, but to God; for no one <sup>①</sup>understands, but <sup>②</sup>in <i>his</i> spirit he speaks mysteries.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>hears</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>by the Spirit</i>  
**14:3** But the one who prophesies speaks to people <i>for</i> edification, <sup>①</sup>exhortation, and consolation.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>encouragement</i>  
**14:4** The one who speaks in a tongue edifies himself; but the one who prophesies edifies the church.  
**14:5** Now I wish that you all spoke in tongues, but rather that you would prophesy; and greater is the one who prophesies than the one who speaks in tongues, unless he interprets, so that the church may receive edification.  
**14:6** But now, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> if I come to you speaking in tongues, how will I benefit you unless I speak to you either by way of revelation, or of knowledge, or of prophecy, or of teaching?  
**14:7** Yet <i>even</i> lifeless <i>instruments,</i> whether flute or harp, in producing a sound, if they do not produce a distinction in the tones, how will it be known what is played on the flute or on the harp?  
**14:8** For if the trumpet produces an indistinct sound, who will prepare himself for battle?  
**14:9** So you too, unless you produce intelligible speech by the tongue, how will it be known what is spoken? For you will <i>just</i> be talking to the air.  
**14:10** There are, perhaps, a great many kinds of <sup>①</sup>languages in the world, and none is incapable of meaning.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>voices</i>  
**14:11** So if I do not know the meaning of the language, I will be unintelligible to the one who speaks, and the one who speaks will be unintelligible <sup>①</sup>to me.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in my estimation</i>  
**14:12** So you too, since you are eager to possess <sup>①</sup>spiritual <i>gifts,</i> strive to excel for the edification of the church.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>spirits</i>  
**14:13** Therefore, one who speaks in a tongue is to pray that he may interpret.  
**14:14** For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind is unproductive.  
**14:15** What is <i>the outcome</i> then? I will pray with the spirit, but I will pray with the mind also; I will sing with the spirit, but I will sing with the mind also.  
**14:16** For otherwise, if you bless <i>God</i> <sup>①</sup>in the spirit <i>only,</i> how will the one who occupies the place of the <sup>②</sup>outsider <i>know to</i> say the “Amen” at your giving of thanks, since he does not understand what you are saying?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>with the</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>private person</i>  
**14:17** For you are giving thanks well <i>enough,</i> but the other person is not edified.  
**14:18** I thank God, I speak in tongues more than you all;  
**14:19** nevertheless, in church I prefer to speak five words with my mind so that I may instruct others also, rather than ten thousand words in a tongue.  
**14:20** Brothers <i>and sisters,</i> do not be children in your thinking; yet in evil be infants, but in your thinking be mature.  
**14:21** In the <sup>①</sup>Law it is written: “BY MEN OF STRANGE TONGUES AND BY THE LIPS OF STRANGERS I WILL SPEAK TO THIS PEOPLE, AND EVEN SO THEY WILL NOT LISTEN TO ME,” says the Lord.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., Old Testament, Isaiah  
**14:22** So then, tongues are for a sign, not to those who believe but to unbelievers; but prophecy is not for unbelievers, but for those who believe.  
**14:23** Therefore if the whole church gathers together and all <i>the people</i> speak in tongues, and <sup>①</sup>outsiders or unbelievers enter, will they not say that you are insane?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>See note 2 v 16  
**14:24** But if all prophesy, and an unbeliever or an <sup>①</sup>outsider enters, he is convicted by all, he is called to account by all;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>See note 2 v 16  
**14:25** the secrets of his heart are disclosed; and so he will fall on his face and worship God, declaring that God is certainly among you.  
**14:26** What is <i>the outcome</i> then, brothers <i>and sisters?</i> When you assemble, each one has a psalm, has a teaching, has a revelation, has a tongue, has an interpretation. All things are to be done for edification.  
**14:27** If anyone speaks in a tongue, <i>it must be</i> by two or at the most three, and <i>each one</i> in turn, and one is to interpret;  
**14:28** but if there is no interpreter, he is to keep silent in church; and have him speak to himself and to God.  
**14:29** Have two or three prophets speak, and have the others pass judgment.  
**14:30** But if a revelation is made to another who is seated, then the first one is to keep silent.  
**14:31** For you can all prophesy one by one, so that all may learn and all may be <sup>①</sup>exhorted;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>encouraged</i>  
**14:32** and the spirits of prophets are subject to prophets;  
**14:33** for God is not <i>a God</i> of confusion, but of peace. <e>A</e>s in all the churches of the <sup>①</sup>saints,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., God’s people  
**14:34** the women are to keep silent in the churches; for they are not permitted to speak, but are to subject themselves, just as the Law also says.  
**14:35** If they desire to learn anything, let them ask their own husbands at home; for it is <sup>①</sup>improper for a woman to speak in church.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>disgraceful</i>  
**14:36** Or was it from you that the word of God <i>first</i> went out? Or has it come to you only?  
**14:37** If anyone thinks that he is a prophet or spiritual, let him recognize that the things which I write to you are the Lord’s commandment.  
**14:38** But if anyone does not recognize <i>this,</i> <sup>①</sup>he is not recognized.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Two early mss <i>let him continue not to recognize</i> it  
**14:39** Therefore, my brothers <i>and sisters,</i> earnestly desire to prophesy, and do not forbid speaking in tongues.  
**14:40** But all things must be done properly and in an orderly way.
