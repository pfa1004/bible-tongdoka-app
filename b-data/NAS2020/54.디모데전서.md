# 54.디모데전서 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus according to the commandment of God our Savior, and of Christ Jesus, <i>who is</i> our hope,  
**1:2** To Timothy, <i>my</i> true <sup>①</sup>son in <i>the</i> faith: Grace, mercy, <i>and</i> peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>child</i>  
**1:3** Just as I urged you <sup>①</sup>upon my departure for Macedonia, to remain on at Ephesus so that you would instruct certain people not to teach strange doctrines,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>while going to</i>  
**1:4** nor to <sup>①</sup>pay attention to myths and endless genealogies, which give rise to useless speculation rather than <i>advance</i> <sup>②</sup>the plan of God, which is by faith, <i>so I urge you now.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>occupy themselves with</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., prob. the plan of salvation  
**1:5** But the goal of our <sup>①</sup>instruction is love from a pure heart, <i>from</i> a good conscience, and <i>from</i> a sincere faith.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>commandment</i>  
**1:6** Some people have strayed from these things and have turned aside to fruitless discussion,  
**1:7** wanting to be teachers of the Law, even though they do not understand either what they are saying or the matters about which they make confident assertions.  
**1:8** But we know that the Law is good, if one uses it lawfully,  
**1:9** realizing the fact that law is not made for a righteous person but for those who are lawless and rebellious, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and worldly, for those who kill their fathers or mothers, for murderers,  
**1:10** for the <sup>①</sup>sexually immoral, homosexuals, <sup>②</sup>slave traders, liars, perjurers, and whatever else is contrary to sound teaching,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>fornicators</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>kidnappers</i>  
**1:11** according to the glorious gospel of the blessed God, with which I have been entrusted.  
**1:12** I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who has strengthened me, because He considered me faithful, putting me into service,  
**1:13** even though I was previously a blasphemer and a persecutor and a violent aggressor. Yet I was shown mercy because I acted ignorantly in unbelief;  
**1:14** and the grace of our Lord was more than abundant, with the faith and love which are <i>found</i> in Christ Jesus.  
**1:15** It is a trustworthy statement, deserving full acceptance, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, among whom I am foremost.  
**1:16** Yet for this reason I found mercy, so that in me as the foremost <i>sinner</i> Jesus Christ might demonstrate His perfect patience as an example for those <sup>①</sup>who would believe in Him for eternal life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>destined to</i>  
**1:17** Now to the King <sup>①</sup>eternal, immortal, invisible, the only God, <i>be</i> honor and glory <sup>②</sup>forever and ever. Amen.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>to the ages of the ages</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>of the ages</i>  
**1:18** This command I entrust to you, Timothy, <i>my</i> <sup>①</sup>son, in accordance with the prophecies previously made concerning you, that by them you fight the good fight,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>child</i>  
**1:19** keeping faith and a good conscience, which some have rejected and suffered shipwreck in regard to <sup>①</sup>their faith.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the</i>  
**1:20**  <sup>①</sup>Among these are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have handed over to Satan, so that they will be taught not to blaspheme.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Of</i>  

## 2장

**2:1** First of all, then, I urge that requests, prayers, intercession, <i>and</i> thanksgiving be made in behalf of all people,  
**2:2** for kings and all who are in <sup>①</sup>authority, so that we may lead a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness and <sup>②</sup>dignity.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>a high position</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>seriousness</i>  
**2:3** This is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Savior,  
**2:4** who wants all people to be saved and to come to the <sup>①</sup>knowledge of the truth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>recognition</i>  
**2:5** For there is one God, <i>and</i> one mediator also between God and mankind, <i>the</i> man Christ Jesus,  
**2:6** who gave Himself as a ransom for all, the testimony <sup>①</sup><i>given</i> at <sup>②</sup>the proper time.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or to be given <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>its own times</i>  
**2:7** For this I was appointed as a <sup>①</sup>preacher and an apostle (I am telling the truth, I am not lying), as a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>herald</i>  
**2:8** Therefore I want the men in every place to pray, lifting up holy hands, without anger and dispute.  
**2:9** Likewise, <i>I want</i> women to adorn themselves with proper clothing, <sup>①</sup>modestly and discreetly, not with braided hair and gold or pearls or expensive apparel,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>with modesty</i>  
**2:10** but rather by means of good works, as is proper for women making a claim to godliness.  
**2:11** A woman must quietly receive instruction with entire submissiveness.  
**2:12** But I do not allow a woman to teach or to exercise authority over a man, but to remain quiet.  
**2:13** For <i>it was</i> Adam <i>who</i> was first <sup>①</sup>created, <i>and</i> then Eve.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>formed</i>  
**2:14** And <i>it was</i> not Adam <i>who</i> was deceived, but the woman was <sup>①</sup>deceived and <sup>②</sup>became a wrongdoer.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>thoroughly deceived</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>has become</i>  
**2:15** But <sup>①</sup>women will be <sup>②</sup>preserved through <sup>③</sup>childbirth—if they continue in faith, love, and sanctity, with moderation.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>she</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>saved</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>the childbirth;</i> or <i>the bearing of children</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** It is a trustworthy statement: if any man aspires to the office of <sup>①</sup>overseer, <i>it is</i> a fine work he desires <i>to do.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>bishop</i>  
**3:2**  <sup>①</sup>An overseer, then, must be above reproach, the husband of one wife, <sup>②</sup>temperate, self-controlled, respectable, hospitable, skillful in teaching,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>The</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>level-headed</i>  
**3:3** not overindulging in wine, not <sup>①</sup>a bully, but gentle, not contentious, free from the love of money.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>combative</i>  
**3:4** <i>He must be</i> one who manages his own household well, keeping his children under control with all dignity  
**3:5** (but if a man does not know how to manage his own household, how will he take care of the church of God?),  
**3:6** <i>and</i> not a new convert, so that he will not become conceited and fall into condemnation <sup>①</sup>incurred by the devil.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of the devil</i>  
**3:7** And he must have a good reputation with those outside <i>the church,</i> so that he will not fall into <sup>①</sup>disgrace and the snare of the devil.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>reproach</i>  
**3:8** Deacons likewise <i>must be</i> men of dignity, not <sup>①</sup>insincere, not prone to <i>drink</i> much wine, not greedy for money,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>given to double-talk</i>  
**3:9** <i>but</i> holding to the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience.  
**3:10** These men must also first be tested; then have them serve as deacons if they are beyond reproach.  
**3:11**  <sup>①</sup>Women <i>must</i> likewise <i>be</i> dignified, not malicious gossips, but <sup>②</sup>temperate, faithful in all things.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., either deacons’ wives or deaconesses <sup>②</sup>Or <i>level-headed</i>  
**3:12** Deacons must be husbands of one wife, <i>and</i> <sup>①</sup>good managers of <i>their</i> children and their own households.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>managing well</i>  
**3:13** For those who have served well as deacons obtain for themselves a <sup>①</sup>high standing and great confidence in the faith that is in Christ Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>good</i>  
**3:14** I am writing these things to you, hoping to come to you before long;  
**3:15** but <sup>①</sup>in case I am delayed, <i>I write</i> so that you will know how one should act in the household of God, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and support of the truth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>if I delay</i>  
**3:16** Beyond question, great is the mystery of godliness:<br>He who was revealed in the flesh,<br>Was <sup>①</sup>vindicated <sup>②</sup>in the Spirit,<br>Seen by angels,<br>Proclaimed among the nations,<br>Believed on in the world,<br>Taken up in glory.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>justified</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>by</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** But the Spirit explicitly says that in later times some will <sup>①</sup>fall away from the faith, paying attention to deceitful spirits and teachings of demons,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., apostatize  
**4:2** by means of the hypocrisy of liars seared in their own conscience as with a branding iron,  
**4:3** who forbid marriage <i>and advocate</i> abstaining from foods which God has created to be gratefully shared in by those who believe and know the truth.  
**4:4** For everything created by God is good, and nothing is to be rejected if it is received with gratitude;  
**4:5** for it is sanctified by means of the word of God and prayer.  
**4:6** In pointing out these things to the brothers <i>and sisters,</i> you will be a good servant of Christ Jesus, <i>constantly</i> nourished on the words of the faith and of the good doctrine which you have been following.  
**4:7** But <sup>①</sup>stay away from worthless stories that are typical of old women. Rather, <sup>②</sup>discipline yourself for the purpose of godliness;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>reject</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>train</i>  
**4:8** for bodily training is <i>just</i> slightly beneficial, but godliness is beneficial for all things, since it holds promise for the present life and <i>also</i> for the <i>life</i> to come.  
**4:9** It is a trustworthy statement deserving full acceptance.  
**4:10** For it is for this we labor and strive, because we have set our hope on the living God, who is the Savior of all mankind, especially of believers.  
**4:11**  <sup>①</sup>Prescribe and teach these things.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Keep commanding and teaching</i>  
**4:12** Let no one look down on your youthfulness, but <i>rather</i> in speech, conduct, love, faith, <i>and</i> purity, show yourself an example <sup>①</sup>of those who believe.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>to</i>  
**4:13** Until I come, give your attention to the <i>public</i> <sup>①</sup>reading, to exhortation, <i>and</i> teaching.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., of Scripture in the church service  
**4:14** Do not neglect the spiritual gift within you, which was granted to you through <i>words of</i> prophecy with the laying on of hands by the <sup>①</sup>council of elders.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>board of elders</i>  
**4:15** Take pains with these things; be <i>absorbed</i> in them, so that your progress will be evident to all.  
**4:16** Pay close attention to yourself and to the teaching; persevere in these things, for as you do this you will save both yourself and those who hear you.  

## 5장

**5:1** Do not sharply rebuke an older man, but <i>rather</i> appeal to <i>him</i> as a father, <i>and to</i> the younger men as brothers,  
**5:2** to the older women as mothers, <i>and</i> to the younger women as sisters, in all purity.  
**5:3** Honor widows who are actually widows;  
**5:4** but if any widow has children or grandchildren, they must first learn to show proper respect for their own family and to give back compensation to their parents; for this is acceptable in the sight of God.  
**5:5** Now she who is actually a widow and has been left alone has set her hope on God, and she continues in requests and prayers night and day.  
**5:6** But she who indulges herself in luxury is dead, <i>even</i> while she lives.  
**5:7**  <sup>①</sup>Give these instructions as well, so that they may be above reproach.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Keep commanding</i>  
**5:8** But if anyone does not provide for his own, and especially for those of his household, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.  
**5:9** A widow is to be put on the list only if she is not less than sixty years old, <i>having been</i> the wife of one man,  
**5:10** having a reputation for good works; <i>and</i> if she has brought up children, if she has shown hospitality to strangers, if she has washed the <sup>①</sup>saints’ feet, if she has assisted those in distress, <i>and</i> if she has devoted herself to every good work.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**5:11** But refuse <i>to register</i> younger widows, for when they feel physical desires alienating them from Christ, they want to get married,  
**5:12** <i>thereby</i> incurring condemnation, because they have <sup>①</sup>ignored their previous pledge.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>broken</i>  
**5:13** At the same time they also learn <i>to be</i> idle, as they go around from house to house; and not merely idle, but also <i>they become</i> gossips and busybodies, talking about things not proper <i>to mention.</i>  
**5:14** Therefore, I want younger <i>widows</i> to get married, have children, manage their households, <i>and</i> give the enemy no opportunity for reproach;  
**5:15** for some have already turned away to follow Satan.  
**5:16** If any woman who is a believer has <i>dependent</i> widows, she must assist them and the church must not be burdened, so that it may assist those who are actually widows.  
**5:17** The elders who lead well are to be considered worthy of double honor, especially those who work hard <sup>①</sup>at preaching and teaching.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in word</i>  
**5:18** For the Scripture says, “YOU SHALL NOT MUZZLE THE OX WHILE IT IS THRESHING,” and “The laborer is worthy of his wages.”  
**5:19** Do not accept an accusation against an elder except on the basis of two or three witnesses.  
**5:20** Those who continue in sin, rebuke in the presence of all, so that the rest also will be fearful <i>of sinning.</i>  
**5:21** I solemnly exhort you in the presence of God and of Christ Jesus and of <i>His</i> chosen angels, to maintain these <i>principles</i> without bias, doing nothing in a <i>spirit of</i> partiality.  
**5:22** Do not lay hands upon anyone too quickly and <sup>①</sup>thereby share <i>responsibility for</i> the sins of others; keep yourself <sup>②</sup>free from sin.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>do not share</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>pure</i>  
**5:23** Do not go on drinking only water, but use a little wine for the sake of your stomach and your frequent ailments.  
**5:24** The sins of some people are quite evident, going before them to judgment; for others, their <i>sins</i> follow after.  
**5:25** Likewise also, deeds that are good are quite evident, and those which are otherwise cannot be concealed.  

## 6장

**6:1** All who are under the yoke as slaves are to regard their own masters as worthy of all honor so that the name of God and <i>our</i> doctrine will not be <sup>①</sup>spoken against.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>blasphemed</i>  
**6:2** Those who have believers as their masters must not be disrespectful to them because they are brothers <i>or sisters,</i> but must serve them all the more, because those who <sup>①</sup>partake of the benefit are believers and beloved. Teach and <sup>②</sup>preach these <i>principles.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>devote themselves to kindness</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>exhort, urge</i>  
**6:3** If anyone advocates a different doctrine and does not <sup>①</sup>agree with sound words, those of our Lord Jesus Christ, and with the doctrine conforming to godliness,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>come to;</i> or <i>come with</i>  
**6:4** he is conceited <i>and</i> understands nothing; but he <sup>①</sup>has a sick craving for controversial questions and disputes about words, from which come envy, strife, abusive language, evil suspicions,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is sick about</i>  
**6:5** and constant friction between people of depraved mind and deprived of the truth, who suppose that <sup>①</sup>godliness is a means of gain.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>religion</i>  
**6:6** But godliness <i>actually</i> is a means of great gain <i>when</i> accompanied by contentment.  
**6:7** For we have brought nothing into the world, so we cannot take anything out of it, either.  
**6:8** If we have food and <sup>①</sup>covering, with these we shall be content.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., any covering for protection, mainly clothing  
**6:9** But those who want to get rich fall into temptation and a trap, and many foolish and harmful desires which plunge people into ruin and destruction.  
**6:10** For the love of money is a root of all <sup>①</sup>sorts of evil, and some by longing for it have wandered away from the faith and pierced themselves with many <sup>②</sup>griefs.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the evils</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>pains</i>  
**6:11** But flee from these things, you man of God, and pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, <sup>①</sup>perseverance, <i>and</i> gentleness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>steadfastness</i>  
**6:12** Fight the good fight of faith; take hold of the eternal life to which you were called, and <i>for which</i> you made the good confession in the presence of many witnesses.  
**6:13** I direct you in the presence of God, who <sup>①</sup>gives life to all things, and of Christ Jesus, who testified the good confession before Pontius Pilate,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>keeps alive</i>  
**6:14** that you keep the commandment without <sup>①</sup>fault <i>or</i> reproach until the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>spot</i>  
**6:15** which He will <sup>①</sup>bring about at <i>the</i> proper time—He who is the blessed and only Sovereign, the King of <sup>②</sup>kings and Lord of <sup>③</sup>lords,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>show</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>those who reign as kings</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>those who rule as lords</i>  
**6:16** who alone possesses immortality and dwells in unapproachable light, whom <sup>①</sup>no one has seen or can see. To Him <i>be</i> honor and eternal dominion! Amen.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>none of mankind</i>  
**6:17** Instruct those who are rich in this present world not to be conceited or to set their hope on the uncertainty of riches, but on God, who richly supplies us with all things to enjoy.  
**6:18** <i>Instruct them</i> to do good, to be rich in good <sup>①</sup>works, to be generous and ready to share,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>deeds</i>  
**6:19** storing up for themselves the treasure of a good foundation for the future, so that they may take hold of that which is truly life.  
**6:20** Timothy, protect what has been entrusted to you, avoiding worldly, empty chatter and the opposing arguments of what is falsely called “knowledge”—  
**6:21** which some have professed and <i>thereby</i> have gone astray <sup>①</sup>from the faith. <e>G</e>race be with you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>concerning</i>
