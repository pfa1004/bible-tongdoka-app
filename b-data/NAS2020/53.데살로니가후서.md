# 53.데살로니가후서 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, <e>T</e>o the church of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:  
**1:2** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:3** We ought always to give thanks to God for you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> as is <i>only</i> fitting, because your faith is increasing abundantly, and the love of each and every one of you toward one another grows <i>ever</i> greater.  
**1:4** As a result, we ourselves speak proudly of you among the churches of God for your <sup>①</sup>perseverance and faith <sup>②</sup>in the midst of all your persecutions and afflictions which you endure.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>steadfastness</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>during all</i>  
**1:5** <i>This is</i> <sup>①</sup>a plain indication of God’s righteous judgment so that you will be considered worthy of the kingdom of God, for which you indeed are suffering.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>proof of</i>  
**1:6**  <sup>①</sup>For after all it is <i>only</i> right <sup>②</sup>for God to repay with affliction those who afflict you,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>If indeed</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>in the sight of</i>  
**1:7** and <i>to give</i> relief to you who are afflicted, <i>along</i> with us, <sup>①</sup>when the Lord Jesus will be revealed from heaven with <sup>②</sup>His mighty angels<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>at the revelation of the Lord Jesus</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>the angels of His power</i>  
**1:8** in flaming fire, dealing out retribution to those who do not know God, and to those who do not obey the gospel of our Lord Jesus.  
**1:9** These people will pay the penalty of eternal destruction, away from the presence of the Lord and from the glory of His power,  
**1:10** when He comes to be glorified among His <sup>①</sup>saints on that day, and to be marveled at among all who have believed—because our testimony to you was believed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>holy ones;</i> i.e., God’s people  
**1:11** To this end also we pray for you always, that our God will consider you worthy of your calling, and fulfill every desire for goodness and the work of faith with power,  
**1:12** so that the name of our Lord Jesus will be glorified in you, and you in Him, in accordance with the grace of our God and <i>the</i> Lord Jesus Christ.  

## 2장

**2:1** Now we ask you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> regarding the <sup>①</sup>coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering together to Him,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>presence</i>  
**2:2** that you not be quickly shaken from your <sup>①</sup>composure or be disturbed either by a spirit, or a <sup>②</sup>message, or a letter as if from us, to the effect that the day of the Lord has come.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>mind</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>word</i>  
**2:3** No one is to deceive you in any way! For <i>it will not come</i> unless the <sup>①</sup>apostasy comes first, and the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of destruction,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>falling away</i> from the faith  
**2:4** who opposes and exalts himself above <sup>①</sup>every so-called god or object of worship, so that he takes his seat in the temple of God, displaying himself as being God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>everyone who is called God</i>  
**2:5** Do you not remember that while I was still with you, I was telling you these things?  
**2:6** And you know what restrains <i>him</i> now, so that he will be revealed in his time.  
**2:7** For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work; only <sup>①</sup>He who now restrains <i>will do so</i> until <sup>②</sup>He is <sup>③</sup>removed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>he</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>he</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>out of the way</i>  
**2:8** Then that lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord will eliminate with the breath of His mouth and bring to an end by the appearance of His <sup>①</sup>coming;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>presence</i>  
**2:9** <i>that is,</i> the one whose <sup>①</sup>coming is in accord with the activity of Satan, with all power and false <sup>②</sup>signs and wonders,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>presence</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., confirming miracles  
**2:10** and with <sup>①</sup>all the deception of wickedness for those who perish, because they did not accept the love of the truth so as to be saved.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>every deception</i>  
**2:11** For this reason God <sup>①</sup>will send upon them <sup>②</sup>a deluding influence so that they will believe <sup>③</sup>what is false,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is sending</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>an activity of error</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>the lie</i>  
**2:12** in order that they all may be <sup>①</sup>judged who did not believe the truth, but <sup>②</sup>took pleasure in wickedness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>condemned</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>approved</i>  
**2:13** But we should always give thanks to God for you, brothers <i>and sisters</i> beloved by the Lord, because God has chosen you <sup>①</sup>from the beginning for salvation <sup>②</sup>through sanctification <sup>③</sup>by the Spirit and faith in the truth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One early ms <i>first fruits</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>in</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>of</i>  
**2:14** It was for this He called you through our gospel, <sup>①</sup>that you may obtain the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>to the obtaining of</i>  
**2:15** So then, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> stand firm and hold on to the traditions which you were taught, whether by word <i>of mouth</i> or by letter <sup>①</sup>from us.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of</i>  
**2:16** Now may our Lord Jesus Christ Himself and God our Father, who has loved us and given us eternal comfort and good hope by grace,  
**2:17** comfort and strengthen your hearts in every good work and word.  

## 3장

**3:1** Finally, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> pray for us that the word of the Lord will <sup>①</sup>spread rapidly and be glorified, just as <i>it was</i> also with you;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>run</i>  
**3:2** and that we will be rescued from troublesome and evil people; for not all have the faith.  
**3:3** But the Lord is faithful, <sup>①</sup>and He will strengthen and protect you <sup>②</sup>from the evil one.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>who will</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>from evil</i>  
**3:4** We have confidence in the Lord concerning you, that you are doing, and will do, what we command.  
**3:5** May the Lord direct your hearts to the love of God and to the perseverance of Christ.  
**3:6** Now we command you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you <sup>①</sup>keep away from every brother <i>or sister</i> who <sup>②</sup>leads a <sup>③</sup>disorderly life and not <i>one</i> in accordance with the tradition which <sup>④</sup>you received from us.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>avoid</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>walks disorderly</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>undisciplined</i> <sup>④</sup>One early ms <i>they</i>  
**3:7** For you yourselves know how you ought to <sup>①</sup>follow our example, because we did not act in an undisciplined way among you,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>imitate us</i>  
**3:8** nor did we eat <sup>①</sup>anyone’s bread <sup>②</sup>without paying for it, but with labor and hardship we <i>kept</i> working night and day so that we would not be a burden to any of you;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>from anyone</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>freely</i>  
**3:9** not because we do not have the right <i>to this,</i> but in order to offer ourselves as a role model for you, so that you would <sup>①</sup>follow our example.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>imitate us</i>  
**3:10** For even when we were with you, we used to give you this order: if anyone is not willing to work, then he is not to eat, either.  
**3:11** For we hear that some among you are leading an undisciplined life, doing no work at all, but acting like busybodies.  
**3:12** Now we command and exhort such persons in the Lord Jesus Christ to work <sup>①</sup>peacefully and eat their own bread.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>with quietness</i>  
**3:13** But as for you, brothers <i>and sisters,</i> do not grow weary of doing good.  
**3:14** If anyone does not obey our <sup>①</sup>instruction <sup>②</sup>in this letter, take special note of that person <i>so as</i> not to associate with him, so that he will be put to shame.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>word</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>through</i>  
**3:15** And <i>yet</i> do not regard <i>that person</i> as an enemy, but <sup>①</sup>admonish <i>that one</i> as a brother <i>or sister.</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>keep admonishing</i>  
**3:16** Now may the Lord of peace Himself continually grant you peace in every <sup>①</sup>circumstance. The Lord be with you all!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>way</i>  
**3:17**  <sup>①</sup>I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand, and this is a distinguishing mark in every letter; this is the way I write.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>The greeting by my hand of Paul</i>  
**3:18** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all.
