# 64.요한3서 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** The elder to the beloved Gaius, whom I love in truth.  
**1:2** Beloved, I pray that in all respects you may prosper and be in good health, just as your soul prospers.  
**1:3** For I was overjoyed when brothers came and testified to your truth, <i>that is,</i> how you are walking in truth.  
**1:4** I have no greater joy than <sup>①</sup>this, to hear of my children walking in the truth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>these things, that I hear</i>  
**1:5** Beloved, you are acting faithfully in whatever you accomplish for the brothers <i>and sisters,</i> and <sup>①</sup>especially <i>when they are</i> strangers;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>this</i>  
**1:6** and they have testified to your love before the church. You will do well to <sup>①</sup>send them on their way in a manner worthy of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., give them whatever they might need for the journey  
**1:7** For they went out for the sake of the Name, accepting nothing from the Gentiles.  
**1:8** Therefore we ought to <sup>①</sup>support such people, so that we may <sup>②</sup>prove to be fellow workers <sup>③</sup>with the truth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>receive as guests</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>become fellow</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>for</i>  
**1:9** I wrote something to the church; but Diotrephes, who loves to be first among them, does not accept <sup>①</sup>what we say.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>us</i>  
**1:10** For this reason, if I come, I will call attention to his deeds which he does, unjustly accusing us with malicious words; and not satisfied with this, he himself does not receive the brothers either, and he forbids those who want <i>to do so</i> and puts <i>them</i> out of the church.  
**1:11** Beloved, do not imitate what is evil, but what is good. The one who does what is good is of God; the one who does what is evil has not seen God.  
**1:12** Demetrius has received a <i>good</i> testimony from everyone, and from the truth itself; and we testify too, and you know that our testimony is true.  
**1:13** I had many things to write to you, but I do not want to write to you with pen and ink;  
**1:14** but I hope to see you shortly, and we will speak <sup>①</sup>face to face.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>mouth to mouth</i>  
**1:15** Peace <i>be</i> to you. The friends greet you. Greet the friends by name.
