## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD which came to Micah of Moresheth in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, <i>and</i> Hezekiah, kings of Judah, <i>and</i> which he saw regarding Samaria and Jerusalem.

**1:3** For behold, the LORD is coming forth from His place.<br>He will come down and tread on the high places of the <sup>①</sup>earth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>land</i>  
**1:4** The mountains will melt under Him<br>And the valleys will be split,<br>Like wax before the fire,<br>Like water poured down a steep place.  
**1:5** All this is due to the <sup>①</sup>wrongdoing of Jacob<br>And the sins of the house of Israel.<br>What is the <sup>②</sup>wrongdoing of Jacob?<br>Is it not Samaria?<br>What is the high <sup>③</sup>place of Judah?<br>Is it not Jerusalem?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>rebellion</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>rebellion</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>places</i>  
**1:6** For I will make Samaria a heap of ruins <sup>①</sup>in the open country,<br>Planting places for a vineyard.<br>I will hurl her stones down into the valley,<br>And lay bare her foundations.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of the field</i>  
**1:7** All of her idols will be crushed,<br>All of her earnings will be burned with fire,<br>And all of her images I will make desolate;<br>For she collected <i>them</i> from a prostitute’s earnings,<br>And to the earnings of a prostitute they will return.  
**1:8** Because of this I must mourn and wail,<br>I must go barefoot and naked;<br>I must do mourning like the jackals,<br>And a mourning like the ostriches.  
**1:9** For her wound is incurable,<br>For it has come to Judah;<br>It has reached the gate of my people,<br><i>Even</i> to Jerusalem.  
**1:10** Do not tell <i>it</i> in Gath,<br>Do not weep at all.<br>At <sup>①</sup>Beth-le-aphrah roll yourself in the dust in mourning.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., house of dust  
**1:11**  <sup>①</sup>Go on your way, inhabitant of <sup>②</sup>Shaphir, in shameful nakedness.<br>The inhabitant of <sup>③</sup>Zaanan does not <sup>④</sup>escape.<br>The mourning of <sup>⑤</sup>Beth-ezel: “He will take from you its <sup>⑥</sup>support.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., Go into captivity <sup>②</sup>I.e., pleasantness <sup>③</sup>I.e., going out <sup>④</sup>Lit <i>go out</i> <sup>⑤</sup>I.e., the house beside <sup>⑥</sup>Lit <i>standing place</i>  
**1:12** For the inhabitant of <sup>①</sup>Maroth<br> <sup>②</sup>Waits for <i>something</i> good,<br>Because a disaster has come down from the LORD<br>To the gate of Jerusalem.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., bitterness <sup>②</sup>Or possibly <i>has been in agony for</i>  
**1:13** Harness the chariot to the team of horses,<br>You inhabitant of Lachish—<br>She was the beginning of sin<br>To the daughter of Zion—<br>Because in you were found<br>The rebellious acts of Israel.  
**1:14** Therefore you will give parting gifts<br>In behalf of Moresheth-gath;<br>The houses of Achzib <i>will</i> become a <sup>①</sup>deception<br>To the kings of Israel.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>achzab,</i> wordplay on <i>Achzib</i>

**1:16** Shave yourself bald, yes, cut off your hair,<br>Because of the children of your delight;<br>Extend your baldness like the eagle,<br>For they will go from you into exile.  

## 2장

**2:1** Woe to those who devise wrongdoing,<br>Who practice evil on their beds!<br> <sup>①</sup>When morning comes, they do it,<br>Because it is in the power of their hands.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>In the light of the morning</i>  
**2:2** They covet fields, so they seize <i>them;</i><br>And houses, so they take <i>them.</i><br>They <sup>①</sup>exploit a man and his house,<br>A person and his inheritance.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>oppress</i>  
**2:3** Therefore this is what the LORD says:<br>“Behold, I am planning against this family a catastrophe<br>From which you cannot remove your necks;<br>And you will not walk haughtily,<br>For it will be an evil time.  
**2:4** On that day they will take up against you a <sup>①</sup>song of mocking<br>And <sup>②</sup>utter a song of mourning <i>and</i> say,<br>‘We are completely destroyed!<br>He exchanges the share of my people;<br>How He removes it from me!<br>To the apostate He apportions our fields.’<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>proverb</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>lament</i>  
**2:5** Therefore you will have no one <sup>①</sup>applying a measuring line<br>For you by lot in the assembly of the LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>casting</i>  
**2:6** ‘Do not prophesy,’ <i>so</i> they prophesy.<br><i>But if</i> <sup>①</sup>they do not prophesy about these things,<br>Insults will not be turned back.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., God’s prophets  
**2:7** Is it being said, house of Jacob:<br>‘Is the Spirit of the LORD impatient?<br>Are these His works?’<br>Do My words not do good<br>For the one walking rightly?  
**2:8**  <sup>①</sup>Recently My people have arisen as an enemy—<br>You strip the robe off the garment<br>From unsuspecting passers-by,<br><i>From</i> those returned from war.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>And yesterday</i>  
**2:9** You evict the women of My people,<br><i>Each one</i> from her pleasant house.<br>From her children you take My splendor forever.  
**2:10** Arise and go,<br>For this is no place of rest<br>Because of the uncleanness that brings on destruction,<br>A painful destruction.  
**2:11** If someone walking <i>after</i> wind and falsehood<br>Had lied <i>and said,</i><br>‘I will prophesy to you about wine and liquor,’<br>He would become a prophet to this people.  
**2:12** “I will certainly assemble all of you, Jacob,<br>I will certainly gather the remnant of Israel.<br>I will put them together like sheep in the fold;<br>Like a flock in the midst of its pasture<br>They will be noisy with people.  
**2:13** The one who breaks through goes up before them;<br>They break through, pass through the gate, and go out by it.<br>So their king passes on before them,<br>And the LORD at their head.”  

## 3장

**3:1** And I said,<br>“Hear now, you <sup>①</sup>leaders of Jacob<br>And rulers of the house of Israel:<br>Is it not for you to know justice?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>heads</i>  
**3:2** You who hate good and love evil,<br>Who tear off their skin from them<br>And their flesh from their bones,  
**3:3** Who eat the flesh of my people,<br>Strip off their skin from them,<br>Smash their bones,<br>And chop <i>them</i> up as for the pot,<br>And as meat in a cauldron!”  
**3:4** Then they will cry out to the LORD,<br>But He will not answer them.<br>Instead, He will hide His face from them at that time<br>Because they have practiced evil deeds.  
**3:5** This is what the LORD says concerning the prophets who lead my people astray:<br><i>When</i> they have <i>something</i> to bite with their teeth,<br>They cry out, “Peace!”<br>But against him who puts nothing in their mouths<br>They declare holy war.  
**3:6** Therefore <i>it will be</i> night for you—without vision,<br>And darkness for you—without divination.<br>The sun will go down on the prophets,<br>And the day will become dark over them.  
**3:7** The seers will be put to shame,<br>And the diviners will be ashamed.<br>Indeed, they will all cover <i>their</i> <sup>①</sup>lips<br>Because there is no answer from God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>mustache</i>  
**3:8** On the other hand, I am filled with power—<br>With the Spirit of the LORD—<br>And with justice and courage<br>To make known to Jacob his rebellious act,<br>And to Israel his sin.  
**3:9** Now hear this, you heads of the house of Jacob<br>And rulers of the house of Israel,<br>Who despise justice<br>And twist everything that is straight,  
**3:10** Who build Zion with bloodshed,<br>And Jerusalem with malice.  
**3:11** Her leaders pronounce judgment for a bribe,<br>Her priests teach for pay,<br>And her prophets divine for money.<br>Yet they lean on the LORD, saying,<br>“Is the LORD not in our midst?<br>Catastrophe will not come upon us.”  
**3:12** Therefore on account of you,<br>Zion will be plowed <i>like</i> a field,<br>Jerusalem will become a heap of ruins,<br>And the mountain of the <sup>①</sup>temple <i>will become</i> high places of a forest.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>house</i>

**4:13** Arise and thresh, daughter of Zion,<br>For I will make your horn iron,<br>And I will make your hoofs bronze,<br>So that you may pulverize many peoples,<br>And dedicate to the LORD their unjust profit,<br>And their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.
