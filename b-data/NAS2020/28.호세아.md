## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD which came to Hosea the son of Beeri, during the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, <i>and</i> Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and during the days of Jeroboam the son of Joash, king of Israel.  
**1:2** When the LORD first spoke through Hosea, the LORD said to Hosea, “Go, take for yourself a wife <sup>①</sup>inclined to infidelity, and children of infidelity; for the land commits flagrant infidelity, <sup>②</sup>abandoning the LORD.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of infidelity;</i> or<i>fornication</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>away from</i>  
**1:3** So he went and took Gomer the daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore him a son.  
**1:4** And the LORD said to him, “Name him Jezreel; for in just a little while I will <sup>①</sup>punish the house of Jehu for the bloodshed of Jezreel, and I will put an end to the kingdom of the house of Israel.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>visit the bloodshed of Jezreel on the house of Jehu</i>  
**1:5** On that day I will break the bow of Israel in the Valley of Jezreel.”  
**1:6** Then she conceived again and gave birth to a daughter. And <sup>①</sup>the LORD said to him, “Name her <sup>②</sup>Lo-ruhamah, for I will no longer take pity on the house of Israel, that I would ever forgive them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>He</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., not having obtained mercy  
**1:7** But I will take pity on the house of Judah and save them by the LORD their God, and will not save them by bow, sword, battle, horses, or horsemen.”  
**1:8** When she had weaned Lo-ruhamah, she conceived and gave birth to a son.  
**1:9** And <sup>①</sup>the LORD said, “Name him <sup>②</sup>Lo-ammi, because you are not My people, and I am not <sup>③</sup>your God.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>He</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., not my people <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>yours</i>  
**1:10**  <sup>①</sup>Yet the number of the sons of Israel<br>Will be like the sand of the sea,<br>Which cannot be measured or counted;<br>And in the place<br>Where it is said to them,<br>“You are not My people,”<br>It will be said to them,<br>“<i>You are</i> the sons of the living God.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch 2:1 in Heb  
**1:11** And the sons of Judah and the sons of Israel will be gathered together,<br>And they will appoint for themselves one leader,<br>And they will go up from the land,<br>For the day of Jezreel <i>will be</i> great.

**2:18** On that day I will also make a covenant for them<br>With the animals of the field,<br>The birds of the sky,<br>And the crawling things of the ground.<br>And I will <sup>①</sup>eliminate the bow, the sword, and war from the land,<br>And will let them lie down in safety.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>break</i>

**2:21** “And it will come about on that day that I will respond,” declares the LORD.<br>“I will respond to the heavens, and they will respond to the earth,  
**2:22** And the earth will respond to the grain, to the new wine, and to the oil,<br>And they will respond to <sup>①</sup>Jezreel.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., God sows  
**2:23** I will sow her for Myself in the land.<br>I will also have compassion on <sup>①</sup>her who had not obtained compassion,<br>And I will say to <sup>②</sup>those who were not My people,<br>‘You are My people!’<br>And <sup>③</sup>they will say, ‘<i>You are</i> my God!’ ”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>Lo-ruhamah</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>Lo-ammi</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>he</i>
