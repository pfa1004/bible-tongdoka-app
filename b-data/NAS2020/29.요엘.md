# 29.요엘 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Joel, the son of Pethuel:  
**1:2** Hear this, you elders,<br>And listen, all inhabitants of the land.<br>Has <i>anything like</i> this happened in your days,<br>Or in your fathers’ days?  
**1:3** Tell your sons about it,<br>And <i>have</i> your sons <i>tell</i> their sons,<br>And their sons the next generation.  
**1:4** What the gnawing locust has left, the <sup>①</sup>swarming locust has eaten;<br>And what the swarming locust has left, the creeping locust has eaten;<br>And what the creeping locust has left, the stripping locust has eaten.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>migratory</i>  
**1:5** Awake, you heavy drinkers, and weep;<br>And wail, all you wine drinkers,<br>Because of the sweet wine,<br>For it has been eliminated from your mouth.  
**1:6** For a nation has <sup>①</sup>invaded my land,<br>Mighty and without number;<br>Its teeth are the teeth of a lion,<br>And it has the jaws of a lioness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>gone up against</i>  
**1:7** It has made my vine a waste<br>And my fig tree a stump.<br>It has stripped them bare and hurled <i>them</i> away;<br>Their branches have become white.  
**1:8** Wail like a virgin clothed with sackcloth<br>For the groom of her youth.  
**1:9** The grain offering and the drink offering have been cut off<br>From the house of the LORD.<br>The priests mourn,<br>The ministers of the LORD.  
**1:10** The field is ruined,<br>The land mourns;<br>For the grain is ruined,<br>The new wine has dried up,<br>Fresh oil has <sup>①</sup>failed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>dried out</i>  
**1:11** Be ashamed, you farm workers,<br>Wail, you vinedressers,<br>For the wheat and the barley;<br>Because the harvest of the field is destroyed.  
**1:12** The vine has dried up<br>And the fig tree has withered;<br>The pomegranate, the palm also, and the apple tree,<br>All the trees of the field have dried up.<br>Indeed, joy has dried up<br>From the sons of mankind.  
**1:13** Put on <i>sackcloth</i><br>And mourn, you priests;<br>Wail, you ministers of the altar!<br>Come, spend the night in sackcloth,<br>You ministers of my God,<br>For the grain offering and the drink offering<br>Have been withheld from the house of your God.  
**1:14** Consecrate a fast,<br>Proclaim a solemn assembly;<br>Gather the elders<br><i>And</i> all the inhabitants of the land<br>To the house of the LORD your God,<br>And cry out to the LORD.  
**1:15** Woe for the day!<br>For the day of the LORD is near,<br>And it will come as destruction from the <sup>①</sup>Almighty.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>Shaddai</i>  
**1:16** Has food not been cut off before our eyes, <i>and</i><br>Joy and rejoicing from the house of our God?  
**1:17** The <sup>①</sup>seeds have dried up under their shovels;<br>The storehouses have become desolate,<br>The grain silos are ruined,<br>Because the grain has dried up.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>dried figs</i>  
**1:18** How the animals have groaned!<br>The herds of cattle have wandered aimlessly<br>Because there is no pasture for them;<br>Even the flocks of sheep have <sup>①</sup>suffered.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>suffered punishment</i>  
**1:19** To You, LORD, I cry out;<br>For fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness,<br>And the flame has burned up all the trees of the field.  
**1:20** Even the animals of the field <sup>①</sup>pant for You;<br>For the stream beds of water are dried up,<br>And fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>long for</i>  

## 2장

**2:1** Blow a trumpet in Zion,<br>And sound an alarm on My holy mountain!<br>Let all the inhabitants of the land tremble,<br>For the day of the LORD is coming;<br>Indeed, it is near,  
**2:2** A day of darkness and gloom,<br>A day of clouds and thick darkness.<br>As dawn is spread over the mountains,<br><i>So</i> there is a great and mighty people;<br>There has never been <i>anything</i> like it,<br>Nor will there be again after it<br>To the years of many generations.  
**2:3** A fire consumes before them,<br>And behind them a flame devours.<br>The land is like the Garden of Eden before them,<br>But a desolate wilderness behind them,<br>And nothing at all escapes them.  
**2:4** Their appearance is like the appearance of horses;<br>And like war horses, so they run.  
**2:5**  <sup>①</sup>With a noise as of chariots<br>They leap about on the tops of the mountains,<br>Like the <sup>②</sup>crackling of a flame of fire consuming the stubble,<br>Like a mighty people drawn up for battle.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Like the noise of chariots</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>sound</i>  
**2:6** Before them the people are in anguish;<br>All faces <sup>①</sup>turn pale.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>become flushed</i>  
**2:7** They run like warriors,<br>They climb the wall like soldiers;<br>And each of them marches <sup>①</sup>in line,<br>Nor do they lose their way.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in his ways</i>  
**2:8** They do not crowd each other,<br>Every warrior of them marches in his path;<br>When they <sup>①</sup>burst through the <sup>②</sup>defenses,<br>They do not break ranks.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>fall</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>weapon,</i> prob. <i>javelin</i>  
**2:9** They storm the city,<br>They run on the wall;<br>They climb into the houses,<br>They enter through the windows like a thief.  
**2:10** Before them the earth quakes,<br>The heavens tremble,<br>The sun and the moon become dark,<br>And the stars lose their brightness.  
**2:11** The LORD utters His voice before His army;<br>His camp is indeed very great,<br>For mighty is one who carries out His word.<br>The day of the LORD is indeed great and very awesome,<br>And who can endure it?  
**2:12** “Yet even now,” declares the LORD,<br>“Return to Me with all your heart,<br>And with fasting, weeping, and mourning;  
**2:13** And tear your heart and not <i>merely</i> your garments.”<br>Now return to the LORD your God,<br>For He is gracious and compassionate,<br>Slow to anger, abounding in mercy<br>And relenting of catastrophe.  
**2:14** Who knows, He might turn and relent,<br>And leave a blessing behind Him,<br><i>Resulting in</i> a grain offering and a drink offering<br>For the LORD your God.  
**2:15** Blow a trumpet in Zion,<br>Consecrate a fast, proclaim a solemn assembly,  
**2:16** Gather the people, sanctify the congregation,<br>Assemble the elders,<br>Gather the children and the nursing infants.<br>Have the groom come out of his room<br>And the bride out of her bridal chamber.  
**2:17** Let the priests, the LORD’S ministers,<br>Weep between the porch and the altar,<br>And let them say, “ <sup>①</sup>Spare Your people, LORD,<br>And do not make Your inheritance a disgrace,<br>With the nations jeering at them.<br>Why should <i>those</i> among the peoples say,<br>‘Where is their God?’ ”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Look compassionately on</i>  
**2:18** Then the LORD <sup>①</sup>will be zealous for His land,<br>And <sup>②</sup>will have compassion for His people.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>was zealous</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>had compassion</i>  
**2:19** The LORD <sup>①</sup>will answer and say to His people,<br>“Behold, I am going to send you grain, new wine, and oil,<br>And you will be satisfied <i>in full</i> with <sup>②</sup>them;<br>And I will never again make you a disgrace among the nations.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>answered and said</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>it</i>  
**2:20** But I will remove the northern <i>army</i> far from you,<br>And I will drive it into a dry and desolate land,<br>Its advance guard into the eastern sea,<br>And its rear guard into the western sea.<br>And its stench will ascend and its odor of decay will come up,<br>Because it has done great things.”  
**2:21** Do not fear, land; shout for joy and rejoice,<br>For the LORD has done great things.  
**2:22** Do not fear, animals of the field,<br>For the pastures of the wilderness have turned green,<br>For the tree has produced its fruit,<br>The fig tree and the vine have yielded <sup>①</sup>in full.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>their wealth</i>  
**2:23** So shout for joy, you sons of Zion,<br>And rejoice in the LORD your God;<br>For He has given you <sup>①</sup>the early rain for <i>your</i> vindication.<br>And He has brought down for you the rain,<br>The <sup>②</sup>early and <sup>③</sup>latter rain <sup>④</sup>as before.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., autumn; or possibly <i>the teacher for righteousness</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., autumn <sup>③</sup>I.e., spring <sup>④</sup>As in ancient versions; MT <i>in the first</i>  
**2:24** The threshing floors will be full of grain,<br>And the vats will overflow with the new wine and oil.  
**2:25** “Then I will compensate you for the years<br>That the <sup>①</sup>swarming locust has eaten,<br>The creeping locust, the stripping locust, and the gnawing locust—<br>My great army which I sent among you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>migratory</i>  
**2:26** You will have plenty to eat and be satisfied,<br>And you will praise the name of the LORD your God,<br>Who has dealt wondrously with you;<br>Then My people will never be put to shame.  
**2:27** So you will know that I am in the midst of Israel,<br>And that I am the LORD your God<br>And there is no other;<br>And My people will never be put to shame.  
**2:28** “ <sup>①</sup>It will come about after this<br>That I will pour out My Spirit on all <sup>②</sup>mankind;<br>And your sons and your daughters will prophesy,<br>Your old men will have dreams,<br>Your young men will see visions.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch 3:1 in Heb <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>flesh</i>  
**2:29** And even on the male and female servants<br>I will pour out My Spirit in those days.  
**2:30** I will <sup>①</sup>display wonders in the sky and on the earth,<br>Blood, fire, and columns of smoke.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>give</i>  
**2:31** The sun will be turned into darkness,<br>And the moon into blood,<br>Before the great and awesome day of the LORD comes.  
**2:32** And it will come about <i>that</i> everyone who calls on the name of the LORD<br>Will <sup>①</sup>be saved;<br>For on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem<br>There will be those who escape,<br>Just as the LORD has said,<br>Even among the survivors whom the LORD calls.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>flee to safety</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** “ <sup>①</sup>For behold, in those days and at that time,<br>When I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch 4:1 in Heb  
**3:2** I will gather all the nations<br>And bring them down to the Valley of <sup>①</sup>Jehoshaphat.<br>Then I will enter into judgment with them there<br>On behalf of My people and My inheritance, Israel,<br>Whom they have scattered among the nations;<br>And they have divided up My land.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., YHWH judges  
**3:3** They have also cast lots for My people,<br> <sup>①</sup>Traded a boy for a prostitute,<br>And sold a girl for wine so that they may drink.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Given</i>  
**3:4** Moreover, what are you to Me, Tyre, Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Are you repaying Me with retribution? But if you are showing Me <i>retribution,</i> swiftly and speedily I will return your retribution on your head!  
**3:5** Since you have taken My silver and My gold, brought My precious <sup>①</sup>treasures to your temples,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>goodly things</i>  
**3:6** and sold the sons of Judah and Jerusalem to the <sup>①</sup>Greeks in order to remove them far from their territory,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>sons of Javan</i>  
**3:7** behold, I am going to stir them up from the place where you have sold them, and return your retribution on your head.  
**3:8** I will also sell your sons and your daughters into the hand of the sons of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans, to a distant nation,” for the LORD has spoken.  
**3:9** Proclaim this among the nations:<br>Prepare for holy war; stir up the warriors!<br>Have all the soldiers come forward, have them come up!  
**3:10** Beat your plowshares into swords,<br>And your pruning hooks into spears;<br>Let the weak <i>man</i> say, “I am a warrior.”  
**3:11**  <sup>①</sup>Hurry and come, all you surrounding nations,<br>And gather yourselves there.<br>Bring down, LORD, Your warriors.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Come help</i>  
**3:12** Let the nations be awakened<br>And come up to the Valley of <sup>①</sup>Jehoshaphat,<br>For there I will sit to judge<br>All the surrounding nations.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., YHWH judges  
**3:13** Put in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe.<br>Come, tread <i>the grapes,</i> for the wine press is full;<br>The vats overflow, for their wickedness is great.  
**3:14** Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of <sup>①</sup>decision!<br>For the day of the LORD is near in the valley of <sup>②</sup>decision.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e., God’s verdict <sup>②</sup>I.e., God’s verdict  
**3:15** The sun and moon have become dark,<br>And the stars have lost their brightness.  
**3:16** The LORD roars from Zion<br>And utters His voice from Jerusalem,<br>And the heavens and the earth quake.<br>But the LORD is a refuge for His people,<br>And a stronghold for the sons of Israel.  
**3:17** Then you will know that I am the LORD your God,<br>Dwelling on Zion, My holy mountain.<br>So Jerusalem will be holy,<br>And strangers will no longer pass through it.  
**3:18** And on that day<br>The mountains will drip with <sup>①</sup>sweet wine,<br>And the hills will flow with milk,<br>And all the brooks of Judah will flow with water;<br>And a spring will go out from the house of the LORD<br>And water the Valley of <sup>②</sup>Shittim.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>freshly pressed-out grape juice</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>acacias</i>  
**3:19** Egypt will become a wasteland,<br>And Edom will become a desolate wilderness,<br>Because of the violence <sup>①</sup>done to the sons of Judah,<br>In whose land they have shed innocent blood.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>of the sons</i>  
**3:20** But Judah will be inhabited forever,<br>And Jerusalem for all generations.  
**3:21** And I will avenge their blood <i>which</i> I have not avenged,<br>For the LORD dwells in Zion.
