# 63.요한2서 (NAS2020)



## 1장

**1:1** The elder to the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in truth; and not only I, but also all who know the truth,  
**1:2** because of the truth which remains in us and will be with us forever:  
**1:3** Grace, mercy, <i>and</i> peace will be with us, from God the Father and from Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.  
**1:4** I was overjoyed to find <i>some</i> of your children walking in truth, just as we have received a commandment <i>to do</i> from the Father.  
**1:5** Now I ask you, lady, not as though <i>I were</i> writing to you a new commandment, but the one which we have had from the beginning, that we love one another.  
**1:6** And this is love, that we walk according to His commandments. This is the commandment, just as you have heard from the beginning, that you are to walk in it.  
**1:7** For many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who do not acknowledge Jesus Christ <i>as</i> coming in the flesh. This is the deceiver and the antichrist.  
**1:8** Watch yourselves, that you do not lose what we have accomplished, but <i>that</i> you may receive a full reward.  
**1:9**  <sup>①</sup>Anyone who <sup>②</sup>goes too far and does not remain in the teaching of Christ, does not have God; the one who remains in the teaching has both the Father and the Son.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Everyone</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>goes on ahead</i>  
**1:10** If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive him into <i>your</i> house, and do not <sup>①</sup>give him a greeting;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>say to him</i>  
**1:11** for the one who <sup>①</sup>gives him a greeting <sup>②</sup>participates in his evil deeds.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>says to him</i> <sup>②</sup>I.e., shares responsibility for them  
**1:12** Though I have many things to write to you, I do not want to <i>do so</i> with paper and ink; but I hope to come to you and speak <sup>①</sup>face to face, so that <sup>②</sup>your joy may be made complete.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>mouth to mouth</i> <sup>②</sup>One early ms <i>our</i>  
**1:13** The children of your chosen sister greet you.
