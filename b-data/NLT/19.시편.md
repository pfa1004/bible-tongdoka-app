# 19.시편 (NLT)



## 1장

**1:1** Oh, the joys of those who do not<br>follow the advice of the wicked,<br>or stand around with sinners,<br>or join in with mockers.  
**1:2** But they delight in the law of the LORD,<br>meditating on it day and night.  
**1:3** They are like trees planted along the riverbank,<br>bearing fruit each season.<br>Their leaves never wither,<br>and they prosper in all they do.  
**1:4** But not the wicked!<br>They are like worthless chaff, scattered by the wind.  
**1:5** They will be condemned at the time of judgment.<br>Sinners will have no place among the godly.  
**1:6** For the LORD watches over the path of the godly,<br>but the path of the wicked leads to destruction.  

## 2장

**2:1** Why are the nations so angry?<br>Why do they waste their time with futile plans?  
**2:2** The kings of the earth prepare for battle;<br>the rulers plot together<br>against the LORD<br>and against his anointed one.  
**2:3** “Let us break their chains,” they cry,<br>“and free ourselves from slavery to God.”  
**2:4** But the one who rules in heaven laughs.<br>The Lord scoffs at them.  
**2:5** Then in anger he rebukes them,<br>terrifying them with his fierce fury.  
**2:6** For the Lord declares, “I have placed my chosen king on the throne<br>in Jerusalem,<sup>①</sup> on my holy mountain.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>on Zion.</i>  
**2:7** The king proclaims the LORD’s decree:<br>“The LORD said to me, ‘You are my son.<sup>①</sup><br>Today I have become your Father.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Son;</i> also in <a name='B:230 2:12'>2:12. <sup>②</sup>Or <i>Today I reveal you as my son.</i>  
**2:8** Only ask, and I will give you the nations as your inheritance,<br>the whole earth as your possession.  
**2:9** You will break<sup>①</sup> them with an iron rod<br>and smash them like clay pots.’”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek version reads <i>rule.</i> Compare <a name='B:730 2:27'>Rev 2:27.  
**2:10** Now then, you kings, act wisely!<br>Be warned, you rulers of the earth!  
**2:11** Serve the LORD with reverent fear,<br>and rejoice with trembling.  
**2:12** Submit to God’s royal son,<sup>①</sup> or he will become angry,<br>and you will be destroyed in the midst of all your activities—<br>for his anger flares up in an instant.<br>But what joy for all who take refuge in him!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain.  

## 3장

**3:1** [A psalm of David, regarding the time David fled from his son Absalom.]<br>O LORD, I have so many enemies;<br>so many are against me.  
**3:2** So many are saying,<br>“God will never rescue him!” <i>Interlude<sup>①</sup></i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Selah.</i> The meaning of this word is uncertain, though it is probably a musical or literary term. It is rendered <i>Interlude</i> throughout the Psalms.  
**3:3** But you, O LORD, are a shield around me;<br>you are my glory, the one who holds my head high.  
**3:4** I cried out to the LORD,<br>and he answered me from his holy mountain. <i>Interlude</i>  
**3:5** I lay down and slept,<br>yet I woke up in safety,<br>for the LORD was watching over me.  
**3:6** I am not afraid of ten thousand enemies<br>who surround me on every side.  
**3:7** Arise, O LORD!<br>Rescue me, my God!<br>Slap all my enemies in the face!<br>Shatter the teeth of the wicked!  
**3:8** Victory comes from you, O LORD.<br>May you bless your people. <i>Interlude</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** [For the choir director: A psalm of David, to be accompanied by stringed instruments.]<br>Answer me when I call to you,<br>O God who declares me innocent.<br>Free me from my troubles.<br>Have mercy on me and hear my prayer.  
**4:2** How long will you people ruin my reputation?<br>How long will you make groundless accusations?<br>How long will you continue your lies? <i>Interlude</i>  
**4:3** You can be sure of this:<br>The LORD set apart the godly for himself.<br>The LORD will answer when I call to him.  
**4:4** Don’t sin by letting anger control you.<br>Think about it overnight and remain silent. <i>Interlude</i>  
**4:5** Offer sacrifices in the right spirit,<br>and trust the LORD.  
**4:6** Many people say, “Who will show us better times?”<br>Let your face smile on us, LORD.  
**4:7** You have given me greater joy<br>than those who have abundant harvests of grain and new wine.  
**4:8** In peace I will lie down and sleep,<br>for you alone, O LORD, will keep me safe.  

## 5장

**5:1** [For the choir director: A psalm of David, to be accompanied by the flute.]<br>O LORD, hear me as I pray;<br>pay attention to my groaning.  
**5:2** Listen to my cry for help, my King and my God,<br>for I pray to no one but you.  
**5:3** Listen to my voice in the morning, LORD.<br>Each morning I bring my requests to you and wait expectantly.  
**5:4** O God, you take no pleasure in wickedness;<br>you cannot tolerate the sins of the wicked.  
**5:5** Therefore, the proud may not stand in your presence,<br>for you hate all who do evil.  
**5:6** You will destroy those who tell lies.<br>The LORD detests murderers and deceivers.  
**5:7** Because of your unfailing love, I can enter your house;<br>I will worship at your Temple with deepest awe.  
**5:8** Lead me in the right path, O LORD,<br>or my enemies will conquer me.<br>Make your way plain for me to follow.  
**5:9** My enemies cannot speak a truthful word.<br>Their deepest desire is to destroy others.<br>Their talk is foul, like the stench from an open grave.<br>Their tongues are filled with flattery.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek version reads <i>with lies.</i> Compare <a name='B:520 3:13'>Rom 3:13.  
**5:10** O God, declare them guilty.<br>Let them be caught in their own traps.<br>Drive them away because of their many sins,<br>for they have rebelled against you.  
**5:11** But let all who take refuge in you rejoice;<br>let them sing joyful praises forever.<br>Spread your protection over them,<br>that all who love your name may be filled with joy.  
**5:12** For you bless the godly, O LORD;<br>you surround them with your shield of love.

## 7장

**7:6** Arise, O LORD, in anger!<br>Stand up against the fury of my enemies!<br>Wake up, my God, and bring justice!  
**7:7** Gather the nations before you.<br>Rule over them from on high.  
**7:8** The LORD judges the nations.<br>Declare me righteous, O LORD,<br>for I am innocent, O Most High!  
**7:9** End the evil of those who are wicked,<br>and defend the righteous.<br>For you look deep within the mind and heart,<br>O righteous God.  
**7:10** God is my shield,<br>saving those whose hearts are true and right.  
**7:11** God is an honest judge.<br>He is angry with the wicked every day.  
**7:12** If a person does not repent,<br>God<sup>①</sup> will sharpen his sword;<br>he will bend and string his bow.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>he.</i>  
**7:13** He will prepare his deadly weapons<br>and shoot his flaming arrows.  
**7:14** The wicked conceive evil;<br>they are pregnant with trouble<br>and give birth to lies.  
**7:15** They dig a deep pit to trap others,<br>then fall into it themselves.  
**7:16** The trouble they make for others backfires on them.<br>The violence they plan falls on their own heads.  
**7:17** I will thank the LORD because he is just;<br>I will sing praise to the name of the LORD Most High.

## 10장

**10:1** O LORD, why do you stand so far away?<br>Why do you hide when I am in trouble?  
**10:2** The wicked arrogantly hunt down the poor.<br>Let them be caught in the evil they plan for others.  
**10:3** For they brag about their evil desires;<br>they praise the greedy and curse the LORD.  
**10:4** The wicked are too proud to seek God.<br>They seem to think that God is dead.  
**10:5** Yet they succeed in everything they do.<br>They do not see your punishment awaiting them.<br>They sneer at all their enemies.  
**10:6** They think, “Nothing bad will ever happen to us!<br>We will be free of trouble forever!”  
**10:7** Their mouths are full of cursing, lies, and threats.<sup>①</sup><br>Trouble and evil are on the tips of their tongues.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek version reads <i>cursing and bitterness.</i> Compare <a name='B:520 3:14'>Rom 3:14.  
**10:8** They lurk in ambush in the villages,<br>waiting to murder innocent people.<br>They are always searching for helpless victims.  
**10:9** Like lions crouched in hiding,<br>they wait to pounce on the helpless.<br>Like hunters they capture the helpless<br>and drag them away in nets.  
**10:10** Their helpless victims are crushed;<br>they fall beneath the strength of the wicked.  
**10:11** The wicked think, “God isn’t watching us!<br>He has closed his eyes and won’t even see what we do!”  
**10:12** Arise, O LORD!<br>Punish the wicked, O God!<br>Do not ignore the helpless!  
**10:13** Why do the wicked get away with despising God?<br>They think, “God will never call us to account.”  
**10:14** But you see the trouble and grief they cause.<br>You take note of it and punish them.<br>The helpless put their trust in you.<br>You defend the orphans.  
**10:15** Break the arms of these wicked, evil people!<br>Go after them until the last one is destroyed.  
**10:16** The LORD is king forever and ever!<br>The godless nations will vanish from the land.  
**10:17** LORD, you know the hopes of the helpless.<br>Surely you will hear their cries and comfort them.  
**10:18** You will bring justice to the orphans and the oppressed,<br>so mere people can no longer terrify them.

**44:3** They did not conquer the land with their swords;<br>it was not their own strong arm that gave them victory.<br>It was your right hand and strong arm<br>and the blinding light from your face that helped them,<br>for you loved them.  
**44:4** You are my King and my God.<br>You command victories for Israel.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>for Jacob.</i> The names “Jacob” and “Israel” are often interchanged throughout the Old Testament, referring sometimes to the individual patriarch and sometimes to the nation.

**44:6** I do not trust in my bow;<br>I do not count on my sword to save me.

**44:14** You have made us the butt of their jokes;<br>they shake their heads at us in scorn.

**44:18** Our hearts have not deserted you.<br>We have not strayed from your path.
