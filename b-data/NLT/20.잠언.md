# 20.잠언 (NLT)



## 1장

**1:1** These are the proverbs of Solomon, David’s son, king of Israel.  
**1:2** Their purpose is to teach people wisdom and discipline,<br>to help them understand the insights of the wise.  
**1:3** Their purpose is to teach people to live disciplined and successful lives,<br>to help them do what is right, just, and fair.  
**1:4** These proverbs will give insight to the simple,<br>knowledge and discernment to the young.  
**1:5** Let the wise listen to these proverbs and become even wiser.<br>Let those with understanding receive guidance  
**1:6** by exploring the meaning in these proverbs and parables,<br>the words of the wise and their riddles.  
**1:7** Fear of the LORD is the foundation of true knowledge,<br>but fools despise wisdom and discipline.  
**1:8** My child,<sup>①</sup> listen when your father corrects you.<br>Don’t neglect your mother’s instruction.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>My son;</i> also in <a name='B:240 1:10'>1:10, <a name='B:240 1:15'>15.  
**1:9** What you learn from them will crown you with grace<br>and be a chain of honor around your neck.  
**1:10** My child, if sinners entice you,<br>turn your back on them!  
**1:11** They may say, “Come and join us.<br>Let’s hide and kill someone!<br>Just for fun, let’s ambush the innocent!  
**1:12** Let’s swallow them alive, like the grave<sup>①</sup>;<br>let’s swallow them whole, like those who go down to the pit of death.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>like Sheol.</i>  
**1:13** Think of the great things we’ll get!<br>We’ll fill our houses with all the stuff we take.  
**1:14** Come, throw in your lot with us;<br>we’ll all share the loot.”  
**1:15** My child, don’t go along with them!<br>Stay far away from their paths.  
**1:16** They rush to commit evil deeds.<br>They hurry to commit murder.  
**1:17** If a bird sees a trap being set,<br>it knows to stay away.  
**1:18** But these people set an ambush for themselves;<br>they are trying to get themselves killed.  
**1:19** Such is the fate of all who are greedy for money;<br>it robs them of life.  
**1:20** Wisdom shouts in the streets.<br>She cries out in the public square.  
**1:21** She calls to the crowds along the main street,<br>to those gathered in front of the city gate:  
**1:22** “How long, you simpletons,<br>will you insist on being simpleminded?<br>How long will you mockers relish your mocking?<br>How long will you fools hate knowledge?  
**1:23** Come and listen to my counsel.<br>I’ll share my heart with you<br>and make you wise.  
**1:24** “I called you so often, but you wouldn’t come.<br>I reached out to you, but you paid no attention.  
**1:25** You ignored my advice<br>and rejected the correction I offered.  
**1:26** So I will laugh when you are in trouble!<br>I will mock you when disaster overtakes you—  
**1:27** when calamity overtakes you like a storm,<br>when disaster engulfs you like a cyclone,<br>and anguish and distress overwhelm you.  
**1:28** “When they cry for help, I will not answer.<br>Though they anxiously search for me, they will not find me.  
**1:29** For they hated knowledge<br>and chose not to fear the LORD.  
**1:30** They rejected my advice<br>and paid no attention when I corrected them.  
**1:31** Therefore, they must eat the bitter fruit of living their own way,<br>choking on their own schemes.  
**1:32** For simpletons turn away from me—to death.<br>Fools are destroyed by their own complacency.  
**1:33** But all who listen to me will live in peace,<br>untroubled by fear of harm.”  

## 2장

**2:1** My child,<sup>①</sup> listen to what I say,<br>and treasure my commands.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>My son.</i>  
**2:2** Tune your ears to wisdom,<br>and concentrate on understanding.  
**2:3** Cry out for insight,<br>and ask for understanding.  
**2:4** Search for them as you would for silver;<br>seek them like hidden treasures.  
**2:5** Then you will understand what it means to fear the LORD,<br>and you will gain knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For the LORD grants wisdom!<br>From his mouth come knowledge and understanding.  
**2:7** He grants a treasure of common sense to the honest.<br>He is a shield to those who walk with integrity.  
**2:8** He guards the paths of the just<br>and protects those who are faithful to him.  
**2:9** Then you will understand what is right, just, and fair,<br>and you will find the right way to go.  
**2:10** For wisdom will enter your heart,<br>and knowledge will fill you with joy.  
**2:11** Wise choices will watch over you.<br>Understanding will keep you safe.  
**2:12** Wisdom will save you from evil people,<br>from those whose words are twisted.  
**2:13** These men turn from the right way<br>to walk down dark paths.  
**2:14** They take pleasure in doing wrong,<br>and they enjoy the twisted ways of evil.  
**2:15** Their actions are crooked,<br>and their ways are wrong.  
**2:16** Wisdom will save you from the immoral woman,<br>from the seductive words of the promiscuous woman.  
**2:17** She has abandoned her husband<br>and ignores the covenant she made before God.  
**2:18** Entering her house leads to death;<br>it is the road to the grave.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>to the spirits of the dead.</i>  
**2:19** The man who visits her is doomed.<br>He will never reach the paths of life.  
**2:20** So follow the steps of the good,<br>and stay on the paths of the righteous.  
**2:21** For only the godly will live in the land,<br>and those with integrity will remain in it.  
**2:22** But the wicked will be removed from the land,<br>and the treacherous will be uprooted.  

## 3장

**3:1** My child,<sup>①</sup> never forget the things I have taught you.<br>Store my commands in your heart.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>My son;</i> also in <a name='B:240 3:11'>3:11, <a name='B:240 3:21'>21.  
**3:2** If you do this, you will live many years,<br>and your life will be satisfying.  
**3:3** Never let loyalty and kindness leave you!<br>Tie them around your neck as a reminder.<br>Write them deep within your heart.  
**3:4** Then you will find favor with both God and people,<br>and you will earn a good reputation.  
**3:5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart;<br>do not depend on your own understanding.  
**3:6** Seek his will in all you do,<br>and he will show you which path to take.  
**3:7** Don’t be impressed with your own wisdom.<br>Instead, fear the LORD and turn away from evil.  
**3:8** Then you will have healing for your body<br>and strength for your bones.  
**3:9** Honor the LORD with your wealth<br>and with the best part of everything you produce.  
**3:10** Then he will fill your barns with grain,<br>and your vats will overflow with good wine.  
**3:11** My child, don’t reject the LORD’s discipline,<br>and don’t be upset when he corrects you.  
**3:12** For the LORD corrects those he loves,<br>just as a father corrects a child in whom he delights.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek version reads <i>loves, / and he punishes those he accepts as his children.</i> Compare <a name='B:650 12:6'>Heb 12:6.  
**3:13** Joyful is the person who finds wisdom,<br>the one who gains understanding.  
**3:14** For wisdom is more profitable than silver,<br>and her wages are better than gold.  
**3:15** Wisdom is more precious than rubies;<br>nothing you desire can compare with her.  
**3:16** She offers you long life in her right hand,<br>and riches and honor in her left.  
**3:17** She will guide you down delightful paths;<br>all her ways are satisfying.  
**3:18** Wisdom is a tree of life to those who embrace her;<br>happy are those who hold her tightly.  
**3:19** By wisdom the LORD founded the earth;<br>by understanding he created the heavens.  
**3:20** By his knowledge the deep fountains of the earth burst forth,<br>and the dew settles beneath the night sky.  
**3:21** My child, don’t lose sight of common sense and discernment.<br>Hang on to them,  
**3:22** for they will refresh your soul.<br>They are like jewels on a necklace.  
**3:23** They keep you safe on your way,<br>and your feet will not stumble.  
**3:24** You can go to bed without fear;<br>you will lie down and sleep soundly.  
**3:25** You need not be afraid of sudden disaster<br>or the destruction that comes upon the wicked,  
**3:26** for the LORD is your security.<br>He will keep your foot from being caught in a trap.  
**3:27** Do not withhold good from those who deserve it<br>when it’s in your power to help them.  
**3:28** If you can help your neighbor now, don’t say,<br>“Come back tomorrow, and then I’ll help you.”  
**3:29** Don’t plot harm against your neighbor,<br>for those who live nearby trust you.  
**3:30** Don’t pick a fight without reason,<br>when no one has done you harm.  
**3:31** Don’t envy violent people<br>or copy their ways.  
**3:32** Such wicked people are detestable to the LORD,<br>but he offers his friendship to the godly.  
**3:33** The LORD curses the house of the wicked,<br>but he blesses the home of the upright.  
**3:34** The LORD mocks the mockers<br>but is gracious to the humble.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek version reads <i>The LORD opposes the proud / but gives grace to the humble.</i> Compare <a name='B:660 4:6'>Jas 4:6; <a name='B:670 5:5'>1 Pet 5:5.  
**3:35** The wise inherit honor,<br>but fools are put to shame!  

## 4장

**4:1** My children,<sup>①</sup> listen when your father corrects you.<br>Pay attention and learn good judgment,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>My sons.</i>  
**4:2** for I am giving you good guidance.<br>Don’t turn away from my instructions.  
**4:3** For I, too, was once my father’s son,<br>tenderly loved as my mother’s only child.  
**4:4** My father taught me,<br>“Take my words to heart.<br>Follow my commands, and you will live.  
**4:5** Get wisdom; develop good judgment.<br>Don’t forget my words or turn away from them.  
**4:6** Don’t turn your back on wisdom, for she will protect you.<br>Love her, and she will guard you.  
**4:7** Getting wisdom is the wisest thing you can do!<br>And whatever else you do, develop good judgment.  
**4:8** If you prize wisdom, she will make you great.<br>Embrace her, and she will honor you.  
**4:9** She will place a lovely wreath on your head;<br>she will present you with a beautiful crown.”  
**4:10** My child,<sup>①</sup> listen to me and do as I say,<br>and you will have a long, good life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>My son;</i> also in <a name='B:240 4:20'>4:20.  
**4:11** I will teach you wisdom’s ways<br>and lead you in straight paths.  
**4:12** When you walk, you won’t be held back;<br>when you run, you won’t stumble.  
**4:13** Take hold of my instructions; don’t let them go.<br>Guard them, for they are the key to life.  
**4:14** Don’t do as the wicked do,<br>and don’t follow the path of evildoers.  
**4:15** Don’t even think about it; don’t go that way.<br>Turn away and keep moving.  
**4:16** For evil people can’t sleep until they’ve done their evil deed for the day.<br>They can’t rest until they’ve caused someone to stumble.  
**4:17** They eat the food of wickedness<br>and drink the wine of violence!  
**4:18** The way of the righteous is like the first gleam of dawn,<br>which shines ever brighter until the full light of day.  
**4:19** But the way of the wicked is like total darkness.<br>They have no idea what they are stumbling over.  
**4:20** My child, pay attention to what I say.<br>Listen carefully to my words.  
**4:21** Don’t lose sight of them.<br>Let them penetrate deep into your heart,  
**4:22** for they bring life to those who find them,<br>and healing to their whole body.  
**4:23** Guard your heart above all else,<br>for it determines the course of your life.  
**4:24** Avoid all perverse talk;<br>stay away from corrupt speech.  
**4:25** Look straight ahead,<br>and fix your eyes on what lies before you.  
**4:26** Mark out a straight path for your feet;<br>stay on the safe path.  
**4:27** Don’t get sidetracked;<br>keep your feet from following evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** My son, pay attention to my wisdom;<br>listen carefully to my wise counsel.  
**5:2** Then you will show discernment,<br>and your lips will express what you’ve learned.  
**5:3** For the lips of an immoral woman are as sweet as honey,<br>and her mouth is smoother than oil.  
**5:4** But in the end she is as bitter as poison,<br>as dangerous as a double-edged sword.  
**5:5** Her feet go down to death;<br>her steps lead straight to the grave.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>to Sheol.</i>  
**5:6** For she cares nothing about the path to life.<br>She staggers down a crooked trail and doesn’t realize it.  
**5:7** So now, my sons, listen to me.<br>Never stray from what I am about to say:  
**5:8** Stay away from her!<br>Don’t go near the door of her house!  
**5:9** If you do, you will lose your honor<br>and will lose to merciless people all you have achieved.  
**5:10** Strangers will consume your wealth,<br>and someone else will enjoy the fruit of your labor.  
**5:11** In the end you will groan in anguish<br>when disease consumes your body.  
**5:12** You will say, “How I hated discipline!<br>If only I had not ignored all the warnings!  
**5:13** Oh, why didn’t I listen to my teachers?<br>Why didn’t I pay attention to my instructors?  
**5:14** I have come to the brink of utter ruin,<br>and now I must face public disgrace.”  
**5:15** Drink water from your own well—<br>share your love only with your wife.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Drink water from your own cistern, / flowing water from your own well.</i>  
**5:16** Why spill the water of your springs in the streets,<br>having sex with just anyone?<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Why spill your springs in the streets, / your streams in the city squares?</i>  
**5:17** You should reserve it for yourselves.<br>Never share it with strangers.  
**5:18** Let your wife be a fountain of blessing for you.<br>Rejoice in the wife of your youth.  
**5:19** She is a loving deer, a graceful doe.<br>Let her breasts satisfy you always.<br>May you always be captivated by her love.  
**5:20** Why be captivated, my son, by an immoral woman,<br>or fondle the breasts of a promiscuous woman?  
**5:21** For the LORD sees clearly what a man does,<br>examining every path he takes.  
**5:22** An evil man is held captive by his own sins;<br>they are ropes that catch and hold him.  
**5:23** He will die for lack of self-control;<br>he will be lost because of his great foolishness.  

## 6장

**6:1** My child,<sup>①</sup> if you have put up security for a friend’s debt<br>or agreed to guarantee the debt of a stranger—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>My son.</i>  
**6:2** if you have trapped yourself by your agreement<br>and are caught by what you said—  
**6:3** follow my advice and save yourself,<br>for you have placed yourself at your friend’s mercy.<br>Now swallow your pride;<br>go and beg to have your name erased.  
**6:4** Don’t put it off; do it now!<br>Don’t rest until you do.  
**6:5** Save yourself like a gazelle escaping from a hunter,<br>like a bird fleeing from a net.  
**6:6** Take a lesson from the ants, you lazybones.<br>Learn from their ways and become wise!  
**6:7** Though they have no prince<br>or governor or ruler to make them work,  
**6:8** they labor hard all summer,<br>gathering food for the winter.  
**6:9** But you, lazybones, how long will you sleep?<br>When will you wake up?  
**6:10** A little extra sleep, a little more slumber,<br>a little folding of the hands to rest—  
**6:11** then poverty will pounce on you like a bandit;<br>scarcity will attack you like an armed robber.  
**6:12** What are worthless and wicked people like?<br>They are constant liars,  
**6:13** signaling their deceit with a wink of the eye,<br>a nudge of the foot, or the wiggle of fingers.  
**6:14** Their perverted hearts plot evil,<br>and they constantly stir up trouble.  
**6:15** But they will be destroyed suddenly,<br>broken in an instant beyond all hope of healing.  
**6:16** There are six things the LORD hates—<br>no, seven things he detests:  
**6:17** haughty eyes,<br>a lying tongue,<br>hands that kill the innocent,  
**6:18** a heart that plots evil,<br>feet that race to do wrong,  
**6:19** a false witness who pours out lies,<br>a person who sows discord in a family.  
**6:20** My son, obey your father’s commands,<br>and don’t neglect your mother’s instruction.  
**6:21** Keep their words always in your heart.<br>Tie them around your neck.  
**6:22** When you walk, their counsel will lead you.<br>When you sleep, they will protect you.<br>When you wake up, they will advise you.  
**6:23** For their command is a lamp<br>and their instruction a light;<br>their corrective discipline<br>is the way to life.  
**6:24** It will keep you from the immoral woman,<br>from the smooth tongue of a promiscuous woman.  
**6:25** Don’t lust for her beauty.<br>Don’t let her coy glances seduce you.  
**6:26** For a prostitute will bring you to poverty,<sup>①</sup><br>but sleeping with another man’s wife will cost you your life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>to a loaf of bread.</i>  
**6:27** Can a man scoop a flame into his lap<br>and not have his clothes catch on fire?  
**6:28** Can he walk on hot coals<br>and not blister his feet?  
**6:29** So it is with the man who sleeps with another man’s wife.<br>He who embraces her will not go unpunished.  
**6:30** Excuses might be found for a thief<br>who steals because he is starving.  
**6:31** But if he is caught, he must pay back seven times what he stole,<br>even if he has to sell everything in his house.  
**6:32** But the man who commits adultery is an utter fool,<br>for he destroys himself.  
**6:33** He will be wounded and disgraced.<br>His shame will never be erased.  
**6:34** For the woman’s jealous husband will be furious,<br>and he will show no mercy when he takes revenge.  
**6:35** He will accept no compensation,<br>nor be satisfied with a payoff of any size.  

## 7장

**7:1** Follow my advice, my son;<br>always treasure my commands.  
**7:2** Obey my commands and live!<br>Guard my instructions as you guard your own eyes.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>as the pupil of your eye.</i>  
**7:3** Tie them on your fingers as a reminder.<br>Write them deep within your heart.  
**7:4** Love wisdom like a sister;<br>make insight a beloved member of your family.  
**7:5** Let them protect you from an affair with an immoral woman,<br>from listening to the flattery of a promiscuous woman.  
**7:6** While I was at the window of my house,<br>looking through the curtain,  
**7:7** I saw some naive young men,<br>and one in particular who lacked common sense.  
**7:8** He was crossing the street near the house of an immoral woman,<br>strolling down the path by her house.  
**7:9** It was at twilight, in the evening,<br>as deep darkness fell.  
**7:10** The woman approached him,<br>seductively dressed and sly of heart.  
**7:11** She was the brash, rebellious type,<br>never content to stay at home.  
**7:12** She is often in the streets and markets,<br>soliciting at every corner.  
**7:13** She threw her arms around him and kissed him,<br>and with a brazen look she said,  
**7:14** “I’ve just made my peace offerings<br>and fulfilled my vows.  
**7:15** You’re the one I was looking for!<br>I came out to find you, and here you are!  
**7:16** My bed is spread with beautiful blankets,<br>with colored sheets of Egyptian linen.  
**7:17** I’ve perfumed my bed<br>with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.  
**7:18** Come, let’s drink our fill of love until morning.<br>Let’s enjoy each other’s caresses,  
**7:19** for my husband is not home.<br>He’s away on a long trip.  
**7:20** He has taken a wallet full of money with him<br>and won’t return until later this month. <sup>①</sup>”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>until the moon is full.</i>  
**7:21** So she seduced him with her pretty speech<br>and enticed him with her flattery.  
**7:22** He followed her at once,<br>like an ox going to the slaughter.<br>He was like a stag caught in a trap,<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>As in Greek and Syriac versions; Hebrew reads <i>slaughter, as shackles are for the discipline of a fool.</i>  
**7:23** awaiting the arrow that would pierce its heart.<br>He was like a bird flying into a snare,<br>little knowing it would cost him his life.  
**7:24** So listen to me, my sons,<br>and pay attention to my words.  
**7:25** Don’t let your hearts stray away toward her.<br>Don’t wander down her wayward path.  
**7:26** For she has been the ruin of many;<br>many men have been her victims.  
**7:27** Her house is the road to the grave.<sup>①</sup><br>Her bedroom is the den of death.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>to Sheol.</i>  

## 8장

**8:1** Listen as Wisdom calls out!<br>Hear as understanding raises her voice!  
**8:2** On the hilltop along the road,<br>she takes her stand at the crossroads.  
**8:3** By the gates at the entrance to the town,<br>on the road leading in, she cries aloud,  
**8:4** “I call to you, to all of you!<br>I raise my voice to all people.  
**8:5** You simple people, use good judgment.<br>You foolish people, show some understanding.  
**8:6** Listen to me! For I have important things to tell you.<br>Everything I say is right,  
**8:7** for I speak the truth<br>and detest every kind of deception.  
**8:8** My advice is wholesome.<br>There is nothing devious or crooked in it.  
**8:9** My words are plain to anyone with understanding,<br>clear to those with knowledge.  
**8:10** Choose my instruction rather than silver,<br>and knowledge rather than pure gold.  
**8:11** For wisdom is far more valuable than rubies.<br>Nothing you desire can compare with it.  
**8:12** “I, Wisdom, live together with good judgment.<br>I know where to discover knowledge and discernment.  
**8:13** All who fear the LORD will hate evil.<br>Therefore, I hate pride and arrogance,<br>corruption and perverse speech.  
**8:14** Common sense and success belong to me.<br>Insight and strength are mine.  
**8:15** Because of me, kings reign,<br>and rulers make just decrees.  
**8:16** Rulers lead with my help,<br>and nobles make righteous judgments.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some Hebrew manuscripts and Greek version read <i>and nobles are judges over the earth.</i>  
**8:17** “I love all who love me.<br>Those who search will surely find me.  
**8:18** I have riches and honor,<br>as well as enduring wealth and justice.  
**8:19** My gifts are better than gold, even the purest gold,<br>my wages better than sterling silver!  
**8:20** I walk in righteousness,<br>in paths of justice.  
**8:21** Those who love me inherit wealth.<br>I will fill their treasuries.  
**8:22** “The LORD formed me from the beginning,<br>before he created anything else.  
**8:23** I was appointed in ages past,<br>at the very first, before the earth began.  
**8:24** I was born before the oceans were created,<br>before the springs bubbled forth their waters.  
**8:25** Before the mountains were formed,<br>before the hills, I was born—  
**8:26** before he had made the earth and fields<br>and the first handfuls of soil.  
**8:27** I was there when he established the heavens,<br>when he drew the horizon on the oceans.  
**8:28** I was there when he set the clouds above,<br>when he established springs deep in the earth.  
**8:29** I was there when he set the limits of the seas,<br>so they would not spread beyond their boundaries.<br>And when he marked off the earth’s foundations,  
**8:30** I was the architect at his side.<br>I was his constant delight,<br>rejoicing always in his presence.  
**8:31** And how happy I was with the world he created;<br>how I rejoiced with the human family!  
**8:32** “And so, my children,<sup>①</sup> listen to me,<br>for all who follow my ways are joyful.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>my sons.</i>  
**8:33** Listen to my instruction and be wise.<br>Don’t ignore it.  
**8:34** Joyful are those who listen to me,<br>watching for me daily at my gates,<br>waiting for me outside my home!  
**8:35** For whoever finds me finds life<br>and receives favor from the LORD.  
**8:36** But those who miss me injure themselves.<br>All who hate me love death.”  

## 9장

**9:1** Wisdom has built her house;<br>she has carved its seven columns.  
**9:2** She has prepared a great banquet,<br>mixed the wines, and set the table.  
**9:3** She has sent her servants to invite everyone to come.<br>She calls out from the heights overlooking the city.  
**9:4** “Come in with me,” she urges the simple.<br>To those who lack good judgment, she says,  
**9:5** “Come, eat my food,<br>and drink the wine I have mixed.
