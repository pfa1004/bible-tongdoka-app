# 25.예레미야애가 (NLT)



## 1장

**1:1** Jerusalem, once so full of people,<br>is now deserted.<br>She who was once great among the nations<br>now sits alone like a widow.<br>Once the queen of all the earth,<br>she is now a slave.  
**1:2** She sobs through the night;<br>tears stream down her cheeks.<br>Among all her lovers,<br>there is no one left to comfort her.<br>All her friends have betrayed her<br>and become her enemies.  
**1:3** Judah has been led away into captivity,<br>oppressed with cruel slavery.<br>She lives among foreign nations<br>and has no place of rest.<br>Her enemies have chased her down,<br>and she has nowhere to turn.  
**1:4** The roads to Jerusalem<sup>①</sup> are in mourning,<br>for crowds no longer come to celebrate the festivals.<br>The city gates are silent,<br>her priests groan,<br>her young women are crying—<br>how bitter is her fate!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Zion;</i> also in <a name='B:310 1:17'>1:17.  
**1:5** Her oppressors have become her masters,<br>and her enemies prosper,<br>for the LORD has punished Jerusalem<br>for her many sins.<br>Her children have been captured<br>and taken away to distant lands.  
**1:6** All the majesty of beautiful Jerusalem<sup>①</sup><br>has been stripped away.<br>Her princes are like starving deer<br>searching for pasture.<br>They are too weak to run<br>from the pursuing enemy.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>of the daughter of Zion.</i>  
**1:7** In the midst of her sadness and wandering,<br>Jerusalem remembers her ancient splendor.<br>But now she has fallen to her enemy,<br>and there is no one to help her.<br>Her enemy struck her down<br>and laughed as she fell.  
**1:8** Jerusalem has sinned greatly,<br>so she has been tossed away like a filthy rag.<br>All who once honored her now despise her,<br>for they have seen her stripped naked and humiliated.<br>All she can do is groan<br>and hide her face.  
**1:9** She defiled herself with immorality<br>and gave no thought to her future.<br>Now she lies in the gutter<br>with no one to lift her out.<br>“LORD, see my misery,” she cries.<br>“The enemy has triumphed.”  
**1:10** The enemy has plundered her completely,<br>taking every precious thing she owns.<br>She has seen foreigners violate her sacred Temple,<br>the place the LORD had forbidden them to enter.  
**1:11** Her people groan as they search for bread.<br>They have sold their treasures for food to stay alive.<br>“O LORD, look,” she mourns,<br>“and see how I am despised.  
**1:12** “Does it mean nothing to you, all you who pass by?<br>Look around and see if there is any suffering like mine,<br>which the LORD brought on me<br>when he erupted in fierce anger.  
**1:13** “He has sent fire from heaven that burns in my bones.<br>He has placed a trap in my path and turned me back.<br>He has left me devastated,<br>racked with sickness all day long.  
**1:14** “He wove my sins into ropes<br>to hitch me to a yoke of captivity.<br>The Lord sapped my strength and turned me over to my enemies;<br>I am helpless in their hands.  
**1:15** “The Lord has treated my mighty men<br>with contempt.<br>At his command a great army has come<br>to crush my young warriors.<br>The Lord has trampled his beloved city<sup>①</sup><br>like grapes are trampled in a winepress.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>the virgin daughter of Judah.</i>  
**1:16** “For all these things I weep;<br>tears flow down my cheeks.<br>No one is here to comfort me;<br>any who might encourage me are far away.<br>My children have no future,<br>for the enemy has conquered us.”  
**1:17** Jerusalem reaches out for help,<br>but no one comforts her.<br>Regarding his people Israel,<sup>①</sup><br>the LORD has said,<br>“Let their neighbors be their enemies!<br>Let them be thrown away like a filthy rag!”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Jacob.</i> The names “Jacob” and “Israel” are often interchanged throughout the Old Testament, referring sometimes to the individual patriarch and sometimes to the nation.  
**1:18** “The LORD is right,” Jerusalem says,<br>“for I rebelled against him.<br>Listen, people everywhere;<br>look upon my anguish and despair,<br>for my sons and daughters<br>have been taken captive to distant lands.  
**1:19** “I begged my allies for help,<br>but they betrayed me.<br>My priests and leaders<br>starved to death in the city,<br>even as they searched for food<br>to save their lives.  
**1:20** “LORD, see my anguish!<br>My heart is broken<br>and my soul despairs,<br>for I have rebelled against you.<br>In the streets the sword kills,<br>and at home there is only death.  
**1:21** “Others heard my groans,<br>but no one turned to comfort me.<br>When my enemies heard about my troubles,<br>they were happy to see what you had done.<br>Oh, bring the day you promised,<br>when they will suffer as I have suffered.  
**1:22** “Look at all their evil deeds, LORD.<br>Punish them,<br>as you have punished me<br>for all my sins.<br>My groans are many,<br>and I am sick at heart.”  

## 2장

**2:1** The Lord in his anger<br>has cast a dark shadow over beautiful Jerusalem.<sup>①</sup><br>The fairest of Israel’s cities lies in the dust,<br>thrown down from the heights of heaven.<br>In his day of great anger,<br>the Lord has shown no mercy even to his Temple.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>the daughter of Zion;</i> also in <a name='B:310 2:8'>2:8, <a name='B:310 2:10'>10, <a name='B:310 2:18'>18. <sup>②</sup>Hebrew <i>his footstool.</i>  
**2:2** Without mercy the Lord has destroyed<br>every home in Israel.<sup>①</sup><br>In his anger he has broken down<br>the fortress walls of beautiful Jerusalem.<sup>②</sup><br>He has brought them to the ground,<br>dishonoring the kingdom and its rulers.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Jacob;</i> also in <a name='B:310 2:3'>2:3b. See <a name='C:@310 1:17'>note on <a name='B:310 1:17'>1:17. <sup>②</sup>Hebrew <i>the daughter of Judah;</i> also in <a name='B:310 2:5'>2:5.  
**2:3** All the strength of Israel<br>vanishes beneath his fierce anger.<br>The Lord has withdrawn his protection<br>as the enemy attacks.<br>He consumes the whole land of Israel<br>like a raging fire.  
**2:4** He bends his bow against his people,<br>as though he were their enemy.<br>His strength is used against them<br>to kill their finest youth.<br>His fury is poured out like fire<br>on beautiful Jerusalem.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>on the tent of the daughter of Zion.</i>  
**2:5** Yes, the Lord has vanquished Israel<br>like an enemy.<br>He has destroyed her palaces<br>and demolished her fortresses.<br>He has brought unending sorrow and tears<br>upon beautiful Jerusalem.  
**2:6** He has broken down his Temple<br>as though it were merely a garden shelter.<br>The LORD has blotted out all memory<br>of the holy festivals and Sabbath days.<br>Kings and priests fall together<br>before his fierce anger.  
**2:7** The Lord has rejected his own altar;<br>he despises his own sanctuary.<br>He has given Jerusalem’s palaces<br>to her enemies.<br>They shout in the LORD’s Temple<br>as though it were a day of celebration.  
**2:8** The LORD was determined<br>to destroy the walls of beautiful Jerusalem.<br>He made careful plans for their destruction,<br>then did what he had planned.<br>Therefore, the ramparts and walls<br>have fallen down before him.  
**2:9** Jerusalem’s gates have sunk into the ground.<br>He has smashed their locks and bars.<br>Her kings and princes have been exiled to distant lands;<br>her law has ceased to exist.<br>Her prophets receive<br>no more visions from the LORD.  
**2:10** The leaders of beautiful Jerusalem<br>sit on the ground in silence.<br>They are clothed in burlap<br>and throw dust on their heads.<br>The young women of Jerusalem<br>hang their heads in shame.  
**2:11** I have cried until the tears no longer come;<br>my heart is broken.<br>My spirit is poured out in agony<br>as I see the desperate plight of my people.<br>Little children and tiny babies<br>are fainting and dying in the streets.  
**2:12** They cry out to their mothers,<br>“We need food and drink!”<br>Their lives ebb away in the streets<br>like the life of a warrior wounded in battle.<br>They gasp for life<br>as they collapse in their mothers’ arms.  
**2:13** What can I say about you?<br>Who has ever seen such sorrow?<br>O daughter of Jerusalem,<br>to what can I compare your anguish?<br>O virgin daughter of Zion,<br>how can I comfort you?<br>For your wound is as deep as the sea.<br>Who can heal you?  
**2:14** Your prophets have said<br>so many foolish things, false to the core.<br>They did not save you from exile<br>by pointing out your sins.<br>Instead, they painted false pictures,<br>filling you with false hope.  
**2:15** All who pass by jeer at you.<br>They scoff and insult beautiful Jerusalem,<sup>①</sup> saying,<br>“Is this the city called ‘Most Beautiful in All the World’<br>and ‘Joy of All the Earth’?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>the daughter of Jerusalem.</i>  
**2:16** All your enemies mock you.<br>They scoff and snarl and say,<br>“We have destroyed her at last!<br>We have long waited for this day,<br>and it is finally here!”  
**2:17** But it is the LORD who did just as he planned.<br>He has fulfilled the promises of disaster<br>he made long ago.<br>He has destroyed Jerusalem without mercy.<br>He has caused her enemies to gloat over her<br>and has given them power over her.  
**2:18** Cry aloud<sup>①</sup> before the Lord,<br>O walls of beautiful Jerusalem!<br>Let your tears flow like a river<br>day and night.<br>Give yourselves no rest;<br>give your eyes no relief.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Their heart cried.</i>  
**2:19** Rise during the night and cry out.<br>Pour out your hearts like water to the Lord.<br>Lift up your hands to him in prayer,<br>pleading for your children,<br>for in every street<br>they are faint with hunger.  
**2:20** “O LORD, think about this!<br>Should you treat your own people this way?<br>Should mothers eat their own children,<br>those they once bounced on their knees?<br>Should priests and prophets be killed<br>within the Lord’s Temple?  
**2:21** “See them lying in the streets—<br>young and old,<br>boys and girls,<br>killed by the swords of the enemy.<br>You have killed them in your anger,<br>slaughtering them without mercy.  
**2:22** “You have invited terrors from all around,<br>as though you were calling them to a day of feasting.<br>In the day of the LORD’s anger,<br>no one has escaped or survived.<br>The enemy has killed all the children<br>whom I carried and raised.”  

## 3장

**3:1** I am the one who has seen the afflictions<br>that come from the rod of the LORD’s anger.  
**3:2** He has led me into darkness,<br>shutting out all light.  
**3:3** He has turned his hand against me<br>again and again, all day long.  
**3:4** He has made my skin and flesh grow old.<br>He has broken my bones.  
**3:5** He has besieged and surrounded me<br>with anguish and distress.  
**3:6** He has buried me in a dark place,<br>like those long dead.  
**3:7** He has walled me in, and I cannot escape.<br>He has bound me in heavy chains.  
**3:8** And though I cry and shout,<br>he has shut out my prayers.  
**3:9** He has blocked my way with a high stone wall;<br>he has made my road crooked.  
**3:10** He has hidden like a bear or a lion,<br>waiting to attack me.  
**3:11** He has dragged me off the path and torn me in pieces,<br>leaving me helpless and devastated.  
**3:12** He has drawn his bow<br>and made me the target for his arrows.  
**3:13** He shot his arrows<br>deep into my heart.  
**3:14** My own people laugh at me.<br>All day long they sing their mocking songs.  
**3:15** He has filled me with bitterness<br>and given me a bitter cup of sorrow to drink.  
**3:16** He has made me chew on gravel.<br>He has rolled me in the dust.  
**3:17** Peace has been stripped away,<br>and I have forgotten what prosperity is.  
**3:18** I cry out, “My splendor is gone!<br>Everything I had hoped for from the LORD is lost!”  
**3:19** The thought of my suffering and homelessness<br>is bitter beyond words.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>is wormwood and gall.</i>  
**3:20** I will never forget this awful time,<br>as I grieve over my loss.  
**3:21** Yet I still dare to hope<br>when I remember this:  
**3:22** The faithful love of the LORD never ends!<sup>①</sup><br>His mercies never cease.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>As in Syriac version; Hebrew reads <i>of the LORD keeps us from destruction.</i>  
**3:23** Great is his faithfulness;<br>his mercies begin afresh each morning.  
**3:24** I say to myself, “The LORD is my inheritance;<br>therefore, I will hope in him!”  
**3:25** The LORD is good to those who depend on him,<br>to those who search for him.  
**3:26** So it is good to wait quietly<br>for salvation from the LORD.  
**3:27** And it is good for people to submit at an early age<br>to the yoke of his discipline:  
**3:28** Let them sit alone in silence<br>beneath the LORD’s demands.  
**3:29** Let them lie face down in the dust,<br>for there may be hope at last.  
**3:30** Let them turn the other cheek to those who strike them<br>and accept the insults of their enemies.  
**3:31** For no one is abandoned<br>by the Lord forever.  
**3:32** Though he brings grief, he also shows compassion<br>because of the greatness of his unfailing love.  
**3:33** For he does not enjoy hurting people<br>or causing them sorrow.  
**3:34** If people crush underfoot<br>all the prisoners of the land,  
**3:35** if they deprive others of their rights<br>in defiance of the Most High,  
**3:36** if they twist justice in the courts—<br>doesn’t the Lord see all these things?  
**3:37** Who can command things to happen<br>without the Lord’s permission?  
**3:38** Does not the Most High<br>send both calamity and good?  
**3:39** Then why should we, mere humans, complain<br>when we are punished for our sins?  
**3:40** Instead, let us test and examine our ways.<br>Let us turn back to the LORD.  
**3:41** Let us lift our hearts and hands<br>to God in heaven and say,  
**3:42** “We have sinned and rebelled,<br>and you have not forgiven us.  
**3:43** “You have engulfed us with your anger, chased us down,<br>and slaughtered us without mercy.  
**3:44** You have hidden yourself in a cloud<br>so our prayers cannot reach you.  
**3:45** You have discarded us as refuse and garbage<br>among the nations.  
**3:46** “All our enemies<br>have spoken out against us.  
**3:47** We are filled with fear,<br>for we are trapped, devastated, and ruined.”  
**3:48** Tears stream from my eyes<br>because of the destruction of my people!  
**3:49** My tears flow endlessly;<br>they will not stop  
**3:50** until the LORD looks down<br>from heaven and sees.  
**3:51** My heart is breaking<br>over the fate of all the women of Jerusalem.  
**3:52** My enemies, whom I have never harmed,<br>hunted me down like a bird.  
**3:53** They threw me into a pit<br>and dropped stones on me.  
**3:54** The water rose over my head,<br>and I cried out, “This is the end!”  
**3:55** But I called on your name, LORD,<br>from deep within the pit.  
**3:56** You heard me when I cried, “Listen to my pleading!<br>Hear my cry for help!”  
**3:57** Yes, you came when I called;<br>you told me, “Do not fear.”  
**3:58** Lord, you have come to my defense;<br>you have redeemed my life.  
**3:59** You have seen the wrong they have done to me, LORD.<br>Be my judge, and prove me right.  
**3:60** You have seen the vengeful plots<br>my enemies have laid against me.  
**3:61** LORD, you have heard the vile names they call me.<br>You know all about the plans they have made.  
**3:62** My enemies whisper and mutter<br>as they plot against me all day long.  
**3:63** Look at them! Whether they sit or stand,<br>I am the object of their mocking songs.  
**3:64** Pay them back, LORD,<br>for all the evil they have done.  
**3:65** Give them hard and stubborn hearts,<br>and then let your curse fall on them!  
**3:66** Chase them down in your anger,<br>destroying them beneath the LORD’s heavens.  

## 4장

**4:1** How the gold has lost its luster!<br>Even the finest gold has become dull.<br>The sacred gemstones<br>lie scattered in the streets!  
**4:2** See how the precious children of Jerusalem,<sup>①</sup><br>worth their weight in fine gold,<br>are now treated like pots of clay<br>made by a common potter.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>precious sons of Zion.</i>  
**4:3** Even the jackals feed their young,<br>but not my people Israel.<br>They ignore their children’s cries,<br>like ostriches in the desert.  
**4:4** The parched tongues of their little ones<br>stick to the roofs of their mouths in thirst.<br>The children cry for bread,<br>but no one has any to give them.  
**4:5** The people who once ate the richest foods<br>now beg in the streets for anything they can get.<br>Those who once wore the finest clothes<br>now search the garbage dumps for food.  
**4:6** The guilt<sup>①</sup> of my people<br>is greater than that of Sodom,<br>where utter disaster struck in a moment<br>and no hand offered help.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>punishment.</i>  
**4:7** Our princes once glowed with health—<br>brighter than snow, whiter than milk.<br>Their faces were as ruddy as rubies,<br>their appearance like fine jewels.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>like lapis lazuli.</i>  
**4:8** But now their faces are blacker than soot.<br>No one recognizes them in the streets.<br>Their skin sticks to their bones;<br>it is as dry and hard as wood.  
**4:9** Those killed by the sword are better off<br>than those who die of hunger.<br>Starving, they waste away<br>for lack of food from the fields.  
**4:10** Tenderhearted women<br>have cooked their own children.<br>They have eaten them<br>to survive the siege.  
**4:11** But now the anger of the LORD is satisfied.<br>His fierce anger has been poured out.<br>He started a fire in Jerusalem<sup>①</sup><br>that burned the city to its foundations.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>in Zion.</i>  
**4:12** Not a king in all the earth—<br>no one in all the world—<br>would have believed that an enemy<br>could march through the gates of Jerusalem.  
**4:13** Yet it happened because of the sins of her prophets<br>and the sins of her priests,<br>who defiled the city<br>by shedding innocent blood.  
**4:14** They wandered blindly<br>through the streets,<br>so defiled by blood<br>that no one dared touch them.  
**4:15** “Get away!” the people shouted at them.<br>“You’re defiled! Don’t touch us!”<br>So they fled to distant lands<br>and wandered among foreign nations,<br>but none would let them stay.  
**4:16** The LORD himself has scattered them,<br>and he no longer helps them.<br>People show no respect for the priests<br>and no longer honor the leaders.  
**4:17** We looked in vain for our allies<br>to come and save us,<br>but we were looking to nations<br>that could not help us.  
**4:18** We couldn’t go into the streets<br>without danger to our lives.<br>Our end was near; our days were numbered.<br>We were doomed!  
**4:19** Our enemies were swifter than eagles in flight.<br>If we fled to the mountains, they found us.<br>If we hid in the wilderness,<br>they were waiting for us there.  
**4:20** Our king—the LORD’s anointed, the very life of our nation—<br>was caught in their snares.<br>We had thought that his shadow<br>would protect us against any nation on earth!  
**4:21** Are you rejoicing in the land of Uz,<br>O people of Edom?<br>But you, too, must drink from the cup of the LORD’s anger.<br>You, too, will be stripped naked in your drunkenness.  
**4:22** O beautiful Jerusalem,<sup>①</sup> your punishment will end;<br>you will soon return from exile.<br>But Edom, your punishment is just beginning;<br>soon your many sins will be exposed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>O daughter of Zion.</i>  

## 5장

**5:1** LORD, remember what has happened to us.<br>See how we have been disgraced!  
**5:2** Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers,<br>our homes to foreigners.  
**5:3** We are orphaned and fatherless.<br>Our mothers are widowed.  
**5:4** We have to pay for water to drink,<br>and even firewood is expensive.  
**5:5** Those who pursue us are at our heels;<br>we are exhausted but are given no rest.  
**5:6** We submitted to Egypt and Assyria<br>to get enough food to survive.  
**5:7** Our ancestors sinned, but they have died—<br>and we are suffering the punishment they deserved!  
**5:8** Slaves have now become our masters;<br>there is no one left to rescue us.  
**5:9** We hunt for food at the risk of our lives,<br>for violence rules the countryside.  
**5:10** The famine has blackened our skin<br>as though baked in an oven.  
**5:11** Our enemies rape the women in Jerusalem<sup>①</sup><br>and the young girls in all the towns of Judah.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>in Zion.</i>  
**5:12** Our princes are being hanged by their thumbs,<br>and our elders are treated with contempt.  
**5:13** Young men are led away to work at millstones,<br>and boys stagger under heavy loads of wood.  
**5:14** The elders no longer sit in the city gates;<br>the young men no longer dance and sing.  
**5:15** Joy has left our hearts;<br>our dancing has turned to mourning.  
**5:16** The garlands have<sup>①</sup> fallen from our heads.<br>Weep for us because we have sinned.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>The crown has.</i>  
**5:17** Our hearts are sick and weary,<br>and our eyes grow dim with tears.  
**5:18** For Jerusalem<sup>①</sup> is empty and desolate,<br>a place haunted by jackals.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Mount Zion.</i>  
**5:19** But LORD, you remain the same forever!<br>Your throne continues from generation to generation.  
**5:20** Why do you continue to forget us?<br>Why have you abandoned us for so long?  
**5:21** Restore us, O LORD, and bring us back to you again!<br>Give us back the joys we once had!  
**5:22** Or have you utterly rejected us?<br>Are you angry with us still?
