# 31.오바디야 (NLT)



## 1장

**1:1** This is the vision that the Sovereign LORD revealed to Obadiah concerning the land of Edom. <b>Edom’s Judgment Announced</b><br>We have heard a message from the LORD<br>that an ambassador was sent to the nations to say,<br>“Get ready, everyone!<br>Let’s assemble our armies and attack Edom!”  
**1:2** The LORD says to Edom,<br>“I will cut you down to size among the nations;<br>you will be greatly despised.  
**1:3** You have been deceived by your own pride<br>because you live in a rock fortress<br>and make your home high in the mountains.<br>‘Who can ever reach us way up here?’<br>you ask boastfully.  
**1:4** But even if you soar as high as eagles<br>and build your nest among the stars,<br>I will bring you crashing down,”<br>says the LORD.  
**1:5** “If thieves came at night and robbed you<br>(what a disaster awaits you!),<br>they would not take everything.<br>Those who harvest grapes<br>always leave a few for the poor.<br>But your enemies will wipe you out completely!  
**1:6** Every nook and cranny of Edom<sup>①</sup><br>will be searched and looted.<br>Every treasure will be found and taken.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Esau;</i> also in 8b, 9, 18, 19, 21.  
**1:7** “All your allies will turn against you.<br>They will help to chase you from your land.<br>They will promise you peace<br>while plotting to deceive and destroy you.<br>Your trusted friends will set traps for you,<br>and you won’t even know about it.  
**1:8** At that time not a single wise person<br>will be left in the whole land of Edom,”<br>says the LORD.<br>“For on the mountains of Edom<br>I will destroy everyone who has understanding.  
**1:9** The mightiest warriors of Teman<br>will be terrified,<br>and everyone on the mountains of Edom<br>will be cut down in the slaughter.  
**1:10** “Because of the violence you did<br>to your close relatives in Israel,<sup>①</sup><br>you will be filled with shame<br>and destroyed forever.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>your brother Jacob.</i> The names “Jacob” and “Israel” are often interchanged throughout the Old Testament, referring sometimes to the individual patriarch and sometimes to the nation.  
**1:11** When they were invaded,<br>you stood aloof, refusing to help them.<br>Foreign invaders carried off their wealth<br>and cast lots to divide up Jerusalem,<br>but you acted like one of Israel’s enemies.  
**1:12** “You should not have gloated<br>when they exiled your relatives to distant lands.<br>You should not have rejoiced<br>when the people of Judah suffered such misfortune.<br>You should not have spoken arrogantly<br>in that terrible time of trouble.  
**1:13** You should not have plundered the land of Israel<br>when they were suffering such calamity.<br>You should not have gloated over their destruction<br>when they were suffering such calamity.<br>You should not have seized their wealth<br>when they were suffering such calamity.  
**1:14** You should not have stood at the crossroads,<br>killing those who tried to escape.<br>You should not have captured the survivors<br>and handed them over in their terrible time of trouble.  
**1:15** “The day is near when I, the LORD,<br>will judge all godless nations!<br>As you have done to Israel,<br>so it will be done to you.<br>All your evil deeds<br>will fall back on your own heads.  
**1:16** Just as you swallowed up my people<br>on my holy mountain,<br>so you and the surrounding nations<br>will swallow the punishment I pour out on you.<br>Yes, all you nations will drink and stagger<br>and disappear from history.  
**1:17** “But Jerusalem<sup>①</sup> will become a refuge for those who escape;<br>it will be a holy place.<br>And the people of Israel<sup>②</sup> will come back<br>to reclaim their inheritance.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Mount Zion.</i> <sup>②</sup>Hebrew <i>house of Jacob;</i> also in 18. See note on 10.  
**1:18** The people of Israel will be a raging fire,<br>and Edom a field of dry stubble.<br>The descendants of Joseph will be a flame<br>roaring across the field, devouring everything.<br>There will be no survivors in Edom.<br>I, the LORD, have spoken!  
**1:19** “Then my people living in the Negev<br>will occupy the mountains of Edom.<br>Those living in the foothills of Judah<sup>①</sup><br>will possess the Philistine plains<br>and take over the fields of Ephraim and Samaria.<br>And the people of Benjamin<br>will occupy the land of Gilead.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>the Shephelah.</i>  
**1:20** The exiles of Israel will return to their land<br>and occupy the Phoenician coast as far north as Zarephath.<br>The captives from Jerusalem exiled in the north<sup>①</sup><br>will return home and resettle the towns of the Negev.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>in Sepharad.</i>  
**1:21** Those who have been rescued<sup>①</sup> will go up to<sup>②</sup> Mount Zion in Jerusalem<br>to rule over the mountains of Edom.<br>And the LORD himself will be king!”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>As in Greek and Syriac versions; Hebrew reads <i>Rescuers.</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>from.</i>
