# 43.요한복음 (NLT)



## 1장

**1:1** In the beginning the Word already existed.<br>The Word was with God,<br>and the Word was God.  
**1:2** He existed in the beginning with God.  
**1:3** God created everything through him,<br>and nothing was created except through him.  
**1:4** The Word gave life to everything that was created,<sup>①</sup><br>and his life brought light to everyone.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>and nothing that was created was created except through him. The Word gave life to everything.</i>  
**1:5** The light shines in the darkness,<br>and the darkness can never extinguish it.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>and the darkness has not understood it.</i>  
**1:6** God sent a man, John the Baptist,<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>a man named John.</i>  
**1:7** to tell about the light so that everyone might believe because of his testimony.  
**1:8** John himself was not the light; he was simply a witness to tell about the light.  
**1:9** The one who is the true light, who gives light to everyone, was coming into the world.  
**1:10** He came into the very world he created, but the world didn’t recognize him.  
**1:11** He came to his own people, and even they rejected him.  
**1:12** But to all who believed him and accepted him, he gave the right to become children of God.  
**1:13** They are reborn—not with a physical birth resulting from human passion or plan, but a birth that comes from God.  
**1:14** So the Word became human<sup>①</sup> and made his home among us. He was full of unfailing love and faithfulness.<sup>②</sup> And we have seen his glory, the glory of the Father’s one and only Son.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>became flesh.</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>grace and truth;</i> also in <a name='B:500 1:17'>1:17.  
**1:15** John testified about him when he shouted to the crowds, “This is the one I was talking about when I said, ‘Someone is coming after me who is far greater than I am, for he existed long before me.’”  
**1:16** From his abundance we have all received one gracious blessing after another.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>received the grace of Christ rather than the grace of the law;</i> Greek reads <i>received grace upon grace.</i>  
**1:17** For the law was given through Moses, but God’s unfailing love and faithfulness came through Jesus Christ.  
**1:18** No one has ever seen God. But the unique One, who is himself God,<sup>①</sup> is near to the Father’s heart. He has revealed God to us.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts read <i>But the one and only Son.</i>  
**1:19** This was John’s testimony when the Jewish leaders sent priests and Temple assistants<sup>①</sup> from Jerusalem to ask John, “Who are you?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>and Levites.</i>  
**1:20** He came right out and said, “I am not the Messiah.”  
**1:21** “Well then, who are you?” they asked. “Are you Elijah?” “No,” he replied. “Are you the Prophet we are expecting?”<sup>①</sup> “No.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>Are you the Prophet?</i> See <a name='B:50 18:15'>Deut 18:15, <a name='B:50 18:18'>18; <a name='B:460 4:5-6'>Mal 4:5-6.  
**1:22** “Then who are you? We need an answer for those who sent us. What do you have to say about yourself?”  
**1:23** John replied in the words of the prophet Isaiah:<br>“I am a voice shouting in the wilderness,<br>‘Clear the way for the LORD’s coming!’”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:290 40:3'>Isa 40:3.  
**1:24** Then the Pharisees who had been sent  
**1:25** asked him, “If you aren’t the Messiah or Elijah or the Prophet, what right do you have to baptize?”  
**1:26** John told them, “I baptize with<sup>①</sup> water, but right here in the crowd is someone you do not recognize.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in;</i> also in <a name='B:500 1:31'>1:31, <a name='B:500 1:33'>33.  
**1:27** Though his ministry follows mine, I’m not even worthy to be his slave and untie the straps of his sandal.”  
**1:28** This encounter took place in Bethany, an area east of the Jordan River, where John was baptizing.  
**1:29** The next day John saw Jesus coming toward him and said, “Look! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!  
**1:30** He is the one I was talking about when I said, ‘A man is coming after me who is far greater than I am, for he existed long before me.’  
**1:31** I did not recognize him as the Messiah, but I have been baptizing with water so that he might be revealed to Israel.”  
**1:32** Then John testified, “I saw the Holy Spirit descending like a dove from heaven and resting upon him.  
**1:33** I didn’t know he was the one, but when God sent me to baptize with water, he told me, ‘The one on whom you see the Spirit descend and rest is the one who will baptize with the Holy Spirit.’  
**1:34** I saw this happen to Jesus, so I testify that he is the Chosen One of God. <sup>①</sup>”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts read <i>the Son of God.</i>  
**1:35** The following day John was again standing with two of his disciples.  
**1:36** As Jesus walked by, John looked at him and declared, “Look! There is the Lamb of God!”  
**1:37** When John’s two disciples heard this, they followed Jesus.  
**1:38** Jesus looked around and saw them following. “What do you want?” he asked them. They replied, “Rabbi” (which means “Teacher”), “where are you staying?”  
**1:39** “Come and see,” he said. It was about four o’clock in the afternoon when they went with him to the place where he was staying, and they remained with him the rest of the day.  
**1:40** Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, was one of these men who heard what John said and then followed Jesus.  
**1:41** Andrew went to find his brother, Simon, and told him, “We have found the Messiah” (which means “Christ” <sup>①</sup>).<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Messiah</i> (a Hebrew term) and <i>Christ</i> (a Greek term) both mean “anointed one.”  
**1:42** Then Andrew brought Simon to meet Jesus. Looking intently at Simon, Jesus said, “Your name is Simon, son of John—but you will be called Cephas” (which means “Peter” <sup>①</sup>).<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The names <i>Cephas</i> (from Aramaic) and <i>Peter</i> (from Greek) both mean “rock.”  
**1:43** The next day Jesus decided to go to Galilee. He found Philip and said to him, “Come, follow me.”  
**1:44** Philip was from Bethsaida, Andrew and Peter’s hometown.  
**1:45** Philip went to look for Nathanael and told him, “We have found the very person Moses<sup>①</sup> and the prophets wrote about! His name is Jesus, the son of Joseph from Nazareth.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>Moses in the law.</i>  
**1:46** “Nazareth!” exclaimed Nathanael. “Can anything good come from Nazareth?” “Come and see for yourself,” Philip replied.  
**1:47** As they approached, Jesus said, “Now here is a genuine son of Israel—a man of complete integrity.”  
**1:48** “How do you know about me?” Nathanael asked. Jesus replied, “I could see you under the fig tree before Philip found you.”  
**1:49** Then Nathanael exclaimed, “Rabbi, you are the Son of God—the King of Israel!”  
**1:50** Jesus asked him, “Do you believe this just because I told you I had seen you under the fig tree? You will see greater things than this.”  
**1:51** Then he said, “I tell you the truth, you will all see heaven open and the angels of God going up and down on the Son of Man, the one who is the stairway between heaven and earth. <sup>①</sup>”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>going up and down on the Son of Man;</i> see <a name='B:10 28:10-17'>Gen 28:10-17. “Son of Man” is a title Jesus used for himself.  

## 2장

**2:1** The next day<sup>①</sup> there was a wedding celebration in the village of Cana in Galilee. Jesus’ mother was there,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>On the third day;</i> see <a name='B:500 1:35'>1:35, <a name='B:500 1:43'>43.  
**2:2** and Jesus and his disciples were also invited to the celebration.  
**2:3** The wine supply ran out during the festivities, so Jesus’ mother told him, “They have no more wine.”  
**2:4** “Dear woman, that’s not our problem,” Jesus replied. “My time has not yet come.”  
**2:5** But his mother told the servants, “Do whatever he tells you.”  
**2:6** Standing nearby were six stone water jars, used for Jewish ceremonial washing. Each could hold twenty to thirty gallons.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>2 or 3 measures</i> [75 to 113 liters].  
**2:7** Jesus told the servants, “Fill the jars with water.” When the jars had been filled,  
**2:8** he said, “Now dip some out, and take it to the master of ceremonies.” So the servants followed his instructions.  
**2:9** When the master of ceremonies tasted the water that was now wine, not knowing where it had come from (though, of course, the servants knew), he called the bridegroom over.  
**2:10** “A host always serves the best wine first,” he said. “Then, when everyone has had a lot to drink, he brings out the less expensive wine. But you have kept the best until now!”  
**2:11** This miraculous sign at Cana in Galilee was the first time Jesus revealed his glory. And his disciples believed in him.  
**2:12** After the wedding he went to Capernaum for a few days with his mother, his brothers, and his disciples.  
**2:13** It was nearly time for the Jewish Passover celebration, so Jesus went to Jerusalem.  
**2:14** In the Temple area he saw merchants selling cattle, sheep, and doves for sacrifices; he also saw dealers at tables exchanging foreign money.  
**2:15** Jesus made a whip from some ropes and chased them all out of the Temple. He drove out the sheep and cattle, scattered the money changers’ coins over the floor, and turned over their tables.  
**2:16** Then, going over to the people who sold doves, he told them, “Get these things out of here. Stop turning my Father’s house into a marketplace!”  
**2:17** Then his disciples remembered this prophecy from the Scriptures: “Passion for God’s house will consume me.”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>“Concern for God’s house will be my undoing.”</i> <a name='B:230 69:9'>Ps 69:9.  
**2:18** But the Jewish leaders demanded, “What are you doing? If God gave you authority to do this, show us a miraculous sign to prove it.”  
**2:19** “All right,” Jesus replied. “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.”  
**2:20** “What!” they exclaimed. “It has taken forty-six years to build this Temple, and you can rebuild it in three days?”  
**2:21** But when Jesus said “this temple,” he meant his own body.  
**2:22** After he was raised from the dead, his disciples remembered he had said this, and they believed both the Scriptures and what Jesus had said.  
**2:23** Because of the miraculous signs Jesus did in Jerusalem at the Passover celebration, many began to trust in him.  
**2:24** But Jesus didn’t trust them, because he knew all about people.  
**2:25** No one needed to tell him about human nature, for he knew what was in each person’s heart.  

## 3장

**3:1** There was a man named Nicodemus, a Jewish religious leader who was a Pharisee.  
**3:2** After dark one evening, he came to speak with Jesus. “Rabbi,” he said, “we all know that God has sent you to teach us. Your miraculous signs are evidence that God is with you.”  
**3:3** Jesus replied, “I tell you the truth, unless you are born again,<sup>①</sup> you cannot see the Kingdom of God.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>born from above;</i> also in <a name='B:500 3:7'>3:7.  
**3:4** “What do you mean?” exclaimed Nicodemus. “How can an old man go back into his mother’s womb and be born again?”  
**3:5** Jesus replied, “I assure you, no one can enter the Kingdom of God without being born of water and the Spirit.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>and spirit.</i> The Greek word for <i>Spirit</i> can also be translated <i>wind;</i> see <a name='B:500 3:8'>3:8.  
**3:6** Humans can reproduce only human life, but the Holy Spirit gives birth to spiritual life.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>what is born of the Spirit is spirit.</i>  
**3:7** So don’t be surprised when I say, ‘You<sup>①</sup> must be born again.’<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Greek word for <i>you</i> is plural; also in <a name='B:500 3:12'>3:12.  
**3:8** The wind blows wherever it wants. Just as you can hear the wind but can’t tell where it comes from or where it is going, so you can’t explain how people are born of the Spirit.”  
**3:9** “How are these things possible?” Nicodemus asked.  
**3:10** Jesus replied, “You are a respected Jewish teacher, and yet you don’t understand these things?  
**3:11** I assure you, we tell you what we know and have seen, and yet you won’t believe our testimony.  
**3:12** But if you don’t believe me when I tell you about earthly things, how can you possibly believe if I tell you about heavenly things?  
**3:13** No one has ever gone to heaven and returned. But the Son of Man<sup>①</sup> has come down from heaven.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts add <i>who lives in heaven.</i> “Son of Man” is a title Jesus used for himself.  
**3:14** And as Moses lifted up the bronze snake on a pole in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up,  
**3:15** so that everyone who believes in him will have eternal life.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>everyone who believes will have eternal life in him.</i>  
**3:16** “For this is how God loved the world: He gave<sup>①</sup> his one and only Son, so that everyone who believes in him will not perish but have eternal life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>For God loved the world so much that he gave.</i>  
**3:17** God sent his Son into the world not to judge the world, but to save the world through him.  
**3:18** “There is no judgment against anyone who believes in him. But anyone who does not believe in him has already been judged for not believing in God’s one and only Son.  
**3:19** And the judgment is based on this fact: God’s light came into the world, but people loved the darkness more than the light, for their actions were evil.  
**3:20** All who do evil hate the light and refuse to go near it for fear their sins will be exposed.  
**3:21** But those who do what is right come to the light so others can see that they are doing what God wants. <sup>①</sup>”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>can see God at work in what he is doing.</i>  
**3:22** Then Jesus and his disciples left Jerusalem and went into the Judean countryside. Jesus spent some time with them there, baptizing people.  
**3:23** At this time John the Baptist was baptizing at Aenon, near Salim, because there was plenty of water there; and people kept coming to him for baptism.  
**3:24** (This was before John was thrown into prison.)  
**3:25** A debate broke out between John’s disciples and a certain Jew<sup>①</sup> over ceremonial cleansing.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts read <i>some Jews.</i>  
**3:26** So John’s disciples came to him and said, “Rabbi, the man you met on the other side of the Jordan River, the one you identified as the Messiah, is also baptizing people. And everybody is going to him instead of coming to us.”  
**3:27** John replied, “No one can receive anything unless God gives it from heaven.  
**3:28** You yourselves know how plainly I told you, ‘I am not the Messiah. I am only here to prepare the way for him.’  
**3:29** It is the bridegroom who marries the bride, and the bridegroom’s friend is simply glad to stand with him and hear his vows. Therefore, I am filled with joy at his success.  
**3:30** He must become greater and greater, and I must become less and less.  
**3:31** “He has come from above and is greater than anyone else. We are of the earth, and we speak of earthly things, but he has come from heaven and is greater than anyone else.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts do not include <i>and is greater than anyone else.</i>  
**3:32** He testifies about what he has seen and heard, but how few believe what he tells them!  
**3:33** Anyone who accepts his testimony can affirm that God is true.  
**3:34** For he is sent by God. He speaks God’s words, for God gives him the Spirit without limit.  
**3:35** The Father loves his Son and has put everything into his hands.  
**3:36** And anyone who believes in God’s Son has eternal life. Anyone who doesn’t obey the Son will never experience eternal life but remains under God’s angry judgment.”  

## 4장

**4:1** Jesus<sup>①</sup> knew the Pharisees had heard that he was baptizing and making more disciples than John<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts read <i>The Lord.</i>  
**4:2** (though Jesus himself didn’t baptize them—his disciples did).  
**4:3** So he left Judea and returned to Galilee.  
**4:4** He had to go through Samaria on the way.  
**4:5** Eventually he came to the Samaritan village of Sychar, near the field that Jacob gave to his son Joseph.  
**4:6** Jacob’s well was there; and Jesus, tired from the long walk, sat wearily beside the well about noontime.  
**4:7** Soon a Samaritan woman came to draw water, and Jesus said to her, “Please give me a drink.”  
**4:8** He was alone at the time because his disciples had gone into the village to buy some food.  
**4:9** The woman was surprised, for Jews refuse to have anything to do with Samaritans.<sup>①</sup> She said to Jesus, “You are a Jew, and I am a Samaritan woman. Why are you asking me for a drink?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts do not include this sentence.  
**4:10** Jesus replied, “If you only knew the gift God has for you and who you are speaking to, you would ask me, and I would give you living water.”  
**4:11** “But sir, you don’t have a rope or a bucket,” she said, “and this well is very deep. Where would you get this living water?  
**4:12** And besides, do you think you’re greater than our ancestor Jacob, who gave us this well? How can you offer better water than he and his sons and his animals enjoyed?”  
**4:13** Jesus replied, “Anyone who drinks this water will soon become thirsty again.  
**4:14** But those who drink the water I give will never be thirsty again. It becomes a fresh, bubbling spring within them, giving them eternal life.”  
**4:15** “Please, sir,” the woman said, “give me this water! Then I’ll never be thirsty again, and I won’t have to come here to get water.”  
**4:16** “Go and get your husband,” Jesus told her.  
**4:17** “I don’t have a husband,” the woman replied. Jesus said, “You’re right! You don’t have a husband—  
**4:18** for you have had five husbands, and you aren’t even married to the man you’re living with now. You certainly spoke the truth!”  
**4:19** “Sir,” the woman said, “you must be a prophet.  
**4:20** So tell me, why is it that you Jews insist that Jerusalem is the only place of worship, while we Samaritans claim it is here at Mount Gerizim,<sup>①</sup> where our ancestors worshiped?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>on this mountain.</i>  
**4:21** Jesus replied, “Believe me, dear woman, the time is coming when it will no longer matter whether you worship the Father on this mountain or in Jerusalem.  
**4:22** You Samaritans know very little about the one you worship, while we Jews know all about him, for salvation comes through the Jews.  
**4:23** But the time is coming—indeed it’s here now—when true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and in truth. The Father is looking for those who will worship him that way.  
**4:24** For God is Spirit, so those who worship him must worship in spirit and in truth.”  
**4:25** The woman said, “I know the Messiah is coming—the one who is called Christ. When he comes, he will explain everything to us.”  
**4:26** Then Jesus told her, “I AM the Messiah!”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>“The ‘I AM’ is here”;</i> or <i>“I am the LORD”;</i> Greek reads <i>“I am, the one speaking to you.”</i> See <a name='B:20 3:14'>Exod 3:14.  
**4:27** Just then his disciples came back. They were shocked to find him talking to a woman, but none of them had the nerve to ask, “What do you want with her?” or “Why are you talking to her?”  
**4:28** The woman left her water jar beside the well and ran back to the village, telling everyone,  
**4:29** “Come and see a man who told me everything I ever did! Could he possibly be the Messiah?”  
**4:30** So the people came streaming from the village to see him.  
**4:31** Meanwhile, the disciples were urging Jesus, “Rabbi, eat something.”  
**4:32** But Jesus replied, “I have a kind of food you know nothing about.”  
**4:33** “Did someone bring him food while we were gone?” the disciples asked each other.  
**4:34** Then Jesus explained: “My nourishment comes from doing the will of God, who sent me, and from finishing his work.  
**4:35** You know the saying, ‘Four months between planting and harvest.’ But I say, wake up and look around. The fields are already ripe<sup>①</sup> for harvest.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>white.</i>  
**4:36** The harvesters are paid good wages, and the fruit they harvest is people brought to eternal life. What joy awaits both the planter and the harvester alike!  
**4:37** You know the saying, ‘One plants and another harvests.’ And it’s true.  
**4:38** I sent you to harvest where you didn’t plant; others had already done the work, and now you will get to gather the harvest.”  
**4:39** Many Samaritans from the village believed in Jesus because the woman had said, “He told me everything I ever did!”  
**4:40** When they came out to see him, they begged him to stay in their village. So he stayed for two days,  
**4:41** long enough for many more to hear his message and believe.  
**4:42** Then they said to the woman, “Now we believe, not just because of what you told us, but because we have heard him ourselves. Now we know that he is indeed the Savior of the world.”  
**4:43** At the end of the two days, Jesus went on to Galilee.  
**4:44** He himself had said that a prophet is not honored in his own hometown.  
**4:45** Yet the Galileans welcomed him, for they had been in Jerusalem at the Passover celebration and had seen everything he did there.  
**4:46** As he traveled through Galilee, he came to Cana, where he had turned the water into wine. There was a government official in nearby Capernaum whose son was very sick.  
**4:47** When he heard that Jesus had come from Judea to Galilee, he went and begged Jesus to come to Capernaum to heal his son, who was about to die.  
**4:48** Jesus asked, “Will you never believe in me unless you see miraculous signs and wonders?”  
**4:49** The official pleaded, “Lord, please come now before my little boy dies.”  
**4:50** Then Jesus told him, “Go back home. Your son will live!” And the man believed what Jesus said and started home.  
**4:51** While the man was on his way, some of his servants met him with the news that his son was alive and well.  
**4:52** He asked them when the boy had begun to get better, and they replied, “Yesterday afternoon at one o’clock his fever suddenly disappeared!”  
**4:53** Then the father realized that that was the very time Jesus had told him, “Your son will live.” And he and his entire household believed in Jesus.  
**4:54** This was the second miraculous sign Jesus did in Galilee after coming from Judea.  

## 5장

**5:1** Afterward Jesus returned to Jerusalem for one of the Jewish holy days.  
**5:2** Inside the city, near the Sheep Gate, was the pool of Bethesda,<sup>①</sup> with five covered porches.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other manuscripts read <i>Beth-zatha;</i> still others read <i>Bethsaida.</i>  
**5:3** Crowds of sick people—blind, lame, or paralyzed—lay on the porches.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts add an expanded conclusion to verse <a name='B:500 5:3'>3 and all of verse <a name='B:500 5:4'>4: <i>waiting for a certain movement of the water,</i> <sup>4 </sup><i>for an angel of the Lord came from time to time and stirred up the water. And the first person to step in after the water was stirred was healed of whatever disease he had.</i>  
**5:4**   
**5:5** One of the men lying there had been sick for thirty-eight years.  
**5:6** When Jesus saw him and knew he had been ill for a long time, he asked him, “Would you like to get well?”  
**5:7** “I can’t, sir,” the sick man said, “for I have no one to put me into the pool when the water bubbles up. Someone else always gets there ahead of me.”  
**5:8** Jesus told him, “Stand up, pick up your mat, and walk!”  
**5:9** Instantly, the man was healed! He rolled up his sleeping mat and began walking! But this miracle happened on the Sabbath,  
**5:10** so the Jewish leaders objected. They said to the man who was cured, “You can’t work on the Sabbath! The law doesn’t allow you to carry that sleeping mat!”  
**5:11** But he replied, “The man who healed me told me, ‘Pick up your mat and walk.’”  
**5:12** “Who said such a thing as that?” they demanded.  
**5:13** The man didn’t know, for Jesus had disappeared into the crowd.  
**5:14** But afterward Jesus found him in the Temple and told him, “Now you are well; so stop sinning, or something even worse may happen to you.”  
**5:15** Then the man went and told the Jewish leaders that it was Jesus who had healed him.  
**5:16** So the Jewish leaders began harassing<sup>①</sup> Jesus for breaking the Sabbath rules.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>persecuting.</i>  
**5:17** But Jesus replied, “My Father is always working, and so am I.”  
**5:18** So the Jewish leaders tried all the harder to find a way to kill him. For he not only broke the Sabbath, he called God his Father, thereby making himself equal with God.  
**5:19** So Jesus explained, “I tell you the truth, the Son can do nothing by himself. He does only what he sees the Father doing. Whatever the Father does, the Son also does.  
**5:20** For the Father loves the Son and shows him everything he is doing. In fact, the Father will show him how to do even greater works than healing this man. Then you will truly be astonished.  
**5:21** For just as the Father gives life to those he raises from the dead, so the Son gives life to anyone he wants.  
**5:22** In addition, the Father judges no one. Instead, he has given the Son absolute authority to judge,  
**5:23** so that everyone will honor the Son, just as they honor the Father. Anyone who does not honor the Son is certainly not honoring the Father who sent him.  
**5:24** “I tell you the truth, those who listen to my message and believe in God who sent me have eternal life. They will never be condemned for their sins, but they have already passed from death into life.  
**5:25** “And I assure you that the time is coming, indeed it’s here now, when the dead will hear my voice—the voice of the Son of God. And those who listen will live.

## 6장

## 8장

**18:31** “Then take him away and judge him by your own law,” Pilate told them. “Only the Romans are permitted to execute someone,” the Jewish leaders replied.  
**18:32** (This fulfilled Jesus’ prediction about the way he would die. <sup>①</sup>)<br>→ <sup>①</sup>See <a name='B:500 12:32-33'>John 12:32-33.  
**18:33** Then Pilate went back into his headquarters and called for Jesus to be brought to him. “Are you the king of the Jews?” he asked him.  
**18:34** Jesus replied, “Is this your own question, or did others tell you about me?”  
**18:35** “Am I a Jew?” Pilate retorted. “Your own people and their leading priests brought you to me for trial. Why? What have you done?”  
**18:36** Jesus answered, “My Kingdom is not an earthly kingdom. If it were, my followers would fight to keep me from being handed over to the Jewish leaders. But my Kingdom is not of this world.”  
**18:37** Pilate said, “So you are a king?” Jesus responded, “You say I am a king. Actually, I was born and came into the world to testify to the truth. All who love the truth recognize that what I say is true.”  
**18:38** “What is truth?” Pilate asked. Then he went out again to the people and told them, “He is not guilty of any crime.  
**18:39** But you have a custom of asking me to release one prisoner each year at Passover. Would you like me to release this ‘King of the Jews’?”  
**18:40** But they shouted back, “No! Not this man. We want Barabbas!” (Barabbas was a revolutionary.)  

## 19장

**19:1** Then Pilate had Jesus flogged with a lead-tipped whip.  
**19:2** The soldiers wove a crown of thorns and put it on his head, and they put a purple robe on him.  
**19:3** “Hail! King of the Jews!” they mocked, as they slapped him across the face.  
**19:4** Pilate went outside again and said to the people, “I am going to bring him out to you now, but understand clearly that I find him not guilty.”  
**19:5** Then Jesus came out wearing the crown of thorns and the purple robe. And Pilate said, “Look, here is the man!”  
**19:6** When they saw him, the leading priests and Temple guards began shouting, “Crucify him! Crucify him!” “Take him yourselves and crucify him,” Pilate said. “I find him not guilty.”  
**19:7** The Jewish leaders replied, “By our law he ought to die because he called himself the Son of God.”  
**19:8** When Pilate heard this, he was more frightened than ever.  
**19:9** He took Jesus back into the headquarters<sup>①</sup> again and asked him, “Where are you from?” But Jesus gave no answer.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>the Praetorium.</i>  
**19:10** “Why don’t you talk to me?” Pilate demanded. “Don’t you realize that I have the power to release you or crucify you?”  
**19:11** Then Jesus said, “You would have no power over me at all unless it were given to you from above. So the one who handed me over to you has the greater sin.”  
**19:12** Then Pilate tried to release him, but the Jewish leaders shouted, “If you release this man, you are no ‘friend of Caesar.’<sup>①</sup> Anyone who declares himself a king is a rebel against Caesar.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>“Friend of Caesar” is a technical term that refers to an ally of the emperor.  
**19:13** When they said this, Pilate brought Jesus out to them again. Then Pilate sat down on the judgment seat on the platform that is called the Stone Pavement (in Hebrew, <i>Gabbatha</i>).  
**19:14** It was now about noon on the day of preparation for the Passover. And Pilate said to the people,<sup>①</sup> “Look, here is your king!”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>Jewish people;</i> also in <a name='B:500 19:20'>19:20.  
**19:15** “Away with him,” they yelled. “Away with him! Crucify him!” “What? Crucify your king?” Pilate asked. “We have no king but Caesar,” the leading priests shouted back.  
**19:16** Then Pilate turned Jesus over to them to be crucified. <b>The Crucifixion</b> So they took Jesus away.  
**19:17** Carrying the cross by himself, he went to the place called Place of the Skull (in Hebrew, <i>Golgotha</i>).  
**19:18** There they nailed him to the cross. Two others were crucified with him, one on either side, with Jesus between them.  
**19:19** And Pilate posted a sign on the cross that read, “Jesus of Nazareth,<sup>①</sup> the King of the Jews.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Jesus the Nazarene.</i>  
**19:20** The place where Jesus was crucified was near the city, and the sign was written in Hebrew, Latin, and Greek, so that many people could read it.  
**19:21** Then the leading priests objected and said to Pilate, “Change it from ‘The King of the Jews’ to ‘He said, I am King of the Jews.’”  
**19:22** Pilate replied, “No, what I have written, I have written.”  
**19:23** When the soldiers had crucified Jesus, they divided his clothes among the four of them. They also took his robe, but it was seamless, woven in one piece from top to bottom.  
**19:24** So they said, “Rather than tearing it apart, let’s throw dice<sup>①</sup> for it.” This fulfilled the Scripture that says, “They divided my garments among themselves and threw dice for my clothing.”<sup>②</sup> So that is what they did.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>cast lots.</i> <sup>②</sup><a name='B:230 22:18'>Ps 22:18.  
**19:25** Standing near the cross were Jesus’ mother, and his mother’s sister, Mary (the wife of Clopas), and Mary Magdalene.  
**19:26** When Jesus saw his mother standing there beside the disciple he loved, he said to her, “Dear woman, here is your son.”  
**19:27** And he said to this disciple, “Here is your mother.” And from then on this disciple took her into his home.  
**19:28** Jesus knew that his mission was now finished, and to fulfill Scripture he said, “I am thirsty.”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>See <a name='B:230 22:15'>Pss 22:15; <a name='B:230 69:21'>69:21.

**19:30** When Jesus had tasted it, he said, “It is finished!” Then he bowed his head and gave up his spirit.  
**19:31** It was the day of preparation, and the Jewish leaders didn’t want the bodies hanging there the next day, which was the Sabbath (and a very special Sabbath, because it was Passover week). So they asked Pilate to hasten their deaths by ordering that their legs be broken. Then their bodies could be taken down.  
**19:32** So the soldiers came and broke the legs of the two men crucified with Jesus.  
**19:33** But when they came to Jesus, they saw that he was already dead, so they didn’t break his legs.  
**19:34** One of the soldiers, however, pierced his side with a spear, and immediately blood and water flowed out.  
**19:35** (This report is from an eyewitness giving an accurate account. He speaks the truth so that you also may continue to believe. <sup>①</sup>)<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts read <i>that you also may believe.</i>  
**19:36** These things happened in fulfillment of the Scriptures that say, “Not one of his bones will be broken,”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:20 12:46'>Exod 12:46; <a name='B:40 9:12'>Num 9:12; <a name='B:230 34:20'>Ps 34:20.

**19:39** With him came Nicodemus, the man who had come to Jesus at night. He brought about seventy-five pounds<sup>①</sup> of perfumed ointment made from myrrh and aloes.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>100 litras</i> [32.7 kilograms].  
**19:40** Following Jewish burial custom, they wrapped Jesus’ body with the spices in long sheets of linen cloth.  
**19:41** The place of crucifixion was near a garden, where there was a new tomb, never used before.  
**19:42** And so, because it was the day of preparation for the Jewish Passover<sup>①</sup> and since the tomb was close at hand, they laid Jesus there.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>because of the Jewish day of preparation.</i>  

## 20장

**20:1** Early on Sunday morning,<sup>①</sup> while it was still dark, Mary Magdalene came to the tomb and found that the stone had been rolled away from the entrance.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>On the first day of the week.</i>  
**20:2** She ran and found Simon Peter and the other disciple, the one whom Jesus loved. She said, “They have taken the Lord’s body out of the tomb, and we don’t know where they have put him!”  
**20:3** Peter and the other disciple started out for the tomb.  
**20:4** They were both running, but the other disciple outran Peter and reached the tomb first.  
**20:5** He stooped and looked in and saw the linen wrappings lying there, but he didn’t go in.  
**20:6** Then Simon Peter arrived and went inside. He also noticed the linen wrappings lying there,  
**20:7** while the cloth that had covered Jesus’ head was folded up and lying apart from the other wrappings.  
**20:8** Then the disciple who had reached the tomb first also went in, and he saw and believed—  
**20:9** for until then they still hadn’t understood the Scriptures that said Jesus must rise from the dead.  
**20:10** Then they went home.  
**20:11** Mary was standing outside the tomb crying, and as she wept, she stooped and looked in.  
**20:12** She saw two white-robed angels, one sitting at the head and the other at the foot of the place where the body of Jesus had been lying.  
**20:13** “Dear woman, why are you crying?” the angels asked her. “Because they have taken away my Lord,” she replied, “and I don’t know where they have put him.”  
**20:14** She turned to leave and saw someone standing there. It was Jesus, but she didn’t recognize him.  
**20:15** “Dear woman, why are you crying?” Jesus asked her. “Who are you looking for?” She thought he was the gardener. “Sir,” she said, “if you have taken him away, tell me where you have put him, and I will go and get him.”  
**20:16** “Mary!” Jesus said. She turned to him and cried out, “Rabboni!” (which is Hebrew for “Teacher”).  
**20:17** “Don’t cling to me,” Jesus said, “for I haven’t yet ascended to the Father. But go find my brothers and tell them, ‘I am ascending to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.’”  
**20:18** Mary Magdalene found the disciples and told them, “I have seen the Lord!” Then she gave them his message.  
**20:19** That Sunday evening<sup>①</sup> the disciples were meeting behind locked doors because they were afraid of the Jewish leaders. Suddenly, Jesus was standing there among them! “Peace be with you,” he said.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>In the evening of that day, the first day of the week.</i>  
**20:20** As he spoke, he showed them the wounds in his hands and his side. They were filled with joy when they saw the Lord!  
**20:21** Again he said, “Peace be with you. As the Father has sent me, so I am sending you.”  
**20:22** Then he breathed on them and said, “Receive the Holy Spirit.  
**20:23** If you forgive anyone’s sins, they are forgiven. If you do not forgive them, they are not forgiven.”  
**20:24** One of the twelve disciples, Thomas (nicknamed the Twin),<sup>①</sup> was not with the others when Jesus came.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>Thomas, who was called Didymus.</i>  
**20:25** They told him, “We have seen the Lord!” But he replied, “I won’t believe it unless I see the nail wounds in his hands, put my fingers into them, and place my hand into the wound in his side.”  
**20:26** Eight days later the disciples were together again, and this time Thomas was with them. The doors were locked; but suddenly, as before, Jesus was standing among them. “Peace be with you,” he said.  
**20:27** Then he said to Thomas, “Put your finger here, and look at my hands. Put your hand into the wound in my side. Don’t be faithless any longer. Believe!”  
**20:28** “My Lord and my God!” Thomas exclaimed.  
**20:29** Then Jesus told him, “You believe because you have seen me. Blessed are those who believe without seeing me.”  
**20:30** The disciples saw Jesus do many other miraculous signs in addition to the ones recorded in this book.  
**20:31** But these are written so that you may continue to believe<sup>①</sup> that Jesus is the Messiah, the Son of God, and that by believing in him you will have life by the power of his name.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts read <i>that you may believe.</i>  

## 21장

**21:1** Later, Jesus appeared again to the disciples beside the Sea of Galilee.<sup>①</sup> This is how it happened.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>Sea of Tiberias,</i> another name for the Sea of Galilee.  
**21:2** Several of the disciples were there—Simon Peter, Thomas (nicknamed the Twin),<sup>①</sup> Nathanael from Cana in Galilee, the sons of Zebedee, and two other disciples.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>Thomas, who was called Didymus.</i>  
**21:3** Simon Peter said, “I’m going fishing.” “We’ll come, too,” they all said. So they went out in the boat, but they caught nothing all night.  
**21:4** At dawn Jesus was standing on the beach, but the disciples couldn’t see who he was.  
**21:5** He called out, “Fellows,<sup>①</sup> have you caught any fish?” “No,” they replied.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>Children.</i>  
**21:6** Then he said, “Throw out your net on the right-hand side of the boat, and you’ll get some!” So they did, and they couldn’t haul in the net because there were so many fish in it.  
**21:7** Then the disciple Jesus loved said to Peter, “It’s the Lord!” When Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he put on his tunic (for he had stripped for work), jumped into the water, and headed to shore.  
**21:8** The others stayed with the boat and pulled the loaded net to the shore, for they were only about a hundred yards<sup>①</sup> from shore.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>200 cubits</i> [90 meters].  
**21:9** When they got there, they found breakfast waiting for them—fish cooking over a charcoal fire, and some bread.  
**21:10** “Bring some of the fish you’ve just caught,” Jesus said.  
**21:11** So Simon Peter went aboard and dragged the net to the shore. There were 153 large fish, and yet the net hadn’t torn.  
**21:12** “Now come and have some breakfast!” Jesus said. None of the disciples dared to ask him, “Who are you?” They knew it was the Lord.  
**21:13** Then Jesus served them the bread and the fish.  
**21:14** This was the third time Jesus had appeared to his disciples since he had been raised from the dead.  
**21:15** After breakfast Jesus asked Simon Peter, “Simon son of John, do you love me more than these? <sup>①</sup>” “Yes, Lord,” Peter replied, “you know I love you.” “Then feed my lambs,” Jesus told him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>more than these others do?</i>  
**21:16** Jesus repeated the question: “Simon son of John, do you love me?” “Yes, Lord,” Peter said, “you know I love you.” “Then take care of my sheep,” Jesus said.  
**21:17** A third time he asked him, “Simon son of John, do you love me?” Peter was hurt that Jesus asked the question a third time. He said, “Lord, you know everything. You know that I love you.” Jesus said, “Then feed my sheep.  
**21:18** “I tell you the truth, when you were young, you were able to do as you liked; you dressed yourself and went wherever you wanted to go. But when you are old, you will stretch out your hands, and others<sup>①</sup> will dress you and take you where you don’t want to go.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts read <i>and another one.</i>  
**21:19** Jesus said this to let him know by what kind of death he would glorify God. Then Jesus told him, “Follow me.”  
**21:20** Peter turned around and saw behind them the disciple Jesus loved—the one who had leaned over to Jesus during supper and asked, “Lord, who will betray you?”  
**21:21** Peter asked Jesus, “What about him, Lord?”  
**21:22** Jesus replied, “If I want him to remain alive until I return, what is that to you? As for you, follow me.”  
**21:23** So the rumor spread among the community of believers<sup>①</sup> that this disciple wouldn’t die. But that isn’t what Jesus said at all. He only said, “If I want him to remain alive until I return, what is that to you?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>the brothers.</i>  
**21:24** This disciple is the one who testifies to these events and has recorded them here. And we know that his account of these things is accurate.  
**21:25** Jesus also did many other things. If they were all written down, I suppose the whole world could not contain the books that would be written.
