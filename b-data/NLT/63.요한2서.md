# 63.요한2서 (NLT)



## 1장

**1:1** This letter is from John, the elder.<sup>①</sup> I am writing to the chosen lady and to her children,<sup>②</sup> whom I love in the truth—as does everyone else who knows the truth—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>From the elder.</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>the church God has chosen and its members.</i>  
**1:2** because the truth lives in us and will be with us forever.  
**1:3** Grace, mercy, and peace, which come from God the Father and from Jesus Christ—the Son of the Father—will continue to be with us who live in truth and love.  
**1:4** How happy I was to meet some of your children and find them living according to the truth, just as the Father commanded.  
**1:5** I am writing to remind you, dear friends,<sup>①</sup> that we should love one another. This is not a new commandment, but one we have had from the beginning.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>I urge you, lady.</i>  
**1:6** Love means doing what God has commanded us, and he has commanded us to love one another, just as you heard from the beginning.  
**1:7** I say this because many deceivers have gone out into the world. They deny that Jesus Christ came<sup>①</sup> in a real body. Such a person is a deceiver and an antichrist.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>will come.</i>  
**1:8** Watch out that you do not lose what we<sup>①</sup> have worked so hard to achieve. Be diligent so that you receive your full reward.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts read <i>you.</i>  
**1:9** Anyone who wanders away from this teaching has no relationship with God. But anyone who remains in the teaching of Christ has a relationship with both the Father and the Son.  
**1:10** If anyone comes to your meeting and does not teach the truth about Christ, don’t invite that person into your home or give any kind of encouragement.  
**1:11** Anyone who encourages such people becomes a partner in their evil work.  
**1:12** I have much more to say to you, but I don’t want to do it with paper and ink. For I hope to visit you soon and talk with you face to face. Then our joy will be complete.  
**1:13** Greetings from the children of your sister,<sup>①</sup> chosen by God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>from the members of your sister church.</i>
