# 29.요엘 (NLT)



## 1장

**1:1** The LORD gave this message to Joel son of Pethuel.  
**1:2** Hear this, you leaders of the people.<br>Listen, all who live in the land.<br>In all your history,<br>has anything like this happened before?  
**1:3** Tell your children about it in the years to come,<br>and let your children tell their children.<br>Pass the story down from generation to generation.  
**1:4** After the cutting locusts finished eating the crops,<br>the swarming locusts took what was left!<br>After them came the hopping locusts,<br>and then the stripping locusts,<sup>①</sup> too!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The precise identification of the four kinds of locusts mentioned here is uncertain.  
**1:5** Wake up, you drunkards, and weep!<br>Wail, all you wine-drinkers!<br>All the grapes are ruined,<br>and all your sweet wine is gone.  
**1:6** A vast army of locusts<sup>①</sup> has invaded my land,<br>a terrible army too numerous to count.<br>Its teeth are like lions’ teeth,<br>its fangs like those of a lioness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>A nation.</i>  
**1:7** It has destroyed my grapevines<br>and ruined my fig trees,<br>stripping their bark and destroying it,<br>leaving the branches white and bare.  
**1:8** Weep like a bride dressed in black,<br>mourning the death of her husband.  
**1:9** For there is no grain or wine<br>to offer at the Temple of the LORD.<br>So the priests are in mourning.<br>The ministers of the LORD are weeping.  
**1:10** The fields are ruined,<br>the land is stripped bare.<br>The grain is destroyed,<br>the grapes have shriveled,<br>and the olive oil is gone.  
**1:11** Despair, all you farmers!<br>Wail, all you vine growers!<br>Weep, because the wheat and barley—<br>all the crops of the field—are ruined.  
**1:12** The grapevines have dried up,<br>and the fig trees have withered.<br>The pomegranate trees, palm trees, and apple trees—<br>all the fruit trees—have dried up.<br>And the people’s joy has dried up with them.  
**1:13** Dress yourselves in burlap and weep, you priests!<br>Wail, you who serve before the altar!<br>Come, spend the night in burlap,<br>you ministers of my God.<br>For there is no grain or wine<br>to offer at the Temple of your God.  
**1:14** Announce a time of fasting;<br>call the people together for a solemn meeting.<br>Bring the leaders<br>and all the people of the land<br>into the Temple of the LORD your God,<br>and cry out to him there.  
**1:15** The day of the LORD is near,<br>the day when destruction comes from the Almighty.<br>How terrible that day will be!  
**1:16** Our food disappears before our very eyes.<br>No joyful celebrations are held in the house of our God.  
**1:17** The seeds die in the parched ground,<br>and the grain crops fail.<br>The barns stand empty,<br>and granaries are abandoned.  
**1:18** How the animals moan with hunger!<br>The herds of cattle wander about confused,<br>because they have no pasture.<br>The flocks of sheep and goats bleat in misery.  
**1:19** LORD, help us!<br>The fire has consumed the wilderness pastures,<br>and flames have burned up all the trees.  
**1:20** Even the wild animals cry out to you<br>because the streams have dried up,<br>and fire has consumed the wilderness pastures.  

## 2장

**2:1** Sound the trumpet in Jerusalem <sup>①</sup>!<br>Raise the alarm on my holy mountain!<br>Let everyone tremble in fear<br>because the day of the LORD is upon us.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Zion;</i> also in <a name='B:360 2:15'>2:15, <a name='B:360 2:23'>23.  
**2:2** It is a day of darkness and gloom,<br>a day of thick clouds and deep blackness.<br>Suddenly, like dawn spreading across the mountains,<br>a great and mighty army appears.<br>Nothing like it has been seen before<br>or will ever be seen again.  
**2:3** Fire burns in front of them,<br>and flames follow after them.<br>Ahead of them the land lies<br>as beautiful as the Garden of Eden.<br>Behind them is nothing but desolation;<br>not one thing escapes.  
**2:4** They look like horses;<br>they charge forward like warhorses.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>like charioteers.</i>  
**2:5** Look at them as they leap along the mountaintops.<br>Listen to the noise they make—like the rumbling of chariots,<br>like the roar of fire sweeping across a field of stubble,<br>or like a mighty army moving into battle.  
**2:6** Fear grips all the people;<br>every face grows pale with terror.  
**2:7** The attackers march like warriors<br>and scale city walls like soldiers.<br>Straight forward they march,<br>never breaking rank.  
**2:8** They never jostle each other;<br>each moves in exactly the right position.<br>They break through defenses<br>without missing a step.  
**2:9** They swarm over the city<br>and run along its walls.<br>They enter all the houses,<br>climbing like thieves through the windows.  
**2:10** The earth quakes as they advance,<br>and the heavens tremble.<br>The sun and moon grow dark,<br>and the stars no longer shine.  
**2:11** The LORD is at the head of the column.<br>He leads them with a shout.<br>This is his mighty army,<br>and they follow his orders.<br>The day of the LORD is an awesome, terrible thing.<br>Who can possibly survive?  
**2:12** That is why the LORD says,<br>“Turn to me now, while there is time.<br>Give me your hearts.<br>Come with fasting, weeping, and mourning.  
**2:13** Don’t tear your clothing in your grief,<br>but tear your hearts instead.”<br>Return to the LORD your God,<br>for he is merciful and compassionate,<br>slow to get angry and filled with unfailing love.<br>He is eager to relent and not punish.  
**2:14** Who knows? Perhaps he will give you a reprieve,<br>sending you a blessing instead of this curse.<br>Perhaps you will be able to offer grain and wine<br>to the LORD your God as before.  
**2:15** Blow the ram’s horn in Jerusalem!<br>Announce a time of fasting;<br>call the people together<br>for a solemn meeting.  
**2:16** Gather all the people—<br>the elders, the children, and even the babies.<br>Call the bridegroom from his quarters<br>and the bride from her private room.  
**2:17** Let the priests, who minister in the LORD’s presence,<br>stand and weep between the entry room to the Temple and the altar.<br>Let them pray, “Spare your people, LORD!<br>Don’t let your special possession become an object of mockery.<br>Don’t let them become a joke for unbelieving foreigners who say,<br>‘Has the God of Israel left them?’”  
**2:18** Then the LORD will pity his people<br>and jealously guard the honor of his land.  
**2:19** The LORD will reply,<br>“Look! I am sending you grain and new wine and olive oil,<br>enough to satisfy your needs.<br>You will no longer be an object of mockery<br>among the surrounding nations.  
**2:20** I will drive away these armies from the north.<br>I will send them into the parched wastelands.<br>Those in the front will be driven into the Dead Sea,<br>and those at the rear into the Mediterranean.<sup>①</sup><br>The stench of their rotting bodies will rise over the land.”<br>Surely the LORD has done great things!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>into the eastern sea, . . . into the western sea.</i>  
**2:21** Don’t be afraid, O land.<br>Be glad now and rejoice,<br>for the LORD has done great things.  
**2:22** Don’t be afraid, you animals of the field,<br>for the wilderness pastures will soon be green.<br>The trees will again be filled with fruit;<br>fig trees and grapevines will be loaded down once more.  
**2:23** Rejoice, you people of Jerusalem!<br>Rejoice in the LORD your God!<br>For the rain he sends demonstrates his faithfulness.<br>Once more the autumn rains will come,<br>as well as the rains of spring.  
**2:24** The threshing floors will again be piled high with grain,<br>and the presses will overflow with new wine and olive oil.  
**2:25** The LORD says, “I will give you back what you lost<br>to the swarming locusts, the hopping locusts,<br>the stripping locusts, and the cutting locusts.<sup>①</sup><br>It was I who sent this great destroying army against you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The precise identification of the four kinds of locusts mentioned here is uncertain.  
**2:26** Once again you will have all the food you want,<br>and you will praise the LORD your God,<br>who does these miracles for you.<br>Never again will my people be disgraced.  
**2:27** Then you will know that I am among my people Israel,<br>that I am the LORD your God, and there is no other.<br>Never again will my people be disgraced.  
**2:28** <sup>①</sup>“Then, after doing all those things,<br>I will pour out my Spirit upon all people.<br>Your sons and daughters will prophesy.<br>Your old men will dream dreams,<br>and your young men will see visions.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Verses <a name='B:360 2:28-32'>2:28-32 are numbered 3:1-5 in Hebrew text.  
**2:29** In those days I will pour out my Spirit<br>even on servants—men and women alike.  
**2:30** And I will cause wonders in the heavens and on the earth—<br>blood and fire and columns of smoke.  
**2:31** The sun will become dark,<br>and the moon will turn blood red<br>before that great and terrible<sup>①</sup> day of the LORD arrives.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek version reads <i>glorious.</i>  
**2:32** But everyone who calls on the name of the LORD<br>will be saved,<br>for some on Mount Zion in Jerusalem will escape,<br>just as the LORD has said.<br>These will be among the survivors<br>whom the LORD has called.  

## 3장

**3:1** <sup>①</sup>“At the time of those events,” says the LORD,<br>“when I restore the prosperity of Judah and Jerusalem,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Verses <a name='B:360 3:1-21'>3:1-21 are numbered 4:1-21 in Hebrew text.  
**3:2** I will gather the armies of the world<br>into the valley of Jehoshaphat.<sup>①</sup><br>There I will judge them<br>for harming my people, my special possession,<br>for scattering my people among the nations,<br>and for dividing up my land.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Jehoshaphat</i> means “the LORD judges.”  
**3:3** They threw dice<sup>①</sup> to decide which of my people<br>would be their slaves.<br>They traded boys to obtain prostitutes<br>and sold girls for enough wine to get drunk.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>They cast lots.</i>  
**3:4** “What do you have against me, Tyre and Sidon and you cities of Philistia? Are you trying to take revenge on me? If you are, then watch out! I will strike swiftly and pay you back for everything you have done.  
**3:5** You have taken my silver and gold and all my precious treasures, and have carried them off to your pagan temples.  
**3:6** You have sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks,<sup>①</sup> so they could take them far from their homeland.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>to the peoples of Javan.</i>  
**3:7** “But I will bring them back from all the places to which you sold them, and I will pay you back for everything you have done.  
**3:8** I will sell your sons and daughters to the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the people of Arabia,<sup>①</sup> a nation far away. I, the LORD, have spoken!”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>to the Sabeans.</i>  
**3:9** Say to the nations far and wide:<br>“Get ready for war!<br>Call out your best warriors.<br>Let all your fighting men advance for the attack.  
**3:10** Hammer your plowshares into swords<br>and your pruning hooks into spears.<br>Train even your weaklings to be warriors.  
**3:11** Come quickly, all you nations everywhere.<br>Gather together in the valley.”<br>And now, O LORD, call out your warriors!  
**3:12** “Let the nations be called to arms.<br>Let them march to the valley of Jehoshaphat.<br>There I, the LORD, will sit<br>to pronounce judgment on them all.  
**3:13** Swing the sickle,<br>for the harvest is ripe.<sup>①</sup><br>Come, tread the grapes,<br>for the winepress is full.<br>The storage vats are overflowing<br>with the wickedness of these people.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek version reads <i>for the harvest time has come.</i> Compare <a name='B:480 4:29'>Mark 4:29.  
**3:14** Thousands upon thousands are waiting in the valley of decision.<br>There the day of the LORD will soon arrive.  
**3:15** The sun and moon will grow dark,<br>and the stars will no longer shine.  
**3:16** The LORD’s voice will roar from Zion<br>and thunder from Jerusalem,<br>and the heavens and the earth will shake.<br>But the LORD will be a refuge for his people,<br>a strong fortress for the people of Israel.  
**3:17** “Then you will know that I, the LORD your God,<br>live in Zion, my holy mountain.<br>Jerusalem will be holy forever,<br>and foreign armies will never conquer her again.  
**3:18** In that day the mountains will drip with sweet wine,<br>and the hills will flow with milk.<br>Water will fill the streambeds of Judah,<br>and a fountain will burst forth from the LORD’s Temple,<br>watering the arid valley of acacias.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>valley of Shittim.</i>  
**3:19** But Egypt will become a wasteland<br>and Edom will become a wilderness,<br>because they attacked the people of Judah<br>and killed innocent people in their land.  
**3:20** “But Judah will be filled with people forever,<br>and Jerusalem will endure through all generations.  
**3:21** I will pardon my people’s crimes,<br>which I have not yet pardoned;<br>and I, the LORD, will make my home<br>in Jerusalem<sup>①</sup> with my people.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Zion.</i>
