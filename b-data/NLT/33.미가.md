# 33.미가 (NLT)



## 1장

**1:1** The LORD gave this message to Micah of Moresheth during the years when Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah were kings of Judah. The visions he saw concerned both Samaria and Jerusalem.  
**1:2** Attention! Let all the people of the world listen!<br>Let the earth and everything in it hear.<br>The Sovereign LORD is making accusations against you;<br>the Lord speaks from his holy Temple.  
**1:3** Look! The LORD is coming!<br>He leaves his throne in heaven<br>and tramples the heights of the earth.  
**1:4** The mountains melt beneath his feet<br>and flow into the valleys<br>like wax in a fire,<br>like water pouring down a hill.  
**1:5** And why is this happening?<br>Because of the rebellion of Israel<sup>①</sup>—<br>yes, the sins of the whole nation.<br>Who is to blame for Israel’s rebellion?<br>Samaria, its capital city!<br>Where is the center of idolatry in Judah?<br>In Jerusalem, its capital!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Jacob;</i> also in <a name='B:400 1:5'>1:5b. The names “Jacob” and “Israel” are often interchanged throughout the Old Testament, referring sometimes to the individual patriarch and sometimes to the nation.  
**1:6** “So I, the LORD, will make the city of Samaria<br>a heap of ruins.<br>Her streets will be plowed up<br>for planting vineyards.<br>I will roll the stones of her walls into the valley below,<br>exposing her foundations.  
**1:7** All her carved images will be smashed.<br>All her sacred treasures will be burned.<br>These things were bought with the money<br>earned by her prostitution,<br>and they will now be carried away<br>to pay prostitutes elsewhere.”  
**1:8** Therefore, I will mourn and lament.<br>I will walk around barefoot and naked.<br>I will howl like a jackal<br>and moan like an owl.  
**1:9** For my people’s wound<br>is too deep to heal.<br>It has reached into Judah,<br>even to the gates of Jerusalem.  
**1:10** Don’t tell our enemies in Gath<sup>①</sup>;<br>don’t weep at all.<br>You people in Beth-leaphrah,<sup>②</sup><br>roll in the dust to show your despair.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Gath</i> sounds like the Hebrew term for “tell.” <sup>②</sup><i>Beth-leaphrah</i> means “house of dust.”  
**1:11** You people in Shaphir,<sup>①</sup><br>go as captives into exile—naked and ashamed.<br>The people of Zaanan<sup>②</sup><br>dare not come outside their walls.<br>The people of Beth-ezel<sup>③</sup> mourn,<br>for their house has no support.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Shaphir</i> means “pleasant.” <sup>②</sup><i>Zaanan</i> sounds like the Hebrew term for “come out.” <sup>③</sup><i>Beth-ezel</i> means “adjoining house.”  
**1:12** The people of Maroth<sup>①</sup> anxiously wait for relief,<br>but only bitterness awaits them<br>as the LORD’s judgment reaches<br>even to the gates of Jerusalem.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Maroth</i> sounds like the Hebrew term for “bitter.”  
**1:13** Harness your chariot horses and flee,<br>you people of Lachish.<sup>①</sup><br>You were the first city in Judah<br>to follow Israel in her rebellion,<br>and you led Jerusalem<sup>②</sup> into sin.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Lachish</i> sounds like the Hebrew term for “team of horses.” <sup>②</sup>Hebrew <i>the daughter of Zion.</i>  
**1:14** Send farewell gifts to Moresheth-gath<sup>①</sup>;<br>there is no hope of saving it.<br>The town of Aczib<sup>②</sup><br>has deceived the kings of Israel.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Moresheth</i> sounds like the Hebrew term for “gift” or “dowry.” <sup>②</sup><i>Aczib</i> means “deception.”  
**1:15** O people of Mareshah,<sup>①</sup><br>I will bring a conqueror to capture your town.<br>And the leaders<sup>②</sup> of Israel<br>will go to Adullam.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Mareshah</i> sounds like the Hebrew term for “conqueror.” <sup>②</sup>Hebrew <i>the glory.</i>  
**1:16** Oh, people of Judah, shave your heads in sorrow,<br>for the children you love will be snatched away.<br>Make yourselves as bald as a vulture,<br>for your little ones will be exiled to distant lands.  

## 2장

**2:1** What sorrow awaits you who lie awake at night,<br>thinking up evil plans.<br>You rise at dawn and hurry to carry them out,<br>simply because you have the power to do so.  
**2:2** When you want a piece of land,<br>you find a way to seize it.<br>When you want someone’s house,<br>you take it by fraud and violence.<br>You cheat a man of his property,<br>stealing his family’s inheritance.  
**2:3** But this is what the LORD says:<br>“I will reward your evil with evil;<br>you won’t be able to pull your neck out of the noose.<br>You will no longer walk around proudly,<br>for it will be a terrible time.”  
**2:4** In that day your enemies will make fun of you<br>by singing this song of despair about you:<br>“We are finished,<br>completely ruined!<br>God has confiscated our land,<br>taking it from us.<br>He has given our fields<br>to those who betrayed us. <sup>①</sup>”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>to those who took us captive.</i>  
**2:5** Others will set your boundaries then,<br>and the LORD’s people will have no say<br>in how the land is divided.  
**2:6** “Don’t say such things,”<br>the people respond.<sup>①</sup><br>“Don’t prophesy like that.<br>Such disasters will never come our way!”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the prophets respond;</i> Hebrew reads <i>they prophesy.</i>  
**2:7** Should you talk that way, O family of Israel?<sup>①</sup><br>Will the LORD’s Spirit have patience with such behavior?<br>If you would do what is right,<br>you would find my words comforting.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>O house of Jacob?</i> See <a name='C:@400 1:5'>note on <a name='B:400 1:5'>1:5a.  
**2:8** Yet to this very hour<br>my people rise against me like an enemy!<br>You steal the shirts right off the backs<br>of those who trusted you,<br>making them as ragged as men<br>returning from battle.  
**2:9** You have evicted women from their pleasant homes<br>and forever stripped their children of all that God would give them.  
**2:10** Up! Begone!<br>This is no longer your land and home,<br>for you have filled it with sin<br>and ruined it completely.  
**2:11** Suppose a prophet full of lies would say to you,<br>“I’ll preach to you the joys of wine and alcohol!”<br>That’s just the kind of prophet you would like!  
**2:12** “Someday, O Israel, I will gather you;<br>I will gather the remnant who are left.<br>I will bring you together again like sheep in a pen,<br>like a flock in its pasture.<br>Yes, your land will again<br>be filled with noisy crowds!  
**2:13** Your leader will break out<br>and lead you out of exile,<br>out through the gates of the enemy cities,<br>back to your own land.<br>Your king will lead you;<br>the LORD himself will guide you.”  

## 3장

**3:1** I said, “Listen, you leaders of Israel!<br>You are supposed to know right from wrong,  
**3:2** but you are the very ones<br>who hate good and love evil.<br>You skin my people alive<br>and tear the flesh from their bones.  
**3:3** Yes, you eat my people’s flesh,<br>strip off their skin,<br>and break their bones.<br>You chop them up<br>like meat for the cooking pot.  
**3:4** Then you beg the LORD for help in times of trouble!<br>Do you really expect him to answer?<br>After all the evil you have done,<br>he won’t even look at you!”  
**3:5** This is what the LORD says:<br>“You false prophets are leading my people astray!<br>You promise peace for those who give you food,<br>but you declare war on those who refuse to feed you.  
**3:6** Now the night will close around you,<br>cutting off all your visions.<br>Darkness will cover you,<br>putting an end to your predictions.<br>The sun will set for you prophets,<br>and your day will come to an end.  
**3:7** Then you seers will be put to shame,<br>and you fortune-tellers will be disgraced.<br>And you will cover your faces<br>because there is no answer from God.”  
**3:8** But as for me, I am filled with power—<br>with the Spirit of the LORD.<br>I am filled with justice and strength<br>to boldly declare Israel’s sin and rebellion.  
**3:9** Listen to me, you leaders of Israel!<br>You hate justice and twist all that is right.  
**3:10** You are building Jerusalem<br>on a foundation of murder and corruption.  
**3:11** You rulers make decisions based on bribes;<br>you priests teach God’s laws only for a price;<br>you prophets won’t prophesy unless you are paid.<br>Yet all of you claim to depend on the LORD.<br>“No harm can come to us,” you say,<br>“for the LORD is here among us.”  
**3:12** Because of you, Mount Zion will be plowed like an open field;<br>Jerusalem will be reduced to ruins!<br>A thicket will grow on the heights<br>where the Temple now stands.  

## 4장

**4:1** In the last days, the mountain of the LORD’s house<br>will be the highest of all—<br>the most important place on earth.<br>It will be raised above the other hills,<br>and people from all over the world will stream there to worship.  
**4:2** People from many nations will come and say,<br>“Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD,<br>to the house of Jacob’s God.<br>There he will teach us his ways,<br>and we will walk in his paths.”<br>For the LORD’s teaching will go out from Zion;<br>his word will go out from Jerusalem.  
**4:3** The LORD will mediate between peoples<br>and will settle disputes between strong nations far away.<br>They will hammer their swords into plowshares<br>and their spears into pruning hooks.<br>Nation will no longer fight against nation,<br>nor train for war anymore.  
**4:4** Everyone will live in peace and prosperity,<br>enjoying their own grapevines and fig trees,<br>for there will be nothing to fear.<br>The LORD of Heaven’s Armies<br>has made this promise!  
**4:5** Though the nations around us follow their idols,<br>we will follow the LORD our God forever and ever.  
**4:6** “In that coming day,” says the LORD,<br>“I will gather together those who are lame,<br>those who have been exiles,<br>and those whom I have filled with grief.  
**4:7** Those who are weak will survive as a remnant;<br>those who were exiles will become a strong nation.<br>Then I, the LORD, will rule from Jerusalem<sup>①</sup><br>as their king forever.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Mount Zion.</i>  
**4:8** As for you, Jerusalem,<br>the citadel of God’s people,<sup>①</sup><br>your royal might and power<br>will come back to you again.<br>The kingship will be restored<br>to my precious Jerusalem.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>As for you, Migdal-eder, / the Ophel of the daughter of Zion.</i>  
**4:9** But why are you now screaming in terror?<br>Have you no king to lead you?<br>Have your wise people all died?<br>Pain has gripped you like a woman in childbirth.  
**4:10** Writhe and groan like a woman in labor,<br>you people of Jerusalem,<sup>①</sup><br>for now you must leave this city<br>to live in the open country.<br>You will soon be sent in exile<br>to distant Babylon.<br>But the LORD will rescue you there;<br>he will redeem you from the grip of your enemies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>O daughter of Zion.</i>  
**4:11** Now many nations have gathered against you.<br>“Let her be desecrated,” they say.<br>“Let us see the destruction of Jerusalem. <sup>①</sup>”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>of Zion.</i>  
**4:12** But they do not know the LORD’s thoughts<br>or understand his plan.<br>These nations don’t know<br>that he is gathering them together<br>to be beaten and trampled<br>like sheaves of grain on a threshing floor.  
**4:13** “Rise up and crush the nations, O Jerusalem!”<sup>①</sup><br>says the LORD.<br>“For I will give you iron horns and bronze hooves,<br>so you can trample many nations to pieces.<br>You will present their stolen riches to the LORD,<br>their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>“Rise up and thresh, O daughter of Zion.”</i>  

## 5장

**5:1** <sup>①</sup>Mobilize! Marshal your troops!<br>The enemy is laying siege to Jerusalem.<br>They will strike Israel’s leader<br>in the face with a rod.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Verse <a name='B:400 5:1'>5:1 is numbered 4:14 in Hebrew text.  
**5:2** <sup>①</sup>But you, O Bethlehem Ephrathah,<br>are only a small village among all the people of Judah.<br>Yet a ruler of Israel,<br>whose origins are in the distant past,<br>will come from you on my behalf.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Verses <a name='B:400 5:2-15'>5:2-15 are numbered 5:1-14 in Hebrew text.  
**5:3** The people of Israel will be abandoned to their enemies<br>until the woman in labor gives birth.<br>Then at last his fellow countrymen<br>will return from exile to their own land.  
**5:4** And he will stand to lead his flock with the LORD’s strength,<br>in the majesty of the name of the LORD his God.<br>Then his people will live there undisturbed,<br>for he will be highly honored around the world.  
**5:5** And he will be the source of peace.<br>When the Assyrians invade our land<br>and break through our defenses,<br>we will appoint seven rulers to watch over us,<br>eight princes to lead us.  
**5:6** They will rule Assyria with drawn swords<br>and enter the gates of the land of Nimrod.<br>He will rescue us from the Assyrians<br>when they pour over the borders to invade our land.  
**5:7** Then the remnant left in Israel<sup>①</sup><br>will take their place among the nations.<br>They will be like dew sent by the LORD<br>or like rain falling on the grass,<br>which no one can hold back<br>and no one can restrain.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>in Jacob;</i> also in <a name='B:400 5:8'>5:8. See <a name='C:@400 1:5'>note on <a name='B:400 1:5'>1:5a.  
**5:8** The remnant left in Israel<br>will take their place among the nations.<br>They will be like a lion among the animals of the forest,<br>like a strong young lion among flocks of sheep and goats,<br>pouncing and tearing as they go<br>with no rescuer in sight.  
**5:9** The people of Israel will stand up to their foes,<br>and all their enemies will be wiped out.  
**5:10** “In that day,” says the LORD,<br>“I will slaughter your horses<br>and destroy your chariots.  
**5:11** I will tear down your walls<br>and demolish your defenses.  
**5:12** I will put an end to all witchcraft,<br>and there will be no more fortune-tellers.  
**5:13** I will destroy all your idols and sacred pillars,<br>so you will never again worship the work of your own hands.  
**5:14** I will abolish your idol shrines with their Asherah poles<br>and destroy your pagan cities.  
**5:15** I will pour out my vengeance<br>on all the nations that refuse to obey me.”  

## 6장

**6:1** Listen to what the LORD is saying:<br>“Stand up and state your case against me.<br>Let the mountains and hills be called to witness your complaints.  
**6:2** And now, O mountains,<br>listen to the LORD’s complaint!<br>He has a case against his people.<br>He will bring charges against Israel.  
**6:3** “O my people, what have I done to you?<br>What have I done to make you tired of me?<br>Answer me!  
**6:4** For I brought you out of Egypt<br>and redeemed you from slavery.<br>I sent Moses, Aaron, and Miriam to help you.  
**6:5** Don’t you remember, my people,<br>how King Balak of Moab tried to have you cursed<br>and how Balaam son of Beor blessed you instead?<br>And remember your journey from Acacia Grove<sup>①</sup> to Gilgal,<br>when I, the LORD, did everything I could<br>to teach you about my faithfulness.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Shittim.</i>  
**6:6** What can we bring to the LORD?<br>Should we bring him burnt offerings?<br>Should we bow before God Most High<br>with offerings of yearling calves?  
**6:7** Should we offer him thousands of rams<br>and ten thousand rivers of olive oil?<br>Should we sacrifice our firstborn children<br>to pay for our sins?  
**6:8** No, O people, the LORD has told you what is good,<br>and this is what he requires of you:<br>to do what is right, to love mercy,<br>and to walk humbly with your God.  
**6:9** Fear the LORD if you are wise!<br>His voice calls to everyone in Jerusalem:<br>“The armies of destruction are coming;<br>the LORD is sending them.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>“Listen to the rod. / Who appointed it?”</i>  
**6:10** What shall I say about the homes of the wicked<br>filled with treasures gained by cheating?<br>What about the disgusting practice<br>of measuring out grain with dishonest measures?<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>of using the short ephah?</i> The ephah was a unit for measuring grain.  
**6:11** How can I tolerate your merchants<br>who use dishonest scales and weights?  
**6:12** The rich among you have become wealthy<br>through extortion and violence.<br>Your citizens are so used to lying<br>that their tongues can no longer tell the truth.  
**6:13** “Therefore, I will wound you!<br>I will bring you to ruin for all your sins.  
**6:14** You will eat but never have enough.<br>Your hunger pangs and emptiness will remain.<br>And though you try to save your money,<br>it will come to nothing in the end.<br>You will save a little,<br>but I will give it to those who conquer you.  
**6:15** You will plant crops<br>but not harvest them.<br>You will press your olives<br>but not get enough oil to anoint yourselves.<br>You will trample the grapes<br>but get no juice to make your wine.  
**6:16** You keep only the laws of evil King Omri;<br>you follow only the example of wicked King Ahab!<br>Therefore, I will make an example of you,<br>bringing you to complete ruin.<br>You will be treated with contempt,<br>mocked by all who see you.”  

## 7장

**7:1** How miserable I am!<br>I feel like the fruit picker after the harvest<br>who can find nothing to eat.<br>Not a cluster of grapes or a single early fig<br>can be found to satisfy my hunger.  
**7:2** The godly people have all disappeared;<br>not one honest person is left on the earth.<br>They are all murderers,<br>setting traps even for their own brothers.  
**7:3** Both their hands are equally skilled at doing evil!<br>Officials and judges alike demand bribes.<br>The people with influence get what they want,<br>and together they scheme to twist justice.  
**7:4** Even the best of them is like a brier;<br>the most honest is as dangerous as a hedge of thorns.<br>But your judgment day is coming swiftly now.<br>Your time of punishment is here, a time of confusion.  
**7:5** Don’t trust anyone—<br>not your best friend or even your wife!  
**7:6** For the son despises his father.<br>The daughter defies her mother.<br>The daughter-in-law defies her mother-in-law.<br>Your enemies are right in your own household!  
**7:7** As for me, I look to the LORD for help.<br>I wait confidently for God to save me,<br>and my God will certainly hear me.  
**7:8** Do not gloat over me, my enemies!<br>For though I fall, I will rise again.<br>Though I sit in darkness,<br>the LORD will be my light.  
**7:9** I will be patient as the LORD punishes me,<br>for I have sinned against him.<br>But after that, he will take up my case<br>and give me justice for all I have suffered from my enemies.<br>The LORD will bring me into the light,<br>and I will see his righteousness.  
**7:10** Then my enemies will see that the LORD is on my side.<br>They will be ashamed that they taunted me, saying,<br>“So where is the LORD—<br>that God of yours?”<br>With my own eyes I will see their downfall;<br>they will be trampled like mud in the streets.  
**7:11** In that day, Israel, your cities will be rebuilt,<br>and your borders will be extended.  
**7:12** People from many lands will come and honor you—<br>from Assyria all the way to the towns of Egypt,<br>from Egypt all the way to the Euphrates River,<sup>①</sup><br>and from distant seas and mountains.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>the river.</i>  
**7:13** But the land<sup>①</sup> will become empty and desolate<br>because of the wickedness of those who live there.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>earth.</i>  
**7:14** O LORD, protect your people with your shepherd’s staff;<br>lead your flock, your special possession.<br>Though they live alone in a thicket<br>on the heights of Mount Carmel,<sup>①</sup><br>let them graze in the fertile pastures of Bashan and Gilead<br>as they did long ago.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>surrounded by a fruitful land.</i>  
**7:15** “Yes,” says the LORD,<br>“I will do mighty miracles for you,<br>like those I did when I rescued you<br>from slavery in Egypt.”  
**7:16** All the nations of the world will stand amazed<br>at what the LORD will do for you.<br>They will be embarrassed<br>at their feeble power.<br>They will cover their mouths in silent awe,<br>deaf to everything around them.  
**7:17** Like snakes crawling from their holes,<br>they will come out to meet the LORD our God.<br>They will fear him greatly,<br>trembling in terror at his presence.  
**7:18** Where is another God like you,<br>who pardons the guilt of the remnant,<br>overlooking the sins of his special people?<br>You will not stay angry with your people forever,<br>because you delight in showing unfailing love.  
**7:19** Once again you will have compassion on us.<br>You will trample our sins under your feet<br>and throw them into the depths of the ocean!  
**7:20** You will show us your faithfulness and unfailing love<br>as you promised to our ancestors Abraham and Jacob long ago.
