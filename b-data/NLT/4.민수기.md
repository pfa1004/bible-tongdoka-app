## 3장

**3:1** This is the family line of Aaron and Moses as it was recorded when the LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai:  
**3:2** The names of Aaron’s sons were Nadab (the oldest), Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.  
**3:3** These sons of Aaron were anointed and ordained to minister as priests.  
**3:4** But Nadab and Abihu died in the LORD’s presence in the wilderness of Sinai when they burned before the LORD the wrong kind of fire, different than he had commanded. Since they had no sons, this left only Eleazar and Ithamar to serve as priests with their father, Aaron.  
**3:5** Then the LORD said to Moses,  
**3:6** “Call forward the tribe of Levi, and present them to Aaron the priest to serve as his assistants.  
**3:7** They will serve Aaron and the whole community, performing their sacred duties in and around the Tabernacle.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>around the Tent of Meeting, doing service at the Tabernacle.</i>  
**3:8** They will also maintain all the furnishings of the sacred tent,<sup>①</sup> serving in the Tabernacle on behalf of all the Israelites.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>the Tent of Meeting;</i> also in <a name='B:40 3:25'>3:25.  
**3:9** Assign the Levites to Aaron and his sons. They have been given from among all the people of Israel to serve as their assistants.  
**3:10** Appoint Aaron and his sons to carry out the duties of the priesthood. But any unauthorized person who goes too near the sanctuary must be put to death.”  
**3:11** And the LORD said to Moses,  
**3:12** “Look, I have chosen the Levites from among the Israelites to serve as substitutes for all the firstborn sons of the people of Israel. The Levites belong to me,  
**3:13** for all the firstborn males are mine. On the day I struck down all the firstborn sons of the Egyptians, I set apart for myself all the firstborn in Israel, both of people and of animals. They are mine; I am the LORD.”  
**3:14** The LORD spoke again to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai. He said,  
**3:15** “Record the names of the members of the tribe of Levi by their families and clans. List every male who is one month old or older.”  
**3:16** So Moses listed them, just as the LORD had commanded.  
**3:17** Levi had three sons, whose names were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.  
**3:18** The clans descended from Gershon were named after two of his descendants, Libni and Shimei.  
**3:19** The clans descended from Kohath were named after four of his descendants, Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.  
**3:20** The clans descended from Merari were named after two of his descendants, Mahli and Mushi. These were the Levite clans, listed according to their family groups.  
**3:21** The descendants of Gershon were composed of the clans descended from Libni and Shimei.  
**3:22** There were 7,500 males one month old or older among these Gershonite clans.  
**3:23** They were assigned the area to the west of the Tabernacle for their camp.  
**3:24** The leader of the Gershonite clans was Eliasaph son of Lael.  
**3:25** These two clans were responsible to care for the Tabernacle, including the sacred tent with its layers of coverings, the curtain at its entrance,  
**3:26** the curtains of the courtyard that surrounded the Tabernacle and altar, the curtain at the courtyard entrance, the ropes, and all the equipment related to their use.  
**3:27** The descendants of Kohath were composed of the clans descended from Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.  
**3:28** There were 8,600<sup>①</sup> males one month old or older among these Kohathite clans. They were responsible for the care of the sanctuary,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some Greek manuscripts read <i>8,300;</i> see total in <a name='B:40 3:39'>3:39.  
**3:29** and they were assigned the area south of the Tabernacle for their camp.  
**3:30** The leader of the Kohathite clans was Elizaphan son of Uzziel.  
**3:31** These four clans were responsible for the care of the Ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, the various articles used in the sanctuary, the inner curtain, and all the equipment related to their use.  
**3:32** Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, was the chief administrator over all the Levites, with special responsibility for the oversight of the sanctuary.  
**3:33** The descendants of Merari were composed of the clans descended from Mahli and Mushi.  
**3:34** There were 6,200 males one month old or older among these Merarite clans.  
**3:35** They were assigned the area north of the Tabernacle for their camp. The leader of the Merarite clans was Zuriel son of Abihail.  
**3:36** These two clans were responsible for the care of the frames supporting the Tabernacle, the crossbars, the pillars, the bases, and all the equipment related to their use.  
**3:37** They were also responsible for the posts of the courtyard and all their bases, pegs, and ropes.  
**3:38** The area in front of the Tabernacle, in the east toward the sunrise,<sup>①</sup> was reserved for the tents of Moses and of Aaron and his sons, who had the final responsibility for the sanctuary on behalf of the people of Israel. Anyone other than a priest or Levite who went too near the sanctuary was to be put to death.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>toward the sunrise, in front of the Tent of Meeting.</i>  
**3:39** When Moses and Aaron counted the Levite clans at the LORD’s command, the total number was 22,000 males one month old or older.  
**3:40** Then the LORD said to Moses, “Now count all the firstborn sons in Israel who are one month old or older, and make a list of their names.  
**3:41** The Levites must be reserved for me as substitutes for the firstborn sons of Israel; I am the LORD. And the Levites’ livestock must be reserved for me as substitutes for the firstborn livestock of the whole nation of Israel.”  
**3:42** So Moses counted the firstborn sons of the people of Israel, just as the LORD had commanded.  
**3:43** The number of firstborn sons who were one month old or older was 22,273.  
**3:44** Then the LORD said to Moses,  
**3:45** “Take the Levites as substitutes for the firstborn sons of the people of Israel. And take the livestock of the Levites as substitutes for the firstborn livestock of the people of Israel. The Levites belong to me; I am the LORD.  
**3:46** There are 273 more firstborn sons of Israel than there are Levites. To redeem these extra firstborn sons,  
**3:47** collect five pieces of silver<sup>①</sup> for each of them (each piece weighing the same as the sanctuary shekel, which equals twenty gerahs).<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>5 shekels</i> [2 ounces or 57 grams].  
**3:48** Give the silver to Aaron and his sons as the redemption price for the extra firstborn sons.”  
**3:49** So Moses collected the silver for redeeming the firstborn sons of Israel who exceeded the number of Levites.  
**3:50** He collected 1,365 pieces of silver<sup>①</sup> on behalf of these firstborn sons of Israel (each piece weighing the same as the sanctuary shekel).<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>1,365 [shekels] of silver</i> [34 pounds or 15.5 kilograms].  
**3:51** And Moses gave the silver for the redemption to Aaron and his sons, just as the LORD had commanded.

**4:41** So this was the total of all those from the Gershonite clans who were eligible to serve at the Tabernacle. Moses and Aaron listed them, just as the LORD had commanded.  
**4:42** The Merarite division was also listed by its clans and families.  
**4:43** The list included all the men between thirty and fifty years of age who were eligible for service in the Tabernacle,

## 5장

**5:1** The LORD gave these instructions to Moses:  
**5:2** “Command the people of Israel to remove from the camp anyone who has a skin disease<sup>①</sup> or a discharge, or who has become ceremonially unclean by touching a dead person.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Traditionally rendered <i>leprosy.</i> The Hebrew word used here describes various skin diseases.  
**5:3** This command applies to men and women alike. Remove them so they will not defile the camp in which I live among them.”  
**5:4** So the Israelites did as the LORD had commanded Moses and removed such people from the camp.  
**5:5** Then the LORD said to Moses,  
**5:6** “Give the following instructions to the people of Israel: If any of the people—men or women—betray the LORD by doing wrong to another person, they are guilty.  
**5:7** They must confess their sin and make full restitution for what they have done, adding an additional 20 percent and returning it to the person who was wronged.  
**5:8** But if the person who was wronged is dead, and there are no near relatives to whom restitution can be made, the payment belongs to the LORD and must be given to the priest. Those who are guilty must also bring a ram as a sacrifice, and they will be purified and made right with the LORD.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>bring a ram for atonement, which will make atonement for them.</i>  
**5:9** All the sacred offerings that the Israelites bring to a priest will belong to him.  
**5:10** Each priest may keep all the sacred donations that he receives.”  
**5:11** And the LORD said to Moses,  
**5:12** “Give the following instructions to the people of Israel. “Suppose a man’s wife goes astray, and she is unfaithful to her husband  
**5:13** and has sex with another man, but neither her husband nor anyone else knows about it. She has defiled herself, even though there was no witness and she was not caught in the act.  
**5:14** If her husband becomes jealous and is suspicious of his wife and needs to know whether or not she has defiled herself,  
**5:15** the husband must bring his wife to the priest. He must also bring an offering of two quarts<sup>①</sup> of barley flour to be presented on her behalf. Do not mix it with olive oil or frankincense, for it is a jealousy offering—an offering to prove whether or not she is guilty.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>1⁄10 of an ephah</i> [2.2 liters].  
**5:16** “The priest will then present her to stand trial before the LORD.  
**5:17** He must take some holy water in a clay jar and pour into it dust he has taken from the Tabernacle floor.  
**5:18** When the priest has presented the woman before the LORD, he must unbind her hair and place in her hands the offering of proof—the jealousy offering to determine whether her husband’s suspicions are justified. The priest will stand before her, holding the jar of bitter water that brings a curse to those who are guilty.

## 11장
