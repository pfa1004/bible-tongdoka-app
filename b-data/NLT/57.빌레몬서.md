# 57.빌레몬서 (NLT)



## 1장

**1:1** This letter is from Paul, a prisoner for preaching the Good News about Christ Jesus, and from our brother Timothy. I am writing to Philemon, our beloved co-worker,  
**1:2** and to our sister Apphia, and to our fellow soldier Archippus, and to the church that meets in your<sup>①</sup> house.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Throughout this letter, <i>you</i> and <i>your</i> are singular except in verses <a name='B:640 1:3'>3, <a name='B:640 1:22'>22, and 25.  
**1:3** May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.  
**1:4** I always thank my God when I pray for you, Philemon,  
**1:5** because I keep hearing about your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all of God’s people.  
**1:6** And I am praying that you will put into action the generosity that comes from your faith as you understand and experience all the good things we have in Christ.  
**1:7** Your love has given me much joy and comfort, my brother, for your kindness has often refreshed the hearts of God’s people.  
**1:8** That is why I am boldly asking a favor of you. I could demand it in the name of Christ because it is the right thing for you to do.  
**1:9** But because of our love, I prefer simply to ask you. Consider this as a request from me—Paul, an old man and now also a prisoner for the sake of Christ Jesus.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>a prisoner of Christ Jesus.</i>  
**1:10** I appeal to you to show kindness to my child, Onesimus. I became his father in the faith while here in prison.  
**1:11** Onesimus<sup>①</sup> hasn’t been of much use to you in the past, but now he is very useful to both of us.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Onesimus</i> means “useful.”  
**1:12** I am sending him back to you, and with him comes my own heart.  
**1:13** I wanted to keep him here with me while I am in these chains for preaching the Good News, and he would have helped me on your behalf.  
**1:14** But I didn’t want to do anything without your consent. I wanted you to help because you were willing, not because you were forced.  
**1:15** It seems you lost Onesimus for a little while so that you could have him back forever.  
**1:16** He is no longer like a slave to you. He is more than a slave, for he is a beloved brother, especially to me. Now he will mean much more to you, both as a man and as a brother in the Lord.  
**1:17** So if you consider me your partner, welcome him as you would welcome me.  
**1:18** If he has wronged you in any way or owes you anything, charge it to me.  
**1:19** I, PAUL, WRITE THIS WITH MY OWN HAND: I WILL REPAY IT. AND I WON’T MENTION THAT YOU OWE ME YOUR VERY SOUL!  
**1:20** Yes, my brother, please do me this favor<sup>①</sup> for the Lord’s sake. Give me this encouragement in Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>onaimen,</i> a play on the name Onesimus.  
**1:21** I am confident as I write this letter that you will do what I ask and even more!  
**1:22** One more thing—please prepare a guest room for me, for I am hoping that God will answer your prayers and let me return to you soon.  
**1:23** Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, sends you his greetings.  
**1:24** So do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my co-workers.  
**1:25** May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
