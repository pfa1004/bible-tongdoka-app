# 34.나훔 (NLT)



## 1장

**1:1** This message concerning Nineveh came as a vision to Nahum, who lived in Elkosh.  
**1:2** The LORD is a jealous God,<br>filled with vengeance and rage.<br>He takes revenge on all who oppose him<br>and continues to rage against his enemies!  
**1:3** The LORD is slow to get angry, but his power is great,<br>and he never lets the guilty go unpunished.<br>He displays his power in the whirlwind and the storm.<br>The billowing clouds are the dust beneath his feet.  
**1:4** At his command the oceans dry up,<br>and the rivers disappear.<br>The lush pastures of Bashan and Carmel fade,<br>and the green forests of Lebanon wither.  
**1:5** In his presence the mountains quake,<br>and the hills melt away;<br>the earth trembles,<br>and its people are destroyed.  
**1:6** Who can stand before his fierce anger?<br>Who can survive his burning fury?<br>His rage blazes forth like fire,<br>and the mountains crumble to dust in his presence.  
**1:7** The LORD is good,<br>a strong refuge when trouble comes.<br>He is close to those who trust in him.  
**1:8** But he will sweep away his enemies<sup>①</sup><br>in an overwhelming flood.<br>He will pursue his foes<br>into the darkness of night.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>As in Greek version; Hebrew reads <i>sweep away her place.</i>  
**1:9** Why are you scheming against the LORD?<br>He will destroy you with one blow;<br>he won’t need to strike twice!  
**1:10** His enemies, tangled like thornbushes<br>and staggering like drunks,<br>will be burned up like dry stubble in a field.  
**1:11** Who is this wicked counselor of yours<br>who plots evil against the LORD?  
**1:12** This is what the LORD says:<br>“Though the Assyrians have many allies,<br>they will be destroyed and disappear.<br>O my people, I have punished you before,<br>but I will not punish you again.  
**1:13** Now I will break the yoke of bondage from your neck<br>and tear off the chains of Assyrian oppression.”  
**1:14** And this is what the LORD says concerning the Assyrians in Nineveh:<br>“You will have no more children to carry on your name.<br>I will destroy all the idols in the temples of your gods.<br>I am preparing a grave for you<br>because you are despicable!”  
**1:15** <sup>①</sup>Look! A messenger is coming over the mountains with good news!<br>He is bringing a message of peace.<br>Celebrate your festivals, O people of Judah,<br>and fulfill all your vows,<br>for your wicked enemies will never invade your land again.<br>They will be completely destroyed!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Verse <a name='B:410 1:15'>1:15 is numbered 2:1 in Hebrew text.  

## 2장

**2:1** <sup>①</sup>Your enemy is coming to crush you, Nineveh.<br>Man the ramparts! Watch the roads!<br>Prepare your defenses! Call out your forces!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Verses <a name='B:410 2:1-13'>2:1-13 are numbered 2:2-14 in Hebrew text.  
**2:2** Even though the destroyer has destroyed Judah,<br>the LORD will restore its honor.<br>Israel’s vine has been stripped of branches,<br>but he will restore its splendor.  
**2:3** Shields flash red in the sunlight!<br>See the scarlet uniforms of the valiant troops!<br>Watch as their glittering chariots move into position,<br>with a forest of spears waving above them.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek and Syriac versions read <i>into position, / the horses whipped into a frenzy.</i>  
**2:4** The chariots race recklessly along the streets<br>and rush wildly through the squares.<br>They flash like firelight<br>and move as swiftly as lightning.  
**2:5** The king shouts to his officers;<br>they stumble in their haste,<br>rushing to the walls to set up their defenses.  
**2:6** The river gates have been torn open!<br>The palace is about to collapse!  
**2:7** Nineveh’s exile has been decreed,<br>and all the servant girls mourn its capture.<br>They moan like doves<br>and beat their breasts in sorrow.  
**2:8** Nineveh is like a leaking water reservoir!<br>The people are slipping away.<br>“Stop, stop!” someone shouts,<br>but no one even looks back.  
**2:9** Loot the silver!<br>Plunder the gold!<br>There’s no end to Nineveh’s treasures—<br>its vast, uncounted wealth.  
**2:10** Soon the city is plundered, empty, and ruined.<br>Hearts melt and knees shake.<br>The people stand aghast,<br>their faces pale and trembling.  
**2:11** Where now is that great Nineveh,<br>that den filled with young lions?<br>It was a place where people—like lions and their cubs—<br>walked freely and without fear.  
**2:12** The lion tore up meat for his cubs<br>and strangled prey for his mate.<br>He filled his den with prey,<br>his caverns with his plunder.  
**2:13** “I am your enemy!”<br>says the LORD of Heaven’s Armies.<br>“Your chariots will soon go up in smoke.<br>Your young men<sup>①</sup> will be killed in battle.<br>Never again will you plunder conquered nations.<br>The voices of your proud messengers will be heard no more.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>young lions.</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** What sorrow awaits Nineveh,<br>the city of murder and lies!<br>She is crammed with wealth<br>and is never without victims.  
**3:2** Hear the crack of whips,<br>the rumble of wheels!<br>Horses’ hooves pound,<br>and chariots clatter wildly.  
**3:3** See the flashing swords and glittering spears<br>as the charioteers charge past!<br>There are countless casualties,<br>heaps of bodies—<br>so many bodies that<br>people stumble over them.  
**3:4** All this because Nineveh,<br>the beautiful and faithless city,<br>mistress of deadly charms,<br>enticed the nations with her beauty.<br>She taught them all her magic,<br>enchanting people everywhere.  
**3:5** “I am your enemy!”<br>says the LORD of Heaven’s Armies.<br>“And now I will lift your skirts<br>and show all the earth your nakedness and shame.  
**3:6** I will cover you with filth<br>and show the world how vile you really are.  
**3:7** All who see you will shrink back and say,<br>‘Nineveh lies in ruins.<br>Where are the mourners?’<br>Does anyone regret your destruction?”  
**3:8** Are you any better than the city of Thebes,<sup>①</sup><br>situated on the Nile River, surrounded by water?<br>She was protected by the river on all sides,<br>walled in by water.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>No-amon;</i> also in <a name='B:410 3:10'>3:10.  
**3:9** Ethiopia<sup>①</sup> and the land of Egypt<br>gave unlimited assistance.<br>The nations of Put and Libya<br>were among her allies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Cush.</i>  
**3:10** Yet Thebes fell,<br>and her people were led away as captives.<br>Her babies were dashed to death<br>against the stones of the streets.<br>Soldiers threw dice<sup>①</sup> to get Egyptian officers as servants.<br>All their leaders were bound in chains.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>They cast lots.</i>  
**3:11** And you, Nineveh, will also stagger like a drunkard.<br>You will hide for fear of the attacking enemy.  
**3:12** All your fortresses will fall.<br>They will be devoured like the ripe figs<br>that fall into the mouths<br>of those who shake the trees.  
**3:13** Your troops will be as weak<br>and helpless as women.<br>The gates of your land will be opened wide to the enemy<br>and set on fire and burned.  
**3:14** Get ready for the siege!<br>Store up water!<br>Strengthen the defenses!<br>Go into the pits to trample clay,<br>and pack it into molds,<br>making bricks to repair the walls.  
**3:15** But the fire will devour you;<br>the sword will cut you down.<br>The enemy will consume you like locusts,<br>devouring everything they see.<br>There will be no escape,<br>even if you multiply like swarming locusts.  
**3:16** Your merchants have multiplied<br>until they outnumber the stars.<br>But like a swarm of locusts,<br>they strip the land and fly away.  
**3:17** Your guards<sup>①</sup> and officials are also like swarming locusts<br>that crowd together in the hedges on a cold day.<br>But like locusts that fly away when the sun comes up,<br>all of them will fly away and disappear.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>princes.</i>  
**3:18** Your shepherds are asleep, O Assyrian king;<br>your princes lie dead in the dust.<br>Your people are scattered across the mountains<br>with no one to gather them together.  
**3:19** There is no healing for your wound;<br>your injury is fatal.<br>All who hear of your destruction<br>will clap their hands for joy.<br>Where can anyone be found<br>who has not suffered from your continual cruelty?
