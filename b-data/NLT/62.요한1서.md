# 62.요한1서 (NLT)



## 1장

**1:1** We proclaim to you the one who existed from the beginning,<sup>①</sup> whom we have heard and seen. We saw him with our own eyes and touched him with our own hands. He is the Word of life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>What was from the beginning.</i>  
**1:2** This one who is life itself was revealed to us, and we have seen him. And now we testify and proclaim to you that he is the one who is eternal life. He was with the Father, and then he was revealed to us.  
**1:3** We proclaim to you what we ourselves have actually seen and heard so that you may have fellowship with us. And our fellowship is with the Father and with his Son, Jesus Christ.  
**1:4** We are writing these things so that you may fully share our joy.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>so that our joy may be complete;</i> some manuscripts read <i>your joy.</i>  
**1:5** This is the message we heard from Jesus<sup>①</sup> and now declare to you: God is light, and there is no darkness in him at all.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>from him.</i>  
**1:6** So we are lying if we say we have fellowship with God but go on living in spiritual darkness; we are not practicing the truth.  
**1:7** But if we are living in the light, as God is in the light, then we have fellowship with each other, and the blood of Jesus, his Son, cleanses us from all sin.  
**1:8** If we claim we have no sin, we are only fooling ourselves and not living in the truth.  
**1:9** But if we confess our sins to him, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all wickedness.  
**1:10** If we claim we have not sinned, we are calling God a liar and showing that his word has no place in our hearts.  

## 2장

**2:1** My dear children, I am writing this to you so that you will not sin. But if anyone does sin, we have an advocate who pleads our case before the Father. He is Jesus Christ, the one who is truly righteous.  
**2:2** He himself is the sacrifice that atones for our sins—and not only our sins but the sins of all the world.  
**2:3** And we can be sure that we know him if we obey his commandments.  
**2:4** If someone claims, “I know God,” but doesn’t obey God’s commandments, that person is a liar and is not living in the truth.  
**2:5** But those who obey God’s word truly show how completely they love him. That is how we know we are living in him.  
**2:6** Those who say they live in God should live their lives as Jesus did.  
**2:7** Dear friends, I am not writing a new commandment for you; rather it is an old one you have had from the very beginning. This old commandment—to love one another—is the same message you heard before.  
**2:8** Yet it is also new. Jesus lived the truth of this commandment, and you also are living it. For the darkness is disappearing, and the true light is already shining.  
**2:9** If anyone claims, “I am living in the light,” but hates a fellow believer,<sup>①</sup> that person is still living in darkness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>hates his brother;</i> also in <a name='B:690 2:11'>2:11.  
**2:10** Anyone who loves a fellow believer<sup>①</sup> is living in the light and does not cause others to stumble.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>loves his brother.</i>  
**2:11** But anyone who hates a fellow believer is still living and walking in darkness. Such a person does not know the way to go, having been blinded by the darkness.  
**2:12** I am writing to you who are God’s children<br>because your sins have been forgiven through Jesus.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>through his name.</i>  
**2:13** I am writing to you who are mature in the faith<sup>①</sup><br>because you know Christ, who existed from the beginning.<br>I am writing to you who are young in the faith<br>because you have won your battle with the evil one.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>to you fathers;</i> also in <a name='B:690 2:14'>2:14.  
**2:14** I have written to you who are God’s children<br>because you know the Father.<br>I have written to you who are mature in the faith<br>because you know Christ, who existed from the beginning.<br>I have written to you who are young in the faith<br>because you are strong.<br>God’s word lives in your hearts,<br>and you have won your battle with the evil one.  
**2:15** Do not love this world nor the things it offers you, for when you love the world, you do not have the love of the Father in you.  
**2:16** For the world offers only a craving for physical pleasure, a craving for everything we see, and pride in our achievements and possessions. These are not from the Father, but are from this world.  
**2:17** And this world is fading away, along with everything that people crave. But anyone who does what pleases God will live forever.  
**2:18** Dear children, the last hour is here. You have heard that the Antichrist is coming, and already many such antichrists have appeared. From this we know that the last hour has come.  
**2:19** These people left our churches, but they never really belonged with us; otherwise they would have stayed with us. When they left, it proved that they did not belong with us.  
**2:20** But you are not like that, for the Holy One has given you his Spirit,<sup>①</sup> and all of you know the truth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>But you have an anointing from the Holy One.</i>  
**2:21** So I am writing to you not because you don’t know the truth but because you know the difference between truth and lies.  
**2:22** And who is a liar? Anyone who says that Jesus is not the Christ.<sup>①</sup> Anyone who denies the Father and the Son is an antichrist.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>not the Messiah.</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>the antichrist.</i>  
**2:23** Anyone who denies the Son doesn’t have the Father, either. But anyone who acknowledges the Son has the Father also.  
**2:24** So you must remain faithful to what you have been taught from the beginning. If you do, you will remain in fellowship with the Son and with the Father.  
**2:25** And in this fellowship we enjoy the eternal life he promised us.  
**2:26** I am writing these things to warn you about those who want to lead you astray.  
**2:27** But you have received the Holy Spirit,<sup>①</sup> and he lives within you, so you don’t need anyone to teach you what is true. For the Spirit<sup>②</sup> teaches you everything you need to know, and what he teaches is true—it is not a lie. So just as he has taught you, remain in fellowship with Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>the anointing from him.</i> <sup>②</sup>Greek <i>the anointing.</i>  
**2:28** And now, dear children, remain in fellowship with Christ so that when he returns, you will be full of courage and not shrink back from him in shame.  
**2:29** Since we know that Christ is righteous, we also know that all who do what is right are God’s children.  

## 3장

**3:1** See how very much our Father loves us, for he calls us his children, and that is what we are! But the people who belong to this world don’t recognize that we are God’s children because they don’t know him.  
**3:2** Dear friends, we are already God’s children, but he has not yet shown us what we will be like when Christ appears. But we do know that we will be like him, for we will see him as he really is.  
**3:3** And all who have this eager expectation will keep themselves pure, just as he is pure.  
**3:4** Everyone who sins is breaking God’s law, for all sin is contrary to the law of God.  
**3:5** And you know that Jesus came to take away our sins, and there is no sin in him.  
**3:6** Anyone who continues to live in him will not sin. But anyone who keeps on sinning does not know him or understand who he is.  
**3:7** Dear children, don’t let anyone deceive you about this: When people do what is right, it shows that they are righteous, even as Christ is righteous.  
**3:8** But when people keep on sinning, it shows that they belong to the devil, who has been sinning since the beginning. But the Son of God came to destroy the works of the devil.  
**3:9** Those who have been born into God’s family do not make a practice of sinning, because God’s life<sup>①</sup> is in them. So they can’t keep on sinning, because they are children of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>because his seed.</i>  
**3:10** So now we can tell who are children of God and who are children of the devil. Anyone who does not live righteously and does not love other believers<sup>①</sup> does not belong to God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>does not love his brother.</i>  
**3:11** This is the message you have heard from the beginning: We should love one another.  
**3:12** We must not be like Cain, who belonged to the evil one and killed his brother. And why did he kill him? Because Cain had been doing what was evil, and his brother had been doing what was righteous.  
**3:13** So don’t be surprised, dear brothers and sisters,<sup>①</sup> if the world hates you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>brothers.</i>  
**3:14** If we love our brothers and sisters who are believers,<sup>①</sup> it proves that we have passed from death to life. But a person who has no love is still dead.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>the brothers;</i> similarly in <a name='B:690 3:16'>3:16.  
**3:15** Anyone who hates another brother or sister<sup>①</sup> is really a murderer at heart. And you know that murderers don’t have eternal life within them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>hates his brother.</i>  
**3:16** We know what real love is because Jesus gave up his life for us. So we also ought to give up our lives for our brothers and sisters.  
**3:17** If someone has enough money to live well and sees a brother or sister<sup>①</sup> in need but shows no compassion—how can God’s love be in that person?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>sees his brother.</i>  
**3:18** Dear children, let’s not merely say that we love each other; let us show the truth by our actions.  
**3:19** Our actions will show that we belong to the truth, so we will be confident when we stand before God.  
**3:20** Even if we feel guilty, God is greater than our feelings, and he knows everything.  
**3:21** Dear friends, if we don’t feel guilty, we can come to God with bold confidence.  
**3:22** And we will receive from him whatever we ask because we obey him and do the things that please him.  
**3:23** And this is his commandment: We must believe in the name of his Son, Jesus Christ, and love one another, just as he commanded us.  
**3:24** Those who obey God’s commandments remain in fellowship with him, and he with them. And we know he lives in us because the Spirit he gave us lives in us.  

## 4장

**4:1** Dear friends, do not believe everyone who claims to speak by the Spirit. You must test them to see if the spirit they have comes from God. For there are many false prophets in the world.  
**4:2** This is how we know if they have the Spirit of God: If a person claiming to be a prophet<sup>①</sup> acknowledges that Jesus Christ came in a real body, that person has the Spirit of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>If a spirit;</i> similarly in <a name='B:690 4:3'>4:3.  
**4:3** But if someone claims to be a prophet and does not acknowledge the truth about Jesus, that person is not from God. Such a person has the spirit of the Antichrist, which you heard is coming into the world and indeed is already here.  
**4:4** But you belong to God, my dear children. You have already won a victory over those people, because the Spirit who lives in you is greater than the spirit who lives in the world.  
**4:5** Those people belong to this world, so they speak from the world’s viewpoint, and the world listens to them.  
**4:6** But we belong to God, and those who know God listen to us. If they do not belong to God, they do not listen to us. That is how we know if someone has the Spirit of truth or the spirit of deception.  
**4:7** Dear friends, let us continue to love one another, for love comes from God. Anyone who loves is a child of God and knows God.  
**4:8** But anyone who does not love does not know God, for God is love.  
**4:9** God showed how much he loved us by sending his one and only Son into the world so that we might have eternal life through him.  
**4:10** This is real love—not that we loved God, but that he loved us and sent his Son as a sacrifice to take away our sins.  
**4:11** Dear friends, since God loved us that much, we surely ought to love each other.  
**4:12** No one has ever seen God. But if we love each other, God lives in us, and his love is brought to full expression in us.  
**4:13** And God has given us his Spirit as proof that we live in him and he in us.  
**4:14** Furthermore, we have seen with our own eyes and now testify that the Father sent his Son to be the Savior of the world.  
**4:15** All who declare that Jesus is the Son of God have God living in them, and they live in God.  
**4:16** We know how much God loves us, and we have put our trust in his love. God is love, and all who live in love live in God, and God lives in them.  
**4:17** And as we live in God, our love grows more perfect. So we will not be afraid on the day of judgment, but we can face him with confidence because we live like Jesus here in this world.  
**4:18** Such love has no fear, because perfect love expels all fear. If we are afraid, it is for fear of punishment, and this shows that we have not fully experienced his perfect love.  
**4:19** We love each other<sup>①</sup> because he loved us first.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>We love.</i> Other manuscripts read <i>We love God;</i> still others read <i>We love him.</i>  
**4:20** If someone says, “I love God,” but hates a fellow believer,<sup>①</sup> that person is a liar; for if we don’t love people we can see, how can we love God, whom we cannot see?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>hates his brother.</i>  
**4:21** And he has given us this command: Those who love God must also love their fellow believers.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>The one who loves God must also love his brother.</i>  

## 5장

**5:1** Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ<sup>①</sup> has become a child of God. And everyone who loves the Father loves his children, too.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the Messiah.</i>  
**5:2** We know we love God’s children if we love God and obey his commandments.  
**5:3** Loving God means keeping his commandments, and his commandments are not burdensome.  
**5:4** For every child of God defeats this evil world, and we achieve this victory through our faith.  
**5:5** And who can win this battle against the world? Only those who believe that Jesus is the Son of God.  
**5:6** And Jesus Christ was revealed as God’s Son by his baptism in water and by shedding his blood on the cross<sup>①</sup>—not by water only, but by water and blood. And the Spirit, who is truth, confirms it with his testimony.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>This is he who came by water and blood.</i>  
**5:7** So we have these three witnesses<sup>①</sup>—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>A few very late manuscripts add <i>in heaven—the Father, the Word, and the Holy Spirit, and these three are one. And we have three witnesses on earth.</i>  
**5:8** the Spirit, the water, and the blood—and all three agree.  
**5:9** Since we believe human testimony, surely we can believe the greater testimony that comes from God. And God has testified about his Son.  
**5:10** All who believe in the Son of God know in their hearts that this testimony is true. Those who don’t believe this are actually calling God a liar because they don’t believe what God has testified about his Son.  
**5:11** And this is what God has testified: He has given us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.  
**5:12** Whoever has the Son has life; whoever does not have God’s Son does not have life.  
**5:13** I have written this to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, so that you may know you have eternal life.  
**5:14** And we are confident that he hears us whenever we ask for anything that pleases him.  
**5:15** And since we know he hears us when we make our requests, we also know that he will give us what we ask for.  
**5:16** If you see a fellow believer<sup>①</sup> sinning in a way that does not lead to death, you should pray, and God will give that person life. But there is a sin that leads to death, and I am not saying you should pray for those who commit it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>a brother.</i>  
**5:17** All wicked actions are sin, but not every sin leads to death.  
**5:18** We know that God’s children do not make a practice of sinning, for God’s Son holds them securely, and the evil one cannot touch them.  
**5:19** We know that we are children of God and that the world around us is under the control of the evil one.  
**5:20** And we know that the Son of God has come, and he has given us understanding so that we can know the true God.<sup>①</sup> And now we live in fellowship with the true God because we live in fellowship with his Son, Jesus Christ. He is the only true God, and he is eternal life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>the one who is true.</i>  
**5:21** Dear children, keep away from anything that might take God’s place in your hearts.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Greek <i>keep yourselves from idols.</i>
