# 20.잠언 (NKJV)



## 1장

**1:1** The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel:  
**1:2** To know wisdom and instruction, To perceive the words of understanding,  
**1:3** To receive the instruction of wisdom, Justice, judgment, and equity;  
**1:4** To give prudence to the simple, To the young man knowledge and discretion—  
**1:5** A wise <i>man</i> will hear and increase learning, And a man of understanding will attain wise counsel,  
**1:6** To understand a proverb and an enigma, The words of the wise and their riddles.  
**1:7** The fear of the LORD <i>is</i> the beginning of knowledge, <i>But</i> fools despise wisdom and instruction.  
**1:8** My son, hear the instruction of your father, And do not forsake the law of your mother;  
**1:9** For they <i>will be</i> a graceful ornament on your head, And chains about your neck.  
**1:10** My son, if sinners entice you, Do not consent.  
**1:11** If they say, “Come with us, Let us lie in wait to <i>shed</i> blood; Let us lurk secretly for the innocent without cause;  
**1:12** Let us swallow them alive like Sheol, And whole, like those who go down to the Pit;  
**1:13** We shall find all <i>kinds</i> of precious possessions, We shall fill our houses with spoil;  
**1:14** Cast in your lot among us, Let us all have one purse”—  
**1:15** My son, do not walk in the way with them, Keep your foot from their path;  
**1:16** For their feet run to evil, And they make haste to shed blood.  
**1:17** Surely, in vain the net is spread In the sight of any bird;  
**1:18** But they lie in wait for their <i>own</i> blood, They lurk secretly for their <i>own</i> lives.  
**1:19** So <i>are</i> the ways of everyone who is greedy for gain; It takes away the life of its owners.  
**1:20** Wisdom calls aloud outside; She raises her voice in the open squares.  
**1:21** She cries out in the chief concourses, At the openings of the gates in the city She speaks her words:  
**1:22** “How long, you simple ones, will you love simplicity? For scorners delight in their scorning, And fools hate knowledge.  
**1:23** Turn at my rebuke; Surely I will pour out my spirit on you; I will make my words known to you.  
**1:24** Because I have called and you refused, I have stretched out my hand and no one regarded,  
**1:25** Because you disdained all my counsel, And would have none of my rebuke,  
**1:26** I also will laugh at your calamity; I will mock when your terror comes,  
**1:27** When your terror comes like a storm, And your destruction comes like a whirlwind, When distress and anguish come upon you.  
**1:28** “Then they will call on me, but I will not answer; They will seek me diligently, but they will not find me.  
**1:29** Because they hated knowledge And did not choose the fear of the LORD,  
**1:30** They would have none of my counsel <i>And</i> despised my every rebuke.  
**1:31** Therefore they shall eat the fruit of their own way, And be filled to the full with their own fancies.  
**1:32** For the turning away of the simple will slay them, And the complacency of fools will destroy them;  
**1:33** But whoever listens to me will dwell safely, And will be secure, without fear of evil.”  

## 2장

**2:1** My son, if you receive my words, And treasure my commands within you,  
**2:2** So that you incline your ear to wisdom, <i>And</i> apply your heart to understanding;  
**2:3** Yes, if you cry out for discernment, <i>And</i> lift up your voice for understanding,  
**2:4** If you seek her as silver, And search for her as <i>for</i> hidden treasures;  
**2:5** Then you will understand the fear of the LORD, And find the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For the LORD gives wisdom; From His mouth <i>come</i> knowledge and understanding;  
**2:7** He stores up sound wisdom for the upright; <i>He is</i> a shield to those who walk uprightly;  
**2:8** He guards the paths of justice, And preserves the way of His saints.  
**2:9** Then you will understand righteousness and justice, Equity <i>and</i> every good path.  
**2:10** When wisdom enters your heart, And knowledge is pleasant to your soul,  
**2:11** Discretion will preserve you; Understanding will keep you,  
**2:12** To deliver you from the way of evil, From the man who speaks perverse things,  
**2:13** From those who leave the paths of uprightness To walk in the ways of darkness;  
**2:14** Who rejoice in doing evil, <i>And</i> delight in the perversity of the wicked;  
**2:15** Whose ways <i>are</i> crooked, And <i>who are</i> devious in their paths;  
**2:16** To deliver you from the immoral woman, From the seductress <i>who</i> flatters with her words,  
**2:17** Who forsakes the companion of her youth, And forgets the covenant of her God.  
**2:18** For her house leads down to death, And her paths to the dead;  
**2:19** None who go to her return, Nor do they regain the paths of life—  
**2:20** So you may walk in the way of goodness, And keep <i>to</i> the paths of righteousness.  
**2:21** For the upright will dwell in the land, And the blameless will remain in it;  
**2:22** But the wicked will be cut off from the earth, And the unfaithful will be uprooted from it.  

## 3장

**3:1** My son, do not forget my law, But let your heart keep my commands;  
**3:2** For length of days and long life And peace they will add to you.  
**3:3** Let not mercy and truth forsake you; Bind them around your neck, Write them on the tablet of your heart,  
**3:4** <i>And</i> so find favor and high esteem In the sight of God and man.  
**3:5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart, And lean not on your own understanding;  
**3:6** In all your ways acknowledge Him, And He shall direct your paths.  
**3:7** Do not be wise in your own eyes; Fear the LORD and depart from evil.  
**3:8** It will be health to your flesh, And strength to your bones.  
**3:9** Honor the LORD with your possessions, And with the firstfruits of all your increase;  
**3:10** So your barns will be filled with plenty, And your vats will overflow with new wine.  
**3:11** My son, do not despise the chastening of the LORD, Nor detest His correction;  
**3:12** For whom the LORD loves He corrects, Just as a father the son <i>in whom</i> he delights.  
**3:13** Happy <i>is</i> the man <i>who</i> finds wisdom, And the man <i>who</i> gains understanding;  
**3:14** For her proceeds <i>are</i> better than the profits of silver, And her gain than fine gold.  
**3:15** She <i>is</i> more precious than rubies, And all the things you may desire cannot compare with her.  
**3:16** Length of days <i>is</i> in her right hand, In her left hand riches and honor.  
**3:17** Her ways <i>are</i> ways of pleasantness, And all her paths <i>are</i> peace.  
**3:18** She <i>is</i> a tree of life to those who take hold of her, And happy <i>are all</i> who retain her.  
**3:19** The LORD by wisdom founded the earth; By understanding He established the heavens;  
**3:20** By His knowledge the depths were broken up, And clouds drop down the dew.  
**3:21** My son, let them not depart from your eyes— Keep sound wisdom and discretion;  
**3:22** So they will be life to your soul And grace to your neck.  
**3:23** Then you will walk safely in your way, And your foot will not stumble.  
**3:24** When you lie down, you will not be afraid; Yes, you will lie down and your sleep will be sweet.  
**3:25** Do not be afraid of sudden terror, Nor of trouble from the wicked when it comes;  
**3:26** For the LORD will be your confidence, And will keep your foot from being caught.  
**3:27** Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due, When it is in the power of your hand to do <i>so.</i>  
**3:28** Do not say to your neighbor, “Go, and come back, And tomorrow I will give <i>it,</i>” When you have it with you.  
**3:29** Do not devise evil against your neighbor, For he dwells by you for safety’s sake.  
**3:30** Do not strive with a man without cause, If he has done you no harm.  
**3:31** Do not envy the oppressor, And choose none of his ways;  
**3:32** For the perverse <i>person is</i> an abomination to the LORD, But His secret counsel <i>is</i> with the upright.  
**3:33** The curse of the LORD <i>is</i> on the house of the wicked, But He blesses the home of the just.  
**3:34** Surely He scorns the scornful, But gives grace to the humble.  
**3:35** The wise shall inherit glory, But shame shall be the legacy of fools.  

## 4장

**4:1** Hear, <i>my</i> children, the instruction of a father, And give attention to know understanding;  
**4:2** For I give you good doctrine: Do not forsake my law.  
**4:3** When I was my father’s son, Tender and the only one in the sight of my mother,  
**4:4** He also taught me, and said to me: “Let your heart retain my words; Keep my commands, and live.  
**4:5** Get wisdom! Get understanding! Do not forget, nor turn away from the words of my mouth.  
**4:6** Do not forsake her, and she will preserve you; Love her, and she will keep you.  
**4:7** Wisdom <i>is</i> the principal thing; <i>Therefore</i> get wisdom. And in all your getting, get understanding.  
**4:8** Exalt her, and she will promote you; She will bring you honor, when you embrace her.  
**4:9** She will place on your head an ornament of grace; A crown of glory she will deliver to you.”  
**4:10** Hear, my son, and receive my sayings, And the years of your life will be many.  
**4:11** I have taught you in the way of wisdom; I have led you in right paths.  
**4:12** When you walk, your steps will not be hindered, And when you run, you will not stumble.  
**4:13** Take firm hold of instruction, do not let go; Keep her, for she <i>is</i> your life.  
**4:14** Do not enter the path of the wicked, And do not walk in the way of evil.  
**4:15** Avoid it, do not travel on it; Turn away from it and pass on.  
**4:16** For they do not sleep unless they have done evil; And their sleep is taken away unless they make <i>someone</i> fall.  
**4:17** For they eat the bread of wickedness, And drink the wine of violence.  
**4:18** But the path of the just <i>is</i> like the shining sun, That shines ever brighter unto the perfect day.  
**4:19** The way of the wicked <i>is</i> like darkness; They do not know what makes them stumble.  
**4:20** My son, give attention to my words; Incline your ear to my sayings.  
**4:21** Do not let them depart from your eyes; Keep them in the midst of your heart;  
**4:22** For they <i>are</i> life to those who find them, And health to all their flesh.  
**4:23** Keep your heart with all diligence, For out of it <i>spring</i> the issues of life.  
**4:24** Put away from you a deceitful mouth, And put perverse lips far from you.  
**4:25** Let your eyes look straight ahead, And your eyelids look right before you.  
**4:26** Ponder the path of your feet, And let all your ways be established.  
**4:27** Do not turn to the right or the left; Remove your foot from evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** My son, pay attention to my wisdom; Lend your ear to my understanding,  
**5:2** That you may preserve discretion, And your lips may keep knowledge.  
**5:3** For the lips of an immoral woman drip honey, And her mouth <i>is</i> smoother than oil;  
**5:4** But in the end she is bitter as wormwood, Sharp as a two-edged sword.  
**5:5** Her feet go down to death, Her steps lay hold of hell.  
**5:6** Lest you ponder <i>her</i> path of life— Her ways are unstable; You do not know <i>them.</i>  
**5:7** Therefore hear me now, <i>my</i> children, And do not depart from the words of my mouth.  
**5:8** Remove your way far from her, And do not go near the door of her house,  
**5:9** Lest you give your honor to others, And your years to the cruel <i>one;</i>  
**5:10** Lest aliens be filled with your wealth, And your labors <i>go</i> to the house of a foreigner;  
**5:11** And you mourn at last, When your flesh and your body are consumed,  
**5:12** And say: “How I have hated instruction, And my heart despised correction!  
**5:13** I have not obeyed the voice of my teachers, Nor inclined my ear to those who instructed me!  
**5:14** I was on the verge of total ruin, In the midst of the assembly and congregation.”  
**5:15** Drink water from your own cistern, And running water from your own well.  
**5:16** Should your fountains be dispersed abroad, Streams of water in the streets?  
**5:17** Let them be only your own, And not for strangers with you.  
**5:18** Let your fountain be blessed, And rejoice with the wife of your youth.  
**5:19** <i>As a</i> loving deer and a graceful doe, Let her breasts satisfy you at all times; And always be enraptured with her love.  
**5:20** For why should you, my son, be enraptured by an immoral woman, And be embraced in the arms of a seductress?  
**5:21** For the ways of man <i>are</i> before the eyes of the LORD, And He ponders all his paths.  
**5:22** His own iniquities entrap the wicked <i>man,</i> And he is caught in the cords of his sin.  
**5:23** He shall die for lack of instruction, And in the greatness of his folly he shall go astray.  

## 6장

**6:1** My son, if you become surety for your friend, <i>If</i> you have shaken hands in pledge for a stranger,  
**6:2** You are snared by the words of your mouth; You are taken by the words of your mouth.  
**6:3** So do this, my son, and deliver yourself; For you have come into the hand of your friend: Go and humble yourself; Plead with your friend.  
**6:4** Give no sleep to your eyes, Nor slumber to your eyelids.  
**6:5** Deliver yourself like a gazelle from the hand <i>of the hunter,</i> And like a bird from the hand of the fowler.  
**6:6** Go to the ant, you sluggard! Consider her ways and be wise,  
**6:7** Which, having no captain, Overseer or ruler,  
**6:8** Provides her supplies in the summer, <i>And</i> gathers her food in the harvest.  
**6:9** How long will you slumber, O sluggard? When will you rise from your sleep?  
**6:10** A little sleep, a little slumber, A little folding of the hands to sleep—  
**6:11** So shall your poverty come on you like a prowler, And your need like an armed man.  
**6:12** A worthless person, a wicked man, Walks with a perverse mouth;  
**6:13** He winks with his eyes, He shuffles his feet, He points with his fingers;  
**6:14** Perversity <i>is</i> in his heart, He devises evil continually, He sows discord.  
**6:15** Therefore his calamity shall come suddenly; Suddenly he shall be broken without remedy.  
**6:16** These six <i>things</i> the LORD hates, Yes, seven <i>are</i> an abomination to Him:  
**6:17** A proud look, A lying tongue, Hands that shed innocent blood,  
**6:18** A heart that devises wicked plans, Feet that are swift in running to evil,  
**6:19** A false witness <i>who</i> speaks lies, And one who sows discord among brethren.  
**6:20** My son, keep your father’s command, And do not forsake the law of your mother.  
**6:21** Bind them continually upon your heart; Tie them around your neck.  
**6:22** When you roam, they will lead you; When you sleep, they will keep you; And <i>when</i> you awake, they will speak with you.  
**6:23** For the commandment <i>is</i> a lamp, And the law a light; Reproofs of instruction <i>are</i> the way of life,  
**6:24** To keep you from the evil woman, From the flattering tongue of a seductress.  
**6:25** Do not lust after her beauty in your heart, Nor let her allure you with her eyelids.  
**6:26** For by means of a harlot <i>A man is reduced</i> to a crust of bread; And an adulteress will prey upon his precious life.  
**6:27** Can a man take fire to his bosom, And his clothes not be burned?  
**6:28** Can one walk on hot coals, And his feet not be seared?  
**6:29** So <i>is</i> he who goes in to his neighbor’s wife; Whoever touches her shall not be innocent.  
**6:30** <i>People</i> do not despise a thief If he steals to satisfy himself when he is starving.  
**6:31** Yet <i>when</i> he is found, he must restore sevenfold; He may have to give up all the substance of his house.  
**6:32** Whoever commits adultery with a woman lacks understanding; He <i>who</i> does so destroys his own soul.  
**6:33** Wounds and dishonor he will get, And his reproach will not be wiped away.  
**6:34** For jealousy <i>is</i> a husband’s fury; Therefore he will not spare in the day of vengeance.  
**6:35** He will accept no recompense, Nor will he be appeased though you give many gifts.  

## 7장

**7:1** My son, keep my words, And treasure my commands within you.  
**7:2** Keep my commands and live, And my law as the apple of your eye.  
**7:3** Bind them on your fingers; Write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to wisdom, “You <i>are</i> my sister,” And call understanding <i>your</i> nearest kin,  
**7:5** That they may keep you from the immoral woman, From the seductress <i>who</i> flatters with her words.  
**7:6** For at the window of my house I looked through my lattice,  
**7:7** And saw among the simple, I perceived among the youths, A young man devoid of understanding,  
**7:8** Passing along the street near her corner; And he took the path to her house  
**7:9** In the twilight, in the evening, In the black and dark night.  
**7:10** And there a woman met him, <i>With</i> the attire of a harlot, and a crafty heart.  
**7:11** She <i>was</i> loud and rebellious, Her feet would not stay at home.  
**7:12** At times <i>she was</i> outside, at times in the open square, Lurking at every corner.  
**7:13** So she caught him and kissed him; With an impudent face she said to him:  
**7:14** “<i>I have</i> peace offerings with me; Today I have paid my vows.  
**7:15** So I came out to meet you, Diligently to seek your face, And I have found you.  
**7:16** I have spread my bed with tapestry, Colored coverings of Egyptian linen.  
**7:17** I have perfumed my bed With myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.  
**7:18** Come, let us take our fill of love until morning; Let us delight ourselves with love.  
**7:19** For my husband <i>is</i> not at home; He has gone on a long journey;  
**7:20** He has taken a bag of money with him, <i>And</i> will come home on the appointed day.”  
**7:21** With her enticing speech she caused him to yield, With her flattering lips she seduced him.  
**7:22** Immediately he went after her, as an ox goes to the slaughter, Or as a fool to the correction of the stocks,  
**7:23** Till an arrow struck his liver. As a bird hastens to the snare, He did not know it <i>would cost</i> his life.  
**7:24** Now therefore, listen to me, <i>my</i> children; Pay attention to the words of my mouth:  
**7:25** Do not let your heart turn aside to her ways, Do not stray into her paths;  
**7:26** For she has cast down many wounded, And all who were slain by her were strong <i>men.</i>  
**7:27** Her house <i>is</i> the way to hell, Descending to the chambers of death.  

## 8장

**8:1** Does not wisdom cry out, And understanding lift up her voice?  
**8:2** She takes her stand on the top of the high hill, Beside the way, where the paths meet.  
**8:3** She cries out by the gates, at the entry of the city, At the entrance of the doors:  
**8:4** “To you, O men, I call, And my voice <i>is</i> to the sons of men.  
**8:5** O you simple ones, understand prudence, And you fools, be of an understanding heart.  
**8:6** Listen, for I will speak of excellent things, And from the opening of my lips <i>will come</i> right things;  
**8:7** For my mouth will speak truth; Wickedness <i>is</i> an abomination to my lips.  
**8:8** All the words of my mouth <i>are</i> with righteousness; Nothing crooked or perverse <i>is</i> in them.  
**8:9** They <i>are</i> all plain to him who understands, And right to those who find knowledge.  
**8:10** Receive my instruction, and not silver, And knowledge rather than choice gold;  
**8:11** For wisdom <i>is</i> better than rubies, And all the things one may desire cannot be compared with her.  
**8:12** “I, wisdom, dwell with prudence, And find out knowledge <i>and</i> discretion.  
**8:13** The fear of the LORD <i>is</i> to hate evil; Pride and arrogance and the evil way And the perverse mouth I hate.  
**8:14** Counsel <i>is</i> mine, and sound wisdom; I <i>am</i> understanding, I have strength.  
**8:15** By me kings reign, And rulers decree justice.  
**8:16** By me princes rule, and nobles, All the judges of the earth.  
**8:17** I love those who love me, And those who seek me diligently will find me.  
**8:18** Riches and honor <i>are</i> with me, Enduring riches and righteousness.  
**8:19** My fruit <i>is</i> better than gold, yes, than fine gold, And my revenue than choice silver.  
**8:20** I traverse the way of righteousness, In the midst of the paths of justice,  
**8:21** That I may cause those who love me to inherit wealth, That I may fill their treasuries.  
**8:22** “The LORD possessed me at the beginning of His way, Before His works of old.  
**8:23** I have been established from everlasting, From the beginning, before there was ever an earth.  
**8:24** When <i>there were</i> no depths I was brought forth, When <i>there were</i> no fountains abounding with water.  
**8:25** Before the mountains were settled, Before the hills, I was brought forth;  
**8:26** While as yet He had not made the earth or the fields, Or the primal dust of the world.  
**8:27** When He prepared the heavens, I <i>was</i> there, When He drew a circle on the face of the deep,  
**8:28** When He established the clouds above, When He strengthened the fountains of the deep,  
**8:29** When He assigned to the sea its limit, So that the waters would not transgress His command, When He marked out the foundations of the earth,  
**8:30** Then I was beside Him<i> as</i> a master craftsman; And I was daily <i>His</i> delight, Rejoicing always before Him,  
**8:31** Rejoicing in His inhabited world, And my delight <i>was</i> with the sons of men.  
**8:32** “Now therefore, listen to me,<i> my</i> children, For blessed <i>are those who</i> keep my ways.  
**8:33** Hear instruction and be wise, And do not disdain <i>it.</i>  
**8:34** Blessed is the man who listens to me, Watching daily at my gates, Waiting at the posts of my doors.  
**8:35** For whoever finds me finds life, And obtains favor from the LORD;  
**8:36** But he who sins against me wrongs his own soul; All those who hate me love death.”  

## 9장

**9:1** Wisdom has built her house, She has hewn out her seven pillars;  
**9:2** She has slaughtered her meat, She has mixed her wine, She has also furnished her table.  
**9:3** She has sent out her maidens, She cries out from the highest places of the city,  
**9:4** “Whoever <i>is</i> simple, let him turn in here!” <i>As for</i> him who lacks understanding, she says to him,  
**9:5** “Come, eat of my bread And drink of the wine I have mixed.  
**9:6** Forsake foolishness and live, And go in the way of understanding.  
**9:7** “He who corrects a scoffer gets shame for himself, And he who rebukes a wicked <i>man only</i> harms himself.  
**9:8** Do not correct a scoffer, lest he hate you; Rebuke a wise <i>man,</i> and he will love you.  
**9:9** Give <i>instruction</i> to a wise <i>man,</i> and he will be still wiser; Teach a just <i>man,</i> and he will increase in learning.  
**9:10** “The fear of the LORD <i>is</i> the beginning of wisdom, And the knowledge of the Holy One <i>is</i> understanding.  
**9:11** For by me your days will be multiplied, And years of life will be added to you.  
**9:12** If you are wise, you are wise for yourself, And <i>if</i> you scoff, you will bear <i>it</i> alone.”  
**9:13** A foolish woman is clamorous; <i>She is</i> simple, and knows nothing.  
**9:14** For she sits at the door of her house, On a seat <i>by</i> the highest places of the city,  
**9:15** To call to those who pass by, Who go straight on their way:  
**9:16** “Whoever <i>is</i> simple, let him turn in here”; And <i>as for</i> him who lacks understanding, she says to him,  
**9:17** “Stolen water is sweet, And bread <i>eaten</i> in secret is pleasant.”  
**9:18** But he does not know that the dead <i>are</i> there, <i>That</i> her guests <i>are</i> in the depths of hell.  

## 10장

**10:1** The proverbs of Solomon: A wise son makes a glad father, But a foolish son <i>is</i> the grief of his mother.  
**10:2** Treasures of wickedness profit nothing, But righteousness delivers from death.  
**10:3** The LORD will not allow the righteous soul to famish, But He casts away the desire of the wicked.  
**10:4** He who has a slack hand becomes poor, But the hand of the diligent makes rich.  
**10:5** He who gathers in summer <i>is</i> a wise son; He who sleeps in harvest <i>is</i> a son who causes shame.  
**10:6** Blessings <i>are</i> on the head of the righteous, But violence covers the mouth of the wicked.  
**10:7** The memory of the righteous <i>is</i> blessed, But the name of the wicked will rot.  
**10:8** The wise in heart will receive commands, But a prating fool will fall.  
**10:9** He who walks with integrity walks securely, But he who perverts his ways will become known.  
**10:10** He who winks with the eye causes trouble, But a prating fool will fall.  
**10:11** The mouth of the righteous <i>is</i> a well of life, But violence covers the mouth of the wicked.  
**10:12** Hatred stirs up strife, But love covers all sins.  
**10:13** Wisdom is found on the lips of him who has understanding, But a rod <i>is</i> for the back of him who is devoid of understanding.  
**10:14** Wise <i>people</i> store up knowledge, But the mouth of the foolish <i>is</i> near destruction.

**11:23** The desire of the righteous <i>is</i> only good, <i>But</i> the expectation of the wicked <i>is</i> wrath.

**11:25** The generous soul will be made rich, And he who waters will also be watered himself.  
**11:26** The people will curse him who withholds grain, But blessing <i>will be</i> on the head of him who sells <i>it.</i>  
**11:27** He who earnestly seeks good finds favor, But trouble will come to him who seeks <i>evil.</i>  
**11:28** He who trusts in his riches will fall, But the righteous will flourish like foliage.  
**11:29** He who troubles his own house will inherit the wind, And the fool <i>will be</i> servant to the wise of heart.  
**11:30** The fruit of the righteous <i>is a</i> tree of life, And he who wins souls <i>is</i> wise.  
**11:31** If the righteous will be recompensed on the earth, How much more the ungodly and the sinner.

**12:17** He <i>who</i> speaks truth declares righteousness, But a false witness, deceit.

**12:20** Deceit is in the heart of those who devise evil, But counselors of peace have joy.

**12:22** Lying lips <i>are</i> an abomination to the LORD, But those who deal truthfully <i>are</i> His delight.  
**12:23** A prudent man conceals knowledge, But the heart of fools proclaims foolishness.  
**12:24** The hand of the diligent will rule, But the lazy <i>man</i> will be put to forced labor.  
**12:25** Anxiety in the heart of man causes depression, But a good word makes it glad.  
**12:26** The righteous should choose his friends carefully, For the way of the wicked leads them astray.  
**12:27** The lazy <i>man</i> does not roast what he took in hunting, But diligence <i>is</i> man’s precious possession.  
**12:28** In the way of righteousness <i>is</i> life, And in <i>its</i> pathway <i>there is</i> no death.  

## 13장

**13:5** A righteous <i>man</i> hates lying, But a wicked <i>man</i> is loathsome and comes to shame.

**13:9** The light of the righteous rejoices, But the lamp of the wicked will be put out.  
**13:10** By pride comes nothing but strife, But with the well-advised <i>is</i> wisdom.  
**13:11** Wealth <i>gained by</i> dishonesty will be diminished, But he who gathers by labor will increase.  
**13:12** Hope deferred makes the heart sick, But <i>when</i> the desire comes, <i>it is</i> a tree of life.  
**13:13** He who despises the word will be destroyed, But he who fears the commandment will be rewarded.

**13:17** A wicked messenger falls into trouble, But a faithful ambassador <i>brings</i> health.  
**13:18** Poverty and shame <i>will come</i> to him who disdains correction, But he who regards a rebuke will be honored.  
**13:19** A desire accomplished is sweet to the soul, But <i>it is</i> an abomination to fools to depart from evil.  
**13:20** He who walks with wise <i>men</i> will be wise, But the companion of fools will be destroyed.  
**13:21** Evil pursues sinners, But to the righteous, good shall be repaid.  
**13:22** A good <i>man</i> leaves an inheritance to his children’s children, But the wealth of the sinner is stored up for the righteous.  
**13:23** Much food <i>is in</i> the fallow <i>ground</i> of the poor, And for lack of justice there is waste.  
**13:24** He who spares his rod hates his son, But he who loves him disciplines him promptly.  
**13:25** The righteous eats to the satisfying of his soul, But the stomach of the wicked shall be in want.  

## 14장

**14:11** The house of the wicked will be overthrown, But the tent of the upright will flourish.

**14:25** A true witness delivers souls, But a deceitful <i>witness</i> speaks lies.  
**14:26** In the fear of the LORD <i>there is</i> strong confidence, And His children will have a place of refuge.

**14:29** <i>He who is</i> slow to wrath has great understanding, But <i>he who is</i> impulsive exalts folly.  
**14:30** A sound heart <i>is</i> life to the body, But envy <i>is</i> rottenness to the bones.  
**14:31** He who oppresses the poor reproaches his Maker, But he who honors Him has mercy on the needy.  
**14:32** The wicked is banished in his wickedness, But the righteous has a refuge in his death.  
**14:33** Wisdom rests in the heart of him who has understanding, But <i>what is</i> in the heart of fools is made known.  
**14:34** Righteousness exalts a nation, But sin <i>is</i> a reproach to <i>any</i> people.  
**14:35** The king’s favor <i>is</i> toward a wise servant, But his wrath <i>is against</i> him who causes shame.

**15:5** A fool despises his father’s instruction, But he who receives correction is prudent.

**15:18** A wrathful man stirs up strife, But <i>he who is</i> slow to anger allays contention.  
**15:19** The way of the lazy <i>man is</i> like a hedge of thorns, But the way of the upright <i>is</i> a highway.  
**15:20** A wise son makes a father glad, But a foolish man despises his mother.

**15:24** The way of life <i>winds</i> upward for the wise, That he may turn away from hell below.  
**15:25** The LORD will destroy the house of the proud, But He will establish the boundary of the widow.  
**15:26** The thoughts of the wicked <i>are</i> an abomination to the LORD, But the words of the pure <i>are</i> pleasant.  
**15:27** He who is greedy for gain troubles his own house, But he who hates bribes will live.  
**15:28** The heart of the righteous studies how to answer, But the mouth of the wicked pours forth evil.  
**15:29** The LORD <i>is</i> far from the wicked, But He hears the prayer of the righteous.  
**15:30** The light of the eyes rejoices the heart, <i>And</i> a good report makes the bones healthy.  
**15:31** The ear that hears the rebukes of life Will abide among the wise.  
**15:32** He who disdains instruction despises his own soul, But he who heeds rebuke gets understanding.  
**15:33** The fear of the LORD <i>is</i> the instruction of wisdom, And before honor <i>is</i> humility.

**16:24** Pleasant words <i>are like</i> a honeycomb, Sweetness to the soul and health to the bones.  
**16:25** There is a way <i>that seems</i> right to a man, But its end <i>is</i> the way of death.  
**16:26** The person who labors, labors for himself, For his <i>hungry</i> mouth drives him <i>on.</i>

**16:28** A perverse man sows strife, And a whisperer separates the best of friends.  
**16:29** A violent man entices his neighbor, And leads him in a way <i>that is</i> not good.  
**16:30** He winks his eye to devise perverse things; He purses his lips <i>and</i> brings about evil.  
**16:31** The silver-haired head <i>is</i> a crown of glory, <i>If</i> it is found in the way of righteousness.  
**16:32** <i>He who is</i> slow to anger <i>is</i> better than the mighty, And he who rules his spirit than he who takes a city.  
**16:33** The lot is cast into the lap, But its every decision <i>is</i> from the LORD.  

## 17장

**17:7** Excellent speech is not becoming to a fool, Much less lying lips to a prince.

**17:20** He who has a deceitful heart finds no good, And he who has a perverse tongue falls into evil.

**17:23** A wicked <i>man</i> accepts a bribe behind the back To pervert the ways of justice.  
**17:24** Wisdom <i>is</i> in the sight of him who has understanding, But the eyes of a fool <i>are</i> on the ends of the earth.  
**17:25** A foolish son <i>is</i> a grief to his father, And bitterness to her who bore him.  
**17:26** Also, to punish the righteous <i>is</i> not good, <i>Nor</i> to strike princes for <i>their</i> uprightness.  
**17:27** He who has knowledge spares his words, <i>And</i> a man of understanding is of a calm spirit.  
**17:28** Even a fool is counted wise when he holds his peace; <i>When</i> he shuts his lips, <i>he is considered</i> perceptive.  

## 18장

**18:5** <i>It is</i> not good to show partiality to the wicked, <i>Or</i> to overthrow the righteous in judgment.  
**18:6** A fool’s lips enter into contention, And his mouth calls for blows.

**18:8** The words of a talebearer <i>are</i> like tasty trifles, And they go down into the inmost body.  
**18:9** He who is slothful in his work Is a brother to him who is a great destroyer.  
**18:10** The name of the LORD <i>is</i> a strong tower; The righteous run to it and are safe.  
**18:11** The rich man’s wealth <i>is</i> his strong city, And like a high wall in his own esteem.  
**18:12** Before destruction the heart of a man is haughty, And before honor <i>is</i> humility.  
**18:13** He who answers a matter before he hears <i>it,</i> It <i>is</i> folly and shame to him.  
**18:14** The spirit of a man will sustain him in sickness, But who can bear a broken spirit?  
**18:15** The heart of the prudent acquires knowledge, And the ear of the wise seeks knowledge.  
**18:16** A man’s gift makes room for him, And brings him before great men.  
**18:17** The first <i>one</i> to plead his cause <i>seems</i> right, Until his neighbor comes and examines him.  
**18:18** Casting lots causes contentions to cease, And keeps the mighty apart.  
**18:19** A brother offended <i>is harder to win</i> than a strong city, And contentions <i>are</i> like the bars of a castle.  
**18:20** A man’s stomach shall be satisfied from the fruit of his mouth; <i>From</i> the produce of his lips he shall be filled.  
**18:21** Death and life <i>are</i> in the power of the tongue, And those who love it will eat its fruit.  
**18:22** <i>He who</i> finds a wife finds a good <i>thing,</i> And obtains favor from the LORD.  
**18:23** The poor <i>man</i> uses entreaties, But the rich answers roughly.  
**18:24** A man <i>who has</i> friends must himself be friendly, But there is a friend <i>who</i> sticks closer than a brother.  

## 19장

**19:4** Wealth makes many friends, But the poor is separated from his friend.

**19:6** Many entreat the favor of the nobility, And every man <i>is</i> a friend to one who gives gifts.  
**19:7** All the brothers of the poor hate him; How much more do his friends go far from him! He may pursue <i>them with</i> words, <i>yet</i> they abandon <i>him.</i>

**19:9** A false witness will not go unpunished, And <i>he who</i> speaks lies shall perish.

**19:12** The king’s wrath <i>is</i> like the roaring of a lion, But his favor <i>is</i> like dew on the grass.  
**19:13** A foolish son <i>is</i> the ruin of his father, And the contentions of a wife <i>are</i> a continual dripping.  
**19:14** Houses and riches <i>are</i> an inheritance from fathers, But a prudent wife <i>is</i> from the LORD.  
**19:15** Laziness casts <i>one</i> into a deep sleep, And an idle person will suffer hunger.  
**19:16** He who keeps the commandment keeps his soul, <i>But</i> he who is careless of his ways will die.  
**19:17** He who has pity on the poor lends to the LORD, And He will pay back what he has given.  
**19:18** Chasten your son while there is hope, And do not set your heart on his destruction.

**19:21** There are many plans in a man’s heart, Nevertheless the LORD’s counsel—that will stand.  
**19:22** What is desired in a man is kindness, And a poor man is better than a liar.  
**19:23** The fear of the LORD <i>leads</i> to life, And <i>he who has it</i> will abide in satisfaction; He will not be visited with evil.  
**19:24** A lazy <i>man</i> buries his hand in the bowl, And will not so much as bring it to his mouth again.  
**19:25** Strike a scoffer, and the simple will become wary; Rebuke one who has understanding, <i>and</i> he will discern knowledge.  
**19:26** He who mistreats <i>his</i> father <i>and</i> chases away <i>his</i> mother <i>Is</i> a son who causes shame and brings reproach.  
**19:27** Cease listening to instruction, my son, And you will stray from the words of knowledge.  
**19:28** A disreputable witness scorns justice, And the mouth of the wicked devours iniquity.

## 20장

**20:10** Diverse weights <i>and</i> diverse measures, They <i>are</i> both alike, an abomination to the LORD.  
**20:11** Even a child is known by his deeds, Whether what he does <i>is</i> pure and right.  
**20:12** The hearing ear and the seeing eye, The LORD has made them both.  
**20:13** Do not love sleep, lest you come to poverty; Open your eyes, <i>and</i> you will be satisfied with bread.

**20:19** He who goes about <i>as</i> a talebearer reveals secrets; Therefore do not associate with one who flatters with his lips.  
**20:20** Whoever curses his father or his mother, His lamp will be put out in deep darkness.

**20:23** Diverse weights <i>are</i> an abomination to the LORD, And dishonest scales <i>are</i> not good.  
**20:24** A man’s steps <i>are</i> of the LORD; How then can a man understand his own way?

**20:26** A wise king sifts out the wicked, And brings the threshing wheel over them.  
**20:27** The spirit of a man <i>is</i> the lamp of the LORD, Searching all the inner depths of his heart.  
**20:28** Mercy and truth preserve the king, And by lovingkindness he upholds his throne.  
**20:29** The glory of young men <i>is</i> their strength, And the splendor of old men <i>is</i> their gray head.  
**20:30** Blows that hurt cleanse away evil, As <i>do</i> stripes the inner depths of the heart.  

## 21장

**21:1** The king’s heart <i>is</i> in the hand of the LORD, <i>Like</i> the rivers of water; He turns it wherever He wishes.

**21:3** To do righteousness and justice <i>Is</i> more acceptable to the LORD than sacrifice.

**21:7** The violence of the wicked will destroy them, Because they refuse to do justice.

**21:11** When the scoffer is punished, the simple is made wise; But when the wise is instructed, he receives knowledge.

**21:13** Whoever shuts his ears to the cry of the poor Will also cry himself and not be heard.  
**21:14** A gift in secret pacifies anger, And a bribe behind the back, strong wrath.  
**21:15** <i>It is</i> a joy for the just to do justice, But destruction <i>will come</i> to the workers of iniquity.  
**21:16** A man who wanders from the way of understanding Will rest in the assembly of the dead.  
**21:17** He who loves pleasure <i>will be</i> a poor man; He who loves wine and oil will not be rich.  
**21:18** The wicked <i>shall be</i> a ransom for the righteous, And the unfaithful for the upright.  
**21:19** Better to dwell in the wilderness, Than with a contentious and angry woman.  
**21:20** <i>There is</i> desirable treasure, And oil in the dwelling of the wise, But a foolish man squanders it.  
**21:21** He who follows righteousness and mercy Finds life, righteousness, and honor.  
**21:22** A wise <i>man</i> scales the city of the mighty, And brings down the trusted stronghold.  
**21:23** Whoever guards his mouth and tongue Keeps his soul from troubles.  
**21:24** A proud <i>and</i> haughty <i>man</i>— “Scoffer” <i>is</i> his name; He acts with arrogant pride.  
**21:25** The desire of the lazy <i>man</i> kills him, For his hands refuse to labor.  
**21:26** He covets greedily all day long, But the righteous gives and does not spare.  
**21:27** The sacrifice of the wicked <i>is</i> an abomination; How much more <i>when</i> he brings it with wicked intent!  
**21:28** A false witness shall perish, But the man who hears <i>him</i> will speak endlessly.  
**21:29** A wicked man hardens his face, But <i>as for</i> the upright, he establishes his way.  
**21:30** <i>There is</i> no wisdom or understanding Or counsel against the LORD.  
**21:31** The horse <i>is</i> prepared for the day of battle, But deliverance <i>is</i> of the LORD.

**22:5** Thorns <i>and</i> snares <i>are</i> in the way of the perverse; He who guards his soul will be far from them.

**22:14** The mouth of an immoral woman <i>is</i> a deep pit; He who is abhorred by the LORD will fall there.

**22:25** Lest you learn his ways And set a snare for your soul.  
**22:26** Do not be one of those who shakes hands in a pledge, One of those who is surety for debts;  
**22:27** If you have nothing <i>with which</i> to pay, Why should he take away your bed from under you?  
**22:28** Do not remove the ancient landmark Which your fathers have set.  
**22:29** Do you see a man <i>who</i> excels in his work? He will stand before kings; He will not stand before unknown <i>men.</i>  

## 23장

**23:2** And put a knife to your throat If you <i>are</i> a man given to appetite.

**23:6** Do not eat the bread of a miser, Nor desire his delicacies;  
**23:7** For as he thinks in his heart, so <i>is</i> he. “Eat and drink!” he says to you, But his heart is not with you.  
**23:8** The morsel you have eaten, you will vomit up, And waste your pleasant words.  
**23:9** Do not speak in the hearing of a fool, For he will despise the wisdom of your words.  
**23:10** Do not remove the ancient landmark, Nor enter the fields of the fatherless;  
**23:11** For their Redeemer <i>is</i> mighty; He will plead their cause against you.  
**23:12** Apply your heart to instruction, And your ears to words of knowledge.  
**23:13** Do not withhold correction from a child, For <i>if</i> you beat him with a rod, he will not die.  
**23:14** You shall beat him with a rod, And deliver his soul from hell.  
**23:15** My son, if your heart is wise, My heart will rejoice—indeed, I myself;  
**23:16** Yes, my inmost being will rejoice When your lips speak right things.  
**23:17** Do not let your heart envy sinners, But <i>be zealous</i> for the fear of the LORD all the day;  
**23:18** For surely there is a hereafter, And your hope will not be cut off.  
**23:19** Hear, my son, and be wise; And guide your heart in the way.  
**23:20** Do not mix with winebibbers, <i>Or</i> with gluttonous eaters of meat;  
**23:21** For the drunkard and the glutton will come to poverty, And drowsiness will clothe <i>a man</i> with rags.  
**23:22** Listen to your father who begot you, And do not despise your mother when she is old.  
**23:23** Buy the truth, and do not sell <i>it, Also</i> wisdom and instruction and understanding.  
**23:24** The father of the righteous will greatly rejoice, And he who begets a wise <i>child</i> will delight in him.  
**23:25** Let your father and your mother be glad, And let her who bore you rejoice.  
**23:26** My son, give me your heart, And let your eyes observe my ways.  
**23:27** For a harlot <i>is</i> a deep pit, And a seductress <i>is</i> a narrow well.  
**23:28** She also lies in wait as <i>for</i> a victim, And increases the unfaithful among men.  
**23:29** Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has contentions? Who has complaints? Who has wounds without cause? Who has redness of eyes?  
**23:30** Those who linger long at the wine, Those who go in search of mixed wine.  
**23:31** Do not look on the wine when it is red, When it sparkles in the cup, <i>When</i> it swirls around smoothly;  
**23:32** At the last it bites like a serpent, And stings like a viper.  
**23:33** Your eyes will see strange things, And your heart will utter perverse things.  
**23:34** Yes, you will be like one who lies down in the midst of the sea, Or like one who lies at the top of the mast, <i>saying:</i>  
**23:35** “They have struck me, <i>but</i> I was not hurt; They have beaten me, but I did not feel <i>it.</i> When shall I awake, that I may seek another <i>drink?</i>”  

## 24장

**24:6** For by wise counsel you will wage your own war, And in a multitude of counselors <i>there is</i> safety.  
**24:7** Wisdom <i>is</i> too lofty for a fool; He does not open his mouth in the gate.  
**24:8** He who plots to do evil Will be called a schemer.  
**24:9** The devising of foolishness <i>is</i> sin, And the scoffer <i>is</i> an abomination to men.

**24:11** Deliver <i>those who</i> are drawn toward death, And hold back <i>those</i> stumbling to the slaughter.

**24:20** For there will be no prospect for the evil <i>man;</i> The lamp of the wicked will be put out.  
**24:21** My son, fear the LORD and the king; Do not associate with those given to change;  
**24:22** For their calamity will rise suddenly, And who knows the ruin those two can bring?  
**24:23** These <i>things</i> also <i>belong</i> to the wise: <i>It is</i> not good to show partiality in judgment.  
**24:24** He who says to the wicked, “You <i>are</i> righteous,” Him the people will curse; Nations will abhor him.  
**24:25** But those who rebuke <i>the wicked</i> will have delight, And a good blessing will come upon them.  
**24:26** He who gives a right answer kisses the lips.  
**24:27** Prepare your outside work, Make it fit for yourself in the field; And afterward build your house.  
**24:28** Do not be a witness against your neighbor without cause, For would you deceive with your lips?  
**24:29** Do not say, “I will do to him just as he has done to me; I will render to the man according to his work.”  
**24:30** I went by the field of the lazy <i>man</i>, And by the vineyard of the man devoid of understanding;  
**24:31** And there it was, all overgrown with thorns; Its surface was covered with nettles; Its stone wall was broken down.  
**24:32** When I saw <i>it,</i> I considered <i>it</i> well; I looked on <i>it and</i> received instruction:  
**24:33** A little sleep, a little slumber, A little folding of the hands to rest;  
**24:34** So shall your poverty come <i>like</i> a prowler, And your need like an armed man.

**25:24** <i>It is</i> better to dwell in a corner of a housetop, Than in a house shared with a contentious woman.  
**25:25** <i>As</i> cold water to a weary soul, So <i>is</i> good news from a far country.  
**25:26** A righteous <i>man</i> who falters before the wicked <i>Is like</i> a murky spring and a polluted well.  
**25:27** <i>It is</i> not good to eat much honey; So to seek one’s own glory <i>is not</i> glory.  
**25:28** Whoever <i>has</i> no rule over his own spirit <i>Is like</i> a city broken down, without walls.  

## 26장

**26:1** As snow in summer and rain in harvest, So honor is not fitting for a fool.  
**26:2** Like a flitting sparrow, like a flying swallow, So a curse without cause shall not alight.  
**26:3** A whip for the horse, A bridle for the donkey, And a rod for the fool’s back.  
**26:4** Do not answer a fool according to his folly, Lest you also be like him.  
**26:5** Answer a fool according to his folly, Lest he be wise in his own eyes.  
**26:6** He who sends a message by the hand of a fool Cuts off <i>his own</i> feet <i>and</i> drinks violence.  
**26:7** <i>Like</i> the legs of the lame that hang limp <i>Is</i> a proverb in the mouth of fools.  
**26:8** Like one who binds a stone in a sling <i>Is</i> he who gives honor to a fool.  
**26:9** <i>Like</i> a thorn <i>that</i> goes into the hand of a drunkard <i>Is</i> a proverb in the mouth of fools.  
**26:10** The great <i>God</i> who formed everything Gives the fool <i>his</i> hire and the transgressor <i>his</i> wages.  
**26:11** As a dog returns to his own vomit, <i>So</i> a fool repeats his folly.  
**26:12** Do you see a man wise in his own eyes? <i>There is</i> more hope for a fool than for him.  
**26:13** The lazy <i>man</i> says, “<i>There is</i> a lion in the road! A fierce lion <i>is</i> in the streets!”  
**26:14** <i>As</i> a door turns on its hinges, So <i>does</i> the lazy <i>man</i> on his bed.  
**26:15** The lazy <i>man</i> buries his hand in the bowl; It wearies him to bring it back to his mouth.  
**26:16** The lazy <i>man is</i> wiser in his own eyes Than seven men who can answer sensibly.  
**26:17** He who passes by <i>and</i> meddles in a quarrel not his own <i>Is like</i> one who takes a dog by the ears.  
**26:18** Like a madman who throws firebrands, arrows, and death,  
**26:19** <i>Is</i> the man <i>who</i> deceives his neighbor, And says, “I was only joking!”  
**26:20** Where <i>there is</i> no wood, the fire goes out; And where <i>there is</i> no talebearer, strife ceases.  
**26:21** <i>As</i> charcoal <i>is</i> to burning coals, and wood to fire, So <i>is</i> a contentious man to kindle strife.  
**26:22** The words of a talebearer <i>are</i> like tasty trifles, And they go down into the inmost body.  
**26:23** Fervent lips with a wicked heart <i>Are like</i> earthenware covered with silver dross.  
**26:24** He who hates, disguises <i>it</i> with his lips, And lays up deceit within himself;  
**26:25** When he speaks kindly, do not believe him, For <i>there are</i> seven abominations in his heart;  
**26:26** <i>Though his</i> hatred is covered by deceit, His wickedness will be revealed before the assembly.  
**26:27** Whoever digs a pit will fall into it, And he who rolls a stone will have it roll back on him.  
**26:28** A lying tongue hates <i>those who are</i> crushed by it, And a flattering mouth works ruin.  

## 27장

**27:1** Do not boast about tomorrow, For you do not know what a day may bring forth.  
**27:2** Let another man praise you, and not your own mouth; A stranger, and not your own lips.  
**27:3** A stone <i>is</i> heavy and sand <i>is</i> weighty, But a fool’s wrath <i>is</i> heavier than both of them.  
**27:4** Wrath <i>is</i> cruel and anger a torrent, But who <i>is</i> able to stand before jealousy?  
**27:5** Open rebuke <i>is</i> better Than love carefully concealed.  
**27:6** Faithful <i>are</i> the wounds of a friend, But the kisses of an enemy <i>are</i> deceitful.  
**27:7** A satisfied soul loathes the honeycomb, But to a hungry soul every bitter thing <i>is</i> sweet.  
**27:8** Like a bird that wanders from its nest <i>Is</i> a man who wanders from his place.  
**27:9** Ointment and perfume delight the heart, And the sweetness of a man’s friend <i>gives delight</i> by hearty counsel.  
**27:10** Do not forsake your own friend or your father’s friend, Nor go to your brother’s house in the day of your calamity; Better <i>is</i> a neighbor nearby than a brother far away.  
**27:11** My son, be wise, and make my heart glad, That I may answer him who reproaches me.  
**27:12** A prudent <i>man</i> foresees evil <i>and</i> hides himself; The simple pass on <i>and</i> are punished.  
**27:13** Take the garment of him who is surety for a stranger, And hold it in pledge <i>when</i> he is surety for a seductress.  
**27:14** He who blesses his friend with a loud voice, rising early in the morning, It will be counted a curse to him.  
**27:15** A continual dripping on a very rainy day And a contentious woman are alike;  
**27:16** Whoever restrains her restrains the wind, And grasps oil with his right hand.  
**27:17** <i>As</i> iron sharpens iron, So a man sharpens the countenance of his friend.  
**27:18** Whoever keeps the fig tree will eat its fruit; So he who waits on his master will be honored.  
**27:19** As in water face <i>reflects</i> face, So a man’s heart <i>reveals</i> the man.  
**27:20** Hell and Destruction are never full; So the eyes of man are never satisfied.  
**27:21** The refining pot <i>is</i> for silver and the furnace for gold, And a man <i>is valued</i> by what others say of him.  
**27:22** Though you grind a fool in a mortar with a pestle along with crushed grain, <i>Yet</i> his foolishness will not depart from him.  
**27:23** Be diligent to know the state of your flocks, <i>And</i> attend to your herds;  
**27:24** For riches <i>are</i> not forever, Nor does a crown <i>endure</i> to all generations.  
**27:25** <i>When</i> the hay is removed, and the tender grass shows itself, And the herbs of the mountains are gathered in,  
**27:26** The lambs <i>will provide</i> your clothing, And the goats the price of a field;  
**27:27** <i>You shall have</i> enough goats’ milk for your food, For the food of your household, And the nourishment of your maidservants.  

## 28장

**28:1** The wicked flee when no one pursues, But the righteous are bold as a lion.  
**28:2** Because of the transgression of a land, many <i>are</i> its princes; But by a man of understanding <i>and</i> knowledge Right will be prolonged.  
**28:3** A poor man who oppresses the poor <i>Is like</i> a driving rain which leaves no food.  
**28:4** Those who forsake the law praise the wicked, But such as keep the law contend with them.  
**28:5** Evil men do not understand justice, But those who seek the LORD understand all.  
**28:6** Better <i>is</i> the poor who walks in his integrity Than one perverse <i>in his</i> ways, though he <i>be</i> rich.  
**28:7** Whoever keeps the law <i>is</i> a discerning son, But a companion of gluttons shames his father.  
**28:8** One who increases his possessions by usury and extortion Gathers it for him who will pity the poor.  
**28:9** One who turns away his ear from hearing the law, Even his prayer <i>is</i> an abomination.  
**28:10** Whoever causes the upright to go astray in an evil way, He himself will fall into his own pit; But the blameless will inherit good.  
**28:11** The rich man <i>is</i> wise in his own eyes, But the poor who has understanding searches him out.  
**28:12** When the righteous rejoice, <i>there is</i> great glory; But when the wicked arise, men hide themselves.  
**28:13** He who covers his sins will not prosper, But whoever confesses and forsakes <i>them</i> will have mercy.  
**28:14** Happy <i>is</i> the man who is always reverent, But he who hardens his heart will fall into calamity.  
**28:15** <i>Like</i> a roaring lion and a charging bear <i>Is</i> a wicked ruler over poor people.  
**28:16** A ruler who lacks understanding <i>is</i> a great oppressor, <i>But</i> he who hates covetousness will prolong <i>his</i> days.  
**28:17** A man burdened with bloodshed will flee into a pit; Let no one help him.  
**28:18** Whoever walks blamelessly will be saved, But <i>he who is</i> perverse <i>in his</i> ways will suddenly fall.  
**28:19** He who tills his land will have plenty of bread, But he who follows frivolity will have poverty enough!  
**28:20** A faithful man will abound with blessings, But he who hastens to be rich will not go unpunished.  
**28:21** To show partiality <i>is</i> not good, Because for a piece of bread a man will transgress.  
**28:22** A man with an evil eye hastens after riches, And does not consider that poverty will come upon him.  
**28:23** He who rebukes a man will find more favor afterward Than he who flatters with the tongue.  
**28:24** Whoever robs his father or his mother, And says, “<i>It is</i> no transgression,” The same <i>is</i> companion to a destroyer.  
**28:25** He who is of a proud heart stirs up strife, But he who trusts in the LORD will be prospered.  
**28:26** He who trusts in his own heart is a fool, But whoever walks wisely will be delivered.  
**28:27** He who gives to the poor will not lack, But he who hides his eyes will have many curses.  
**28:28** When the wicked arise, men hide themselves; But when they perish, the righteous increase.  

## 29장

**29:1** He who is often rebuked, <i>and</i> hardens <i>his</i> neck, Will suddenly be destroyed, and that without remedy.  
**29:2** When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice; But when a wicked <i>man</i> rules, the people groan.  
**29:3** Whoever loves wisdom makes his father rejoice, But a companion of harlots wastes <i>his</i> wealth.  
**29:4** The king establishes the land by justice, But he who receives bribes overthrows it.  
**29:5** A man who flatters his neighbor Spreads a net for his feet.  
**29:6** By transgression an evil man is snared, But the righteous sings and rejoices.  
**29:7** The righteous considers the cause of the poor, <i>But</i> the wicked does not understand <i>such</i> knowledge.  
**29:8** Scoffers set a city aflame, But wise <i>men</i> turn away wrath.  
**29:9** <i>If</i> a wise man contends with a foolish man, Whether <i>the fool</i> rages or laughs, <i>there is</i> no peace.  
**29:10** The bloodthirsty hate the blameless, But the upright seek his well-being.  
**29:11** A fool vents all his feelings, But a wise <i>man</i> holds them back.  
**29:12** If a ruler pays attention to lies, All his servants <i>become</i> wicked.  
**29:13** The poor <i>man</i> and the oppressor have this in common: The LORD gives light to the eyes of both.  
**29:14** The king who judges the poor with truth, His throne will be established forever.  
**29:15** The rod and rebuke give wisdom, But a child left <i>to himself</i> brings shame to his mother.  
**29:16** When the wicked are multiplied, transgression increases; But the righteous will see their fall.  
**29:17** Correct your son, and he will give you rest; Yes, he will give delight to your soul.  
**29:18** Where <i>there is</i> no revelation, the people cast off restraint; But happy <i>is</i> he who keeps the law.  
**29:19** A servant will not be corrected by mere words; For though he understands, he will not respond.  
**29:20** Do you see a man hasty in his words? <i>There is</i> more hope for a fool than for him.  
**29:21** He who pampers his servant from childhood Will have him as a son in the end.  
**29:22** An angry man stirs up strife, And a furious man abounds in transgression.  
**29:23** A man’s pride will bring him low, But the humble in spirit will retain honor.  
**29:24** Whoever is a partner with a thief hates his own life; He swears to tell the truth, but reveals nothing.  
**29:25** The fear of man brings a snare, But whoever trusts in the LORD shall be safe.  
**29:26** Many seek the ruler’s favor, But justice for man <i>comes</i> from the LORD.  
**29:27** An unjust man <i>is</i> an abomination to the righteous, And <i>he who is</i> upright in the way <i>is</i> an abomination to the wicked.  

## 30장

**30:1** The words of Agur the son of Jakeh, <i>his</i> utterance. This man declared to Ithiel—to Ithiel and Ucal:  
**30:2** Surely I <i>am</i> more stupid than <i>any</i> man, And do not have the understanding of a man.  
**30:3** I neither learned wisdom Nor have knowledge of the Holy One.  
**30:4** Who has ascended into heaven, or descended? Who has gathered the wind in His fists? Who has bound the waters in a garment? Who has established all the ends of the earth? What <i>is</i> His name, and what <i>is</i> His Son’s name, If you know?  
**30:5** Every word of God <i>is</i> pure; He <i>is</i> a shield to those who put their trust in Him.  
**30:6** Do not add to His words, Lest He rebuke you, and you be found a liar.  
**30:7** Two <i>things</i> I request of You (Deprive me not before I die):  
**30:8** Remove falsehood and lies far from me; Give me neither poverty nor riches— Feed me with the food allotted to me;  
**30:9** Lest I be full and deny <i>You,</i> And say, “Who <i>is</i> the LORD?” Or lest I be poor and steal, And profane the name of my God.  
**30:10** Do not malign a servant to his master, Lest he curse you, and you be found guilty.  
**30:11** <i>There is</i> a generation <i>that</i> curses its father, And does not bless its mother.  
**30:12** <i>There is</i> a generation <i>that is</i> pure in its own eyes, <i>Yet</i> is not washed from its filthiness.  
**30:13** <i>There is</i> a generation—oh, how lofty are their eyes! And their eyelids are lifted up.  
**30:14** <i>There is</i> a generation whose teeth <i>are like</i> swords, And whose fangs <i>are like</i> knives, To devour the poor from off the earth, And the needy from <i>among</i> men.  
**30:15** The leech has two daughters— Give <i>and</i> Give! There are three <i>things that</i> are never satisfied, Four never say, “Enough!”:  
**30:16** The grave, The barren womb, The earth <i>that</i> is not satisfied with water— And the fire never says, “Enough!”  
**30:17** The eye <i>that</i> mocks <i>his</i> father, And scorns obedience to <i>his</i> mother, The ravens of the valley will pick it out, And the young eagles will eat it.  
**30:18** There are three <i>things which</i> are too wonderful for me, Yes, four <i>which</i> I do not understand:  
**30:19** The way of an eagle in the air, The way of a serpent on a rock, The way of a ship in the midst of the sea, And the way of a man with a virgin.  
**30:20** This <i>is</i> the way of an adulterous woman: She eats and wipes her mouth, And says, “I have done no wickedness.”  
**30:21** For three <i>things</i> the earth is perturbed, Yes, for four it cannot bear up:  
**30:22** For a servant when he reigns, A fool when he is filled with food,  
**30:23** A hateful <i>woman</i> when she is married, And a maidservant who succeeds her mistress.  
**30:24** There are four <i>things which</i> are little on the earth, But they <i>are</i> exceedingly wise:  
**30:25** The ants <i>are</i> a people not strong, Yet they prepare their food in the summer;  
**30:26** The rock badgers are a feeble folk, Yet they make their homes in the crags;  
**30:27** The locusts have no king, Yet they all advance in ranks;  
**30:28** The spider skillfully grasps with its hands, And it is in kings’ palaces.  
**30:29** There are three <i>things which</i> are majestic in pace, Yes, four <i>which</i> are stately in walk:  
**30:30** A lion, <i>which is</i> mighty among beasts And does not turn away from any;  
**30:31** A greyhound, A male goat also, And a king <i>whose</i> troops <i>are</i> with him.  
**30:32** If you have been foolish in exalting yourself, Or if you have devised evil, <i>put your</i> hand on <i>your</i> mouth.  
**30:33** For <i>as</i> the churning of milk produces butter, And wringing the nose produces blood, So the forcing of wrath produces strife.  

## 31장

**31:1** The words of King Lemuel, the utterance which his mother taught him:  
**31:2** What, my son? And what, son of my womb? And what, son of my vows?  
**31:3** Do not give your strength to women, Nor your ways to that which destroys kings.  
**31:4** <i>It is</i> not for kings, O Lemuel, <i>It is</i> not for kings to drink wine, Nor for princes intoxicating drink;  
**31:5** Lest they drink and forget the law, And pervert the justice of all the afflicted.  
**31:6** Give strong drink to him who is perishing, And wine to those who are bitter of heart.  
**31:7** Let him drink and forget his poverty, And remember his misery no more.  
**31:8** Open your mouth for the speechless, In the cause of all <i>who are</i> appointed to die.  
**31:9** Open your mouth, judge righteously, And plead the cause of the poor and needy.  
**31:10** Who can find a virtuous wife? For her worth <i>is</i> far above rubies.  
**31:11** The heart of her husband safely trusts her; So he will have no lack of gain.  
**31:12** She does him good and not evil All the days of her life.  
**31:13** She seeks wool and flax, And willingly works with her hands.  
**31:14** She is like the merchant ships, She brings her food from afar.  
**31:15** She also rises while it is yet night, And provides food for her household, And a portion for her maidservants.  
**31:16** She considers a field and buys it; From her profits she plants a vineyard.  
**31:17** She girds herself with strength, And strengthens her arms.  
**31:18** She perceives that her merchandise <i>is</i> good, And her lamp does not go out by night.  
**31:19** She stretches out her hands to the distaff, And her hand holds the spindle.  
**31:20** She extends her hand to the poor, Yes, she reaches out her hands to the needy.  
**31:21** She is not afraid of snow for her household, For all her household <i>is</i> clothed with scarlet.  
**31:22** She makes tapestry for herself; Her clothing <i>is</i> fine linen and purple.  
**31:23** Her husband is known in the gates, When he sits among the elders of the land.  
**31:24** She makes linen garments and sells <i>them,</i> And supplies sashes for the merchants.  
**31:25** Strength and honor <i>are</i> her clothing; She shall rejoice in time to come.  
**31:26** She opens her mouth with wisdom, And on her tongue <i>is</i> the law of kindness.  
**31:27** She watches over the ways of her household, And does not eat the bread of idleness.  
**31:28** Her children rise up and call her blessed; Her husband <i>also,</i> and he praises her:  
**31:29** “Many daughters have done well, But you excel them all.”  
**31:30** Charm <i>is</i> deceitful and beauty <i>is</i> passing, But a woman <i>who</i> fears the LORD, she shall be praised.  
**31:31** Give her of the fruit of her hands, And let her own works praise her in the gates.
