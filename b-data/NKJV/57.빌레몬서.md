# 57.빌레몬서 (NKJV)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy <i>our</i> brother,<br>To Philemon our beloved <i>friend</i> and fellow laborer,  
**1:2** to <sup>①</sup>the beloved Apphia, Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church in your house:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>NU <i>our sister Apphia</i>  
**1:3** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:4** I thank my God, making mention of you always in my prayers,  
**1:5** hearing of your love and faith which you have toward the Lord Jesus and toward all the saints,  
**1:6** that the sharing of your faith may become effective by the acknowledgment of every good thing which is in <sup>①</sup>you in Christ Jesus.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>NU, M <i>us</i>  
**1:7** For we <sup>①</sup>have great <sup>②</sup>joy and <sup>③</sup>consolation in your love, because the <sup>④</sup>hearts of the saints have been refreshed by you, brother.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>NU <i>had</i> <sup>②</sup>M <i>thanksgiving</i> <sup>③</sup><i>comfort</i> <sup>④</sup>Lit. <i>inward parts</i>, heart, liver, and lungs  
**1:8** Therefore, though I might be very bold in Christ to command you what is fitting,  
**1:9** <i>yet</i> for love’s sake I rather appeal <i>to you</i>—being such a one as Paul, the aged, and now also a prisoner of Jesus Christ—  
**1:10** I appeal to you for my son Onesimus, whom I have begotten <i>while</i> in my chains,  
**1:11** who once was unprofitable to you, but now is profitable to you and to me.  
**1:12** I am sending him <sup>①</sup>back. You therefore receive him, that is, my own <sup>②</sup>heart,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>NU <i>back to you in person, that is, my own heart,</i> <sup>②</sup>See v. 7.  
**1:13** whom I wished to keep with me, that on your behalf he might minister to me in my chains for the gospel.  
**1:14** But without your consent I wanted to do nothing, that your good deed might not be by compulsion, as it were, but voluntary.  
**1:15** For perhaps he departed for a while for this <i>purpose,</i> that you might receive him forever,  
**1:16** no longer as a slave but more than a slave—a beloved brother, especially to me but how much more to you, both in the flesh and in the Lord.  
**1:17** If then you count me as a partner, receive him as <i>you would</i> me.  
**1:18** But if he has wronged you or owes anything, put that on my account.  
**1:19** I, Paul, am writing with my own hand. I will repay—not to mention to you that you owe me even your own self besides.  
**1:20** Yes, brother, let me have joy from you in the Lord; refresh my heart in the Lord.  
**1:21** Having confidence in your obedience, I write to you, knowing that you will do even more than I say.  
**1:22** But, meanwhile, also prepare a guest room for me, for I trust that through your prayers I shall be granted to you.  
**1:23** Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, greets you,  
**1:24** <i>as do</i> Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke, my fellow laborers.  
**1:25** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <i>be</i> with your spirit. Amen.
