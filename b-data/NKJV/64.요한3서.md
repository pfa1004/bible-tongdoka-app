# 64.요한3서 (NKJV)



## 1장

**1:1** The Elder,<br>To the beloved Gaius, whom I love in truth:  
**1:2** Beloved, I pray that you may prosper in all things and be in health, just as your soul prospers.  
**1:3** For I rejoiced greatly when brethren came and testified of the truth <i>that is</i> in you, just as you walk in the truth.  
**1:4** I have no greater joy than to hear that my children walk in <sup>①</sup>truth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>NU <i>the truth</i>  
**1:5** Beloved, you do faithfully whatever you do for the brethren <sup>①</sup>and for strangers,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>NU <i>and especially for</i>  
**1:6** who have borne witness of your love before the church. <i>If</i> you send them forward on their journey in a manner worthy of God, you will do well,  
**1:7** because they went forth for His name’s sake, taking nothing from the Gentiles.  
**1:8** We therefore ought to receive<sup>①</sup> such, that we may become fellow workers for the truth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>NU <i>support</i>  
**1:9** I wrote to the church, but Diotrephes, who loves to have the preeminence among them, does not receive us.  
**1:10** Therefore, if I come, I will call to mind his deeds which he does, prating<sup>①</sup> against us with malicious words. And not content with that, he himself does not receive the brethren, and forbids those who wish to, putting <i>them</i> out of the church.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>talking nonsense</i>  
**1:11** Beloved, do not imitate what is evil, but what is good. He who does good is of God, <sup>①</sup>but he who does evil has not seen God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>NU, M omit <i>but</i>  
**1:12** Demetrius has a <i>good</i> testimony from all, and from the truth itself. And we also <sup>①</sup>bear witness, and you know that our testimony is true.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>testify</i>  
**1:13** I had many things to write, but I do not wish to write to you with pen and ink;  
**1:14** but I hope to see you shortly, and we shall speak face to face.<br>Peace to you. Our friends greet you. Greet the friends by name.
