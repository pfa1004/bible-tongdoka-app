# 19.시편 (NJB)



## 1장

**1:1** How blessed is anyone who rejects the advice of the wicked and does not take a stand in the path that sinners tread, nor a seat in company with cynics,  
**1:2** but who delights in the law of Yahweh and murmurs his law day and night.  
**1:3** Such a one is like a tree planted near streams; it bears fruit in season and its leaves never wither, and every project succeeds.  
**1:4** How different the wicked, how different! Just like chaff blown around by the wind  
**1:5** the wicked will not stand firm at the Judgement nor sinners in the gathering of the upright.  
**1:6** For Yahweh watches over the path of the upright, but the path of the wicked is doomed.  

## 2장

**2:1** Why this uproar among the nations, this impotent muttering of the peoples?  
**2:2** Kings of the earth take up position, princes plot together against Yahweh and his anointed,  
**2:3** 'Now let us break their fetters! Now let us throw off their bonds!'  
**2:4** He who is enthroned in the heavens laughs, Yahweh makes a mockery of them,  
**2:5** then in his anger rebukes them, in his rage he strikes them with terror.  
**2:6** 'I myself have anointed my king on Zion my holy mountain.'  
**2:7** I will proclaim the decree of Yahweh: He said to me, 'You are my son, today have I fathered you.  
**2:8** Ask of me, and I shall give you the nations as your birthright, the whole wide world as your possession.  
**2:9** With an iron sceptre you will break them, shatter them like so many pots.'  
**2:10** So now, you kings, come to your senses, you earthly rulers, learn your lesson!  
**2:11** In fear be submissive to Yahweh;  
**2:12** with trembling kiss his feet, lest he be angry and your way come to nothing, for his fury flares up in a moment. How blessed are all who take refuge in him!

**3:7** Arise, Yahweh, rescue me, my God! You strike all my foes across the face, you break the teeth of the wicked.  
**3:8** In Yahweh is salvation, on your people, your blessing!Pause

**4:2** Children of men, how long will you be heavy of heart, why love what is vain and chase after illusions?Pause  
**4:3** Realise that Yahweh performs wonders for his faithful, Yahweh listens when I call to him.  
**4:4** Be careful not to sin, speak in your hearts, and on your beds keep silence. Pause  
**4:5** Loyally offer sacrifices, and trust in Yahweh.  
**4:6** Many keep saying, 'Who will put happiness before our eyes?' Let the light of your face shine on us. Yahweh,  
**4:7** to my heart you are a richer joy than all their corn and new wine.  
**4:8** In peace I lie down and at once fall asleep, for it is you and none other, Yahweh, who make me rest secure.  

## 5장

**5:2** Listen to my cry for help, my King and my God! To you I pray,  
**5:3** Yahweh. At daybreak you hear my voice; at daybreak I lay my case before you and fix my eyes on you.  
**5:4** You are not a God who takes pleasure in evil, no sinner can be your guest.  
**5:5** Boasters cannot stand their ground under your gaze. You hate evil-doers,  
**5:6** liars you destroy; the violent and deceitful Yahweh detests.  
**5:7** But, so great is your faithful love, I may come into your house, and before your holy temple bow down in reverence of you.  
**5:8** In your saving justice, Yahweh, lead me, because of those who lie in wait for me; make your way plain before me.  
**5:9** Not a word from their lips can be trusted, through and through they are destruction, their throats are wide -- open graves, their tongues seductive.  
**5:10** Lay the guilt on them, God, make their intrigues their own downfall; for their countless offences, thrust them from you, since they have rebelled against you.  
**5:11** But joy for all who take refuge in you, endless songs of gladness! You shelter them, they rejoice in you, those who love your name.  
**5:12** It is you who bless the upright, Yahweh, you surround them with favour as with a shield.

## 10장

**10:1** Why, Yahweh, do you keep so distant, stay hidden in times of trouble?  
**10:2** In his pride the wicked hunts down the weak, who is caught in the schemes he devises.  
**10:3** The wicked is proud of his inmost desires, by his blasphemies the grasping spurns Yahweh,  
**10:4** the wicked in his arrogance does not look very far; 'There is no God,' is his only thought.  
**10:5** In all circumstances his step is assured; your judgements are above his head. His rivals? He scoffs at them all.  
**10:6** He says in his heart, 'I shall never be shaken,' free of trouble himself,  
**10:7** he wishes it on others. His speech is full of lies and browbeating, under his tongue lurk spite and wickedness.  
**10:8** In the undergrowth he lies in ambush, in his hiding-place he murders the innocent. He watches intently for the downtrodden,  
**10:9** lurking unseen like a lion in his lair, lurking to pounce on the poor; he pounces on him and drags him off in his net.  
**10:10** He keeps watch, crouching down low, the poor wretch falls into his clutches;  
**10:11** he says in his heart, 'God forgets, he has turned away his face to avoid seeing the end.'  
**10:12** Rise, Yahweh! God, raise your hand, do not forget the afflicted!  
**10:13** Why should the wicked spurn God, assuring himself you will never follow it up?  
**10:14** You have seen for yourself the trouble and vexation, you watch so as to take it in hand. The oppressed relies on you; you are the only recourse of the orphan.  
**10:15** Break the arm of the wicked and evil, seek out wickedness till there is none left to be found.  
**10:16** Yahweh is king for ever and ever, the heathen has vanished from his country.  
**10:17** Yahweh, you listen to the laments of the poor, you give them courage, you grant them a hearing,  
**10:18** to give judgement for the orphaned and exploited, so that earthborn humans may strike terror no more.

**17:8** Guard me as the pupil of an eye, shelter me in the shadow of your wings

## 18장

**18:1** [For the choirmaster Of David, the servant of Yahweh, who addressed the words of this song to Yahweh when Yahweh had delivered him from all his enemies and from the clutches of Saul. He said:] I love you, Yahweh, my strength (my Saviour, you have saved me from violence).  
**18:2** Yahweh is my rock and my fortress, my deliverer is my God. I take refuge in him, my rock, my shield, my saving strength, my stronghold, my place of refuge.  
**18:3** I call to Yahweh who is worthy of praise, and I am saved from my foes.  
**18:4** With Death's breakers closing in on me, Belial's torrents ready to swallow me,  
**18:5** Sheol's snares every side of me, Death's traps lying ahead of me,  
**18:6** I called to Yahweh in my anguish, I cried for help to my God; from his Temple he heard my voice, my cry came to his ears.  
**18:7** Then the earth quaked and rocked, the mountains' foundations shuddered, they quaked at his blazing anger.  
**18:8** Smoke rose from his nostrils, from his mouth devouring fire (coals were kindled at it).  
**18:9** He parted the heavens and came down, a storm-cloud underneath his feet;  
**18:10** riding one of the winged creatures, he flew, soaring on the wings of the wind.  
**18:11** His covering he made the darkness, his pavilion dark waters and dense cloud.  
**18:12** A brightness lit up before him, hail and blazing fire.  
**18:13** Yahweh thundered from the heavens, the Most High made his voice heard.  
**18:14** He shot his arrows and scattered them, he hurled his lightning and routed them.  
**18:15** The very springs of ocean were exposed, the world's foundations were laid bare, at your roaring, Yahweh, at the blast of breath from your nostrils!  
**18:16** He reached down from on high, snatched me up, pulled me from the watery depths,  
**18:17** rescued me from my mighty foe, from my enemies who were stronger than I.  
**18:18** They assailed me on my day of disaster but Yahweh was there to support me;  
**18:19** he freed me, set me at large, he rescued me because he loves me.  
**18:20** Yahweh rewards me for my uprightness, as my hands are pure, so he repays me,  
**18:21** since I have kept the ways of Yahweh, and not fallen away from my God.  
**18:22** His judgements are all before me, his statutes I have not put away from me.  
**18:23** I am blameless before him, I keep myself clear of evil.  
**18:24** So Yahweh repaid me for acting uprightly because he could see I was pure.  
**18:25** You are faithful to the faithful, blameless with the blameless,  
**18:26** sincere to the sincere, but cunning to the crafty,  
**18:27** you save a people that is humble and humiliate those with haughty looks.  
**18:28** Yahweh, you yourself are my lamp, my God lights up my darkness;  
**18:29** with you I storm the rampart, with my God I can scale any wall.  
**18:30** This God, his way is blameless; the word of Yahweh is refined in the furnace, for he alone is the shield of all who take refuge in him.  
**18:31** For who is God but Yahweh, who is a rock but our God?  
**18:32** This God who girds me with strength, who makes my way free from blame,  
**18:33** who makes me as swift as a deer and sets me firmly on the heights,  
**18:34** who trains my hands for battle, my arms to bend a bow of bronze.  
**18:35** You give me your invincible shield (your right hand upholds me) you never cease to listen to me,  
**18:36** you give me the strides of a giant, give me ankles that never weaken.  
**18:37** I pursue my enemies and overtake them, not turning back till they are annihilated;  
**18:38** I strike them down and they cannot rise, they fall, they are under my feet.  
**18:39** You have girded me with strength for the fight, bent down my assailants beneath me,  
**18:40** made my enemies retreat before me; and those who hate me I destroy.  
**18:41** They cry out, there is no one to save; to Yahweh, but no answer comes.  
**18:42** I crumble them like dust before the wind, trample them like the mud of the streets.  
**18:43** You free me from the quarrels of my people, you place me at the head of the nations, a people I did not know are now my servants;  
**18:44** foreigners come wooing my favour, no sooner do they hear than they obey me;  
**18:45** foreigners grow faint of heart, they come trembling out of their fastnesses.  
**18:46** Life to Yahweh! Blessed be my rock! Exalted be the God of my salvation,  
**18:47** the God who gives me vengeance, and subjects whole peoples to me,  
**18:48** who rescues me from my raging enemies. You lift me high above those who attack me, you deliver me from the man of violence.  
**18:49** For this I will praise you, Yahweh, among the nations, and sing praise to your name.  
**18:50** He saves his king time after time, displays his faithful love for his anointed, for David and his heirs for ever.  

## 19장

**19:1** [For the choirmaster Psalm Of David] The heavens declare the glory of God, the vault of heaven proclaims his handiwork,  
**19:2** day discourses of it to day, night to night hands on the knowledge.  
**19:3** No utterance at all, no speech, not a sound to be heard,  
**19:4** but from the entire earth the design stands out, this message reaches the whole world. High above, he pitched a tent for the sun,  
**19:5** who comes forth from his pavilion like a bridegroom, delights like a champion in the course to be run.  
**19:6** Rising on the one horizon he runs his circuit to the other, and nothing can escape his heat.  
**19:7** The Law of Yahweh is perfect, refreshment to the soul; the decree of Yahweh is trustworthy, wisdom for the simple.  
**19:8** The precepts of Yahweh are honest, joy for the heart; the commandment of Yahweh is pure, light for the eyes.  
**19:9** The fear of Yahweh is pure, lasting for ever; the judgements of Yahweh are true, upright, every one,  
**19:10** more desirable than gold, even than the finest gold; his words are sweeter than honey, that drips from the comb.  
**19:11** Thus your servant is formed by them; observing them brings great reward.  
**19:12** But who can detect his own failings? Wash away my hidden faults.  
**19:13** And from pride preserve your servant, never let it be my master. So shall I be above reproach, free from grave sin.  
**19:14** May the words of my mouth always find favour, and the whispering of my heart, in your presence, Yahweh, my rock, my redeemer.

**74:6** hacking at the panels, they battered them down with axe and pick;  
**74:7** they set fire to your sanctuary, profanely rased to the ground the dwelling-place of your name.  
**74:8** They said to themselves, 'Let us crush them at one stroke!' They burned down every sacred shrine in the land.

**74:15** You released the springs and brooks, and turned primordial rivers into dry land.  
**74:16** Yours is the day and yours the night, you caused sun and light to exist,

**74:18** Remember, Yahweh, the enemy's blasphemy, a foolish people insults your name.  
**74:19** Do not surrender your turtledove to the beast; do not forget for ever the life of your oppressed people.  
**74:20** Look to the covenant! All the hiding-places of the land are full, haunts of violence.  
**74:21** Do not let the downtrodden retreat in confusion, give the poor and needy cause to praise your name.  
**74:22** Arise, God, champion your own cause, remember how fools blaspheme you all day long!
