# 20.잠언 (NJB)



## 1장

**1:1** The proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel:  
**1:2** for learning what wisdom and discipline are, for understanding words of deep meaning,  
**1:3** for acquiring a disciplined insight, uprightness, justice and fair dealing;  
**1:4** for teaching sound judgement to the simple, and knowledge and reflection to the young;  
**1:5** Let the wise listen and learn yet more, and a person of discernment will acquire the art of guidance.  
**1:6** for perceiving the meaning of proverbs and obscure sayings, the sayings of the sages and their riddles.  
**1:7** The fear of Yahweh is the beginning of knowledge; fools spurn wisdom and discipline.  
**1:8** Listen, my child, to your father's instruction, do not reject your mother's teaching:  
**1:9** they will be a crown of grace for your head, a circlet for your neck.  
**1:10** My child, if sinners try to seduce you, do not go with them.  
**1:11** If they say, 'Come with us: let us lie in ambush to shed blood; if we plan an ambush for the innocent without provocation,  
**1:12** we can swallow them alive, like Sheol, and whole, like those who sink into oblivion.  
**1:13** We shall find treasures of every sort, we shall fill our houses with plunder;  
**1:14** throw in your lot with us: one purse between us all.'  
**1:15** My child, do not follow them in their way, keep your steps out of their path  
**1:16** for their feet hasten to evil, they are quick to shed blood;  
**1:17** for the net is spread in vain if any winged creature can see it.  
**1:18** It is for their own blood such people lie in wait, their ambush is against their own selves!  
**1:19** Such are the paths of all who seek dishonest gain: which robs of their lives all who take it for their own.  
**1:20** Wisdom calls aloud in the streets, she raises her voice in the public squares;  
**1:21** she calls out at the street corners, she delivers her message at the city gates.  
**1:22** 'You simple people, how much longer will you cling to your simple ways? How much longer will mockers revel in their mocking and fools go on hating knowledge?  
**1:23** Pay attention to my warning. To you I will pour out my heart and tell you what I have to say.  
**1:24** Since I have called and you have refused me, since I have beckoned and no one has taken notice,  
**1:25** since you have ignored all my advice and rejected all my warnings,  
**1:26** I, for my part, shall laugh at your distress, I shall jeer when terror befalls you,  
**1:27** when terror befalls you, like a storm, when your distress arrives, like a whirlwind, when ordeal and anguish bear down on you.  
**1:28** Then they will call me, but I shall not answer, they will look eagerly for me and will not find me.  
**1:29** They have hated knowledge, they have not chosen the fear of Yahweh,  
**1:30** they have taken no notice of my advice, they have spurned all my warnings:  
**1:31** so they will have to eat the fruits of their own ways of life, and choke themselves with their own scheming.  
**1:32** For the errors of the simple lead to their death, the complacency of fools works their own ruin;  
**1:33** but whoever listens to me may live secure, will have quiet, fearing no mischance.'  

## 2장

**2:1** My child, if you take my words to heart, if you set store by my commandments,  
**2:2** tuning your ear to wisdom, tuning your heart to understanding,  
**2:3** yes, if your plea is for clear perception, if you cry out for understanding,  
**2:4** if you look for it as though for silver, search for it as though for buried treasure,  
**2:5** then you will understand what the fear of Yahweh is, and discover the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For Yahweh himself is giver of wisdom, from his mouth issue knowledge and understanding.  
**2:7** He reserves his advice for the honest, a shield to those whose ways are sound;  
**2:8** he stands guard over the paths of equity, he keeps watch over the way of those faithful to him.  
**2:9** Then you will understand uprightness, equity and fair dealing, the paths that lead to happiness.  
**2:10** When wisdom comes into your heart and knowledge fills your soul with delight,  
**2:11** then prudence will be there to watch over you, and understanding will be your guardian  
**2:12** to keep you from the way that is evil, from those whose speech is deceitful,  
**2:13** from those who leave the paths of honesty to walk the roads of darkness:  
**2:14** those who find their joy in doing wrong, and their delight in deceitfulness,  
**2:15** whose tracks are twisted, and the paths that they tread crooked.  
**2:16** To keep you, too, from the woman who belongs to another, from the stranger, with her wheedling words;  
**2:17** she has left the partner of her younger days, she has forgotten the covenant of her God;  
**2:18** her house is tilting towards Death, down to the Shades go her paths.  
**2:19** Of those who go to her not one returns, they never regain the paths of life.  
**2:20** Thus you will tread the way of good people, persisting in the paths of the upright.  
**2:21** For the land will be for the honest to live in, the innocent will have it for their home;  
**2:22** while the wicked will be cut off from the land, and the faithless rooted out of it.  

## 3장

**3:1** My child, do not forget my teaching, let your heart keep my principles,  
**3:2** since they will increase your length of days, your years of life and your well-being.  
**3:3** Let faithful love and constancy never leave you: tie them round your neck, write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**3:4** Thus you will find favour and success in the sight of God and of people.  
**3:5** Trust wholeheartedly in Yahweh, put no faith in your own perception;  
**3:6** acknowledge him in every course you take, and he will see that your paths are smooth.  
**3:7** Do not congratulate yourself on your own wisdom, fear Yahweh and turn your back on evil:  
**3:8** health-giving, this, to your body, relief to your bones.  
**3:9** Honour Yahweh with what goods you have and with the first-fruits of all your produce;  
**3:10** then your barns will be filled with corn, your vats overflowing with new wine.  
**3:11** My child, do not scorn correction from Yahweh, do not resent his reproof;  
**3:12** for Yahweh reproves those he loves, as a father the child whom he loves.  
**3:13** Blessed are those who have discovered wisdom, those who have acquired understanding!  
**3:14** Gaining her is more rewarding than silver, her yield is more valuable than gold.  
**3:15** She is beyond the price of pearls, nothing you could covet is her equal.  
**3:16** In her right hand is length of days; in her left hand, riches and honour.  
**3:17** Her ways are filled with delight, her paths all lead to contentment.  
**3:18** She is a tree of life for those who hold her fast, those who cling to her live happy lives.  
**3:19** In wisdom, Yahweh laid the earth's foundations, in understanding he spread out the heavens.  
**3:20** Through his knowledge the depths were cleft open, and the clouds distil the dew.  
**3:21** My child, hold to sound advice and prudence, never let them out of sight;  
**3:22** they will give life to your soul and beauty to your neck.  
**3:23** You will go on your way in safety, your feet will not stumble.  
**3:24** When you go to bed, you will not be afraid; once in bed, your sleep will be sweet.  
**3:25** Have no fear either of sudden terror or of attack mounted by wicked men,  
**3:26** since Yahweh will be your guarantor, he will keep your steps from the snare.  
**3:27** Refuse no kindness to those who have a right to it, if it is in your power to perform it.  
**3:28** Do not say to your neighbour, 'Go away! Come another time! I will give it you tomorrow,' if you can do it now.  
**3:29** Do not plot harm against your neighbour who is living unsuspecting beside you.  
**3:30** Do not pick a groundless quarrel with anyone who has done you no harm.  
**3:31** Do not envy the man of violence, never model your conduct on his;  
**3:32** for the wilful wrong-doer is abhorrent to Yahweh, who confides only in the honest.  
**3:33** Yahweh's curse lies on the house of the wicked, but he blesses the home of the upright.  
**3:34** He mocks those who mock, but accords his favour to the humble.  
**3:35** Glory is the portion of the wise, all that fools inherit is contempt.  

## 4장

**4:1** Listen, my children, to a father's instruction; pay attention, and learn what understanding is.  
**4:2** What I am offering you is sound doctrine: do not forsake my teaching.  
**4:3** I too was once a child with a father, in my mother's eyes a tender child, unique.  
**4:4** This was what he used to teach me, 'Let your heart treasure what I have to say, keep my principles and you will live;  
**4:5** acquire wisdom, acquire understanding, never forget her, never deviate from my words.  
**4:6** Do not desert her, she will keep you safe; love her, she will watch over you.  
**4:7** The first principle of wisdom is: acquire wisdom; at the cost of all you have, acquire understanding!  
**4:8** Hold her close, and she will make you great; embrace her, and she will be your pride;  
**4:9** she will provide a graceful garland for your head, bestow a crown of honour on you.'  
**4:10** Listen, my child, take my words to heart, and the years of your life will be multiplied.  
**4:11** I have educated you in the ways of wisdom, I have guided you along the path of honesty.  
**4:12** When you walk, your going will be unhindered, if you run, you will not stumble.  
**4:13** Hold fast to discipline, never let her go, keep your eyes on her, she is your life.  
**4:14** Do not follow the path of the wicked, do not walk the way that the evil go.  
**4:15** Avoid it, do not take it, turn your back on it, pass it by.  
**4:16** For they cannot sleep unless they have first done wrong, they miss their sleep if they have not made someone stumble;  
**4:17** for the bread of wickedness is what they eat, and the wine of violence is what they drink.  
**4:18** The path of the upright is like the light of dawn, its brightness growing to the fullness of day;  
**4:19** the way of the wicked is as dark as night, they cannot tell the obstacles they stumble over.  
**4:20** My child, pay attention to what I am telling you, listen carefully to my words;  
**4:21** do not let them out of your sight, keep them deep in your heart.  
**4:22** For they are life to those who find them and health to all humanity.  
**4:23** More than all else, keep watch over your heart, since here are the wellsprings of life.  
**4:24** Turn your back on the mouth that misleads, keep your distance from lips that deceive.  
**4:25** Let your eyes be fixed ahead, your gaze be straight before you.  
**4:26** Let the path you tread be level and all your ways be firm.  
**4:27** Turn neither to right nor to left, keep your foot clear of evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** My son, pay attention to my wisdom, listen carefully to what I know;  
**5:2** so that you may preserve discretion and your lips may guard knowledge. Take no notice of a loose-living woman,  
**5:3** for the lips of the adulteress drip with honey, her palate is more unctuous than oil,  
**5:4** but in the end she is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a two-edged sword.  
**5:5** Her feet go down to death, Sheol the goal of her steps;  
**5:6** far from following the path of life, her course is uncertain and she does not know it.  
**5:7** And now, son, listen to me, never deviate from what I say:  
**5:8** set your course as far from her as possible, go nowhere near the door of her house,  
**5:9** or she will hand over your honour to others, the years of your life to a man without pity,  
**5:10** and strangers will batten on your property, and your produce go to the house of a stranger,  
**5:11** and, at your ending, your body and flesh having been consumed, you will groan  
**5:12** and exclaim, 'Alas, I hated discipline, my heart spurned all correction;  
**5:13** I would not attend to the voice of my masters, I would not listen to those who tried to teach me.  
**5:14** Now I have come to nearly every kind of misery, in the assembly and in the community.'  
**5:15** Drink the water from your own storage-well, fresh water from your own spring.  
**5:16** Even if your fountains overflow outside, your streams of water in the public squares:  
**5:17** let them be for you alone, and not for strangers with you.  
**5:18** May your fountain-head be blessed! Find joy with the wife you married in your youth,  
**5:19** fair as a hind, graceful as a fawn: hers the breasts that ever fill you with delight, hers the love that ever holds you captive.  
**5:20** Why be seduced, my son, by someone else's wife, and fondle the breast of a woman who belongs to another?  
**5:21** For the eyes of Yahweh observe human ways, and survey all human paths.  
**5:22** The wicked is snared in his own misdeeds, is caught in the meshes of his own sin.  
**5:23** For want of discipline, he dies, led astray by his own excessive folly.  

## 6장

**6:1** My child, if you have gone surety for your neighbour, if you have guaranteed the bond of a stranger,  
**6:2** if you have committed yourself with your lips, if through words of yours you have been entrapped,  
**6:3** do this, my child, to extricate yourself -- since you have put yourself in the power of your neighbour: go, humble yourself, plead with your neighbour,  
**6:4** give your eyes no sleep, your eyelids no rest,  
**6:5** break free like a gazelle from the trap, like a bird from the fowler's clutches.  
**6:6** Idler, go to the ant; ponder her ways and grow wise:  
**6:7** no one gives her orders, no overseer, no master,  
**6:8** yet all through the summer she gets her food ready, and gathers her supplies at harvest time.  
**6:9** How long do you intend to lie there, idler? When are you going to rise from your sleep?  
**6:10** A little sleep, a little drowsiness, a little folding of the arms to lie back,  
**6:11** and poverty comes like a vagrant and, like a beggar, dearth.  
**6:12** A scoundrel, a vicious man, he goes with a leer on his lips,  
**6:13** winking his eye, shuffling his foot, beckoning with his finger.  
**6:14** Trickery in his heart, always scheming evil, he sows dissension.  
**6:15** Disaster will overtake him sharply for this, suddenly, irretrievably, he will be broken.  
**6:16** There are six things that Yahweh hates, seven that he abhors:  
**6:17** a haughty look, a lying tongue, hands that shed innocent blood,  
**6:18** a heart that weaves wicked plots, feet that hurry to do evil,  
**6:19** a false witness who lies with every breath, and one who sows dissension among brothers.  
**6:20** Keep your father's precept, my child, do not spurn your mother's teaching.  
**6:21** Bind them ever to your heart, tie them round your neck.  
**6:22** While you are active, they will guide you, when you fall asleep, they will watch over you, when you wake up, they will converse with you.  
**6:23** For the precept is a lamp, the teaching is a light; correction and discipline are the way to life,  
**6:24** preserving you from the woman of bad character, from the wheedling talk of a woman who belongs to another.  
**6:25** Do not covet her beauty in your heart or let her captivate you with the play of her eyes;  
**6:26** a prostitute can be bought for a hunk of bread, but a married woman aims to snare a precious life.  
**6:27** Can a man carry fire inside his shirt without setting his clothes alight?  
**6:28** Can you walk on red-hot coals without burning your feet?  
**6:29** Just so, the man who makes love to his neighbour's wife: no one who touches her will get off unpunished.  
**6:30** People attach but little blame to a thief who steals only to satisfy his hunger;  
**6:31** yet even he, if caught, will have to repay sevenfold and hand over all his family resources.  
**6:32** But the adulterer has no sense; he works his own destruction.  
**6:33** All he will get is blows and contempt, and dishonour never to be blotted out.  
**6:34** For jealousy inflames the husband who will show no mercy when the day comes for revenge;  
**6:35** he will not consider any compensation; lavish what gifts you may, he will not be placated.  

## 7장

**7:1** My child, keep my words, and treasure my precepts,  
**7:2** keep my precepts and you will live, keep my teaching as the apple of your eye.  
**7:3** Bind these to your fingers, write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to Wisdom, 'You are my sister!' Call Understanding your relation,  
**7:5** to save yourself from the woman that belongs to another, from the stranger, with her seductive words.  
**7:6** While I was at the window of my house, I was looking out through the lattice  
**7:7** and I saw, among the callow youths, I noticed among the lads, one boy who had no sense.  
**7:8** Going along the lane, near the corner where she lives, he reaches the path to her house,  
**7:9** at twilight when day is declining, at dead of night and in the dark.  
**7:10** And look, a woman is coming to meet him, dressed like a prostitute, false of heart.  
**7:11** She is loud and brazen; her feet cannot rest at home.  
**7:12** Once in the street, once in the square, she lurks at every corner.  
**7:13** She catches hold of him, she kisses him, the bold-faced creature says to him,  
**7:14** 'I had to offer a communion sacrifice, I have discharged my vows today;  
**7:15** that is why I came out to meet you, to look for you, and now I have found you.  
**7:16** I have spread coverlets over my divan, embroidered stuff, Egyptian material,  
**7:17** I have sprinkled my bed with myrrh, with aloes and cinnamon.  
**7:18** Come on, we'll make love as much as we like, till morning. Let us enjoy the delights of love!  
**7:19** For my husband is not at home, he has gone on a very long journey,  
**7:20** taking his moneybags with him; he will not be back till the moon is full.'  
**7:21** With her persistent coaxing she overcomes him, lures him on with her wheedling patter.  
**7:22** Forthwith he follows her, like an ox on its way to the slaughterhouse, like a madman on his way to the stocks,  
**7:23** until an arrow pierces him to the liver, like the bird that dashes into the net without realising that its life is at stake.  
**7:24** And now, son, listen to me, pay attention to the words I have to say:  
**7:25** do not let your heart stray into her ways, or wander into her paths;  
**7:26** she has done so many to death, and the strongest have all been her victims.  
**7:27** Her house is the way to Sheol, the descent to the courts of death.  

## 8장

**8:1** Is not Wisdom calling? Is not Understanding raising her voice?  
**8:2** On the heights overlooking the road, at the crossways, she takes her stand;  
**8:3** by the gates, at the entrance to the city, on the access-roads, she cries out,  
**8:4** 'I am calling to you, all people, my words are addressed to all humanity.  
**8:5** Simpletons, learn how to behave, fools, come to your senses.  
**8:6** Listen, I have something important to tell you, when I speak, my words are right.  
**8:7** My mouth proclaims the truth, for evil is abhorrent to my lips.  
**8:8** All the words from my mouth are upright, nothing false there, nothing crooked,  
**8:9** everything plain, if you can understand, straight, if you have acquired knowledge.  
**8:10** Accept my discipline rather than silver, and knowledge of me in preference to finest gold.  
**8:11** For Wisdom is more precious than jewels, and nothing else is so worthy of desire.  
**8:12** 'I, Wisdom, share house with Discretion, I am mistress of the art of thought.  
**8:13** (Fear of Yahweh means hatred of evil.) I hate pride and arrogance, wicked behaviour and a lying mouth.  
**8:14** To me belong good advice and prudence, I am perception: power is mine!  
**8:15** By me monarchs rule and princes decree what is right;  
**8:16** by me rulers govern, so do nobles, the lawful authorities.  
**8:17** I love those who love me; whoever searches eagerly for me finds me.  
**8:18** With me are riches and honour, lasting wealth and saving justice.  
**8:19** The fruit I give is better than gold, even the finest, the return I make is better than pure silver.  
**8:20** I walk in the way of uprightness in the path of justice,  
**8:21** to endow my friends with my wealth and to fill their treasuries.  
**8:22** 'Yahweh created me, first-fruits of his fashioning, before the oldest of his works.  
**8:23** From everlasting, I was firmly set, from the beginning, before the earth came into being.  
**8:24** The deep was not, when I was born, nor were the springs with their abounding waters.  
**8:25** Before the mountains were settled, before the hills, I came to birth;  
**8:26** before he had made the earth, the countryside, and the first elements of the world.  
**8:27** When he fixed the heavens firm, I was there, when he drew a circle on the surface of the deep,  
**8:28** when he thickened the clouds above, when the sources of the deep began to swell,  
**8:29** when he assigned the sea its boundaries -- and the waters will not encroach on the shore -- when he traced the foundations of the earth,  
**8:30** I was beside the master craftsman, delighting him day after day, ever at play in his presence,  
**8:31** at play everywhere on his earth, delighting to be with the children of men.  
**8:32** 'And now, my children, listen to me. Happy are those who keep my ways.  
**8:33** Listen to instruction and become wise, do not reject it.  
**8:34** Blessed, whoever listens to me, who day after day keeps watch at my gates to guard my portals.  
**8:35** For whoever finds me finds life, and obtains the favour of Yahweh;  
**8:36** but whoever misses me harms himself, all who hate me are in love with death.'  

## 9장

**9:1** Wisdom has built herself a house, she has hewn her seven pillars,  
**9:2** she has slaughtered her beasts, drawn her wine, she has laid her table.  
**9:3** She has despatched her maidservants and proclaimed from the heights above the city,  
**9:4** 'Who is simple? Let him come this way.' To the fool she says,  
**9:5** 'Come and eat my bread, drink the wine which I have drawn!  
**9:6** Leave foolishness behind and you will live, go forwards in the ways of perception.'  
**9:7** Reprove a mocker and you attract contempt, rebuke the wicked and you attract dishonour.  
**9:8** Do not rebuke the mocker, he will hate you. Rebuke the wise and he will love you for it.  
**9:9** Be open with the wise, he grows wiser still, teach the upright, he will gain yet more.  
**9:10** The first principle of wisdom is the fear of Yahweh, What God's holy ones know -- this is understanding.  
**9:11** For by me your days will be multiplied, and your years of life increased.  
**9:12** Are you wise? You are wise to your own good. A mocker? The burden is yours alone.  
**9:13** A silly woman acts on impulse, is foolish and knows nothing.  
**9:14** She sits at the door of her house, on a throne high up in the city,  
**9:15** calling to the passers-by, who are walking straight past on their way,  
**9:16** 'Who is simple? Turn aside, come over here.' To the fool she says,  
**9:17** 'Stolen waters are sweet, and bread tastes better when eaten in secret.'  
**9:18** But the fool does not know that this is where the Shades are and that her guests are already in the vales of Sheol.  

## 10장

**10:1** The proverbs of Solomon. A wise child is a father's joy, a foolish child a mother's grief.  
**10:2** Treasures wickedly come by give no benefit, but uprightness brings delivery from death.  
**10:3** Yahweh does not let the upright go hungry, but he thwarts the greed of the wicked.  
**10:4** A slack hand brings poverty, but the hand of the diligent brings wealth.  
**10:5** Reaping at harvest-time is the mark of the prudent, sleeping at harvest-time is the sign of the worthless.  
**10:6** Blessings are on the head of the upright, but the mouth of the godless is a cover for violence.  
**10:7** The upright is remembered with blessings, the name of the wicked rots away.  
**10:8** The wise of heart takes orders, but a gabbling fool heads for ruin.  
**10:9** Anyone whose ways are honourable walks secure, but whoever follows crooked ways is soon unmasked.  
**10:10** A wink of the eye brings trouble, a bold rebuke brings peace.  
**10:11** The mouth of the upright is a life-giving fountain, but the mouth of the godless is a cover for violence.  
**10:12** Hatred provokes disputes, but love excuses all offences.  
**10:13** On the lips of the discerning is found wisdom, on the back of a fool, the stick.  
**10:14** Wise people store up knowledge, but the mouth of a fool makes ruin imminent.  
**10:15** The wealth of the rich is their stronghold, poverty is the undoing of the weak.  
**10:16** The wage of the upright affords life, but sin is all the wicked earns.  
**10:17** Whoever abides by discipline, walks towards life, whoever ignores correction goes astray.  
**10:18** Liars' lips are a cover for hatred, whoever utters slander is a fool.  
**10:19** A flood of words is never without fault; whoever controls the lips is wise.  
**10:20** The tongue of the upright is purest silver, the heart of the wicked is of trumpery value.  
**10:21** The lips of the upright nourish many peoples, but fools die for want of sense.  
**10:22** The blessing of Yahweh is what brings riches, to this, hard toil has nothing to add.  
**10:23** A fool takes pleasure in doing wrong, the intelligent in cultivating wisdom.  
**10:24** What the wicked fears overtakes him, what the upright desires comes to him as a present.  
**10:25** When the storm is over, the wicked is no more, but the upright stands firm for ever.  
**10:26** As vinegar to the teeth, smoke to the eyes, so the sluggard to the one who sends him.  
**10:27** The fear of Yahweh adds length to life, the years of the wicked will be cut short.  
**10:28** The hope of the upright is joy, the expectations of the wicked come to nothing.  
**10:29** The way of Yahweh is a rampart for the honest, for evil-doers nothing but ruin.  
**10:30** The upright will never have to give way, but the land will offer no home for the wicked.  
**10:31** The mouth of the upright utters wisdom, the tongue that deceives will be cut off.  
**10:32** The lips of the upright know about kindness, the mouth of the wicked about deceit.  

## 11장

**11:1** A false balance is abhorrent to Yahweh, a just weight is pleasing to him.  
**11:2** Pride comes first; disgrace soon follows; with the humble is wisdom found.  
**11:3** The honest have their own honesty for guidance, the treacherous are ruined by their own perfidy.  
**11:4** In the day of retribution riches will be useless, but uprightness delivers from death.  
**11:5** The uprightness of the good makes their way straight, the wicked fall by their own wickedness.  
**11:6** Their uprightness sets the honest free, the treacherous are imprisoned by their own desires.  
**11:7** The hope of the wicked perishes with death, hope placed in riches comes to nothing.  
**11:8** The upright escapes affliction, the wicked incurs it instead.

**30:28** lizards, which you can catch in your hand, yet they frequent the palaces of kings.  
**30:29** There are three things of stately tread, four, indeed, of stately walk:  
**30:30** the lion, bravest of beasts, he will draw back from nothing;  
**30:31** a vigorous cock, a he-goat, and the king when he harangues his people.  
**30:32** If you have been foolish enough to fly into a passion and now have second thoughts, lay your hand on your lips.

## 31장

**31:1** The sayings of Lemuel king of Massa, taught him by his mother:  
**31:2** What, my son! What, son of my womb! What, son of my vows!  
**31:3** Do not expend your energy on women nor your wealth on those who ruin kings.  
**31:4** Not for kings, O Lemuel, not for kings the drinking of wine, not for princes the love of liquor,  
**31:5** for fear that in liquor they forget what they have decreed and pervert the course of justice against all the poor.  
**31:6** Procure strong drink for someone about to die, wine for him whose heart is heavy:  
**31:7** let him drink and forget his misfortune, and remember his misery no more.  
**31:8** Make your views heard, on behalf of the dumb, on behalf of all the unwanted;  
**31:9** make your views heard, pronounce an upright verdict, defend the cause of the poor and the wretched.  
**31:10** The truly capable woman -- who can find her? She is far beyond the price of pearls.  
**31:11** Her husband's heart has confidence in her, from her he will derive no little profit.  
**31:12** Advantage and not hurt she brings him all the days of her life.  
**31:13** She selects wool and flax, she does her work with eager hands.  
**31:14** She is like those merchant vessels, bringing her food from far away.  
**31:15** She gets up while it is still dark giving her household their food, giving orders to her serving girls.  
**31:16** She sets her mind on a field, then she buys it; with what her hands have earned she plants a vineyard.  
**31:17** She puts her back into her work and shows how strong her arms can be.  
**31:18** She knows that her affairs are going well; her lamp does not go out at night.  
**31:19** She sets her hands to the distaff, her fingers grasp the spindle.  
**31:20** She holds out her hands to the poor, she opens her arms to the needy.  
**31:21** Snow may come, she has no fears for her household, with all her servants warmly clothed.  
**31:22** She makes her own quilts, she is dressed in fine linen and purple.  
**31:23** Her husband is respected at the city gates, taking his seat among the elders of the land.  
**31:24** She weaves materials and sells them, she supplies the merchant with sashes.  
**31:25** She is clothed in strength and dignity, she can laugh at the day to come.  
**31:26** When she opens her mouth, she does so wisely; on her tongue is kindly instruction.  
**31:27** She keeps good watch on the conduct of her household, no bread of idleness for her.  
**31:28** Her children stand up and proclaim her blessed, her husband, too, sings her praises:  
**31:29** 'Many women have done admirable things, but you surpass them all!'  
**31:30** Charm is deceitful, and beauty empty; the woman who fears Yahweh is the one to praise.  
**31:31** Give her a share in what her hands have worked for, and let her works tell her praises at the city gates.
