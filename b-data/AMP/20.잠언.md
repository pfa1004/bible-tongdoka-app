# 20.잠언 (AMP)



## 1장

**1:1** ¶ The proverbs (truths obscurely expressed, maxims) of Solomon son of David, king of Israel:  
**1:2** ¶ To know [skillful and godly] wisdom and instruction;<br>To discern <i>and</i> comprehend the words of understanding <i>and</i> insight,  
**1:3** To receive instruction in wise behavior <i>and</i> the discipline of wise thoughtfulness,<br>Righteousness, justice, and integrity;  
**1:4** That prudence (good judgment, astute common sense) may be given to the naive <i>or</i> inexperienced [who are easily misled],<br>And knowledge and discretion (intelligent discernment) to the youth,  
**1:5** The wise will hear and increase their learning,<br>And the person of understanding will acquire wise counsel <i>and</i> the skill [to steer his course wisely and lead others to the truth],  
**1:6** To understand a proverb and a figure [of speech] <i>or</i> an enigma with its interpretation,<br>And the words of the wise and their riddles [that require reflection].  
**1:7** ¶ The [reverent] fear of the LORD [that is, worshiping Him and regarding Him as truly awesome] is the beginning <i>and</i> the preeminent part of knowledge [its starting point and its essence];<br><i>But arrogant</i> <sup>①</sup>fools despise [skillful and godly] wisdom and instruction <i>and</i> self-discipline.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Proverbs various kinds of fools are discussed including those who are closed-minded, over-confident, and reject instruction and correction—the unteachable; those who lack spiritual insight—the spiritually blind; and those who are flippant, hardened, and who deliberately choose to reject God and wisdom—the arrogant.  
**1:8** My son, hear the instruction of your father,<br>And do not reject the teaching of your mother.  
**1:9** For they are a garland of grace on your head,<br>And chains <i>and</i> ornaments [of gold] around your neck.  
**1:10** My son, if sinners entice you,<br>Do not consent.  
**1:11** If they say, “Come with us;<br>Let us lie in wait to <i>shed</i> blood,<br>Let us ambush the innocent without cause;  
**1:12** Let us swallow them alive like Sheol (the place of the dead),<br>Even whole, as those who go down to the pit [of death];  
**1:13** We will find <i>and</i> take all kinds of precious possessions,<br>We will fill our houses with spoil;  
**1:14** Throw in your lot with us [they insist];<br>We will all have one money bag [in common],”  
**1:15** My son, do not walk on the road with them;<br>Keep your foot [far] away from their path,  
**1:16** For their feet run to evil,<br>And they hurry to shed blood.  
**1:17** Indeed, it is useless to spread the <i>baited</i> net<br>In the sight of any bird;  
**1:18** But [when these people set a trap for others] they lie in wait for their own blood;<br>They set an ambush for their own lives [and rush to their destruction].  
**1:19** So are the ways of everyone who is greedy for gain;<br>Greed takes away the lives of its possessors.  
**1:20**  <sup>①</sup>Wisdom shouts in the street,<br>She raises her voice in the markets;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Wisdom is personified as a woman in vv <a name="B:240 1:20">20-33</a> and speaks, in the first person, of godly wisdom. Read the word “wisdom” as “the wisdom of God” and see the wonderful power of this book.  
**1:21** She calls out at the head of the noisy streets [where large crowds gather];<br>At the entrance of the city gates she speaks her words:  
**1:22** “How long, O naive ones [you who are easily misled], will you love being simple-minded <i>and</i> undiscerning?<br><i>How long</i> will scoffers [who ridicule and deride] delight in scoffing,<br><i>How long</i> will fools [who obstinately mock truth] hate knowledge?  
**1:23** “If you will turn <i>and</i> pay attention to my rebuke,<br>Behold, I [Wisdom] will pour out my spirit on you;<br>I will make my words known to you.  
**1:24** “Because I called and you refused [to answer],<br>I stretched out my hand and no one has paid attention [to my offer];  
**1:25** And you treated all my counsel as nothing<br>And would not accept my reprimand,  
**1:26** I also will laugh at your disaster;<br>I will mock when your dread <i>and</i> panic come,  
**1:27** When your dread <i>and</i> panic come like a storm,<br>And your disaster comes like a whirlwind,<br>When anxiety and distress come upon you [as retribution].  
**1:28** “Then they will call upon me (Wisdom), but I will not answer;<br>They will seek me eagerly but they will not find me,  
**1:29** Because they hated knowledge<br>And did not choose the fear of the LORD [that is, obeying Him with reverence and awe-filled respect],  
**1:30** They would not accept my counsel,<br>And they spurned all my rebuke.  
**1:31** “Therefore they shall eat of the fruit of their own [wicked] way<br>And be satiated with [the penalty of] their own devices.  
**1:32** “For the turning away of the <sup>①</sup>naive will kill them,<br>And the careless ease of [self-righteous] fools will destroy them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>simple ones</i>.  
**1:33** “But whoever listens to me (Wisdom) will live securely <i>and</i> in confident trust<br>And will be at ease, without fear <i>or</i> dread of evil.”  

## 2장

**2:1** ¶ My son, if you will receive my words<br>And treasure my commandments within you,  
**2:2** So that your ear is attentive to [skillful and godly] wisdom,<br>And apply your heart to understanding [seeking it conscientiously and striving for it eagerly];  
**2:3** Yes, if you cry out for insight,<br>And lift up your voice for understanding;  
**2:4** If you seek skillful <i>and</i> godly wisdom as you would silver<br>And search for her as you would hidden treasures;  
**2:5** Then you will understand the [reverent] fear of the LORD [that is, worshiping Him and regarding Him as truly awesome]<br>And discover the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For the LORD gives [skillful and godly] wisdom;<br>From His mouth come knowledge and understanding.  
**2:7** He stores away sound wisdom for the righteous [those who are in right standing with Him];<br><i>He is</i> a shield to those who walk in integrity [those of honorable character and moral courage],  
**2:8** He guards the paths of justice;<br>And He preserves the way of His saints (believers).  
**2:9** Then you will understand righteousness and justice [in every circumstance]<br>And integrity and every good path.  
**2:10** For [skillful and godly] wisdom will enter your heart<br>And knowledge will be pleasant to your soul.  
**2:11** Discretion will watch over you,<br>Understanding <i>and</i> discernment will guard you,  
**2:12** To keep you from the way of evil <i>and</i> the evil man,<br>From the man who speaks perverse things;  
**2:13** From those who leave the paths of uprightness<br>To walk in the ways of darkness;  
**2:14** Who find joy in doing evil<br>And delight in the perversity of evil,  
**2:15** Whose paths are crooked,<br>And who are devious in their ways;  
**2:16** To keep you from the immoral woman;<br>From the seductress with her flattering words,  
**2:17** Who leaves the companion (husband) of her youth,<br>And forgets the covenant of her God.  
**2:18** For her house leads down to death<br>And her paths lead to the dead;  
**2:19** None who go to her return again,<br>Nor do they regain the paths of life—  
**2:20** So you will walk in the way of good men [that is, those of personal integrity, moral courage and honorable character],<br>And keep to the paths of the righteous.  
**2:21** For the upright [those who are in right standing with God] will live in the land<br>And those [of integrity] who are blameless [in God’s sight] will remain in it;  
**2:22** But the wicked will be cut off from the land<br>And the treacherous shall be [forcibly] uprooted <i>and</i> removed from it.  

## 3장

**3:1** ¶ My son, do not forget my <sup>①</sup>teaching,<br>But let your heart keep my commandments;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>law</i>.  
**3:2** For length of days and years of life [worth living]<br>And tranquility <i>and</i> prosperity [the wholeness of life’s blessings] they will add to you.  
**3:3** Do not let mercy <i>and</i> kindness and truth leave you [instead let these qualities define you];<br>Bind them [securely] around your neck,<br>Write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**3:4** So find favor and high esteem<br>In the sight of God and man.  
**3:5** Trust in <i>and</i> rely confidently on the LORD with all your heart<br>And do not rely on your own insight <i>or</i> understanding.  
**3:6**  <sup>①</sup>In all your ways know <i>and</i> acknowledge <i>and</i> recognize Him,<br>And He will make your paths straight <i>and</i> smooth [removing obstacles that block your way].<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One of the ancient rabbis said that all the essence of the Torah (Law) depends on this verse.  
**3:7** Do not be wise in your own eyes;<br>Fear the LORD [with reverent awe and obedience] and turn [entirely] away from evil.  
**3:8** It will be health to your body [your marrow, your nerves, your sinews, your muscles—all your inner parts]<br>And refreshment (physical well-being) to your bones.  
**3:9** Honor the LORD with your wealth<br>And with the first fruits of all your crops (income);  
**3:10** Then your barns will be abundantly filled<br>And your vats will overflow with new wine.  
**3:11** My son, do not reject <i>or</i> take lightly the discipline of the LORD [learn from your mistakes and the testing that comes from His correction through discipline];<br>Nor despise His rebuke,  
**3:12** For those whom the LORD loves He corrects,<br>Even as a father <i>corrects</i> the son in whom he delights.  
**3:13** ¶ Happy [blessed, considered fortunate, to be admired] is the man who finds [skillful and godly] wisdom,<br>And the man who gains understanding <i>and</i> insight [learning from God’s word and life’s experiences],  
**3:14** For wisdom’s profit is better than the profit of silver,<br>And her gain is better than fine gold.  
**3:15** She is more precious than rubies;<br>And nothing you can wish for compares with her [in value].  
**3:16** Long life is in her right hand;<br>In her left hand are riches and honor.  
**3:17** Her ways are highways of pleasantness <i>and</i> favor,<br>And all her paths are peace.  
**3:18** She is a tree of life to those who take hold of her,<br>And happy [blessed, considered fortunate, to be admired] is everyone who holds her tightly.  
**3:19** The LORD by His wisdom has founded the earth;<br>By His understanding He has established the heavens.  
**3:20** By His knowledge the deeps were broken up<br>And the clouds drip with dew.  
**3:21** My son, let them not escape from your sight,<br>But keep sound wisdom and discretion,  
**3:22** And they will be life to your soul (your inner self)<br>And a gracious adornment to your neck (your outer self).  
**3:23** Then you will walk on your way [of life] securely<br>And your foot will not stumble.  
**3:24** When you lie down, you will not be afraid;<br>When you lie down, your sleep will be sweet.  
**3:25** Do not be afraid of sudden fear<br>Nor of the storm of the wicked when it comes [since you will be blameless];  
**3:26** For the LORD will be your confidence, firm <i>and</i> strong,<br>And will keep your foot from being caught [in a trap].  
**3:27** ¶ <sup>①</sup>Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due [its rightful recipients],<br>When it is in your power to do it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Using this verse as a proof text, the ancient rabbis offered the example of an employer who tells his foreman to hire workers at four <i>zuz</i> (small silver coins) each to do a job, and the foreman hires them at three instead. Even though they accepted the terms, the rabbis rule that they have the right to complain. Compare this with the parable of the landowner who hires workers for a denarius each. The difference there is that the first group receives a fair wage, while the last is overpaid as a matter of generosity (<a name="B:470 20:15">MAT 20:15</a>).  
**3:28** Do not say to your neighbor, “Go, and come back,<br>And tomorrow I will give it,”<br>When you have it with you.  
**3:29** Do not devise evil against your neighbor,<br>Who lives securely beside you.  
**3:30** Do not quarrel with a man without cause,<br>If he has done you no harm.  
**3:31** Do not envy a man of violence<br>And do not choose any of his ways.  
**3:32** For the devious are repulsive to the LORD;<br>But His private counsel is with the upright [those with spiritual integrity and moral courage].  
**3:33** The curse of the LORD is on the house of the wicked,<br>But He blesses the home of the just <i>and</i> righteous.  
**3:34** Though He scoffs at the scoffers <i>and</i> scorns the scorners,<br>Yet He gives His grace [His undeserved favor] to the humble [those who give up self-importance].  
**3:35** The wise will inherit honor <i>and</i> glory,<br>But dishonor <i>and</i> shame is conferred on fools.  

## 4장

**4:1** ¶ Hear, O children, the instruction of a father,<br>And pay attention [and be willing to learn] so that you may gain understanding <i>and</i> intelligent discernment.  
**4:2** For I give you good doctrine;<br>Do not <sup>①</sup>turn away from my instruction.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>forsake</i>.  
**4:3** When I was a son with my father (David),<br>Tender and the only son in the sight of my mother (Bathsheba),  
**4:4** He taught me and said to me,<br>“Let your heart hold fast my words;<br>Keep my commandments and live.  
**4:5** “Get [skillful and godly] wisdom! Acquire understanding [actively seek spiritual discernment, mature comprehension, and logical interpretation]!<br>Do not forget nor turn away from the words of my mouth.  
**4:6** “Do not <sup>①</sup>turn away from her (Wisdom) and she will guard <i>and</i> protect you;<br>Love her, and she will watch over you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>forsake</i>.  
**4:7** “The beginning of wisdom is: Get [skillful and godly] wisdom [it is preeminent]!<br>And with all your acquiring, get understanding [actively seek spiritual discernment, mature comprehension, and logical interpretation].  
**4:8** “Prize wisdom [and exalt her], and she will exalt you;<br>She will honor you if you embrace her.  
**4:9** “She will place on your head a garland of grace;<br>She will present you with a crown of beauty <i>and</i> glory.”  
**4:10** ¶ Hear, my son, and accept my sayings,<br>And the years of your life will be many.  
**4:11** I have instructed you in the way of [skillful and godly] wisdom;<br>I have led you in upright paths.  
**4:12** When you walk, your steps will not be impeded [for your path will be clear and open];<br>And when you run, you will not stumble.  
**4:13** Take hold of instruction; [actively seek it, grip it firmly and] do not let go.<br>Guard her, for she is your life.  
**4:14** Do not enter the path of the wicked,<br>And do not go the way of evil men.  
**4:15** Avoid it, do not travel on it;<br>Turn away from it and pass on.  
**4:16** For the wicked cannot sleep unless they do evil;<br>And they are deprived of sleep unless they make someone stumble <i>and</i> fall.  
**4:17** For they eat the bread of wickedness<br>And drink the wine of violence.  
**4:18** But the path of the just (righteous) is like the light of dawn,<br>That shines brighter and brighter until [it reaches its full strength and glory in] the perfect day.  
**4:19** The way of the wicked is like [deep] darkness;<br>They do not know over what they stumble.  
**4:20** ¶ My son, pay attention to my words <i>and</i> be willing to learn;<br>Open your ears to my sayings.  
**4:21** Do not let them escape from your sight;<br>Keep them in the center of your heart.  
**4:22** For they are life to those who find them,<br>And healing <i>and</i> health to all their flesh.  
**4:23** Watch over your heart with all diligence,<br>For from it <i>flow</i> the springs of life.  
**4:24** Put away from you a deceitful (lying, misleading) mouth,<br>And put devious lips far from you.  
**4:25** Let your eyes look directly ahead [toward the path of moral courage]<br>And let your gaze be fixed straight in front of you [toward the path of integrity].  
**4:26** Consider well <i>and</i> watch carefully the path of your feet,<br>And all your ways will be steadfast <i>and</i> sure.  
**4:27** Do not turn away to the right nor to the left [where evil may lurk];<br>Turn your foot from [the path of] evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** ¶ My son, be attentive to my wisdom [godly wisdom learned by costly experience],<br>Incline your ear to my understanding;  
**5:2** That you may exercise discrimination <i>and</i> discretion (good judgment),<br>And your lips may reserve knowledge <i>and</i> answer wisely [to temptation].  
**5:3** For the lips of an immoral woman drip honey [like a honeycomb]<br>And her speech is smoother than oil;  
**5:4** But in the end she is bitter like [the extract of] wormwood,<br>Sharp as a two-edged sword.  
**5:5** Her feet go down to death;<br>Her steps take hold of Sheol (the nether world, the place of the dead),  
**5:6** So that she does not think [seriously] about the path of life;<br>Her ways are aimless <i>and</i> unstable; you cannot know where her path leads.  
**5:7** ¶ Now then, my sons, listen to me<br>And do not depart from (forget) the words of my mouth.  
**5:8** Let your way [in life] be far from her,<br>And do not go near the door of her house [avoid even being near the places of temptation],  
**5:9** Or you will give your honor to others,<br>And your years to the cruel one,  
**5:10** And strangers will be filled with your strength<br>And your hard-earned wealth will go to the house of a foreigner [who does not know God];  
**5:11** And you will groan when your <i>life is</i> ending,<br>When your flesh and your body are consumed;  
**5:12** And you say, “How I hated instruction <i>and</i> discipline,<br>And my heart despised correction <i>and</i> reproof!  
**5:13** “I have not listened to the voice of my teachers,<br>Nor have I inclined my ear to those who instructed me.  
**5:14** “I was almost in total ruin<br>In the midst of the assembly and congregation.”  
**5:15** ¶ <sup>①</sup>Drink water from your own <sup>②</sup>cistern [of a pure marriage relationship]<br>And fresh running water from your own well.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>All of the Ten Commandments are reflected in the book of Proverbs; here it is the seventh, “You shall not commit adultery.” <sup>②</sup>Vv <a name="B:240 5:15">15-18</a> describe the purity of a man’s relationship in marriage. The cistern and well represent his wife.  
**5:16** Should your <sup>①</sup>springs (children) be dispersed,<br>As streams of water in the streets?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e. children of one’s promiscuity who are fatherless.  
**5:17** [Confine yourself to your own wife.] Let <i>your children</i> be yours alone,<br>And not <i>the children</i> of strangers with you.  
**5:18** Let your fountain (wife) be blessed [with the rewards of fidelity],<br>And rejoice in the wife of your youth.  
**5:19** <i>Let her be as</i> a loving hind and graceful doe,<br>Let her breasts refresh <i>and</i> satisfy you at all times;<br>Always be <sup>①</sup>exhilarated <i>and</i> delight in her love.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>stagger</i> (with passion).  
**5:20** Why should you, my son, be exhilarated with an immoral woman<br>And embrace the bosom of an outsider (pagan)?  
**5:21** For the ways of man are directly before the eyes of the LORD,<br>And He carefully watches all of his paths [all of his comings and goings].  
**5:22** The iniquities done by a wicked man will trap him,<br>And he will be held with the cords of his sin.  
**5:23** He will die for lack of instruction (discipline),<br>And in the greatness of his foolishness he will go astray <i>and</i> be lost.  

## 6장

**6:1** ¶ My son, if you have become surety (guaranteed a debt or obligation) for your neighbor,<br>If you have given your pledge for [the debt of] a stranger <i>or</i> another [outside your family],  
**6:2** If you have been snared with the words of your lips,<br>If you have been trapped by the speech of your mouth,  
**6:3** Do this now, my son, and release yourself [from the obligation];<br><sup>①</sup>Since you have come into the hand of your neighbor,<br>Go humble yourself, and plead with your neighbor [to pay his debt and release you].<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Under the ancient laws governing debt, a man obligated for another’s debt could lose his money, land, bed, and clothing; and if these were not sufficient, he and his wife and children could be sold as slaves, not to be released until the next Year of Jubilee—fifty years after the previous one.  
**6:4** Give no [unnecessary] sleep to your eyes,<br>Nor slumber to your eyelids;  
**6:5** Tear yourself away like a gazelle from the hand of <i>the hunter</i><br>And like a bird from the hand of the fowler.  
**6:6** ¶ Go to the ant, O lazy one;<br>Observe her ways and be wise,  
**6:7** Which, having no chief,<br>Overseer or ruler,  
**6:8** She prepares her food in the summer<br>And brings in her provisions [of food for the winter] in the harvest.  
**6:9** How long will you lie down, O lazy one?<br>When will you arise from your sleep [and learn self-discipline]?  
**6:10** “Yet a little sleep, a little slumber,<br>A little folding of the hands to lie down <i>and</i> rest”—  
**6:11** So your poverty will come like an <i>approaching</i> prowler who walks [slowly, but surely]<br>And your need [will come] like an armed man [making you helpless].  
**6:12** ¶ A worthless person, a wicked man,<br>Is one who walks with a perverse (corrupt, vulgar) mouth.  
**6:13** Who winks with his eyes [in mockery], who shuffles his feet [to signal],<br>Who points with his fingers [to give subversive instruction];  
**6:14** Who perversely in his heart plots trouble <i>and</i> evil continually;<br>Who spreads discord <i>and</i> strife.  
**6:15** Therefore [the crushing weight of] his disaster will come suddenly <i>upon him</i>;<br>Instantly he will be broken, and there will be no healing <i>or</i> remedy [because he has no heart for God].  
**6:16** ¶ These six things the LORD hates;<br>Indeed, seven are repulsive to Him:  
**6:17** A proud look [the attitude that makes one overestimate oneself and discount others], a lying tongue,<br>And hands that shed innocent blood,  
**6:18** A heart that creates wicked plans,<br>Feet that run swiftly to evil,  
**6:19** A false witness who breathes out lies [even half-truths],<br>And one who spreads discord (rumors) among brothers.  
**6:20** ¶ My son, be guided by your father’s [God-given] commandment (instruction)<br>And do not <sup>①</sup>reject the teaching of your mother;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>forsake</i>.  
**6:21** Bind them continually upon your heart (in your thoughts),<br>And tie them around your neck.  
**6:22** When you walk about, they (the godly teachings of your parents) will guide you;<br>When you sleep, they will keep watch over you;<br>And when you awake, they will talk to you.  
**6:23** For the commandment is a lamp, and the teaching [of the law] is light,<br>And reproofs (rebukes) for discipline are the way of life,  
**6:24** To keep you from the evil woman,<br>From [the flattery of] the smooth tongue of an immoral woman.  
**6:25** Do not desire (lust after) her beauty in your heart,<br>Nor let her capture you with her eyelashes.  
**6:26** For on account of a prostitute one is reduced to a piece of bread [to be eaten up],<br>And the immoral woman hunts [with a hook] the precious life [of a man].  
**6:27** Can a man take fire to his chest<br>And his clothes not be burned?  
**6:28** Or can a man walk on hot coals<br>And his feet not be scorched?  
**6:29** So is the one who goes in to his neighbor’s wife;<br>Whoever touches her will not be found innocent <i>or</i> go unpunished.  
**6:30** People do not despise a thief if he steals<br>To satisfy himself when he is hungry;  
**6:31** But when he is found, he must repay seven times [what he stole];<br>He must give all the property of his house [if necessary to meet his fine].  
**6:32** But whoever commits adultery with a woman lacks common sense <i>and</i> sound judgment <i>and</i> an understanding [of moral principles];<br>He who would destroy his soul does it.  
**6:33** Wounds and disgrace he will find,<br>And his reproach (blame) will not be blotted out.  
**6:34** For jealousy enrages the [wronged] husband;<br>He will not spare [the guilty one] on the day of vengeance.  
**6:35** He will not accept any ransom [offered to buy him off from demanding full punishment];<br>Nor will he be satisfied though you offer him many gifts (bribes).  

## 7장

**7:1** ¶ My son, keep my words<br>And treasure my commandments within you [so they are readily available to guide you].  
**7:2** Keep my commandments and live,<br>And keep my teaching <i>and</i> law as the apple of your eye.  
**7:3** Bind them [securely] on your fingers;<br>Write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to [skillful and godly] wisdom, “You are my sister,”<br>And regard understanding <i>and</i> intelligent insight as your intimate friends;  
**7:5** That they may keep you from the immoral woman,<br>From the foreigner [who does not observe God’s laws and] who flatters with her [smooth] words.  
**7:6** ¶ For at the window of my house<br>I looked out through my lattice.  
**7:7** And among the naive [the inexperienced and gullible],<br>I saw among the youths<br>A young man lacking [good] sense,  
**7:8** Passing through the street near her corner;<br>And he took the path to her house  
**7:9** In the twilight, in the evening;<br>In the black and dark night.  
**7:10** And there a woman met him,<br>Dressed as a prostitute and sly <i>and</i> cunning of heart.  
**7:11** She was boisterous and rebellious;<br><sup>①</sup>She would not stay at home.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Her feet do not</i>.

## 11장

**11:1** ¶ A false balance <i>and</i> dishonest business practices are extremely offensive to the LORD,<br>But an accurate scale is His delight.  
**11:2** When pride comes [boiling up with an arrogant attitude of self-importance], then come dishonor <i>and</i> shame,<br>But with the humble [the teachable who have been chiseled by trial and who have learned to walk humbly with God] there is wisdom <i>and</i> soundness of mind.  
**11:3** The integrity <i>and</i> moral courage of the upright will guide them,<br>But the crookedness of the treacherous will destroy them.  
**11:4** Riches will not provide security in the day of wrath <i>and</i> judgment,<br>But righteousness rescues from death.  
**11:5** The righteousness of the blameless will smooth their way <i>and</i> keep it straight,<br>But the wicked will fall by his own wickedness.  
**11:6** The righteousness of the upright will rescue them,<br>But the treacherous will be caught by their own greed.  
**11:7** When the wicked man dies, his expectation will perish;<br>And the hope of [godless] strong men perishes.

He said that a fox once told a wolf to come with him and he would give him abundant food. It was night, and the fox led the wolf to a well with a bucket at each end of the rope. The fox got into the bucket at the top of the well and rode down to the bottom. When the wolf asked him what he was doing, the fox pointed to the reflection of the moon in the water and said, “There is plenty of cheese and meat down here. Get in the other bucket and come down right away!” The wolf did, and as he went down the fox was pulled up in the first bucket. When the fox got to the top, the wolf said, “How am I supposed to get out?” Thereupon the fox quoted this verse to him.

**11:14** Where there is no [wise, intelligent] guidance, the people fall [and go off course like a ship without a helm],<br>But in the abundance of [wise and godly] counselors there is victory.  
**11:15** He who puts up security <i>and</i> guarantees a debt for an outsider will surely suffer [for his foolishness],<br>But he who hates (declines) being a guarantor is secure [from its penalties].  
**11:16** A gracious <i>and</i> good woman attains honor,<br>And ruthless men attain riches [but not respect].  
**11:17** The merciful <i>and</i> generous man benefits his soul [for his behavior returns to bless him],<br>But the cruel <i>and</i> callous man does himself harm.  
**11:18** The wicked man earns deceptive wages,<br>But he who sows righteousness <i>and</i> lives his life with integrity will have a true reward [that is both permanent and satisfying].  
**11:19** He who is steadfast in righteousness <i>attains</i> life,<br>But he who pursues evil <i>attains</i> his own death.  
**11:20** The perverse in heart are repulsive <i>and</i> shamefully vile to the LORD,<br>But those who are blameless <i>and</i> above reproach in their walk are His delight!  
**11:21** Assuredly, the evil man will not go unpunished,<br>But the descendants of the righteous will be freed.  
**11:22** As a ring of gold in a swine’s snout,<br>So is a beautiful woman who is without discretion [her lack of character mocks her beauty].  
**11:23** The desire of the righteous brings only good,<br>But the expectation of the wicked brings wrath.  
**11:24** There is the one who [generously] scatters [abroad], and yet increases all the more;<br>And there is the one who withholds what is justly due, <i>but it results</i> only in want <i>and</i> poverty.

**11:27** He who diligently seeks good seeks favor <i>and</i> grace,<br>But he who seeks evil, evil will come to him.

**31:13** She looks for wool and flax<br>And works with willing hands in delight.  
**31:14** She is like the merchant ships [abounding with treasure];<br>She brings her [household’s] food from far away.  
**31:15** She rises also while it is still night<br>And gives food to her household<br>And assigns tasks to her maids.  
**31:16** She considers a field before she buys <i>or</i> accepts it [expanding her business prudently];<br>With her profits she plants fruitful vines in her vineyard.  
**31:17** She equips herself with strength [spiritual, mental, and physical fitness for her God-given task]<br>And makes her arms strong.  
**31:18** She sees that her gain is good;<br>Her lamp does not go out, but it burns continually through the night [she is prepared for whatever lies ahead].  
**31:19** She stretches out her hands to the <sup>①</sup>distaff,<br>And her hands hold the spindle [as she spins wool into thread for clothing].<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e. the staff that holds the textile fibers for spinning.  
**31:20** She opens <i>and</i> extends her hand to the poor,<br>And she reaches out her filled hands to the needy.  
**31:21** She does not fear the snow for her household,<br>For all in her household are clothed in [expensive] scarlet [wool].  
**31:22** She makes for herself coverlets, cushions, <i>and</i> rugs of tapestry.<br>Her clothing is linen, pure <i>and</i> fine, and purple [wool].  
**31:23** Her husband is known in the [city’s] gates,<br>When he sits among the elders of the land.  
**31:24** She makes [fine] linen garments and sells them;<br>And supplies sashes to the merchants.  
**31:25** Strength and dignity are her clothing <i>and</i> her position is strong and secure;<br>And she smiles at the future [knowing that she and her family are prepared].  
**31:26** She opens her mouth in [skillful and godly] wisdom,<br>And the teaching of kindness is on her tongue [giving counsel and instruction].  
**31:27** She looks well to how things go in her household,<br>And does not eat the bread of idleness.  
**31:28** Her children rise up and call her blessed (happy, prosperous, to be admired);<br>Her husband also, and he praises her, <i>saying</i>,
