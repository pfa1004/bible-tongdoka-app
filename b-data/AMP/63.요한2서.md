# 63.요한2서 (AMP)



## 1장

**1:1** ¶ The elder [of the church addresses this letter] to the elect (chosen) <sup>①</sup>lady and her children, whom I love in truth—and not only I, but also all who know <i>and</i> understand the truth—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Many scholars believe the words “lady and her children” refer to a specific woman and her family; some others view the words “lady and her children” as the personification of a church and its members.  
**1:2** because of the truth which lives in our hearts and will be with us forever:  
**1:3** Grace, mercy, and peace (inner calm, a sense of spiritual well-being) will be with us, from God the Father and from Jesus Christ, the Father’s Son, in truth and love.  
**1:4** ¶ I was greatly delighted to find <i>some</i> of your children walking in truth, just as we have been commanded by the Father.  
**1:5** Now I ask you, lady, not as if <i>I were</i> writing to you a new commandment, but [simply reminding you of] the one which we have had from the beginning, that we <sup>①</sup>love <i>and</i> unselfishly seek the best for one another.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The key to understanding this and other statements about love is to know that this love (the Greek word <i>agape</i>) is not so much a matter of emotion as it is of doing things for the benefit of another person, that is, having an unselfish concern for another and a willingness to seek the best for another.  
**1:6** And this is love: that we walk in accordance with His commandments <i>and</i> are guided continually by His precepts. This is the commandment, just as you have heard from the beginning, that you should [always] walk in love.  
**1:7** ¶ For many deceivers [heretics, posing as Christians] have gone out into the world, those who do not acknowledge <i>and</i> confess the coming of Jesus Christ in the flesh (bodily form). This [person, the kind who does this] is the deceiver and the <sup>①</sup>antichrist [that is, the antagonist of Christ].<br>→ <sup>①</sup>John is not referring to the Antichrist of Revelation, but is using the term generally of anyone who is opposed to the cause of Christ.  
**1:8** Watch yourselves, so that you do not lose what we have accomplished together, but that you may receive a full <i>and</i> perfect reward [when He grants rewards to faithful believers].  
**1:9** Anyone who runs on ahead and does not remain in the doctrine of Christ [that is, one who is not content with what He taught], does not have God; but the one who <i>continues</i> to remain in the teaching [of Christ does have God], he has both the Father and the Son.  
**1:10** If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching [but diminishes or adds to the doctrine of Christ], do not receive <i>or</i> welcome him into your house, and do not give him a greeting <i>or</i> any encouragement;  
**1:11** for the one who gives him a greeting [who encourages him or wishes him success, unwittingly] participates in his evil deeds.  
**1:12** I have many things to write to you, but I prefer not to do so with paper (papyrus) and black (ink); but I hope to come to you and speak with you <sup>①</sup>face to face, so that <sup>②</sup>your joy may be complete.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>mouth to mouth</i>. <sup>②</sup>One early ms reads <i>our</i>.  
**1:13** ¶ The children of your elect (chosen) sister greet you.
