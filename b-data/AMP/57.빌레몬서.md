# 57.빌레몬서 (AMP)



## 1장

**1:1** ¶ Paul, a prisoner [for the sake] of Christ Jesus (the Messiah, the Anointed), and our brother Timothy, To Philemon our dearly beloved friend and fellow worker,  
**1:2** and to [your wife] Apphia our sister, and to <sup>①</sup>Archippus our fellow soldier [in ministry], and to the <sup>②</sup>church that meets in your <sup>③</sup>house:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Perhaps the son of Philemon and Apphia. <sup>②</sup>Philemon was responsible to see that this letter was shared with his fellow Colossian believers. <sup>③</sup>Prior to the third century A.D. churches customarily met in private homes.  
**1:3** Grace to you and peace [inner calm and spiritual well-being] from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:4** I thank my God always, making mention of you in my prayers,  
**1:5** because I hear of your love and of your faith which you have toward the Lord Jesus and toward all the <sup>①</sup>saints (God’s people).<br>→ <sup>①</sup>All born-again believers (saints) have been reborn from above—spiritually transformed, renewed, made holy and set apart for God’s purpose.  
**1:6** I pray that the sharing of your faith may become effective <i>and</i> powerful because of your <i>accurate</i> knowledge of every good thing which is ours in Christ.  
**1:7** For I have had great joy and comfort <i>and</i> encouragement from your love, because the hearts of the saints (God’s people) have been refreshed through you, my brother.  
**1:8** ¶ Therefore [on the basis of these facts], though I have enough confidence in Christ to order you to do what is appropriate,  
**1:9** yet for love’s sake I prefer to appeal <i>to you</i>—since I am such a person as Paul, an old man, and now also a prisoner [for the sake] of Christ Jesus—  
**1:10** I appeal to you for my [own spiritual] child Onesimus, whom I have fathered [in the faith] while a captive in these chains.  
**1:11** Once he was useless to you, but now he is indeed <sup>①</sup>useful to you as well as to me.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Paul makes a play on words here because <i>Onesimus</i> means “useful” or “profitable.”  
**1:12** I have sent him back to you in person, that is, <i>like sending</i> my very heart.  
**1:13** I would have chosen to keep him with me, so that he might minister to me on your behalf during my imprisonment for the gospel;  
**1:14** but I did not want to do anything without first getting your consent, so that your goodness would not be, in effect, by compulsion but of your own free will.  
**1:15** Perhaps it was for this reason that he was separated from you for a while, so that you would have him back forever,  
**1:16** no longer as a slave, but [as someone] more than a slave, as a brother [in Christ], especially dear to me, but how much more to you, both in the flesh [as a servant] and in the Lord [as a fellow believer].  
**1:17** ¶ So if you consider me a partner, welcome <i>and</i> accept him as you would me.  
**1:18** But if he has wronged you in any way or owes you anything, charge that to my account;  
**1:19** I, Paul, write this with my <sup>①</sup>own hand, I will repay it <i>in full</i> (not to mention to you that you <sup>②</sup>owe to me even your own self as well).<br>→ <sup>①</sup>By writing this with his own hand, Paul accepted legal liability. <sup>②</sup>Philemon evidently was saved through Paul’s ministry and therefore owed Paul a debt that could not be repaid.  
**1:20** Yes, brother, let me have some benefit <i>and</i> joy from you in the Lord; refresh my heart in Christ.  
**1:21** ¶ I write to you [perfectly] confident of your obedient compliance, <sup>①</sup>since I know that you will do even more than I ask.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>This was probably a subtle suggestion by Paul to emancipate Onesimus.  
**1:22** ¶ At the same time also prepare a guest room for me [in expectation of a visit], for I hope that through your prayers I will be [granted the gracious privilege of] coming to you [at Colossae].  
**1:23** ¶ Greetings to you from Epaphras, my fellow prisoner here in [the cause of] Christ Jesus,  
**1:24** and from Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers.  
**1:25** ¶ The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
