# 66.요한계시록 (AMP)



## 1장

**1:1** ¶ <i>This is</i> the <sup>①</sup>revelation of Jesus Christ [His unveiling of the divine mysteries], which God [the Father] gave to Him to show to His bond-servants (believers) the things which must soon take place [in their entirety]; and He sent and communicated it by His angel (divine messenger) to His bond-servant John,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The God-given disclosures contained in this book relate to both Christ and future events.  
**1:2** who testified <i>and</i> gave supporting evidence to the word of God and to the testimony of Jesus Christ, <i>even</i> to everything that he saw [in his visions].  
**1:3**  <sup>①</sup>Blessed (happy, prosperous, to be admired) is he who reads and those who hear the words of the prophecy, and who keep the things which are written in it [heeding them and taking them to heart]; for the time [of fulfillment] is near.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>This is the first of the seven promised blessings (beatitudes) of the <i>Revelation</i>. See also <a name="B:730 14:13">14:13</a>; <a name="B:730 16:15">16:15</a>; <a name="B:730 19:9">19:9</a>; <a name="B:730 20:6">20:6</a>; <a name="B:730 22:7">22:7</a>, <a name="B:730 22:14">14</a>.  
**1:4**  <sup>①</sup>John, to the seven churches that are in [the province of] <sup>②</sup>Asia: <sup>③</sup>Grace [be granted] to you and peace [inner calm and spiritual well-being], from Him Who is [existing forever] and Who was [continually existing in the past] and Who is to come, and from <sup>④</sup>the seven Spirits that are before His throne,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>John and his brother James were among the first apostles chosen by Jesus, and were the sons of Zebedee and Salome. In addition to the <i>Revelation</i>, John wrote the Gospel and the three letters that bear his name. <sup>②</sup>The Roman province of Asia (now western Turkey) was formed in 129 B.C. <sup>③</sup>Like Paul, the apostle John takes the standard Jewish greeting of “Peace [Heb <i>shalom</i>] to you” and modifies it by adding <i>grace</i>, without which no one could obtain peace with God. <sup>④</sup>A literal translation, perhaps referring to the perfect fullness of the ministry of the Holy Spirit.  
**1:5** and from Jesus Christ, the <sup>①</sup>faithful <i>and</i> trustworthy Witness, the <sup>②</sup>Firstborn of the dead, and the Ruler of the kings of the earth. To Him who [always] loves us and who [has once for all] <sup>③</sup>freed us [or washed us] from our sins by His own blood (His sacrificial death)—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the witness, the faithful</i>. <sup>②</sup>Jesus was not the first person to be brought back to life, since He raised others from the dead (the most notable being Lazarus, John 11), but they had to experience death again. Jesus is the first to be restored to life with a glorified (immortal, everlasting) body, never again to die. The word <i>Firstborn</i> may point not only to Jesus’ own resurrection but also to His divine power to resurrect others. The Firstborn (preeminent) is given power over all things. <sup>③</sup>Some manuscripts use “freed” <i>(lusanti)</i> while others use “washed” <i>(lousanti)</i>. Either reading conveys a similar theological conclusion: Jesus has taken away our sins by His blood.  
**1:6** and formed us into a kingdom [as His subjects], <sup>①</sup>priests to His God and Father—to Him be the glory <i>and</i> the power <i>and</i> the majesty and the dominion forever and ever. Amen.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>All Christians become priests to God in order to offer spiritual sacrifices (<a name="B:670 2:5">1PE 2:5</a>) and proclaim His praises (<a name="B:670 2:9">1PE 2:9</a>), and they will reign with Christ in the Millennium (<a name="B:730 20:6">20:6</a>).  
**1:7** <e>Behold, He is coming with the clouds</e>, and every eye will see Him, even those who pierced Him; and all the tribes (nations) of the earth will mourn over Him [realizing their sin and guilt, and anticipating the coming wrath]. <sup>①</sup>So it is to be. Amen.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Yes, amen</i>.  
**1:8** ¶ “I am the <sup>①</sup>Alpha and the Omega [the Beginning and the End],” says the Lord God, “Who is [existing forever] and Who was [continually existing in the past] and Who is to come, the Almighty [the Omnipotent, the Ruler of all].”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>An expression of God’s absolute supremacy over all things.  
**1:9** I, John, your brother and companion in the <sup>①</sup>tribulation and kingdom and patient endurance <i>which are</i> in Jesus, was on the island called Patmos, [exiled there] because of [my preaching of] the word of God [regarding eternal salvation] and the testimony of Jesus <i>Christ</i>.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e. persecution because of one’s faith.  
**1:10** I was in the <sup>①</sup>Spirit [in special communication with the Holy Spirit and empowered to receive and record the revelation from Jesus Christ] on the <sup>②</sup>Lord’s Day, and I heard behind me a loud voice like the <i>sound</i> of a trumpet,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>spirit</i>. The Greek wording is not decisive, so John could be referring either to being in special communion with the Holy Spirit, or to being in a trance-like state in his own spirit like that experienced by Peter at Joppa (cf <a name="B:510 10:10">ACT 10:10, 11</a>). <sup>②</sup>The early church used this term to refer to Sunday, the day of Jesus’ resurrection. The term <i>Kyriake</i> is also the regular word for Sunday in modern Greek.  
**1:11** saying, “Write on a scroll what you see [in this revelation], and send it to the <sup>①</sup>seven churches—to Ephesus and to Smyrna and to Pergamum and to Thyatira and to Sardis and to Philadelphia and to Laodicea.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>These were actual churches in Asia Minor (now western Turkey), and in John’s time there would have been only one church body in each of the cities. They are mentioned in the order in which they would be visited by a messenger traveling northward on the imperial road from Ephesus to Laodicea.  
**1:12** ¶ Then I turned to see the voice that was speaking with me. And after turning I saw seven golden lampstands;  
**1:13** and in the midst of the lampstands <i>I saw</i> someone <sup>①</sup>like the Son of Man, dressed in a robe reaching to His feet, and with a golden sash wrapped around His chest.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>like a son of man</i>. The phrase is a translation of a Hebrew idiom which refers to a man, and John could simply be describing his first impression. But since this proves to be a vision of Christ (v <a name="B:730 1:18">18</a>), John probably is referring to Him by the title that Jesus so often applied to Himself, a title originating in the Messianic prophecy of Daniel.  
**1:14** His head and His hair were white like white wool, [glistening white] like snow; and His [all-seeing] eyes were [flashing] like a flame of fire [piercing into my being].  
**1:15** His feet were like burnished [white-hot] bronze, refined in a furnace, and His voice was [powerful] like the sound of many waters.  
**1:16** In His right hand He held seven stars, and from His mouth came a sharp two-edged sword [of judgment]; and His face [reflecting His majesty and the <sup>①</sup>Shekinah glory] was like the sun shining in [all] its power [at midday].<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The visible, divine Presence.  
**1:17** ¶ When I saw Him, I fell at His feet as though dead. And He placed His right hand on me and said, “Do not be afraid; I am the First and the Last [absolute Deity, the Son of God],  
**1:18** and the Ever-living One [living in and beyond all time and space]. I died, but see, I am alive forevermore, and I have the keys of [absolute control and victory over] death and of Hades (the realm of the dead).  
**1:19** So write the things <sup>①</sup>which you have seen [in the vision], and the things <sup>②</sup>which are [now happening], and the things <sup>③</sup>which will take place after these things.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e. the vision of ch 1. <sup>②</sup>I.e. the events of the present moment (chs 2; 3). <sup>③</sup>I.e. the events to come in chs 4-22, after the messages to the churches in chs 2; 3.  
**1:20** As for the mystery of the seven stars which you saw in My right hand, and the seven golden lampstands: the seven stars are the angels (<sup>①</sup>divine messengers) of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are the seven churches.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Greek word for “messenger” is <i>aggelos</i>. When the word is referring to a heavenly being, it is usually not translated, but rather transliterated as “angel”; that is, the Greek letters are converted to their English equivalents, based on the sound of the Greek word. When the word is referring to a human, it is instead translated as “messenger” or the equivalent. So whether “angel” or “messenger” is used depends on the context.  

## 2장

**2:1** ¶ “To the angel (divine messenger) of the church in <sup>①</sup>Ephesus write: “These are the words of the One who holds [firmly] the seven stars [which are the angels or messengers of the seven churches] in His right hand, the One who walks among the seven golden lampstands (the seven churches):<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ephesus, the largest city in Roman-controlled Asia Minor, developed into a major trade center because of its access to the Aegean Sea. It was the primary center for the worship of the goddess Artemis (Diana). The Temple of Artemis, the largest building in ancient times, was considered one of the Seven Wonders of the World. The open-air theatre in Ephesus could accommodate 25,000 spectators and was used for both drama and gladiatorial combat.  
**2:2** ¶ ‘I know <sup>①</sup>your deeds and your toil, and your patient endurance, and that you cannot tolerate those who are evil, and have tested <i>and</i> critically appraised those who call themselves apostles (special messengers, personally chosen representatives, of Christ), and [in fact] are not, and have found them to be liars <i>and</i> impostors;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Here through <a name="B:730 3:18">3:18</a>, “your” and “you” are in the singular, referring to the <i>angel</i> of each church. Much of what is said is rebuke and admonishment, so if the <i>angels</i> are heavenly beings, they may serve in some way as representatives of the sinful people in their churches. Jewish tradition maintained that every nation and individual has a guardian angel, and that when God is about to punish a nation, He first punishes its angel. There is even a story of Michael, the guardian angel of Israel, being rebuked by God for the sins committed in the time of Ezekiel. So the original readers of Revelation might have assumed that the angels here are the guardian angels of the individual churches, sharing responsibility for the actions of the members.  
**2:3** and [I know that] you [who believe] are enduring patiently and are bearing up for My name’s sake, and that you have not grown weary [of being faithful to the truth].  
**2:4** But I have <i>this</i> [charge] against you, that you have left your first love [you have lost the depth of love that you first had for Me].  
**2:5** So remember <i>the heights</i> from which you have fallen, and repent [change your inner self—your old way of thinking, your sinful behavior—seek God’s will] and do the works you did at first [when you first knew Me]; otherwise, I will visit you and remove your lampstand (the church, its impact) from its place—unless you repent.  
**2:6** Yet you have this [to your credit], that you hate the works <i>and</i> corrupt teachings of the <sup>①</sup>Nicolaitans [that mislead and delude the people], which I also hate.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>There is scant information about the cult of the Nicolaitans, but it appears they adopted a syncretistic worship, combining Christianity and idolatry. They may have held heretical views similar to those mentioned in vv <a name="B:730 2:14">14, 20</a>.  
**2:7** He who has an ear, let him hear <i>and</i> heed what the Spirit says to the churches. To him who <sup>①</sup>overcomes [the world through believing that Jesus is the Son of God], I will grant [the privilege] to eat [the fruit] from the tree of life, which is in the Paradise of God.’<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The definition of one who overcomes is recorded in <a name="B:690 5:5">1JN 5:5</a>.  
**2:8** “And to the angel (divine messenger) of the church in <sup>①</sup>Smyrna write: “These are the words of the First and the Last [absolute Deity, the Son of God] who died and came to life [again]:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>In ancient times Smyrna (modern Izmir, Turkey) was a beautiful, cosmopolitan city. Located on the Aegean coast, it vied with Ephesus and Pergamum in importance in Asia Minor. It was closely identified with Rome and the cult of emperor worship. Smyrna was later the home of Polycarp, the great Christian church leader who, at the age of eighty-six, was burned at the stake for his refusal to worship the Roman emperor.  
**2:9** ¶ ‘I know your suffering and your poverty (but you are rich), and how you are blasphemed <i>and</i> slandered by those who say they are Jews and are not, but are a synagogue of Satan [they are Jews only by blood, and do not believe and truly honor the God whom they claim to worship].  
**2:10** Fear nothing that you are about to suffer. Be aware that the devil is about to throw some of you into prison, that you may be tested [in your faith], and for ten days you will have tribulation. Be faithful to the point of death [if you must die for your faith], and I will give you the crown [consisting] of life.  
**2:11** He who has an ear, let him hear <i>and</i> heed what the Spirit says to the churches. He who overcomes [the world through believing that Jesus is the Son of God] will not be hurt by the second death (<sup>①</sup>the lake of fire).’<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Valley of Hinnom located south of Jerusalem was the place where garbage burned continuously. Often regarded in ancient times as symbolic of hell (the lake of fire), reserved for the wicked. Paradise or Abraham’s bosom (cf <a name="B:490 16:22">LUK 16:22</a>) was the place reserved for the good in anticipation of heaven. Mentioned in <a name="B:470 5:22">MAT 5:22</a>, <a name="B:470 5:29">29</a>, <a name="B:470 5:30">30</a>; <a name="B:470 10:28">10:28</a>; <a name="B:470 18:9">18:9</a>; <a name="B:470 23:15">23:15</a>, <a name="B:470 23:33">33</a>; <a name="B:480 9:43">MAK 9:43, 45</a>, <a name="B:480 9:47">47</a>; <a name="B:490 12:5">LUK 12:5</a>; <a name="B:660 3:6">JAM 3:6</a>.  
**2:12** “And to the angel (divine messenger) of the church in <sup>①</sup>Pergamum write: “These are the words of Him who has <i>and</i> wields the sharp two-edged sword [in judgment]:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Pergamum (modern Bergama, Turkey) was built on a promontory about sixteen miles from the Aegean Sea. It was famous as a worship center for many pagan gods including Zeus (Jupiter, king of gods), Dionysus (Bacchus, god of wine and intoxication), and Asklepios (god of healing). The enormous altar dedicated to Zeus is on display in the Pergamon Museum in Berlin. Pergamum also had an important temple dedicated to the worship of Caesar. Refusal to take part in the imperial cult was considered treason. A culturally sophisticated city as well as a center for pagan worship, Pergamum’s library, believed to contain over 200,000 volumes, was second only to the Library of Alexandria.  
**2:13** ¶ ‘I know where you dwell, [a place] where Satan sits enthroned. Yet you are holding fast to My name, and you did not deny <sup>①</sup>My faith even in the days of <sup>②</sup>Antipas, My witness, My faithful one, who was killed (martyred) among you, where Satan dwells.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e. your faith in Me. <sup>②</sup>Antipas was a pastor or leader of the church at Pergamum.  
**2:14** But I have a few things against you, because you have there some [among you] who are holding to the [corrupt] teaching of Balaam, who taught <sup>①</sup>Balak to put a stumbling block before the sons of Israel, [enticing them] to eat things that had been sacrificed to idols and to commit [acts of sexual] <sup>②</sup>immorality.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The king of Moab in the time of the exodus, who hired Balaam to curse Israel. <sup>②</sup>Pagan worship often included the use of temple prostitutes who served as “priestesses.”  
**2:15** You also have some who in the same way are holding to the teaching of the <sup>①</sup>Nicolaitans.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>See note v <a name="B:730 2:6">6</a>.  
**2:16** Therefore repent [change your inner self—your old way of thinking, your sinful behavior—seek God’s will]; or else I am coming to you quickly, and I will make war <i>and</i> fight against them with the sword of My mouth [in judgment].  
**2:17** He who has an ear, let him hear <i>and</i> heed what the Spirit says to the churches. To him who overcomes [the world through believing that Jesus is the Son of God], to him I will give [the privilege of eating] <i>some</i> of the <sup>①</sup>hidden manna, and I will give him a <sup>②</sup>white stone with a new name engraved on the stone which no one knows except the one who receives it.’<br>→ <sup>①</sup>This probably refers to the pot of manna placed in the Ark of the Covenant by Moses (<a name="B:20 16:33">EXO 16:33</a>; <a name="B:650 9:4">HEB 9:4</a>). Manna was a food supernaturally provided to Israel by God during their wanderings in the wilderness after the exodus from Egypt. <sup>②</sup>White stones were sometimes used as tickets of admission to public assemblies. Here the white stone may symbolize admission to the Messiah’s banquet.  
**2:18** “And to the angel (divine messenger) of the church in <sup>①</sup>Thyatira write: “These are the words of the Son of God, who has eyes [that flash] like a flame of fire [in righteous judgment], and whose feet are like burnished [white-hot] bronze:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Thyatira (modern Akhisar, Turkey) was famous for its dyeing and indigo trade. It was the home of Lydia, the seller of purple fabrics, who was converted under the ministry of Paul (<a name="B:510 16:14">ACT 16:14</a>). Located in a valley, midway between Pergamum and Sardis, it was originally established as a military base by one of the generals in the army of Alexander the Great.  
**2:19** ¶ ‘I know your deeds, your love and faith and service and patient endurance, and that your last deeds are more numerous <i>and</i> greater than the first.  
**2:20** But I have this [charge] against you, that you tolerate the woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess [claiming to be inspired], and she teaches and misleads My bond-servants so that they commit [acts of sexual] immorality and eat food sacrificed to idols.  
**2:21** I gave her time to repent [to change her inner self and her sinful way of thinking], but she has no desire to repent of her immorality <i>and</i> refuses to do so.  
**2:22**  <sup>①</sup>Listen carefully, I will throw her on a bed <i>of sickness</i>, and those who commit adultery with her [I will bring] into great anguish, unless they repent of her deeds.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Behold</i>.  
**2:23** And I will kill her children (followers) with <sup>①</sup>pestilence [thoroughly annihilating them], and all the churches will know [without any doubt] that I am He who searches the <sup>②</sup>minds and hearts [the innermost thoughts, purposes]; and I will give to each one of you [a reward or punishment] according to your deeds.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>death</i>. <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>kidneys</i>, i.e. inner man. In Jewish thought, the kidneys were considered a source of wisdom (cf <a name="B:230 16:7">PS 16:7</a>).  
**2:24** But to the rest of you in Thyatira, who do not hold this teaching, who have not explored <i>and</i> known the <sup>①</sup>depths of Satan, as they call them—I place no other burden on you,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e. participated in and experienced evil.  
**2:25** except to hold tightly to what you have until I come.  
**2:26** And he who overcomes [the world through believing that Jesus is the Son of God] and he who keeps My deeds [doing things that please Me] until the [very] end, <e>to him I will give authority</e> <i>and</i> <e>power over the nations</e>;  
**2:27** <e>and he shall shepherd</e> <i>and</i> <e>rule them with a rod of iron, as the earthen pots are broken in pieces</e>, as I also have received <i>authority</i> [and power to rule them] from My Father;  
**2:28** and I will give him the <sup>①</sup>Morning Star.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>In <a name="B:730 22:16">22:16</a> this expression designates Christ as the true Messiah, <i>the root and the descendant of David</i>. Here, the gift probably is the privilege of knowing Christ at a much higher level (see <a name="B:570 3:8">PHL 3:8</a>).  
**2:29** He who has an ear, let him hear <i>and</i> heed what the Spirit says to the churches.’  

## 3장

**3:1** ¶ “To the angel (divine messenger) of the church in <sup>①</sup>Sardis write: “These are the words of Him who has <sup>②</sup>the seven Spirits of God and the seven stars: ‘I know your deeds; you have a name (reputation) that you are alive, but [in reality] you are dead.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Sardis (modern Sart, Turkey), in Roman times, was a wealthy city known for the manufacture and dyeing of woolen fabric and carpets. Originally, it was the capital of the ancient Lydian Empire and was the first city to mint coinage (600 B.C.). A stream that flowed through its market place carried gold dust from a nearby mountain. King Croesus of Lydia (560-546 B.C.) financed the building of the great Temple of Artemis in Ephesus (550 B.C.). The city was conquered twice, under Cyrus the Great and under Antiochus, due to lack of adequate watchfulness. <sup>②</sup>See note <a name="B:730 1:4">1:4</a>.  
**3:2** Wake up, and strengthen <i>and</i> reaffirm what remains [of your faithful commitment to Me], which is about to die; for I have not found [any of] your deeds completed in the sight of My God <i>or</i> meeting His requirements.  
**3:3** So remember <i>and</i> take to heart the lessons you have received and heard. Keep <i>and</i> obey them, and repent [change your sinful way of thinking, and demonstrate your repentance with new behavior that proves a conscious decision to turn away from sin]. So then, if you do not wake up, I will come like a thief, and you will not know at what hour I will come to you.  
**3:4** But you [still] have a few people in Sardis who have not soiled their clothes [that is, contaminated their character and personal integrity with sin]; and they will walk with Me [dressed] in white, because they are worthy (righteous).  
**3:5** He who overcomes [the world through believing that Jesus is the Son of God] will accordingly be dressed in white clothing; and I will never blot out his name from the Book of Life, and I will confess <i>and</i> openly acknowledge his name before My Father and before His angels [saying that he is one of Mine].  
**3:6** He who has an ear, let him hear <i>and</i> heed what the Spirit says to the churches.’  
**3:7** “And to the angel (divine messenger) of the church in <sup>①</sup>Philadelphia write: “These are the words of the Holy One, the True One, He who has the key [to the house] of David, He who opens and no one will [be able to] shut, and He who shuts and no one opens:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Philadelphia (modern Alashehir, Turkey) was a commercial center located on elevated ground overlooking the fertile plain of the Hermus (Gediz) River. In 189 B.C. the king of Pergamum established Philadelphia and named the city to honor his brother.  
**3:8** ¶ ‘I know your deeds. See, I have set before you an open door which no one is able to shut, for you have a little power, and have kept My word, and have not renounced <i>or</i> denied My name.  
**3:9** Take note, I will make <i>those</i> of the synagogue of Satan, who say that they are Jews and are not, but lie—I will make them come and bow down at your feet and <i>make them</i> know [without any doubt] that I have loved you.  
**3:10** Because you have kept the word of My endurance [My command to persevere], I will keep you [safe] from the hour of trial, that <i>hour</i> which is about to come on the whole [inhabited] world, to test those who live on the earth.  
**3:11** I am coming quickly. Hold tight what you have, so that no one will take your crown [by leading you to renounce the faith].  
**3:12** He who overcomes [the world through believing that Jesus is the Son of God], I will make him a <sup>①</sup>pillar in the temple of My God; he will most certainly never be put out of it, and I will write on him the name of My God, and the name of the city of My God, the new Jerusalem, which descends out of heaven from My God, and My [own] new name.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Like the common phrase “pillar of the community,” this is a metaphor of great honor, but far more so. The statement that the one who overcomes will never leave the temple is assurance that he will never lose the privilege of being in the presence of God and serving Him.  
**3:13** He who has an ear, let him hear <i>and</i> heed what the Spirit says to the churches.’  
**3:14** “To the angel (divine messenger) of the church in <sup>①</sup>Laodicea write: “These are the words of the Amen, the trusted <i>and</i> faithful and true Witness, the <sup>②</sup>Beginning <i>and</i> Origin of God’s creation:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Laodicea, located beside the Lycus River, was a prosperous city about forty miles south of Philadelphia. It had a well-respected financial center, thriving textile production industry (specializing in black wool), and a leading medical school that produced a famous eye salve. Two other cities were in this general area: Colossae, known for its mountain spring of cold, fresh water; and Hierapolis, a site famous for its hot, therapeutic mineral springs. <sup>②</sup>I.e. Origin of Source.  
**3:15** ¶ ‘I know your deeds, that you are <sup>①</sup>neither cold (invigorating, refreshing) nor hot (healing, therapeutic); I wish that you were <sup>②</sup>cold or hot.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>This phrase and the word <i>lukewarm</i> in v <a name="B:730 3:16">16</a> form a metaphor that is probably based on the quality of the water supply of Laodicea, which was delivered by aqueduct from the hot mineral springs about five miles away. During its journey, the water cooled to a warm or lukewarm temperature. <sup>②</sup>I.e. alive, productive—beneficial in one way or the other.  
**3:16** So because you are lukewarm (spiritually useless), and neither hot nor cold, I will vomit you out of My mouth [rejecting you with disgust].

Such a combination would not be used; and if it were, the sum would then be only eighteen, because in alphabet-based numbering systems like ancient Hebrew and Greek, place values are not used (10’s, 100’s etc.), but the letters are treated more like Roman numerals. What John is most likely indicating is that the letters of the name will add up to 666 (the best Greek manuscripts have the number spelled out), and there could be many different combinations of letters that would do so. Determining the values of the individual letters is another matter. John probably assumed that the name would be Hebrew or Greek, and in Hebrew vowels for the most part are not represented as letters. Further complications arise when names from other nationalities are considered, and are converted for valuation. For example, at various times it has been argued that the name “Nero Caesar” adds up to 666 when transliterated to Hebrew as NRWN QSR (according to the Greek form); but in the Talmud, the name is transliterated as NYRWN QYSR, which totals 686. Also, the original Latin form of the name (without the second “n”), if converted directly to Hebrew, adds up to 616, and a few ancient manuscripts had that number.

**22:4** they will [be privileged to] see His face, and His name will be on their foreheads.  
**22:5** And there will no longer be night; they have no need for lamplight or sunlight, because the Lord God will illumine them; and they will reign [as kings] forever and ever.  
**22:6** Then he said to me, “These words are faithful and true.” And the Lord, the God of the spirits of the prophets, has sent His angel [as a representative] to show His bond-servants the things that must soon take place.

**22:8** ¶ I, John, am the one who heard and saw these things. And when I heard and saw them, I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel who showed me these things.  
**22:9** But he said to me, “Do not do that. I am a fellow servant with you and your brothers the prophets and with those who heed <i>and</i> remember [the truths contained in] the words of this book. Worship God.”  
**22:10** ¶ And he said to me, “Do not seal up the words of the prophecy of this book, for the time [of their fulfillment] is <sup>①</sup>near.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>While Daniel was instructed to seal his prophecy, John was now told to reveal his (see <a name="B:340 12:4">DAN 12:4</a>).  
**22:11** Let the one who does wrong, still do wrong; and the one who is filthy (vile, impure), still be filthy; and the one who is righteous (just, upright), still be righteous; and the one who is holy, still be holy.”  
**22:12** ¶ “Behold, I (Jesus) am coming quickly, and My reward is with Me, <sup>①</sup>to give to each one <sup>②</sup>according to the merit of his deeds (earthly works, faithfulness).<br>→ <sup>①</sup>I.e. the final judgment for the believer will occur when he stands before Christ to have his fidelity and service judged, and the appropriate reward determined. <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>as his work is</i>.  
**22:13** I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End [the Eternal One].”  
**22:14** ¶ <sup>①</sup>Blessed (happy, prosperous, to be admired) are those who wash their robes [in the blood of Christ by believing and trusting in Him—the righteous who do His commandments], so that they may have the right to the tree of life, and may enter by the gates into the city.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>This is the seventh and last of the promised blessings. See note <a name="B:730 1:3">1:3</a>.  
**22:15** Outside are the dogs [the godless, the impure, those of low moral character] and the sorcerers [with their intoxicating drugs, and magic arts], and the immoral persons [the perverted, the molesters, and the adulterers], and the murderers, and the idolaters, and everyone who loves and practices lying (deception, cheating).  
**22:16** “I, Jesus, have sent My angel to testify to you <i>and</i> to give you assurance of these things for the churches. I am the Root (the Source, the Life) and the Offspring of David, the radiant <i>and</i> bright Morning Star.”  
**22:17** ¶ The [Holy] Spirit and the bride (the church, believers) say, “Come.” And let the one who hears say, “Come.” And let the one who is thirsty come; let the one who wishes take <i>and</i> drink the water of life without cost.  
**22:18** ¶ I testify <i>and</i> warn everyone who hears the words of the prophecy of this book [its predictions, consolations, and admonitions]: if anyone adds [anything] to them, God will add to him the plagues (afflictions, calamities) which are written in this book;  
**22:19** and if anyone takes away from <i>or</i> distorts the words of the book of this prophecy, God will take away [from that one] his share from the tree of life and from the holy city (new Jerusalem), which are written in this book.  
**22:20** ¶ He who testifies <i>and</i> affirms these things says, “Yes, I am coming quickly.” Amen. Come, Lord Jesus.  
**22:21** ¶ The grace of the Lord Jesus (the Christ, the Messiah) be with all [the saints—all believers, those set apart for God]. Amen.
