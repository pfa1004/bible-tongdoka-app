# 33.미가 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Micah the Moreshite—what he saw regarding Samaria and Jerusalem in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.  
**1:2** Listen, all you peoples;<br>pay attention, earth<sup>①</sup> and everyone in it!<br>The Lord GOD will be a witness against you,<br>the Lord, from his holy temple.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>land</i>  
**1:3** Look, the LORD is leaving his place<br>and coming down to trample<br>the heights<sup>①</sup> of the earth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>high places</i>  
**1:4** The mountains will melt beneath him,<br>and the valleys will split apart,<br>like wax near a fire,<br>like water cascading down a mountainside.  
**1:5** All this will happen because of Jacob’s rebellion<br>and the sins of the house of Israel.<br>What is the rebellion of Jacob?<br>Isn’t it Samaria?<br>And what is the high place of Judah?<br>Isn’t it Jerusalem?  
**1:6** Therefore, I will make Samaria<br>a heap of ruins in the countryside,<br>a planting area for a vineyard.<br>I will roll her stones into the valley<br>and expose her foundations.  
**1:7** All her carved images will be smashed to pieces;<br>all her wages will be burned in the fire,<br>and I will destroy all her idols.<br>Since she collected the wages of a prostitute,<br>they will be used again for a prostitute.  
**1:8** Because of this I will lament and wail;<br>I will walk barefoot and naked.<br>I will howl like the jackals<br>and mourn like ostriches.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>eagle owls;</i> lit <i>daughters of the desert</i>  
**1:9** For her wound is incurable<br>and has reached even Judah;<br>it has approached my people’s city gate,<br>as far as Jerusalem.  
**1:10** Don’t announce it in Gath,<br>don’t weep at all.<br>Roll in the dust in Beth-leaphrah.  
**1:11** Depart in shameful nakedness,<br>you residents of Shaphir;<br>the residents of Zaanan will not come out.<br>Beth-ezel is lamenting;<br>its support<sup>①</sup> is taken from you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>its standing place;</i> Hb obscure  
**1:12** Though the residents of Maroth<br>anxiously wait for something good,<br>disaster has come from the LORD<br>to the gate of Jerusalem.  
**1:13** Harness the horses to the chariot,<br>you residents of Lachish.<br>This was the beginning of sin for Daughter Zion<br>because Israel’s acts of rebellion can be traced to you.  
**1:14** Therefore, send farewell gifts to Moresheth-gath;<br>the houses of Achzib are a deception<br>to the kings of Israel.  
**1:15** I will again bring a conqueror<br>against you who live in Mareshah.<br>The nobility<sup>①</sup> of Israel will come to Adullam.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>glory</i>  
**1:16** Shave yourselves bald and cut off your hair<br>in sorrow for your precious children;<br>make yourselves as bald as an eagle,<br>for they have been taken from you into exile.  

## 2장

**2:1** Woe to those who dream up wickedness<br>and prepare evil plans on their beds!<br>At morning light they accomplish it<br>because the power is in their hands.  
**2:2** They covet fields and seize them;<br>they also take houses.<br>They deprive a man of his home,<br>a person of his inheritance.  
**2:3** Therefore, the LORD says:<br>I am now planning a disaster<br>against this nation;<br>you cannot free your necks from it.<br>Then you will not walk so proudly<br>because it will be an evil time.  
**2:4** In that day one will take up a taunt against you<br>and lament mournfully, saying,<br>“We are totally ruined!<br>He measures out the allotted land of my people.<br>How he removes it from me!<br>He allots our fields to traitors.”  
**2:5** Therefore, there will be no one<br>in the assembly of the LORD<br>to divide the land by casting lots.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>LORD stretching the measuring line by lot</i>  
**2:6** “Quit your preaching,” they<sup>①</sup> preach.<br>“They should not preach these things;<br>shame will not overtake us.”<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>= the prophets <sup>②</sup>Text emended; MT reads <i>things. Shame will not depart</i>  
**2:7** House of Jacob, should it be asked,<br>“Is the Spirit of the LORD impatient?<br>Are these the things he does?”<br>Don’t my words bring good<br>to the one who walks uprightly?  
**2:8** But recently my people have risen up<br>like an enemy:<br>You strip off the splendid robe<br>from those who are passing through confidently,<br>like those returning from war.  
**2:9** You force the women of my people<br>out of their comfortable homes,<br>and you take my blessing<sup>①</sup><br>from their children forever.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Perhaps the land  
**2:10** Get up and leave,<br>for this is not your place of rest<br>because defilement brings destruction—<br>a grievous destruction!  
**2:11** If a man comes<br>and utters empty lies—<br>“I will preach to you about wine and beer”—<br>he would be just the preacher for this people!  
**2:12** I will indeed gather all of you, Jacob;<br>I will collect the remnant of Israel.<br>I will bring them together like sheep in a pen,<br>like a flock in the middle of its pasture.<br>It will be noisy with people.  
**2:13** One who breaks open the way<br>will advance before them;<br>they will break out, pass through the city gate,<br>and leave by it.<br>Their King will pass through before them,<br>the LORD as their leader.  

## 3장

**3:1** Then I said, “Now listen, leaders of Jacob,<br>you rulers of the house of Israel.<br>Aren’t you supposed to know what is just?  
**3:2** You hate good and love evil.<br>You tear off people’s skin<br>and strip their flesh from their bones.  
**3:3** You eat the flesh of my people<br>after you strip their skin from them<br>and break their bones.<br>You chop them up<br>like flesh for the cooking pot,<br>like meat in a cauldron.”  
**3:4** Then they will cry out to the LORD,<br>but he will not answer them.<br>He will hide his face from them at that time<br>because of the crimes they have committed.  
**3:5** This is what the LORD says<br>concerning the prophets<br>who lead my people astray,<br>who proclaim peace<br>when they have food to sink their teeth into<br>but declare war against the one<br>who puts nothing in their mouths.  
**3:6** Therefore, it will be night for you—<br>without visions;<br>it will grow dark for you—<br>without divination.<br>The sun will set on these prophets,<br>and the daylight will turn black over them.  
**3:7** Then the seers will be ashamed<br>and the diviners disappointed.<br>They will all cover their mouths<sup>①</sup><br>because there will be no answer from God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>mustache</i>  
**3:8** As for me, however, I am filled with power<br>by the Spirit of the LORD,<br>with justice and courage,<br>to proclaim to Jacob his rebellion<br>and to Israel his sin.  
**3:9** Listen to this, leaders of the house of Jacob,<br>you rulers of the house of Israel,<br>who abhor justice<br>and pervert everything that is right,  
**3:10** who build Zion with bloodshed<br>and Jerusalem with injustice.  
**3:11** Her leaders issue rulings for a bribe,<br>her priests teach for payment,<br>and her prophets practice divination for silver.<br>Yet they lean on the LORD, saying,<br>“Isn’t the LORD among us?<br>No disaster will overtake us.”  
**3:12** Therefore, because of you,<br>Zion will be plowed like a field,<br>Jerusalem will become ruins,<br>and the temple’s mountain<br>will be a high thicket.  

## 4장

**4:1** In the last days<br>the mountain of the LORD’s house<br>will be established<br>at the top of the mountains<br>and will be raised above the hills.<br>Peoples will stream to it,  
**4:2** and many nations will come and say,<br>“Come, let’s go up to the mountain of the LORD,<br>to the house of the God of Jacob.<br>He will teach us about his ways<br>so we may walk in his paths.”<br>For instruction will go out of Zion<br>and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.  
**4:3** He will settle disputes among many peoples<br>and provide arbitration for strong nations<br>that are far away.<br>They will beat their swords into plows<br>and their spears into pruning knives.<br>Nation will not take up the sword against nation,<br>and they will never again train for war.  
**4:4** But each person will sit under his grapevine<br>and under his fig tree<br>with no one to frighten him.<br>For the mouth of the LORD of Armies<br>has spoken.  
**4:5** Though all the peoples walk<br>in the name of their own gods,<br>we will walk in the name of the LORD our God<br>forever and ever.  
**4:6** On that day—this is the LORD’s declaration—I will assemble the lame<br>and gather the scattered,<br>those I have injured.  
**4:7** I will make the lame into a remnant,<br>those far removed into a strong nation.<br>Then the LORD will reign over them in Mount Zion<br>from this time on and forever.  
**4:8** And you, watchtower for the flock,<br>fortified hill<sup>①</sup> of Daughter Zion,<br>the former rule will come to you;<br>sovereignty will come to Daughter Jerusalem.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>flock, Ophel</i>  
**4:9** Now, why are you shouting loudly?<br>Is there no king with you?<br>Has your counselor perished<br>so that anguish grips you like a woman in labor?  
**4:10** Writhe and cry out,<sup>①</sup> Daughter Zion,<br>like a woman in labor,<br>for now you will leave the city<br>and camp in the open fields.<br>You will go to Babylon;<br>there you will be rescued;<br>there the LORD will redeem you<br>from the grasp of your enemies!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hb obscure  
**4:11** Many nations have now assembled against you;<br>they say, “Let her be defiled,<br>and let us feast our eyes on Zion.”  
**4:12** But they do not know the LORD’s intentions<br>or understand his plan,<br>that he has gathered them<br>like sheaves to the threshing floor.  
**4:13** Rise and thresh, Daughter Zion,<br>for I will make your horns iron<br>and your hooves bronze<br>so you can crush many peoples.<br>Then you<sup>①</sup> will set apart their plunder<br>for the LORD,<br>their wealth for the Lord of the whole earth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>LXX, Syr, Tg; MT reads <i>I</i>  

## 5장

**5:1** Now, daughter who is under attack,<br>you slash yourself in grief;<br>a siege is set against us!<br>They are striking the judge of Israel<br>on the cheek with a rod.  
**5:2** Bethlehem Ephrathah,<br>you are small among the clans of Judah;<br>one will come from you<br>to be ruler over Israel for me.<br>His origin<sup>①</sup> is from antiquity,<br>from ancient times.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>His going out</i>  
**5:3** Therefore, Israel will be abandoned until the time<br>when she who is in labor has given birth;<br>then the rest of the ruler’s brothers will return<br>to the people of Israel.  
**5:4** He will stand and shepherd them<br>in the strength of the LORD,<br>in the majestic name of the LORD his God.<br>They will live securely,<br>for then his greatness will extend<br>to the ends of the earth.  
**5:5** He will be their peace.<br>When Assyria invades our land,<br>when it marches against our fortresses,<br>we will raise against it seven shepherds,<br>even eight leaders of men.  
**5:6** They will shepherd the land of Assyria with the sword,<br>the land of Nimrod with a drawn blade.<sup>①</sup><br>So he will rescue us from Assyria<br>when it invades our land,<br>when it marches against our territory.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Aq, Vg; MT, Sym read <i>Nimrod at its gateways</i>  
**5:7** Then the remnant of Jacob<br>will be among many peoples<br>like dew from the LORD,<br>like showers on the grass,<br>which do not wait for anyone<br>or linger for mankind.  
**5:8** Then the remnant of Jacob<br>will be among the nations, among many peoples,<br>like a lion among animals of the forest,<br>like a young lion among flocks of sheep,<br>which tramples and tears as it passes through,<br>and there is no one to rescue them.  
**5:9** Your hand will be lifted up against your adversaries,<br>and all your enemies will be destroyed.  
**5:10** In that day—this is the LORD’s declaration—I will remove your horses from you<br>and wreck your chariots.  
**5:11** I will remove the cities of your land<br>and tear down all your fortresses.  
**5:12** I will remove sorceries from your hands,<br>and you will not have any more fortune-tellers.  
**5:13** I will remove your carved images<br>and sacred pillars from you<br>so that you will no longer worship<br>the work of your hands.  
**5:14** I will pull up the Asherah poles from among you<br>and demolish your cities.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>shrines</i>  
**5:15** I will take vengeance in anger and wrath<br>against the nations that have not obeyed me.  

## 6장

**6:1** Now listen to what the LORD is saying:<br>Rise, plead your case before the mountains,<br>and let the hills hear your complaint.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>voice</i>  
**6:2** Listen to the LORD’s lawsuit,<br>you mountains and enduring foundations of the earth,<br>because the LORD has a case against his people,<br>and he will argue it against Israel.  
**6:3** My people, what have I done to you,<br>or how have I wearied you?<br>Testify against me!  
**6:4** Indeed, I brought you up from the land of Egypt<br>and redeemed you from that place of slavery.<br>I sent Moses, Aaron, and Miriam ahead of you.  
**6:5** My people,<br>remember what King Balak of Moab proposed,<br>what Balaam son of Beor answered him,<br>and what happened from the Acacia Grove<sup>①</sup> to Gilgal<br>so that you may acknowledge<br>the LORD’s righteous acts.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>from Shittim</i>  
**6:6** What should I bring before the LORD<br>when I come to bow before God on high?<br>Should I come before him with burnt offerings,<br>with year-old calves?  
**6:7** Would the LORD be pleased with thousands of rams<br>or with ten thousand streams of oil?<br>Should I give my firstborn for my transgression,<br>the offspring of my body for my own sin?  
**6:8** Mankind, he has told each of you what is good<br>and what it is the LORD requires of you:<br>to act justly,<br>to love faithfulness,<br>and to walk humbly with your God.  
**6:9** The voice of the LORD calls out to the city<sup>①</sup><br>(and it is wise to fear your name):<br>“Pay attention to the rod<br>and the one who ordained it.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>= Jerusalem <sup>②</sup>Or <i>attention, you tribe. Who has ordained it?;</i> Hb obscure  
**6:10** Are there still<sup>①</sup> the treasures of wickedness<br>and the accursed short measure<br>in the house of the wicked?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hb obscure  
**6:11** Can I excuse wicked scales<br>or bags of deceptive weights?  
**6:12** For the wealthy of the city are full of violence,<br>and its residents speak lies;<br>the tongues in their mouths are deceitful.  
**6:13** “As a result, I have begun to strike you severely,<sup>①</sup><br>bringing desolation because of your sins.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>LXX, Aq, Theod, Syr, Vg; MT reads <i>I have made you sick by striking you down</i>  
**6:14** You will eat but not be satisfied,<br>for there will be hunger within you.<br>What you acquire, you cannot save,<br>and what you do save,<br>I will give to the sword.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hb obscure  
**6:15** You will sow but not reap;<br>you will press olives<br>but not anoint yourself with oil;<br>and you will tread grapes<br>but not drink the wine.  
**6:16** The statutes of Omri<br>and all the practices of Ahab’s house<br>have been observed;<br>you have followed their policies.<br>Therefore, I will make you a desolate place<br>and the city’s<sup>①</sup> residents an object of contempt;<sup>②</sup><br>you will bear the scorn of my people.”<sup>③</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and its</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>residents a hissing</i> <sup>③</sup>LXX reads <i>of the peoples</i>  

## 7장

**7:1** How sad for me!<br>For I am like one who—<br>when the summer fruit has been gathered<br>after the gleaning of the grape harvest—<br>finds no grape cluster to eat,<br>no early fig, which I crave.  
**7:2** Faithful people have vanished from the land;<br>there is no one upright among the people.<br>All of them wait in ambush to shed blood;<br>they hunt each other with a net.  
**7:3** Both hands are good at accomplishing evil:<br>the official and the judge demand a bribe;<br>when the powerful man communicates his evil desire,<br>they plot it together.  
**7:4** The best of them is like a brier;<br>the most upright is worse than a hedge of thorns.<br>The day of your watchmen,<br>the day of your punishment, is coming;<br>at this time their panic is here.  
**7:5** Do not rely on a friend;<br>don’t trust in a close companion.<br>Seal your mouth<br>from the woman who lies in your arms.  
**7:6** Surely a son considers his father a fool,<br>a daughter opposes her mother,<br>and a daughter-in-law is against her mother-in-law;<br>a man’s enemies are the men of his own household.  
**7:7** But I will look to the LORD;<br>I will wait for the God of my salvation.<br>My God will hear me.  
**7:8** Do not rejoice over me, my enemy!<br>Though I have fallen, I will stand up;<br>though I sit in darkness,<br>the LORD will be my light.  
**7:9** Because I have sinned against him,<br>I must endure the LORD’s fury<br>until he champions my cause<br>and establishes justice for me.<br>He will bring me into the light;<br>I will see his salvation.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>righteousness</i>  
**7:10** Then my enemy will see,<br>and she will be covered with shame,<br>the one who said to me,<br>“Where is the LORD your God?”<br>My eyes will look at her in triumph;<br>at that time she will be trampled<br>like mud in the streets.  
**7:11** A day will come for rebuilding your walls;<br>on that day your boundary will be extended.  
**7:12** On that day people will come to you<br>from Assyria and the cities of Egypt,<br>even from Egypt to the Euphrates River<br>and from sea to sea<br>and mountain to mountain.  
**7:13** Then the earth will become a wasteland<br>because of its inhabitants<br>and as a result of their actions.  
**7:14** Shepherd your people with your staff,<br>the flock that is your possession.<br>They live alone in a woodland<br>surrounded by pastures.<br>Let them graze in Bashan and Gilead<br>as in ancient times.  
**7:15** I will perform miracles for them<sup>①</sup><br>as in the days of your exodus<br>from the land of Egypt.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= Israel  
**7:16** Nations will see and be ashamed<br>of<sup>①</sup> all their power.<br>They will put their hands over their mouths,<br>and their ears will become deaf.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>ashamed in spite of</i>  
**7:17** They will lick the dust like a snake;<br>they will come trembling out of their hiding places<br>like reptiles slithering on the ground.<br>They will tremble in the presence of the LORD our God;<br>they will stand in awe of you.  
**7:18** Who is a God like you,<br>forgiving iniquity and passing over rebellion<br>for the remnant of his inheritance?<br>He does not hold on to his anger forever<br>because he delights in faithful love.  
**7:19** He will again have compassion on us;<br>he will vanquish our iniquities.<br>You will cast all our<sup>①</sup> sins<br>into the depths of the sea.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some Hb mss, LXX, Syr, Vg; other Hb mss read <i>their</i>  
**7:20** You will show loyalty to Jacob<br>and faithful love to Abraham,<br>as you swore to our ancestors<br>from days long ago.
