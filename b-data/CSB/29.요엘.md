# 29.요엘 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Joel son of Pethuel:  
**1:2** Hear this, you elders;<br>listen, all you inhabitants of the land.<br>Has anything like this ever happened in your days<br>or in the days of your ancestors?  
**1:3** Tell your children about it,<br>and let your children tell their children,<br>and their children the next generation.  
**1:4** What the devouring locust has left,<br>the swarming locust has eaten;<br>what the swarming locust has left,<br>the young locust has eaten;<br>and what the young locust has left,<br>the destroying locust has eaten.  
**1:5** Wake up, you drunkards, and weep;<br>wail, all you wine drinkers,<br>because of the sweet wine,<br>for it has been taken from your mouth.  
**1:6** For a nation has invaded my land,<br>powerful and without number;<br>its teeth are the teeth of a lion,<br>and it has the fangs of a lioness.  
**1:7** It has devastated my grapevine<br>and splintered my fig tree.<br>It has stripped off its bark and thrown it away;<br>its branches have turned white.  
**1:8** Grieve like a young woman dressed in sackcloth,<br>mourning for the husband of her youth.  
**1:9** Grain and drink offerings have been cut off<br>from the house of the LORD;<br>the priests, who are ministers of the LORD, mourn.  
**1:10** The fields are destroyed;<br>the land grieves;<br>indeed, the grain is destroyed;<br>the new wine is dried up;<br>and the fresh oil fails.  
**1:11** Be ashamed, you farmers,<br>wail, you vinedressers,<sup>①</sup><br>over the wheat and the barley,<br>because the harvest of the field has perished.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>The farmers are dismayed, the vinedressers wail</i>  
**1:12** The grapevine is dried up,<br>and the fig tree is withered;<br>the pomegranate, the date palm, and the apple—<br>all the trees of the orchard—have withered.<br>Indeed, human joy has dried up.  
**1:13** Dress in sackcloth and lament, you priests;<br>wail, you ministers of the altar.<br>Come and spend the night in sackcloth,<br>you ministers of my God,<br>because grain and drink offerings<br>are withheld from the house of your God.  
**1:14** Announce a sacred fast;<br>proclaim a solemn assembly!<br>Gather the elders<br>and all the residents of the land<br>at the house of the LORD your God,<br>and cry out to the LORD.  
**1:15** Woe because of that day!<br>For the day of the LORD is near<br>and will come as devastation from the Almighty.  
**1:16** Hasn’t the food been cut off<br>before our eyes,<br>joy and gladness<br>from the house of our God?  
**1:17** The seeds lie shriveled in their casings.<sup>①</sup><br>The storehouses are in ruin,<br>and the granaries are broken down,<br>because the grain has withered away.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>clods;</i> Hb obscure  
**1:18** How the animals groan!<br>The herds of cattle wander in confusion<br>since they have no pasture.<br>Even the flocks of sheep and goats suffer punishment.  
**1:19** I call to you, LORD,<br>for fire has consumed<br>the pastures of the wilderness,<br>and flames have devoured<br>all the trees of the orchard.  
**1:20** Even the wild animals cry out to<sup>①</sup> you,<br>for the river beds are dried up,<br>and fire has consumed<br>the pastures of the wilderness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>animals pant for;</i> Hb obscure  

## 2장

**2:1** Blow the ram’s horn in Zion;<br>sound the alarm on my holy mountain!<br>Let all the residents of the land tremble,<br>for the day of the LORD is coming;<br>in fact, it is near—  
**2:2** a day of darkness and gloom,<br>a day of clouds and total darkness,<br>like the dawn spreading over the mountains;<br>a great and strong people appears,<br>such as never existed in ages past<br>and never will again<br>in all the generations to come.  
**2:3** A fire devours in front of them,<br>and behind them a flame blazes.<br>The land in front of them<br>is like the garden of Eden,<br>but behind them,<br>it is like a desert wasteland;<br>there is no escape from them.  
**2:4** Their appearance is like that of horses,<br>and they gallop like war horses.  
**2:5** They bound on the tops of the mountains.<br>Their sound is like the sound of chariots,<br>like the sound of fiery flames consuming stubble,<br>like a mighty army deployed for war.  
**2:6** Nations writhe in horror before them;<br>all faces turn pale.  
**2:7** They attack as warriors attack;<br>they scale walls as men of war do.<br>Each goes on his own path,<br>and they do not change their course.  
**2:8** They do not push each other;<br>each proceeds on his own path.<br>They dodge the arrows, never stopping.  
**2:9** They storm the city;<br>they run on the wall;<br>they climb into the houses;<br>they enter through the windows like thieves.  
**2:10** The earth quakes before them;<br>the sky shakes.<br>The sun and moon grow dark,<br>and the stars cease their shining.  
**2:11** The LORD makes his voice heard<br>in the presence of his army.<br>His camp is very large;<br>those who carry out his command are powerful.<br>Indeed, the day of the LORD is terrible and dreadful—<br>who can endure it?  
**2:12** Even now—this is the LORD’s declaration—turn to me with all your heart,<br>with fasting, weeping, and mourning.  
**2:13** Tear your hearts,<br>not just your clothes,<br>and return to the LORD your God.<br>For he is gracious and compassionate,<br>slow to anger, abounding in faithful love,<br>and he relents from sending disaster.  
**2:14** Who knows? He may turn and relent<br>and leave a blessing behind him,<br>so you can offer a grain offering and a drink offering<br>to the LORD your God.  
**2:15** Blow the ram’s horn in Zion!<br>Announce a sacred fast;<br>proclaim a solemn assembly.  
**2:16** Gather the people;<br>sanctify the congregation;<br>assemble the aged;<sup>①</sup><br>gather the infants,<br>even babies nursing at the breast.<br>Let the groom leave his bedroom,<br>and the bride her honeymoon chamber.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>elders</i>  
**2:17** Let the priests, the LORD’s ministers,<br>weep between the portico and the altar.<br>Let them say,<br>“Have pity on your people, LORD,<br>and do not make your inheritance a disgrace,<br>an object of scorn among the nations.<br>Why should it be said among the peoples,<br>‘Where is their God?’”  
**2:18** Then the LORD became jealous for his land and spared his people.  
**2:19** The LORD answered his people:<br>Look, I am about to send you<br>grain, new wine, and fresh oil.<br>You will be satiated with them,<br>and I will no longer make you<br>a disgrace among the nations.  
**2:20** I will drive the northerner far from you<br>and banish him to a dry and desolate land,<br>his front ranks into the Dead Sea,<br>and his rear guard into the Mediterranean Sea.<br>His stench will rise;<br>yes, his rotten smell will rise,<br>for he has done astonishing things.  
**2:21** Don’t be afraid, land;<br>rejoice and be glad,<br>for the LORD has done astonishing things.  
**2:22** Don’t be afraid, wild animals,<br>for the wilderness pastures have turned green,<br>the trees bear their fruit,<br>and the fig tree and grapevine yield their riches.  
**2:23** Children of Zion, rejoice and be glad<br>in the LORD your God,<br>because he gives you the autumn rain<sup>①</sup><br>for your vindication.<sup>②</sup><br>He sends showers for you,<br>both autumn and spring rain as before.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the teacher of righteousness</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>righteousness</i>  
**2:24** The threshing floors will be full of grain,<br>and the vats will overflow<br>with new wine and fresh oil.  
**2:25** I will repay you for the years<br>that the swarming locust ate,<br>the young locust, the destroying locust,<br>and the devouring locust—<br>my great army that I sent against you.  
**2:26** You will have plenty to eat and be satisfied.<br>You will praise the name of the LORD your God,<br>who has dealt wondrously with you.<br>My people will never again be put to shame.  
**2:27** You will know that I am present in Israel<br>and that I am the LORD your God,<br>and there is no other.<br>My people will never again be put to shame.  
**2:28** After this<br>I will pour out my Spirit on all humanity;<br>then your sons and your daughters will prophesy,<br>your old men will have dreams,<br>and your young men will see visions.  
**2:29** I will even pour out my Spirit<br>on the male and female slaves in those days.  
**2:30** I will display wonders<br>in the heavens and on the earth:<br>blood, fire, and columns of smoke.  
**2:31** The sun will be turned to darkness<br>and the moon to blood<br>before the great and terrible day of the LORD comes.  
**2:32** Then everyone who calls<br>on the name of the LORD will be saved,<br>for there will be an escape<br>for those on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem,<br>as the LORD promised,<br>among the survivors the LORD calls.  

## 3장

**3:1** Yes, in those days and at that time,<br>when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem,  
**3:2** I will gather all the nations<br>and take them to the Valley of Jehoshaphat.<sup>①</sup><br>I will enter into judgment with them there<br>because of my people, my inheritance Israel.<br>The nations have scattered the Israelites<br>in foreign countries<br>and divided up my land.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= The LORD Will Judge  
**3:3** They cast lots for my people;<br>they bartered a boy for a prostitute<br>and sold a girl for wine to drink.  
**3:4** And also: Tyre, Sidon, and all the territories of Philistia—what are you to me? Are you paying me back or trying to get even with me? I will quickly bring retribution on your heads.  
**3:5** For you took my silver and gold and carried my finest treasures to your temples.  
**3:6** You sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks to remove them far from their own territory.  
**3:7** Look, I am about to rouse them up from the place where you sold them; I will bring retribution on your heads.  
**3:8** I will sell your sons and daughters to the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans,<sup>①</sup> to a distant nation, for the LORD has spoken.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Probably the south Arabian kingdom of Sheba (modern Yemen)  
**3:9** Proclaim this among the nations:<br>Prepare for holy war;<br>rouse the warriors;<br>let all the men of war advance and attack!  
**3:10** Beat your plows into swords<br>and your pruning knives into spears.<br>Let even the weakling say, “I am a warrior.”  
**3:11** Come quickly,<sup>①</sup> all you surrounding nations;<br>gather yourselves.<br>Bring down your warriors there, LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>LXX, Syr, Tg read <i>Gather yourselves and come;</i> Hb obscure  
**3:12** Let the nations be roused<br>and come to the Valley of Jehoshaphat,<br>for there I will sit down<br>to judge all the surrounding nations.  
**3:13** Swing the sickle<br>because the harvest is ripe.<br>Come and trample the grapes<br>because the winepress is full;<br>the wine vats overflow<br>because the wickedness of the nations is extreme.  
**3:14** Multitudes, multitudes<br>in the valley of decision!<br>For the day of the LORD is near<br>in the valley of decision.  
**3:15** The sun and moon will grow dark,<br>and the stars will cease their shining.  
**3:16** The LORD will roar from Zion<br>and make his voice heard from Jerusalem;<br>heaven and earth will shake.<br>But the LORD will be a refuge for his people,<br>a stronghold for the Israelites.  
**3:17** Then you will know<br>that I am the LORD your God,<br>who dwells in Zion, my holy mountain.<br>Jerusalem will be holy,<br>and foreigners will never overrun it again.  
**3:18** In that day<br>the mountains will drip with sweet wine,<br>and the hills will flow with milk.<br>All the streams of Judah will flow with water,<br>and a spring will issue from the LORD’s house,<br>watering the Valley of Acacias.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Shittim</i>  
**3:19** Egypt will become desolate,<br>and Edom a desert wasteland,<br>because of the violence done to the people of Judah<br>in whose land they shed innocent blood.  
**3:20** But Judah will be inhabited forever,<br>and Jerusalem from generation to generation.  
**3:21** I will pardon their bloodguilt,<sup>①</sup><br>which I have not pardoned,<br>for the LORD dwells in Zion.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>LXX, Syr read <i>I will avenge their blood</i>
