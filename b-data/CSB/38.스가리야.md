# 38.스가리야 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** In the eighth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to the prophet Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo:  
**1:2** “The LORD was extremely angry with your ancestors.  
**1:3** So tell the people, ‘This is what the LORD of Armies says: Return to me—this is the declaration of the LORD of Armies—and I will return to you, says the LORD of Armies.  
**1:4** Do not be like your ancestors; the earlier prophets proclaimed to them: This is what the LORD of Armies says: Turn from your evil ways and your evil deeds. But they did not listen or pay attention to me—this is the LORD’s declaration.  
**1:5** Where are your ancestors now? And do the prophets live forever?  
**1:6** But didn’t my words and my statutes that I commanded my servants the prophets overtake your ancestors? ’” So the people repented and said, “As the LORD of Armies decided to deal with us for our ways and our deeds, so he has dealt with us.”  
**1:7** On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, which is the month of Shebat, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to the prophet Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo:  
**1:8** I looked out in the night and saw a man riding on a chestnut<sup>①</sup> horse. He was standing among the myrtle trees in the valley.<sup>②</sup> Behind him were chestnut, brown, and white horses.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>red</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>depths</i>  
**1:9** I asked, “What are these, my lord?” The angel who was talking to me replied, “I will show you what they are.”  
**1:10** Then the man standing among the myrtle trees explained, “They are the ones the LORD has sent to patrol the earth.”  
**1:11** They reported to the angel of the LORD standing among the myrtle trees, “We have patrolled the earth, and right now the whole earth is calm and quiet.”  
**1:12** Then the angel of the LORD responded, “How long, LORD of Armies, will you withhold mercy from Jerusalem and the cities of Judah that you have been angry with these seventy years?”  
**1:13** The LORD replied with kind and comforting words to the angel who was speaking with me.  
**1:14** So the angel who was speaking with me said, “Proclaim: The LORD of Armies says: I am extremely jealous for Jerusalem and Zion.  
**1:15** I am fiercely angry with the nations that are at ease, for I was a little angry, but they made the destruction worse.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>they helped for evil</i>  
**1:16** Therefore, this is what the LORD says: In mercy, I have returned to Jerusalem; my house will be rebuilt within it—this is the declaration of the LORD of Armies—and a measuring line will be stretched out over Jerusalem.  
**1:17** “Proclaim further: This is what the LORD of Armies says: My cities will again overflow with prosperity; the LORD will once more comfort Zion and again choose Jerusalem.”  
**1:18** Then I looked up and saw four horns.  
**1:19** So I asked the angel who was speaking with me, “What are these?” And he said to me, “These are the horns that scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”  
**1:20** Then the LORD showed me four craftsmen.  
**1:21** I asked, “What are they coming to do?” He replied, “These are the horns that scattered Judah so no one could raise his head. These craftsmen have come to terrify them, to cut off<sup>①</sup> the horns of the nations that raised a horn against the land of Judah to scatter it.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>throw down</i>  

## 2장

**2:1** I looked up and saw a man with a measuring line in his hand.  
**2:2** I asked, “Where are you going?” He answered me, “To measure Jerusalem to determine its width and length.”  
**2:3** Then the angel who was speaking with me went out, and another angel went out to meet him.  
**2:4** He said to him, “Run and tell this young man: Jerusalem will be inhabited without walls because of the number of people and animals in it.”  
**2:5** The declaration of the LORD: “I myself will be a wall of fire around it, and I will be the glory within it.”  
**2:6** “Listen! Listen! Flee from the land of the north”—this is the LORD’s declaration—“for I have scattered you like the four winds of heaven”—this is the LORD’s declaration.  
**2:7** “Listen, Zion! Escape, you who are living with Daughter Babylon.”  
**2:8** For the LORD of Armies says this: “In pursuit of his glory, he sent me against the nations plundering you, for whoever touches you touches the pupil<sup>①</sup> of my<sup>②</sup> eye.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>apple</i> <sup>②</sup>Alt Hb tradition; MT reads <i>his</i>  
**2:9** For look, I am raising my hand against them, and they will become plunder for their own servants. Then you will know that the LORD of Armies has sent me.  
**2:10** “Daughter Zion, shout for joy and be glad, for I am coming to dwell among you”—this is the LORD’s declaration.  
**2:11** “Many nations will join themselves to the LORD on that day and become my<sup>①</sup> people. I will dwell among you, and you will know that the LORD of Armies has sent me to you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>LXX, Syr read <i>his</i>  
**2:12** The LORD will take possession of Judah as his portion in the Holy Land, and he will once again choose Jerusalem.  
**2:13** Let all humanity be silent before the LORD, for from his holy dwelling he has roused himself.”  

## 3장

**3:1** Then he showed me the high priest Joshua standing before the angel of the LORD, with Satan<sup>①</sup> standing at his right side to accuse him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the accuser</i>  
**3:2** The LORD<sup>①</sup> said to Satan, “The LORD rebuke you, Satan! May the LORD who has chosen Jerusalem rebuke you! Isn’t this man a burning stick snatched from the fire?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Syr reads <i>The Angel of the LORD</i>  
**3:3** Now Joshua was dressed with filthy<sup>①</sup> clothes as he stood before the angel.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Probably stained with human excrement  
**3:4** So the angel of the LORD<sup>①</sup> spoke to those<sup>②</sup> standing before him, “Take off his filthy clothes!” Then he said to him, “See, I have removed your iniquity from you, and I will clothe you with festive robes.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>he</i> <sup>②</sup>= the angels  
**3:5** Then I said, “Let them put a clean turban on his head.” So a clean turban was placed on his head, and they clothed him in garments while the angel of the LORD was standing nearby.  
**3:6** Then the angel of the LORD charged Joshua,  
**3:7** “This is what the LORD of Armies says: If you walk in my ways and keep my mandates, you will both rule my house and take care of my courts; I will also grant you access among these who are standing here.  
**3:8** “Listen, High Priest Joshua, you and your colleagues sitting before you; indeed, these men are a sign that I am about to bring my servant, the Branch.  
**3:9** Notice the stone I have set before Joshua; on that one stone are seven eyes. I will engrave an inscription on it”—this is the declaration of the LORD of Armies—“and I will take away the iniquity of this land in a single day.  
**3:10** On that day, each of you will invite his neighbor to sit under his vine and fig tree.” This is the declaration of the LORD of Armies.  

## 4장

**4:1** The angel who was speaking with me then returned and roused me as one awakened out of sleep.  
**4:2** He asked me, “What do you see?” I replied, “I see a solid gold lampstand with a bowl at the top. The lampstand also has seven lamps at the top with seven spouts for each of<sup>①</sup> the lamps.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>seven lips to</i>  
**4:3** There are also two olive trees beside it, one on the right of the bowl and the other on its left.”  
**4:4** Then I asked the angel who was speaking with me, “What are these, my lord?”  
**4:5** “Don’t you know what they are?” replied the angel who was speaking with me. I said, “No, my lord.”  
**4:6** So he answered me, “This is the word of the LORD to Zerubbabel: ‘Not by strength or by might, but by my Spirit,’ says the LORD of Armies.  
**4:7** ‘What are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel you will become a plain. And he will bring out the capstone accompanied by shouts of: Grace, grace to it! ’”  
**4:8** Then the word of the LORD came to me:  
**4:9** “Zerubbabel’s hands have laid the foundation of this house, and his hands will complete it. Then you will know that the LORD of Armies has sent me to you.  
**4:10** For who despises the day of small things? These seven eyes of the LORD, which scan throughout the whole earth, will rejoice when they see the ceremonial stone<sup>①</sup> in Zerubbabel’s hand.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the tin stone</i>  
**4:11** I asked him, “What are the two olive trees on the right and left of the lampstand?”  
**4:12** And I questioned him further, “What are the two streams<sup>①</sup> of the olive trees, from which the golden oil is pouring through the two golden conduits?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>branches</i>  
**4:13** Then he inquired of me, “Don’t you know what these are?” “No, my lord,” I replied.  
**4:14** “These are the two anointed ones,”<sup>①</sup> he said, “who stand by the Lord of the whole earth.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= Joshua and Zerubbabel  

## 5장

**5:1** I looked up again and saw a flying scroll.  
**5:2** “What do you see?” he asked me. “I see a flying scroll,” I replied, “thirty feet<sup>①</sup> long and fifteen feet<sup>②</sup> wide.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>20 cubits</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>10 cubits</i>  
**5:3** Then he said to me, “This is the curse that is going out over the whole land, for everyone who is a thief, contrary to what is written on one side, has gone unpunished,<sup>①</sup> and everyone who swears falsely, contrary to what is written on the other side, has gone unpunished.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>side, will be removed</i>  
**5:4** I will send it out,”—this is the declaration of the LORD of Armies—“and it will enter the house of the thief and the house of the one who swears falsely by my name. It will stay inside his house and destroy it along with its timbers and stones.”  
**5:5** Then the angel who was speaking with me came forward and told me, “Look up and see what this is that is approaching.”  
**5:6** So I asked, “What is it?” He responded, “It’s a measuring basket<sup>①</sup> that is approaching.” And he continued, “This is their iniquity<sup>②</sup> in all the land.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>It’s an ephah</i> <sup>②</sup>One Hb ms, LXX, Syr; other Hb mss read <i>eye</i>  
**5:7** Then a lead cover was lifted, and there was a woman sitting inside the basket.  
**5:8** “This is Wickedness,” he said. He shoved her down into the basket and pushed the lead weight over its opening.  
**5:9** Then I looked up and saw two women approaching with the wind in their wings. Their wings were like those of a stork, and they lifted up the basket between earth and sky.  
**5:10** So I asked the angel who was speaking with me, “Where are they taking the basket?”  
**5:11** “To build a shrine for it in the land of Shinar,” he told me. “When that is ready, the basket will be placed there on its pedestal.”  

## 6장

**6:1** Then I looked up again and saw four chariots coming from between two mountains. The mountains were made of bronze.  
**6:2** The first chariot had chestnut<sup>①</sup> horses, the second chariot black horses,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>red</i>  
**6:3** the third chariot white horses, and the fourth chariot dappled horses—all strong horses.  
**6:4** So I inquired of the angel who was speaking with me, “What are these, my lord?”  
**6:5** The angel told me, “These are the four spirits<sup>①</sup> of heaven going out after presenting themselves to the Lord of the whole earth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>winds</i>  
**6:6** The one with the black horses is going to the land of the north, the white horses are going after them, but the dappled horses are going to the land of the south.”  
**6:7** As the strong horses went out, they wanted to go patrol the earth, and the LORD said, “Go, patrol the earth.” So they patrolled the earth.  
**6:8** Then he summoned me saying, “See, those going to the land of the north have pacified my Spirit in the northern land.”  
**6:9** The word of the LORD came to me:  
**6:10** “Take an offering from the exiles, from Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah, who have arrived from Babylon, and go that same day to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah.  
**6:11** Take silver and gold, make a crown,<sup>①</sup> and place it on the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>crowns</i>  
**6:12** You are to tell him: This is what the LORD of Armies says: Here is a man whose name is Branch; he will branch out from his place and build the LORD’s temple.  
**6:13** Yes, he will build the LORD’s temple; he will bear royal splendor and will sit on his throne and rule. There will be a priest on his throne, and there will be peaceful counsel between the two of them.  
**6:14** The crown will reside in the LORD’s temple as a memorial to Heldai, Tobijah, Jedaiah, and Hen<sup>①</sup> son of Zephaniah.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Probably Josiah  
**6:15** People who are far off will come and build the LORD’s temple, and you will know that the LORD of Armies has sent me to you. This will happen when you fully obey the LORD your God.”  

## 7장

**7:1** In the fourth year of King Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, which is Chislev.  
**7:2** Now the people of Bethel had sent Sharezer, Regem-melech, and their men to plead for the LORD’s favor  
**7:3** by asking the priests who were at the house of the LORD of Armies as well as the prophets, “Should we mourn and fast in the fifth month as we have done these many years?”  
**7:4** Then the word of the LORD of Armies came to me:  
**7:5** “Ask all the people of the land and the priests: When you fasted and lamented in the fifth and in the seventh months for these seventy years, did you really fast for me?  
**7:6** When you eat and drink, don’t you eat and drink simply for yourselves?  
**7:7** Aren’t these the words that the LORD proclaimed through the earlier prophets when Jerusalem was inhabited and secure,<sup>①</sup> along with its surrounding cities, and when the southern region and the Judean foothills were inhabited?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>prosperous</i>  
**7:8** The word of the LORD came to Zechariah:  
**7:9** “The LORD of Armies says this: ‘Make fair decisions. Show faithful love and compassion to one another.  
**7:10** Do not oppress the widow or the fatherless, the resident alien or the poor, and do not plot evil in your hearts against one another.’  
**7:11** But they refused to pay attention and turned a stubborn shoulder; they closed their ears so they could not hear.  
**7:12** They made their hearts like a rock so as not to obey the law or the words that the LORD of Armies had sent by his Spirit through the earlier prophets. Therefore intense anger came from the LORD of Armies.  
**7:13** Just as he had called, and they would not listen, so when they called, I would not listen, says the LORD of Armies.  
**7:14** I scattered them with a windstorm over all the nations that had not known them, and the land was left desolate behind them, with no one coming or going. They turned a pleasant land into a desolation.”  

## 8장

**8:1** The word of the LORD of Armies came:  
**8:2** The LORD of Armies says this: “I am extremely jealous for Zion; I am jealous for her with great wrath.”  
**8:3** The LORD says this: “I will return to Zion and live in Jerusalem. Then Jerusalem will be called the Faithful City; the mountain of the LORD of Armies will be called the Holy Mountain.”  
**8:4** The LORD of Armies says this: “Old men and women will again sit along the streets of Jerusalem, each with a staff in hand because of advanced age.  
**8:5** The streets of the city will be filled with boys and girls playing in them.”  
**8:6** The LORD of Armies says this: “Though it may seem impossible to the remnant of this people in those days, should it also seem impossible to me?”—this is the declaration of the LORD of Armies.  
**8:7** The LORD of Armies says this: “I will save my people from the land of the east and the land of the west.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>sunset</i>  
**8:8** I will bring them back to live in Jerusalem. They will be my people, and I will be their faithful and righteous God.”  
**8:9** The LORD of Armies says this: “Let your hands be strong, you who now hear these words that the prophets spoke when the foundations were laid for the rebuilding of the temple, the house of the LORD of Armies.  
**8:10** For prior to those days neither people nor animals had wages. There was no safety from the enemy for anyone who came or went, for I turned everyone against his neighbor.  
**8:11** But now, I will not treat the remnant of this people as in the former days”—this is the declaration of the LORD of Armies.  
**8:12** “For they will sow in peace: the vine will yield its fruit, the land will yield its produce, and the skies will yield their dew. I will give the remnant of this people all these things as an inheritance.  
**8:13** As you have been a curse among the nations, house of Judah and house of Israel, so I will save you, and you will be a blessing. Don’t be afraid; let your hands be strong.”  
**8:14** For the LORD of Armies says this: “As I resolved to treat you badly when your ancestors provoked me to anger, and I did not relent,” says the LORD of Armies,  
**8:15** “so I have resolved again in these days to do what is good to Jerusalem and the house of Judah. Don’t be afraid.  
**8:16** These are the things you must do: Speak truth to one another; make true and sound decisions within your city gates.  
**8:17** Do not plot evil in your hearts against your neighbor, and do not love perjury, for I hate all this”—this is the LORD’s declaration.  
**8:18** Then the word of the LORD of Armies came to me:  
**8:19** The LORD of Armies says this: “The fast of the fourth month, the fast of the fifth, the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth will become times of joy, gladness, and cheerful festivals for the house of Judah. Therefore, love truth and peace.”  
**8:20** The LORD of Armies says this: “Peoples will yet come, the residents of many cities;  
**8:21** the residents of one city will go to another, saying: Let’s go at once to plead for the LORD’s favor and to seek the LORD of Armies. I am also going.  
**8:22** Many peoples and strong nations will come to seek the LORD of Armies in Jerusalem and to plead for the LORD’s favor.”  
**8:23** The LORD of Armies says this: “In those days, ten men from nations of every language will grab the robe of a Jewish man tightly, urging: Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you.”

## 11장

**11:1** Open your gates, Lebanon,<br>and fire will consume your cedars.  
**11:2** Wail, cypress, for the cedar has fallen;<br>the glorious trees are destroyed!<br>Wail, oaks of Bashan,<br>for the stately forest has fallen!  
**11:3** Listen to the wail of the shepherds,<br>for their glory is destroyed.<br>Listen to the roar of young lions,<br>for the thickets of the Jordan are<sup>①</sup> destroyed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>for the majesty of the Jordan is</i>  
**11:4** The LORD my God says this: “Shepherd the flock intended for slaughter.  
**11:5** Those who buy them slaughter them but are not punished. Those who sell them say, ‘Blessed be the LORD because I have become rich!’ Even their own shepherds have no compassion for them.  
**11:6** Indeed, I will no longer have compassion on the inhabitants of the land”—this is the LORD’s declaration. “Instead, I will turn everyone over to his neighbor and his king. They will devastate the land, and I will not rescue it from their hand.”  
**11:7** So I shepherded the flock intended for slaughter, the oppressed of the flock.<sup>①</sup> I took two staffs, calling one Favor and the other Union, and I shepherded the flock.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>LXX reads <i>slaughter that belonged to the sheep merchants</i>  
**11:8** In one month I got rid of three shepherds. I became impatient with them, and they also detested me.  
**11:9** Then I said, “I will no longer shepherd you. Let what is dying die, and let what is perishing perish; let the rest devour each other’s flesh.”  
**11:10** Next I took my staff called Favor and cut it in two, annulling the covenant I had made with all the peoples.  
**11:11** It was annulled on that day, and so the oppressed of the flock<sup>①</sup> who were watching me knew that it was the word of the LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>LXX reads <i>and the sheep merchants</i>
