## 2장

**2:1** After Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of King Herod, wise men from the east arrived in Jerusalem,  
**2:2** saying, “Where is he who has been born king of the Jews? For we saw his star at its rising and have come to worship him.”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>to pay him homage</i>

**2:4** So he assembled all the chief priests and scribes of the people and asked them where the Messiah would be born.  
**2:5** “In Bethlehem of Judea,” they told him, “because this is what was written by the prophet:  
**2:6** <b>And you, Bethlehem,</b> in the land of Judah,<br>are by no means <b>least among the rulers of Judah:</b><br><b>Because out of you will come a ruler</b><br><b>who will shepherd my people Israel.</b>”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Mc <a name='B:400 5:2'>5:2

**2:9** After hearing the king, they went on their way. And there it was—the star they had seen at its rising. It led them until it came and stopped above the place where the child was.  
**2:10** When they saw the star, they were overwhelmed with joy.

**2:14** So he got up, took the child and his mother during the night, and escaped to Egypt.  
**2:15** He stayed there until Herod’s death, so that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled: <b>Out of Egypt I called my Son.</b><sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hs <a name='B:350 11:1'>11:1

**2:20** saying, “Get up, take the child and his mother, and go to the land of Israel, because those who intended to kill the child are dead.”  
**2:21** So he got up, took the child and his mother, and entered the land of Israel.  
**2:22** But when he heard that Archelaus was ruling over Judea in place of his father Herod, he was afraid to go there. And being warned in a dream, he withdrew to the region of Galilee.  
**2:23** Then he went and settled in a town called Nazareth to fulfill what was spoken through the prophets, that he would be called a Nazarene.

**3:11** “I baptize you with<sup>①</sup> water for repentance, but the one who is coming after me is more powerful than I. I am not worthy to remove<sup>②</sup> his sandals. He himself will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>to carry</i>

**3:14** But John tried to stop him, saying, “I need to be baptized by you, and yet you come to me?”  
**3:15** Jesus answered him, “Allow it for now, because this is the way for us to fulfill all righteousness.” Then John allowed him to be baptized.
