# 6.여호수아 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** After the death of Moses the LORD’s servant, the LORD spoke to Joshua son of Nun, Moses’s assistant:  
**1:2** “Moses my servant is dead. Now you and all the people prepare to cross over the Jordan to the land I am giving the Israelites.  
**1:3** I have given you every place where the sole of your foot treads, just as I promised Moses.  
**1:4** Your territory will be from the wilderness and Lebanon to the great river, the Euphrates River—all the land of the Hittites—and west to the Mediterranean Sea.  
**1:5** No one will be able to stand against you as long as you live. I will be with you, just as I was with Moses. I will not leave you or abandon you.  
**1:6** “Be strong and courageous, for you will distribute the land I swore to their ancestors to give them as an inheritance.  
**1:7** Above all, be strong and very courageous to observe carefully the whole instruction my servant Moses commanded you. Do not turn from it to the right or the left, so that you will have success wherever you go.  
**1:8** This book of instruction must not depart from your mouth; you are to meditate on<sup>①</sup> it day and night so that you may carefully observe everything written in it. For then you will prosper and succeed in whatever you do.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>to recite</i>  
**1:9** Haven’t I commanded you: be strong and courageous? Do not be afraid or discouraged, for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go.”  
**1:10** Then Joshua commanded the officers of the people,  
**1:11** “Go through the camp and tell the people, ‘Get provisions ready for yourselves, for within three days you will be crossing the Jordan to go in and take possession of the land the LORD your God is giving you to inherit.’”  
**1:12** Joshua said to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh,  
**1:13** “Remember what Moses the LORD’s servant commanded you when he said, ‘The LORD your God will give you rest, and he will give you this land.’  
**1:14** Your wives, dependents, and livestock may remain in the land Moses gave you on this side of the Jordan. But your best soldiers must cross over in battle formation<sup>①</sup> ahead of your brothers and help them<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>over armed</i>  
**1:15** until the LORD gives your brothers rest, as he has given you, and they too possess the land the LORD your God is giving them. You may then return to the land of your inheritance and take possession of what Moses the LORD’s servant gave you on the east side of the Jordan.”  
**1:16** They answered Joshua, “Everything you have commanded us we will do, and everywhere you send us we will go.  
**1:17** We will obey you, just as we obeyed Moses in everything. Certainly the LORD your God will be with you, as he was with Moses.  
**1:18** Anyone who rebels against your order and does not obey your words in all that you command him, will be put to death. Above all, be strong and courageous!”  

## 2장

**2:1** Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two men as spies from the Acacia Grove,<sup>①</sup> saying, “Go and scout the land, especially Jericho.” So they left, and they came to the house of a prostitute named Rahab, and stayed there.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>from Shittim</i>  
**2:2** The king of Jericho was told, “Look, some of the Israelite men have come here tonight to investigate the land.”  
**2:3** Then the king of Jericho sent word to Rahab and said, “Bring out the men who came to you and entered your house, for they came to investigate the entire land.”  
**2:4** But the woman had taken the two men and hidden them. So she said, “Yes, the men did come to me, but I didn’t know where they were from.  
**2:5** At nightfall, when the city gate was about to close, the men went out, and I don’t know where they were going. Chase after them quickly, and you can catch up with them!”  
**2:6** But she had taken them up to the roof and hidden them among the stalks of flax that she had arranged on the roof.  
**2:7** The men pursued them along the road to the fords of the Jordan, and as soon as they left to pursue them, the city gate was shut.  
**2:8** Before the men fell asleep, she went up on the roof  
**2:9** and said to them, “I know that the LORD has given you this land and that the terror of you has fallen on us, and everyone who lives in the land is panicking because of you.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>land panics at your approach</i>  
**2:10** For we have heard how the LORD dried up the water of the Red Sea before you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to Sihon and Og, the two Amorite kings you completely destroyed across the Jordan.  
**2:11** When we heard this, we lost heart, and everyone’s courage failed<sup>①</sup> because of you, for the LORD your God is God in heaven above and on earth below.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and spirit no longer remained in anyone</i>  
**2:12** Now please swear to me by the LORD that you will also show kindness to my father’s family, because I showed kindness to you. Give me a sure sign<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>a sign of truth</i>  
**2:13** that you will spare the lives of my father, mother, brothers, sisters, and all who belong to them, and save us from death.”  
**2:14** The men answered her, “We will give our lives for yours. If you don’t report our mission, we will show kindness and faithfulness to you when the LORD gives us the land.”  
**2:15** Then she let them down by a rope through the window, since she lived in a house that was built into the wall of the city.  
**2:16** “Go to the hill country so that the men pursuing you won’t find you,” she said to them. “Hide there for three days until they return; afterward, go on your way.”  
**2:17** The men said to her, “We will be free from this oath you made us swear,  
**2:18** unless, when we enter the land, you tie this scarlet cord to the window through which you let us down. Bring your father, mother, brothers, and all your father’s family into your house.  
**2:19** If anyone goes out the doors of your house, his death will be his own fault, and we will be innocent. But if anyone with you in the house should be harmed, his death will be our fault.  
**2:20** And if you report our mission, we are free from the oath you made us swear.”  
**2:21** “Let it be as you say,” she replied, and she sent them away. After they had gone, she tied the scarlet cord to the window.  
**2:22** So the two men went into the hill country and stayed there three days until the pursuers had returned. They searched all along the way, but did not find them.  
**2:23** Then the men returned, came down from the hill country, and crossed the Jordan. They went to Joshua son of Nun and reported everything that had happened to them.  
**2:24** They told Joshua, “The LORD has handed over the entire land to us. Everyone who lives in the land is also panicking because of us.”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>land also panics at our approach</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** Joshua started early the next morning and left the Acacia Grove<sup>①</sup> with all the Israelites. They went as far as the Jordan and stayed there before crossing.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>left Shittim</i>  
**3:2** After three days the officers went through the camp  
**3:3** and commanded the people, “When you see the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God carried by the Levitical priests, you are to break camp and follow it.  
**3:4** But keep a distance of about a thousand yards<sup>①</sup> between yourselves and the ark. Don’t go near it, so that you can see the way to go, for you haven’t traveled this way before.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>2,000 cubits</i>  
**3:5** Joshua told the people, “Consecrate yourselves, because the LORD will do wonders among you tomorrow.”  
**3:6** Then he said to the priests, “Carry the ark of the covenant and go on ahead of the people.” So they carried the ark of the covenant and went ahead of them.  
**3:7** The LORD spoke to Joshua: “Today I will begin to exalt you in the sight of all Israel, so they will know that I will be with you just as I was with Moses.  
**3:8** Command the priests carrying the ark of the covenant: When you reach the edge of the water,<sup>①</sup> stand in the Jordan.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>waters of the Jordan</i>  
**3:9** Then Joshua told the Israelites, “Come closer and listen to the words of the LORD your God.”  
**3:10** He said, “You will know that the living God is among you and that he will certainly dispossess before you the Canaanites, Hethites, Hivites, Perizzites, Girgashites, Amorites, and Jebusites  
**3:11** when the ark of the covenant of the Lord of the whole earth goes ahead of you into the Jordan.  
**3:12** Now choose twelve men from the tribes of Israel, one man for each tribe.  
**3:13** When the feet<sup>①</sup> of the priests who carry the ark of the LORD, the Lord of the whole earth, come to rest in the Jordan’s water, its water will be cut off. The water flowing downstream will stand up in a mass.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>soles of the feet</i>  
**3:14** When the people broke camp to cross the Jordan, the priests carried the ark of the covenant ahead of the people.  
**3:15** Now the Jordan overflows its banks throughout the harvest season. But as soon as the priests carrying the ark reached the Jordan, their feet touched the water at its edge  
**3:16** and the water flowing downstream stood still, rising up in a mass that extended as far as<sup>①</sup> Adam, a city next to Zarethan. The water flowing downstream into the Sea of the Arabah—the Dead Sea—was completely cut off, and the people crossed opposite Jericho.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Alt Hb tradition reads <i>mass at</i>  
**3:17** The priests carrying the ark of the LORD’s covenant stood firmly on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan, while all Israel crossed on dry ground until the entire nation had finished crossing the Jordan.  

## 4장

**4:1** After the entire nation had finished crossing the Jordan, the LORD spoke to Joshua:  
**4:2** “Choose twelve men from the people, one man for each tribe,  
**4:3** and command them: Take twelve stones from this place in the middle of the Jordan where the priests<sup>①</sup> are standing, carry them with you, and set them down at the place where you spend the night.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>feet of the priests</i>, also in v. <a name='B:60 4:9'>9  
**4:4** So Joshua summoned the twelve men he had selected from the Israelites, one man for each tribe,  
**4:5** and said to them, “Go across to the ark of the LORD your God in the middle of the Jordan. Each of you lift a stone onto his shoulder, one for each of the Israelite tribes,  
**4:6** so that this will be a sign among you. In the future, when your children ask you, ‘What do these stones mean to you? ’  
**4:7** you should tell them, ‘The water of the Jordan was cut off in front of the ark of the LORD’s covenant. When it crossed the Jordan, the Jordan’s water was cut off.’ Therefore these stones will always be a memorial for the Israelites.”  
**4:8** The Israelites did just as Joshua had commanded them. The twelve men took stones from the middle of the Jordan, one for each of the Israelite tribes, just as the LORD had told Joshua. They carried them to the camp and set them down there.  
**4:9** Joshua also set up twelve stones in the middle<sup>①</sup> of the Jordan where the priests who carried the ark of the covenant were standing. The stones are still there today.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Now Joshua set up the twelve stones that had been in the middle</i>  
**4:10** The priests carrying the ark continued standing in the middle of the Jordan until everything was completed that the LORD had commanded Joshua to tell the people, in keeping with all that Moses had commanded Joshua. The people hurried across,  
**4:11** and after everyone had finished crossing, the priests with the ark of the LORD crossed in the sight of the people.  
**4:12** The Reubenites, Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh went in battle formation in front of the Israelites, as Moses had instructed them.  
**4:13** About forty thousand equipped for war crossed to the plains of Jericho in the LORD’s presence.  
**4:14** On that day the LORD exalted Joshua in the sight of all Israel, and they revered him throughout his life, as they had revered Moses.  
**4:15** The LORD told Joshua,  
**4:16** “Command the priests who carry the ark of the testimony to come up from the Jordan.”  
**4:17** So Joshua commanded the priests, “Come up from the Jordan.”  
**4:18** When the priests carrying the ark of the LORD’s covenant came up from the middle of the Jordan, and their feet<sup>①</sup> stepped out on solid ground, the water of the Jordan resumed its course, flowing over all the banks as before.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and the soles of the feet of the priests</i>  
**4:19** The people came up from the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month, and camped at Gilgal on the eastern limits of Jericho.  
**4:20** Then Joshua set up in Gilgal the twelve stones they had taken from the Jordan,  
**4:21** and he said to the Israelites, “In the future, when your children ask their fathers, ‘What is the meaning of these stones?’  
**4:22** you should tell your children, ‘Israel crossed the Jordan on dry ground.’  
**4:23** For the LORD your God dried up the water of the Jordan before you until you had crossed over, just as the LORD your God did to the Red Sea, which he dried up before us until we had crossed over.  
**4:24** This is so that all the peoples of the earth may know that the LORD’s hand is strong, and so that you may always fear the LORD your God.”  

## 5장

**5:1** When all the Amorite kings across the Jordan to the west and all the Canaanite kings near the sea heard how the LORD had dried up the water of the Jordan before the Israelites until they had crossed over, they lost heart and their courage failed because of the Israelites.  
**5:2** At that time the LORD said to Joshua, “Make flint knives and circumcise the Israelite men again.”  
**5:3** So Joshua made flint knives and circumcised the Israelite men at Gibeath-haaraloth.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>The Hill of Foreskins</i>  
**5:4** This is the reason Joshua circumcised them: All the people who came out of Egypt who were males—all the men of war—had died in the wilderness along the way after they had come out of Egypt.  
**5:5** Though all the people who came out were circumcised, none of the people born in the wilderness along the way were circumcised after they had come out of Egypt.  
**5:6** For the Israelites wandered in the wilderness forty years until all the nation’s men of war who came out of Egypt had died off because they did not obey the LORD. So the LORD vowed never to let them see the land he had sworn to their ancestors to give us, a land flowing with milk and honey.  
**5:7** He raised up their sons in their place; it was these Joshua circumcised. They were still uncircumcised, since they had not been circumcised along the way.  
**5:8** After the entire nation had been circumcised, they stayed where they were in the camp until they recovered.  
**5:9** The LORD then said to Joshua, “Today I have rolled away the disgrace of Egypt from you.” Therefore, that place is still called Gilgal<sup>①</sup> today.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>=to roll  
**5:10** While the Israelites camped at Gilgal on the plains of Jericho, they observed the Passover on the evening of the fourteenth day of the month.  
**5:11** The day after Passover they ate unleavened bread and roasted grain from the produce of the land.  
**5:12** And the day after they ate from the produce of the land, the manna ceased. Since there was no more manna for the Israelites, they ate from the crops of the land of Canaan that year.  
**5:13** When Joshua was near Jericho, he looked up and saw a man standing in front of him with a drawn sword in his hand. Joshua approached him and asked, “Are you for us or for our enemies?”  
**5:14** “Neither,” he replied. “I have now come as commander of the LORD’s army.” Then Joshua bowed with his face to the ground in homage and asked him, “What does my lord want to say to his servant?”  
**5:15** The commander of the LORD’s army said to Joshua, “Remove the sandals from your feet, for the place where you are standing is holy.” And Joshua did that.  

## 6장

**6:1** Now Jericho was strongly fortified because of the Israelites—no one leaving or entering.  
**6:2** The LORD said to Joshua, “Look, I have handed Jericho, its king, and its best soldiers over to you.  
**6:3** March around the city with all the men of war, circling the city one time. Do this for six days.  
**6:4** Have seven priests carry seven ram’s-horn trumpets in front of the ark. But on the seventh day, march around the city seven times, while the priests blow the ram’s horns.  
**6:5** When there is a prolonged blast of the horn and you hear its sound, have all the troops give a mighty shout. Then the city wall will collapse, and the troops will advance, each man straight ahead.”  
**6:6** So Joshua son of Nun summoned the priests and said to them, “Take up the ark of the covenant and have seven priests carry seven ram’s horns in front of the ark of the LORD.”  
**6:7** He said to the troops, “Move forward, march around the city, and have the armed men go ahead of the ark of the LORD.”  
**6:8** After Joshua had spoken to the troops, seven priests carrying seven ram’s horns before the LORD moved forward and blew the ram’s horns; the ark of the LORD’s covenant followed them.  
**6:9** While the ram’s horns were blowing, the armed men went in front of the priests who blew the ram’s horns, and the rear guard went behind the ark.  
**6:10** But Joshua had commanded the troops, “Do not shout or let your voice be heard. Don’t let one word come out of your mouth until the time I say, ‘Shout! ’ Then you are to shout.”  
**6:11** So the ark of the LORD was carried around the city, circling it once. They returned to the camp and spent the night there.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>at the camp</i>  
**6:12** Joshua got up early the next morning. The priests took the ark of the LORD,  
**6:13** and the seven priests carrying seven ram’s horns marched in front of the ark of the LORD. While the ram’s horns were blowing, the armed men went in front of them, and the rear guard went behind the ark of the LORD.  
**6:14** On the second day they marched around the city once and returned to the camp. They did this for six days.  
**6:15** Early on the seventh day, they started at dawn and marched around the city seven times in the same way. That was the only day they marched around the city seven times.  
**6:16** After the seventh time, the priests blew the ram’s horns, and Joshua said to the troops, “Shout! For the LORD has given you the city.  
**6:17** But the city and everything in it are set apart to the LORD for destruction. Only Rahab the prostitute and everyone with her in the house will live, because she hid the messengers we sent.  
**6:18** But keep yourselves from the things set apart, or you will be set apart for destruction. If you<sup>①</sup> take any of those things, you will set apart the camp of Israel for destruction and make trouble for it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>LXX reads <i>you covet and;</i> Jos <a name='B:60 7:21'>7:21  
**6:19** For all the silver and gold, and the articles of bronze and iron, are dedicated to the LORD and must go into the LORD’s treasury.”  
**6:20** So the troops shouted, and the ram’s horns sounded. When they heard the blast of the ram’s horns, the troops gave a great shout, and the wall collapsed. The troops advanced into the city, each man straight ahead, and they captured the city.  
**6:21** They completely destroyed everything in the city with the sword—every man and woman, both young and old, and every ox, sheep, and donkey.  
**6:22** Joshua said to the two men who had scouted the land, “Go to the prostitute’s house and bring the woman out of there, and all who are with her, just as you swore to her.”  
**6:23** So the young men who had scouted went in and brought out Rahab and her father, mother, brothers, and all who belonged to her. They brought out her whole family and settled them outside the camp of Israel.  
**6:24** They burned the city and everything in it, but they put the silver and gold and the articles of bronze and iron into the treasury of the LORD’s house.  
**6:25** However, Joshua spared Rahab the prostitute, her father’s family, and all who belonged to her, because she hid the messengers Joshua had sent to spy on Jericho, and she still lives in Israel today.  
**6:26** At that time Joshua imposed this curse:<br>The man who undertakes<br>the rebuilding of this city, Jericho,<br>is cursed before the LORD.<br>He will lay its foundation<br>at the cost of his firstborn;<br>he will finish its gates<br>at the cost of his youngest.  
**6:27** And the LORD was with Joshua, and his fame spread throughout the land.  

## 7장

**7:1** The Israelites, however, were unfaithful regarding the things set apart for destruction. Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took some of what was set apart, and the LORD’s anger burned against the Israelites.  
**7:2** Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, which is near Beth-aven, east of Bethel, and told them, “Go up and scout the land.” So the men went up and scouted Ai.  
**7:3** After returning to Joshua they reported to him, “Don’t send all the people, but send about two thousand or three thousand<sup>①</sup> men to attack Ai. Since the people of Ai are so few, don’t wear out all our people there.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>send two or three military units of</i>  
**7:4** So about three thousand men<sup>①</sup> went up there, but they fled from the men of Ai.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>men from the people</i>  
**7:5** The men of Ai struck down about thirty-six of them and chased them from outside the city gate to the quarries,<sup>①</sup> striking them down on the descent. As a result, the people lost heart.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>to Shebarim</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>people’s hearts melted and became like water</i>  
**7:6** Then Joshua tore his clothes and fell facedown to the ground before the ark of the LORD until evening, as did the elders of Israel; they all put dust on their heads.  
**7:7** “Oh, Lord GOD,” Joshua said, “why did you ever bring these people across the Jordan to hand us over to the Amorites for our destruction? If only we had been content to remain on the other side of the Jordan!  
**7:8** What can I say, Lord, now that Israel has turned its back and run from its enemies?  
**7:9** When the Canaanites and all who live in the land hear about this, they will surround us and wipe out our name from the earth. Then what will you do about your great name?”  
**7:10** The LORD then said to Joshua, “Stand up! Why have you fallen facedown?  
**7:11** Israel has sinned. They have violated my covenant that I appointed for them. They have taken some of what was set apart. They have stolen, deceived, and put those things with their own belongings.  
**7:12** This is why the Israelites cannot stand against their enemies. They will turn their backs and run from their enemies, because they have been set apart for destruction. I will no longer be with you unless you remove from among you what is set apart.  
**7:13** “Go and consecrate the people. Tell them to consecrate themselves for tomorrow, for this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: There are things that are set apart among you, Israel. You will not be able to stand against your enemies until you remove what is set apart.  
**7:14** In the morning, present yourselves tribe by tribe. The tribe the LORD selects is to come forward clan by clan. The clan the LORD selects is to come forward family by family. The family the LORD selects is to come forward man by man.  
**7:15** The one who is caught with the things set apart must be burned, along with everything he has, because he has violated the LORD’s covenant and committed an outrage in Israel.”  
**7:16** Joshua got up early the next morning. He had Israel come forward tribe by tribe, and the tribe of Judah was selected.  
**7:17** He had the clans of Judah come forward, and the Zerahite clan was selected. He had the Zerahite clan come forward by heads of families,<sup>①</sup> and Zabdi was selected.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>forward man by man</i>  
**7:18** He then had Zabdi’s family come forward man by man, and Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was selected.  
**7:19** So Joshua said to Achan, “My son, give glory to the LORD, the God of Israel, and make a confession to him.<sup>①</sup> I urge you, tell me what you have done. Don’t hide anything from me.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>and praise him</i>  
**7:20** Achan replied to Joshua, “It is true. I have sinned against the LORD, the God of Israel. This is what I did:  
**7:21** When I saw among the spoils a beautiful cloak from Babylon,<sup>①</sup> five pounds<sup>②</sup> of silver, and a bar of gold weighing a pound and a quarter,<sup>③</sup> I coveted them and took them. You can see for yourself. They are concealed in the ground inside my tent, with the silver under the cloak.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Shinar</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>200 shekels</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>50 shekels</i>

**7:23** They took the things from inside the tent, brought them to Joshua and all the Israelites, and spread them out in the LORD’s presence.  
**7:24** Then Joshua and all Israel with him took Achan son of Zerah, the silver, the cloak, and the bar of gold, his sons and daughters, his ox, donkey, and sheep, his tent, and all that he had, and brought them up to the Valley of Achor.  
**7:25** Joshua said, “Why have you brought us trouble? Today the LORD will bring you trouble!” So all Israel stoned them<sup>①</sup> to death. They burned their bodies, threw stones on them,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>him</i>  
**7:26** and raised over him a large pile of rocks that remains still today. Then the LORD turned from his burning anger. Therefore that place is called the Valley of Achor<sup>①</sup> still today.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>of Trouble</i>  

## 8장

**8:1** The LORD said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid or discouraged. Take all the troops with you and go attack Ai. Look, I have handed over to you the king of Ai, his people, city, and land.  
**8:2** Treat Ai and its king as you did Jericho and its king, except that you may plunder its spoil and livestock for yourselves. Set an ambush behind the city.”  
**8:3** So Joshua and all the troops set out to attack Ai. Joshua selected thirty thousand of his best soldiers and sent them out at night.  
**8:4** He commanded them, “Pay attention. Lie in ambush behind the city, not too far from it, and all of you be ready.  
**8:5** Then I and all the people who are with me will approach the city. When they come out against us as they did the first time, we will flee from them.  
**8:6** They will come after us until we have drawn them away from the city, for they will say, ‘They are fleeing from us as before.’ While we are fleeing from them,  
**8:7** you are to come out of your ambush and seize the city. The LORD your God will hand it over to you.  
**8:8** After taking the city, set it on fire. Follow the LORD’s command—see that you do as I have ordered you.”  
**8:9** So Joshua sent them out, and they went to the ambush site and waited between Bethel and Ai, to the west of Ai. But he spent that night with the troops.  
**8:10** Joshua started early the next morning and mobilized them. Then he and the elders of Israel led the people up to Ai.  
**8:11** All the troops who were with him went up and approached the city, arriving opposite Ai, and camped to the north of it, with a valley between them and the city.
