# 22.아가 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** The Song of Songs, which is Solomon’s.  
**1:2** Oh, that he would kiss me with the kisses of his mouth!<br>For your caresses<sup>①</sup> are more delightful than wine.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>acts of love</i>  
**1:3** The fragrance of your perfume is intoxicating;<br>your name is perfume poured out.<br>No wonder young women<sup>①</sup> adore you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>wonder virgins</i>  
**1:4** Take me with you—let’s hurry.<br>Oh, that the king would bring<sup>①</sup> me to his chambers.<br>We will rejoice and be glad in you;<br>we will celebrate your caresses more than wine.<br>It is only right that they adore you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>The king has brought</i>  
**1:5** Daughters of Jerusalem,<br>I am dark like the tents of Kedar,<br>yet lovely like the curtains of Solomon.  
**1:6** Do not stare at me because I am dark,<br>for the sun has gazed on me.<br>My mother’s sons were angry with me;<br>they made me take care of the vineyards.<br>I have not taken care of my own vineyard.  
**1:7** Tell me, you whom I love:<br>Where do you pasture your sheep?<br>Where do you let them rest at noon?<br>Why should I be like one who veils herself<sup>①</sup><br>beside the flocks of your companions?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>who wanders</i>  
**1:8** <sup>①</sup> If you do not know,<br>most beautiful of women,<br>follow<sup>②</sup> the tracks of the flock,<br>and pasture your young goats<br>near the shepherds’ tents.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some understand the young women to be the speakers in this verse. <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>go out for yourself into</i>  
**1:9** I compare you, my darling,<br>to a<sup>①</sup> mare among Pharaoh’s chariots.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>my</i>  
**1:10** Your cheeks are beautiful with jewelry,<br>your neck with its necklace.  
**1:11** We will make gold jewelry for you,<br>accented with silver.  
**1:12** While the king is on his couch,<sup>①</sup><br>my perfume<sup>②</sup> releases its fragrance.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>is at his table</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>nard</i>  
**1:13** The one I love is a sachet of myrrh to me,<br>spending the night between my breasts.  
**1:14** The one I love is a cluster of henna blossoms to me,<br>in the vineyards of En-gedi.  
**1:15** How beautiful you are, my darling.<br>How very beautiful!<br>Your eyes are doves.  
**1:16** How handsome you are, my love.<br>How delightful!<br>Our bed is verdant;  
**1:17** the beams of our house are cedars,<br>and our rafters are cypresses.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>firs</i>, or <i>pines</i>  

## 2장

**2:1** I am a wildflower<sup>①</sup> of Sharon,<br>a lily<sup>②</sup> of the valleys.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Traditionally <i>rose</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>lotus</i>  
**2:2** Like a lily among thorns,<br>so is my darling among the young women.  
**2:3** Like an apricot<sup>①</sup> tree among the trees of the forest,<br>so is my love among the young men.<br>I delight to sit in his shade,<br>and his fruit is sweet to my taste.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>apple</i>  
**2:4** He brought me to the banquet hall,<sup>①</sup><br>and he looked on me with love.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the house of wine</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>and his banner over me is love</i>  
**2:5** Sustain me with raisins;<br>refresh me with apricots,<sup>①</sup><br>for I am lovesick.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>apples</i>  
**2:6** May his left hand be under my head,<br>and his right arm embrace me.  
**2:7** Young women of Jerusalem, I charge you<br>by the gazelles and the wild does of the field,<br>do not stir up or awaken love<br>until the appropriate time.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>until it pleases</i>  
**2:8** Listen! My love is approaching.<br>Look! Here he comes,<br>leaping over the mountains,<br>bounding over the hills.  
**2:9** My love is like a gazelle<br>or a young stag.<br>See, he is standing behind our wall,<br>gazing through the windows,<br>peering through the lattice.  
**2:10** My love calls to me:<br>Arise, my darling.<br>Come away, my beautiful one.  
**2:11** For now the winter is past;<br>the rain has ended and gone away.  
**2:12** The blossoms appear in the countryside.<br>The time of singing<sup>①</sup> has come,<br>and the turtledove’s cooing is heard in our land.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>pruning</i>  
**2:13** The fig tree ripens its figs;<br>the blossoming vines give off their fragrance.<br>Arise, my darling.<br>Come away, my beautiful one.  
**2:14** My dove, in the clefts of the rock,<br>in the crevices of the cliff,<br>let me see your face,<sup>①</sup><br>let me hear your voice;<br>for your voice is sweet,<br>and your face is lovely.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>form</i>  
**2:15** <sup>①</sup> Catch the foxes for us—<br>the little foxes that ruin the vineyards—<br>for our vineyards are in bloom.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The speaker could be the woman, the man, or both.  
**2:16** My love is mine and I am his;<br>he feeds among the lilies.  
**2:17** Until the day breaks<sup>①</sup><br>and the shadows flee,<br>turn around, my love, and be like a gazelle<br>or a young stag on the divided mountains.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>breathes</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>the Bether mountains</i>, or <i>the mountains of spices;</i> Hb obscure  

## 3장

**3:1** In my bed at night<sup>①</sup><br>I sought the one I love;<br>I sought him, but did not find him.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>bed night after night</i> <sup>②</sup>LXX adds <i>I called him, but he did not answer me</i>  
**3:2** I will arise now and go about the city,<br>through the streets and the plazas.<br>I will seek the one I love.<br>I sought him, but did not find him.  
**3:3** The guards who go about the city found me.<br>I asked them, “Have you seen the one I love?”  
**3:4** I had just passed them<br>when I found the one I love.<br>I held on to him and would not let him go<br>until I brought him to my mother’s house—<br>to the chamber of the one who conceived me.  
**3:5** Young women of Jerusalem, I charge you<br>by the gazelles and the wild does of the field,<br>do not stir up or awaken love<br>until the appropriate time.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>until it pleases</i>  
**3:6** Who is this coming up from the wilderness<br>like columns of smoke,<br>scented with myrrh and frankincense<br>from every fragrant powder of the merchant?  
**3:7** Look! Solomon’s bed<br>surrounded by sixty warriors<br>from the mighty men of Israel.  
**3:8** All of them are skilled with swords<br>and trained in warfare.<br>Each has his sword at his side<br>to guard against the terror of the night.  
**3:9** King Solomon made a carriage for himself<br>with wood from Lebanon.  
**3:10** He made its posts of silver,<br>its back<sup>①</sup> of gold,<br>and its seat of purple.<br>Its interior is inlaid with love<sup>②</sup><br>by the young women of Jerusalem.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>base</i>, or <i>canopy</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>leather</i>  
**3:11** Go out, young women of Zion,<br>and gaze at King Solomon,<br>wearing the crown his mother placed on him<br>on the day of his wedding—<br>the day of his heart’s rejoicing.  

## 4장

**4:1** How beautiful you are, my darling.<br>How very beautiful!<br>Behind your veil,<br>your eyes are doves.<br>Your hair is like a flock of goats<br>streaming down Mount Gilead.  
**4:2** Your teeth are like a flock of newly shorn sheep<br>coming up from washing,<br>each one bearing twins,<br>and none has lost its young.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and no one bereaved among them</i>  
**4:3** Your lips are like a scarlet cord,<br>and your mouth<sup>①</sup> is lovely.<br>Behind your veil,<br>your brow<sup>②</sup> is like a slice of pomegranate.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>speech</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>temple</i>, or <i>cheek</i>, or <i>lips</i>  
**4:4** Your neck is like the tower of David,<br>constructed in layers.<br>A thousand shields are hung on it—<br>all of them shields of warriors.  
**4:5** Your breasts are like two fawns,<br>twins of a gazelle, that feed among the lilies.  
**4:6** Until the day breaks<sup>①</sup><br>and the shadows flee,<br>I will make my way to the mountain of myrrh<br>and the hill of frankincense.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>breathes</i>  
**4:7** You are absolutely beautiful, my darling;<br>there is no imperfection in you.  
**4:8** Come with me from Lebanon,<sup>①</sup> my bride;<br>come with me from Lebanon!<br>Descend from the peak of Amana,<br>from the summit of Senir and Hermon,<br>from the dens of the lions,<br>from the mountains of the leopards.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Hb, the word for <i>Lebanon</i> is similar to “frankincense” in Sg <a name='B:260 4:6'>4:6,<a name='B:260 4:14'>14,<a name='B:260 4:15'>15.  
**4:9** You have captured my heart, my sister, my bride.<br>You have captured my heart with one glance of your eyes,<br>with one jewel of your necklace.  
**4:10** How delightful your caresses are, my sister, my bride.<br>Your caresses are much better than wine,<br>and the fragrance of your perfume than any balsam.  
**4:11** Your lips drip sweetness like the honeycomb, my bride.<br>Honey and milk are under your tongue.<br>The fragrance of your garments is like the fragrance of Lebanon.  
**4:12** My sister, my bride, you are a locked garden—<br>a locked garden<sup>①</sup> and a sealed spring.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some Hb mss read <i>locked fountain</i>  
**4:13** Your branches are a paradise<sup>①</sup> of pomegranates<br>with choicest fruits;<br>henna with nard,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>park</i>, or <i>orchard</i>  
**4:14** nard and saffron, calamus and cinnamon,<br>with all the trees of frankincense,<br>myrrh and aloes,<br>with all the best spices.  
**4:15** You are a garden spring,<br>a well of flowing water<br>streaming from Lebanon.  
**4:16** Awaken, north wind;<br>come, south wind.<br>Blow on my garden,<br>and spread the fragrance of its spices.<br>Let my love come to his garden<br>and eat its choicest fruits.  

## 5장

**5:2** I was sleeping, but my heart was awake.<br>A sound! My love was knocking!<br>Open to me, my sister, my darling,<br>my dove, my perfect one.<br>For my head is drenched with dew,<br>my hair with droplets of the night.  
**5:3** I have taken off my clothing.<br>How can I put it back on?<br>I have washed my feet.<br>How can I get them dirty?  
**5:4** My love thrust his hand through the opening,<br>and my feelings were stirred for him.  
**5:5** I rose to open for my love.<br>My hands dripped with myrrh,<br>my fingers with flowing myrrh<br>on the handles of the bolt.  
**5:6** I opened to my love,<br>but my love had turned and gone away.<br>My heart sank<sup>①</sup> because he had left.<sup>②</sup><br>I sought him, but did not find him.<br>I called him, but he did not answer.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>My soul went out</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>spoken</i>

**5:8** Young women of Jerusalem, I charge you,<br>if you find my love,<br>tell him that I am lovesick.  
**5:9** What makes the one you love better than another,<br>most beautiful of women?<br>What makes him better than another,<br>that you would give us this charge?  
**5:10** My love is fit and strong,<sup>①</sup><br>notable among ten thousand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>is radiant and ruddy</i>  
**5:11** His head is purest gold.<br>His hair is wavy<sup>①</sup><br>and black as a raven.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>is like palm leaves;</i> Hb obscure  
**5:12** His eyes are like doves<br>beside flowing streams,<br>washed in milk<br>and set like jewels.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>milk sitting in fullness</i>  
**5:13** His cheeks are like beds of spice,<br>mounds of<sup>①</sup> perfume.<br>His lips are lilies,<br>dripping with flowing myrrh.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>LXX, Vg read <i>spice, yielding</i>  
**5:14** His arms<sup>①</sup> are rods of gold<br>set<sup>②</sup> with beryl.<br>His body<sup>③</sup> is an ivory panel<br>covered with lapis lazuli.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>hands</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>filled;</i> Sg <a name='B:260 5:2'>5:2,<a name='B:260 5:12'>12 <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>abdomen</i>

**5:16** His mouth is sweetness.<br>He is absolutely desirable.<br>This is my love, and this is my friend,<br>young women of Jerusalem.
