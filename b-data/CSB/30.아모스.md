## 1장

**1:1** The words of Amos, who was one of the sheep breeders<sup>①</sup> from Tekoa—what he saw regarding Israel in the days of King Uzziah of Judah and Jeroboam son of Jehoash, king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the shepherds</i>  
**1:2** He said:<br>The LORD roars from Zion<br>and makes his voice heard from Jerusalem;<br>the pastures of the shepherds mourn,<sup>①</sup><br>and the summit of Carmel withers.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>dry up</i>  
**1:3** The LORD says:<br>I will not relent from punishing Damascus<br>for three crimes, even four,<br>because they threshed Gilead with iron sledges.  
**1:4** Therefore, I will send fire against Hazael’s palace,<br>and it will consume Ben-hadad’s citadels.  
**1:5** I will break down the gates<sup>①</sup> of Damascus.<br>I will cut off the ruler from the Valley of Aven,<br>and the one who wields the scepter from Beth-eden.<br>The people of Aram will be exiled to Kir.<br>The LORD has spoken.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>gate bars</i>  
**1:6** The LORD says:<br>I will not relent from punishing Gaza<br>for three crimes, even four,<br>because they exiled a whole community,<br>handing them over to Edom.  
**1:7** Therefore, I will send fire against the walls of Gaza,<br>and it will consume its citadels.  
**1:8** I will cut off the ruler from Ashdod,<br>and the one who wields the scepter from Ashkelon.<br>I will also turn my hand against Ekron,<br>and the remainder of the Philistines will perish.<br>The Lord GOD has spoken.  
**1:9** The LORD says:<br>I will not relent from punishing Tyre<br>for three crimes, even four,<br>because they handed over<br>a whole community of exiles to Edom<br>and broke<sup>①</sup> a treaty of brotherhood.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and did not remember</i>

**1:11** The LORD says:<br>I will not relent from punishing Edom<br>for three crimes, even four,<br>because he pursued his brother with the sword.<br>He stifled his compassion,<br>his anger tore at him continually,<br>and he harbored his rage incessantly.  
**1:12** Therefore, I will send fire against Teman,<br>and it will consume the citadels of Bozrah.  
**1:13** The LORD says:<br>I will not relent from punishing the Ammonites<br>for three crimes, even four,<br>because they ripped open<br>the pregnant women of Gilead<br>in order to enlarge their territory.  
**1:14** Therefore, I will set fire to the walls of Rabbah,<br>and it will consume its citadels.<br>There will be shouting on the day of battle<br>and a violent wind on the day of the storm.  
**1:15** Their king and his princes<br>will go into exile together.<br>The LORD has spoken.  

## 2장

**2:1** The LORD says:<br>I will not relent from punishing Moab<br>for three crimes, even four,<br>because he burned the bones<br>of the king of Edom to lime.  
**2:2** Therefore, I will send fire against Moab,<br>and it will consume the citadels of Kerioth.<br>Moab will die with a tumult,<br>with shouting and the sound of the ram’s horn.  
**2:3** I will cut off the judge from the land<br>and kill all its officials with him.<br>The LORD has spoken.  
**2:4** The LORD says:<br>I will not relent from punishing Judah<br>for three crimes, even four,<br>because they have rejected the instruction of the LORD<br>and have not kept his statutes.<br>The lies that their ancestors followed<br>have led them astray.  
**2:5** Therefore, I will send fire against Judah,<br>and it will consume the citadels of Jerusalem.  
**2:6** The LORD says:<br>I will not relent from punishing Israel<br>for three crimes, even four,<br>because they sell a righteous person for silver<br>and a needy person for a pair of sandals.  
**2:7** They trample the heads of the poor<br>on the dust of the ground<br>and obstruct the path of the needy.<br>A man and his father have sexual relations<br>with the same girl,<br>profaning my holy name.

**2:9** Yet I destroyed the Amorite as Israel advanced;<br>his height was like the cedars,<br>and he was as sturdy as the oaks;<br>I destroyed his fruit above and his roots beneath.  
**2:10** And I brought you from the land of Egypt<br>and led you forty years in the wilderness<br>in order to possess the land of the Amorite.  
**2:11** I raised up some of your sons as prophets<br>and some of your young men as Nazirites.<br>Is this not the case, Israelites? This is the LORD’s declaration.  
**2:12** But you made the Nazirites drink wine<br>and commanded the prophets,<br>“Do not prophesy.”  
**2:13** Look, I am about to crush<sup>①</sup> you in your place<br>as a wagon crushes when full of grain.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>hinder;</i> Hb obscure

**2:15** The archer will not stand his ground,<br>the one who is swift of foot<br>will not save himself,<br>and the one riding a horse will not save his life.  
**2:16** Even the most courageous of the warriors<br>will flee naked on that day—this is the LORD’s declaration.
