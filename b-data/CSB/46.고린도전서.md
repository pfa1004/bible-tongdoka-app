# 46.고린도전서 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** Paul, called as an apostle of Christ Jesus by God’s will, and Sosthenes our brother:  
**1:2** To the church of God at Corinth, to those sanctified in Christ Jesus, called as saints, with all those in every place who call on the name of Jesus Christ our Lord—both their Lord and ours.  
**1:3** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:4** I always thank my God for you because of the grace of God given to you in Christ Jesus,  
**1:5** that you were enriched in him in every way, in all speech and all knowledge.  
**1:6** In this way, the testimony about Christ was confirmed among you,  
**1:7** so that you do not lack any spiritual gift as you eagerly wait for the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:8** He will also strengthen you to the end, so that you will be blameless in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:9** God is faithful; you were called by him into fellowship with his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.  
**1:10** Now I urge you, brothers and sisters, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that all of you agree in what you say, that there be no divisions among you, and that you be united with the same understanding and the same conviction.  
**1:11** For it has been reported to me about you, my brothers and sisters, by members of Chloe’s people, that there is rivalry among you.  
**1:12** What I am saying is this: One of you says, “I belong to Paul,” or “I belong to Apollos,” or “I belong to Cephas,” or “I belong to Christ.”  
**1:13** Is Christ divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Or were you baptized in Paul’s name?  
**1:14** I thank God<sup>①</sup><sup>②</sup> that I baptized none of you except Crispus and Gaius,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss omit <i>God</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>I am thankful</i>  
**1:15** so that no one can say you were baptized in my name.  
**1:16** I did, in fact, baptize the household of Stephanas; beyond that, I don’t recall if I baptized anyone else.  
**1:17** For Christ did not send me to baptize, but to preach the gospel—not with eloquent wisdom, so that the cross of Christ will not be emptied of its effect.  
**1:18** For the word of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but it is the power of God to us who are being saved.  
**1:19** For it is written,<br><b>I will destroy the wisdom of the wise,</b><br><b>and I will set aside the intelligence of the intelligent.</b>  
**1:20** Where is the one who is wise? Where is the teacher of the law?<sup>①</sup> Where is the debater of this age? Hasn’t God made the world’s wisdom foolish?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>scholar</i>  
**1:21** For since, in God’s wisdom, the world did not know God through wisdom, God was pleased to save those who believe through the foolishness of what is preached.  
**1:22** For the Jews ask for signs and the Greeks seek wisdom,  
**1:23** but we preach Christ crucified, a stumbling block to the Jews and foolishness to the Gentiles.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss read <i>Greeks</i>  
**1:24** Yet to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ is the power of God and the wisdom of God,  
**1:25** because God’s foolishness is wiser than human wisdom, and God’s weakness is stronger than human strength.  
**1:26** Brothers and sisters, consider your calling: Not many were wise from a human perspective,<sup>①</sup> not many powerful, not many of noble birth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>wise according to the flesh</i>  
**1:27** Instead, God has chosen what is foolish in the world to shame the wise, and God has chosen what is weak in the world to shame the strong.  
**1:28** God has chosen what is insignificant and despised in the world—what is viewed as nothing—to bring to nothing what is viewed as something,  
**1:29** so that no one<sup>①</sup> may boast in his presence.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>that not all flesh</i>  
**1:30** It is from him that you are in Christ Jesus, who became wisdom from God for us—our righteousness, sanctification, and redemption,  
**1:31** in order that, as it is written: <b>Let the one who boasts, boast in the Lord.</b><sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Jr <a name='B:300 9:24'>9:24  

## 2장

**2:1** When I came to you, brothers and sisters, announcing the mystery<sup>①</sup> of God to you, I did not come with brilliance of speech or wisdom.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss read <i>testimony</i>  
**2:2** I decided to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ and him crucified.  
**2:3** I came to you in weakness, in fear, and in much trembling.  
**2:4** My speech and my preaching were not with persuasive words of wisdom<sup>①</sup> but with a demonstration of the Spirit’s power,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss read <i>human wisdom</i>  
**2:5** so that your faith might not be based on human wisdom but on God’s power.  
**2:6** We do, however, speak a wisdom among the mature, but not a wisdom of this age, or of the rulers of this age, who are coming to nothing.  
**2:7** On the contrary, we speak God’s hidden wisdom in a mystery, a wisdom God predestined before the ages for our glory.  
**2:8** None of the rulers of this age knew this wisdom, because if they had known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.  
**2:9** But as it is written,<br><b>What no eye has seen, no ear has heard,</b><br><b>and no human heart has conceived—</b><br><b>God has prepared these things for those who love him.</b><sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Is <a name='B:290 52:15'>52:15; <a name='B:290 64:4'>64:4  
**2:10** Now God has revealed these things to us by the Spirit, since the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God.  
**2:11** For who knows a person’s thoughts<sup>①</sup> except his spirit within him? In the same way, no one knows the thoughts of God except the Spirit of God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>things</i>  
**2:12** Now we have not received the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who comes from God, so that we may understand what has been freely given to us by God.  
**2:13** We also speak these things, not in words taught by human wisdom, but in those taught by the Spirit, explaining spiritual things to spiritual people.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>things with spiritual words</i>  
**2:14** But the person without the Spirit<sup>①</sup> does not receive what comes from God’s Spirit, because it is foolishness to him; he is not able to understand it since it is evaluated<sup>②</sup> spiritually.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>natural person</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>judged</i>, or <i>discerned</i>, also in v. <a name='B:530 2:15'>15  
**2:15** The spiritual person, however, can evaluate<sup>①</sup> everything, and yet he himself cannot be evaluated by anyone.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>judge</i>, or <i>discern</i>  
**2:16** For<br><b>who has known the Lord’s mind,</b><br><b>that he may instruct him?</b><sup>①</sup> But we have the mind of Christ.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Is <a name='B:290 40:13'>40:13  

## 3장

**3:1** For my part, brothers and sisters, I was not able to speak to you as spiritual people but as people of the flesh, as babies in Christ.  
**3:2** I gave you milk to drink, not solid food, since you were not yet ready for it. In fact, you are still not ready,  
**3:3** because you are still worldly. For since there is envy and strife<sup>①</sup> among you, are you not worldly and behaving like mere humans?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss add <i>and divisions</i>  
**3:4** For whenever someone says, “I belong to Paul,” and another, “I belong to Apollos,” are you not acting like mere humans?  
**3:5** What then is Apollos? What is Paul? They are servants through whom you believed, and each has the role the Lord has given.  
**3:6** I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth.  
**3:7** So, then, neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but only God who gives the growth.  
**3:8** Now he who plants and he who waters are one,<sup>①</sup> and each will receive his own reward according to his own labor.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>of equal status</i>, or <i>united in purpose</i>  
**3:9** For we are God’s coworkers.<sup>①</sup> You are God’s field, God’s building.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>are coworkers belonging to God</i>  
**3:10** According to God’s grace that was given to me, I have laid a foundation as a skilled master builder,<sup>①</sup> and another builds on it. But each one is to be careful how he builds on it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>wise master builder</i>  
**3:11** For no one can lay any other foundation other than what has been laid down. That foundation is Jesus Christ.  
**3:12** If anyone builds on the foundation with gold, silver, costly stones, wood, hay, or straw,  
**3:13** each one’s work will become obvious. For the day will disclose it, because it will be revealed by fire; the fire will test the quality of each one’s work.  
**3:14** If anyone’s work that he has built survives, he will receive a reward.  
**3:15** If anyone’s work is burned up, he will experience<sup>①</sup> loss, but he himself will be saved—but only as through fire.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>suffer</i>  
**3:16** Don’t you yourselves know that you are God’s temple and that the Spirit of God lives in you?  
**3:17** If anyone destroys God’s temple, God will destroy him; for God’s temple is holy, and that is what you are.  
**3:18** Let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you thinks he is wise in this age, let him become a fool so that he can become wise.  
**3:19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God, since it is written, <b>He catches the wise in their craftiness</b>;<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Jb <a name='B:220 5:13'>5:13  
**3:20** and again, <b>The Lord knows that the reasonings</b> <b>of the wise are futile.</b><sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ps <a name='B:230 94:11'>94:11  
**3:21** So let no one boast in human leaders, for everything is yours—  
**3:22** whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life or death or things present or things to come—everything is yours,  
**3:23** and you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.  

## 4장

**4:1** A person should think of us in this way: as servants of Christ and managers of the mysteries of God.  
**4:2** In this regard, it is required that managers be found faithful.  
**4:3** It is of little importance to me that I should be judged by you or by any human court.<sup>①</sup> In fact, I don’t even judge myself.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>a human day</i>  
**4:4** For I am not conscious of anything against myself, but I am not justified by this. It is the Lord who judges me.  
**4:5** So don’t judge anything prematurely, before the Lord comes, who will both bring to light what is hidden in darkness and reveal the intentions of the hearts. And then praise will come to each one from God.  
**4:6** Now, brothers and sisters, I have applied these things to myself and Apollos for your benefit, so that you may learn from us the meaning of the saying: “Nothing beyond what is written.” The purpose is that none of you will be arrogant, favoring one person over another.  
**4:7** For who makes you so superior? What do you have that you didn’t receive? If, in fact, you did receive it, why do you boast as if you hadn’t received it?  
**4:8** You are already full! You are already rich! You have begun to reign as kings without us—and I wish you did reign, so that we could also reign with you!  
**4:9** For I think God has displayed us, the apostles, in last place, like men condemned to die: We have become a spectacle to the world, both to angels and to people.  
**4:10** We are fools for Christ, but you are wise in Christ! We are weak, but you are strong! You are distinguished, but we are dishonored!  
**4:11** Up to the present hour we are both hungry and thirsty; we are poorly clothed, roughly treated, homeless;  
**4:12** we labor, working with our own hands. When we are reviled, we bless; when we are persecuted, we endure it;  
**4:13** when we are slandered, we respond graciously. Even now, we are like the scum of the earth, like everyone’s garbage.  
**4:14** I’m not writing this to shame you, but to warn you as my dear children.  
**4:15** For you may have countless instructors in Christ, but you don’t have many fathers. For I became your father in Christ Jesus through the gospel.  
**4:16** Therefore I urge you to imitate me.  
**4:17** This is why I have sent Timothy to you. He is my dearly loved and faithful child in the Lord. He will remind you about my ways in Christ Jesus, just as I teach everywhere in every church.  
**4:18** Now some are arrogant, as though I were not coming to you.  
**4:19** But I will come to you soon, if the Lord wills, and I will find out not the talk, but the power of those who are arrogant.  
**4:20** For the kingdom of God is not a matter of talk but of power.  
**4:21** What do you want? Should I come to you with a rod, or in love and a spirit of gentleness?  

## 5장

**5:1** It is actually reported that there is sexual immorality among you, and the kind of sexual immorality that is not even tolerated<sup>①</sup> among the Gentiles—a man is sleeping with his father’s wife.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss read <i>named</i>  
**5:2** And you are arrogant! Shouldn’t you be filled with grief and remove from your congregation the one who did this?  
**5:3** Even though I am absent in the body, I am present in spirit. As one who is present with you in this way, I have already pronounced judgment on the one who has been doing such a thing.  
**5:4** When you are assembled in the name of our Lord Jesus, and I am with you in spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus,  
**5:5** hand that one over to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, so that his spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord.  
**5:6** Your boasting is not good. Don’t you know that a little leaven<sup>①</sup> leavens the whole batch of dough?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>yeast</i>, also in vv. <a name='B:530 5:7'>7,<a name='B:530 5:8'>8

**5:8** Therefore, let us observe the feast, not with old leaven or with the leaven of malice and evil, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

## 6장

**6:1** If any of you has a dispute against another, how dare you take it to court before the unrighteous,<sup>①</sup> and not before the saints?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Unbelievers; v. 6  
**6:2** Or don’t you know that the saints will judge the world? And if the world is judged by you, are you unworthy to judge the trivial cases?  
**6:3** Don’t you know that we will judge angels—how much more matters of this life?  
**6:4** So if you have such matters, do you appoint as your judges those who have no standing in the church?  
**6:5** I say this to your shame! Can it be that there is not one wise person among you who is able to arbitrate between fellow believers?  
**6:6** Instead, brother goes to court against brother, and that before unbelievers!  
**6:7** As it is, to have legal disputes against one another is already a defeat for you. Why not rather be wronged? Why not rather be cheated?  
**6:8** Instead, you yourselves do wrong and cheat—and you do this to brothers and sisters!  
**6:9** Don’t you know that the unrighteous will not inherit God’s kingdom? Do not be deceived: No sexually immoral people, idolaters, adulterers, or males who have sex with males,<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Both passive and active participants in homosexual acts  
**6:10** no thieves, greedy people, drunkards, verbally abusive people, or swindlers will inherit God’s kingdom.  
**6:11** And some of you used to be like this. But you were washed, you were sanctified, you were justified in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and by the Spirit of our God.  
**6:12** “Everything is permissible for me,” but not everything is beneficial. “Everything is permissible for me,” but I will not be mastered by anything.  
**6:13** “Food is for the stomach and the stomach for food,” and God will do away with both of them. However, the body is not for sexual immorality but for the Lord, and the Lord for the body.  
**6:14** God raised up the Lord and will also raise us up by his power.  
**6:15** Don’t you know that your bodies are a part of Christ’s body? So should I take a part of Christ’s body and make it part of a prostitute? Absolutely not!  
**6:16** Don’t you know that anyone joined to a prostitute is one body with her? For Scripture says, <b>The two will become one flesh.</b><sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gn <a name='B:10 2:24'>2:24  
**6:17** But anyone joined to the Lord is one spirit with him.  
**6:18** Flee sexual immorality! Every other sin<sup>①</sup> a person commits is outside the body, but the person who is sexually immoral sins against his own body.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Every sin</i>  
**6:19** Don’t you know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have from God? You are not your own,  
**6:20** for you were bought at a price. So glorify God with your body.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss add <i>and in your spirit, which belong to God.</i>  

## 7장

**7:1** Now in response to the matters you wrote<sup>①</sup> about: “It is good for a man not to have sexual relations with a woman.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss add <i>to me</i>  
**7:2** But because sexual immorality is so common,<sup>①</sup> each man should have sexual relations with his own wife, and each woman should have sexual relations with her own husband.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>because of immoralities</i>  
**7:3** A husband should fulfill his marital duty to his wife, and likewise a wife to her husband.  
**7:4** A wife does not have the right over her own body, but her husband does. In the same way, a husband does not have the right over his own body, but his wife does.  
**7:5** Do not deprive one another—except when you agree for a time, to devote yourselves to<sup>①</sup> prayer. Then come together again; otherwise, Satan may tempt you because of your lack of self-control.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss add <i>fasting and to</i>  
**7:6** I say this as a concession, not as a command.  
**7:7** I wish that all people were as I am. But each has his own gift from God, one person has this gift, another has that.  
**7:8** I say to the unmarried<sup>①</sup> and to widows: It is good for them if they remain as I am.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>widowers</i>  
**7:9** But if they do not have self-control, they should marry, since it is better to marry than to burn with desire.  
**7:10** To the married I give this command—not I, but the Lord—a wife is not to leave<sup>①</sup> her husband.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>separate from</i>, or <i>divorce</i>  
**7:11** But if she does leave, she must remain unmarried or be reconciled to her husband—and a husband is not to divorce his wife.  
**7:12** But I (not the Lord) say to the rest: If any brother has an unbelieving wife and she is willing to live with him, he must not divorce her.  
**7:13** Also, if any woman has an unbelieving husband and he is willing to live with her, she must not divorce her husband.  
**7:14** For the unbelieving husband is made holy by the wife, and the unbelieving wife is made holy by the husband.<sup>①</sup> Otherwise your children would be unclean, but as it is they are holy.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>the brother</i>  
**7:15** But if the unbeliever leaves, let him leave. A brother or a sister is not bound in such cases. God has called you<sup>①</sup> to live in peace.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss read <i>us</i>  
**7:16** Wife, for all you know, you might save your husband. Husband, for all you know, you might save your wife.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Wife, how do you know that you will save your husband? Husband, how do you know that you will save your wife?</i>  
**7:17** Let each one live his life in the situation the Lord assigned when God called him.<sup>①</sup> This is what I command in all the churches.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>called each</i>  
**7:18** Was anyone already circumcised when he was called? He should not undo his circumcision. Was anyone called while uncircumcised? He should not get circumcised.  
**7:19** Circumcision does not matter and uncircumcision does not matter. Keeping God’s commands is what matters.  
**7:20** Let each of you remain in the situation<sup>①</sup> in which he was called.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in the calling</i>  
**7:21** Were you called while a slave? Don’t let it concern you. But if you can become free, by all means take the opportunity.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>But even though you can become free, make the most of your position as a slave</i>  
**7:22** For he who is called by the Lord as a slave is the Lord’s freedman. Likewise he who is called as a free man is Christ’s slave.  
**7:23** You were bought at a price; do not become slaves of people.  
**7:24** Brothers and sisters, each person is to remain with God in the situation in which he was called.  
**7:25** Now about virgins:<sup>①</sup> I have no command from the Lord, but I do give an opinion as one who by the Lord’s mercy is faithful.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>betrothed</i>, or <i>those not yet married</i>  
**7:26** Because of the present distress, I think that it is good for a man to remain as he is.  
**7:27** Are you bound to a wife? Do not seek to be released. Are you released from a wife? Do not seek a wife.  
**7:28** However, if you do get married, you have not sinned, and if a virgin<sup>①</sup> marries, she has not sinned. But such people will have trouble in this life,<sup>②</sup> and I am trying to spare you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>betrothed woman</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>in the flesh</i>  
**7:29** This is what I mean, brothers and sisters: The time is limited, so from now on those who have wives should be as though they had none,  
**7:30** those who weep as though they did not weep, those who rejoice as though they did not rejoice, those who buy as though they didn’t own anything,  
**7:31** and those who use the world as though they did not make full use of it. For this world in its current form is passing away.  
**7:32** I want you to be without concerns. The unmarried man is concerned about the things of the Lord—how he may please the Lord.  
**7:33** But the married man is concerned about the things of the world—how he may please his wife—  
**7:34** and his interests are divided. The unmarried woman or virgin is concerned about the things of the Lord, so that she may be holy both in body and in spirit. But the married woman is concerned about the things of the world—how she may please her husband.  
**7:35** I am saying this for your own benefit, not to put a restraint on you, but to promote what is proper and so that you may be devoted to the Lord without distraction.  
**7:36** If any man thinks he is acting improperly toward the virgin he is engaged to,<sup>①</sup> if she is getting beyond the usual age for marriage, and he feels he should marry—he can do what he wants. He is not sinning; they can get married.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>toward his virgin daughter</i>  
**7:37** But he who stands firm in his heart (who is under no compulsion, but has control over his own will) and has decided in his heart to keep her as his fiancée, will do well.  
**7:38** So, then, he who marries<sup>①</sup> his fiancée does well, but he who does not marry<sup>②</sup> will do better.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>marries off</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>marry her off</i>  
**7:39** A wife is bound<sup>①</sup> as long as her husband is living. But if her husband dies, she is free to be married to anyone she wants—only in the Lord.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss add <i>by law</i>  
**7:40** But she is happier if she remains as she is, in my opinion. And I think that I also have the Spirit of God.  

## 8장

**8:1** Now about food sacrificed to idols: We know that “we all have knowledge.” Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up.  
**8:2** If anyone thinks he knows anything, he does not yet know it as he ought to know it.  
**8:3** But if anyone loves God, he is known by him.  
**8:4** About eating food sacrificed to idols, then, we know that “an idol is nothing in the world,”<sup>①</sup> and that “there is no God but one.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>an idol has no real existence</i>  
**8:5** For even if there are so-called gods, whether in heaven or on earth—as there are many “gods” and many “lords”—  
**8:6** yet for us there is one God, the Father. All things are from him, and we exist for him. And there is one Lord, Jesus Christ. All things are through him, and we exist through him.  
**8:7** However, not everyone has this knowledge. Some have been so used to idolatry up until now that when they eat food sacrificed to an idol, their conscience, being weak, is defiled.

**12:4** Now there are different gifts, but the same Spirit.  
**12:5** There are different ministries, but the same Lord.  
**12:6** And there are different activities, but the same God works all of them in each person.  
**12:7** A manifestation of the Spirit is given to each person for the common good:

**12:25** so that there would be no division in the body, but that the members would have the same concern for each other.

**12:30** Do all have gifts of healing? Do all speak in tongues? Do all interpret?

## 15장

**15:1** Now I want to make clear for you, brothers and sisters, the gospel I preached to you, which you received, on which you have taken your stand  
**15:2** and by which you are being saved, if you hold to the message I preached to you—unless you believed in vain.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>believed without careful thought</i>, or <i>believed in vain</i>  
**15:3** For I passed on to you as most important what I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures,  
**15:4** that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures,  
**15:5** and that he appeared to Cephas, then to the Twelve.  
**15:6** Then he appeared to over five hundred brothers and sisters at one time; most of them are still alive, but some have fallen asleep.  
**15:7** Then he appeared to James, then to all the apostles.  
**15:8** Last of all, as to one born at the wrong time,<sup>①</sup> he also appeared to me.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>one whose birth was unusual</i>  
**15:9** For I am the least of the apostles, not worthy to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the church of God.  
**15:10** But by the grace of God I am what I am, and his grace toward me was not in vain. On the contrary, I worked harder than any of them, yet not I, but the grace of God that was with me.  
**15:11** Whether, then, it is I or they, so we proclaim and so you have believed.  
**15:12** Now if Christ is proclaimed as raised from the dead, how can some of you say, “There is no resurrection of the dead”?  
**15:13** If there is no resurrection of the dead, then not even Christ has been raised;  
**15:14** and if Christ has not been raised, then our proclamation is in vain, and so is your faith.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>proclamation is useless, and your faith also is useless</i>, or <i>proclamation is empty, and your faith also is empty</i>  
**15:15** Moreover, we are found to be false witnesses about God, because we have testified wrongly about God that he raised up Christ—whom he did not raise up, if in fact the dead are not raised.  
**15:16** For if the dead are not raised, not even Christ has been raised.  
**15:17** And if Christ has not been raised, your faith is worthless; you are still in your sins.  
**15:18** Those, then, who have fallen asleep in Christ have also perished.  
**15:19** If we have put our hope in Christ for this life only, we should be pitied more than anyone.  
**15:20** But as it is, Christ has been raised from the dead, the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep.  
**15:21** For since death came through a man, the resurrection of the dead also comes through a man.  
**15:22** For just as in Adam all die, so also in Christ all will be made alive.  
**15:23** But each in his own order: Christ, the firstfruits; afterward, at his coming, those who belong to Christ.  
**15:24** Then comes the end, when he hands over the kingdom to God the Father, when he abolishes all rule and all authority and power.  
**15:25** For he must reign until he puts all his enemies under his feet.  
**15:26** The last enemy to be abolished is death.  
**15:27** For <b>God has put everything under his feet.</b><sup>①</sup> Now when it says “everything” is put under him, it is obvious that he who puts everything under him is the exception.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ps 8:6; 110:1  
**15:28** When everything is subject to Christ, then the Son himself will also be subject to the one who subjected everything to him, so that God may be all in all.  
**15:29** Otherwise what will they do who are being baptized for the dead?<sup>①</sup> If the dead are not raised at all, then why are people baptized for them?<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>baptized on account of the dead</i> <sup>②</sup>Other mss read <i>for the dead</i>  
**15:30** Why are we in danger every hour?  
**15:31** I face death every day, as surely as I may boast about you, brothers and sisters, in Christ Jesus our Lord.  
**15:32** If I fought wild beasts in Ephesus as a mere man, what good did that do me? If the dead are not raised, <b>Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die.</b><sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Is <a name='B:290 22:13'>22:13  
**15:33** Do not be deceived: “Bad company corrupts good morals.”  
**15:34** Come to your senses<sup>①</sup> and stop sinning; for some people are ignorant about God. I say this to your shame.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Sober up</i>  
**15:35** But someone will ask, “How are the dead raised? What kind of body will they have when they come?”  
**15:36** You fool! What you sow does not come to life unless it dies.  
**15:37** And as for what you sow—you are not sowing the body that will be, but only a seed, perhaps of wheat or another grain.  
**15:38** But God gives it a body as he wants, and to each of the seeds its own body.  
**15:39** Not all flesh is the same flesh; there is one flesh for humans, another for animals, another for birds, and another for fish.  
**15:40** There are heavenly bodies and earthly bodies, but the splendor of the heavenly bodies is different from that of the earthly ones.  
**15:41** There is a splendor of the sun, another of the moon, and another of the stars; in fact, one star differs from another star in splendor.  
**15:42** So it is with the resurrection of the dead: Sown in corruption, raised in incorruption;  
**15:43** sown in dishonor, raised in glory; sown in weakness, raised in power;  
**15:44** sown a natural body, raised a spiritual body. If there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual body.  
**15:45** So it is written, <b>The first man Adam became a living being</b>;<sup>①</sup> the last Adam became a life-giving spirit.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gn <a name='B:10 2:7'>2:7  
**15:46** However, the spiritual is not first, but the natural, then the spiritual.  
**15:47** The first man was from the earth, a man of dust; the second man is<sup>①</sup> from heaven.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss add <i>the Lord</i>  
**15:48** Like the man of dust, so are those who are of the dust; like the man of heaven, so are those who are of heaven.  
**15:49** And just as we have borne the image of the man of dust, we will also bear the image of the man of heaven.  
**15:50** What I am saying, brothers and sisters, is this: Flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of God, nor can corruption inherit incorruption.  
**15:51** Listen, I am telling you a mystery: We will not all fall asleep, but we will all be changed,  
**15:52** in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet. For the trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised incorruptible, and we will be changed.  
**15:53** For this corruptible body must be clothed with incorruptibility, and this mortal body must be clothed with immortality.  
**15:54** When this corruptible body is clothed with incorruptibility, and this mortal body is clothed with immortality, then the saying that is written will take place:<br><b>Death has been swallowed up</b> <b>in victory.</b><sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Is <a name='B:290 25:8'>25:8  
**15:55** <b>Where, death, is your victory?</b><br><b>Where, death, is your sting?</b><sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hs <a name='B:350 13:14'>13:14  
**15:56** The sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the law.  
**15:57** But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ!  
**15:58** Therefore, my dear brothers and sisters, be steadfast, immovable, always excelling in the Lord’s work, because you know that your labor in the Lord is not in vain.  

## 16장

**16:1** Now about the collection for the saints: Do the same as I instructed the Galatian churches.  
**16:2** On the first day of the week, each of you is to set something aside and save in keeping with how he is prospering, so that no collections will need to be made when I come.  
**16:3** When I arrive, I will send with letters those you recommend to carry your gift to Jerusalem.  
**16:4** If it is suitable for me to go as well, they will travel with me.  
**16:5** I will come to you after I pass through Macedonia—for I will be traveling through Macedonia—  
**16:6** and perhaps I will remain with you or even spend the winter, so that you may send me on my way wherever I go.  
**16:7** I don’t want to see you now just in passing, since I hope to spend some time with you, if the Lord allows.  
**16:8** But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost,  
**16:9** because a wide door for effective ministry has opened for me<sup>①</sup>—yet many oppose me.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>door has opened to me, great and effective</i>  
**16:10** If Timothy comes, see that he has nothing to fear while with you, because he is doing the Lord’s work, just as I am.  
**16:11** So let no one look down on him. Send him on his way in peace so that he can come to me, because I am expecting him with the brothers.  
**16:12** Now about our brother Apollos: I strongly urged him to come to you with the brothers, but he was not at all willing to come now. However, he will come when he has an opportunity.  
**16:13** Be alert, stand firm in the faith, be courageous,<sup>①</sup> be strong.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>act like men</i>  
**16:14** Do everything in love.  
**16:15** Brothers and sisters, you know the household of Stephanas: They are the firstfruits of Achaia and have devoted themselves to serving the saints. I urge you  
**16:16** also to submit to such people, and to everyone who works and labors with them.  
**16:17** I am delighted to have Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus present, because these men have made up for your absence.  
**16:18** For they have refreshed my spirit and yours. Therefore recognize such people.  
**16:19** The churches of Asia send you greetings. Aquila and Priscilla send you greetings warmly in the Lord, along with the church that meets in their home.  
**16:20** All the brothers and sisters send you greetings. Greet one another with a holy kiss.  
**16:21** This greeting is in my own hand—Paul.  
**16:22** If anyone does not love the Lord, a curse be on him. Our Lord, come!<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Aramaic <i>Marana tha</i>  
**16:23** The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.  
**16:24** My love be with all of you in Christ Jesus.
