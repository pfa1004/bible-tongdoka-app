# 20.잠언 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** The proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel:  
**1:2** For learning wisdom and discipline;<br>for understanding insightful sayings;  
**1:3** for receiving prudent instruction<br>in righteousness, justice, and integrity;  
**1:4** for teaching shrewdness to the inexperienced,<sup>①</sup><br>knowledge and discretion to a young man—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>simple</i>, or <i>gullible</i>  
**1:5** let a wise person listen and increase learning,<br>and let a discerning person obtain guidance—  
**1:6** for understanding a proverb or a parable,<sup>①</sup><br>the words of the wise, and their riddles.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>an enigma</i>  
**1:7** The fear of the LORD<br>is the beginning of knowledge;<br>fools despise wisdom and discipline.  
**1:8** Listen, my son, to your father’s instruction,<br>and don’t reject your mother’s teaching,  
**1:9** for they will be a garland of favor on your head<br>and pendants<sup>①</sup> around your neck.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>chains</i>  
**1:10** My son, if sinners entice you,<br>don’t be persuaded.  
**1:11** If they say—“Come with us!<br>Let’s set an ambush and kill someone.<sup>①</sup><br>Let’s attack some innocent person just for fun!<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Let’s ambush for blood</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>person for no reason</i>  
**1:12** Let’s swallow them alive, like Sheol,<br>whole, like those who go down to the Pit.  
**1:13** We’ll find all kinds of valuable property<br>and fill our houses with plunder.  
**1:14** Throw in your lot with us,<br>and we’ll all share the loot”<sup>①</sup>—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>us; one bag will be for all of us</i>  
**1:15** my son, don’t travel that road with them<br>or set foot on their path,  
**1:16** because their feet run toward evil<br>and they hurry to shed blood.  
**1:17** It is useless to spread a net<br>where any bird can see it,  
**1:18** but they set an ambush to kill themselves;<sup>①</sup><br>they attack their own lives.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>they ambush for their blood</i>  
**1:19** Such are the paths of all who make profit dishonestly;<br>it takes the lives of those who receive it.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>takes the life of its masters</i>  
**1:20** Wisdom calls out in the street;<br>she makes her voice heard in the public squares.  
**1:21** She cries out above<sup>①</sup> the commotion;<br>she speaks at the entrance of the city gates:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>at the head of</i>  
**1:22** “How long, inexperienced ones, will you love ignorance?<br>How long will you mockers enjoy mocking<br>and you fools hate knowledge?  
**1:23** If you respond to my warning,<sup>①</sup><br>then I will pour out my spirit on you<br>and teach you my words.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>you turn back to my reprimand</i>  
**1:24** Since I called out and you refused,<br>extended my hand and no one paid attention,  
**1:25** since you neglected all my counsel<br>and did not accept my correction,  
**1:26** I, in turn, will laugh at your calamity.<br>I will mock when terror strikes you,  
**1:27** when terror strikes you like a storm<br>and your calamity comes like a whirlwind,<br>when trouble and stress overcome you.  
**1:28** Then they will call me, but I won’t answer;<br>they will search for me, but won’t find me.  
**1:29** Because they hated knowledge,<br>didn’t choose to fear the LORD,  
**1:30** were not interested in my counsel,<br>and rejected all my correction,  
**1:31** they will eat the fruit of their way<br>and be glutted with their own schemes.  
**1:32** For the apostasy of the inexperienced will kill them,<br>and the complacency of fools will destroy them.  
**1:33** But whoever listens to me will live securely<br>and be undisturbed by the dread of danger.”  

## 2장

**2:1** My son, if you accept my words<br>and store up my commands within you,  
**2:2** listening closely<sup>①</sup> to wisdom<br>and directing your heart to understanding;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>you, stretching out your ear</i>  
**2:3** furthermore, if you call out to insight<br>and lift your voice to understanding,  
**2:4** if you seek it like silver<br>and search for it like hidden treasure,  
**2:5** then you will understand the fear of the LORD<br>and discover the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For the LORD gives wisdom;<br>from his mouth come knowledge and understanding.  
**2:7** He stores up success<sup>①</sup> for the upright;<br>He is a shield for those who live with integrity<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>resourcefulness</i>  
**2:8** so that he may guard the paths of justice<br>and protect the way of his faithful followers.  
**2:9** Then you will understand righteousness, justice,<br>and integrity—every good path.  
**2:10** For wisdom will enter your heart,<br>and knowledge will delight you.  
**2:11** Discretion will watch over you,<br>and understanding will guard you.  
**2:12** It will rescue you from the way of evil—<br>from anyone who says perverse things,  
**2:13** from those who abandon the right paths<br>to walk in ways of darkness,  
**2:14** from those who enjoy doing evil<br>and celebrate perversion,  
**2:15** whose paths are crooked,<br>and whose ways are devious.  
**2:16** It will rescue you from a forbidden woman,<br>from a wayward woman with her flattering talk,  
**2:17** who abandons the companion of her youth<br>and forgets the covenant of her God;  
**2:18** for her house sinks down to death<br>and her ways to the land of the departed spirits.  
**2:19** None return who go to her;<br>none reach the paths of life.  
**2:20** So follow the way of the good,<br>and keep to the paths of the righteous.  
**2:21** For the upright will inhabit the land,<br>and those of integrity will remain in it;  
**2:22** but the wicked will be cut off from the land,<br>and the treacherous ripped out of it.  

## 3장

**3:1** My son, don’t forget my teaching,<br>but let your heart keep my commands;  
**3:2** for they will bring you<br>many days, a full life,<sup>①</sup> and well-being.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>days, years of life</i>  
**3:3** Never let loyalty and faithfulness leave you.<br>Tie them around your neck;<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**3:4** Then you will find favor and high regard<br>with God and people.  
**3:5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart,<br>and do not rely on your own understanding;  
**3:6** in all your ways know him,<br>and he will make your paths straight.  
**3:7** Don’t be wise in your own eyes;<br>fear the LORD and turn away from evil.  
**3:8** This will be healing for your body<sup>①</sup><br>and strengthening for your bones.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>navel</i>  
**3:9** Honor the LORD with your possessions<br>and with the first produce of your entire harvest;  
**3:10** then your barns will be completely filled,<br>and your vats will overflow with new wine.  
**3:11** Do not despise the LORD’s instruction, my son,<br>and do not loathe his discipline;  
**3:12** for the LORD disciplines the one he loves,<br>just as a father disciplines the son in whom he delights.  
**3:13** Happy is a man who finds wisdom<br>and who acquires understanding,  
**3:14** for she is more profitable than silver,<br>and her revenue is better than gold.  
**3:15** She is more precious than jewels;<br>nothing you desire can equal her.  
**3:16** Long life<sup>①</sup> is in her right hand;<br>in her left, riches and honor.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Length of days</i>  
**3:17** Her ways are pleasant,<br>and all her paths, peaceful.  
**3:18** She is a tree of life to those who embrace her,<br>and those who hold on to her are happy.  
**3:19** The LORD founded the earth by wisdom<br>and established the heavens by understanding.  
**3:20** By his knowledge the watery depths broke open,<br>and the clouds dripped with dew.  
**3:21** Maintain sound wisdom and discretion.<br>My son, don’t lose sight of them.  
**3:22** They will be life for you<sup>①</sup><br>and adornment<sup>②</sup> for your neck.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>be your throat</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>grace</i>  
**3:23** Then you will go safely on your way;<br>your foot will not stumble.  
**3:24** When you lie<sup>①</sup> down, you will not be afraid;<br>you will lie down, and your sleep will be pleasant.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>LXX reads <i>sit</i>  
**3:25** Don’t fear sudden danger<br>or the ruin of the wicked when it comes,  
**3:26** for the LORD will be your confidence<sup>①</sup><br>and will keep your foot from a snare.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>be at your side</i>  
**3:27** When it is in your power,<sup>①</sup><br>don’t withhold good from the one to whom it belongs.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>in the power of your hands</i>  
**3:28** Don’t say to your neighbor, “Go away! Come back later.<br>I’ll give it tomorrow”—when it is there with you.  
**3:29** Don’t plan any harm against your neighbor,<br>for he trusts you and lives near you.  
**3:30** Don’t accuse anyone without cause,<br>when he has done you no harm.  
**3:31** Don’t envy a violent man<br>or choose any of his ways;  
**3:32** for the devious are detestable to the LORD,<br>but he is a friend<sup>①</sup> to the upright.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>confidential counsel</i>  
**3:33** The LORD’s curse is on the household of the wicked,<br>but he blesses the home of the righteous;  
**3:34** He mocks those who mock<br>but gives grace to the humble.  
**3:35** The wise will inherit honor,<br>but he holds up fools to dishonor.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>but haughty fools dishonor</i>, or <i>but fools exalt dishonor</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** Listen, sons, to a father’s discipline,<br>and pay attention so that you may gain understanding,  
**4:2** for I am giving you good instruction.<br>Don’t abandon my teaching.  
**4:3** When I was a son with my father,<br>tender and precious to my mother,  
**4:4** he taught me and said,<br>“Your heart must hold on to my words.<br>Keep my commands and live.  
**4:5** Get wisdom, get understanding;<br>don’t forget or turn away from the words from my mouth.  
**4:6** Don’t abandon wisdom, and she will watch over you;<br>love her, and she will guard you.  
**4:7** Wisdom is supreme—so get wisdom.<br>And whatever else you get, get understanding.  
**4:8** Cherish her, and she will exalt you;<br>if you embrace her, she will honor you.  
**4:9** She will place a garland of favor on your head;<br>she will give you a crown of beauty.”  
**4:10** Listen, my son. Accept my words,<br>and you will live many years.  
**4:11** I am teaching you the way of wisdom;<br>I am guiding you on straight paths.  
**4:12** When you walk, your steps will not be hindered;<br>when you run, you will not stumble.  
**4:13** Hold on to instruction; don’t let go.<br>Guard it, for it is your life.  
**4:14** Keep off the path of the wicked;<br>don’t proceed on the way of evil ones.  
**4:15** Avoid it; don’t travel on it.<br>Turn away from it, and pass it by.  
**4:16** For they can’t sleep<br>unless they have done what is evil;<br>they are robbed of sleep<br>unless they make someone stumble.  
**4:17** They eat the bread of wickedness<br>and drink the wine of violence.  
**4:18** The path of the righteous is like the light of dawn,<br>shining brighter and brighter until midday.  
**4:19** But the way of the wicked is like the darkest gloom;<br>they don’t know what makes them stumble.  
**4:20** My son, pay attention to my words;<br>listen closely to my sayings.  
**4:21** Don’t lose sight of them;<br>keep them within your heart.  
**4:22** For they are life to those who find them,<br>and health to one’s whole body.  
**4:23** Guard your heart above all else,<sup>①</sup><br>for it is the source of life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>heart with all diligence</i>  
**4:24** Don’t let your mouth speak dishonestly,<br>and don’t let your lips talk deviously.  
**4:25** Let your eyes look forward;<br>fix your gaze<sup>①</sup> straight ahead.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>eyelids</i>  
**4:26** Carefully consider the path<sup>①</sup> for your feet,<br>and all your ways will be established.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Clear a path</i>  
**4:27** Don’t turn to the right or to the left;<br>keep your feet away from evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** My son, pay attention to my wisdom;<br>listen closely<sup>①</sup> to my understanding<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>wisdom; stretch out your ear</i>  
**5:2** so that you may maintain discretion<br>and your lips safeguard knowledge.  
**5:3** Though the lips of the forbidden woman drip honey<br>and her words are<sup>①</sup> smoother than oil,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>her palate is</i>  
**5:4** in the end she’s as bitter as wormwood<br>and as sharp as a double-edged sword.  
**5:5** Her feet go down to death;<br>her steps head straight for Sheol.  
**5:6** She doesn’t consider the path of life;<br>she doesn’t know that her ways are unstable.  
**5:7** So now, sons, listen to me,<br>and don’t turn away from the words from my mouth.  
**5:8** Keep your way far from her.<br>Don’t go near the door of her house.  
**5:9** Otherwise, you will give up your vitality to others<br>and your years to someone cruel;  
**5:10** strangers will drain your resources,<br>and your hard-earned pay will end up in a foreigner’s house.  
**5:11** At the end of your life, you will lament<br>when your physical body has been consumed,  
**5:12** and you will say, “How I hated discipline,<br>and how my heart despised correction.  
**5:13** I didn’t obey my teachers<br>or listen closely<sup>①</sup> to my instructors.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>or turn my ear</i>  
**5:14** I am on the verge of complete ruin<br>before the entire community.”  
**5:15** Drink water from your own cistern,<br>water flowing from your own well.  
**5:16** Should your springs flow in the streets,<br>streams in the public squares?  
**5:17** They should be for you alone<br>and not for you to share with strangers.  
**5:18** Let your fountain be blessed,<br>and take pleasure in the wife of your youth.  
**5:19** A loving deer, a graceful doe<sup>①</sup>—<br>let her breasts always satisfy you;<br>be lost in her love forever.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>graceful mountain goat</i>  
**5:20** Why, my son, would you lose yourself<br>with a forbidden woman<br>or embrace a wayward woman?  
**5:21** For a man’s ways are before the LORD’s eyes,<br>and he considers all his paths.  
**5:22** A wicked man’s iniquities will trap him;<br>he will become tangled in the ropes of his own sin.  
**5:23** He will die because there is no discipline,<br>and be lost because of his great stupidity.  

## 6장

**6:1** My son, if you have put up security for your neighbor<sup>①</sup><br>or entered into an agreement with<sup>②</sup> a stranger,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>friend</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>or slapped hands for</i>  
**6:2** you have been snared by the words of your mouth<br>trapped by the words from your mouth.  
**6:3** Do this, then, my son, and free yourself,<br>for you have put yourself in your neighbor’s power:<br>Go, humble yourself, and plead with your neighbor.  
**6:4** Don’t give sleep to your eyes<br>or slumber to your eyelids.  
**6:5** Escape like a gazelle from a hunter,<sup>①</sup><br>like a bird from a hunter’s trap.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>hand</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>hand</i>  
**6:6** Go to the ant, you slacker!<br>Observe its ways and become wise.  
**6:7** Without leader, administrator, or ruler,  
**6:8** it prepares its provisions in summer;<br>it gathers its food during harvest.  
**6:9** How long will you stay in bed, you slacker?<br>When will you get up from your sleep?  
**6:10** A little sleep, a little slumber,<br>a little folding of the arms to rest,  
**6:11** and your poverty will come like a robber,<br>your need, like a bandit.  
**6:12** A worthless person, a wicked man<br>goes around speaking dishonestly,  
**6:13** winking his eyes, signaling with his feet,<br>and gesturing with his fingers.  
**6:14** He always plots evil with perversity in his heart;<br>he stirs up trouble.  
**6:15** Therefore calamity will strike him suddenly;<br>he will be shattered instantly, beyond recovery.  
**6:16** The LORD hates six things;<br>in fact, seven are detestable to him:  
**6:17** arrogant eyes, a lying tongue,<br>hands that shed innocent blood,  
**6:18** a heart that plots wicked schemes,<br>feet eager to run to evil,  
**6:19** a lying witness who gives false testimony,<br>and one who stirs up trouble among brothers.  
**6:20** My son, keep your father’s command,<br>and don’t reject your mother’s teaching.  
**6:21** Always bind them to your heart;<br>tie them around your neck.  
**6:22** When you walk here and there, they will guide you;<br>when you lie down, they will watch over you;<br>when you wake up, they will talk to you.  
**6:23** For a command is a lamp, teaching is a light,<br>and corrective discipline is the way to life.  
**6:24** They will protect you from an evil woman,<sup>①</sup><br>from the flattering tongue of a wayward woman.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>LXX reads <i>from a married woman</i>  
**6:25** Don’t lust in your heart for her beauty<br>or let her captivate you with her eyelashes.  
**6:26** For a prostitute’s fee is only a loaf of bread,<sup>①</sup><br>but the wife of another man<sup>②</sup> goes after a precious life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>On account of a prostitute, one is left with only a loaf of bread</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>but a wife of a man</i>  
**6:27** Can a man embrace fire<br>and his clothes not be burned?  
**6:28** Can a man walk on burning coals<br>without scorching his feet?  
**6:29** So it is with the one who sleeps with<br>another man’s wife;<br>no one who touches her will go unpunished.  
**6:30** People don’t despise the thief if he steals<br>to satisfy himself when he is hungry.  
**6:31** Still, if caught, he must pay seven times as much;<br>he must give up all the wealth in his house.  
**6:32** The one who commits adultery<sup>①</sup> lacks sense;<br>whoever does so destroys himself.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>commits adultery with a woman</i>  
**6:33** He will get a beating<sup>①</sup> and dishonor,<br>and his disgrace will never be removed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>plague</i>  
**6:34** For jealousy enrages a husband,<br>and he will show no mercy when he takes revenge.  
**6:35** He will not be appeased by anything<br>or be persuaded by lavish bribes.  

## 7장

**7:3** Tie them to your fingers;<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,”<br>and call understanding your relative.  
**7:5** She will keep you from a forbidden woman,<br>a wayward woman with her flattering talk.  
**7:6** At the window of my house<br>I looked through my lattice.  
**7:7** I saw among the inexperienced,<sup>①</sup><br>I noticed among the youths,<br>a young man lacking sense.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>simple</i>, or <i>gullible</i>, or <i>naive</i>

## 8장

**8:1** Doesn’t wisdom call out?<br>Doesn’t understanding make her voice heard?  
**8:2** At the heights overlooking the road,<br>at the crossroads, she takes her stand.  
**8:3** Beside the gates leading into the city,<br>at the main entrance, she cries out:  
**8:4** “People, I call out to you;<br>my cry is to the children of Adam.  
**8:5** Learn to be shrewd, you who are inexperienced;<br>develop common sense, you who are foolish.  
**8:6** Listen, for I speak of noble things,<br>and what my lips say is right.  
**8:7** For my mouth tells the truth,<br>and wickedness is detestable to my lips.  
**8:8** All the words from my mouth are righteous;<br>none of them are deceptive or perverse.  
**8:9** All of them are clear to the perceptive,<br>and right to those who discover knowledge.  
**8:10** Accept my instruction instead of silver,<br>and knowledge rather than pure gold.  
**8:11** For wisdom is better than jewels,<br>and nothing desirable can equal it.  
**8:12** I, wisdom, share a home with shrewdness<br>and have knowledge and discretion.  
**8:13** To fear the LORD is to hate evil.<br>I hate arrogant pride, evil conduct,<br>and perverse speech.  
**8:14** I possess good advice and sound wisdom;<sup>①</sup><br>I have understanding and strength.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>resourcefulness</i>  
**8:15** It is by me that kings reign<br>and rulers enact just law;  
**8:16** by me, princes lead,<br>as do nobles and all righteous judges.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some Hb mss, LXX read <i>nobles who judge the earth</i>  
**8:17** I love those who love me,<br>and those who search for me find me.  
**8:18** With me are riches and honor,<br>lasting wealth and righteousness.  
**8:19** My fruit is better than solid gold,<br>and my harvest than pure silver.  
**8:20** I walk in the ways of righteousness,<br>along the paths of justice,  
**8:21** giving wealth as an inheritance to those who love me,<br>and filling their treasuries.
