## 2장

**2:1** On the third day a wedding took place in Cana of Galilee. Jesus’s mother was there,  
**2:2** and Jesus and his disciples were invited to the wedding as well.  
**2:3** When the wine ran out, Jesus’s mother told him, “They don’t have any wine.”  
**2:4** “What has this concern of yours to do with me,<sup>①</sup> woman?” Jesus asked. “My hour has not yet come.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>“What does that have to do with you and me;</i> lit <i>“What to me and to you;</i> Mt 8:29; Mk 1:24; 5:7; Lk 8:28  
**2:5** “Do whatever he tells you,” his mother told the servants.  
**2:6** Now six stone water jars had been set there for Jewish purification. Each contained twenty or thirty gallons.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>two or three measures</i>  
**2:7** “Fill the jars with water,” Jesus told them. So they filled them to the brim.  
**2:8** Then he said to them, “Now draw some out and take it to the headwaiter.”<sup>①</sup> And they did.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>ruler of the table</i>    
**2:9** When the headwaiter tasted the water (after it had become wine), he did not know where it came from—though the servants who had drawn the water knew. He called the groom  
**2:10** and told him, “Everyone sets out the fine wine first, then, after people are drunk, the inferior. But you have kept the fine wine until now.”  
**2:11** Jesus did this, the first of his signs, in Cana of Galilee. He revealed his glory, and his disciples believed in him.  
**2:12** After this, he went down to Capernaum, together with his mother, his brothers, and his disciples, and they stayed there only a few days.  
**2:13** The Jewish Passover was near, and so Jesus went up to Jerusalem.  
**2:14** In the temple he found people selling oxen, sheep, and doves, and he also found the money changers sitting there.  
**2:15** After making a whip out of cords, he drove everyone out of the temple with their sheep and oxen. He also poured out the money changers’ coins and overturned the tables.  
**2:16** He told those who were selling doves, “Get these things out of here! Stop turning my Father’s house into a marketplace!”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>a house of business</i>  
**2:17** And his disciples remembered that it is written: <b>Zeal</b> <b>for your house will consume</b> <b>me.</b><sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ps <a name='B:230 69:9'>69:9  
**2:18** So the Jews replied to him, “What sign will you show us for doing these things?”  
**2:19** Jesus answered, “Destroy this temple,<sup>①</sup> and I will raise it up in three days.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sanctuary</i>, also in vv. <a name='B:500 2:20'>20,<a name='B:500 2:21'>21  
**2:20** Therefore the Jews said, “This temple took forty-six years to build,<sup>①</sup> and will you raise it up in three days?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>was built forty-six years ago</i>  
**2:21** But he was speaking about the temple of his body.  
**2:22** So when he was raised from the dead, his disciples remembered that he had said this, and they believed the Scripture and the statement Jesus had made.  
**2:23** While he was in Jerusalem during the Passover Festival, many believed in his name when they saw the signs he was doing.  
**2:24** Jesus, however, would not entrust himself to them, since he knew them all  
**2:25** and because he did not need anyone to testify about man; for he himself knew what was in man.  

## 3장

**3:1** There was a man from the Pharisees named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews.  
**3:2** This man came to him at night and said, “Rabbi, we know that you are a teacher who has come from God, for no one could perform these signs you do unless God were with him.”  
**3:3** Jesus replied, “Truly I tell you, unless someone is born again,<sup>①</sup> he cannot see the kingdom of God.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>from above</i>, also in v. <a name='B:500 3:7'>7

**3:5** Jesus answered, “Truly I tell you, unless someone is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God.  
**3:6** Whatever is born of the flesh is flesh, and whatever is born of the Spirit is spirit.  
**3:7** Do not be amazed that I told you that you must be born again.  
**3:8** The wind blows where it pleases, and you hear its sound, but you don’t know where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit.”  
**3:9** “How can these things be?” asked Nicodemus.  
**3:10** “Are you a teacher<sup>①</sup> of Israel and don’t know these things?” Jesus replied.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the teacher</i>  
**3:11** “Truly I tell you, we speak what we know and we testify to what we have seen, but you do not accept our testimony.  
**3:12** If I have told you about earthly things and you don’t believe, how will you believe if I tell you about heavenly things?  
**3:13** No one has ascended into heaven except the one who descended from heaven— the Son of Man.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss add <i>who is in heaven</i>  
**3:14** “Just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up,  
**3:15** so that everyone who believes in him may<sup>①</sup> have eternal life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss add <i>not perish, but</i>  
**3:16** For God loved the world in this way:<sup>①</sup> He gave<sup>②</sup> his one and only Son, so that everyone who believes in him will not perish but have eternal life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>this much</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>For in this way God loved the world, and so he gave</i>, or <i>For God so loved the world that he gave</i>

**3:18** Anyone who believes in him is not condemned, but anyone who does not believe is already condemned, because he has not believed in the name of the one and only Son of God.  
**3:19** This is the judgment: The light has come into the world, and people loved darkness rather than the light because their deeds were evil.  
**3:20** For everyone who does evil hates the light and avoids it,<sup>①</sup> so that his deeds may not be exposed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and does not come to the light</i>

**3:24** since John had not yet been thrown into prison.  
**3:25** Then a dispute arose between John’s disciples and a Jew<sup>①</sup> about purification.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss read <i>and the Jews</i>  
**3:26** So they came to John and told him, “Rabbi, the one you testified about, and who was with you across the Jordan, is baptizing—and everyone is going to him.”  
**3:27** John responded, “No one can receive anything unless it has been given to him from heaven.  
**3:28** You yourselves can testify that I said, ‘I am not the Messiah, but I’ve been sent ahead of him.’  
**3:29** He who has the bride is the groom. But the groom’s friend, who stands by and listens for him, rejoices greatly<sup>①</sup> at the groom’s voice. So this joy of mine is complete.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>with joy rejoices</i>  
**3:30** He must increase, but I must decrease.”  
**3:31** The one who comes from above is above all. The one who is from the earth is earthly and speaks in earthly terms.<sup>①</sup> The one who comes from heaven is above all.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>of earthly things</i>

**3:33** The one who has accepted his testimony has affirmed that God is true.  
**3:34** For the one whom God sent speaks God’s words, since he<sup>①</sup> gives the Spirit without measure.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss read <i>since God</i>

**3:36** The one who believes in the Son has eternal life, but the one who rejects the Son<sup>①</sup> will not see life; instead, the wrath of God remains on him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>refuses to believe in the Son</i>, or <i>disobeys the Son</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** When Jesus<sup>①</sup> learned that the Pharisees had heard he was making and baptizing more disciples than John<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss read <i>the Lord</i>  
**4:2** (though Jesus himself was not baptizing, but his disciples were),  
**4:3** he left Judea and went again to Galilee.  
**4:4** He had to travel through Samaria;  
**4:5** so he came to a town of Samaria called Sychar near the property<sup>①</sup> that Jacob had given his son Joseph.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>piece of land</i>  
**4:6** Jacob’s well was there, and Jesus, worn out from his journey, sat down at the well. It was about noon.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>about the sixth hour</i>  
**4:7** A woman of Samaria came to draw water. “Give me a drink,” Jesus said to her,  
**4:8** because his disciples had gone into town to buy food.

**4:13** Jesus said, “Everyone who drinks from this water will get thirsty again.

**4:18** “For you’ve had five husbands, and the man you now have is not your husband. What you have said is true.”  
**4:19** “Sir,” the woman replied, “I see that you are a prophet.

**4:26** Jesus told her, “I, the one speaking to you, am he.”  
**4:27** Just then his disciples arrived, and they were amazed that he was talking with a woman. Yet no one said, “What do you want?” or “Why are you talking with her?”  
**4:28** Then the woman left her water jar, went into town, and told the people,  
**4:29** “Come, see a man who told me everything I ever did. Could this be the Messiah?”  
**4:30** They left the town and made their way to him.  
**4:31** In the meantime the disciples kept urging him, “Rabbi, eat something.”  
**4:32** But he said, “I have food to eat that you don’t know about.”  
**4:33** The disciples said to one another, “Could someone have brought him something to eat?”  
**4:34** “My food is to do the will of him who sent me and to finish his work,” Jesus told them.  
**4:35** “Don’t you say, ‘There are still four more months, and then comes the harvest’? Listen to what I’m telling you: Open<sup>①</sup> your eyes and look at the fields, because they are ready<sup>②</sup> for harvest.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Raise</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>white</i>

**4:37** For in this case the saying is true: ‘One sows and another reaps.’  
**4:38** I sent you to reap what you didn’t labor for; others have labored, and you have benefited from<sup>①</sup> their labor.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>you have entered into</i>

**4:43** After two days he left there for Galilee.  
**4:44** (Jesus himself had testified that a prophet has no honor in his own country.)  
**4:45** When they entered Galilee, the Galileans welcomed him because they had seen everything he did in Jerusalem during the festival. For they also had gone to the festival.  
**4:46** He went again to Cana of Galilee, where he had turned the water into wine. There was a certain royal official whose son was ill at Capernaum.  
**4:47** When this man heard that Jesus had come from Judea into Galilee, he went to him and pleaded with him to come down and heal his son, since he was about to die.  
**4:48** Jesus told him, “Unless you people see signs and wonders, you will not believe.”  
**4:49** “Sir,” the official said to him, “come down before my boy dies.”

**4:53** The father realized this was the very hour at which Jesus had told him, “Your son will live.” So he himself believed, along with his whole household.  
**4:54** Now this was also the second sign Jesus performed after he came from Judea to Galilee.  

## 5장

**5:1** After this, a Jewish festival took place, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.

Then the first one who got in after the water was stirred up recovered from whatever ailment he had.</i>  
**5:4**   
**5:5** One man was there who had been disabled for thirty-eight years.  
**5:6** When Jesus saw him lying there and realized he had already been there a long time, he said to him, “Do you want to get well?”  
**5:7** “Sir,” the disabled man answered, “I have no one to put me into the pool when the water is stirred up, but while I’m coming, someone goes down ahead of me.”  
**5:8** “Get up,” Jesus told him, “pick up your mat and walk.”  
**5:9** Instantly the man got well, picked up his mat, and started to walk. Now that day was the Sabbath,  
**5:10** and so the Jews said to the man who had been healed, “This is the Sabbath. The law prohibits you from picking up your mat.”  
**5:11** He replied, “The man who made me well told me, ‘Pick up your mat and walk.’”  
**5:12** “Who is this man who told you, ‘Pick up your mat and walk’?” they asked.  
**5:13** But the man who was healed did not know who it was, because Jesus had slipped away into the crowd that was there.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>slipped away, there being a crowd in that place</i>
