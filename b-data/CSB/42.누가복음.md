# 42.누가복음 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** Many have undertaken to compile a narrative about the events that have been fulfilled<sup>①</sup> among us,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>events that have been accomplished</i>, or <i>events most surely believed</i>  
**1:2** just as the original eyewitnesses and servants of the word handed them down to us.  
**1:3** So it also seemed good to me, since I have carefully investigated everything from the very first, to write to you in an orderly sequence, most honorable Theophilus,  
**1:4** so that you may know the certainty of the things about which you have been instructed.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>informed</i>  
**1:5** In the days of King Herod of Judea, there was a priest of Abijah’s division named Zechariah. His wife was from the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth.  
**1:6** Both were righteous in God’s sight, living without blame according to all the commands and requirements of the Lord.  
**1:7** But they had no children because Elizabeth could not conceive, and both of them were well along in years.  
**1:8** When his division was on duty and he was serving as priest before God,  
**1:9** it happened that he was chosen by lot, according to the custom of the priesthood, to enter the sanctuary of the Lord and burn incense.  
**1:10** At the hour of incense the whole assembly of the people was praying outside.  
**1:11** An angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing to the right of the altar of incense.  
**1:12** When Zechariah saw him, he was terrified and overcome with fear.  
**1:13** But the angel said to him, “Do not be afraid, Zechariah, because your prayer has been heard. Your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you will name him John.  
**1:14** There will be joy and delight for you, and many will rejoice at his birth.  
**1:15** For he will be great in the sight of the Lord and will never drink wine or beer. He will be filled with the Holy Spirit while still in his mother’s womb.  
**1:16** He will turn many of the children of Israel to the Lord their God.  
**1:17** And he will go before him in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of fathers to their children, and the disobedient to the understanding of the righteous, to make ready for the Lord a prepared people.”  
**1:18** “How can I know this?” Zechariah asked the angel. “For I am an old man, and my wife is well along in years.”  
**1:19** The angel answered him, “I am Gabriel, who stands in the presence of God, and I was sent to speak to you and tell you this good news.  
**1:20** Now listen. You will become silent and unable to speak until the day these things take place, because you did not believe my words, which will be fulfilled in their proper time.”  
**1:21** Meanwhile, the people were waiting for Zechariah, amazed that he stayed so long in the sanctuary.  
**1:22** When he did come out, he could not speak to them. Then they realized that he had seen a vision in the sanctuary. He was making signs to them and remained speechless.  
**1:23** When the days of his ministry were completed, he went back home.  
**1:24** After these days his wife Elizabeth conceived and kept herself in seclusion for five months. She said,  
**1:25** “The Lord has done this for me. He has looked with favor in these days to take away my disgrace among the people.”  
**1:26** In the sixth month, the angel Gabriel was sent by God to a town in Galilee called Nazareth,  
**1:27** to a virgin engaged<sup>①</sup> to a man named Joseph, of the house of David. The virgin’s name was Mary.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>betrothed</i>  
**1:28** And the angel came to her and said, “Greetings, favored woman! The Lord is with you.”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss add <i>Blessed are you among women.</i>  
**1:29** But she was deeply troubled by this statement, wondering what kind of greeting this could be.  
**1:30** Then the angel told her, “Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God.  
**1:31** Now listen: You will conceive and give birth to a son, and you will name him Jesus.  
**1:32** He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High, and the Lord God will give him the throne of his father David.  
**1:33** He will reign over the house of Jacob forever, and his kingdom will have no end.”  
**1:34** Mary asked the angel, “How can this be, since I have not had sexual relations with a man?”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>since I do not know a man</i>  
**1:35** The angel replied to her, “The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. Therefore, the holy one to be born will be called the Son of God.  
**1:36** And consider your relative Elizabeth—even she has conceived a son in her old age, and this is the sixth month for her who was called childless.  
**1:37** For nothing will be impossible with God.”  
**1:38** “See, I am the Lord’s servant,” said Mary. “May it happen to me as you have said.” Then the angel left her.  
**1:39** In those days Mary set out and hurried to a town in the hill country of Judah  
**1:40** where she entered Zechariah’s house and greeted Elizabeth.  
**1:41** When Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, the baby leaped inside her, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.  
**1:42** Then she exclaimed with a loud cry, “Blessed are you among women, and your child will be blessed!<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and the fruit of your abdomen</i> (or <i>womb</i>) <i>is blessed</i>  
**1:43** How could this happen to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?  
**1:44** For you see, when the sound of your greeting reached my ears, the baby leaped for joy inside me.  
**1:45** Blessed is she who has believed that the Lord would fulfill what he has spoken to her!”  
**1:46** And Mary said:<br>My soul magnifies the Lord,  
**1:47** and my spirit rejoices in God my Savior,  
**1:48** because he has looked with favor<br>on the humble condition of his servant.<br>Surely, from now on all generations<br>will call me blessed,  
**1:49** because the Mighty One<br>has done great things for me,<br>and his name is holy.  
**1:50** His mercy is from generation to generation<br>on those who fear him.  
**1:51** He has done a mighty deed with his arm;<br>he has scattered the proud<br>because of the thoughts of their hearts;  
**1:52** he has toppled the mighty from their thrones<br>and exalted the lowly.  
**1:53** He has satisfied the hungry with good things<br>and sent the rich away empty.  
**1:54** He has helped his servant Israel,<br>remembering his mercy  
**1:55** to Abraham and his descendants<sup>①</sup> forever,<br>just as he spoke to our ancestors.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>offspring;</i> lit <i>seed</i>  
**1:56** And Mary stayed with her about three months; then she returned to her home.  
**1:57** Now the time had come for Elizabeth to give birth, and she had a son.  
**1:58** Then her neighbors and relatives heard that the Lord had shown her his great mercy, and they rejoiced with her.  
**1:59** When they came to circumcise the child on the eighth day, they were going to name him Zechariah, after his father.  
**1:60** But his mother responded, “No. He will be called John.”  
**1:61** Then they said to her, “None of your relatives has that name.”  
**1:62** So they motioned to his father to find out what he wanted him to be called.  
**1:63** He asked for a writing tablet and wrote, “His name is John.” And they were all amazed.  
**1:64** Immediately his mouth was opened and his tongue set free, and he began to speak, praising God.  
**1:65** Fear came on all those who lived around them, and all these things were being talked about throughout the hill country of Judea.  
**1:66** All who heard about him took it to heart, saying, “What then will this child become?” For, indeed, the Lord’s hand was with him.  
**1:67** Then his father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied:  
**1:68** Blessed is the Lord, the God of Israel,<br>because he has visited<br>and provided redemption for his people.  
**1:69** He has raised up a horn of salvation for us<br>in the house of his servant David,  
**1:70** just as he spoke by the mouth<br>of his holy prophets in ancient times;  
**1:71** salvation from our enemies<br>and from the hand of those who hate us.  
**1:72** He has dealt mercifully with our ancestors<br>and remembered his holy covenant—  
**1:73** the oath that he swore to our father Abraham,<br>to grant that we,  
**1:74** having been rescued<br>from the hand of our enemies,<br>would serve him without fear  
**1:75** in holiness and righteousness<br>in his presence all our days.  
**1:76** And you, child, will be called<br>a prophet of the Most High,<br>for you will go before the Lord<br>to prepare his ways,  
**1:77** to give his people knowledge of salvation<br>through the forgiveness of their sins.  
**1:78** Because of our God’s merciful compassion,<br>the dawn from on high will visit us  
**1:79** to shine on those who live in darkness<br>and the shadow of death,<br>to guide our feet into the way of peace.  
**1:80** The child grew up and became strong in spirit,<sup>①</sup> and he was in the wilderness until the day of his public appearance to Israel.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>strong in the Spirit</i>  

## 2장

**2:5** to be registered along with Mary, who was engaged to him<sup>①</sup> and was pregnant.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>betrothed</i>

**2:16** They hurried off and found both Mary and Joseph, and the baby who was lying in the manger.  
**2:17** After seeing them, they reported the message they were told about this child,  
**2:18** and all who heard it were amazed at what the shepherds said to them.  
**2:19** But Mary was treasuring up all these things in her heart and meditating on them.  
**2:20** The shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things they had seen and heard, which were just as they had been told.  
**2:21** When the eight days were completed for his circumcision, he was named Jesus—the name given by the angel before he was conceived.  
**2:22** And when the days of their purification according to the law of Moses were finished, they brought him up to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord  
**2:23** (just as it is written in the law of the Lord,<b>Every firstborn male will be dedicated</b><sup>①</sup> <b>to the Lord</b><sup>②</sup>)<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>be called holy</i> <sup>②</sup>Ex <a name='B:20 13:2'>13:2,<a name='B:20 13:12'>12  
**2:24** and to offer a sacrifice (according to what is stated in the law of the Lord, <b>a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons</b><sup>①</sup>).<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lv <a name='B:30 5:11'>5:11; <a name='B:30 12:8'>12:8  
**2:25** There was a man in Jerusalem whose name was Simeon. This man was righteous and devout, looking forward to Israel’s consolation, and the Holy Spirit was on him.  
**2:26** It had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he would not see death before he saw the Lord’s Messiah.  
**2:27** Guided by the Spirit, he entered the temple. When the parents brought in the child Jesus to perform for him what was customary under the law,  
**2:28** Simeon took him up in his arms, praised God, and said,  
**2:29** Now, Master,<br>you can dismiss your servant in peace,<br>as you promised.  
**2:30** For my eyes have seen your salvation.  
**2:31** You have prepared it<br>in the presence of all peoples—  
**2:32** a light for revelation to the Gentiles<sup>①</sup><br>and glory to your people Israel.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the nations</i>  
**2:33** His father and mother<sup>①</sup> were amazed at what was being said about him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss read <i>But Joseph and his mother</i>  
**2:34** Then Simeon blessed them and told his mother Mary, “Indeed, this child is destined to cause the fall and rise of many in Israel and to be a sign that will be opposed<sup>①</sup>—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>spoken against</i>

**2:40** The boy grew up and became strong, filled with wisdom, and God’s grace was on him.  
**2:41** Every year his parents traveled to Jerusalem for the Passover Festival.  
**2:42** When he was twelve years old, they went up according to the custom of the festival.  
**2:43** After those days were over, as they were returning, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem, but his parents<sup>①</sup> did not know it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other mss read <i>but Joseph and his mother</i>  
**2:44** Assuming he was in the traveling party, they went a day’s journey. Then they began looking for him among their relatives and friends.  
**2:45** When they did not find him, they returned to Jerusalem to search for him.  
**2:46** After three days, they found him in the temple sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions.  
**2:47** And all those who heard him were astounded at his understanding and his answers.  
**2:48** When his parents saw him, they were astonished, and his mother said to him, “Son, why have you treated us like this? Your father and I have been anxiously searching for you.”  
**2:49** “Why were you searching for me?” he asked them. “Didn’t you know that it was necessary for me to be in my Father’s house?”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>be involved in my Father’s interests</i> (or <i>things</i>), or <i>be among my Father’s people</i>

**2:51** Then he went down with them and came to Nazareth and was obedient to them. His mother kept all these things in her heart.  
**2:52** And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and with people.  

## 3장

**3:1** In the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar, while Pontius Pilate was governor of Judea, Herod was tetrarch<sup>①</sup> of Galilee, his brother Philip tetrarch of the region of Iturea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias tetrarch of Abilene,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>ruler</i>  
**3:2** during the high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas, God’s word came to John the son of Zechariah in the wilderness.  
**3:3** He went into all the vicinity of the Jordan, proclaiming a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins,  
**3:4** as it is written in the book of the words of the prophet Isaiah:<br><b>A voice of one crying out in the wilderness:</b><br><b>Prepare the way for the Lord;</b><br><b>make his paths straight!</b>  
**3:5** <b>Every valley will be filled,</b><br><b>and every mountain and hill will be made low;</b><sup>①</sup><br><b>the crooked will become straight,</b><br><b>the rough ways smooth,</b><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>be humbled</i>  
**3:6** <b>and everyone will see the salvation of God.</b><sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Is 40:3-5  
**3:7** He then said to the crowds who came out to be baptized by him, “Brood of vipers! Who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?  
**3:8** Therefore produce fruit consistent with repentance. And don’t start saying to yourselves, ‘We have Abraham as our father,’ for I tell you that God is able to raise up children for Abraham from these stones.  
**3:9** The ax is already at the root of the trees. Therefore, every tree that doesn’t produce good fruit will be cut down and thrown into the fire.”  
**3:10** “What then should we do?” the crowds were asking him.  
**3:11** He replied to them, “The one who has two shirts must share with someone who has none, and the one who has food must do the same.”  
**3:12** Tax collectors also came to be baptized, and they asked him, “Teacher, what should we do?”  
**3:13** He told them, “Don’t collect any more than what you have been authorized.”  
**3:14** Some soldiers also questioned him, “What should we do?” He said to them, “Don’t take money from anyone by force or false accusation, and be satisfied with your wages.”  
**3:15** Now the people were waiting expectantly, and all of them were questioning in their hearts whether John might be the Messiah.  
**3:16** John answered them all, “I baptize you with water, but one who is more powerful than I am is coming. I am not worthy to untie the strap of his sandals. He will baptize you with<sup>①</sup> the Holy Spirit and fire.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in</i>  
**3:17** His winnowing shovel is in his hand to clear his threshing floor and gather the wheat into his barn, but the chaff he will burn with fire that never goes out.”  
**3:18** Then, along with many other exhortations, he proclaimed good news to the people.  
**3:19** But when John rebuked Herod the tetrarch because of Herodias, his brother’s wife, and all the evil things he had done,  
**3:20** Herod added this to everything else—he locked up John in prison.  
**3:21** When all the people were baptized, Jesus also was baptized. As he was praying, heaven opened,  
**3:22** and the Holy Spirit descended on him in a physical appearance like a dove. And a voice came from heaven: “You are my beloved Son; with you I am well-pleased.”  
**3:23** As he began his ministry, Jesus was about thirty years old and was thought to be the<br>son of Joseph, son of Heli,  
**3:24** son of Matthat, son of Levi, son of Melchi, son of Jannai, son of Joseph,  
**3:25** son of Mattathias, son of Amos, son of Nahum, son of Esli, son of Naggai,  
**3:26** son of Maath, son of Mattathias, son of Semein, son of Josech, son of Joda,  
**3:27** son of Joanan, son of Rhesa, son of Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, son of Neri,  
**3:28** son of Melchi, son of Addi, son of Cosam, son of Elmadam, son of Er,

**3:30** son of Simeon, son of Judah, son of Joseph, son of Jonam, son of Eliakim,

**24:4** While they were perplexed about this, suddenly two men stood by them in dazzling clothes.  
**24:5** So the women were terrified and bowed down to the ground. “Why are you looking for the living among the dead?” asked the men.
