# 25.예레미야애가 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** How<sup>①</sup> she sits alone,<br>the city once crowded with people!<br>She who was great among the nations<br>has become like a widow.<br>The princess among the provinces<br>has been put to forced labor.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The stanzas in Lm <a name='B:310 1:1-4'>1–4 form an acrostic.  
**1:2** She weeps bitterly during the night,<br>with tears on her cheeks.<br>There is no one to offer her comfort,<br>not one from all her lovers.<sup>①</sup><br>All her friends have betrayed her;<br>they have become her enemies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= Jerusalem’s political allies  
**1:3** Judah has gone into exile<br>following<sup>①</sup> affliction and harsh slavery;<br>she lives among the nations<br>but finds no place to rest.<br>All her pursuers have overtaken her<br>in narrow places.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>because of</i>  
**1:4** The roads to Zion mourn,<br>for no one comes to the appointed festivals.<br>All her gates are deserted;<br>her priests groan,<br>her young women grieve,<br>and she herself is bitter.  
**1:5** Her adversaries have become her masters;<br>her enemies are at ease,<br>for the LORD has made her suffer<br>because of her many transgressions.<br>Her children have gone away<br>as captives before the adversary.  
**1:6** All the splendor has vanished<br>from Daughter Zion.<br>Her leaders are like stags<br>that find no pasture;<br>they stumble away exhausted<br>before the hunter.  
**1:7** During the days of her affliction and homelessness<br>Jerusalem remembers all her precious belongings<br>that were hers in days of old.<br>When her people fell into the adversary’s hand,<br>she had no one to help.<br>The adversaries looked at her,<br>laughing over her downfall.  
**1:8** Jerusalem has sinned grievously;<br>therefore, she has become an object of scorn.<sup>①</sup><br>All who honored her now despise her,<br>for they have seen her nakedness.<br>She herself groans and turns away.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>become impure</i>  
**1:9** Her uncleanness stains her skirts.<br>She never considered her end.<br>Her downfall was astonishing;<br>there was no one to comfort her.<br>LORD, look on my affliction,<br>for the enemy boasts.  
**1:10** The adversary has seized<br>all her precious belongings.<br>She has even seen the nations<br>enter her sanctuary—<br>those you had forbidden<br>to enter your assembly.  
**1:11** All her people groan<br>while they search for bread.<br>They have traded their precious belongings for food<br>in order to stay alive.<br>LORD, look and see<br>how I have become despised.  
**1:12** Is this nothing to you, all you who pass by?<br>Look and see!<br>Is there any pain like mine,<br>which was dealt out to me,<br>which the LORD made me suffer<br>on the day of his burning anger?  
**1:13** He sent fire from on high into my bones;<br>he made it descend.<sup>①</sup><br>He spread a net for my feet<br>and turned me back.<br>He made me desolate,<br>sick all day long.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>DSS, LXX; MT reads <i>bones, and it prevailed against them</i>  
**1:14** My transgressions have been formed into a yoke,<sup>①</sup><sup>②</sup><br>fastened together by his hand;<br>they have been placed on my neck,<br>and the Lord has broken my strength.<br>He has handed me over<br>to those I cannot withstand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some Hb mss, LXX read <i>He kept watch over my transgressions</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>The yoke of my transgressions is bound;</i> Hb obscure  
**1:15** The Lord has rejected<br>all the mighty men within me.<br>He has summoned an army<sup>①</sup> against me<br>to crush my young warriors.<br>The Lord has trampled Virgin Daughter Judah<br>like grapes in a winepress.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>has announced an appointed time</i>  
**1:16** I weep because of these things;<br>my eyes flow<sup>①</sup> with tears.<br>For there is no one nearby to comfort me,<br>no one to keep me alive.<br>My children are desolate<br>because the enemy has prevailed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>my eye, my eye flows</i>  
**1:17** Zion stretches out her hands;<br>there is no one to comfort her.<br>The LORD has issued a decree against Jacob<br>that his neighbors should be his adversaries.<br>Jerusalem has become<br>something impure among them.  
**1:18** The LORD is just,<br>for I have rebelled against his command.<br>Listen, all you people;<br>look at my pain.<br>My young women and young men<br>have gone into captivity.  
**1:19** I called to my lovers,<br>but they betrayed me.<br>My priests and elders<br>perished in the city<br>while searching for food<br>to keep themselves alive.  
**1:20** LORD, see how I am in distress.<br>I am churning within;<br>my heart is broken,<sup>①</sup><br>for I have been very rebellious.<br>Outside, the sword takes the children;<br>inside, there is death.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is turned within me</i>  
**1:21** People have heard me groaning,<br>but there is no one to comfort me.<br>All my enemies have heard of my misfortune;<br>they are glad that you have caused it.<br>Bring on the day you have announced,<br>so that they may become like me.  
**1:22** Let all their wickedness come before you,<br>and deal with them<br>as you have dealt with me<br>because of all my transgressions.<br>For my groans are many,<br>and I am sick at heart.  

## 2장

**2:1** How the Lord has overshadowed<br>Daughter Zion with his anger!<br>He has thrown down Israel’s glory<br>from heaven to earth.<br>He did not acknowledge his footstool<br>in the day of his anger.  
**2:2** Without compassion the Lord has swallowed up<br>all the dwellings of Jacob.<br>In his wrath he has demolished<br>the fortified cities of Daughter Judah.<br>He brought them to the ground<br>and defiled the kingdom and its leaders.  
**2:3** He has cut off every horn of Israel<br>in his burning anger<br>and withdrawn his right hand<br>in the presence of the enemy.<br>He has blazed against Jacob like a flaming fire<br>that consumes everything.  
**2:4** He has strung his bow like an enemy;<br>his right hand is positioned like an adversary.<br>He has killed everyone who was the delight to the eye,<br>pouring out his wrath like fire<br>on the tent of Daughter Zion.  
**2:5** The Lord is like an enemy;<br>he has swallowed up Israel.<br>He swallowed up all its palaces<br>and destroyed its fortified cities.<br>He has multiplied mourning and lamentation<br>within Daughter Judah.  
**2:6** He has wrecked his temple<sup>①</sup><br>as if it were merely a shack in a field,<sup>②</sup><br>destroying his place of meeting.<br>The LORD has abolished<br>appointed festivals and Sabbaths in Zion.<br>He has despised king and priest<br>in his fierce anger.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>booth</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>it were a garden</i>  
**2:7** The Lord has rejected his altar,<br>repudiated his sanctuary;<br>he has handed the walls of her palaces<br>over to the enemy.<br>They have raised a shout in the house of the LORD<br>as on the day of an appointed festival.  
**2:8** The LORD determined to destroy<br>the wall of Daughter Zion.<br>He stretched out a measuring line<br>and did not restrain himself from destroying.<br>He made the ramparts and walls grieve;<br>together they waste away.  
**2:9** Zion’s gates have fallen to the ground;<br>he has destroyed and shattered the bars on her gates.<br>Her king and her leaders live among the nations,<br>instruction<sup>①</sup> is no more,<br>and even her prophets receive<br>no vision from the LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the law</i>  
**2:10** The elders of Daughter Zion<br>sit on the ground in silence.<br>They have thrown dust on their heads<br>and put on sackcloth.<br>The young women of Jerusalem<br>have bowed their heads to the ground.  
**2:11** My eyes are worn out from weeping;<br>I am churning within.<br>My heart is poured out in grief<sup>①</sup><br>because of the destruction of my dear people,<br>because infants and nursing babies faint<br>in the streets of the city.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>My liver is poured out on the ground</i>  
**2:12** They cry out to their mothers,<br>“Where is the grain and wine?”<br>as they faint like the wounded<br>in the streets of the city,<br>as their life pours out<br>in the arms of their mothers.  
**2:13** What can I say on your behalf?<br>What can I compare you to, Daughter Jerusalem?<br>What can I liken you to,<br>so that I may console you, Virgin Daughter Zion?<br>For your ruin is as vast as the sea.<br>Who can heal you?  
**2:14** Your prophets saw visions for you<br>that were empty and deceptive;<sup>①</sup><br>they did not reveal your iniquity<br>and so restore your fortunes.<br>They saw pronouncements for you<br>that were empty and misleading.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>insipid</i>  
**2:15** All who pass by<br>scornfully clap their hands at you.<br>They hiss and shake their heads<br>at Daughter Jerusalem:<br>Is this the city that was called<br>the perfection of beauty,<br>the joy of the whole earth?  
**2:16** All your enemies<br>open their mouths against you.<br>They hiss and gnash their teeth,<br>saying, “We have swallowed her up.<br>This is the day we have waited for!<br>We have lived to see it.”  
**2:17** The LORD has done what he planned;<br>he has accomplished his decree,<br>which he ordained in days of old.<br>He has demolished without compassion,<br>letting the enemy gloat over you<br>and exalting the horn of your adversaries.  
**2:18** The hearts of the people cry out to the Lord.<br>Wall of Daughter Zion,<br>let your tears run down like a river<br>day and night.<br>Give yourself no relief<br>and your<sup>①</sup> eyes no rest.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>and the daughter of your</i>  
**2:19** Arise, cry out in the night<br>from the first watch of the night.<br>Pour out your heart like water<br>before the Lord’s presence.<br>Lift up your hands to him<br>for the lives of your children<br>who are fainting from hunger<br>at the head of every street.  
**2:20** LORD, look and consider<br>to whom you have done this.<br>Should women eat their own children,<br>the infants they have nurtured?<sup>①</sup><br>Should priests and prophets<br>be killed in the Lord’s sanctuary?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>infants in a healthy condition;</i> Hb obscure  
**2:21** Both young and old<br>are lying on the ground in the streets.<br>My young women and young men<br>have fallen by the sword.<br>You have killed them in the day of your anger,<br>slaughtering without compassion.  
**2:22** You summon those who terrorize me<sup>①</sup> on every side,<br>as if for an appointed festival day;<br>on the day of the LORD’s anger<br>no one escaped or survived.<br>My enemy has destroyed<br>those I nurtured<sup>②</sup> and reared.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>terrors</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>I bore healthy;</i> Hb obscure  

## 3장

**3:1** I am the man who has seen affliction<br>under the rod of God’s wrath.  
**3:2** He has driven me away and forced me to walk<br>in darkness instead of light.  
**3:3** Yes, he repeatedly turns his hand<br>against me all day long.  
**3:4** He has worn away my flesh and skin;<br>he has broken my bones.  
**3:5** He has laid siege against me,<br>encircling me with bitterness and hardship.  
**3:6** He has made me dwell in darkness<br>like those who have been dead for ages.  
**3:7** He has walled me in so I cannot get out;<br>he has weighed me down with chains.  
**3:8** Even when I cry out and plead for help,<br>he blocks out my prayer.  
**3:9** He has walled in my ways with blocks of stone;<br>he has made my paths crooked.  
**3:10** He is<sup>①</sup> a bear waiting in ambush,<br>a lion in hiding.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is to me</i>  
**3:11** He forced me off my way and tore me to pieces;<br>he left me desolate.  
**3:12** He strung his bow<br>and set me as the target for his arrow.  
**3:13** He pierced my kidneys<br>with shafts from his quiver.  
**3:14** I am a laughingstock to all my people,<sup>①</sup><br>mocked by their songs all day long.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some Hb mss, LXX, Vg; other Hb mss, Syr read <i>all peoples</i>  
**3:15** He filled me with bitterness,<br>satiated me with wormwood.  
**3:16** He ground my teeth with gravel<br>and made me cower<sup>①</sup> in the dust.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>and trampled me</i>  
**3:17** I have been deprived<sup>①</sup> of peace;<br>I have forgotten what prosperity is.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Syr, Vg; MT reads <i>You deprived my soul</i>  
**3:18** Then I thought, “My future<sup>①</sup> is lost,<br>as well as my hope from the LORD.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>splendor</i>  
**3:19** Remember<sup>①</sup> my affliction and my homelessness,<br>the wormwood and the poison.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>I remember</i>  
**3:20** I continually remember them<br>and have become depressed.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Alt Hb tradition reads <i>and you cause me to collapse</i>  
**3:21** Yet I call this to mind,<br>and therefore I have hope:  
**3:22** Because of the LORD’s faithful love<br>we do not perish,<sup>①</sup><br>for his mercies never end.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>One Hb mss, Syr, Tg read <i>The LORD’s faithful love, indeed, does not perish</i>  
**3:23** They are new every morning;<br>great is your faithfulness!  
**3:24** I say, “The LORD is my portion,<br>therefore I will put my hope in him.”  
**3:25** The LORD is good to those who wait for him,<br>to the person who seeks him.  
**3:26** It is good to wait quietly<br>for salvation from the LORD.  
**3:27** It is good for a man to bear the yoke<br>while he is still young.  
**3:28** Let him sit alone and be silent,<br>for God has disciplined<sup>①</sup> him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>has laid a burden on</i>  
**3:29** Let him put his mouth in the dust—<br>perhaps there is still hope.  
**3:30** Let him offer his cheek<br>to the one who would strike him;<br>let him be filled with disgrace.  
**3:31** For the Lord<br>will not reject us forever.  
**3:32** Even if he causes suffering,<br>he will show compassion<br>according to the abundance of his faithful love.  
**3:33** For he does not enjoy bringing affliction<br>or suffering on mankind.  
**3:34** Crushing all the prisoners of the land<sup>①</sup><br>beneath one’s feet,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>earth</i>  
**3:35** denying justice to a man<br>in the presence of the Most High,  
**3:36** or subverting a person in his lawsuit—<br>the Lord does not approve of these things.  
**3:37** Who is there who speaks and it happens,<br>unless the Lord has ordained it?  
**3:38** Do not both adversity and good<br>come from the mouth of the Most High?  
**3:39** Why should any living person complain,<br>any man, because of the punishment for his sins?  
**3:40** Let’s examine and probe our ways,<br>and turn back to the LORD.  
**3:41** Let’s lift up our hearts and our hands<br>to God in heaven:  
**3:42** “We have sinned and rebelled;<br>you have not forgiven.  
**3:43** “You have covered yourself in anger and pursued us;<br>you have killed without compassion.  
**3:44** You have covered yourself with a cloud<br>so that no prayer can get through.  
**3:45** You have made us disgusting filth<br>among the peoples.  
**3:46** “All our enemies<br>open their mouths against us.  
**3:47** We have experienced panic and pitfall,<br>devastation and destruction.”  
**3:48** My eyes flow with streams of tears<br>because of the destruction of my dear people.  
**3:49** My eyes overflow unceasingly,<br>without end,  
**3:50** until the LORD looks down<br>from heaven and sees.  
**3:51** My eyes bring me grief<br>because of the fate of all the women in my city.  
**3:52** For no reason, my enemies<sup>①</sup><br>hunted me like a bird.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Those who were my enemies for no reason</i>  
**3:53** They smothered my life in<sup>①</sup> a pit<br>and threw stones on me.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>They ended my life in;</i> Hb obscure  
**3:54** Water flooded over my head,<br>and I thought, “I’m going to die!”  
**3:55** I called on your name, LORD,<br>from the depths of the pit.  
**3:56** You heard my plea:<br>Do not ignore my cry for relief.  
**3:57** You came near whenever I called you;<br>you said, “Do not be afraid.”  
**3:58** You championed my cause, Lord;<br>you redeemed my life.  
**3:59** LORD, you saw the wrong done to me;<br>judge my case.  
**3:60** You saw all their vengefulness,<br>all their plots against me.  
**3:61** LORD, you heard their insults,<br>all their plots against me.  
**3:62** The slander<sup>①</sup> and murmuring of my opponents<br>attack me all day long.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>lips</i>  
**3:63** When they sit and when they rise, look,<br>I am mocked by their songs.  
**3:64** You will pay them back what they deserve, LORD,<br>according to the work of their hands.  
**3:65** You will give them a heart filled with anguish.<sup>①</sup><br>May your curse be on them!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>them an obstinate heart;</i> Hb obscure  
**3:66** You will pursue them in anger and destroy them<br>under your heavens.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>under the LORD’s heavens</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** How the gold has become tarnished,<br>the fine gold become dull!<br>The stones of the temple<sup>①</sup> lie scattered<br>at the head of every street.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>The sacred gems</i>  
**4:2** Zion’s precious children—<br>once worth their weight in pure gold—<br>how they are regarded as clay jars,<br>the work of a potter’s hands!  
**4:3** Even jackals offer their breasts<br>to nurse their young,<br>but my dear people have become cruel<br>like ostriches in the wilderness.  
**4:4** The nursing baby’s tongue<br>clings to the roof of his mouth from thirst.<br>Infants beg for food,<br>but no one gives them any.  
**4:5** Those who used to eat delicacies<br>are destitute in the streets;<br>those who were reared in purple garments<br>huddle in trash heaps.  
**4:6** The punishment of my dear people<br>is greater than that of Sodom,<br>which was overthrown in an instant<br>without a hand laid on it.  
**4:7** Her dignitaries were brighter than snow,<br>whiter than milk;<br>their bodies<sup>①</sup> were more ruddy than coral,<br>their appearance like lapis lazuli.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>bones</i>  
**4:8** Now they appear darker than soot;<br>they are not recognized in the streets.<br>Their skin has shriveled on their bones;<br>it has become dry like wood.  
**4:9** Those slain by the sword are better off<br>than those slain by hunger,<br>who waste away, pierced with pain<br>because the fields lack produce.  
**4:10** The hands of compassionate women<br>have cooked their own children;<br>they became their food<br>during the destruction of my dear people.  
**4:11** The LORD has exhausted his wrath,<br>poured out his burning anger;<br>he has ignited a fire in Zion,<br>and it has consumed her foundations.  
**4:12** The kings of the earth<br>and all the world’s inhabitants did not believe<br>that an enemy or adversary<br>could enter Jerusalem’s gates.  
**4:13** Yet it happened because of the sins of her prophets<br>and the iniquities of her priests,<br>who shed the blood of the righteous within her.  
**4:14** Blind, they stumbled in the streets,<br>defiled by this blood,<br>so that no one dared<br>to touch their garments.  
**4:15** “Stay away! Unclean!” people shouted at them.<br>“Away, away! Don’t touch us!”<br>So they wandered aimlessly.<br>It was said among the nations,<br>“They can stay here no longer.”  
**4:16** The LORD himself has scattered them;<br>he no longer watches over them.<br>The priests are not respected;<br>the elders find no favor.  
**4:17** All the while our eyes were failing<br>as we looked in vain for help;<br>we watched from our towers<br>for a nation that would not save us.  
**4:18** Our steps were closely followed<br>so that we could not walk in our streets.<br>Our end approached; our time ran out.<br>Our end had come!  
**4:19** Those who chased us were swifter<br>than eagles in the sky;<br>they relentlessly pursued us over the mountains<br>and ambushed us in the wilderness.  
**4:20** The LORD’s anointed, the breath of our life,<sup>①</sup><br>was captured in their traps.<br>We had said about him,<br>“We will live under his protection among the nations.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>nostrils</i>  
**4:21** So rejoice and be glad, Daughter Edom,<br>you resident of the land of Uz!<br>Yet the cup will pass to you as well;<br>you will get drunk and expose yourself.  
**4:22** Daughter Zion, your punishment is complete;<br>he will not lengthen your exile.<sup>①</sup><br>But he will punish your iniquity, Daughter Edom,<br>and will expose your sins.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>not deport you again</i>  

## 5장

**5:1** LORD, remember what has happened to us.<br>Look, and see our disgrace!  
**5:2** Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers,<br>our houses to foreigners.  
**5:3** We have become orphans, fatherless;<br>our mothers are widows.  
**5:4** We must pay for the water we drink;<br>our wood comes at a price.  
**5:5** We are closely pursued;<br>we are tired, and no one offers us rest.  
**5:6** We made a treaty with<sup>①</sup> Egypt<br>and with Assyria, to get enough food.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>We gave the hand to</i>  
**5:7** Our ancestors sinned; they no longer exist,<br>but we bear their punishment.  
**5:8** Slaves rule over us;<br>no one rescues us from them.  
**5:9** We secure our food at the risk of our lives<br>because of the sword in the wilderness.  
**5:10** Our skin is as hot<sup>①</sup> as an oven<br>from the ravages of hunger.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>black;</i> Hb obscure  
**5:11** Women have been raped in Zion,<br>virgins in the cities of Judah.  
**5:12** Princes have been hung up by their hands;<br>elders are shown no respect.  
**5:13** Young men labor at millstones;<br>boys stumble under loads of wood.  
**5:14** The elders have left the city gate,<br>the young men, their music.  
**5:15** Joy has left our hearts;<br>our dancing has turned to mourning.  
**5:16** The crown has fallen from our head.<br>Woe to us, for we have sinned.  
**5:17** Because of this, our heart is sick;<br>because of these, our eyes grow dim:  
**5:18** because of Mount Zion, which lies desolate<br>and has jackals prowling in it.  
**5:19** You, LORD, are enthroned forever;<br>your throne endures from generation to generation.  
**5:20** Why do you continually forget us,<br>abandon us for our entire lives?  
**5:21** LORD, bring us back to yourself, so we may return;<br>renew our days as in former times,  
**5:22** unless you have completely rejected us<br>and are intensely angry with us.
