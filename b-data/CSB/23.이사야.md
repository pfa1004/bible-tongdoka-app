# 23.이사야 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision concerning Judah and Jerusalem that Isaiah son of Amoz saw during the reigns<sup>①</sup> of Kings Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah of Judah.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>ca 792–686 BC  
**1:2** Listen, heavens, and pay attention, earth,<br>for the LORD has spoken:<br>“I have raised children<sup>①</sup> and brought them up,<br>but they have rebelled against me.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sons</i>, also in v. <a name='B:290 1:4'>4  
**1:3** The ox knows its owner,<br>and the donkey its master’s feeding trough,<br>but Israel does not know;<br>my people do not understand.”  
**1:4** Oh sinful nation,<br>people weighed down with iniquity,<br>brood of evildoers,<br>depraved children!<br>They have abandoned the LORD;<br>they have despised the Holy One of Israel;<br>they have turned their backs on him.  
**1:5** Why do you want more beatings?<br>Why do you keep on rebelling?<br>The whole head is hurt,<br>and the whole heart is sick.  
**1:6** From the sole of the foot even to the head,<br>no spot is uninjured—<br>wounds, welts, and festering sores<br>not cleansed, bandaged,<br>or soothed with oil.  
**1:7** Your land is desolate,<br>your cities burned down;<br>foreigners devour your fields<br>right in front of you—<br>a desolation, like a place demolished by foreigners.  
**1:8** Daughter Zion is abandoned<br>like a shelter in a vineyard,<br>like a shack in a cucumber field,<br>like a besieged city.  
**1:9** If the LORD of Armies<br>had not left us a few survivors,<br>we would be like Sodom,<br>we would resemble Gomorrah.  
**1:10** Hear the word of the LORD,<br>you rulers of Sodom!<br>Listen to the instruction of our God,<br>you people of Gomorrah!  
**1:11** “What are all your sacrifices to me?”<br>asks the LORD.<br>“I have had enough of burnt offerings and rams<br>and the fat of well-fed cattle;<br>I have no desire for the blood of bulls,<br>lambs, or male goats.  
**1:12** When you come to appear before me,<br>who requires this from you—<br>this trampling of my courts?  
**1:13** Stop bringing useless offerings.<br>Your incense is detestable to me.<br>New Moons and Sabbaths,<br>and the calling of solemn assemblies—<br>I cannot stand iniquity with a festival.  
**1:14** I hate your New Moons and prescribed festivals.<br>They have become a burden to me;<br>I am tired of putting up with them.  
**1:15** When you spread out your hands in prayer,<br>I will refuse to look at you;<br>even if you offer countless prayers,<br>I will not listen.<br>Your hands are covered with blood.  
**1:16** “Wash yourselves. Cleanse yourselves.<br>Remove your evil deeds from my sight.<br>Stop doing evil.  
**1:17** Learn to do what is good.<br>Pursue justice.<br>Correct the oppressor.<sup>①</sup><br>Defend the rights of the fatherless.<br>Plead the widow’s cause.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Aid the oppressed</i>  
**1:18** “Come, let’s settle this,”<br>says the LORD.<br>“Though your sins are scarlet,<br>they will be as white as snow;<br>though they are crimson red,<br>they will be like wool.  
**1:19** If you are willing and obedient,<br>you will eat the good things of the land.  
**1:20** But if you refuse and rebel,<br>you will be devoured by the sword.”<br>For the mouth of the LORD has spoken.  
**1:21** The faithful town—<br>what an adulteress<sup>①</sup> she has become!<br>She was once full of justice.<br>Righteousness once dwelt in her,<br>but now, murderers!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>prostitute</i>  
**1:22** Your silver has become dross to be discarded,<br>your beer<sup>①</sup> is diluted with water.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>wine</i>  
**1:23** Your rulers are rebels,<br>friends of thieves.<br>They all love graft<br>and chase after bribes.<br>They do not defend the rights of the fatherless,<br>and the widow’s case never comes before them.  
**1:24** Therefore the Lord GOD of Armies,<br>the Mighty One of Israel, declares:<br>“Ah, I will get even with my foes;<br>I will take revenge against my enemies.  
**1:25** I will turn my hand against you<br>and will burn away your dross completely;<sup>①</sup><br>I will remove all your impurities.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>dross as with lye</i>  
**1:26** I will restore your judges to what they were at first,<br>and your advisers to what they were at the start.<br>Afterward you will be called the Righteous City,<br>a Faithful Town.”  
**1:27** Zion will be redeemed by justice,<br>those who repent, by righteousness.  
**1:28** At the same time both rebels and sinners will be broken,<br>and those who abandon the LORD will perish.  
**1:29** Indeed, they<sup>①</sup> will be ashamed of the sacred trees<br>you desired,<br>and you will be embarrassed because of the garden shrines<br>you have chosen.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some Hb mss; other Hb mss, Tg read <i>you</i>  
**1:30** For you will become like an oak<br>whose leaves are withered,<br>and like a garden without water.  
**1:31** The strong one will become tinder,<br>and his work a spark;<br>both will burn together,<br>with no one to extinguish the flames.  

## 2장

**2:1** The vision that Isaiah son of Amoz saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem:  
**2:2** In the last days<br>the mountain of the LORD’s house will be established<br>at the top of the mountains<br>and will be raised above the hills.<br>All nations will stream to it,  
**2:3** and many peoples will come and say,<br>“Come, let’s go up to the mountain of the LORD,<br>to the house of the God of Jacob.<br>He will teach us about his ways<br>so that we may walk in his paths.”<br>For instruction will go out of Zion<br>and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.  
**2:4** He will settle disputes among the nations<br>and provide arbitration for many peoples.<br>They will beat their swords into plows<br>and their spears into pruning knives.<br>Nation will not take up the sword against nation,<br>and they will never again train for war.  
**2:5** House of Jacob,<br>come and let’s walk in the LORD’s light.  
**2:6** For you have abandoned your people,<br>the house of Jacob,<br>because they are full of divination from the East<br>and of fortune-tellers like the Philistines.<br>They are in league<sup>①</sup> with foreigners.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>They teem</i>, or <i>They partner;</i> Hb obscure  
**2:7** Their<sup>①</sup><sup>②</sup> land is full of silver and gold,<br>and there is no limit to their treasures;<br>their land is full of horses,<br>and there is no limit to their chariots.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Its</i> <sup>②</sup>= the house of Jacob  
**2:8** Their land is full of worthless idols;<br>they worship the work of their hands,<br>what their fingers have made.  
**2:9** So humanity is brought low,<br>and each person is humbled.<br>Do not forgive them!  
**2:10** Go into the rocks<br>and hide in the dust<br>from the terror of the LORD<br>and from his majestic splendor.  
**2:11** The pride of mankind<sup>①</sup> will be humbled,<br>and human loftiness will be brought low;<br>the LORD alone will be exalted on that day.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Mankind’s proud eyes</i>  
**2:12** For a day belonging to the LORD of Armies is coming<br>against all that is proud and lofty,<br>against all that is lifted up—it will be humbled—  
**2:13** against all the cedars of Lebanon,<br>lofty and lifted up,<br>against all the oaks of Bashan,  
**2:14** against all the high mountains,<br>against all the lofty hills,  
**2:15** against every high tower,<br>against every fortified wall,  
**2:16** against every ship of Tarshish,<br>and against every splendid sea vessel.  
**2:17** The pride of mankind will be brought low,<br>and human loftiness will be humbled;<br>the LORD alone will be exalted on that day.  
**2:18** The worthless idols will vanish completely.  
**2:19** People will go into caves in the rocks<br>and holes in the ground,<br>away from the terror of the LORD<br>and from his majestic splendor,<br>when he rises to terrify the earth.  
**2:20** On that day people will throw<br>their worthless idols of silver and gold,<br>which they made to worship,<br>to the moles and the bats.  
**2:21** They will go into the caves of the rocks<br>and the crevices in the cliffs,<br>away from the terror of the LORD<br>and from his majestic splendor,<br>when he rises to terrify the earth.  
**2:22** Put no more trust in a mere human,<br>who has only the breath in his nostrils.<br>What is he really worth?  

## 3장

**3:1** Note this: The Lord GOD of Armies<br>is about to remove from Jerusalem and from Judah<br>every kind of security:<br>the entire supply of bread and water,  
**3:2** heroes and warriors,<br>judges and prophets,<br>fortune-tellers and elders,  
**3:3** commanders of fifty and dignitaries,<br>counselors, cunning magicians,<sup>①</sup> and necromancers.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>skilled craftsmen</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>mediums</i>  
**3:4** “I will make youths their leaders,<br>and unstable rulers<sup>①</sup> will govern them.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>mischief-makers</i>  
**3:5** The people will oppress one another,<br>man against man, neighbor against neighbor;<br>the young will act arrogantly toward the old,<br>and the worthless toward the honorable.  
**3:6** A man will even seize his brother<br>in his father’s house, saying,<br>“You have a cloak—you be our leader!<br>This heap of rubble will be under your control.”  
**3:7** On that day he will cry out, saying,<br>“I’m not a healer.<br>I don’t even have food or clothing in my house.<br>Don’t make me the leader of the people!”  
**3:8** For Jerusalem has stumbled<br>and Judah has fallen<br>because they have spoken and acted against the LORD,<br>defying his glorious presence.  
**3:9** The look on their faces testifies against them,<br>and like Sodom, they flaunt their sin;<br>they do not conceal it.<br>Woe to them,<br>for they have brought disaster on themselves.  
**3:10** Tell the righteous that it will go well for them,<br>for they will eat the fruit of their labor.  
**3:11** Woe to the wicked—it will go badly for them,<br>for what they have done will be done to them.  
**3:12** Youths oppress my people,<br>and women rule over them.<br>My people, your leaders mislead you;<br>they confuse the direction of your paths.  
**3:13** The LORD rises to argue the case<br>and stands to judge the people.  
**3:14** The LORD brings this charge<br>against the elders and leaders of his people:<br>“You have devastated the vineyard.<br>The plunder from the poor is in your houses.  
**3:15** Why do you crush my people<br>and grind the faces of the poor?” This is the declaration of the Lord GOD of Armies.  
**3:16** The LORD also says:<br>Because the daughters of Zion are haughty,<br>walking with heads held high<br>and seductive eyes,<br>prancing along,<br>jingling their ankle bracelets,  
**3:17** the Lord will put scabs on the heads<br>of the daughters of Zion,<br>and the LORD will shave their foreheads bare.  
**3:18** On that day the Lord will strip their finery: ankle bracelets, headbands, crescents,  
**3:19** pendants, bracelets, veils,  
**3:20** headdresses, ankle jewelry, sashes, perfume bottles, amulets,  
**3:21** signet rings, nose rings,  
**3:22** festive robes, capes, cloaks, purses,  
**3:23** garments, linen clothes, turbans, and shawls.  
**3:24** Instead of perfume there will be a stench;<br>instead of a belt, a rope;<br>instead of beautifully styled hair, baldness;<br>instead of fine clothes, sackcloth;<br>instead of beauty, branding.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>DSS read <i>shame</i>  
**3:25** Your men will fall by the sword,<br>your warriors in battle.  
**3:26** Then her gates will lament and mourn;<br>deserted, she will sit on the ground.

**5:13** Therefore my people will go into exile<br>because they lack knowledge;<br>her<sup>①</sup> dignitaries are starving,<br>and her masses are parched with thirst.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>its</i>

## 6장

**6:1** In the year that King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord seated on a high and lofty throne, and the hem of his robe filled the temple.  
**6:2** Seraphim<sup>①</sup> were standing above him; they each had six wings: with two they covered their faces, with two they covered their feet, and with two they flew.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= heavenly beings  
**6:3** And one called to another:<br>Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of Armies;<br>his glory fills the whole earth.  
**6:4** The foundations of the doorways shook at the sound of their voices, and the temple was filled with smoke.  
**6:5** Then I said:<br>Woe is me for I am ruined<sup>①</sup><br>because I am a man of unclean lips<br>and live among a people of unclean lips,<br>and because my eyes have seen the King,<br>the LORD of Armies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>I must be silent</i>  
**6:6** Then one of the seraphim flew to me, and in his hand was a glowing coal that he had taken from the altar with tongs.  
**6:7** He touched my mouth with it and said:<br>Now that this has touched your lips,<br>your iniquity is removed<br>and your sin is atoned for.  
**6:8** Then I heard the voice of the Lord asking:<br>Who will I send?<br>Who will go for us? I said:<br>Here I am. Send me.  
**6:9** And he replied:<br>Go! Say to these people:<br>Keep listening, but do not understand;<br>keep looking, but do not perceive.  
**6:10** Make the minds<sup>①</sup> of these people dull;<br>deafen their ears and blind their eyes;<br>otherwise they might see with their eyes<br>and hear with their ears,<br>understand with their minds,<br>turn back, and be healed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>heart</i>  
**6:11** Then I said, “Until when, Lord?” And he replied:<br>Until cities lie in ruins without inhabitants,<br>houses are without people,<br>the land is ruined and desolate,  
**6:12** and the LORD drives the people far away,<br>leaving great emptiness in the land.  
**6:13** Though a tenth will remain in the land,<br>it will be burned again.<br>Like the terebinth or the oak<br>that leaves a stump when felled,<br>the holy seed is the stump.  

## 7장

**7:1** This took place during the reign of Ahaz, son of Jotham, son of Uzziah king of Judah: Aram’s King Rezin and Israel’s King Pekah son of Remaliah went to fight against Jerusalem, but they were not able to conquer it.  
**7:2** When it became known to the house of David that Aram had occupied Ephraim, the heart of Ahaz<sup>①</sup> and the hearts of his people trembled like trees of a forest shaking in the wind.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Aram has rested upon Ephraim, his heart</i>  
**7:3** The LORD said to Isaiah, “Go out with your son Shear-jashub<sup>①</sup> to meet Ahaz at the end of the conduit of the upper pool, by the road to the Launderer’s Field.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= A Remnant Will Return  
**7:4** Say to him: Calm down and be quiet. Don’t be afraid or cowardly because of these two smoldering sticks, the fierce anger of Rezin and Aram, and the son of Remaliah.  
**7:5** For Aram, along with Ephraim and the son of Remaliah, has plotted harm against you. They say,  
**7:6** ‘Let’s go up against Judah, terrorize it, and conquer it for ourselves. Then we can install Tabeel’s son as king in it.’”  
**7:7** This is what the Lord GOD says:<br>It will not happen; it will not occur.  
**7:8** The chief city of Aram is Damascus,<br>the chief of Damascus is Rezin<br>(within sixty-five years<br>Ephraim will be too shattered to be a people),  
**7:9** the chief city of Ephraim is Samaria,<br>and the chief of Samaria is the son of Remaliah.<br>If you do not stand firm in your faith,<br>then you will not stand at all.  
**7:10** Then the LORD spoke again to Ahaz:  
**7:11** “Ask for a sign from the LORD your God—it can be as deep as Sheol or as high as heaven.”  
**7:12** But Ahaz replied, “I will not ask. I will not test the LORD.”  
**7:13** Isaiah said, “Listen, house of David! Is it not enough for you to try the patience of men? Will you also try the patience of my God?  
**7:14** Therefore, the Lord himself will give you<sup>①</sup> a sign: See, the virgin will conceive,<sup>②</sup> have a son, and name him Immanuel.<sup>③</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Hb, the word <i>you</i> is pl <sup>②</sup>Or <i>virgin is pregnant, will</i> <sup>③</sup>= God With Us

**7:16** For before the boy knows to reject what is bad and choose what is good, the land of the two kings you dread will be abandoned.  
**7:17** The LORD will bring on you, your people, and your father’s house such a time as has never been since Ephraim separated from Judah: He will bring the king of Assyria.”  
**7:18** On that day<br>the LORD will whistle to flies<br>at the farthest streams of the Nile<br>and to bees in the land of Assyria.  
**7:19** All of them will come and settle<br>in the steep ravines, in the clefts of the rocks,<br>in all the thornbushes, and in all the water holes.  
**7:20** On that day the Lord will use a razor hired from beyond the Euphrates River—the king of Assyria—to shave the hair on your heads, the hair on your legs, and even your beards.  
**7:21** On that day<br>a man will raise a young cow and two sheep,  
**7:22** and from the abundant milk they give<br>he will eat curds,<br>for every survivor in the land will eat curds and honey.  
**7:23** And on that day<br>every place where there were a thousand vines,<br>worth a thousand pieces of silver,<br>will become thorns and briers.  
**7:24** A man will go there with bow and arrows<br>because the whole land will be thorns and briers.  
**7:25** You will not go to all the hills<br>that were once tilled with a hoe,<br>for fear of the thorns and briers.<br>Those hills will be places for oxen to graze<br>and for sheep to trample.
