# 35.하박국 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** The pronouncement that the prophet Habakkuk saw.  
**1:2** How long, LORD, must I call for help<br>and you do not listen<br>or cry out to you about violence<br>and you do not save?  
**1:3** Why do you force me to look at injustice?<br>Why do you tolerate<sup>①</sup> wrongdoing?<br>Oppression and violence are right in front of me.<br>Strife is ongoing, and conflict escalates.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>observe</i>, also in v. <a name='B:420 1:13'>13  
**1:4** This is why the law is ineffective<br>and justice never emerges.<br>For the wicked restrict the righteous;<br>therefore, justice comes out perverted.  
**1:5** Look at the nations<sup>①</sup> and observe—<br>be utterly astounded!<br>For I am doing something in your days<br>that you will not believe<br>when you hear about it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>DSS, LXX, Syr read <i>Look, you treacherous people</i>  
**1:6** Look! I am raising up the Chaldeans,<sup>①</sup><br>that bitter, impetuous nation<br>that marches across the earth’s open spaces<br>to seize territories not its own.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= the Babylonians  
**1:7** They are fierce and terrifying;<br>their views of justice and sovereignty<br>stem from themselves.  
**1:8** Their horses are swifter than leopards<br>and more fierce<sup>①</sup> than wolves of the night.<br>Their horsemen charge ahead;<br>their horsemen come from distant lands.<br>They fly like eagles, swooping to devour.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>and quicker</i>  
**1:9** All of them come to do violence;<br>their faces are set in determination.<sup>①</sup><br>They gather prisoners like sand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hb obscure  
**1:10** They mock kings,<br>and rulers are a joke to them.<br>They laugh at every fortress<br>and build siege ramps to capture it.  
**1:11** Then they sweep by like the wind<br>and pass through.<br>They are guilty;<sup>①</sup> their strength is their god.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>wind, and transgress and incur guilt</i>  
**1:12** Are you not from eternity, LORD my God?<br>My Holy One, you<sup>①</sup> will not die.<br>LORD, you appointed them to execute judgment;<br>my Rock, you destined them to punish us.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Alt Hb tradition reads <i>we</i>  
**1:13** Your eyes are too pure to look on evil,<br>and you cannot tolerate wrongdoing.<br>So why do you tolerate those who are treacherous?<br>Why are you silent<br>while one<sup>①</sup> who is wicked swallows up<br>one<sup>②</sup> who is more righteous than himself?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= Babylon <sup>②</sup>= Judah  
**1:14** You have made mankind<br>like the fish of the sea,<br>like marine creatures that have no ruler.  
**1:15** The Chaldeans pull them all up with a hook,<br>catch them in their dragnet,<br>and gather them in their fishing net;<br>that is why they are glad and rejoice.  
**1:16** That is why they sacrifice to their dragnet<br>and burn incense to their fishing net,<br>for by these things their portion is rich<br>and their food plentiful.  
**1:17** Will they therefore empty their net<br>and continually slaughter nations without mercy?  

## 2장

**2:1** I will stand at my guard post<br>and station myself on the lookout tower.<br>I will watch to see what he will say to me<br>and what I should<sup>①</sup> reply about my complaint.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Syr reads <i>what he will</i>  
**2:2** The LORD answered me:<br>Write down this vision;<br>clearly inscribe it on tablets<br>so one may easily read it.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>one who reads in it may run</i>  
**2:3** For the vision is yet for the appointed time;<br>it testifies about the end and will not lie.<br>Though it delays, wait for it,<br>since it will certainly come and not be late.  
**2:4** Look, his ego is inflated;<sup>①</sup><br>he is without integrity.<br>But the righteous one will live by his faith.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hb obscure <sup>②</sup>Or <i>faithfulness</i>  
**2:5** Moreover, wine<sup>①</sup> betrays;<br>an arrogant man is never at rest.<sup>②</sup><br>He enlarges his appetite like Sheol,<br>and like Death he is never satisfied.<br>He gathers all the nations to himself;<br>he collects all the peoples for himself.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>DSS read <i>wealth</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>man does not endure;</i> Hb obscure  
**2:6** Won’t all of these take up a taunt against him,<br>with mockery and riddles about him?<br>They will say: “Woe to him who amasses what is not his—<br>how much longer?—<br>and loads himself with goods taken in pledge.”  
**2:7** Won’t your creditors suddenly arise,<br>and those who disturb you wake up?<br>Then you will become spoil for them.  
**2:8** Since you have plundered many nations,<br>all the peoples who remain will plunder you—<br>because of human bloodshed<br>and violence against lands, cities,<br>and all who live in them.  
**2:9** Woe to him who dishonestly makes<br>wealth for his house<sup>①</sup><br>to place his nest on high,<br>to escape the grasp of disaster!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>dynasty</i>  
**2:10** You have planned shame for your house<br>by wiping out many peoples<br>and sinning against your own self.  
**2:11** For the stones will cry out from the wall,<br>and the rafters will answer them<br>from the woodwork.  
**2:12** Woe to him who builds a city with bloodshed<br>and founds a town with injustice!  
**2:13** Is it not from the LORD of Armies<br>that the peoples labor only to fuel the fire<br>and countries exhaust themselves for nothing?  
**2:14** For the earth will be filled<br>with the knowledge of the LORD’s glory,<br>as the water covers the sea.  
**2:15** Woe to him who gives his neighbors drink,<br>pouring out your wrath<sup>①</sup><br>and even making them drunk,<br>in order to look at their nakedness!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>venom</i>  
**2:16** You will be filled with disgrace instead of glory.<br>You also—drink,<br>and expose your uncircumcision!<sup>①</sup><br>The cup in the LORD’s right hand<br>will come around to you,<br>and utter disgrace will cover your glory.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>DSS, LXX, Aq, Syr, Vg read <i>and stagger</i>  
**2:17** For your violence against Lebanon<br>will overwhelm you;<br>the destruction of animals will terrify you<sup>①</sup><br>because of your human bloodshed and violence<br>against lands, cities, and all who live in them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>DSS, LXX, Aq, Syr, Tg, Vg; MT reads <i>them</i>  
**2:18** What use is a carved idol<br>after its craftsman carves it?<br>It is only a cast image, a teacher of lies.<br>For the one who crafts its shape trusts in it<br>and makes worthless idols that cannot speak.  
**2:19** Woe to him who says to wood: Wake up!<br>or to mute stone: Come alive!<br>Can it teach?<br>Look! It may be plated with gold and silver,<br>yet there is no breath in it at all.  
**2:20** But the LORD is in his holy temple;<br>let the whole earth<br>be silent in his presence.  

## 3장

**3:1** A prayer of the prophet Habakkuk. According to <i>Shigionoth</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Perhaps a passionate song with rapid changes of rhythm, or a dirge  
**3:2** LORD, I have heard the report about you;<br>LORD, I stand in awe of your deeds.<br>Revive your work in these years;<br>make it known in these years.<br>In your wrath remember mercy!  
**3:3** God comes from Teman,<br>the Holy One from Mount Paran. <i>Selah</i> His splendor covers the heavens,<br>and the earth is full of his praise.  
**3:4** His brilliance is like light;<br>rays are flashing from his hand.<br>This is where his power is hidden.  
**3:5** Plague goes before him,<br>and pestilence follows in his steps.  
**3:6** He stands and shakes<sup>①</sup> the earth;<br>he looks and startles the nations.<br>The age-old mountains break apart;<br>the ancient hills sink down.<br>His pathways are ancient.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>surveys</i>  
**3:7** I see the tents of Cushan<sup>①</sup> in distress;<br>the tent curtains of the land of Midian tremble.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= Midian  
**3:8** Are you angry at the rivers, LORD?<br>Is your wrath against the rivers?<br>Or is your fury against the sea<br>when you ride on your horses,<br>your victorious chariot?  
**3:9** You took the sheath from your bow;<br>the arrows are ready<sup>①</sup> to be used with an oath.<sup>②</sup> <i>Selah</i> You split the earth with rivers.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>set</i> <sup>②</sup>Hb obscure  
**3:10** The mountains see you and shudder;<br>a downpour of water sweeps by.<br>The deep roars with its voice<br>and lifts its waves<sup>①</sup> high.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>hands</i>  
**3:11** Sun and moon stand still in their lofty residence,<br>at the flash of your flying arrows,<br>at the brightness of your shining spear.  
**3:12** You march across the earth with indignation;<br>you trample down the nations in wrath.  
**3:13** You come out to save your people,<br>to save your anointed.<sup>①</sup><br>You crush the leader of the house of the wicked<br>and strip him from foot<sup>②</sup> to neck. <i>Selah</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Davidic king or the nation of Israel <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>foundation</i>  
**3:14** You pierce his head<br>with his own spears;<br>his warriors storm out to scatter us,<br>gloating as if ready to secretly devour the weak.  
**3:15** You tread the sea with your horses,<br>stirring up the vast water.  
**3:16** I heard, and I trembled within;<br>my lips quivered at the sound.<br>Rottenness entered my bones;<br>I trembled where I stood.<br>Now I must quietly wait for the day of distress<br>to come against the people invading us.  
**3:17** Though the fig tree does not bud<br>and there is no fruit on the vines,<br>though the olive crop fails<br>and the fields produce no food,<br>though the flocks disappear from the pen<br>and there are no herds in the stalls,  
**3:18** yet I will celebrate in the LORD;<br>I will rejoice in the God of my salvation!  
**3:19** The LORD my Lord is my strength;<br>he makes my feet like those of a deer<br>and enables me to walk on mountain heights! For the choir director: on<sup>①</sup> stringed instruments.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>on my</i>
