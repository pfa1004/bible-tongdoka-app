# 31.오바디야 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision of Obadiah. This is what the Lord GOD has said about Edom:<br>We have heard a message from the LORD;<br>an envoy has been sent among the nations:<br>“Rise up, and let’s go to war against her.”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>= Edom  
**1:2** Look, I will make you insignificant<br>among the nations;<br>you will be deeply despised.  
**1:3** Your arrogant heart has deceived you,<br>you who live in clefts of the rock<sup>①</sup><sup>②</sup><br>in your home on the heights,<br>who say to yourself,<br>“Who can bring me down to the ground?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in Sela;</i> probably = Petra <sup>②</sup>Probably Petra  
**1:4** Though you seem to soar<sup>①</sup> like an eagle<br>and make your nest among the stars,<br>even from there I will bring you down. This is the LORD’s declaration.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>to build high</i>  
**1:5** If thieves came to you,<br>if marauders by night—<br>how ravaged you would be!—<br>wouldn’t they steal only what they wanted?<br>If grape pickers came to you,<br>wouldn’t they leave a few grapes?  
**1:6** How Esau will be pillaged,<br>his hidden treasures searched out!  
**1:7** Everyone who has a treaty with you<br>will drive you to the border;<br>everyone at peace with you<br>will deceive and conquer you.<br>Those who eat your bread<br>will set<sup>①</sup> a trap for you.<br>He will be unaware of it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some LXX mss, Sym, Tg, Vg; MT reads <i>They will set your bread as</i>  
**1:8** In that day—this is the LORD’s declaration—will I not eliminate the wise ones of Edom<br>and those who understand<br>from the hill country of Esau?  
**1:9** Teman,<sup>①</sup> your warriors will be terrified<br>so that everyone from the hill country of Esau<br>will be destroyed by slaughter.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= a region or city in Edom  
**1:10** You will be covered with shame<br>and destroyed forever<br>because of violence done to your brother Jacob.  
**1:11** On the day you stood aloof,<br>on the day strangers captured his wealth,<sup>①</sup><br>while foreigners entered his city gate<br>and cast lots for Jerusalem,<br>you were just like one of them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>forces</i>  
**1:12** Do not<sup>①</sup> gloat over your brother<br>in the day of his calamity;<br>do not rejoice over the people of Judah<br>in the day of their destruction;<br>do not boastfully mock<sup>②</sup><br>in the day of distress.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>You should not</i> throughout vv. <a name='B:380 1:12-14'>12-14 <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>not make your mouth big</i>  
**1:13** Do not enter my people’s city gate<br>in the day of their disaster.<br>Yes, you—do not gloat over their misery<br>in the day of their disaster,<br>and do not appropriate their possessions<br>in the day of their disaster.  
**1:14** Do not stand at the crossroads<sup>①</sup><br>to cut off their fugitives,<br>and do not hand over their survivors<br>in the day of distress.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hb obscure  
**1:15** For the day of the LORD is near,<br>against all the nations.<br>As you have done, it will be done to you;<br>what you deserve will return on your own head.  
**1:16** As you have drunk on my holy mountain,<br>so all the nations will drink continually.<br>They will drink and gulp down<br>and be as though they had never been.  
**1:17** But there will be a deliverance on Mount Zion,<br>and it will be holy;<br>the house of Jacob will dispossess<br>those who dispossessed them.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>DSS, LXX, Syr, Vg, Tg; MT reads <i>Jacob will possess its inheritance</i>  
**1:18** Then the house of Jacob will be a blazing fire,<br>and the house of Joseph, a burning flame,<br>but the house of Esau will be stubble;<br>Jacob<sup>①</sup> will set them on fire and consume Edom.<sup>②</sup><br>Therefore no survivor will remain<br>of the house of Esau,<br>for the LORD has spoken.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>they</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>them</i>  
**1:19** People from the Negev will possess<br>the hill country of Esau;<br>those from the Judean foothills will possess<br>the land of the Philistines.<br>They<sup>①</sup> will possess<br>the territories of Ephraim and Samaria,<br>while Benjamin will possess Gilead.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= The house of Jacob  
**1:20** The exiles of the Israelites who are in Halah<sup>①</sup><br>and who are among the Canaanites as far as Zarephath<br>as well as the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad<br>will possess the cities of the Negev.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>of this host of the Israelites;</i> Hb obscure  
**1:21** Saviors<sup>①</sup> will ascend Mount Zion<br>to rule over the hill country of Esau,<br>and the kingdom will be the LORD’s.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Those who have been delivered</i>
