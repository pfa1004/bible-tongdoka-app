# 34.나훔 (CSB)



## 1장

**1:1** The pronouncement concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.  
**1:2** The LORD is a jealous and avenging God;<br>the LORD takes vengeance<br>and is fierce in<sup>①</sup> wrath.<br>The LORD takes vengeance against his foes;<br>he is furious with his enemies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>is a master of</i>  
**1:3** The LORD is slow to anger but great in power;<br>the LORD will never leave the guilty unpunished.<br>His path is in the whirlwind and storm,<br>and clouds are the dust beneath his feet.  
**1:4** He rebukes the sea and dries it up,<br>and he makes all the rivers run dry.<br>Bashan and Carmel wither;<br>even the flower of Lebanon withers.  
**1:5** The mountains quake before him,<br>and the hills melt;<br>the earth trembles<sup>①</sup><sup>②</sup> at his presence—<br>the world and all who live in it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some emend to <i>earth is laid waste</i> <sup>②</sup>Lit <i>lifts</i>  
**1:6** Who can withstand his indignation?<br>Who can endure his burning anger?<br>His wrath is poured out like fire;<br>even rocks are shattered before him.  
**1:7** The LORD is good,<br>a stronghold in a day of distress;<br>he cares for those who take refuge in him.  
**1:8** But he will completely destroy Nineveh<sup>①</sup><br>with an overwhelming flood,<br>and he will chase his enemies into darkness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>her place</i>  
**1:9** Whatever you<sup>①</sup> plot against the LORD,<br>he will bring it to complete destruction;<br>oppression will not rise up a second time.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= Nineveh  
**1:10** For they will be consumed<br>like entangled thorns,<br>like the drink of a drunkard<br>and like straw that is fully dry.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hb obscure  
**1:11** One has gone out from you,<sup>①</sup><br>who plots evil against the LORD,<br>and is a wicked counselor.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Possibly Nineveh  
**1:12** This is what the LORD says:<br>Though they are strong<sup>①</sup> and numerous,<br>they will still be mowed down,<br>and he<sup>②</sup> will pass away.<br>Though I have punished you,<sup>③</sup><br>I will punish you no longer.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>intact</i> <sup>②</sup>Either the king of Assyria or his army <sup>③</sup>= Judah  
**1:13** For I will now break off his yoke from you<br>and tear off your shackles.  
**1:14** The LORD has issued an order concerning you:<br>There will be no offspring<br>to carry on your name.<sup>①</sup><br>I will eliminate the carved idol and cast image<br>from the house of your gods;<br>I will prepare your grave,<br>for you are contemptible.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>It will not be sown from your name any longer</i>  
**1:15** Look to the mountains—<br>the feet of the herald,<br>who proclaims peace.<br>Celebrate your festivals, Judah;<br>fulfill your vows.<br>For the wicked one will never again<br>march through you;<br>he will be entirely wiped out.  

## 2장

**2:1** One who scatters is coming up against you.<br>Man the fortifications!<br>Watch the road!<br>Brace<sup>①</sup> yourself!<br>Summon all your strength!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>Strengthen</i>  
**2:2** For the LORD will restore the majesty of Jacob,<br>yes,<sup>①</sup> the majesty of Israel,<br>though ravagers have ravaged them<br>and ruined their vine branches.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>like</i>  
**2:3** The shields of his<sup>①</sup> warriors are dyed red;<br>the valiant men are dressed in scarlet.<br>The fittings of the chariot flash like fire<br>on the day of its battle preparations,<br>and the spears are brandished.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= the army commander attacking Nineveh  
**2:4** The chariots dash madly through the streets;<br>they rush around in the plazas.<br>They look like torches;<br>they dart back and forth like lightning.  
**2:5** He gives orders to his officers;<br>they stumble as they advance.<br>They race to its wall;<br>the protective shield is set in place.  
**2:6** The river gates are opened,<br>and the palace erodes away.  
**2:7** Beauty<sup>①</sup> is stripped;<sup>②</sup><br>she is carried away;<br>her ladies-in-waiting moan<br>like the sound of doves<br>and beat their breasts.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Text emended; MT reads <i>Huzzab</i> <sup>②</sup>Hb obscure  
**2:8** Nineveh has been like a pool of water<br>from her first days,<sup>①</sup><br>but they are fleeing.<br>“Stop! Stop!” they cry,<br>but no one turns back.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hb obscure  
**2:9** “Plunder the silver! Plunder the gold!”<br>There is no end to the treasure,<br>an abundance of every precious thing.  
**2:10** Desolation, decimation, devastation!<br>Hearts melt,<br>knees tremble,<br>insides churn,<br>every face grows pale!  
**2:11** Where is the lions’ lair,<br>or the feeding ground of the young lions,<br>where the lion and lioness prowled,<br>and the lion’s cub,<br>with nothing to frighten them away?  
**2:12** The lion mauled whatever its cubs needed<br>and strangled prey for its lionesses.<br>It filled up its dens with the kill,<br>and its lairs with mauled prey.  
**2:13** Beware, I am against you. This is the declaration of the LORD of Armies. I will make your chariots go up in smoke,<sup>①</sup><br>and the sword will devour your young lions.<br>I will cut off your prey from the earth,<br>and the sound of your messengers<br>will never be heard again.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>will burn her chariots in smoke</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** Woe to the city of blood,<br>totally deceitful,<br>full of plunder,<br>never without prey.  
**3:2** The crack of the whip<br>and rumble of the wheel,<br>galloping horse<br>and jolting chariot!  
**3:3** Charging horseman,<br>flashing sword,<br>shining spear;<br>heaps of slain,<br>mounds of corpses,<br>dead bodies without end—<br>they stumble over their dead.  
**3:4** Because of the continual prostitution of the prostitute,<br>the attractive mistress of sorcery,<br>who treats nations and clans like merchandise<br>by her prostitution and sorcery,  
**3:5** I am against you. This is the declaration of the LORD of Armies. I will lift your skirts over your face<br>and display your nakedness to nations,<br>your shame to kingdoms.  
**3:6** I will throw filth on you<br>and treat you with contempt;<br>I will make a spectacle of you.  
**3:7** Then all who see you will recoil from you, saying,<br>“Nineveh is devastated;<br>who will show sympathy to her?”<br>Where can I find anyone to comfort you?  
**3:8** Are you better than Thebes<sup>①</sup><br>that sat along the Nile<br>with water surrounding her,<br>whose rampart was the sea,<br>the river<sup>②</sup><sup>③</sup> her wall?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hb <i>No-amon</i> <sup>②</sup>LXX, Syr, Vg read <i>water</i> <sup>③</sup>Lit <i>sea from sea</i>  
**3:9** Cush and Egypt were her endless source of strength;<br>Put and Libya were among her<sup>①</sup> allies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Lit <i>your</i>  
**3:10** Yet she became an exile;<br>she went into captivity.<br>Her children were also dashed to pieces<br>at the head of every street.<br>They cast lots for her dignitaries,<br>and all her nobles were bound in chains.  
**3:11** You<sup>①</sup> also will become drunk;<br>you will hide.<sup>②</sup><br>You also will seek refuge from the enemy.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>= Nineveh <sup>②</sup>Or <i>will be overcome</i>  
**3:12** All your fortresses are fig trees<br>with figs that ripened first;<br>when shaken, they fall—<br>right into the mouth of the eater!  
**3:13** Look, your troops are like women among you;<br>your land’s city gates<br>are wide open to your enemies.<br>Fire will devour the bars of your gates.  
**3:14** Draw water for the siege;<br>strengthen your fortresses.<br>Step into the clay and tread the mortar;<br>take hold of the brick-mold!  
**3:15** The fire will devour you there;<br>the sword will cut you down.<br>It will devour you like the young locust.<br>Multiply yourselves like the young locust;<br>multiply like the swarming locust!  
**3:16** You have made your merchants<br>more numerous than the stars of the sky.<br>The young locust strips<sup>①</sup> the land<br>and flies away.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sheds its skin</i>  
**3:17** Your court officials are like the swarming locust,<br>and your scribes like clouds of locusts,<br>which settle on the walls on a cold day;<br>when the sun rises, they take off,<br>and no one knows where they are.  
**3:18** King of Assyria, your shepherds slumber;<br>your officers sleep.<br>Your people are scattered across the mountains<br>with no one to gather them together.  
**3:19** There is no remedy for your injury;<br>your wound is severe.<br>All who hear the news about you<br>will clap their hands because of you,<br>for who has not experienced<br>your constant cruelty?
