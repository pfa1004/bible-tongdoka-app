# 19.시편 (NIRV)



## 1장

**1:1** BOOK I Blessed is the one who obeys the law of the Lord. He doesn't follow the advice of evil people. He doesn't make a habit of doing what sinners do. He doesn't join those who make fun of the Lord and his law.  
**1:2** Instead, he takes delight in the law of the Lord. He thinks about his law day and night.  
**1:3** He is like a tree that is planted near a stream of water. It always bears its fruit at the right time. Its leaves don't dry up. Everything godly people do turns out well.  
**1:4** Sinful people are not like that at all. They are like straw that the wind blows away.  
**1:5** When the Lord judges them, their life will come to an end. Sinners won't have any place among those who are godly.  
**1:6** The Lord watches over the lives of those who are godly. But the lives of sinful people will lead to their death.  

## 2장

**2:2** The kings of the earth take their stand against the Lord. The rulers of the earth gather together against his anointed king.  
**2:3** "Let us break free from their chains," they say. "Let us throw off their ropes."  
**2:4** The One who sits on his throne in heaven laughs. The Lord makes fun of those rulers and their plans.

**2:6** He says to them, "I have placed my king on my holy mountain of Zion."

**2:8** Ask me, and I will give the nations to you. All nations on earth will belong to you.  
**2:9** You will rule them with an iron rod. You will break them to pieces like clay pots."

**2:12** Obey the son completely, or he will be angry. Your way of life will lead to your death. His anger can blaze out at any moment. Blessed are all those who go to him for safety.

**139:2** You know when I sit down and when I get up. You know what I'm thinking even though you are far away.  
**139:3** You know when I go out to work and when I come back home. You know exactly how I live.  
**139:4** Lord, even before I speak a word, you know all about it.  
**139:5** You are all around me. You are behind me and in front of me. You hold me in your power.  
**139:6** I'm amazed at how well you know me. It's more than I can understand.  
**139:7** How can I get away from your Spirit? Where can I go to escape from you?  
**139:8** If I go up to the heavens, you are there. If I lie down in the deepest parts of the earth, you are also there.  
**139:9** Suppose I were to rise with the sun in the east and then cross over to the west where it sinks into the ocean.  
**139:10** Your hand would always be there to guide me. Your right hand would still be holding me close.  
**139:11** Suppose I were to say, "I'm sure the darkness will hide me. The light around me will become as dark as night."  
**139:12** Even that darkness would not be dark to you. The night would shine like the day, because darkness is like light to you.  
**139:13** You created the deepest parts of my being. You put me together inside my mother's body.  
**139:14** How you made me is amazing and wonderful. I praise you for that. What you have done is wonderful. I know that very well.  
**139:15** None of my bones was hidden from you when you made me inside my mother's body. That place was as dark as the deepest parts of the earth. When you were putting me together there,  
**139:16** your eyes saw my body even before it was formed. You planned how many days I would live. You wrote down the number of them in your book before I had lived through even one of them.  
**139:17** God, your thoughts about me are priceless. No one can possibly add them all up.  
**139:18** If I could count them, they would be more than the grains of sand. If I were to fall asleep counting and then wake up, you would still be there with me.  
**139:19** God, I wish you would kill the people who are evil! I wish those murderers would get away from me!  
**139:20** They are your enemies. They misuse your name. They misuse it for their own evil purposes.  
**139:21** Lord, I really hate those who hate you! I really hate those who rise up against you!  
**139:22** I have nothing but hatred for them. I consider them to be my enemies.  
**139:23** God, see what is in my heart. Know what is there. Put me to the test. Know what I'm thinking.  
**139:24** See if there's anything in my life you don't like. Help me live in the way that is always right.
