# 21.전도서 (NIRV)



## 1장

**1:1** These are the words of the Teacher. He was the son of David. He was also king in Jerusalem.  
**1:2** "Meaningless! Everything is meaningless!" says the Teacher. "Everything is completely meaningless! Nothing has any meaning."  
**1:3** What does a man get for all of his work? Why does he work so hard on this earth?  
**1:4** People come and people go. But the earth remains forever.  
**1:5** The sun rises. Then it sets. And then it hurries back to where it rises.  
**1:6** The wind blows to the south. Then it turns to the north. Around and around it goes. It always returns to where it started.  
**1:7** Every stream flows into the ocean. But the ocean never gets full. The streams return to the place they came from.  
**1:8** All things are tiresome. They are more tiresome than anyone can say. But our eyes never see enough of anything. Our ears never hear enough.  
**1:9** Everything that has ever been will come back again. Everything that has ever been done will be done again. Nothing is new on earth.  
**1:10** There isn't anything about which someone can say, "Look! Here's something new." It was already here long ago. It was here before we were.  
**1:11** No one remembers the men of long ago. Even those who haven't been born yet won't be remembered by those who will be born after them.  
**1:12** I, the Teacher, was king over Israel in Jerusalem.  
**1:13** I spent all of my time studying. I used my wisdom to check everything out. I looked into everything that is done on earth. What a heavy load God has put on men!  
**1:14** I've seen what is done on this earth. It doesn't have any meaning. It's like chasing the wind.  
**1:15** People can't straighten things that are twisted. They can't count things that don't even exist.  
**1:16** I said to myself, "Look, my wisdom has really been growing. In fact, I'm now wiser than anyone who ruled over Jerusalem in the past. I have a lot of wisdom and knowledge."  
**1:17** Then I used my mind to understand what it really means to be wise. And I wanted to know what foolish pleasure is all about. But I found out that that's also like chasing the wind.  
**1:18** A lot of human wisdom leads to a lot of sorrow. More knowledge only brings more sadness.  

## 2장

**2:1** I said to myself, "Come on. I'll put pleasure to the test. I want to find out what is good." But that also proved to be meaningless.  
**2:2** "Laughter is foolish," I said. "And what can pleasure do for me?"  
**2:3** I tried cheering myself up by drinking wine. I even tried living in a foolish way. But wisdom was still guiding my mind. I wanted to see what was really important for men to do on earth during the few days of their lives.  
**2:4** So I started some large projects. I built houses for myself. I planted vineyards.  
**2:5** I made gardens and parks. I planted all kinds of fruit trees in them.  
**2:6** I made lakes to water groves of healthy trees.  
**2:7** I bought male and female slaves. And I had other slaves who were born in my house. I also owned more herds and flocks than anyone in Jerusalem ever had before.  
**2:8** I stored up silver and gold for myself. I gathered up the treasures of kings and their kingdoms. I got some male and female singers. I also got many women for myself. Women delight the hearts of men.  
**2:9** I became far more important than anyone in Jerusalem had ever been before. And in spite of everything, I didn't lose my wisdom.  
**2:10** I gave myself everything my eyes wanted. There wasn't any pleasure that I refused to give myself. I took delight in everything I did. And that was what I got for all of my work.  
**2:11** But then I looked over everything my hands had done. I saw what I had worked so hard to get. And nothing had any meaning. It was like chasing the wind. Nothing was gained on this earth.  
**2:12** I decided to think about wisdom. I also thought about foolish pleasure. What more can a new king do? Can he do anything more than others have already done?  
**2:13** I saw that wisdom is better than foolishness, just as light is better than darkness.  
**2:14** The eyes of a wise man see things clearly. A person who is foolish lives in darkness. But I finally realized that death catches up with both of them.  
**2:15** Then I thought, "What happens to a foolish person will catch up with me too. So what do I gain by being wise?" I said to myself, "That doesn't have any meaning either."  
**2:16** Like a foolish person, a wise man won't be remembered very long. In days to come, both of them will be forgotten. Like a person who is foolish, a wise man must die too!  
**2:17** So I hated life. That's because the work that is done on this earth made me sad. None of it has any meaning. It's like chasing the wind.  
**2:18** I hated everything I had worked for on earth. I'll have to leave all of it to someone who lives after me.  
**2:19** And who knows whether he will be wise or foolish? Either way, he'll take over everything on earth I've worked so hard for. That doesn't have any meaning either.  
**2:20** So I began to lose hope because of all of my hard work on this earth.  
**2:21** A man might use wisdom, knowledge and skill to do his work. But then he has to leave everything he owns to someone who hasn't worked for it. That doesn't have any meaning either. In fact, it isn't fair.  
**2:22** What does a man get for all of his hard work on earth? What does he get for all of his worries?  
**2:23** As long as he lives, his work is nothing but pain and sorrow. Even at night his mind can't rest. That doesn't have any meaning either.  
**2:24** A man can't do anything better than eat and drink and be satisfied with his work. I'm finally seeing that those things also come from the hand of God.  
**2:25** Without his help, who can eat or find pleasure?  
**2:26** God gives wisdom, knowledge and happiness to a man who pleases him. But to a sinner he gives the task of gathering and storing up wealth. Then the sinner must hand it over to the one who pleases God. That doesn't have any meaning either. It's like chasing the wind.  

## 3장

**3:1** There is a time for everything. There's a time for everything that is done on earth.  
**3:2** There is a time to be born. And there's a time to die. There is a time to plant. And there's a time to pull up what is planted.  
**3:3** There is a time to kill. And there's a time to heal. There is a time to tear down. And there's a time to build up.  
**3:4** There is a time to cry. And there's a time to laugh. There is a time to be sad. And there's a time to dance.  
**3:5** There is a time to scatter stones. And there's a time to gather them. There is a time to hug. And there's a time not to hug.  
**3:6** There is a time to search. And there's a time to stop searching. There is a time to keep. And there's a time to throw away.  
**3:7** There is a time to tear. And there's a time to mend. There is a time to be silent. And there's a time to speak.  
**3:8** There is a time to love. And there's a time to hate. There is a time for war. And there's a time for peace.  
**3:9** What does the worker get for his hard work?  
**3:10** I've seen the heavy load God has put on men.  
**3:11** He has made everything beautiful in its time. He has also given men a sense of what he's been doing down through the ages. But they can't completely figure out what he's done from the beginning to the end.  
**3:12** They should be happy and do good while they live. I know there's nothing better for them to do than that.  
**3:13** Everyone should eat and drink. People should be satisfied with all of their hard work. That is God's gift to them.  
**3:14** I know that everything God does will last forever. Nothing can be added to it. And nothing can be taken from it. God does that so men will have respect for him.  
**3:15** Everything that now exists has already been. And what is coming has existed before. God will judge those who treat others badly.  
**3:16** Here's something else I saw on earth. Where people should be treated right, they are treated wrong. Where people should be treated fairly, they are treated unfairly.  
**3:17** I said to myself, "God will judge godly and sinful people alike. He has a time for every act. He has a time for everything that is done."  
**3:18** I also thought, "God puts human beings to the test. Then they can see they are just like animals.  
**3:19** What happens to animals happens to people too. Death waits for people and animals alike. People die, just as animals do. All of them have the same breath. People don't have any advantage over animals. Nothing has any meaning.  
**3:20** "People and animals go to the same place. All of them come from dust. And all of them return to dust.  
**3:21** Who can know whether the spirit of a man goes up? Who can tell whether the spirit of an animal goes down into the earth?"  
**3:22** So man should enjoy his work. That's what God made him for. I saw that there's nothing better for him to do than that. After all, who can show him what will happen after he is gone?  

## 4장

**4:1** I looked and saw how much people were suffering on this earth. I saw the tears of those who are suffering. They don't have anyone to comfort them. Power is on the side of those who beat them down. Those who are suffering don't have anyone to comfort them.  
**4:2** Then I announced that those who have already died are happier than those who are still alive.  
**4:3** But someone who hasn't been born yet is better off than the dead or the living. That's because he hasn't seen the evil things that are done on earth.  
**4:4** I also saw that man works hard and accomplishes a lot. But he does it only because he wants what his neighbor has. That doesn't have any meaning either. It's like chasing the wind.  
**4:5** A foolish person folds his hands and doesn't work. And that destroys him.  
**4:6** One handful with peace and quiet is better than two handfuls with hard work. Working too hard is like chasing the wind.  
**4:7** Again I saw something on earth that didn't mean anything.  
**4:8** A man lived all by himself. He didn't have any sons or brothers. His hard work never ended. But he wasn't happy with what he had. "Who am I working so hard for?" he asked. "Why don't I get the things I enjoy?" That doesn't have any meaning either. In fact, it's a very bad deal!  
**4:9** Two people are better than one. They can help each other in everything they do.  
**4:10** Suppose someone falls down. Then his friend can help him up. But suppose the man who falls down doesn't have anyone to help him up. Then feel sorry for him!  
**4:11** Or suppose two people lie down together. Then they'll keep warm. But how can one person keep warm alone?  
**4:12** One person could be overpowered. But two people can stand up for themselves. And a rope made out of three cords isn't easily broken.  
**4:13** A poor but wise young man is better off than an old but foolish king. That king doesn't pay attention to a warning anymore.  
**4:14** The young man might have come from prison to become king. Or he might have been born poor within the kingdom but still became king.  
**4:15** I saw that everyone was following the young man who had become the new king.  
**4:16** At first, all of the people served him when he became king. But those who came later weren't pleased with the way he was ruling. That doesn't have any meaning either. It's like chasing the wind.  

## 5장

**5:1** Be careful what you say when you go to God's house. Go there to listen. Don't be like foolish people when you offer your sacrifice. They do what is wrong and don't even know it.  
**5:2** Don't be too quick to speak. Don't be in a hurry to say anything to God. He is in heaven. You are on earth. So use only a few words when you speak.  
**5:3** Dreams come to people when they worry a lot. When foolish people talk, they use too many words.  
**5:4** When you make a promise to God, don't wait too long to carry it out. He isn't pleased with foolish people. So do what you have promised.  
**5:5** It is better to make no promise at all than to make a promise and not keep it.  
**5:6** Don't let your mouth cause you to sin. Don't object to the temple messenger. Don't say, "My promise was a mistake." Why should God be angry with what you say? Why should he destroy what you have done?  
**5:7** Dreaming too much and talking too much are meaningless. So have respect for God.  
**5:8** Suppose you see poor people being mistreated somewhere. And what is being done to them isn't right or fair. Don't be surprised by that. One official is watched by a higher one. Others who are even higher are watching both of them.  
**5:9** All of them take what the land produces. And the king himself takes his share from the fields.  
**5:10** Anyone who loves money never has enough. Anyone who loves wealth is never satisfied with what he gets. That doesn't have any meaning either.  
**5:11** As more and more goods are made, more and more people use them up. So how can those goods benefit their owner? All he can do is look at them with longing.  
**5:12** The sleep of a worker is sweet. It doesn't matter whether he eats a little or a lot. But the wealth of a rich man keeps him awake at night.  
**5:13** I've seen something very evil on earth. It's when wealth is stored up and then brings harm to its owner.  
**5:14** It's also when wealth is lost because of an unwise business deal. Then there won't be anything left for the owner's son.  
**5:15** A man is born naked. He comes into the world with nothing. And he goes out of it with nothing. He doesn't get anything from his work that he can take with him.  
**5:16** Here's something else that is very evil. A man is born, and a man dies. And what does he get for his work? Nothing. It's like working for the wind.  
**5:17** All his life he eats in darkness. His life is full of trouble, suffering and anger.  
**5:18** I realized that it's good and proper for a man to eat and drink. It's good for him to be satisfied with his hard work on this earth. That's what he should do during the few days of life God has given him. That's what God made him for.  
**5:19** Sometimes God gives a man wealth and possessions. He makes it possible for him to enjoy them. He helps him accept the life he has given him. He helps him to be happy in his work. All of those things are gifts from God.  
**5:20** A man like that doesn't have to think about how his life is going. That's because God fills his heart with joy.  

## 6장

**6:1** I've seen another evil thing on this earth. And it's a heavy load on men.  
**6:2** God gives a man wealth, possessions and honor. He has everything his heart longs for. But God doesn't let him enjoy those things. Instead, strangers enjoy them. That doesn't have any meaning. It's a very evil thing.  
**6:3** A man might have a hundred children. He might live a long time. But suppose he can't enjoy his wealth. And suppose he isn't buried in the proper way. Then it doesn't matter how long he lives. I'm telling you that a baby that is born dead is better off than he is.  
**6:4** That kind of birth doesn't have any meaning. The baby dies in darkness and leaves this world. And in darkness it is forgotten.  
**6:5** It didn't even see the sun. It didn't know anything at all. But it has more rest than that man does.  
**6:6** And that's true even if he lives for 2,000 years but doesn't get to enjoy his wealth. All people die and go to the grave, don't they?  
**6:7** Man eats up everything he works to get. But he is never satisfied.  
**6:8** What advantage does a wise man have over someone who is foolish? What does a poor man gain by knowing how to act toward others?  
**6:9** Being satisfied with what you have is better than always wanting more. That doesn't have any meaning either. It's like chasing the wind.  
**6:10** God has already planned what now exists. He has already decided what man is. A man can't argue with the One who is stronger than he is.  
**6:11** The more words people use, the less meaning there is. And that doesn't help anyone.  
**6:12** Who knows what's good for a man? He lives for only a few meaningless days. He passes through life like a shadow. Who can tell him what will happen on earth after he is gone?  

## 7장

**7:1** A good name is better than fine perfume. People can learn more from sobbing when someone dies than from being happy when someone is born.  
**7:2** So it's better to go where people are sobbing than to go where people are having a good time. Everyone will die someday. Those who are still living should really think about that.  
**7:3** Sadness is good for the heart. That's why sorrow is better than laughter.  
**7:4** Those who are wise are found where there is sorrow. But foolish people are found where there is pleasure.  
**7:5** Pay attention to a wise man's warning. That's better than listening to the songs of those who are foolish.  
**7:6** A foolish person's laughter is like the crackling of thorns burning under a pot. That doesn't have any meaning either.  
**7:7** When a wise man takes wealth by force, he becomes foolish. It is sinful to take money from people who want special favors.  
**7:8** The end of a matter is better than its beginning. So it's better to be patient than proud.  
**7:9** Don't become angry quickly. Anger lives in the hearts of foolish people.  
**7:10** Don't say, "Why were things better in the good old days?" It isn't wise to ask that kind of question.  
**7:11** Wisdom is a good thing. It's like getting a share of the family wealth. It benefits those who live on this earth.  
**7:12** Wisdom provides safety, just as money provides safety. But here's the advantage of wisdom. It guards the lives of those who have it.  
**7:13** Think about what God has done. Who can make straight what he has made crooked?  
**7:14** When times are good, be happy. But when times are bad, here's something to think about. God has made bad times. He has also made good times. So a man can't find out anything about what's ahead for him.  
**7:15** In my meaningless life here's what I've seen. I've seen a godly man dying even though he is godly. And I've seen a sinful man living a long time even though he is sinful.  
**7:16** Don't claim to be better than you are. And don't claim to be wiser than you are. Why destroy yourself?  
**7:17** Don't be too sinful. And don't be foolish. Why die before your time comes?  
**7:18** It's good to hold on to both of those things. Don't let go of either one. A man who has respect for God will avoid going too far in either direction.  
**7:19** Wisdom makes one wise man more powerful than ten rulers in a city.  
**7:20** There isn't anyone on earth who does only what is right and never sins.  
**7:21** Don't pay attention to everything people say. If you do, you might hear your servant calling down a curse on you.  
**7:22** Many times you yourself have called down curses on others. Deep down inside, you know that's true.  
**7:23** I used wisdom to put all of those things to the test. I said, "I've made up my mind to be wise." But it was more than I could accomplish.  
**7:24** No matter what else wisdom may be, it's far away and very deep. Who can find it?  
**7:25** So I tried to understand wisdom more completely. I wanted to study it and figure it out. I tried to find out everything I could about it. I tried to understand why it's foolish to be evil. I wanted to see why choosing foolishness is so unwise.  
**7:26** A woman who hunts a man down is more painful than death. Her heart is like a trap. Her hands are like chains. A man who pleases God will try to get away from her. But she will trap a sinner.  
**7:27** "Look," says the Teacher. "Here's what I've discovered. "I added one thing to another to find out everything I could about wisdom.  
**7:28** I searched and searched but found very little. I did find one honest man among a thousand. But I didn't find one honest woman among a thousand.  
**7:29** Here's the only other thing I found. God made men honest. But they've made many evil plans."  

## 8장

**8:1** Who is like a wise man? Who knows how to explain things? Wisdom makes a man's face bright. It softens the look on his face.  
**8:2** I'm telling you to obey the king's command. You took an oath to serve him. You made a promise to God.  
**8:3** Don't be in a hurry to quit your job in the palace. Don't stand up for something the king doesn't like. He'll do anything he wants to.  
**8:4** He has the final word. So who can ask him, "What are you doing?"  
**8:5** No one who obeys his command will be harmed. Those who are wise will know the proper time and way to approach him.  
**8:6** There's a proper time and way for people to do everything. That's true even though a man might be suffering greatly.  
**8:7** No one knows what lies ahead. So who can tell a person what's going to happen?  
**8:8** He can't stop the wind from blowing. And he doesn't have the power to decide when he will die. No one is let out of the army in times of war. And evil won't let go of those who practice it.  
**8:9** I understood all of those things. I used my mind to study everything that's done on earth. A man sometimes makes life hard for others. But he ends up hurting himself.  
**8:10** I also saw sinful people being buried. They used to come and go from the place of worship. And others praised them in the city where they worshiped. That doesn't have any meaning either.  
**8:11** Sometimes the sentence for a crime isn't carried out quickly. So people make plans to commit even more crimes.  
**8:12** An evil man may be guilty of a hundred crimes and still live a long time. But I know that things will go better with men who have great respect for God.  
**8:13** Sinful people don't respect God. So things won't go well with them. Like a shadow, they won't be around very long.  
**8:14** Here's something else on this earth that doesn't have any meaning. Sometimes godly men get what sinful people should receive. And sinful men get what godly people should receive. Here's what I'm telling you. That doesn't have any meaning either.  
**8:15** So I advise everyone to enjoy life. A man on this earth can't do anything better than eat and drink and be glad. Then he will enjoy his work. He'll be happy all the days of the life God has given him on earth.  
**8:16** I used my mind to understand what it really means to be wise. I wanted to observe the hard work man does on earth. He doesn't close his eyes and go to sleep day or night.  
**8:17** I saw everything God has done. No one can understand what happens on earth. Man might try very hard to figure it out. But he still can't discover what it all means. A wise man might claim he knows. But he can't really understand it either.  

## 9장

**9:1** I thought about all of those things. I realized that those who are wise and do what is right are under God's control. What they do is also under his control. But a man doesn't know whether God will show favor to him.  
**9:2** Everyone will die someday. Death comes to godly and sinful people alike. It comes to good and bad people alike. It comes to "clean" and "unclean" people alike. Those who offer sacrifices and those who don't offer them also die. A good person dies, and so does a sinner. Those who take oaths die. So do those who are afraid to take them.  
**9:3** Here's what is so bad about everything that happens on this earth. Death catches up with all of us. Also, the hearts of people are full of evil. They live in foolish pleasure. After that, they join those who have already died.  
**9:4** Anyone who is living still has hope. Even a live dog is better off than a dead lion!  
**9:5** People who are still alive know they'll die. But those who have died don't know anything. They don't receive any more rewards. And they are soon forgotten.  
**9:6** Their love, hate and jealousy disappear. They will never share again in anything that happens on earth.  
**9:7** Go and enjoy your food. Be joyful as you drink your wine. Now is the time God favors what you do.  
**9:8** Always wear white clothes to show you are happy. Anoint your head with olive oil.  
**9:9** You love your wife. So enjoy life with her. Do it all the days of this meaningless life God has given you on earth. That's what he made you for. That's what you get for all of your hard work on earth.  
**9:10** No matter what you do, work at it with all your might. Remember, you are going to your grave. And there isn't any work or planning or knowledge or wisdom there.  
**9:11** Here's something else I've seen on this earth. Races aren't always won by those who run fast. Battles aren't always won by those who are strong. Wise people don't always have plenty of food. Clever people aren't always wealthy. Those who have learned a lot aren't always favored. God controls the timing of every event. He also controls how things turn out.  
**9:12** A man doesn't know when trouble will come to him. Fish are caught in nets. Birds are taken in traps. And people are trapped by hard times that come when they don't expect them.  
**9:13** Here's something else I saw on this earth. I saw an example of wisdom that touched me deeply.  
**9:14** There was once a small city. Only a few people lived there. A powerful king attacked it. He brought in war machines all around it.  
**9:15** A certain man lived in that city. He was poor but wise. He used his wisdom to save the city. But no one remembered that poor man.  
**9:16** So I said, "It's better to be wise than to be powerful." But people looked down on the poor man's wisdom. No one paid any attention to what he said.  
**9:17** People should listen to the quiet words of those who are wise. That's better than paying attention to the shouts of a ruler of foolish people.  
**9:18** Wisdom is better than weapons of war. But one sinner destroys a lot of good.  

## 10장

**10:1** Dead flies give perfume a bad smell. And a little foolishness can make a lot of wisdom useless.  
**10:2** The hearts of wise people lead them on the right path. But the hearts of foolish people take them down the wrong path.  
**10:3** A foolish person doesn't have any sense at all. He shows everyone he is foolish. He does it even when he is walking along the road.  
**10:4** Suppose a ruler gets very angry with you. If he does, don't quit your job in the palace. Stay calm. That will overcome the effects of your big mistakes.  
**10:5** Here's something evil I've seen on this earth. And it's the kind of mistake that rulers make.  
**10:6** Foolish people are given many important jobs. Rich people are given unimportant ones.  
**10:7** I've seen slaves on horseback. I've also seen princes who were forced to walk as if they were slaves.  
**10:8** Anyone who digs a pit might fall into it. Anyone who breaks through a wall might be bitten by a snake.  
**10:9** Anyone who removes stones from rock pits might get hurt. Anyone who cuts logs might get wounded.  
**10:10** Suppose the blade of an ax is dull. And its edge hasn't been sharpened. Then more effort is needed to use it. But skill will bring success.  
**10:11** Suppose a snake bites before it is charmed. Then there isn't any benefit in being a snake charmer.  
**10:12** A man who is wise says gracious things. But a foolish person is destroyed by what his own lips speak.  
**10:13** At first what he says is foolish. In the end his words are very evil.  
**10:14** He talks too much. No one knows what lies ahead for him. Who can tell him what will happen after he is gone?  
**10:15** The work a foolish person does makes him tired. He doesn't even know the way to town.  
**10:16** How terrible it is for a land whose king used to be a servant! How terrible if its princes get drunk in the morning!  
**10:17** How blessed is the land whose king was born into the royal family! How blessed if its princes eat and drink at the proper time! How blessed if they eat and drink to become strong and not to get drunk!  
**10:18** When a man won't work, the roof falls down. When his hands aren't busy, the house leaks.  
**10:19** People laugh at a dinner party. And wine makes life happy. People think money can buy everything.  
**10:20** Don't call down curses on the king. Don't even think about doing it. Don't call down curses on rich people. Don't even do it in your bedroom. A bird might fly away and carry your words. It might report what you said.  

## 11장

**11:1** Put your money into trade across the ocean. After a while you will earn something from it.  
**11:2** Give shares of what you earn to a lot of people. After all, you don't know what great trouble might come on the land.  
**11:3** Clouds that are full of water pour rain down on the earth. A tree might fall to the south or the north. It will stay in the place where it falls.  
**11:4** Anyone who keeps on watching the wind won't plant seeds. Anyone who keeps looking at the clouds won't gather crops.  
**11:5** You don't know the path the wind takes. You don't know how a baby is made inside its mother. So you can't understand how God works either. He made everything.  
**11:6** In the morning plant your seeds. In the evening keep your hands busy. You don't know what will succeed. It may be one or the other. Or both might do equally well.  
**11:7** Light is sweet. People enjoy being out in the sun.  
**11:8** No matter how many years a man might live, let him enjoy all of them. But let him remember the dark days. There will be many of them. Nothing that's going to happen will have any meaning.  
**11:9** Young man, be happy while you are still young. Let your heart be joyful while you are still strong. Do what your heart tells you to do. Go after what your eyes look at. But I want you to know that God will judge you for everything you do.  
**11:10** So drive worry out of your heart. Get rid of all of your troubles. Being young and strong doesn't have any meaning.  

## 12장

**12:1** Remember the One who created you. Remember him while you are still young. Think about him before your times of trouble come. The years will come when you will say, "I don't find any pleasure in them."  
**12:2** That's when the sunlight will become dark. The moon and the stars will also grow dark. And the clouds will return after it rains.  
**12:3** Remember your Creator before those who guard the house tremble with old age. That's when strong men will be bent over. The women who grind grain will stop because there are so few of them left. Those who look through the windows won't be able to see very well.  
**12:4** Remember your Creator before the front doors are closed. That's when the sound of grinding will fade away. Old men will rise up when they hear birds singing. But they will barely hear any of their songs.  
**12:5** Remember your Creator before you become afraid of places that are too high. You will also be terrified because of danger in the streets. Remember your Creator before the almond trees have buds on them. That's when grasshoppers will drag themselves along. Old men will not want to make love anymore. Man will go to his dark home in the grave. And those who sob over the dead will walk around in the streets.  
**12:6** Remember your Creator before the silver cord is cut. That's when the golden bowl will be broken. The wheel will be broken at the well. The pitcher will be smashed at the spring.  
**12:7** Remember your Creator before you return to the dust you came from. That's when your spirit will go back to God who gave it.  
**12:8** "Meaningless! Everything is meaningless!" says the Teacher. "Nothing has any meaning."  
**12:9** The Teacher was wise. He gave knowledge to people. He put many proverbs to the test. He thought about them carefully. Then he wrote them down in order.  
**12:10** He did his best to find just the right words. And what he wrote was honest and true.  
**12:11** The sayings of those who are wise move people to take action. Their collected sayings really nail things down. They are given to us by one Shepherd.  
**12:12** My son, be careful not to pay attention to anything that is added to them. Books will never stop being written. Too much studying makes people tired.  
**12:13** Everything has now been heard. And here's the final thing I want to say. Have respect for God and obey his commandments. That's what everyone should do.  
**12:14** God will judge everything people do. That includes everything they try to hide. He'll judge everything, whether it's good or evil.
