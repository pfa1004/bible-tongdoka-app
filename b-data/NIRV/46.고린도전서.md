# 46.고린도전서 (NIRV)



## 1장

**1:1** I, Paul, am writing this letter. I have been chosen to be an apostle of Christ Jesus just as God planned. Our brother Sosthenes joins me in writing.  
**1:2** We are sending this letter to you, the members of God's church in Corinth. You have been made holy because you belong to Christ Jesus. God has chosen you to be his holy people. He has done the same for all those everywhere who pray to our Lord Jesus Christ. Jesus is their Lord and ours.  
**1:3** May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.  
**1:4** I always thank God for you. I thank him because of the grace he has given to you who belong to Christ Jesus.  
**1:5** You have been blessed in every way because of him. All your teaching of the truth is better. Your understanding of it is more complete.  
**1:6** Our witness about Christ has been proved to be true in you.  
**1:7** There is no gift of the Holy Spirit that you don't have. You are full of hope as you wait for our Lord Jesus Christ to come again.  
**1:8** God will keep you strong to the very end. Then you will be without blame on the day our Lord Jesus Christ returns.  
**1:9** God is faithful. He has chosen you to share life with his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.  
**1:10** Brothers and sisters, I ask all of you to agree with one another. I make my appeal in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. Then you won't take sides. You will be in complete agreement in all that you think.  
**1:11** My brothers and sisters, some people who live in Chloe's house have told me you are arguing with each other.  
**1:12** Here is what I mean. One of you says, "I follow Paul." Another says, "I follow Apollos." Another says, "I follow Peter." And still another says, "I follow Christ."  
**1:13** Does Christ take sides? Did Paul die on the cross for you? Were you baptized in the name of Paul?  
**1:14** I'm thankful that I didn't baptize any of you except Crispus and Gaius.  
**1:15** No one can say that you were baptized in my name.  
**1:16** It's true that I also baptized those who live in the house of Stephanas. Besides that, I don't remember if I baptized anyone else.  
**1:17** Christ did not send me to baptize. He sent me to preach the good news. He commanded me not to use the kind of wisdom that people commonly use. That would take all the power away from the cross of Christ.  
**1:18** The message of the cross seems foolish to those who are lost and dying. But it is God's power to us who are being saved.  
**1:19** It is written, "I will destroy the wisdom of those who are wise. I will do away with the cleverness of those who think they are so smart." --(Isaiah 29:14)  
**1:20** Where is the wise person? Where is the educated person? Where are the great thinkers of this world? Hasn't God made the wisdom of the world foolish?  
**1:21** God wisely planned that the world would not know him through its own wisdom. It pleased God to use the foolish things we preach to save those who believe.  
**1:22** Jews require miraculous signs. Greeks look for wisdom.  
**1:23** But we preach about Christ and his death on the cross. That is very hard for Jews to accept. And everyone else thinks it's foolish.  
**1:24** But there are those God has chosen, both Jews and others. To them Christ is God's power and God's wisdom.  
**1:25** The foolish things of God are wiser than human wisdom. The weakness of God is stronger than human strength.  
**1:26** Brothers and sisters, think of what you were when God chose you. Not many of you were considered wise by human standards. Not many of you were powerful. Not many of you belonged to important families.  
**1:27** But God chose the foolish things of the world to shame the wise. He chose the weak things of the world to shame the strong.  
**1:28** God chose the things of this world that are common and looked down on. He chose what is not considered to be important to do away with what is considered to be important.  
**1:29** So no one can brag to God.  
**1:30** Because of what God has done, you belong to Christ Jesus. He has become God's wisdom for us. He makes us right with God. He makes us holy and sets us free.  
**1:31** It is written, "The one who brags should brag about what the Lord has done."--(Jeremiah 9:24)  

## 2장

**2:1** Brothers and sisters, when I came to you I didn't come with fancy words or great wisdom. I preached to you the truth about God's love.  
**2:2** I made up my mind to pay attention to only one thing while I was with you. That one thing was Jesus Christ and his death on the cross.  
**2:3** When I came to you, I was weak and afraid and trembling all over.  
**2:4** I didn't preach my message with clever and compelling words. As I preached, the Holy Spirit showed his power.  
**2:5** That was so you would believe not because of human wisdom but because of God's power.  
**2:6** The words we speak to those who have grown in the faith are wise. Our words are different from the words of the wise people or rulers of this world. People like that aren't going anywhere.  
**2:7** No, we speak about God's secret wisdom. His wisdom has been hidden. But before time began, God planned that his wisdom would bring us heavenly glory.  
**2:8** None of the rulers of this world understood God's wisdom. If they had, they would not have nailed the Lord of glory to the cross.  
**2:9** It is written, "No eye has seen, no ear has heard, no mind has known what God has prepared for those who love him." --(Isaiah 64:4)  
**2:10** But God has shown it to us through his Spirit. The Spirit understands all things. He understands even the deep things of God.  
**2:11** Who can know the thoughts of another person? Only a person's own spirit can know them. In the same way, only the Spirit of God knows God's thoughts.  
**2:12** We have not received the spirit of the world. We have received the Spirit who is from God. The Spirit helps us understand what God has freely given us.  
**2:13** That is what we speak about. We don't use words taught to us by people. We use words taught to us by the Holy Spirit. We use the words of the Spirit to teach the truths of the Spirit.  
**2:14** Some people don't have the Holy Spirit. They don't accept the things that come from the Spirit of God. Things like that are foolish to them. They can't understand them. In fact, such things can't be understood without the Spirit's help.  
**2:15** Everyone who has the Spirit can judge all things. But no one can judge those who have the Spirit. It is written,  
**2:16** "Who can ever know what is in the Lord's mind? Can anyone ever teach him?" --(Isaiah 40:13) But we have the mind of Christ.  

## 3장

**3:1** Brothers and sisters, I couldn't speak to you as if you were guided by the Holy Spirit. I had to speak to you as if you were following the ways of the world. You aren't growing as Christ wants you to. You are still like babies.  
**3:2** The words I spoke to you were like milk, not like solid food. You weren't ready for solid food yet. And you still aren't ready for it.  
**3:3** You are still following the ways of the world. Some of you are jealous. Some of you argue. So aren't you following the ways of the world? Aren't you acting like ordinary human beings?  
**3:4** One of you says, "I follow Paul." Another says, "I follow Apollos." Aren't you acting like ordinary human beings?  
**3:5** After all, what is Apollos? And what is Paul? We are only people who serve. We helped you to believe. The Lord has given each of us our own work to do.  
**3:6** I planted the seed. Apollos watered it. But God made it grow.  
**3:7** So the one who plants is not important. The one who waters is not important. It is God who makes things grow. He is the One who is important.  
**3:8** The one who plants and the one who waters have the same purpose. The Lord will give each of us a reward for our work.  
**3:9** We work together with God. You are like God's field. You are like his building.  
**3:10** God has given me the grace to lay a foundation as a master builder. Now someone else is building on it. But each one should build carefully.  
**3:11** No one can lay any other foundation than the one that has already been laid. That foundation is Jesus Christ.  
**3:12** A person may build on it using gold, silver, jewels, wood, hay or straw.  
**3:13** But each person's work will be shown for what it is. On judgment day it will be brought to light. It will be put through fire. The fire will test how good everyone's work is.  
**3:14** If the building doesn't burn up, God will give the builder a reward for his work.  
**3:15** If the building burns up, the builder will lose everything. The builder will be saved, but only like one escaping through the flames.  
**3:16** Don't you know that you yourselves are God's temple? God's Spirit lives in you.  
**3:17** If anyone destroys God's temple, God will destroy him. God's temple is holy. And you are that temple.  
**3:18** Don't fool yourselves. Suppose some of you think you are wise by the standards of the world. Then you should become a "fool" so that you can become wise.  
**3:19** The wisdom of this world is foolish in God's eyes. It is written, "God catches wise people in their own tricks."--(Job 5:13)  
**3:20** It is also written, "The Lord knows that the thoughts of the wise don't amount to anything."--(Psalm 94:11)  
**3:21** So no more bragging about human beings! All things are yours.  
**3:22** That means Paul or Apollos or Peter or the world or life or death or the present or the future. All are yours.  
**3:23** You are joined to Christ and belong to him. And Christ is joined to God.  

## 4장

**4:1** Here is how you should think of us. We serve Christ. We are trusted with God's secret truth.  
**4:2** Those who have been given a trust must prove that they are faithful.  
**4:3** I care very little if I am judged by you or by any human court. I don't even judge myself.  
**4:4** I don't feel I have done anything wrong. But that doesn't mean I'm not guilty. The Lord judges me.  
**4:5** So don't judge anything before the appointed time. Wait until the Lord returns. He will bring to light what is hidden in the dark. He will show the real reasons why people do what they do. At that time each person will receive praise from God.  
**4:6** Brothers and sisters, I have used myself and Apollos as examples to help you. You can learn from us the meaning of the saying, "Don't go beyond what is written." Then you won't be proud that you follow one person instead of another.  
**4:7** Who makes you different from anyone else? What do you have that you did not receive? And if you did receive it, why do you brag as though you did not?  
**4:8** You already have everything you want, don't you? Have you already become rich? Have you begun to rule as kings? And did you do that without us? I wish that you really had begun to rule. Then we could rule with you!  
**4:9** It seems to me that God has put us apostles on display at the end of a parade. We are like men sentenced to die in front of a crowd. We have been made a show for the whole creation to see. Angels and people are staring at us.  
**4:10** We are fools for Christ. But you are so wise in Christ! We are weak. But you are so strong! You are honored. But we are looked down on!  
**4:11** Up to this very hour we are hungry and thirsty. We are dressed in rags. We are being treated badly. We have no homes.  
**4:12** We work hard with our own hands. When others call down a curse on us, we bless them. When we are attacked, we put up with it.  
**4:13** When others say bad things about us, we answer kindly. Up to this moment we have become the world's garbage. We are everybody's trash.  
**4:14** I am not writing this to shame you. You are my dear children, and I want to warn you.  
**4:15** You may have 10,000 believers in Christ watching over you. But you don't have many fathers. I became your father by serving Christ Jesus and telling you the good news.  
**4:16** So I'm asking you to follow my example.  
**4:17** That's the reason I'm sending Timothy to you. He is like a son to me, and I love him. He is faithful in serving the Lord. He will remind you of my way of life in serving Christ Jesus. And that agrees with what I teach everywhere in every church.  
**4:18** Some of you have become proud. You act as if I weren't coming to you.  
**4:19** But I will come very soon, if that's what the Lord wants. Then I will find out how those proud people are talking. I will also find out what power they have.  
**4:20** The kingdom of God is not a matter of talk. It is a matter of power.  
**4:21** Which do you want? Should I come to you with a whip? Or should I come in love and with a gentle spirit?  

## 5장

**5:1** It is actually reported that there is sexual sin among you. I'm told that a man is living with his father's wife and is having sex with her. Even people who do not know God don't commit that sin.  
**5:2** And you are proud! Shouldn't you be filled with sadness instead? Shouldn't you have put the man who did that out of your church?  
**5:3** Even though I am not right there with you, I am with you in spirit. And I have already judged the one who did that, just as if I were there.  
**5:4** When you come together in the name of our Lord Jesus, I will be with you in spirit. The power of our Lord Jesus will also be with you.  
**5:5** When you come together like that, hand that man over to Satan. Then his sinful nature will be destroyed. His spirit will be saved on the day the Lord returns.  
**5:6** Your bragging is not good. It is like yeast. Don't you know that just a little yeast works its way through the whole batch of dough?  
**5:7** Get rid of the old yeast. Be like a new batch of dough without yeast. That is what you really are, because Christ has been offered up for us. He is our Passover lamb.  
**5:8** So let us keep the Feast, but not with the old yeast. I'm talking about yeast that is full of hatred and evil. Let us keep the Feast with bread made without yeast. Let us do it with bread that is honest and true.  
**5:9** I wrote a letter to you to tell you to stay away from people who commit sexual sins.  
**5:10** I didn't mean the people of this world who sin that way or who always want more and more. I didn't mean those who cheat or who worship statues of gods. In that case you would have to leave this world!  
**5:11** But here is what I am writing to you. You must stay away from anyone who claims to be a believer but who does those things. Stay away from anyone who commits sexual sins or who always wants more and more things. Stay away from a person who worships statues of gods or who tells lies about others. Stay away from anyone who gets drunk or who cheats. Don't even eat with a person like that.  
**5:12** Is it my business to judge those outside the church? Aren't you supposed to judge those inside the church?  
**5:13** God will judge those outside. Scripture says, "Get rid of that evil person!"--(Deuteronomy 17:7; 19:19; 21:21; 22:21,24; 24:7)  

## 6장

**6:1** Suppose one of you wants to bring a charge against another believer. Should you take it to the ungodly to be judged? Why not take it to God's people?  
**6:2** Don't you know that God's people will judge the world? And if you are going to judge the world, aren't you able to judge small cases?  
**6:3** Don't you know that we will judge angels? Then we should be able to judge the things of this life even more!  
**6:4** So if you want to press charges in matters like that, appoint as judges members of the church who aren't very important!  
**6:5** I say this to shame you. Is it possible that no one among you is wise enough to judge matters between believers?  
**6:6** Instead, one believer goes to court against another. And this happens in front of unbelievers!  
**6:7** The very fact that you take another believer to court means you have lost the battle already. Why not be treated wrongly? Why not be cheated?  
**6:8** Instead, you yourselves cheat and do wrong. And you do it to your brothers and sisters.  
**6:9** Don't you know that evil people will not receive God's kingdom? Don't be fooled. Those who commit sexual sins will not receive the kingdom. Neither will those who worship statues of gods or commit adultery. Neither will men who are prostitutes or who commit homosexual acts.  
**6:10** Neither will thieves or those who always want more and more. Neither will those who are often drunk or tell lies or cheat. People who live like that will not receive God's kingdom.  
**6:11** Some of you used to do those things. But your sins were washed away. You were made holy. You were made right with God. All of that was done in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and by the Spirit of our God.  
**6:12** Some of you say, "Everything is permitted for me." But not everything is good for me. Again some of you say, "Everything is permitted for me." But I will not be controlled by anything.  
**6:13** Some of you say, "Food is for the stomach. And the stomach is for food." But God will destroy both of them. The body is not meant for sexual sins. The body is meant for the Lord. And the Lord is meant for the body.  
**6:14** By his power God raised the Lord from the dead. He will also raise us up.  
**6:15** Don't you know that your bodies belong to the body of Christ? Should I take what belongs to Christ and join it to a prostitute? Never!  
**6:16** Don't you know that when you join yourself to a prostitute, you become one with her in body? Scripture says, "The two will become one."--(Genesis 2:24)  
**6:17** But anyone who is joined to the Lord becomes one with him in spirit.  
**6:18** Keep far away from sexual sins. All the other sins a person commits are outside his body. But sexual sins are sins against one's own body.  
**6:19** Don't you know that your bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit? The Spirit is in you. You have received him from God. You do not belong to yourselves.  
**6:20** Christ has paid the price for you. So use your bodies in a way that honors God.  

## 7장

**7:1** Now I want to deal with the things you wrote me about. Some of you say, "It is good for a man not to have sex with a woman."  
**7:2** But since there is so much sexual sin, each man should have his own wife. And each woman should have her own husband.  
**7:3** A husband should satisfy his wife's sexual needs. And a wife should satisfy her husband's sexual needs.  
**7:4** The wife's body does not belong only to her. It also belongs to her husband. In the same way, the husband's body does not belong only to him. It also belongs to his wife.  
**7:5** You shouldn't stop giving yourselves to each other except when you both agree to do so. And that should be only to give yourselves time to pray for a while. Then you should come together again. In that way, Satan will not tempt you when you can't control yourselves.  
**7:6** I say those things to you as my advice, not as a command.  
**7:7** I wish all of you were like me. But you each have your own gift from God. One has this gift. Another has that.  
**7:8** I speak to those who are not married. I also speak to widows. It is good for you to stay single like me.  
**7:9** But if you can't control yourselves, you should get married. It is better to get married than to burn with sexual longing.  
**7:10** I give a command to those who are married. It is a direct command from the Lord, not from me. A wife must not leave her husband.  
**7:11** But if she does, she must not get married again. Or she can go back to her husband. And a husband must not divorce his wife.  
**7:12** I also have something to say to everyone else. It is from me, not a direct command from the Lord. Suppose a brother has a wife who is not a believer. If she is willing to live with him, he must not divorce her.  
**7:13** And suppose a woman has a husband who is not a believer. If he is willing to live with her, she must not divorce him.  
**7:14** The unbelieving husband has been made holy through his wife. The unbelieving wife has been made holy through her believing husband. If that were not the case, your children would not be pure and clean. But as it is, they are holy.  
**7:15** If the unbeliever leaves, let that person go. In that case, a believing man or woman does not have to stay married. God wants us to live in peace.  
**7:16** Wife, how do you know if you can save your husband? Husband, how do you know if you can save your wife?  
**7:17** But each of you should remain in the place in life that the Lord has given you. Stay as you were when God chose you. That's the rule all the churches must follow.  
**7:18** Was a man already circumcised when God chose him? Then he should not become uncircumcised. Was he uncircumcised when God chose him? Then he should not be circumcised.  
**7:19** Being circumcised means nothing. Being uncircumcised means nothing. Doing what God commands is what counts.  
**7:20** Each of you should stay as you were when God chose you.  
**7:21** Were you a slave when God chose you? Don't let it trouble you. But if you can get your master to set you free, do it.  
**7:22** Those who were slaves when the Lord chose them are now the Lord's free people. Those who were free when God chose them are now slaves of Christ.  
**7:23** Christ has paid the price for you. Don't become slaves of human beings.  
**7:24** Brothers and sisters, you are accountable to God. So all of you should stay as you were when God chose you.  
**7:25** Now I want to say something about virgins. I have no direct command from the Lord. But I give my opinion. Because of the Lord's mercy, I give it as one who can be trusted.  
**7:26** Times are hard for you right now. So I think it's good for you to stay as you are.  
**7:27** Are you married? Then don't get a divorce. Are you single? Then don't look for a wife.  
**7:28** But if you get married, you have not sinned. And if a virgin gets married, she has not sinned. But those who get married will have many troubles in this life. I want to save you from that.  
**7:29** Brothers and sisters, what I mean is that the time is short. From now on, those who have a husband or wife should live as if they did not.  
**7:30** Those who are sad should live as if they were not. Those who are happy should live as if they were not. Those who buy something should live as if it were not theirs to keep.  
**7:31** Those who use the things of the world should not become all wrapped up in them. The world as it now exists is passing away.  
**7:32** I don't want you to have anything to worry about. A single man is concerned about the Lord's matters. He wants to know how he can please the Lord.  
**7:33** But a married man is concerned about the matters of this world. He wants to know how he can please his wife.  
**7:34** His concerns pull him in two directions. A single woman or a virgin is concerned about the Lord's matters. She wants to serve the Lord with both body and spirit. But a married woman is concerned about the matters of this world. She wants to know how she can please her husband.  
**7:35** I'm saying those things for your own good. I'm not trying to hold you back. I want you to be free to live in a way that is right. I want you to give yourselves completely to the Lord.  
**7:36** Suppose a man thinks he is not acting properly toward the virgin he has promised to marry. Suppose she is getting old, and he feels that he should marry her. He should do as he wants. He is not sinning. They should get married.  
**7:37** But suppose the man has decided not to marry the virgin. And suppose he has no compelling need to get married and can control himself. If he has made up his mind not to get married, he also does the right thing.  
**7:38** So then, the man who marries the virgin does the right thing. But the man who doesn't marry her does an even better thing.  
**7:39** A woman has to stay married to her husband as long as he lives. If he dies, she is free to marry anyone she wants to. But the one she marries must belong to the Lord.  
**7:40** In my opinion, she is happier if she stays single. And I also think that I am led by the Spirit of God in saying that.  

## 8장

**8:1** Now I want to deal with food offered to statues of gods. We know that we all have knowledge. Knowledge makes people proud. But love builds them up.  
**8:2** Those who think they know something still don't know as they should.  
**8:3** But those who love God are known by God.  
**8:4** So then, here is what I say about eating food that is offered to statues of gods. We know that a god made by human hands is really nothing at all in the world. We know there is only one God.  
**8:5** There may be so-called gods either in heaven or on earth. In fact, there are many "gods" and many "lords."  
**8:6** But for us there is only one God. He is the Father. All things came from him, and we live for him. And there is only one Lord. He is Jesus Christ. All things came because of him, and we live because of him.  
**8:7** But not everyone knows that. Some people still think that statues of gods are real gods. When they eat food that was offered to statues of gods, they think of it as food that was offered to real gods. And because they have a weak sense of what is right and wrong, they feel guilty.  
**8:8** But food doesn't bring us close to God. We are no worse if we don't eat. We are no better if we do eat.  
**8:9** But be careful how you use your freedom. Be sure it doesn't trip up someone who is weaker than you.  
**8:10** Suppose you who have that knowledge are eating in a temple of one of those gods. And suppose someone who has a weak sense of what is right and wrong sees you. Won't that person become bold and eat what has been offered to statues of gods?  
**8:11** If so, then your knowledge destroys that weak brother or sister for whom Christ died.  
**8:12** When you sin against other believers in that way, you harm their weak sense of what is right and wrong. By doing that you sin against Christ.  
**8:13** So what should I do if what I eat causes my brother or sister to fall into sin? I will never eat meat again. In that way, I will not cause them to fall.  

## 9장

**9:1** Am I not free? Am I not an apostle? Haven't I seen Jesus our Lord? Aren't you the result of my work for the Lord?  
**9:2** Even though others may not think of me as an apostle, I am certainly one to you! You are the proof that I am the Lord's apostle.  
**9:3** That is what I say to stand up for myself when people judge me.  
**9:4** Don't we have the right to eat and drink?  
**9:5** Don't we have the right to take a believing wife with us when we travel? The other apostles do. The Lord's brothers do. Peter does.  
**9:6** Or are Barnabas and I the only ones who have to work for a living?  
**9:7** Who serves as a soldier but doesn't get paid? Who plants a vineyard but doesn't eat any of its grapes? Who takes care of a flock but doesn't drink any of the milk?  
**9:8** Do I say that from only a human point of view? The Law says the same thing.  
**9:9** Here is what is written in the Law of Moses. "Do not stop an ox from eating while it helps separate the grain from the straw."--(Deuteronomy 25:4) Is it oxen that God is concerned about?  
**9:10** Doesn't he say that for us? Yes, it was written for us. When a farmer plows and separates the grain, he does it because he hopes to share in the crop.  
**9:11** We have planted spiritual seed among you. Is it too much to ask that we receive from you some of the things we need?  
**9:12** Others have the right to receive help from you. Don't we have even more right to do so? But we didn't use that right. No, we have put up with everything. We didn't want to keep the good news of Christ from spreading.  
**9:13** Don't you know that those who work in the temple get their food from the temple? Don't you know that those who serve at the altar eat from what is offered on the altar?  
**9:14** In the same way, those who preach the good news should receive their living from their work. That is what the Lord has commanded.  
**9:15** But I haven't used any of those rights. And I'm not writing because I hope you will do things like that for me. I would rather die than have anyone take away my pride in my work.  
**9:16** But when I preach the good news, I can't brag. I have to preach it. How terrible it will be for me if I do not preach the good news!  
**9:17** If I preach because I want to, I get a reward. If I preach because I have to, I'm only doing my duty.  
**9:18** Then what reward do I get? Here is what it is. I am able to preach the good news free of charge. And I can do it without making use of my rights when I preach it.  
**9:19** I am free. I don't belong to anyone. But I make myself a slave to everyone. I do it to win as many as I can to Christ.  
**9:20** To the Jews I became like a Jew. That was to win the Jews. To those under the law I became like one who was under the law, even though I myself am not under the law. That was to win those under the law.  
**9:21** To those who don't have the law I became like one who doesn't have the law. I am not free from God's law. I am under Christ's law. Now I can win those who don't have the law.  
**9:22** To those who are weak I became weak. That was to win the weak. I have become all things to all people so that in all possible ways I might save some.  
**9:23** I do all of that because of the good news. And I want to share in its blessings.  
**9:24** In a race all the runners run. But only one gets the prize. You know that, don't you? So run in a way that will get you the prize.  
**9:25** All who take part in the games train hard. They do it to get a crown that will not last. But we do it to get a crown that will last forever.  
**9:26** So I do not run like someone who doesn't run toward the finish line. I do not fight like a boxer who hits nothing but air.  
**9:27** No, I train my body and bring it under control. Then after I have preached to others, I myself will not break the rules and fail to win the prize.  

## 10장

**10:1** Brothers and sisters, here is what I want you to know about our people who lived long ago. They were all led by the cloud. They all walked through the Red Sea.  
**10:2** They were all baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea.  
**10:3** They all ate the same supernatural food.  
**10:4** They all drank the same supernatural water. They drank from the supernatural rock that went with them. That rock was Christ.  
**10:5** But God was not pleased with most of them. Their bodies were scattered all over the desert.  
**10:6** Now those things happened as examples for us. They are supposed to keep us from longing for evil things, as the people of Israel did.  
**10:7** So don't worship statues of gods, as some of them did. It is written, "The people sat down to eat and drink. Then they got up to dance wildly in front of their god."--(Exodus 32:6)  
**10:8** We should not commit sexual sins, as some of them did. In one day 23,000 of them died.  
**10:9** We should not put the Lord to the test, as some of them did. They were killed by snakes.  
**10:10** Don't tell your leaders how unhappy you are with them. That's what some of the people of Israel did. And they were killed by the destroying angel.  
**10:11** Those things happened to them as examples for us. They were written down to warn us who are living at the time when God's work is being completed.  
**10:12** So be careful. When you think you are standing firm, you might fall.  
**10:13** You are tempted in the same way all other human beings are. God is faithful. He will not let you be tempted any more than you can take. But when you are tempted, God will give you a way out so that you can stand up under it.  
**10:14** My dear friends, run away from statues of gods. Don't worship them.  
**10:15** I'm talking to people who are reasonable. Judge for yourselves what I say.  
**10:16** When we give thanks for the cup at the Lord's Supper, aren't we sharing in the blood of Christ? When we break the bread, aren't we sharing in the body of Christ?  
**10:17** Just as there is one loaf, so we who are many are one body. We all eat from the one loaf.  
**10:18** Think about the people of Israel. Don't those who eat the offerings share in the altar?  
**10:19** Do I mean that what is offered to a statue of a god is anything? Do I mean that a statue of a god is anything?  
**10:20** No! But what is offered by those who worship statues of gods is really offered to demons. It is not offered to God. I don't want you to be sharing with demons.  
**10:21** You can't drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons too. You can't have a part in both the Lord's table and the table of demons.  
**10:22** Are we trying to make the Lord jealous? Are we stronger than he is?  
**10:23** You say, "Everything is permitted." But not everything is good for us. Again you say, "Everything is permitted." But not everything builds us up.  
**10:24** We should not look out for our own interests. Instead, we should look out for the interests of others.  
**10:25** Eat anything that is sold in the meat market. Don't ask if it's right or wrong.  
**10:26** Scripture says, "The earth belongs to the Lord. And so does everything in it."--(Psalm 24:1)  
**10:27** Suppose an unbeliever invites you to a meal and you want to go. Then eat anything that is put in front of you. Don't ask if it's right or wrong.  
**10:28** But suppose someone says to you, "This food has been offered to a statue of a god." Then don't eat it. Keep in mind the good of the one who told you. And don't eat because of a sense of what is right and wrong.  
**10:29** I'm talking about the other person's sense of what is right and wrong, not yours. Why should my freedom be judged by what someone else thinks?  
**10:30** Suppose I give thanks when I eat. Then why should I be blamed for eating food I thank God for?  
**10:31** So eat and drink and do everything else for the glory of God.  
**10:32** Don't do anything that causes another person to trip and fall. It doesn't matter if that person is a Jew or a Greek or a member of God's church.  
**10:33** Follow my example. I try to please everyone in every way. I'm not looking out for what is good for me. I'm looking out for the interests of others. I do it so that they might be saved.  

## 11장

**11:1** Follow my example, just as I follow the example of Christ.  
**11:2** I praise you for being faithful in remembering me. I also praise you for staying true to all my teachings, just as I gave them to you.  
**11:3** Now I want you to know that the head of every man is Christ. The head of the woman is the man. And the head of Christ is God.  
**11:4** Every man who prays or prophesies with his head covered brings shame on his head.  
**11:5** And every woman who prays or prophesies with her head uncovered brings shame on her head. It is just as if her head were shaved.  
**11:6** What if a woman does not cover her head? She should have her hair cut off. But it is shameful for her to cut her hair or shave it off. So she should cover her head.  
**11:7** A man should not cover his head. He is the likeness and glory of God. But the woman is the glory of the man.  
**11:8** The man did not come from the woman. The woman came from the man.  
**11:9** Also, the man was not created for the woman. The woman was created for the man.  
**11:10** That's why a woman should have her head covered. It shows that she is under authority. She should also cover her head because of the angels.  
**11:11** But here is how things are for those who belong to the Lord. The woman is not independent of the man. And the man is not independent of the woman.  
**11:12** The woman came from the man, and the man is born from the woman. But everything comes from God.  
**11:13** You be the judge. Is it proper for a woman to pray to God without covering her head?  
**11:14** Suppose a man has long hair. Doesn't the very nature of things teach you that it is shameful?  
**11:15** And suppose a woman has long hair. Doesn't the very nature of things teach you that it is her glory? Long hair is given to her as a covering.  
**11:16** If anyone wants to argue about that, we don't have any other practice. And God's churches don't either.  
**11:17** In the following matters, I don't praise you. Your meetings do more harm than good.  
**11:18** First, here is what people are telling me. When you come together as a church, you take sides. And in some ways I believe it.  
**11:19** No doubt you need to take sides in order to show which of you God agrees with!  
**11:20** When you come together, it is not the Lord's Supper you eat.  
**11:21** As you eat, each of you goes ahead without waiting for anyone else. One remains hungry and another gets drunk.  
**11:22** Don't you have homes to eat and drink in? Or do you think so little of God's church that you shame those in it who have nothing? What should I say to you? Should I praise you for that? Certainly not!  
**11:23** I passed on to you what I received from the Lord. On the night the Lord Jesus was handed over to his enemies, he took bread.  
**11:24** When he had given thanks, he broke it. He said, "This is my body. It is given for you. Every time you eat it, do it in memory of me."  
**11:25** In the same way, after supper he took the cup. He said, "This cup is the new covenant in my blood. Every time you drink it, do it in memory of me."  
**11:26** When you eat the bread and drink the cup, you are announcing the Lord's death until he comes again.  
**11:27** So do not eat the bread or drink the cup of the Lord in a way that isn't worthy of him. If you do, you will be guilty of sinning against the body and blood of the Lord.  
**11:28** A person should take a careful look at himself before he eats the bread and drinks from the cup.  
**11:29** Anyone who eats and drinks must recognize the body of the Lord. If he doesn't, God will judge him for it.  
**11:30** That is why many of you are weak and sick. That is why a number of you have died.  
**11:31** We should judge ourselves. Then we would not be found guilty.  
**11:32** When the Lord judges us, he corrects us. Then we will not be judged along with the rest of the world.  
**11:33** My brothers and sisters, when you come together to eat, wait for each other.  
**11:34** Those who are hungry should eat at home. Then when you come together, you will not be judged. When I come, I will give you more directions.  

## 12장

**12:1** Brothers and sisters, I want you to know about the gifts of the Holy Spirit.  
**12:2** You know that at one time you were unbelievers. You were somehow drawn away to worship statues of gods that couldn't even speak.  
**12:3** So I tell you that no one who is speaking with the help of God's Spirit says, "May Jesus be cursed." And without the help of the Holy Spirit no one can say, "Jesus is Lord."  
**12:4** There are different kinds of gifts. But they are all given by the same Spirit.  
**12:5** There are different ways to serve. But they all come from the same Lord.  
**12:6** There are different ways to work. But the same God makes it possible for all of us to have all those different things.  
**12:7** The Holy Spirit is given to each of us in a special way. That is for the good of all.  
**12:8** To some people the Spirit gives the message of wisdom. To others the same Spirit gives the message of knowledge.  
**12:9** To others the same Spirit gives faith. To others that one Spirit gives gifts of healing.  
**12:10** To others he gives the power to do miracles. To others he gives the ability to prophesy. To others he gives the ability to tell the spirits apart. To others he gives the ability to speak in different kinds of languages they had not known before. And to still others he gives the ability to explain what was said in those languages.  
**12:11** All of the gifts are produced by one and the same Spirit. He gives them to each person, just as he decides.  
**12:12** There is one body. But it has many parts. Even though it has many parts, they make up one body. It is the same with Christ.  
**12:13** We were all baptized by one Holy Spirit into one body. It didn't matter whether we were Jews or Greeks, slaves or free people. We were all given the same Spirit to drink.  
**12:14** The body is not made up of just one part. It has many parts.  
**12:15** Suppose the foot says, "I am not a hand. So I don't belong to the body." It is still part of the body.  
**12:16** And suppose the ear says, "I am not an eye. So I don't belong to the body." It is still part of the body.  
**12:17** If the whole body were an eye, how could it hear? If the whole body were an ear, how could it smell?  
**12:18** God has placed each part in the body just as he wanted it to be.  
**12:19** If all the parts were the same, how could there be a body?  
**12:20** As it is, there are many parts. But there is only one body.  
**12:21** The eye can't say to the hand, "I don't need you!" The head can't say to the feet, "I don't need you!"  
**12:22** In fact, it is just the opposite. The parts of the body that seem to be weaker are the ones we can't do without.  
**12:23** The parts that we think are less important we treat with special honor. The private parts aren't shown. But they are treated with special care.  
**12:24** The parts that can be shown don't need special care. But God has joined together all the parts of the body. And he has given more honor to the parts that didn't have any.  
**12:25** In that way, the parts of the body will not take sides. All of them will take care of each other.  
**12:26** If one part suffers, every part suffers with it. If one part is honored, every part shares in its joy.  
**12:27** You are the body of Christ. Each one of you is a part of it.  
**12:28** First, God has appointed apostles in the church. Second, he has appointed prophets. Third, he has appointed teachers. Then he has appointed people who do miracles and those who have gifts of healing. He also appointed those able to help others, those able to direct things, and those who can speak in different kinds of languages they had not known before.  
**12:29** Is everyone an apostle? Is everyone a prophet? Is everyone a teacher? Do all work miracles?  
**12:30** Do all have gifts of healing? Do all speak in languages they had not known before? Do all explain what is said in those languages?  
**12:31** But above all, you should want the more important gifts. And now I will show you the best way of all.  

## 13장

**13:1** Suppose I speak in the languages of human beings and of angels. If I don't have love, I am only a loud gong or a noisy cymbal.  
**13:2** Suppose I have the gift of prophecy. Suppose I can understand all the secret things of God and know everything about him. And suppose I have enough faith to move mountains. If I don't have love, I am nothing at all.  
**13:3** Suppose I give everything I have to poor people. And suppose I give my body to be burned. If I don't have love, I get nothing at all.  
**13:4** Love is patient. Love is kind. It does not want what belongs to others. It does not brag. It is not proud.  
**13:5** It is not rude. It does not look out for its own interests. It does not easily become angry. It does not keep track of other people's wrongs.  
**13:6** Love is not happy with evil. But it is full of joy when the truth is spoken.  
**13:7** It always protects. It always trusts. It always hopes. It never gives up.  
**13:8** Love never fails. But prophecy will pass away. Speaking in languages that had not been known before will end. And knowledge will pass away.  
**13:9** What we know now is not complete. What we prophesy now is not perfect.  
**13:10** But when what is perfect comes, the things that are not perfect will pass away.  
**13:11** When I was a child, I talked like a child. I thought like a child. I had the understanding of a child. When I became a man, I put childish ways behind me.  
**13:12** Now we see only a dim likeness of things. It is as if we were seeing them in a mirror. But someday we will see clearly. We will see face to face. What I know now is not complete. But someday I will know completely, just as God knows me completely.  
**13:13** The three most important things to have are faith, hope and love. But the greatest of them is love.  

## 14장

**14:1** Follow the way of love. You should also want the gifts the Holy Spirit gives. Most of all, you should want the gift of prophecy.  
**14:2** Anyone who speaks in a language he had not known before doesn't speak to people. He speaks only to God. In fact, no one understands that person. What he says with his spirit remains a mystery.  
**14:3** But anyone who prophesies speaks to people. He says things to make them stronger, to give them hope and to comfort them.  
**14:4** Those who speak in other languages build themselves up. But those who prophesy build up the church.  
**14:5** I would like all of you to speak in other languages. But I would rather have you prophesy. Those who prophesy are more helpful than those who speak in other languages. But that is not the case if those who speak in other languages explain what they have said. Then the whole church can be built up.  
**14:6** Brothers and sisters, suppose I were to come to you and speak in other languages. What good would I be to you? None! I would need to come with new truth or knowledge, or a prophecy or a teaching.  
**14:7** Here are some examples. Certain objects make sounds. Take a flute or a harp. No one will know what the tune is unless different notes are played.  
**14:8** Also, if the trumpet call isn't clear, who will get ready for battle?  
**14:9** It's the same with you. You must speak words that people understand. If you don't, no one will know what you are saying. You will just be speaking into the air.  
**14:10** It is true that there are all kinds of languages in the world. And they all have meaning.  
**14:11** But if I don't understand what someone is saying, I am a stranger to that person. And that person is a stranger to me.  
**14:12** It's the same with you. You want to have gifts of the Spirit. So try to do your best in using gifts that build up the church.  
**14:13** For that reason, those who speak in languages they had not known before should pray that they can explain what they say.  
**14:14** If I pray in another language, my spirit prays. But my mind does not pray.  
**14:15** So what should I do? I will pray with my spirit. But I will also pray with my mind. I will sing with my spirit. But I will also sing with my mind.  
**14:16** Suppose you are praising God with your spirit. And suppose there are visitors among you who don't understand what's going on. How can they say "Amen" when you give thanks? They don't know what you are saying.  
**14:17** You might be giving thanks well enough. But the others are not being built up.  
**14:18** I thank God that I speak in other languages more than all of you do.  
**14:19** But in the church I would rather speak five words that people can understand than 10,000 words in another language. Then I would be teaching others.  
**14:20** Brothers and sisters, stop thinking like children. Be like babies as far as evil is concerned. But be grown up in your thinking.  
**14:21** In the Law it is written, "Through people who speak unfamiliar languages and through the lips of strangers I will speak to these people. But even then they will not listen to me." --(Isaiah 28:11,12) That is what the Lord says.  
**14:22** So speaking in other languages is a sign for those who don't believe. It is not a sign for those who do believe. But prophecy is for believers. It is not for those who don't believe.  
**14:23** Suppose the whole church comes together and everyone speaks in other languages. And suppose visitors or unbelievers come in. Won't they say you are out of your minds?  
**14:24** But suppose unbelievers or visitors come in while everyone is prophesying. Then they will be shown by all who speak that they are sinners. They will be judged by all.  
**14:25** The secrets of their hearts will be brought out into the open. They will fall down and worship God. They will exclaim, "God is really here among you!"  
**14:26** Brothers and sisters, what should we say then? When you come together, every one of you brings something. You bring a hymn or a teaching or a word from God. You bring a message in another language or explain what was said. All of those things must be done to make the church strong.  
**14:27** No more than two or three people should speak in another language. And they should speak one at a time. Then someone must explain what was said.  
**14:28** If there is no one to explain, the speakers should keep quiet in the church. They can speak to themselves and to God.  
**14:29** Only two or three prophets are supposed to speak. Others should decide if what is being said is true.  
**14:30** What if a message from God comes to someone else who is sitting there? Then the one who is speaking should stop.  
**14:31** Those who prophesy can all take turns. In that way, everyone can be taught and be given hope.  
**14:32** Those who prophesy should control their speaking.  
**14:33** God is not a God of disorder. He is a God of peace. As in all the churches of God's people,  
**14:34** women should remain silent in the meetings. They are not allowed to speak. They must follow the lead of those who are in authority, as the Law says.  
**14:35** If they have a question about something, they should ask their own husbands at home. It is shameful for women to speak in church meetings.  
**14:36** Did the word of God begin with you? Or are you the only people it has reached?  
**14:37** Suppose some think they are prophets or have gifts of the Holy Spirit. They should agree that what I am writing to you is the Lord's command.  
**14:38** Anyone who does not recognize that will not be recognized.  
**14:39** Brothers and sisters, you should want to prophesy. And don't stop people from speaking in languages they had not known before.  
**14:40** But everything should be done in a proper and orderly way.  

## 15장

**15:1** Brothers and sisters, I want to remind you of the good news I preached to you. You received it and have put your faith in it.  
**15:2** Because you believed the good news, you are saved. But you must hold firmly to the message I preached to you. If you don't, you have believed it for nothing.  
**15:3** What I received I passed on to you. And it is the most important of all. Here is what it is. Christ died for our sins, just as Scripture said he would.  
**15:4** He was buried. He was raised from the dead on the third day, just as Scripture said he would be.  
**15:5** He appeared to Peter. Then he appeared to the Twelve.  
**15:6** After that, he appeared to more than 500 believers at the same time. Most of them are still living. But some have died.  
**15:7** He appeared to James. Then he appeared to all the apostles.  
**15:8** Last of all, he also appeared to me. I was like someone who wasn't born at the right time or in a normal way.  
**15:9** I am the least important of the apostles. I'm not even fit to be called an apostle. I tried to destroy God's church.  
**15:10** But because of God's grace I am what I am. And his grace was not wasted on me. No, I have worked harder than all the other apostles. But I didn't do the work. God's grace was with me.  
**15:11** So whether it was I or the other apostles who preached to you, that is what we preach. And that is what you believed.  
**15:12** We have preached that Christ has been raised from the dead. So how can some of you say that no one rises from the dead?  
**15:13** If no one rises from the dead, then not even Christ has been raised.  
**15:14** And if Christ has not been raised, what we preach doesn't mean anything. Your faith doesn't mean anything either.  
**15:15** More than that, we would be lying about God. We have given witness that God raised Christ from the dead. But he did not raise him if the dead are not raised.  
**15:16** If the dead are not raised, then Christ has not been raised either.  
**15:17** And if Christ has not been raised, your faith doesn't mean anything. Your sins have not been forgiven.  
**15:18** Those who have died believing in Christ are also lost.  
**15:19** Do we have hope in Christ only for this life? Then people should pity us more than anyone else.  
**15:20** But Christ really has been raised from the dead. He is the first of all those who will rise.  
**15:21** Death came because of what a man did. Rising from the dead also comes because of what a man did.  
**15:22** Because of Adam, all people die. So because of Christ, all will be made alive.  
**15:23** But here is the order of events. Christ is the first of those who rise from the dead. When he comes back, those who belong to him will be raised.  
**15:24** Then the end will come. Christ will destroy all rule, authority and power. He will hand over the kingdom to God the Father.  
**15:25** Christ must rule until he has put all his enemies under his control.  
**15:26** The last enemy that will be destroyed is death.  
**15:27** Scripture says that God "has put everything under his control."--(Psalm 8:6) It says that "everything" has been put under him. But it is clear that this does not include God himself, who puts everything under Christ.  
**15:28** When he has done that, the Son also will be under God's rule. God puts everything under the Son. In that way, God will be all in all.  
**15:29** Suppose no one rises from the dead. Then what will people do who are baptized for the dead? Suppose the dead are not raised at all. Then why are people baptized for them?  
**15:30** And why would we put ourselves in danger every hour?  
**15:31** I die every day. I really mean that, brothers and sisters. Here is something you can be sure of. I take pride in what Christ Jesus our Lord has done for you through my work.  
**15:32** Did I fight wild animals in Ephesus for only human reasons? Then what have I gotten for it? If the dead are not raised, "Let us eat and drink, because tomorrow we will die." --(Isaiah 22:13)  
**15:33** Don't let anyone fool you. "Bad companions make a good person bad."  
**15:34** You should come back to your senses and stop sinning. Some of you don't know anything about God. I say this to make you ashamed.  
**15:35** But someone might ask, "How are the dead raised? What kind of body will they have?"  
**15:36** How foolish! What you plant doesn't come to life unless it dies.  
**15:37** When you plant something, it isn't a completely grown plant that you put in the ground. You only plant a seed. Maybe it's wheat or something else.  
**15:38** But God gives the seed a body just as he has planned. And to each kind of seed he gives its own body.  
**15:39** All earthly creatures are not the same. People have one kind of body. Animals have another. Birds have another kind. Fish have still another.  
**15:40** There are also heavenly bodies as well as earthly bodies. Heavenly bodies have one kind of glory. Earthly bodies have another.  
**15:41** The sun has one kind of glory. The moon has another kind. The stars have still another. And one star's glory is different from that of another star.  
**15:42** It will be like that with bodies that are raised from the dead. The body that is planted does not last forever. The body that is raised from the dead lasts forever.  
**15:43** It is planted without honor. But it is raised in glory. It is planted in weakness. But it is raised in power.  
**15:44** It is planted as an earthly body. But it is raised as a spiritual body. Just as there is an earthly body, there is also a spiritual body.  
**15:45** It is written, "The first man Adam became a living person."--(Genesis 2:7) The last Adam became a spirit that gives life.  
**15:46** What is spiritual did not come first. What is earthly came first. What is spiritual came after that.  
**15:47** The first man came from the dust of the earth. The second man came from heaven.  
**15:48** Those who belong to the earth are like the one who came from the earth. And those who are spiritual are like the one who came from heaven.  
**15:49** We are like the earthly man. And we will be like the man from heaven.  
**15:50** Brothers and sisters, here is what I'm telling you. Bodies made of flesh and blood can't share in the kingdom of God. And what dies can't share in what never dies.  
**15:51** Listen! I am telling you a mystery. We will not all die. But we will all be changed.  
**15:52** That will happen in a flash, as quickly as you can wink an eye. It will happen when the last trumpet sounds. The trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised to live forever. And we will be changed.  
**15:53** Our natural bodies don't last forever. They must be dressed with what does last forever. What dies must be dressed with what does not die.  
**15:54** In fact, that is going to happen. What does not last will be dressed with what lasts forever. What dies will be dressed with what does not die. Then what is written will come true. It says, "Death has been swallowed up. It has lost the battle."--(Isaiah 25:8)  
**15:55** "Death, where is the battle you thought you were winning? Death, where is your sting?" --(Hosea 13:14)  
**15:56** The sting of death is sin. And the power of sin is the law.  
**15:57** But let us give thanks to God! He wins the battle for us because of what our Lord Jesus Christ has done.  
**15:58** My dear brothers and sisters, stand firm. Don't let anything move you. Always give yourselves completely to the work of the Lord. Because you belong to the Lord, you know that your work is not worthless.  

## 16장

**16:1** Now I want to deal with the offering of money for God's people. Do what I told the churches in Galatia to do.  
**16:2** On the first day of every week, each of you should put some money away. The amount should be in keeping with how much money you make. Save the money so that you won't have to take up an offering when I come.  
**16:3** When I arrive, I will send some people with your gift to Jerusalem. They will be people you consider to be good. And I will give them letters that explain who they are.  
**16:4** If it seems good for me to go also, they will go with me.  
**16:5** After I go through Macedonia, I will come to you. I will only be passing through Macedonia.  
**16:6** But I might stay with you for a while. I might even spend the winter. Then you can help me on my journey everywhere I go.  
**16:7** I don't want to see you now while I am just passing through. I hope to spend some time with you, if the Lord allows it.  
**16:8** But I will stay at Ephesus until the day of Pentecost.  
**16:9** A door has opened wide for me to do some good work here. There are many people who oppose me.  
**16:10** Timothy might come to you. Make sure he has nothing to worry about while he is with you. He is doing the work of the Lord, just as I am.  
**16:11** No one should refuse to accept him. Send him safely on his way so he can return to me. I'm expecting him to come back along with the others.  
**16:12** I want to say something about our brother Apollos. I tried my best to get him to go to you with the others. But he didn't want to go right now. He will go when he can.  
**16:13** Be on your guard. Stand firm in the faith. Be brave. Be strong.  
**16:14** Be loving in everything you do.  
**16:15** You know that the first believers in Achaia were from the family of Stephanas. They have spent all their time serving God's people. Brothers and sisters, I am asking you  
**16:16** to follow the lead of people like them. Follow everyone who joins in the task and works hard at it.  
**16:17** I was glad when Stephanas, Fortunatus and Achaicus arrived. They have supplied me with what you couldn't give me.  
**16:18** They renewed my spirit, and yours also. People like that are worthy of honor.  
**16:19** The churches in Asia Minor send you greetings. Aquila and Priscilla greet you warmly because of the Lord's love. So does the church that meets in their house.  
**16:20** All the brothers and sisters here send you greetings. Greet one another with a holy kiss.  
**16:21** I, Paul, am writing this greeting with my own hand.  
**16:22** If anyone does not love the Lord, let a curse be on that person! Come, Lord!  
**16:23** May the grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.  
**16:24** I give my love to all of you who belong to Christ Jesus. Amen.
