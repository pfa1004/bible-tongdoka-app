# 20.잠언 (NIRV)



## 1장

**1:1** These are the proverbs of Solomon. He was the son of David and the king of Israel.  
**1:2** Proverbs teach you wisdom and train you. They help you understand wise sayings.  
**1:3** They provide you with training and help you live wisely. They lead to what is right and honest and fair.  
**1:4** They give understanding to childish people. They give knowledge and good sense to those who are young.  
**1:5** Let wise people listen and add to what they have learned. Let those who understand what is right get guidance.  
**1:6** What I'm teaching also helps you understand proverbs and stories. It helps you understand the sayings and riddles of those who are wise.  
**1:7** If you really want to gain knowledge, you must begin by having respect for the Lord. But foolish people hate wisdom and training.  
**1:8** My son, listen to your father's advice. Don't turn away from your mother's teaching.  
**1:9** What they teach you will be like a beautiful crown on your head. It will be like a chain to decorate your neck.  
**1:10** My son, if sinners tempt you, don't give in to them.  
**1:11** They might say, "Come along with us. Let's hide and wait to spill someone's blood. Let's catch some harmless people in our trap.  
**1:12** Let's swallow them alive, as the grave does. Let's swallow them whole, like those who go down into the pit.  
**1:13** We'll get all kinds of valuable things. We'll fill our houses with what we steal.  
**1:14** Come and join our gang. We'll share everything we have."  
**1:15** My son, don't go along with them. Don't even set your feet on their paths.  
**1:16** They are always in a hurry to sin. They are quick to spill someone's blood.  
**1:17** How useless it is to spread a net while all the birds are watching!  
**1:18** Those who hide and wait will spill their own blood. They will be caught in their own trap.  
**1:19** That's what happens to everyone who goes after money in the wrong way. That kind of money takes away the lives of those who get it.  
**1:20** Wisdom calls out in the street. She raises her voice in public places.  
**1:21** At the noisy street corners she cries out. Here is what she says near the gates of the city.  
**1:22** "How long will you childish people love your childish ways? How long will you rude people enjoy making fun of God and others? How long will you foolish people hate knowledge?  
**1:23** Suppose you had paid attention to my warning. Then I would have poured out my heart to you. I would have told you what I was thinking.  
**1:24** But you turned away from me when I called out to you. None of you paid attention when I reached out my hand.  
**1:25** You turned away from all my advice. You wouldn't accept my warning.  
**1:26** So I will laugh at you when you are in danger. I will make fun of you when hard times come.  
**1:27** I will laugh when hard times hit you like a storm. I will laugh when danger comes your way like a windstorm. I will make fun of you when suffering and trouble come.  
**1:28** "Then you will call to me. But I won't answer. You will look for me. But you won't find me.  
**1:29** You hated knowledge. You didn't choose to have respect for the Lord.  
**1:30** You wouldn't accept my advice. You turned your backs on my warnings.  
**1:31** So you will eat the fruit of the way you have lived. You will choke on the fruit of what you have planned.  
**1:32** "Childish people go down the wrong path. They will die. Foolish people are satisfied with the way they live. They will be destroyed.  
**1:33** But those who listen to me will live in safety. They will not worry. They won't be afraid of getting hurt."  

## 2장

**2:1** My son, accept my words. Store up my commands inside you.  
**2:2** Let your ears listen to wisdom. Apply your heart to understanding.  
**2:3** Call out for the ability to be wise. Cry out for understanding.  
**2:4** Look for it as you would look for silver. Search for it as you would search for hidden treasure.  
**2:5** Then you will understand how to have respect for the Lord. You will find out how to know God.  
**2:6** The Lord gives wisdom. Knowledge and understanding come from his mouth.  
**2:7** He stores up success for honest people. He is like a shield to those who live without blame. He keeps them safe.  
**2:8** He guards the path of those who are honest. He watches over the way of his faithful ones.  
**2:9** You will understand what is right and honest and fair. You will understand the right way to live.  
**2:10** Your heart will become wise. Your mind will delight in knowledge.  
**2:11** Good sense will keep you safe. Understanding will guard you.  
**2:12** Wisdom will save you from the ways of evil men. It will save you from men who twist their words.  
**2:13** Men like that leave the straight paths to walk in dark ways.  
**2:14** They take delight in doing what is wrong. They take joy in twisting everything around.  
**2:15** Their paths are crooked. Their ways are not straight.  
**2:16** Wisdom will save you from a woman who commits adultery. It will save you from a sinful wife and her tempting words.  
**2:17** She leaves the man she married when she was young. She breaks the promise she made to her God.  
**2:18** Her house leads down to death. Her paths lead to the spirits of the dead.  
**2:19** No one who goes to her comes back or reaches the paths of life.  
**2:20** You will walk in the ways of good people. You will follow the paths of those who do right.  
**2:21** Honest people will live in the land. Those who are without blame will remain in it.  
**2:22** But sinners will be cut off from the land. Those who aren't faithful will be torn away from it.  

## 3장

**3:1** My son, do not forget my teaching. Keep my commands in your heart.  
**3:2** They will help you live for many years. They will bring you success.  
**3:3** Don't let love and truth ever leave you. Tie them around your neck. Write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**3:4** Then you will find favor and a good name in the eyes of God and people.  
**3:5** Trust in the Lord with all your heart. Do not depend on your own understanding.  
**3:6** In all your ways remember him. Then he will make your paths smooth and straight.  
**3:7** Don't be wise in your own eyes. Have respect for the Lord and avoid evil.  
**3:8** That will bring health to your body. It will make your bones strong.  
**3:9** Honor the Lord with your wealth. Give him the first share of all your crops.  
**3:10** Then your storerooms will be so full they can't hold everything. Your huge jars will spill over with fresh wine.  
**3:11** My son, do not hate the Lord's training. Do not object when he corrects you.  
**3:12** The Lord trains those he loves. He is like a father who trains the son he is pleased with.  
**3:13** Blessed is the one who finds wisdom. Blessed is the one who gains understanding.  
**3:14** Wisdom pays better than silver does. She earns more than gold does.  
**3:15** She is worth more than rubies. Nothing you want can compare with her.  
**3:16** Long life is in her right hand. In her left hand are riches and honor.  
**3:17** Her ways are pleasant ways. All her paths lead to peace.  
**3:18** She is a tree of life to those who hold her close. Those who hold on to her will be blessed.  
**3:19** By wisdom the Lord laid the earth's foundations. Through understanding he set the heavens in place.  
**3:20** By his knowledge the seas were separated, and the clouds dropped their dew.  
**3:21** My son, hold on to good sense and the understanding of what is right. Don't let them out of your sight.  
**3:22** They will be life for you. They will be like a gracious necklace around your neck.  
**3:23** Then you will go on your way in safety. You will not trip and fall.  
**3:24** When you lie down, you won't be afraid. When you lie down, you will sleep soundly.  
**3:25** Don't be terrified by sudden trouble. Don't be afraid when sinners are destroyed.  
**3:26** The Lord is the one you will trust in. He will keep your feet from being caught in a trap.  
**3:27** Don't hold back good from those who are worthy of it. Don't hold it back when you can help.  
**3:28** Suppose you have something to give. Don't say to your neighbor, "Come back later. I'll give it to you tomorrow."  
**3:29** Don't plan to harm your neighbor. He lives near you and trusts you.  
**3:30** Don't bring charges against a man without any reason. He has not harmed you.  
**3:31** Don't be jealous of a man who hurts others. Don't choose any of his ways.  
**3:32** The Lord really hates sinful people. But he makes honest people his closest friends.  
**3:33** The Lord puts a curse on the houses of sinners. But he blesses the homes of those who do what is right.  
**3:34** He laughs at proud people who make fun of others. But he gives grace to those who are not proud.  
**3:35** Wise people receive honor. But the Lord puts foolish people to shame.  

## 4장

**4:1** My children, listen to a father's teaching. Pay attention and gain understanding.  
**4:2** I give you good advice. So don't turn away from what I teach you.  
**4:3** I was once a young boy in my father's house. I was my mother's only child.  
**4:4** My father taught me. He said, "Hold on to my words with all your heart. Keep my commands. Then you will live.  
**4:5** Get wisdom. Get understanding. Don't forget my words. Don't turn away from them.  
**4:6** Stay close to wisdom, and she will keep you safe. Love her, and she will watch over you.  
**4:7** Wisdom is best. So get wisdom. No matter what it costs, get understanding.  
**4:8** Value wisdom, and she will lift you up. Hold her close, and she will honor you.  
**4:9** She will set a beautiful crown on your head. She will give you a glorious crown."  
**4:10** My son, listen. Accept what I say. Then you will live for many years.  
**4:11** I guide you in the way of wisdom. I lead you along straight paths.  
**4:12** When you walk, nothing will slow you down. When you run, you won't trip and fall.  
**4:13** Hold on to my teaching. Don't let it go. Guard it well. It is your life.  
**4:14** Don't take the path of evil people. Don't live the way sinners do.  
**4:15** Stay away from their path. Don't travel on it. Turn away from it. Go on your way.  
**4:16** Sinners can't sleep until they do what is evil. They can't rest until they make someone fall.  
**4:17** They do evil just as easily as they eat food. They hurt others as easily as they drink wine.  
**4:18** The path of those who do what is right is like the first gleam of dawn. It shines brighter and brighter until the full light of day.  
**4:19** But the way of those who do what is wrong is like deep darkness. They don't know what makes them trip and fall.  
**4:20** My son, pay attention to what I say. Listen closely to my words.  
**4:21** Don't let them out of your sight. Keep them in your heart.  
**4:22** They are life to those who find them. They are health to your whole body.  
**4:23** Above everything else, guard your heart. It is where your life comes from.  
**4:24** Don't speak with twisted words. Keep evil talk away from your lips.  
**4:25** Let your eyes look straight ahead. Keep looking right in front of you.  
**4:26** Make level paths for your feet to walk on. Only go on ways that are firm.  
**4:27** Don't turn to the right or left. Keep your feet from the path of evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** My son, pay attention to my wisdom. Listen carefully to my wise sayings.  
**5:2** Then you will continue to have good sense. Your lips will keep on speaking words of knowledge.  
**5:3** A woman who commits adultery has lips that drip honey. What she says is smoother than oil.  
**5:4** But in the end she is like bitter poison. She cuts like a sword that has two edges.  
**5:5** Her feet go down to death. Her steps lead straight to the grave.  
**5:6** She doesn't give any thought to her way of life. Her paths are crooked, but she doesn't realize it.  
**5:7** My sons, listen to me. Don't turn away from what I say.  
**5:8** Stay on a path far away from that evil woman. Don't even go near the door of her house.  
**5:9** If you do, you will give your best strength to others. You will give the best years of your life to someone who is mean.  
**5:10** Strangers will use up all of your wealth. Your hard work will make someone else rich.  
**5:11** At the end of your life you will groan. Your skin and your body will be worn out.  
**5:12** You will say, "How I hated to take advice! How my heart refused to be corrected!  
**5:13** I would not obey my teachers. I wouldn't listen to those who taught me.  
**5:14** I was almost totally destroyed. It happened right in front of the whole community."  
**5:15** Drink water from your own well. Drink running water from your own spring.  
**5:16** Should your springs pour out into the streets? Should your streams of water pour out in public places?  
**5:17** No! Let them belong to you alone. Never share them with strangers.  
**5:18** May your fountain be blessed. May the wife you married when you were young make you happy.  
**5:19** She is like a loving doe, a graceful deer. May her breasts always satisfy you. May you always be captured by her love.  
**5:20** My son, why be captured by a woman who commits adultery? Why hug the wife of another man?  
**5:21** The Lord watches a man's ways. He studies all of his paths.  
**5:22** A sinner is trapped by his own evil acts. He is held tight by the ropes of his sins.  
**5:23** He will die because he refused to be corrected. His sins will capture him because he was very foolish.  

## 6장

**6:1** My son, don't put up money for what your neighbor owes. Don't agree to pay up for someone else.  
**6:2** Don't be trapped by what you have said. Don't be caught by the words of your mouth.  
**6:3** Instead, my son, do something to free yourself. Don't fall into your neighbor's hands. Don't be proud. Hurry and make your appeal to your neighbor.  
**6:4** Don't let your eyes go to sleep. Don't let your eyelids close.  
**6:5** As a deer frees itself from a hunter, free yourself. As a bird frees itself from a trapper, free yourself.  
**6:6** You people who don't want to work, think about the ant! Consider its ways and be wise!  
**6:7** It has no commander. It has no leader or ruler.  
**6:8** But it stores up its food in summer. It gathers its food at harvest time.  
**6:9** You lazy people, how long will you lie there? When will you get up from your sleep?  
**6:10** You might sleep a little or take a little nap. You might even fold your hands and rest.  
**6:11** Then you would be poor, as if someone had robbed you. You would have little, as if someone had stolen from you.  
**6:12** A worthless and evil man goes around saying twisted things with his mouth.  
**6:13** He winks with his eyes. He makes signals with his feet. He motions with his fingers.  
**6:14** His plans are evil. He has lies in his heart. He is always stirring up fights.  
**6:15** Trouble will catch up with him in an instant. He will suddenly be destroyed. Nothing can save him.  
**6:16** There are six things the Lord hates. In fact, he hates seven things.  
**6:17** The Lord hates proud eyes, a lying tongue, and hands that kill those who aren't guilty.  
**6:18** He also hates hearts that make evil plans, feet that are quick to do evil,  
**6:19** any witness who pours out lies, and anyone who stirs up family fights.  
**6:20** My son, keep your father's commands. Don't turn away from your mother's teaching.  
**6:21** Tie them to your heart forever. Put them around your neck.  
**6:22** When you walk, they will guide you. When you sleep, they will watch over you. When you wake up, they will speak to you.  
**6:23** Your father's commands are like a lamp. Your mother's teaching is like a light. And the training that corrects you leads to life.  
**6:24** It keeps you from a sinful woman. It keeps you from the smooth tongue of a woman who commits adultery.  
**6:25** Don't hunger in your heart after her beauty. Don't let her eyes capture you.  
**6:26** A prostitute leaves you with only a loaf of bread. Another man's wife hunts your very life.  
**6:27** You can't shovel fire into your lap without burning your clothes.  
**6:28** You can't walk on hot coals without burning your feet.  
**6:29** It's the same for anyone who has sex with another man's wife. Anyone who touches her will be punished.  
**6:30** People don't hate a thief who steals to fill his empty stomach.  
**6:31** But when he is caught, he must pay seven times as much as he stole. It may even cost him everything he has.  
**6:32** A man who commits adultery has no sense. Anyone who does it destroys himself.  
**6:33** He will be beaten up and dishonored. His shame will never be wiped away.  
**6:34** Jealousy stirs up a husband's anger. He will show no mercy when he gets even.  
**6:35** He won't accept any payment. He won't take any money, no matter how much he is offered.  

## 7장

**7:1** My son, obey my words. Store up my commands inside you.  
**7:2** Obey my commands and you will live. Guard my teachings as you would your own eyes.  
**7:3** Tie them on your fingers. Write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to wisdom, "You are my sister." Call understanding a member of your family.  
**7:5** They will keep you from a woman who commits adultery. They will keep you from the smooth talk of a sinful wife.  
**7:6** I stood at the window of my house. I looked out through it.  
**7:7** Among those who were childish I saw a young man who had no sense.  
**7:8** He went down the street near that sinful woman's corner. He walked toward her house.  
**7:9** The sun had gone down. Day was fading. The darkness of night was falling.  
**7:10** A woman came out to meet him. She was dressed like a prostitute and had a clever plan.  
**7:11** She was a loud and pushy woman. She never stayed at home.  
**7:12** Sometimes in the streets, sometimes at other places, at every corner she would wait.  
**7:13** She took hold of the young man and kissed him. With a bold face she spoke to him.  
**7:14** She said, "At home I have meat left over from my offerings. Today I offered what I had promised I would.  
**7:15** So I came out to meet you. I looked for you. And I have found you!  
**7:16** I have covered my bed with colored sheets from Egypt.  
**7:17** I've perfumed my bed with spices. I used myrrh, aloes and cinnamon.  
**7:18** Come, let's drink our fill of love until morning. Let's enjoy ourselves by having sex!  
**7:19** My husband isn't home. He's gone on a long journey.  
**7:20** He took his bag full of money. He won't be home for several days."  
**7:21** She led him down the wrong path with her clever words. She charmed him with her smooth talk.  
**7:22** All at once he followed her. He was like an ox going to be killed. He was like a deer stepping into a trap  
**7:23** until an arrow struck its liver. He was like a bird rushing into a trap. Little did he know it would cost him his life!  
**7:24** My sons, listen to me. Pay attention to what I say.  
**7:25** Don't let your hearts turn to her ways. Don't step onto her paths.  
**7:26** She has brought down a lot of men. She has killed a huge crowd.  
**7:27** Her house is a road to the grave. It leads down to the place of the dead.  

## 8장

**8:1** Doesn't wisdom call out? Doesn't understanding raise her voice?  
**8:2** On the high roads along the way, she takes her place where the paths meet.  
**8:3** Beside the gates leading into the city, she cries out at the entrances.  
**8:4** She says, "Men, I call out to you. I raise my voice to all human beings.  
**8:5** You who are childish, get some good sense. You who are foolish, gain understanding.  
**8:6** Listen! I have worthy things to say. I open my lips to speak what is right.  
**8:7** My mouth speaks what is true. My lips hate evil.  
**8:8** All the words of my mouth are honest. None of them is twisted or sinful.  
**8:9** To those who have understanding, all my words are right. To those who have knowledge, they are true.  
**8:10** Choose my teaching instead of silver. Choose knowledge rather than fine gold.  
**8:11** Wisdom is worth more than rubies. Nothing you want can compare with her.  
**8:12** "I, wisdom, live together with understanding. I have knowledge and good sense.  
**8:13** To have respect for the Lord is to hate evil. I hate pride and bragging. I hate evil ways and twisted words.  
**8:14** I have good sense and give good advice. I have understanding and power.  
**8:15** By me kings rule. Leaders make laws that are fair.  
**8:16** By me princes govern. By me all nobles rule on earth.  
**8:17** I love those who love me. Those who look for me find me.  
**8:18** With me are riches and honor. With me are lasting wealth and success.  
**8:19** My fruit is better than fine gold. My gifts are better than the finest silver.  
**8:20** I walk in ways that are honest. I take paths that are right.  
**8:21** I leave riches to those who love me. I give them more than they have room for.  
**8:22** "The Lord created me as the first of his works, before his acts of long ago.  
**8:23** I was formed at the very beginning. I was formed before the world began.  
**8:24** Before there were any oceans, I was born. There weren't any springs of water at that time.  
**8:25** Before the mountains were settled in place, I was born. Before there were any hills, I was born.  
**8:26** It happened before the Lord made the earth and its fields. It was before he made the dust of the world.  
**8:27** I was there when he set the heavens in place. When he marked out the place where the sky meets the sea, I was there.  
**8:28** That was when he put the clouds above. It was when he fixed the ocean springs in place.  
**8:29** It was when he set limits for the sea so that the waters had to obey his command. When he marked out the foundations of the earth, I was there.  
**8:30** I was the skilled worker at his side. I was filled with delight day after day. I was always happy to be with him.  
**8:31** His whole world filled me with joy. I took delight in all human beings.  
**8:32** "My children, listen to me. Blessed are those who keep my ways.  
**8:33** Listen to my teaching and be wise. Don't turn away from it.  
**8:34** Blessed is the one who listens to me. He watches every day at my doors. He waits beside my doorway.  
**8:35** Those who find me find life. They receive favor from the Lord.  
**8:36** But those who don't find me harm only themselves. Everyone who hates me loves death."  

## 9장

**9:1** Wisdom has built her house. She has made its seven pillars.  
**9:2** She has prepared her meat and mixed her wine. She has also set her table.  
**9:3** She has sent out her female servants. She calls out from the highest point of the city.  
**9:4** She says, "Let all who are childish come in here!" She speaks to those who have no sense.  
**9:5** "Come and eat my food. Drink the wine I have mixed.  
**9:6** Leave your childish ways and you will live. Walk in the way that leads to understanding.  
**9:7** "When you correct someone who makes fun of others, you might be laughed at. When you warn a sinner, you might get hurt.  
**9:8** Don't warn those who make fun of others. They will hate you. Warn those who are wise. They will love you.  
**9:9** Teach a wise man. He will become even wiser. Teach a person who does right. He will learn even more.  
**9:10** "If you really want to become wise, you must begin by having respect for the Lord. To know the Holy One is to gain understanding.  
**9:11** Through me, you will live a long time. Years will be added to your life.  
**9:12** If you are wise, your wisdom will reward you. If you make fun of others, you alone will suffer."  
**9:13** The woman called Foolishness is loud. She doesn't control herself. She doesn't know anything.  
**9:14** She sits at the door of her house. She sits at the highest point of the city.  
**9:15** She calls out to those who pass by. She calls out to those who go straight on their way.  
**9:16** She says, "Let all who are childish come in here!" She speaks to those who have no sense.  
**9:17** She says, "Stolen water is sweet. Food eaten in secret tastes good!"  
**9:18** But they don't know that dead people are there. They don't know that her guests are in the deepest parts of the grave.  

## 10장

**10:1** These are the proverbs of Solomon. A wise son makes his father glad. But a foolish son brings sorrow to his mother.  
**10:2** Riches that are gained by sinning aren't worth anything. But doing what is right saves you from death.  
**10:3** The Lord gives those who do right the food they need. But he lets those who do wrong go hungry.  
**10:4** Hands that don't want to work make you poor. But hands that work hard bring wealth to you.  
**10:5** A child who gathers crops in summer is wise. But a child who sleeps at harvest time brings shame.  
**10:6** Blessings are like crowns on the heads of those who do right. But the trouble caused by what sinners say destroys them.  
**10:7** To remember those who do right is a blessing. But the names of those who do wrong will rot.  
**10:8** A wise heart accepts commands. But foolish chattering destroys you.  
**10:9** Anyone who lives without blame walks safely. But anyone who takes a crooked path will get caught.  
**10:10** An evil wink gets you into trouble. And foolish chattering destroys you.  
**10:11** The mouths of those who do right are a fountain of life. But the trouble caused by what sinners say destroys them.  
**10:12** Hate stirs up fights. But love erases all sins by forgiving them.  
**10:13** Wisdom is found on the lips of those who understand what is right. But those who have no sense are punished.  
**10:14** Wise people store up knowledge. But the mouths of foolish people destroy them.  
**10:15** The wealth of rich people is like a city that makes them feel safe. But having nothing destroys those who are poor.  
**10:16** People who do what is right earn life. But what sinners earn causes them to be punished.  
**10:17** Anyone who pays attention to his training is on his way to life. But anyone who refuses to be corrected leads others down the wrong path.  
**10:18** Anyone who hides hatred has lying lips. And anyone who spreads lies is foolish.  
**10:19** Those who talk a lot are likely to sin. But those who control their tongues are wise.  
**10:20** The tongues of those who do right are like fine silver. But the hearts of those who do wrong aren't worth very much.  
**10:21** The words of those who do right benefit many people. But those who are foolish die because they have no sense.  
**10:22** The blessing of the Lord brings wealth. Trouble doesn't come with it.  
**10:23** A foolish person finds pleasure in doing evil things. But a man who has understanding takes delight in wisdom.  
**10:24** What sinners are afraid of will catch up with them. But those who do right will get what they want.  
**10:25** When the storm is over, sinners are gone. But those who do right stand firm forever.  
**10:26** Anyone who doesn't want to work hurts those who send him. He is like vinegar on the teeth or smoke in the eyes.  
**10:27** Having respect for the Lord leads to a longer life. But the years of evil people are cut short.  
**10:28** Those who do right can expect joy. But the hopes of sinners are bound to fail.  
**10:29** The way of the Lord leads to a safe place for those who do right. But it destroys those who do evil.  
**10:30** Those who do right will never be removed from the land. But those who do wrong will not remain in it.  
**10:31** The mouths of those who do right produce wisdom. But tongues that speak twisted words will be cut out.  
**10:32** Those who do right know the proper thing to say. But those who do wrong speak only twisted words.  

## 11장
