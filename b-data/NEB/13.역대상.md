## 2장

**2:1**  These were the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun,  
**2:2** Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad and Asher.   
**2:3** The sons of Judah: Er, Onan and Shelah; the mother of these three was a Canaanite woman, Bathshua Er, Judah's eldest son, displeased the LORD and the LORD slew him.  
**2:4** Then Tamar, Judah's daughter-in-law, bore him Perez and Zerah, making in all five sons of Judah.  
**2:5** The sons of Perez: Hezron and Hamul.  
**2:6** The sons of Zerah: Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol and Darda, five in all. The son of Zimri: Carmi .  
**2:7** The son of Carmi: Achar, who troubled Israel by his violation of the sacred ban.  
**2:8** The son of Ethan: Azariah.  
**2:9**  The sons of Hezron: Jerahmeel, Ram and Caleb.  
**2:10** Ram was the father of Amminadab, Amminadab father of Nahshon prince of Judah.  
**2:11** Nahshon was the father of Salma, Salma father of Boaz,  
**2:12** Boaz father of Obed, Obed father of Jesse.  
**2:13** The eldest son of Jesse was Eliab, the second Abinadab, the third Shimea,  
**2:14** the fourth Nethaneel, the fifth Raddai,  
**2:15** the sixth Ozem, the seventh David;  
**2:16** their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. The sons of Zeruiah: Abishai, Joab and Asahel, three in all.  
**2:17** Abigail was the mother of Amasa; his father was Jether the Ishmaelite.   
**2:18**  Caleb son of Hezron had Jerioth by Azubah his wife ; these were her sons: Jesher, Shobab and Ardon.  
**2:19** When Azubah died, Caleb married Ephrath, who bore him Hur.  
**2:20** Hur was the father of Uri, and Uri father of Bezalel.  
**2:21** Later, Hezron, then sixty years of age, had intercourse with the daughter of Machir father of Gilead, having married her, and she bore Segub.  
**2:22** Segub was the father of Jair, who had twenty-three cities in Gilead.  
**2:23** Geshur and Aram took from them Havvoth-jair, and Kenath and its dependent villages, a total of sixty towns. All these were descendants of Machir father of Gilead.  
**2:24** After the death of Hezron, Caleb had intercourse with Ephrathah and she bore him Ashhur the founder of Tekoa.   
**2:25**  The sons of Jerahmeel eldest son of Hezron by Ahijah were Ram the eldest, Bunah, Oren and Ozem.  
**2:26** Jerahmeel had another wife, whose name was Atarah; she was the mother of Onam.  
**2:27** The sons of Ram eldest son of Jerahmeel: Maaz, Jamin and Eker.  
**2:28** The sons of Onam: Shammai and Jada. The sons of Shammai: Nadab and Abishur.  
**2:29** The name of Abishur's wife was Abihail; she bore him Ahban and Molid.  
**2:30** The sons of Nadab: Seled and Ephraim; Seled died without children.  
**2:31** Ephraim's son was Ishi, Ishi's son Sheshan, Sheshan's son Ahlai.  
**2:32** The sons of Jada brother of Shammai: Jether and Jonathan; Jether died without children.  
**2:33** The sons of Jonathan: Peleth and Zaza. These were the descendants of Jerahmeel.   
**2:34** Sheshan had daughters but no sons. He had an Egyptian servant named Jarha;  
**2:35** he gave his daughter in marriage to this Jarha, and she bore him Attai.  
**2:36** Attai was the father of Nathan, Nathan father of Zabad,  
**2:37** Zabad father of Ephlal, Ephlal father of Obed,  
**2:38** Obed father of Jehu, Jehu father of Azariah,  
**2:39** Azariah father of Helez, Helez father of Elasah,  
**2:40** Elasah father of Sisamai, Sisamai father of Shallum,  
**2:41** Shallum father of Jekamiah, and Jekamiah father of Elishama.   
**2:42**  The sons of Caleb brother of Jerahmeel: Mesha the eldest, founder of Ziph, and Mareshah founder of Hebron.  
**2:43** The sons of Hebron: Korah, Tappuah, Rekem and Shema.  
**2:44** Shema was the father of Raham father of Jorkoam, and Rekem was the father of Shammai.  
**2:45** The son of Shammai was Maon, and Maon was the founder of Beth-zur.  
**2:46** Ephah, Caleb's concubine, was the mother of Haran, Moza and Gazez; Haran was the father of Gazez.  
**2:47** The sons of Jahdai: Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah and Shaaph.  
**2:48** Maacah, Caleb's concubine, was the mother of Sheber and Tirhanah;  
**2:49** she bore also Shaaph founder of Madmannah, and Sheva founder of Machbenah and Gibea. Caleb also had a daughter named Achsah.   
**2:50** The descendants of Caleb: the sons of Hur, the eldest son of Ephrathah: Shobal the founder of Kiriath-jearim,  
**2:51** Salma the founder of Bethlehem, and Hareph the founder of Beth-gader.  
**2:52** Shobal the founder of Kiriathjearim was the father of Reaiah and the ancestor of half the Manahethites .   
**2:53** The clans of Kiriath-jearim: Ithrites, Puhites, Shumathites, and Mishraites, from whom were descended the Zareathites and the Eshtaulites.   
**2:54** The descendants of Salma: Bethlehem, the Netophathites, Ataroth, Beth-joab, half the Manahethites, and the Zorites.   
**2:55** The clans of Sophrites living at Jabez: Tirathites, Shimeathites, and Suchathites. These were Kenites who were connected by marriage with the ancestor of the Rechabites.

**4:4**   
**4:5** Ashhur the founder of Tekoa had two wives, Helah and Naarah.

**4:25** his son Shallum, his son Mibsam and his son Mishma.  
**4:26** The sons of Mishma: his son Hamuel, his son Zaccur and his son Shimei.

**4:29** Bilhah, Ezem, Tolad,

**4:34** Meshobab, Jamlech, Joshah son of Amaziah,  
**4:35** Joel, Jehu son of Josibiah, son of Seraiah, son of Asiel,  
**4:36** Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, Benaiah,  
**4:37** Ziza son of Shiphi, son of Allon, son of Jedaiah, son of Shimri, son of Shemaiah,  
**4:38** whose names are recorded as princes in their clans, and their families had greatly increased.  
**4:39** They then went from the approaches to Gedor east of the valley in search of pasture for their flocks.  
**4:40** They found rich and good pasture in a wide stretch of open country where everything was quiet and peaceful; before then it had been occupied by Hamites.  
**4:41** During the reign of Hezekiah king of Judah these whose names are written above came and destroyed the tribes of Ham and the Meunites whom they found there. They annihilated them so that no trace of them has remained to this day; and they occupied the land in their place, for there was pasture for their flocks.  
**4:42** Of their number five hundred Simeonites invaded the hill-country of Seir, led by Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi.  
**4:43** They destroyed all who were left of the surviving Amalekites; and they live there still.  

## 5장

**5:1**  The sons of Reuben, the eldest of Israel's sons. (He was, in fact, the first son born, but because he had committed incest with a wife of his father's the rank of the eldest was transferred to the sons of Joseph, Israel's son, who, however, could not be registered as the eldest son.  
**5:2** Judah held the leading place among his brothers because he fathered a ruler, and the rank of the eldest was his, not Joseph's.)  
**5:3** The sons of Reuben, the eldest of Israel's sons: Enoch, Pallu, Hezron and Carmi.  
**5:4** The sons of Joel: his son Shemaiah, his son Gog, his son Shimei,  
**5:5** his son Micah, his son Reaia, his son Baal,  
**5:6** his son Beerah, whom Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria carried away into exile; he was a prince of the Reubenites.  
**5:7** His kinsmen, family by family, as registered in their tribal lists: Jeiel the chief, Zechariah,  
**5:8** Bela son of Azaz, son of Shema, son of Joel. They lived in Aroer, and their lands stretched as far as Nebo and Baal-meon.  
**5:9** Eastwards they occupied territory as far as the edge of the desert which stretches from the river Euphrates, for they had large numbers of cattle in Gilead.  
**5:10** During Saul's reign they made war on the Hagarites, whom they conquered, occupying their encampments over all the country east of Gilead.   
**5:11**  Adjoining them were the Gadites, occupying the district of Bashan as far as Salcah:  
**5:12** Joel the chief; second in rank, Shapham; then Jaanai and Shaphat in Bashan.  
**5:13** Their fellow-tribesmen belonged to the families of Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jachan, Zia and Heber, seven in all.  
**5:14** These were the sons of Abihail son of Huri, son of Jaroah, son of Gilead, son of Michael, son of Jeshishai, son of Jahdo, son of Buz.  
**5:15** Ahi son of Abdiel, son of Guni, was head of their family;  
**5:16** they lived in Gilead, in Bashan and its villages, in all the common land of Sharon as far as it stretched.  
**5:17** These registers were all compiled in the reigns of Jotham king of Judah and Jeroboam king of Israel.   
**5:18**  The sons of Reuben, Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh: of their fighting men armed with shield and sword, their archers and their battle-trained soldiers, forty-four thousand seven hundred and sixty were ready for active service.  
**5:19** They made war on the Hagarites, Jetur, Nephish, and Nodab.  
**5:20** They were given help against them, for they cried to their God for help in the battle, and because they trusted him he listened to their prayer, and the Hagarites and all their allies surrendered to them .  
**5:21** They drove off their cattle, fifty thousand camels, two hundred and fifty thousand sheep, and two thousand asses, and they took a hundred thousand captives.  
**5:22**  Many had been killed, for the war was of God's making, and they occupied the land instead of them until the exile.   
**5:23** Half the tribe of Manasseh lived in the land from Bashan to Baalhermon, Senir, and Mount Hermon, and were numerous also in Lebanon.  
**5:24** The heads of their families were: Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel, all men of ability and repute, heads of their families.  
**5:25**  But they sinned against the God of their fathers, and turned wantonly to worship the gods of the peoples whom God had destroyed before them.  
**5:26** So the God of Israel stirred up Pul king of Assyria, that is Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria, and he carried into exile Reuben, Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh. He took them to Halah, Habor, Hara, and the river Gozan, where they are to this day.

Thou hast won a name for thyself by great and terrible deeds, driving out nations before thy people whom thou didst redeem from Egypt.  
**17:22** Thou hast made thy people Israel thy own for ever, and thou, O LORD, hast become their God.

**17:26** Thou, O LORD, art God, and thou hast made these noble promises to thy servant;

**19:19** When Hadadezer's men saw that they had been worsted by Israel, they sued for peace and submitted to David. The Aramaeans were never again willing to give support to the Ammonites.

## 21장

Consider now what answer I am to take back to him who sent me.'

**21:23** Ornan answered David, 'Take it and let your majesty do as he thinks fit; see, here are the oxen for whole-offerings, the threshing-sledges for the fuel, and the wheat for the grain-offering; I give you everything.'  
**21:24** But King David said to Ornan, 'No, I will pay the full price; I will not present to the LORD what is yours, or offer a whole-offering which has cost me nothing.'  
**21:25** So David gave Ornan six hundred shekels of gold for the site,  
**21:26** and built an altar to the LORD there; on this he offered whole-offerings and shared-offerings, and called upon the LORD, who answered him with fire falling from heaven on the altar of whole-offering.  
**21:27** Then, at the LORD's command, the angel sheathed his sword.   
**21:28** It was when David saw that the LORD had answered him at the threshing-floor of Ornan the Jebusite that he offered sacrifice there.  
**21:29** The tabernacle of the LORD and the altar of whole-offering which Moses had made in the wilderness were then at the hill-shrine in Gibeon;  
**21:30** but David had been unable to go there and seek God's guidance, so shocked and shaken was he at the sight of the angel's sword.

**28:8** Now therefore, in the presence of all Israel, the assembly of the LORD, and within the hearing of our God, I bid you all study carefully the commandments of the LORD your God, that you may possess this good land and hand it down as an inheritance for all time to your children after you.

**28:10** Remember, then, that the LORD has chosen you to build a house for a sanctuary: be steadfast and do it.'   
**28:11** David gave Solomon his son the plan of the porch of the temple and its buildings, strong-rooms, roof-chambers and inner courts, and the shrine of expiation ;

**28:18** the weight also of refined gold for the altar of incense, and of gold for the model of the chariot, that is the cherubim with their wings outspread to screen the Ark of the Covenant of the LORD.

**28:20** Then David said to Solomon his son, 'Be steadfast and resolute and do it; be neither faint-hearted nor dismayed, for the LORD God, my God, will be with you; he will neither fail you nor forsake you, until you have finished all the work needed for the service of the house of the LORD.  
**28:21** Here are the divisions of the priests and the Levites, ready for all the service of the house of God. In all the work you will have the help of every willing craftsman for any task; and the officers and all the people will be entirely at your command.'  

## 29장

**29:1**  King David then said to the whole assembly, 'My son Solomon is the one chosen by God, Solomon alone, a boy of tender years; and this is a great work, for it is a palace not for man but for the LORD God.  
**29:2** Now to the best of my strength I have made ready for the house of my God gold for the gold work, silver for the silver, bronze for the bronze, iron for the iron, and wood for the woodwork, together with cornelian and other gems for setting, stones for mosaic work, precious stones of every sort, and marble in plenty.  
**29:3** Further, because I delight in the house of my God, I give my own private store of gold and silver for the house of my God—over and above all the store which I have collected for the sanctuary—  
**29:4** namely three thousand talents of gold, gold from Ophir, and seven thousand talents of fine silver for overlaying the walls of the buildings,  
**29:5** for providing gold for the gold work, silver for the silver, and for any work to be done by skilled craftsmen. Now who is willing to give with open hand to the LORD today?'   
**29:6** Then the heads of families, the officers administering the tribes of Israel, the officers over units of a thousand and a hundred, and the officers in charge of the king's service, responded willingly  
**29:7** and gave for the work of the house of God five thousand talents of gold, ten thousand darics, ten thousand talents of silver, eighteen thousand talents of bronze, and a hundred thousand talents of iron.  
**29:8** Further, those who possessed precious stones gave them to the treasury of the house of the LORD, into the charge of Jehiel the Gershonite.  
**29:9** The people rejoiced at this willing response, because in the loyalty of their hearts they had given willingly to the LORD; King David also was full of joy,  
**29:10**  and he blessed the LORD in the presence of all the assembly and said, 'Blessed art thou, LORD God of our father Israel, from of old and for ever.  
**29:11** Thine, O LORD, is the greatness, the power, the glory, the splendour, and the majesty; for everything in heaven and on earth is thine , thine, O LORD, is the sovereignty, and thou art exalted over all as head.  
**29:12** Wealth and honour come from thee; thou rulest over all; might and power are of thy disposing; thine it is to give power and strength to all.  
**29:13** And now, we give thee thanks, our God, and praise thy glorious name.   
**29:14** 'But what am I, and what is my people, that we should be able to give willingly like this? For everything comes from thee, and it is only of thy gifts that we give to thee.  
**29:15** We are aliens before thee and settlers, as were all our fathers; our days on earth are like a shadow, we have no abiding place.  
**29:16** O LORD our God, from thee comes all this wealth that we have laid up to build a house in honour of thy holy name, and everything is thine.  
**29:17** I know, O my God, that thou dost test the heart and that plain honesty pleases thee; with an honest heart I have given all these gifts willingly, and have rejoiced now to see thy people here present give willingly to thee.  
**29:18** O LORD God of Abraham, Isaac and Israel our fathers, maintain this purpose for ever in thy people's thoughts and direct their hearts toward thyself.  
**29:19** Grant that Solomon my son may loyally keep thy commandments, thy solemn charge, and thy statutes, that he may fulfil them all and build the palace for which I have prepared.'   
**29:20** Then, turning to the whole assembly, David said, 'Now bless the LORD your God.' So all the assembly blessed the LORD the God of their fathers, bowing low and prostrating themselves before the LORD and the king.  
**29:21** The next day they sacrificed to the LORD and offered whole-offerings to him, a thousand oxen, a thousand rams, a thousand lambs, with the prescribed drink-offerings, and abundant sacrifices for all Israel.  
**29:22** So they ate and drank before the LORD that day with great rejoicing. They then appointed Solomon, David's son, king a second time and anointed him as the LORD's prince, and Zadok as priest.  
**29:23** So Solomon sat on the LORD's throne as king in place of his father David, and he prospered and all Israel obeyed him.  
**29:24** All the officers and the warriors, as well as all the sons of King David, swore fealty to King Solomon.  
**29:25** The LORD made Solomon stand very high in the eyes of all Israel, and bestowed upon him sovereignty such as no king in Israel had had before him.   
**29:26**  David son of Jesse had ruled over the whole of Israel,  
**29:27** and the length of his reign over Israel was forty years; he ruled for seven years in Hebron, and for thirty-three in Jerusalem.  
**29:28** He died in ripe old age, full of years, wealth, and honour; and Solomon his son ruled in his place.  
**29:29** The events of King David's reign from first to last are recorded in the books of Samuel the seer, of Nathan the prophet, and of Gad the seer,  
**29:30** with a full account of his reign, his prowess, and of the times through which he and Israel and all the kingdoms of the world had passed.
