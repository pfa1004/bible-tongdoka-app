# 11.열왕기상 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  KING DAVID WAS NOW A VERY OLD MAN and, though they wrapped clothes round him, he could not keep warm.  
**1:2** So his household said to him, 'Let us find a young virgin for your majesty, to attend you and take care of you; and let her lie in your bosom, sir, and make you warm.'  
**1:3** So they searched all over Israel for a beautiful maiden and found Abishag, a Shunammite, and brought her to the king.  
**1:4** She was a very beautiful girl, and she took care of the king and waited on him, but he had no intercourse with her.   
**1:5**  Now Adonijah, whose mother was Haggith, was boasting that he was to be king; and he had already provided himself with chariots and horsemen and fifty outrunners.  
**1:6** Never in his life had his father corrected him or asked why he behaved as he did. He was a very handsome man, too, and was next in age to Absalom.  
**1:7** He talked with Joab son of Zeruiah and with Abiathar the priest, and they gave him their strong support;  
**1:8** but Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei, and David's bodyguard of heroes, did not take his side.  
**1:9** Adonijah then held a sacrifice of sheep, oxen, and buffaloes at the stone Zoheleth beside En-rogel, and he invited all his royal brothers and all those officers of the household who were of the tribe of Judah.  
**1:10** But he did not invite Nathan the prophet, Benaiah and the bodyguard, or Solomon his brother.   
**1:11**  Then Nathan said to Bathsheba, the mother of Solomon, 'Have you not heard that Adonijah son of Haggith has become king, all unknown to our lord David?  
**1:12** Now come, let me advise you what to do for your own safety and for the safety of your son Solomon.  
**1:13** Go in and see King David and say to him, "Did not your majesty swear to me, your servant, that my son Solomon should succeed you as king; that it was he who should sit on your throne? Why then has Adonijah become king?"  
**1:14** Then while you are still speaking there with the king, I will follow you in and tell the whole story.'   
**1:15** So Bathsheba went to the king in his private chamber; he was now very old, and Abishag the Shunammite was waiting on him.  
**1:16** Bathsheba bowed before the king and prostrated herself. 'What do you want?' said the king.  
**1:17** She answered, 'My lord, you swore to me your servant, by the LORD your God, that my son Solomon should succeed you as king, and that he should sit on your throne.  
**1:18** But now, here is Adonijah become king, all unknown to your majesty.  
**1:19** He has sacrificed great numbers of oxen, buffaloes, and sheep, and has invited to the feast all the king's sons, and Abiathar the priest, and Joab the commander-in-chief, but he has not invited your servant Solomon.  
**1:20** And now, your majesty, all Israel is looking to you to announce who is to succeed you on the throne.  
**1:21** Otherwise, when you, sir, rest with your forefathers, my son Solomon and I shall be treated as criminals.'  
**1:22** She was still speaking to the king when Nathan the prophet arrived.  
**1:23** The king was told that Nathan was there; he came into the king's presence and prostrated himself with his face to the ground.  
**1:24** 'My lord,' he said, 'your majesty must, I suppose, have declared that Adonijah should succced you and that he should sit on your throne.  
**1:25** He has today gone down and sacrificed great numbers of oxen, buffaloes, and sheep, and has invited to the feast all the king's sons, Joab the commander-in-chief, and Abiathar the priest; and at this very moment they are eating and drinking in his presence and shouting, "Long live King Adonijah!"  
**1:26** But he has not invited me your servant, Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, or your servant Solomon.  
**1:27** Has this been done by your majesty's authority, while we your servants have not been told who should succeed you on the throne?'  
**1:28** Thereupon King David said, 'Call Bathsheba', and she came into the king's presence and stood before him.  
**1:29** Then the king swore an oath to her: 'As the LORD lives, who has delivered me from all my troubles:  
**1:30** I swore by the LORD the God of Israel that Solomon your son should succeed me and that he should sit on my throne, and this day I give effect to my oath.'  
**1:31** Bathsheba bowed low to the king and prostrated herself; and she said, 'May my lord King David live for ever!'   
**1:32** Then King David said, 'Call Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada.' They came into the king's presence  
**1:33** and he gave them these orders: 'Take the officers of the household with you; mount my son Solomon on the king's mule and escort him down to Gihon.  
**1:34** There Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet shall anoint him king over Israel. Sound the trumpet and shout, "Long live King Solomon!"  
**1:35** Then escort him home again, and he shall come and sit on my throne and reign in my place; for he is the man that I have appointed prince over Israel and Judah.'  
**1:36** Benaiah son of Jehoiada answered the king, 'It shall be done. And may the LORD, the God of my lord the king, confirm it!  
**1:37** As the LORD has been with your majesty, so may he be with Solomon; may he make his throne even greater than the throne of my lord King David.'  
**1:38** So Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada, together with the Kerethite and Pelethite guards, went down and mounted Solomon on King David's mule and escorted him to Gihon.  
**1:39** Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the Tent of the LORD and anointed Solomon; they sounded the trumpet and all the people shouted, 'Long live King Solomon!'  
**1:40** Then all the people escorted him home in procession, with great rejoicing and playing of pipes, so that the very earth split with the noise.   
**1:41** Adonijah and his guests had finished their banquet when the noise reached their ears. Joab, hearing the sound of the trumpet, exclaimed, 'What is all this uproar in the city? What has happened?'  
**1:42** While he was still speaking, Jonathan son of Abiathar the priest arrived. 'Come in', said Adonijah. 'You are an honourable man and bring good news.'  
**1:43** 'Far otherwise,' Jonathan replied; 'our lord King David has made Solomon king  
**1:44** and has sent with him Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada, together with the Kerethite and Pelethite guards; they have mounted him on the king's mule,  
**1:45** and Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet have anointed him king at Gihon, and they have now escorted him home rejoicing, and the city is in an uproar. That was the noise you heard.  
**1:46** More than that, Solomon has taken his seat on the royal throne.  
**1:47** Yes, and the officers of the household have been to greet our lord King David with these words: "May your God make the name of Solomon your son more famous than your own and his throne even greater than yours", and the king bowed upon his couch.  
**1:48** What is more, he said this: "Blessed be the LORD the God of Israel who has set a successor on my throne this day while I am still alive to see it." '  
**1:49** Then Adonijah's guests all rose in panic and scattered.  
**1:50** Adonijah himself, in fear of Solomon, sprang up and went to the altar and caught hold of its horns.  
**1:51** Then a message was sent to Solomon: 'Adonijah is afraid of King Solomon; he has taken hold of the horns of the altar and has said, "Let King Solomon first swear to me that he will not put his servant to the sword." '  
**1:52** Solomon said, 'If he proves himself a man of worth, not a hair of his head shall fall to the ground; but if he is found to be troublesome, he shall die.'  
**1:53** Then King Solomon sent and had him brought down from the altar; he came in and prostrated himself before the king, and Solomon ordered him home.  

## 2장

**2:1**  When the time of David's death drew near, he gave this last charge to his son Solomon:  
**2:2** 'I am going the way of all the earth. Be strong and show yourself a man.  
**2:3** Fulfil your duty to the LORD your God; conform to his ways, observe his statutes and his commandments, his judgements and his solemn precepts, as they are written in the law of Moses, so that you may prosper in whatever you do and whichever way you turn,  
**2:4** and that the LORD may fulfil this promise that he made about me: "If your descendants take care to walk faithfully in my sight with all their heart and with all their soul, you shall never lack a successor on the throne of Israel."  
**2:5** You know how Joab son of Zeruiah treated me and what he did to two commanders-in-chief in Israel, Abner son of Ner and Amasa son of Jether. He killed them both, breaking the peace by bloody acts of war; and with that blood he stained the belt about my waist and the sandals on my feet.  
**2:6** Do as your wisdom prompts you, and do not let his grey hairs go down to the grave in peace.  
**2:7** Show constant friendship to the family of Barzillai of Gilead; let them have their place at your table; they befriended me when I was a fugitive from your brother Absalom.  
**2:8** Do not forget Shimei son of Gera, the Benjamite from Bahurim, who cursed me bitterly the day I went to Mahanaim. True, he came down to meet me at the Jordan, and I swore by the LORD that I would not put him to death.  
**2:9** But you do not need to let him go unpunished now; you are a wise man and will know how to deal with him; bring down his grey hairs in blood to the grave.'   
**2:10**  So David rested with his forefathers and was buried in the city of David,  
**2:11** having reigned over Israel for forty years, seven in Hebron and thirty-three in Jerusalem;  
**2:12** and Solomon succeeded his father David as king and was firmly established on the throne.   
**2:13**  THEN ADONIJAH SON OF HAGGITH came to Bathsheba, the mother of Solomon. 'Do you come as a friend?' she asked. 'As a friend,' he answered;  
**2:14** 'I have something to say to you.' 'Tell me', she said.  
**2:15** 'You know', he went on, 'that the throne was mine and that all Israel was looking to me to be king; but I was passed over and the throne has gone to my brother; it was his by the LORD's will.  
**2:16** And now I have one request to make of you; do not refuse me.' 'What is it?' she said.  
**2:17** He answered, 'Will you ask King Solomon (he will never refuse you) to give me Abishag the Shunammite in marriage?'  
**2:18** 'Very well,' said Bathsheba, 'I will speak for you to the king.'  
**2:19** So Bathsheba went in to King Solomon to speak for Adonijah. The king rose to meet her and kissed her, and seated himself on his throne. A throne was set for the king's mother and she sat at his right hand.  
**2:20** Then she said, 'I have one small request to make of you; do not refuse me.' 'What is it, mother?' he replied; 'I will not refuse you.'  
**2:21** 'It is this, that Abishag the Shunammite should be given to your brother Adonijah in marriage.'  
**2:22** At that Solomon answered his mother, 'Why do you ask for Abishag the Shunammite as wife for Adonijah? you might as well ask for the throne, for he is my elder brother and has both Abiathar the priest and Joab son of Zeruiah on his side.'  
**2:23** Then King Solomon swore by the LORD: 'So help me God, Adonijah shall pay for this with his life.  
**2:24** As the LORD lives, who has established me and set me on the throne of David my father and has founded a house for me as he promised, this very day Adonijah shall be put to death!'  
**2:25** Thereupon King Solomon gave Benaiah son of Jehoiada his orders, and he struck him down and he died.   
**2:26**  Abiathar the priest was told by the king to go off to Anathoth to his own estate. 'You deserve to die,' he said, 'but in spite of this day's work I shall not put you to death, for you carried the Ark of the Lord GOD before my father David, and you shared in all the hardships that he endured.'  
**2:27** So Solomon dismissed Abiathar from his office as priest of the LORD, and so fulfilled the sentence that the LORD had pronounced against the house of Eli in Shiloh.   
**2:28** News of all this reached Joab, and he fled to the Tent of the LORD and caught hold of the horns of the altar; for he had sided with Adonijah, though not with Absalom.  
**2:29** When King Solomon learnt that Joab had fled to the Tent of the LORD and that he was by the altar, he sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada with orders to strike him down.  
**2:30** Benaiah came to the Tent of the LORD and ordered Joab in the king's name to come away; but he said, 'No; I will die here.' Benaiah reported Joab's answer to the king,  
**2:31** and the king said, 'Let him have his way; strike him down and bury him, and so rid me and my father's house of the guilt for the blood that he wantonly shed.  
**2:32** The LORD will hold him responsible for his own death, because he struck down two innocent men who were better men than he, Abner son of Ner, commander of the army of Israel, and Amasa son of Jether, commander of the army of Judah, and ran them through with the sword, without my father David's knowledge.  
**2:33** The guilt of their blood shall recoil on Joab and his descendants for all time; but David and his descendants, his house and his throne, will enjoy perpetual prosperity from the LORD.'  
**2:34** So Benaiah son of Jehoiada went up to the altar and struck Joab down and killed him, and he was buried in his house on the edge of the wilderness.  
**2:35** Thereafter the king appointed Benaiah son of Jehoiada to command the army in his place, and installed Zadok the priest in place of Abiathar.   
**2:36**  Next the king sent for Shimei and said to him, 'Build yourself a house in Jerusalem and stay there; you are not to leave the city for any other place.  
**2:37** If ever you leave it and cross the gorge of the Kidron, you shall die; make no mistake about that. Your blood will be on your own head.'  
**2:38** And Shimei said to the king, 'I accept your sentence; I will do as your majesty commands.' So for a long time Shimei remained in Jerusalem;  
**2:39** but three years later two of his slaves ran away to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath. When Shimei heard that his slaves were in Gath,  
**2:40** he immediately saddled his ass and went there to Achish in search of his slaves; he came to Gath and returned with them.  
**2:41** When King Solomon was told that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath and back,  
**2:42** he sent for him and said, 'Did I not require you to swear by the LORD? Did I not give you this solemn warning: "If ever you leave this city for any other place, you shall die; make no mistake about it"? And you said, "I accept your sentence; I obey."  
**2:43** Why then have you not kept the oath which you swore by the LORD, and the order which I gave you?  
**2:44** Shimei, you know in your own heart all the mischief you did to my father David; the LORD is now making that mischief recoil on your own head.  
**2:45** But King Solomon is blessed and the throne of David will be secure before the LORD for all time.'  
**2:46** The king then gave orders to Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he went out and struck Shimei down; and he died. Thus Solomon's royal power was securely established.  

## 3장

**3:1**  Solomon allied himself to Pharaoh king of Egypt by marrying his daughter. He brought her to the City of David, until he had finished building his own house and the house of the LORD and the wall round Jerusalem.  
**3:2** The people however continued to sacrifice at the hill-shrines, for till then no house had been built in honour of the name of the LORD.  
**3:3** Solomon himself loved the LORD, conforming to the precepts laid down by his father David; but he too slaughtered and burnt sacrifices at the hill-shrines.   
**3:4** Now King Solomon went to Gibeon to offer a sacrifice, for that was the chief hill-shrine, and he used to offer a thousand whole-offerings on its altar.  
**3:5**  There that night the LORD God appeared to him in a dream and said, 'What shall I give you? Tell me.'  
**3:6** And Solomon answered, 'Thou didst show great and constant love to thy servant David my father, because he walked before thee in loyalty, righteousness, and integrity of heart; and thou hast maintained this great and constant love towards him and hast now given him a son to succeed him on the throne.  
**3:7** Now, O LORD my God, thou hast made thy servant king in place of my father David, though I am in mere child, unskilled in leadership.  
**3:8** And I am here in the midst of thy people, the people of thy choice, too many to be numbered or counted.  
**3:9** Give thy servant, therefore, a heart with skill to listen, so that he may govern thy people justly and distinguish good from evil. For who is equal lo the task of governing this great people of thine?'  
**3:10** The Lord was well pleased that Solomon had asked for this,  
**3:11** and he said to him, 'Because you have asked for this, and not for long life for yourself, or for wealth, or for the lives of your enemies, but have asked for discernment in administering justice,  
**3:12** I grant your request; I give you a heart so wise and so understanding that there has been none like you before your time nor will be after you.  
**3:13** I give you furthermore those things for which you did not ask, such wealth and honour as no king of your time can match.  
**3:14** And if you conform to my ways and observe my ordinances and commandments, as your father David did, I will give you long life.'  
**3:15** Then he awoke, and knew it was a dream. <br>Solomon came to Jerusalem and stood before the Ark of the Covenant of the Lord; there he sacrificed whole-offerings and brought shared-offerings, and gave a feast to all his household.   
**3:16**  Then there came into the king's presence two women who were prostitutes and stood before him.  
**3:17** The first said, 'My lord, this woman and I share the same house, and I gave birth to a child when she was there with me.  
**3:18** On the third day after my baby was born she too gave birth to a child. We were quite alone; no one else was with us in the house; only the two of us were there.  
**3:19** During the night this woman's child died because she overlaid it,  
**3:20** and she got up in the middle of the night, took my baby from my side while I, your servant, was asleep, and laid it in her bosom, putting her dead child in mine.  
**3:21** When I got up in the morning to feed my baby, I found him dead; but when I looked at him closely, I found that it was not the child that I had borne.'  
**3:22** The other woman broke in, 'No; the living child is mine; yours is the dead one', while the first retorted, 'No; the dead child is yours; mine is the living one.' So they went on arguing in the king's presence.  
**3:23** The king thought to himself, 'One of them says, "This is my child, the living one; yours is the dead one." The other says, "No; it is your child that is dead and mine that is alive." '  
**3:24** Then he said, 'Fetch me a sword.' They brought in a sword  
**3:25** and the king gave the order: 'Cut the living child in two and give half to one and half to the other.'  
**3:26** At this the woman who was the mother of the living child, moved with love for her child, said to the king, 'Oh! sir, let her have the baby; whatever you do, do not kill it.' The other said, 'Let neither of us have it; cut it in two.'  
**3:27** Thereupon the king gave judgement: 'Give the living baby to the first woman; do not kill it. She is its mother.'  
**3:28** When Israel heard the judgement which the king had given, they all stood in awe of him; for they saw that he had the wisdom of God within him to administer justice.  

## 4장

**4:1**  KING SOLOMON REIGNED OVER ISRAEL.  
**4:2**  His officers were as follows: <br>In charge of the calendar : Azariah son of Zadok the priest.   
**4:3** Adjutant-general : Ahijah son of Shisha. <br>Secretary of state: Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud.   
**4:4** Commander of the army: Benaiah son of Jehoiada. <br>Priests: Zadok and Abiathar.   
**4:5** Superintendent of the regional governors: Azariah son of Nathan. <br>King's Friend: Zabud son of Nathan.   
**4:6** Comptroller of the household: Ahishar. <br>Superintendent of the forced levy: Adoniram son of Abda.   
**4:7** Solomon had twelve regional governors over Israel and they supplied the food for the king and the royal household, each being responsible for one month's provision in the year.  
**4:8** These were their names: <br>Ben-hur in the hill-country of Ephraim.   
**4:9** Ben-dekar in Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth-shemesh, Elon, and Beth-hanan.   
**4:10** Ben-hesed in Aruboth; he had charge also of Socoh and all the land of Hepher.   
**4:11** Ben-abinadab, who had married Solomon's daughter Taphath, in all the district of Dor.   
**4:12** Baana son of Ahilud in Taanach and Megiddo, all Beth-shean as far as Abel-meholah beside Zartanah, and from Beth-shean below Jezreel as far as Jokmeam.   
**4:13** Ben-geber in Ramoth-gilead, including the tent-villages of Jair son of Manasseh in Gilead and the region of Argob in Bashan, sixty large walled cities with gate-bars of bronze.   
**4:14** Ahinadab son of Iddo in Mahanaim.   
**4:15** Ahimaaz in Naphtali; he also had married a daughter of Solomon, Basmath.   
**4:16** Baanah son of Hushai in Asher and Aloth.   
**4:17** Jehoshaphat son of Paruah in Issachar.   
**4:18** Shimei son of Elah in Benjamin.   
**4:19** Geber son of Uri in Gilead, the land of Sihon king of the Amorites and of Og king of Bashan. <br>In addition, one governor over all the governors in the land.   
**4:20**  The people of Judah and Israel were countless as the sands of the sea; they ate and they drank, and enjoyed life.  
**4:21** Solomon ruled over all the kingdoms from the river Euphrates to Philistia and as far as the frontier of Egypt; they paid tribute and were subject to him all his life.   
**4:22** Solomon's provision for one day was thirty kor of flour and sixty kor of meal,  
**4:23** ten fat oxen and twenty oxen from the pastures and a hundred sheep, as well as stags, gazelles, roebucks, and fattened fowl.  
**4:24** For he was paramount over all the land west of the Euphrates from Tiphsah to Gaza, ruling all the kings west of the river; and he enjoyed peace on all sides.  
**4:25** All through his reign Judah and Israel continued at peace, every man under his own vine and fig-tree, from Dan to Beersheba.   
**4:26** Solomon had forty thousand chariot-horses in his stables and twelve thousand cavalry horses.   
**4:27** The regional governors, each for a month in turn, supplied provisions for King Solomon and for all who came to his table, they never fell short in their deliveries.  
**4:28** They provided also barley and straw, each according to his duty, for the horses and chariot-horses where it was required.   
**4:29** And God gave Solomon depth of wisdom and insight, and understanding as wide as the sand on the sea-shore,  
**4:30** so that Solomon's wisdom surpassed that of all the men of the east and of all Egypt.  
**4:31** For he was wiser than any man, wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, Kalcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol; his fame spread among all the surrounding nations.  
**4:32** He uttered three thousand proverbs, and his songs numbered a thousand and five.  
**4:33** He discoursed of trees, from the cedar of Lebanon down In the marjoram that grows out of the wall, of beasts and birds, of reptiles and fishes.  
**4:34** Men of all races came to listen to the wisdom of Solomon, and from all the kings of the earth who had heard of his wisdom he received gifts.  

## 5장

**5:1**  WHEN HIRAM KING OF TYRE heard that Solomon had been anointed king in his father's place, he sent envoys to him, because he had always been a friend of David.  
**5:2**  Solomon sent this answer to Hiram:  
**5:3** 'You know that my father David could not build a house in honour of the name of the LORD his God, because he was surrounded by armed nations until the LORD made them subject to him.  
**5:4** But now on every side the LORD my God has given me peace; there is no one to oppose me, I fear no attack.  
**5:5** So I propose to build a house in honour of the name of the LORD my God, following the promise given by the LORD to my father David: "Your son whom I shall set on the throne in your place will build the house in honour of my name."  
**5:6** If therefore you will now give orders that cedars be felled and brought from Lebanon, my men will work with yours, and I will pay you for your men whatever sum you fix; for, as you know, we have none so skilled at felling timber as your Sidonians.'   
**5:7** When Hiram received Solomon's message, he was greatly pleased and said, 'Blessed be the LORD today who has given David a wise son to rule over this great people.'  
**5:8** And he sent this reply to Solomon: 'I have received your message. In this matter of timber, both cedar and pine, I will do all you wish.  
**5:9** My men shall bring down the logs from Lebanon to the sea and I will make them up into rafts to be floated to the place you appoint; I will have them broken up there and you can remove them. You, on your part, will meet my wishes if you provide the food for my household.'  
**5:10** So Hiram kept Solomon supplied with all the cedar and pine that he wanted,  
**5:11** and Solomon supplied Hiram with twenty thousand kor of wheat as food for his household and twenty kor of oil of pounded olives; Solomon gave this yearly to Hiram.  
**5:12** (The LORD had given Solomon wisdom as he had promised him; there was peace between Hiram and Solomon and they concluded an alliance.)  
**5:13** King Solomon raised a forced levy from the whole of Israel amounting to thirty thousand men.  
**5:14** He sent them to Lebanon in monthly relays of ten thousand, so that the men spent one month in Lebanon and two at home; Adoniram was superintendent of the whole levy.  
**5:15** Solomon had also seventy thousand hauliers and eighty thousand quarrymen,  
**5:16** apart from the three thousand three hundred foremen in charge of the work who superintended the labourers.  
**5:17** By the king's orders they quarried huge, massive blocks for laying the foundation of the LORD's house in hewn stone.  
**5:18** Solomon's and Hiram's builders and the Gebalites shaped the blocks and prepared both timber and stone for the building of the house.  

## 6장

**6:1**  It was in the four hundred and eightieth year after the Israelites had come out of Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon's reign over Israel, in the second month of that year, the month of Ziv, that he began to build the house of the LORD.   
**6:2** The house which King Solomon built for the LORD was sixty cubits long by twenty cubits broad, and its height was thirty cubits.  
**6:3** The vestibule in front of the sanctuary was twenty cubits long, spanning the whole breadth of the house, while it projected ten cubits in front of the house;  
**6:4** and he furnished the house with embrasures.  
**6:5** Then he built a terrace against its wall round both the sanctuary and the inner shrine. He made arcades all round:  
**6:6** the lowest arcade was five cubits in depth, the middle six, and the highest seven; for he made rebates all round the outside of the main wall so that the bearer beams might not be set into the walls.  
**6:7** In the building of the house, only blocks of undressed stone direct from the quarry were used; no hammer or axe or any iron tool whatever was heard in the house while it was being built.   
**6:8** The entrance to the lowest arcade was in the right-hand corner of the house; there was access by a spiral stairway from that to the middle arcade, and from the middle arcade to the highest.  
**6:9** (9-10) So he built the house and finished it, having constructed the terrace five cubits high against the whole building, braced the house with struts of cedar and roofed it with beams and coffering of cedar.   
**6:10**   
**6:11** Then the word of the LORD came to Solomon,  
**6:12** saying, 'As for this house which you are building, if you are obedient to my ordinances and conform to my precepts and loyally observe all my commands, then I will fulfil my promise to you, the promise I gave to your father David,  
**6:13** and I will dwell among the Israelites and never forsake my people Israel.'   
**6:14** So Solomon built the LORD's house and finished it.  
**6:15**  He lined the inner walls of the house with cedar boards, covering the interior from floor to rafters with wood; the floor he laid with boards of pine.

## 12장

**12:7** And they said, 'If today you are willing to serve this people, show yourself their servant now and speak kindly to them, and they will be your servants ever after.'

**12:9** and asked them, 'What answer do you advise me to give to this people's request that I should lighten the yoke which my father laid on them?'

**12:17** and Rehoboam ruled over those Israelites who lived in the cities of Judah.   
**12:18** Then King Rehoboam sent out Adoram, the commander of the forced levies, but the Israelites stoned him to death; thereupon King Rehoboam mounted his chariot in haste and fled to Jerusalem.  
**12:19** From that day to this, the whole of Israel has been in rebellion against the house of David.   
**12:20** When the men of Israel heard that Jeroboam had returned, they sent and called him to the assembly and made him king over the whole of Israel. The tribe of Judah alone followed the house of David.

**12:23** 'Say to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to the house of Judah and to Benjamin and the rest of the people,  
**12:24** "This is the word of the LORD: You shall not go up to make war on your kinsmen the Israelites. Return to your homes, for this is my will." ' So they listened to the word of the LORD and returned home, as the LORD had told them.   
**12:25**  THEN JEROBOAM REBUILT SHECHEM in the hill-country of Ephraim and took up residence there; from there he went out and built Penuel.  
**12:26** 'As things now stand,' he said to himself, 'the kingdom will revert to the house of David.

**12:28** After giving thought to the matter he made two calves of gold and said to the people, 'It is too much trouble for you to go up to Jerusalem; here are your gods, Israel, that brought you up from Egypt.'  
**12:29** One he set up at Bethel and the other he put at Dan,  
**12:30** and this thing became a sin in Israel; the people went to Bethel to worship the one, and all the way to Dan to worship the other.  
**12:31** He set up shrines on the hill-tops also and appointed priests from every class of the people, who did not belong to the Levites.  
**12:32**  He instituted a pilgrim-feast on the fifteenth day of the eighth month like that in Judah, and he offered sacrifices upon the altar. This he did at Bethel, sacrificing to the calves that he had made and compelling the priests of the hill-shrines, which he had set up, to serve at Bethel.  
**12:33** So he went up to the altar that he had made at Bethel on the fifteenth day of the eighth month; there, in a month of his own choosing, he instituted for the Israelites a pilgrim-feast and himself went up to the altar to burn the sacrifice.  

## 13장

**13:1**  As Jeroboam stood by the altar to burn the sacrifice, a man of God from Judah, moved by the word of the LORD, appeared at Bethel.  
**13:2** He inveighed against the altar in the LORD's name, crying out, 'O altar, altar! This is the word of the LORD: "Listen! A child shall be born to the house of David, named Josiah. He will sacrifice upon you the priests of the hill-shrines who make offerings upon you, and he will burn human bones upon you." '  
**13:3** He gave a sign the same day: 'This is the sign which the LORD has ordained: This altar will be rent in pieces and the ashes upon it will be spilt.'  
**13:4** When King Jeroboam heard the sentence which the man of God pronounced against the altar at Bethel, he pointed to him from the altar and said, 'Seize that man!' Immediately the hand which he had pointed at him became paralysed, so that he could not draw it back.  
**13:5** The altar too was rent in pieces and the ashes were spilt, in fulfilment of the sign that the man of God had given at the LORD's command.  
**13:6** The king appealed to the man of God to pacify the LORD his God and pray for him that his hand might be restored. The man of God did as he asked; his hand was restored and became as it had been before.  
**13:7** Then the king said to the man of God, 'Come home and take refreshment at my table, and let me give you a present.'  
**13:8** But the man of God answered, 'If you were to give me half your house, I would not enter it with you: I will eat and drink nothing in this place,  
**13:9** for the LORD's command to me was to eat and drink nothing, and not to go back by the way I came.'  
**13:10** So he went back another way; he did not return by the road he had taken to Bethel.   
**13:11**  At that time there was an aged prophet living in Bethel. His sons came and recounted to him all that the man of God had done in Bethel that day; they also told their father what he had said to the king.  
**13:12** Their father said to them, 'Which road did he take?' They pointed out the road taken by the man of God who had come from Judah.  
**13:13** He said to his sons, 'Saddle an ass for me.' They saddled the ass, and he mounted it  
**13:14** and went after the man of God. He found him seated under a terebinth and said to him, 'Are you the man of God who came from Judah?' And he said, 'Yes, I am.'  
**13:15** 'Come home and eat with me', said the prophet.  
**13:16** 'I cannot go back with you or enter your house,' said the other; 'I can neither eat not drink with you in this place,  
**13:17** for it was told me by the word of the LORD: "You shall eat and drink nothing there, nor shall you go back the way you came." '  
**13:18** And the old man said to him, 'I also am a prophet, as you are; and an angel commanded me by the word of the LORD to bring you home with me to eat and drink with me.' He was lying;  
**13:19** but the man of Judah went back with him and ate and drank in his house.  
**13:20** While they were still seated at table the word of the LORD came to the prophet who had brought him back,  
**13:21** and he cried out to the man of God from Judah, 'This is the word of the LORD: "You have defied the word of the LORD your God and have not obeyed his command;  
**13:22** you have come back to eat and to drink in the place where he forbade it; therefore your body shall not be laid in the grave of your forefathers." '   
**13:23** After they had eaten and drunk, he saddled an ass for the prophet whom he had brought back.  
**13:24** As he went on his way a lion met him and killed him, and his body was left lying in the road, with the ass and the lion both standing beside it.  
**13:25** Some passers-by saw the body lying in the road and the lion standing beside it, and they brought the news to the city where the old prophet lived.  
**13:26** When the prophet who had caused him to break his journey heard it, he said, 'It is the man of God who defied the word of the LORD. The LORD has given him to the lion, and it has broken his neck and killed him in fulfilment of the word of the LORD.'  
**13:27** He told his sons to saddle an ass and, when they had saddled it,  
**13:28** he set out and found the body lying in the road with the ass and the lion standing beside it; the lion had neither devoured the body nor broken the back of the ass.  
**13:29** Then the prophet lifted the body of the man of God, laid it on the ass and brought it back to his own city to mourn over it and bury it.  
**13:30** He laid the body in his own grave and they mourned for him, saying, 'My brother, my brother!'  
**13:31** After burying him, he said to his sons, 'When I die, bury me in the grave where the man of God lies buried; lay my bones beside his;  
**13:32** for the sentence which he pronounced at the LORD's command against the altar in Bethel and all the hill-shrines of Samaria shall be carried out.'   
**13:33**  After this Jeroboam still did not abandon his evil ways but went on appointing priests for the hill-shrines from all classes of the people; any man who offered himself he would consecrate to be priest of a hill-shrine.  
**13:34** By doing this he brought guilt upon his own house and doomed it to utter destruction.

**17:17** Afterwards the son of this woman, the mistress of the house, fell ill and grew worse and worse, until at last his breathing ceased.

**17:20** Then he called out to the LORD, 'O LORD my God, is this thy care for the widow with whom I lodge, that thou hast been so cruel to her son?'  
**17:21** Then he breathed deeply upon the child three times and called on the LORD, 'O LORD my God, let the breath of life, I pray, return to the body of this child.'  
**17:22** The LORD listened to Elijah's cry, and the breath of life returned to the child's body, and he revived;  
**17:23** Elijah lifted him up and took him down from the roof into the house, gave him to his mother and said, 'Look, your son is alive.'

## 18장

**18:1**  Time went by, and in the third year the word of the LORD came to Elijah: 'Go and show yourself to Ahab, and I will send rain upon the land.'  
**18:2** So he went to show himself to Ahab. At this time the famine in Samaria was at its height,  
**18:3** and Ahab summoned Obadiah, the comptroller of his household, a devout worshipper of the LORD.  
**18:4** When Jezebel massacred the prophets of the LORD, he had taken a hundred of them and hidden them in caves, fifty by fifty, giving them food and drink to keep them alive.  
**18:5** Ahab said to Obadiah, 'Let us go through the land, both of us, to every spring and gully; if we can find enough grass we may keep the horses and mules alive and lose none of our cattle.'  
**18:6** They divided the land between them for their survey, Ahab going one way by himself and Obadiah another.   
**18:7** As Obadiah was on his way, Elijah met him. Obadiah recognized him and fell prostrate before him and said, 'Can it be you, my lord Elijah?'  
**18:8** 'Yes,' he said, 'it is I; go and tell your master that Elijah is here.'  
**18:9** 'What wrong have I done?' said Obadiah. 'Why should you give me into Ahab's hands? He will put me to death.  
**18:10** As the LORD your God lives, there is no nation or kingdom to which my master has not sent in search of you. If they said, "He is not here", he made that kingdom or nation swear on oath that they could not find you.  
**18:11** Yet now you say, "Go and tell your master that Elijah is here." What will happen?  
**18:12** As soon as I leave you, the spirit of the LORD will carry you away, who knows where? I shall go and tell Ahab, and when he fails to find you, he will kill me. Yet I have been a worshipper of the LORD from boyhood.  
**18:13** Have you not been told, my lord, what I did when Jezebel put the LORD's prophets to death, how I hid a hundred of them in caves, fifty by fifty, and kept them alive with food and drink?  
**18:14** And now you say, "Go and tell your master that Elijah is here"! He will kill me.'  
**18:15** Elijah answered, 'As the LORD of Hosts lives, whose servant I am, I swear that I will show myself to him this very day.'  
**18:16** So Obadiah went to find Ahab and gave him the message, and Ahab went to meet Elijah.   
**18:17** As soon as Ahab saw Elijah, he said to him, 'Is it you, you troubler of Israel?'  
**18:18** 'It is not I who have troubled Israel,' he replied, 'but you and your father's family, by forsaking the commandments of the LORD and following Baal.  
**18:19** But now, send and summon all Israel to meet me on Mount Carmel, and the four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal with them and the four hundred prophets of the goddess Asherah, who are Jezebel's pensioners.'  
**18:20** So Ahab sent out to all the Israelites and assembled the prophets on Mount Carmel.  
**18:21** Elijah stepped forward and said to the people, 'How long will you sit on the fence? If the LORD is God, follow him; but if Baal, then follow him.' Not a word did they answer.  
**18:22** Then Elijah said to the people, 'I am the only prophet of the LORD still left, but there are four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal.  
**18:23** Bring two bulls; let them choose one for themselves, cut it up and lay it on the wood without setting fire to it, and I will prepare the other and lay it on the wood without setting fire to it.  
**18:24** You shall invoke your god by name and I will invoke the LORD by name; and the god who answers by fire, he is God.' And all the people shouted their approval.   
**18:25** Then Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, 'Choose one of the bulls and offer it first, for there are more of you; invoke your god by name, but do not set fire to the wood.'  
**18:26** So they took the bull provided for them and offered it, and they invoked Baal by name from morning until noon, crying, 'Baal, Baal, answer us'; but there was no sound, no answer. They danced wildly beside the altar they had set up.  
**18:27** At midday Elijah mocked them: 'Call louder, for he is a god; it may be he is deep in thought, or engaged, or on a journey; or he may have gone to sleep and must be woken up.'  
**18:28** They cried still louder and, as was their custom, gashed themselves with swords and spears until the blood ran.  
**18:29** All afternoon they raved and ranted till the hour of the regular sacrifice, but still there was no sound, no answer, no sign of attention.   
**18:30** Then Elijah said to all the people, 'Come here to me.' They all came, and he repaired the altar of the LORD which had been torn down.  
**18:31** He took twelve stones, one for each tribe of the sons of Jacob, the man named Israel by the word of the LORD.  
**18:32** With these stones he built an altar in the name of the LORD; he dug a trench round it big enough to hold two measures of seed;  
**18:33** he arranged the wood, cut up the bull and laid it on the wood.  
**18:34** Then he said, 'fill four jars with water and pour it on the whole-offering and on the wood.' They did so, and he said, 'Do it again.' They did it again, and he said, 'Do it a third time.' They did it a third time,  
**18:35** and the water ran all round the altar and even filled the trench.  
**18:36** At the hour of the regular sacrifice the prophet Elijah came forward and said, 'LORD God of Abraham, of Isaac, and of Israel, let it be known today that thou art God in Israel and that I am thy servant and have done all these things at thy command.  
**18:37** Answer me, O LORD, answer me and let this people know that thou, LORD, art God and that it is thou that hast caused them to be back-sliders .'  
**18:38** Then the fire of the LORD fell. It consumed the whole-offering, the wood, the stones, and the earth, and licked up the water in the trench.  
**18:39** When all the people saw it, they fell prostrate and cried, 'The LORD is God, the LORD is God.'  
**18:40** Then Elijah said to them, 'Seize the prophets of Baal; let not one of them escape.' They seized them, and Elijah took them down to the Kishon and slaughtered them there in the valley.   
**18:41**  Elijah said to Ahab, 'Go back now, eat and drink, for I hear the sound of coming rain.'  
**18:42** He did so, while Elijah himself climbed to the crest of Carmel. There he crouched on the ground with his face between his knees.  
**18:43** He said to his servant, 'Go and look out to the west.' He went and looked; 'There is nothing to see', he said. Seven times Elijah ordered him back, and seven times he went.  
**18:44** The seventh time he said, 'I see a cloud no bigger than a man's hand, coming up from the west.' 'Now go', said Elijah, 'and tell Ahab to harness his chariot and be off, or the rain will stop him.'  
**18:45** Meanwhile the sky had grown black with clouds, the wind rose. and heavy rain began to fall. Ahab mounted his chariot and set off for Jezreel;  
**18:46** but the power of the LORD had come upon Elijah: he tucked up his robe and ran before Ahab all the way to Jezreel.

## 22장

**22:1**  FOR THREE YEARS THERE WAS NO WAR between the Aramaeans and the Israelites,  
**22:2**  but in the third year Jehoshaphat king of Judah went down to visit the king of Israel.  
**22:3** The latter said to his courtiers, 'You know that Ramoth-gilead belongs to us, and yet we do nothing to recover it from the king of Aram.'  
**22:4** He said to Jehoshaphat, 'Will you join me in attacking Ramoth-gilead?' Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, 'What is mine is yours: myself, my people, and my horses.'  
**22:5** Then Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, 'first let us seek counsel from the LORD.'  
**22:6** The king of Israel assembled the prophets, some four hundred of them, and asked them, 'Shall I attack Ramoth-gilead or shall I refrain?' 'Attack,' they answered; 'the Lord will deliver it into your hands.'  
**22:7** Jehoshaphat asked, 'Is there no other prophet of the LORD here through whom we may seek guidance?'  
**22:8** 'There is one more', the king of Israel answered, 'through whom we may seek guidance of the LORD, but I hate the man, because he prophesies no good for me; never anything but evil. His name is Micaiah son of Imlah.' Jehoshaphat exclaimed, 'My lord king, let no such word pass your lips!'  
**22:9** So the king of Israel called one of his eunuchs and told him to fetch Micaiah son of Imlah with all speed.   
**22:10** The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah were seated on their thrones, in shining armour, at the entrance to the gate of Samaria, and all the prophets were prophesying before them.  
**22:11** One of them, Zedekiah son of Kenaanah, made himself horns of iron and said, 'This is the word of the LORD: "With horns like these you shall gore the Aramaeans and make an end of them." '  
**22:12** In the same vein all the prophets prophesied, 'Attack Ramoth-gilead and win the day; the LORD will deliver it into your hands.'  
**22:13** The messenger sent to fetch Micaiah told him that the prophets had with one voice given the king a favourable answer. 'And mind you agree with them', he added.  
**22:14** 'As the LORD lives,' said Micaiah, 'I will say only what the LORD tells me to say.'   
**22:15** When Micaiah came into the king's presence, the king said to him, 'Micaiah, shall we attack Ramoth-gilead or shall we refrain?' 'Attack and win the day,' he said; 'the LORD will deliver it into your hands.'  
**22:16** 'How often must I adjure you', said the king, 'to tell me nothing but the truth in the name of the LORD?'  
**22:17** Then Micaiah said, 'I saw all Israel scattered on the mountains, like sheep without a shepherd; and I heard the LORD say, "They have no master, let them go home in peace." '  
**22:18** The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, 'Did I not tell you that he never prophesies good for me, nothing but evil?'  
**22:19** Micaiah went on, 'Listen now to the word of the LORD. I saw the LORD seated on his throne, with all the host of heaven in attendance on his right and on his left.  
**22:20** The LORD said, "Who will entice Ahab to attack and fall on Ramoth-gilead?" One said one thing and one said another;  
**22:21** then a spirit came forward and stood before the LORD and said, "I will entice him."  
**22:22** "How?" said the LORD. "I will go out", he said, "and be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets." "You shall entice him," said the LORD, "and you shall succeed; go and do it."  
**22:23** You see, then, how the LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouth of all these prophets of yours, because he has decreed disaster for you.'  
**22:24** Then Zedekiah son of Kenaanah came up to Micaiah and struck him in the face: 'And how did the spirit of the LORD pass from me to speak to you?' he said.  
**22:25** Micaiah answered, 'That you will find out on the day when you run into an inner room to hide yourself.'  
**22:26** Then the king of Israel ordered Micaiah to be arrested and committed to the custody of Amon the governor of the city and Joash the king's son ,  
**22:27** 'Lock this fellow up', he said, 'and give him prison diet of bread and water until I come home in safety.'  
**22:28** Micaiah retorted, 'If you do return in safety, the LORD has not spoken by me.'   
**22:29**  So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah marched on Ramoth-gilead,  
**22:30** and the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, 'I will disguise myself to go into battle, but you shall wear your royal robes.' So he went into battle in disguise.  
**22:31** Now the king of Aram had commanded the thirty-two captains of his chariots not to engage all and sundry but the king of Israel alone.  
**22:32** When the captains saw Jehoshaphat, they thought he was the king of Israel and turned to attack him. But Jehoshaphat cried out  
**22:33** and, when the captains saw that he was not the king of Israel, they broke off the attack on him.  
**22:34** But one man drew his bow at random and hit the king of Israel where the breastplate joins the plates of the armour. So he said to his driver, 'Wheel round and take me out of the line; I am wounded.'  
**22:35** When the day's fighting reached its height, the king was facing the Aramaeans propped up in his chariot, and the blood from his wound flowed down upon the floor of the chariot; and in the evening he died.  
**22:36** At sunset the herald went through the ranks, crying, 'Every man to his city, every man to his country.'  
**22:37** Thus died the king. He was brought to Samaria and they buried him there.  
**22:38** The chariot was swilled out at the pool of Samaria, and the dogs licked up the blood, and the prostitutes washed themselves in it, in fulfilment of the word the LORD had spoken.   
**22:39**  Now the other acts and events of Ahab's reign, the ivory house and all the cities he built, are recorded in the annals of the kings of Israel.  
**22:40** So Ahab rested with his forefathers and was succeeded by his son Ahaziah.   
**22:41**  Jehoshaphat son of Asa had become king of Judah in the fourth year of Ahab king of Israel.  
**22:42** He was thirty-five years old when he came to the throne, and he reigned in Jerusalem for twenty-five years; his mother was Azubah daughter of Shilhi.  
**22:43** He followed in the footsteps of Asa his father and did not swerve from them; he did what was right in the eyes of the LORD. But the hill-shrines were allowed to remain; the people continued to slaughter and burn sacrifices there.  
**22:44** 44Jehoshaphat remained at peace with the king of Israel.  
**22:45** The other events of Jehoshaphat's reign, his exploits and his wars, are recorded in the annals of the kings of Judah.  
**22:46** But he did away with such of the male prostitutes attached to the shrines as were still left over from the days of Asa his father.   
**22:47** There was no king in Edom, only a Viceroy of Jehoshaphat;  
**22:48** he built merchantmen to sail to Ophir for gold, but they never made the journey because they were wrecked at Ezion-geber.  
**22:49** Ahaziah son of Ahab proposed to Jehoshaphat that his own men should go to sea with his; but Jehoshaphat would not consent.   
**22:50** Jehoshaphat rested with his forefathers and was buried with them in the city of David his father, and was succeeded by his son Joram.   
**22:51**  Ahaziah son of Ahab became king of Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and reigned over Israel for two years.  
**22:52** He did what was wrong in the eyes of the LORD, following in the footsteps of his father and mother and in those of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had led Israel into sin.  
**22:53** He served Baal and worshipped him, and provoked the anger of the LORD the God of Israel, as his father had done.
