# 1.창세기 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  IN THE BEGINNING OF CREATION, when God made heaven and earth ,  
**1:2** the earth was without form and void, with darkness over the face of the abyss, and a mighty wind that swept over the surface of the waters.  
**1:3** God said, 'Let there be light', and there was light;  
**1:4** and God saw that the light was good, and he separated light from darkness.  
**1:5** He called the light day, and the darkness night. So evening came, and morning came, the first day.  
**1:6**  God said, 'Let there be a vault between the waters, to separate water from water.'  
**1:7** So God made the vault, and separated the water under the vault from the water above it, and so it was;  
**1:8** and God called the vault heaven. Evening came, and morning came, a second day.  
**1:9**  God said, 'Let the waters under heaven be gathered into one place, so that dry land may appear'; and so it was.  
**1:10** God called the dry land earth, and the gathering of the waters he called seas; and God saw that it was good.  
**1:11** Then God said, 'Let the earth produce fresh growth, let there be on the earth plants bearing seed, fruit-trees bearing fruit each with seed according to its kind.' So it was;  
**1:12** the earth yielded fresh growth, plants bearing seed according to their kind and trees bearing fruit each with seed according to its kind; and God saw that it was good.  
**1:13** Evening came, and morning came, a third day.  
**1:14**  God said, 'Let there be lights in the vault of heaven to separate day from night, and let them serve as signs both for festivals and for seasons and years.  
**1:15** Let them also shine in the vault of heaven to give light on earth.' So it was;  
**1:16** God made the two great lights, the greater to govern the day and the lesser to govern the night; and with them he made the stars.  
**1:17** God put these lights in the vault of heaven to give light on earth,  
**1:18** to govern day and night, and to separate light from darkness ; and God saw that it was good.  
**1:19** Evening came, and morning came, a fourth day.  
**1:20**  God said, 'Let the waters teem with countless living creatures, and let birds fly above the earth across the vault of heaven.'  
**1:21** God then created the great sea-monsters and all living creatures that move and swarm in the waters, according to their kind, and every kind of bird; and God saw that it was good.  
**1:22** So he blessed them and said, 'Be fruitful and increase, fill the waters of the seas; and let the birds increase on land.'  
**1:23** Evening came, and morning came, a fifth day.  
**1:24**  God said, 'Let the earth bring forth living creatures, according to their kind: cattle, reptiles, and wild animals, all according to their kind.' So it was ;  
**1:25** God made wild animals, cattle, and all reptiles, each according to its kind; and he saw that it was good.  
**1:26** Then God said, 'Let us make man in our image and likeness to rule the fish in the sea, the birds of heaven, the cattle, all wild animals on earth, and all reptiles that crawl upon the earth.'  
**1:27** So God created man in his own image; in the image of God he created him ; male and female he created them.  
**1:28** God blessed them and said to them, 'Be fruitful and increase, fill the earth and subdue it, rule over the fish in the sea, the birds of heaven, and every living thing that moves upon the earth.'  
**1:29** God also said, 'I give you all plants that bear seed everywhere on earth, and every tree bearing fruit which yields seed: they shall be yours for food.  
**1:30** All green plants I give for food to the wild animals, to all the birds of heaven, and to all reptiles on earth, every living creature.' So it was;  
**1:31** and God saw all that he had made, and it was very good. Evening came, and morning came, a sixth day.  

## 2장

**2:1**  Thus heaven and earth were completed with all their mighty throng.  
**2:2** On the sixth day God completed all the work he had been doing, and on the seventh day he ceased from all his work.  
**2:3** God blessed the seventh day and made it holy, because on that day he ceased from all the work he had set himself to do.  
**2:4**  This is the story of the making of heaven and earth when they were created.  
**2:5**  WHEN THE LORD GOD MADE EARTH AND HEAVEN, there was neither shrub nor plant growing wild upon the earth, because the LORD God had sent no rain on the earth; nor was there any man to till the ground.  
**2:6** A flood used to rise out of the earth and water all the surface of the ground.  
**2:7** Then the LORD God formed a man from the dust of the ground and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life. Thus the man became a living creature.  
**2:8** Then the LORD God planted a garden in Eden away to the east, and there he put the man whom he had formed.  
**2:9** The LORD God made trees spring from the ground, all trees pleasant to look at and good for food ; and in the middle of the garden he set the tree of life and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.  
**2:10**  There was a river flowing from Eden to water the garden, and when it left the garden it branched into four streams.  
**2:11** The name of the first is Pishon; that is the river which encircles all the land of Havilah, where the gold is.  
**2:12** The gold of that land is good; bdellium and cornelians are also to be found there.  
**2:13** The name of the second river is Gihon; this is the one which encircles all the land of Cush.  
**2:14** The name of the third is Tigris; this is the river which runs east of Asshur. The fourth river is the Euphrates.  
**2:15**  The LORD God took the man and put him in the garden of Eden to till it and care for it.  
**2:16** He told the man, 'You may eat from every tree in the garden,  
**2:17** but not from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil; for on the day that you eat from it, you will certainly die.'  
**2:18** Then the LORD God said, 'It is not good for the man to be alone. I will provide a partner for him.'  
**2:19** So God formed out of the ground all the wild animals and all the birds of heaven. He brought them to the man to see what he would call them, and whatever the man called each living creature, that was its name.  
**2:20** Thus the man gave names to all cattle, to the birds of heaven, and to every wild animal; but for the man himself no partner had yet been found.  
**2:21** And so the LORD God put the man into a trance, and while he slept, he took one of his ribs and closed the flesh over the place.  
**2:22** The LORD God then built up the rib, which he had taken out of the man, into a woman. He brought her to the man,  
**2:23** and the man said: 'Now this, at last — bone from my bones, flesh from my flesh! — this shall be called woman , for from man was this taken.'  
**2:24** That is why a man leaves his father and mother and is united to his wife, and the two become one flesh.  
**2:25** Now they were both naked, the man and his wife, but they had no feeling of shame towards one another.  

## 3장

**3:1**  THE SERPENT WAS MORE CRAFTY than any wild creature that the LORD God had made. He said to the woman, 'Is it true that God has forbidden you to eat from any tree in the garden?'  
**3:2** The woman answered the serpent,'We may eat the fruit of any tree in the garden,  
**3:3** except for the tree in the middle of the garden; God has forbidden us either to eat or to touch the fruit of that; if we do, we shall die.'  
**3:4** The serpent said, 'Of course you will not die.  
**3:5** God knows that as soon as you eat it, your eyes will be opened and you will be like gods knowing both good and evil.'  
**3:6** When the woman saw that the fruit of the tree was good to eat, and that it was pleasing to the eye and tempting to contemplate, she took some and ate it. She also gave her husband some and he ate it.  
**3:7** Then the eyes of both of them were opened and they discovered that they were naked ; so they stitched fig-leaves together and made themselves loincloths.  
**3:8** The man and his wife heard the sound of the LORD God walking in the garden at the time of the evening breeze and hid from the LORD God among the trees of the garden.  
**3:9** But the LORD God called to the man and said to him, 'Where are you?'  
**3:10**  He replied, 'I heard the sound as you were walking in the garden, and I was afraid because I was naked, and I hid myself.'  
**3:11** God answered, 'Who told you that you were naked? Have you eaten from the tree which I forbade you ?'  
**3:12** The man said, 'The woman you gave me for a companion, she gave me fruit from the tree and I ate it.'  
**3:13** Then the LORD God said to the woman, 'What is this that you have done?' The woman said, 'The serpent tricked me, and I ate.'  
**3:14** Then the LORD God said to the serpent: 'Because you have done this you are accursed more than all cattle and all wild creatures. On your belly you shall crawl, and dust you shall eat all the days of your life.  
**3:15** I will put enmity between you and the woman, between your brood and hers. They shall strike at your head, and you shall strike at their heel.'  
**3:16**  To the woman he said: 'I will increase your labour and your groaning, and in labour you shall bear children. You shall be eager for your husband, and he shall be your master.'  
**3:17**  And to the man he said: 'Because you have listened to your wife and have eaten from the tree which I forbade you, accursed shall be the ground on your account. With labour you shall win your food from it all the days of your life.  
**3:18** It will grow thorns and thistles for you, none but wild plants for you to eat.  
**3:19** You shall gain your bread by the sweat of your brow until you return to the ground; for from it you were taken. Dust you are, to dust you shall return.'  
**3:20**  The man called his wife Eve because she was the mother of all who live.  
**3:21** The LORD God made tunics of skins for Adam and his wife and clothed them.  
**3:22** He said, 'The man has become like one of us, knowing good and evil; what if he now reaches out his hand and takes fruit from the tree of life also, eats it and lives for ever?'  
**3:23** So the LORD God drove him out of the garden of Eden to till the ground from which he had been taken.  
**3:24** He cast him out, and to the east of the garden of Eden he stationed the cherubim and a sword whirling and flashing to guard the way to the tree of life.  

## 4장

**4:1**  The man lay with his wife Eve, and she conceived and gave birth to Cain. She said, 'With the help of the LORD I have brought a man into being.'  
**4:2** Afterwards she had another child, his brother Abel. Abel was a shepherd and Cain a tiller of the soil.  
**4:3** The day came when Cain brought some of the produce of the soil as a gift to the LORD;  
**4:4** and Abel brought some of the first-born of his flock, the fat portions of them . The LORD received Abel and his gift with favour;  
**4:5** but Cain and his gift he did not receive. Cain was very angry and his face fell.  
**4:6** Then the LORD said to Cain, 'Why are you so angry and cast down?  
**4:7**  'If you do well, you are accepted; if not, sin is a demon crouching at the door. It shall be eager for you, and you will be mastered by it .'  
**4:8**  Cain said to his brother Abel, 'Let us go into the open country.' While they were there, Cain attacked his brother Abel and murdered him.  
**4:9** Then the LORD said to Cain, 'Where is your brother Abel?' Cain answered, 'I do not know. Am I my brother's keeper?'  
**4:10** The LORD said, 'What have you done? Hark! your brother's blood that has been shed is crying out to me from the ground.  
**4:11** Now you are accursed, and banished from the ground which has opened its mouth wide to receive your brother's blood, which you have shed.  
**4:12** When you till the ground, it will no longer yield you its wealth. You shall be a vagrant and a wanderer on earth.'  
**4:13** Cain said to the LORD, 'My punishment is heavier than I can bear;  
**4:14** thou hast driven me today from the ground, and I must hide myself from thy presence. I shall be a vagrant and a wanderer on earth, and anyone who meets me can kill me.'  
**4:15** The LORD answered him, 'No: if anyone kills Cain, Cain shall be avenged sevenfold.' So the LORD put a mark on Cain, in order that anyone meeting him should not kill him.  
**4:16** Then Cain went out from the LORD's presence and settled in the land of Nod to the east of Eden.  
**4:17**  Then Cain lay with his wife; and she conceived and bore Enoch. Cain was then building a city, which he named Enoch after his son.  
**4:18** Enoch begot Irad; Irad begot Mehujael; Mehuiael begot Methushael; Methushael begot Lamech.  
**4:19** Lamech married two wives, one named Adah and the other Zillah.  
**4:20** Adah bore Jabal who was the ancestor of herdsmen who live in tents;  
**4:21** and his brother's name was Jubal; he was the ancestor of those who play the harp and pipe.  
**4:22** Zillah, the other wife, bore Tubal-cain, the master of all coppersmiths and blacksmiths, and Tubal-cain's sister was Naamah.  
**4:23** Lamech said to his wives: 'Adah and Zillah, listen to me ; wives of Lamech, mark what I say: I kill a man for wounding me, a young man for a blow.  
**4:24** Cain may be avenged seven times, but Lamech seventy-seven.'  
**4:25**  Adam lay with his wife again. She bore a son, and named him Seth , 'for', she said, 'God has granted me another son in place of Abel, because Cain killed him.'  
**4:26** Seth too had a son, whom he named Enosh. At that time men began to invoke the LORD by name.  

## 5장

**5:1**  THIS IS THE RECORD of the descendants of Adam. On the day when God created man he made him in the likeness of God.  
**5:2** He created them male and female, and on the day when he created them, he blessed them and called them man.  
**5:3**  Adam was one hundred and thirty years old when he begot a son in his likeness and image, and named him Seth.  
**5:4** After the birth of Seth he lived eight hundred years, and had other sons and daughters.  
**5:5** He lived nine hundred and thirty years, and then he died.  
**5:6**  Seth was one hundred and five years old when he begot Enosh.  
**5:7** After the birth of Enosh he lived eight hundred and seven years, and had other sons and daughters.  
**5:8** He lived nine hundred and twelve years, and then he died.  
**5:9**  Enosh was ninety years old when he begot Kenan.  
**5:10** After the birth of Kenan he lived eight hundred and fifteen years, and had other sons and daughters.  
**5:11** He lived nine hundred and five years, and then he died.  
**5:12**  Kenan was seventy years old when he begot Mahalalel. .  
**5:13** After the birth of Mahalalel he lived eight hundred and forty years, and had other sons and daughters.  
**5:14** He lived nine hundred and ten years, and then he died.  
**5:15**  Mahalalel was sixty-five years old when he begot Jared.  
**5:16** After the birth of Jared he lived eight hundred and thirty years, and had other sons and daughters.  
**5:17** He lived eight hundred and ninety-five years, and then he died.  
**5:18**  Jared was one hundred and sixty-two years old when he begot Enoch.  
**5:19** After the birth of Enoch he lived eight hundred years, and had other sons and daughters.  
**5:20** He lived nine hundred and sixty-two years, and then he died.  
**5:21**  Enoch was sixty-five years old when he begot Methuselah.  
**5:22** After the birth of Methuselah, Enoch walked with God for three hundred years, and had other sons and daughters.  
**5:23** He lived three hundred and sixty-five years.  
**5:24** Having walked with God, Enoch was seen no more, because God had taken him away.  
**5:25**  Methuselah was one hundred and eighty-seven years old when he begot Lamech.  
**5:26** After the birth of Lamech he lived for seven hundred and eighty-two years, and had other sons and daughters.  
**5:27** He lived nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and then he died.  
**5:28**  Lamech was one hundred and eighty-two years old when he begot a son.  
**5:29** He named him Noah, saying, 'This boy will bring us relief from our work, and from the hard labour that has come upon us because of the LORD's curse upon the ground.'  
**5:30** After the birth of Noah, he lived for five hundred and ninety-five years, and had other sons and daughters.  
**5:31** Lamech lived seven hundred and seventy-seven years, and then he died.  
**5:32** Noah was five hundred years old when he begot Shem, Ham and Japheth.  

## 6장

**6:1**  WHEN MANKIND BEGAN TO INCREASE and to spread all over the earth and daughters were born to them,  
**6:2** the sons of the gods saw that the daughters of men were beautiful; so they took for themselves such women as they chose.  
**6:3** But the LORD said, 'My life-giving spirit shall not remain in man for ever; he for his part is mortal flesh: he shall live for a hundred and twenty years.'  
**6:4**  In those days , when the sons of the gods had intercourse with the daughters of men and got children by them, the Nephilim were on earth. They were the heroes of old, men of renown.  
**6:5**  When the LORD saw that man had done much evil on earth and that his thoughts and inclinations were always evil,  
**6:6** he was sorry that he had made man on earth, and he was grieved at heart.  
**6:7** He said, 'This race of men whom I have created, I will wipe them off the face of the earth—man and beast, reptiles and birds. I am sorry that I ever made them.'  
**6:8** But Noah had won the LORD's favour.  
**6:9**  This is the story of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, the one blameless man of his time; he walked with God.  
**6:10** He had three sons, Shem, Ham and Japeth.  
**6:11** Now God saw that the whole world was corrupt and full of violence.  
**6:12** In his sight the world had become corrupted, for all men had lived corrupt lives on earth.  
**6:13** God said to Noah, 'The loathsomeness of all mankind has become plain to me, for through them the earth is full of violence. I intend to destroy them, and the earth with them.  
**6:14** Make yourself an ark with ribs of cypress; cover it with reeds and coat it inside and out with pitch.  
**6:15** This is to be its plan: the length of the ark shall be three hundred cubits, its breadth fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits.  
**6:16** You shall make a roof for the ark, giving it a fall of one cubit when complete; and put a door in the side of the ark, and build three decks, upper, middle, and lower.  
**6:17**  I intend to bring the waters of the flood over the earth to destroy every human being that has the spirit of life; everything on earth shall perish.  
**6:18** But with you I will make a covenant, and you shall go into the ark, you and your sons, your wife and your sons' wives with you.  
**6:19** And you shall bring living creatures of every kind into the ark to keep them alive with you, two of each kind, a male and a female;  
**6:20** two of every kind of bird, beast, and reptile, shall come to you to be kept alive.  
**6:21** See that you take and store every kind of food that can be eaten; this shall be food for you and for them'.  
**6:22** Exactly as God had commanded him, so Noah did.  

## 7장

**7:1**  The LORD said to Noah, 'Go into the ark, you and all your household; for I have seen that you alone are righteous before me in this generation.  
**7:2** Take with you seven pairs, male and female, of all beasts that are ritually clean, and one pair, male and female, of all beasts that are not clean;  
**7:3** also seven pairs. male and female, of every bird—to ensure that life continues on earth.  
**7:4** In seven days' time I will send rain over the earth for forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe off the face of the earth every living thing that I have made.'  
**7:5** Noah did all that the LORD had commanded him.  
**7:6** He was six hundred years old when the waters of the flood came upon the earth.  
**7:7**  And so, to escape the waters of the flood, Noah went into the ark with his sons, his wife, and his sons' wives.  
**7:8** (8-9) And into the ark with Noah went one pair, male and female, of all beasts, clean and unclean, of birds and of everything that crawls on the ground, two by two, as God had commanded.  
**7:9**   
**7:10** Towards the end of seven days the waters of the flood came upon the earth.  
**7:11** In the year when Noah was six hundred years old, on the seventeenth day of the second month, on that very day, all the springs of the great abyss broke through, the windows of the sky were opened,  
**7:12** and rain fell on the earth for forty days and forty nights.  
**7:13** On that very day Noah entered the ark with his sons, Shem, Ham and Japheth, his own wife, and his three sons' wives.  
**7:14** Wild animals of every kind, cattle of every kind, reptiles of every kind that move upon the ground, and birds of every kind—  
**7:15** all came to Noah in the ark, two by two of all creatures that had life in them.  
**7:16** Those which came were one male and one female of all living things; they came in as God had commanded Noah, and the LORD closed the door on him.  
**7:17** The flood continued upon the earth for forty days, and the waters swelled and lifted up the ark so that it rose high above the ground.  
**7:18** They swelled and increased over the earth, and the ark floated on the surface of the waters.  
**7:19** More and more the waters increased over the earth until they covered all the high mountains everywhere under heaven.  
**7:20** The waters increased and the mountains were covered to a depth of fifteen cubits.  
**7:21** Every living creature that moves on earth perished, birds, cattle, wild animals, all reptiles, and all mankind.  
**7:22** Everything died that had the breath of life in its nostrils, everything on dry land.  
**7:23** God wiped out every living thing that existed on earth, man and beast, reptile and bird; they were all wiped out over the whole earth, and only Noah and his company in the ark survived.  

## 8장

**8:1**  (7:24) When the waters had increased over the earth for a hundred and fifty days, (8:1) God thought of Noah and all the wild animals and the cattle with him in the ark, and he made a wind pass over the earth, and the waters began to subside.  
**8:2** The springs of the abyss were stopped up, and so were the windows of the sky; the downpour from the skies was checked.  
**8:3** The water gradually receded from the earth, and by the end of a hundred and fifty days it had disappeared.  
**8:4** On the seventeenth day of the seventh month the ark grounded on a mountain in Ararat.  
**8:5** The water continued to recede until the tenth month, and on the first day of the tenth month the tops of the mountains could be seen.  
**8:6**  After forty days Noah opened the trap-door that he had made in the ark,  
**8:7** and released a raven to see whether the water had subsided, but the bird continued flying to and fro until the water on the earth had dried up.  
**8:8** Noah waited for seven days , and then he released a dove from the ark to see whether the water on the earth had subsided further.  
**8:9** But the dove found no place where she could settle, and so she came back to him in the ark, because there was water over the whole surface of the earth. Noah stretched out his hand, caught her and took her into the ark.  
**8:10** He waited another seven days and again released the dove from the ark.  
**8:11** She came back to him towards evening with a newly plucked olive leaf in her beak. Then Noah knew for certain that the water on the earth had subsided still further.  
**8:12** He waited yet another seven days and released the dove, but she never came back.  
**8:13** And so it came about that, on the first day of the first month of his six hundred and first year, the water had dried up on the earth, and Noah removed the hatch and looked out of the ark. The surface of the ground was dry.  
**8:14**  By the twenty-seventh day of the second month the whole earth was dry.  
**8:15** And God said to Noah,  
**8:16** 'Come out of the ark, you and your wife, your sons and their wives.  
**8:17** Bring out every living creature that is with you, live things of every kind, bird and beast and every reptile that moves on the ground, and let them swarm over the earth and be fruitful and increase there.'  
**8:18** So Noah came out with his sons, his wife, and his sons' wives.  
**8:19** Every wild animal, all cattle, every bird, and every reptile that moves on the ground, came out of the ark by families.  
**8:20** Then Noah built an altar to the LORD. He took ritually clean beasts and birds of every kind, and offered whole-offerings on the altar.  
**8:21** When the LORD smelt the soothing odour, he said within himself, 'Never again will I curse the ground because of man, however evil his inclinations may be from his youth upwards. I will never again kill every living creature, as I have just done.   
**8:22**  While the earth lasts seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night, shall never cease.'  

## 9장

**9:1**  GOD BLESSED NOAH and his sons and said to them, 'Be fruitful and increase and fill the earth.  
**9:2** The fear and dread of you shall fall upon all wild animals on earth, on all birds of heaven, on everything that moves upon the ground and all fish in the sea; they are given into your hands.  
**9:3** Every creature that lives and moves shall be food for you; I give you them all, as once I gave you all green plants.  
**9:4** But you must not eat the flesh with the life, which is the blood, still in it.  
**9:5** And further, for your life-blood I will demand satisfaction; from every animal I will require it, and from a man also I will require satisfaction for the death of his fellow-man.   
**9:6**  He that sheds the blood of a man, for that man his blood shall be shed; for in the image of God has God made man.  
**9:7**  But you must be fruitful and increase, swarm throughout the earth and rule over it.  
**9:8**  God spoke to Noah and to his sons with him:  
**9:9** 'I now make my covenant with you and with your descendants after you,  
**9:10** and with every living creature that is with you, all birds and cattle, all the wild animals with you on earth, all that have come out of the ark.  
**9:11** I will make my covenant with you: never again shall all living creatures be destroyed by the waters of the flood, never again shall there be a flood to lay waste the earth.'  
**9:12**  God said, 'This is the sign of the covenant which I establish between myself and you and every living creature with you, to endless generations:  
**9:13**  My bow I set in the cloud, sign of the covenant between myself and earth.  
**9:14** When I cloud the sky over the earth, the bow shall be seen in the cloud.  
**9:15**  Then will I remember the covenant which I have made between myself and you and living things of every kind. Never again shall the waters become a flood to destroy all living creatures.  
**9:16** The bow shall be in the cloud; when I see it, it will remind me of the everlasting covenant between God and living things on earth of every kind.'  
**9:17** God said to Noah, 'This is the sign of the covenant which I make between myself and all that lives on earth.'  
**9:18**  The sons of Noah who came out of the ark were Shem, Ham and Japheth; Ham was the father of Canaan.  
**9:19** These three were the sons of Noah, and their descendants spread over the whole earth.  
**9:20**  Noah, a man of the soil, began the planting of vineyards.  
**9:21** He drank some of the wine, became drunk and lay naked inside his tent.  
**9:22** When Ham, father of Canaan, saw his father naked, he told his two brothers outside.  
**9:23** So Shem and Japheth took a cloak, put it on their shoulders and walked backwards, and so covered their father's naked body; their faces were turned the other way, so that they did not see their father naked.  
**9:24** When Noah woke from his drunken sleep, he learnt what his youngest son had done to him,  
**9:25** and said: 'Cursed be Canaan, slave of slaves shall he be to his brothers.'  
**9:26**  And he continued: 'Bless, O LORD, the tents of Shem; may Canaan be his slave.  
**9:27** May God extend Japheth's bounds, let him dwell in the tents of Shem, may Canaan be their slave.'  
**9:28**  After the flood Noah lived for three hundred and fifty years,  
**9:29** and he was nine hundred and fifty years old when he died.

**50:18** His brothers also wept and prostrated themselves before him; they said, 'You see, we are your slaves.'  
**50:19** But Joseph said to them, 'Do not be afraid. Am I in the place of God?  
**50:20** You meant to do me harm; but God meant to bring good out of it by preserving the lives of many people, as we see today.  
**50:21** Do not be afraid. I will provide for you and your dependants.' Thus he comforted them and set their minds at rest.  
**50:22**  Joseph remained in Egypt, he and his father's household. He lived there to be a hundred and ten years old  
**50:23** and saw Ephraim's children to the third generation; he also recognized as his the children of Manasseh's son Machir.  
**50:24** He said to his brothers, 'I am dying; but God will not fail to come to your aid and take you from here to the land which he promised on oath to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob.'  
**50:25** He made the sons of Israel take an oath, saying, 'When God thus comes to your aid, you must take my bones with you from here.'  
**50:26** So Joseph died at the age of a hundred and ten. He was embalmed and laid in a coffin in Egypt.
