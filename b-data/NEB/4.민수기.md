# 4.민수기 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  ON THE FIRST DAY OF THE SECOND MONTH in the second year after the Israelites came out of Egypt, the LORD spoke to Moses at the Tent of the Presence in the wilderness of Sinai in these words:  
**1:2** 'Number the whole community of Israel by families in the father's line, recording the name of every male person  
**1:3** aged twenty years and upwards fit for military service. You and Aaron are to make a detailed list of them by their tribal hosts,  
**1:4** and you shall have to assist you one head of family from each tribe.  
**1:5** These are their names: <br>of Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur;   
**1:6** of Simeon, Shelurniel son of Zurishaddai;   
**1:7** of Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab;   
**1:8** of Issachar, Nethaneel son of Zuar;   
**1:9** of Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon;   
**1:10** of Joseph: of Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud; <br>of Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur;   
**1:11** of Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni;   
**1:12** of Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;   
**1:13** of Asher, Pagiel son of Ocran;   
**1:14** of Gad, Eliasaph son of Reuel;   
**1:15** of Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan.'   
**1:16** These were the conveners of the whole community, chiefs of their fathers' tribes and heads of Israelite clans.  
**1:17** So Moses and Aaron took these men who had been indicated by name.  
**1:18** They summoned the whole community on the first day of the second month, and they registered their descent by families in the father's line, recording every male person aged twenty years and upwards,  
**1:19** as the LORD had told Moses to do. Thus it was that he drew up the detailed lists in the wilderness of Sinai:   
**1:20** The tribal list of Reuben, Israel's eldest son, by families in the father's line, with the name of every male person aged twenty years and upwards fit for service,  
**1:21** the number in the list of the tribe of Reuben being forty-six thousand five hundred.   
**1:22** The tribal list of Simeon, by families in the father's line, with the name of every male person aged twenty years and upwards fit for service,  
**1:23** the number in the list of the tribe of Simeon being fifty-nine thousand three hundred.   
**1:24** The tribal list of Gad, by families in the father's line, with the names of all men aged twenty years and upwards fit for service,  
**1:25** the number in the list of the tribe of Gad being forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.   
**1:26** The tribal list of Judah, by families in the father's line, with the names of all men aged twenty years and upwards fit for service,  
**1:27** the number in the list of the tribe of Judah being seventy-four thousand six hundred.   
**1:28** The tribal list of Issachar, by families in the father's line, with the names of all men aged twenty years and upwards fit for service,  
**1:29** the number in the list of the tribe of Issachar being fifty-four thousand four hundred.   
**1:30** The tribal list of Zebulun, by families in the father's line, with the names of all men aged twenty years and upwards fit for service,  
**1:31** the number in the list of the tribe of Zebulun being fifty-seven thousand four hundred.   
**1:32** The tribal lists of Joseph: that of Ephraim, by families in the father's line, with the names of all men aged twenty years and upwards fit for service,  
**1:33** the number in the list of the tribe of Ephraim being forty thousand five hundred;  
**1:34** that of Manasseh, by families in the father's line, with the names of all men aged twenty years and upwards fit for service,  
**1:35** the number in the list of the tribe of Manasseh being thirty-two thousand two hundred.   
**1:36** The tribal list of Benjamin, by families in the father's line, with the names of all men aged twenty years and upwards fit for service,  
**1:37** the number in the list of the tribe of Benjamin being thirty-five thousand four hundred.   
**1:38** The tribal list of Dan, by families in the father's line, with the names of all men aged twenty years and upwards fit for service,  
**1:39** the number in the list of the tribe of Dan being sixty-two thousand seven hundred.   
**1:40** The tribal list of Asher, by families in the father's line, with the names of all men aged twenty years and upwards fit for service,  
**1:41** the number in the list of the tribe of Asher being forty-one thousand five hundred.   
**1:42** The tribal list of Naphtali, by families in the father's line, with the names of all men aged twenty years and upwards fit for service,  
**1:43** the number in the list of the tribe of Naphtali being fifty-three thousand four hundred.   
**1:44** These were the numbers recorded in the detailed lists by Moses and Aaron and the twelve chiefs of Israel, each representing one tribe and being the head of a family.  
**1:45** The total number of Israelites aged twenty years and upwards fit for service, recorded in the lists of fathers' families,  
**1:46** was six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.  
**1:47** A list of the Levites by their fathers' families was not made.   
**1:48** The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**1:49** 'You shall not record the total number of the Levites or make a detailed list of them among the Israelites.  
**1:50** You shall put the Levites in charge of the Tabernacle of the Tokens, with its equipment and everything in it. They shall carry the Tabernacle and all its equipment; they alone shall be its attendants and shall pitch their tents round it.  
**1:51** The Levites shall take the Tabernacle down when it is due to move and shall put it up when it halts; any unqualified person who comes near it shall be put to death.  
**1:52** All other Israelites shall pitch their tents, each tribal host in its proper camp and under its own standard.  
**1:53** But the Levites shall encamp round the Tabernacle of the Tokens, so that divine wrath may not follow the whole community of Israel; the Tabernacle of the Tokens shall be in their keeping.'   
**1:54** The Israelites did exactly as the LORD had told Moses to do.

## 3장

**3:1**  THESE WERE THE DESCENDANTS OF AARON and Moses at the time when the LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai.  
**3:2** The names of the sons of Aaron were Nadab the eldest, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.  
**3:3** These were the names of Aaron's sons, the anointed priests who had been installed in the priestly office.  
**3:4** Nadab and Abihu fell dead before the LORD because they had presented illicit fire before the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai. They left no sons; Eleazar and Ithamar continued to perform the priestly office in their father's presence.   
**3:5**  The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**3:6** 'Bring forward the tribe of Levi and appoint them to serve Aaron the priest and to minister to him.  
**3:7** They shall be in attendance on him and on the whole community before the Tent of the Presence, undertaking the service of the Tabernacle.  
**3:8** They shall be in charge of all the equipment in the Tent of the Presence, and be in attendance on the Israelites, undertaking the service of the Tabernacle.  
**3:9** You shall assign the Levites to Aaron and his sons as especially dedicated to him out of all the Israelites.  
**3:10** To Aaron and his line you shall commit the priestly office and they shall perform its duties; any unqualified person who intrudes upon it shall be put to death.'

**3:12** 'I take the Levites for myself out of all the Israelites as a substitute for the eldest male child of every woman; the Levites shall be mine.

## 5장

**5:1**  THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES and said:  
**5:2** Command the Israelites to expel from the camp everyone who suffers from a malignant skin-disease or a discharge, and everyone ritually unclean from contact with a corpse.  
**5:3** You shall put them outside the camp, both male and female, so that they will not defile your camps in which I dwell among you.  
**5:4** The Israelites did this: they put them outside the camp. As the LORD had said when he spoke to Moses, so the Israelites did.   
**5:5**  The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**5:6** Say to the Israelites: When anyone, man or woman, wrongs another and thereby breaks faith with the LORD, that person has incurred guilt which demands reparation.  
**5:7** He shall confess the sin he has committed, make restitution in full with the addition of one fifth, and give it to the man to whom compensation is due.  
**5:8** If there is no next-of-kin to whom compensation can be paid, the compensation payable in that case shall be the LORD's, for the use of the priest, in addition to the ram of expiation with which the priest makes expiation for him.   
**5:9** Every contribution made by way of holy-gift which the Israelites bring to the priest shall be the priest's.  
**5:10** The priest shall have the holy-gifts which a man gives; whatever is given to him shall be his.   
**5:11**  The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**5:12** Speak to the Israelites in these words: When a married woman goes astray, is unfaithful to her husband, and has sexual intercourse with another man,  
**5:13** and this happens without the husband's knowledge, and the crime is undetected, because, though she has been defiled, there is no direct evidence against her and she was not caught in the act,  
**5:14** but when in such a case a fit of jealousy comes over the husband which causes him to suspect his wife, she being in fact defiled, or when, on the other hand, a fit of jealousy comes over a husband which causes him to suspect his wife, when she is not in fact defiled,  
**5:15** then in either case, the husband shall bring his wife to the priest together with the prescribed offering for her, a tenth of an ephah of barley meal. He shall not pour oil on it nor put frankincense on it, because it is a grain-offering for jealousy, a grain-offering of protestation conveying an imputation of guilt.  
**5:16** The priest shall bring her forward and set her before the LORD.  
**5:17** He shall take clean water in an earthenware vessel, and shall take dust from the floor of the Tabernacle and add it to the water.  
**5:18** He shall set the woman before the LORD, uncover her head, and place the grain-offering of protestation in her hands; it is a grain-offering for jealousy. The priest shall hold in his own hand the water of contention which brings out the truth.  
**5:19** He shall then put the woman on oath and say to her, 'If no man has had intercourse with you, if you have not gone astray and let yourself become defiled while owing obedience to your husband, then may your innocence be established by the water of contention which brings out the truth.  
**5:20** But if, while owing him obedience, you have gone astray and let yourself become de?led, if any man other than your husband has had intercourse with you'  
**5:21** (the priest shall here put the woman on oath with an adjuration, and shall continue), 'may the LORD make an example of you among your people in adjurations and in swearing of oaths by bringing upon you miscarriage and untimely birth;  
**5:22** and this water that brings out the truth shall enter your body, bringing upon you miscarriage and untimely birth.' The woman shall respond, 'Amen, Amen.'  
**5:23** The priest shall write these curses on a scroll and wash them off into the water of contention;  
**5:24** he shall make the woman drink the water that brings out the truth, and the water shall enter her body.  
**5:25** The priest shall take the grain-offering for jealousy from the woman's hand, present it as a special gift before the LORD, and offer it at the altar.  
**5:26** He shall take a handful from the grain-offering by way of token, and burn it at the altar; after this he shall make the woman drink the water.  
**5:27** If she has let herself become defiled and has been unfaithful to her husband, then when the priest makes her drink the water that brings out the truth and the water has entered her body, she will suffer a miscarriage or untimely birth, and her name will become an example in adjuration among her kin.  
**5:28** But if the woman has not let herself become defiled and is pure, then her innocence is established and she will bear her child.   
**5:29** Such is the law for cases of jealousy, where a woman, owing obedience to her husband, goes astray and lets herself become defiled,  
**5:30** or where a fit of jealousy comes over a man which causes him to suspect his wife. He shall set her before the LORD, and the priest shall deal with her as this law prescribes.  
**5:31** No guilt will attach to the husband, but the woman shall bear the penalty of her guilt.  

## 6장

**6:1**  The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**6:2** Speak to the Israelites in these words: When anyone, man or woman, makes a special vow dedicating himself to the LORD as a Nazirite ,  
**6:3** he shall abstain from wine and strong drink. These he shall not drink, nor anything made from the juice of grapes; nor shall he eat grapes, fresh or dried.  
**6:4** During the whole term of his vow he shall eat nothing that comes from the vine, nothing whatever, shoot or berry.  
**6:5** During the whole term of his vow no razor shall touch his head; he shall let his hair grow long and plait it until he has completed the term of his dedication: he shall keep himself holy to the LORD.  
**6:6** During the whole term of his vow he shall not go near a corpse,

**19:1**  THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES and Aaron and said:

**19:8** The man who burnt the cow shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, but he also remains unclean till sunset.  
**19:9** Then a man who is clean shall collect the ashes of the cow and deposit them outside the camp in a clean place. They shall be reserved for use by the Israelite community in the water of ritual puri?cation; for the cow is a sin-offering.  
**19:10** The man who collected the ashes of the cow shall wash his clothes, but he remains unclean till sunset. This rule shall be binding for all time on the Israelites and on the alien who is living with them.   
**19:11**  Whoever touches a corpse shall be ritually unclean for seven days.  
**19:12** He shall get himself purified with the water of ritual purification on the third day and on the seventh day, and then he shall be clean. If he is not purified both on the third day and on the seventh, he shall not be clean.  
**19:13** Everyone who touches a corpse, that is the body of a man who has died, and does not purify himself, defiles the Tabernacle of the LORD. That person shall be cut off from Israel. The water of purification has not been flung over him; he remains unclean, and his impurity is still upon him.

**19:15** and every open vessel which has no covering tied over it shall also be unclean. In the open,  
**19:16** anyone who touches a man killed with a weapon or one who has died naturally, or who touches a human bone or a grave, shall be unclean for seven days.  
**19:17** For such uncleanness, they shall take some of the ash from the burnt mass of the sin-offering and add fresh water to it in a vessel.  
**19:18** Then a man who is clean shall take marjoram, dip it in the water, and sprinkle the tent with all the vessels in it and all the people who were there, or the man who has touched a human bone, a corpse (whether the man was killed or died naturally), or a grave.  
**19:19** The man who is clean shall sprinkle the unclean man on the third day and on the seventh; on the seventh day he shall purify him; then the man shall wash his clothes and bathe in water, and at sunset he shall be clean.  
**19:20** If a man who is unclean does not get himself purified, that person shall be cut off from the assembly, because he has defiled the sanctuary of the LORD. The water of purification has not been flung over him: he is unclean.  
**19:21** This rule shall be binding on you for all time. The man who sprinkles the water of purification shall also wash his clothes, and whoever touches the water shall be unclean till sunset.  
**19:22** Whatever the unclean man touches shall be unclean, and any person who touches that shall be unclean till sunset.

## 30장

**30:1**  THEN MOSES SPOKE to the heads of the Israelite tribes and said, This is the LORD's command:  
**30:2** When a man makes a vow to the LORD or swears an oath and so puts himself under a binding obligation, he must not break his word. Every word he has spoken, he must make good.  
**30:3** When a woman, still young and living in her father's house, makes a vow to the LORD or puts herself under a binding obligation,  
**30:4** if her father hears of it and keeps silence, then any such vow or obligation shall be valid.  
**30:5** But if her father disallows it when he hears of it, none of her vows or obligations shall be valid; the LORD will absolve her, because her father has disallowed it.  
**30:6** If the woman is married when she is under a vow or a binding obligation rashly uttered,  
**30:7** then if her husband hears of it and keeps silence when he hears, her vow or obligation by which she has bound herself shall be valid.  
**30:8** If, however, her husband disallows it when he hears of it and repudiates the vow which she has taken upon herself or the rash utterance with which she has bound herself, then the LORD will absolve her.  
**30:9** Every vow by which a widow or a divorced woman has bound herself shall be valid.  
**30:10** But if it is in her husband's house that a woman makes a vow or puts herself under a binding obligation by an oath,  
**30:11** and her husband, hearing of it, keeps silence and does not disallow it, then every vow and obligation under which she has put herself shall be valid;  
**30:12** but if her husband clearly repudiates them when he hears of them, then nothing that she has uttered, whether vow or obligation, shall be valid. Her husband has repudiated them, and the LORD will absolve her.   
**30:13** The husband can confirm or repudiate any vow or oath by which a woman binds herself to mortification.  
**30:14** If he maintains silence day after day, he thereby confirms every vow or obligation under which she has put herself: he confirms them, because he kept silence at the time when he heard them.

**35:12** These cities shall be places of refuge from the vengeance of the dead man's next-of-kin, so that the homicide shall not be put to death without standing his trial before the community.  
**35:13** The cities appointed as places of refuge shall be six in number,  
**35:14** three east of the Jordan and three in Canaan.

**35:18** If a man has a wooden thing in his hand capable of causing death, and strikes another man and he dies, he is a murderer: the murderer must be put to death.
