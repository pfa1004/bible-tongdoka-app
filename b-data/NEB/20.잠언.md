# 20.잠언 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  The proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel,   
**1:2** by which men will come to wisdom and instruction <br>and will understand words that bring understanding,   
**1:3** and by which they will gain a well-instructed intelligence, <br>righteousness, justice, and probity.   
**1:4** The simple will be endowed with shrewdness <br>and the young with knowledge and prudence.   
**1:5** If the wise man listens, he will increase his learning, <br>and the man of understanding will acquire skill   
**1:6** to understand proverbs and parables, <br>the sayings of wise men and their riddles.   
**1:7**  The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge, <br>but fools scorn wisdom and discipline.   
**1:8** Attend, my son, to your father's instruction <br>and do not reject the teaching of your mother;   
**1:9** for they are a garland of grace on your head <br>and a chain of honour round your neck.   
**1:10** My son, bad men may tempt you   
**1:11** and say, <br>'Come with us; let us lie in wait for someone's blood; <br>let us waylay an innocent man who has done us no harm.   
**1:12** Like Sheol we will swallow them alive; <br>though blameless, they shall be like men who go down to the abyss.   
**1:13** We shall take rich treasure of every sort <br>and fill our homes with booty;   
**1:14** throw in your lot with us, <br>and we will have a common purse.'   
**1:15** My son, do not go along with them, <br>keep clear of their ways;   
**1:16** they hasten hot-foot into crime, <br>impatient to shed blood.   
**1:17** In vain is a net spread wide <br>if any bird that flies can see it.   
**1:18** These men lie in wait for their own blood <br>and waylay no one but themselves.   
**1:19** This is the fate of men eager for ill-gotten gain: <br>it robs those who get it of their lives.   
**1:20**  Wisdom cries aloud in the open air, <br>she raises her voice in public places;   
**1:21** she calls at the top of the busy street <br>and proclaims at the open gates of the city:   
**1:22** 'Simple fools, how long will you be content with your simplicity? <br>   
**1:23** If only you would respond to my reproof, <br>I would give you my counsel <br>and teach you my precepts.   
**1:24** But because you refused to listen when I called, <br>because no one attended when I stretched out my hand,   
**1:25** because you spurned all my advice <br>and would have nothing to do with my reproof,   
**1:26** I in my turn will laugh at your doom <br>and deride you when terror comes upon you,   
**1:27** when terror comes upon you like a hurricane <br>and your doom descends like a whirlwind . <br>Insolent men delight in their insolence; <br>stupid men hate knowledge. <br>   
**1:28** When they call upon me, I will not answer them; <br>when they search for me, they shall not find me.   
**1:29** Because they hate knowledge <br>and have not chosen to fear the LORD,   
**1:30** because they have not accepted my counsel <br>and have spurned all my reproof,   
**1:31** they shall eat the fruits of their behaviour <br>and have a surfeit of their own devices;   
**1:32** for the simpleton turns a deaf ear and comes to grief, <br>and the stupid are ruined by their own complacency.   
**1:33** But whoever listens to me shall live without a care, <br>undisturbed by fear of misfortune.'  

## 2장

**2:1**  My son, if you take my words to heart <br>and lay up my commands in your mind,   
**2:2** giving your attention to wisdom <br>and your mind to understanding,   
**2:3** if you summon discernment to your aid <br>and invoke understanding,   
**2:4** if you seek her out like silver <br>and dig for her like buried treasure,   
**2:5** then you will understand the fear of the LORD <br>and attain to the knowledge of God,   
**2:6** for the LORD bestows wisdom <br>and teaches knowledge and understanding.   
**2:7** Out of his store he endows the upright with ability <br>as a shield for those who live blameless lives;   
**2:8** for he guards the course of justice <br>and keeps watch over the way of his loyal servants.   
**2:9** Then you will understand what is right and just <br>and keep only to the good man's path;   
**2:10** for wisdom will sink into your mind, <br>and knowledge will be your heart's delight.   
**2:11** Prudence will keep watch over you, <br>understanding will guard you,   
**2:12** it will save you from evil ways <br>and from men whose talk is subversive,   
**2:13** who forsake the honest course <br>to walk in ways of darkness,   
**2:14** who rejoice in doing evil <br>and exult in evil and subversive acts,   
**2:15** whose own ways are crooked, <br>whose tracks are devious.   
**2:16** It will save you from the adulteress, <br>from the loose woman with her seductive words,   
**2:17** who forsakes the teaching of her childhood <br>and has forgotten the covenant of her God;   
**2:18** for her path runs downhill towards death, <br>and her course is set for the land of the dead.   
**2:19** No one who resorts to her finds his way back <br>or regains the path to life.   
**2:20** See then that you follow the footsteps of good men <br>and keep to the course of the righteous;   
**2:21** for the upright shall dwell on earth <br>and blameless men remain there;   
**2:22** but the wicked shall be uprooted from it <br>and traitors weeded out.  

## 3장

**3:1**  My son, do not forget my teaching, <br>but guard my commands in your heart;   
**3:2** for long life and years in plenty <br>will they bring you, and prosperity as well.   
**3:3** Let your good faith and loyalty never fail, <br>but bind them about your neck.   
**3:4** Thus will you win favour and success <br>in the sight of God and man.   
**3:5** Put all your trust in the LORD <br>and do not rely on your own understanding.   
**3:6** Think of him in all your ways, <br>and he will smooth your path.   
**3:7** Do not think how wise you are, <br>but fear the LORD and turn from evil.   
**3:8** Let that be the medicine to keep you in health, <br>the liniment for your limbs.   
**3:9** Honour the LORD with your wealth <br>as the first charge on all your earnings;   
**3:10** then your granaries will be filled with corn <br>and your vats bursting with new wine.   
**3:11** My son, do not spurn the LORD's correction <br>or take offence at his reproof;   
**3:12** for those whom he loves the LORD reproves, <br>and he punishes a favourite son.   
**3:13** Happy he who has found wisdom, <br>and the man who has acquired understanding;   
**3:14** for wisdom is more profitable than silver, <br>and the gain she brings is better than gold.   
**3:15** She is more precious than red coral, <br>and all your jewels are no match for her.   
**3:16** Long life is in her right hand, <br>in her left hand are riches and honour.   
**3:17** Her ways are pleasant ways <br>and all her paths lead to prosperity.   
**3:18** She is a staff of life to all who grasp her, <br>and those who hold her fast are safe.   
**3:19** In wisdom the LORD founded the earth <br>and by understanding he set the heavens in their place;   
**3:20** by his knowledge the depths burst forth <br>and the clouds dropped dew.   
**3:21** My son, keep watch over your ability and prudence, <br>do not let them slip from sight;   
**3:22** they shall be a charm hung about your neck <br>and an ornament on your breast.   
**3:23** Then you will go your way without a care, <br>and your feet will not stumble.   
**3:24** When you sit, you need have no fear; <br>when you lie down, your sleep will be pleasant.   
**3:25** Do not be afraid when fools are frightened <br>or when ruin comes upon the wicked;   
**3:26** for the LORD will be at your side, <br>and he will keep your feet clear of the trap.   
**3:27** Refuse no man any favour that you owe him <br>when it lies in your power to pay it.   
**3:28** Do not say to your friend, 'Come back again; <br>you shall have it tomorrow'—when you have it already.   
**3:29** Plot no evil against your friend, <br>your unsuspecting neighbour.   
**3:30** Do not pick a quarrel with a man for no reason, <br>if he has not done you a bad turn.   
**3:31** Do not emulate a lawless man, <br>do not choose to follow his footsteps;   
**3:32** for one who is not straight is detestable to the LORD, <br>but upright men are in God's confidence.   
**3:33** The LORD's curse rests on the house of the evildoer, <br>while he blesses the home of the righteous.   
**3:34** Though God himself meets the arrogant with arrogance, <br>yet he bestows his favour on the meek .   
**3:35** Wise men are adorned with honour, <br>but the coat on a fool's back is contempt.  

## 4장

**4:1**  Listen, my sons, to a father's instruction, <br>consider attentively how to gain understanding;   
**4:2** for it is sound learning I give you; <br>so do not forsake my teaching.   
**4:3** I too have been a father's son, <br>tender in years, my mother's only child.   
**4:4** He taught me and said to me: <br>Hold fast to my words with all your heart, <br>keep my commands and you will have life.   
**4:5** Do not forget or turn a deaf ear to what I say.   
**4:6**   
**4:7** The first thing is to acquire wisdom; <br>gain understanding though it cost you all you have. <br>(6) Do not forsake her, and she will keep you safe; <br>love her, and she will guard you;   
**4:8** cherish her, and she will lift you high; <br>if only you embrace her, she will bring you to honour.   
**4:9** She will set a garland of grace on your head <br>and bestow on you a crown of glory.   
**4:10** Listen, my son, take my words to heart, <br>and the years of your life shall be multiplied.   
**4:11** I will guide you in the paths of wisdom <br>and lead you in honest ways.   
**4:12** As you walk you will not slip, <br>and, if you run, nothing will bring you down.   
**4:13** Cling to instruction and never let it go; <br>observe it well, for it is your life.   
**4:14** Do not take to the course of the wicked <br>or follow the way of evil men;   
**4:15** do not set foot on it, but avoid it; <br>turn aside and go on your way.   
**4:16** For they cannot sleep unless they have done some wrong; <br>unless they have been someone's downfall they lose their sleep.   
**4:17** The bread they eat is the fruit of crime <br>and they drink wine got by violence.   
**4:18** The course of the righteous is like morning light, <br>growing brighter till it is broad day;   
**4:19** but the ways of the wicked are like darkness at night, <br>and they do not know what has been their downfall.   
**4:20** My son, attend to my speech, <br>pay heed to my words;   
**4:21** do not let them slip out of your mind, <br>keep them close in your heart;   
**4:22** for they are life to him who finds them, <br>and health to his whole body.   
**4:23** Guard your heart more than any treasure, <br>for it is the source of all life.   
**4:24** Keep your mouth from crooked speech <br>and your lips from deceitful talk.   
**4:25** Let your eyes look straight before you, <br>fix your gaze upon what lies ahead.   
**4:26** Look out for the path that your feet must take, <br>and your ways will be secure.   
**4:27** Swerve neither to right nor left, <br>and keep clear of every evil thing.  

## 5장

**5:1**  My son, attend to my wisdom <br>and listen to my good counsel,   
**5:2** so that you may observe proper prudence <br>and your speech be informed with knowledge.   
**5:3** For though the lips of an adulteress drip honey <br>and her tongue is smoother than oil,   
**5:4** yet in the end she is more bitter than wormwood, <br>and sharp as a two-edged sword.   
**5:5** Her feet go downwards on the path to death, <br>her course is set for Sheol.   
**5:6** She does not watch for the road that leads to life; <br>her course turns this way and that, and what does she care?   
**5:7** Now, my son, listen to me <br>and do not ignore What I say:   
**5:8** keep well away from her <br>and do not go near the door of her house;   
**5:9** or you will lose your dignity in the eyes of others <br>and your honour before strangers;   
**5:10** strangers will batten on your wealth, <br>and your hard-won gains pass to another man's family.   
**5:11** The end will be that you will starve, <br>you will shrink to mere skin and bones.   
**5:12** Then you will say, 'Why did I hate correction <br>and set my heart against reproof?   
**5:13** I did not listen to the voice of my teachers <br>or pay attention to my masters.   
**5:14** I soon earned a bad name <br>and was despised in the public assembly.'   
**5:15** Drink water from your own cistern <br>and running water from your own spring;   
**5:16** do not let your well overflow into the road, <br>your runnels of water pour into the street;   
**5:17** let them be yours alone, <br>not shared with strangers.   
**5:18** Let your fountain, the wife of your youth, <br>be blessed, rejoice in her,   
**5:19** a lovely doe, a graceful hind, let her be your companion; <br>you will at all times be bathed in her love, <br>and her love will continually wrap you round. <br>(22) <br>Wherever you turn, she will guide you; <br>when you lie in bed, she will watch over you, <br>and when you wake she will talk with you.   
**5:20** Why, my son, are you wrapped up in the love of an adulteress? <br>Why do you embrace a loose woman?   
**5:21** For a man's ways are always in the LORD's sight <br>who watches for every path that he must take.   
**5:22** The wicked man is caught in his own iniquities <br>and held fast in the toils of his own sin;   
**5:23** he will perish for want of discipline, <br>wrapped in the shroud of his boundless folly.  

## 6장

**6:1**  My son, if you pledge yourself to another man <br>and stand surety for a stranger,   
**6:2** if you are caught by your promise, <br>trapped by some promise you have made,   
**6:3** do what I now tell you <br>and save yourself, my son: <br>when you fall into another man's power, <br>bestir yourself, go and pester the man,   
**6:4** give yourself no rest, <br>allow yourself no sleep.   
**6:5** Save yourself like a gazelle from the toils, <br>like a bird from the grasp of the fowler.   
**6:6** Go to the ant, you sluggard, <br>watch her ways and get wisdom.   
**6:7** She has no overseer, <br>no governor or ruler;   
**6:8** but in summer she prepares her store of food <br>and lays in her supplies at harvest.   
**6:9** How long, you sluggard, will you lie abed? <br>When will you rouse yourself from sleep?   
**6:10** A little sleep, a little slumber, <br>a little folding of the hands in rest,   
**6:11** and poverty will come upon you like a robber, <br>want like a ruffian.   
**6:12** A scoundrel, a mischievous man, is he <br>who prowls about with crooked talk—   
**6:13** a wink of the eye, <br>a touch with the foot, <br>a sign with the fingers.   
**6:14** Subversion is the evil that he is plotting, <br>he stirs up quarrels all the time.   
**6:15** Down comes disaster suddenly upon him; <br>suddenly he is broken beyond all remedy.   
**6:16** Six things the LORD hates, <br>seven things are detestable to him:   
**6:17** a proud eye, a false tongue, <br>hands that shed innocent blood,   
**6:18** a heart that forges thoughts of mischief, <br>and feet that run swiftly to do evil,   
**6:19** a false witness telling a pack of lies, <br>and one who stirs up quarrels between brothers.   
**6:20**  My son, observe your father's commands <br>and do not reject the teaching of your mother;   
**6:21** wear them always next your heart <br>and bind them close about your neck;   
**6:22**   
**6:23** for a command is a lamp, and teaching a light, <br>reproof and correction point the way of life,   
**6:24** to keep you from the wife of another man, <br>from the seductive tongue of the loose woman.   
**6:25** Do not desire her beauty in your heart <br>or let her glance provoke you;   
**6:26** for a prostitute can be had for the price of a loaf, <br>but a married woman is out for bigger game.   
**6:27** Can a man kindle fire in his bosom <br>without burning his clothes?   
**6:28** If a man walks on hot coals, <br>will his feet not be scorched?   
**6:29** So is he who sleeps with his neighbour's wife; <br>no one can touch such a woman and go free.   
**6:30** Is not a thief contemptible when he steals <br>to satisfy his appetite, even if he is hungry?   
**6:31** And, if he is caught, must he not pay seven times over <br>and surrender all that his house contains?   
**6:32** So one who commits adultery is a senseless fool: <br>he dishonours the woman and ruins himself;   
**6:33** he will get nothing but blows and contumely <br>and will never live down the disgrace;   
**6:34** for a husband's anger is a jealous anger <br>and in the day of vengeance he will show no mercy;   
**6:35** compensation will not buy his forgiveness ; <br>no bribe, however large, will purchase his connivance.  

## 7장

**7:1** My son, keep my words, <br>store up my commands in your mind.   
**7:2** Keep my commands if you would live, <br>and treasure my teaching as the apple of your eye.   
**7:3** Wear them like a ring on your finger; <br>write them on the tablet of your memory.   
**7:4** Call Wisdom your sister,. <br>greet Understanding as a familiar friend;   
**7:5** then they will save you from the adulteress, <br>from the loose woman with her seductive words.   
**7:6**  I glanced out of the window of my house, <br>I looked down through the lattice,   
**7:7** and I saw among simple youths, <br>there amongst the boys I noticed <br>a lad, a foolish lad,   
**7:8** passing along the street, at the corner, <br>stepping out in the direction of her house   
**7:9** at twilight, as the day faded, <br>at dusk as the night grew dark;   
**7:10** suddenly a woman came to meet him, <br>dressed like a prostitute, full of wiles,   
**7:11** flighty and inconstant, <br>a woman never content to stay at home,   
**7:12** lying in wait at every corner, <br>now in the street, now in the public squares.   
**7:13** She caught hold of him and kissed him; <br>brazenly she accosted him and said,   
**7:14** 'I have had a sacrifice, an offering, to make <br>and I have paid my vows today;   
**7:15** that is why I have come out to meet you, <br>to watch for you and find you.   
**7:16** I have spread coverings on my bed <br>of coloured linen from Egypt.   
**7:17** I have sprinkled my bed with myrrh, <br>my clothes with aloes and cassia.   
**7:18** Come! Let us drown ourselves in pleasure, <br>let us spend a whole night of love;   
**7:19** for the man of the house is away, <br>he has gone on a long journey,   
**7:20** he has taken a bag of silver with him; <br>until the moon is full he will not be home.'   
**7:21** Persuasively she led him on, <br>she pressed him with seductive words.   
**7:22** Like a simple fool he followed her, <br>like an ox on its way to the slaughter-house, <br>like an antelope bounding into the noose,   
**7:23** like a bird hurrying into the trap; <br>he did not know that he was risking his life <br>until the arrow pierced his vitals.   
**7:24** But now, my son, listen to me, <br>attend to what I say.   
**7:25** Do not let your heart entice you into her ways, <br>do not stray down her paths;   
**7:26** many has she pierced and laid low, <br>and her victims are without number.   
**7:27** Her house is the entrance to Sheol, <br>which leads down to the halls of death.  

## 8장

**8:1**  Hear how Wisdom lifts her voice <br>and Understanding cries out.   
**8:2** She stands at the cross-roads, <br>by the wayside, at the top of the hill;   
**8:3** beside the gate, at the entrance to the city, <br>at the entry by the open gate she calls aloud:   
**8:4** 'Men, it is to you I call, <br>I appeal to every man:   
**8:5** understand, you simple fools, what it is to be shrewd; <br>you stupid people, understand what sense means.   
**8:6** Listen! For I will speak clearly, <br>you will have plain speech from me;   
**8:7** for I speak nothing but truth <br>and my lips detest wicked talk.   
**8:8** All that I say is right, <br>not a word is twisted or crooked.   
**8:9** All is straightforward to him who can understand, <br>all is plain to the man who has knowledge.   
**8:10** Accept instruction and not silver, <br>knowledge rather than pure gold;   
**8:11** for wisdom is better than red coral, <br>no jewels can match her.   
**8:12** I am Wisdom, I bestow shrewdness <br>and show the way to knowledge and prudence.   
**8:13** <br> Pride, presumption, evil courses, <br>subversive talk, all these I hate.   
**8:14** I have force, I also have ability; <br>understanding and power are mine.   
**8:15** Through me kings are sovereign <br>and governors make just laws.   
**8:16** Through me princes act like princes, <br>from me all rulers on earth derive their nobility.   
**8:17** Those who love me I love, <br>those who search for me find me.   
**8:18** In my hands are riches and honour, <br>boundless wealth and the rewards of virtue.   
**8:19** My harvest is better than gold, fine gold, <br>and my revenue better than pure silver.   
**8:20** I follow the course of virtue, <br>my path is the path of justice;   
**8:21** I endow with riches those who love me <br>and I will fill their treasuries.   
**8:22** 'The LORD created me the beginning of his works, <br>before all else that he made, long ago.   
**8:23** Alone, I was fashioned in times long past, <br>at the beginning, long before earth itself.   
**8:24** When there was yet no ocean I was born, <br>no springs brimming with water.   
**8:25** Before the mountains were settled in their place, <br>long before the hills I was born,   
**8:26** when as yet he had made neither land nor lake <br>nor the first clod of earth.   
**8:27** When he set the heavens in their place I was there, <br>when he girdled the ocean with the horizon,   
**8:28** when he fixed the canopy of clouds overhead <br>and set the springs of ocean firm in their place,   
**8:29** when he prescribed its limits for the sea <br>and knit together earth's foundations.   
**8:30** Then I was at his side each day, <br>his darling and delight, <br>playing in his presence continually,   
**8:31** playing on the earth, when he had finished it, <br>while my delight was in mankind.   
**8:32** (32-33) 'Now, my sons, listen to me, <br>listen to instruction and grow wise, do not reject it. <br>Happy is the man who keeps to my ways,   
**8:33**   
**8:34** happy the man who listens to me, <br>watching daily at my threshold <br>with his eyes on the doorway;   
**8:35** for he who finds me finds life <br>and wins favour with the LORD,   
**8:36** while he who finds me not, hurts himself, <br>and all who hate me are in love with death.'  

## 9장

**9:1**  Wisdom has built her house, <br>she has hewn her seven pillars;   
**9:2** she has killed a beast and spiced her wine, <br>and she has spread her table.   
**9:3** She has sent out her maidens to proclaim <br>from the highest part of the town,   
**9:4** 'Come in, you simpletons.' <br>She says also to the fool,   
**9:5** 'Come, dine with me <br>and taste the wine that I have spiced.   
**9:6** Cease to be silly, and you will live, <br>you will grow in understanding.'   
**9:7** Correct an insolent man, and be sneered at for your pains; <br>correct a bad man, and you will put yourself in the wrong.   
**9:8** Do not correct the insolent or they will hate you; <br>correct a wise man, and he will be your friend.   
**9:9** Lecture a wise man, and he will grow wiser; <br>teach a righteous man, and his learning. will increase.   
**9:10** The first step to wisdom is the fear of the LORD, <br>and knowledge of the Holy One is understanding;   
**9:11** for through me your days will be multiplied <br>and years will be added to your life.   
**9:12** If you are wise, it will be to your own advantage; <br>if you are haughty, you alone are to blame.   
**9:13** The Lady Stupidity is a flighty creature; <br>the simpleton, she cares for nothing.   
**9:14** She sits at the door of her house, <br>on a seat in the highest part of the town,   
**9:15** to invite the passers-by indoors <br>as they hurry on their way:   
**9:16** 'Come in, you simpletons', she says. <br>She says also to the fool,   
**9:17** 'Stolen water is sweet <br>and bread got by stealth tastes good.'   
**9:18** Little does he know that death lurks there, <br>that her guests are in the depths of Sheol.  

## 10장

**10:1**  The proverbs of Solomon: <br>A wise son brings joy to his father; <br>a foolish son is his mother's bane.   
**10:2** Ill-gotten wealth brings no profit; <br>uprightness is a safeguard against death.   
**10:3** The LORD does not let the righteous go hungry , <br>but he disappoints the cravings of the wicked.   
**10:4** Idle hands make a man poor; <br>busy hands grow rich.   
**10:5** A thoughtful son puts by in summer; <br>a son who sleeps at harvest is a disgrace.   
**10:6** Blessings are showered on the righteous; <br>the wicked are choked by their own violence.   
**10:7** The righteous are remembered in blessings; <br>the name of the wicked turns rotten.   
**10:8** A wise man takes a command to heart; <br>a foolish talker comes to grief.   
**10:9** A blameless life makes for security; <br>crooked ways bring a man down.   
**10:10** To wink at a fault causes trouble; <br>a frank rebuke leads to peace.   
**10:11** The words of good men are a fountain of life; <br>the wicked are choked by their own violence.   
**10:12** Hate is always picking a quarrel, <br>but love turns a blind eye to every fault.   
**10:13** The man of understanding has wisdom on his lips; <br>a rod is in store for the back of the fool.
