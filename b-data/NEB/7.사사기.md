# 7.사사기 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  AFTER THE DEATH OF JOSHUA the Israelites inquired of the LORD which tribe should attack the Canaanites first.  
**1:2** The LORD answered, 'Judah shall attack. I hereby deliver the country into his power.'  
**1:3** Judah said to his brother Simeon, 'Go forward with me into my allotted territory, and let us do battle with the Canaanites; then I in turn will go with you into your territory.' So Simeon went with him;  
**1:4** then Judah advanced to the attack, and the LORD delivered the Canaanites and Perizzites into their hands. They slaughtered ten thousand of them at Bezek.  
**1:5** There they came upon Adoni-bezek, engaged him in battle and defeated the Canaanites and Perizzites.  
**1:6** Adoni-bezek fled, but they pursued him, took him prisoner and cut off his thumbs and his great toes.  
**1:7** Adoni-bezek said, 'I once had seventy kings whose thumbs and great toes were cut off picking up the scraps from under my table. What I have done God has done to me.' He was brought to Jerusalem and died there.   
**1:8**  The men of Judah made an assault on Jerusalem and captured it, put its people to the sword and set fire to the city.  
**1:9** Then they turned south to fight the Canaanites of the hill-country, the Negeb, and the Shephelah.  
**1:10** Judah attacked the Canaanites in Hebron, formerly called Kiriath-arba, and defeated Sheshai, Ahiman and Talmai.  
**1:11**  From there they marched against the inhabitants of Debir, formerly called Kiriath-sepher.  
**1:12** Caleb said, 'Whoever attacks Kiriath-sepher and captures it, to him I will give my daughter Achsah in marriage.'  
**1:13** Othniel, son of Caleb's younger brother Kenaz, captured it, and Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah.  
**1:14** When she came to him, he incited her to ask her father for a piece of land. As she sat on the ass, she broke, wind, and Caleb said, 'What did you mean by that?'  
**1:15** She replied, 'I want to ask a favour of you. You have put me in this dry Negeb; you must give me pools of water as well.' So Caleb gave her the upper pool and the lower pool.   
**1:16**  The descendants of Moses' father-in-law, the Kenite, went up with the men of Judah from the Vale of Palm Trees to the wilderness of Judah which is in the Negeb of Arad and settled among the Amalekites.  
**1:17** Judah then accompanied his brother Simeon, attacked the Canaanites in Zephath and destroyed it; hence the city was called Hormah .  
**1:18** Judah took Gaza, Ashkelon, and Ekron, and the territory of each.  
**1:19** The LORD was with Judah and they occupied the hill-country, but they could not drive out the inhabitants of the Vale because they had chariots of iron.  
**1:20** Hebron was given to Caleb as Moses had directed, and he drove out the three sons of Anak.  
**1:21** But the Benjamites did not drive out the Jebusites of Jerusalem; and the Jebusites have lived on in Jerusalem with the Benjamites till the present day.   
**1:22**  The tribes of Joseph attacked Bethel, and the LORD was with them.  
**1:23** They sent spies to Bethel, formerly called Luz.  
**1:24** These spies saw a man coming out of the city and said to him, 'Show us how to enter the city, and we will see that you come to no harm.'  
**1:25** So he showed them how to enter, and they put the city to the sword, but let the man and his family go free.  
**1:26** He went into Hittite country, built a city and named it Luz, which is still its name today.   
**1:27**  Manasseh did not drive out the inhabitants of Beth-shean with its villages, nor of Taanach, Dor, Ibleam, and Megiddo, with the villages of each of them; the Canaanites held their ground in that region.  
**1:28** Later, when Israel became strong, they put them to forced labour, but they never completely drove them out.   
**1:29** Ephraim did not drive out the Canaanites who lived in Gezer, but the Canaanites lived among them there.   
**1:30** Zebulun did not drive out the inhabitants of Kitron and Nahalol, but the Canaanites lived among them and were put to forced labour.   
**1:31** Asher did not drive out the inhabitants of Acco and Sidon, of Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik and Rehob.  
**1:32** Thus the Asherites lived among the Canaanite inhabitants and did not drive them out.   
**1:33** Naphtali did not drive out the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and of Beth-anath, but lived among the Canaanite inhabitants and put the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath to forced labour.   
**1:34** The Amorites pressed the Danites back into the hill-country and did not allow them to come down into the Vale.  
**1:35** The Amorites held their ground in Mount Heres and in Aijalon and Shaalbim, but the tribes of Joseph increased their pressure on them until they reduced them to forced labour.   
**1:36** The boundary of the Edomites ran from the ascent of Akrabbim, upwards from Sela.  

## 2장

**2:1**  The angel of the LORD came up from Gilgal to Bokim, and said, 'I brought you up out of Egypt and into the country which I vowed I would give to your forefathers. I said, I will never break my covenant with you,  
**2:2** and you in turn must make no covenant with the inhabitants of the country; you must pull down their altars. But you did not obey me, and look what you have done!  
**2:3** So I said, I will not drive them out before you; they will decoy you, and their gods will shut you fast in the trap.'  
**2:4** When the angel of the LORD said this to the Israelites, they all wept and wailed,  
**2:5** and so the place was called Bokim ; and they offered sacrifices there to the LORD.   
**2:6**  JOSHUA DISMISSED THE PEOPLE, and the Israelites went off to occupy the country, each man to his allotted portion.  
**2:7** As long as Joshua was alive and the elders who survived him—everyone, that is, who had witnessed the whole great work which the LORD had done for Israel—the people worshipped the LORD.  
**2:8** At the age of a hundred and ten Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died,  
**2:9** and they buried him within the border of his own property in Timnath-heres north of Mount Gaash in the hill-country of Ephraim.  
**2:10** Of that whole generation, all were gathered to their forefathers, and another generation followed who did not acknowledge the LORD and did not know what he had done for Israel.  
**2:11**  Then the Israelites did what was wrong in the eyes of the LORD, and worshipped the Baalim .  
**2:12** They forsook the LORD, their fathers' God who had brought them out of Egypt, and went after other gods, gods of the races among whom they lived; they bowed down before them and provoked the LORD to anger;  
**2:13** they forsook the LORD and worshipped the Baal and the Ashtaroth .  
**2:14** The LORD in his anger made them the prey of bands of raiders and plunderers; he sold them to their enemies all around them, and they could no longer make a stand.  
**2:15** Every time they went out to battle the LORD brought disaster upon them, as he had said when he gave them his solemn warning, and they were in dire straits.   
**2:16** The LORD set judges over them, who rescued them from the marauding bands.  
**2:17** Yet they did not listen even to these judges, but turned wantonly to worship other gods and bowed down before them; all too soon they abandoned the path of obedience to the LORD's commands which their forefathers had followed. They did not obey the LORD.  
**2:18** Whenever the LORD set up a judge over them, he was with that judge, and kept them safe from their enemies so long as he lived. The LORD would relent as often as he heard them groaning under oppression and ill-treatment.  
**2:19** But as soon as the judge was dead, they would relapse into deeper corruption than their forefathers and give their allegiance to other gods, worshipping them and bowing down before them. They gave up none of their evil practices and their wilful ways.  
**2:20** And the LORD was angry with Israel and said, 'This nation has broken the covenant which I laid upon their forefathers and has not obeyed me,  
**2:21** and now, of all the nations which Joshua left at his death, I will not drive out to make room for them one single man.  
**2:22** By their means I will test Israel, to see whether or not they will keep strictly to the way of the LORD as their forefathers did.'  
**2:23** So the LORD left those nations alone and made no haste to drive them out or give them into Joshua's hands.  

## 3장

**3:1**  These are the nations which the LORD left as a means of testing all the Israelites who had not taken part in the battles for Canaan,  
**3:2** his purpose being to teach succeeding generations of Israel, or those at least who had not learnt in former times, how to make war.  
**3:3** These were: the five lords of the Philistines, all the Canaanites, the Sidoniahs, and the Hivites who lived in Mount Lebanon from Mount Baal-hermon as far as Lebo-hamath.  
**3:4** His purpose also was to test whether the Israelites would obey the commands which the LORD had given to their forefathers through Moses.  
**3:5** Thus the Israelites lived among the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.  
**3:6** They took their daughters in marriage and gave their own daughters to their sons; and they worshipped their gods.   
**3:7**  The Israelites did what was wrong in the eyes of the LORD; they forgot the LORD their God and worshipped the Baalirn and the Asheroth .  
**3:8** The LORD was angry with Israel and he sold them to Cushan-rishathaim, king of Aram-naharaim , who kept them in subjection for eight years.  
**3:9** Then the Israelites cried to the LORD for help and he raised up a man to deliver them, Othniel son of Caleb's younger brother Kenaz, and he set them free.  
**3:10** The spirit of the LORD came upon him and he became judge over Israel. He took the field, and the LORD delivered Cushan-rishathaim king of Aram into his hands; Othniel was too strong for him.  
**3:11** Thus the land was at peace for forty years until Othniel son of Kenaz died.   
**3:12**  Once again the Israelites did what was wrong in the eyes of the LORD, and because of this he roused Eglon king of Moab against Israel.  
**3:13** Eglon mustered the Ammonites and the Amalekites, advanced to attack Israel and took possession of the Vale of Palm Trees.  
**3:14** The Israelites were subject to Eglon king of Moab for eighteen years.  
**3:15** When they cried to the LORD for help, he raised up a man to deliver them, Ehud son of Gera the Benjamite, who was left-handed. The Israelites sent him to pay their tribute to Eglon king of Moab.  
**3:16** Ehud made himself a two-edged sword, only fifteen inches long, which he fastened on his right side under his clothes,  
**3:17** and he brought the tribute to Eglon king of Moab. Eglon was a very fat man.  
**3:18** When Ehud had finished presenting the tribute, he sent on the men who had carried it,  
**3:19** and he himself turned back from the Carved Stones at Gilgal. 'My lord king,' he said, 'I have a word for you in private.' Eglon called for silence and dismissed all his attendants.  
**3:20** Ehud then came up to him as he sat in the roof-chamber of his summer palace and said, 'I have a word from God for you.'  
**3:21** So Eglon rose from his seat, and Ehud reached with his left hand, drew the sword from his right side and drove it into his belly.  
**3:22** The hilt went in after the blade and the fat closed over the blade; he did not draw the sword out but left it protruding behind.  
**3:23** Ehud went out to the porch, shut the doors on him and fastened them.  
**3:24** When he had gone away, Eglon's servants came and, finding the doors fastened, they said, 'He must be relieving himself in the closet of his summer palace.'  
**3:25** They waited until they were ashamed to delay any longer, and still he did not open the doors of the roof-chamber. So they took the key and opened the doors; and there was their master lying on the floor dead.  
**3:26** While they had been waiting, Ehud made his escape; he passed the Carved Stones and escaped to Seirah.  
**3:27** When he arrived there, he sounded the trumpet in the hill-country of Ephraim, and the Israelites came down from the hills with him at their head.  
**3:28** He said to them, 'Follow me, for the LORD has delivered your enemy the Moabites into your hands.' Down they came after him, and they seized the fords of the Jordan against the Moabites and allowed no man to cross.  
**3:29** They killed that day some ten thousand Moabites, all of them men of substance and all fighting men; not one escaped.  
**3:30** Thus Moab on that day became subject to Israel, and the land was at peace for eighty years.   
**3:31**  After Ehud there was Shamgar of Beth-anath . He killed six hundred Philistines with an ox-goad, and he too delivered Israel.  

## 4장

**4:1**  After Ehud's death the Israelites once again did what was wrong in the eyes of the LORD,  
**4:2** so he sold them to Jabin the Canaanite king, who ruled in Hazor. The commander of his forces was Sisera, who lived in Harosheth-of-the-Gentiles.  
**4:3** The Israelites cried to the LORD for help, because Sisera had nine hundred chariots of iron and had oppressed Israel harshly for twenty years.  
**4:4** At that time Deborah wife of Lappidoth , a prophetess, was judge in Israel.  
**4:5** It was her custom to sit beneath the Palm-tree of Deborah between Ramah and Bethel in the hill-country of Ephraim, and the Israelites went up to her for justice.  
**4:6** She sent for Barak son of Abinoam from Kedesh in Naphtali and said to him, 'These are the commands of the LORD the God of Israel: "Go and draw ten thousand men from Naphtali and Zebulun and bring them with you to Mount Tabor,  
**4:7** and I will draw Sisera, Jabin's commander, to the Torrent of Kishon with his chariots and all his rabble, and there I will deliver them into your hands." '  
**4:8** Barak answered her, 'If you go with me, I will go; but if you will not go, neither will I.'  
**4:9** 'Certainly I will go with you,' she said, 'but this venture will bring you no glory, because the LORD will leave Sisera to fall into the hands of a woman.' So Deborah rose and went with Barak to Kedesh.  
**4:10** Barak summoned Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh and marched up with ten thousand men, and Deborah went with him.   
**4:11** Now Heber the Kenite had parted company with the Kenites, the descendants of Hobab, Moses' brother-in-law, and he had pitched his tent at Elon-bezaanannim near Kedesh.   
**4:12** Word was brought to Sisera that Barak son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor;  
**4:13** so he summoned all his chariots, nine hundred chariots of iron, and his troops, from Harosheth-of-the-Gentiles to the Torrent of Kishon.  
**4:14** Then Deborah said to Barak, 'Up! This day the LORD gives Sisera into your hands. Already the LORD has gone out to battle before you.' So Barak came charging down from Mount Tabor with ten thousand men at his back.  
**4:15** The LORD put Sisera to rout with all his chariots and his army before Barak's onslaught; but Sisera himself dismounted from his chariot and fled on foot.  
**4:16** Barak pursued the chariots and the army as far as Harosheth, and the whole army was put to the sword and perished; not a man was left alive.  
**4:17** Meanwhile Sisera fled on foot to the tent of Jael wife of Heber the Kenite, because Jabin king of Hazor and the household of Heber the Kenite were at peace.  
**4:18** Jael came out to meet Sisera and said to him, 'Come in here, my lord, come in; do not be afraid.' So he went into the tent, and she covered him with a rug.  
**4:19** He said to her, 'Give me some water to drink; I am thirsty.' She opened a skin full of milk, gave him a drink and covered him up again.  
**4:20** He said to her, 'Stand at the tent door, and if anybody comes and asks if someone is here, say No.'  
**4:21** But Jael, Heber's wife, took a tent-peg, picked up a hammer, crept up to him, and drove the peg into his skull as he lay sound asleep. His brains oozed out on the ground, his limbs twitched, and he died.  
**4:22** When Barak came up in pursuit of Sisera, Jael went out to meet him and said to him, 'Come, I will show you the man you are looking for.' He went in with her, and there was Sisera lying dead with the tent-peg in his skull.  
**4:23** That day God gave victory to the Israelites over Jabin king of Canaan,  
**4:24** and they pressed home their attacks upon that king of Canaan until they had made an end of him.  

## 5장

**5:1**  That day Deborah and Barak son of Abinoam sang this song:   
**5:2**  For the leaders, the leaders in Israel, for the people who answered the call, bless ye the LORD.  
**5:3** Hear me, you kings; princes, give ear; I will sing, I will sing to the LORD. I will raise a psalm to the LORD the God of Israel.  
**5:4** O LORD, at thy setting forth from Seir, when thou camest marching out of the plains of Edom, earth trembled; heaven quaked; the clouds streamed down in torrents.  
**5:5** Mountains shook in fear before the LORD, the lord of Sinai, before the LORD, the God of Israel.  
**5:6** In the days of Shamgar of Beth-anath , in the days of Jael, caravans plied no longer; men who had followed the high roads went round by devious paths.  
**5:7** Champions there were none, none left in Israel, until I , Deborah, arose, arose, a mother in Israel.  
**5:8** They chose new gods, they consorted with demons . Not a shield, not a lance was to be seen in the forty thousand of Israel.  
**5:9** Be proud at heart, you marshals of Israel; you among the people that answered the call, bless ye the LORD.  
**5:10** You that ride your tawny she-asses, that sit on saddle-cloths, and you that take the road afoot, ponder this well.  
**5:11** Hark, the sound of the players striking up in the places where the women draw water! It is the victories of the LORD that they commemorate there, his triumphs as the champion of Israel. Down to the gates came the LORD's people:  
**5:12** 'Rouse, rouse yourself, Deborah, rouse yourself, lead out the host. Up, Barak! Take prisoners in plenty, son of Abinoam.  
**5:13** Then down marched the column and its chieftains, the people of the LORD marched down like warriors.  
**5:14** The men of Ephraim showed a brave front in the vale, crying, 'With you, Benjamin! Your clansmen are here!' From Machir down came the marshals, from Zebulun the bearers of the musterer's staff.  
**5:15** Issachar joined with Deborah in the uprising , Issachar stood by Barak; down into the valley they rushed. But Reuben, he was split into factions, great were their heart-searchings.  
**5:16** What made you linger by the cattle-pens to listen to the shrill calling of the shepherds?   
**5:17** Gilead stayed beyond Jordan; and Dan, why did he tarry by the ships? Asher lingered by the sea-shore, by its creeks he stayed.  
**5:18** The people of Zebulun risked their very lives, so did Naphtali on the heights of the battlefield.  
**5:19** Kings came, they fought; then fought the kings of Canaan at Taanach by the waters of Megiddo; no plunder of silver did they take.  
**5:20** The stars fought from heaven, the stars in their courses fought against Sisera.  
**5:21** The Torrent of Kishon swept him away, the Torrent barred his flight, the Torrent of Kishon; march on in might, my soul!  
**5:22** Then hammered the hooves of his horses, his chargers galloped, galloped away.  
**5:23** A curse on Meroz, said the angel of the LORD; a curse, a curse on its inhabitants, because they brought no help to the LORD, no help to the LORD and the fighting men.  
**5:24** Blest above women be Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite; blest above all women in the tents.  
**5:25** He asked for water: she gave him milk, she offered him curds in a bowl fit for a chieftain.  
**5:26** She stretched out her hand for the tent-peg, her right hand to hammer the weary. With the hammer she struck Sisera, she crushed his head; she struck and his brains ebbed out.  
**5:27** At her feet he sank down, he fell, he lay; at her feet he sank down and fell. Where he sank down, there he fell, done to death.  
**5:28** The mother of Sisera peered through the lattice, through the window she peered and shrilly cried, 'Why are his chariots so long coming? Why is the clatter of his chariots so long delayed?'  
**5:29** The wisest of her princesses answered her, yes, she found her own answer:  
**5:30** 'They must be finding spoil, taking their shares, a wench to each man, two wenches, booty of dyed stuffs for Sisera, booty of dyed stuffs, dyed stuff, and striped, two lengths of striped stuff— to grace the victor's neck.'  
**5:31** So perish all thine enemies, O LORD; but let all who love thee be like the sun rising in strength. <br>The land was at peace for forty years.  

## 6장

**6:1**  THE ISRAELITES DID WHAT WAS WRONG in the eyes of the LORD and he delivered them into the hands of Midian for seven years.  
**6:2** The Midianites were too strong for Israel, and the Israelites were forced to find themselves hollow places in the mountains, and caves and strongholds.  
**6:3** If the Israelites had sown their seed, the Midianites and the Amalekites and other eastern tribes would come up and attack Israel.  
**6:4** They then pitched their camps in the country and destroyed the crops as far as the outskirts of Gaza, leaving nothing to support life in Israel, sheep or ox or ass.  
**6:5** They came up with their herds and their tents, like a swarm of locusts; they and their camels were past counting. They had come into the land for its growing crop ,  
**6:6** and so the Israelites were brought to destitution by the Midianites, and they cried to the LORD for help.  
**6:7** When the Israelites cried to the LORD because of what they had suffered from the Midianites,  
**6:8** he sent them a prophet who said to them, 'These are the words of the LORD the God of Israel:  
**6:9** I brought you up from Egypt, that land of slavery. I delivered you from the Egyptians and from all your oppressors. I drove them out before you and gave you their lands.  
**6:10** I said to you, "I am the LORD your God: do not stand in awe of the gods of the Amorites in whose country you are settling." But you did not listen to me.'   
**6:11** Now the angel of the LORD came and sat under the terebinth at Ophrah which belonged to Joash the Abiezrite. His son Gideon was threshing wheat in the winepress, so that he might get it away quickly from the Midianites.  
**6:12** The angel of the LORD showed himself to Gideon and said, 'You are a brave man, and the LORD is with you.'  
**6:13** Gideon said, 'But pray, my lord, if the LORD really is with us, why has all this happened to us? What has become of all those wonderful deeds of his, of which we have heard from our fathers, when they told us how the LORD brought us out of Egypt? But now the LORD has cast us off and delivered us into the power of the Midianites.'  
**6:14** The LORD turned to him and said, 'Go and use this strength of yours to free Israel from the power of the Midianites. It is I that send you.'  
**6:15** Gideon said, 'Pray, my lord, how can I save Israel? Look at my clan: it is the weakest in Manasseh, and I am the least in my father's family.'  
**6:16** The LORD answered, 'I will be with you, and you shall lay low all Midian as one man.'  
**6:17** He replied, 'If I stand so well with you, give me a sign that it is you who speak to me.  
**6:18** Please do not leave this place until I come with my gift and lay it before you.' He answered, 'I will stay until you come back.'  
**6:19** So Gideon went in, prepared a kid and made an ephah of flour into unleavened cakes. He put the meat in a basket, poured the broth into a pot and brought it out to him under the terebinth. As he approached,  
**6:20** the angel of God said to him, 'Take the meat and the cakes, and put them here on the rock and pour out the broth', and he did so.  
**6:21** Then the angel of the LORD reached out the staff in his hand and touched the meat and the cakes with the tip of it. Fire sprang up from the rock and consumed the meat and the cakes; and the angel of the LORD was no more to be seen.  
**6:22** Then Gideon knew that it was the angel of the LORD and said, 'Alas, Lord GOD! Then it is true: I have seen the angel of the LORD face to face.'  
**6:23** But the LORD said to him, 'Peace be with you; do not be afraid', you shall not die.'  
**6:24** So Gideon built an altar there to the LORD and named it Jehovah-shalom . It stands to this day at Ophrah-of-the-Abiezrites.   
**6:25** That night the LORD said to Gideon, 'Take a young bull of your father's, the yearling bull , tear down the altar of Baal which belongs to your father and cut down the sacred pole which stands beside it.  
**6:26** Then build an altar of the proper pattern to the LORD your God on the top of this earthwork ; take the yearling bull and offer it as a whole-offering with the wood of the sacred pole that you cut down.'  
**6:27** So Gideon took ten of his servants and did as the LORD had told him. He was afraid of his father's family and his fellow-citizens, and so he did it by night, and not by day.  
**6:28** When the citizens rose early in the morning, they found the altar of Baal overturned and the sacred pole which had stood beside it cut down and the yearling bull offered up as a whole-offering on the altar which he had built.  
**6:29** They asked each other who had done it, and, after searching inquiries, were told that it was Gideon son of Joash.  
**6:30** So the citizens said to Joash, 'Bring out your son. He has overturned the altar of Baal and cut down the sacred pole beside' it, and he must die.'  
**6:31** But as they crowded round him Joash retorted, 'Are you pleading Baal's cause then? Do you think that it is for you to save him? Whoever pleads his cause shall be put to death at dawn. If Baal is a god, and someone has torn down his altar, let him take up his own cause.'  
**6:32** That day Joash named Gideon Jerubbaal , saying, 'Let Baal plead his cause against this man, for he has torn down his altar.'   
**6:33** All the Midianites, the Amalekites, and the eastern tribes joined forces, crossed the river and camped in the Vale of Jezreel.  
**6:34** Then the spirit of the LORD took possession of Gideon; he sounded the trumpet and the Abiezrites were called out to follow him.  
**6:35** He sent messengers all through Manasseh; and they too were called out. He sent messengers to Asher, Zebulun, and Naphtali, and they came up to meet the others.  
**6:36** Gideon said to God, 'If thou wilt deliver Israel through me as thou hast promised—  
**6:37** now, look, I am putting a fleece of wool on the threshing-floor. If there is dew only on the fleece and all the ground is dry, then I shall be sure that thou wilt deliver Israel through me, as thou hast promised.'  
**6:38** And that is what happened. He rose early next day and wrung out the fleece, and he squeezed enough dew from it to fill a bowl with water.  
**6:39** Gideon then said to God, 'Do not be angry with me, but give me leave to speak once again. Let me, I pray thee, make one more test with the fleece. This time let the fleece alone be dry, and all the ground be covered with dew.'  
**6:40** God let it be so that night: the fleece alone was dry, and on all the ground there was dew.  

## 7장

**7:1**  Jerubbaal, that is Gideon, and all the people with him rose early and pitched camp at En-harod ; the Midianite camp was in the vale to the north of the hill of Moreh.  
**7:2** The LORD said to Gideon, 'The people with you are more than I need to deliver Midian into their hands: Israel will claim the glory for themselves and say that it is their own strength that has given them the victory.  
**7:3** Now make a proclamation for all the people to hear, that anyone who is scared or frightened is to leave Mount Galud at once and go back home.' Twenty-two thousand of them went, and ten thousand were left.  
**7:4** The LORD then said to Gideon, 'There are still too many. Bring them down to the water, and I will separate them for you there. When I say to you, "This man shall go with you", he shall go; and if I say, "This man shall not go with you", he shall not go.'  
**7:5** So Gideon brought the people down to the water and the LORD said to him, 'Make every man who laps the water with his tongue like a dog stand on one side, and on the other every man who goes down on his knees and drinks.'  
**7:6** The number of those who lapped was three hundred, and all the rest went down on their knees to drink, putting their hands to their mouths.  
**7:7** The LORD said to Gideon, 'With the three hundred men who lapped I will save you and deliver Midian into your hands, and all the rest may go home.'  
**7:8** So Gideon sent all these Israelites home, but he kept the three hundred, and they took with them the jars and the trumpets which the people had. The Midianite camp was below him in the vale.   
**7:9** That night the LORD said to him, 'Go down at once and attack the camp, for I have delivered it into your hands.  
**7:10** If you are afraid to do so, then go down first with your servant Purah  
**7:11** and listen to what they are saying. That will give you courage to go down and attack the camp.' So he and his servant Purah went down to the part of the camp where the fighting men lay.  
**7:12** Now the Midianites, the Amalekites, and the eastern tribes were so many that they lay there in the valley like a swarm of locusts; there was no counting their camels; in number they were like grains of sand on the sea-shore.  
**7:13** When Gideon came close, there was a man telling his companion a dream. He said, 'I dreamt that I saw a hard, stale barley-cake rolling over and over through the Midianite camp; it came to a tent, hit it and turned it upside down, and the tent collapsed.'  
**7:14** The other answered, 'Depend upon it, this is the sword of Gideon son of Joash the Israelite. God has delivered Midian and the whole army into his hands.'  
**7:15** When Gideon heard the story of the dream and its interpretation, he prostrated himself. Then he went back to the Israelite camp and said, 'Up! The LORD has delivered the camp of the Midianites into your hands.'  
**7:16** He divided the three hundred men into three companies, and gave every man a trumpet and an empty jar with a torch inside it.  
**7:17** Then he said to them, 'Watch me: when I come to the edge of the camp, do exactly as I do.  
**7:18** When I and my men blow our trumpets, you too all round the camp will blow your trumpets, and shout, "For the LORD and for Gideon!" '   
**7:19** Gideon and the hundred men who were with him reached the outskirts of the camp at the beginning of the middle watch; the sentries had just been posted. They blew their trumpets and smashed their jars.  
**7:20** The three companies all blew their trumpets and smashed their jars, then grasped the torches in their left hands and the trumpets in their right, and shouted, 'A sword for the LORD and for Gideon!'  
**7:21** Every man stood where he was, all round the camp, and the whole camp leapt up in a panic and fled.  
**7:22** The three hundred blew their trumpets, and throughout the camp the LORD set every man against his neighbour. The army fled as far as Beth-shittah in Zererah, as far as the ridge of Abel-meholah by Tabbath.  
**7:23** The Israelites from Naphtali and Asher and all Manasseh were called out and they pursued the Midianites.  
**7:24** Gideon sent men through all the hill-country of Ephraim with this message: 'Come down and cut off the Midianites. Hold the fords of the Jordan against them as far as Beth-barah.' So all the Ephraimites were called out and they held the fords of the Jordan as far as Beth-barah.  
**7:25** They captured the two Midianite princes, Oreb and Zeeb. Oreb they killed at the Rock of Oreb, and Zeeb by the Winepress of Zeeb, and they kept up the pursuit of the Midianites; afterwards they brought the heads of Oreb and Zeeb across the Jordan to Gideon.  

## 8장

**8:1**  The men of Ephraim said to Gideon, 'Why have you treated us like this? Why did you not summon us when you went to fight Midian?'; and they reproached him violently.  
**8:2** But he said to them, 'What have I done compared with you? Are not Ephraim's gleanings better than the whole vintage of Abiezer?  
**8:3** God has delivered Oreb and Zeeb, the princes of Midian, into your hands. What have I done compared with you?' At these words of his, their anger died down.   
**8:4** Gideon came to the Jordan, and he and his three hundred men crossed over to continue the pursuit, weary though they were.  
**8:5** He said to the men of Succoth, 'Will you give these men of mine some bread, for they are weary, and I am pursuing Zebah and Zalmunna, the kings of Midian?'  
**8:6** But the chief men of Succoth replied, 'Are Zebah and Zalmunna already in your hands, that we should give your army bread?'  
**8:7** Gideon said, 'For that, when the LORD delivers Zebah and Zalmunna into my hands, I will thresh your bodies with desert thorns and briars.'  
**8:8** He went on from there to Penuel and made the same request; the men of Penuel answered like the men of Succoth.  
**8:9** He said to the men of Penuel, 'When I return safely, I will pull down your castle.'   
**8:10** Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor with their army of fifteen thousand men. These were all that remained of the whole host of the eastern tribes; a hundred and twenty thousand armed men had fallen in battle.  
**8:11** Gideon advanced along the track used by the tent-dwellers east of Nobah and Jogbehah, and his attack caught the army when they were off their guard.  
**8:12** Zebah and Zalmunna fled; but he went in pursuit of these Midianite kings and captured them both; and their whole army melted away.   
**8:13** As Gideon son of Joash was returning from the battle by the Ascent of Heres,  
**8:14** he caught a young man from Succoth. He questioned him, and one by one he numbered off the names of the rulers of Succoth and its elders, seventy-seven in all.  
**8:15** Gideon then came to the men of Succoth and said, 'Here are Zebah and Zalmunna, about whom you taunted me. "Are Zebah and Zalmunna", you said, "already in your hands, that we should give your weary men bread?" '  
**8:16** Then he took the elders of the city and he disciplined those men of Succoth with desert thorns and briars.  
**8:17** He also pulled down the castle of Penuel and put the men of the city to death.  
**8:18** Then he said to Zebah and Zalmunna, 'What of the men you killed in Tabor?' They answered, 'They were like you, every one had the look of a king's son.'  
**8:19** 'They were my brothers,' he said, 'my mother's sons. I swear by the LORD, if you had let them live I would not have killed you';  
**8:20** and he said to his eldest son Jether, 'Up with you, and kill them.' But he was still only a lad, and did not draw his sword, because he was afraid.  
**8:21** So Zebah and Zalmunna said, 'Rise up yourself and dispatch us, for you have a man's strength.' So Gideon rose and killed them both, and he took the crescents from the necks of their camels.   
**8:22** After this the Israelites said to Gideon, 'You have saved us from the Midianites; now you be our ruler, you and your son and your grandson.'  
**8:23** Gideon replied, 'I will not rule over you, nor shall my son; the LORD will rule over you.'  
**8:24** Then he said, 'I have a request to make: will every one of you give me the earrings from his booty?'—for the enemy wore golden earrings, being Ishmaelites.  
**8:25** They said, 'Of course, we will give them.' So a cloak was spread out and every man threw on to it the golden earrings from his booty.  
**8:26** The earrings for which he asked weighed seventeen hundred shekels of gold; this was in addition to the crescents and pendants and the purple cloaks worn by the Midianite kings, not counting the chains on the necks of their camels.  
**8:27** Gideon made it into an ephod and he set it up in his own city of Ophrah. All the Israelites turned wantonly to its worship, and it became a trap to catch Gideon and his household.   
**8:28** Thus the Midianites were subdued by the Israelites; they could no longer hold up their heads. For forty years the land was at peace, all the lifetime of Gideon,  
**8:29**  that is Jerubbaal son of Joash; and he retired to his own home.  
**8:30** Gideon had seventy sons, his own offspring, for he had many wives.  
**8:31** He had a concubine who lived in Shechem, and she also bore him a son, whom he named Abimelech.  
**8:32** Gideon son of Joash died at a ripe old age and was buried in his father's grave at Ophrah-of-the-Abiezrites.  
**8:33** After his death, the Israelites again went wantonly to the worship of the Baalim and made Baal-berith their god.  
**8:34** They forgot the LORD their God who had delivered them from their enemies on every side,  
**8:35** and did not show to the family of Jerubbaal, that is Gideon, the loyalty that was due to them for all the good he had done for Israel.  

## 9장

**9:1**  ABIMELECH SON OF JERUBBAAL went to Shechem to his mother's brothers, and spoke with them and with all the clan of his mother's family.  
**9:2** 'I beg you,' he said, 'whisper a word in the ears of the chief citizens of Shechem. Ask them which is better for them: that seventy men, all the sons of Jerubbaal, should rule over them, or one man. Tell them to remember that I am their own flesh and blood.'  
**9:3** So his mother's brothers repeated all this to each of them on his behalf; and they were moved to come over to Abimelech's side, because, as they said, he was their brother.  
**9:4** They gave him seventy pieces of silver from the temple of Baal-berith, and with these he hired idle and reckless men, who followed him.  
**9:5** He came to his father's house in Ophrah and butchered his seventy brothers, the sons of Jerubbaal, on a single stone block, all but Jotham the youngest, who survived because he had hidden himself.  
**9:6** Then all the citizens of Shechem and all Beth-millo came together and made Abimelech king beside the old propped-up terebinth at Shechem.   
**9:7** When this was reported to Jotham, he went and stood on the summit of Mount Gerizim. He cried at the top of his voice: 'Listen to me, you citizens of Shechem, and may God listen to you:   
**9:8** 'Once upon a time the trees came to anoint a king, and they said to the olive-tree: Be king over us.  
**9:9** But the olive-tree answered: What, leave my rich oil by which gods and men are honoured, to come and hold sway over the trees?   
**9:10** 'So the trees said to the fig-tree: Then will you come and be king over us?  
**9:11** But the fig-tree answered: What, leave my good fruit and all its sweetness, to come and hold sway over the trees?   
**9:12** 'So the trees said to the vine: Then will you come and be king over us?  
**9:13** But the vine answered: What, leave my new wine which gladdens gods and men, to come and hold sway over the trees?   
**9:14** 'Then all the trees said to the thorn-bush: Will you then be king over us?  
**9:15** And the thorn said to the trees: If you really mean to anoint me as your king, then come under the protection of my shadow; if not, fire shall come out of the thorn and burn up the cedars of Lebanon.'   
**9:16** Then Jotham said, 'Now, have you acted fairly and honestly in making Abimelech king? Have you done the right thing by Jerubbaal and his household? Have you given my father his due—  
**9:17** who fought for you, and threw himself into the forefront of the battle and delivered you from the Midianites?  
**9:18** Today you have risen against my father's family, butchered his seventy sons on a single stone block, and made Abimelech, the son of his slave-girl, king over the citizens of Shechem because he is your brother.  
**9:19** In this day's work have you acted fairly and honestly by Jerubbaal and his family? If so, I wish you joy in Abimelech and wish him joy in you!  
**9:20** If not, may fire come out of Abimelech and burn up the citizens of Shechem and all Beth-millo; may fire also come out from the citizens of Shechem and Beth-millo and burn up Abimelech.'  
**9:21** After which Jotham slipped away and made his escape; he came to Beer, and there he settled out of reach of his brother Abimelech.   
**9:22** After Abimelech had been prince over Israel for three years,  
**9:23** God sent an evil spirit to make a breach between Abimelech and the citizens of Shechem, and they played him false.  
**9:24** This was done on purpose, so that the violent murder of the seventy sons of Jerubbaal might recoil on their brother Abimelech who did the murder and on the citizens of Shechem who encouraged him to do it.  
**9:25** The citizens of Shechem set men to lie in wait for him on the hill-tops, but they robbed all who passed that way, and so the news reached Abimelech.   
**9:26** Now Gaal son of Ebed came with his kinsmen to Shechem, and the citizens of Shechem transferred their allegiance to him.  
**9:27** They went out into the country-side, picked the early grapes in their vineyards, trod them in the winepress and held festival. They went into the temple of their god, where they ate and drank and reviled Abimelech.  
**9:28** 'Who is Abimelech,' said Gaal son of Ebed, 'and who are the Shechemites, that we should be his subjects? Have not this son of Jerubbaal and his lieutenant Zebul been subjects of the men of Hamor the father of Shechem? Why indeed should we be subject to him?  
**9:29** If only this people were in my charge I should know how to get rid of Abimelech! I would say to him, "Get your men together, and come out and fight." '  
**9:30** When Zebul the governor of the city heard what Gaal son of Ebed said, he was very angry.  
**9:31** He resorted to a ruse and sent messengers to Abimelech to say, 'Gaal son of Ebed and his kinsmen have come to Shechem and are turning the city against you.  
**9:32** Get up now in the night, you and the people with you, and lie in wait in the open country.  
**9:33** Then be up in the morning at sunrise, and advance rapidly against the city. When he and his people come out, do to him what the situation demands.'  
**9:34** So Abimelech and his people rose in the night, and lay in wait to attack Shechem, in four companies.  
**9:35** Gaal son of Ebed came out and stood in the entrance of the city gate, and Abimelech and his people rose from their hiding-place.  
**9:36** Gaal saw them and said to Zebul, 'There are people coming down from the tops of the hills', but Zebul replied, 'What you see is the shadow of the hills, looking like men.'  
**9:37** Once more Gaal said, 'There are people coming down from the central ridge of the hills, and one company is coming along the road of the Soothsayers' Terebinth.'  
**9:38** Then Zebul said to him, 'Where are your brave words now? You said, "Who is Abimelech that we should be subject to him?" Are not these the people you despised? Go out and fight him.'  
**9:39** Gaal led the citizens of Shechem out and attacked Abimelech,  
**9:40** but Abimelech routed him and he fled. The ground was strewn with corpses all the way to the entrance of the gate.  
**9:41** Abimelech established himself in Arumah, and Zebul drove away Gaal and his kinsmen and allowed them no place in Shechem.   
**9:42** Next day the people came out into the open, and this was reported to Abimelech.  
**9:43** He on his side took his supporters, divided them into three companies and lay in wait in the open country; and when he saw the people coming out of the city, he rose and attacked them.  
**9:44** Abimelech and the company with him advanced rapidly and took up position at the entrance of the city gate, while the other two companies advanced against all those who were in the open and struck them down.  
**9:45** Abimelech kept up the attack on the city all that day and captured it; he killed the people in it, pulled the city down and sowed the site with salt.  
**9:46** When the occupants of the castle of Shechem heard of this, they went into the great hall of the temple of El-berith.  
**9:47** It was reported to Abimelech that all the occupants of the castle of Shechem had collected together.  
**9:48** So he and his people went up Mount Zalmon carrying axes; there he cut brushwood, and took it and hoisted it on his shoulder. He said to his men, 'You see what I am doing; be quick and do the same.'  
**9:49** So each man cut brushwood; then they followed Abimelech and laid the brushwood against the hall, and burnt it over their heads. Thus all the occupants of the castle of Shechem died, about a thousand men and women.   
**9:50** Abimelech then went to Thebez, besieged it and took it.  
**9:51** There was a strong castle in the middle of the city, and all the citizens, men and women, took refuge there. They shut themselves in and went on to the roof.  
**9:52** Abimelech came up to the castle and attacked it. As he approached the entrance to the castle to set fire to it,  
**9:53** a woman threw a millstone down on his head and fractured his skull.  
**9:54** He called hurriedly to his young armour-bearer and said, 'Draw your sword and dispatch me, or men will say of me: A woman killed him.' So the young man ran him through and he died.  
**9:55** When the Israelites saw that Abimelech was dead, they all went back to their homes.  
**9:56** It was thus that God requited the crime which Abimelech had committed against his father by the murder of his seventy brothers,  
**9:57** and brought all the wickedness of the men of Shechem on their own heads. The curse of Jotham son of Jerubbaal came home to them.

## 11장

**11:1**  Jephthah the Gileadite was a great warrior; he was the son of Gilead by a prostitute.  
**11:2** But Gilead had a wife who bore him several sons, and when they grew up they drove Jephthah away; they said to him, 'You have no inheritance in our father's house; you are another woman's son.'  
**11:3** So Jephthah, to escape his brothers, went away and settled in the land of Tob, and swept up a number of idle men who followed him.   
**11:4** The time came when the Ammonites made war on Israel,  
**11:5** and when the fighting began, the elders of Gilead went to fetch Jephthah from the land of Tob.  
**11:6** They said to him, 'Come and be our commander so that we can fight the Ammonites.'  
**11:7** But Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, 'You drove me from my father's house in hatred. Why come to me now when you are in trouble?'  
**11:8** 'It is because of that', they replied, 'that we have turned to you now. Come with us and fight the Ammonites, and become lord over all the inhabitants of Gilead.'  
**11:9** Jephthah said to them, 'If you ask me back to fight the Ammonites and if the LORD delivers them into my hands, then I will be your lord.'  
**11:10** The elders of Gilead said again to Jephthah, 'We swear by the LORD, who shall be witness between us, that we will do what you say.'  
**11:11** Jephthah then went with the elders of Gilead, and the people made him their lord and commander. And at Mizpah, in the presence of the LORD, Jephthah repeated all that he had said.   
**11:12** Jephthah sent a mission to the king of Ammon to ask what quarrel he had with them that made him invade their country.  
**11:13** The king gave Jephthah's men this answer: 'When the Israelites came up from Egypt, they took our land from the Arnon as far as the Jabbok and the Jordan. Give us back these lands in peace.'  
**11:14** Jephthah sent a second mission to the king of Ammon,  
**11:15** and they said, 'This is Jephthah's answer: Israel did not take either the Moabite country or the Ammonite country.  
**11:16** When they came up from Egypt, the Israelites passed through the wilderness to the Red Sea and came to Kadesh.  
**11:17** They then sent envoys to the king of Edom asking him to grant them passage through his country, but the king of Edom would not hear of it. They sent also to the king of Moab, but he was not willing; so Israel remained in Kadesh.  
**11:18** They then passed through the wilderness, skirting Edom and Moab, and kept to the east of Moab. They encamped beside the Arnon, but they did not enter Moabite territory, because the Arnon is the frontier of Moab.  
**11:19** Israel then sent envoys to the king of the Amorites, Sihon king of Heshbon, asking him to give them free passage through his country to their destination.  
**11:20** But Sihon would not grant Israel free passage through his territory; he mustered all his people, encamped in Jahaz and fought Israel.  
**11:21** But the LORD the God of Israel delivered Sihon and all his people into the hands of Israel; they defeated them and occupied all the territory of the Amorites in that region.  
**11:22** They took all the Amorite territory from the Arnon to the Jabbok and from the wilderness to the Jordan.  
**11:23** The LORD the God of Israel drove out the Amorites for the benefit of his people Israel. And do you now propose to take their place?  
**11:24** It is for you to possess whatever Kemosh your god gives you; and all that the LORD our God gave us as we advanced is ours.  
**11:25** For that matter, are you any better than Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab? Did he ever quarrel with Israel or attack them?  
**11:26** For three hundred years Israelites have lived in Heshbon and its dependent villages, in Aroer and its villages, and in all the towns by the Arnon. Why did you not oust them during all that time?  
**11:27** We have done you no wrong; it is you who are doing us wrong by attacking us. The LORD who is judge will judge this day between the Israelites and the Ammonites.'  
**11:28** But the king of the Ammonites would not listen to the message which Jephthah had sent him.   
**11:29** Then the spirit of the LORD came upon Jephthah and he passed through Gilead and Manasseh, by Mizpeh of Gilead, and from Mizpeh over to the Ammonites.  
**11:30** Jephthah made this vow to the LORD: 'If thou wilt deliver the Ammonites into my hands,  
**11:31** then the first creature that comes out of the door of my house to meet me when I return from them in peace shall be the LORD's; I will offer that as a whole-offering.'  
**11:32** So Jephthah crossed over to attack the Ammonites, and the LORD delivered them into his hands.  
**11:33** He routed them with great slaughter all the way from Aroer to Minnith, taking twenty towns, and as far as Abel-keramim. Thus Israel crushed Ammon.  
**11:34**  But when Jephthah came to his house in Mizpah, who should come out to meet him with tambourines and dances but his daughter, and she his only child; he had no other, neither son nor daughter.  
**11:35** When he saw her, he rent his clothes and said, 'Alas, my daughter, you have broken my heart, such trouble you have brought upon me. I have made a vow to the LORD and I cannot go back.'  
**11:36** She replied, 'Father, you have made a vow to the LORD; do to me what you have solemnly vowed, since the LORD has avenged you on the Ammonites, your enemies.  
**11:37** But, father, grant me this one favour. For two months let me be, that I may roam the hills with my companions and mourn that I must die a virgin.'  
**11:38** 'Go', he said, and he let her depart for two months. She went with her companions and mourned her virginity on the hills.  
**11:39** At the end of two months she came back to her father, and he fulfilled the vow he had made; she died a virgin. It became a tradition  
**11:40** that the daughters of Israel should go year by year and commemorate the fate of Jephthah's daughter, four days in every year.

## 13장

**20:9** This is what we will now do to Gibeah. We will draw lots for the attack:  
**20:10** and we will take ten men out of every hundred in all the tribes of Israel, a hundred out of every thousand, and a thousand out of every ten thousand, to collect provisions from the people for those who have taken the field against Gibeah in Benjamin to avenge the outrage committed in Israel.'  
**20:11** Thus all the Israelites to a man were massed against the town.   
**20:12** The tribes of Israel sent men all through the tribe of Benjamin saying, 'What is this wicked thing which has happened in your midst?  
**20:13** Hand over to us those scoundrels in Gibeah, and we will put them to death and purge Israel of this wickedness.' But the Benjamites refused to listen to their fellow-Israelites.  
**20:14** They flocked from their cities to Gibeah to go to war with the Israelites,  
**20:15** and that day they mustered out of their cities twenty-six thousand men armed with swords. There were also seven hundred picked men from Gibeah,  
**20:16** left-handed men, who could sling a stone and not miss by a hair's breadth.  
**20:17** The Israelites, without Benjamin, numbered four hundred thousand men armed with swords, every one a fighting man.  
**20:18**  The Israelites at once moved on to Bethel, and there they sought an oracle from God, asking, 'Which of us shall attack Benjamin first?', and the LORD's answer was, 'Judah shall attack first.'  
**20:19** So the Israelites set out at dawn and encamped opposite Gibeah.  
**20:20** They advanced to do battle with Benjamin and drew up their forces before the town.  
**20:21** The Benjamites made a sally from Gibeah and left twenty-two thousand of Israel dead on the field that day.

**20:23** (23) The Israelites went up to Bethel , lamented before the LORD until evening and inquired whether they should again attack their brother Benjamin. The LORD said, 'Yes, attack him.' (22)Then the Israelites took fresh courage and again formed up on the same ground as the first day.  
**20:24** So the second day they advanced against the Benjamites,

**20:27** In those days the Ark of the Covenant of God was there,  
**20:28** and Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron, served before the LORD . The Israelites inquired of the LORD and said, 'Shall we again march out,to battle against Benjamin our brother or shall we desist?' The LORD answered, 'Attack him: tomorrow I will deliver him into your hands.'  
**20:29** Israel then posted men in ambush all round Gibeah.   
**20:30** On the third day the Israelites advanced against the Benjamites and drew up their forces at Gibeah as they had before;  
**20:31** and the Benjamites sallied out to meet the army. They were drawn away from the town and began the attack as before by killing a few Israelites, about thirty , on the highways which led across open country, one to Bethel and the other to Gibeah.  
**20:32** They thought they were defeating them once again, but the Israelites had planned a retreat to draw them away from the town out on to the highways.  
**20:33** Meanwhile the main body of Israelites left their positions and re-formed in Baal-tamar, while those in ambush, ten thousand picked men all told, burst out from their position in the neighbourhood of Gibeah and came in on the east of the town.  
**20:34** There was soon heavy fighting; yet the Benjamites did not suspect the disaster that was threatening them.  
**20:35** So the LORD put Benjamin to flight before Israel, and on that day the Israelites killed twenty-five thousand one hundred Benjamites, all armed men.   
**20:36**  The men of Benjamin now saw that they had been defeated, for all that the Israelites, trusting in the ambush which they had set by Gibeah, had given way before them.  
**20:37** The men in ambush made a sudden dash on Gibeah, fell on the town from all sides and put all the inhabitants to the sword.  
**20:38** The agreed signal between the Israelites and those in ambush was to be a column of smoke sent up from the town.  
**20:39** The Israelites then faced about in the battle; and Benjamin began to cut down the Israelites, killing about thirty of them , in the belief that they were defeating them as they had done in the first encounter.  
**20:40** As the column of smoke began to go up from the town, the Benjamites looked back and thought the whole town was going up in flames.  
**20:41** When the Israelites faced about, the Benjamites saw that disaster had overtaken them and were seized with panic.  
**20:42** They turned and fled before the Israelites in the direction of the wilderness, but the fighting caught up with them and soon those from the town were among them,  
**20:43** cutting them down. They hemmed in the Benjamites, pursuing them without respite , and overtook them at a point to the east of Gibeah.  
**20:44** Eighteen thousand of the Benjamites fell, all of them fighting men.  
**20:45** The survivors turned and fled into the wilderness towards the Rock of Rimmon. The Israelites picked off the stragglers on the roads, five thousand of them, and chased them until they had cut down and killed two thousand more.  
**20:46** Twenty-five thousand armed men of Benjamin fell in battle that day, all fighting men.  
**20:47** The six hundred who survived turned and fled into the wilderness as far as the Rock of Rimmon, and there they remained for four months.  
**20:48** The Israelites then turned back to deal with the Benjamites, and put to the sword the people in the towns and the cattle, every creature that they found; they also set fire to every town within their reach.  

## 21장

**21:1**  In Mizpah the Israelites had bound themselves by oath that none of them would marry his daughter to a Benjamite.  
**21:2** The people now came to Bethel and remained there in God's presence till sunset, raising their voices in loud lamentation.  
**21:3** They said, 'O LORD God of Israel, why has it happened in Israel that one tribe should this day be lost to Israel?'  
**21:4** Next day the people rose early, built an altar there and offered whole-offerings and shared-offerings.  
**21:5** At that the Israelites asked themselves whether among all the tribes of Israel there was anyone who did not go up to the assembly before the LORD; for under the terms of the great oath anyone who had not gone up to the LORD at Mizpah was to be put to death.  
**21:6** And the Israelites felt remorse over their brother Benjamin, because, as they said, 'This day Israel has lost one whole tribe.'  
**21:7** So they asked, 'What shall we do for wives for those who are left? We have sworn to the LORD not to give any of our daughters to them in marriage.  
**21:8** Is there anyone in all the tribes of Israel who did not go up to the LORD at Mizpah ?' Now it happened that no one from Jabesh-gilead had come to the camp for the assembly;  
**21:9** so when they held a roll-call of the people, they found that no inhabitant of Jabesh-gilead was present.  
**21:10** Thereupon the community sent off twelve thousand fighting men with orders to go and put the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead to the sword, men, women, and dependants.  
**21:11** 'This is what you shall do,' they said: 'put to death every male person, and every woman who has had intercourse with a man, but spare any who are virgins.'  
**21:12** This they did. Among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead they found four hundred young women who were virgins and had not had intercourse with a man, and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh in Canaan.  
**21:13** Then the whole community sent messengers to the Benjamites at the Rock of Rimmon to parley with them, and peace was proclaimed.  
**21:14** At this the Benjamites came back, and were given those of the women of Jabesh-gilead who had been spared; but these were not enough.   
**21:15** The people were still full of remorse over Benjamin because the LORD had made this gap in the tribes of Israel,  
**21:16** and the elders of the community said, 'What shall we do for wives for the rest? All the women in Benjamin have been massacred.'  
**21:17** They said, 'Heirs there must be for the remnant of Benjamin who have escaped! Then Israel will not see one of its tribes blotted out.  
**21:18** We cannot give them our own daughters in marriage because we have sworn that there shall be a curse on the man who gives a wife to a Benjamite.'  
**21:19** Then they bethought themselves of the pilgrimage in honour of the LORD, made every year to Shiloh, the place which lies to the north of Bethel, on the east side of the highway from Bethel to Shechem and to the south of Lebonah.  
**21:20** They said to the Benjamites, 'Go and hide in the vineyards  
**21:21** and keep watch. When the girls of Shiloh come out to dance, sally out of the vineyards, and each of you seize one of them for his wife; then make your way home to the land of Benjamin.  
**21:22** Then, if their fathers or brothers come and complain to you, say to them, "Let us keep them with your approval, for none of us has captured a wife in battle. Had you offered them to us, the guilt would be yours." '   
**21:23** All this the Benjamites did. They carried off as many wives as they needed, snatching them as they danced; then they went their way and returned to their patrimony, rebuilt their cities and settled in them.  
**21:24** The Israelites also dispersed by tribes and families, and every man went back to his own patrimony.   
**21:25** In those days there was no king in Israel and every man did what was right in his own eyes.
