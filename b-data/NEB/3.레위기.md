# 3.레위기 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  THE LORD SUMMONED MOSES and spoke to him from the Tent of the Presence, and said,  
**1:2** Say this to the Israelites: When any man among you presents an animal as an offering to the LORD, the offering may be presented either from the herd or from the flock.   
**1:3** If his offering is a whole-offering from the cattle, he shall present a male without blemish ; he shall present it at the entrance to the Tent of the Presence before the LORD so as to secure acceptance for himself.  
**1:4** He shall lay his hand on the head of the victim and it will be accepted on his behalf to make expiation for him.  
**1:5** He shall slaughter the bull before the LORD, and the Aaronite priests shall present the blood and fling it against the altar all round at the entrance of the Tent of the Presence.  
**1:6** He shall then flay the victim and cut it up.  
**1:7** The sons of Aaron the priest shall kindle a fire On the altar and arrange wood on the fire.  
**1:8** The Aaronite priests shall arrange the pieces, including the head and the suet, on the wood on the altar-fire,  
**1:9** the entrails and shins shall be washed in water, and the priest shall burn it all on the altar as a whole-offering, a food-offering of soothing odour to the LORD.   
**1:10** If the man's whole-offering is from the flock, either from the rams or from the goats, he shall present a male without blemish.  
**1:11** He shall slaughter it before the LORD at the north side of the altar, and the Aaronite priests shall fling the blood against the altar all round.  
**1:12** He shall cut it up, and the priest shall arrange the pieces, together with the head and the suet, on the wood on the altar-fire,  
**1:13** the entrails and shins shall be washed in water, and the priest shall present and burn it all on the altar: it is a whole-offering, a food-offering of soothing odour to the LORD.   
**1:14** If a man's offering to the LORD is a whole-offering of birds, he shall present turtle-doves or young pigeons as his offering.  
**1:15** The priest shall present it at the altar, and shall wrench off the head and burn it on the altar; and the blood shall be drained out against the side of the altar.  
**1:16** He shall take away the crop and its contents in one piece, and throw it to the east side of the altar where the ashes are.  
**1:17** He shall tear it by its wings without severing them completely, and shall burn it on the altar, on top of the wood of the altar-fire: it is a whole-offering, a food-offering of soothing odour to the LORD.  

## 2장

**2:1**  When any person presents a grain-offering to the LORD, his offering shall be of flour. He shall pour oil on it and add frankincense to it.  
**2:2** He shall bring it to the Aaronite priests, one of whom shall scoop up a handful of the flour and oil with all the frankincense. The priest shall burn this as a token on the altar, a food-offering of soothing odour to the LORD.  
**2:3** The remainder of the grain-offering belongs to Aaron and his sons: it is most sacred, it is taken from the food-offerings of the LORD.   
**2:4** When you present as a grain-offering something baked in an oven, it shall consist of unleavened cakes of flour mixed with oil and unleavened wafers smeared with oil.  
**2:5** If your offering is a grain-offering cooked on a griddle, it shall be an unleavened cake of flour mixed with oil.  
**2:6** Crumble it in pieces and pour oil on it. This is a grain-offering.   
**2:7** If your offering is a grain-offering cooked in a pan, it shall be made of flour with oil.  
**2:8** Bring an offering made up in this way to the LORD and present it to the priest, who shall bring it to the altar;  
**2:9** then he shall set aside part of the grain-offering as a token and burn it on the altar, a food-offering of soothing odour to the LORD.  
**2:10** The remainder of the grain-offering belongs to Aaron and his sons: it is most sacred, it is taken from the food-offerings of the LORD.   
**2:11** No grain-offering which you present to the LORD shall be made of anything that ferments; you shall not burn any leaven or any honey as a food-offering to the LORD.  
**2:12** As for your offering of flrstfruits, you shall present them to the LORD, but they shall not be offered up at the altar as a soothing odour.  
**2:13** Every offering of yours which is a grain-offering shall be salted; you shall not fail to put the salt of your covenant with God on your grain-offering. Salt shall accompany all offerings.   
**2:14** If you present to the LORD a grain-offering of first-ripe grain, you must present fresh corn roasted, crushed meal from fully ripened corn.  
**2:15** You shall add oil to it and put frankincense upon it. This is a grain-offering.  
**2:16** The priest shall burn as its token some of the crushed meal, some of the oil, and all the frankincense as a food-offering to the LORD.  

## 3장

**3:1**  If a man's offering is a shared-offering from the cattle, male or female, he shall present it without blemish before the LORD.  
**3:2** He shall lay his hand on the head of the victim and slaughter it at the entrance to the Tent of the Presence. The Aaronite priests shall fling the blood against the altar all round.  
**3:3** One of them shall present part of the shared-offering as a food-offering to the LORD: he shall remove the fat covering the entrails and all the fat upon the entrails,  
**3:4** the two kidneys with the fat on them beside the haunches, and the long lobe of the liver with the kidneys.  
**3:5** The Aaronites shall burn it on the altar on top of the whole-offering which is upon the wood on the fire, a food-offering of soothing odour to the LORD.   
**3:6** If a man's offering as a shared-offering to the LORD is from the flock, male or female, he shall present it without blemish.  
**3:7** If he is presenting a ram as his offering, he shall present it before the LORD,  
**3:8** lay his hand on the head of the victim and slaughter it in front of the Tent of the Presence. The Aaronites shall then fling its blood against the altar all round.  
**3:9** He shall present part of the shared-offering as a food-offering to the LORD; he shall remove its fat, the entire fat-tail cut off close by the spine, the fat covering the entrails and all the fat upon the entrails,  
**3:10** the two kidneys with the fat on them beside the haunches, and the long lobe of the liver with the kidneys.  
**3:11** The priest shall burn it at the altar, as food offered to the LORD.   
**3:12** If the man's offering is a goat, he shall present it before the LORD,  
**3:13** lay his hand on its head and slaughter it in front of the Tent of the Presence. The Aaronites shall then fling its blood against the altar all round.  
**3:14** He shall present part of the victim as a food-offering to the LORD, he shall remove the fat covering the entrails and all the fat upon the entrails,  
**3:15** the two kidneys with the fat on them beside the haunches, and the long lobe of the liver with the kidneys.  
**3:16** The priest shall burn this at the altar, as a food-offering of soothing odour. All fat belongs to the LORD.  
**3:17** This is a rule for all time from generation to generation wherever you live: you shall not eat any fat or any blood.  

## 4장

**4:1**  THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES and said,  
**4:2** Say this to the Israelites: These are the rules for any man who inadvertently transgresses any of the commandments of the LORD and does anything prohibited by them:   
**4:3** If the anointed priest sins so as to bring guilt on the people, for the sin he has committed he shall present to the LORD a young bull without blemish as a sin-offering.  
**4:4** He shall bring the bull to the entrance of the Tent of the Presence before the LORD, lay his hand on its head and slaughter it before the LORD.  
**4:5** The anointed priest shall then take some of its blood and bring it to the Tent of the Presence.  
**4:6** He shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle some of the blood in front of the sacred Veil seven times before the LORD.  
**4:7** The priest shall then put some of the blood before the LORD in the Tent of the Presence on the horns of the altar where fragrant incense is burnt, and he shall pour the rest of the bull's blood at the base of the altar of whole-offering at the entrance of the Tent of the Presence.  
**4:8** He shall set aside all the fat from the bull of the sin-offering; he shall set aside the fat covering the entrails and all the fat upon the entrails,  
**4:9** the two kidneys with the fat on them beside the haunches, and the long lobe of the liver with the kidneys.  
**4:10** It shall be set aside as the fat from the ox at the shared-offering is set aside. The priest shall burn the pieces of fat on the altar of whole-offering.  
**4:11** But the skin of the bull and all its flesh, including head and shins, its entrails and offal,  
**4:12** the whole of it, he shall take away outside the camp to a place ritually clean, where the ash-heap is, and destroy it on a wood-fire on top of the ash-heap.   
**4:13** If the whole community of Israel sins inadvertently and the matter is not known to the assembly, if they do what is forbidden in any commandment of the LORD and so incur guilt,  
**4:14** then, when the sin they have committed is notified to them, the assembly shall present a young bull as a sin-offering and shall bring it in front of the Tent of the Presence.  
**4:15** The elders of the community shall lay their hands on the victim's head before the LORD, and it shall be slaughtered before the LORD.  
**4:16** The anointed priest shall then bring some of the blood to the Tent of the Presence,  
**4:17** dip his finger in it and sprinkle it in front of the Veil seven times before the LORD.  
**4:18** He shall put some of the blood on the horns of the altar before the LORD in the Tent of the Presence and pour all the rest at the base of the altar of whole-offering at the entrance of the Tent of the Presence.  
**4:19** He shall then set aside all the fat from the bull and burn it on the altar.  
**4:20** He shall deal with this bull as he deals with the bull of the sin-offering, and in this way the priest shall make expiation for their guilt and they shall be forgiven.  
**4:21** He shall take the bull outside the camp and burn it as the other bull was burnt. This is a sin-offering for the assembly.   
**4:22** When a man of standing sins by doing inadvertently what is forbidden in any commandment of the LORD his God, thereby incurring guilt,  
**4:23** and the sin he has committed is made known to him, he shall bring as his offering a he-goat without blemish.  
**4:24** He shall lay his hand on the goat's head and shall slaughter it before the LORD in the place where the whole-offering is slaughtered. It is a sin-offering.  
**4:25** The priest shall then take some of the blood of the victim with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of whole-offering. He shall pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar of whole-offering.  
**4:26** He shall burn all the fat at the altar in the same way as the fat of the shared-offering. Thus the priest shall make expiation for that man's sin, and it shall be forgiven him.   
**4:27** If any person among the common people sins inadvertently and does what is forbidden in any commandment of the LORD, thereby incurring guilt,  
**4:28** and the sin he has committed is made known to him, he shall bring as his offering for the sin which he has committed a she-goat without blemish.  
**4:29** He shall lay his hand on the head of the victim and slaughter it in the place where the whole-offering is slaughtered.  
**4:30** The priest shall then take some of its blood with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of whole-offering. All the rest of the blood he shall pour at the base of the altar.  
**4:31** He shall remove all its fat as the fat of the shared-offering is removed, and the priest shall burn it on the altar as a soothing odour to the LORD. So the priest shall make expiation for that person's guilt, and it shall be forgiven him.   
**4:32** If the man brings a sheep as his offering for sin, it shall be a ewe without blemish.  
**4:33** He shall lay his hand on the head of the victim and slaughter it as a sin-offering in the place where the whole-offering is slaughtered.  
**4:34** The priest shall then take some of the blood of the victim with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of whole-offering. All the rest of the blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar.  
**4:35** He shall remove all the fat, as the fat of the sheep is removed from the shared-offering. The priest shall burn the pieces of fat at the altar on top of the food-offerings to the LORD, and shall make expiation for the sin that the man has committed, and it shall be forgiven him.  

## 5장

**5:1**  IF A PERSON HEARS a solemn adjuration to give evidence as a witness to something he has seen or heard and does not declare what he knows, he commits a sin and must accept responsibility.   
**5:2** If a person touches anything unclean, such as the dead body of an unclean animal, whether wild or domestic, or of an unclean reptile,  
**5:3** or if he touches anything unclean in a man, whatever that uncleanness may be, and it is concealed by him although he is aware of it, he shall incur guilt.  
**5:4** Or if a person rashly utters an oath to do something evil or good, in any matter in which such a man may swear a rash oath, and it is concealed by him although he is aware of it, he shall in either case incur guilt.  
**5:5** Whenever a man incurs guilt in any of these cases and confesses how he has sinned therein,  
**5:6** he shall bring to the LORD, as his penalty for the sin that he has committed, a female of the flock, either a ewe or a she-goat, as a sin-offering, and the priest shall make expiation for him on account of his sin which he has committed, and he shall be pardoned.   
**5:7** But if he cannot afford as much as a young animal, he shall bring to the LORD for the sin he has committed two turtle-doves or two young pigeons, one for a sin-offering and the other for a whole-offering.  
**5:8** He shall bring them to the priest, and present first the one intended for the sin-offering. He shall wrench its head back without severing it.  
**5:9** He shall sprinkle some of the blood of the victim against the side of the altar, and what is left of the blood shall be drained out at the base of the altar: it is a sin-offering.  
**5:10** He shall deal with the second bird as a whole-offering according to custom, and the priest shall make expiation for the sin the man has committed, and it shall be forgiven him.   
**5:11** If the man cannot afford two turtle-doves or two young pigeons, for his sin he shall bring as his offering a tenth of an ephah of flour, as a sin-offering. He shall add no oil to it nor put frankincense on it, because it is a sin-offering.  
**5:12** He shall bring it to the priest, who shall scoop up a handful from it as a token and burn it on the altar on the food-offerings to the LORD: it is a sin~offering.  
**5:13** The priest shall make expiation for the sin the man has committed in any one of these cases, and it shall be forgiven him. The remainder belongs to the priest, as with the grain-offering.   
**5:14**  The LORD spoke to Moses and said:  
**5:15** When any person commits an offence by inadvertently defaulting in dues sacred to the LORD, he shall bring as his guilt-offering to the LORD a ram without blemish from the flock, the value to be determined by you in silver shekels according to the sacred standard, for a guilt-offering;  
**5:16** he shall make good his default in sacred dues, adding one fifth. He shall give it to the priest, who shall make expiation for his sin with the ram of the guilt-offering, and it shall be forgiven him.   
**5:17** If and when any person sins unwittingly and does what is forbidden by any commandment of the LORD, thereby incurring guilt, he must accept responsibility.  
**5:18** He shall bring to the priest as a guilt-offering a ram without blemish from the flock, valued by you, and the priest shall make expiation for the error into which he has unwittingly fallen, and it shall be forgiven him.  
**5:19** It is a guilt-offering ; he has been guilty of an offence against the LORD.  

## 6장

**6:1**  The LORD spoke to Moses and said:  
**6:2** When any person sins and commits a grievous fault against the LORD, whether he lies to a fellow-countryman about a deposit or contract, or a theft, Or wrongs him by extortion,  
**6:3** Or finds lost property and then lies about it, and swears a false oath in regard to any sin of this sort that he commits—  
**6:4**  if he does this, thereby incurring guilt, he shall restore what he has stolen or gained by extortion, or the deposit left with him or the lost property which he found,  
**6:5** or anything at all concerning which he swore a false oath. He shall make full restitution, adding one fifth to it, and give it back to the aggrieved party on the day when he offers his guilt-offering.  
**6:6** He shall bring to the LORD as his guilt-offering a ram without blemish from the flock, valued by you, as a guilt-offering.  
**6:7** The priest shall make expiation for his guilt before the LORD, and he shall be forgiven for any act which has brought guilt upon him.   
**6:8**  THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES and said,  
**6:9** Give this command to Aaron and his sons: This is the law of the whole-offering. The whole-offering shall remain on the altar-hearth all night till morning, and the altar-fire shall be kept burning there.  
**6:10** Then the priest, having donned his linen robe and put on linen drawers to cover himself, shall remove the ashes to which the fire reduces the whole-offering on the altar and put them beside the altar.  
**6:11** He shall then change into other garments and take the ashes outside the camp to a ritually clean place.  
**6:12** The fire shall be kept burning on the altar; it shall never go out. Every morning the priest shall have fresh wood burning thereon, arrange the whole-offering on it, and on top bum the fat from the shared-offerings.  
**6:13** Fire shall always be kept burning on the altar; it shall not go out.   
**6:14** This is the law of the grain-offering. The Aaronites shall present it before the LORD in front of the altar.  
**6:15** The priest shall set aside a handful of the flour from it, with the oil of the grain-offering, and all the frankincense on it. He shall burn this token of it on the altar as a soothing odour to the LORD.  
**6:16** The remainder Aaron and his sons shall eat. It shall be eaten in the form of unleavened cakes and in a holy place. They shall eat it in the court of the Tent of the Presence.  
**6:17** It shall not be baked with leaven. I have allotted this to them as their share of my food-offerings. Like the sin-offering and the guilt-offering, it is most sacred.  
**6:18** Any male descendant of Aaron may eat it, as a due from the food-offerings to the LORD, for generation after generation for all time. Whatever touches them is to be forfeit as sacred.   
**6:19** The LORD spoke to Moses and said:  
**6:20** This is the offering which Aaron and his sons shall present to the LORD : one tenth of an ephah of flour, the usual grain-offering, half of it in the morning and half in the evening.  
**6:21** It shall be cooked with oil on a griddle; you shall bring it well-mixed, and so present it crumbled in small pieces as a grain-offering, a soothing odour to the LORD.  
**6:22** The anointed priest in the line of Aaron shall offer it. This is a rule binding for all time. It shall be burnt in sacrifice to the LORD as a complete offering.  
**6:23** Every grain-offering of a priest shall be a complete offering; it shall not be eaten.   
**6:24**  The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**6:25** Speak to Aaron and his sons in these words: This is the law of the sin-offering. The sin-offering shall be slaughtered before the LORD in the place where the whole-offering is slaughtered; it is most sacred.  
**6:26** The priest who officiates shall eat of the flesh; it shall be eaten in a sacred place, in the court of the Tent of the Presence.  
**6:27** Whatever touches its flesh is to be forfeit as sacred. If any of the blood is splashed on a garment, that shall be washed in a sacred place.  
**6:28** An earthenware vessel in which the sin-offering is boiled shall be smashed. If it has been boiled in a copper vessel, that shall be scoured and rinsed with water.  
**6:29** Any male of priestly family may eat of this offering; it is most sacred.  
**6:30** If, however, part of the blood is brought to the Tent of the Presence to make expiation in the holy place, the sin-offering shall not be eaten; it shall be destroyed by fire.  

## 7장

**7:1**  This is the law of the guilt-offering: it is most sacred.  
**7:2** The guilt-offering shall be slaughtered in the place where the whole-offering is slaughtered, and its blood shall be flung against the altar all round.  
**7:3** The priest shall set aside and present all the fat from it: the fat-tail and the fat covering the entrails,  
**7:4** the two kidneys with the fat on them beside the haunches, and the long lobe of the liver with the kidneys.  
**7:5** The priest shall burn these pieces on the altar as a food-offering to the LORD; it is a guilt-offering.  
**7:6** Any male of priestly family may eat it. It shall be eaten in a sacred place; it is most sacred.  
**7:7** There is one law for both sin-offering and guilt-offering: they shall belong to the priest who performs the rite of expiation.  
**7:8** The skin of any man's whole-offering shall belong to the priest who presents it.  
**7:9** Every grain-offering baked in an oven and everything that is cooked in a pan or on a griddle shall belong to the priest who presents it.  
**7:10** Every grain-offering, whether mixed with oil or dry, shall be shared equally among all the Aaronites.   
**7:11**  This is the law of the shared-offering presented to the LORD.  
**7:12** If a man presents it as a thank-offering, then, in addition to the thank-offering, he shall present unleavened cakes mixed with oil, wafers of unleavened flour smeared with oil, and well-mixed flour and flat cakes mixed with oil.  
**7:13** He shall present flat cakes of leavened bread in addition to his shared thank-offering.  
**7:14** One part of every offering he shall present as a contribution for the LORD: it shall belong to the priest who flings the blood of the shared-offering against the altar.  
**7:15** The flesh shall be eaten on the day of its presentation; none of it shall be put aside till morning.   
**7:16** If a man's sacrifice is a votive offering or a freewill offering, it may be eaten on the day it is presented or on the next day.  
**7:17** Any flesh left over on the third day shall be destroyed by fire.  
**7:18** If any flesh of his shared-offering is eaten on the third day, the man who has presented it shall not be accepted. It will not be counted to his credit, it shall be reckoned as tainted and the person who eats any of it shall accept responsibility.  
**7:19** No flesh which comes into contact with anything unclean shall be eaten; it shall be destroyed by fire. <br>The flesh may be eaten by anyone who is clean,  
**7:20** but the person who, while unclean, eats flesh from a shared-offering presented to the LORD shall be cut off from his father's kin.  
**7:21** When any person is contaminated by contact with anything unclean, be it man, beast, or reptile, and then eats. any of the flesh from the shared-offerings presented to the LORD, that person shall be cut off from his father's kin.   
**7:22** The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**7:23** Speak to the Israelites in these words: You shall not eat the fat of any ox, sheep, or goat.  
**7:24** The fat of an animal that has died a natural death or has been mauled by wild beasts may be put to any other use, but you shall not eat it.  
**7:25** Every man who eats fat from a beast of which he has presented any part as a food-offering to the LORD shall be cut off from his father's kin.   
**7:26** You shall eat none of the blood, whether of bird or of beast, wherever you may live.  
**7:27** Every person who eats any of the blood shall be cut off from his father's kin.   
**7:28** The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**7:29** Speak to the Israelites in these words: Whoever comes to present a shared-offering shall set aside part of it as an offering to the LORD.  
**7:30** With his own hands he shall bring the food-offerings to the LORD. He shall also bring the fat together with the breast which is to be presented as a special gift before the LORD;  
**7:31** the priest shall burn the fat on the altar, but the breast shall belong to Aaron and his descendants.  
**7:32** You shall give the right hind-leg of your shared-offerings as a contribution for the priest;  
**7:33** it shall be the perquisite of the Aaronite who presents the blood and the fat of the shared-offering.  
**7:34** I have taken from the Israelites the breast of the special gift and the leg of the contribution made out of the shared-offerings, and have given them as a due from the Israelites to Aaron the priest and his descendants for all time.  
**7:35** This is the portion prescribed for Aaron and his descendants out of the LORD's food-offerings, appointed on the day when they were presented as priests to the LORD;  
**7:36** and on the day when they were anointed, the LORD commanded that these prescribed portions should be given to them by the Israelites. This is a rule binding on their descendants for all time.   
**7:37** This, then, is the law of the whole-offering, the grain-offering, the sin-offering, the guilt-offering, the installation-offerings, and the shared-offerings,  
**7:38** with which the LORD charged Moses on Mount Sinai on the day when he commanded the Israelites to present their offerings to the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai.  

## 8장

**8:1**  THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES and said,  
**8:2** 'Take Aaron and his sons with him, the vestments, the anointing oil, the ox for a sin-offering, the two rams, and the basket of unleavened cakes,  
**8:3** and assemble all the community at the entrance to the Tent of the Presence.'  
**8:4** Moses did as the LORD had commanded him, and the community assembled at the entrance to the Tent of the Presence.  
**8:5** He told the community that this was what the LORD had commanded.  
**8:6** He presented Aaron and his sons and washed them in water.  
**8:7** He invested Aaron with the tunic, girded him with the sash, robed him with the mantle, put the ephod on him, tied it with its waist-band and fastened the ephod to him with the band.  
**8:8** He put the breast-piece on him and set the Urim and Thummim in it.  
**8:9** He then put the turban upon his head and set the gold rosette as a symbol of holy dedication on the front of the turban, as the LORD had commanded him.  
**8:10** Moses then took the anointing oil, anointed the Tabernacle and all that was within it and consecrated them.  
**8:11** He sprinkled some of the oil seven times on the altar, anointing the altar, all its vessels, the basin and its stand, to consecrate them.  
**8:12** He poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron's head and so consecrated him.  
**8:13** Moses then brought the sons of Aaron forward, invested them with tunics, girded them with sashes and tied their tall head-dresses on them, as the LORD had commanded him.   
**8:14** He then brought up the ox for the sin-offering; Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head,  
**8:15** and he slaughtered it. Moses took some of the blood and put it with his finger on the horns round the altar. Thus he purified the altar, and when he had poured out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar, he consecrated it by making expiation for it.  
**8:16** He took all the fat upon the entrails, the long lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat, and burnt them on the altar,  
**8:17** but the ox, its skin, its flesh, and its offal, he destroyed by fire outside the camp, as the LORD had commanded him.   
**8:18** Moses then brought forward the ram of the whole-offering; Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the ram's head,  
**8:19** and he slaughtered it. Moses flung its blood against the altar all round.  
**8:20** He cut the ram up and burnt the head, the pieces, and the suet.  
**8:21** He washed the entrails and the shins in water and burnt the whole on the altar. This was a whole-offering, a food-offering of soothing odour to the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.   
**8:22** Moses then brought forward the second ram, the ram for the installation of priests. Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon its head,  
**8:23** and he slaughtered it. Moses took some of its blood and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear, on his right thumb, and on the big toe of his right foot.  
**8:24** He then brought forward the sons of Aaron, put some of the blood on the lobes of their right ears, on their right thumbs, and on the big toes of their right feet. He flung the rest of the blood against the altar all round;  
**8:25** he took the fat, the fat-tail, the fat covering the entrails, the long lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with their fat, and the right leg.

**8:33** You shall not leave the entrance to the Tent of the Presence for seven days, until the day which completes the period of your installation, for it lasts seven days.  
**8:34** What was done this day followed the LORD's command to make expiation for you.  
**8:35** You shall stay at the entrance to the Tent of the Presence day and night for seven days, keeping vigil to the LORD, so that you do not die, for so I was commanded.'

## 11장

**11:1**  THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES and Aaron and said, .  
**11:2** Speak to the Israelites in these words: Of all animals on land these are the creatures you may eat:  
**11:3** you may eat any animal which has a parted foot or a cloven hoof and also chews the cud;  
**11:4** those which have only a cloven hoof or only chew the cud you may not eat. These are: the camel, because it chews the cud but has not a cloven hoof; you shall regard it as unclean;  
**11:5** the rock-badger , because it chews the cud but has not a parted foot; you shall regard it as unclean;  
**11:6** the hare, because it chews the cud but has not a parted foot; you shall regard it as unclean;  
**11:7** the pig, because it has a parted foot and a cloven hoof but does not chew the cud; you shall regard it as unclean.  
**11:8** You shall not eat their flesh or even touch their dead bodies; you shall regard them us unclean.   
**11:9** Of creatures that live in water these you may eat: all those that have fins and scales, whether in salt water or fresh;  
**11:10** but all that have neither fins nor scales, whether in salt or fresh water, including both small creatures in shoals and larger creatures, you shall regard as vermin.  
**11:11** They shall be vermin to you; you shall not eat their flesh, and their dead bodies you shall treat as those of vermin.  
**11:12** Every creature in the water that has neither fins nor scales shall be vermin to you.   
**11:13** These are the birds you shall regard as vermin, and for this reason they shall not be eaten: the griffon-vulture , the black vulture, and the bearded vulture ,  
**11:14** the kite and every kind of falcon;  
**11:15** every kind of crow ,  
**11:16** the desert-owl, the short-eared owl, the long-eared owl, and every kind of hawk;  
**11:17** the tawny owl, the fisher-owl, and the screech-owl;  
**11:18** the little owl, the horned owl, the osprey,  
**11:19** the stork every kind of cormorant, the hoopoe, and the bat.   
**11:20** All teeming winged creatures that go on four legs shall be vermin to you,  
**11:21** except those which have legs jointed above their feet for leaping on the ground.  
**11:22** Of these you may eat every kind of great locust, every kind of long-headed locust, every kind of green locust, and every kind of desert locust.  
**11:23** Every other teeming winged creature that has four legs you shall regard as vermin;  
**11:24** you would make yourselves unclean with them: whoever touches their dead bodies shall be unclean till evening.  
**11:25** Whoever picks up their dead bodies shall wash his clothes but remain unclean till evening.   
**11:26** You shall regard as unclean every animal which has a parted foot but has not a cloven hoof and does not chew the cud: whoever touches them shall be unclean.  
**11:27** You shall regard as unclean all four-footed wild animals that go on flat paws; whoever touches their dead bodies shall be unclean till evening.  
**11:28** Whoever takes up their dead bodies shall wash his clothes but remain unclean till evening. You shall regard them as unclean.   
**11:29** You shall regard these as unclean among creatures that teem on the ground: the mole-rat , the jerboa, and every kind of thorn-tailed lizard;  
**11:30** the gecko, the sand-gecko, the wall-gecko, the great lizard, and the chameleon.  
**11:31** You shall regard these as unclean among teeming creatures; whoever touches them when they are dead shall be unclean till evening.  
**11:32** Anything on which any of them falls when they are dead shall be unclean, any article of wood or garment or skin or sacking, any article in regular use; it shall be plunged into water but shall remain unclean till evening, when it shall be clean.  
**11:33** If any of these falls into an earthenware vessel, its contents shall be unclean and it shall be smashed.  
**11:34** Any food on which water from such a vessel is poured shall be unclean, and any drink in such a vessel shall be unclean.  
**11:35** Anything on which the dead body of such a creature falls shall be unclean; an oven or a stove shall be broken, for they are unclean and you shall treat them as such;  
**11:36** but a spring or a cistern where water collects shall remain clean, though whatever touches the dead body shall be unclean.  
**11:37** When any of their dead bodies falls on seed intended for sowing, it remains clean;  
**11:38** but if the seed has been soaked in water and any dead body falls on it, you shall treat it as unclean.   
**11:39** When any animal allowed as food dies, all that touch the carcass shall be unclean till evening.  
**11:40** Whoever eats any of the carcass shall wash his clothes but remain unclean till evening; whoever takes up the carcass shall wash his clothes and be unclean till evening.  
**11:41** All creatures that teem on the ground are vermin; they shall not be eaten.  
**11:42** All creatures that teem on the ground, crawl on their bellies, go on all fours or have many legs, you shall not eat, because they are vermin which contaminate.  
**11:43** You shall not contaminate yourselves through any teeming creature. You shall not defile yourselves with them and make yourselves unclean by them.  
**11:44** For I am the LORD your God; you shall make yourselves holy and keep yourselves holy, because I am holy. You shall not defile yourselves with any teeming creature that creeps on the ground.  
**11:45** I am the LORD who brought you up from Egypt to become your God. You shall keep yourselves holy, because I am holy.   
**11:46** This, then, is the law concerning beast and bird, every living creature that swims in the water and every living creature that teems on the land.  
**11:47** It is to make a distinction between the unclean and the clean, between living creatures that may be eaten and living creatures that may not be eaten.

## 13장

**13:1**  The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron and said:  
**13:2** When any man has a discoloration on the skin of his body, a pustule or inflammation, and it may develop into the sores of a malignant skin-disease, he shall be brought to the priest, either to Aaron or to one of his sons.  
**13:3** The priest shall examine the sore on the skin; if the hairs on the sore have turned white and it appears to be deeper than the skin, it shall be considered the sore of a malignant skin-disease, and the priest, after examination, shall pronounce him ritually unclean.  
**13:4** But if the inflammation on his skin is white and seems no deeper than the skin, and the hairs have not turned white, the priest shall isolate the affected person for seven days.  
**13:5** If, when he examines him on the seventh day, the sore remains as it was and has not spread in the skin, he shall keep him in isolation for another seven days.  
**13:6** When the priest examines him again on the seventh day, if the sore has faded and has not spread in the skin, the priest shall pronounce him ritually clean. It is only a scab; the man shall wash his clothes and so be clean.  
**13:7** But if the scab spreads on the skin after he has been to the priest to be pronounced ritually clean, the man shall show himself a second time to the priest.  
**13:8** The priest shall examine him again, and if it continues to spread, he shall pronounce him ritually unclean; it is a malignant skin-disease.   
**13:9** When anyone has the sores of a malignant skin-disease, he shall be brought to the priest,  
**13:10** and the priest shall examine him. If there is a white mark on the skin, turning the hairs white, and an ulceration appears in the mark,  
**13:11** it is a chronic skin-disease on the body, and the priest shall pronounce him ritually unclean; there is no need for isolation because he is unclean already.  
**13:12** If the skin-disease breaks out and covers the affected person from head to foot as far as the priest can see,  
**13:13** the priest shall examine him, and if he finds the condition spread all over the body, he shall pronounce him ritually clean. It has all gone white; he is clean.  
**13:14** But from the moment when raw flesh appears, the man shall be considered unclean.  
**13:15** When the priest sees it, he shall pronounce him unclean. Raw flesh is to be considered unclean; it is a malignant skin-disease.  
**13:16** On the other hand, when the raw flesh heals and turns white, the man shall go to the priest,  
**13:17** who shall examine him, and if the sores have gone white, he shall pronounce him clean. He is ritually clean.   
**13:18** When a fester appears on the skin and heals up,  
**13:19** but is followed by a white mark or reddish-white inflammation on the site of the fester, the man shall show himself to the priest.  
**13:20** The priest shall examine him; if it seems to be beneath the skin and the hairs have turned white, the priest shall pronounce him ritually unclean. It is a malignant skin-disease which has broken out on the site of the fester.  
**13:21** But if the priest on examination finds that it has no white hairs, is not beneath the skin and has faded, he shall isolate him for seven days.  
**13:22** If the affection has spread at all in the skin, then the priest shall pronounce him unclean; for it is a malignant skin-disease.  
**13:23** But if the inflammation is no worse and has not spread, it is only the scar of the fester, and the priest shall pronounce him ritually clean.   
**13:24** Again, in the case of a burn on the skin, if the raw spot left by the burn becomes a reddish-white or white inflammation,  
**13:25** the priest shall examine it. If the hairs on the inflammation have turned white and it is deeper than the skin, it is a malignant skin-disease which has broken out at the site of the burn. The priest shall pronounce the man ritually unclean; it is a malignant skin-disease.  
**13:26** But if the priest on examination finds that there is no white hair on the inflammation and it is not beneath the skin and has faded, he shall keep him in isolation for seven days.  
**13:27** When the priest examines him on the seventh day, if the inflammation has spread at all in the skin, the priest shall pronounce him unclean; it is a malignant skin-disease.  
**13:28** But if the inflammation is no worse, has not spread and has faded, it is only a mark from the burn. The priest shall pronounce him ritually clean because it is the scar of the burn.   
**13:29** When a man, or woman, has a sore on the head or chin,  
**13:30** the priest shall examine it; and if it seems deeper than the skin and the hair is yellow and sparse, the priest shall pronounce him ritually unclean; it is a scurf, a malignant skin-disease of the head or chin.  
**13:31** But when the priest sees the sore, if it appears to be no deeper than the skin and yet there is no yellow hair on the place, the priest shall isolate the affected person for seven days.  
**13:32** He shall examine the sore on the seventh day: if the scurf has not spread and there are no yellow hairs on it and it seems no deeper than the skin,  
**13:33** the man shall get himself shaved except for the scurfy part, and the priest shall keep him in isolation for another seven days.  
**13:34** The priest shall examine it again on the seventh day, and if the scurf has not spread on the skin and appears to be no deeper than the skin, the priest shall pronounce him clean. The man shall wash his clothes and so be ritually clean.  
**13:35** But if the scurf spreads at all in the skin after the man has been pronounced clean,  
**13:36** the priest shall examine him again. If it has spread in the skin, the priest need not even look for yellow hair; the man is unclean.  
**13:37** If, however, the scurf remains as it was but black hair has begun to grow on it, it has healed. The man is ritually clean and the priest shall pronounce him so.   
**13:38** When a man, or woman, has inflamed patches on the skin and they are white,  
**13:39** the priest shall examine them. If they are white and fading, it is dull-white leprosy that has broken out on the skin. The man is ritually clean.   
**13:40** When a man's hair falls out from his head, he is bald behind but not ritually unclean.  
**13:41** If the hair falls out from the front of the scalp, he is bald on the forehead but clean.  
**13:42** But if on the bald patch behind or on the forehead there is a reddish-white sore, it is a malignant skin-disease breaking out on those parts.  
**13:43** The priest shall examine him, and if the discoloured sore on the bald patch behind or on the forehead is reddish-white, similar in appearance to a malignant skin-disease on the body,  
**13:44** the man is suffering from such a disease, he is ritually unclean and the priest must not fail to pronounce him so. The symptoms are in this case on his head.   
**13:45** One who suffers from a malignant skin-disease shall wear his clothes torn, leave his hair dishevelled, conceal his upper lip, and cry, 'Unclean, unclean.'  
**13:46** So long as the sore persists, he shall be considered ritually unclean. The man is unclean: he shall live apart and must stay outside the settlement.   
**13:47**  When there is a stain of mould, whether in a garment of wool or linen,  
**13:48** or in the warp or weft of linen or wool, or in a skin or anything made of skin;  
**13:49** if the stain is greenish or reddish in the garment or skin, or in the warp or weft, or in anything made of skin, it is a stain of mould which must he shown to the priest.  
**13:50** The priest shall examine it and put the stained material aside for seven days.  
**13:51** On the seventh day he shall examine it again. If the stain has spread in the garment, warp, weft, or skin, whatever the use of the skin, the stain is a rotting mould: it is ritually unclean.  
**13:52** He shall burn the garment or the warp or weft, whether wool or linen, or anything of skin which is stained; because it is a rotting mould, it must be destroyed by fire.  
**13:53** But if the priest sees that the stain has not spread in the garment, warp or weft, or anything made of skin,  
**13:54** he shall give orders for the stained material to be washed, and then he shall put it aside for another seven days.  
**13:55** After it has been washed the priest shall examine the stain; if it has not changed its appearance, although it has not spread, it is unclean and you shall destroy it by fire, whether the rot is on the right side or the wrong.  
**13:56** If the priest examines it and finds the stain faded after being washed, he shall tear it out of the garment, skin, warp, or weft.  
**13:57** If, however, the stain reappears in the garment, warp or weft, or in anything of skin, it is breaking out afresh and you shall destroy by fire whatever is stained.  
**13:58** If you wash the garment, warp, weft, or anything of skin and the stain disappears, it shall be washed a second time and then it shall be ritually clean.   
**13:59** This is the law concerning stain of mould in a garment of wool or linen, in warp or weft, or in anything made of skin; by it they shall be pronounced clean or unclean.  

## 14장

**14:1**  THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES and said:  
**14:2** This is the law concerning a man suffering from a malignant skin-disease. On the day when he is to be cleansed he shall be brought to the priest.  
**14:3** The priest shall go outside the camp and examine him. If the man is healed of his disease,  
**14:4** then the priest shall order two clean small birds to be brought alive for the man who is to be cleansed, together with cedar-wood, scarlet thread, and marjoram .  
**14:5** He shall order one of the birds to be killed over an earthenware bowl containing fresh water.  
**14:6** He shall then take the living bird and the cedar-wood, scarlet thread, and marjoram and dip them and the living bird in the blood of the bird that has been killed over the fresh water.  
**14:7** He shall sprinkle the blood seven times on the man who is to be cleansed from his skin-disease and so cleanse him; the living bird he shall release to fly away over the open country.  
**14:8** The man to be cleansed shall wash his clothes, shave off all his hair, bathe in water and so be ritually clean. He may then enter the camp but must stay outside his tent for seven days.  
**14:9** On the seventh day he shall shave off all the hair on his head, his beard, and his eyebrows, and then shave the rest of his hair, wash his clothes and bathe in water; then he shall be ritually clean.   
**14:10** On the eighth day he shall bring two yearling rams and one yearling ewe, all three without blemish, a grain-offering of three tenths of an ephah of flour mixed with oil, and one log of oil.  
**14:11** The officiating priest shall place the man to be cleansed and his offerings before the LORD at the entrance to the Tent of the Presence.  
**14:12** He shall then take one of the rams and offer it with the log of oil as a guilt-offering, presenting them as a special gift before the LORD.  
**14:13** The ram shall be slaughtered where the sin-offerings and the whole-offerings are slaughtered, within the sacred precincts, because the guilt-offering, like the sin-offering, belongs to the priest. It is most sacred.  
**14:14** The priest shall then take some of the blood of the guilt-offering and put it on the lobe of the right ear of the man to be cleansed, and on his right thumb and the big toe of his right foot.  
**14:15** He shall next take the log of oil and pour some of it on the palm of his own left hand,  
**14:16** dip his right forefinger into the oil on his left palm and sprinkle some of it with his finger seven times before the LORD.  
**14:17** He shall then put some of the oil remaining on his palm on the lobe of the right ear of the man to be cleansed, on his right thumb and on the big toe of his right foot, on top of the blood of the guilt-offering.  
**14:18** The remainder of the oil on the priest's palm shall be put upon the head of the man to be cleansed, and thus the priest shall make expiation for him before the LORD.  
**14:19** The priest shall then perform the sin-offering and make expiation for the uncleanness of the man who is to be cleansed.  
**14:20** After this he shall slaughter the whole-offering and offer it and the grain-offering on the altar. Thus the priest shall make expiation for him, and then he shall be clean.   
**14:21** If the man is poor and cannot afford these offerings, he shall bring one young ram as a guilt-offering to be a special gift making expiation for him, and a grain-offering of a tenth of an ephah of flour mixed with oil, and a log of oil,  
**14:22** also two turtle-doves or two young pigeons, whichever he can afford, one for a sin-offering and the other for a whole-offering.  
**14:23** He shall bring them to the priest for his cleansing on the eighth day, at the entrance to the Tent of the Presence before the LORD.  
**14:24** The priest shall take the ram for the guilt-offering and the log of oil, and shall present them as a special gift before the LORD.  
**14:25** The ram for the guilt-offering shall then be slaughtered, and the priest shall take some of the blood of the guilt-offering, and put it on the lobe of the right ear of the man to be cleansed and on his right thumb and on the big toe of his right foot.  
**14:26** He shall pour some of the oil on the palm of his own left hand  
**14:27** and sprinkle some of it with his right forefinger seven times before the LORD.  
**14:28** He shall then put some of the oil remaining on his palm on the lobe of the right ear of the man to be cleansed, and on his right thumb and on the big toe of his right foot exactly where the blood of the guilt-offering was put.  
**14:29** The remainder of the oil on the priest's palm shall be put upon the head of the man to be cleansed to make expiation for him before the LORD.  
**14:30** Of the birds which the man has been able to afford, turtle-doves or young pigeons, whichever it may be,  
**14:31** the priest shall deal with one as a sin-offering and with the other as a whole-offering and shall make the grain-offering with them. Thus the priest shall make expiation before the LORD for the man who is to be cleansed.  
**14:32** This is the law for the man with a malignant skin-disease who cannot afford the regular offering for his cleansing.   
**14:33**  The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron and said:  
**14:34** When you have entered the land of Canaan which I give you to occupy, if I inflict a fungous infection upon a house in the land you have occupied,  
**14:35** its owner shall come and report to the priest that there appears to him to be a patch of infection in his house.  
**14:36** The priest shall order the house to be cleared before he goes in to examine the infection, or everything in it will become. unclean. After this the priest shall go in to inspect the house.  
**14:37** If on inspection he finds the patch on the walls consists of greenish or reddish depressions, apparently going deeper than the surface,  
**14:38** he shall go out of the house and, standing at the entrance, shall put it in quarantine for seven days.  
**14:39** On the seventh day he shall come back and inspect the house, and if the patch has spread in the walls,  
**14:40** he shall order the infected stones to be pulled out and thrown away outside the city in an unclean place.  
**14:41** He shall then have the house scraped inside throughout, and all the daub they have scraped off shall be tipped outside the city in an unclean place.  
**14:42** They shall take fresh stones to replace the others and replaster the house with fresh daub.   
**14:43** If the infection reappears in the house and spreads after the stones have been pulled out and the house scraped and redaubed,  
**14:44** the priest shall come and inspect it. If the infection has spread in the house, it is a corrosive growth; the house is unclean.  
**14:45** The house shall be demolished, stones, timber, and daub, and it shall all be taken away outside the city to an unclean place.  
**14:46** Anyone who has entered the house during the time it has been in quarantine shall be unclean till evening.  
**14:47** Anyone who has slept or eaten a meal in the house shall wash his clothes.  
**14:48** But if, when the priest goes into the house and inspects it, he finds that the infection has not spread after the redaubing, then he shall pronounce the house ritually clean, because the infection has been cured.   
**14:49** In order to rid the house of impurity, he shall take two small birds, cedar-wood, scarlet thread, and marjoram.  
**14:50** He shall kill one of the birds over an earthenware bowl containing fresh water.  
**14:51** He shall then take the cedar-wood, marjoram, and scarlet thread, together with the living bird, dip them in the blood of the bird that has been killed and in the fresh water, and sprinkle the house seven times.  
**14:52** Thus he shall purify the house, using the blood of the bird, the fresh water, the living bird, the cedar-wood, the marjoram, and the scarlet thread.  
**14:53** He shall set the living bird free outside the city to fly away over the open country, and make expiation for the house; and then it shall be clean.   
**14:54** This is the law for all malignant skin-diseases, and for scurf,  
**14:55** for mould in clothes and fungus in houses,  
**14:56** for a discoloration of the skin, scab, and inflammation,  
**14:57** to declare when these are pronounced unclean and when clean. This is the law for skin-disease, mould, and fungus.

**15:2** Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When any man has a discharge from his body, the discharge is ritually unclean.  
**15:3** This is the law concerning the uncleanness due to his discharge whether it continues or has been stopped; in either case he is unclean.   
**15:4** Every bed on which the man with a discharge lies down shall be ritually unclean, and everything on which he sits shall be unclean.  
**15:5** Any man who touches the bed shall wash his clothes, bathe in water and remain unclean till evening.  
**15:6** Whoever sits on anything on which the man with a discharge has sat shall wash his clothes, bathe in water and remain unclean till evening.  
**15:7** Whoever touches the body of the man with a discharge shall wash his clothes, bathe in water and remain unclean till evening.  
**15:8** If the man spits on one who is ritually clean, the latter shall wash his clothes, bathe in water and remain unclean till evening.  
**15:9** Everything on which the man sits when riding shall be unclean.  
**15:10** Whoever touches anything that has been under him shall be unclean till evening, and whoever handles such things shall wash his clothes, bathe in water and remain unclean till evening.  
**15:11** Anyone whom the man with a discharge touches without having rinsed his hands in water shall wash his clothes, bathe in water and remain unclean till evening.  
**15:12** Any earthenware bowl touched by the man shall be smashed, and every wooden bowl shall be rinsed with water.   
**15:13** When the man is cleansed from his discharge, he shall reckon seven days to his cleansing, wash his clothes, bathe his body in fresh water and be ritually clean.  
**15:14** On the eighth day he shall obtain two turtle-doves or two young pigeons and, coming before the LORD at the entrance to the Tent of the Presence, shall give them to the priest.  
**15:15** The priest shall deal with one as a sin-offering and the other as a whole-offering, and shall make for him before the LORD the expiation required by the discharge.

**15:17** Every piece of clothing or skin on which there is any semen shall be washed and remain unclean till evening.  
**15:18** This applies also to the woman with whom a man has had intercourse; they shall both bathe themselves in water and remain unclean till evening.   
**15:19** When a woman has a discharge of blood, her impurity shall last for seven days; anyone who touches her shall be unclean till evening.  
**15:20** Everything on which she lies or sits during her impurity shall be unclean.  
**15:21** Anyone who touches her bed shall wash his clothes, bathe in water and remain unclean till evening.  
**15:22** Whoever touches anything on which she sits shall wash his clothes, bathe in water and remain unclean till evening.  
**15:23** If he is on the bed or seat where she is sitting, by touching it he shall become unclean till evening.  
**15:24** If a man goes so far as to have intercourse with her and any of her discharge gets on to him, then he shall be unclean for seven days, and every bed on which he lies down shall be unclean.   
**15:25** When a woman has a prolonged discharge of blood not at the time of her menstruation, or when her discharge continues beyond the period of menstruation, her impurity shall last all the time of her discharge; she shall be unclean as during the period of her menstruation.  
**15:26** Any bed on which she lies during the time of her discharge shall be like that which she used during menstruation, and everything on which she sits shall be unclean as in her menstrual uncleanness.  
**15:27** Every person who touches them shall be unclean; he shall wash his clothes, bathe in water and remain unclean till evening.  
**15:28** If she is cleansed from her discharge, she shall reckon seven days and after that she shall be ritually clean.  
**15:29** On the eighth day she shall obtain two turtle-doves or two young pigeons and bring them to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of the Presence.  
**15:30** The priest shall deal with one as a sin-offering and with the other as a whole-offering, and make for her before the LORD the expiation required by her unclean discharge.   
**15:31** In this way you shall warn the Israelites against uncleanness, in order that they may not bring uncleanness upon the Tabernacle where I dwell among them, and so die.   
**15:32** This is the law for the man who has a discharge, or who has an emission of semen and is thereby unclean,  
**15:33** and for the woman who is suffering her menstruation—for everyone, male or female, who has a discharge, and for the man who has intercourse with a woman who is unclean.

**16:17** No other man shall be within the Tent of the Presence from the time when he enters the sanctuary to make expiation until he comes out, and he shall make expiation for himself, his household, and the whole assembly of Israel.

**16:31** This is a sabbath of sacred rest for you, and you shall mortify yourselves; it is a rule binding for all time.

**16:33** and shall make expiation for the holy sanctuary, the Tent of the Presence, and the altar, on behalf of the priests and the whole assembly of the people.  
**16:34** This shall become a rule binding on you for all time, to make for the Israelites once a year the expiation required by all their sins.

**21:1**  THE LORD SAID TO MOSES, Say to the priests, the sons of Aaron: A priest shall not render himself unclean for the death of any of his kin  
**21:2** except for a near blood-relation, that is for mother, father, son, daughter, brother,  
**21:3** or full sister who is unmarried and a virgin;  
**21:4** nor shall he make himself unclean for any married woman among his father's kin, and so profane himself.   
**21:5** Priests shall not make bald patches on their heads as a sign of mourning, nor cut the edges of their beards, nor gash their bodies.  
**21:6** They shall be holy to their God, and they shall not profane the name of their God, because they present the food-offerings of the LORD, the food of their God, and they shall be holy.  
**21:7** A priest shall not marry a prostitute or a girl who has lost her virginity, nor shall he marry a woman divorced from her husband; for he is holy to his God.  
**21:8** You shall keep him holy because he presents the food of your God; you shall regard him as holy because I the LORD, I who hallow them, am holy.  
**21:9** When a priest's daughter profanes herself by becoming a prostitute, she profanes her father. She shall be burnt to death.   
**21:10** The high priest, the one among his fellows who has had the anointing oil poured on his head and has been consecrated to wear the vestments, shall neither leave his hair dishevelled nor tear his clothes.  
**21:11** He shall not enter the place where any man's dead body lies; not even for his father or his mother shall he render himself unclean.  
**21:12** He shall not go out of the sanctuary for fear that he dishonour the sanctuary of his God, because the consecration of the anointing oil of his God is upon him. I am the LORD.  
**21:13** He shall marry a woman who is still a virgin.  
**21:14** He shall not marry a widow, a divorced woman, a woman who has lost her virginity, or a prostitute, but only a virgin from his father's kin;  
**21:15** he shall not dishonour his descendants among his father's kin, for I am the LORD who hallows him.   
**21:16** The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**21:17** Speak to Aaron in these words: No man among your descendants for all time who has any physical defect shall come and present the food of his God.  
**21:18** No man with a defect shall come, whether a blind man, a lame man, a man stunted or overgrown,  
**21:19** a man deformed in foot or hand,  
**21:20** or with mis-shapen brows or a film over his eye or a discharge from it, a man who has a scab or eruption or has had a testicle ruptured.  
**21:21** No descendant of Aaron the priest who has any defect in his body shall approach to present the food-offerings of the LORD; because he has a defect he shall not approach to present the food of his God.  
**21:22** He may eat the bread of God both from the holy-gifts and from the holiest of holy-gifts,  
**21:23** but he shall not come up to the Veil nor approach the altar, because he has a defect in his body. Thus he shall not profane my sanctuaries, because I am the LORD who hallows them.   
**21:24** Thus did Moses speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the Israelites.  

## 22장

**22:1**  The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**22:2** Tell Aaron and his sons that they must be careful in the handling of the holy-gifts of the Israelites which they hallow to me, lest they profane my holy name. I am the LORD.  
**22:3** Say to them: Any man of your descent for all time who while unclean approaches the holy-gifts which the Israelites hallow to the LORD shall be cut off from my presence. I am the LORD.  
**22:4** No man descended from Aaron who suffers from a malignant skin-disease, or has a discharge, shall eat of the holy-gifts until he is cleansed. A man who touches anything which makes him unclean or who has an emission of semen,  
**22:5** a man who touches any vermin which makes him unclean or any human being who makes him unclean:  
**22:6** any person who touches such a thing shall be unclean till sunset and unless he washes his body shall not eat of the holy-gifts.  
**22:7** When the sun goes down, he shall be clean, and after that he may eat from the holy-gifts, because they are his food.  
**22:8** He shall not eat an animal that has died a natural death or has been mauled by wild beasts, thereby making himself unclean. I am the LORD.  
**22:9** The priests shall observe my charge, lest they make themselves guilty and die for profaning my name. I am the LORD who hallows them.  
**22:10** No unqualified person may eat any holy-gift; nor may a stranger lodging with a priest or a hired man eat a holy-gift.  
**22:11** A slave bought by a priest with his own money may do so, and slaves born in his household may also share his food.  
**22:12** When a priest's daughter marries an unqualified person, she shall not eat any of the contributions of holy-gifts;  
**22:13** but if she is widowed or divorced and is childless and comes back to her father's house as in her childhood, she shall share her father's food. No unqualified person may eat any of it.   
**22:14** When a man inadvertently eats a holy-gift, he shall make good the holy-gift to the priest, adding a fifth to its value.  
**22:15** The priests shall not profane the holy-gifts of the Israelites which they set aside for the LORD;  
**22:16** they shall not let men eat their holy-gifts and so incur guilt and its penalty, because I am the LORD who hallows them.   
**22:17** The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**22:18** Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the Israelites in these words: When any man of the house of Israel or any alien in Israel presents, whether in fulfilment of a vow or for a freewill offering, such an offering as is presented to the LORD for a whole-offering  
**22:19** so as to win acceptance for yourselves, it shall be a male without defect, of cattle, sheep, or goats.  
**22:20** You shall not present anything which is defective, because it will not be acceptable on your behalf.  
**22:21** When a man presents a shared-offering to the LORD, whether cattle or sheep, to fulfil a special vow or as a freewill offering, if it is to be acceptable it must be perfect; there shall be no defect in it.  
**22:22** You shall present to the LORD nothing blind, disabled, mutilated, with running sore, scab, or eruption, nor set any such creature on the altar as a food-offering to the LORD.  
**22:23** If a bull or a sheep is overgrown or stunted, you may make of it a freewill offering, but it will not be acceptable in fulfilment of a vow.  
**22:24** If its testicles have been crushed or bruised, torn or cut, you shall not present it to the LORD, this is forbidden in your land.   
**22:25** You shall not procure any such creature from a foreigner and present it as food for your God. Their deformity is inherent in them, a permanent defect, and they will not be acceptable on your behalf.   
**22:26** The LORD spoke to Moses and said:  
**22:27** When a calf, a lamb, or a kid is born, it must not be taken from its mother for seven days. From the eighth day onwards it will be acceptable when offered as a food-offering to the LORD.  
**22:28** You shall not slaughter a cow or sheep at the same time as its young.  
**22:29** When you make a thank-offering to the LORD, you shall sacrifice it so as to win acceptance for yourselves;  
**22:30** it shall be eaten that same day, and none be left till morning. I am the LORD.   
**22:31** You shall observe my commandments and perform them. I am the LORD.  
**22:32** You shall not profane my holy name ; I will be hallowed among the Israelites. I am the LORD who hallows you,  
**22:33** who brought you out of Egypt to become your God. I am the LORD.  

## 23장

**23:1**  THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES AND SAID,  
**23:2** Speak to the Israelites in these words: These are the appointed seasons of the LORD, and you shall proclaim them as sacred assemblies; these are my appointed seasons.  
**23:3** On six days work may be done, but every seventh day is a sabbath of sacred rest, a day of sacred assembly, on which you shall do no work. Wherever you live, it is the LORD's sabbath.   
**23:4** These are the appointed seasons of the LORD, the sacred assemblies which you shall proclaim in their appointed order.  
**23:5**  In the first month on the fourteenth day between dusk and dark is the LORD's Passover.  
**23:6** On the fifteenth day of this month begins the LORD's pilgrim-feast of Unleavened Bread; for seven days you shall eat unleavened cakes.  
**23:7** On the first day there shall be a sacred assembly; you shall not do your daily work.  
**23:8** For seven days you shall present your food-offerings to the LORD. On the seventh day also there shall be a sacred assembly; you shall not do your daily work.   
**23:9** The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**23:10** Speak to the Israelites in these words: When you enter the land which I give you, and you reap its harvest, you shall bring the first sheaf of your harvest to the priest.  
**23:11** He shall present the sheaf as a special gift before the LORD on the day after the sabbath, so as to gain acceptance for yourselves.  
**23:12** On the day you present the sheaf, you shall prepare a perfect yearling ram for a whole-offering to the LORD,  
**23:13** with the proper grain-offering, two tenths of an ephah of flour mixed with oil, as a food-offering to the LORD, of soothing odour, and also with the proper drink-offering, a quarter of a hin of wine.  
**23:14** You shall eat neither bread, nor grain, parched or fully ripened, during that day, the day on which you bring your God his offering; this is a rule binding on your descendants for all time wherever you live.   
**23:15**  From the day after the sabbath, the day on which you bring your sheaf as a special gift, you shall count seven full weeks.  
**23:16** The day after the seventh sabbath will make fifty days, and then you shall present to the LORD a grain-offering from the new crop.  
**23:17** You shall bring from your homes two loaves as a special gift; they shall contain two tenths of an ephah of flour and shall be baked with leaven. They are the LORD's firstfruits.  
**23:18** In addition to the bread you shall present seven perfect yearling sheep, one young bull, and two rams. They shall be a whole-offering to the LORD with the proper grain-offering and the proper drink-offering, a food-offering of soothing odour to the LORD.  
**23:19** You shall also prepare one he-goat for a sin-offering and two yearling sheep for a shared-offering,  
**23:20** and the priest shall present them in addition to the bread of the firstfruits as a special gift before the LORD. They shall be a holy-gift to the LORD for the priest.  
**23:21** On that same day you shall proclaim a sacred assembly for yourselves; you shall not do your daily work. This is a rule binding on your descendants for all time wherever you live.   
**23:22** When you reap the harvest in your land, you shall not reap right into the edges of your field, neither shall you glean the fallen ears. You shall leave them for the poor and for the alien. I am the LORD your God.   
**23:23**  The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**23:24** Speak to the Israelites in these words: In the seventh month you shall keep the first day as a sacred rest, a day of remembrance and acclamation, a day of sacred assembly.  
**23:25** You shall not do your daily work; you shall present a food-offering to the LORD.   
**23:26**  The LORD spoke to Moses and said:  
**23:27** Further, the tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of Atonement. There shall be a sacred assembly; you shall mortify yourselves and present a food-offering to the LORD.  
**23:28** On that same day you shall do no work because it is a day of expiation, to make expiation for you before the LORD your God.  
**23:29** Therefore every person who does not mortify himself on that day shall be cut off from his father's kin.  
**23:30** I will extirpate any person who does any work on that day.  
**23:31** You shall do no work; it is a rule binding on your descendants for all time wherever you live.  
**23:32** It is for you a sabbath of sacred rest, and you shall mortify yourselves. From the evening of the ninth day to the following evening you shall keep your sabbath-rest.   
**23:33**  The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**23:34** Speak to the Israelites in these words: On the fifteenth day of this seventh month the LORD's pilgrim-feast of Tabernacles begins, and it lasts for seven days.  
**23:35** On the first day there shall be a sacred assembly; you shall not do your daily work.  
**23:36** For seven days you shall present a food-offering to the LORD; and on the eighth day there shall be a sacred assembly, and you shall present a food-offering to the LORD. It is the closing ceremony; you shall not do your daily work.   
**23:37** These are the appointed seasons of the LORD which you shall proclaim as sacred assemblies for presenting food-offerings to the LORD, whole-offerings and grain-offerings, shared-offerings and drink-offerings, each on its day,  
**23:38** besides the LORD's sabbaths and all your gifts, your vows, and your freewill offerings to the LORD.   
**23:39** Further, from the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when the harvest has been gathered, you shall keep the LORD's pilgrim-feast for seven days. The first day is a sacred rest and so is the eighth day,  
**23:40** On the first day you shall take the fruit of citrus-trees, palm fronds, and leafy branches, and willows from the riverside, and you shall rejoice before the LORD your God for seven days.  
**23:41** You shall keep this as a pilgrim-feast in the LORD's honour for seven days every year. It is a rule binding for all time on your descendants; in the seventh month you shall hold this pilgrim-feast.  
**23:42** You shall live in arbours for seven days, all who are native Israelites,  
**23:43** so that your descendants may be reminded how I made the Israelites live in arbours when I brought them out of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.   
**23:44** Thus Moses announced to the Israelites the appointed seasons of the LORD.  

## 24장

**24:1**  THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES and said:  
**24:2** Command the Israelites to take pure oil of pounded olives ready for the regular mounting of the lamp  
**24:3** outside the Veil of the Tokens in the Tent of the Presence. Aaron shall keep the lamp in trim regularly from dusk to dawn before the LORD: this is a rule binding on your descendants for all time.  
**24:4** The lamps on the lamp-stand, ritually clean, shall be regularly kept in trim by him before the LORD.   
**24:5**  You shall take flour and bake it into twelve loaves, two tenths of an ephah to each.  
**24:6** You shall arrange them in two rows, six to a row on the table, ritually clean, before the LORD.  
**24:7** You shall sprinkle pure frankincense on the rows, and this shall be a token of the bread, offered to the LORD as a food-offering.  
**24:8** Sabbath after sabbath he shall arrange it regularly before the LORD as a gift from the Israelites. This is a covenant for ever;  
**24:9** it is the privilege of Aaron and his sons, and they shall eat the bread in a holy place, because it is the holiest of holy-gifts. It is his due out of the food-offerings of the LORD for all time.   
**24:10**  Now there was in the Israelite camp a man whose mother was an Israelite and his father an Egyptian;  
**24:11** his mother's name was Shelomith daughter of Dibri of the tribe of Dan; and he went out and became involved in a brawl with an Israelite of pure descent. He uttered the Holy Name in blasphemy, so they brought him to Moses;  
**24:12** and they kept him in custody until the LORD's will should be clearly made known to them.   
**24:13** The LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**24:14** Take the man who blasphemed out of the camp. Everyone who heard him shall put a hand on his head, and then all the community shall stone him to death.  
**24:15** You shall say to the Israelites: When any man whatever blasphemes his God, he shall accept responsibility for his sin.  
**24:16** Whoever utters the Name of the LORD shall be put to death: all the community shall stone him; alien or native, if he utters the Name, he shall be put to death.   
**24:17** When one man strikes another and kills him, he shall be put to death.  
**24:18** Whoever strikes a beast and kills it shall make restitution, life for life.  
**24:19** When one man injures and disfigures his fellow-countryman, it shall be done to him as he has done;  
**24:20** fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; the injury and disfigurement that he has inflicted upon another shall in turn be inflicted upon him.   
**24:21** Whoever strikes a beast and kills it shall make restitution, but whoever strikes a man and kills him shall be put to death.  
**24:22** You shall have one penalty for alien and native alike. For I am the LORD your God.   
**24:23** Thus did Moses speak to the Israelites, and they took the man who blasphemed out of the camp and stoned him to death. The Israelites did as the LORD had commanded Moses.  

## 25장

**25:1**  THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES on Mount Sinai and said,  
**25:2** Speak to the Israelites in these words: When you enter the land which I give you, the land shall keep sabbaths to the LORD.  
**25:3** For six years you may sow your fields and for six years prune your vineyards and gather the harvest,  
**25:4** but in the seventh year the land shall keep a sabbath of sacred rest, a sabbath to the LORD. You shall not sow your field nor prune your vineyard.  
**25:5** You shall not harvest the crop that grows from fallen grain, nor gather in the grapes from the unpruned vines. It shall be a year of sacred rest for the land.  
**25:6** Yet what the land itself produces in the sabbath year shall be food for you, for your male and female slaves, for your hired man, and for the stranger lodging under your roof,  
**25:7** for your cattle and for the wild animals in your country. Everything it produces may be used for food.   
**25:8**  You shall count seven sabbaths of years, that is seven times seven years, forty-nine years,  
**25:9** and in the seventh month on the tenth day of the month, on the Day of Atonement, you shall send the ram's horn round. You shall send it through all your land to sound a blast,  
**25:10** and so you shall hallow the fiftieth year and proclaim liberation in the land for all its inhabitants. You shall make this your year of jubilee. Every man of you shall return to his patrimony, every man to his family.  
**25:11** The fiftieth year shall be your jubilee. You shall not sow, and you shall not harvest the self-sown crop, nor shall you gather in the grapes from the unpruned vines,  
**25:12** because it is a jubilee, to be kept holy by you. You shall eat the produce direct from the land.   
**25:13** In this year of jubilee you shall return, every one of you, to his patrimony.  
**25:14** When you sell or buy land amongst yourselves, neither party shall drive a hard bargain.  
**25:15** You shall pay your fellow-countryman according to the number of years since the jubilee, and he shall sell to you according to the number of annual crops.  
**25:16** The more years there are to run, the higher the price, the fewer the years, the lower, because he is selling you a series of crops.  
**25:17** You must not victimize one another, but you shall fear your God, because I am the LORD your God.  
**25:18**  Observe my statutes, keep my judgements and carry them out; and you shall live in the land in security.  
**25:19** The land shall yield its harvest; you shall eat your fill and live there secure.  
**25:20** If you ask what you are to eat during the seventh year, seeing that you will neither sow nor gather the harvest,  
**25:21** I will ordain my blessing for you in the sixth year and the land shall produce a crop to carry over three years.  
**25:22** When you sow in the eighth year, you will still be eating from the earlier crop; you shall eat the old until the new crop is gathered in the ninth year.   
**25:23** No land shall be sold outright, because the land is mine, and you are coming into it as aliens and settlers.  
**25:24** Throughout the whole land of your patrimony, you shall allow land which has been sold to be redeemed.   
**25:25**  When one of you is reduced to poverty and sells part of his patrimony, his next-of-kin who has the duty of redemption shall come and redeem what his kinsman has sold.  
**25:26** When a man has no such next-of-kin and himself becomes able to afford its redemption,  
**25:27** he shall take into account the years since the sale and pay the purchaser the balance up to the jubilee. Then he may return to his patrimony.  
**25:28** But if the man cannot afford to buy back the property, it shall remain in the hands of the purchaser till the year of jubilee. It shall then revert to the original owner, and he shall return to his patrimony.   
**25:29** When a man sells a dwelling-house in a walled town, he shall retain the right of redemption till the end of the year of the sale; for a time he shall have the right of redemption.  
**25:30** If it is not redeemed before a full year is out, the house in the walled town shall vest in perpetuity in the buyer and his descendants; it shall not revert at the jubilee.  
**25:31** Houses in unwalled hamlets shall be treated as property in the open country: the right of redemption shall hold good, and in any case the house shall revert at the jubilee.  
**25:32** Levites shall have the perpetual right to redeem houses of their own patrimony in towns belonging to them.  
**25:33** If one of the Levites does not redeem his house in such a town, then it shall still revert to him at the jubilee, because the houses in Levite towns are their patrimony in Israel.  
**25:34** The common land surrounding their towns shall not be sold, because it is their property in perpetuity.   
**25:35**  When your brother-Israelite is reduced to poverty and cannot support himself in the community, you shall assist him as you would an alien or a stranger, and he shall live with you.  
**25:36** You shall not charge him interest on a loan, either by deducting it in advance from the capital sum, or by adding it on repayment. You shall fear your God, and your brother shall live with you;  
**25:37** you shall not deduct interest when advancing him money nor add interest to the payment due for food supplied on credit.  
**25:38** I am the LORD your God who brought you out of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan and to become your God.   
**25:39**  When your brother is reduced to poverty and sells himself to you, you shall not use him to work for you as a slave.  
**25:40** His status shall be that of a hired man or a stranger lodging with you; he shall work for you until the year of jubilee.  
**25:41** He shall then leave your service, with his children, and go back to his family and to his ancestral property:  
**25:42** because they are my slaves whom I brought out of Egypt, they shall not be sold as slaves are sold.  
**25:43** You shall not drive him with ruthless severity, but you shall fear your God.  
**25:44** Such slaves as you have, male or female, shall come from the nations round about you; from them you may buy slaves.  
**25:45** You may also buy the children of those who have settled and lodge with you and such of their family as are born in the land. These may become your property,  
**25:46** and you may leave them to your sons after you; you may use them as slaves permanently. But your fellow-Israelites you shall not drive with ruthless severity.   
**25:47** When an alien or a stranger living with you becomes rich, and your brother becomes poor and sells himself to the alien or stranger or to a member of some alien family,  
**25:48** he shall have the right of redemption after he has sold himself. One of his brothers may redeem him,  
**25:49** or his uncle, his cousin, or any blood-relation of his family, or, if he can afford it, he may redeem himself.  
**25:50** He and his purchaser together shall reckon from the year when he sold himself to the year of jubilee, and the price shall be adjusted to the number of years. His period of service with his owner shall be reckoned at the rate of a hired man.  
**25:51** If there are still many years to run to the year of jubilee, he must repay for his redemption a proportionate amount of the sum for which he sold himself;  
**25:52** if there are few, he shall reckon and repay accordingly.  
**25:53** He shall have the status of a labourer hired from year to year, and you shall not let him be driven with ruthless severity by his owner.  
**25:54** If the man is not redeemed in the intervening years, he and his children shall be released in the year of jubilee;  
**25:55** for it is to me that the Israelites are slaves, my slaves whom I brought out of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.  

## 26장

**26:1**  YOU SHALL NOT MAKE IDOLS for yourselves; you shall not erect a carved image or a sacred pillar; you shall not put a figured stone on your land to prostrate yourselves upon, because I am the LORD your God.  
**26:2** You shall keep my sabbaths and revere my sanctuary. I am the LORD.   
**26:3** If you conform to my statutes, if you observe my commandments and carry them out,  
**26:4** I will give you rain at the proper time; the land shall yield its produce and the trees of the country-side their fruit.  
**26:5** Threshing shall last till vintage and vintage till sowing; you shall eat your fill and live secure in your land.  
**26:6** I will give peace in the land, and you shall lie down to sleep with no one to terrify you. I will rid your land of dangerous beasts and it shall not be ravaged by war.  
**26:7** You shall put your enemies to flight and they shall fall in battle before you.  
**26:8** Five of you shall pursue a hundred and a hundred of you ten thousand; so shall your enemies fall in battle before you.  
**26:9** I will look upon you with favour, I will make you fruitful and increase your numbers: I will give my covenant with you its full effect.  
**26:10** Your old harvest shall last you in store until you have to clear out the old to make room for the new.  
**26:11** I will establish my Tabernacle among you and will not spurn you.  
**26:12** I will walk to and fro among you; I will become your God and you shall become my people.  
**26:13** I am the LORD your God who brought you out of Egypt and let you be their slaves no longer; I broke the bars of your yoke and enabled you to walk upright.   
**26:14**  But if you do not listen to me, if you fail to keep all these commandments of mine, if you reject my statutes,  
**26:15** if you spurn my judgements, and do not obey all my commandments, but break my covenant,  
**26:16** then be sure that this is what I will do: I will bring upon you sudden terror, wasting disease, recurrent fever, and plagues that dim the sight and cause the appetite to fail. You shall sow your seed to no purpose, for your enemies shall eat the crop.  
**26:17** I will set my face against you, and you shall be routed by your enemies. Those that hate you shall hound you on until you run when there is no pursuit.   
**26:18** If after all this you do not listen to me, I will go on to punish you seven times over for your sins.  
**26:19** I will break down your stubborn pride. I will make the sky above you like iron and the earth beneath you like bronze.  
**26:20** Your strength shall be spent in vain; your land shall not yield its produce nor the trees of the land their fruit.   
**26:21** If you still defy me and refuse to listen, I will multiply your calamities seven times, as your sins deserve.  
**26:22** I will send wild beasts among you; they shall tear your children from you, destroy your cattle and bring your numbers low; and your roads shall be deserted.  
**26:23** If after all this you have not learnt discipline but still defy me,  
**26:24** I in turn will defy you and scourge you seven times over for your sins.  
**26:25** I will bring war in vengeance upon you, vengeance irrevocable under covenant; you shall be herded into your cities, I will send pestilence among you, and you shall be given over to the enemy.  
**26:26** I will cut short your daily bread until ten women can bake your bread in a single oven; they shall dole it out by weight, and though you eat, you shall not be satisfied.   
**26:27** If in spite of this you do not listen to me and still defy me,  
**26:28** I will defy you in anger, and I myself will punish you seven times over for your sins.  
**26:29** Instead of meat you shall eat your sons and your daughters.  
**26:30** I will destroy your hill-shrines and demolish your incense-altars. I will pile your rotting carcasses on the rotting logs that were your idols, and I will spurn you.  
**26:31** I will make your cities desolate and destroy your sanctuaries; the soothing odour of your offerings I will not accept.  
**26:32** I will destroy your land, and the enemies who occupy it shall be appalled.  
**26:33** I will scatter you among the heathen, and I will pursue you with the naked sword; your land shall be desolate and your cities heaps of rubble.  
**26:34** Then, all the time that it lies desolate, while you are in exile in the land of your enemies, your land shall enjoy its sabbaths to the full.  
**26:35** All the time of its desolation it shall have the sabbath rest which it did not have when you lived there.  
**26:36** And I will make those of you who are left in the land of your enemies so ridden with fear that, when a leaf flutters behind them in the wind, they shall run as if it were the sword behind them; they shall fall with no one in pursuit.  
**26:37** Though no one pursues them they shall stumble over one another, as if the sword were behind them, and there shall be no stand made against the enemy.  
**26:38** You shall meet your end among the heathen, and your enemies' land shall swallow you up.  
**26:39** Those who are left shall pine away in an enemy land under their own iniquities; and with their fathers' iniquities upon them too, they shall pine away as they did.   
**26:40** But though they confess their iniquity, their own and their fathers', their treachery, and even their defiance of me,  
**26:41** I will defy them in my turn and carry them off into their enemies' land. Yet if then their stubborn spirit is broken and they accept their punishment in full,  
**26:42** I will remember my covenant with Jacob and my covenant with Isaac, yes, and my covenant with Abraham, and I will remember the land.  
**26:43** The land shall be rid of its people and enjoy in full its sabbaths while it lies desolate, and they shall pay in full the penalty because they rejected my judgements and spurned my statutes.  
**26:44** Yet even then, in their enemies' land, I shall not have rejected nor spurned them, bringing them to an end and so breaking my covenant with them, because I am the LORD their God.  
**26:45** I will remember on their behalf the covenant with the men of former times whom I brought out of Egypt in full sight of all the nations, that I might be their God. I am the LORD.   
**26:46** These are the statutes, the judgements, and the laws which the LORD established between himself and the Israelites on Mount Sinai through Moses.  

## 27장

**27:1**  THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES and said,  
**27:2** Speak to the Israelites in these words: When a man makes a special vow to the LORD which requires your valuation of living persons,  
**27:3** a male between twenty and sixty years old shall be valued at fifty silver shekels, that is shekels by the sacred standard.  
**27:4** If it is a female, she shall be valued at thirty shekels.  
**27:5** If the person is between five years old and twenty, the valuation shall be twenty shekels for a male and ten for a female.  
**27:6** If the person is between a month and five years old, the valuation shall be five shekels for a male and three for a female.  
**27:7** If the person is over sixty and a male, the valuation shall be fifteen shekels, but if a female, ten shekels.  
**27:8** If the man is too poor to pay the amount of your valuation, the person shall be set before the priest, and the priest shall value him according to the sum which the man who makes the vow can afford: the priest shall make the valuation.   
**27:9** If the vow concerns a beast such as may be offered as an offering to the LORD, then every gift shall be holy to the LORD.  
**27:10** He shall not change it for another, or substitute good for bad or bad for good. But if a substitution is in fact made of one beast for another, then both the original beast and its substitute shall be holy to the LORD.  
**27:11** If the vow concerns any unclean beast such as may not be offered as an offering to the LORD, then the animal shall be brought before the priest,  
**27:12** and he shall value it whether good or bad. The priest's valuation shall be decisive;  
**27:13** in case of redemption the payment shall be increased by one fifth.   
**27:14** When a man dedicates his house as holy to the LORD, the priest shall value it whether good or bad, and the priest's valuation shall be decisive.  
**27:15** If the donor redeems his house, he shall pay the amount of the valuation increased by one fifth, and the house shall be his.   
**27:16** If a man dedicates to the LORD part of his ancestral land, you shall value it according to the amount of seed-corn it can carry, at the rate of fifty shekels of silver for a homer of barley seed.  
**27:17** If he dedicates his land from the year of jubilee, it shall stand at your valuation;  
**27:18** but if he dedicates it after the year of jubilee, the priest shall estimate the price in silver according to the number of years remaining till the next year of jubilee, and this shall be deducted from your valuation.  
**27:19** If the man who dedicates his field should redeem it, he shall pay the amount of your valuation in silver, increased by one fifth, and it shall be his.  
**27:20** If he does not redeem it but sells the land to another man, it shall no longer be redeemable;  
**27:21** when the land reverts at the year of jubilee, it shall be like land that has been devoted, holy to the LORD. It shall belong to the priest as his patrimony.   
**27:22** If a man dedicates to the LORD land which he has bought, land which is not part of his ancestral land,  
**27:23** the priest shall estimate the amount of the value for the period until the year of jubilee, and the man shall give the amount fixed as at that day; it is holy to the LORD.  
**27:24** At the year of jubilee the land shall revert to the man from whom he bought it, whose patrimony it is.  
**27:25** Every valuation you make shall be made by the sacred standard (twenty gerahs to the shekel).   
**27:26** Notwithstanding, no man may dedicate to the LORD the first-born of a beast which in any case has to be offered as a first-born, whether an ox or a sheep. It is the LORD's.  
**27:27** If it is any unclean beast, he may redeem it at your valuation and shall add one fifth; but if it is not redeemed, it shall be sold at your valuation.  
**27:28** Notwithstanding, nothing which a man devotes to the LORD irredeemably from his own property, whether man or beast or ancestral land, may be sold or redeemed. Everything so devoted is most holy to the LORD.  
**27:29** No human being thus devoted may be redeemed, but he shall be put to death.   
**27:30** Every tithe on land, whether from grain or from the fruit of a tree, belongs to the LORD; it is holy to the LORD.  
**27:31** If a man wishes to redeem any of his tithe, he shall pay its value increased by one fifth.  
**27:32** Every tenth creature that passes under the counting rod shall be holy to the LORD; this applies to all tithes of cattle and sheep.  
**27:33** There shall be no inquiry whether it is good or bad, and no substitution. If any substitution is made, then both the tithe-animal and its substitute shall be forfeit as holy; it shall not be redeemed.   
**27:34** These are the commandments which the LORD gave Moses for the Israelites on Mount Sinai.
