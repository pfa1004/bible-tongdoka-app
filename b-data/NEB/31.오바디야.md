# 31.오바디야 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  The vision of Obadiah: what the Lord GOD has said concerning Edom. <br>When a herald was sent out among the nations, crying, <br>'Rouse yourselves; <br>let us rouse ourselves to battle against Edom', <br>I heard this message from the LORD:  
**1:2**  Look, I make you the least of all nations, <br>an object of contempt.   
**1:3** Your proud, insolent heart has led you astray; <br>you who haunt the crannies among the rocks, <br>making your home on the heights, <br>you say to yourself, 'Who can bring me to the ground?'   
**1:4** Though you soar as high as a vulture <br>and your nest is set among the stars, <br>thence I will bring you down. <br>This is the very word of the LORD.  
**1:5**  If thieves or robbers come to you by night, <br>though your loss be heavy, <br>they will steal only what they want; <br>if vintagers come to you, <br>will they not leave gleanings?   
**1:6** But see how Esau's treasure is ransacked, <br>his secret wealth hunted out!   
**1:7** All your former allies march you to the frontier, <br>your confederates mislead you and bring you low, <br>your own kith and kin lay a snare for your feet, <br>a snare that works blindly, without wisdom.   
**1:8** And on that very day <br>I will destroy all the sages of Edom <br>and leave no wisdom on the mount of Esau. <br>This is the very word of the LORD.   
**1:9** Then shall your Warriors, O Teman, be so enfeebled, <br>that every man shall be cut down on the mount of Esau.   
**1:10**  For the murderous violence done to your brother Jacob <br>you shall be covered with shame and cut off for ever.   
**1:11** On the day when you stood aloof, <br>on the day when strangers carried off his wealth, <br>when foreigners trooped in by his gates <br>and parcelled out Jerusalem by lot, <br>you yourselves were of one mind with them.   
**1:12** Do not gloat over your brother on the day of his misfortune, <br>nor rejoice over Judah on his day of ruin; <br>do not boast on the day of distress,   
**1:13** nor enter my people's gates on the day of his downfall. <br>Do not gloat over his fall on the day of his downfall <br>nor seize his treasure on the day of his downfall.   
**1:14** Do not wait at the cross-roads to cut off his fugitives <br>nor betray the survivors on the day of distress.  
**1:15**  For soon the day of the LORD will come on all the nations: <br>you shall be treated as you have treated others, <br>and your deeds will recoil on your own head.   
**1:16** The draught that you have drunk on my holy mountain <br>all the nations shall drink continually; <br>they shall drink and gulp down <br>and shall be as though they had never been;   
**1:17**  but on Mount Zion there shall be those that escape, <br>and it shall be holy, <br>and Jacob shall dispossess those that dispossessed them.   
**1:18** Then shall the house of Jacob be fire, <br>the house of Joseph fame, <br>and the house of Esau shall be chaff; <br>they shall blaze through it and consume it, <br>and the house of Esau shall have no survivor. <br>The LORD has spoken.   
**1:19** Then they shall possess the Negeb, the mount of Esau, <br>and the Shephelah of the Philistines; <br>they shall possess the country-side of Ephraim and Samaria, <br>and Benjamin shall possess Gilead.   
**1:20** Exiles of Israel shall possess Canaan as far as Zarephath, <br>exiles of Jerusalem shall possess the cities of the Negeb.   
**1:21** Those who find safety on Mount Zion shall go up <br>to hold sway over the mount of Esau, <br>and dominion shall belong to the LORD.
