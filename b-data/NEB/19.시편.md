# 19.시편 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  Happy is the man who does not take the wicked for his guide nor walk the road that sinners tread nor take his seat among the scornful;  
**1:2** the law of the LORD is his delight, the law his meditation night and day.  
**1:3** He is like a tree planted beside a watercourse, which yields its fruit in season and its leaf never withers: in all that he does he prospers.  
**1:4** Wicked men are not like this; they are like chaff driven by the wind.  
**1:5** So when judgement comes the wicked shall not stand firm, nor shall sinners stand in the assembly of the righteous.  
**1:6** The LORD watches over the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked is doomed.  

## 2장

**2:1**  Why are the nations in turmoil? Why do the peoples hatch their futile plots?  
**2:2** The kings of the earth stand ready, and the rulers conspire together against the LORD and his anointed king.  
**2:3** 'Let us break their fetters,' they cry, 'let us throw off their chains!'  
**2:4** The Lord who sits enthroned in heaven laughs them to scorn;  
**2:5** then he rebukes them in anger, he threatens them in his wrath. Of me he says,  
**2:6** 'I have enthroned my king on Zion my holy mountain.'  
**2:7** I will repeat the LORD's decree: 'You are my son,' he said; 'this day I become your father.  
**2:8** Ask of me what you will: I will give you nations as your inheritance, the ends of the earth as your possession.  
**2:9** You shall break them with a rod of iron, you shall shatter them like a clay pot.'  
**2:10** Be mindful then, you kings; learn your lesson, rulers of the earth:  
**2:11** (11-12) worship the LORD with reverence; tremble, and kiss the king , lest the LORD be angry and you are struck down in mid course; for his anger flares up in a moment. Happy are all who find refuge in him.  

## 3장

**3:1**  LORD, how my enemies have multiplied! Many rise up against me,  
**3:2** many there are who say of me, 'God will not bring him victory.'  
**3:3** But thou, LORD, art a shield to cover me: thou art my glory, and thou dost raise my head high.  
**3:4** I cry aloud to the LORD, and he answers me from his holy mountain.  
**3:5** I lie down and sleep, and I wake again, for the LORD upholds me.  
**3:6** I will not fear the nations in their myriads who set on me from all sides.  
**3:7** Rise up, LORD; save me, O my God. Thou dost strike all my foes across the face and breakest the teeth of the wicked.  
**3:8**  Thine is the victory, O LORD, and may thy blessing rest upon thy people.  

## 4장

**4:1**  Answer me when I call, O God, maintainer of my right, I was hard pressed, and thou didst set me at large; be gracious to me now and hear my prayer.  
**4:2** Mortal men, how long will you pay me not honour but dishonour, or set your heart on trifles and run after lies?  
**4:3** Know that the LORD has shown me his marvellous love; the LORD hears when I call to him.  
**4:4** However angry your hearts, do not do wrong; though you lie abed resentful , do not break silence:  
**4:5** pay your due of sacrifice, and trust in the LORD.  
**4:6** There are many who say, 'If only we might be prosperous again! But the light of thy presence has fled from us, O LORD.'  
**4:7** Yet in my heart thou hast put more happiness than they enjoyed when there was corn and wine in plenty.  
**4:8**  Now I will lie down in peace, and sleep; for thou alone, O LORD, makest me live unafraid.  

## 5장

**5:1**  Listen to my words, O LORD, consider my inmost thoughts;  
**5:2** heed my cry for help, my king and my God.  
**5:3** In the morning, when I say my prayers, thou wilt hear me. I set out my morning sacrifice and watch for thee, O LORD.  
**5:4** For thou art not a God who welcomes wickedness; evil can be no guest of thine !  
**5:5** There is no place for arrogance before thee; thou hatest evildoers,  
**5:6** thou makest an end of all liars. The LORD detests traitors and men of blood.  
**5:7** But I, through thy great love, may come into thy house, and bow low toward thy holy temple in awe of thee.  
**5:8** Lead me, LORD, in thy righteousness, because my enemies are on the watch; give me a straight path to follow.  
**5:9** There is no trusting what they say, they are nothing but wind. Their throats are an open sepulchre; smooth talk runs off their tongues.  
**5:10** Bring ruin on them, O God; let them fall by their own devices. Cast them out, after all their rebellions, for they have defied thee.  
**5:11** But let all who take refuge in thee rejoice, let them for ever break into shouts of joy; shelter those who love thy name, that they may exult in thee.  
**5:12** For thou, O LORD, wilt bless the righteous; thou wilt hedge him round with favour as with a shield.  

## 6장

**6:1**  O LORD, do not condemn me in thy anger, do not punish me in thy fury.  
**6:2** Be merciful to me, O LORD, for I am weak; heal me, my very bones are shaken;  
**6:3** my soul quivers in dismay. And thou, O LORD—how long?  
**6:4** Come back, O LORD; set my soul free, deliver me for thy love's sake.  
**6:5** None talk of thee among the dead; who praises thee in Sheol?  
**6:6** I am wearied with groaning; all night long my pillow is wet with tears, I soak my bed with weeping.  
**6:7** Grief dims my eyes; they are worn out with all my woes.  
**6:8** Away from me, all you evildoers, for the LORD has heard the sound of my weeping.  
**6:9** The LORD has heard my entreaty; the LORD will accept my prayer.  
**6:10** All my enemies shall be confounded and dismayed; they shall turn away in sudden confusion.  

## 7장

**7:1**  O LORD my God, in thee I find refuge; save me, rescue me from my pursuers,  
**7:2** before they tear at my throat like a lion and carry me off beyond hope of rescue.  
**7:3** O LORD my God, if I have done any of these things— if I have stained my hands with guilt,  
**7:4** if I have repaid a friend evil for good or set free an enemy who attacked me without cause,  
**7:5** may my adversary come after me and overtake me, trample my life to the ground and lay my honour in the dust!  
**7:6**  Arise, O LORD, in thy anger, rouse thyself in wrath against my foes. Awake, my God who hast ordered that justice be done;  
**7:7** let the peoples assemble around thee, and take thou thy seat on high above them.  
**7:8** O LORD, thou who dost pass sentence on the nations, O LORD, judge me as my righteousness deserves, for I am clearly innocent.  
**7:9** Let wicked men do no more harm, establish the reign of righteousness , thou who examinest both heart and mind, thou righteous God.  
**7:10**  God, the High God, is my shield who saves men of honest heart.  
**7:11** God is a just judge, every day he requites the raging enemy.  
**7:12**  He sharpens his sword, strings his bow and makes it ready.  
**7:13** He has prepared his deadly shafts and tipped his arrows with fire.  
**7:14** But the enemy is in labour with iniquity; he conceives mischief, and his brood is lies.  
**7:15** He has made a pit and dug it deep, and he himself shall fall into the hole that he has made.  
**7:16** His mischief shall recoil upon himself, and his violence fall on his own head.  
**7:17**  I will praise the LORD for his righteousness and sing a psalm to the name of the LORD Most High.  

## 8장

**8:1**  O LORD our sovereign, how glorious is thy name in all the earth! Thy majesty is praised high as the heavens.  
**8:2** Out of the mouths of babes, of infants at the breast, thou hast rebuked the mighty, silencing enmity and vengeance to teach thy foes a lesson.  
**8:3** When I look up at thy heavens, the work of thy fingers, the moon and the stars set in their place by thee,  
**8:4** what is man that thou shouldst remember him, mortal man that thou shouldst care for him?  
**8:5** Yet thou hast made him little less than a god, crowning him with glory and honour.  
**8:6** Thou makest him master over all thy creatures; thou hast put everything under his feet:  
**8:7** all sheep and oxen, all the wild beasts,  
**8:8** the birds in the air and the fish in the sea, and all that moves along the paths of ocean.  
**8:9**  O LORD our sovereign, how glorious is thy name in all the earth!  

## 9장

**9:1**  I will praise thee, O LORD, with all my heart, I will tell the story of thy marvellous acts.  
**9:2** I will rejoice and exult in thee, I will praise thy name in psalms, O thou Most High,  
**9:3** when my enemies turn back, when they fall headlong and perish at thy appearing;  
**9:4** for thou hast upheld my right and my cause, seated on thy throne, thou righteous judge.  
**9:5** Thou hast rebuked the nations and overwhelmed the ungodly, thou hast blotted out their name for all time.  
**9:6** The strongholds of the enemy are thrown down for evermore; thou hast laid their cities in ruins, all memory of them is lost;  
**9:7** The LORD thunders , he sits enthroned for ever: he has set up his throne, his judgement-seat.  
**9:8** He it is who will judge the world with justice and try the cause of the peoples fairly.  
**9:9** So may the LORD be a tower of strength for the oppressed, a tower of strength in time of need,  
**9:10** that those who acknowledge thy name may trust in thee; for thou, LORD, dost not forsake those who seek thee.  
**9:11** Sing psalms to the LORD who dwells in Zion, proclaim his deeds among the nations.  
**9:12** For the Avenger of blood has remembered men's desire, and has not forgotten the cry of the poor.  
**9:13**  Have pity on me, O LORD; look upon my affliction, thou who hast lifted me up and caught me back from the gates of death,  
**9:14** that I may repeat all thy praise and exult at this deliverance in the gates of Zion's city.  
**9:15**  The nations have plunged into a pit of their own making; their own feet are entangled in the net which they hid.  
**9:16** Now the LORD makes himself known. Justice is done: the wicked man is trapped in his own devices.  
**9:17** They rush blindly down to Sheol, the wicked, all the nations who are heedless of God.  
**9:18** But the poor shall not always be unheeded nor the hope of the destitute be always vain.  
**9:19** Arise, LORD, give man no chance to boast his strength; summon the nations before thee for judgement.  
**9:20** Strike them with fear, O LORD, let the nations know that they are but men.  

## 10장

**10:1**  Why stand so far off, LORD, hiding thyself in time of need?  
**10:2** The wicked man in his pride hunts down the poor: may his crafty schemes be his own undoing!  
**10:3** The wicked man is obsessed with his own desires, and in his greed gives wickedness his blessing;  
**10:4** arrogant as he is, he scorns the LORD and leaves no place for God in all his schemes.  
**10:5** His ways are always devious; thy judgements are beyond his grasp , and he scoffs at all restraint.  
**10:6** He says to himself, 'I shall never be shaken; no misfortune can check my course .'  
**10:7** His mouth is full of lies and violence; mischief and trouble lurk under his tongue.  
**10:8** He lies in ambush in the villages and murders innocent men by stealth. He is watching intently for some poor wretch;  
**10:9** he seizes him and drags him away in his net; he crouches stealthily, like a lion in its lair crouching to seize its victim;  
**10:10** the good man is struck down and sinks to the ground, and poor wretches fall into his toils.  
**10:11** He says to himself, 'God has forgotten, he has hidden his face and has seen nothing.'  
**10:12**  Arise, LORD, set thy hand to the task; do not forget the poor, O God.  
**10:13** Why, O God, has the wicked man rejected thee and said to himself that thou dost not care?  
**10:14** Thou seest that mischief and trouble are his companions, thou takest the matter into thy own hands. The poor victim commits himself to thee; fatherless, he finds in thee his helper.  
**10:15** Break the power of wickedness and wrong; hunt out all wickedness until thou canst find no more.  
**10:16**  The LORD is king for ever and ever; the nations have vanished from his land.  
**10:17** Thou hast heard the lament of the humble, O LORD, and art attentive to their heart's desire,  
**10:18** bringing justice to the orphan and the downtrodden that fear may never drive men from their homes again.  

## 11장

**11:1**  In the LORD I have found my refuge; why do you say to me, 'flee to the mountains like a bird;  
**11:2** see how the wicked string their bows and fit the arrow to the string, to shoot down honest men out of the darkness'?  
**11:3** When foundations are undermined, what can the good man do?  
**11:4** The LORD is in his holy temple, the LORD's throne is in heaven. His eye is upon mankind, he takes their measure at a glance.  
**11:5** The LORD weighs just and unjust and hates with all his soul the lover of violence.  
**11:6** He shall rain down red-hot coals upon the wicked; brimstone and scorching winds shall be the cup they drink.  
**11:7** For the LORD is just and loves just dealing; his face is turned towards the upright man.  

## 12장

**12:1**  Help, LORD, for loyalty is no more; good faith between man and man is over.  
**12:2** One man lies to another: they talk with smooth lip and double heart.  
**12:3** May the LORD make an end of such smooth lips and the tongue that talks so boastfully!  
**12:4** They said, 'Our tongue can win the day. Words are our ally; who can master us?'  
**12:5** 'For the ruin of the poor, for the groans of the needy, now I will arise,' says the LORD, 'I will place him in the safety for which he longs.'  
**12:6**  The words of the LORD are pure words: silver refined in a crucible, gold seven times purified.  
**12:7** Do thou, LORD, protect us and guard us from a profligate and evil generation .  
**12:8** The wicked flaunt themselves on every side, while profligacy stands high among mankind.  

## 13장

**13:1**  How long, O LORD, wilt thou quite forget me? How long wilt thou hide thy face from me?  
**13:2** How long must I suffer anguish in my soul, grief in my heart, day and night? How long shall my enemy lord it over me?  
**13:3** Look now and answer me, O LORD my God. Give light to my eyes lest I sleep the sleep of death,  
**13:4** lest my adversary say, 'I have overthrown him', and my enemies rejoice at my downfall.  
**13:5** But for my part I trust in thy true love. My heart shall rejoice, for thou hast set me free.  
**13:6** I will sing to the LORD, who has granted all my desire.  

## 14장

**14:1**  The impious fool says in his heart, 'There is no God. How vile men are, how depraved and loathsome; not one does anything good!  
**14:2** The LORD looks down from heaven on all mankind to see if any act wisely, if any seek out God.  
**14:3** But all are disloyal, all are rotten to the core; not one does anything good, no, not even one.  
**14:4**  Shall they not rue it, all evildoers who devour my people as men devour bread, and never call upon the LORD?  
**14:5** There they were in dire alarm; for God was in the brotherhood of the godly.  
**14:6** The resistance of their victim was too much for them, because the LORD was his refuge.  
**14:7**  If only Israel's deliverance might come out of Zion! When the LORD restores his people's fortunes, let Jacob rejoice, let Israel be glad.  

## 15장

**15:1**  O LORD, who may lodge in thy tabernacle? Who may dwell on thy holy mountain?  
**15:2** The man of blameless life, who does what is right and speaks the truth from his heart;  
**15:3** who has no malice on his tongue, who never wrongs a friend and tells no tales against his neighbour;  
**15:4** the man who shows his scorn for the worthless and honours all who fear the LORD; who swears to his own hurt and does not retract;  
**15:5** who does not put his money out to usury and takes no bribe against an innocent man. He who does these things shall never be brought low.  

## 16장

**16:1**  Keep me, O God, for in thee have I found refuge.  
**16:2** I have said to the LORD, 'Thou, Lord, art my felicity.'  
**16:3** The gods whom earth holds sacred are all worthless, and cursed are all who make them their delight ,  
**16:4** those who run after them find trouble without end. I will not offer them libations of blood nor take their names upon my lips.  
**16:5** Thou, LORD, my allotted portion, thou my cup, thou dost enlarge my boundaries:  
**16:6** the lines fall for me in pleasant places, indeed I am well content with my inheritance.  
**16:7** I will bless the LORD who has given me counsel: in the night-time wisdom comes to me in my inward parts.  
**16:8** I have set the LORD continually before me: with him at my right hand I cannot be shaken.  
**16:9** Therefore my heart exults and my spirit rejoices, my body too rests unafraid;  
**16:10** for thou wilt not abandon me to Sheol not suffer thy faithful servant to see the pit.  
**16:11**  Thou wilt show me the path of life; in thy presence is the fullness of joy, in thy right hand pleasures for evermore.  

## 17장

**17:1**  Hear, LORD, my plea for justice, give my cry a hearing, listen to my prayer, for it is innocent of all deceit.  
**17:2** Let judgement in my cause issue from thy lips, let thine eyes be fixed on justice.  
**17:3** Thou hast tested my heart and watched me all night long; thou hast assayed me and found in me no mind to evil.  
**17:4** I will not speak of the deeds of men; I have taken good note of all thy sayings.  
**17:5** I have not strayed from the course of duty; I have followed thy path and never stumbled.  
**17:6** I call upon thee, O God, for thou wilt answer me. Bend down thy ear to me, listen to my words.  
**17:7** Show me how marvellous thy true love can be, who with thy hand dost save all who seek sanctuary from their enemies.  
**17:8** Keep me like the apple of thine eye; hide me in the shadow of thy wings  
**17:9** from the wicked who obstruct me, from deadly foes who throng round me.  
**17:10** They have stifled all compassion; their mouths are full of pride;  
**17:11** they press me hard , now they hem me in, on the watch to bring me to the ground.  
**17:12** The enemy is like a lion eager for prey, like a young lion crouching in ambush.  
**17:13** Arise, LORD, meet him face to face and bring him down. Save my life from the wicked;  
**17:14** make an end of them with thy sword. With thy hand, O LORD, make an end of them ; thrust them out of this world in the prime of their life, gorged as they are with thy good things, blest with many sons and leaving their children wealth in plenty.  
**17:15**  But my plea is just: I shall see thy face, and be blest with a vision of thee when I awake.  

## 18장

**18:1**  I love thee, O LORD my strength.  
**18:2**  The LORD is my stronghold, my fortress and my champion, my God, my rock where I find safety, my shield, my mountain refuge, my strong tower.  
**18:3** I will call on the LORD to whom all praise is due, and I shall be delivered from my enemies.  
**18:4** When the bonds of death held me fast, destructive torrents overtook me,  
**18:5** the bonds of Sheol tightened round me, the snares of death were set to catch me;  
**18:6** then in anguish of heart I cried to the LORD, I called for help to my God; he heard me from his temple, and my cry reached his ears.  
**18:7** The earth heaved and quaked, the foundations of the mountains shook; they heaved, because he was angry.  
**18:8** Smoke rose from his nostrils, devouring fire came out of his mouth, glowing coals and searing heat.  
**18:9** He swept the skies aside as he descended, thick darkness lay under his feet.  
**18:10** He rode on a cherub, he flew through the air; he swooped on the wings of the wind.  
**18:11** He made darkness around him his hiding-place and dense vapour his canopy .  
**18:12** Thick clouds came out of the radiance before him, hailstones and glowing coals.  
**18:13** The LORD thundered from the heavens and the voice of the Most High spoke out .  
**18:14** He loosed his arrows, he sped them far and wide, he shot forth lightning shafts and sent them echoing.  
**18:15** The channels of the sea-bed were revealed, the foundations of earth laid bare at the LORD's rebuke, at the blast of the breath of his nostrils.  
**18:16** He reached down from the height and took me, he drew me out of mighty waters,  
**18:17** he rescued me from my enemies, strong as they were, from my foes when they grew too powerful for me.  
**18:18** They confronted me in the hour of my peril, but the LORD was my buttress.  
**18:19** He brought me out into an open place, he rescued me because he delighted in me.  
**18:20** The LORD rewarded me as my righteousness deserved; my hands were clean, and he requited me.  
**18:21** For I have followed the ways of the LORD and have not turned wickedly from my God;  
**18:22** all his laws are before my eyes, I have not failed to follow his decrees.  
**18:23** In his sight I was blameless and kept myself from wilful sing  
**18:24** the LORD requited me as my righteousness deserved and the purity of my life in his eyes.  
**18:25**  With the loyal thou showest thyself loyal and with the blameless man blameless.  
**18:26** With the savage man thou showest thyself savage, and tortuous with the perverse.  
**18:27** Thou deliverest humble folk, and bringest proud looks down to earth.  
**18:28** Thou, LORD, dost make my lamp burn bright, and my God will lighten my darkness.  
**18:29** With thy help I leap over a bank, by God's aid I spring over a wall.  
**18:30**  The way of God is perfect, the LORD's word has stood the test; he is the shield of all who take refuge in him.  
**18:31** What god is there but the LORD? What rock but our God?—  
**18:32** the God who girds me with strength and makes my way blameless,  
**18:33** who makes me swift as a hind and sets me secure on the mountains;  
**18:34** who trains my hands for battle, and my arms aim an arrow tipped with bronze.  
**18:35**  Thou hast given me the shield of thy salvation, thy hand sustains me, thy providence makes me great.  
**18:36** Thou givest me room for my steps, my feet have not faltered.  
**18:37** I pursue my enemies and overtake them, I do not return until I have made an end of them.  
**18:38** I strike them down and they will never rise again; they fall beneath my feet.  
**18:39** Thou dost arm me with strength for the battle and dost subdue my foes before me.  
**18:40** Thou settest my foot on my enemies' necks, and I bring to nothing those that hate me.  
**18:41** They cry out and there is no one to help them, they cry to the LORD and he does not answer.  
**18:42** I will pound them fine as dust before the wind, like mud in the streets will I trample them .  
**18:43** Thou dost deliver me from the clamour of the people, and makest me master of the nations. A people I never knew shall be my subjects;  
**18:44** as soon as they hear tell of me, they shall obey me, and foreigners shall come cringing to me.  
**18:45** Foreigners shall be brought captive to me, and emerge from their strongholds.  
**18:46**  The LORD lives, blessed is my rock, high above all is God who saves me.  
**18:47**  O God, who grantest me vengeance, who layest nations prostrate at my feet,  
**18:48** who dost rescue me from my foes and set me over my enemies, thou dost deliver me from violent men.  
**18:49** Therefore, LORD, I will praise thee among the nations and sing psalms to thy name,  
**18:50** to one who gives his king great victories and in all his acts keeps faith with his anointed king, with David and his descendants for ever.  

## 19장

**19:1**  The heavens tell out the glory of God, the vault of heaven reveals his handiwork.  
**19:2** One day speaks to another, night with night shares its knowledge,  
**19:3** and this without speech or language or sound of any voice.  
**19:4** Their music goes out through all the earth, their words reach to the end of the world. In them a tent is fixed for the sun,  
**19:5** who comes out like a bridegroom from his wedding canopy, rejoicing like a strong man to run his race.  
**19:6** His rising is at one end of the heavens, his circuit touches their farthest ends; and nothing is hidden from his heat.  
**19:7**  The law of the LORD is perfect and revives the soul. The LORD's instruction never fails, and makes the simple wise.  
**19:8** The precepts of the LORD are right and rejoice the heart. The commandment of the LORD shines clear and gives light to the eyes.  
**19:9** The fear of the LORD is pure and abides for ever. The LORD's decrees are true and righteous every one,  
**19:10** more to be desired than gold, pure gold in plenty, sweeter than syrup or honey from the comb.  
**19:11** It is these that give thy servant warning, and he who keeps them wins a great reward.  
**19:12**  Who is aware of his unwitting sins? Cleanse me of any secret fault.  
**19:13** Hold back thy servant also from sins of self-will, lest they get the better of me. Then I shall be blameless and innocent of any great transgression.  
**19:14**  May all that I say and think be acceptable to thee, O LORD, my rock and my redeemer!  

## 20장

**20:1**  May the LORD answer you in the hour of trouble! The name of Jacob's God be your tower of strength,  
**20:2** give you help from the sanctuary and send you support from Zion!  
**20:3** May he remember all your offerings and look with favour on your rich sacrifices,  
**20:4** give you your heart's desire and grant success to all your plans!  
**20:5** Let us sing aloud in praise of your victory, let us do homage to the name of our God! The LORD grant all you ask!  
**20:6**  Now I know that the LORD has given victory to his anointed king: he will answer him from his holy heaven with the victorious might of his right hand.  
**20:7** Some boast of chariots and some of horses, but our boast is the name of the LORD our God.  
**20:8** They totter and fall, but we rise up and are full of courage.  
**20:9**  O LORD, save the king, and answer us in the hour of our calling.  

## 21장

**21:1**  The king rejoices in thy might, O LORD: well may he exult in thy victory,  
**21:2** for thou hast given him his heart's desire and hast not refused him what he asked.  
**21:3** Thou dost welcome him with blessings and prosperity and set a crown of fine gold upon his head.  
**21:4** He asked of thee life, and thou didst give it him, length of days for ever and ever.  
**21:5** Thy salvation has brought him great glory; thou dost invest him with majesty and honour,  
**21:6** for thou bestowest blessings on him for evermore and dost make him glad with joy in thy presence.  
**21:7** The king puts his trust in the LORD; the loving care of the Most High holds him unshaken.  
**21:8**  Your hand shall reach all your enemies: your right hand shall reach those who hate you;  
**21:9** at your coming you shall plunge them into a fiery furnace; the LORD in his anger will strike them down, and fire shall consume them.  
**21:10** It will exterminate their offspring from the earth and rid mankind of their posterity.  
**21:11** For they have aimed wicked blows at you, they have plotted mischief but could not prevail;  
**21:12** but you will catch them round the shoulders and will aim with your bow-strings at their faces.

**135:2** who stand in the house of the LORD, in the temple courts of our God.  
**135:3** Praise the LORD, for that is good; honour his name with psalms, for that is pleasant.  
**135:4** The LORD has chosen Jacob to be his own and Israel as his special treasure.  
**135:5** I know that the LORD is great, that our Lord is above all gods.  
**135:6** Whatever the LORD pleases, that he does, in heaven and on earth, in the sea, in the depths of ocean.  
**135:7** He brings up the mist from the ends of the earth, he opens rifts for the rain, and brings the wind out of his storehouses.  
**135:8** He struck down all the first-born in Egypt, both man and beast.  
**135:9** In Egypt he sent signs and portents against Pharaoh and all his subjects.  
**135:10** He struck down mighty nations and slew great kings,  
**135:11** Sihon king of the Amorites, Og the king of Bashan, and all the princes of Canaan,  
**135:12** and gave their land to Israel, to Israel his people as their patrimony.  
**135:13** O LORD, thy name endures for ever; thy renown, O LORD, shall last for all generations.  
**135:14** The LORD will give his people justice and have compassion on his servants.  
**135:15** The gods of the nations are idols of silver and gold, made by the hands of men.  
**135:16** They have mouths that cannot speak and eyes that cannot see;  
**135:17** they have ears that do not hear, and there is no breath in their nostrils .  
**135:18** Their makers grow like them, and so do all who trust in them.  
**135:19** O house of Israel, bless the LORD; O house of Aaron, bless the LORD.  
**135:20** O house of Levi, bless the LORD; you who fear the LORD, bless the LORD.  
**135:21** Blessed from Zion be the LORD who dwells in Jerusalem. O praise the LORD.

## 139장

**139:1**  LORD, thou hast examined me and knowest me.  
**139:2** Thou knowest all, whether I sit down or rise up; thou hast discerned my thoughts from afar.  
**139:3** Thou hast traced my journey and my resting places, and art familiar with all my paths.  
**139:4** For there is not a word on my tongue but thou, LORD, knowest them all .  
**139:5** Thou hast kept close guard before me and behind and hast spread thy hand over me.  
**139:6** Such knowledge is beyond my understanding, so high that I cannot reach it.  
**139:7** Where can I escape from thy spirit? Where can I flee from thy presence?  
**139:8** If I climb up to heaven, thou art there; if I make my bed in Sheol, again I find thee.  
**139:9** If I take my flight to the frontiers of the morning or dwell at the limit of the western sea,  
**139:10** even there thy hand will meet me and thy right hand will hold me fast.  
**139:11** If I say, 'Surely darkness will steal over me, night will close around me',  
**139:12** darkness is no darkness for thee and night is luminous as day; to thee both dark and light are one.  
**139:13** Thou it was who didst fashion my inward parts; thou didst knit me together in my mother's womb.  
**139:14** I will praise thee, for thou dost fill me with awe; wonderful thou art, and wonderful thy works. Thou knowest me through and through:  
**139:15** my body is no mystery to thee, how I was secretly kneaded into shape and patterned in the depths of the earth.  
**139:16** Thou didst see my limbs unformed in the womb, and in thy book they are all recorded; day by day they were fashioned, not one of them was late in growing .  
**139:17** How deep I find thy thoughts, O God, how inexhaustible their themes!  
**139:18** Can I count them? They outnumber the grains of sand; to finish the count, my years must equal thine.  
**139:19** O God, if only thou wouldst slay the wicked! If those men of blood would but leave me in peace—  
**139:20** those who provoke thee with deliberate evil and rise in vicious rebellion against thee!  
**139:21** How I hate them, O LORD, that hate thee! I am cut to the quick when they oppose thee;  
**139:22** I hate them with undying hatred; I hold them all my enemies.  
**139:23** Examine me, O God, and know my thoughts; test me, and understand my misgivings.  
**139:24** Watch lest I follow any path that grieves thee; guide me in the ancient ways.
