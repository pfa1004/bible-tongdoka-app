# 22.아가 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  (Bride) I will sing the song of all songs to Solomon  
**1:2** that he may smother me with kisses. Your love is more fragrant than wine,  
**1:3** fragrant is the scent of your perfume, and your name like perfume poured out , for this the maidens love you.  
**1:4** Take me with you, and we will run together; bring me into your chamber, O king. (Companions) Let us rejoice and be glad for you; let us praise your love more than wine, and your caresses more than any song.  
**1:5** (Bride) I am dark but lovely, daughters of Jerusalem, like the tents of Kedar or the tent-curtains of Shalmah.  
**1:6** Do not look down on me; a little dark I may be because I am scorched by the sun. My mother's sons were displeased with me, they sent me to watch over the vineyards; so I did not watch over my own vineyard.  
**1:7** Tell me, my true love, where you mind your flocks, where you rest them at midday, that I may not be left picking lice as I sit among your companions' herds.  
**1:8** (Bridegroom) If you yourself do not know, O fairest of women, go, follow the tracks of the sheep and mind your kids by the shepherds' huts.  
**1:9** I would compare you, my dearest, to Pharaoh's chariot-horses.  
**1:10** Your cheeks are lovely between plaited tresses, your neck with its jewelled chains.  
**1:11** (Companions) We will make you braided plaits of gold set with beads of silver.  
**1:12** (Bride) While the king reclines on his couch, my spikenard gives forth its scent.  
**1:13** My beloved is for me a bunch of myrrh as he lies on my breast,  
**1:14** my beloved is for me a cluster of henna-blossom from the vineyards of En-gedi.  
**1:15** (Bridegroom) How beautiful you are, my dearest, O how beautiful, your eyes are like doves!  
**1:16** (Bride) How beautiful you are, O my love, and how pleasant! (Bridegroom) Our couch is shaded with branches;  
**1:17** the beams of our house are of cedar, our ceilings are all of fir.  

## 2장

**2:1**  (Bride) I am an asphodel in Sharon, a lily growing in the valley.  
**2:2** (Bridegroom) No, a lily among thorns is my dearest among girls.  
**2:3** (Bride) Like an apricot-tree among the trees of the wood, so is my beloved among boys. To sit in its shadow was my delight, and its fruit was sweet to my taste.  
**2:4** He took me into the wine-garden and gave me loving glances.  
**2:5** He refreshed me with raisins, he revived me with apricots; for I was faint with love.  
**2:6** His left arm was under my head, his right arm was round me.  
**2:7** (Bridegroom) I charge you, daughters of Jerusalem, by the spirits and the goddesses of the field: Do not rouse her, do not disturb my love until she is ready .  
**2:8** (Bride) Hark! My beloved! Here he comes, bounding over the mountains, leaping over the hills.  
**2:9** My beloved is like a gazelle or a young wild goat: there he stands outside our wall, peeping in at the windows, glancing through the lattice.  
**2:10** My beloved answered, he said to me: Rise up, my darling; my fairest, come away.  
**2:11** For now the winter is past, the rains are over and gone;  
**2:12** the flowers appear in the country-side; the time is coming when the birds will sing, and the turtle-dove's cooing will be heard in our land;  
**2:13** when the green figs will ripen on the fig-trees and the vines give forth their fragrance. Rise up, my darling; my fairest, come away.  
**2:14** (Bridegroom) My dove, that hides in holes in the cliffs or in crannies on the high ledges, let me see your face, let me hear your voice; for your voice is pleasant, your face is lovely.  
**2:15** (Companions) Catch for us the jackals, the little jackals , that spoil our vineyards, when the vines are in flower.  
**2:16** (Bride) My beloved is mine and I am his; he delights in the lilies.  
**2:17** While the day is cool and the shadows are dispersing, turn, my beloved, and show yourself a gazelle or a young wild goat on the hills where cinnamon grows .  

## 3장

**3:1**  Night after night on my bed I have sought my true love; I have sought him but not found him, I have called him but he has not answered.  
**3:2** I said, 'I will rise and go the rounds of the city, through the streets and the squares, seeking my true love.' I sought him but I did not find him, I called him but he did not answer.  
**3:3** The watchmen, going the rounds of the city, met me, and I asked, 'Have you seen my true love?'  
**3:4** Scarcely had I left them behind me when I met my true love. I seized him and would not let him go until I had brought him to my mother's house, to the room of her who conceived me.  
**3:5** (Bridegroom) I charge you, daughters of Jerusalem, by the spirits and the goddesses of the field: Do not rouse her, do not disturb my love until she is ready .  
**3:6** (Companions) What is this coming up from the wilderness like a column of smoke from burning myrrh or frankincense, from all the powdered spices that merchants bring?  
**3:7** Look; it is Solomon carried in his litter; sixty of Israel's chosen warriors are his escort,  
**3:8** all of them skilled swordsmen, all trained to handle arms, each with his sword ready at his side to ward off the demon of the night.  
**3:9** The palanquin which King Solomon had made for himself was of wood from Lebanon.  
**3:10** Its poles he had made of silver, its head-rest of gold; its seat was of purple stuff, and its lining was of leather.  
**3:11** Come out, daughters of Jerusalem; you daughters of Zion, come out and welcome King Solomon, wearing the crown with which his mother has crowned him, on his wedding day, on his day of joy.  

## 4장

**4:1**  (Bridegroom) How beautiful you are, my dearest, how beautiful! Your eyes behind your veil are like doves, your hair like a flock of goats streaming down Mount Gilead.  
**4:2** Your teeth are like a flock of ewes just shorn which have come up fresh from the dipping; each ewe has twins and none has cast a lamb.  
**4:3** Your lips are like a scarlet thread, and your words are delightful ; your parted lips behind your veil are like a pomegranate cut open.  
**4:4** Your neck is like David's tower, which is built with winding courses; a thousand bucklers hang upon it, and all are warriors' shields.  
**4:5** Your two breasts are like two fawns, twin fawns of a gazelle .  
**4:6** While the day is cool and the shadows are dispersing, I will go to the mountains of myrrh and to the hills of frankincense.  
**4:7** You are beautiful, my dearest, beautiful without a flaw.  
**4:8** Come from Lebanon, my bride; come with me from Lebanon. Hurry down from the top of Amana, from Senir's top and Hermon's, from the lions' lairs, and the hills the leopards haunt.  
**4:9** You have stolen my heart , my sister, you have stolen it , my bride, with one of your eyes, with one jewel of your necklace.  
**4:10** How beautiful are your breasts, my sister, my bride! Your love is more fragrant than wine, and your perfumes sweeter than any spices.  
**4:11** Your lips drop sweetness like the honeycomb, my bride, syrup and milk are under your tongue, and your dress has the scent of Lebanon.  
**4:12**   
**4:13** Your two cheeks are an orchard of pomegranate an orchard full of rare fruits :  
**4:14** spikenard and saffron, sweet-cane and cinnamon with every incense-bearing tree, myrrh and aloes with all the choicest spices. (12) My sister, my bride, is a garden close-locked, a garden close-locked, a fountain sealed.  
**4:15** (Bride) The fountain in my garden is a spring of running water pouring down from Lebanon.  
**4:16**  Awake, north wind, and come, south wind; blow upon my garden that its perfumes may pour forth, that my beloved may come to his garden and enjoy its rare fruits.  

## 5장

**5:1**  (Bridegroom) I have come to my garden, my sister and bride, and have plucked my myrrh with my spices; I have eaten my honey and my syrup, I have drunk my wine and my milk. Eat, friends, and drink, until you are drunk with love.  
**5:2** (Bride) I sleep but my heart is awake. Listen! My beloved is knocking: 'Open to me, my sister, my dearest, my dove, my perfect one; for my head is drenched with dew, my locks with the moisture of the night.'  
**5:3** 'I have stripped off my dress; must I put it on again? I have washed my feet; must I soil them again?'  
**5:4** When my beloved slipped his hand through the latch-hole, my bowels stirred within me.  
**5:5** When I arose to open for my beloved, my hands dripped with myrrh; the liquid myrrh from my fingers ran over the knobs of the bolt.  
**5:6** With my own hands I opened to my love, but my love had turned away and gone by; my heart sank when he turned his back. I sought him but I did not find him, I called him but he did not answer.  
**5:7** The watchmen, going the rounds of the city, met me; they struck me and wounded me; the watchmen on the walls took away my cloak.  
**5:8** I charge you, daughters of Jerusalem, if you find my beloved, will you not tell him that I am faint with love?  
**5:9** (Companions) What is your beloved more than any other, O fairest of women? What is your beloved more than any other, that you give us this charge?  
**5:10** (Bride) My beloved is fair and ruddy, a paragon among ten thousand.  
**5:11** His head is gold, finest gold; his locks are like palm-fronds .  
**5:12** His eyes are like doves beside brooks of water, splashed by the milky water as they sit where it is drawn.  
**5:13** His cheeks are like beds of spices or chests full of perfumes; his lips are lilies, and drop liquid myrrh;  
**5:14** his hands are golden rods set in topaz; his belly a plaque of ivory overlaid with lapis lazuli.  
**5:15** His legs are pillars of marble in sockets of finest gold; his aspect is like Lebanon, noble as cedars.  
**5:16** His whispers are sweetness itself, wholly desirable. Such is my beloved, such is my darling, daughters of Jerusalem.  

## 6장

**6:1**  (Companions) Where has your beloved gone, O fairest of women? Which way did your beloved go, that we may help you to seek him?  
**6:2** (Bride) My beloved has gone down to his garden, to the beds where balsam grows, to delight in the garden and to pick the lilies.  
**6:3** I am my beloved's, and my beloved is mine, he who delights in the lilies.  
**6:4** (Bridegroom) You are beautiful, my dearest, as Tirzah, lovely as Jerusalem .  
**6:5** Turn. your eyes away from me; they dazzle me. Your hair is like a flock of goats streaming down Mount Gilead;  
**6:6** your teeth are like a flock of ewes come up fresh from the dipping, each ewe has twins and none has cast a lamb.  
**6:7** Your parted lips behind your veil are like a pomegranate cut open.  
**6:8** There may be sixty princesses, eighty concubines, and young women past counting,  
**6:9** but there is one alone, my dove, my perfect one, her mother's only child, devoted to the mother who bore her; young girls see her and call her happy, princesses and concubines praise her.  
**6:10** Who is this that looks out like the dawn, beautiful as the moon, bright as the sun, majestic as the starry heavens?  
**6:11** I went down to a garden of nut-trees to look at the rushes by the stream, to see if the vine had budded or the pomegranates were in flower.  
**6:12** I did not know myself; she made me feel more than a prince reigning over the myriads of his people.  
**6:13**  (Companions) Come back, come back, Shulammite maiden, come back, that we may gaze upon you. (Bridegroom) How you love to gaze on the Shulammite maiden, as she moves between the lines of dancers!  

## 7장

**7:1**  How beautiful are your sandalled feet, O prince's daughter! The curves of your thighs are like jewels, the work of a skilled craftsman.  
**7:2** Your navel is a rounded goblet that never shall want for spiced wine. Your belly is a heap of wheat fenced in by lilies.  
**7:3** Your two breasts are like two fawns, twin fawns of a gazelle.  
**7:4** Your neck is like a tower of ivory. Your eyes are the pools in Heshbon, beside the gate of the crowded city . Your nose is like towering Lebanon that looks towards Damascus.  
**7:5** You carry your head like Carmel; the flowing hair on your head is lustrous black, your tresses are braided with ribbons.  
**7:6** How beautiful, how entrancing you are, my loved one, daughter of delights!  
**7:7** You are stately as a palm-tree, and your breasts are the clusters of dates.  
**7:8** I said, 'I will climb up into the palm to grasp its fronds.' May I find your breasts like clusters of grapes on the vine, the scent of your breath like apricots,  
**7:9** and your whispers like spiced wine flowing smoothly to welcome my caresses, gliding down through lips and teeth.  
**7:10** (Bride) I am my beloved's, his longing is all for me.  
**7:11** Come, my beloved, let us go out into the fields to lie among the henna-bushes;  
**7:12** let us go early to the vineyards and see if the vine has budded or its blossom opened, if the pomegranates are in flower. There will I give you my love,  
**7:13** when the mandrakes give their perfume, and all rare fruits are ready at our door, fruits new and old which I have in store for you, my love.  

## 8장

**8:1**  If only you were my own true brother that sucked my mother's breasts! Then, if I found you outside, I would kiss you, and no man would despise me.  
**8:2** I would lead you to the room of the mother who bore me, bring you to her house for you to embrace me ; I would give you mulled wine to drink and the fresh juice of pomegranates,  
**8:3** your left arm under my head and your right arm round me.  
**8:4** (Bridegroom) I charge you, daughters of Jerusalem: Do not rouse her, do not disturb my love until she is ready .  
**8:5** (Companions) Who is this coming up from the wilderness leaning on her beloved? (Bridegroom) Under the apricot-trees I roused you, there where your mother was in labour with you, there where she who bore you was in labour.  
**8:6** Wear me as a seal upon your heart, as a seal upon your arm; for love is strong as death, passion cruel as the grave; it blazes up like blazing fire, fiercer than any flame.  
**8:7** Many waters cannot quench love, no flood can sweep it away; if a man were to offer for love the whole wealth of his house, it would be utterly scorned.  
**8:8** (Companions) We have a little sister who has no breasts; what shall we do for our sister when she is asked in marriage?  
**8:9** If she is a wall, we will build on it a silver parapet, but if she is a door, we will close it up with planks of cedar.  
**8:10** (Bride) I am a wall and my breasts are like towers; so in his eyes I am as one who brings contentment.  
**8:11** Solomon has a vineyard at Baal-hamon; he has let out his vineyard to guardians, and each is to bring for its fruit a thousand pieces of silver.  
**8:12** But my vineyard is mine to give; the thousand pieces are yours, O Solomon, and the guardians of the fruit shall have two hundred.  
**8:13** (Bridegroom) My bride, you who sit in my garden, what is it that my friends are listening to? Let me also hear your voice.  
**8:14**  (Bride) Come into the open, my beloved, and show yourself like a gazelle or a young wild goat on the spice-bearing mountains.
