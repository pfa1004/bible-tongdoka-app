# 2.출애굽기 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  THESE ARE THE NAMES of the Israelites who entered Egypt with Jacob, each with his household:  
**1:2** Reuben, Simeon, Levi and Judah;  
**1:3** Issachar, Zebulun and Benjamin;  
**1:4** Dan and Naphtali, Gad and Asher.  
**1:5** There were seventy of them all told, all direct descendants of Jacob. Joseph was already in Egypt.   
**1:6** In course of time Joseph died, he and all his brothers and that whole generation.  
**1:7** Now the Israelites were fruitful and prolific; they increased in numbers and became very powerful , so that the country was overrun by them.  
**1:8** Then a new king ascended the throne of Egypt, one who knew nothing of Joseph.  
**1:9** He said to his people, 'These Israelites have become too many and too strong for us.  
**1:10** We must take precautions to see that they do not increase any further; or we shall find that, if war breaks out, they will join the enemy and fight against us, and they will become masters of the country.'  
**1:11** So they were made to work in gangs with officers set over them, to break their spirit with heavy labour. This is how Pharaoh's store-cities, Pithom and Rameses, were built.  
**1:12** But the more harshly they were treated, the more their numbers increased beyond all bounds, until the Egyptians came to loathe the sight of them.  
**1:13** So they treated their Israelite slaves with ruthless severity,  
**1:14** and made life bitter for them with cruel servitude, setting them to work on clay and brick-making, and all sorts of work in the fields. In short they made ruthless use of them as slaves in every kind of hard labour.   
**1:15** Then the king of Egypt spoke to the Hebrew midwives, whose names were Shiphrah and Puah.  
**1:16** 'When you are attending the Hebrew women in childbirth,' he told them, 'watch as the child is delivered and if it is a boy, kill him; if it is a girl, let her live.'  
**1:17** But they were God-fearing women. They did not do what the king of Egypt had told them to do, but let the boys live.  
**1:18** So he summoned those Hebrew midwives and asked them why they had done this and let the boys live.  
**1:19** They told Pharaoh that Hebrew women were not like Egyptian women. When they were in labour they gave birth before the midwife could get to them.  
**1:20** So God made the midwives prosper, and the people increased in numbers and in strength.  
**1:21** God gave the midwives homes and families of their own, because they feared him.  
**1:22** Pharaoh then ordered all his people to throw every new-born Hebrew boy into the Nile, but to let the girls live.  

## 2장

**2:1**  A descendant of Levi married a Levite woman  
**2:2** who conceived and bore a son. When she saw what a fine child he was, she hid him for three months,  
**2:3** but she could conceal him no longer. So she got a rush basket for him, made it watertight with clay and tar, laid him in it, and put it among the reeds by the bank of the Nile.  
**2:4** The child's sister took her stand at a distance to see what would happen to him.  
**2:5** Pharaoh's daughter came down to bathe in the river, while her ladies-in-waiting walked along the bank. She noticed the basket among the reeds and sent her slave-girl for it. She took it from her  
**2:6** and when she opened it, she saw the child. It was crying, and she was filled with pity for it. 'Why,' she said, 'it is a little Hebrew boy.'  
**2:7** Thereupon the sister said to Pharaoh's daughter, 'Shall I go and fetch one of the Hebrew women as a wet-nurse to suckle the child for you?'  
**2:8** Pharaoh's daughter told her to go; so the girl went and called the baby's mother.  
**2:9** Then Pharaoh's daughter said to her, 'Here is the child, suckle him for me, and I will pay you for it myself.' So the woman took the child and suckled him.  
**2:10** When the child was old enough, she brought him to Pharaoh's daughter, who adopted him and called him Moses , 'because', she said, 'I drew him out of the water.'  
**2:11**  ONE DAY WHEN MOSES WAS GROWN UP, he went out to his own kinsmen and saw them at their heavy labour. He saw an Egyptian strike one of his fellow Hebrews.  
**2:12** He looked this way and that, and, seeing there was no one about, he struck the Egyptian down and hid his body in the sand.  
**2:13** When he went out next day, two Hebrews were fighting together. He asked the man who was in the wrong, 'Why are you striking him?'  
**2:14** 'Who set you up as an officer and judge over us?' the man replied. 'Do you mean to murder me as you murdered the Egyptian?' Moses was alarmed. 'The thing must have become known', he said to himself.  
**2:15** When Pharaoh heard of it, he tried to put Moses to death, but Moses made good his escape and settled in the land of Midian.   
**2:16** Now the priest of Midian had seven daughters. One day as Moses sat by a well, they came to draw water and filled the troughs to water their father's sheep.  
**2:17** Some shepherds came and drove them away; but Moses got up, took the girls' part and watered their sheep himself.  
**2:18** When the girls came back to their father Reuel, he 'asked, 'How is it that you are back so quickly today?'  
**2:19** 'An Egyptian rescued us from the shepherds,' they answered; 'and he even drew the water for us and watered the sheep.'  
**2:20** 'But where is he then?' he said to his daughters. 'Why did you leave him behind? Go and invite him to eat with us.'  
**2:21** So it came about that Moses agreed to live with the man, and he gave Moses his daughter Zipporah in marriage.  
**2:22** She bore him a son, and Moses called him Gershom, 'because', he said, 'I have become an alien living in a foreign land.'   
**2:23** YEARS PASSED, and the king of Egypt died, but the Israelites still groaned in slavery. They cried out, and their appeal for rescue from their slavery rose up to God.  
**2:24** He heard their groaning, and remembered his covenant with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob;  
**2:25** he saw the plight of Israel, and he took heed of it.  

## 3장

**3:1**  Moses was minding the flock of his father-in-law Jethro, priest of Midian. He led the flock along the side of the wilderness and came to Horeb, the mountain of God.  
**3:2** There the angel of the LORD appeared to him in the flame of a burning bush. Moses noticed that, although the bush was on fire, it was not being burnt up;  
**3:3** so he said to himself, 'I must go across to see this wonderful sight. Why does not the bush burn away?'  
**3:4** When the LORD saw that Moses had turned aside to look, he called to him out of the bush, 'Moses, Moses.' And Moses answered, 'Yes, I am here.'  
**3:5** God said, 'Come no nearer; take off your sandals; the place where you are standing is holy ground.'  
**3:6** Then he said, 'I am the God of your forefathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, the God of Jacob.' Moses covered his face, for he was afraid to gaze on God.   
**3:7** The LORD said, 'I have indeed seen the misery of my people in Egypt. I have heard their outcry against their slave-masters. I have taken heed of their sufferings,  
**3:8** and have come down to rescue them from the power of Egypt, and to bring them up out of that country into a fine, broad land; it is a land ?owing with milk and honey, the home of Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.  
**3:9** The outcry of the Israelites has now reached me; yes, I have seen the brutality of the Egyptians towards them.  
**3:10** Come now; I will send you to Pharaoh and you shall bring my people Israel out of Egypt.'  
**3:11** 'But who am I,' Moses said to God, 'that I should go to Pharaoh, and that I should bring the Israelites out of Egypt?'  
**3:12** God answered, 'I am ' with you. This shall be the proof that it is I who have sent you: when you have brought the people out of Egypt, you shall all worship God here on this mountain.'   
**3:13** Then Moses said to God, 'If I go to the Israelites and tell them that the God of their forefathers has sent me to them, and they ask me his name, what shall I say?'  
**3:14** God answered,"I AM, that is who I am. Tell them that I AM has sent you to them.'  
**3:15** And, God said further, 'You must tell the Israelites this, that it is JEHOVAH the God of their forefathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, the God of Jacob, who has sent you to them. This is my name for ever; this is my. title in every generation.'  
**3:16** Go and assemble the elders of Israel and tell them that JEHOVAH the God of their forefathers, the God of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, has appeared to you and has said,, "I have indeed turned my eyes towards you; I have marked all that has been done to you in Egypt, and  
**3:17** I am resolved to bring you up out of your misery in Egypt, into the country of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, a land flowing with milk and honey."  
**3:18** They will listen to you, and then you and the elders of Israel must go to the king of Egypt. Tell him, "It has happened that the LORD the God of the Hebrews met us. So now give us leave to go a three days' journey Into the wilderness to offer sacrifice to the LORD our God."  
**3:19** I know well that the king of Egypt will not give you leave unless he is compelled.  
**3:20** I shall then stretch out my hand and assail the Egyptians with all the miracles I shall work among them. After that he will send you away.  
**3:21** Further, I will bring this people into such favour with the Egyptians that, when you go, you will not go empty-handed.  
**3:22** Every woman shall ask her neighbour or any woman who lives in her house for jewellery of silver and gold and for clothing. Load your sons and daughters with them, and plunder Egypt.'  

## 4장

**4:1**  Moses answered, 'But they will never believe me or listen to me; they will say, "The LORD did not appear to you." '  
**4:2** The LORD said, 'What have you there in your hand? A staff", Moses answered.  
**4:3** The LORD said, 'Throw it on the ground.' Moses threw it down and it turned into a snake. He ran away from it,  
**4:4** but the LORD said, 'Put your hand out and seize it by the tail.' He did so and gripped it firmly, mid it turned back into a staff in his hand.  
**4:5** 'This is to convince the people that the LORD the God of their forefathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, the God of Jacob, has appeared to you.'  
**4:6** Then the LORD said, 'Put your hand inside the fold of your cloak.' He did so, and when he drew it out the skin was diseased, white as snow.  
**4:7** The LORD said, 'Put it back again', and he did so. When he drew it out this time it was a healthy us the rest of his body.  
**4:8** 'Now,' said the LORD, 'if they do not believe you and do not accept the evidence of the first sign, they may accept the evidence of the second.  
**4:9** But if they are not convinced even by these two signs, and will not accept what you say, then fetch some water from the Nile and pour it out on the dry ground, and the water you take from the Nile will turn to blood on the ground.'   
**4:10** But Moses said, 'O LORD, I have never been a man of ready speech, never in my life, not even now that thou hast spoken to me; I am slow and hesitant of speech.'  
**4:11** The LORD said to him, 'Who is it that gives man speech? Who makes him dumb or deaf? Who makes him clear-sighted or blind? Is it not I, the LORD?  
**4:12** Go now; I will help your speech and tell you what to say.'  
**4:13** But Moses still protested, 'No, Lord, send whom thou wilt.'  
**4:14** At this the LORD grew angry with Moses and said, 'Have you not a brother, Aaron the Levite? He, I know, will do all the speaking. He is already on his way out to meet you, and he will be glad indeed to see you.  
**4:15** You shall speak to him and put the words in his mouth; I will help both of you to speak and tell you both what to do.  
**4:16** He will do all the speaking to the people for you, he will be the mouthpiece, and you will be the god he speaks for.  
**4:17** But take this staff, for with it you are to work the signs.'  
**4:18**  At length Moses went back to Jethro his father-in-law and said, 'Let me return to my kinsfolk in Egypt and see if they are still alive.' Jethro told him to go and wished him well.   
**4:19** THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES in Midian and said to him, 'Go back to Egypt, for all those who wished to kill you are dead.'  
**4:20** So Moses took his wife and children, mounted them on an ass and set out for Egypt with the staff of God in his hand.  
**4:21** The LORD said to Moses, 'While you are on your way back to Egypt, keep in mind all the portents I have given you power to show. You shall display these before Pharaoh, but I will make him obstinate and he will not let the people go.  
**4:22** Then tell Pharaoh that these are the words of the LORD:  
**4:23** "Israel is my first-born son. I have told you to let my son go, so that he may worship me. You have refused to let him go, so I will kill your first-born son." '   
**4:24** During the journey, while they were encamped for the night, the LORD met Moses, meaning to kill him,  
**4:25** but Zipporah picked up a sharp flint, cut off her son's foreskin, and touched him with it, saying, 'You are my blood-bridegroom.'  
**4:26** So the LORD let Moses alone. Then she said , 'Blood-bride-groom by circumcision.'   
**4:27** Meanwhile the LORD had ordered Aaron to go and meet Moses in the wilderness. Aaron went and met him at the mountain of God, and he kissed him.  
**4:28** Then Moses told Aaron everything, the words the LORD had sent him to say and the signs he had commanded him to perform.  
**4:29** Moses and Aaron went and assembled all the elders of Israel.  
**4:30** Aaron told them everything that the LORD had said to Moses; he performed the signs before the people,  
**4:31** and they were convinced. They heard that the LORD had shown his concern for the Israelites and seen their misery; and they bowed themselves to the ground in worship.  

## 5장

**5:1**  After this, Moses and Aaron came to Pharaoh and said, 'These are the words of the LORD the God of Israel: "Let my people go so that they may keep my pilgrim-feast in the wilderness." '  
**5:2** 'Who is the LORD,' asked Pharaoh, 'that I should obey him and let Israel go? I care nothing for the LORD: and I tell you I will not let Israel go.'  
**5:3** They replied, 'It has happened that the God of the Hebrews met us. So let us go three days' journey into the wilderness to offer sacrifice to the LORD our God, or else he will attack us with pestilence or sword.'  
**5:4** But the king of Egypt said, 'Moses and Aaron, what do you mean by distracting the people from their work? Back to your labours!  
**5:5** Your people already outnumber the native Egyptians; yet you would have them stop working!'   
**5:6** That very day Pharaoh ordered the people's overseers and their foremen  
**5:7** not to supply the people with the straw used in making bricks, as they had done hitherto. 'Let them go and collect their own straw,  
**5:8** but see that they produce the same tally of bricks as before. On no account reduce it. They are a lazy people, and that is why they are clamouring to go and offer sacrifice to their god.  
**5:9** Keep the men hard at work; let them attend to that and take no notice of a pack of lies.'  
**5:10** The overseers and foremen went out and said to the people, 'Pharaoh's orders are that no more straw is to be supplied.  
**5:11** Go and get it for yourselves wherever you can find it; but there will be no reduction in your daily task.'  
**5:12** So the people scattered all over Egypt to gather stubble for straw,  
**5:13** while the overseers kept urging them on, bidding them complete, day after day, the same quantity as when straw was supplied.  
**5:14** Then the Israelite foremen were flogged because they were held responsible by Pharaoh's overseers, who asked them, 'Why did you not complete the usual number of bricks yesterday or today?'  
**5:15** So the foremen came and appealed to Pharaoh: 'Why do you treat your servants like this?' they said.  
**5:16** 'We are given no straw, yet they keep on telling us to make bricks. Here are we being flogged, but it is your people's fault.'  
**5:17** But Pharaoh replied, 'You are lazy, you are lazy. That is why you talk about going to offer sacrifice to the LORD.  
**5:18** Now go; get on with your work. You will be given no straw, but you must produce the tally of bricks.'  
**5:19** When they were told that they must not let the daily tally of bricks fall short, the Israelite foremen saw that they were in trouble.  
**5:20** As they came out from Pharaoh's presence they found Moses and Aaron waiting to meet them,  
**5:21** and said, 'May this bring the LORD's judgement down upon you: you have made us stink in the nostrils of Pharaoh and his subjects; you have put a sword in their hands to kill us.'  
**5:22**  Moses went back to the LORD, and said, 'Why, O Lord, hast thou brought misfortune on this people? And why didst thou ever send me?  
**5:23** Since I first went to Pharaoh to speak in thy name he has heaped misfortune on thy people, and thou hast done nothing at all to rescue them.'  

## 6장

**6:1**  The LORD answered, 'Now you shall see what I will do to Pharaoh. In the end Pharaoh will let them go with a strong hand, nay, will drive them from his country with an outstretched arm.'  
**6:2**  God spoke to Moses and said, 'I am the LORD.  
**6:3** I appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as God Almighty. But I did not let myself be known to them by my name JEHOVAH .  
**6:4** Moreover, I made a covenant with them to give them Canaan, the land where they settled for a time as foreigners.  
**6:5** And now I have heard the groaning of the Israelites, enslaved by the Egyptians, and I have called my covenant to mind.  
**6:6** Say therefore to the Israelites, "I am the LORD. I will release you from your labours in Egypt. I will rescue you from slavery there. I will redeem you with arm outstretched and with mighty acts of judgement.  
**6:7** I will adopt you as my people, and I will become your God. You shall know that I, the LORD, am your God, the God who releases. you from your labours in Egypt.  
**6:8** I will lead you to the land which I swore with uplifted hand to give to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob. I will give it to you for your possession. I am the LORD." '   
**6:9** Moses repeated these words to the Israelites, but they did not listen to him; they had become impatient because of their cruel slavery.   
**6:10** Then the LORD spoke to Moses and said,  
**6:11** 'Go and tell Pharaoh king of Egypt to set the Israelites free to leave his country.'  
**6:12** Moses made answer in the presence of the LORD, 'If the Israelites do not listen to me, how will Pharaoh listen to such a halting speaker as I am?'   
**6:13** Thus the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron and gave them their commission to the Israelites and to Pharaoh, namely that they should bring the Israelites out of Egypt.

**6:15** Sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Saul, who was the son of a Canaanite woman; these were the families of Simeon.   
**6:16** These were the names of the sons of Levi in order of seniority: Gershon, Kohath and Merari. Levi lived to be a hundred and thirty-seven.   
**6:17** Sons of Gershon, family by family: Libni and Shimei.   
**6:18** Sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel. Kohath lived to be a hundred and thirty-three.   
**6:19** Sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. <br>These were the families of Levi in order of seniority.  
**6:20** Amram married his father's sister Jochebed, and she bore him Aaron and Moses. Amram lived to be a hundred and thirty-seven.   
**6:21** Sons of Izhar: Korah, Nepheg and Zichri.   
**6:22** Sons of Uzziel: Mishael, Elzaphan and Sithri.   
**6:23** Aaron married Elisheba, who was the daughter of Amminadab and the sister of Nahshon, and she bore him Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.   
**6:24** Sons of Korah: Assir, Elkanah and Abiasaph; these were the Korahite families.   
**6:25** Eleazar son of Aaron married one of the daughters of Putiel, and she bore him Phinehas. These were the heads of the Levite families, family by family.   
**6:26** It was this Aaron, together with Moses, to whom the LORD said, 'Bring the Israelites out of Egypt, mustered in their tribal hosts.'  
**6:27** These were the men who told Pharaoh king of Egypt to let the Israelites leave Egypt. It was this same Moses and Aaron.  
**6:28**  WHEN THE LORD SPOKE TO MOSES in Egypt  
**6:29** he said, 'I am the LORD. Tell Pharaoh king of Egypt all that I say to you.'  
**6:30** Moses made answer in the presence of the LORD, 'I am a halting speaker; how will Pharaoh listen to me?'  

## 7장

**7:1**  The LORD answered Moses, 'See now, I have made you like a god for Pharaoh, with your brother Aaron as your spokesman.  
**7:2** You must tell your brother Aaron all I bid you say, and he will tell Pharaoh, and Pharaoh will let the Israelites go out of his country;  
**7:3** but I will make him stubborn. Then will I show sign after sign and portent after portent in the land of Egypt.  
**7:4** But Pharaoh will not listen to you, so I will assert my power in Egypt, and with mighty sets of judgement I will bring my people, the Israelites, out of Egypt in their tribal hosts.  
**7:5** When I put forth my power against the Egyptians and bring the Israelites out from them, then Egypt will know that I am the LORD.'  
**7:6** So Moses and Aaron did exactly as the LORD had commanded.  
**7:7** At the time when they spoke to Pharaoh, Moses was eighty years old and Aaron eighty-three.   
**7:8**  The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,  
**7:9** 'If Pharaoh demands some portent from you, then vou, Moses, must say to Aaron, "Take your staff and throw it down in front of Pharaoh, and it will turn into a serpent." '  
**7:10** When Moses and Aaron came to Pharaoh, they did as the LORD had told them. Aaron threw down his staff in front of Pharaoh and his courtiers, and it turned into a serpent.  
**7:11** At this Pharaoh summoned the wise men and the sorcerers, and the Egyptian magicians too did the same thing by their spells.  
**7:12** Every man threw his staff down, and each staff turned into a serpent; but Aaron's staff swallowed up theirs.  
**7:13** Pharaoh, however, was obstinate; as the LORD had foretold, he would not listen to Moses and Aaron.   
**7:14**  Then the LORD said to Moses, 'Pharaoh is obdurate: he has refused to set the people free.  
**7:15** Go to him in the morning on his way out to the river. Stand and wait on the bank of the Nile to meet him, and take with you the staff that turned into a snake.  
**7:16** Say this to him: "The LORD the God of the Hebrews sent me to bid you let his people go in order to worship him in the wilderness. So far you have not listened to his words;  
**7:17** so now the LORD says, 'By this you shall know that I am the LORD. With this rod that I have in my hand, I shall now strike the water in the Nile and it will be changed into blood.  
**7:18** The fish will die and the river will stink, and the Egyptians will be unable to drink water from the Nile." '  
**7:19** The LORD then told Moses to say to Aaron, 'Take your staff and stretch your hand out over the waters of Egypt, its rivers and its streams, and over every pool and cistern, to turn them into blood. There shall be blood throughout the whole of Egypt, blood even in their wooden bowl and jars of stone.'  
**7:20** So Moses and Aaron did as the LORD had commanded. He lifted up his stuff and struck the water of the Nile in the sight of Pharaoh and his courtiers, and all the water was changed into blood.  
**7:21** The fish died and the river stank, and the Egyptians could not drink water from the Nile. There was blood everywhere in Egypt.  
**7:22** But the Egyptian magicians did the same thing by their spells; and still Pharaoh remained obstinate, as the LORD had foretold, and did not listen to Moses and Aaron.  
**7:23** He turned away, went into his house and dismissed the matter from his mind.  
**7:24** Then the Egyptians all dug for drinking water round about the river, because they could not drink from the waters of the Nile itself.  
**7:25** This lasted for seven days from the time when the LORD struck the Nile.  

## 8장

**8:1**  The LORD then told Moses to go into Pharaoh's presence and say to him, 'These are the words of the LORD: "Let my people go in order to worship me.  
**8:2** If you refuse to let them go, I will plague the whole of your territory with frogs.  
**8:3** The Nile shall swarm with them. They shall come up from the river into your house, into your bedroom and on to your bed, into the houses of your courtiers and your people, into your ovens and your kneading-troughs.  
**8:4** The frogs shall clamber over you, your people, and your courtiers." '  
**8:5** Then the LORD told Moses to say to Aaron, 'Take your staff in your hand and stretch it out over the rivers, streams, and pools, to bring up frogs upon the land of Egypt.'  
**8:6** So Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt, and the frogs came up and covered all the land.  
**8:7** The magicians did the same thing by their spells: they too brought up frogs upon the land of Egypt.  
**8:8** Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron. 'Pray to the LORD', he said, 'to take the frogs away from me and my people, and I will let the people go to sacrifice to the LORD.'  
**8:9** Moses said, 'Of your royal favour, appoint a time when I may intercede for you and your courtiers and people, so that you and your houses may be rid of the frogs, and none be left except in the Nile.'  
**8:10** 'Tomorrow', Pharaoh said. 'It shall be as you say,' replied Moses, 'so that you may know there is no one like our God, the LORD.  
**8:11** The frogs shall depart from you, from your houses, your courtiers, and your people: none shall be left except in the Nile.'  
**8:12** Moses and Aaron left Pharaoh's presence, and Moses appealed to the LORD to remove the frogs which he had brought on Pharaoh.  
**8:13** The LORD did as Moses had asked, and in house and courtyard and in the open the frogs all perished.  
**8:14** They piled them into countless heaps and the land stank;  
**8:15** but when Pharaoh found that he was given relief he became obdurate; as the LORD had foretold, he did not listen to Moses and Aaron.   
**8:16**  The LORD then told Moses to say to Aaron, 'Stretch out your staff and strike the dust on the ground, and it will turn into maggots throughout the land of Egypt',  
**8:17** and they obeyed. Aaron stretched out his staff and struck the dust, and it turned into maggots on man and beast. All the dust turned into maggots throughout the land of Egypt.  
**8:18** The magicians tried to produce maggots in the same way by their spells, but they failed. The maggots were everywhere, on man and beast.  
**8:19** 'It is the finger of God', said the magicians to Pharaoh, but Pharaoh remained obstinate; as the LORD had foretold, he did not listen to them.   
**8:20**  The LORD told Moses to rise early in the morning and stand in Pharaoh's path as he went out to the river and to say to him, 'These are the words of the LORD: "Let my people go in order to worship me.  
**8:21** If you do not let my people go, I will send swarms of flies upon you, your courtiers, your people, and your houses. The houses of the Egyptians shall be filled with the swarms and so shall all the land they live in,  
**8:22** but on that day I will make an exception of Goshen, the land where my people live: there shall be no swarms there. Thus you shall know that I, the LORD, am here in the land.  
**8:23** I will make a distinction between my people and yours. Tomorrow this sign shall appear." '  
**8:24** The LORD did this; dense swarms of flies infested Pharaoh's house and those of his courtiers; throughout Egypt the land was threatened with ruin by the swarms.  
**8:25** Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said to them, 'Go and sacrifice to your God, but in this country.'  
**8:26** 'That we cannot do,' replied Moses, 'because the victim we shall sacrifice to the LORD our God is an abomination to the Egyptians. If the Egyptians see us offer such an animal, will they not stone us to death?  
**8:27** We must go a three days' journey into the wilderness to sacrifice to the LORD our God, as he commands us.'
