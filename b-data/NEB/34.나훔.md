# 34.나훔 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  An oracle about Nineveh: the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.  
**1:2**  The LORD is a jealous god, a god of vengeance; the LORD takes vengeance and is quick to anger.   
**1:3**  In whirlwind and storm he goes on his way, and the clouds are the dust beneath his feet.  
**1:4** He rebukes the sea and dries it up and makes all the streams fail. Bashan and Carmel languish, and on Lebanon the young shoots wither.  
**1:5** The mountains quake before him, the hills heave and swell, and the earth, the world and all that lives in it, are in tumult at his presence.  
**1:6** Who can stand before his wrath? Who can resist his fury? His anger pours out like a stream of fire, and the rocks melt before him.  
**1:7** The LORD is a sure refuge for those who look to him in time of distress; he cares for all who seek his protection  
**1:8** and brings them safely through the sweeping flood; he makes a final end of all who oppose him and pursues his enemies into darkness.  
**1:9**  No adversaries dare oppose him twice; all are burnt up like tangled briars. Why do you make plots against the LORD? He himself will make an end of you all. From you has come forth a wicked counsellor, plotting evil against the LORD. The LORD takes vengeance on his adversaries, against his enemies he directs his wrath; with skin scorched black, they are consumed like stubble that is parched and dry.

## 2장

**2:1**  (2) The LORD will restore the pride of Jacob and Israel alike, although plundering hordes have stripped them bare and pillaged their vines.  
**2:2**  (1) The battering-ram is mounted against your bastions, the siege is closing in. Watch the road and brace yourselves; put forth all your strength.  
**2:3** The shields of their warriors are gleaming red, their soldiers are all in scarlet; their chariots, when the line is formed, are like flickering fire; squadrons of horse advance on the city  
**2:4** in mad frenzy ; they jostle one another in the outskirts, like waving torches;  
**2:5** the leaders display their prowess as they dash to and fro like lightning, rushing in headlong career; they hasten to the wall, and mantelets are set in position.  
**2:6** The sluices of the rivers are opened, the palace topples down;  
**2:7** the train of captives goes into exile, their slave-girls are carried off,. moaning like doves and beating their breasts;  
**2:8** and Nineveh has become like a pool of water, like the waters round her, which are ebbing away. 'Stop! Stop!' they cry; but none turns back.  
**2:9**  Spoil is taken, spoil of silver and gold; there is no end to the store, treasure beyond the costliest that man can desire.  
**2:10** Plundered, pillaged, stripped bare! Courage melting and knees giving way, writhing limbs, and faces drained of colour!  
**2:11** Where now is the lions' den, the cave where the lion cubs lurked, where the lion and lioness and young cubs went unafraid,  
**2:12** the lion which killed to satisfy its whelps and for its mate broke the neck of the kill, mauling its prey to fill its lair, filling its den with the mauled prey?   
**2:13** I am against you, says the LORD of Hosts, I will smoke out your pride , and a sword shall devour your cubs. I will leave you no more prey on the earth, and the sound of your feeding shall no more be heard.  

## 3장

**3:1**  Ah! blood-stained city, steeped in deceit, full of pillage, never empty of prey!  
**3:2** Hark to the crack of the whip, the rattle of wheels and stamping of horses, bounding chariots,  
**3:3** chargers rearing swords gleaming, flash of spears! The dead are past counting, their bodies lie in heaps, corpses innumerable, men stumbling over corpses—  
**3:4** all for a wanton's monstrous wantonness, fair-seeming, a mistress of sorcery, who beguiled nations and tribes by her wantonness and her sorceries.  
**3:5** I am against you, says the LORD of Hosts, I will uncover your breasts to your disgrace. and expose your naked body to every nation, to every kingdom your shame.  
**3:6** I will cast loathsome filth over you, I will count you obscene and treat you like excrement.  
**3:7**  Then all who see you will shrink from you and say, 'Nineveh is laid waste; who will console her?' Where shall I look for anyone to comfort you?  
**3:8** Will you fare better than No-amon?— she that lay by the streams of the Nile, surrounded by water, whose rampart was the Nile, waters her wall;  
**3:9** Cush and Egypt were her strength, and it was boundless, Put and the Libyans brought her help.  
**3:10** She too became an exile and went into captivity, her infants too were dashed to the ground at every street-corner, her nobles were shared out by lot, all her great men were thrown into chains.  
**3:11** You too shall hire yourself out, flaunting your sex; you too shall seek refuge from the enemy.  
**3:12** Your fortifications are like figs when they ripen: if they are shaken, they fall into the mouth of the eater.  
**3:13** The troops in your midst are a pack of women, the gates of your country stand open to the enemy, and fire consumes their bars.  
**3:14** Draw yourselves water for the siege, strengthen your fortifications; down into the clay, trample the mortar, repair the brickwork.  
**3:15** Even then the fire will consume you, and the sword will cut you down . Make yourselves many as the locusts, make yourselves many as the hoppers,  
**3:16** a swarm which spreads out and then flies away. You have spies as numerous as the stars in the sky;  
**3:17** your secret agents are like locusts, your commanders like the hoppers which lie dormant in the walls on a cold day; but when the sun rises, they scurry off, and no one knows where they have gone.  
**3:18** Your shepherds slumber, O king of Assyria, your flock-masters lie down to rest; your troops are scattered over the hills, and no one rounds them up.  
**3:19** Your wounds cannot be assuaged, your injury is mortal; all who have heard of your fate clap their hands in joy. Are there any whom your ceaseless cruelty has not borne down?
