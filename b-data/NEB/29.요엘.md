# 29.요엘 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  The word of the LORD which came to Joel son of Pethuel.  
**1:2**  Listen, you elders; hear me, all you who live in the land: has the like of this happened in all your days or in your fathers' days?  
**1:3** Tell it to your sons and they may tell theirs; let them pass it on from generation to generation.  
**1:4** What the locust has left the swarm eats, what the swarm has left the hopper eats, and what the hopper has left the grub eats.  
**1:5** Wake up, you drunkards, and lament your fate; mourn for the fresh wine, all you wine-drinkers, because it is lost to you.  
**1:6** For a horde has overrun my land, mighty and past counting; their teeth are a lion's teeth, they have the fangs of a lioness.  
**1:7** They have ruined my vines and left my fig-trees broken and leafless, they have plucked them bare and stripped them of their bark; they have left the branches white.  
**1:8**  Wail like a virgin wife in sackcloth, wailing over the bridegroom of her youth:  
**1:9** the drink-offering and grain-offering are lost to the house of the LORD. Mourn, you priests, ministers of the LORD,  
**1:10** the fields are ruined, the parched earth mourns; for the corn is ruined, the new wine is desperate, the oil has failed.  
**1:11** Despair, you husbandmen; you vinedressers, lament, because the wheat and the barley, the harvest of the field, is lost.  
**1:12** The vintage is desperate, and the fig-tree has failed; pomegranate, palm, and apple, all the trees of the country-side are parched, and none make merry over harvest.  
**1:13**  Priests, put on sackcloth and beat your breasts; lament, you ministers of the altar; come, lie in sackcloth all night long, you ministers of my God; for grain-offering and drink-offering are withheld from the house of your God.  
**1:14** Proclaim a solemn fast, appoint a day of abstinence. You elders, summon all that live in the land to come together in the house of your God, and cry to the LORD.  
**1:15** Alas! the day is near, the day of the LORD: it comes, a mighty destruction from the Almighty.  
**1:16** Look! it stares us in the face; the house of our God has lost its food, lost all its joy and gladness.  
**1:17** The soil is parched, the dykes are dry, the granaries are deserted, the barns ruinous; for the rains have failed.  
**1:18** The cattle are exhausted, the herds of oxen distressed because they have no pasture; the flocks of sheep waste away.  
**1:19** To thee I cry, O LORD; for fire has devoured the open pastures and the flames have burnt up all the trees of the country-side.  
**1:20** The very cattle in the ?eld look up to thee; for the water-channels are dried up, and ?re has devoured the open pastures.  

## 2장

**2:1**  Blow the trumpet in Zion, sound the alarm upon my holy hill; let all that live in the land tremble, for the day of the LORD has come,  
**2:2** surely a day of darkness and gloom is upon us, a day of cloud and dense fog; like a blackness spread over the mountains a mighty, countless host appears; their like has never been known, nor ever shall be in ages to come;  
**2:3** their vanguard a devouring fire, their rearguard leaping flame; before them the land is a garden of Eden, behind them a wasted wilderness; nothing survives their march.  
**2:4** On they come, like squadrons of horse, like war-horses they charge;  
**2:5** bounding over the peaks they advance with the rattle of chariots, like flames of fire burning up the stubble, like a countless host in battle array.  
**2:6** Before them nations tremble, every face turns pale.  
**2:7** Like warriors they charge, they mount the walls like men at arms, each marching in line, no confusion in the ranks,  
**2:8** none jostling his neighbour, none breaking line. They plunge through streams without halting their advance;  
**2:9** they burst into the city, leap on to the wall, climb into the houses, entering like thieves through the windows.  
**2:10** Before them the earth shakes, the heavens shudder, sun and moon are darkened, and the stars forbear to shine.  
**2:11** The LORD thunders before his host; his is a mighty army, countless are those who do his bidding. Great is the day of the LORD and terrible, who can endure it?  
**2:12**  And yet, the LORD says, even now turn back to me with your whole heart, fast, and weep, and beat your breasts.  
**2:13** Rend your hearts and not your garments; turn back to the LORD your God; for he is gracious and compassionate, long-suffering and ever constant, always ready to repent of the threatened evil,  
**2:14** It may be he will turn back and repent and leave a blessing behind him, blessing enough for grain-offering and drink-offering for the LORD your God.  
**2:15**  Blow the trumpet in Zion, proclaim a solemn fast, appoint a day of abstinence;  
**2:16** gather the people together, proclaim a solemn assembly; summon the elders, gather the children, yes, babes at the breast; bid the bridegroom leave his chamber and the bride her bower.  
**2:17** Let the priests, the ministers of the LORD, stand weeping between the porch and the altar and say, 'Spare thy people, O LORD, thy own people, expose them not to reproach, lest other nations make them a byword and everywhere men ask, "Where is their God?" '  
**2:18**  Then the LORD's love burned with zeal for his land, and he was moved with compassion for his people.  
**2:19** He answered their appeal and said, I will send you corn, and new wine, and oil, and you shall have your fill; I will expose you no longer to the reproach of other nations.  
**2:20** I will remove the northern peril far away from you and banish them into a land parched and waste, their vanguard into the eastern sea and their rear into the western, and the stench shall rise from their rotting corpses because of their proud deeds!  
**2:21** Earth, be not afraid, rejoice and be glad; for the LORD himself has done a proud deed.  
**2:22** Be not afraid, you cattle in the field; for the pastures shall be green, the trees shall bear fruit, the fig and the vine yield their harvest.  
**2:23** O people of Zion, rejoice and be glad in the LORD your God, who gives you good food in due measure and sends down rain as of old.  
**2:24** The threshing-floors shall be heaped with grain, the vats shall overflow with new wine and oil.  
**2:25** So I will make good the years that the swarm has eaten, hopper and grub and locust, my great army which I sent against you;  
**2:26** and you shall eat, you shall eat your fill and praise the name of the LORD your God who has done wonders for you ,  
**2:27** and you shall know that I am present in Israel, that I and no other am the LORD your God; and my people shall not again be brought to shame.  
**2:28**  Thereafter the day shall come when I will pour out my spirit on all mankind; your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams and your young men see visions;  
**2:29** I will pour out my spirit in those days even upon slaves and slave-girls.  
**2:30** I will show portents in the sky and on earth, blood and fire and columns of smoke;  
**2:31** the sun shall be turned into darkness and the moon into blood. before the great and terrible day of the LORD comes.  
**2:32** Then everyone who invokes the LORD by name shall be saved:, for when the LORD gives the word there shall yet be survivors on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem a remnant whom the LORD will call .

**3:2** I will gather all the nations together and lead them down to the Valley of the LORD's Judgement and there bring them to judgement on behalf of Israel, my own possession; for they have scattered my people throughout their own countries, have taken each their portion of my land  
**3:3**  and shared out my people by lot, <br>bartered a boy for a whore, <br>and sold a girl for wine and drunk it down.  
**3:4**  What are you to me, Tyre and Sidon and all the districts of Philistia? Can you pay me back for anything I have done? Is there anything that you can do to me? Swiftly and speedily I will make your deeds recoil upon your own heads;  
**3:5** for you have taken my silver and my gold and carried off my costly treasures into your temples;  
**3:6** you have sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks, and removed them far beyond their own frontiers.  
**3:7** But I will rouse them to leave the places to which you have sold them. I will make your deeds recoil upon your own heads:  
**3:8** I will sell your sons and your daughters to the people of Judah, and they shall sell them to the Sabaeans, a nation far away. The LORD has spoken.
