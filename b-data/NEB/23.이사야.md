## 1장

**1:1**  THE VISION RECEIVED BY ISAIAH son of Amoz concerning Judah and Jerusalem during the reigns of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.  
**1:2**  Hark you heavens, and earth give ear, for the LORD has spoken: I have sons whom I reared and brought up, but they have rebelled against me.  
**1:3** The ox knows its owner and the ass its master's stall; but Israel, my own people, has no knowledge, no discernment.  
**1:4**  O sinful nation, people loaded with iniquity, race of evildoers, wanton destructive children who have deserted the LORD, spurned the Holy One of Israel and turned your backs on him.  
**1:5** Where can you still be struck if you will be disloyal still? Your head is covered with sores, your body diseased;  
**1:6** from head to foot there is not a sound spot in you— nothing but bruises and weals and raw wounds which have not felt compress or bandage or soothing oil.  
**1:7** Your country is desolate, your cities lie in ashes. Strangers devour your land before your eyes; it is desolate as Sodom in its overthrow.  
**1:8** Only Zion is left, like a watchman's shelter in a vineyard, a shed in a field of cucumbers, a city well guarded.  
**1:9** If the LORD of Hosts had not left us a remnant, we should soon have been like Sodom, no better than Gomorrah. .  
**1:10** Hear the word of the LORD, you rulers of Sodom; attend, you people of Gomorrah, to the instruction of our God:  
**1:11** Your countless sacrifices, what are they to me? says the LORD. I am sated with whole-offerings of rams and the fat of buffaloes; I have no desire for the blood of bulls, of sheep and of he-goats.  
**1:12** Whenever you come to enter my presence— who asked you for this? No more shall you trample my courts. The offer of your gifts is useless, the reek of sacrifice is abhorrent to me. New moons and sabbaths and assemblies, sacred seasons and ceremonies, I cannot endure.  
**1:13**   
**1:14** I cannot tolerate your new moons and your festivals; they have become a burden to me, and I can put up with them no longer.  
**1:15** When you lift your hands outspread in prayer, I will hide my eyes from you. Though you offer countless prayers, I will not listen. There is blood on your hands;  
**1:16** wash yourselves and be clean. Put away the evil of your deeds, away out of my sight.  
**1:17** Cease to do evil and learn to do right, pursue justice and champion the oppressed; give the orphan his rights, plead the widow's cause.  
**1:18**  Come now, let us argue it out, says the LORD. Though your sins are scarlet, they may become white as snow; though they are dyed crimson, they may yet be like wool.  
**1:19** Obey with a will, and you shall eat the best that earth yields;  
**1:20** but, if you refuse and rebel, locust-beans shall be your only food . The LORD himself has spoken.  
**1:21**  How the faithful city has played the whore, once the home of justice where righteousness dwelt— but now murderers!  
**1:22** Your silver has turned into base metal and your liquor is diluted with water.  
**1:23** Your very rulers are rebels, confederate with thieves; every man of them loves a bribe and itches for a gift; they do not give the orphan his rights, and the widow's cause never comes before them.  
**1:24**  This therefore is the word of the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, the Mighty One of Israel: Enough! I will secure a respite from my foes and take vengeance on my enemies.  
**1:25** Once again I will act against you to re?ne away your base metal as with potash and purge all your impurities;  
**1:26** I will again make your judges what once they were and your counsellors like those of old. Then at length you shall be called the home of righteousness, the faithful city.  
**1:27** Justice shall redeem Zion and righteousness her repentant people.  
**1:28** Rebels and sinners shall be broken together, and those who forsake the LORD shall cease to be.  
**1:29** For the sacred oaks in which you delighted shall fail you, the garden-shrines of your fancy shall disappoint you.  
**1:30** You shall be like a terebinth whose leaves have withered, like a garden without water;  
**1:31** the strongest tree shall become like tow, and what is made of it shall go up in sparks, and the two shall burst into flames together with no one to quench them.  

## 2장

**2:1**  This is the word which Isaiah son of Amoz received in a vision concerning Judah and Jerusalem.   
**2:2**  In days to come the mountain of the LORD's house shall be set over all other mountains, lifted high above the hills. All the nations shall come streaming to it,  
**2:3** and many peoples shall come and say, 'Come, let us climb up on to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob, that he may teach us his ways and we may walk in his paths.' For instruction issues from Zion, and out of Jerusalem comes the word of the LORD;  
**2:4** he will be judge between nations, arbiter among many peoples. They shall beat their swords into mattocks and their spears into pruning-knives ; nation shall not lift sword against nation nor ever again be trained for war.  
**2:5**  O people of Jacob, come, let us walk in the light of the LORD.  
**2:6** Thou hast abandoned thy people the house of Jacob; for they are crowded with traders and barbarians like the Philistines, and with the children of foreigners everywhere.  
**2:7** Their land is filled with silver and gold, and there is no end to their treasure; their land is filled with horses, and there is no end to their chariots;  
**2:8** their land is filled with idols, and they bow down to the work of their own hands, to what their fingers have made.  
**2:9** Mankind shall be brought low, all men shall be humbled; and how can they raise themselves ?  
**2:10** Get you into the rocks and hide yourselves, in the ground from the dread of the LORD and the splendour of his majesty.  
**2:11** Man's proud eyes shall be humbled, the loftiness of men brought low, and the LORD alone shall be exalted on that day.  
**2:12**  For the LORD of Hosts has a day of doom waiting for all that is proud and lofty, for all that is high and lifted up,  
**2:13** for all the cedars of Lebanon, lofty and high, and for all the oaks of Bashan,  
**2:14** for all lofty mountains and for all high hills,  
**2:15** for every high tower and for every sheer wall,  
**2:16** for all ships of Tarshish and all the dhows of Arabia.  
**2:17** Then man's pride shall be brought low, and the loftiness of man shall be humbled, and the LORD alone shall be exalted on that day,  
**2:18** while the idols shall pass away utterly.  
**2:19** Get you into caves in the rocks and crevices in the ground from the dread of the LORD and the splendour of his majesty, when he rises to inspire the earth with fear.  
**2:20** On that day a man shall fling away his idols of silver and his idols of gold which he has made for himself to worship; he shall fling them to the dung-beetles and the bats,  
**2:21** and creep into clefts in the rocks and crannies in the cliffs from the dread of the LORD and the splendour of his majesty, when he rises to inspire the earth with fear.  
**2:22** Have no more to do with man, for what is he worth? He is no more than the breath in his nostrils.  

## 3장

**3:1**  Be warned: the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, is stripping Jerusalem and Judah of every prop and stay ,  
**3:2** warrior and soldier, judge and prophet, diviner and elder,  
**3:3** captains of companies and men of rank, counsellor, magician, and cunning enchanter.  
**3:4** Then I will appoint mere boys to be their captains, who shall govern as the fancy takes them;  
**3:5** the people shall deal harshly each man with his fellow and with his neighbour; children shall break out against their elders, and nobodies against men of substance.  
**3:6** If a man takes hold of his brother in his father's house, saying, 'You have a cloak, you shall be our chief; our stricken family shall be under you',  
**3:7** he will cry out that day and say, 'I will not be your master; there is neither bread nor cloak in my house, and you shall not make me head of the clan.'  
**3:8**  Jerusalem is stricken and Judah fallen because they have spoken and acted against the LORD, rebelling against the glance of his glorious eye.  
**3:9** The look on their faces testifies against them; like Sodom they proclaim their sins and do not conceal them . Woe upon them! they have earned their own disaster.  
**3:10** Happy the righteous man! all goes well with him, for such men enjoy the fruit of their actions.  
**3:11** Woe betide the wicked! with him all goes ill, for he reaps the reward that he has earned.  
**3:12** Money-lenders strip my people bare, and usurers lord it over them. O my people! your guides lead you astray and confuse the path that you should take.  
**3:13**  The LORD comes forward to argue his case and stands to judge his people.  
**3:14** The LORD opens the indictment against the elders of his people and their officers: You have ravaged the vineyard, and the spoils of the poor are in your houses.  
**3:15** Is it nothing to you that you crush my people and grind the faces of the poor? This is the very word of the Lord, the LORD of Hosts.  
**3:16**  Then the LORD said: Because the women of Zion hold themselves high and walk with necks outstretched and wanton glances, moving with mincing gait and jingling feet,  
**3:17** the Lord will give the women of Zion bald heads, the LORD will strip the hair from their foreheads.  
**3:18**  In that day the Lord will take away all finery: anklets, discs, crescents,  
**3:19** pendants, bangles, coronets,  
**3:20** head-bands, armlets, necklaces, lockets, charms,  
**3:21** signets, nose-rings,  
**3:22** fine dresses, mantles, cloaks, flounced skirts,  
**3:23** scarves of gauze, kerchiefs of linen, turbans, and flowing veils.  
**3:24**  So instead of perfume you shall have the stench of decay, and a rope in place of a girdle, baldness instead of hair elegantly coiled, a loin-cloth of sacking instead of a mantle, and branding instead of beauty.  
**3:25** Your men shall fall by the sword, and your warriors in battle;  
**3:26** then Zion's gates shall mourn and lament, and she shall sit on the ground stripped bare.

## 6장

**6:1**  IN THE YEAR OF KING UZZIAH'S DEATH I saw the Lord seated on a throne, high and exalted, and the skirt of his robe filled the temple.  
**6:2** About him were attendant seraphim, and each had six wings; one pair covered his face and one pair his feet, and one pair was spread in flight.  
**6:3** They were calling ceaselessly to one another, Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of Hosts: the whole earth is full of his glory.  
**6:4**  And, as each one called, the threshold shook to its foundations, while the house was filled with smoke.  
**6:5** Then I cried, Woe is me! I am lost, for I am a man of unclean lips and I dwell among a people of unclean lips; yet with these eyes I have seen the King, the LORD of Hosts.  
**6:6**  Then one of the seraphim flew to me carrying in his hand a glowing coal which he had taken from the altar with a pair of tongs.  
**6:7** He touched my mouth with it and said, See, this has touched your lips; your iniquity is removed, and your sin is wiped away.  
**6:8**  Then I heard the Lord saying, Whom shall I send? Who will go for me? And I answered, Here am I; send me.  
**6:9** He said, Go and tell this people: You may listen and listen, but you will not understand. You may look and look again, but you will never know.   
**6:10** This people's wits are dulled, their ears are deafened and their eyes blinded, so that they cannot see with their eyes nor listen with their ears nor understand with their wits, so that they may turn and be healed.  
**6:11**  Then I asked, How long, O Lord? And he answered, Until cities fall in ruins and are deserted, houses are left without people, and the land goes to ruin and lies waste,  
**6:12** until the LORD has sent all mankind far away, and the whole country is one vast desolation.  
**6:13** Even if a tenth part of its people remain there, they too will be exterminated {like an oak or a terebinth, a sacred pole thrown out from its place in a hill-shrine }.  

## 7장

**7:1**  WHILE AHAZ SON OF JOTHAM and grandson of Uzziah was king of Judah, Rezin king of Aram with Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, marched on Jerusalem, but could not force a battle.  
**7:2** When the house of David heard that the Aramaeans had come to terms with the Ephraimites, king and people were shaken like forest trees in the wind.  
**7:3** Then the LORD said to Isaiah, Go out with your son Shear-jashub to meet Ahaz at the end of the conduit of the Upper Pool by the causeway leading to the Fuller's Field,  
**7:4** and say to him, Be on your guard, keep calm; do not be frightened or unmanned by these two smouldering stumps of firewood, because Rezin and his Aramaeans with Remaliah's son are burning with rage.  
**7:5** The Aramaeans with Ephraim and Remaliah's son have laid their plans against you, saying,  
**7:6** Let us invade Judah and break her spirit , let us make her join with us, and set the son of Tabeal on the throne.  
**7:7** Therefore the Lord GOD has said: This shall not happen now, and never shall,  
**7:8** for all that the chief city of Aram is Damascus, and Rezin is the chief of Damascus; within sixty-five years Ephraim shall cease to be a nation,  
**7:9**  for all that Samaria is the chief city of Ephraim, and Remaliah's son the chief of Samaria. Have ?rm faith, or you will not stand ?rm.  
**7:10**  Once again the LORD spoke to Ahaz and said,  
**7:11** Ask the LORD your God for a sign, from lowest Sheol or from highest heaven.  
**7:12** But Ahaz said, No, I will not put the LORD to the test by asking for a sign.  
**7:13** Then the answer came: Listen, house of David. Are you not content to wear out men's patience? Must you also wear out the patience of my God?  
**7:14** Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign: A young woman is with child, and she will bear a son, and will call him Immanuel .  
**7:15** By the time that he has learnt to reject evil and choose good, he will be eating curds and honey ;  
**7:16** before that child has learnt to reject evil and choose good, desolation will come upon the land before whose two kings you cower now.  
**7:17** The LORD will bring on you, your people, and your house, a time the like of which has not been seen since Ephraim broke away from Judah .  
**7:18**  On that day the LORD will whistle for the fly from the distant streams of Egypt and for the bee from Assyria.  
**7:19** They shall all come and settle in the precipitous ravines and in the clefts of the rock; camel-thorn and stink-wood shall be black with them.  
**7:20** On that day the Lord shall shave the head and body with a razor hired on the banks of the Euphrates , and it shall remove the beard as well.  
**7:21** On that day a man shall save alive a young cow and two ewes;  
**7:22** and he shall get so much milk that he eats curds; for all who are left in the land shall eat curds and honey.  
**7:23** On that day every place where there used to be a thousand vines worth a thousand pieces of silver shall be given over to thorns and briars.  
**7:24** A man shall go there only to hunt with bow and arrows, for thorns and briars cover the whole land;  
**7:25** and no one who fears thorns and briars shall set foot on any of those hills once worked with the hoe. Oxen shall be turned loose on them, and sheep shall trample them.

**8:4** Before the boy can say Father or Mother, the wealth of Damascus and the spoils of Samaria shall be carried off and presented to the king of Assyria.   
**8:5**  Once again the LORD said to me:  
**8:6**  Because this nation has rejected the waters of Shiloah, which run so softly and gently ,

**8:9** Take note, you nations, and be dismayed. Listen, all you distant parts of the earth: you may arm yourselves but will be dismayed; you may arm yourselves but will be dismayed.  
**8:10** Make your plans, but they will be foiled, propose what you please, but it shall not stand; for God is with us?  
**8:11**  These were the words of the LORD to me, for his hand was strong upon me; and he warned me not to follow the ways of this people:  
**8:12**  You shall not say 'too hard' of everything that this people calls hard; you shall neither dread nor fear that which they fear.  
**8:13** It is the LORD of Hosts whom you must count 'hard' ; he it is whom you must fear and dread.

**8:18** See, I and the sons whom the LORD has given me are to be signs and portents in Israel, sent by the LORD of Hosts who dwells on Mount Zion.  
**8:19** But men will say to you, 'Seek guidance of ghosts and familiar spirits who squeak and gibber; a nation may surely seek guidance of its gods, of the dead on behalf of the living,

**8:21**  So despondency and fear will come over them, and then, when they are afraid and fearful, they will turn against their king and their gods. Then, whether they turn their gaze upwards  
**8:22** or look down, everywhere is distress and darkness inescapable, constraint and gloom that cannot be avoided; (9:1a) for there is no escape for an oppressed people.
