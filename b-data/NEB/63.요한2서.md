# 63.요한2서 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  THE ELDER <br>to the Lady chosen by God, and her children, whom I love in truth—and not I alone but all who know the truth—  
**1:2** for the sake of the truth that dwells among us and will be with us for ever.   
**1:3**  Grace, mercy, and peace shall be with us from God the Father and from Jesus Christ the Son of the Father, in truth and love.  
**1:4**  I was delighted to find that some of your children are living by the truth, as we were commanded by the Father.  
**1:5** And now I have a request to make of you. Do not think I am giving a new command; I am recalling the one we have had before us from the beginning: let us love one another.  
**1:6** And love means following the commands of God. This is the command which was given you from the beginning, to be your rule of life.   
**1:7**  Many deceivers have gone out into the world, who do not acknowledge Jesus Christ as coming in the flesh. These are the persons described as the Antichrist, the arch-deceiver.  
**1:8** Beware of them, so that you may not lose all that we worked for, but receive your reward in full.   
**1:9**  Anyone who runs ahead too far, and does not stand by the doctrine of the Christ, is without God; he who stands by that doctrine possesses both the Father and the Son.  
**1:10** If anyone comes to you who does not bring this doctrine, do not welcome him into your house or give him a greeting;  
**1:11** for anyone who gives him a greeting is an accomplice in his wicked deeds.   
**1:12**  I have much to write to you, but I do not care to put it down in black and white. But I hope to visit you and talk with you face to face, so that our joy may be complete.  
**1:13** The children of your Sister, chosen by God, send their greetings.
