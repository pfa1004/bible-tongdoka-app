# 24.예레미야 (NEB)



## 1장

**1:1**  THE WORDS OF JEREMIAH son of Hilkiah, one of the priests at Anathoth in Benjamin.  
**1:2** The word of the LORD came to him in the thirteenth year of the reign of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah;  
**1:3** also during the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah, was completed. In the fifth month the people of Jerusalem were carried away into exile.   
**1:4**  THE WORD OF THE LORD CAME TO ME:  
**1:5** 'Before I formed you in the womb I knew you for my own; before you were born I consecrated you, I appointed you a prophet to the nations.'  
**1:6** 'Ah! Lord GOD,' I answered, 'I do not know how to speak; I am only a child.'  
**1:7** But the LORD said, 'Do not call yourself a child; for you shall go to whatever people I send you and say whatever I tell you to say.  
**1:8** Fear none of them, for I am with you and will keep you safe.' This was the very word of the LORD.  
**1:9** Then the LORD stretched out his hand and touched my mouth, and said to me, 'I put my words into your mouth.  
**1:10** This day I give you authority over nations and over kingdoms, to pull down and to uproot, to destroy and to demolish, to build and to plant.'   
**1:11**  The word of the LORD came to me: 'What is it that you see, Jeremiah?' 'An almond in early bloom ', I answered.  
**1:12** 'You are right,' said the LORD to me, 'for I am early on the watch to carry out my purpose.'  
**1:13** The word of the LORD came to me a second time: 'What is it that you see?' 'A cauldron', I said, 'on a fire, fanned by the wind; it is tilted away from the north.'  
**1:14** The LORD said: From the north disaster shall flare up against all who live in this land;  
**1:15** for now I summon all peoples and kingdoms of the north, says the LORD. Their kings shall come and each shall set up his throne before the gates of Jerusalem, against her walls on every side, and against all the cities of Judah.  
**1:16** I will state my case against my people for all the wrong they have done in forsaking me, in burning sacrifices to other gods, worshipping the work of their own hands.  
**1:17** Brace yourself, Jeremiah; stand up and speak to them. Tell them everything I bid you, do not let your spirit break at sight of them, or I will break you before their eyes.  
**1:18** This day I make you a fortified city, a pillar of iron, a wall of bronze, to stand fast against the whole land, against the kings and princes of Judah, its priests and its people.  
**1:19** They will make war on you but shall not overcome you, for I am with you and will keep you safe. This is the very word of the LORD.  

## 2장

**2:1**  THE WORD OF THE LORD CAME TO ME:  
**2:2** Go, make a proclamation that all Jerusalem shall hear: These are the words of the LORD: I remember the unfailing devotion of your youth, the love of your bridal days, when you followed me in the wilderness, through a land unsown.  
**2:3** Israel then was holy to the LORD, the firstfruits of his harvest; no one who devoured her went unpunished, evil always overtook them. This is the very word of the LORD.  
**2:4**  Listen to the word of the LORD, people of Jacob, families of Israel, one and all.  
**2:5** These are the words of the LORD: What fault did your forefathers find in me, that they wandered far from me, pursuing empty phantoms and themselves becoming empty;  
**2:6** that they did not ask, 'Where is the LORD, who brought us up from Egypt, and led us through the wilderness, through a country of deserts and shifting sands, a country barren and ill-omened, where no man ever trod, no man made his home?'  
**2:7** I brought you into a fruitful land to enjoy its fruit and the goodness of it; but when you entered upon it you defiled it and made the home I gave you loathsome.  
**2:8** The priests no longer asked, 'Where is the LORD?' Those who handled the law had no thought of me, the shepherds of the people rebelled against me; the prophets prophesied in the name of Baal and followed gods powerless to help.  
**2:9** Therefore I will bring a charge against you once more, says the LORD, against you and against your descendants.  
**2:10** Cross to the coasts and islands of Kittim and see, send to Kedar and consider well, see whether there has been anything like this:  
**2:11** has a nation ever changed its gods, although they were no gods? But my people have exchanged their Glory for a god altogether powerless.  
**2:12** Stand aghast at this, you heavens, tremble in utter despair, says the LORD.  
**2:13** Two sins have my people committed: they have forsaken me, a spring of living water, and they have hewn out for themselves cisterns, cracked cisterns that can hold no water.  
**2:14**  Is Israel a slave? Was he born in slavery? If not, why has he been despoiled?  
**2:15** Why do lions roar and growl at him? Why has his land been laid waste, why are his cities razed to the ground and abandoned?  
**2:16** Men of Noph and Tahpanhes will break your heads.  
**2:17** Is it not your desertion of the LORD your God that brings all this upon you?  
**2:18** And now, why should you make off to Egypt to drink the waters of the Shihor? Or why make off to Assyria to drink the waters of the River?  
**2:19** It is your own wickedness that will punish you, your own apostasy that will condemn you. See for yourselves how bitter a thing it is and how evil, to forsake the LORD your God and revere me no longer. This is the very word of the Lord GOD of Hosts.  
**2:20**  Ages ago you broke your yoke and snapped your traces, crying, 'I will not be your slave'; and you sprawled in promiscuous vice on all the hill-tops, under every spreading tree.  
**2:21** I planted you as a choice red vine, true stock all of you, yet now you are turned into a vine debased and worthless!  
**2:22** The stain of your sin is still there and I see it, though you wash with soda and do not stint the soap. This is the very word of the Lord GOD.  
**2:23** How can you say, 'I am not polluted, not I! I have not followed the Baalim'? Look how you conducted yourself in the valley; remember what you have done. You have been like a she-camel, twisting and turning as she runs,  
**2:24** rushing alone into the wilderness, snuffing the wind in her lust; who can restrain her in her heat? No one need tire himself out in pursuit of her; she is easily found at mating time.  
**2:25** Why not save your feet from stony ground and your throats from thirst? But you said, 'No; I am desperate. I love foreign gods and I must go after them.'  
**2:26**  As a thief is ashamed when he is found out, so the people of Israel feel ashamed, they, their kings, their princes, their priests and their prophets;  
**2:27** they say 'You are our father' to a block of wood and cry 'Mother' to a stone. But on me they have turned their backs and averted their faces from me. And now on the day of disaster they say, 'Rise up and save us.'  
**2:28** Where are they, those gods you made for yourselves? Let them come and save you in the day of disaster. For you, Judah, have as many gods as you have towns .  
**2:29** The LORD answers, Why argue your case with me? You are rebels, every one of you.  
**2:30** In vain I struck down your sons, the lesson was not learnt; still your own sword devoured your prophets like a ravening lion.  
**2:31**  Have I shown myself inhospitable to Israel like some wilderness or waterless land? Why do my people say, 'We have broken away; we will never come back to thee'?  
**2:32** Will a girl forget her finery or a bride her ribbons? Yet my people have forgotten me over and over again.  
**2:33** How well you pick your way in search of lovers! Why! even the worst of women can learn from you.  
**2:34** Yes, and there is blood on the corners of your robe— the life-blood of the innocent poor. You did not get it by housebreaking but by your sacrifices under every oak.  
**2:35** You say, 'I am innocent; surely his anger has passed away.' But I will challenge your claim to have done no sin.  
**2:36** Why do you so lightly change your course? Egypt will fail you as Assyria did;  
**2:37** you shall go out from here, each of you with his hands above his head, for the LORD repudiates those in whom you trusted, and from them you shall gain nothing.  

## 3장

**3:1**  If a man puts away his wife and she leaves him, and if she then becomes another's, may he go back to her again? Is not that woman defiled, a forbidden thing? You have played the harlot with many lovers; can you come back to me? says the LORD.  
**3:2** Look up to the high bare places and see: where have you not been ravished? You sat by the wayside to catch lovers, like an Arab lurking in the desert, and defiled the land with your fornication and your wickedness.  
**3:3** Therefore the showers were withheld and the spring rain failed. But yours was a harlot's brow, and you were resolved to show no shame.  
**3:4** Not so long since, you called me 'Father, dear friend of my youth', thinking,  
**3:5** 'Will he be angry for ever? Will he rage eternally?' This is how you spoke; you have done evil and gone unchallenged.  
**3:6**  In the reign of King Josiah, the LORD said to me, Do you see what apostate Israel did? She went up to every hill-top and under every spreading tree, and there she played the whore.  
**3:7** Even after she had done all this, I said to her, Come back to me, but she would not. That faithless woman, her sister Judah, saw it all;  
**3:8** she saw too that I had put apostate Israel away and given her a note of divorce because she had committed adultery. Yet that faithless woman, her sister Judah, was not afraid; she too has gone and played the whore.  
**3:9** She defiled the land with her thoughtless harlotry and her adulterous worship of stone and wood.  
**3:10** In spite of all this that faithless woman, her sister Judah, has not come back to me in good faith: but only in pretence. This is the very word of the LORD.   
**3:11** The LORD said to me, Apostate Israel is less to blame than that faithless woman Judah.  
**3:12** Go and proclaim this message to the north: Come back to me, apostate Israel, says the LORD, I will no longer frown on you. For my love is unfailing, says the LORD, I will not be angry for ever.  
**3:13** Only you must acknowledge your wrongdoing, confess your rebellion against the LORD your God. Confess your promiscuous traffic with foreign gods under every spreading tree, confess that you have not obeyed me. This is the very word of the LORD.  
**3:14**  Come back to me, apostate children, says the LORD, for I am patient with you, and I will take you, one from a city and two from a clan, and bring you to Zion.  
**3:15** There will I give you shepherds after my own heart, and they shall lead you with knowledge and understanding.  
**3:16** In those days, when you have increased and become fruitful in the land, says the LORD, men shall speak no more of the Ark of the Covenant of the LORD; they shall not think of it nor remember it nor resort to it; it will be needed no more.  
**3:17** At that time Jerusalem shall be called the Throne of the LORD. All nations shall gather in Jerusalem to honour the LORD's name; never again shall they follow the promptings of their evil and stubborn hearts.  
**3:18** In those days Judah shall join Israel, and together they shall come from a northern land into the land I gave their fathers as their patrimony.   
**3:19**  I said, How gladly would I treat you as a son, giving you a pleasant land, a patrimony fairer than that of any nation! I said, You shall call me Father and never cease to follow me.  
**3:20** But like a woman who is unfaithful to her lover, so you, Israel, were unfaithful to me. This is the very word of the LORD.  
**3:21** Hark, a sound of weeping on the bare places, Israel's people pleading for mercy! For they have taken to crooked ways and ignored the LORD their God.  
**3:22** Come back to me, wayward sons; I will heal your apostasy. O LORD, we come! We come to thee; for thou art our God.  
**3:23** There is no help in worship on the hill-tops, no help from clamour on the heights; truly in the LORD our God is Israel's only salvation.  
**3:24** From our early days Baal, god of shame, has devoured the fruits of our fathers' labours, their flocks and herds, their sons and daughters.  
**3:25** Let us lie down in shame, wrapped round by our dishonour, for we have sinned against the LORD our God, both we and our fathers, from our early days till now, and we have not obeyed the LORD our God.—  

## 4장

**4:1**  If you will but come back, O Israel, if you will but come back to me, says the LORD, if you will banish your loathsome idols from my sight, and stray no more,  
**4:2** if you swear by the life of the LORD, in truth, in justice and uprightness, then shall the nations pray to be blessed like you and in you shall they boast.  
**4:3**  These are the words of the LORD to the men of Judah and Jerusalem: Break up your fallow ground, do not sow among thorns,  
**4:4** circumcise yourselves to the service of the LORD, circumcise your hearts, men of Judah and dwellers in Jerusalem, lest the fire of my fury blaze up and burn unquenched, because of your evil doings.  
**4:5**  Tell this in Judah, proclaim it in Jerusalem, blow the trumpet throughout the land, sound the muster, give the command, Stand to!—and let us fall back on the fortified cities.  
**4:6** Raise the signal—To Zion! make for safety, lose no time, for I bring disaster out of the north, and dire destruction.  
**4:7** A lion has come out from his lair, the destroyer of nations; he has struck his tents, he has broken camp, to harry your land and lay your cities waste and unpeopled.  
**4:8** Well may you put on sackcloth, beat the breast and wail, for the anger of the LORD is not averted from us.  
**4:9**  On that day, says the LORD, the hearts of the king and his officers shall fail them, priests shall be struck with horror and prophets dumbfounded.  
**4:10**  And I said, O Lord GOD, thou surely didst deceive this people and Jerusalem in saying, 'You shall have peace', while the sword is at our throats.   
**4:11** At that time this people and Jerusalem shall be told: <br> A scorching wind from the high bare places in the wilderness sweeps down upon my people, no breeze for winnowing or for cleansing;  
**4:12** a wind too strong for these will come at my bidding, and now I will state my case against them.  
**4:13**  Like clouds the enemy advances with a whirlwind of chariots; his horses are swifter than eagles— alas, we are overwhelmed!  
**4:14** O Jerusalem, wash the wrongdoing from your heart and you may yet be saved; how long will you cherish your evil schemes?  
**4:15** Hark, a runner from Dan, tidings of evil from Mount Ephraim!  
**4:16** Tell all this to the nations, proclaim the doom of Jerusalem: hordes of invaders come from a distant land, howling against the cities of Judah.  
**4:17** Their pickets are closing in all round her, because she has rebelled against me. This is the very word of the LORD.  
**4:18** Your own ways, your own deeds have brought all this upon you; this is your punishment, and all this comes of your rebellion .  
**4:19**  Oh, the writhing of my bowels and the throbbing of my heart! I cannot keep silence. I hear the sound of the trumpet, the sound of the battle-cry.  
**4:20** Crash upon crash, the land goes down in ruin, my tents are thrown down, their coverings torn to shreds.  
**4:21** How long must I see the standard raised and hear the trumpet call?  
**4:22** My people are fools, they know nothing of me; silly children, with no understanding, they are clever only in wrongdoing, and of doing right they know nothing.  
**4:23**  I saw the earth, and it was without form and void; the heavens, and their light was gone.  
**4:24** I saw the mountains, and they reeled; all the hills rocked to and fro.  
**4:25** I saw, and there was no man, and the very birds had taken flight.  
**4:26** I saw, and the farm-land was wilderness, and the towns all razed to the ground, before the LORD in his anger.  
**4:27** These are the words of the LORD: The whole land shall be desolate, though I will not make an end of it.  
**4:28** Therefore the earth will mourn and the heavens above turn black. For I have made known my purpose; I will not relent or change my mind.  
**4:29** At the sound of the horsemen and archers the whole country is in flight; they creep into caves, they hide in thickets, they scramble up the crags. Every town is forsaken, no one dwells there.  
**4:30** And you, what are you doing? When you dress yourself in scarlet, deck yourself out with golden ornaments, and make your eyes big with antimony, you are beautifying yourself to no purpose. Your lovers spurn you and are out for your life.  
**4:31** I hear a sound as of a woman in labour, the sharp cry of one bearing her first child. It is Zion, gasping for breath, clenching her fists. Ah me! I am weary, weary of slaughter.  

## 5장

**5:1**  Go up and down the streets of Jerusalem and see for yourselves; search her wide squares: can you find any man who acts justly, who seeks the truth, that I may forgive that city?  
**5:2** Men may swear by the life of the LORD but they only perjure themselves.  
**5:3** O LORD, are thine eyes not set upon the truth? Thou didst strike them down, but they took no heed; didst pierce them to the heart, but they refused to learn. They set their faces harder than flint and refused to come back.  
**5:4** I said, 'After all, these are the poor, these are stupid folk, who do not know the way of the LORD, the ordinances of their God.  
**5:5** I will go to the great and speak with them; for they will know the way of the LORD, the ordinances of their God.' But they too have broken the yoke and snapped their traces.  
**5:6** Therefore a lion out of the scrub shall strike them down, a wolf from the plains shall ravage them; a leopard shall prowl about their cities and maul any who venture out. For their rebellious deeds are many, their apostasies past counting.  
**5:7** How can I forgive you for all this? Your sons have forsaken me and sworn by gods that are no gods. I gave them all they needed, yet they preferred adultery, and haunted the brothels;  
**5:8** each neighs after another man's wife, like a well-fed and lusty stallion.  
**5:9** Shall I not punish them for this? the LORD asks. Shall I not take vengeance on such a people?  
**5:10**  Go along her rows of vines and slash them, yet do not make an end of them. Hack away her green branches, for they are not the LORD's.  
**5:11** Faithless are Israel and Judah, both faithless to me. This is the very word of the LORD.  
**5:12** They have denied the LORD, saying, 'He does not exist. No evil shall come upon us; we shall never see sword or famine.  
**5:13** The prophets will prove mere wind, the word not in them.'  
**5:14**  And so, because you talk in this way, these are the words of the LORD the God of Hosts to me: I will make my words a fire in your mouth; and it shall burn up this people like brushwood.  
**5:15** I bring against you, Israel, a nation from afar, an ancient people established long ago, says the LORD. A people whose language you do not know, whose speech you will not understand;  
**5:16** they are all mighty warriors, their jaws are a grave, wide open,  
**5:17** to devour your harvest and your bread, to devour your sons and your daughters, to devour your flocks and your herds, to devour your vines and your fig-trees. They shall batter down the cities in which you trust , walled though they are.  
**5:18**  But in those days, the LORD declares, I will still not make an end of you.  
**5:19** When you ask, 'Why has the LORD our God done all this to us?' I shall answer, 'As you have forsaken me and served alien gods in your own land, so shall you serve foreigners in a land that is not yours.'   
**5:20**  Tell this to the people of Jacob, proclaim it in Judah:  
**5:21** Listen, you foolish and senseless people, who have eyes and see nothing, ears and hear nothing.  
**5:22** Have you no fear of me? says the LORD; will you not shiver before me, before me, who made the shivering sand to bound the sea, a barrier it never can pass? Its waves heave and toss but they are powerless; roar as they may, they cannot pass.  
**5:23** But this people has a rebellious and defiant heart, rebels they have been and now they are clean gone.  
**5:24** They did not say to themselves, 'Let us fear the LORD our God, who gives us the rains of autumn and spring showers in their turn, who brings us unfailingly fixed seasons of harvest.'  
**5:25** But your wrongdoing has upset nature's order, and your sins have kept from you her kindly gifts.  
**5:26** For among my people there are wicked men, who lay snares like a fowler's net and set deadly traps to catch men.  
**5:27** Their houses are full of fraud, as a cage is full of birds. They grow rich and grand,  
**5:28** bloated and rancorous; their thoughts are all of evil, and they refuse to do justice, the claims of the orphan they do not put right nor do they grant justice to the poor.  
**5:29** Shall I not punish them for this? says the LORD; shall I not take vengeance on such a people?  
**5:30** An appalling thing, an outrage, has appeared in this land:  
**5:31** prophets prophesy lies and priests go hand in hand with them, and my people love to have it so. How will you fare at the end of it all?  

## 6장

**6:1**  Save yourselves, men of Benjamin, come out of Jerusalem, blow the trumpet in Tekoa, fire the beacon on Beth-hakkerem, for calamity looms from the north and great disaster.  
**6:2** Zion, delightful and lovely: her end is near—  
**6:3** she to whom the shepherds come and bring their flocks with them. There they pitch their tents all round her, each grazing his own strip of pasture.  
**6:4** Declare war solemnly against her; come, let us attack her at noon. Too late! the day declines and the shadows lengthen.  
**6:5** Come then, let us attack her by night and destroy her palaces.  
**6:6** These are the words of the LORD of Hosts: Cut down the trees of Jerusalem and raise siege-ramps against her, the city whose name is Licence, oppression is rampant in her.  
**6:7** As a well keeps its water fresh, so she keeps her evil fresh. Violence and outrage echo in her streets; sickness and wounds stare me in the face.  
**6:8** Learn your lesson, Jerusalem, lest my love for you be torn from my heart, and I leave you desolate, a land where no one can live.  
**6:9**  These are the words of the LORD of Hosts: Glean the remnant of Israel like a vine, pass your hand like a vintager one last time over the branches.  
**6:10** To whom can I address myself, to whom give solemn warning? Who will hear me? Their ears are uncircumcised; they cannot listen; they treat the LORD's word as a reproach; they show no concern with it.  
**6:11** But I am full of the anger of the LORD, I cannot hold it in. I must pour it out on the children in the street and on the young men in their gangs. Man and wife alike shall be caught in it, the greybeard and the very old.  
**6:12** Their houses shall be turned over to others, their fields and their women alike. For I will raise my hand, says the LORD, against the people of the country.  
**6:13** For all, high and low, are out for ill-gotten gain; prophets and priests are frauds, every one of them;  
**6:14** they dress my people's wound, but skin-deep only, with their saying, 'All is well.' All well? Nothing is well!  
**6:15** Are they ashamed when they practise their abominations? Ashamed? Not they! They can never be put out of countenance. Therefore they shall fall with a great crash , and be brought to the ground on the day of my reckoning. The LORD has said it.  
**6:16**  These are the words of the LORD: Stop at the cross-roads; look for the ancient paths; ask, 'Where is the way that leads to what is good?' Then take that way, and you will find rest for yourselves. But they said, 'We will not.'  
**6:17** Then I will appoint watchmen to direct you; listen for their trumpet-call. But they said, 'We will not.'  
**6:18** Therefore hear, you nations, and take note, all you who witness it, of the plight of this people.  
**6:19** Listen, O earth, I bring ruin on them, the harvest of all their scheming; for they have given no thought to my words and have spurned my instruction.  
**6:20** What good is it to me if frankincense is brought from Sheba and fragrant spices from distant lands? I will not accept your whole-offerings, your sacrifices do not please me.  
**6:21** Therefore these are the words of the LORD: I will set obstacles before this people which shall bring them to the ground; fathers and sons, friends and neighbours shall all perish together.  
**6:22**  These are the words of the LORD: See, a people is coming from a northern land, a great nation rouses itself from earth's farthest corners.  
**6:23** They come with bow and sabre, cruel men and pitiless, bestriding their horses, they sound like the thunder of the sea, they are like men arrayed for battle against you, Zion.  
**6:24** We have heard tell of them and our hands hang limp, agony grips us, the anguish of a woman in labour.  
**6:25** Do not go out into the country, do not walk by the high road; for the foe, sword in hand, is a terror let loose.  
**6:26** Daughter of my people, wrap yourself in sackcloth, sprinkle ashes over yourself, wail bitterly, as one who mourns an only son; in an instant shall the marauder be upon us.  
**6:27** I have appointed you an assayer of my people; you will know how to test them and will assay their conduct;  
**6:28** arch-rebels all of them, mischief-makers, corrupt to a man.  
**6:29** The bellows puff and blow, the furnace glows; in vain does the refiner smelt the ore, lead, copper and iron are not separated out.  
**6:30** Call them spurious silver; for the LORD has spurned them.  

## 7장

**7:1**  THIS WORD CAME FROM THE LORD to Jeremiah.  
**7:2** Stand at the gate of the LORD's house and there make your proclamation: Listen to the words of the LORD, all you men of Judah who come in through these gates to worship him.  
**7:3** These are the words of the LORD of Hosts the God of Israel: Mend your ways and your doings, that I may let you live in this place.  
**7:4** You keep saying, 'This place is the temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD!' This catchword of yours is a lie; put no trust in it.  
**7:5** Mend your ways and your doings, deal fairly with one another,  
**7:6** do not oppress the alien, the orphan, and the widow, shed no innocent blood in this place, do not run after other gods to your own ruin.  
**7:7** Then will I let you live in this place, in the land which I gave long ago to your forefathers for all time.  
**7:8** You gain nothing by putting your trust in this lie.  
**7:9** You steal, you murder, you commit adultery and perjury, you burn sacrifices to Baal, you run after other gods whom you have not known;  
**7:10** then you come and stand before me in this house, which bears my name, and say, 'We are safe'; safe, you think, to indulge in all these abominations.  
**7:11** Do you think that this house, this house which bears my name, is a robbers' cave? I myself have seen all this, says the LORD.  
**7:12** Go to my shrine at Shiloh, which once I made a dwelling for my Name, and see what I did to it because of the wickedness of my people Israel.  
**7:13** And now you have done all these things, says the LORD; though I took pains to speak to you, you did not listen, and though I called, you gave no answer.  
**7:14** Therefore what I did to Shiloh I will do to this house which bears my name, the house in which you put your trust, the place I gave to you and your forefathers;  
**7:15** I will fling you away out of my sight, as I flung away all your kinsfolk, the whole brood of Ephraim.   
**7:16**  Offer up no prayer, Jeremiah, for this people, raise no plea or prayer on their behalf, and do not intercede with me; for I will not listen to you.  
**7:17** Do you not see what is going on in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?  
**7:18** Children are gathering wood, fathers lighting fires, women kneading dough to make crescent-cakes in honour of the queen of heaven; and drink-offerings are poured out to other gods than me—all to provoke and hurt me.  
**7:19** But is it I, says the LORD, whom they hurt? No; it is themselves, covering their own selves with shame.  
**7:20** Therefore, says the Lord GOD, my anger and my fury shall fall on this place, on man and beast, on trees and crops, and it shall burn unquenched.   
**7:21** These are the words of the LORD of Hosts the God of Israel: Add whole-offerings to sacrifices and eat the flesh if you will.  
**7:22** But when I brought your forefathers out of Egypt, I gave them no commands about whole-offering and sacrifice; I said not a word about them.

**18:4** Now and then a vessel he was making out of the clay would be spoilt in his hands, and then he would start again and mould it into another vessel to his liking.  
**18:5** Then the word of the LORD came to me:  
**18:6** Can I not deal with you, Israel, says the LORD, as the potter deals with his clay? You are clay in my hands like the clay in his, O house of Israel.  
**18:7** At any moment I may threaten to uproot a nation or a kingdom, to pull it down and destroy it.  
**18:8** But if the nation which I have threatened turns back from its wicked ways, then I shall think better of the evil I had in mind to bring on it.  
**18:9** Or at any moment I may decide to build or to plant a nation or a kingdom.  
**18:10** But if it does evil in my sight and does not obey me, I shall think better of the good I had in mind for it.  
**18:11** Go now and tell the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem that these are the words of the LORD: I am the potter; I am preparing evil for you and perfecting my designs against you. Turn back, every one of you, from his evil course; mend your ways and your doings.  
**18:12** But they answer, 'Things are past hope. We will do as we like, and each of us will follow the promptings of his own wicked and stubborn heart.'  
**18:13**  Therefore these are the words of the LORD: Inquire among the nations: who ever heard the like of this? The virgin Israel has done a thing most horrible.  
**18:14** Will the snow cease to fall on the rocky slopes of Lebanon? Will the cool rain streaming in torrents ever fail?

**18:16** their own land they lay waste, and men will jeer at it for ever in contempt. All who go by will be horror-struck and shake their heads.

**18:20** Is good to be repaid with evil ? Remember how I stood before thee, pleading on their behalf to avert thy wrath from them.  
**18:21** Therefore give their sons over to famine, leave them at the mercy of the sword. Let their women be childless and widowed, let death carry off their men, let their young men be cut down in battle.  
**18:22** Bring raiders upon them without warning, and let screams of terror ring out from their houses. For they have dug a pit to catch me and have hidden snares for my feet.  
**18:23** Well thou knowest, O LORD, all their murderous plots against me. Do not blot out their wrongdoing or annul their sin; when they are brought stumbling into thy presence, deal with them on the day of thy anger.

**23:28** If a prophet has a dream, let him tell his dream; if he has my word, let him speak my word in truth. What has chaff to do with grain? says the LORD.  
**23:29** Do not my words scorch like fire? says the LORD. Are they not like a hammer that splinters rock?  
**23:30** I am against the prophets, says the LORD, who steal my words from one another for their own use.  
**23:31** I am against the prophets, says the LORD, who concoct words of their own and then say, 'This is his very word.'  
**23:32** I am against the prophets, says the LORD, who dream lies and retail them, misleading my people with wild and reckless falsehoods. It was not I who sent them or commissioned them, and they will do this people no good. This is the very word of the LORD.   
**23:33**  When you are asked by this people or by a prophet or priest what the burden of the LORD's message is, you shall answer, You are his burden, and I shall throw you down, says the LORD.  
**23:34** If prophet or priest or layman uses the term 'the LORD's burden', I will punish that man and his family.  
**23:35** The form of words you shall use in speaking amongst yourselves is: 'What answer has the LORD given?' or, 'What has the LORD said?'  
**23:36** You shall never again mention 'the burden of the LORD'; that is reserved for the man to whom he entrusts his message. If you do, you will make nonsense of the words of the living God, the LORD of Hosts our God.  
**23:37** This is the form you shall use in speaking to a prophet: 'What answer has the LORD given?' or, 'What has the LORD said?'  
**23:38** But to any of you who do say, 'the burden of the LORD', the LORD speaks thus: Because you say, 'the burden of the LORD', though I sent to tell you not to say it,  
**23:39** therefore I myself will carry you like a burden and throw you down, casting out of my sight both you and the city which I gave to you and to your forefathers.  
**23:40** I will inflict on you endless reproach, endless shame which shall never be forgotten.

**26:23** to fetch Uriah from Egypt, and they brought him to the king. He had him put to death by the sword, and his body flung into the burial-place of the common people.

**31:13** Then shall the girl show her joy in the dance, young men and old shall rejoice; I will turn their mourning into gladness, I will relent and give them joy to outdo their sorrow.  
**31:14** I will satisfy the priests with the fat of the land and fill my people with my bounty. This is the very word of the LORD.  
**31:15**  These are the words of the LORD: Hark, lamentation is heard in Ramah, and bitter weeping, Rachel weeping for her sons. She refuses to be comforted: they are no more.  
**31:16**  These are the words of the LORD: Cease your loud weeping, shed no more tears; for there shall be a reward for your toil, they shall return from the land of the enemy.  
**31:17** You shall leave descendants after you ; your sons shall return to their own land.  
**31:18** I listened; Ephraim was rocking in his grief: 'Thou hast trained me to the yoke like an unbroken calf, and now I am trained; restore me, let me return, for thou, LORD, art my God.  
**31:19** Though I broke loose I have repented: now that I am tamed I beat my breast; in shame and remorse I reproach myself for the sins of my youth.'

**31:23**  These are the words of the LORD of Hosts the God of Israel: Once more shall these words be heard in the land of Judah and in her cities, when I restore their fortunes: The LORD bless you, the LORD, your true goal , your holy mountain.

**31:25** For I have given deep draughts to the thirsty, and satisfied those who were faint with hunger.  
**31:26**  Thereupon I woke and looked about me, and my dream had been pleasant.

## 32장

**32:1**  The word which came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah (the eighteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar).  
**32:2** At that time the forces of the Babylonian king were besieging Jerusalem, and the prophet Jeremiah was imprisoned in the court of the guard-house attached to the royal palace.  
**32:3** Zedekiah king of Judah had imprisoned him after demanding what he meant by this prophecy: 'These are the words of the LORD: I will deliver this city into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he shall take it.  
**32:4** Zedekiah king of Judah will not escape from the Chaldaeans but will be surrendered to the king of Babylon; he will speak with him face to face and see him with his own eyes.  
**32:5** Zedekiah will be taken to Babylon and will remain there until I turn my thoughts to him, says the LORD. However much you fight against the Chaldaeans you will have no success.'   
**32:6** Jeremiah said, The word of the LORD came to me:  
**32:7** Hanamel son of your uncle Shallum is coming to see you and will say, 'Buy my field at Anathoth; you have the right of redemption, as next of kin, to buy it.'  
**32:8** As the LORD had foretold, my cousin Hanamel came to the court of the guard-house and said, 'Buy my field at Anathoth in Benjamin. You have the right of redemption and possession as next of kin; buy it.' I knew that this was the LORD's message;  
**32:9** so I bought the field at Anathoth from my cousin Hanamel and weighed out the price, seventeen shekels of silver.  
**32:10** I signed and sealed the deed and had it witnessed; then I weighed out the money on the scales.  
**32:11** I took my copies of the deed of purchase, both the sealed and the unsealed,  
**32:12** and gave them to Baruch son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah, in the presence of Hanamel my cousin, of the witnesses whose names were on the deed of purchase, and of the Judaeans sitting in the court of the guard-house.  
**32:13** In the presence of them all I gave my instructions to Baruch:  
**32:14** These are the words of the LORD of Hosts the God of Israel: Take these copies of the deed of purchase, the sealed and the unsealed, and deposit them in an earthenware jar so that they may be preserved for a long time.  
**32:15** For these are the words of the LORD of Hosts the God of Israel: The time will come when houses, fields, and vineyards will again be bought and sold in this land.  
**32:16**  After I had given the deed of purchase to Baruch son of Neriah, I prayed to the LORD:  
**32:17** O Lord GOD, thou hast made the heavens and the earth by thy great strength and with thy outstretched arm; nothing is impossible for thee.  
**32:18** Thou keepest faith with thousands and thou dost requite the sins of fathers on to the heads of their sons. O great and mighty God whose name is the LORD of Hosts,  
**32:19** great are thy purposes and mighty thy actions. Thine eyes watch all the ways of men, and thou rewardest each according to his ways and as his deeds deserve.  
**32:20** Thou didst work signs and portents in Egypt and hast continued them to this day, both in Israel and amongst all men, and hast won for thyself a name that lives on to this day.  
**32:21** Thou didst bring thy people Israel out of Egypt with signs and portents, with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and with terrible power.  
**32:22** Thou didst give them this land which thou didst promise with an oath to their forefathers, a land flowing with milk and honey.  
**32:23** They came and took possession of it, but they did not obey thee or follow thy law, they disobeyed all thy commands; and so thou hast brought this disaster upon them.  
**32:24** Look at the siege-ramps, the men who are advancing to take the city, and the city given over to its assailants from Chaldaea, the victim of sword, famine, and pestilence. The word thou hast spoken is fulfilled and thou dost see it.  
**32:25** And yet thou hast bidden me buy the field, O Lord GOD, and have the deed witnessed, even though the city is given to the Chaldaeans.   
**32:26** These are the words of the LORD to Jeremiah:  
**32:27** I am the LORD, the God of all flesh; is anything impossible for me?  
**32:28** Therefore these are the words of the LORD: I will deliver this city into the hands of the Chaldaeans and of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and he shall take it.  
**32:29** The Chaldaeans who are fighting against this city will enter it, set it on fire and burn it down, with the houses on whose roofs sacrifices have been burnt to Baal and drink-offerings poured out to other gods, by which I was provoked to anger.   
**32:30** From their earliest days Israel and Judah have been doing what is wrong in my eyes, provoking me to anger by their actions, says the LORD.  
**32:31** For this city has so roused my anger and my fury, from the time it was built down to this day,  
**32:32** that I would rid myself of it. Israel and Judah, their kings, officers, priests, prophets, and everyone living in Jerusalem and Judah have provoked me to anger by their wrongdoing.  
**32:33** They have turned their backs on me and averted their faces; though I took pains to teach them, they would not hear or learn their lesson.  
**32:34** They set up their loathsome idols in the house which bears my name and so defiled it.  
**32:35** They built shrines to Baal in the Valley of Ben-hinnom, to surrender their sons and daughters to Molech. It was no command of mine, nor did it ever enter my thought to do this abominable thing and lead Judah into sin.   
**32:36** Now, therefore, these are the words of the LORD the God of Israel to this city of which you say, 'It is being given over to the king of Babylon, with sword, famine, and pestilence':  
**32:37** I will gather them from all the lands to which I banished them in my anger, rage, and fury, and I will bring them back to this place and let them dwell there undisturbed.  
**32:38** They shall become my people and I will become their God.  
**32:39** I will give them one heart and one way of life so that they shall fear me at all times, for their own good and the good of their children after them.  
**32:40** I will enter into an eternal covenant with them, to follow them unfailingly with my bounty; I will fill their hearts with fear of me, and so they will not turn away from me.  
**32:41** I will rejoice over them, rejoice to do them good, and faithfully with all my heart and soul I will plant them in this land.  
**32:42** For these are the words of the LORD: As I brought on this people such great disaster, so will I bring them all the prosperity which I now promise them.  
**32:43** Fields shall again be bought and sold in this land of which you now say, 'It is desolate, without man or beast; it is given over to the Chaldaeans.'  
**32:44** Fields shall be bought and sold, deeds signed, sealed, and witnessed, in Benjamin, in the neighbourhood of Jerusalem, in the cities of Judah, of the hill-country, of the Shephelah, and of the Negeb, for I will restore their fortunes. This is the very word of the LORD.

**33:3** If you call to me I will answer you, and tell you great and mysterious things which you do not understand.

**33:7** I will restore their fortunes and build them again as once they were.

For I will restore the fortunes of the land as once they were. This is the word of the LORD.

**33:25** These are the words of the LORD: If I had not made my law for day and night nor established a fixed order in heaven and earth,  
**33:26** then I would spurn the descendants of Jacob and of my servant David, and would not take any of David's line to be rulers over the descendants of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. But now I will restore their fortunes and have compassion upon them.

**34:4** But listen to the LORD's word to you, Zedekiah king of Judah. This is his word: You shall not die by the sword;  
**34:5** you will die a peaceful death, and they will kindle fires in your honour like the fires kindled in former times for the kings your ancestors who preceded you. 'Alas, my lord!' they will say as they beat their breasts in mourning for you. This I have spoken. This is the very word of the LORD.  
**34:6** The prophet Jeremiah repeated all this to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem  
**34:7** when the army of the king of Babylon was attacking Jerusalem and the remaining cities of Judah, namely Lachish and Azekah. These were the only fortified cities left in Judah.   
**34:8**  The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD after Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people in Jerusalem to proclaim an act of freedom for the slaves.  
**34:9** All who had Hebrew slaves, male or female, were to set them free; they were not to keep their fellow Judaeans in servitude.  
**34:10** All the officers and people, having made this covenant to set free their slaves, both male and female, and not to keep them in servitude any longer, fulfilled its terms and let them go.  
**34:11** Afterwards, however, they changed their minds and forced back again into slavery the men and women whom they had freed.  
**34:12** Then this word came from the LORD to Jeremiah:  
**34:13** These are the words of the LORD the God of Israel: I made a covenant with your forefathers on the day that I brought them out of Egypt, out of the land of slavery. These were its terms:  
**34:14** 'Within seven years each of you shall set free any Hebrew who has sold himself to you as a slave and has served you for six years; you shall set him free.' Your forefathers did not listen to me or obey me.  
**34:15** You, on the contrary, recently proclaimed an act of freedom for the slaves and made a covenant in my presence, in the house that bears my name, and so have done what is right in my eyes.  
**34:16** But you too have profaned my name. You have all taken back the slaves you had set free and you have forced them, both male and female, to be your slaves again.  
**34:17** Therefore these are the words of the LORD: After you had proclaimed an act of freedom, a deliverance for your kinsmen and your neighbours, you did not obey me; so I will proclaim a deliverance for you, says the LORD, a deliverance over to sword, to pestilence, and to famine, and I will make you repugnant to all the kingdoms of the earth.  
**34:18** You have disregarded my covenant and have not fulfilled the terms to which you yourselves had agreed; so I will make you like the calf of the covenant when they cut it into two and passed between the pieces.  
**34:19** Those who passed between the pieces of the calf were the officers of Judah and Jerusalem, the eunuchs and priests and all the people of the land.  
**34:20** I will give them up to their enemies who seek their lives, and their bodies shall be food for birds of prey and wild beasts.  
**34:21** I will deliver Zedekiah king of Judah and his officers to their enemies who seek their lives and to the army of the king of Babylon, which is now raising the siege.  
**34:22** I will give the command, says the LORD, and will bring them back to this city. They shall attack it and take it and burn it down, and I will make the cities of Judah desolate and unpeopled.

## 36장

**36:1**  IN THE FOURTH YEAR OF JEHOIAKIM son of Josiah, king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from the LORD:  
**36:2** Take a scroll and write on it every word that I have spoken to you about Jerusalem and Judah and all the nations, from the day that I first spoke to you in the reign of Josiah down to the present day.  
**36:3** Perhaps the house of Judah will be warned of the calamity that I am planning to bring on them, and every man will abandon his evil course; then I will forgive their wrongdoing and their sin.  
**36:4** So Jeremiah called Baruch son of Neriah, and he wrote on the scroll at Jeremiah's dictation all the words which the LORD had spoken to him.  
**36:5** He gave Baruch this instruction: 'I am prevented from going to the LORD's house.  
**36:6** You must go there in my place on a fast-day and read the words of the LORD in the hearing of the people from the scroll you have written at my dictation. You shall read them in the hearing of all the men of Judah who come in from their cities.  
**36:7** Then perhaps they will present a petition to the LORD and every man will abandon his evil course; for the LORD has spoken against this people in great anger and wrath.'  
**36:8** Baruch son of Neriah did all that the prophet Jeremiah had told him to do, and read the words of the LORD in the LORD's house out of the book.   
**36:9** In the ninth month of the fifth year of the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, all the people in Jerusalem and all who came there from the cities of Judah proclaimed a fast before the LORD.  
**36:10** Then Baruch read Jeremiah's words in the house of the LORD out of the book in the hearing of all the people; he read them from the room of Gemariah son of the adjutant-general Shaphan in the upper court at the entrance to the new gate of the LORD's house.  
**36:11**  Micaiah son of Gemariah, son of Shaphan, heard all the words of the LORD out of the book  
**36:12** and went down to the palace, to the adjutant-general's room where all the officers were gathered—Elishama the adjutant-general, Delaiah son of Shemaiah, Elnathan son of Akbor, Gemariah son of Shaphan, Zedekiah son of Hananiah and all the other officers.  
**36:13** There Micaiah repeated all the words he had heard when Baruch read out of the book in the people's hearing.  
**36:14** Then the officers sent Jehudi son of Nethaniah, son of Shelemiah, son of Cushi, to Baruch with this message: 'Come here and bring the scroll from which you read in the people's hearing.' So Baruch son of Neriah brought the scroll to them,  
**36:15** and they said, 'Sit down and read it to us.'  
**36:16** When they heard what he read, they turned to each other trembling and said, 'We must report this to the king.'  
**36:17** They asked Baruch to tell them how he had come to write all this.  
**36:18** He said to them, 'Jeremiah dictated every word of it to me, and I wrote it down in ink in the book.'  
**36:19** The officers said to Baruch, 'You and Jeremiah must go into hiding so that no one may know where you are.'  
**36:20**  When they had deposited the scroll in the room of Elishama the adjutant-general, they went to the court and reported everything to the king.   
**36:21** The king sent Jehudi to fetch the scroll. When he had fetched it from the room of Elishama the adjutant-general, he read it to the king and to all the officers in attendance.  
**36:22** It was the ninth month of the year, and the king was sitting in his winter apartments with a fire burning in a brazier in front of him.  
**36:23** When Jehudi had read three or four columns of the scroll, the king cut them off with a penknife and threw them into the fire in the brazier. He went on doing so until the whole scroll had been thrown on the fire.  
**36:24** Neither the king nor any of his courtiers who heard these words showed any fear or rent their clothes;  
**36:25** and though Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah begged the king not to burn the scroll, he would not listen to them.  
**36:26** The king then ordered Jerahmeel, a royal prince , Seraiah son of Azriel, and Shelemiah son of Abdeel to fetch the scribe Baruch and the prophet Jeremiah; but the LORD had hidden them.   
**36:27**  After the king had burnt the scroll with all that Baruch had written on it at Jeremiah's dictation, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:  
**36:28** Now take another scroll and write on it all the words that were on the first scroll which Jehoiakim king of Judah burnt.  
**36:29** You shall say to Jehoiakim king of Judah, These are the words of the LORD: You burnt this scroll and said, Why have you written here that the king of Babylon shall come and destroy this land and exterminate both men and beasts?  
**36:30** Therefore these are the words of the LORD about Jehoiakim king of Judah: He shall have no one to succeed him on the throne of David, and his dead body shall be exposed to scorching heat by day and frost by night.  
**36:31** I will punish him and also his offspring and his courtiers for their wickedness, and I will bring down on them and on the inhabitants of Jerusalem and on the men of Judah all the calamities with which I threatened them, and to which they turned a deaf ear.  
**36:32** Then Jeremiah took another scroll and gave it to the scribe Baruch son of Neriah, who wrote on it at Jeremiah's dictation all the words of the book which Jehoiakim king of Judah had burnt; and much else was added to the same effect.  

## 37장

**37:1**  King Zedekiah son of Josiah was set on the throne of Judah by Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, in succession to Coniah son of Jehoiakim.  
**37:2** Neither he nor his courtiers nor the people of the land listened to the words which the LORD spoke through the prophet Jeremiah.   
**37:3** King Zedekiah sent Jehucal son of Shelemiah and the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah to the prophet Jeremiah to say to him, 'Pray for us to the LORD our God.'  
**37:4** At the time Jeremiah was free to come and go among the people; he had not yet been thrown into prison.  
**37:5** Meanwhile, Pharaoh's army had marched out of Egypt, and when the Chaldaeans who were besieging Jerusalem heard of it they raised the siege.  
**37:6** Then this word came from the LORD to the prophet Jeremiah:  
**37:7** These are the words of the LORD the God of Israel: Say to the king of Judah who sent you to consult me, Pharaoh's army which marched out to help you is on its way back to Egypt, its own land,  
**37:8** and the Chaldaeans will return to the attack. They will capture this city and burn it to the ground.  
**37:9** These are the words of the LORD: Do not deceive yourselves, do not imagine that the Chaldaeans will go away and leave you alone. They will not go;  
**37:10** for even if you defeated the whole Chaldaean force with which you are now fighting, and only the wounded were left lying in their tents, they would rise and burn down the city.   
**37:11**  When the Chaldaean army had raised the siege of Jerusalem because of the advance of Pharaoh's army,  
**37:12** Jeremiah was on the point of leaving Jerusalem to go into Benjamite territory and take possession of his patrimony in the presence of the people there.  
**37:13** Irijah son of Shelemiah, son of Hananiah, the officer of the guard, was in the Benjamin Gate when Jeremiah reached it, and he arrested the prophet, accusing him of going over to the Chaldaeans.  
**37:14** 'It is a lie,' said Jeremiah; 'I am not going over to the Chaldaeans.' Irijah would not listen to him but arrested him and brought him before the officers.  
**37:15** The officers were indignant with Jeremiah; they flogged him and imprisoned him in the house of Jonathan the scribe, which they had converted into a prison;  
**37:16** for Jeremiah had been put into a vaulted pit beneath the house, and here he remained for a long time.   
**37:17** King Zedekiah had Jeremiah brought to him and consulted him privately in the palace, asking him if there was a word from the LORD. 'Indeed there is,' said Jeremiah; 'you shall fall into the hands of the king of Babylon.'  
**37:18** Then Jeremiah said to King Zedekiah, 'What wrong have I done to you or your courtiers or this people? Why have you thrown me into prison?  
**37:19** Where are your prophets who prophesied that the king of Babylon would not attack you or your country?  
**37:20** I pray you now, my lord king, give me a hearing and let my petition be presented: do not send me back to the house of Jonathan the scribe, or I shall die there.'  
**37:21** Then King Zedekiah gave the order and Jeremiah was committed to the court of the guard-house and was granted a daily ration of one loaf from the Street of the Bakers, until the bread in the city was all gone. So Jeremiah remained in the court of the guard-house.  

## 38장

**38:1**  Shephatiah son of Mattan, Gedaliah son of Pashhur, Jucal son of Shelemiah, and Pashhur son of Malchiah heard what Jeremiah was saying to all the people:  
**38:2** These are the words of the LORD: Whoever remains in this city shall die by sword, by famine, or by pestilence, but whoever goes out to surrender to the Chaldaeans shall survive; he shall survive, he shall take home his life and nothing more.  
**38:3** These are the words of the LORD: This city will fall into the hands of the king of Babylon's army, and they will capture it.  
**38:4** Then the officers said to the king, 'The man must be put to death. By talking in this way he is discouraging the soldiers and the rest of the people left in the city. He is pursuing not the people's welfare but their ruin.'  
**38:5** King Zedekiah said, 'He is in your hands; the king is powerless against you.'  
**38:6** So they took Jeremiah and threw him into the pit , in the court of the guard-house, letting him down with ropes. There was no water in the pit, only mud, and Jeremiah sank in the mud.  
**38:7** (7-8) Now Ebed-melech the Cushite, a eunuch, who was in the palace, heard that they had thrown Jeremiah into the pit and went to tell the king, who was seated in the Benjamin Gate.  
**38:8**   
**38:9** 'Your majesty,' he said, 'these men have shown great wickedness in their treatment of the prophet Jeremiah. They have thrown him into the pit, and when there is no more bread in the city he will die of hunger where he lies.'  
**38:10** Thereupon the king told Ebed-melech the Cushite to take three men with him and hoist Jeremiah out of the pit before he died.  
**38:11** So Ebed-melech went to the palace with the men and took some tattered, cast-off clothes from the wardrobe and let them down with ropes to Jeremiah in the pit.  
**38:12** Ebed-melech the Cushite said to Jeremiah, 'Put these old clothes under your armpits to ease the ropes.' Jeremiah did this,  
**38:13** and they pulled him up out of the pit with the ropes; and he remained in the court of the guard-house.   
**38:14**  King Zedekiah had the prophet Jeremiah brought to him by the third entrance to the LORD's house and said to him, 'I want to ask you something; hide nothing from me.'  
**38:15** Jeremiah answered, 'If I speak out, you will certainly.put me to death; if I offer you any advice, you will not take it.'  
**38:16** But King Zedekiah swore to Jeremiah privately, 'By the life of the LORD who gave us our lives, I will not put you to death, nor will I hand you over to these men who are seeking to take your life.'  
**38:17** Jeremiah said to Zedekiah, 'These are the words of the LORD the God of Hosts, the God of Israel: If you go out and surrender to the officers of the king of Babylon, you shall live and this city shall not be burnt down; you and your family shall live.  
**38:18** But if you do not surrender to the officers of the king of Babylon, the city shall fall into the hands of the Chaldaeans, and they shall burn it down, and you will not escape them.'  
**38:19** King Zedekiah said to Jeremiah, 'I am afraid of the Judaeans who have gone over to the enemy. I fear the Chaldaeans will give me up to them and I shall be roughly handled.'  
**38:20** Jeremiah answered. 'They will not give you up. If you obey the LORD in everything I tell you, all will be well with you and you shall live.  
**38:21** But if you refuse to go out and surrender, this is what the LORD has shown me:  
**38:22** all the women left in the king of Judah's palace will be led out to the officers of the king of Babylon and they will say: Your own friends have misled you and have been too strong for you; they have let your feet sink in the mud and have turned away and left you.  
**38:23**  All your women and children will be led out to the Chaldaeans, and you will not escape; you will be seized by the king of Babylon and this city will be burnt down.'  
**38:24** Zedekiah said to Jeremiah, 'Let no one know about this. and you shall not be put to death.  
**38:25** If the officers hear that I have been speaking with you and they come to you and say, "Tell us what you said to the king and what he said to you; hide nothing from us, and we will not put you to death",  
**38:26** then answer, "I was presenting a petition to the king not to send me back to the house of Jonathan to die there."'  
**38:27** The officers all came to Jeremiah and questioned him, and he said to them just what the king had told him to say; so their talk came to an end and they were none the wiser.  
**38:28** Jeremiah remained in the court of the guard-house till the day Jerusalem fell.  

## 39장

**39:1**  IN THE TENTH MONTH OF THE NINTH YEAR of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, Nebuchadrezzar advanced with all his army against Jerusalem, and they laid siege to it.  
**39:2** In the fourth month of the eleventh year of Zedekiah, on the ninth day of the month, the city was thrown open.  
**39:3** All the officers of the king of Babylon came in and took their seats in the middle gate: Nergalsarezer of Simmagir, Nebusarsekim the chief eunuch , Nergalsarezer the commander of the frontier troops , and all the other officers of the king of Babylon.  
**39:4** When Zedekiah king of Judah saw them, he and all his armed escort left the city and fled by night by way of the king's garden through the gate called Between the Two Walls. They escaped towards the Arabah,  
**39:5** but the Chaldaean army pursued them and overtook Zedekiah in the lowlands of Jericho. The king was seized and brought before Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath, and he pleaded his case before him.  
**39:6** The king of Babylon slew Zedekiah's sons before his eyes at Riblah; he also put to death the nobles of Judah.  
**39:7** Then Zedekiah's eyes were put out, and he was bound in fetters of bronze to be brought to Babylon.  
**39:8** The Chaldaeans burnt the royal palace and the house of the LORD and the houses of the people, and pulled down the walls of Jerusalem.  
**39:9** Nebuzaradan captain of the bodyguard deported to Babylon the rest of the people left in the city, those who had deserted to him and any remaining artisans .  
**39:10** At the same time the captain of the guard left behind the weakest class of the people, those who owned nothing at all, and made them vine-dressers and labourers.   
**39:11**  Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon sent orders about Jeremiah to Nebuzaradan captain of the guard.  
**39:12** 'Take him,' he said; 'take special care of him, and do him no harm of any kind, but do for him whatever he says.'  
**39:13** So Nebuzaradan captain of the guard sent Nebushazban the chief eunuch, Nergalsarezer the commander of the frontier troops, and all the chief officers of the king of Babylon,  
**39:14** and they fetched Jeremiah from the court of the guard-house and handed him over to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, to take him out to the Residence. So he stayed with his own people.   
**39:15**  The word of the LORD had come to Jeremiah while he was under arrest in the court of the guard-house:  
**39:16** Go and say to Ebed-melech the Cushite, These are the words of the LORD of Hosts the God of Israel: I will make good the words I have spoken against this city, foretelling ruin and not prosperity, and when that day comes you will be there to see it.  
**39:17** But I will preserve you on that day, says the LORD, and you shall not be handed over to the men you fear.  
**39:18** I will keep you safe and you shall not fall a victim to the sword; because you trusted in me you shall escape, you shall take home your life and nothing more. This is the very word of the LORD.  

## 40장

**40:1**  THE WORD WHICH CAME FROM THE LORD concerning Jeremiah: Nebuzaradan captain of the guard had taken him in chains to Ramah along with the other exiles from Jerusalem and Judah who were being deported to Babylon;  
**40:2** and there he set him free, and took it upon himself to say to Jeremiah, 'The LORD your God threatened this place with disaster,  
**40:3** and has duly carried out his threat that this should happen to all of you because you have sinned against the LORD and not obeyed him.  
**40:4** But as for you, Jeremiah, today I remove the fetters from your wrists. Come with me to Babylon if you wish, and I will take special care of you; but if you prefer not to come, well and good. The whole country lies before you; go wherever you think. best.' Jeremiah had not yet answered  
**40:5** when Nebuzaradan went on , 'Go back to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon has appointed governor of the cities of Judah, and stay with him openly; or else go wherever you choose.' Then the captain of the guard granted him an allowance of food, and gave him a present, and so took leave of him.  
**40:6** Jeremiah then came to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah and stayed with him among the people left in the land.  
**40:7**  When all the captains of the armed bands in the country-side and their men heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam governor of the land, and had put him in charge of the weakest class of the population, men, women, and children, who had not been deported to Babylon,  
**40:8** they came to him at Mizpah; Ishmael son of Nethaniah came, and Johanan and Jonathan sons of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth, the sons of Ephai from Netophah, and Jezaniah of Beth-maacah, with their men.  
**40:9** Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, gave them all this assurance: 'Have no fear of the Chaldaean officers. Settle down in the land and serve the king of Babylon; and then all will be well with you.  
**40:10** I am to stay in Mizpah and attend upon the Chaldaeans whenever they come, and you are to gather in the summer-fruits, wine, and oil, store them in jars, and settle in the towns you have taken over.'  
**40:11** The Judaeans also, in Moab, Ammon, Edom and other countries, heard that the king of Babylon had left a remnant in Judah and that he had set over them Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan.  
**40:12** The Judaeans, therefore, from all the places where they were scattered, came back to Judah and presented themselves before Gedaliah at Mizpah; and they gathered in a considerable store of fruit and wine.   
**40:13**  Johanan son of Kareah and all the captains of the armed bands from the country-side came to Gedaliah at Mizpah  
**40:14** and said to him, 'Do you know that Baalis king of the Ammonites has sent Ishmael son of Nethaniah to assassinate you?' But Gedaliah son of Ahikam did not believe them.  
**40:15** Then Johanan son of Kareah said in private to Gedaliah, 'Let me go, unknown to anyone else, and kill Ishmael son of Nethaniah. Why allow him to assassinate you, and so let all the Judaeans who have rallied round you be scattered and the remnant of Judah lost?'  
**40:16** Gedaliah son of Ahikam answered him, 'Do no such thing. Your story about Ishmael is a lie.'  

## 41장

**41:1** In the seventh month Ishmael son of Nethaniah, son of Elishama, who was a member of the royal house, came with ten men to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah.  
**41:2** While they were at table with him there, Ishmael son of Nethaniah and the ten men with him rose to their feet and assassinated Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon had appointed governor of the land.  
**41:3** They also murdered the Judaeans with him in Mizpah and the Chaldaeans who happened to be there.  
**41:4** The second day after the murder of Gedaliah, while it was not yet common knowledge,  
**41:5** there came eighty men from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria. They had shaved off their beards, their clothes were rent and their bodies gashed, and they were carrying grain-offerings and frankincense to take to the house of the LORD.  
**41:6** Ishmael son of Nethaniah came out weeping from Mizpah to meet them and, when he met them, he said, 'Come to Gedaliah son of Ahikam.'  
**41:7** But as soon as they reached the centre of the town, Ishmael son of Nethaniah and his men murdered them and threw their bodies into a pit,  
**41:8** all except ten of them who said to Ishmael, 'Do not kill us, for we have a secret hoard in the country, wheat and barley, oil and honey.' So he held his hand and did not kill them with the others.  
**41:9** The pit into which he threw the bodies of those whose death he had caused by using Gedaliah's name was the pit which King Asa had made when threatened by Baasha king of Israel; and the dead bodies filled it.  
**41:10** He rounded up the rest of the people in Mizpah, that is the king's daughters and all who remained in Mizpah when Nebuzaradan captain of the guard appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam governor; and with these he set out to cross over into Ammon.  
**41:11** When Johanan son of Kareah and all the captains of the armed bands heard of the crimes committed by Ishmael son of Nethaniah,  
**41:12** they took all the men they had and went to attack him. They found him by the great pool in Gibeon.  
**41:13** The people with Ishmael were glad when they saw Johanan son of Kareah and the captains of the armed bands with him;  
**41:14** and all whom Ishmael had taken prisoner at Mizpah turned and joined Johanan son of Kareah.  
**41:15** But Ishmael son of Nethaniah escaped from Johanan with eight men, and they made their way to the Ammonites.   
**41:16** Johanan son of Kareah and all the captains of the armed bands took from Mizpah the survivors whom he had rescued from Ishmael son of Nethaniah after the murder of Gedaliah son of Ahikam—men, armed and unarmed, women, children, and eunuchs, whom he had brought back from Gibeon.  
**41:17** They started out and broke their journey at Kimham's holding near Bethlehem, on their way into Egypt  
**41:18** to escape the Chaldaeans. They were afraid because Ishmael son of Nethaniah had assassinated Gedaliah son of Ahikam, whom the king of Babylon had appointed governor of the country.  

## 42장

**42:1**  All the captains of the armed bands, including Johanan son of Kareah and Azariah son of Hoshaiah, together with the people, high and low,  
**42:2** came to the prophet Jeremiah and said to him, 'May our petition be acceptable to you: Pray to the LORD your God on our behalf and on behalf of this remnant; for, as you see for yourself, only a few of us remain out of many.  
**42:3** Pray that the LORD your God may tell us which way we ought to go and what we ought to do.'  
**42:4** Then the prophet Jeremiah said to them, 'I have heard your request and will pray to the LORD your God as you desire, and whatever answer the LORD gives I will tell you; I will keep nothing back.'  
**42:5** They said to Jeremiah, 'May the LORD be a true and faithful witness against us if we do not keep our oath! We swear that we will do whatever the LORD your God sends you to tell us.  
**42:6** Whether we like it or not, we will obey the LORD our God to whom we send you, in order that it may be well with us; we will obey the LORD our God.'   
**42:7**  Within ten days the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah;  
**42:8** so he summoned Johanan son of Kareah, all the captains of the armed bands with him, and all the people, both high and low.  
**42:9** He said to them, These are the words of the LORD the God of Israel, to whom you sent me to present your petition:  
**42:10** If you will stay in this land, then I will build you up and not pull you down, I will plant you and not uproot you; I grieve for the disaster which I have brought upon you.  
**42:11** Do not be afraid of the king of Babylon whom you now fear. Do not be afraid of him, says the LORD; for I am with you, to save you and deliver you from his power.  
**42:12** I will show you compassion, and he too will have compassion on you; he will let you stay on your own soil.  
**42:13** But it may be that you will disobey the LORD your God and say, 'We will not stay in this land.  
**42:14** No, we will go to Egypt, where we shall see no sign of war, never hear the sound of the trumpet, and not starve for want of bread; and there we will live.'  
**42:15** Then hear the word of the LORD, you remnant of Judah. These are the words of the LORD of Hosts the God of Israel: If you are bent on going to Egypt, if you do settle there,  
**42:16** then the sword you fear will overtake you in Egypt, and the famine you dread will still be with you, even in Egypt, and there you will die.  
**42:17** All the men who are bent on going to Egypt and settling there will die by sword, by famine, or by pestilence; not one shall escape or survive the calamity which I will bring upon them.  
**42:18** These are the words of the LORD of Hosts the God of Israel: As my anger and my wrath were poured out upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so will my wrath be poured out upon you when you go to Egypt; you will become an object of execration and horror, of ridicule and reproach; you will never see this place again.  
**42:19** To you, then, remnant of Judah, the LORD says. Do not go to Egypt. Make no mistake, I can bear witness against you this day.  
**42:20** You deceived yourselves when you sent me to the LORD your God and said, 'Pray for us to the LORD our God; tell us all that the LORD our God says and we will do it.'  
**42:21** I have told you everything today; but you have not obeyed the LORD your God in what he sent me to tell you.  
**42:22** So now be sure of this: you will die by sword, by famine, and by pestilence in the place where you desire to go and make your home.  

## 43장

**43:1**  When Jeremiah had finished reciting to the people all that the LORD their God had sent him to say,  
**43:2** Azariah son of Hoshaiah and Johanan son of Kareah and their party had the effrontery to say to Jeremiah, 'You are lying; the LORD our God has not sent you to forbid us to go and make our home in Egypt.  
**43:3** Baruch son of Neriah has incited you against us in order to put us in the power of the Chaldaeans, so that they may kill us or deport us to Babylon.'  
**43:4** Johanan son of Kareah and the captains of the armed bands and all the people refused to obey the LORD and stay in Judah.  
**43:5** So Johanan son of Kareah and the captains collected the remnant of Judah, all who had returned from the countries among which they had been scattered to make their home in Judah—  
**43:6** men, women and children, including the king's daughters, all the people whom Nebuzaradan captain of the guard had left with Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, as well as the prophet Jeremiah and Baruch son of Neriah;  
**43:7** these all went to Egypt and came to Tahpanhes, disobeying the LORD.   
**43:8** The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah at Tahpanhes:  
**43:9** Take some large stones and set them in cement in the pavement at the entrance to Pharaoh's palace in Tahpanhes. Let the Judaeans see you do it  
**43:10** and say to them, These are the words of the LORD of Hosts the God of Israel: I will send for my servant Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and he will place his throne on these stones that I have set there, and spread his canopy over them.  
**43:11** He will then proceed to strike Egypt down, killing those doomed to death, taking captive those who are for captivity, and putting to the sword those who are for the sword.  
**43:12** He will set fire to the temples of the Egyptian gods, burning the buildings and carrying the gods into captivity. He will scour the land of Egypt as a shepherd scours his clothes to rid them of lice. He will leave Egypt with his purpose achieved.  
**43:13** He will smash the sacred pillars of Beth-shemesh in Egypt and burn down the temples of the Egyptian gods.  

## 44장

**44:1**  The word that came to Jeremiah for all the Judaeans who were living in Egypt, in Migdol, Tahpanhes, Noph, and the district of Pathros:  
**44:2** These are the words of the LORD of Hosts the God of Israel: You have seen the calamity that I brought upon Jerusalem and all the cities of Judah: today they are laid waste and left uninhabited,  
**44:3** all because of the wickedness of those who provoked me to anger by going after other gods, gods unknown to them, by burning sacrifices to them. It was you and your fathers who did this.  
**44:4** I took pains to send all my servants the prophets to you with this warning: 'Do not do this abominable thing which I hate.'  
**44:5** But your fathers would not listen; they paid no heed. They did not give up their wickedness or cease to burn sacrifices to other gods;  
**44:6** so my anger and wrath raged like a fire through the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem, and they became the desolate ruin that they are today.   
**44:7** Now these are the words of the LORD the God of Hosts, the God of Israel: Why bring so great a disaster upon yourselves? Why bring destruction upon Judaeans, men and women, children and babes, and leave yourselves without a survivor?  
**44:8** This is what comes of your provoking me by all your idolatry in burning sacrifices to other gods in Egypt where you have made your home. You will destroy yourselves and become an object of ridicule and reproach to all the nations of the earth.  
**44:9** Have you forgotten all the wickedness committed by your forefathers, by the kings of Judah and their wives, by yourselves and your wives in the land of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?  
**44:10** To this day you have shown no remorse, no reverence; you have not conformed to the law and the statutes which I set before you and your forefathers.  
**44:11** These, therefore, are the words of the LORD of Hosts the God of Israel: I have made up my mind to bring calamity upon you and exterminate the people of Judah.  
**44:12** I will deal with the remnant of Judah who were bent on going to make their home in Egypt; in Egypt they shall all meet their end. Some shall fall by the sword, others will meet their end by famine. High and low alike will die by sword or by famine and will be an object of execration and horror, of ridicule and reproach.  
**44:13** I will punish those who live in Egypt as I punished those in Jerusalem, by sword, famine, and pestilence.  
**44:14** Those who had remained in Judah came to make their home in Egypt, confident that they would return and live once more in Judah. But they shall not return ; not one of them shall survive, not one escape.   
**44:15** Then all the men who knew that their wives were burning sacrifices to other gods and the crowds of women standing by answered Jeremiah,  
**44:16** 'We will not listen to what you tell us in the name of the LORD.  
**44:17** We intend to fulfil all the promises by which we have bound ourselves: we will burn sacrifices to the queen of heaven and pour drink-offerings to her as we used to do, we and our fathers, our kings and our princes, in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. We then had food in plenty and were content; no calamity touched us.  
**44:18** But from the time we left off burning sacrifices to the queen of heaven and pouring drink-offerings to her, we have been in great want, and in the end we have fallen victims to sword and famine.'  
**44:19** And the women said, 'When we burnt sacrifices to the queen of heaven and poured drink-offerings to her, our husbands knew full well that we were making crescent-cakes marked with her image and pouring drink-offerings to her.'  
**44:20** When Jeremiah received this answer from these men and women and all the people, he said,  
**44:21** 'The LORD did not forget those sacrifices which you and your fathers, your kings and princes and the people of the land burnt in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, and they mounted up in his mind  
**44:22** until he could no longer tolerate them, so wicked were your deeds and so abominable the things you did. Your land became a desolate waste, an object of horror and ridicule, with no inhabitants, as it still is.  
**44:23** This calamity has come upon you because you burnt these sacrifices and sinned against the LORD and did not obey the LORD or conform to his laws, statutes, and teachings.'   
**44:24** Jeremiah further said to all the people and to the women, Listen to the word of the LORD, all you from Judah who live in Egypt.  
**44:25** These are the words of the LORD of Hosts the God of Israel: You women have made your actions match your words. 'We will carry out our vows', you said, 'to burn sacrifices to the queen of heaven and to pour drink-offerings to her.' Well then, fulfil your vows by all means, and make your words good.  
**44:26** But listen to the word of the LORD, all you from Judah who live in Egypt. I have sworn by my great name, says the LORD, that my name shall never again be on the lips of the men of Judah; they shall no longer swear in Egypt, 'By the life of the Lord GOD.'  
**44:27** I am on the watch to bring you evil and not good, and all the men of Judah who are in Egypt shall meet their end by sword and by famine until not one is left . It is then that all the survivors of Judah who have made their home in Egypt shall know whose word prevails, theirs or mine.

**52:1**  ZEDEKIAH WAS TWENTY-ONE YEARS OLD when he came to the throne, and he reigned in Jerusalem for eleven years; his mother was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah.  
**52:2** He did what was wrong in the eyes of the LORD, as Jehoiakim had done.  
**52:3** Jerusalem and Judah so angered the LORD that in the end he banished them from his sight; and Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.   
**52:4** In the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon advanced with all his army against Jerusalem, invested it and erected watch-towers against it on every side;  
**52:5** the siege lasted till the eleventh year of King Zedekiah.  
**52:6** In the fourth month of that year, on the ninth day of the month, when famine was severe in the city and there was no food for the common people,  
**52:7** the city was thrown open. When Zedekiah king of Judah saw this, he and all his armed escort left the city and fled by night through the gate called Between the Two Walls, near the king's garden. They escaped towards the Arabah, although the Chaldaeans were surrounding the city.  
**52:8** But the Chaldaean army pursued the king and overtook him in the lowlands of Jericho; and all his company was dispersed.  
**52:9** The king was seized and brought before the king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath, where he pleaded his case before him.  
**52:10** The king of Babylon slew Zedekiah's sons before his eyes; he also put to death all the princes of Judah in Riblah.  
**52:11** Then the king of Babylon put Zedekiah's eyes out, bound him with fetters of bronze, brought him to Babylon and committed him to prison till the day of his death.   
**52:12** In the fifth month, on the tenth day of the month, in the nineteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, Nebuzaradan, captain of the king's bodyguard , came to Jerusalem  
**52:13** and set fire to the house of the LORD and the royal palace; all the houses in the city, including the mansion of Gedaliah were burnt down.  
**52:14** The Chaldaean forces with the captain of the guard pulled down the walls all round Jerusalem.  
**52:15**  Nebuzaradan captain of the guard deported the rest of the people left in the city, those who had deserted to the king of Babylon and any remaining artisans.  
**52:16** The captain of the guard left only the weakest class of people to be vine-dressers and labourers.   
**52:17** The Chaldaeans broke up the pillars of bronze in the house of the LORD, the trolleys, and the sea of bronze, and took the metal to Babylon.  
**52:18** They took also the pots, shovels, snuffers, tossing-bowls, saucers, and all the vessels of bronze used in the service of the temple.  
**52:19** The captain of the guard took away the precious metal, whether gold or silver, of which the cups, firepans, tossing-bowls, pots, lamp-stands, saucers, and flagons were made.  
**52:20** The bronze of the two pillars, of the one sea and of the twelve oxen supporting it, which King Solomon had made for the house of the LORD, was beyond weighing.  
**52:21** The one pillar was eighteen cubits high and twelve cubits in circumference; it was hollow and the metal was four fingers thick.  
**52:22** It had a capital of bronze, five cubits high, and a decoration of network and pomegranates ran all round it, wholly of bronze. The other pillar, with its pomegranates, was exactly like it.  
**52:23** Ninety-six pomegranates were exposed to view and there were a hundred in all on the network all round.   
**52:24**  The captain of the guard took Seraiah the chief priest and Zephaniah the deputy chief priest and the three on duty at the entrance;  
**52:25** he took also from the city a eunuch who was in charge of the fighting men, seven of those with right of access to the king who were still in the city, the adjutant-general whose duty was to muster the people for war, and sixty men of the people who were still there.  
**52:26** These Nebuzaradan captain of the guard brought to the king of Babylon at Riblah.  
**52:27** There, in the land of Hamath. the king of Babylon had them flogged and put to death. So Judah went into exile from their own land.
