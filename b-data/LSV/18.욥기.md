# 18.욥기 (LSV)



## 1장

**1:1** There has been a man in the land of Uz - his name Job - and that man has been perfect and upright - both fearing God, and turning aside from evil.  
**1:2** And there are borne to him seven sons and three daughters,  
**1:3** and his substance is seven thousand sheep, and three thousand camels, and five hundred pairs of oxen, and five hundred female donkeys, and a very abundant service; and that man is greater than any of the sons of the east.  
**1:4** And his sons have gone and made a banquet - the house of each [in] his day - and have sent and called to their three sisters to eat and to drink with them;  
**1:5** and it comes to pass, when they have gone around the days of the banquet, that Job sends and sanctifies them, and has risen early in the morning, and caused burnt-offerings to ascend - the number of them all - for Job said, “Perhaps my sons have sinned, yet blessed God in their heart.” Thus Job does all the days.  
**1:6** And the day is, that sons of God come to station themselves by YHWH, and there also comes Satan in their midst.  
**1:7** And YHWH says to Satan, “Where do you come from?” And Satan answers YHWH and says, “From going to and fro in the land, and from walking up and down on it.”  
**1:8** And YHWH says to Satan, “Have you set your heart against My servant Job because there is none like him in the land, a man perfect and upright, fearing God, and turning aside from evil?”  
**1:9** And Satan answers YHWH and says, “Is Job fearing God for nothing?  
**1:10** Have You not made a hedge for him, and for his house, and for all that he has - all around?  
**1:11** You have blessed the work of his hands, and his substance has spread in the land, and yet, put forth Your hand now, and strike against anything that he has - if not, he blesses You to Your face!”  
**1:12** And YHWH says to Satan, “Behold, all that he has [is] in your hand, only do not put forth your hand to him.” And Satan goes out from the presence of YHWH.  
**1:13** And the day is, that his sons and his daughters are eating and drinking wine in the house of their brother, the firstborn.  
**1:14** And a messenger has come to Job and says, “The oxen have been plowing, and the female donkeys feeding by their sides,  
**1:15** and Sheba falls, and takes them, and they have struck the young men by the mouth of the sword, and I have escaped - only I alone - to declare [it] to you.”  
**1:16** While this [one] is speaking another has also come and says, “Fire of God has fallen from the heavens, and burns among the flock, and among the young men, and consumes them, and I have escaped - only I alone - to declare [it] to you.”  
**1:17** While this [one] is speaking another also has come and says, “Chaldeans made three heads, and rush on the camels, and take them, and they have struck the young men by the mouth of the sword, and I have escaped - only I alone - to declare [it] to you.”  
**1:18** While this [one] is speaking another has also come and says, “Your sons and your daughters are eating, and drinking wine, in the house of their brother, the firstborn.  
**1:19** And behold, a great wind has come from over the wilderness, and strikes against the four corners of the house, and it falls on the young men, and they are dead, and I have escaped - only I alone - to declare [it] to you.”  
**1:20** And Job rises, and tears his robe, and shaves his head, and falls to the earth, and pays respect,  
**1:21** and he says, “I came forth naked from the womb of my mother, || And naked I return there. YHWH has given and YHWH has taken; Let the Name of YHWH be blessed.”  
**1:22** In all this Job has not sinned, nor given folly to God.  

## 2장

**2:1** And the day is, that sons of God come to station themselves by YHWH, and there also comes Satan in their midst to station himself by YHWH.  
**2:2** And YHWH says to Satan, “From where have you come?” And Satan answers YHWH and says, “From going to and fro in the land, and from walking up and down in it.”  
**2:3** And YHWH says to Satan, “Have you set your heart to My servant Job because there is none like him in the land, a man perfect and upright, fearing God and turning aside from evil? And still he is keeping hold on his integrity, and you move Me against him to swallow him up for nothing!”  
**2:4** And Satan answers YHWH and says, “A skin for a skin, and all that a man has he gives for his life.  
**2:5** Yet, put forth Your hand now, and strike to his bone and to his flesh - if not, he blesses You to Your face!”  
**2:6** And YHWH says to Satan, “Behold, he [is] in your hand; only take care of his life.”  
**2:7** And Satan goes forth from the presence of YHWH, and strikes Job with a severe ulcer from the sole of his foot to his crown.  
**2:8** And he takes to him a potsherd to scrape himself with it, and he is sitting in the midst of the ashes.  
**2:9** And his wife says to him, “You are still keeping hold on your integrity: bless God and die.”  
**2:10** And he says to her, “As one of the foolish women speaks, you speak; indeed, do we receive the good from God, and we do not receive the bad?” In all this Job has not sinned with his lips.  
**2:11** And three of the friends of Job hear of all this evil that has come on him, and they each come in from his place - Eliphaz the Temanite, and Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite - and they have met together to come to bemoan him, and to comfort him;  
**2:12** and they lift up their eyes from afar and have not discerned him, and they lift up their voice and weep, and each tears his robe, and sprinkle dust on their heads - heavenward.  
**2:13** And they sit with him on the earth seven days and seven nights, and there is none speaking to him a word when they have seen that the pain has been very great.  

## 3장

**3:1** After this Job has opened his mouth, and reviles his day.  
**3:2** And Job answers and says:  
**3:3** “Let the day perish in which I am born, || And the night that has said: A man-child has been conceived.  
**3:4** That day - let it be darkness, || Do not let God require it from above, || Nor let light shine on it.  
**3:5** Let darkness and death-shade redeem it, || Let a cloud dwell on it, || Let them terrify it as the most bitter of days.  
**3:6** That night - let thick darkness take it, || Let it not be united to days of the year, || Let it not come into the number of months.  
**3:7** Behold! That night - let it be barren, || Let no singing come into it.  
**3:8** Let the cursers of day mark it, || Who are ready to wake up Leviathan.  
**3:9** Let the stars of its twilight be dark, || Let it wait for light, and there is none, || And let it not look on the eyelids of the dawn.  
**3:10** Because it has not shut the doors || Of the womb that was mine! And hide misery from my eyes.  
**3:11** Why do I not die from the womb? I have come forth from the belly and gasp!  
**3:12** Why have knees been before me? And what [are] breasts, that I suck?  
**3:13** For now, I have lain down, and am quiet, I have slept - then there is rest to me,  
**3:14** With kings and counselors of earth, || These building ruins for themselves.  
**3:15** Or with princes - they have gold, || They are filling their houses [with] silver.  
**3:16** (Or I am not as a hidden abortion, || As infants - they have not seen light.)  
**3:17** There the wicked have ceased troubling, || And there the wearied rest in power.  
**3:18** Together prisoners have been at ease, || They have not heard the voice of an exactor,  
**3:19** Small and great [are] the same there. And a servant [is] free from his lord.  
**3:20** Why does He give light to the miserable, and life to the bitter soul?  
**3:21** Who are waiting for death, and it is not, || And they seek it above hid treasures.  
**3:22** Who are glad - to joy, || They rejoice when they find a grave.  
**3:23** To a man whose way has been hidden, || And whom God shuts up?  
**3:24** For before my food, my sighing comes, || And my roarings [are] poured out as waters.  
**3:25** For I feared a fear and it meets me, || And what I was afraid of comes to me.  
**3:26** I was not safe - nor was I quiet - Nor was I at rest - and trouble comes!”  

## 4장

**4:1** And Eliphaz the Temanite answers and says:  
**4:2** “Has one tried a word with you? You are weary! And who is able to keep in words?  
**4:3** Behold, you have instructed many, || And feeble hands you make strong.  
**4:4** Your words raise up the stumbling one, || And you strengthen bowing knees.  
**4:5** But now, it comes to you, || And you are weary; It strikes to you, and you are troubled.  
**4:6** Is your reverence not your confidence? Your hope - the perfection of your ways?  
**4:7** Now remember, || Who, being innocent, has perished? And where have the upright been cut off?  
**4:8** As I have seen - plowers of iniquity, || And sowers of misery, reap it!  
**4:9** From the breath of God they perish, || And from the spirit of His anger [are] consumed.  
**4:10** The roaring of a lion, || And the voice of a fierce lion, || And teeth of young lions have been broken.  
**4:11** An old lion is perishing without prey, || And the whelps of the lioness separate.  
**4:12** And a thing is secretly brought to me, || And my ear receives a little of it.  
**4:13** In thoughts from visions of the night, || In the falling of deep sleep on men,  
**4:14** Fear has met me, and trembling, || And the multitude of my bones caused to fear.  
**4:15** And a spirit passes before my face, || The hair of my flesh stands up;  
**4:16** It stands, and I do not discern its aspect, || A likeness [is] before my eyes, || Silence! And I hear a voice:  
**4:17** Is mortal man more righteous than God? Is a man cleaner than his Maker?  
**4:18** Behold, He puts no credence in His servants, || Nor sets praise in His messengers.  
**4:19** Also - the inhabitants of houses of clay || (Whose foundation [is] in the dust, || They bruise them before a moth).  
**4:20** From morning to evening are beaten down, || Without any regarding, they perish forever.  
**4:21** Has their excellence not been removed with them? They die, and not in wisdom!”  

## 5장

**5:1** “Pray, call, is there any to answer you? And to which of the holy ones do you turn?  
**5:2** For provocation slays the perverse, || And envy puts to death the simple,  
**5:3** I have seen the perverse taking root, || And I mark his habitation straight away,  
**5:4** His sons are far from safety, || And they are bruised in the gate, || And there is no deliverer.  
**5:5** Whose harvest the hungry eat, || And even take it from the thorns, || And the designing swallowed their wealth.  
**5:6** For sorrow does not come forth from the dust, || Nor does misery spring up from the ground.  
**5:7** For man is born to misery, || And the sparks go high to fly.  
**5:8** Yet I inquire for God, || And for God I give my word,  
**5:9** Doing great things, and there is no searching. Wonderful, until there is no numbering.  
**5:10** Who is giving rain on the face of the land, || And is sending waters on the out-places.  
**5:11** To set the low on a high place, || And the mourners have been high [in] safety.  
**5:12** Making void thoughts of the cunning, || And their hands do not execute wisdom.  
**5:13** Capturing the wise in their subtlety, || And the counsel of wrestling ones was hurried,  
**5:14** By day they meet darkness, || And as night - they grope at noon.  
**5:15** He saves the needy from the sword in their mouth, || And from a strong hand,  
**5:16** And there is hope for the poor, || And perverseness has shut her mouth.  
**5:17** Behold, the blessedness of mortal man, || God reproves him: And do not despise the discipline of the Mighty,  
**5:18** For He pains, and He binds up, || He strikes, and His hands heal.  
**5:19** In six distresses He delivers you, || And in seven evil does not strike on you.  
**5:20** In famine He has redeemed you from death, || And in battle from the hands of the sword.  
**5:21** When the tongue scourges you are hid, || And you are not afraid of destruction, || When it comes.  
**5:22** At destruction and at hunger you mock, || And of the beast of the earth, || You are not afraid.  
**5:23** (For with sons of the field [is] your covenant, || And the beast of the field || Has been at peace with you.)  
**5:24** And you have known that your tent [is] peace, || And inspected your habitation, and do not err,  
**5:25** And have known that your seed [is] numerous, || And your offspring as the herb of the earth;  
**5:26** You come in full age to the grave, || As the going up of a stalk in its season.  
**5:27** Behold, this - we searched it out - it [is] right, listen; And you, know for yourself!”  

## 6장

**6:1** And Job answers and says:  
**6:2** “O that my provocation were thoroughly weighed, || And my calamity in balances || They would lift up together!  
**6:3** For now it is heavier than the sands of the sea, || Therefore my words have been rash.  
**6:4** For arrows of the Mighty [are] with me, || Whose poison is drinking up my spirit. Terrors of God array themselves [for] me!  
**6:5** Does a wild donkey bray over tender grass? Does an ox low over his provender?  
**6:6** Is an insipid thing eaten without salt? Is there sense in the drivel of dreams?  
**6:7** My soul is refusing to touch! They [are] as my sickening food.  
**6:8** O that my request may come, || That God may grant my hope!  
**6:9** That God would please - and bruise me, || Loose His hand and cut me off!  
**6:10** And yet it is my comfort || (And I exult in pain - He does not spare), || That I have not hidden || The sayings of the Holy One.  
**6:11** What [is] my power that I should hope? And what [is] my end that I should prolong my life?  
**6:12** Is my strength the strength of stones? Is my flesh bronze?  
**6:13** Is my help not with me, || And substance driven from me?  
**6:14** To a despiser of his friends [is] shame, || And the fear of the Mighty he forsakes.  
**6:15** My brothers have deceived as a brook, || As a stream of brooks they pass away.  
**6:16** That are black because of ice, || By them snow hides itself.  
**6:17** By the time they are warm they have been cut off, || By its being hot they have been || Extinguished from their place.  
**6:18** The paths turn aside of their way, || They ascend into emptiness, and are lost.  
**6:19** Passengers of Tema looked expectingly, || Travelers of Sheba hoped for them.  
**6:20** They were ashamed that one has trusted, || They have come to it and are confounded.  
**6:21** Surely now you have become the same! You see a downfall, and are afraid.  
**6:22** Is it because I said, Give to me? And, By your power bribe for me?  
**6:23** And, Deliver me from the hand of an adversary? And, Ransom me from the hand of terrible ones?  
**6:24** Show me, and I keep silent, || And what I have erred, let me understand.  
**6:25** How powerful have been upright sayings, || And what reproof from you reproves?  
**6:26** For reproof - do you reckon words? And for wind - sayings of the desperate?  
**6:27** You cause anger to fall on the fatherless, || And are strange to your friend.  
**6:28** And now, please, look on me, || Even to your face do I lie?  
**6:29** Please turn back, let it not be perverseness, || Indeed, turn back again - my righteousness [is] in it.  
**6:30** Is there perverseness in my tongue? Does my palate not discern calamity?”  

## 7장

**7:1** “Is there not warfare to man on earth? And his days as the days of a hired worker?  
**7:2** As a servant desires the shadow, || And as a hired worker expects his wage,  
**7:3** So I have been caused to inherit months of vanity, || And they numbered nights of misery to me.  
**7:4** If I lay down, then I have said, When do I rise, || And evening has been measured? And I have been full of tossings until dawn.  
**7:5** My flesh has been clothed [with] worms, || And a clod of dust, || My skin has been shriveled and is loathsome,  
**7:6** My days swifter than a loom, || And they are consumed without hope.  
**7:7** Remember that my life [is] a breath, || My eye does not turn back to see good.  
**7:8** The eye of my beholder does not behold me. Your eyes [are] on me - and I am not.  
**7:9** A cloud has been consumed, and it goes, || So he who is going down to Sheol does not come up.  
**7:10** He does not turn to his house again, || Nor does his place discern him again.  
**7:11** Also I do not withhold my mouth - I speak in the distress of my spirit, I talk in the bitterness of my soul.  
**7:12** Am I a sea [monster], or a dragon, || That You set a watch over me?  
**7:13** When I said, My bed comforts me, || In my talking He takes away my couch.  
**7:14** And You have frightened me with dreams, || And You terrify me from visions,  
**7:15** And my soul chooses strangling, || Death rather than my bones.  
**7:16** I have wasted away - I do not live for all time. Cease from me, for my days [are] vanity.  
**7:17** What [is] man that You magnify him? And that You set Your heart to him?  
**7:18** And inspect him in the mornings, || [And] in the evenings try him?  
**7:19** How long do You not look from me? You do not desist until I swallow my spittle.  
**7:20** I have sinned, what do I do to You, || O watcher of man? Why have You set me for a mark to You, || And I am for a burden to myself - and what?  
**7:21** You do not take away my transgression, || And [do not] cause my iniquity to pass away, || Because now, I lie down in dust, || And You have sought me - and I am not!”  

## 8장

**8:1** And Bildad the Shuhite answers and says:  
**8:2** “Until when do you speak these things? And a strong wind - sayings of your mouth?  
**8:3** Does God pervert judgment? And does the Mighty One pervert justice?  
**8:4** If your sons have sinned before Him, || And He sends them away, || By the hand of their transgression,  
**8:5** If you seek for God early, || And make supplication to the Mighty,  
**8:6** If you [are] pure and upright, || Surely now He wakes for you, || And has completed || The habitation of your righteousness.  
**8:7** And your beginning has been small, || And your latter end is very great.  
**8:8** For inquire, please, of a former generation, || And prepare for a search of their fathers,  
**8:9** For we [are] of yesterday, and we do not know, || For our days [are] a shadow on earth.  
**8:10** Do they not show you - speak to you, || And from their heart bring forth words?  
**8:11** Does a rush rise without a marsh? A reed increase without water?  
**8:12** While it [is] in its budding - uncropped, || Even before any herb it withers.  
**8:13** So [are] the paths of all forgetting God, || And the hope of the profane perishes,  
**8:14** Whose confidence is loathsome, || And the house of a spider his trust.  
**8:15** He leans on his house - and it does not stand, || He takes hold on it - and it does not abide.  
**8:16** He [is] green before the sun, || And over his garden his branch goes out.  
**8:17** His roots are wrapped by a heap, || He looks for a house of stones.  
**8:18** If [one] destroys him from his place, || Then it has feigned concerning him, || I have not seen you!  
**8:19** Behold, this [is] the joy of His way, || And from the dust others spring up.  
**8:20** Behold, God does not reject the perfect, || Nor takes hold on the hand of evildoers.  
**8:21** While He fills your mouth with laughter, || And your lips with shouting,  
**8:22** Those hating you put on shame, || And the tent of the wicked is not!”  

## 9장

**9:1** And Job answers and says:  
**9:2** “Truly I have known that [it is] so, || But how is man righteous with God?  
**9:3** If he delights to strive with Him - He does not answer him one of a thousand.  
**9:4** Wise in heart and strong in power - Who has hardened toward Him and is at peace?  
**9:5** Who is removing mountains, || And they have not known, || Who has overturned them in His anger.  
**9:6** Who is shaking earth from its place, || And its pillars move themselves.  
**9:7** Who is commanding to the sun, and it does not rise, || And the stars He seals up.  
**9:8** Stretching out the heavens by Himself, || And treading on the heights of the sea,  
**9:9** Making the Great Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades, || And the inner chambers of the south.  
**9:10** Doing great things until there is no searching, || And wonderful, until there is no numbering.  
**9:11** Behold, He goes over by me, and I do not see, || And He passes on, and I do not attend to it.  
**9:12** Behold, He snatches away, who brings it back? Who says to Him, What [are] You doing?  
**9:13** God does not turn back His anger, || Proud helpers have bowed under Him.  
**9:14** How much less do I answer Him? Choose out my words with Him?  
**9:15** Whom, though I were righteous, I do not answer, || For my judgment I make supplication.  
**9:16** Though I had called and He answers me, I do not believe that He gives ear [to] my voice.  
**9:17** Because He bruises me with a storm, || And has multiplied my wounds for nothing.  
**9:18** He does not permit me to refresh my spirit, || But fills me with bitter things.  
**9:19** If of power, behold, the Strong One; And if of judgment - who convenes me?  
**9:20** If I am righteous, my mouth declares me wicked; [If] I am perfect, it declares me perverse.  
**9:21** I am perfect; I do not know my soul, I despise my life.  
**9:22** It is the same thing, therefore I said, || He is consuming the perfect and the wicked.  
**9:23** If a scourge puts to death suddenly, He laughs at the trial of the innocent.  
**9:24** Earth has been given || Into the hand of the wicked. He covers the faces of her judges, || If not - where, who [is] he?  
**9:25** My days have been swifter than a runner, || They have fled, they have not seen good,  
**9:26** They have passed on with ships of reed, || As an eagle darts on food.  
**9:27** Though I say, I forget my talking, || I forsake my corner, and I brighten up!  
**9:28** I have been afraid of all my griefs, || I have known that You do not acquit me.  
**9:29** I become wicked; why [is] this? I labor [in] vain.  
**9:30** If I have washed myself with snow-water, || And purified my hands with soap,  
**9:31** Then You dip me in corruption, || And my garments have detested me.  
**9:32** But if a man like myself - I answer Him, || We come together into judgment.  
**9:33** If there were a mediator between us, || He places his hand on us both.  
**9:34** He turns aside His rod from off me, || And His terror does not make me afraid,  
**9:35** I speak, and do not fear Him, but I am not right with myself.”  

## 10장

**10:1** “My soul has been weary of my life, I leave off my talking to myself, I speak in the bitterness of my soul.  
**10:2** I say to God, Do not condemn me, || Let me know why You strive [with] me.  
**10:3** Is it good for You that You oppress? That You despise the labor of Your hands, || And shine on the counsel of the wicked?  
**10:4** Do you have eyes of flesh? Do You see as man sees?  
**10:5** [Are] Your days as the days of man? Your years as the days of a man?  
**10:6** That You inquire for my iniquity, || And seek for my sin?  
**10:7** For You know that I am not wicked, || And there is no deliverer from Your hand.  
**10:8** Your hands have taken pains about me, || And they make me together all around, || And You swallow me up!  
**10:9** Please remember || That You have made me as clay, || And You bring me back to dust.  
**10:10** Do You not pour me out as milk? And curdle me as cheese?  
**10:11** Skin and flesh You put on me, || And fence me with bones and sinews.  
**10:12** Life and kindness You have done with me. And Your inspection has preserved my spirit.  
**10:13** And these You have laid up in Your heart, I have known that this [is] with You.  
**10:14** If I sinned, then You have observed me, || And do not acquit me from my iniquity,  
**10:15** If I have done wickedly - woe to me, || And righteously - I do not lift up my head, || Full of shame - then see my affliction,  
**10:16** And it rises - as a lion You hunt me. And You turn back - You show Yourself wonderful in me.  
**10:17** You renew Your witnesses against me, and multiply Your anger with me, || Changes and warfare [are] with me.  
**10:18** And why from the womb || Have You brought me forth? I expire, and the eye does not see me.  
**10:19** I am as [if] I had not been, || I am brought from the belly to the grave,  
**10:20** Are my days not few? Cease then, and put from me, || And I brighten up a little,  
**10:21** Before I go, and do not return, || To a land of darkness and death-shade,  
**10:22** A land of obscurity as thick darkness, || Death-shade - and no order, || And the shining [is] as thick darkness.”  

## 11장

**11:1** And Zophar the Naamathite answers and says:  
**11:2** “Is a multitude of words not answered? And is a man of lips justified?  
**11:3** Your boastings make men keep silent, || You scorn, and none is causing blushing!  
**11:4** And you say, My discourse [is] pure, || And I have been clean in Your eyes.  
**11:5** And yet, O that God had spoken! And opens His lips with you.  
**11:6** And declares to you secrets of wisdom, for counsel has foldings. And know that God forgets of your iniquity for you.  
**11:7** Do you find out God by searching? To perfection find out the Mighty One?  
**11:8** Heights of the heavens - what [can] you do? Deeper than Sheol - what [can] you know?  
**11:9** Its measure [is] longer than earth, and broader than the sea.  
**11:10** If He passes on, and shuts up, and assembles, || Who then reverses it?  
**11:11** For He has known men of vanity, || And He sees iniquity, || And one does not consider [it]!  
**11:12** And empty man is bold, || And man is born [as] the colt of a wild donkey.  
**11:13** If you have prepared your heart, || And have spread out your hands to Him,  
**11:14** If iniquity [is] in your hand, put it far off, || And do not let perverseness dwell in your tents.  
**11:15** For then you lift up your face from blemish, || And you have been firm, and do not fear.  
**11:16** For you forget misery, || As waters passed away you remember.  
**11:17** And age rises above the noon, || You fly - you are as the morning.  
**11:18** And you have trusted because there is hope, || And searched - in confidence you lie down,  
**11:19** And you have rested, || And none is causing trembling, || And many have begged [at] your face;
