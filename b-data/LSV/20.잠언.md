# 20.잠언 (LSV)



## 1장

**1:1** Proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel:  
**1:2** For knowing wisdom and instruction, || For understanding sayings of intelligence,  
**1:3** For receiving the instruction of wisdom, || Righteousness, judgment, and uprightness,  
**1:4** For giving to simple ones - prudence, || To a youth - knowledge and discretion.  
**1:5** (The wise hear and increase learning, || And the intelligent obtain counsels.)  
**1:6** For understanding a proverb and its sweetness, || Words of the wise and their acute sayings.  
**1:7** Fear of YHWH [is the] beginning of knowledge, || Fools have despised wisdom and instruction!  
**1:8** Hear, my son, the instruction of your father, || And do not leave the law of your mother,  
**1:9** For they [are] a graceful wreath to your head, || And chains to your neck.  
**1:10** My son, if sinners entice you, do not be willing.  
**1:11** If they say, “Come with us, we lay wait for blood, || We watch secretly for the innocent without cause,  
**1:12** We swallow them as Sheol - alive, || And whole - as those going down [to] the pit,  
**1:13** We find every precious substance, || We fill our houses [with] spoil,  
**1:14** You cast your lot among us, || One purse is - to all of us.”  
**1:15** My son! Do not go in the way with them, || Withhold your foot from their path,  
**1:16** For their feet run to evil, || And they hurry to shed blood.  
**1:17** Surely in vain is the net spread out before the eyes of any bird.  
**1:18** And they lay wait for their own blood, || They watch secretly for their own lives.  
**1:19** So [are] the paths of every gainer of dishonest gain, || It takes the life of its owners.  
**1:20** Wisdom cries aloud in an out-place, || She gives forth her voice in broad places,  
**1:21** She calls at the head of the multitudes, || In the openings of the gates, || In the city she says her sayings:  
**1:22** “Until when, you simple, do you love simplicity? And have scorners desired their scorning? And do fools hate knowledge?  
**1:23** Turn back at my reproof, behold, || I pour forth my spirit to you, || I make known my words with you.  
**1:24** Because I have called, and you refuse, || I stretched out my hand, and none is attending,  
**1:25** And you slight all my counsel, || And you have not desired my reproof.  
**1:26** I also laugh in your calamity, || I deride when your fear comes,  
**1:27** When your fear comes as destruction, || And your calamity comes as a windstorm, || When adversity and distress come on you.  
**1:28** Then they call me, and I do not answer, || They seek me earnestly, and do not find me.  
**1:29** Because that they have hated knowledge, || And have not chosen the fear of YHWH.  
**1:30** They have not consented to my counsel, || They have despised all my reproof,  
**1:31** And they eat of the fruit of their way, || And they are filled from their own counsels.  
**1:32** For the turning of the simple slays them, || And the security of the foolish destroys them.  
**1:33** And whoever is listening to me dwells confidently, || And [is] quiet from fear of evil!”  

## 2장

**2:1** My son, if you accept my sayings, || And lay up my commands with you,  
**2:2** To cause your ear to attend to wisdom, || You incline your heart to understanding,  
**2:3** For if you call for intelligence, || [And] give forth your voice for intelligence,  
**2:4** If you seek her as silver, || And search for her as hid treasures,  
**2:5** Then you understand the fear of YHWH, || And you find the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For YHWH gives wisdom, || Knowledge and understanding from His mouth.  
**2:7** Even to lay up substance for the upright, || A shield for those walking uprightly.  
**2:8** To keep the paths of judgment, || And He preserves the way of His saints.  
**2:9** Then you understand righteousness, || And judgment, and uprightness - every good path.  
**2:10** For wisdom comes into your heart, || And knowledge is pleasant to your soul,  
**2:11** Thoughtfulness watches over you, || Understanding keeps you,  
**2:12** To deliver you from an evil way, || From any speaking contrary things,  
**2:13** Who are forsaking paths of uprightness, || To walk in ways of darkness,  
**2:14** Who are rejoicing to do evil, || They delight in [the] contrariness of the wicked,  
**2:15** Whose paths [are] crooked, || Indeed, they are perverted in their ways.  
**2:16** To deliver you from the strange woman, || From the stranger who has made her sayings smooth,  
**2:17** Who is forsaking the guide of her youth, || And has forgotten the covenant of her God.  
**2:18** For her house has inclined to death, || And her paths to Rephaim.  
**2:19** None going in to her return, || Nor do they reach the paths of life.  
**2:20** That you go in the way of the good, || And keep the paths of the righteous.  
**2:21** For the upright inhabit the earth, || And the perfect are left in it,  
**2:22** And the wicked are cut off from the earth, || And treacherous dealers plucked out of it!  

## 3장

**3:1** My son! Do not forget my law, || And let your heart keep my commands,  
**3:2** For [the] length of [your] days and years, || Life and peace they add to you.  
**3:3** Do not let kindness and truth forsake you, || Bind them on your neck, || Write them on the tablet of your heart,  
**3:4** And find grace and good understanding || In the eyes of God and man.  
**3:5** Trust to YHWH with all your heart, || And do not lean to your own understanding.  
**3:6** In all your ways know Him, || And He makes your paths straight.  
**3:7** Do not be wise in your own eyes, || Fear YHWH, and turn aside from evil.  
**3:8** It is healing to your navel, || And moistening to your bones.  
**3:9** Honor YHWH from your substance, || And from the beginning of all your increase;  
**3:10** And your barns are filled [with] plenty, || And your presses break forth [with] new wine.  
**3:11** Discipline of YHWH, my son, do not despise, || And do not be distressed with His reproof,  
**3:12** For whom YHWH loves He reproves, || Even as a father the son He is pleased with.  
**3:13** O the blessedness of a man [who] has found wisdom, || And of a man [who] brings forth understanding.  
**3:14** For better [is] her merchandise || Than the merchandise of silver, || And than gold - her increase.  
**3:15** She [is] precious above rubies, || And all your pleasures are not comparable to her.  
**3:16** Length of days [is] in her right hand, || In her left [are] wealth and honor.  
**3:17** Her ways [are] ways of pleasantness, || And all her paths [are] peace.  
**3:18** She [is] a tree of life to those laying hold on her, || And whoever is retaining her [is] blessed.  
**3:19** YHWH founded the earth by wisdom, || He prepared the heavens by understanding.  
**3:20** By His knowledge depths have been broken, || And clouds drop dew.  
**3:21** My son! Do not let them turn from your eyes, || Keep wisdom and thoughtfulness,  
**3:22** And they are life to your soul, and grace to your neck.  
**3:23** Then you go your way confidently, || And your foot does not stumble.  
**3:24** If you lie down, you are not afraid, || Indeed, you have lain down, || And your sleep has been sweet.  
**3:25** Do not be afraid of sudden fear, || And of the desolation of the wicked when it comes.  
**3:26** For YHWH is at your side, || And He has kept your foot from capture.  
**3:27** Do not withhold good from its owners, || When your hand [is] toward God to do [it].  
**3:28** Do not say to your friend, || “Go, and return, and tomorrow I give,” || When substance [is] with you.  
**3:29** Do not devise evil against your neighbor, || And he sitting confidently with you.  
**3:30** Do not strive with a man without cause, || If he has not done you evil.  
**3:31** Do not be envious of a man of violence, || Nor fix on any of his ways.  
**3:32** For the perverted [is] an abomination to YHWH, || And His secret counsel [is] with the upright.  
**3:33** The curse of YHWH [is] in the house of the wicked. And He blesses the habitation of the righteous.  
**3:34** If He scorns the scorners, || Yet He gives grace to the humble.  
**3:35** The wise inherit glory, || And fools are bearing away shame!  

## 4장

**4:1** Hear, you sons, the instruction of a father, || And give attention to know understanding.  
**4:2** For I have given to you good learning, do not forsake my law.  
**4:3** For I have been a son to my father - tender, || And an only one before my mother.  
**4:4** And he directs me, and he says to me: “Let your heart retain my words, || Keep my commands, and live.  
**4:5** Get wisdom, get understanding, || Do not forget, nor turn away || From the sayings of my mouth.  
**4:6** Do not forsake her, and she preserves you, || Love her, and she keeps you.  
**4:7** The first thing [is] wisdom - get wisdom, || And with all your getting get understanding.  
**4:8** Exalt her and she lifts you up, || She honors you when you embrace her.  
**4:9** She gives a wreath of grace to your head, || She gives you a crown of beauty freely.”  
**4:10** Hear, my son, and receive my sayings, || And years of life [are] multiplied to you.  
**4:11** I have directed you in a way of wisdom, || I have caused you to tread in paths of uprightness.  
**4:12** In your walking your step is not restricted, || And if you run, you do not stumble.  
**4:13** Lay hold on instruction, do not desist, || Keep her, for she [is] your life.  
**4:14** Do not enter into the path of the wicked, || And do not be blessed in a way of evildoers.  
**4:15** Avoid it, do not pass over into it, || Turn aside from it, and pass on.  
**4:16** For they do not sleep if they do no evil, || And their sleep has been taken away violently, || If they do not cause [some] to stumble.  
**4:17** For they have eaten bread of wickedness, || And they drink wine of violence.  
**4:18** And the path of the righteous [is] as a shining light, || Going and brightening until the day is established,  
**4:19** The way of the wicked [is] as darkness, || They have not known at what they stumble.  
**4:20** My son, give attention to my words, || Incline your ear to my sayings,  
**4:21** Do not let them turn aside from your eyes, || Preserve them in the midst of your heart.  
**4:22** For they [are] life to those finding them, || And healing to all their flesh.  
**4:23** Above every charge keep your heart, || For out of it [are] the outgoings of life.  
**4:24** Turn aside a contrary mouth from you, || And put perverse lips far from you,  
**4:25** Your eyes look straightforward, || And your eyelids look straight before you.  
**4:26** Ponder the path of your feet, || And all your ways [are] established.  
**4:27** Do not incline [to] the right or to the left, || Turn aside your foot from evil!  

## 5장

**5:1** My son! Give attention to my wisdom, || Incline your ear to my understanding,  
**5:2** To observe thoughtfulness, || And your lips keep knowledge.  
**5:3** For the lips of a strange woman drop honey, || And her mouth [is] smoother than oil,  
**5:4** And her latter end [is] bitter as wormwood, || Sharp as a sword [with] mouths.  
**5:5** Her feet are going down to death, || Her steps take hold of Sheol.  
**5:6** The path of life - lest you ponder, || Her paths have moved - you do not know.  
**5:7** And now, you sons, listen to me, || And do not turn from sayings of my mouth.  
**5:8** Keep your way far from off her, || And do not come near to the opening of her house,  
**5:9** Lest you give your splendor to others, || And your years to the fierce,  
**5:10** Lest strangers be filled [with] your power, || And your labors in the house of a stranger,  
**5:11** And you have howled in your latter end, || In the consumption of your flesh and your food,  
**5:12** And have said, “How I have hated instruction, || And my heart has despised reproof,  
**5:13** And I have not listened to the voice of my teachers, || And have not inclined my ear to my teachers.  
**5:14** As a little thing I have been all evil, || In the midst of an assembly and a congregation.”  
**5:15** Drink waters out of your own cistern, || Even flowing ones out of your own well.  
**5:16** Let your fountains be scattered abroad, || In broad places streams of waters.  
**5:17** Let them be to you for yourself, || And not to strangers with you.  
**5:18** Let your fountain be blessed, || And rejoice because of the wife of your youth,  
**5:19** A doe of loves, and a roe of grace! Let her loves satisfy you at all times, || Magnify yourself in her love continually.  
**5:20** And why do you magnify yourself, || My son, with a stranger? And embrace the bosom of a strange woman?  
**5:21** For the ways of each are before the eyes of YHWH, || And He is pondering all his paths.  
**5:22** His own iniquities capture the wicked, || And he is holden with the ropes of his sin.  
**5:23** He dies without instruction, || And magnifies himself in the abundance of his folly!  

## 6장

**6:1** My son! If you have been guarantor for your friend, || Have struck your hand for a stranger,  
**6:2** Have been snared with sayings of your mouth, || Have been captured with sayings of your mouth,  
**6:3** Do this now, my son, and be delivered, || For you have come into the hand of your friend. Go, trample on yourself, and strengthen your friend,  
**6:4** Do not give sleep to your eyes, || And slumber to your eyelids,  
**6:5** Be delivered as a roe from the hand, || And as a bird from the hand of a fowler.  
**6:6** Go to the ant, O slothful one, || See her ways and be wise;  
**6:7** Which has no captain, overseer, and ruler,  
**6:8** She prepares her bread in summer, || She has gathered her food in harvest.  
**6:9** Until when, O slothful one, do you lie? When do you arise from your sleep?  
**6:10** A little sleep, a little slumber, || A little clasping of the hands to rest,  
**6:11** And your poverty has come as a traveler, || And your want as an armed man.  
**6:12** A man of worthlessness, a man of iniquity, || Walking [with] perverseness of mouth,  
**6:13** Winking with his eyes, speaking with his feet, || Directing with his fingers,  
**6:14** Contrariness [is] in his heart, devising evil at all times, || He sends forth contentions.  
**6:15** Therefore his calamity comes suddenly, || He is broken instantly - and no healing.  
**6:16** These six has YHWH hated, || Indeed, seven [are] abominations to His soul:  
**6:17** High eyes, || False tongues, || And hands shedding innocent blood,  
**6:18** A heart devising thoughts of vanity, || Feet hastening to run to evil,  
**6:19** A false witness [who] breathes out lies, || And one sending forth contentions between brothers.  
**6:20** Keep, my son, the command of your father, || And do not leave the law of your mother.  
**6:21** Bind them on your heart continually, || Tie them on your neck.  
**6:22** In your going up and down, it leads you, || In your lying down, it watches over you, || And you have awoken - it talks [with] you.  
**6:23** For the command [is] a lamp, || And the Law a light, || And a way of life [are] reproofs of instruction,  
**6:24** To preserve you from an evil woman, || From the flattery of the tongue of a strange woman.  
**6:25** Do not desire her beauty in your heart, || And do not let her take you with her eyelids.  
**6:26** For a harlot consumes to a cake of bread, || And an adulteress hunts the precious soul.  
**6:27** Does a man take fire into his bosom, || And are his garments not burned?  
**6:28** Does a man walk on the hot coals, || And are his feet not scorched?  
**6:29** So [is] he who has gone in to the wife of his neighbor, || None who touches her is innocent.  
**6:30** They do not despise the thief, || When he steals to fill his soul when he is hungry,  
**6:31** And being found he repays sevenfold, || He gives all the substance of his house.  
**6:32** He who commits adultery [with] a woman lacks heart, || He who does it is destroying his soul.  
**6:33** He finds a stroke and shame, || And his reproach is not wiped away,  
**6:34** For jealousy [is] the fury of a man, || And he does not spare in a day of vengeance.  
**6:35** He does not accept the appearance of any atonement, || Indeed, he does not consent, || Though you multiply bribes!  

## 7장

**7:1** My son! Keep my sayings, || And lay up my commands with you.  
**7:2** Keep my commands, and live, || And my law as the pupil of your eye.  
**7:3** Bind them on your fingers, || Write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to wisdom, “You [are] my sister.” And cry to understanding, “Relative!”  
**7:5** To preserve you from a strange woman, || From a stranger who has made her sayings smooth.  
**7:6** For at a window of my house, || I have looked out through my casement,  
**7:7** And I see among the simple ones, || I discern among the sons, || A young man lacking understanding,  
**7:8** Passing on in the street, near her corner, || And the way [to] her house he steps,  
**7:9** In the twilight - in the evening of day, || In the darkness of night and blackness.  
**7:10** And behold, a woman to meet him - (A harlot’s dress, and watchful of heart,  
**7:11** She [is] noisy, and stubborn, her feet do not rest in her house.  
**7:12** Now in an out-place, now in broad places, || And she lies in wait near every corner) -   
**7:13** And she laid hold on him and kissed him, || She has hardened her face and says to him,  
**7:14** “Sacrifices of peace-offerings [are] by me, || Today I have completed my vows.  
**7:15** Therefore I have come forth to meet you, || To earnestly seek your face, and I find you.  
**7:16** I decked my bed [with] ornamental coverings, || Carved works - cotton of Egypt.  
**7:17** I sprinkled my bed [with] myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.  
**7:18** Come, we are filled [with] love until the morning, || We delight ourselves in loves.  
**7:19** For the man is not in his house, || He has gone on a long journey.  
**7:20** He has taken a bag of money in his hand, || At the day of the new moon he comes to his house.”  
**7:21** She turns him aside with the abundance of her speech, || She forces him with the flattery of her lips.  
**7:22** He is going after her straight away, he comes as an ox to the slaughter, || And as a chain to the discipline of a fool,  
**7:23** Until an arrow splits his liver, || As a bird has hurried to a snare, || And has not known that it [is] for its life.  
**7:24** And now, you sons, listen to me, || And give attention to sayings of my mouth.  
**7:25** Do not let your heart turn to her ways, || Do not wander in her paths,  
**7:26** For many [are] the wounded she caused to fall, || And mighty [are] all her slain ones.  
**7:27** The ways of Sheol - her house, || Going down to inner chambers of death!  

## 8장

**8:1** Does wisdom not call? And understanding give forth her voice?  
**8:2** At the head of high places by the way, || She has stood between the paths,  
**8:3** At the side of the gates, at the mouth of the city, || The entrance of the openings, she cries aloud,  
**8:4** “To you, O men, I call, || And my voice [is] to the sons of men.  
**8:5** Understand, you simple ones, prudence, || And you fools, understand the heart,  
**8:6** Listen, for I speak noble things, || And the opening of my lips [is] uprightness.  
**8:7** For my mouth utters truth, || And wickedness [is] an abomination to my lips.  
**8:8** All the sayings of my mouth [are] in righteousness, || Nothing in them is contrary and perverse.  
**8:9** All of them [are] plain to the intelligent, || And upright to those finding knowledge.  
**8:10** Receive my instruction, and not silver, || And knowledge rather than choice gold.  
**8:11** For wisdom [is] better than rubies, || Indeed, all delights are not comparable with it.  
**8:12** I, wisdom, have dwelt with prudence, || And I find out a knowledge of purposes.  
**8:13** The fear of YHWH [is] to hate evil; Pride, and arrogance, and an evil way, || And a contrary mouth, I have hated.  
**8:14** Counsel and substance [are] mine, || I [am] understanding, I have might.  
**8:15** By me kings reign, and princes decree righteousness,  
**8:16** By me chiefs rule, and nobles, || All judges of earth.  
**8:17** I love those loving me, || And those seeking me earnestly find me.  
**8:18** Wealth and honor [are] with me, || Lasting substance and righteousness.  
**8:19** My fruit [is] better than gold, even fine gold, || And my increase than choice silver.  
**8:20** I cause to walk in a path of righteousness, || In midst of paths of judgment,  
**8:21** To cause my lovers to inherit substance, || Indeed, I fill their treasures.  
**8:22** YHWH possessed me - the beginning of His way, || Before His works since then.  
**8:23** I was anointed from the age, from the first, || From former states of the earth.  
**8:24** In there being no depths, I was brought forth, || In there being no fountains heavy [with] waters,  
**8:25** Before mountains were sunk, || Before heights, I was brought forth.  
**8:26** While He had not made the earth, and out-places, || And the top of the dusts of the world.  
**8:27** In His preparing the heavens I [am] there, || In His decreeing a circle on the face of the deep,  
**8:28** In His strengthening clouds above, || In His making strong fountains of the deep,  
**8:29** In His setting for the sea its limit, || And the waters do not transgress His command, || In His decreeing the foundations of earth,  
**8:30** Then I am near Him, a workman, || And I am a delight - day by day. Rejoicing before Him at all times,  
**8:31** Rejoicing in the habitable part of His earth, || And my delights [are] with the sons of men.  
**8:32** And now, you sons, listen to me, || Indeed, blessed are they who keep my ways.  
**8:33** Hear instruction, and be wise, and do not slight.  
**8:34** O the blessedness of the man listening to me, || To watch at my doors day by day, || To watch at the doorposts of my entrance.  
**8:35** For whoever is finding me, has found life, || And brings out goodwill from YHWH.  
**8:36** And whoever is missing me, is wronging his soul, || All hating me have loved death!”  

## 9장

**9:1** Wisdom has built her house, || She has hewn out her pillars - seven.  
**9:2** She has slaughtered her slaughter, || She has mingled her wine, || Indeed, she has arranged her table.  
**9:3** She has sent forth her girls, || She cries on the tops of the high places of the city:  
**9:4** “Who [is] simple? Let him turn aside here.” Whoever lacks heart: she has said to him,  
**9:5** “Come, eat of my bread, || And drink of the wine I have mingled.  
**9:6** Forsake the simple and live, || And be blessed in the way of understanding.”  
**9:7** The instructor of a scorner || Is receiving for it - shame, || And a reprover of the wicked - his blemish.  
**9:8** Do not reprove a scorner, lest he hate you, || Give reproof to the wise, and he loves you.  
**9:9** Give to the wise, and he is wiser still, || Make known to the righteous, || And he increases learning.  
**9:10** The commencement of wisdom [is] the fear of YHWH, || And a knowledge of the Holy Ones [is] understanding.  
**9:11** For by me your days multiply, || And years of life are added to you.  
**9:12** If you have been wise, you have been wise for yourself, || And you have scorned - you bear [it] alone.  
**9:13** A foolish woman [is] noisy, || Simple, and has not known what.  
**9:14** And she has sat at the opening of her house, || On a throne - the high places of the city,  
**9:15** To call to those passing by the way, || Who are going straight [on] their paths.  
**9:16** “Who [is] simple? Let him turn aside here.” And whoever lacks heart - she said to him,  
**9:17** “Stolen waters are sweet, || And hidden bread is pleasant.”  
**9:18** And he has not known that Rephaim [are] there, || Her invited ones in deep places of Sheol!  

## 10장

**10:1** Proverbs of Solomon. A wise son causes a father to rejoice, || And a foolish son [is] an affliction to his mother.  
**10:2** Treasures of wickedness do not profit, || And righteousness delivers from death.  
**10:3** YHWH does not cause the soul of the righteous to hunger, || And He thrusts away the desire of the wicked.  
**10:4** Poor [is] he who is working [with] a slothful hand, || And the hand of the diligent makes rich.  
**10:5** Whoever is gathering in summer [is] a wise son, || Whoever is sleeping in harvest [is] a son causing shame.  
**10:6** Blessings [are] for the head of the righteous, || And the mouth of the wicked covers violence.  
**10:7** The remembrance of the righteous [is] for a blessing, || And the name of the wicked rots.  
**10:8** The wise in heart accepts commands, || And a talkative fool kicks.  
**10:9** Whoever is walking in integrity walks confidently, || And whoever is perverting his ways is known.  
**10:10** Whoever is winking the eye gives grief, || And a talkative fool kicks.  
**10:11** A fountain of life [is] the mouth of the righteous, || And the mouth of the wicked covers violence.  
**10:12** Hatred awakens contentions, || And love covers over all transgressions.  
**10:13** Wisdom is found in the lips of the intelligent, || And a rod [is] for the back of him who is lacking understanding.  
**10:14** The wise lay up knowledge, and the mouth of a fool [is] near ruin.  
**10:15** The wealth of the rich [is] his strong city, || The ruin of the poor [is] their poverty.  
**10:16** The wage of the righteous [is] for life, || The increase of the wicked for sin.  
**10:17** A traveler to life [is] he who is keeping instruction, || And whoever is forsaking rebuke is erring.  
**10:18** Whoever is covering hatred with lying lips, || And whoever is bringing out an evil report is a fool.  
**10:19** In the abundance of words transgression does not cease, || And whoever is restraining his lips [is] wise.  
**10:20** The tongue of the righteous [is] chosen silver, || The heart of the wicked - as a little thing.  
**10:21** The lips of the righteous delight many, || And fools die for lack of heart.  
**10:22** The blessing of YHWH - it makes rich, || And He adds no grief with it.  
**10:23** To execute inventions [is] as play to a fool, || And wisdom to a man of understanding.
