# 34.나훔 (NRSV)



## 1장

**1:1** An oracle concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum of Elkosh.  
**1:2** A jealous and avenging God is the LORD,<br>the LORD is avenging and wrathful;<br>the LORD takes vengeance on his adversaries<br>and rages against his enemies.  
**1:3** The LORD is slow to anger but great in power,<br>and the LORD will by no means clear the guilty.<br>His way is in whirlwind and storm,<br>and the clouds are the dust of his feet.  
**1:4** He rebukes the sea and makes it dry,<br>and he dries up all the rivers;<br>Bashan and Carmel wither,<br>and the bloom of Lebanon fades.  
**1:5** The mountains quake before him,<br>and the hills melt;<br>the earth heaves before him,<br>the world and all who live in it.  
**1:6** Who can stand before his indignation?<br>Who can endure the heat of his anger?<br>His wrath is poured out like fire,<br>and by him the rocks are broken in pieces.  
**1:7** The LORD is good,<br>a stronghold in a day of trouble;<br>he protects those who take refuge in him,  
**1:8** even in a rushing flood.<br>He will make a full end of his adversaries,<sup>①</sup><br>and will pursue his enemies into darkness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk: Heb <i>of her place</i>  
**1:9** Why do you plot against the LORD?<br>He will make an end;<br>no adversary will rise up twice.  
**1:10** Like thorns they are entangled,<br>like drunkards they are drunk;<br>they are consumed like dry straw.  
**1:11** From you one has gone out<br>who plots evil against the LORD,<br>one who counsels wickedness.  
**1:12** Thus says the LORD,<br>“Though they are at full strength and many,<sup>①</sup><br>they will be cut off and pass away.<br>Though I have afflicted you,<br>I will afflict you no more.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**1:13** And now I will break off his yoke from you<br>and snap the bonds that bind you.”  
**1:14** The LORD has commanded concerning you:<br>“Your name shall be perpetuated no longer;<br>from the house of your gods I will cut off<br>the carved image and the cast image.<br>I will make your grave, for you are worthless.”  
**1:15** <f><sup>①</sup> Look! On the mountains the feet of one<br>who brings good tidings,<br>who proclaims peace!<br>Celebrate your festivals, O Judah,<br>fulfill your vows,<br>for never again shall the wicked invade you;<br>they are utterly cut off.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch <a name='B:410 2:1'>2.1</a> in Heb  

## 2장

**2:1** A shatterer<sup>①</sup> has come up against you.<br>Guard the ramparts;<br>watch the road;<br>gird your loins;<br>collect all your strength.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>scatterer</i>  
**2:2** (For the LORD is restoring the majesty of Jacob,<br>as well as the majesty of Israel,<br>though ravagers have ravaged them<br>and ruined their branches.)  
**2:3** The shields of his warriors are red;<br>his soldiers are clothed in crimson.<br>The metal on the chariots flashes<br>on the day when he musters them;<br>the chargers<sup>①</sup> prance.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn Compare Gk Syr: Heb <i>cypresses</i>  
**2:4** The chariots race madly through the streets,<br>they rush to and fro through the squares;<br>their appearance is like torches,<br>they dart like lightning.  
**2:5** He calls his officers;<br>they stumble as they come forward;<br>they hasten to the wall,<br>and the mantelet<sup>①</sup> is set up.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**2:6** The river gates are opened,<br>the palace trembles.  
**2:7** It is decreed<sup>①</sup> that the city<sup>②</sup> be exiled,<br>its slave women led away,<br>moaning like doves<br>and beating their breasts.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>it</i>  
**2:8** Nineveh is like a pool<br>whose waters<sup>①</sup> run away.<br>“Halt! Halt!”—<br>but no one turns back.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn Compare Gk: Heb <i>a pool, from the days that she has become, and they</i>  
**2:9** “Plunder the silver,<br>plunder the gold!<br>There is no end of treasure!<br>An abundance of every precious thing!”  
**2:10** Devastation, desolation, and destruction!<br>Hearts faint and knees tremble,<br>all loins quake,<br>all faces grow pale!  
**2:11** What became of the lions’ den,<br>the cave<sup>①</sup> of the young lions,<br>where the lion goes,<br>and the lion’s cubs, with no one to disturb them?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>pasture</i>  
**2:12** The lion has torn enough for his whelps<br>and strangled prey for his lionesses;<br>he has filled his caves with prey<br>and his dens with torn flesh.  
**2:13** See, I am against you, says the LORD of hosts, and I will burn your<sup>①</sup> chariots in smoke, and the sword shall devour your young lions; I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers shall be heard no more.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>her</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** Ah! City of bloodshed,<br>utterly deceitful, full of booty—<br>no end to the plunder!  
**3:2** The crack of whip and rumble of wheel,<br>galloping horse and bounding chariot!  
**3:3** Horsemen charging,<br>flashing sword and glittering spear,<br>piles of dead,<br>heaps of corpses,<br>dead bodies without end—<br>they stumble over the bodies!  
**3:4** Because of the countless debaucheries of the prostitute,<br>gracefully alluring, mistress of sorcery,<br>who enslaves<sup>①</sup> nations through her debaucheries,<br>and peoples through her sorcery,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>sells</i>  
**3:5** I am against you,<br>says the LORD of hosts,<br>and will lift up your skirts over your face;<br>and I will let nations look on your nakedness<br>and kingdoms on your shame.  
**3:6** I will throw filth at you<br>and treat you with contempt,<br>and make you a spectacle.  
**3:7** Then all who see you will shrink from you and say,<br>“Nineveh is devastated; who will bemoan her?”<br>Where shall I seek comforters for you?  
**3:8** Are you better than Thebes<sup>①</sup><br>that sat by the Nile,<br>with water around her,<br>her rampart a sea,<br>water her wall?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>No-amon</i>  
**3:9** Ethiopia<sup>①</sup> was her strength,<br>Egypt too, and that without limit;<br>Put and the Libyans were her<sup>②</sup> helpers.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Nubia</i>; Heb <i>Cush</i> <sup>②</sup>Gk: Heb <i>your</i>  
**3:10** Yet she became an exile,<br>she went into captivity;<br>even her infants were dashed in pieces<br>at the head of every street;<br>lots were cast for her nobles,<br>all her dignitaries were bound in fetters.  
**3:11** You also will be drunken,<br>you will go into hiding;<sup>①</sup><br>you will seek<br>a refuge from the enemy.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**3:12** All your fortresses are like fig trees<br>with first-ripe figs—<br>if shaken they fall<br>into the mouth of the eater.  
**3:13** Look at your troops:<br>they are women in your midst.<br>The gates of your land<br>are wide open to your foes;<br>fire has devoured the bars of your gates.  
**3:14** Draw water for the siege,<br>strengthen your forts;<br>trample the clay,<br>tread the mortar,<br>take hold of the brick mold!  
**3:15** There the fire will devour you,<br>the sword will cut you off.<br>It will devour you like the locust.<br>Multiply yourselves like the locust,<br>multiply like the grasshopper!  
**3:16** You increased your merchants<br>more than the stars of the heavens.<br>The locust sheds its skin and flies away.  
**3:17** Your guards are like grasshoppers,<br>your scribes like swarms<sup>①</sup> of locusts<br>settling on the fences<br>on a cold day—<br>when the sun rises, they fly away;<br>no one knows where they have gone.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**3:18** Your shepherds are asleep,<br>O king of Assyria;<br>your nobles slumber.<br>Your people are scattered on the mountains<br>with no one to gather them.  
**3:19** There is no assuaging your hurt,<br>your wound is mortal.<br>All who hear the news about you<br>clap their hands over you.<br>For who has ever escaped<br>your endless cruelty?
