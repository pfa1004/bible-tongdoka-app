# 63.요한2서 (NRSV)



## 1장

**1:1** The elder to the elect lady and her children, whom I love in the truth, and not only I but also all who know the truth,  
**1:2** because of the truth that abides in us and will be with us forever:  
**1:3** Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us from God the Father and from<sup>①</sup> Jesus Christ, the Father’s Son, in truth and love.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other ancient authorities add <i>the Lord</i>  
**1:4** I was overjoyed to find some of your children walking in the truth, just as we have been commanded by the Father.  
**1:5** But now, dear lady, I ask you, not as though I were writing you a new commandment, but one we have had from the beginning, let us love one another.  
**1:6** And this is love, that we walk according to his commandments; this is the commandment just as you have heard it from the beginning—you must walk in it.  
**1:7** Many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who do not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh; any such person is the deceiver and the antichrist!  
**1:8** Be on your guard, so that you do not lose what we<sup>①</sup> have worked for, but may receive a full reward.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other ancient authorities read <i>you</i>  
**1:9** Everyone who does not abide in the teaching of Christ, but goes beyond it, does not have God; whoever abides in the teaching has both the Father and the Son.  
**1:10** Do not receive into the house or welcome anyone who comes to you and does not bring this teaching;  
**1:11** for to welcome is to participate in the evil deeds of such a person.  
**1:12** Although I have much to write to you, I would rather not use paper and ink; instead I hope to come to you and talk with you face to face, so that our joy may be complete.  
**1:13** The children of your elect sister send you their greetings.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other ancient authorities add <i>Amen</i>
