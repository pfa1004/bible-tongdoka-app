## 1장

**1:1** The Song of Songs, which is Solomon’s.  
**1:2** Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth!<br>For your love is better than wine,  
**1:3** your anointing oils are fragrant,<br>your name is perfume poured out;<br>therefore the maidens love you.  
**1:4** Draw me after you, let us make haste.<br>The king has brought me into his chambers.<br>We will exult and rejoice in you;<br>we will extol your love more than wine;<br>rightly do they love you.  
**1:5** I am black and beautiful,<br>O daughters of Jerusalem,<br>like the tents of Kedar,<br>like the curtains of Solomon.  
**1:6** Do not gaze at me because I am dark,<br>because the sun has gazed on me.<br>My mother’s sons were angry with me;<br>they made me keeper of the vineyards,<br>but my own vineyard I have not kept!  
**1:7** Tell me, you whom my soul loves,<br>where you pasture your flock,<br>where you make it lie down at noon;<br>for why should I be like one who is veiled<br>beside the flocks of your companions?  
**1:8** If you do not know,<br>O fairest among women,<br>follow the tracks of the flock,<br>and pasture your kids<br>beside the shepherds’ tents.  
**1:9** I compare you, my love,<br>to a mare among Pharaoh’s chariots.  
**1:10** Your cheeks are comely with ornaments,<br>your neck with strings of jewels.  
**1:11** We will make you ornaments of gold,<br>studded with silver.  
**1:12** While the king was on his couch,<br>my nard gave forth its fragrance.  
**1:13** My beloved is to me a bag of myrrh<br>that lies between my breasts.  
**1:14** My beloved is to me a cluster of henna blossoms<br>in the vineyards of En-gedi.  
**1:15** Ah, you are beautiful, my love;<br>ah, you are beautiful;<br>your eyes are doves.  
**1:16** Ah, you are beautiful, my beloved,<br>truly lovely.<br>Our couch is green;  
**1:17** the beams of our house are cedar,<br>our rafters<sup>①</sup> are pine.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  

## 2장

**2:1** I am a rose<sup>①</sup> of Sharon,<br>a lily of the valleys.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>crocus</i>  
**2:2** As a lily among brambles,<br>so is my love among maidens.  
**2:3** As an apple tree among the trees of the wood,<br>so is my beloved among young men.<br>With great delight I sat in his shadow,<br>and his fruit was sweet to my taste.  
**2:4** He brought me to the banqueting house,<br>and his intention toward me was love.  
**2:5** Sustain me with raisins,<br>refresh me with apples;<br>for I am faint with love.  
**2:6** O that his left hand were under my head,<br>and that his right hand embraced me!  
**2:7** I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem,<br>by the gazelles or the wild does:<br>do not stir up or awaken love<br>until it is ready!  
**2:8** The voice of my beloved!<br>Look, he comes,<br>leaping upon the mountains,<br>bounding over the hills.  
**2:9** My beloved is like a gazelle<br>or a young stag.<br>Look, there he stands<br>behind our wall,<br>gazing in at the windows,<br>looking through the lattice.  
**2:10** My beloved speaks and says to me:<br>“Arise, my love, my fair one,<br>and come away;  
**2:11** for now the winter is past,<br>the rain is over and gone.  
**2:12** The flowers appear on the earth;<br>the time of singing has come,<br>and the voice of the turtledove<br>is heard in our land.  
**2:13** The fig tree puts forth its figs,<br>and the vines are in blossom;<br>they give forth fragrance.<br>Arise, my love, my fair one,<br>and come away.  
**2:14** O my dove, in the clefts of the rock,<br>in the covert of the cliff,<br>let me see your face,<br>let me hear your voice;<br>for your voice is sweet,<br>and your face is lovely.  
**2:15** Catch us the foxes,<br>the little foxes,<br>that ruin the vineyards—<br>for our vineyards are in blossom.”  
**2:16** My beloved is mine and I am his;<br>he pastures his flock among the lilies.  
**2:17** Until the day breathes<br>and the shadows flee,<br>turn, my beloved, be like a gazelle<br>or a young stag on the cleft mountains.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>on the mountains of Bether</i>; meaning of Heb uncertain  

## 3장

**3:1** Upon my bed at night<br>I sought him whom my soul loves;<br>I sought him, but found him not;<br>I called him, but he gave no answer.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk: Heb lacks this line

**3:3** The sentinels found me,<br>as they went about in the city.<br>“Have you seen him whom my soul loves?”  
**3:4** Scarcely had I passed them,<br>when I found him whom my soul loves.<br>I held him, and would not let him go<br>until I brought him into my mother’s house,<br>and into the chamber of her that conceived me.

**3:8** all equipped with swords<br>and expert in war,<br>each with his sword at his thigh<br>because of alarms by night.  
**3:9** King Solomon made himself a palanquin<br>from the wood of Lebanon.  
**3:10** He made its posts of silver,<br>its back of gold, its seat of purple;<br>its interior was inlaid with love.<sup>①</sup><br>Daughters of Jerusalem,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain

## 4장

**4:4** Your neck is like the tower of David,<br>built in courses;<br>on it hang a thousand bucklers,<br>all of them shields of warriors.  
**4:5** Your two breasts are like two fawns,<br>twins of a gazelle,<br>that feed among the lilies.  
**4:6** Until the day breathes<br>and the shadows flee,<br>I will hasten to the mountain of myrrh<br>and the hill of frankincense.  
**4:7** You are altogether beautiful, my love;<br>there is no flaw in you.

**4:16** Awake, O north wind,<br>and come, O south wind!<br>Blow upon my garden<br>that its fragrance may be wafted abroad.<br>Let my beloved come to his garden,<br>and eat its choicest fruits.  

## 5장

**5:1** I come to my garden, my sister, my bride;<br>I gather my myrrh with my spice,<br>I eat my honeycomb with my honey,<br>I drink my wine with my milk.<br>Eat, friends, drink,<br>and be drunk with love.

**5:3** I had put off my garment;<br>how could I put it on again?<br>I had bathed my feet;<br>how could I soil them?  
**5:4** My beloved thrust his hand into the opening,<br>and my inmost being yearned for him.
