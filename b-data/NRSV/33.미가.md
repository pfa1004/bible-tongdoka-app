## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Micah of Moresheth in the days of Kings Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.  
**1:2** Hear, you peoples, all of you;<br>listen, O earth, and all that is in it;<br>and let the Lord GOD be a witness against you,<br>the Lord from his holy temple.  
**1:3** For lo, the LORD is coming out of his place,<br>and will come down and tread upon the high places of the earth.  
**1:4** Then the mountains will melt under him<br>and the valleys will burst open,<br>like wax near the fire,<br>like waters poured down a steep place.  
**1:5** All this is for the transgression of Jacob<br>and for the sins of the house of Israel.<br>What is the transgression of Jacob?<br>Is it not Samaria?<br>And what is the high place<sup>①</sup> of Judah?<br>Is it not Jerusalem?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>what are the high places</i>  
**1:6** Therefore I will make Samaria a heap in the open country,<br>a place for planting vineyards.<br>I will pour down her stones into the valley,<br>and uncover her foundations.  
**1:7** All her images shall be beaten to pieces,<br>all her wages shall be burned with fire,<br>and all her idols I will lay waste;<br>for as the wages of a prostitute she gathered them,<br>and as the wages of a prostitute they shall again be used.  
**1:8** For this I will lament and wail;<br>I will go barefoot and naked;<br>I will make lamentation like the jackals,<br>and mourning like the ostriches.  
**1:9** For her wound<sup>①</sup> is incurable.<br>It has come to Judah;<br>it has reached to the gate of my people,<br>to Jerusalem.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Syr Vg: Heb <i>wounds</i>  
**1:10** Tell it not in Gath,<br>weep not at all;<br>in Beth-leaphrah<br>roll yourselves in the dust.  
**1:11** Pass on your way,<br>inhabitants of Shaphir,<br>in nakedness and shame;<br>the inhabitants of Zaanan<br>do not come forth;<br>Beth-ezel is wailing<br>and shall remove its support from you.  
**1:12** For the inhabitants of Maroth<br>wait anxiously for good,<br>yet disaster has come down from the LORD<br>to the gate of Jerusalem.  
**1:13** Harness the steeds to the chariots,<br>inhabitants of Lachish;<br>it was the beginning of sin<br>to daughter Zion,<br>for in you were found<br>the transgressions of Israel.  
**1:14** Therefore you shall give parting gifts<br>to Moresheth-gath;<br>the houses of Achzib shall be a deception<br>to the kings of Israel.  
**1:15** I will again bring a conqueror upon you,<br>inhabitants of Mareshah;<br>the glory of Israel<br>shall come to Adullam.  
**1:16** Make yourselves bald and cut off your hair<br>for your pampered children;<br>make yourselves as bald as the eagle,<br>for they have gone from you into exile.

## 3장

**3:1** And I said:<br>Listen, you heads of Jacob<br>and rulers of the house of Israel!<br>Should you not know justice?—  
**3:2** you who hate the good and love the evil,<br>who tear the skin off my people,<sup>①</sup><br>and the flesh off their bones;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>from them</i>

**3:4** Then they will cry to the LORD,<br>but he will not answer them;<br>he will hide his face from them at that time,<br>because they have acted wickedly.  
**3:5** Thus says the LORD concerning the prophets<br>who lead my people astray,<br>who cry “Peace”<br>when they have something to eat,<br>but declare war against those<br>who put nothing into their mouths.

**3:7** the seers shall be disgraced,<br>and the diviners put to shame;<br>they shall all cover their lips,<br>for there is no answer from God.  
**3:8** But as for me, I am filled with power,<br>with the spirit of the LORD,<br>and with justice and might,<br>to declare to Jacob his transgression<br>and to Israel his sin.  
**3:9** Hear this, you rulers of the house of Jacob<br>and chiefs of the house of Israel,<br>who abhor justice<br>and pervert all equity,  
**3:10** who build Zion with blood<br>and Jerusalem with wrong!  
**3:11** Its rulers give judgment for a bribe,<br>its priests teach for a price,<br>its prophets give oracles for money;<br>yet they lean upon the LORD and say,<br>“Surely the LORD is with us!<br>No harm shall come upon us.”  
**3:12** Therefore because of you<br>Zion shall be plowed as a field;<br>Jerusalem shall become a heap of ruins,<br>and the mountain of the house a wooded height.
