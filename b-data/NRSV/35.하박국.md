# 35.하박국 (NRSV)



## 1장

**1:1** The oracle that the prophet Habakkuk saw.  
**1:2** O LORD, how long shall I cry for help,<br>and you will not listen?<br>Or cry to you “Violence!”<br>and you will not save?  
**1:3** Why do you make me see wrongdoing<br>and look at trouble?<br>Destruction and violence are before me;<br>strife and contention arise.  
**1:4** So the law becomes slack<br>and justice never prevails.<br>The wicked surround the righteous—<br>therefore judgment comes forth perverted.  
**1:5** Look at the nations, and see!<br>Be astonished! Be astounded!<br>For a work is being done in your days<br>that you would not believe if you were told.  
**1:6** For I am rousing the Chaldeans,<br>that fierce and impetuous nation,<br>who march through the breadth of the earth<br>to seize dwellings not their own.  
**1:7** Dread and fearsome are they;<br>their justice and dignity proceed from themselves.  
**1:8** Their horses are swifter than leopards,<br>more menacing than wolves at dusk;<br>their horses charge.<br>Their horsemen come from far away;<br>they fly like an eagle swift to devour.  
**1:9** They all come for violence,<br>with faces pressing<sup>①</sup> forward;<br>they gather captives like sand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**1:10** At kings they scoff,<br>and of rulers they make sport.<br>They laugh at every fortress,<br>and heap up earth to take it.  
**1:11** Then they sweep by like the wind;<br>they transgress and become guilty;<br>their own might is their god!  
**1:12** Are you not from of old,<br>O LORD my God, my Holy One?<br>You<sup>①</sup> shall not die.<br>O LORD, you have marked them for judgment;<br>and you, O Rock, have established them for punishment.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ancient Heb tradition: MT <i>We</i>  
**1:13** Your eyes are too pure to behold evil,<br>and you cannot look on wrongdoing;<br>why do you look on the treacherous,<br>and are silent when the wicked swallow<br>those more righteous than they?  
**1:14** You have made people like the fish of the sea,<br>like crawling things that have no ruler.  
**1:15** The enemy<sup>①</sup> brings all of them up with a hook;<br>he drags them out with his net,<br>he gathers them in his seine;<br>so he rejoices and exults.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>He</i>  
**1:16** Therefore he sacrifices to his net<br>and makes offerings to his seine;<br>for by them his portion is lavish,<br>and his food is rich.  
**1:17** Is he then to keep on emptying his net,<br>and destroying nations without mercy?  

## 2장

**2:1** I will stand at my watchpost,<br>and station myself on the rampart;<br>I will keep watch to see what he will say to me,<br>and what he<sup>①</sup> will answer concerning my complaint.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Syr: Heb <i>I</i>  
**2:2** Then the LORD answered me and said:<br>Write the vision;<br>make it plain on tablets,<br>so that a runner may read it.  
**2:3** For there is still a vision for the appointed time;<br>it speaks of the end, and does not lie.<br>If it seems to tarry, wait for it;<br>it will surely come, it will not delay.  
**2:4** Look at the proud!<br>Their spirit is not right in them,<br>but the righteous live by their faith.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>faithfulness</i>  
**2:5** Moreover, wealth<sup>①</sup> is treacherous;<br>the arrogant do not endure.<br>They open their throats wide as Sheol;<br>like Death they never have enough.<br>They gather all nations for themselves,<br>and collect all peoples as their own.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Other Heb Mss read <i>wine</i>  
**2:6** Shall not everyone taunt such people and, with mocking riddles, say about them,<br>“Alas for you who heap up what is not your own!”<br>How long will you load yourselves with goods taken in pledge?  
**2:7** Will not your own creditors suddenly rise,<br>and those who make you tremble wake up?<br>Then you will be booty for them.  
**2:8** Because you have plundered many nations,<br>all that survive of the peoples shall plunder you—<br>because of human bloodshed, and violence to the earth,<br>to cities and all who live in them.  
**2:9** “Alas for you who get evil gain for your house,<br>setting your nest on high<br>to be safe from the reach of harm!”  
**2:10** You have devised shame for your house<br>by cutting off many peoples;<br>you have forfeited your life.  
**2:11** The very stones will cry out from the wall,<br>and the plaster<sup>①</sup> will respond from the woodwork.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>beam</i>  
**2:12** “Alas for you who build a town by bloodshed,<br>and found a city on iniquity!”  
**2:13** Is it not from the LORD of hosts<br>that peoples labor only to feed the flames,<br>and nations weary themselves for nothing?  
**2:14** But the earth will be filled<br>with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD,<br>as the waters cover the sea.  
**2:15** “Alas for you who make your neighbors drink,<br>pouring out your wrath<sup>①</sup> until they are drunk,<br>in order to gaze on their nakedness!”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>poison</i>  
**2:16** You will be sated with contempt instead of glory.<br>Drink, you yourself, and stagger!<sup>①</sup><br>The cup in the LORD’s right hand<br>will come around to you,<br>and shame will come upon your glory!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Q Ms Gk: MT <i>be uncircumcised</i>  
**2:17** For the violence done to Lebanon will overwhelm you;<br>the destruction of the animals will terrify you—<sup>①</sup><br>because of human bloodshed and violence to the earth,<br>to cities and all who live in them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Syr: Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**2:18** What use is an idol<br>once its maker has shaped it—<br>a cast image, a teacher of lies?<br>For its maker trusts in what has been made,<br>though the product is only an idol that cannot speak!  
**2:19** Alas for you who say to the wood, “Wake up!”<br>to silent stone, “Rouse yourself!”<br>Can it teach?<br>See, it is gold and silver plated,<br>and there is no breath in it at all.  
**2:20** But the LORD is in his holy temple;<br>let all the earth keep silence before him!  

## 3장

**3:1** A prayer of the prophet Habakkuk according to Shigionoth.  
**3:2** O LORD, I have heard of your renown,<br>and I stand in awe, O LORD, of your work.<br>In our own time revive it;<br>in our own time make it known;<br>in wrath may you remember mercy.  
**3:3** God came from Teman,<br>the Holy One from Mount Paran.<e> <i>Selah</i> </e><br>His glory covered the heavens,<br>and the earth was full of his praise.  
**3:4** The brightness was like the sun;<br>rays came forth from his hand,<br>where his power lay hidden.  
**3:5** Before him went pestilence,<br>and plague followed close behind.  
**3:6** He stopped and shook the earth;<br>he looked and made the nations tremble.<br>The eternal mountains were shattered;<br>along his ancient pathways<br>the everlasting hills sank low.  
**3:7** I saw the tents of Cushan under affliction;<br>the tent-curtains of the land of Midian trembled.  
**3:8** Was your wrath against the rivers,<sup>①</sup> O LORD?<br>Or your anger against the rivers,<sup>②</sup><br>or your rage against the sea,<sup>③</sup><br>when you drove your horses,<br>your chariots to victory?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>against River</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>against River</i> <sup>③</sup>Or <i>against Sea</i>  
**3:9** You brandished your naked bow,<br>sated<sup>①</sup> were the arrows at your command.<sup>②</sup><e> <i>Selah</i> </e><br>You split the earth with rivers.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>oaths</i> <sup>②</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**3:10** The mountains saw you, and writhed;<br>a torrent of water swept by;<br>the deep gave forth its voice.<br>The sun<sup>①</sup> raised high its hands;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>It</i>  
**3:11** the moon<sup>①</sup> stood still in its exalted place,<br>at the light of your arrows speeding by,<br>at the gleam of your flashing spear.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>sun, moon</i>  
**3:12** In fury you trod the earth,<br>in anger you trampled nations.  
**3:13** You came forth to save your people,<br>to save your anointed.<br>You crushed the head of the wicked house,<br>laying it bare from foundation to roof.<sup>①</sup><e> <i>Selah</i> </e><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**3:14** You pierced with their<sup>①</sup> own arrows the head<sup>②</sup> of his warriors,<sup>③</sup><br>who came like a whirlwind to scatter us,<sup>④</sup><br>gloating as if ready to devour the poor who were in hiding.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>leader</i> <sup>②</sup>Vg Compare Gk Syr: Meaning of Heb uncertain <sup>③</sup>Heb <i>me</i> <sup>④</sup>Heb <i>his</i>  
**3:15** You trampled the sea with your horses,<br>churning the mighty waters.  
**3:16** I hear, and I tremble within;<br>my lips quiver at the sound.<br>Rottenness enters into my bones,<br>and my steps tremble<sup>①</sup> beneath me.<br>I wait quietly for the day of calamity<br>to come upon the people who attack us.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn Compare Gk: Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**3:17** Though the fig tree does not blossom,<br>and no fruit is on the vines;<br>though the produce of the olive fails,<br>and the fields yield no food;<br>though the flock is cut off from the fold,<br>and there is no herd in the stalls,  
**3:18** yet I will rejoice in the LORD;<br>I will exult in the God of my salvation.  
**3:19** GOD, the Lord, is my strength;<br>he makes my feet like the feet of a deer,<br>and makes me tread upon the heights.<sup>①</sup><br>To the leader: with stringed<sup>②</sup> instruments.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>my heights</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>my stringed</i>
