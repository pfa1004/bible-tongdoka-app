# 25.예레미야애가 (NRSV)



## 1장

**1:1** How lonely sits the city<br>that once was full of people!<br>How like a widow she has become,<br>she that was great among the nations!<br>She that was a princess among the provinces<br>has become a vassal.  
**1:2** She weeps bitterly in the night,<br>with tears on her cheeks;<br>among all her lovers<br>she has no one to comfort her;<br>all her friends have dealt treacherously with her,<br>they have become her enemies.  
**1:3** Judah has gone into exile with suffering<br>and hard servitude;<br>she lives now among the nations,<br>and finds no resting place;<br>her pursuers have all overtaken her<br>in the midst of her distress.  
**1:4** The roads to Zion mourn,<br>for no one comes to the festivals;<br>all her gates are desolate,<br>her priests groan;<br>her young girls grieve,<sup>①</sup><br>and her lot is bitter.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**1:5** Her foes have become the masters,<br>her enemies prosper,<br>because the LORD has made her suffer<br>for the multitude of her transgressions;<br>her children have gone away,<br>captives before the foe.  
**1:6** From daughter Zion has departed<br>all her majesty.<br>Her princes have become like stags<br>that find no pasture;<br>they fled without strength<br>before the pursuer.  
**1:7** Jerusalem remembers,<br>in the days of her affliction and wandering,<br>all the precious things<br>that were hers in days of old.<br>When her people fell into the hand of the foe,<br>and there was no one to help her,<br>the foe looked on mocking<br>over her downfall.  
**1:8** Jerusalem sinned grievously,<br>so she has become a mockery;<br>all who honored her despise her,<br>for they have seen her nakedness;<br>she herself groans,<br>and turns her face away.  
**1:9** Her uncleanness was in her skirts;<br>she took no thought of her future;<br>her downfall was appalling,<br>with none to comfort her.<br>“O LORD, look at my affliction,<br>for the enemy has triumphed!”  
**1:10** Enemies have stretched out their hands<br>over all her precious things;<br>she has even seen the nations<br>invade her sanctuary,<br>those whom you forbade<br>to enter your congregation.  
**1:11** All her people groan<br>as they search for bread;<br>they trade their treasures for food<br>to revive their strength.<br>Look, O LORD, and see<br>how worthless I have become.  
**1:12** Is it nothing to you,<sup>①</sup> all you who pass by?<br>Look and see<br>if there is any sorrow like my sorrow,<br>which was brought upon me,<br>which the LORD inflicted<br>on the day of his fierce anger.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**1:13** From on high he sent fire;<br>it went deep into my bones;<br>he spread a net for my feet;<br>he turned me back;<br>he has left me stunned,<br>faint all day long.  
**1:14** My transgressions were bound<sup>①</sup> into a yoke;<br>by his hand they were fastened together;<br>they weigh on my neck,<br>sapping my strength;<br>the Lord handed me over<br>to those whom I cannot withstand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**1:15** The LORD has rejected<br>all my warriors in the midst of me;<br>he proclaimed a time against me<br>to crush my young men;<br>the Lord has trodden as in a wine press<br>the virgin daughter Judah.  
**1:16** For these things I weep;<br>my eyes flow with tears;<br>for a comforter is far from me,<br>one to revive my courage;<br>my children are desolate,<br>for the enemy has prevailed.  
**1:17** Zion stretches out her hands,<br>but there is no one to comfort her;<br>the LORD has commanded against Jacob<br>that his neighbors should become his foes;<br>Jerusalem has become<br>a filthy thing among them.  
**1:18** The LORD is in the right,<br>for I have rebelled against his word;<br>but hear, all you peoples,<br>and behold my suffering;<br>my young women and young men<br>have gone into captivity.  
**1:19** I called to my lovers<br>but they deceived me;<br>my priests and elders<br>perished in the city<br>while seeking food<br>to revive their strength.  
**1:20** See, O LORD, how distressed I am;<br>my stomach churns,<br>my heart is wrung within me,<br>because I have been very rebellious.<br>In the street the sword bereaves;<br>in the house it is like death.  
**1:21** They heard how I was groaning,<br>with no one to comfort me.<br>All my enemies heard of my trouble;<br>they are glad that you have done it.<br>Bring on the day you have announced,<br>and let them be as I am.  
**1:22** Let all their evil doing come before you;<br>and deal with them<br>as you have dealt with me<br>because of all my transgressions;<br>for my groans are many<br>and my heart is faint.  

## 2장

**2:1** How the Lord in his anger<br>has humiliated<sup>①</sup> daughter Zion!<br>He has thrown down from heaven to earth<br>the splendor of Israel;<br>he has not remembered his footstool<br>in the day of his anger.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**2:2** The Lord has destroyed without mercy<br>all the dwellings of Jacob;<br>in his wrath he has broken down<br>the strongholds of daughter Judah;<br>he has brought down to the ground in dishonor<br>the kingdom and its rulers.  
**2:3** He has cut down in fierce anger<br>all the might of Israel;<br>he has withdrawn his right hand from them<br>in the face of the enemy;<br>he has burned like a flaming fire in Jacob,<br>consuming all around.  
**2:4** He has bent his bow like an enemy,<br>with his right hand set like a foe;<br>he has killed all in whom we took pride<br>in the tent of daughter Zion;<br>he has poured out his fury like fire.  
**2:5** The Lord has become like an enemy;<br>he has destroyed Israel.<br>He has destroyed all its palaces,<br>laid in ruins its strongholds,<br>and multiplied in daughter Judah<br>mourning and lamentation.  
**2:6** He has broken down his booth like a garden,<br>he has destroyed his tabernacle;<br>the LORD has abolished in Zion<br>festival and sabbath,<br>and in his fierce indignation has spurned<br>king and priest.  
**2:7** The Lord has scorned his altar,<br>disowned his sanctuary;<br>he has delivered into the hand of the enemy<br>the walls of her palaces;<br>a clamor was raised in the house of the LORD<br>as on a day of festival.  
**2:8** The LORD determined to lay in ruins<br>the wall of daughter Zion;<br>he stretched the line;<br>he did not withhold his hand from destroying;<br>he caused rampart and wall to lament;<br>they languish together.  
**2:9** Her gates have sunk into the ground;<br>he has ruined and broken her bars;<br>her king and princes are among the nations;<br>guidance is no more,<br>and her prophets obtain<br>no vision from the LORD.  
**2:10** The elders of daughter Zion<br>sit on the ground in silence;<br>they have thrown dust on their heads<br>and put on sackcloth;<br>the young girls of Jerusalem<br>have bowed their heads to the ground.  
**2:11** My eyes are spent with weeping;<br>my stomach churns;<br>my bile is poured out on the ground<br>because of the destruction of my people,<br>because infants and babes faint<br>in the streets of the city.  
**2:12** They cry to their mothers,<br>“Where is bread and wine?”<br>as they faint like the wounded<br>in the streets of the city,<br>as their life is poured out<br>on their mothers’ bosom.  
**2:13** What can I say for you, to what compare you,<br>O daughter Jerusalem?<br>To what can I liken you, that I may comfort you,<br>O virgin daughter Zion?<br>For vast as the sea is your ruin;<br>who can heal you?  
**2:14** Your prophets have seen for you<br>false and deceptive visions;<br>they have not exposed your iniquity<br>to restore your fortunes,<br>but have seen oracles for you<br>that are false and misleading.  
**2:15** All who pass along the way<br>clap their hands at you;<br>they hiss and wag their heads<br>at daughter Jerusalem;<br>“Is this the city that was called<br>the perfection of beauty,<br>the joy of all the earth?”  
**2:16** All your enemies<br>open their mouths against you;<br>they hiss, they gnash their teeth,<br>they cry: “We have devoured her!<br>Ah, this is the day we longed for;<br>at last we have seen it!”  
**2:17** The LORD has done what he purposed,<br>he has carried out his threat;<br>as he ordained long ago,<br>he has demolished without pity;<br>he has made the enemy rejoice over you,<br>and exalted the might of your foes.  
**2:18** Cry aloud<sup>①</sup> to the Lord!<br>O wall of daughter Zion!<br>Let tears stream down like a torrent<br>day and night!<br>Give yourself no rest,<br>your eyes no respite!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>Their heart cried</i>  
**2:19** Arise, cry out in the night,<br>at the beginning of the watches!<br>Pour out your heart like water<br>before the presence of the Lord!<br>Lift your hands to him<br>for the lives of your children,<br>who faint for hunger<br>at the head of every street.  
**2:20** Look, O LORD, and consider!<br>To whom have you done this?<br>Should women eat their offspring,<br>the children they have borne?<br>Should priest and prophet be killed<br>in the sanctuary of the Lord?  
**2:21** The young and the old are lying<br>on the ground in the streets;<br>my young women and my young men<br>have fallen by the sword;<br>in the day of your anger you have killed them,<br>slaughtering without mercy.  
**2:22** You invited my enemies from all around<br>as if for a day of festival;<br>and on the day of the anger of the LORD<br>no one escaped or survived;<br>those whom I bore and reared<br>my enemy has destroyed.  

## 3장

**3:1** I am one who has seen affliction<br>under the rod of God’s<sup>①</sup> wrath;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>his</i>  
**3:2** he has driven and brought me<br>into darkness without any light;  
**3:3** against me alone he turns his hand,<br>again and again, all day long.  
**3:4** He has made my flesh and my skin waste away,<br>and broken my bones;  
**3:5** he has besieged and enveloped me<br>with bitterness and tribulation;  
**3:6** he has made me sit in darkness<br>like the dead of long ago.  
**3:7** He has walled me about so that I cannot escape;<br>he has put heavy chains on me;  
**3:8** though I call and cry for help,<br>he shuts out my prayer;  
**3:9** he has blocked my ways with hewn stones,<br>he has made my paths crooked.  
**3:10** He is a bear lying in wait for me,<br>a lion in hiding;  
**3:11** he led me off my way and tore me to pieces;<br>he has made me desolate;  
**3:12** he bent his bow and set me<br>as a mark for his arrow.  
**3:13** He shot into my vitals<br>the arrows of his quiver;  
**3:14** I have become the laughingstock of all my people,<br>the object of their taunt-songs all day long.  
**3:15** He has filled me with bitterness,<br>he has sated me with wormwood.  
**3:16** He has made my teeth grind on gravel,<br>and made me cower in ashes;  
**3:17** my soul is bereft of peace;<br>I have forgotten what happiness is;  
**3:18** so I say, “Gone is my glory,<br>and all that I had hoped for from the LORD.”  
**3:19** The thought of my affliction and my homelessness<br>is wormwood and gall!  
**3:20** My soul continually thinks of it<br>and is bowed down within me.  
**3:21** But this I call to mind,<br>and therefore I have hope:  
**3:22** The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases,<sup>①</sup><br>his mercies never come to an end;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Syr Tg: Heb <i>LORD, we are not cut off</i>  
**3:23** they are new every morning;<br>great is your faithfulness.  
**3:24** “The LORD is my portion,” says my soul,<br>“therefore I will hope in him.”  
**3:25** The LORD is good to those who wait for him,<br>to the soul that seeks him.  
**3:26** It is good that one should wait quietly<br>for the salvation of the LORD.  
**3:27** It is good for one to bear<br>the yoke in youth,  
**3:28** to sit alone in silence<br>when the Lord has imposed it,  
**3:29** to put one’s mouth to the dust<br>(there may yet be hope),  
**3:30** to give one’s cheek to the smiter,<br>and be filled with insults.  
**3:31** For the Lord will not<br>reject forever.  
**3:32** Although he causes grief, he will have compassion<br>according to the abundance of his steadfast love;  
**3:33** for he does not willingly afflict<br>or grieve anyone.  
**3:34** When all the prisoners of the land<br>are crushed under foot,  
**3:35** when human rights are perverted<br>in the presence of the Most High,  
**3:36** when one’s case is subverted<br>—does the Lord not see it?  
**3:37** Who can command and have it done,<br>if the Lord has not ordained it?  
**3:38** Is it not from the mouth of the Most High<br>that good and bad come?  
**3:39** Why should any who draw breath complain<br>about the punishment of their sins?  
**3:40** Let us test and examine our ways,<br>and return to the LORD.  
**3:41** Let us lift up our hearts as well as our hands<br>to God in heaven.  
**3:42** We have transgressed and rebelled,<br>and you have not forgiven.  
**3:43** You have wrapped yourself with anger and pursued us,<br>killing without pity;  
**3:44** you have wrapped yourself with a cloud<br>so that no prayer can pass through.  
**3:45** You have made us filth and rubbish<br>among the peoples.  
**3:46** All our enemies<br>have opened their mouths against us;  
**3:47** panic and pitfall have come upon us,<br>devastation and destruction.  
**3:48** My eyes flow with rivers of tears<br>because of the destruction of my people.  
**3:49** My eyes will flow without ceasing,<br>without respite,  
**3:50** until the LORD from heaven<br>looks down and sees.  
**3:51** My eyes cause me grief<br>at the fate of all the young women in my city.  
**3:52** Those who were my enemies without cause<br>have hunted me like a bird;  
**3:53** they flung me alive into a pit<br>and hurled stones on me;  
**3:54** water closed over my head;<br>I said, “I am lost.”  
**3:55** I called on your name, O LORD,<br>from the depths of the pit;  
**3:56** you heard my plea, “Do not close your ear<br>to my cry for help, but give me relief!”  
**3:57** You came near when I called on you;<br>you said, “Do not fear!”  
**3:58** You have taken up my cause, O Lord,<br>you have redeemed my life.  
**3:59** You have seen the wrong done to me, O LORD;<br>judge my cause.  
**3:60** You have seen all their malice,<br>all their plots against me.  
**3:61** You have heard their taunts, O LORD,<br>all their plots against me.  
**3:62** The whispers and murmurs of my assailants<br>are against me all day long.  
**3:63** Whether they sit or rise—see,<br>I am the object of their taunt-songs.  
**3:64** Pay them back for their deeds, O LORD,<br>according to the work of their hands!  
**3:65** Give them anguish of heart;<br>your curse be on them!  
**3:66** Pursue them in anger and destroy them<br>from under the LORD’s heavens.  

## 4장

**4:1** How the gold has grown dim,<br>how the pure gold is changed!<br>The sacred stones lie scattered<br>at the head of every street.  
**4:2** The precious children of Zion,<br>worth their weight in fine gold—<br>how they are reckoned as earthen pots,<br>the work of a potter’s hands!  
**4:3** Even the jackals offer the breast<br>and nurse their young,<br>but my people has become cruel,<br>like the ostriches in the wilderness.  
**4:4** The tongue of the infant sticks<br>to the roof of its mouth for thirst;<br>the children beg for food,<br>but no one gives them anything.  
**4:5** Those who feasted on delicacies<br>perish in the streets;<br>those who were brought up in purple<br>cling to ash heaps.  
**4:6** For the chastisement<sup>①</sup> of my people has been greater<br>than the punishment<sup>②</sup> of Sodom,<br>which was overthrown in a moment,<br>though no hand was laid on it.<sup>③</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>iniquity</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>sin</i> <sup>③</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**4:7** Her princes were purer than snow,<br>whiter than milk;<br>their bodies were more ruddy than coral,<br>their hair<sup>①</sup> like sapphire.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain <sup>②</sup>Or <i>lapis lazuli</i>  
**4:8** Now their visage is blacker than soot;<br>they are not recognized in the streets.<br>Their skin has shriveled on their bones;<br>it has become as dry as wood.  
**4:9** Happier were those pierced by the sword<br>than those pierced by hunger,<br>whose life drains away, deprived<br>of the produce of the field.  
**4:10** The hands of compassionate women<br>have boiled their own children;<br>they became their food<br>in the destruction of my people.  
**4:11** The LORD gave full vent to his wrath;<br>he poured out his hot anger,<br>and kindled a fire in Zion<br>that consumed its foundations.  
**4:12** The kings of the earth did not believe,<br>nor did any of the inhabitants of the world,<br>that foe or enemy could enter<br>the gates of Jerusalem.  
**4:13** It was for the sins of her prophets<br>and the iniquities of her priests,<br>who shed the blood of the righteous<br>in the midst of her.  
**4:14** Blindly they wandered through the streets,<br>so defiled with blood<br>that no one was able<br>to touch their garments.  
**4:15** “Away! Unclean!” people shouted at them;<br>“Away! Away! Do not touch!”<br>So they became fugitives and wanderers;<br>it was said among the nations,<br>“They shall stay here no longer.”  
**4:16** The LORD himself has scattered them,<br>he will regard them no more;<br>no honor was shown to the priests,<br>no favor to the elders.  
**4:17** Our eyes failed, ever watching<br>vainly for help;<br>we were watching eagerly<br>for a nation that could not save.  
**4:18** They dogged our steps<br>so that we could not walk in our streets;<br>our end drew near; our days were numbered;<br>for our end had come.  
**4:19** Our pursuers were swifter<br>than the eagles in the heavens;<br>they chased us on the mountains,<br>they lay in wait for us in the wilderness.  
**4:20** The LORD’s anointed, the breath of our life,<br>was taken in their pits—<br>the one of whom we said, “Under his shadow<br>we shall live among the nations.”  
**4:21** Rejoice and be glad, O daughter Edom,<br>you that live in the land of Uz;<br>but to you also the cup shall pass;<br>you shall become drunk and strip yourself bare.  
**4:22** The punishment of your iniquity, O daughter Zion, is accomplished,<br>he will keep you in exile no longer;<br>but your iniquity, O daughter Edom, he will punish,<br>he will uncover your sins.  

## 5장

**5:1** Remember, O LORD, what has befallen us;<br>look, and see our disgrace!  
**5:2** Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers,<br>our homes to aliens.  
**5:3** We have become orphans, fatherless;<br>our mothers are like widows.  
**5:4** We must pay for the water we drink;<br>the wood we get must be bought.  
**5:5** With a yoke<sup>①</sup> on our necks we are hard driven;<br>we are weary, we are given no rest.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Symmachus: Heb lacks <i>With a yoke</i>  
**5:6** We have made a pact with<sup>①</sup> Egypt and Assyria,<br>to get enough bread.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>have given the hand to</i>  
**5:7** Our ancestors sinned; they are no more,<br>and we bear their iniquities.  
**5:8** Slaves rule over us;<br>there is no one to deliver us from their hand.  
**5:9** We get our bread at the peril of our lives,<br>because of the sword in the wilderness.  
**5:10** Our skin is black as an oven<br>from the scorching heat of famine.  
**5:11** Women are raped in Zion,<br>virgins in the towns of Judah.  
**5:12** Princes are hung up by their hands;<br>no respect is shown to the elders.  
**5:13** Young men are compelled to grind,<br>and boys stagger under loads of wood.  
**5:14** The old men have left the city gate,<br>the young men their music.  
**5:15** The joy of our hearts has ceased;<br>our dancing has been turned to mourning.  
**5:16** The crown has fallen from our head;<br>woe to us, for we have sinned!  
**5:17** Because of this our hearts are sick,<br>because of these things our eyes have grown dim:  
**5:18** because of Mount Zion, which lies desolate;<br>jackals prowl over it.  
**5:19** But you, O LORD, reign forever;<br>your throne endures to all generations.  
**5:20** Why have you forgotten us completely?<br>Why have you forsaken us these many days?  
**5:21** Restore us to yourself, O LORD, that we may be restored;<br>renew our days as of old—  
**5:22** unless you have utterly rejected us,<br>and are angry with us beyond measure.
