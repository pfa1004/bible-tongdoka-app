# 31.오바디야 (NRSV)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision of Obadiah.<br>Thus says the Lord GOD concerning Edom:<br>We have heard a report from the LORD,<br>and a messenger has been sent among the nations:<br>“Rise up! Let us rise against it for battle!”  
**1:2** I will surely make you least among the nations;<br>you shall be utterly despised.  
**1:3** Your proud heart has deceived you,<br>you that live in the clefts of the rock,<sup>①</sup><br>whose dwelling is in the heights.<br>You say in your heart,<br>“Who will bring me down to the ground?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>clefts of Sela</i>  
**1:4** Though you soar aloft like the eagle,<br>though your nest is set among the stars,<br>from there I will bring you down,<br>—says the LORD.  
**1:5** If thieves came to you,<br>if plunderers by night<br>—how you have been destroyed!—<br>would they not steal only what they wanted?<br>If grape-gatherers came to you,<br>would they not leave gleanings?  
**1:6** How Esau has been pillaged,<br>his treasures searched out!  
**1:7** All your allies have deceived you,<br>they have driven you to the border;<br>your confederates have prevailed against you;<br>those who ate<sup>①</sup> your bread have set a trap for you—<br>there is no understanding of it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb lacks <i>those who ate</i>  
**1:8** On that day, says the LORD,<br>I will destroy the wise out of Edom,<br>and understanding out of Mount Esau.  
**1:9** Your warriors shall be shattered, O Teman,<br>so that everyone from Mount Esau will be cut off.  
**1:10** For the slaughter and violence done to your brother Jacob,<br>shame shall cover you,<br>and you shall be cut off forever.  
**1:11** On the day that you stood aside,<br>on the day that strangers carried off his wealth,<br>and foreigners entered his gates<br>and cast lots for Jerusalem,<br>you too were like one of them.  
**1:12** But you should not have gloated<sup>①</sup> over<sup>②</sup> your brother<br>on the day of his misfortune;<br>you should not have rejoiced over the people of Judah<br>on the day of their ruin;<br>you should not have boasted<br>on the day of distress.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>But do not gloat</i> (and similarly through verse <a name='B:380 1:14'>14</a>) <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>on the day of</i>  
**1:13** You should not have entered the gate of my people<br>on the day of their calamity;<br>you should not have joined in the gloating over Judah’s<sup>①</sup> disaster<br>on the day of his calamity;<br>you should not have looted his goods<br>on the day of his calamity.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>his</i>  
**1:14** You should not have stood at the crossings<br>to cut off his fugitives;<br>you should not have handed over his survivors<br>on the day of distress.  
**1:15** For the day of the LORD is near against all the nations.<br>As you have done, it shall be done to you;<br>your deeds shall return on your own head.  
**1:16** For as you have drunk on my holy mountain,<br>all the nations around you shall drink;<br>they shall drink and gulp down,<sup>①</sup><br>and shall be as though they had never been.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**1:17** But on Mount Zion there shall be those that escape,<br>and it shall be holy;<br>and the house of Jacob shall take possession of those who dispossessed them.  
**1:18** The house of Jacob shall be a fire,<br>the house of Joseph a flame,<br>and the house of Esau stubble;<br>they shall burn them and consume them,<br>and there shall be no survivor of the house of Esau;<br>—for the LORD has spoken.  
**1:19** Those of the Negeb shall possess Mount Esau,<br>and those of the Shephelah the land of the Philistines;<br>they shall possess the land of Ephraim and the land of Samaria,<br>and Benjamin shall possess Gilead.  
**1:20** The exiles of the Israelites who are in Halah<sup>①</sup><br>shall possess<sup>②</sup> Phoenicia as far as Zarephath;<br>and the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad<br>shall possess the towns of the Negeb.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>in this army</i> <sup>②</sup>Cn: Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**1:21** Those who have been saved<sup>①</sup> shall go up to Mount Zion<br>to rule Mount Esau;<br>and the kingdom shall be the LORD’s.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Saviors</i>
