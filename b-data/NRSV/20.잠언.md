# 20.잠언 (NRSV)



## 1장

**1:1** The proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel:  
**1:2** For learning about wisdom and instruction,<br>for understanding words of insight,  
**1:3** for gaining instruction in wise dealing,<br>righteousness, justice, and equity;  
**1:4** to teach shrewdness to the simple,<br>knowledge and prudence to the young—  
**1:5** let the wise also hear and gain in learning,<br>and the discerning acquire skill,  
**1:6** to understand a proverb and a figure,<br>the words of the wise and their riddles.  
**1:7** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge;<br>fools despise wisdom and instruction.  
**1:8** Hear, my child, your father’s instruction,<br>and do not reject your mother’s teaching;  
**1:9** for they are a fair garland for your head,<br>and pendants for your neck.  
**1:10** My child, if sinners entice you,<br>do not consent.  
**1:11** If they say, “Come with us, let us lie in wait for blood;<br>let us wantonly ambush the innocent;  
**1:12** like Sheol let us swallow them alive<br>and whole, like those who go down to the Pit.  
**1:13** We shall find all kinds of costly things;<br>we shall fill our houses with booty.  
**1:14** Throw in your lot among us;<br>we will all have one purse”—  
**1:15** my child, do not walk in their way,<br>keep your foot from their paths;  
**1:16** for their feet run to evil,<br>and they hurry to shed blood.  
**1:17** For in vain is the net baited<br>while the bird is looking on;  
**1:18** yet they lie in wait—to kill themselves!<br>and set an ambush—for their own lives!  
**1:19** Such is the end<sup>①</sup> of all who are greedy for gain;<br>it takes away the life of its possessors.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk: Heb <i>are the ways</i>  
**1:20** Wisdom cries out in the street;<br>in the squares she raises her voice.  
**1:21** At the busiest corner she cries out;<br>at the entrance of the city gates she speaks:  
**1:22** “How long, O simple ones, will you love being simple?<br>How long will scoffers delight in their scoffing<br>and fools hate knowledge?  
**1:23** Give heed to my reproof;<br>I will pour out my thoughts to you;<br>I will make my words known to you.  
**1:24** Because I have called and you refused,<br>have stretched out my hand and no one heeded,  
**1:25** and because you have ignored all my counsel<br>and would have none of my reproof,  
**1:26** I also will laugh at your calamity;<br>I will mock when panic strikes you,  
**1:27** when panic strikes you like a storm,<br>and your calamity comes like a whirlwind,<br>when distress and anguish come upon you.  
**1:28** Then they will call upon me, but I will not answer;<br>they will seek me diligently, but will not find me.  
**1:29** Because they hated knowledge<br>and did not choose the fear of the LORD,  
**1:30** would have none of my counsel,<br>and despised all my reproof,  
**1:31** therefore they shall eat the fruit of their way<br>and be sated with their own devices.  
**1:32** For waywardness kills the simple,<br>and the complacency of fools destroys them;  
**1:33** but those who listen to me will be secure<br>and will live at ease, without dread of disaster.”  

## 2장

**2:1** My child, if you accept my words<br>and treasure up my commandments within you,  
**2:2** making your ear attentive to wisdom<br>and inclining your heart to understanding;  
**2:3** if you indeed cry out for insight,<br>and raise your voice for understanding;  
**2:4** if you seek it like silver,<br>and search for it as for hidden treasures—  
**2:5** then you will understand the fear of the LORD<br>and find the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For the LORD gives wisdom;<br>from his mouth come knowledge and understanding;  
**2:7** he stores up sound wisdom for the upright;<br>he is a shield to those who walk blamelessly,  
**2:8** guarding the paths of justice<br>and preserving the way of his faithful ones.  
**2:9** Then you will understand righteousness and justice<br>and equity, every good path;  
**2:10** for wisdom will come into your heart,<br>and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul;  
**2:11** prudence will watch over you;<br>and understanding will guard you.  
**2:12** It will save you from the way of evil,<br>from those who speak perversely,  
**2:13** who forsake the paths of uprightness<br>to walk in the ways of darkness,  
**2:14** who rejoice in doing evil<br>and delight in the perverseness of evil;  
**2:15** those whose paths are crooked,<br>and who are devious in their ways.  
**2:16** You will be saved from the loose<sup>①</sup> woman,<br>from the adulteress with her smooth words,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>strange</i>  
**2:17** who forsakes the partner of her youth<br>and forgets her sacred covenant;  
**2:18** for her way<sup>①</sup> leads down to death,<br>and her paths to the shades;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>house</i>  
**2:19** those who go to her never come back,<br>nor do they regain the paths of life.  
**2:20** Therefore walk in the way of the good,<br>and keep to the paths of the just.  
**2:21** For the upright will abide in the land,<br>and the innocent will remain in it;  
**2:22** but the wicked will be cut off from the land,<br>and the treacherous will be rooted out of it.  

## 3장

**3:1** My child, do not forget my teaching,<br>but let your heart keep my commandments;  
**3:2** for length of days and years of life<br>and abundant welfare they will give you.  
**3:3** Do not let loyalty and faithfulness forsake you;<br>bind them around your neck,<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**3:4** So you will find favor and good repute<br>in the sight of God and of people.  
**3:5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart,<br>and do not rely on your own insight.  
**3:6** In all your ways acknowledge him,<br>and he will make straight your paths.  
**3:7** Do not be wise in your own eyes;<br>fear the LORD, and turn away from evil.  
**3:8** It will be a healing for your flesh<br>and a refreshment for your body.  
**3:9** Honor the LORD with your substance<br>and with the first fruits of all your produce;  
**3:10** then your barns will be filled with plenty,<br>and your vats will be bursting with wine.  
**3:11** My child, do not despise the LORD’s discipline<br>or be weary of his reproof,  
**3:12** for the LORD reproves the one he loves,<br>as a father the son in whom he delights.  
**3:13** Happy are those who find wisdom,<br>and those who get understanding,  
**3:14** for her income is better than silver,<br>and her revenue better than gold.  
**3:15** She is more precious than jewels,<br>and nothing you desire can compare with her.  
**3:16** Long life is in her right hand;<br>in her left hand are riches and honor.  
**3:17** Her ways are ways of pleasantness,<br>and all her paths are peace.  
**3:18** She is a tree of life to those who lay hold of her;<br>those who hold her fast are called happy.  
**3:19** The LORD by wisdom founded the earth;<br>by understanding he established the heavens;  
**3:20** by his knowledge the deeps broke open,<br>and the clouds drop down the dew.  
**3:21** My child, do not let these escape from your sight:<br>keep sound wisdom and prudence,  
**3:22** and they will be life for your soul<br>and adornment for your neck.  
**3:23** Then you will walk on your way securely<br>and your foot will not stumble.  
**3:24** If you sit down,<sup>①</sup> you will not be afraid;<br>when you lie down, your sleep will be sweet.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk: Heb <i>lie down</i>  
**3:25** Do not be afraid of sudden panic,<br>or of the storm that strikes the wicked;  
**3:26** for the LORD will be your confidence<br>and will keep your foot from being caught.  
**3:27** Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due,<sup>①</sup><br>when it is in your power to do it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>from its owners</i>  
**3:28** Do not say to your neighbor, “Go, and come again,<br>tomorrow I will give it”—when you have it with you.  
**3:29** Do not plan harm against your neighbor<br>who lives trustingly beside you.  
**3:30** Do not quarrel with anyone without cause,<br>when no harm has been done to you.  
**3:31** Do not envy the violent<br>and do not choose any of their ways;  
**3:32** for the perverse are an abomination to the LORD,<br>but the upright are in his confidence.  
**3:33** The LORD’s curse is on the house of the wicked,<br>but he blesses the abode of the righteous.  
**3:34** Toward the scorners he is scornful,<br>but to the humble he shows favor.  
**3:35** The wise will inherit honor,<br>but stubborn fools, disgrace.  

## 4장

**4:1** Listen, children, to a father’s instruction,<br>and be attentive, that you may gain<sup>①</sup> insight;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>know</i>  
**4:2** for I give you good precepts:<br>do not forsake my teaching.  
**4:3** When I was a son with my father,<br>tender, and my mother’s favorite,  
**4:4** he taught me, and said to me,<br>“Let your heart hold fast my words;<br>keep my commandments, and live.  
**4:5** Get wisdom; get insight: do not forget, nor turn away<br>from the words of my mouth.  
**4:6** Do not forsake her, and she will keep you;<br>love her, and she will guard you.  
**4:7** The beginning of wisdom is this: Get wisdom,<br>and whatever else you get, get insight.  
**4:8** Prize her highly, and she will exalt you;<br>she will honor you if you embrace her.  
**4:9** She will place on your head a fair garland;<br>she will bestow on you a beautiful crown.”  
**4:10** Hear, my child, and accept my words,<br>that the years of your life may be many.  
**4:11** I have taught you the way of wisdom;<br>I have led you in the paths of uprightness.  
**4:12** When you walk, your step will not be hampered;<br>and if you run, you will not stumble.  
**4:13** Keep hold of instruction; do not let go;<br>guard her, for she is your life.  
**4:14** Do not enter the path of the wicked,<br>and do not walk in the way of evildoers.  
**4:15** Avoid it; do not go on it;<br>turn away from it and pass on.  
**4:16** For they cannot sleep unless they have done wrong;<br>they are robbed of sleep unless they have made someone stumble.  
**4:17** For they eat the bread of wickedness<br>and drink the wine of violence.  
**4:18** But the path of the righteous is like the light of dawn,<br>which shines brighter and brighter until full day.  
**4:19** The way of the wicked is like deep darkness;<br>they do not know what they stumble over.  
**4:20** My child, be attentive to my words;<br>incline your ear to my sayings.  
**4:21** Do not let them escape from your sight;<br>keep them within your heart.  
**4:22** For they are life to those who find them,<br>and healing to all their flesh.  
**4:23** Keep your heart with all vigilance,<br>for from it flow the springs of life.  
**4:24** Put away from you crooked speech,<br>and put devious talk far from you.  
**4:25** Let your eyes look directly forward,<br>and your gaze be straight before you.  
**4:26** Keep straight the path of your feet,<br>and all your ways will be sure.  
**4:27** Do not swerve to the right or to the left;<br>turn your foot away from evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** My child, be attentive to my wisdom;<br>incline your ear to my understanding,  
**5:2** so that you may hold on to prudence,<br>and your lips may guard knowledge.  
**5:3** For the lips of a loose<sup>①</sup> woman drip honey,<br>and her speech is smoother than oil;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>strange</i>  
**5:4** but in the end she is bitter as wormwood,<br>sharp as a two-edged sword.  
**5:5** Her feet go down to death;<br>her steps follow the path to Sheol.  
**5:6** She does not keep straight to the path of life;<br>her ways wander, and she does not know it.  
**5:7** And now, my child,<sup>①</sup> listen to me,<br>and do not depart from the words of my mouth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Vg: Heb <i>children</i>  
**5:8** Keep your way far from her,<br>and do not go near the door of her house;  
**5:9** or you will give your honor to others,<br>and your years to the merciless,  
**5:10** and strangers will take their fill of your wealth,<br>and your labors will go to the house of an alien;  
**5:11** and at the end of your life you will groan,<br>when your flesh and body are consumed,  
**5:12** and you say, “Oh, how I hated discipline,<br>and my heart despised reproof!  
**5:13** I did not listen to the voice of my teachers<br>or incline my ear to my instructors.  
**5:14** Now I am at the point of utter ruin<br>in the public assembly.”  
**5:15** Drink water from your own cistern,<br>flowing water from your own well.  
**5:16** Should your springs be scattered abroad,<br>streams of water in the streets?  
**5:17** Let them be for yourself alone,<br>and not for sharing with strangers.  
**5:18** Let your fountain be blessed,<br>and rejoice in the wife of your youth,  
**5:19** a lovely deer, a graceful doe.<br>May her breasts satisfy you at all times;<br>may you be intoxicated always by her love.  
**5:20** Why should you be intoxicated, my son, by another woman<br>and embrace the bosom of an adulteress?  
**5:21** For human ways are under the eyes of the LORD,<br>and he examines all their paths.  
**5:22** The iniquities of the wicked ensnare them,<br>and they are caught in the toils of their sin.  
**5:23** They die for lack of discipline,<br>and because of their great folly they are lost.  

## 6장

**6:1** My child, if you have given your pledge to your neighbor,<br>if you have bound yourself to another,<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>a stranger</i>  
**6:2** you are snared by the utterance of your lips,<sup>①</sup><br>caught by the words of your mouth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn Compare Gk Syr: Heb <i>the words of your mouth</i>  
**6:3** So do this, my child, and save yourself,<br>for you have come into your neighbor’s power:<br>go, hurry,<sup>①</sup> and plead with your neighbor.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>humble yourself</i>  
**6:4** Give your eyes no sleep<br>and your eyelids no slumber;  
**6:5** save yourself like a gazelle from the hunter,<sup>①</sup><br>like a bird from the hand of the fowler.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>from the hand</i>  
**6:6** Go to the ant, you lazybones;<br>consider its ways, and be wise.  
**6:7** Without having any chief<br>or officer or ruler,  
**6:8** it prepares its food in summer,<br>and gathers its sustenance in harvest.  
**6:9** How long will you lie there, O lazybones?<br>When will you rise from your sleep?  
**6:10** A little sleep, a little slumber,<br>a little folding of the hands to rest,  
**6:11** and poverty will come upon you like a robber,<br>and want, like an armed warrior.  
**6:12** A scoundrel and a villain<br>goes around with crooked speech,  
**6:13** winking the eyes, shuffling the feet,<br>pointing the fingers,  
**6:14** with perverted mind devising evil,<br>continually sowing discord;  
**6:15** on such a one calamity will descend suddenly;<br>in a moment, damage beyond repair.  
**6:16** There are six things that the LORD hates,<br>seven that are an abomination to him:  
**6:17** haughty eyes, a lying tongue,<br>and hands that shed innocent blood,  
**6:18** a heart that devises wicked plans,<br>feet that hurry to run to evil,  
**6:19** a lying witness who testifies falsely,<br>and one who sows discord in a family.  
**6:20** My child, keep your father’s commandment,<br>and do not forsake your mother’s teaching.  
**6:21** Bind them upon your heart always;<br>tie them around your neck.  
**6:22** When you walk, they<sup>①</sup> will lead you;<br>when you lie down, they<sup>②</sup> will watch over you;<br>and when you awake, they<sup>③</sup> will talk with you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>it</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>it</i> <sup>③</sup>Heb <i>it</i>  
**6:23** For the commandment is a lamp and the teaching a light,<br>and the reproofs of discipline are the way of life,  
**6:24** to preserve you from the wife of another,<sup>①</sup><br>from the smooth tongue of the adulteress.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk: MT <i>the evil woman</i>  
**6:25** Do not desire her beauty in your heart,<br>and do not let her capture you with her eyelashes;  
**6:26** for a prostitute’s fee is only a loaf of bread,<sup>①</sup><br>but the wife of another stalks a man’s very life.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn Compare Gk Syr Vg Tg: Heb <i>for because of a harlot to a piece of bread</i>  
**6:27** Can fire be carried in the bosom<br>without burning one’s clothes?  
**6:28** Or can one walk on hot coals<br>without scorching the feet?  
**6:29** So is he who sleeps with his neighbor’s wife;<br>no one who touches her will go unpunished.  
**6:30** Thieves are not despised who steal only<br>to satisfy their appetite when they are hungry.  
**6:31** Yet if they are caught, they will pay sevenfold;<br>they will forfeit all the goods of their house.  
**6:32** But he who commits adultery has no sense;<br>he who does it destroys himself.  
**6:33** He will get wounds and dishonor,<br>and his disgrace will not be wiped away.  
**6:34** For jealousy arouses a husband’s fury,<br>and he shows no restraint when he takes revenge.  
**6:35** He will accept no compensation,<br>and refuses a bribe no matter how great.  

## 7장

**7:1** My child, keep my words<br>and store up my commandments with you;  
**7:2** keep my commandments and live,<br>keep my teachings as the apple of your eye;  
**7:3** bind them on your fingers,<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,”<br>and call insight your intimate friend,  
**7:5** that they may keep you from the loose<sup>①</sup> woman,<br>from the adulteress with her smooth words.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>strange</i>  
**7:6** For at the window of my house<br>I looked out through my lattice,  
**7:7** and I saw among the simple ones,<br>I observed among the youths,<br>a young man without sense,  
**7:8** passing along the street near her corner,<br>taking the road to her house  
**7:9** in the twilight, in the evening,<br>at the time of night and darkness.  
**7:10** Then a woman comes toward him,<br>decked out like a prostitute, wily of heart.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**7:11** She is loud and wayward;<br>her feet do not stay at home;  
**7:12** now in the street, now in the squares,<br>and at every corner she lies in wait.  
**7:13** She seizes him and kisses him,<br>and with impudent face she says to him:  
**7:14** “I had to offer sacrifices,<br>and today I have paid my vows;  
**7:15** so now I have come out to meet you,<br>to seek you eagerly, and I have found you!

**7:17** I have perfumed my bed with myrrh,<br>aloes, and cinnamon.

**7:21** With much seductive speech she persuades him;<br>with her smooth talk she compels him.  
**7:22** Right away he follows her,<br>and goes like an ox to the slaughter,<br>or bounds like a stag toward the trap<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn Compare Gk: Meaning of Heb uncertain

**7:24** And now, my children, listen to me,<br>and be attentive to the words of my mouth.  
**7:25** Do not let your hearts turn aside to her ways;<br>do not stray into her paths.  
**7:26** For many are those she has laid low,<br>and numerous are her victims.  
**7:27** Her house is the way to Sheol,<br>going down to the chambers of death.  

## 8장

**8:6** Hear, for I will speak noble things,<br>and from my lips will come what is right;

**8:20** I walk in the way of righteousness,<br>along the paths of justice,

**8:33** Hear instruction and be wise,<br>and do not neglect it.  
**8:34** Happy is the one who listens to me,<br>watching daily at my gates,<br>waiting beside my doors.  
**8:35** For whoever finds me finds life<br>and obtains favor from the LORD;

## 9장

**9:1** Wisdom has built her house,<br>she has hewn her seven pillars.  
**9:2** She has slaughtered her animals, she has mixed her wine,<br>she has also set her table.  
**9:3** She has sent out her servant-girls, she calls<br>from the highest places in the town,  
**9:4** “You that are simple, turn in here!”<br>To those without sense she says,  
**9:5** “Come, eat of my bread<br>and drink of the wine I have mixed.  
**9:6** Lay aside immaturity,<sup>①</sup> and live,<br>and walk in the way of insight.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>simpleness</i>  
**9:7** Whoever corrects a scoffer wins abuse;<br>whoever rebukes the wicked gets hurt.
