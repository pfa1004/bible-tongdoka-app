# 29.요엘 (NRSV)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Joel son of Pethuel:  
**1:2** Hear this, O elders,<br>give ear, all inhabitants of the land!<br>Has such a thing happened in your days,<br>or in the days of your ancestors?  
**1:3** Tell your children of it,<br>and let your children tell their children,<br>and their children another generation.  
**1:4** What the cutting locust left,<br>the swarming locust has eaten.<br>What the swarming locust left,<br>the hopping locust has eaten,<br>and what the hopping locust left,<br>the destroying locust has eaten.  
**1:5** Wake up, you drunkards, and weep;<br>and wail, all you wine-drinkers,<br>over the sweet wine,<br>for it is cut off from your mouth.  
**1:6** For a nation has invaded my land,<br>powerful and innumerable;<br>its teeth are lions’ teeth,<br>and it has the fangs of a lioness.  
**1:7** It has laid waste my vines,<br>and splintered my fig trees;<br>it has stripped off their bark and thrown it down;<br>their branches have turned white.  
**1:8** Lament like a virgin dressed in sackcloth<br>for the husband of her youth.  
**1:9** The grain offering and the drink offering are cut off<br>from the house of the LORD.<br>The priests mourn,<br>the ministers of the LORD.  
**1:10** The fields are devastated,<br>the ground mourns;<br>for the grain is destroyed,<br>the wine dries up,<br>the oil fails.  
**1:11** Be dismayed, you farmers,<br>wail, you vinedressers,<br>over the wheat and the barley;<br>for the crops of the field are ruined.  
**1:12** The vine withers,<br>the fig tree droops.<br>Pomegranate, palm, and apple—<br>all the trees of the field are dried up;<br>surely, joy withers away<br>among the people.  
**1:13** Put on sackcloth and lament, you priests;<br>wail, you ministers of the altar.<br>Come, pass the night in sackcloth,<br>you ministers of my God!<br>Grain offering and drink offering<br>are withheld from the house of your God.  
**1:14** Sanctify a fast,<br>call a solemn assembly.<br>Gather the elders<br>and all the inhabitants of the land<br>to the house of the LORD your God,<br>and cry out to the LORD.  
**1:15** Alas for the day!<br>For the day of the LORD is near,<br>and as destruction from the Almighty<sup>①</sup> it comes.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Traditional rendering of Heb <i>Shaddai</i>  
**1:16** Is not the food cut off<br>before our eyes,<br>joy and gladness<br>from the house of our God?  
**1:17** The seed shrivels under the clods,<sup>①</sup><br>the storehouses are desolate;<br>the granaries are ruined<br>because the grain has failed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**1:18** How the animals groan!<br>The herds of cattle wander about<br>because there is no pasture for them;<br>even the flocks of sheep are dazed.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Compare Gk Syr Vg: Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**1:19** To you, O LORD, I cry.<br>For fire has devoured<br>the pastures of the wilderness,<br>and flames have burned<br>all the trees of the field.  
**1:20** Even the wild animals cry to you<br>because the watercourses are dried up,<br>and fire has devoured<br>the pastures of the wilderness.  

## 2장

**2:1** Blow the trumpet in Zion;<br>sound the alarm on my holy mountain!<br>Let all the inhabitants of the land tremble,<br>for the day of the LORD is coming, it is near—  
**2:2** a day of darkness and gloom,<br>a day of clouds and thick darkness!<br>Like blackness spread upon the mountains<br>a great and powerful army comes;<br>their like has never been from of old,<br>nor will be again after them<br>in ages to come.  
**2:3** Fire devours in front of them,<br>and behind them a flame burns.<br>Before them the land is like the garden of Eden,<br>but after them a desolate wilderness,<br>and nothing escapes them.  
**2:4** They have the appearance of horses,<br>and like war-horses they charge.  
**2:5** As with the rumbling of chariots,<br>they leap on the tops of the mountains,<br>like the crackling of a flame of fire<br>devouring the stubble,<br>like a powerful army<br>drawn up for battle.  
**2:6** Before them peoples are in anguish,<br>all faces grow pale.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**2:7** Like warriors they charge,<br>like soldiers they scale the wall.<br>Each keeps to its own course,<br>they do not swerve from<sup>①</sup> their paths.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Syr Vg: Heb <i>they do not take a pledge along</i>  
**2:8** They do not jostle one another,<br>each keeps to its own track;<br>they burst through the weapons<br>and are not halted.  
**2:9** They leap upon the city,<br>they run upon the walls;<br>they climb up into the houses,<br>they enter through the windows like a thief.  
**2:10** The earth quakes before them,<br>the heavens tremble.<br>The sun and the moon are darkened,<br>and the stars withdraw their shining.  
**2:11** The LORD utters his voice<br>at the head of his army;<br>how vast is his host!<br>Numberless are those who obey his command.<br>Truly the day of the LORD is great;<br>terrible indeed—who can endure it?  
**2:12** Yet even now, says the LORD,<br>return to me with all your heart,<br>with fasting, with weeping, and with mourning;  
**2:13** rend your hearts and not your clothing.<br>Return to the LORD, your God,<br>for he is gracious and merciful,<br>slow to anger, and abounding in steadfast love,<br>and relents from punishing.  
**2:14** Who knows whether he will not turn and relent,<br>and leave a blessing behind him,<br>a grain offering and a drink offering<br>for the LORD, your God?  
**2:15** Blow the trumpet in Zion;<br>sanctify a fast;<br>call a solemn assembly;  
**2:16** gather the people.<br>Sanctify the congregation;<br>assemble the aged;<br>gather the children,<br>even infants at the breast.<br>Let the bridegroom leave his room,<br>and the bride her canopy.  
**2:17** Between the vestibule and the altar<br>let the priests, the ministers of the LORD, weep.<br>Let them say, “Spare your people, O LORD,<br>and do not make your heritage a mockery,<br>a byword among the nations.<br>Why should it be said among the peoples,<br>‘Where is their God?’”  
**2:18** Then the LORD became jealous for his land,<br>and had pity on his people.  
**2:19** In response to his people the LORD said:<br>I am sending you<br>grain, wine, and oil,<br>and you will be satisfied;<br>and I will no more make you<br>a mockery among the nations.  
**2:20** I will remove the northern army far from you,<br>and drive it into a parched and desolate land,<br>its front into the eastern sea,<br>and its rear into the western sea;<br>its stench and foul smell will rise up.<br>Surely he has done great things!  
**2:21** Do not fear, O soil;<br>be glad and rejoice,<br>for the LORD has done great things!  
**2:22** Do not fear, you animals of the field,<br>for the pastures of the wilderness are green;<br>the tree bears its fruit,<br>the fig tree and vine give their full yield.  
**2:23** O children of Zion, be glad<br>and rejoice in the LORD your God;<br>for he has given the early rain<sup>①</sup> for your vindication,<br>he has poured down for you abundant rain,<br>the early and the later rain, as before.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**2:24** The threshing floors shall be full of grain,<br>the vats shall overflow with wine and oil.  
**2:25** I will repay you for the years<br>that the swarming locust has eaten,<br>the hopper, the destroyer, and the cutter,<br>my great army, which I sent against you.  
**2:26** You shall eat in plenty and be satisfied,<br>and praise the name of the LORD your God,<br>who has dealt wondrously with you.<br>And my people shall never again be put to shame.  
**2:27** You shall know that I am in the midst of Israel,<br>and that I, the LORD, am your God and there is no other.<br>And my people shall never again be put to shame.  
**2:28** <f><sup>①</sup> Then afterward<br>I will pour out my spirit on all flesh;<br>your sons and your daughters shall prophesy,<br>your old men shall dream dreams,<br>and your young men shall see visions.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch <a name='B:360 3:1'>3.1</a> in Heb  
**2:29** Even on the male and female slaves,<br>in those days, I will pour out my spirit.  
**2:30** I will show portents in the heavens and on the earth, blood and fire and columns of smoke.  
**2:31** The sun shall be turned to darkness, and the moon to blood, before the great and terrible day of the LORD comes.  
**2:32** Then everyone who calls on the name of the LORD shall be saved; for in Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there shall be those who escape, as the LORD has said, and among the survivors shall be those whom the LORD calls.  

## 3장

**3:1** <f><sup>①</sup> For then, in those days and at that time, when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch 4.1 in Heb  
**3:2** I will gather all the nations and bring them down to the valley of Jehoshaphat, and I will enter into judgment with them there, on account of my people and my heritage Israel, because they have scattered them among the nations. They have divided my land,  
**3:3** and cast lots for my people, and traded boys for prostitutes, and sold girls for wine, and drunk it down.  
**3:4** What are you to me, O Tyre and Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Are you paying me back for something? If you are paying me back, I will turn your deeds back upon your own heads swiftly and speedily.  
**3:5** For you have taken my silver and my gold, and have carried my rich treasures into your temples.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>palaces</i>  
**3:6** You have sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks, removing them far from their own border.  
**3:7** But now I will rouse them to leave the places to which you have sold them, and I will turn your deeds back upon your own heads.  
**3:8** I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hand of the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans, to a nation far away; for the LORD has spoken.  
**3:9** Proclaim this among the nations:<br>Prepare war,<sup>①</sup><br>stir up the warriors.<br>Let all the soldiers draw near,<br>let them come up.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>sanctify war</i>  
**3:10** Beat your plowshares into swords,<br>and your pruning hooks into spears;<br>let the weakling say, “I am a warrior.”  
**3:11** Come quickly,<sup>①</sup><br>all you nations all around,<br>gather yourselves there.<br>Bring down your warriors, O LORD.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Meaning of Heb uncertain  
**3:12** Let the nations rouse themselves,<br>and come up to the valley of Jehoshaphat;<br>for there I will sit to judge<br>all the neighboring nations.  
**3:13** Put in the sickle,<br>for the harvest is ripe.<br>Go in, tread,<br>for the wine press is full.<br>The vats overflow,<br>for their wickedness is great.  
**3:14** Multitudes, multitudes,<br>in the valley of decision!<br>For the day of the LORD is near<br>in the valley of decision.  
**3:15** The sun and the moon are darkened,<br>and the stars withdraw their shining.  
**3:16** The LORD roars from Zion,<br>and utters his voice from Jerusalem,<br>and the heavens and the earth shake.<br>But the LORD is a refuge for his people,<br>a stronghold for the people of Israel.  
**3:17** So you shall know that I, the LORD your God,<br>dwell in Zion, my holy mountain.<br>And Jerusalem shall be holy,<br>and strangers shall never again pass through it.  
**3:18** In that day<br>the mountains shall drip sweet wine,<br>the hills shall flow with milk,<br>and all the stream beds of Judah<br>shall flow with water;<br>a fountain shall come forth from the house of the LORD<br>and water the Wadi Shittim.  
**3:19** Egypt shall become a desolation<br>and Edom a desolate wilderness,<br>because of the violence done to the people of Judah,<br>in whose land they have shed innocent blood.  
**3:20** But Judah shall be inhabited forever,<br>and Jerusalem to all generations.  
**3:21** I will avenge their blood, and I will not clear the guilty,<sup>①</sup><br>for the LORD dwells in Zion.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Syr: Heb <i>I will hold innocent their blood that I have not held innocent</i>
