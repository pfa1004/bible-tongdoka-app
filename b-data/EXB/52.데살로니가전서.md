# 52.데살로니가전서 (EXB)



## 1장

**1:1** From Paul, Silas, and Timothy [1 and 2 Timothy]. To the church in Thessalonica [of the Thessalonians; a city in Macedonia, present-day northern Greece], the church in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ: Grace and peace to you.  
**1:2** The Faith of the Thessalonians We always thank God for all of you and mention you when we pray.  
**1:3** We continually recall before God our Father ·the things you have done because of your faith [your work of/from faith], ·the work you have done because of your love [your labor of/from love] and your ·continuing strength because of your hope [endurance of/from hope] in our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:4** Brothers and sisters, God loves you, and we know he has chosen you,  
**1:5** because the ·Good News [Gospel] we brought to you came not only with words, but with power, with the Holy Spirit, and with ·sure knowledge that it is true [great conviction]. You know how we lived when we were with you ·in order to help you [for your sake].  
**1:6** And you became ·like us and like [imitators of us and of] the Lord. You ·suffered much [experienced many trials/much persecution], but still you accepted the teaching with the joy that comes from the Holy Spirit.  
**1:7** So you became an ·example to [model for] all the believers in Macedonia [the Roman province in which Thessalonica was located, present-day northern Greece] and Achaia [present-day southern Greece].  
**1:8** And the Lord’s ·teaching [message; word] ·spread [rang out; sounded forth] from you not only into Macedonia and Achaia, but now your faith in God has ·become known [gone forth] everywhere. So we do not need to say anything about it.  
**1:9** ·People everywhere [For they themselves] are ·telling [reporting; announcing] about the way you ·accepted [welcomed] us when we were there with you. They tell how you ·stopped worshiping idols [turned to God from idols] to serve the living and true God,  
**1:10** and to wait for his Son from heaven, whom God raised from the dead. He is Jesus, who ·saves [rescues; delivers] us from ·God’s angry judgment that is sure to come [the coming wrath].  

## 2장

**2:1** Paul’s Work in Thessalonica Brothers and sisters, you ·know [yourselves know; are well aware that] our ·visit [coming] to you was not ·a failure [without results; in vain].  
**2:2** Before we came to you, we suffered and were ·insulted [mistreated] in Philippi [a city in Macedonia], as you know. But ·our God helped us to be brave and [we were emboldened in our God] to tell you ·his Good News [the Gospel of God] in spite of great opposition.  
**2:3** [For] Our ·appeal [encouragement; exhortation] does not come from ·lies [error] or ·wrong reasons [false motives; impurity], nor ·were we trying to trick you [with guile/deceit].  
**2:4** But we speak the ·Good News [Gospel] because God ·tested [approved] us and ·trusted us to do [or entrusted us with] it. When we speak, we are not trying to please people, but God, who ·tests [examines; approves] our hearts.  
**2:5** You know that we never ·tried to influence you by saying nice things about you [came with words of flattery]. ·We were not trying to get your money; we had no selfishness to hide from you […nor with hidden motives of greed]. God ·knows that this is true [is (our) witness].  
**2:6** We were not looking for human ·praise [glory], from you or anyone else,  
**2:7** even though as apostles of Christ we could have ·used our authority over you [thrown our weight around; exercised our prerogatives]. But we were very gentle with you, like a ·mother [nurse; nursing mother] caring for her [own] little children.  
**2:8** Because we ·loved [had such affection for/devotion to] you, we were ·happy [pleased; delighted] to share not only God’s ·Good News [Gospel] with you, but ·even [also] our own lives. You had become so ·dear [beloved] to us!  
**2:9** Brothers and sisters, ·I know [surely] you remember our hard work and ·difficulties [hardship; labor]. We worked night and day so we would not burden any of you while we preached God’s ·Good News [Gospel] to you.  
**2:10** When we were with you, we lived in a holy and ·honest [righteous; just] way, ·without fault [blameless; innocent]. You ·know this is true, and so does [are witnesses, and so is] God.  
**2:11** You know that we treated each of you as a father treats his own children.  
**2:12** We ·encouraged [exhorted; urged] you, we ·urged [encouraged; comforted] you, and we ·insisted [appealed/bore witness to you] that you ·live good lives for [walk worthy of] God, who calls you to his ·glorious kingdom [kingdom and glory].  
**2:13** ·Also [For this reason], we always thank God because when you ·heard his message [received the message you heard] from us, you accepted it not as ·merely human words [a human message], but for what it truly is—·the word of [a message from] God, which ·works in [transforms] you who believe.  
**2:14** [For] Brothers and sisters, ·your experiences have been like those [or you became imitators] of God’s churches in Christ Jesus that are in Judea [the original church in Jerusalem and others nearby]. You suffered the same things from the people of your own country, as they suffered from the Jews  
**2:15** who killed both the Lord Jesus and the prophets and ·forced us to leave that country [drove us out; or persecuted us]. They do not please God and are ·against [hostile/opposed to] all people.  
**2:16** ·They try [or …because they try; or …by trying] to stop us from ·teaching [speaking/preaching to] the Gentiles so they may be saved. By doing this, they are ·increasing [filling/heaping up] their sins to the limit. The ·anger [wrath] of God ·has come to [or has drawn near to; or will overtake] them ·at last [or completely; or until the end (of the age)].  
**2:17** Paul Wants to Visit Them Again Brothers and sisters, though we were ·separated [torn away; orphaned] from you for a short time, ·our thoughts were still with you [physically, not spiritually…; in face, not in heart…]. We wanted very much to see you and tried hard to do so.  
**2:18** We wanted to come to you. I, Paul, tried to come ·more than once [again and again; both once and twice], but Satan ·stopped [hindered; prevented] us.  
**2:19** ·You are [For who is…?] our hope, our joy, and the crown we will ·take pride in [boast about] when our Lord Jesus Christ comes.  
**2:20** Truly you are our glory and our joy.  

## 3장

**3:1** When we could ·not wait any [endure it no] longer, we decided it was best to stay in Athens alone  
**3:2** and send Timothy to you. Timothy, our brother, ·works with us [is our coworker] for God ·and helps us tell people the Good News about [in the Gospel of] Christ. We sent him to strengthen and ·encourage [comfort; or exhort] you in your faith  
**3:3** so none of you would be ·upset [disturbed; shaken] by these ·troubles [trials]. [For] You yourselves know that we ·must face these troubles [are destined/appointed for this].  
**3:4** [For] Even when we were with you, we told you [in advance] we all would have to ·suffer [be persecuted; suffer hardship/affliction], and you know it has happened.  
**3:5** Because of this, when I could ·wait [endure it] no longer, I sent Timothy to you so I could learn about your faith. I was afraid ·the devil [the tempter] had tempted you, and perhaps our hard work would have been ·wasted [in vain; for nothing].  
**3:6** But Timothy now has come back to us from you and has brought us good news about your faith and love. He told us that you always remember us in a good way and that you ·want [long] to see us just as much as we ·want [long] to see you.  
**3:7** So, brothers and sisters, ·while we have much [in all of our] ·trouble [distress] and ·suffering [trials; persecution], we are ·encouraged [comforted; reassured] about you because of your faith.  
**3:8** ·Our life is really full [For now we live/are alive (again)] ·if you stand [since you are standing] ·strong [firm; fast] in the Lord.  
**3:9** ·We cannot thank God enough [For what thanks can we give to God…?] for all the joy we feel ·in God’s presence [before our God] because of you.  
**3:10** Night and day we continue praying ·with all our heart [most earnestly] that we can see you again and ·give you all the things you need [strengthen/supply what is lacking] to make your faith strong.  
**3:11** Now may our God and Father himself and our Lord Jesus ·prepare [clear; direct] the way for us to come to you.  
**3:12** May the Lord make your love grow more and ·multiply [abound; overflow] for each other and for all people ·so that you will love others as we love you [or just as our love abounds for you].  
**3:13** ·May your hearts […so that your hearts may] be made strong so that you will be holy and ·without fault [blameless] before our God and Father when our Lord Jesus comes with all his ·holy ones [saints; or holy angels].  

## 4장

**4:1** A Life that Pleases God [Finally; or Now then; Furthermore] Brothers and sisters, we taught you how to ·live [walk] in a way that will please God, and you are living that way. Now we ask and ·encourage [urge; appeal to] you in the Lord Jesus to ·live that way [excel; abound] even more.  
**4:2** [For] You know what ·we told [instructions/commands we gave] you to do ·by the authority of [through] the Lord Jesus.  
**4:3** ·God wants you to be holy and [or For this is God’s will, your sanctification:] to stay away from sexual sins.  
**4:4** He wants each of you to ·learn to control your own body [or take a wife for yourself; or live with your own wife; gain/possess his own vessel] in a way that is holy and honorable.  
**4:5** ·Don’t use your body for sexual sin […not in lustful passion] like the ·people [Gentiles] who do not know God.  
**4:6** Also, do not ·wrong [exploit; transgress] or ·cheat [take advantage of] another ·Christian [brother (or sister)] in this way. The Lord ·will punish people who do those [is the avenger concerning these] things as we have already told you and ·warned [solemnly testified to] you.  
**4:7** [For] God did not call us to ·a life of sin [impurity], but to holiness.  
**4:8** ·So [Therefore] the person who ·refuses to obey [disregards; rejects; despises] this teaching is not simply ·disobeying [disregarding; rejecting; despising] ·a human being [human authority], but God, who gives you his Holy Spirit.  
**4:9** We do not need to write you about ·having love for your Christian family [brotherly love], because God has already taught you to love each other.  
**4:10** And truly you do love the ·Christians [brothers (and sisters)] in all of Macedonia. Now we encourage you, brothers and sisters, to ·love them [excel; abound] even more.  
**4:11** ·Do all you can […and to aspire] to live a peaceful life. ·Take care of [Attend to; Mind] your own business, and ·do your own work [work with your hands] as we have already ·told [instructed; commanded] you.  
**4:12** If you do, then you will ·win the respect of [or live a respectable/proper life before] ·unbelievers [outsiders], and you will not have to depend on others for what you need.  
**4:13** The Lord’s Coming Brothers and sisters, we ·want you to know [do not want you to be uninformed] about those ·Christians who have died [who sleep; a euphemism for death] so you will not ·be sad [grieve], as ·others [the rest] who have no hope.  
**4:14** [For] We believe that Jesus died and that he rose again. ·So, through him, God will raise with Jesus those who have died [or So God will bring with Jesus those who have died/fallen asleep in him].  
**4:15** What we tell you now is ·the Lord’s own message [by the word of the Lord]. We who are ·living [still alive] when the Lord comes again will [certainly; surely] not go before those who have ·already died [fallen asleep].  
**4:16** The Lord himself will come down from heaven with a loud ·command [or shout], ·with [or accompanied by; or preceded by] the voice of the archangel [a leading or ruling angel], and with the trumpet call of God. And ·those who have died believing [the dead] in Christ will rise first.  
**4:17** After that, we who are ·still alive [or alive and are left] will be ·gathered [caught; taken] up with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. And [so; in this way] we will be with the Lord forever.  
**4:18** So ·encourage [comfort] each other with these words.  

## 5장

**5:1** Be Ready for the Lord’s Coming Now, brothers and sisters, we do not need to write you about times and ·dates [seasons; related to end-time events].  
**5:2** You know ·very well [accurately] that the ·day the Lord comes again [day of the Lord; the time of Christ’s return and the judgments associated with it] will ·be a surprise, like a thief that comes in the night [come like a thief in the night].  
**5:3** While people are saying, “·We have peace and we are safe [Peace and safety/security],” they will be destroyed ·quickly [suddenly]. It is like pains that come quickly to a woman having a baby. Those people will not escape.  
**5:4** But you, brothers and sisters, are not ·living in darkness [in darkness], and so that day will not ·surprise you [or overtake you] like a thief.  
**5:5** [For] You are all ·people who belong to the light [children/sons of the light] and ·who belong to [children/sons of] the day. We do not belong to the night or to darkness.  
**5:6** So we should not be like other people who are sleeping [spiritually], but we should be ·alert [awake] and ·have self-control [sober].  
**5:7** [For] Those who sleep sleep at night. Those who get drunk get drunk at night.  
**5:8** But we belong to the day, so we should ·control ourselves [be sober]. We should ·wear faith and love to protect us [put on the breastplate/body armor of faith and love], and the hope of salvation should be our helmet.  
**5:9** God did not ·choose [appoint; destine] us ·to suffer his anger [for wrath] but to ·have [receive; gain; possess] salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**5:10** ·Jesus […who] died for us so that we can live together with him, whether we are ·alive [awake] or ·dead [asleep] when he comes.  
**5:11** So encourage each other and ·give each other strength [build each other up], just as you are doing now.  
**5:12** Final Instructions and Greetings Now, brothers and sisters, we ask you to ·appreciate [acknowledge; respect] those who work hard among you, who ·lead [or care for] you in the Lord and ·teach [instruct; admonish] you.  
**5:13** ·Respect [Regard; Esteem] them ·with a very special [or very highly with your] love because of the work they do. Live in peace with each other.  
**5:14** We ·ask [urge; exhort; encourage] you, brothers and sisters, to ·warn [admonish; rebuke] those who ·do not work [are idle; or are undisciplined]. ·Encourage [Comfort] the people who are ·afraid [apprehensive; fainthearted; discouraged]. Help those who are weak. Be patient with everyone.  
**5:15** ·Be sure [See to it] that no one pays back ·wrong [evil] for ·wrong [evil], but always ·try to do [strive for; pursue] what is good for each other and for all people.  
**5:16** Always ·be joyful [rejoice].  
**5:17** Pray ·continually [without ceasing],  
**5:18** and give thanks ·whatever happens [in all circumstances; in everything]. That is ·what God wants [God’s will] for you in Christ Jesus.  
**5:19** Do not ·hold back the work of [stifle; quench; extinguish] the Holy Spirit.  
**5:20** Do not treat ·prophecy [prophecies; occurring in the church] ·as if it were unimportant [with contempt].  
**5:21** But test everything. ·Keep [Hold on to] what is good,  
**5:22** and stay away from ·everything that is [every form/kind of] evil.  
**5:23** Now may God himself, the God of peace, ·make you holy in every way [sanctify you completely/through and through]. May your whole self—spirit, soul, and body—be kept ·faultless [blameless] when our Lord Jesus Christ comes.  
**5:24** The One who calls you is ·trustworthy [faithful], and he will ·do this [make this happen].  
**5:25** Brothers and sisters, pray for us.  
**5:26** Greet all ·believers [the brothers (and sisters)] with a holy kiss.  
**5:27** I ·tell [solemnly charge; adjure] you ·by the authority of [or before; or in] the Lord to read this letter to all the believers.  
**5:28** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.
