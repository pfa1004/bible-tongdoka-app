# 31.오바디야 (EXB)



## 1장

**1:1** The Lord Will Punish the Edomites This is the vision of Obadiah. This is what the Lord God says about Edom [the Edomites were descendants of Esau, Jacob’s twin brother ; they were often in conflict with Israel]: We have heard a ·message [report] from the Lord. A ·messenger [envoy] has been sent among the nations, saying, “·Attack [Rise up]! Let’s go ·attack [rise to battle against] Edom!”  
**1:2** The Lord Speaks to the Edomites “·Soon [Look; Behold] I will make you ·the smallest of [or weak among the] nations. You will be greatly ·hated [despised] by everyone.  
**1:3** Your pride has ·fooled [deceived] you, you who live in the ·hollow places of the cliff [clefts of the rock]. Your home is ·up high [on the heights], you who say to yourself, ‘·No one can [Who can…?] bring me down to the ground.’  
**1:4** Even if you ·fly high [soar] like the eagle and make your nest among the stars, I will bring you down from there,” says the Lord.  
**1:5** “You will really be ·ruined [cut off]! If thieves came to you, if ·robbers [plunderers] came by night, they would steal only enough for themselves. If workers came and picked the grapes from your vines, they would leave ·a few behind [some for the poor; the gleanings].  
**1:6** But you, Edom, will ·really lose everything [be cleaned/searched out]! People will find all your hidden treasures!  
**1:7** All ·the people who are your friends [your allies; the people of your covenant/treaty] will force you ·out of the land [to the border]. The people ·who are at peace with you [or who promised you peace; of your peace] will ·trick [deceive] you and ·defeat [overpower] you. Those who eat your bread [indicating fellowship and friendship] with you now are planning a trap for you, and you will ·not notice it [be taken by surprise; have no understanding].”  
**1:8** The Lord says, “On that day I will surely destroy the wise people from Edom, and those with understanding from the mountains of Edom.  
**1:9** Then, city of Teman, your ·best warriors [mighty men] will be ·afraid [dismayed], and everyone from the mountains of Edom will be ·killed [cut down in the slaughter].  
**1:10** You did violence to your ·relatives, the Israelites [brother Jacob], so you will be covered with shame and destroyed forever.  
**1:11** You stood ·aside without helping [aloof] ·while [on the day] strangers carried Israel’s ·treasures [wealth; or army] away. When foreigners entered Israel’s city gate and threw lots to decide ·what part of Jerusalem they would take [for Jerusalem], you were like one of them.  
**1:12** Commands That Edom Broke “Edom, ·do not laugh at [or you should not have gloated over] your ·brother Israel [brother] in his time of ·trouble [misfortune] or ·be happy [rejoice] about the ·people [sons; descendants] of Judah ·when they are destroyed [on the day of destruction]. Do not ·brag [boast; act with arrogance] ·when cruel things are done to them [on the day of distress].  
**1:13** Do not enter the city gate of my people [to plunder their goods] in their ·time [day] of ·trouble [disaster; the Hebrew word for “trouble” sounds like Edom] or ·laugh at their problems [gloat over them] in their ·time [day] of ·trouble [disaster]. Do not ·take their treasures [loot their wealth] in their ·time [day] of ·trouble [disaster].  
**1:14** Do not stand at the crossroads to ·destroy [cut off] ·those who are trying to escape [their fugitives]. Do not capture ·those who escape alive [their survivors] and turn them over to their enemy in their ·time [day] of ·trouble [adversity].  
**1:15** The Nations Will Be Judged “The ·Lord’s day of judging [day of the Lord] is ·coming soon [near] to all the nations. ·The same evil things you did to other people [As you have done] ·will happen [it will be done] to you; ·they [recompense; reprisal] will come back upon your own head.  
**1:16** Because you drank in my ·Temple [holy mountain], all the nations will drink ·on and on [continually]. They will drink and ·drink [swallow] ·until they disappear [and be as though they were not].  
**1:17** But on Mount Zion ·some will escape the judgment [there will be escape], and it will be a holy place. The ·people [house] of Jacob will ·take back their land from those who ·took it from them [possess their possessions].  
**1:18** The ·people [house] of Jacob will be like a fire and the ·people [house] of Joseph [the Israelite tribes of Ephraim and Manasseh (Joseph’s sons, Jacob’s grandsons), representing the northern kingdom] like a flame. But the ·people [house] of Esau [the Edomites] will be like ·dry stalks [stubble]. The ·people [house] of Jacob will set them on fire and burn them up. There will be no ·one left [survivors] of the ·people [house] of Esau.” ·This will happen because [For] the Lord has said it.  
**1:19** ·Then God’s people will regain southern Judah from Edom; they will take back [The Negev shall possess] the mountains of ·Edom [Esau]. ·They will take back the western hills from [The Shephelah/foothills shall possess] the Philistines. They will ·regain [possess] the ·lands [fields] of Ephraim and Samaria, and Benjamin will ·take over [possess] Gilead [east of the Jordan River].  
**1:20** ·People from [This host of the sons/children of] Israel who once were ·forced to leave their homes [exiled] will take the land of the Canaanites, all the way to Zarephath [in Phoenicia in the far northeast]. ·People from Judah who once were forced to leave Jerusalem and [The exiles of Jerusalem who] live in Sepharad [unknown location, perhaps Sardis (in present-day Turkey)] will take back the cities of ·southern Judah [the Negev].  
**1:21** ·Powerful warriors [Deliverers; Saviors; or Those who have been rescued/delivered] will go up on Mount Zion, where they will rule ·the people living on Edom’s mountains [Mount Esau]. And the kingdom will belong to the Lord.
