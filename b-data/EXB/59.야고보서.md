# 59.야고보서 (EXB)



## 1장

**1:1** From James [one of Jesus’ brothers and a leader in the early church;], a ·servant [slave; bond-servant] of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ. To ·all of God’s people [the twelve tribes; an allusion to the twelve tribes of Israel; referring either to Jewish Christians or all believers as the new covenant people of God] ·who are scattered everywhere in the world [in the Diaspora/Dispersion; a reference to the scattering of the Jews during the Babylonian captivity, now applied to the church]: Greetings.  
**1:2** Faith and Wisdom My brothers and sisters [fellow believers], when you have many kinds of ·troubles [trials; testing], ·you should be full of joy [consider it all/pure joy],  
**1:3** because you know that these troubles test your faith, and this will give you ·patience [perserverance; endurance].  
**1:4** [And] Let your ·patience [perserverance; endurance] ·show itself perfectly in what you do [have its full effect; finish its work]. Then you will be ·perfect and complete [mature and whole; or completely mature] and will ·have everything you need [lack nothing].  
**1:5** But if any of you ·needs [lacks] wisdom, you should ask God for it. He is generous to everyone and will give you wisdom ·without criticizing you [without finding fault; ungrudgingly].  
**1:6** But when you ask God, you must ·believe [ask with faith] and not doubt. Anyone who doubts is like a wave in the sea, ·blown up and down [driven and tossed] by the wind.  
**1:7** [For] Such doubters are ·thinking two different things at the same time [double-minded], and they ·cannot decide about anything they do [are unstable in all they do]. They should not ·think [expect] they will receive anything from the Lord.  
**1:8** (Included in v. 7.)  
**1:9** True Riches ·Believers [Brothers or sisters] who are ·poor [in lowly/humble circumstances] should ·take pride [boast] ·that God has made them spiritually rich [in being raised up; in their exaltation; in their high position].  
**1:10** [But] Those who are ·rich [wealthy] should ·take pride [boast] ·that God has shown them that they are spiritually poor [in their humiliation; in their lowly state]. [Because] The rich will ·die [pass away; wither] like a ·wild flower in the grass [or flower in the field].  
**1:11** [For] The sun rises with ·burning [scorching] heat and ·dries up [withers] the ·plants [or grass]. The flower falls off, and its beauty is ·gone [destroyed]. In the same way the rich will ·die [wither away] ·while they are still taking care of business [in the midst of their pursuits; in his journeys].  
**1:12** Temptation Is Not from God ·When people are tempted and still continue strong, they should be happy [Blessed is the one who perserveres/endures trials/temptations]. After they have ·proved their faith [stood/endured/passed the test], ·God will reward them with life forever [they will receive the crown of life; alludes to the laurel crown given for a victory]. ·God [He] promised this to all those who love him.  
**1:13** When people are ·tempted [undergoing a trial], they should not say, “God is tempting me.” [For] God cannot be tempted by evil, and he himself does not tempt anyone.  
**1:14** But ·people are [each person is] tempted when their own evil desire ·leads [lures; drags] them away and ·traps [entices; lures] them.  
**1:15** ·This desire leads to sin [Then, after desire is conceived, it gives birth to sin], and then the sin ·grows [or becomes full-grown] and ·brings [gives birth to] death.  
**1:16** My ·dear [beloved] brothers and sisters [fellow believers], do not be ·fooled [deceived] about this.  
**1:17** Every good ·action [or act of giving] and every perfect gift ·is from God [comes from above]. These good gifts come down from the ·Creator of the sun, moon, and stars [the father of lights; referring to God’s creation of the heavenly bodies (;)], who does not change like their shifting shadows.  
**1:18** God ·decided [chose; willed] to give us ·life [birth] through the ·word of truth [true message] so we might be ·the most important [or the first of many; a kind of firstfruits; firstfruits were the first crops to be harvested and guaranteed the coming harvest] of all ·the things he made [or his creatures].  
**1:19** Listening and Obeying My ·dear [beloved] brothers and sisters [fellow believers], [under-stand/know this:] ·always be willing to listen and slow to speak. Do not become angry easily [be quick to listen, slow to speak, slow to anger],  
**1:20** ·because anger will not help you live the right kind of life God wants [because human anger does not produce God’s righteousness].  
**1:21** So ·put out of your life [get rid of; lay aside] every ·evil thing [moral filth/defilement] and every ·kind of wrong [excessive evil]. Then in ·gentleness [humility] ·accept [welcome] God’s ·teaching [word] that is planted in your hearts, which can save ·you [your souls; referring to the whole person].  
**1:22** ·Do what God’s teaching says; when you only listen and do nothing, you are fooling yourselves [Be doers of the word, and not only hearers, deceiving yourselves].  
**1:23** Those who hear God’s ·teaching [word] and do nothing are like people who look at ·themselves [their natural face; the face of their birth] in a mirror.  
**1:24** [For] They ·see their faces [gaze at themselves] and then go away and quickly forget what they looked like.  
**1:25** But ·the truly happy people are [blessed are] those who ·carefully study [look intently into; stoop down to look at] God’s perfect law that makes people free [probably refers to the law of Moses as it is fulfilled in Christ], and they ·continue to study it [persevere in it]. ·They do not forget what they heard, but they obey what God’s teaching says [not being a forgetful hearer but an active doer]. Those who do this will be ·made happy [blessed].  
**1:26** The True Way to Worship God People who think they are religious but ·say things they should not say [do not bridle their tongues] are just ·fooling [deceiving] themselves. Their “religion” is ·worth nothing [empty; meaningless].  
**1:27** Religion that God the Father accepts as pure and ·without fault [undefiled] is this: caring for orphans and widows ·who need help [in their trouble/distress/persecution], and keeping yourself ·free from the world’s evil influence [unstained/unpolluted by the world].  

## 2장

**2:1** Love All People My dear brothers and sisters [fellow believers], as believers in our glorious Lord Jesus Christ, never ·think some people are more important than others [show favoritism/partiality].  
**2:2** Suppose someone comes into your ·church meeting [gathering; assembly; the word can mean “synagogue,” but here refers to a house church gathering] wearing nice clothes and a gold ring. At the same time a poor person comes in wearing ·old, dirty [shabby; filthy] clothes.  
**2:3** You ·show special attention to [take notice of; look favorably on] the one wearing nice clothes and say, “Please, sit here in this good seat.” But you say to the poor person, “Stand over there,” or, “Sit on the floor ·by my feet [or beside my footstool].”  
**2:4** ·What are you doing? You are making some people more important than others [Are you not discriminating among yourselves…?], and ·with evil thoughts you are deciding that one person is better [or you are judging with evil thoughts; or you are acting like judges with evil motives].  
**2:5** Listen, my ·dear [beloved] brothers and sisters [fellow believers]! ·God chose [Did not God choose…?] the poor in the world to be rich with faith and to ·receive [be heirs of] the kingdom God promised to those who love him.  
**2:6** But you ·show no respect to [dishonor; have insulted] the poor. ·The rich are always trying to control your lives. They are the ones who take you to court [Don’t the rich exploit/oppress you and drag you into court?].  
**2:7** ·And they are the ones who speak against Jesus, who owns you [or Don’t they slander the noble/good name by which you were called? Don’t they blaspheme the noble/good name that was invoked over you? The “name” may be the person’s name or the name of Jesus, to whom they belong].  
**2:8** This royal law [because God the king decreed it, or because it is the supreme law] is found in the Scriptures: “Love your neighbor as you love yourself.” ·If you [or If you really; or If you, however,] ·obey [fulfill; carry out] this law, you are doing ·right [well].  
**2:9** But if you ·treat one person as being more important than another [show favoritism/partiality], you are sinning. You are ·guilty of breaking God’s law [convicted by the law as lawbreakers/ transgressors].  
**2:10** [For] A person who ·follows [keeps; obeys] all of God’s law but ·fails to obey even one command [stumbles/falls in one point] is guilty of breaking all the commands in that law.  
**2:11** [For] The same God who said, “You must not ·be guilty of [commit] adultery,” also said, “You must not murder anyone.” So if you do not ·take part in [commit] adultery but you murder someone, ·you are guilty of breaking all of God’s law [you have become a breaker/transgressor of the law].  
**2:12** ·In everything you say and do, remember that you [So speak and so act as those who] will be judged by the law ·that makes people free [of freedom/liberty].  
**2:13** ·So you must show mercy to others, or God will not show mercy to you when he judges you [For judgment is without mercy to the one who does not act mercifully]. ·But the person who shows mercy can stand without fear at the judgment [But mercy triumphs over judgment].  
**2:14** Faith and Good Works My brothers and sisters [fellow believers], if people ·say [claim] they have faith, but do nothing, ·their faith is worth nothing [what good/profit/gain is it?]. Can faith like that save them?  
**2:15** [Suppose; If] A brother or sister in Christ ·might need clothes or food [is naked and lacks daily food].  
**2:16** If you say to that person, “·God be with you [or I wish you well; Go in peace]! ·I hope you stay warm and get plenty to eat, [Be warmed and filled]” but you do not ·give what that person needs [provide for the needs of their body], ·your words are worth nothing [what good/profit/gain is that?].  
**2:17** In the same way, faith by itself—·that does nothing [if it does not have works]—is dead.  
**2:18** [But] Someone might say, “You have faith, but I have ·deeds [works].” ·Show me [Prove; Demonstrate] your faith without ·doing anything [works], and I will ·show you [prove; demonstrate] my faith by ·what I do [my works].  
**2:19** You believe ·there is one God [or that God is one]. ·Good [You do well]! But the demons believe that, too, and they ·tremble with fear [shudder].  
**2:20** You ·foolish [senseless; emptyheaded] person! ·Must you be shown [Do you want evidence; Can’t you see] that faith ·that does nothing [without works] is ·worth nothing [useless; barren]?  
**2:21** Abraham, our ·ancestor [father; patriarch], was ·made right with God [justified] by what he did when he offered his son Isaac on the altar.  
**2:22** So you see that Abraham’s faith and ·the things he did [his works] worked together. His faith was made ·perfect [complete] by ·what he did [his works].  
**2:23** This ·shows the full meaning of [fulfills] the Scripture that says: “Abraham believed God, and ·God accepted Abraham’s faith, and that faith made him right with God [it was credited/counted to him as righteousness].” And Abraham was called God’s friend.  
**2:24** So you see that people are ·made right with God [justified] by ·what they do [their works], not by faith only.  
**2:25** ·Another example is Rahab, the prostitute, who was ·made right with God [justified] by ·something she did [works]. She welcomed the ·spies [messengers; envoys] into her home and ·helped them escape [sent them out] by a different road [she is named in Jesus’ genealogy, and in as a hero of the faith].  
**2:26** Just as a person’s body that does not have a spirit [the force of life that animates the body] is dead, so faith ·that does nothing [without works] is dead!  

## 3장

**3:1** Controlling the Things We Say My ·brothers and sisters [fellow believers], not many of you should become teachers, because you know that we who teach will be judged more strictly.  
**3:2** We all ·make many mistakes [stumble in many ways]. If people ·never said anything wrong [did not stumble in speech/teaching/word], they would be perfect and able to ·control their entire selves, too [bridle their whole body also].  
**3:3** When we put bits into the mouths of horses to make them obey us, we ·can control [guide; direct] their whole bodies.  
**3:4** Also a ship is very big, and it is pushed by ·strong [fierce] winds. But a very small rudder ·controls [guides; steers] that big ship, making it go wherever the pilot wants.  
**3:5** It is the same with the tongue. It is a small ·part [member] of the body, but it ·brags [boasts] about great things. A big forest fire can be started with only a little flame.  
**3:6** And the tongue is like a fire. It is a whole world of ·evil [iniquity; unrighteousness] among ·the parts of our bodies [our members]. The tongue ·spreads its evil through [pollutes; stains; corrupts] the whole body. The tongue is set on fire by hell [Gehenna; a valley outside of Jerusalem where in the OT period children were sacrificed to a pagan god; later used as a burning trash heap; a metaphor for hell], and it starts a fire that influences ·all of life [the wheel of birth/life].  
**3:7** [For] People can tame every ·kind [species] of wild animal, bird, reptile, and ·fish [sea creature], and they have tamed them,  
**3:8** but no one can tame the tongue. It is ·wild and evil [or a restless evil; or an uncontrollable evil] and full of deadly poison.  
**3:9** ·We use our tongues to [With it we] ·praise [bless] our Lord and Father, but ·then [with it] we curse people, whom God made ·like himself [in his likeness].  
**3:10** Praises and curses come from the same mouth! My ·brothers and sisters [fellow believers], this should not happen.  
**3:11** Do ·good [fresh; sweet] and ·bad [stale; or salt; bitter] water flow from the same [opening of a] spring?  
**3:12** My ·brothers and sisters [fellow believers], can a fig tree make olives, or can a grapevine make figs? No! ·And a well full of salty water cannot give good water [Neither can salt water make sweet].  
**3:13** True Wisdom Are there those among you who are truly wise and ·understanding [insightful; discerning]? Then they should show it by ·living right [their good life/conduct/lifestyle] and ·doing good things [their deeds/works] with a ·gentleness [humility; meekness] that comes from wisdom.  
**3:14** But if you have bitter ·jealousy [or envy] and ·are selfish [have selfish ambition] in your hearts, do not ·brag [boast]. Your ·bragging [boasting] is a lie ·that hides [that denies; against] the truth [or Don’t cover up the truth with bragging or lying].  
**3:15** That kind of “wisdom” does not come ·from God [down from above; that is, from heaven] but ·from the world [is earthly]. It is ·not spiritual [natural; “soulish”; human life apart from God]; it is ·from the devil [demonic].  
**3:16** Where ·jealousy [or envy] and ·selfishness [selfish ambition] are, there will be ·confusion [chaos; disorder] and every ·kind of evil [evil thing/practice].  
**3:17** But the wisdom that comes from ·God [above] is first of all pure, then peaceful [peace-loving], gentle [patient; considerate], and ·easy to please [or willing to yield; or open to reason]. This wisdom is ·always ready to help those who are troubled and to do good for others [full of mercy and good fruits]. It is ·always fair [impartial] and ·honest [sincere; unhypocritical].  
**3:18** ·People who work for peace in a peaceful way plant a good crop of right living [or Peacemakers who sow with peace will harvest a crop of justice/righteousness].  

## 4장

**4:1** Give Yourselves to God Do you know where your ·fights [conflicts; wars] and ·arguments [disputes; quarrels; battles] come from? They come from ·the selfish desires [cravings] that war ·within you [in your members; probably parts of the body, but could be members of the church].  
**4:2** You ·want [desire] things, but you ·do not [or cannot] have them. So you ·are ready to kill [murder] and are ·jealous [envious] of other people, but you still cannot get what you want. So you argue and ·fight [wage war]. You do not get what you want, because you do not ask God.  
**4:3** Or when you ask, you do not receive because ·the reason you ask is wrong [or you ask with the wrong motives; you ask badly/wrongly]. You want things so you ·can use [spend] them for your own pleasures.  
**4:4** ·So, you are not loyal to God [You adulterers!; often adultery is used in a spiritual sense for betraying God]! ·You should know [Don’t you know…?] that ·loving [friendship with] the world is ·the same as hating [hostility/enmity toward] God. Anyone who wants to be a friend of the world becomes God’s enemy.  
**4:5** Do you think the Scripture ·means nothing that says [or says for no reason], ·“The Spirit that God made to live in us wants us for himself alone”? [or “God yearns jealously for the spirit he has made to live in us”?; or “The spirit God placed within us tends to jealously desire things”?; for God’s jealousy for his people see].  
**4:6** But God gives us even more grace, as ·the Scripture [it; or he] says, “God ·is against [opposes; resists] the proud, but he gives grace to the humble.”  
**4:7** So ·give yourselves completely [submit] to God. ·Stand against [Resist] the devil, and the devil will ·run [flee] from you.  
**4:8** Come near to God, and God will come near to you. You sinners, ·clean sin out of your lives [cleanse/purify your hands; a metaphor for cleaning up your behavior]. ·You who are trying to follow God and the world at the same time [You double-minded ones], ·make your thinking pure [purify your hearts; a metaphor for cleaning up your interior life].  
**4:9** ·Be sad [Lament], ·cry [mourn], and weep! Change your laughter into ·crying [mourning] and your joy into ·sadness [gloom; sorrow].  
**4:10** Humble yourself in the Lord’s presence, and he will ·honor you [exalt you; lift you up].  
**4:11** You Are Not the Judge Brothers and sisters [fellow believers], do not ·tell evil lies about [slander] each other. If you ·speak against [slander] your ·fellow believers [a brother or sister] or judge them, you are judging and ·speaking against [slandering] the law [because the law commanded love]. And when you are judging the law, you are no longer a ·follower [doer] of the law. You have become a judge [sitting in judgment over the law].  
**4:12** ·God is the only [There is one] ·Lawmaker [Lawgiver] and Judge. He is the only One who can save and destroy. ·So it is not right for you [So who are you…?] to judge your neighbor.  
**4:13** Let God Plan Your Life ·Some of you say [Come now, you who say], “Today or tomorrow we will go to some city. We will stay there a year, do business, and make money.”  
**4:14** But you do not know what will happen tomorrow! Your life is like a ·mist [vapor; puff of smoke]. ·You can see it [It appears] for a short time, but then it ·goes away [vanishes].  
**4:15** ·So [or Instead] you should say, “If the Lord ·wants [wills; wishes], we will live and do this or that.”  
**4:16** But now you are ·proud [arrogant; probably referring to their plans for the future] and you brag [boast]. All of this bragging [boasting] is wrong [evil; wicked].  
**4:17** [Therefore] Anyone who knows the right thing to do, but does not do it, ·is sinning [for him it is sin].  

## 5장

**5:1** A Warning to the Rich You rich people, ·listen [Come now]! Cry [Weep] and be very sad because of the ·troubles [miseries] that are coming to you.  
**5:2** Your riches have rotted, and your clothes have been eaten by moths.  
**5:3** Your gold and silver have ·rusted [corroded], and that ·rust [corrosion] will be a ·proof that you were wrong [witness/testimony/evidence against you]. It will eat your ·bodies [flesh] like fire. You ·saved [hoarded; stored up] your treasure ·for [or in] the last days [just before judgment day, when such treasures will be useless].  
**5:4** [Listen; Behold] The ·pay [wages] you ·did not give [defrauded from] the workers who mowed your fields cries out against you, and the cries of the ·workers [harvesters] have ·been heard by [reached the ears of] the Lord ·All-Powerful [of Hosts/Armies; Sabaoth; God’s warrior name referring to the angelic army].  
**5:5** Your life on earth was full of ·rich living [luxury] and ·pleasing yourselves with everything you wanted [pleasure; self-indulgence]. You made ·yourselves [your hearts] fat, ·like an animal ready to be killed [in/for a day of slaughter; a farm animal gorging itself, unaware it is being fattened for slaughter].  
**5:6** You have ·judged guilty [condemned] and then murdered innocent people, who ·were not against [did not resist/oppose] you.  
**5:7** Be Patient [Therefore] Brothers and sisters [fellow believers], be patient until the Lord comes again. [See; Behold,] A farmer patiently waits for his ·valuable [precious] ·crop [fruit] to grow from the earth and for it to receive the ·autumn and spring [early and late] rains.  
**5:8** You, too, must be patient. ·Do not give up hope [Strengthen your hearts], because ·the Lord is coming soon [the Lord’s coming draws near/is at hand].  
**5:9** Brothers and sisters [fellow believers], do not ·complain [grumble] against each other or you will be judged guilty. ·And [Look; Behold] the Judge is ·ready to come [standing at the door]!  
**5:10** Brothers and sisters [fellow believers], follow the example of the prophets who spoke ·for [in the name of] the Lord. They suffered many hard things, but they ·were patient [endured].  
**5:11** [Look; or Indeed] ·We say they are happy [or We consider them blessed] because they ·did not give up [endured; persevered]. You have heard about Job’s ·patience [or endurance; perseverance], and you know the Lord’s purpose for him in the end. You know the Lord is full of mercy and is ·kind [compassionate].  
**5:12** Be Careful What You Say My brothers and sisters [fellow believers], above all, do not ·use an oath when you make a promise [swear; take a vow]. Don’t use the name of heaven, earth, or anything else to prove what you say. ·When you mean yes, say only yes, and when you mean no, say only no [Let your “Yes” be yes and your “No” be no] so you will not be ·judged guilty [condemned].  
**5:13** The Power of Prayer Anyone who is ·having troubles [suffering] should pray. Anyone who is ·happy [joyful] should ·sing praises [sing psalms].  
**5:14** Anyone who is sick should call the church’s elders. They should pray for and ·pour oil on the person [anoint that person with olive oil; anointing probably indicates dedicating or setting aside the person to God’s care] in the name of the Lord.  
**5:15** And the prayer that is said with faith will ·make the sick person well [save the sick; the same Greek word is commonly used for both physical healing and spiritual salvation]; the Lord will ·heal [raise up] that person. And if the person has sinned, the sins will be forgiven.  
**5:16** [Therefore,] Confess your sins to each other and pray for each other so God can heal you. ·When a believing person prays, great things happen [The prayer of a righteous person is powerful and effective].  
**5:17** Elijah was a human being just like us. He prayed [earnestly; with prayer] that it would not rain, and it did not rain on the land for three and a half years!  
**5:18** Then Elijah prayed again, and ·the rain came down from the sky [or heaven gave rain], and the land produced crops again.  
**5:19** Saving a Soul My brothers and sisters [fellow believers], if one of you ·wanders away [strays] from the truth, and someone helps that person come back,  
**5:20** ·remember [know] this: Anyone who brings a sinner back from the ·wrong way [error/wandering of his ways] will save that sinner’s soul from death and will ·cause many sins to be forgiven [cover a multitude of sins].
