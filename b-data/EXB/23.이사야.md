**66:3** But those people who kill bulls as a sacrifice to me are like those who kill people. Those who kill sheep as a sacrifice are like those who break the necks of dogs. Those who give me ·grain [gift; tribute] offerings are like those who offer me the blood of pigs [dogs and pigs were ritually unclean animals and were forbidden as sacrifices].
