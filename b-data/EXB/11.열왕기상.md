## 1장

**1:3** After searching everywhere in Israel for a beautiful young woman, they found a girl named Abishag from Shunam and brought her to the king.  
**1:4** The girl was very beautiful, and she cared for the king and served him. But the king did not have sexual relations with her.  
**1:5** Adonijah was the son of King David and Haggith, and he ·was very proud [began boasting; exalted himself]. “I will be the king,” he said. So he got chariots and ·horses [or horsemen; charioteers] for himself and fifty men ·for his personal bodyguard [to run ahead of him].  
**1:6** Now David had never ·interfered with [disciplined; crossed; rebuked] Adonijah by ·questioning what he did [asking, “Why are you doing that?”]. Born next after Absalom, Adonijah was a very handsome man.  
**1:7** Adonijah ·spoke [conferred] with Joab son of Zeruiah and Abiathar the priest, and they agreed to ·help [support] him.  
**1:8** But Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei, and King David’s ·special guard [mighty men] did not join Adonijah.  
**1:9** Then Adonijah killed some sheep, ·cows [oxen], and fat calves for sacrifices at the Stone of Zoheleth near the spring of Rogel. He invited all his brothers, the other sons of King David, to come, as well as all the men of Judah.  
**1:10** But Adonijah did not invite Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, ·his father’s special guard [the mighty men], or his brother Solomon.

**1:14** While you are still talking to the king, I will come in and ·tell him that what you have said about Adonijah is true [confirm your words].”  
**1:15** So Bathsheba went in to see the aged king in his bedroom, where Abishag, the girl from Shunam, was caring for him.

**1:19** He has killed many ·cows [oxen], fat calves, and sheep for sacrifices. And he has invited all your sons, as well as Abiathar the priest and Joab the commander of the army, but he did not invite Solomon, who serves you.  
**1:20** My master and king, ·all the Israelites are watching you [the eyes of Israel are on you], waiting for you to decide who will ·be [sit on the throne of my lord the] king after you.  
**1:21** Otherwise, as soon as ·you die [my lord the king sleeps with his fathers/ancestors], Solomon and I will be treated as ·criminals [offenders].”  
**1:22** While Bathsheba was still talking with the king, Nathan the prophet arrived.  
**1:23** The servants told the king, “Nathan the prophet is here.” So Nathan went to the king and ·bowed facedown [prostrated himself] on the ground before him.  
**1:24** Nathan said, “My ·master and [lord the] king, have you ·said [decreed] that Adonijah will be the king after you and that he will ·rule [sit] on your throne?

**1:29** Then the king ·made this promise [vowed; swore this oath], “The Lord has ·saved [rescued; delivered] me from all ·trouble [adversity; danger; distress]. As surely as he lives,

**1:49** Then all of Adonijah’s guests were ·afraid [terrified; panicked], and they left quickly and scattered.

**1:51** Then someone told Solomon, “Adonijah is afraid of you, so he is at the altar, holding on to its ·corners [horns]. He says, ‘Tell King Solomon to promise me today that he will not ·kill me [put his servant to death with the sword].’”
