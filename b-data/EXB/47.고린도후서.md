## 1장

**1:1** From Paul, an ·apostle [messenger] of Christ Jesus. ·I am an apostle because that is what God wanted […by the will of God]. Also from Timothy [1–2 Timothy] our ·brother in Christ [brother]. To the church of God in Corinth, and to all ·of God’s people [the saints] everywhere in Achaia [the Roman province where the city of Corinth was located, present day southern Greece]:  
**1:2** Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:3** Paul Gives Thanks to God ·Praise be to [Blessed is/be] the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father who is full of ·mercy [compassion] and all ·comfort [encouragement].  
**1:4** He ·comforts [encourages] us ·every time we have [in all our] ·troubles [trials; tribulation], so when others have ·trouble [any trials/tribulation], we can ·comfort [encourage] them with the same ·comfort [encouragement] God gives us.  
**1:5** [For just as] ·We share in the many sufferings of Christ [Christ’s sufferings abound in us]. In the same way, ·much comfort comes to us [our comfort abounds] through Christ.  
**1:6** If we have ·troubles [trials; tribulation], it is for your ·comfort [encouragement] and salvation, and if we ·have comfort [are encouraged], ·you also have comfort [or it is for your comfort/encouragement]. This helps you to accept patiently the same sufferings we have.  
**1:7** Our hope for you is ·strong [unshaken; firm], knowing that as you share in our sufferings, you will also share in the ·comfort [encouragement] we receive.  
**1:8** Brothers and sisters, we want you to know about the ·trouble [trial; tribulation] we suffered in Asia [a Roman province in present-day western Turkey]. We had great burdens there that were beyond our own strength, so that we even ·gave up hope of living [despaired of life].  
**1:9** Truly, in our own hearts we believed we ·would die [had been sentenced to death]. But this happened so we would not trust in ourselves but in God, who raises people from the dead.  
**1:10** God ·saved [rescued; delivered] us from these great dangers of death, and he will continue to ·save [rescue; deliver] us. We have put our hope in him, and he will ·save [rescue; deliver] us again.  
**1:11** ·And you can [or …as you] help us with your prayers. Then many people will give thanks for us—·that God blessed [for the gift/favor given to] us because of their many prayers.  
**1:12** The Change in Paul’s Plans This is ·what we are proud of [our boast], ·and I can say it with a clear conscience [the testimony/witness of our conscience]: In everything we have done in the world, and especially with you, we have had an ·honest [or generous] and sincere heart from God. We did this by God’s grace, not by ·the kind of wisdom the world has [worldly/fleshly wisdom].  
**1:13** We write to you ·only what you can read and understand [or in a clear and straightforward manner]. And I hope that as you have understood ·some things [part of the situation; in part] about us, you may come to ·know everything [understand fully] about us [some of Paul’s previous contacts and correspondence had produced misunderstanding or conflict]. Then you can ·be proud [boast] of us, as we will ·be proud [boast] of you on the day ·our Lord Jesus Christ comes again [of our Lord Jesus Christ; judgment day].  
**1:14** (Included in v. 13.)  
**1:15** I was so sure of all this that I made plans to visit you first so you could ·be blessed twice [have a second grace].  
**1:16** I planned to visit you on my way to Macedonia [the northern part of present-day Greece] and again on my way back [the visit anticipated in]. I wanted to ·get help from you for my trip [be sent by you] to Judea.  
**1:17** [So; Therefore] Do you think that I made these plans ·without really meaning it [lightly; with vacillation]? Or maybe you think I make plans ·as the world does [or using only human standards/reason; according to the flesh], so that I say yes, yes and at the same time no, no.  
**1:18** But ·since you can believe God [God is trustworthy/faithful], you can believe that what we tell you is never both yes and no.  
**1:19** [For] The Son of God, Jesus Christ, that Silas and Timothy and I preached to you, was not yes and no. [But; Rather] In ·Christ [him] it has always been yes.  
**1:20** The yes to all of God’s promises is in ·Christ [him], and through Christ we say ·yes [amen; from a Hebrew term meaning “yes,” or “it is true”] to the glory of God.  
**1:21** Remember, God is the One who makes you and us ·strong [stand firm; established] in Christ. God ·made us his chosen people [or commissioned us; anointed us].  
**1:22** He put his ·mark on us to show that we are his [seal on us; of ownership], and he put his Spirit in our hearts ·to be a guarantee for all he has promised [as a pledge/deposit/downpayment].  
**1:23** Now I ·ask [call on; appeal to] God to be my witness ·that this is true [or staking my life on it; against my soul/life; a very serious vow or oath]: The reason I did not come back to Corinth was to ·keep you from being punished or hurt [spare you].  
**1:24** We are not trying to ·control [rule; lord it over] your faith. You ·are strong [stand firm; are well established] in faith. But we are workers with you for your own joy.  

## 2장

**2:1** So I decided that my next visit to you would not be another ·one to make you sad [painful/sorrowful one; Paul’s first visit had resulted in conflict, rejection, and hurt feelings].  
**2:2** If I ·make you sad [cause you pain/sorrow], who will make me glad? Only you can make me glad—·particularly the person [or those] whom I made sad [either the church generally, or a particular opponent].  
**2:3** I wrote you a letter for this reason: that when I came to you I would not be made ·sad [sorrowful] by the people who should ·make me happy [bring me joy; Paul wrote a severe letter (now lost) after his painful visit to call the church to repentance]. I felt sure of all of you, that you would share my joy.  
**2:4** When I wrote to you before, I was very ·troubled [distressed] and ·unhappy [anguished] in my heart, and I wrote with many tears. I did not write to make you ·sad [sorrowful], but to let you know how much I love you.  
**2:5** Forgive the Sinner ·Someone [If someone…; evidently the ringleader who opposed Paul on his previous visit] there among you has caused sadness, ·not […it is not] to me, but to all of you. **2:6** The punishment that ·most of you [the majority] gave him is enough for him [the church as a whole has now sided with Paul and disciplined this individual].  
**2:7** But now you should forgive him and ·comfort [encourage] him to keep him from ·having too much sadness and giving up completely [being overwhelmed/swallowed up by excessive sorrow/grief].  
**2:8** So I ·beg [urge; encourage] you to ·show [reaffirm] that you love him.  
**2:9** I wrote you to test you and to see if you obey in everything [Paul’s “severe” letter  evidently called the church to submit again to his authority].  
**2:10** If you forgive someone, I also forgive him. And what I have forgiven—if I had anything to forgive—I forgave it for you, ·as if Christ were with me [or in the presence of Christ].  
**2:11** I did this so that Satan would not ·win anything from [outwit; take advantage of] us, because we ·know very well [are not ignorant of] what Satan’s ·plans [schemes; intentions] are.  
**2:12** Paul’s Concern in Troas When I came to Troas [a city in northwest Asia Minor] to preach the Good News of Christ, ·the Lord gave me a good opportunity there [a door opened for me by/in the Lord].  
**2:13** But I had no ·peace [rest in my spirit], because I did not find my brother Titus. So I said good-bye to them at Troas and went to Macedonia. [Paul evidently sent his severe letter  with Titus, and now awaited the church’s response. Starting in he digresses into a long expression of joy because of their favorable reaction. **2:14** Victory Through Christ But thanks be to God, who always leads us as captives in Christ’s victory ·parade [procession; the image is of a victorious Roman general leading his army and his captives through the streets]. God uses us to spread ·his knowledge everywhere like a sweet-smelling perfume [the aroma/fragrance of the knowledge of him; incense or spices were burned during such victory parades].  
**2:15** ·Our offering to God is this: [or For God’s sake; or To God] We are the ·sweet smell [aroma; fragrance] of Christ among those who are being saved and among those who are ·being lost [perishing; headed for destruction].  
**2:16** To those who are ·lost [perishing; headed for destruction], we are the ·smell [aroma] of death that brings death, but to those who are being saved, we are the ·smell [aroma] of life that brings life. So who is ·able [qualified; adequate] to do this work?  
**2:17** We do not ·sell [peddle] the word of God for a profit as many other people do. But in Christ we speak the truth ·before [in the presence of] God, as ·messengers of [envoys of; from] God.  

## 3장

**3:1** Servants of the New Agreement Are we starting to ·brag about [praise; commend] ourselves again? Do we need letters of ·introduction [recommendation] to you or from you, like some other people [Paul’s opponents evidently carried letters of reference (perhaps from Jerusalem)]?  
**3:2** You yourselves are our letter, written on our hearts, known and read by everyone [the changed lives of the Corinthians prove Paul’s credibility as an apostle of Jesus Christ].  
**3:3** You show that you are a letter from Christ ·sent through us [delivered by us; or produced by us; the result of our ministry]. This letter is not written with ink but ·with [by] the Spirit of the living God. It is not written on stone tablets [the law of Moses was written on stone tablets] but on human hearts [hearts of flesh].  
**3:4** We can say this, because through Christ we ·feel certain before God [have confidence in God’s presence; or can trust in God].  
**3:5** We are not saying that we ·can do this work ourselves [are able/competent/adequate to consider anything as from ourselves]. ·It is God who makes us able to do all that we do [But our ability/competence/adequacy is from God].  
**3:6** He made us ·able [adequate; competent] to be servants of a new ·agreement from himself to his people [covenant]. This new ·agreement [covenant] is not ·a written law [of the letter], but it is of the Spirit. The ·written law [letter] brings death, but the Spirit gives life.  
**3:7** The ·law [or old system; ministry] that brought death was ·written in words [engraved with letters] on stone. It came with ·God’s glory [glory], which made Moses’ face so ·bright [glorious] that the ·Israelites [children of Israel] could not ·continue to look [gaze] at it. But that glory ·later disappeared [was fading; or was made ineffective (by the veil on his face)].  
**3:8** So ·surely [will not…?] the ·new way that brings [ministry of] the Spirit has even more glory.  
**3:9** [For] If the ·law that judged people guilty of sin [ministry of condemnation] had glory, surely the ·new way that makes people right with God [ministry of righteousness] has much greater glory.  
**3:10** For that which had glory [the law] ·really loses its glory [has no glory] when it is compared to the much greater glory [the new way through the Spirit].  
**3:11** [For] If that which ·disappeared [faded; was made ineffective; the law] came with glory, then that which ·continues forever [remains; abides; the new way through the Spirit] has much greater glory.  
**3:12** We have this hope, so we ·are very bold [or speak with boldness/confidence].  
**3:13** We are not like Moses, who put a ·covering [veil] over his face so the ·Israelites [children of Israel] would not see it. The glory was ·disappearing [fading; or made ineffective], and Moses did not want them to see it end.  
**3:14** But their minds were ·closed [stubborn; hardened], and even today that same ·covering [veil] ·hides the meaning [remains] when they read the old ·agreement [covenant]. That covering is ·taken away [not lifted, because it is removed] only through Christ.  
**3:15** Even today, when they read ·the law of Moses [Moses], there is a ·covering over [veil laying upon] their ·minds [hearts].  
**3:16** But when a person ·changes and follows [turns to] the Lord, that ·covering [veil] is taken away.  
**3:17** The Lord is the Spirit, and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom.  
**3:18** ·Our faces, then, are not covered [With an unveiled face…]. We all ·show [reflect; or behold; or contemplate] the Lord’s glory, and we are being ·changed [transformed] ·to be like him [into the same image]. This change in us ·brings ever greater glory [or is from one degree of glory to another; is from glory to glory], which comes from the Lord, who is the Spirit.  

## 4장

**4:1** Preaching the Good News [Therefore,] Since God in his mercy gave us this ·work to do [ministry], we don’t ·give up [lose heart; become discouraged].  
**4:2** But we have ·turned away from [rejected; renounced] ·secret [underhanded] and shameful ways. We ·use no trickery [do not walk in deception], and we do not ·change [distort] the ·teaching [word] of God. We ·teach the truth plainly [fully/openly disclose the truth], ·showing everyone who we are so that they can know in their hearts what kind of people we are [commending ourselves to every person’s conscience] in God’s sight.  
**4:3** If the ·Good News [Gospel] that we preach is ·hidden [veiled], it is ·hidden [veiled] only to those who are ·lost [perishing].  
**4:4** The ·devil who rules this world [god of this age] has blinded the minds of ·those who do not believe [unbelievers]. […so that] They cannot see the light of the Good News—the Good News ·about [that reveals] the glory of Christ, who is ·exactly like [the image of] God.  
**4:5** [For] We do not preach about ourselves, but we preach that Jesus Christ is Lord and that we are your ·servants [slaves; bondservants] for Jesus.  
**4:6** [For; Because] God who said, “Let the light shine out of the darkness,” is the same God who made his light shine in our hearts ·by letting us know [or to give us the knowledge of] the glory of God that is in the face of Christ.  
**4:7** Spiritual Treasure in Clay Jars [But] We have this treasure ·from God, but we are like clay jars that hold the treasure [in clay jars]. This shows that the ·great [extraordinary; transcendent] power is from God, not from us.  
**4:8** We have ·troubles all around us [or all kinds of troubles/trials], but we are not ·defeated [crushed]. We ·do not know what to do [are perplexed/bewildered], but we do not ·give up the hope of living [despair].  
**4:9** We are ·persecuted [pursued], but ·God does not leave us [not abandoned/left behind]. We are ·hurt [struck down; knocked over] sometimes, but we are not destroyed.  
**4:10** We always carry the death of Jesus in our own bodies [Paul was in constant danger of the kind of violent death Jesus experienced] so that the life of Jesus [resurrection life] can also be ·seen [revealed; manifested] in our bodies.  
**4:11** [For] We are alive, but for Jesus we are always ·in danger of [being handed over to] death so that the life of Jesus can be ·seen [revealed; manifested] in our ·bodies that die [mortal flesh].  
**4:12** So death is working in us, but life is working in you.  
**4:13** It is written in the Scriptures, “I believed, so I spoke.” ·Our faith is like this, too [Having the same spirit of faith,…]. We also believe, and so we speak.  
**4:14** We know that ·God [the one] who raised the Lord Jesus from the dead will also raise us with Jesus and will ·bring us together with you into his presence [present (us) with you].  
**4:15** All these things are for ·you [your benefit], so that the grace of God that is ·being given [expanding; increasing] to more and more people will bring increasing thanks to God for his glory.  
**4:16** Living by Faith So we do not ·give up [despair; lose heart]. Though our ·physical body [outer person] is ·becoming older and weaker [decaying; being destroyed], our ·spirit inside us [inner (person)] is ·made new [being renewed] every day.  
**4:17** ·We have small troubles for a while now, but they [For our brief and insignificant trials/tribulations] are ·helping us gain [or producing in us] an eternal ·glory [burden/weight of glory] that ·is much greater than [overwhelmingly exceeds] the troubles.  
**4:18** We set our eyes not on what we see but on what we cannot see. [For; Because] What we see ·will last only a short time [is temporary/transitory], but what we cannot see ·will last forever [is eternal].  

## 5장

**5:1** [For] We know that ·when [if] our ·body [earthly house]—the tent we live in here on earth—is destroyed, ·God will have a house for us [we have a building from God]. It will not be made by human hands, but will be an eternal home ·in heaven [or in the heavens].  
**5:2** But now we ·groan [sigh] in this ·tent [or body; one], longing to be clothed in our heavenly ·home [dwelling place],  
**5:3** because it will clothe us so we will not be naked.  
**5:4** While we live in this ·body [tent], we ·have burdens [are weighed down], and we ·groan [sigh]. We do not want to be ·naked [stripped; unclothed], but we want to be clothed with our heavenly home. Then ·this body that dies [the mortal] will be ·fully covered with [swallowed up by] life.  
**5:5** This is what God ·made [designed; prepared] us for, and he has given us the Spirit to be a ·guarantee for this new life [deposit; down payment].  
**5:6** So we always have courage. We know that while we ·live [are at home] in this body, we are ·away [absent; or exiles] from the Lord.  
**5:7** We ·live [walk] by ·what we believe [faith], not by ·what we can see [sight].  
**5:8** So I say that we ·have courage [or are confident]. We really ·want [would prefer] to be ·away [absent; or exiled] from this body and be at home with the Lord.  
**5:9** Our only ·goal [aim; ambition] is to please ·God [him] whether we ·live here [are at home] or ·there [are absent/exiled],  
**5:10** because we must all stand before ·Christ to be judged [the Bema/judgment seat of Christ; the Bema was a raised platform from which civic leaders made pronouncements and rendered judgment]. […so that] Each of us will receive what we should get—good or bad—for the things we did in the earthly body.  
**5:11** Becoming Friends with God [Therefore] Since we know what it means to fear the Lord, we try to persuade people [either about the truth of the Gospel or about Paul’s good motives]. God knows what we really are, and I hope that in your ·hearts [consciences] you know, too.  
**5:12** We are not trying to ·prove [commend] ourselves to you again, but we are giving you a ·reason [opportunity] to ·be proud of [boast about] us. **5:13** If we are out of our minds, it is for God. If we have our right minds, it is for you.  
**5:14** [For] The love of Christ ·controls [compels; drives] us, because we ·know [are convinced; have concluded] that One died for all, so all have died [we died spiritually with Christ, the penalty for our sins].  
**5:15** Christ died for all so that those who live would ·not continue to [no longer] live for themselves, but for him who died for them and was raised from the dead.  
**5:16** [So; As a result] From ·this time [now] on we do not think of anyone ·as the world does [or from a merely human perspective; according to the flesh]. [Although] In the past we thought of Christ ·as the world thinks [or as nothing more than a man; according to the flesh], but we no longer think of him in that way.  
**5:17** If anyone belongs to Christ, ·there is a new creation [the new creation has arrived; or that person has become a new creation]. The old things have gone; [look; behold] ·everything is made new [the new has come]!  
**5:18** All this is from God, who through Christ ·made peace between us and [reconciled us to] himself, and gave us the ·work of telling everyone about the peace we can have with him [ministry/service of reconciliation].  
**5:19** [For] God was in Christ, ·making peace between the world and [reconciling the world to] himself. In Christ, God did not ·hold the world guilty of its sins [count their trespasses against them]. And he ·gave [committed/entrusted to] us this message of ·peace [reconciliation].  
**5:20** So we ·have been sent to speak [are ambassadors] for Christ. It is as if God is ·calling to [urging; exhorting; encouraging] you through us. We speak for Christ when we ·beg [implore; urge] you to be ·at peace with [reconciled to] God.  
**5:21** God made ·Christ [the one] who ·had no sin [or never sinned; did not know sin] to become sin for us, so that in ·Christ [him] we could become ·right with [the righteousness of] God.

## 7장
