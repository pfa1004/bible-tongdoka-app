**1:8** If we say we ·have no sin [or have no sin nature; or are not guilty of sinning], we are ·fooling [deceiving] ourselves, and the truth is not in us.

**2:13** I write to you, fathers [probably senior members of the congregation], ·because [or that] you know the One who existed from the beginning [Christ]. I write to you, young people, ·because [or that] you have ·defeated [conquered; overcome] the Evil One.  
**2:14** I ·write [or have written] to you, children, ·because [or that] you ·know [have come to know] the Father. I ·write [or have written] to you, young people, ·because [or that] you are strong; the ·teaching [word] of God ·lives [abides; remains] in you, and you have ·defeated [conquered; overcome] the Evil One.

**2:18** Reject the Enemies of Christ My dear children, ·these are the last days [it is the last hour; suggesting urgency, though not claiming the end was near]. [Just as] You have heard that the ·enemy of Christ [antichrist] is coming, and now many ·enemies of Christ [antichrists; false teachers] are already here. **2:20** You have the ·gift [anointing; probably the Holy Spirit and/or the spiritual gift of the Word of God applied to their hearts;] that the Holy One gave you [from the Holy One; a reference to God the Father or more likely Jesus Christ], so you all ·know the truth [or have knowledge; know].  
**2:21** I do not write to you because you do not know the truth but because you do know the truth. And you know that no lie comes from the truth.  
**2:22** Who is the liar? It is the person who ·does not accept [denies; repudiates] Jesus as the ·Christ [Messiah; Anointed One]. This is the ·enemy of Christ [antichrist]: the person who ·does not accept [denies; repudiates] the Father and ·his [the] Son.  
**2:23** Whoever ·does not accept [denies; repudiates] the Son does not have the Father. But whoever ·confesses [acknowledges; accepts] the Son has the Father, too.  
**2:24** [As for you] Be sure you ·continue to follow [abide/remain in] ·the teaching [what] you heard from the beginning [when they first became Christians;]. If you ·continue to follow [abide/remain in] what you heard from the beginning, you will [also; indeed] ·stay [abide; remain] in the Son and in the Father.  
**2:25** And this is ·what the Son [the promise which he himself] promised to us—·life forever [eternal life].  
**2:26** I ·am writing [or have written] ·this letter [these things] about those people who are ·trying to lead you the wrong way [deceiving you; leading you astray].  
**2:27** [As for you] ·Christ gave you a special gift that is still in you [The anointing that you received from him abides/remains in you], so you do not need ·any other teacher [anyone to teach you]. [But just as] His ·gift [anointing] teaches you about everything, and it is true, not false. So ·continue to live in Christ, as his gift taught you [just as he taught you, abide/remain in him].  
**2:28** ·Yes [Now], my dear children, ·live [abide; remain] in him so that when ·Christ comes back [he appears/is revealed], we can be ·without fear [confident] and not be ashamed [of him] ·in his presence [at his coming].  
**2:29** ·Since [If] you know that ·Christ [he] is righteous, you know that all who ·do right [practice righteousness] ·are God’s children [have been born of/begotten by him].

**3:4** ·The person [Everyone] who ·sins [commits sin] ·breaks God’s law [commits lawlessness/iniquity; referring to the false teachers]. Yes, sin is ·living against God’s law [lawlessness; iniquity].  
**3:5** You know that ·Christ came [he has appeared/was revealed] to take away sins and that there is no sin in ·Christ [him].  
**3:6** So anyone who ·lives [abides; remains] in ·Christ [him] does not ·go on sinning [or sin; the Christian ideal, an implicit call to avoid sin]. Anyone who ·goes on sinning [or sins] has ·never really understood Christ and has never known him [neither seen him nor known him].  
**3:7** Dear children, do not let anyone ·lead you the wrong way [deceive you]. The one who does what is right is righteous, just as ·Christ [he] is righteous.  
**3:8** Anyone who ·continues to sin [or sins] belongs to the devil, because the devil has been sinning since the beginning. The Son of God ·came [was revealed; appeared] for this purpose: to destroy the devil’s work.  
**3:9** ·Those [All] who are ·God’s children [born of/begotten by God] do not ·continue sinning [or sin], because ·the new life from God [or God’s message; or God’s Spirit; his seed/sperm] ·remains [abides] in them. **3:12** Do not be like Cain who belonged to the Evil One and ·killed [murdered] his brother. And why did he ·kill [murder] him? Because the things Cain did were evil, and the things his brother did were ·good [righteous; just].

**3:16** This is how we know what real love is: ·Jesus [he] ·gave [laid down] his life for us. So we should ·give [lay down] our lives for our brothers and sisters [fellow believers].  
**3:17** Suppose someone has ·enough to live [the world’s possessions/goods] and sees a brother or sister [fellow believer] in need, but ·does not help [closes off his heart/compassion from him]. ·Then God’s love is not living in that person [How does God’s love abide/remain in him?].  
**3:18** My children, we should love people not only ·with words and talk [in word and tongue], but ·by our actions and true caring [or by showing true love through our actions; in deed and truth].  
**3:19** ·This is the way [By this] we ·know [will know; perhaps in a future moment of crisis] that we belong to the ·way of truth [truth]. ·When [or If] our hearts [our conscience] ·make us feel guilty [condemn/convict us], ·we can still have peace before God [our hearts can be reassured before him]. **4:13** [By this] We know that we ·live [abide; remain] in God and he ·lives [abides; remains] in us, because he gave us [of; from] his Spirit [we share in his Spirit].  
**4:14** We have seen and can ·testify [witness; proclaim] that the Father sent his Son ·to be [or as] the Savior of the world.  
**4:15** Whoever ·confesses [acknowledges] that Jesus is the Son of God has God ·living [abiding; remaining] inside, and that person ·lives [abides; remains] in God.  
**4:16** And so we ·know [have come to know] the love that God has for us, and we ·trust [believe; rely on] that love. God is love. Those who ·live [abide; remain] in love ·live [abide; remain] in God, and God ·lives [abides; remains] in them.  
**4:17** This is how love ·is made perfect [is made complete; comes to full expression] ·in [or among] us: that we can ·be without fear [have boldness; have confidence] on the day of judgment, because in this world we are like him [probably referring to Christ, our example of love].

**4:20** If people say, “I love God,” but hate their brothers or sisters [fellow believers], they are liars. [For] Those who do not love their brothers and sisters [fellow believers], whom they have seen, cannot love God, whom they have never seen.  
**4:21** And ·God gave us this command [we have this command from him]: Those who love God must also love their brothers and sisters [fellow believers].
