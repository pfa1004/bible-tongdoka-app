## 1장

**1:1** The Lord Calls His People Back In the eighth month [October/November on our calendar; the Hebrew month of Cheshvan] of the second year Darius was king [520 bc; Darius was king of Persia 522–486 bc], the ·Lord spoke his word [word of the Lord came] to the prophet Zechariah son of Berekiah, who was the son of Iddo [a priestly family]. The Lord said,  
**1:2** “The Lord was very angry with your ·ancestors [fathers].  
**1:3** So tell the people: This is what the Lord ·All-Powerful [Almighty; of Heaven’s Armies; of hosts] says: ‘Return to me, and I will return to you,’ says the Lord ·All-Powerful [Almighty; of Heaven’s Armies; of hosts].  
**1:4** Don’t be like your ·ancestors [fathers]. ·In the past the [The former/earlier] prophets said to them: This is what the Lord ·All-Powerful [Almighty; of Heaven’s Armies; of hosts] says: ‘·Stop [Turn from] your evil ways and evil actions.’ But they wouldn’t listen or pay attention to me, says the Lord.  
**1:5** Your ·ancestors [fathers] ·are dead [where are they…?], and those prophets ·didn’t [do they…?] live forever.  
**1:6** I commanded my words and ·laws [statutes] to my servants the prophets, and ·they preached to [or they came true to; did they not overtake…?] your ·ancestors [fathers], who returned to me. They said, ‘The Lord ·All-Powerful [Almighty; of Heaven’s Armies; of hosts] did as he ·said he would [determined to do]. He punished us for ·the way we lived and for what we did [our ways and deeds].’”  
**1:7** The Vision of the Horses It was on the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month [February 15, 519 bc], which is the month of Shebat, in Darius’s second year as king. The ·Lord spoke his word [word of the Lord came] to the prophet Zechariah son of Berekiah, who was the son of Iddo.  
**1:8** During the night I had a vision. I saw a man ·riding [or sitting on] a red horse. He was standing among some myrtle trees in a ravine, with red, brown, and white horses behind him.  
**1:9** I asked, “What are these, ·sir [or my lord]?” The ·angel [messenger] who was talking with me answered, “I’ll show you what they are.”  
**1:10** Then the man standing among the myrtle trees explained, “They are the ones the Lord ·sent [sent to walk/patrol] through all the earth.”  
**1:11** Then they spoke to the ·Lord’s angel [or angel of the Lord; an OT figure closely identified with the Lord himself], who was standing among the myrtle trees. They said, “We have ·gone [walked; patrolled] through all the earth, and [look; behold] everything is calm and quiet.”  
**1:12** Then the ·Lord’s angel [angel of the Lord] asked, “Lord ·All-Powerful [Almighty; of Heaven’s Armies; of hosts], how long will ·it be before you show [you withhold] mercy to Jerusalem and the cities of Judah? You have been angry with them for seventy years now.”  
**1:13** So the Lord answered the ·angel [messenger] who was talking with me, and his words were comforting and good.  
**1:14** Then the ·angel [messenger] who was talking to me said to me, “Announce this: This is what the Lord ·All-Powerful [Almighty; of Heaven’s Armies; of hosts] says: ‘I ·have a strong love [am very jealous/zealous] for Jerusalem.  
**1:15** And I am very angry with the nations that feel so ·safe [secure; at ease]. I was only a little angry at them, but they ·made things worse [added to/increased the calamity/disaster].’  
**1:16** “So this is what the Lord says: ‘I will return to Jerusalem with mercy. My ·Temple [house] will be rebuilt,’ says the Lord ·All-Powerful [Almighty; of Heaven’s Armies; of hosts], ‘and the measuring line will be ·used to rebuild [stretched out over] Jerusalem.’  
**1:17** “Also announce: This is what the Lord ·All-Powerful [Almighty; of Heaven’s Armies; of hosts] says: ‘My towns will ·be rich again [overflow with prosperity]. The Lord will comfort Jerusalem again, and I will again choose Jerusalem.’”  
**1:18** The Vision of the Horns Then I looked up and saw four ·animal horns [horns; animal horns symbolized strength].  
**1:19** I asked the ·angel [messenger] who was talking with me, “What are these?” He said, “These are the horns that scattered the people of Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”  
**1:20** Then the Lord showed me four craftsmen.  
**1:21** I asked, “What are they coming to do?” He answered, “They have come to ·scare [terrify] and throw down the horns. These horns scattered the people of Judah so that no one could even lift up his head. These horns stand for the nations that ·attacked [lifted up their horns against] the people of Judah and scattered them.”  

## 2장

**2:2** I asked him, “Where are you going?” He said to me, “I am going to measure Jerusalem, to see how wide and how long it is.”  
**2:3** Then the ·angel [messenger] who was talking with me left, and another ·angel [messenger] came out to meet him.  
**2:4** The second ·angel [messenger] said to him, “Run and tell that young man, ‘Jerusalem will become a city without walls, because there will be so many people and ·cattle [livestock] in it.

**2:6** “·Oh no! Oh no [or Come! Come; or Up! Up]! Run away from ·Babylon [the land of the north], because I have scattered you like the four winds of heaven,” says the Lord.  
**2:7** “·Oh no [or Come; or Up], ·Jerusalem [Zion; the location of the Temple]! Escape, you who live in ·Babylon [Daughter Babylon].”  
**2:8** This is what the Lord ·All-Powerful [Almighty; of Heaven’s Armies; of hosts] says: “After ·he has honored me and [or the Glorious One; or for his own glory he] sent me against the nations who ·took your possessions [plundered you]—because whoever touches you ·hurts [touches] ·what is precious to me [the apple of his eye; the pupil/little man of his eye]—

**2:11** “At that time people from many nations will join with the Lord and will become my people. Then I will live among you, and you will know that the Lord ·All-Powerful [Almighty; of Heaven’s Armies; of hosts] has sent me to you.
