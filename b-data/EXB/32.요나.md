## 1장

**1:1** God Calls and Jonah Runs The ·Lord spoke his word [word of the Lord came] to Jonah son of Amittai:  
**1:2** “Get up, go to the great city of Nineveh [the capital of Assyria], and ·preach [cry out] against it, because ·I see the evil things they do [its wickedness has come to my attention/up before me].”  
**1:3** But Jonah got up to run away from the Lord by going to Tarshish [probably Tartessos in southwest Spain, the opposite direction from Nineveh]. He went to the city of Joppa, where he found a ship that was going to the city of Tarshish. Jonah paid for the trip and went aboard, planning to go to Tarshish to run away from the Lord.  
**1:4** But the Lord ·sent [hurled] a great wind on the sea, which made the sea so stormy that the ship was in danger of breaking apart.  
**1:5** The sailors were afraid, and each man cried to his own god. They began throwing the cargo from the ship into the sea to make the ship lighter. But Jonah had gone down far inside the ship to lie down, and he fell fast asleep.  
**1:6** The captain of the ship came and said, “Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray to your god! Maybe your god will ·pay attention to [take notice of] us, and we won’t die!”  
**1:7** Then the men said to each other, “Let’s throw lots to see who caused these troubles to happen to us.” When they threw lots, the lot ·showed that the trouble had happened because of [singled out; fell upon] Jonah.  
**1:8** Then they said to him, “Tell us, who caused our trouble? What is your job? Where do you come from? What is your country? Who are your people?”  
**1:9** Then Jonah said to them, “I am a Hebrew. I ·fear [worship] the Lord, the God of heaven, who made the sea and the land.”  
**1:10** The men were very afraid, and they asked Jonah, “What terrible thing did you do?” (They knew he was running away from the Lord because he had told them.)  
**1:11** Since the wind and the waves of the sea were becoming much stronger, they said to him, “What should we do to you to make the sea calm down for us?”  
**1:12** Jonah said to them, “Pick me up, and throw me into the sea, and then it will calm down. I know it is my fault that this great storm has come on you.”  
**1:13** Instead, the men ·tried [dug in] to row the ship back to the land, but they could not, because the sea was becoming more stormy.  
**1:14** Jonah’s Punishment So the men cried to the Lord, “Lord, please don’t let us die because of this man’s life; please don’t ·think we are [hold us] guilty of ·killing an innocent person [innocent blood]. Lord, you have caused all this to happen; you wanted it this way.”  
**1:15** So they picked up Jonah and threw him into the sea, and the sea ·became calm [ceased raging].  
**1:16** Then they began to fear the Lord very much; they offered a sacrifice to the Lord and made ·promises [vows] to him.  
**1:17** The Lord ·caused [appointed; provided] a big fish to swallow Jonah, and Jonah was ·inside [in the belly of] the fish three days and three nights.

## 3장

**3:1** God Calls and Jonah Obeys The ·Lord spoke his word [word of the Lord came] to Jonah ·again [a second time] and said,  
**3:2** “Get up, go to the great city Nineveh, and ·preach [cry out] to it what I tell you to say.”  
**3:3** So Jonah obeyed the Lord and got up and went to Nineveh. It was a very large city; just to walk ·across it [or through it all] took a person three days.  
**3:4** After Jonah had entered the city and walked for one day, he preached to the people, saying, “After forty days, Nineveh will be ·destroyed [overthrown]!”  
**3:5** The people of Nineveh believed God. They ·announced that they would fast [called for/decreed a fast], and they put on ·rough cloth [burlap; sackcloth; to show their sorrow and repentance]. All the people in the city did this, from the ·most important to the least important [greatest to the least].  
**3:6** When the king of Nineveh heard this news, he got up from his throne, took off his robe, and covered himself with ·rough cloth [burlap; sackcloth] and sat in ashes [to show sorrow and repentance].  
**3:7** He ·sent this announcement [issued a proclamation] through Nineveh: By ·command [the decree] of the king and his ·important men [nobles]: No person or animal, herd or flock, will be allowed to taste anything. Do not let them eat food or drink water.  
**3:8** But every person and animal should be covered with ·rough cloth [burlap; sackcloth], and people should ·cry loudly [pray earnestly] to God. Everyone must turn away from evil living and stop doing ·harm [violence] all the time.  
**3:9** Who knows? Maybe God will ·change his mind [relent]. Maybe he will ·stop being angry [turn from his fierce anger], and then we will not ·die [perish].  
**3:10** When God saw what the people did, that they stopped doing evil, he ·changed his mind [relented] and did not carry out the ·destruction [disaster; punishment] he had threatened.
