# 61.베드로후서 (EXB)



## 1장

**1:1** From ·Simon [Simeon; a version of Simon closer to the Hebrew name] Peter, a ·servant [slave] and apostle of Jesus Christ. To ·you [those] who have received a faith as ·valuable [precious; privileged] as ours, ·because our God and Savior Jesus Christ does what is right [or through the righteousness/justice of our God and Savior Jesus Christ].  
**1:2** [May] Grace and peace be ·given to you more and more [lavished upon you; multiplied to you], ·because you truly know [or as you grow in your knowledge; through/in the knowledge of] God and Jesus our Lord.  
**1:3** God Has Given Us Blessings ·Jesus has the power of God, by which he has given us [His divine power has given us] everything we need ·to live and to serve God [or to live a godly life; for life and godliness]. We have these things ·because we know [or as we better come to know; through the knowledge of] ·Jesus, who called us [the one who called us; may refer to Jesus or God the Father] by his glory and ·goodness [virtue; moral excellence].  
**1:4** Through these he gave us the very great and precious promises. […so that] With these gifts you can ·share in [partake of; participate in] ·God’s nature [the divine nature; which enables us to live forever], ·and the world will not ruin you with its evil desires [or having escaped the world’s decay that was caused by its evil desires].  
**1:5** ·Because you have these blessings [For this very reason; the reasons stated in], ·do your best [make every effort; strive] to ·add these things to [or increase these things in] your lives: to your faith, add ·goodness [virtue; moral excellence]; and to your ·goodness [virtue; moral excellence], add knowledge;  
**1:6** and to your knowledge, add self-control; and to your self-control, add ·patience [or perseverance]; and to your ·patience [or perseverance], add ·service for God [devotion; piety; godliness];  
**1:7** and to your ·service for God [devotion; piety; godliness], add ·kindness for your brothers and sisters in Christ [affection for fellow believers; brotherly love/affection; Greek: philadelphia]; and to ·this kindness [affection; brotherly love/affection], add love [Greek: agapē].  
**1:8** If all these things are in you and are ·growing [increasing], they will ·help you to be useful and productive [keep you from being ineffective/idle and unfruitful/unproductive] in your knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:9** ·But [or For] anyone who does not have these things ·cannot see clearly [is nearsighted]. He is blind ·and [or because he] has forgotten that he was made clean from his past sins.  
**1:10** [Therefore] My brothers and sisters, ·try hard [make every effort; strive] to ·be certain that you really are called and chosen by God [confirm your calling and election]. [For] If you do all these things, you will never ·fall [stumble].  
**1:11** ·And [For in this way] you will be given a very ·great [rich; lavish] ·welcome [entrance; arrival] into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.  
**1:12** You know these things, and you are ·very strong [established] in the truth [that has come to you; that you now have], but I will always ·help you remember [remind you of] them.  
**1:13** I think it is right for me to ·help you remember [arouse you with a reminder] as long as I am in this ·body [tent; a metaphor for the transitory earthly body].  
**1:14** I know I must soon ·leave this body [put off my tent; die], as our Lord Jesus Christ ·has shown [made clear to] me.  
**1:15** I will ·try my best [make every effort; strive] so that you may be able [always] to remember these things even after ·I am gone [my departure; Peter’s death].  
**1:16** We Saw Christ’s Glory [For] When we ·told [made known to] you about the ·powerful coming [or power and coming] of our Lord Jesus Christ, we were not ·telling [following; repeating] ·just clever stories that someone invented [or cleverly-concocted myths]. But we ·saw the greatness of Jesus with our own eyes [were eyewitness of his majesty].  
**1:17** [For] ·Jesus heard the voice of God, the Greatest Glory [When that voice came to him from the Majestic Glory], when he received honor and glory from God the Father. The voice said, “This is my Son, whom I love, and I am very pleased with him [a reference to the Transfiguration].”  
**1:18** We heard that voice from heaven while we were with Jesus on the ·holy [sacred] mountain.  
**1:19** ·This makes us more sure about the message the prophets gave [or And we also have a reliable message from the prophets; And we have the prophetic word confirmed; probably referring to the Old Testament]. ·It is good for you [You do well] to follow closely what they said as you would follow a ·light [lamp] shining in a dark place, until the day ·begins [dawns] and the morning star rises in your hearts [the planet Venus as seen before sunrise, here symbolizing Christ].  
**1:20** ·Most [First] of all, you must understand this: No prophecy in the Scriptures ·ever comes from the prophet’s own interpretation [or is a matter of one’s own interpretation].  
**1:21** No prophecy ever came from ·what a person wanted to say [human will/intention], but people ·led [carried; moved] by the Holy Spirit spoke words from God. [True prophecy originates with God, not with the prophet.]  

## 2장

**2:1** False Teachers There used to be false prophets among God’s people just as you will have some false teachers ·in your group [among you]. They will secretly ·teach [bring in; introduce] ·things that are wrong—teachings that will cause people to be lost [destructive heresies/opinions/factions]. They will even ·refuse to accept [deny] the Master [Jesus] who bought ·their freedom [them; as a master purchases a slave]. So they will bring quick ·ruin [destruction] on themselves.  
**2:2** Many will follow their ·evil [depraved; debauched; licentious] ways and ·say evil things about [malign; slander] the way of truth.  
**2:3** ·Those false teachers only want your money, so [In their greed] they will ·use [exploit] you ·by telling you lies [with deceptive/false words]. Their judgment spoken against them long ago is ·still coming [not idle], and their ruin ·is certain [does not sleep].  
**2:4** [For if] When angels sinned, God did not ·let them go free without punishment [spare them]. [But] He sent them to ·hell [Tartarus; a Greek term for the underworld] and put them in caves of darkness where they are being held for judgment.  
**2:5** And God ·punished the world long ago [did not spare the ancient world] when he brought a flood to the world that was full of ·people who were against him [the ungodly]. But God ·saved [protected; kept] Noah, ·who preached about being right with God [a preacher of righteousness; Jewish tradition described Noah preaching repentance], and seven other people with him [his wife plus his three sons and their wives].  
**2:6** And God also ·destroyed [condemned] the evil cities of Sodom and Gomorrah by burning them until they were ashes. He made those cities an example ·of what will happen to [or for future generations of] ·those who are against God [the ungodly].  
**2:7** But he saved Lot from those cities. Lot, a ·good [righteous] man, was ·troubled [distressed; or oppressed] because of the ·filthy lives [depraved behavior] of ·evil [lawless] people.  
**2:8** (Lot was a ·good [righteous] man, but because he lived with evil people ·every day [day after day], his ·good heart [righteous soul] was ·hurt [tormented] by the ·evil things [lawless deeds] he saw and heard.)  
**2:9** So the Lord knows how to save ·those who serve him [the godly] ·when troubles come [from trial/testing/temptation]. He will hold ·evil people [the wicked/unrighteous] ·and punish them, while waiting for the judgment day [or while they wait for their punishment on judgment day].  
**2:10** That punishment is especially for those who ·live by doing the evil things their sinful selves want [go after the flesh with defiling passion/lust] and who ·hate [despise] authority. These false teachers are bold and ·do anything they want [arrogant; self-willed]. They ·are not afraid [do not tremble] to ·speak against [slander; blaspheme] ·the angels [the glorious ones; probably angelic beings; unclear whether referring to good or evil angels].  
**2:11** But even the angels, who are much stronger and more powerful ·than false teachers [or than the evil angels; unclear whether referring to false teachers or to “the glorious ones” (seen as evil angels) of], do not ·accuse them with insults [bring a slanderous charge against them] before the Lord [see].  
**2:12** But these people ·speak against [slander; blaspheme] things they do not understand. They are like ·animals that act without thinking [irrational animals], animals [of simple instinct] born to be caught and killed. And, ·like animals, these false teachers will be destroyed [or like the evil angels, these false teachers will be destroyed; in their destruction they will be destroyed].  
**2:13** ·They have caused many people to suffer, so they themselves will suffer. That is their pay for what they have done […suffering harm as the wage of unrighteousness; a wordplay based on the similarity of the Greek words translated “suffering harm” and “unrighteousness”]. They take pleasure in ·openly doing evil [doing evil/carousing in the daylight], so they are like dirty spots and ·stains [blemishes] among you. They delight in deceiving you while ·eating meals [or feasting] with you [perhaps an allusion to the fellowship meal, or “love feast,” celebrated with the Lord’s Supper].  
**2:14** ·Every time they look at a woman they want her [They have eyes full of adultery], and ·their desire for sin is never satisfied [or they never stop sinning]. They ·lead weak people into the trap of sin [ensnare/entice/lure unstable people/souls], and they have ·taught [exercised; trained] their hearts to be greedy. ·God will punish them [Accursed children; under God’s curse]!  
**2:15** These false teachers ·left [abandoned] the ·right [or straight] road and ·lost their way [wandered away; went astray], following the way Balaam went. Balaam, the son of ·Beor [or Bosor], loved ·being paid for doing wrong [the wages of unrighteousness].  
**2:16** But a donkey, which cannot talk, ·told Balaam he was sinning [rebuked his wrongdoing]. It spoke with a ·man’s [human] voice and stopped the prophet’s ·crazy thinking [madness].  
**2:17** Those false teachers are like ·springs [or wells] without water and ·clouds [or mists] blown by a ·storm [whirlwind; squall]. A place in the ·blackest [deepest; gloomy] darkness has been kept for them.  
**2:18** They ·brag with [speak with bombastic, boastful] words that ·mean nothing [are empty]. By their ·evil [fleshly] desires they ·lead people into the trap of sin—[entice] people who ·are just beginning to escape [or have barely escaped] from others who live in error.  
**2:19** They promise them freedom [perhaps from the law or from fear of judgment], but they themselves are not free. They are slaves of ·things that will be destroyed [corruption; depravity]. For people are slaves of anything that ·controls [masters; overpowers; defeats] them [this last sentence may be a common proverb].  
**2:20** They ·were made free [escaped] from the ·evil [depravity; defilement] in the world by knowing our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. But if they ·return to [get entangled again with] evil things and those things ·control [master; overpower; defeat] them, then ·it is worse for them than it was before [their last/final state is worse than the first].  
**2:21** ·Yes, [For] it would be better for them to have never known the ·right way [or the way of righteousness] than to know it and to turn away from the holy ·teaching [commandment; law] that was ·given [passed on; handed down] to them.  
**2:22** What they did is like this true ·saying [proverb; parable]: “A dog ·goes back to what it has thrown up [returns to its vomit],” and, “After a pig is washed, it goes back and rolls in the mud.”  

## 3장

**3:1** Jesus Will Come Again ·My friends [Beloved], this is [now] the second letter I have written you [the first is probably 1 Peter] to ·help your honest minds remember [awaken/arouse your sincere understanding/intentions with a reminder].  
**3:2** I want you to ·think about [remember; recall] the words the holy prophets spoke in the past, and remember the command our Lord and Savior gave us through your apostles.  
**3:3** It is most important for you to understand what will happen in the last days. ·People [Scoffers] will ·laugh at [scoff at; ridicule] you. They will ·live doing the evil things they want to do [indulge their own desires/lusts].  
**3:4** [And] They will say, “·Jesus promised to come again. Where is he [Where is his promised coming]? [For] Our ·fathers [ancestors] have ·died [fallen asleep], but ·the world [all things] continues the way it has been since ·it was made [it began with creation].”  
**3:5** But they ·do not want to remember [willfully forget/ignore] what happened long ago. By the word of God heaven ·was made [came to be;], and the earth was made from water and with water.  
**3:6** ·Then [Through these; either the water and the word of God, or the heavens and earth which poured forth their water] the world was flooded and destroyed ·with water [being deluged with water].  
**3:7** And that same word of God is ·keeping [reserving; holding in store] heaven and earth that we now have in order to be destroyed by fire. They are being kept for the judgment day and the destruction of ·all who are against God [the ungodly/impious people].  
**3:8** But do not forget [ignore; miss] this one thing, ·dear friends [beloved]: To the Lord one day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years is like one day [our perception of time is not the same as God’s].  
**3:9** The Lord is not ·slow [or late] in doing what he promised—the way some people understand ·slowness [lateness]. But God is being patient with you. He does not want anyone to ·be lost [perish], but he wants all people to ·change their hearts and lives [come to repentance].  
**3:10** But the day of the Lord will come like a thief [with surprise and danger]. The ·skies [heavens] will ·disappear [pass away;] with a loud noise. ·Everything in them [The elements; either everything in the cosmos or specifically the heavenly bodies] will be ·destroyed [or dissolved] by ·fire [heat; burning], and the earth and ·everything in it [or all the deeds done on it] will be exposed.  
**3:11** In that way everything will be ·destroyed [or dissolved]. So what kind of people should you be? You should live holy lives and ·serve God [be godly/pious],  
**3:12** as you wait for and ·look forward to [strive for; or hurry; hasten] the coming of the day of God. When that day comes, the ·skies [or heavens] will be ·destroyed [or dissolved] with fire, and ·everything in them [the elements] will melt with ·heat [fire; burning].  
**3:13** But God made a promise to us, and we are waiting for a new heaven and a new earth where ·goodness [righteousness] ·lives [makes its home].  
**3:14** [Therefore] ·Dear friends [Beloved], ·since [or while] you are waiting for this to happen, ·do your best [make every effort; strive] to be [found] without ·sin [spot] and without ·fault [blemish; the characteristics of the false teachers in]. ·Try [Make every effort; Strive] to be at peace with God.  
**3:15** ·Remember [Consider; Bear in mind] that ·we are saved because our Lord is patient [our Lord’s patience is our salvation; his delay in coming allows more to be saved]. Our ·dear [beloved] brother Paul told you the same thing when he wrote to you with the wisdom that God gave him.  
**3:16** He writes about ·this [these things] in all his letters. Some things in Paul’s letters are hard to understand, and people who are ·ignorant [untaught] and ·weak in faith [unstable] ·explain these things falsely [twist/distort them]. They also ·falsely explain [twist; distort] the other Scriptures, ·but they are destroying themselves by doing this [or which will lead to their destruction].  
**3:17** [Therefore] ·Dear friends [Beloved], since you already ·know [are forewarned] about this, be ·careful [on guard]. Do not let those ·evil [lawless; unprincipled] people lead you away ·by the wrong they do [or by their deception; or into error]. Be ·careful [on guard] so you will not fall from your own ·strong faith [stability; firm position].  
**3:18** But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. Glory be to him now and ·forever [to the day of eternity]! ·Amen [So be it].
