## 1장

**1:1** Job, the Good Man A man named Job lived in the land of Uz [east of Israel in Edom (present-day Jordan)]. He was an ·honest [innocent; blameless] and ·innocent [virtuous] man; he ·honored [feared; respected] God and stayed away from evil [these terms are used to describe the wise in Proverbs].  
**1:2** Job had seven sons and three daughters [indicating a large and complete family].  
**1:3** He owned seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred teams of oxen, and five hundred female donkeys. He also had a large number of servants. He was the ·greatest [or richest] man among all the people of the East [the expected reward of wisdom].  
**1:4** Job’s sons took turns holding feasts [on their day; perhaps birthday celebrations] in their homes and invited their sisters to eat and drink with them.  
**1:5** After a feast was over, Job would send and have them ·made clean [consecrated; made holy]. Early in the morning Job would offer a burnt offering [an atonement offering] for each of them, because he thought, “My children may have sinned and ·cursed [blessed; a euphemism for “cursed”] God in their hearts.” Job did this every time.  
**1:6** Satan Appears Before the Lord One day the ·angels [sons of God] came to ·show themselves [stand] before the Lord, and ·Satan [the Satan; means “the Accuser” or “the Adversary”; either the Devil or a member of God’s heavenly court] was with them.  
**1:7** The Lord said to Satan, “Where have you come from?” Satan answered the Lord, “I have been ·wandering around [roaming] the earth, ·going back and forth in [patrolling] it.”  
**1:8** Then the Lord said to Satan, “Have you noticed [considered; set your heart on] my servant Job? No one else on earth is like him. He is an honest and innocent man, honoring God and staying away from evil.”  
**1:9** But Satan answered the Lord, “·Job honors God for a good reason [Does Job honor/fear/respect God for no good reason?].  
**1:10** ·You have [Don’t you…?] put a ·wall [hedge; to protect from danger] around him, his family, and everything he owns. You have blessed ·the things he has done [all the works of his hands]. His flocks and herds ·are so large they almost cover [burst forth on] the land.  
**1:11** But ·reach out [stretch forth] your hand and ·destroy [afflict] everything he has, and [see if] he will curse you to your face.”  
**1:12** The Lord said to Satan, “All right, then. Everything Job has is in your ·power [hand], but ·you must not touch Job himself [but don’t send your hand against him].” Then Satan left the Lord’s presence.  
**1:13** One day Job’s sons and daughters were ·eating and drinking wine [feasting; celebrating] together at the ·oldest [firstborn] brother’s house.  
**1:14** A messenger came to Job and said, “The oxen were plowing and the donkeys were eating grass nearby,  
**1:15** when the Sabeans [a people from southern Arabia] attacked and carried them away. They killed the servants with swords, and I am the only one who escaped to tell you!”  
**1:16** The messenger was still speaking when another messenger arrived and said, “·Lightning [Fire] from God fell from ·the sky [heaven]. It burned up the sheep and the servants, and I am the only one who escaped to tell you!”  
**1:17** The second messenger was still speaking when another messenger arrived and said, “The ·Babylonians [Chaldeans; a people located in present-day southern Iraq] sent three ·groups of attackers [raiding parties] that swept down and stole your camels and killed the servants [with the sword]. I am the only one who escaped to tell you!”  
**1:18** The third messenger was still speaking when another messenger arrived and said, “Your sons and daughters were ·eating and drinking wine [feasting; celebrating] together at the ·oldest [firstborn] brother’s house.  
**1:19** Suddenly a ·great [strong; mighty] wind came from the desert, hitting all four corners of the house at once. The house fell in on the young people, and they are all dead. I am the only one who escaped to tell you!”  
**1:20** When Job heard this, he got up and tore his robe and shaved his head [ancient mourning customs]. Then he bowed down to the ground to worship God.  
**1:21** He said: “I was naked when I ·was born [came from my mother’s womb], and I will be naked when I ·die [return there]. The Lord gave these things to me, and he has taken them away. ·Praise [Blessed be] the name of the Lord.”  
**1:22** In all this Job did not sin or blame God.
