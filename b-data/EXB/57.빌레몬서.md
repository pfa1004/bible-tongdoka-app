# 57.빌레몬서 (EXB)



## 1장

**1:1** From Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and from Timothy, our brother. To Philemon, our ·dear friend [brother] and ·worker with us [coworker];  
**1:2** to Apphia [perhaps Philemon’s wife], our sister; to Archippus [possibly Philemon’s son], ·a worker with us [our fellow soldier]; and to the church that meets in your home:  
**1:3** Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:4** Philemon’s Love and Faith I always thank my God when I ·mention [remember] you in my prayers,  
**1:5** because I hear about the love you have for all ·God’s holy people [the saints] and the faith you have in the Lord Jesus.  
**1:6** I pray that the ·faith you share [the sharing/fellowship of your faith] may ·make you [enable/empower you to] understand every blessing we have in Christ.  
**1:7** I have great joy and comfort, my brother, because the love you have shown to ·God’s people [the saints] has refreshed ·them [their hearts; their inward parts; the seat of emotions].  
**1:8** Accept Onesimus as a Brother So, in Christ, I could be bold and order you to do what is ·right [required; proper; your duty].  
**1:9** But because I love you, I am ·pleading with [appealing to; urging; encouraging] you instead. I, Paul, an old man now and also a prisoner [in Rome, about ad 60] for Christ Jesus,  
**1:10** am ·pleading with [appealing to; urging; encouraging] you for my child Onesimus, ·who became my child [whom I begat/fathered; Paul evidently led Onesimus to Christ in Rome] while I was in prison.  
**1:11** In the past he was ·useless [unprofitable; worthless] to you, but now he has become ·useful [helpful; valuable] for both you and me [a play on words, since Onesimus means “useful” or “helpful”].  
**1:12** I am sending him back to you, and ·with him I am sending my own heart [or he is my very heart].  
**1:13** I wanted to keep him with me so that ·in your place [or on your behalf] he might ·help [serve] me while I am in prison for the ·Good News [Gospel].  
**1:14** But I did not want to do anything without ·asking you first [your consent] so that any good you do for me will be because you want to do it, not ·because I forced you [out of compulsion].  
**1:15** [For] Maybe Onesimus was separated from you for a short time so you could have him back forever—  
**1:16** no longer as a slave, but better than a slave, as a loved brother. ·I love him very much, but you will love him even more […especially to me, but more so to you], both ·as a person [or in the natural realm; in the flesh] and ·as a believer in the Lord [or in the spiritual realm; in the Lord].  
**1:17** So if you consider me your partner, ·welcome [receive; accept] Onesimus as you would ·welcome [receive; accept] me.  
**1:18** If he has ·done anything wrong to [defrauded; harmed] you or if he owes you anything, charge that to me.  
**1:19** I, Paul, am writing this with my own hand [contrary to his usual practice of using a scribe, or amanuensis]. I will pay it back, and I will ·say nothing about what [make no mention that] you owe me for your ·own life [very self; Paul had evidently led Philemon to Christ].  
**1:20** ·So [Yes], my brother, I ask ·that you do this for me [for this benefit/favor from you] in the Lord: Refresh my heart in Christ.  
**1:21** I write this letter, ·knowing [confident of your obedience/compliance, knowing] that you will do what I ask you and even more.  
**1:22** One more thing—prepare a ·room [guest room] for me in which to stay, because I hope God will answer your prayers and I will be ·able to come [restored; granted] to you.  
**1:23** Final Greetings Epaphras, a prisoner with me for Christ Jesus, sends greetings to you.  
**1:24** And also Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, ·workers together with me [my coworkers], send greetings.  
**1:25** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
