# 63.요한2서 (EXB)



## 1장

**1:1** Do Not Help False Teachers From the ·Elder [or old man; Greek: presbyteros, referring to advanced age, a church office, or both;]. To the ·chosen [elect] lady [most likely a metaphor for a church] and her children [the members of that church]: I love all of you in the truth [the truth about the Gospel of Jesus Christ], and all those who know the truth love you.  
**1:2** We love you because of the truth that ·lives [abides; remains] in us and will be with us forever.  
**1:3** Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and his Son, Jesus Christ, will be with us in truth and love.  
**1:4** I ·was very happy [rejoiced greatly] to learn that some of your children are ·following the way of [living by; walking in] truth [living as the Gospel requires], as the Father commanded us.  
**1:5** And now, dear lady, this is not a new command [I am writing] but is the same command we have had from the beginning. I ask you that we all love each other.  
**1:6** And ·love means [this is love:] ·living the way God commanded us to live [walking according to his commands]. As you have heard from the beginning, his command is this: ·Live a life of love [You must walk in it; that is, in love].  
**1:7** [For] Many ·false teachers [deceivers] ·are in [have gone out into] the world now who do not confess that Jesus Christ came to earth ·as a human [in the flesh]. Anyone who does not confess this is ·a false teacher [the deceiver] and ·an enemy of Christ [the antichrist; one who radically opposes Christ].  
**1:8** ·Be careful [Watch] yourselves that you do not lose everything you have worked for, but that you receive your full reward.  
**1:9** Anyone who ·goes beyond [runs ahead of] Christ’s teaching and does not ·continue to follow only his teaching [abide/remain in it] does not have God. But whoever ·continues to follow [abides/remains in] ·the teaching of Christ [the teaching] has both the Father and the Son.  
**1:10** If someone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not ·greet [welcome] that person or ·accept [receive] them into your house.  
**1:11** If you welcome such a person, you ·share [participate] in the evil work.  
**1:12** I have many things to write to you, but I do not want to use paper and ink. Instead, I hope to ·come to [visit] you and talk face to face so ·we can be full of joy [our joy can be complete].  
**1:13** The children of your ·chosen [elect] sister [see ; probably refers to another church] greet you.
