## 2장

**2:2** Someone told the king of Jericho, “[Look; Behold] Some men from Israel have come here tonight to spy out the land.”  
**2:3** So the king of Jericho sent this message to Rahab: “Bring out the men who came to you and entered your house. They have come to spy out our whole land.”  
**2:4** But the woman had hidden the two men. She said, “They did come here, but I didn’t know where they came from.  
**2:5** In the evening, when it was time to close the city gate, they left. I don’t know where they went, but if you go quickly, maybe you can catch them.”  
**2:6** (The woman had taken the men up to the roof and had hidden them there under stalks of flax that she had spread out.)  
**2:7** So the king’s men went out looking for the spies on the road that leads to the ·crossings [fords] of the Jordan River. The city gate was closed just after the king’s men left the city.  
**2:8** Before the spies went to sleep for the night, Rahab went up to the roof.  
**2:9** She said to them, “I know the Lord has given this land to your people. ·You frighten us very much [A terror of you has fallen on us]. Everyone living in this land is ·terribly afraid of [melting away before] you

**2:11** When we heard this, ·we were very frightened [our hearts melted]. ·Now our men are afraid to fight you [And there rose up no spirit/breath in a man because of you] because the Lord your God ·rules [is God in] the heavens above and the earth below!

**2:16** She said to them, “Go into the hills so the ·king’s men [pursuers] will not find you. Hide there for three days. After the ·king’s men [pursuers] return, you may go on your way.”

**2:19** If anyone leaves your house and is killed, ·it is his own fault [his blood is on his head]. We ·cannot be responsible for him [are innocent]. If anyone in your house ·is hurt [has a hand laid on them], ·we will be responsible [his blood will be on our head].

**2:21** Rahab answered, “·I agree to this [Let it be according to your words].” So she sent them away, and they left. Then she tied the ·red [scarlet] rope in the window.  
**2:22** The men left and went into the hills where they stayed for three days. The ·king’s men [pursuers] looked for them all along the road, but after three days, they returned to the city without finding them.
