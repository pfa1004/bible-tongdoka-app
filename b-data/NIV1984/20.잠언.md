# 20.잠언 (NIV1984)



## 1장

**1:1** Prologue: Purpose and Theme<br>The proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel:  
**1:2** for attaining wisdom and discipline;<br>for understanding words of insight;  
**1:3** for acquiring a disciplined and prudent life,<br>doing what is right and just and fair;  
**1:4** for giving prudence to the simple,<br>knowledge and discretion to the young—  
**1:5** let the wise listen and add to their learning,<br>and let the discerning get guidance—  
**1:6** for understanding proverbs and parables,<br>the sayings and riddles of the wise.  
**1:7** The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge,<br>but fools despise wisdom and discipline.  
**1:8** Exhortations to Embrace Wisdom<br>Warning Against Enticement<br>Listen, my son, to your father’s instruction<br>and do not forsake your mother’s teaching.  
**1:9** They will be a garland to grace your head<br>and a chain to adorn your neck.  
**1:10** My son, if sinners entice you,<br>do not give in to them.  
**1:11** If they say, “Come along with us;<br>let’s lie in wait for someone’s blood,<br>let’s waylay some harmless soul;  
**1:12** let’s swallow them alive, like the grave,<br>and whole, like those who go down to the pit;  
**1:13** we will get all sorts of valuable things<br>and fill our houses with plunder;  
**1:14** throw in your lot with us,<br>and we will share a common purse”—  
**1:15** my son, do not go along with them,<br>do not set foot on their paths;  
**1:16** for their feet rush into sin,<br>they are swift to shed blood.  
**1:17** How useless to spread a net<br>in full view of all the birds!  
**1:18** These men lie in wait for their own blood;<br>they waylay only themselves!  
**1:19** Such is the end of all who go after ill-gotten gain;<br>it takes away the lives of those who get it.  
**1:20** Warning Against Rejecting Wisdom<br>Wisdom calls aloud in the street,<br>she raises her voice in the public squares;  
**1:21** at the head of the noisy streets she cries out,<br>in the gateways of the city she makes her speech:  
**1:22** “How long will you simple ones love your simple ways?<br>How long will mockers delight in mockery<br>and fools hate knowledge?  
**1:23** If you had responded to my rebuke,<br>I would have poured out my heart to you<br>and made my thoughts known to you.  
**1:24** But since you rejected me when I called<br>and no one gave heed when I stretched out my hand,  
**1:25** since you ignored all my advice<br>and would not accept my rebuke,  
**1:26** I in turn will laugh at your disaster;<br>I will mock when calamity overtakes you—  
**1:27** when calamity overtakes you like a storm,<br>when disaster sweeps over you like a whirlwind,<br>when distress and trouble overwhelm you.  
**1:28** “Then they will call to me but I will not answer;<br>they will look for me but will not find me.  
**1:29** Since they hated knowledge<br>and did not choose to fear the Lord,  
**1:30** since they would not accept my advice<br>and spurned my rebuke,  
**1:31** they will eat the fruit of their ways<br>and be filled with the fruit of their schemes.  
**1:32** For the waywardness of the simple will kill them,<br>and the complacency of fools will destroy them;  
**1:33** but whoever listens to me will live in safety<br>and be at ease, without fear of harm.”  

## 2장

**2:1** Moral Benefits of Wisdom<br>My son, if you accept my words<br>and store up my commands within you,  
**2:2** turning your ear to wisdom<br>and applying your heart to understanding,  
**2:3** and if you call out for insight<br>and cry aloud for understanding,  
**2:4** and if you look for it as for silver<br>and search for it as for hidden treasure,  
**2:5** then you will understand the fear of the Lord<br>and find the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For the Lord gives wisdom,<br>and from his mouth come knowledge and understanding.  
**2:7** He holds victory in store for the upright,<br>he is a shield to those whose walk is blameless,  
**2:8** for he guards the course of the just<br>and protects the way of his faithful ones.  
**2:9** Then you will understand what is right and just<br>and fair—every good path.  
**2:10** For wisdom will enter your heart,<br>and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul.  
**2:11** Discretion will protect you,<br>and understanding will guard you.  
**2:12** Wisdom will save you from the ways of wicked men,<br>from men whose words are perverse,  
**2:13** who leave the straight paths<br>to walk in dark ways,  
**2:14** who delight in doing wrong<br>and rejoice in the perverseness of evil,  
**2:15** whose paths are crooked<br>and who are devious in their ways.  
**2:16** It will save you also from the adulteress,<br>from the wayward wife with her seductive words,  
**2:17** who has left the partner of her youth<br>and ignored the covenant she made before God.  
**2:18** For her house leads down to death<br>and her paths to the spirits of the dead.  
**2:19** None who go to her return<br>or attain the paths of life.  
**2:20** Thus you will walk in the ways of good men<br>and keep to the paths of the righteous.  
**2:21** For the upright will live in the land,<br>and the blameless will remain in it;  
**2:22** but the wicked will be cut off from the land,<br>and the unfaithful will be torn from it.  

## 3장

**3:1** Further Benefits of Wisdom<br>My son, do not forget my teaching,<br>but keep my commands in your heart,  
**3:2** for they will prolong your life many years<br>and bring you prosperity.  
**3:3** Let love and faithfulness never leave you;<br>bind them around your neck,<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**3:4** Then you will win favor and a good name<br>in the sight of God and man.  
**3:5** Trust in the Lord with all your heart<br>and lean not on your own understanding;  
**3:6** in all your ways acknowledge him,<br>and he will make your paths straight.  
**3:7** Do not be wise in your own eyes;<br>fear the Lord and shun evil.  
**3:8** This will bring health to your body<br>and nourishment to your bones.  
**3:9** Honor the Lord with your wealth,<br>with the firstfruits of all your crops;  
**3:10** then your barns will be filled to overflowing,<br>and your vats will brim over with new wine.  
**3:11** My son, do not despise the Lord’s discipline<br>and do not resent his rebuke,  
**3:12** because the Lord disciplines those he loves,<br>as a father the son he delights in.  
**3:13** Blessed is the man who finds wisdom,<br>the man who gains understanding,  
**3:14** for she is more profitable than silver<br>and yields better returns than gold.  
**3:15** She is more precious than rubies;<br>nothing you desire can compare with her.  
**3:16** Long life is in her right hand;<br>in her left hand are riches and honor.  
**3:17** Her ways are pleasant ways,<br>and all her paths are peace.  
**3:18** She is a tree of life to those who embrace her;<br>those who lay hold of her will be blessed.  
**3:19** By wisdom the Lord laid the earth’s foundations,<br>by understanding he set the heavens in place;  
**3:20** by his knowledge the deeps were divided,<br>and the clouds let drop the dew.  
**3:21** My son, preserve sound judgment and discernment,<br>do not let them out of your sight;  
**3:22** they will be life for you,<br>an ornament to grace your neck.  
**3:23** Then you will go on your way in safety,<br>and your foot will not stumble;  
**3:24** when you lie down, you will not be afraid;<br>when you lie down, your sleep will be sweet.  
**3:25** Have no fear of sudden disaster<br>or of the ruin that overtakes the wicked,  
**3:26** for the Lord will be your confidence<br>and will keep your foot from being snared.  
**3:27** Do not withhold good from those who deserve it,<br>when it is in your power to act.  
**3:28** Do not say to your neighbor,<br>“Come back later; I’ll give it tomorrow”—<br>when you now have it with you.  
**3:29** Do not plot harm against your neighbor,<br>who lives trustfully near you.  
**3:30** Do not accuse a man for no reason—<br>when he has done you no harm.  
**3:31** Do not envy a violent man<br>or choose any of his ways,  
**3:32** for the Lord detests a perverse man<br>but takes the upright into his confidence.  
**3:33** The Lord’s curse is on the house of the wicked,<br>but he blesses the home of the righteous.  
**3:34** He mocks proud mockers<br>but gives grace to the humble.  
**3:35** The wise inherit honor,<br>but fools he holds up to shame.  

## 4장

**4:1** Wisdom Is Supreme<br>Listen, my sons, to a father’s instruction;<br>pay attention and gain understanding.  
**4:2** I give you sound learning,<br>so do not forsake my teaching.  
**4:3** When I was a boy in my father’s house,<br>still tender, and an only child of my mother,  
**4:4** he taught me and said,<br>“Lay hold of my words with all your heart;<br>keep my commands and you will live.  
**4:5** Get wisdom, get understanding;<br>do not forget my words or swerve from them.  
**4:6** Do not forsake wisdom, and she will protect you;<br>love her, and she will watch over you.  
**4:7** Wisdom is supreme; therefore get wisdom.<br>Though it cost all you have, get understanding.  
**4:8** Esteem her, and she will exalt you;<br>embrace her, and she will honor you.  
**4:9** She will set a garland of grace on your head<br>and present you with a crown of splendor.”  
**4:10** Listen, my son, accept what I say,<br>and the years of your life will be many.  
**4:11** I guide you in the way of wisdom<br>and lead you along straight paths.  
**4:12** When you walk, your steps will not be hampered;<br>when you run, you will not stumble.  
**4:13** Hold on to instruction, do not let it go;<br>guard it well, for it is your life.  
**4:14** Do not set foot on the path of the wicked<br>or walk in the way of evil men.  
**4:15** Avoid it, do not travel on it;<br>turn from it and go on your way.  
**4:16** For they cannot sleep till they do evil;<br>they are robbed of slumber till they make someone fall.  
**4:17** They eat the bread of wickedness<br>and drink the wine of violence.  
**4:18** The path of the righteous is like the first gleam of dawn,<br>shining ever brighter till the full light of day.  
**4:19** But the way of the wicked is like deep darkness;<br>they do not know what makes them stumble.  
**4:20** My son, pay attention to what I say;<br>listen closely to my words.  
**4:21** Do not let them out of your sight,<br>keep them within your heart;  
**4:22** for they are life to those who find them<br>and health to a man’s whole body.  
**4:23** Above all else, guard your heart,<br>for it is the wellspring of life.  
**4:24** Put away perversity from your mouth;<br>keep corrupt talk far from your lips.  
**4:25** Let your eyes look straight ahead,<br>fix your gaze directly before you.  
**4:26** Make level paths for your feet<br>and take only ways that are firm.  
**4:27** Do not swerve to the right or the left;<br>keep your foot from evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** Warning Against Adultery<br>My son, pay attention to my wisdom,<br>listen well to my words of insight,  
**5:2** that you may maintain discretion<br>and your lips may preserve knowledge.  
**5:3** For the lips of an adulteress drip honey,<br>and her speech is smoother than oil;  
**5:4** but in the end she is bitter as gall,<br>sharp as a double-edged sword.  
**5:5** Her feet go down to death;<br>her steps lead straight to the grave.  
**5:6** She gives no thought to the way of life;<br>her paths are crooked, but she knows it not.  
**5:7** Now then, my sons, listen to me;<br>do not turn aside from what I say.  
**5:8** Keep to a path far from her,<br>do not go near the door of her house,  
**5:9** lest you give your best strength to others<br>and your years to one who is cruel,  
**5:10** lest strangers feast on your wealth<br>and your toil enrich another man’s house.  
**5:11** At the end of your life you will groan,<br>when your flesh and body are spent.  
**5:12** You will say, “How I hated discipline!<br>How my heart spurned correction!  
**5:13** I would not obey my teachers<br>or listen to my instructors.  
**5:14** I have come to the brink of utter ruin<br>in the midst of the whole assembly.”  
**5:15** Drink water from your own cistern,<br>running water from your own well.  
**5:16** Should your springs overflow in the streets,<br>your streams of water in the public squares?  
**5:17** Let them be yours alone,<br>never to be shared with strangers.  
**5:18** May your fountain be blessed,<br>and may you rejoice in the wife of your youth.  
**5:19** A loving doe, a graceful deer—<br>may her breasts satisfy you always,<br>may you ever be captivated by her love.  
**5:20** Why be captivated, my son, by an adulteress?<br>Why embrace the bosom of another man’s wife?  
**5:21** For a man’s ways are in full view of the Lord,<br>and he examines all his paths.  
**5:22** The evil deeds of a wicked man ensnare him;<br>the cords of his sin hold him fast.  
**5:23** He will die for lack of discipline,<br>led astray by his own great folly.  

## 6장

**6:1** Warnings Against Folly<br>My son, if you have put up security for your neighbor,<br>if you have struck hands in pledge for another,  
**6:2** if you have been trapped by what you said,<br>ensnared by the words of your mouth,  
**6:3** then do this, my son, to free yourself,<br>since you have fallen into your neighbor’s hands:<br>Go and humble yourself;<br>press your plea with your neighbor!  
**6:4** Allow no sleep to your eyes,<br>no slumber to your eyelids.  
**6:5** Free yourself, like a gazelle from the hand of the hunter,<br>like a bird from the snare of the fowler.  
**6:6** Go to the ant, you sluggard;<br>consider its ways and be wise!  
**6:7** It has no commander,<br>no overseer or ruler,  
**6:8** yet it stores its provisions in summer<br>and gathers its food at harvest.  
**6:9** How long will you lie there, you sluggard?<br>When will you get up from your sleep?  
**6:10** A little sleep, a little slumber,<br>a little folding of the hands to rest—  
**6:11** and poverty will come on you like a bandit<br>and scarcity like an armed man.  
**6:12** A scoundrel and villain,<br>who goes about with a corrupt mouth,  
**6:13** who winks with his eye,<br>signals with his feet<br>and motions with his fingers,  
**6:14** who plots evil with deceit in his heart—<br>he always stirs up dissension.  
**6:15** Therefore disaster will overtake him in an instant;<br>he will suddenly be destroyed—without remedy.  
**6:16** There are six things the Lord hates,<br>seven that are detestable to him:  
**6:17** haughty eyes,<br>a lying tongue,<br>hands that shed innocent blood,  
**6:18** a heart that devises wicked schemes,<br>feet that are quick to rush into evil,  
**6:19** a false witness who pours out lies<br>and a man who stirs up dissension among brothers.  
**6:20** Warning Against Adultery<br>My son, keep your father’s commands<br>and do not forsake your mother’s teaching.  
**6:21** Bind them upon your heart forever;<br>fasten them around your neck.  
**6:22** When you walk, they will guide you;<br>when you sleep, they will watch over you;<br>when you awake, they will speak to you.  
**6:23** For these commands are a lamp,<br>this teaching is a light,<br>and the corrections of discipline<br>are the way to life,  
**6:24** keeping you from the immoral woman,<br>from the smooth tongue of the wayward wife.  
**6:25** Do not lust in your heart after her beauty<br>or let her captivate you with her eyes,  
**6:26** for the prostitute reduces you to a loaf of bread,<br>and the adulteress preys upon your very life.  
**6:27** Can a man scoop fire into his lap<br>without his clothes being burned?  
**6:28** Can a man walk on hot coals<br>without his feet being scorched?  
**6:29** So is he who sleeps with another man’s wife;<br>no one who touches her will go unpunished.  
**6:30** Men do not despise a thief if he steals<br>to satisfy his hunger when he is starving.  
**6:31** Yet if he is caught, he must pay sevenfold,<br>though it costs him all the wealth of his house.  
**6:32** But a man who commits adultery lacks judgment;<br>whoever does so destroys himself.  
**6:33** Blows and disgrace are his lot,<br>and his shame will never be wiped away;  
**6:34** for jealousy arouses a husband’s fury,<br>and he will show no mercy when he takes revenge.  
**6:35** He will not accept any compensation;<br>he will refuse the bribe, however great it is.  

## 7장

**7:1** Warning Against the Adulteress<br>My son, keep my words<br>and store up my commands within you.  
**7:2** Keep my commands and you will live;<br>guard my teachings as the apple of your eye.  
**7:3** Bind them on your fingers;<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,”<br>and call understanding your kinsman;  
**7:5** they will keep you from the adulteress,<br>from the wayward wife with her seductive words.  
**7:6** At the window of my house<br>I looked out through the lattice.  
**7:7** I saw among the simple,<br>I noticed among the young men,<br>a youth who lacked judgment.  
**7:8** He was going down the street near her corner,<br>walking along in the direction of her house  
**7:9** at twilight, as the day was fading,<br>as the dark of night set in.  
**7:10** Then out came a woman to meet him,<br>dressed like a prostitute and with crafty intent.  
**7:11** (She is loud and defiant,<br>her feet never stay at home;  
**7:12** now in the street, now in the squares,<br>at every corner she lurks.)  
**7:13** She took hold of him and kissed him<br>and with a brazen face she said:  
**7:14** “I have fellowship offerings at home;<br>today I fulfilled my vows.  
**7:15** So I came out to meet you;<br>I looked for you and have found you!  
**7:16** I have covered my bed<br>with colored linens from Egypt.  
**7:17** I have perfumed my bed<br>with myrrh, aloes and cinnamon.  
**7:18** Come, let’s drink deep of love till morning;<br>let’s enjoy ourselves with love!  
**7:19** My husband is not at home;<br>he has gone on a long journey.  
**7:20** He took his purse filled with money<br>and will not be home till full moon.”  
**7:21** With persuasive words she led him astray;<br>she seduced him with her smooth talk.  
**7:22** All at once he followed her<br>like an ox going to the slaughter,<br>like a deer stepping into a noose  
**7:23** till an arrow pierces his liver,<br>like a bird darting into a snare,<br>little knowing it will cost him his life.  
**7:24** Now then, my sons, listen to me;<br>pay attention to what I say.  
**7:25** Do not let your heart turn to her ways<br>or stray into her paths.  
**7:26** Many are the victims she has brought down;<br>her slain are a mighty throng.  
**7:27** Her house is a highway to the grave,<br>leading down to the chambers of death.  

## 8장

**8:1** Wisdom’s Call<br>Does not wisdom call out?<br>Does not understanding raise her voice?  
**8:2** On the heights along the way,<br>where the paths meet, she takes her stand;  
**8:3** beside the gates leading into the city,<br>at the entrances, she cries aloud:  
**8:4** “To you, O men, I call out;<br>I raise my voice to all mankind.  
**8:5** You who are simple, gain prudence;<br>you who are foolish, gain understanding.  
**8:6** Listen, for I have worthy things to say;<br>I open my lips to speak what is right.  
**8:7** My mouth speaks what is true,<br>for my lips detest wickedness.  
**8:8** All the words of my mouth are just;<br>none of them is crooked or perverse.  
**8:9** To the discerning all of them are right;<br>they are faultless to those who have knowledge.  
**8:10** Choose my instruction instead of silver,<br>knowledge rather than choice gold,  
**8:11** for wisdom is more precious than rubies,<br>and nothing you desire can compare with her.  
**8:12** “I, wisdom, dwell together with prudence;<br>I possess knowledge and discretion.  
**8:13** To fear the Lord is to hate evil;<br>I hate pride and arrogance,<br>evil behavior and perverse speech.  
**8:14** Counsel and sound judgment are mine;<br>I have understanding and power.  
**8:15** By me kings reign<br>and rulers make laws that are just;  
**8:16** by me princes govern,<br>and all nobles who rule on earth.  
**8:17** I love those who love me,<br>and those who seek me find me.  
**8:18** With me are riches and honor,<br>enduring wealth and prosperity.  
**8:19** My fruit is better than fine gold;<br>what I yield surpasses choice silver.  
**8:20** I walk in the way of righteousness,<br>along the paths of justice,  
**8:21** bestowing wealth on those who love me<br>and making their treasuries full.  
**8:22** “The Lord brought me forth as the first of his works,<br>before his deeds of old;  
**8:23** I was appointed from eternity,<br>from the beginning, before the world began.  
**8:24** When there were no oceans, I was given birth,<br>when there were no springs abounding with water;  
**8:25** before the mountains were settled in place,<br>before the hills, I was given birth,  
**8:26** before he made the earth or its fields<br>or any of the dust of the world.  
**8:27** I was there when he set the heavens in place,<br>when he marked out the horizon on the face of the deep,  
**8:28** when he established the clouds above<br>and fixed securely the fountains of the deep,  
**8:29** when he gave the sea its boundary<br>so the waters would not overstep his command,<br>and when he marked out the foundations of the earth.  
**8:30** Then I was the craftsman at his side.<br>I was filled with delight day after day,<br>rejoicing always in his presence,  
**8:31** rejoicing in his whole world<br>and delighting in mankind.  
**8:32** “Now then, my sons, listen to me;<br>blessed are those who keep my ways.  
**8:33** Listen to my instruction and be wise;<br>do not ignore it.  
**8:34** Blessed is the man who listens to me,<br>watching daily at my doors,<br>waiting at my doorway.  
**8:35** For whoever finds me finds life<br>and receives favor from the Lord.  
**8:36** But whoever fails to find me harms himself;<br>all who hate me love death.”  

## 9장

**9:1** Invitations of Wisdom and of Folly<br>Wisdom has built her house;<br>she has hewn out its seven pillars.  
**9:2** She has prepared her meat and mixed her wine;<br>she has also set her table.  
**9:3** She has sent out her maids, and she calls<br>from the highest point of the city.  
**9:4** “Let all who are simple come in here!”<br>she says to those who lack judgment.  
**9:5** “Come, eat my food<br>and drink the wine I have mixed.  
**9:6** Leave your simple ways and you will live;<br>walk in the way of understanding.  
**9:7** “Whoever corrects a mocker invites insult;<br>whoever rebukes a wicked man incurs abuse.  
**9:8** Do not rebuke a mocker or he will hate you;<br>rebuke a wise man and he will love you.  
**9:9** Instruct a wise man and he will be wiser still;<br>teach a righteous man and he will add to his learning.  
**9:10** “The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom,<br>and knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.  
**9:11** For through me your days will be many,<br>and years will be added to your life.  
**9:12** If you are wise, your wisdom will reward you;<br>if you are a mocker, you alone will suffer.”  
**9:13** The woman Folly is loud;<br>she is undisciplined and without knowledge.  
**9:14** She sits at the door of her house,<br>on a seat at the highest point of the city,  
**9:15** calling out to those who pass by,<br>who go straight on their way.  
**9:16** “Let all who are simple come in here!”<br>she says to those who lack judgment.  
**9:17** “Stolen water is sweet;<br>food eaten in secret is delicious!”  
**9:18** But little do they know that the dead are there,<br>that her guests are in the depths of the grave.  

## 10장

**10:1** Proverbs of Solomon<br>The proverbs of Solomon:<br>A wise son brings joy to his father,<br>but a foolish son grief to his mother.  
**10:2** Ill-gotten treasures are of no value,<br>but righteousness delivers from death.  
**10:3** The Lord does not let the righteous go hungry<br>but he thwarts the craving of the wicked.  
**10:4** Lazy hands make a man poor,<br>but diligent hands bring wealth.  
**10:5** He who gathers crops in summer is a wise son,<br>but he who sleeps during harvest is a disgraceful son.  
**10:6** Blessings crown the head of the righteous,<br>but violence overwhelms the mouth of the wicked.  
**10:7** The memory of the righteous will be a blessing,<br>but the name of the wicked will rot.  
**10:8** The wise in heart accept commands,<br>but a chattering fool comes to ruin.  
**10:9** The man of integrity walks securely,<br>but he who takes crooked paths will be found out.  
**10:10** He who winks maliciously causes grief,<br>and a chattering fool comes to ruin.  
**10:11** The mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life,<br>but violence overwhelms the mouth of the wicked.  
**10:12** Hatred stirs up dissension,<br>but love covers over all wrongs.  
**10:13** Wisdom is found on the lips of the discerning,<br>but a rod is for the back of him who lacks judgment.  
**10:14** Wise men store up knowledge,<br>but the mouth of a fool invites ruin.  
**10:15** The wealth of the rich is their fortified city,<br>but poverty is the ruin of the poor.

## 30장

**30:1** Sayings of Agur<br>The sayings of Agur son of Jakeh—an oracle:<br>This man declared to Ithiel,<br>to Ithiel and to Ucal:  
**30:2** “I am the most ignorant of men;<br>I do not have a man’s understanding.  
**30:3** I have not learned wisdom,<br>nor have I knowledge of the Holy One.  
**30:4** Who has gone up to heaven and come down?<br>Who has gathered up the wind in the hollow of his hands?<br>Who has wrapped up the waters in his cloak?<br>Who has established all the ends of the earth?<br>What is his name, and the name of his son?<br>Tell me if you know!  
**30:5** “Every word of God is flawless;<br>he is a shield to those who take refuge in him.  
**30:6** Do not add to his words,<br>or he will rebuke you and prove you a liar.  
**30:7** “Two things I ask of you, O Lord;<br>do not refuse me before I die:  
**30:8** Keep falsehood and lies far from me;<br>give me neither poverty nor riches,<br>but give me only my daily bread.  
**30:9** Otherwise, I may have too much and disown you<br>and say, ‘Who is the Lord?’<br>Or I may become poor and steal,<br>and so dishonor the name of my God.  
**30:10** “Do not slander a servant to his master,<br>or he will curse you, and you will pay for it.

**30:14** those whose teeth are swords<br>and whose jaws are set with knives<br>to devour the poor from the earth,<br>the needy from among mankind.  
**30:15** “The leech has two daughters.<br>‘Give! Give!’ they cry.<br>“There are three things that are never satisfied,<br>four that never say, ‘Enough!’:  
**30:16** the grave, the barren womb,<br>land, which is never satisfied with water,<br>and fire, which never says, ‘Enough!’  
**30:17** “The eye that mocks a father,<br>that scorns obedience to a mother,<br>will be pecked out by the ravens of the valley,<br>will be eaten by the vultures.  
**30:18** “There are three things that are too amazing for me,<br>four that I do not understand:
