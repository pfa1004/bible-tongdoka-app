## 1장

**1:1** The word of the Lord that came to Hosea son of Beeri during the reigns of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and during the reign of Jeroboam son of Jehoash king of Israel:  
**1:2** Hosea’s Wife and Children<br>When the Lord began to speak through Hosea, the Lord said to him, “Go, take to yourself an adulterous wife and children of unfaithfulness, because the land is guilty of the vilest adultery in departing from the Lord.”  
**1:3** So he married Gomer daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore him a son.  
**1:4** Then the Lord said to Hosea, “Call him Jezreel, because I will soon punish the house of Jehu for the massacre at Jezreel, and I will put an end to the kingdom of Israel.  
**1:5** In that day I will break Israel’s bow in the Valley of Jezreel.”  
**1:6** Gomer conceived again and gave birth to a daughter. Then the Lord said to Hosea, “Call her Lo-Ruhamah, for I will no longer show love to the house of Israel, that I should at all forgive them.  
**1:7** Yet I will show love to the house of Judah; and I will save them—not by bow, sword or battle, or by horses and horsemen, but by the Lord their God.”  
**1:8** After she had weaned Lo-Ruhamah, Gomer had another son.  
**1:9** Then the Lord said, “Call him Lo-Ammi, for you are not my people, and I am not your God.  
**1:10** “Yet the Israelites will be like the sand on the seashore, which cannot be measured or counted. In the place where it was said to them, ‘You are not my people,’ they will be called ‘sons of the living God.’  
**1:11** The people of Judah and the people of Israel will be reunited, and they will appoint one leader and will come up out of the land, for great will be the day of Jezreel.  

## 2장

**2:16** “In that day,” declares the Lord,<br>“you will call me ‘my husband’;<br>you will no longer call me ‘my master.’  
**2:17** I will remove the names of the Baals from her lips;<br>no longer will their names be invoked.  
**2:18** In that day I will make a covenant for them<br>with the beasts of the field and the birds of the air<br>and the creatures that move along the ground.<br>Bow and sword and battle<br>I will abolish from the land,<br>so that all may lie down in safety.  
**2:19** I will betroth you to me forever;<br>I will betroth you in righteousness and justice,<br>in love and compassion.  
**2:20** I will betroth you in faithfulness,<br>and you will acknowledge the Lord.  
**2:21** “In that day I will respond,”<br>declares the Lord—<br>“I will respond to the skies,<br>and they will respond to the earth;  
**2:22** and the earth will respond to the grain,<br>the new wine and oil,<br>and they will respond to Jezreel.  
**2:23** I will plant her for myself in the land;<br>I will show my love to the one I called ‘Not my loved one.’<br>I will say to those called ‘Not my people,’ ‘You are my people’;<br>and they will say, ‘You are my God.’”  

## 3장

**3:1** Hosea’s Reconciliation With His Wife<br>The Lord said to me, “Go, show your love to your wife again, though she is loved by another and is an adulteress. Love her as the Lord loves the Israelites, though they turn to other gods and love the sacred raisin cakes.”  
**3:2** So I bought her for fifteen shekels of silver and about a homer and a lethek of barley.  
**3:3** Then I told her, “You are to live with me many days; you must not be a prostitute or be intimate with any man, and I will live with you.”  
**3:4** For the Israelites will live many days without king or prince, without sacrifice or sacred stones, without ephod or idol.  
**3:5** Afterward the Israelites will return and seek the Lord their God and David their king. They will come trembling to the Lord and to his blessings in the last days.  

## 4장

**4:5** You stumble day and night,<br>and the prophets stumble with you.<br>So I will destroy your mother—  
**4:6** my people are destroyed from lack of knowledge.<br>“Because you have rejected knowledge,<br>I also reject you as my priests;<br>because you have ignored the law of your God,<br>I also will ignore your children.  
**4:7** The more the priests increased,<br>the more they sinned against me;<br>they exchanged their Glory for something disgraceful.  
**4:8** They feed on the sins of my people<br>and relish their wickedness.  
**4:9** And it will be: Like people, like priests.<br>I will punish both of them for their ways<br>and repay them for their deeds.  
**4:10** “They will eat but not have enough;<br>they will engage in prostitution but not increase,<br>because they have deserted the Lord<br>to give themselves  
**4:11** to prostitution,<br>to old wine and new,<br>which take away the understanding  
**4:12** of my people.<br>They consult a wooden idol<br>and are answered by a stick of wood.<br>A spirit of prostitution leads them astray;<br>they are unfaithful to their God.  
**4:13** They sacrifice on the mountaintops<br>and burn offerings on the hills,<br>under oak, poplar and terebinth,<br>where the shade is pleasant.<br>Therefore your daughters turn to prostitution<br>and your daughters-in-law to adultery.

**4:16** The Israelites are stubborn,<br>like a stubborn heifer.<br>How then can the Lord pasture them<br>like lambs in a meadow?  
**4:17** Ephraim is joined to idols;<br>leave him alone!  
**4:18** Even when their drinks are gone,<br>they continue their prostitution;<br>their rulers dearly love shameful ways.  
**4:19** A whirlwind will sweep them away,<br>and their sacrifices will bring them shame.

## 13장

**13:1** The Lord’s Anger Against Israel<br>When Ephraim spoke, men trembled;<br>he was exalted in Israel.<br>But he became guilty of Baal worship and died.  
**13:2** Now they sin more and more;<br>they make idols for themselves from their silver,<br>cleverly fashioned images,<br>all of them the work of craftsmen.<br>It is said of these people,<br>“They offer human sacrifice<br>and kiss the calf-idols.”  
**13:3** Therefore they will be like the morning mist,<br>like the early dew that disappears,<br>like chaff swirling from a threshing floor,<br>like smoke escaping through a window.  
**13:4** “But I am the Lord your God,<br>who brought you out of Egypt.<br>You shall acknowledge no God but me,<br>no Savior except me.  
**13:5** I cared for you in the desert,<br>in the land of burning heat.  
**13:6** When I fed them, they were satisfied;<br>when they were satisfied, they became proud;<br>then they forgot me.  
**13:7** So I will come upon them like a lion,<br>like a leopard I will lurk by the path.  
**13:8** Like a bear robbed of her cubs,<br>I will attack them and rip them open.<br>Like a lion I will devour them;<br>a wild animal will tear them apart.  
**13:9** “You are destroyed, O Israel,<br>because you are against me, against your helper.  
**13:10** Where is your king, that he may save you?<br>Where are your rulers in all your towns,<br>of whom you said,<br>‘Give me a king and princes’?  
**13:11** So in my anger I gave you a king,<br>and in my wrath I took him away.  
**13:12** The guilt of Ephraim is stored up,<br>his sins are kept on record.  
**13:13** Pains as of a woman in childbirth come to him,<br>but he is a child without wisdom;<br>when the time arrives,<br>he does not come to the opening of the womb.  
**13:14** “I will ransom them from the power of the grave;<br>I will redeem them from death.<br>Where, O death, are your plagues?<br>Where, O grave, is your destruction?<br>“I will have no compassion,  
**13:15** even though he thrives among his brothers.<br>An east wind from the Lord will come,<br>blowing in from the desert;<br>his spring will fail<br>and his well dry up.<br>His storehouse will be plundered<br>of all its treasures.  
**13:16** The people of Samaria must bear their guilt,<br>because they have rebelled against their God.<br>They will fall by the sword;<br>their little ones will be dashed to the ground,<br>their pregnant women ripped open.”  

## 14장

**14:1** Repentance to Bring Blessing<br>Return, O Israel, to the Lord your God.<br>Your sins have been your downfall!  
**14:2** Take words with you<br>and return to the Lord.<br>Say to him:<br>“Forgive all our sins<br>and receive us graciously,<br>that we may offer the fruit of our lips.  
**14:3** Assyria cannot save us;<br>we will not mount war-horses.<br>We will never again say ‘Our gods’<br>to what our own hands have made,<br>for in you the fatherless find compassion.”  
**14:4** “I will heal their waywardness<br>and love them freely,<br>for my anger has turned away from them.  
**14:5** I will be like the dew to Israel;<br>he will blossom like a lily.<br>Like a cedar of Lebanon<br>he will send down his roots;  
**14:6** his young shoots will grow.<br>His splendor will be like an olive tree,<br>his fragrance like a cedar of Lebanon.  
**14:7** Men will dwell again in his shade.<br>He will flourish like the grain.<br>He will blossom like a vine,<br>and his fame will be like the wine from Lebanon.  
**14:8** O Ephraim, what more have I to do with idols?<br>I will answer him and care for him.<br>I am like a green pine tree;<br>your fruitfulness comes from me.”  
**14:9** Who is wise? He will realize these things.<br>Who is discerning? He will understand them.<br>The ways of the Lord are right;<br>the righteous walk in them,<br>but the rebellious stumble in them.
