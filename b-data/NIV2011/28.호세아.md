# 28.호세아 (NIV2011)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Hosea son of Beeri during the reigns of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and during the reign of Jeroboam son of Jehoash<sup>①</sup> king of Israel:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Joash, </i>a variant of <i>Jehoash</i>  
**1:2** When the LORD began to speak through Hosea, the LORD said to him, “Go, marry a promiscuous woman and have children with her, for like an adulterous wife this land is guilty of unfaithfulness to the LORD.”  
**1:3** So he married Gomer daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore him a son.  
**1:4** Then the LORD said to Hosea, “Call him Jezreel, because I will soon punish the house of Jehu for the massacre at Jezreel, and I will put an end to the kingdom of Israel.  
**1:5** In that day I will break Israel’s bow in the Valley of Jezreel.”  
**1:6** Gomer conceived again and gave birth to a daughter. Then the LORD said to Hosea, “Call her Lo-Ruhamah (which means “not loved”), for I will no longer show love to Israel, that I should at all forgive them.  
**1:7** Yet I will show love to Judah; and I will save them—not by bow, sword or battle, or by horses and horsemen, but I, the LORD their God, will save them.”  
**1:8** After she had weaned Lo-Ruhamah, Gomer had another son.  
**1:9** Then the LORD said, “Call him Lo-Ammi (which means “not my people”), for you are not my people, and I am not your God.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>your I AM</i>  
**1:10** “Yet the Israelites will be like the sand on the seashore, which cannot be measured or counted. In the place where it was said to them, ‘You are not my people,’ they will be called ‘children of the living God.’  
**1:11** The people of Judah and the people of Israel will come together; they will appoint one leader and will come up out of the land, for great will be the day of Jezreel.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Hebrew texts 1:10,11 is numbered 2:1,2.

## 3장

**3:1** The LORD said to me, “Go, show your love to your wife again, though she is loved by another man and is an adulteress. Love her as the LORD loves the Israelites, though they turn to other gods and love the sacred raisin cakes.”  
**3:2** So I bought her for fifteen shekels<sup>①</sup> of silver and about a homer and a lethek<sup>②</sup> of barley.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>That is, about 6 ounces or about 170 grams <sup>②</sup>A homer and a lethek possibly weighed about 430 pounds or about 195 kilograms.  
**3:3** Then I told her, “You are to live with me many days; you must not be a prostitute or be intimate with any man, and I will behave the same way toward you.”  
**3:4** For the Israelites will live many days without king or prince, without sacrifice or sacred stones, without ephod or household gods.  
**3:5** Afterward the Israelites will return and seek the LORD their God and David their king. They will come trembling to the LORD and to his blessings in the last days.

**4:4** “But let no one bring a charge,<br>let no one accuse another,<br>for your people are like those<br>who bring charges against a priest.

**4:17** Ephraim is joined to idols;<br>leave him alone!  
**4:18** Even when their drinks are gone,<br>they continue their prostitution;<br>their rulers dearly love shameful ways.  
**4:19** A whirlwind will sweep them away,<br>and their sacrifices will bring them shame.

**11:3** It was I who taught Ephraim to walk,<br>taking them by the arms;<br>but they did not realize<br>it was I who healed them.  
**11:4** I led them with cords of human kindness,<br>with ties of love.<br>To them I was like one who lifts<br>a little child to the cheek,<br>and I bent down to feed them.   
**11:5** “Will they not return to Egypt<br>and will not Assyria rule over them<br>because they refuse to repent?  
**11:6** A sword will flash in their cities;<br>it will devour their false prophets<br>and put an end to their plans.  
**11:7** My people are determined to turn from me.<br>Even though they call me God Most High,<br>I will by no means exalt them.   
**11:8** “How can I give you up, Ephraim?<br>How can I hand you over, Israel?<br>How can I treat you like Admah?<br>How can I make you like Zeboyim?<br>My heart is changed within me;<br>all my compassion is aroused.  
**11:9** I will not carry out my fierce anger,<br>nor will I devastate Ephraim again.<br>For I am God, and not a man—<br>the Holy One among you.<br>I will not come against their cities.  
**11:10** They will follow the LORD;<br>he will roar like a lion.<br>When he roars,<br>his children will come trembling from the west.  
**11:11** They will come from Egypt,<br>trembling like sparrows,<br>from Assyria, fluttering like doves.<br>I will settle them in their homes,”<br>declares the LORD.  
**11:12** Ephraim has surrounded me with lies,<br>Israel with deceit.<br>And Judah is unruly against God,<br>even against the faithful Holy One.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Hebrew texts this verse (11:12) is numbered 12:1.  

## 12장

**12:1** <sup>①</sup>Ephraim feeds on the wind;<br>he pursues the east wind all day<br>and multiplies lies and violence.<br>He makes a treaty with Assyria<br>and sends olive oil to Egypt.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Hebrew texts 12:1-14 is numbered 12:2-15.  
**12:2** The LORD has a charge to bring against Judah;<br>he will punish Jacob<sup>①</sup> according to his ways<br>and repay him according to his deeds.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Jacob </i>means <i>he grasps the heel, </i>a Hebrew idiom for <i>he takes advantage of </i>or <i>he deceives.</i>  
**12:3** In the womb he grasped his brother’s heel;<br>as a man he struggled with God.  
**12:4** He struggled with the angel and overcame him;<br>he wept and begged for his favor.<br>He found him at Bethel<br>and talked with him there—  
**12:5** the LORD God Almighty,<br>the LORD is his name!  
**12:6** But you must return to your God;<br>maintain love and justice,<br>and wait for your God always.   
**12:7** The merchant uses dishonest scales<br>and loves to defraud.  
**12:8** Ephraim boasts,<br>“I am very rich; I have become wealthy.<br>With all my wealth they will not find in me<br>any iniquity or sin.”   
**12:9** “I have been the LORD your God<br>ever since you came out of Egypt;<br>I will make you live in tents again,<br>as in the days of your appointed festivals.  
**12:10** I spoke to the prophets,<br>gave them many visions<br>and told parables through them.”   
**12:11** Is Gilead wicked?<br>Its people are worthless!<br>Do they sacrifice bulls in Gilgal?<br>Their altars will be like piles of stones<br>on a plowed field.  
**12:12** Jacob fled to the country of Aram<sup>①</sup>;<br>Israel served to get a wife,<br>and to pay for her he tended sheep.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>That is, Northwest Mesopotamia  
**12:13** The LORD used a prophet to bring Israel up from Egypt,<br>by a prophet he cared for him.  
**12:14** But Ephraim has aroused his bitter anger;<br>his Lord will leave on him the guilt of his bloodshed<br>and will repay him for his contempt.  

## 13장

**13:1** When Ephraim spoke, people trembled;<br>he was exalted in Israel.<br>But he became guilty of Baal worship and died.  
**13:2** Now they sin more and more;<br>they make idols for themselves from their silver,<br>cleverly fashioned images,<br>all of them the work of craftsmen.<br>It is said of these people,<br>“They offer human sacrifices!<br>They kiss<sup>①</sup> calf-idols!”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>“Men who sacrifice / kiss</i>  
**13:3** Therefore they will be like the morning mist,<br>like the early dew that disappears,<br>like chaff swirling from a threshing floor,<br>like smoke escaping through a window.   
**13:4** “But I have been the LORD your God<br>ever since you came out of Egypt.<br>You shall acknowledge no God but me,<br>no Savior except me.  
**13:5** I cared for you in the wilderness,<br>in the land of burning heat.  
**13:6** When I fed them, they were satisfied;<br>when they were satisfied, they became proud;<br>then they forgot me.  
**13:7** So I will be like a lion to them,<br>like a leopard I will lurk by the path.  
**13:8** Like a bear robbed of her cubs,<br>I will attack them and rip them open;<br>like a lion I will devour them—<br>a wild animal will tear them apart.   
**13:9** “You are destroyed, Israel,<br>because you are against me, against your helper.  
**13:10** Where is your king, that he may save you?<br>Where are your rulers in all your towns,<br>of whom you said,<br>‘Give me a king and princes’?  
**13:11** So in my anger I gave you a king,<br>and in my wrath I took him away.  
**13:12** The guilt of Ephraim is stored up,<br>his sins are kept on record.  
**13:13** Pains as of a woman in childbirth come to him,<br>but he is a child without wisdom;<br>when the time arrives,<br>he doesn’t have the sense to come out of the womb.   
**13:14** “I will deliver this people from the power of the grave;<br>I will redeem them from death.<br>Where, O death, are your plagues?<br>Where, O grave, is your destruction? “I will have no compassion,  
**13:15** even though he thrives among his brothers.<br>An east wind from the LORD will come,<br>blowing in from the desert;<br>his spring will fail<br>and his well dry up.<br>His storehouse will be plundered<br>of all its treasures.  
**13:16** The people of Samaria must bear their guilt,<br>because they have rebelled against their God.<br>They will fall by the sword;<br>their little ones will be dashed to the ground,<br>their pregnant women ripped open.”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Hebrew texts this verse (13:16) is numbered 14:1.  

## 14장

**14:1** <sup>①</sup>Return, Israel, to the LORD your God.<br>Your sins have been your downfall!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Hebrew texts 14:1-9 is numbered 14:2-10.  
**14:2** Take words with you<br>and return to the LORD.<br>Say to him:<br>“Forgive all our sins<br>and receive us graciously,<br>that we may offer the fruit of our lips.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>offer our lips as sacrifices of bulls</i>  
**14:3** Assyria cannot save us;<br>we will not mount warhorses.<br>We will never again say ‘Our gods’<br>to what our own hands have made,<br>for in you the fatherless find compassion.”   
**14:4** “I will heal their waywardness<br>and love them freely,<br>for my anger has turned away from them.  
**14:5** I will be like the dew to Israel;<br>he will blossom like a lily.<br>Like a cedar of Lebanon<br>he will send down his roots;  
**14:6** his young shoots will grow.<br>His splendor will be like an olive tree,<br>his fragrance like a cedar of Lebanon.  
**14:7** People will dwell again in his shade;<br>they will flourish like the grain,<br>they will blossom like the vine—<br>Israel’s fame will be like the wine of Lebanon.  
**14:8** Ephraim, what more have I<sup>①</sup> to do with idols?<br>I will answer him and care for him.<br>I am like a flourishing juniper;<br>your fruitfulness comes from me.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or Hebrew; Septuagint <i>What more has Ephraim</i>  
**14:9** Who is wise? Let them realize these things.<br>Who is discerning? Let them understand.<br>The ways of the LORD are right;<br>the righteous walk in them,<br>but the rebellious stumble in them.
