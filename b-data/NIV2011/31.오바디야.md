# 31.오바디야 (NIV2011)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision of Obadiah. This is what the Sovereign LORD says about Edom—<br>We have heard a message from the LORD:<br>An envoy was sent to the nations to say,<br>“Rise, let us go against her for battle”—  
**1:2** “See, I will make you small among the nations;<br>you will be utterly despised.  
**1:3** The pride of your heart has deceived you,<br>you who live in the clefts of the rocks<sup>①</sup><br>and make your home on the heights,<br>you who say to yourself,<br>‘Who can bring me down to the ground?’<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>of Sela</i>  
**1:4** Though you soar like the eagle<br>and make your nest among the stars,<br>from there I will bring you down,”<br>declares the LORD.  
**1:5** “If thieves came to you,<br>if robbers in the night—<br>oh, what a disaster awaits you!—<br>would they not steal only as much as they wanted?<br>If grape pickers came to you,<br>would they not leave a few grapes?  
**1:6** But how Esau will be ransacked,<br>his hidden treasures pillaged!  
**1:7** All your allies will force you to the border;<br>your friends will deceive and overpower you;<br>those who eat your bread will set a trap for you,<sup>①</sup><br>but you will not detect it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew for this clause is uncertain.  
**1:8** “In that day,” declares the LORD,<br>“will I not destroy the wise men of Edom,<br>those of understanding in the mountains of Esau?  
**1:9** Your warriors, Teman, will be terrified,<br>and everyone in Esau’s mountains<br>will be cut down in the slaughter.  
**1:10** Because of the violence against your brother Jacob,<br>you will be covered with shame;<br>you will be destroyed forever.  
**1:11** On the day you stood aloof<br>while strangers carried off his wealth<br>and foreigners entered his gates<br>and cast lots for Jerusalem,<br>you were like one of them.  
**1:12** You should not gloat over your brother<br>in the day of his misfortune,<br>nor rejoice over the people of Judah<br>in the day of their destruction,<br>nor boast so much<br>in the day of their trouble.  
**1:13** You should not march through the gates of my people<br>in the day of their disaster,<br>nor gloat over them in their calamity<br>in the day of their disaster,<br>nor seize their wealth<br>in the day of their disaster.  
**1:14** You should not wait at the crossroads<br>to cut down their fugitives,<br>nor hand over their survivors<br>in the day of their trouble.   
**1:15** “The day of the LORD is near<br>for all nations.<br>As you have done, it will be done to you;<br>your deeds will return upon your own head.  
**1:16** Just as you drank on my holy hill,<br>so all the nations will drink continually;<br>they will drink and drink<br>and be as if they had never been.  
**1:17** But on Mount Zion will be deliverance;<br>it will be holy,<br>and Jacob will possess his inheritance.  
**1:18** Jacob will be a fire<br>and Joseph a flame;<br>Esau will be stubble,<br>and they will set him on fire and destroy him.<br>There will be no survivors<br>from Esau.”<br>The LORD has spoken.   
**1:19** People from the Negev will occupy<br>the mountains of Esau,<br>and people from the foothills will possess<br>the land of the Philistines.<br>They will occupy the fields of Ephraim and Samaria,<br>and Benjamin will possess Gilead.  
**1:20** This company of Israelite exiles who are in Canaan<br>will possess the land as far as Zarephath;<br>the exiles from Jerusalem who are in Sepharad<br>will possess the towns of the Negev.  
**1:21** Deliverers will go up on<sup>①</sup> Mount Zion<br>to govern the mountains of Esau.<br>And the kingdom will be the LORD’s.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>from</i>
