# 19.시편 (NIV2011)



## 1장

**1:1** Blessed is the one<br>who does not walk in step with the wicked<br>or stand in the way that sinners take<br>or sit in the company of mockers,  
**1:2** but whose delight is in the law of the LORD,<br>and who meditates on his law day and night.  
**1:3** That person is like a tree planted by streams of water,<br>which yields its fruit in season<br>and whose leaf does not wither—<br>whatever they do prospers.   
**1:4** Not so the wicked!<br>They are like chaff<br>that the wind blows away.  
**1:5** Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment,<br>nor sinners in the assembly of the righteous.   
**1:6** For the LORD watches over the way of the righteous,<br>but the way of the wicked leads to destruction.  

## 2장

**2:1** Why do the nations conspire<sup>①</sup><br>and the peoples plot in vain?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew; Septuagint <i>rage</i>  
**2:2** The kings of the earth rise up<br>and the rulers band together<br>against the LORD and against his anointed, saying,  
**2:3** “Let us break their chains<br>and throw off their shackles.”   
**2:4** The One enthroned in heaven laughs;<br>the Lord scoffs at them.  
**2:5** He rebukes them in his anger<br>and terrifies them in his wrath, saying,  
**2:6** “I have installed my king<br>on Zion, my holy mountain.”  
**2:7** I will proclaim the LORD’s decree:<br>He said to me, “You are my son;<br>today I have become your father.  
**2:8** Ask me,<br>and I will make the nations your inheritance,<br>the ends of the earth your possession.  
**2:9** You will break them with a rod of iron<sup>①</sup>;<br>you will dash them to pieces like pottery.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>will rule them with an iron scepter </i>(see Septuagint and Syriac)  
**2:10** Therefore, you kings, be wise;<br>be warned, you rulers of the earth.  
**2:11** Serve the LORD with fear<br>and celebrate his rule with trembling.  
**2:12** Kiss his son, or he will be angry<br>and your way will lead to your destruction,<br>for his wrath can flare up in a moment.<br>Blessed are all who take refuge in him.  

## 3장

**3:2** Many are saying of me,<br>“God will not deliver him.”<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Hebrew has <i>Selah </i>(a word of uncertain meaning) here and at the end of verses 4 and 8.

**3:4** I call out to the LORD,<br>and he answers me from his holy mountain.   
**3:5** I lie down and sleep;<br>I wake again, because the LORD sustains me.  
**3:6** I will not fear though tens of thousands<br>assail me on every side.   
**3:7** Arise, LORD!<br>Deliver me, my God!<br>Strike all my enemies on the jaw;<br>break the teeth of the wicked.   
**3:8** From the LORD comes deliverance.<br>May your blessing be on your people.
