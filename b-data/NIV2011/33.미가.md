# 33.미가 (NIV2011)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Micah of Moresheth during the reigns of Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah—the vision he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.  
**1:2** Hear, you peoples, all of you,<br>listen, earth and all who live in it,<br>that the Sovereign LORD may bear witness against you,<br>the Lord from his holy temple.  
**1:3** Look! The LORD is coming from his dwelling place;<br>he comes down and treads on the heights of the earth.  
**1:4** The mountains melt beneath him<br>and the valleys split apart,<br>like wax before the fire,<br>like water rushing down a slope.  
**1:5** All this is because of Jacob’s transgression,<br>because of the sins of the people of Israel.<br>What is Jacob’s transgression?<br>Is it not Samaria?<br>What is Judah’s high place?<br>Is it not Jerusalem?   
**1:6** “Therefore I will make Samaria a heap of rubble,<br>a place for planting vineyards.<br>I will pour her stones into the valley<br>and lay bare her foundations.  
**1:7** All her idols will be broken to pieces;<br>all her temple gifts will be burned with fire;<br>I will destroy all her images.<br>Since she gathered her gifts from the wages of prostitutes,<br>as the wages of prostitutes they will again be used.”  
**1:8** Because of this I will weep and wail;<br>I will go about barefoot and naked.<br>I will howl like a jackal<br>and moan like an owl.  
**1:9** For Samaria’s plague is incurable;<br>it has spread to Judah.<br>It has reached the very gate of my people,<br>even to Jerusalem itself.  
**1:10** Tell it not in Gath<sup>①</sup>;<br>weep not at all.<br>In Beth Ophrah<sup>②</sup><br>roll in the dust.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Gath </i>sounds like the Hebrew for <i>tell</i><i>.</i> <sup>②</sup><i>Beth Ophrah </i>means <i>house of dust.</i>  
**1:11** Pass by naked and in shame,<br>you who live in Shaphir.<sup>①</sup><br>Those who live in Zaanan<sup>②</sup><br>will not come out.<br>Beth Ezel is in mourning;<br>it no longer protects you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Shaphir </i>means <i>pleasant.</i> <sup>②</sup><i>Zaanan </i>sounds like the Hebrew for <i>come out</i><i>.</i>  
**1:12** Those who live in Maroth<sup>①</sup> writhe in pain,<br>waiting for relief,<br>because disaster has come from the LORD,<br>even to the gate of Jerusalem.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Maroth </i>sounds like the Hebrew for <i>bitter.</i>  
**1:13** You who live in Lachish,<br>harness fast horses to the chariot.<br>You are where the sin of Daughter Zion began,<br>for the transgressions of Israel were found in you.  
**1:14** Therefore you will give parting gifts<br>to Moresheth Gath.<br>The town of Akzib<sup>①</sup> will prove deceptive<br>to the kings of Israel.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Akzib </i>means <i>deception.</i>  
**1:15** I will bring a conqueror against you<br>who live in Mareshah.<sup>①</sup><br>The nobles of Israel<br>will flee to Adullam.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Mareshah </i>sounds like the Hebrew for <i>conqueror</i><i>.</i>  
**1:16** Shave your head in mourning<br>for the children in whom you delight;<br>make yourself as bald as the vulture,<br>for they will go from you into exile.  

## 2장

**2:1** Woe to those who plan iniquity,<br>to those who plot evil on their beds!<br>At morning’s light they carry it out<br>because it is in their power to do it.  
**2:2** They covet fields and seize them,<br>and houses, and take them.<br>They defraud people of their homes,<br>they rob them of their inheritance.  
**2:3** Therefore, the LORD says:<br>“I am planning disaster against this people,<br>from which you cannot save yourselves.<br>You will no longer walk proudly,<br>for it will be a time of calamity.  
**2:4** In that day people will ridicule you;<br>they will taunt you with this mournful song:<br>‘We are utterly ruined;<br>my people’s possession is divided up.<br>He takes it from me!<br>He assigns our fields to traitors.’ ”   
**2:5** Therefore you will have no one in the assembly of the LORD<br>to divide the land by lot.  
**2:6** “Do not prophesy,” their prophets say.<br>“Do not prophesy about these things;<br>disgrace will not overtake us.”  
**2:7** You descendants of Jacob, should it be said,<br>“Does the LORD become<sup>①</sup> impatient?<br>Does he do such things?” “Do not my words do good<br>to the one whose ways are upright?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Is the Spirit of the LORD</i>  
**2:8** Lately my people have risen up<br>like an enemy.<br>You strip off the rich robe<br>from those who pass by without a care,<br>like men returning from battle.  
**2:9** You drive the women of my people<br>from their pleasant homes.<br>You take away my blessing<br>from their children forever.  
**2:10** Get up, go away!<br>For this is not your resting place,<br>because it is defiled,<br>it is ruined, beyond all remedy.  
**2:11** If a liar and deceiver comes and says,<br>‘I will prophesy for you plenty of wine and beer,’<br>that would be just the prophet for this people!  
**2:12** “I will surely gather all of you, Jacob;<br>I will surely bring together the remnant of Israel.<br>I will bring them together like sheep in a pen,<br>like a flock in its pasture;<br>the place will throng with people.  
**2:13** The One who breaks open the way will go up before them;<br>they will break through the gate and go out.<br>Their King will pass through before them,<br>the LORD at their head.”  

## 3장

**3:1** Then I said,<br>“Listen, you leaders of Jacob,<br>you rulers of Israel.<br>Should you not embrace justice,  
**3:2** you who hate good and love evil;<br>who tear the skin from my people<br>and the flesh from their bones;  
**3:3** who eat my people’s flesh,<br>strip off their skin<br>and break their bones in pieces;<br>who chop them up like meat for the pan,<br>like flesh for the pot?”   
**3:4** Then they will cry out to the LORD,<br>but he will not answer them.<br>At that time he will hide his face from them<br>because of the evil they have done.  
**3:5** This is what the LORD says:<br>“As for the prophets<br>who lead my people astray,<br>they proclaim ‘peace’<br>if they have something to eat,<br>but prepare to wage war against anyone<br>who refuses to feed them.  
**3:6** Therefore night will come over you, without visions,<br>and darkness, without divination.<br>The sun will set for the prophets,<br>and the day will go dark for them.  
**3:7** The seers will be ashamed<br>and the diviners disgraced.<br>They will all cover their faces<br>because there is no answer from God.”  
**3:8** But as for me, I am filled with power,<br>with the Spirit of the LORD,<br>and with justice and might,<br>to declare to Jacob his transgression,<br>to Israel his sin.   
**3:9** Hear this, you leaders of Jacob,<br>you rulers of Israel,<br>who despise justice<br>and distort all that is right;  
**3:10** who build Zion with bloodshed,<br>and Jerusalem with wickedness.  
**3:11** Her leaders judge for a bribe,<br>her priests teach for a price,<br>and her prophets tell fortunes for money.<br>Yet they look for the LORD’s support and say,<br>“Is not the LORD among us?<br>No disaster will come upon us.”  
**3:12** Therefore because of you,<br>Zion will be plowed like a field,<br>Jerusalem will become a heap of rubble,<br>the temple hill a mound overgrown with thickets.  

## 4장

**4:1** In the last days<br>the mountain of the LORD’s temple will be established<br>as the highest of the mountains;<br>it will be exalted above the hills,<br>and peoples will stream to it.  
**4:2** Many nations will come and say,<br>“Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD,<br>to the temple of the God of Jacob.<br>He will teach us his ways,<br>so that we may walk in his paths.”<br>The law will go out from Zion,<br>the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.  
**4:3** He will judge between many peoples<br>and will settle disputes for strong nations far and wide.<br>They will beat their swords into plowshares<br>and their spears into pruning hooks.<br>Nation will not take up sword against nation,<br>nor will they train for war anymore.  
**4:4** Everyone will sit under their own vine<br>and under their own fig tree,<br>and no one will make them afraid,<br>for the LORD Almighty has spoken.  
**4:5** All the nations may walk<br>in the name of their gods,<br>but we will walk in the name of the LORD<br>our God for ever and ever.  
**4:6** “In that day,” declares the LORD,<br>“I will gather the lame;<br>I will assemble the exiles<br>and those I have brought to grief.  
**4:7** I will make the lame my remnant,<br>those driven away a strong nation.<br>The LORD will rule over them in Mount Zion<br>from that day and forever.  
**4:8** As for you, watchtower of the flock,<br>stronghold<sup>①</sup> of Daughter Zion,<br>the former dominion will be restored to you;<br>kingship will come to Daughter Jerusalem.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>hill</i>  
**4:9** Why do you now cry aloud—<br>have you no king<sup>①</sup>?<br>Has your ruler<sup>②</sup> perished,<br>that pain seizes you like that of a woman in labor?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>King</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>Ruler</i>  
**4:10** Writhe in agony, Daughter Zion,<br>like a woman in labor,<br>for now you must leave the city<br>to camp in the open field.<br>You will go to Babylon;<br>there you will be rescued.<br>There the LORD will redeem you<br>out of the hand of your enemies.   
**4:11** But now many nations<br>are gathered against you.<br>They say, “Let her be defiled,<br>let our eyes gloat over Zion!”  
**4:12** But they do not know<br>the thoughts of the LORD;<br>they do not understand his plan,<br>that he has gathered them like sheaves to the threshing floor.  
**4:13** “Rise and thresh, Daughter Zion,<br>for I will give you horns of iron;<br>I will give you hooves of bronze,<br>and you will break to pieces many nations.”<br>You will devote their ill-gotten gains to the LORD,<br>their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.  

## 5장

**5:1** <sup>①</sup>Marshal your troops now, city of troops,<br>for a siege is laid against us.<br>They will strike Israel’s ruler<br>on the cheek with a rod.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Hebrew texts 5:1 is numbered 4:14, and 5:2-15 is numbered 5:1-14.  
**5:2** “But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah,<br>though you are small among the clans<sup>①</sup> of Judah,<br>out of you will come for me<br>one who will be ruler over Israel,<br>whose origins are from of old,<br>from ancient times.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>rulers</i>  
**5:3** Therefore Israel will be abandoned<br>until the time when she who is in labor bears a son,<br>and the rest of his brothers return<br>to join the Israelites.   
**5:4** He will stand and shepherd his flock<br>in the strength of the LORD,<br>in the majesty of the name of the LORD his God.<br>And they will live securely, for then his greatness<br>will reach to the ends of the earth.   
**5:5** And he will be our peace<br>when the Assyrians invade our land<br>and march through our fortresses.<br>We will raise against them seven shepherds,<br>even eight commanders,  
**5:6** who will rule<sup>①</sup> the land of Assyria with the sword,<br>the land of Nimrod with drawn sword.<sup>②</sup><br>He will deliver us from the Assyrians<br>when they invade our land<br>and march across our borders.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>crush</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>Nimrod in its gates</i>  
**5:7** The remnant of Jacob will be<br>in the midst of many peoples<br>like dew from the LORD,<br>like showers on the grass,<br>which do not wait for anyone<br>or depend on man.  
**5:8** The remnant of Jacob will be among the nations,<br>in the midst of many peoples,<br>like a lion among the beasts of the forest,<br>like a young lion among flocks of sheep,<br>which mauls and mangles as it goes,<br>and no one can rescue.  
**5:9** Your hand will be lifted up in triumph over your enemies,<br>and all your foes will be destroyed.  
**5:10** “In that day,” declares the LORD,<br>“I will destroy your horses from among you<br>and demolish your chariots.  
**5:11** I will destroy the cities of your land<br>and tear down all your strongholds.  
**5:12** I will destroy your witchcraft<br>and you will no longer cast spells.  
**5:13** I will destroy your idols<br>and your sacred stones from among you;<br>you will no longer bow down<br>to the work of your hands.  
**5:14** I will uproot from among you your Asherah poles<sup>①</sup><br>when I demolish your cities.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>That is, wooden symbols of the goddess Asherah  
**5:15** I will take vengeance in anger and wrath<br>on the nations that have not obeyed me.”  

## 6장

**6:1** Listen to what the LORD says:<br>“Stand up, plead my case before the mountains;<br>let the hills hear what you have to say.   
**6:2** “Hear, you mountains, the LORD’s accusation;<br>listen, you everlasting foundations of the earth.<br>For the LORD has a case against his people;<br>he is lodging a charge against Israel.   
**6:3** “My people, what have I done to you?<br>How have I burdened you? Answer me.  
**6:4** I brought you up out of Egypt<br>and redeemed you from the land of slavery.<br>I sent Moses to lead you,<br>also Aaron and Miriam.  
**6:5** My people, remember<br>what Balak king of Moab plotted<br>and what Balaam son of Beor answered.<br>Remember your journey from Shittim to Gilgal,<br>that you may know the righteous acts of the LORD.”   
**6:6** With what shall I come before the LORD<br>and bow down before the exalted God?<br>Shall I come before him with burnt offerings,<br>with calves a year old?  
**6:7** Will the LORD be pleased with thousands of rams,<br>with ten thousand rivers of olive oil?<br>Shall I offer my firstborn for my transgression,<br>the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?  
**6:8** He has shown you, O mortal, what is good.<br>And what does the LORD require of you?<br>To act justly and to love mercy<br>and to walk humbly<sup>①</sup> with your God.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>prudently</i>  
**6:9** Listen! The LORD is calling to the city—<br>and to fear your name is wisdom—<br>“Heed the rod and the One who appointed it.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew for this line is uncertain.  
**6:10** Am I still to forget your ill-gotten treasures, you wicked house,<br>and the short ephah,<sup>①</sup> which is accursed?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>An ephah was a dry measure.  
**6:11** Shall I acquit someone with dishonest scales,<br>with a bag of false weights?  
**6:12** Your rich people are violent;<br>your inhabitants are liars<br>and their tongues speak deceitfully.  
**6:13** Therefore, I have begun to destroy you,<br>to ruin<sup>①</sup> you because of your sins.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Therefore, I will make you ill and destroy you; / I will ruin</i>  
**6:14** You will eat but not be satisfied;<br>your stomach will still be empty.<sup>①</sup><br>You will store up but save nothing,<br>because what you save<sup>②</sup> I will give to the sword.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew for this word is uncertain. <sup>②</sup>Or <i>You will press toward birth but not give birth, / and what you bring to birth</i>  
**6:15** You will plant but not harvest;<br>you will press olives but not use the oil,<br>you will crush grapes but not drink the wine.  
**6:16** You have observed the statutes of Omri<br>and all the practices of Ahab’s house;<br>you have followed their traditions.<br>Therefore I will give you over to ruin<br>and your people to derision;<br>you will bear the scorn of the nations.<sup>①</sup>”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Septuagint; Hebrew <i>scorn due my people</i>  

## 7장

**7:1** What misery is mine!<br>I am like one who gathers summer fruit<br>at the gleaning of the vineyard;<br>there is no cluster of grapes to eat,<br>none of the early figs that I crave.  
**7:2** The faithful have been swept from the land;<br>not one upright person remains.<br>Everyone lies in wait to shed blood;<br>they hunt each other with nets.  
**7:3** Both hands are skilled in doing evil;<br>the ruler demands gifts,<br>the judge accepts bribes,<br>the powerful dictate what they desire—<br>they all conspire together.  
**7:4** The best of them is like a brier,<br>the most upright worse than a thorn hedge.<br>The day God visits you has come,<br>the day your watchmen sound the alarm.<br>Now is the time of your confusion.  
**7:5** Do not trust a neighbor;<br>put no confidence in a friend.<br>Even with the woman who lies in your embrace<br>guard the words of your lips.  
**7:6** For a son dishonors his father,<br>a daughter rises up against her mother,<br>a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law—<br>a man’s enemies are the members of his own household.   
**7:7** But as for me, I watch in hope for the LORD,<br>I wait for God my Savior;<br>my God will hear me.  
**7:8** Do not gloat over me, my enemy!<br>Though I have fallen, I will rise.<br>Though I sit in darkness,<br>the LORD will be my light.  
**7:9** Because I have sinned against him,<br>I will bear the LORD’s wrath,<br>until he pleads my case<br>and upholds my cause.<br>He will bring me out into the light;<br>I will see his righteousness.  
**7:10** Then my enemy will see it<br>and will be covered with shame,<br>she who said to me,<br>“Where is the LORD your God?”<br>My eyes will see her downfall;<br>even now she will be trampled underfoot<br>like mire in the streets.   
**7:11** The day for building your walls will come,<br>the day for extending your boundaries.  
**7:12** In that day people will come to you<br>from Assyria and the cities of Egypt,<br>even from Egypt to the Euphrates<br>and from sea to sea<br>and from mountain to mountain.  
**7:13** The earth will become desolate because of its inhabitants,<br>as the result of their deeds.  
**7:14** Shepherd your people with your staff,<br>the flock of your inheritance,<br>which lives by itself in a forest,<br>in fertile pasturelands.<sup>①</sup><br>Let them feed in Bashan and Gilead<br>as in days long ago.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>in the middle of Carmel</i>  
**7:15** “As in the days when you came out of Egypt,<br>I will show them my wonders.”   
**7:16** Nations will see and be ashamed,<br>deprived of all their power.<br>They will put their hands over their mouths<br>and their ears will become deaf.  
**7:17** They will lick dust like a snake,<br>like creatures that crawl on the ground.<br>They will come trembling out of their dens;<br>they will turn in fear to the LORD our God<br>and will be afraid of you.  
**7:18** Who is a God like you,<br>who pardons sin and forgives the transgression<br>of the remnant of his inheritance?<br>You do not stay angry forever<br>but delight to show mercy.  
**7:19** You will again have compassion on us;<br>you will tread our sins underfoot<br>and hurl all our iniquities into the depths of the sea.  
**7:20** You will be faithful to Jacob,<br>and show love to Abraham,<br>as you pledged on oath to our ancestors<br>in days long ago.
