# 20.잠언 (NIV2011)



## 1장

**1:1** The proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel:  
**1:2** for gaining wisdom and instruction;<br>for understanding words of insight;  
**1:3** for receiving instruction in prudent behavior,<br>doing what is right and just and fair;  
**1:4** for giving prudence to those who are simple,<sup>①</sup><br>knowledge and discretion to the young—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Hebrew word rendered <i>simple </i>in Proverbs denotes a person who is gullible, without moral direction and inclined to evil.  
**1:5** let the wise listen and add to their learning,<br>and let the discerning get guidance—  
**1:6** for understanding proverbs and parables,<br>the sayings and riddles of the wise.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>understanding a proverb, namely, a parable, / and the sayings of the wise, their riddles</i>  
**1:7** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge,<br>but fools<sup>①</sup> despise wisdom and instruction.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Hebrew words rendered <i>fool </i>in Proverbs, and often elsewhere in the Old Testament, denote a person who is morally deficient.  
**1:8** Listen, my son, to your father’s instruction<br>and do not forsake your mother’s teaching.  
**1:9** They are a garland to grace your head<br>and a chain to adorn your neck.   
**1:10** My son, if sinful men entice you,<br>do not give in to them.  
**1:11** If they say, “Come along with us;<br>let’s lie in wait for innocent blood,<br>let’s ambush some harmless soul;  
**1:12** let’s swallow them alive, like the grave,<br>and whole, like those who go down to the pit;  
**1:13** we will get all sorts of valuable things<br>and fill our houses with plunder;  
**1:14** cast lots with us;<br>we will all share the loot”—  
**1:15** my son, do not go along with them,<br>do not set foot on their paths;  
**1:16** for their feet rush into evil,<br>they are swift to shed blood.  
**1:17** How useless to spread a net<br>where every bird can see it!  
**1:18** These men lie in wait for their own blood;<br>they ambush only themselves!  
**1:19** Such are the paths of all who go after ill-gotten gain;<br>it takes away the life of those who get it.  
**1:20** Out in the open wisdom calls aloud,<br>she raises her voice in the public square;  
**1:21** on top of the wall<sup>①</sup> she cries out,<br>at the city gate she makes her speech:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Septuagint; Hebrew <i>/ at noisy street corners</i>  
**1:22** “How long will you who are simple love your simple ways?<br>How long will mockers delight in mockery<br>and fools hate knowledge?  
**1:23** Repent at my rebuke!<br>Then I will pour out my thoughts to you,<br>I will make known to you my teachings.  
**1:24** But since you refuse to listen when I call<br>and no one pays attention when I stretch out my hand,  
**1:25** since you disregard all my advice<br>and do not accept my rebuke,  
**1:26** I in turn will laugh when disaster strikes you;<br>I will mock when calamity overtakes you—  
**1:27** when calamity overtakes you like a storm,<br>when disaster sweeps over you like a whirlwind,<br>when distress and trouble overwhelm you.   
**1:28** “Then they will call to me but I will not answer;<br>they will look for me but will not find me,  
**1:29** since they hated knowledge<br>and did not choose to fear the LORD.  
**1:30** Since they would not accept my advice<br>and spurned my rebuke,  
**1:31** they will eat the fruit of their ways<br>and be filled with the fruit of their schemes.  
**1:32** For the waywardness of the simple will kill them,<br>and the complacency of fools will destroy them;  
**1:33** but whoever listens to me will live in safety<br>and be at ease, without fear of harm.”  

## 2장

**2:1** My son, if you accept my words<br>and store up my commands within you,  
**2:2** turning your ear to wisdom<br>and applying your heart to understanding—  
**2:3** indeed, if you call out for insight<br>and cry aloud for understanding,  
**2:4** and if you look for it as for silver<br>and search for it as for hidden treasure,  
**2:5** then you will understand the fear of the LORD<br>and find the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For the LORD gives wisdom;<br>from his mouth come knowledge and understanding.  
**2:7** He holds success in store for the upright,<br>he is a shield to those whose walk is blameless,  
**2:8** for he guards the course of the just<br>and protects the way of his faithful ones.   
**2:9** Then you will understand what is right and just<br>and fair—every good path.  
**2:10** For wisdom will enter your heart,<br>and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul.  
**2:11** Discretion will protect you,<br>and understanding will guard you.   
**2:12** Wisdom will save you from the ways of wicked men,<br>from men whose words are perverse,  
**2:13** who have left the straight paths<br>to walk in dark ways,  
**2:14** who delight in doing wrong<br>and rejoice in the perverseness of evil,  
**2:15** whose paths are crooked<br>and who are devious in their ways.   
**2:16** Wisdom will save you also from the adulterous woman,<br>from the wayward woman with her seductive words,  
**2:17** who has left the partner of her youth<br>and ignored the covenant she made before God.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>covenant of her God</i>  
**2:18** Surely her house leads down to death<br>and her paths to the spirits of the dead.  
**2:19** None who go to her return<br>or attain the paths of life.   
**2:20** Thus you will walk in the ways of the good<br>and keep to the paths of the righteous.  
**2:21** For the upright will live in the land,<br>and the blameless will remain in it;  
**2:22** but the wicked will be cut off from the land,<br>and the unfaithful will be torn from it.  

## 3장

**3:1** My son, do not forget my teaching,<br>but keep my commands in your heart,  
**3:2** for they will prolong your life many years<br>and bring you peace and prosperity.   
**3:3** Let love and faithfulness never leave you;<br>bind them around your neck,<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**3:4** Then you will win favor and a good name<br>in the sight of God and man.   
**3:5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart<br>and lean not on your own understanding;  
**3:6** in all your ways submit to him,<br>and he will make your paths straight.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>will direct your paths</i>  
**3:7** Do not be wise in your own eyes;<br>fear the LORD and shun evil.  
**3:8** This will bring health to your body<br>and nourishment to your bones.   
**3:9** Honor the LORD with your wealth,<br>with the firstfruits of all your crops;  
**3:10** then your barns will be filled to overflowing,<br>and your vats will brim over with new wine.   
**3:11** My son, do not despise the LORD’s discipline,<br>and do not resent his rebuke,  
**3:12** because the LORD disciplines those he loves,<br>as a father the son he delights in.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew; Septuagint <i>loves, / and he chastens everyone he accepts as his child</i>  
**3:13** Blessed are those who find wisdom,<br>those who gain understanding,  
**3:14** for she is more profitable than silver<br>and yields better returns than gold.  
**3:15** She is more precious than rubies;<br>nothing you desire can compare with her.  
**3:16** Long life is in her right hand;<br>in her left hand are riches and honor.  
**3:17** Her ways are pleasant ways,<br>and all her paths are peace.  
**3:18** She is a tree of life to those who take hold of her;<br>those who hold her fast will be blessed.   
**3:19** By wisdom the LORD laid the earth’s foundations,<br>by understanding he set the heavens in place;  
**3:20** by his knowledge the watery depths were divided,<br>and the clouds let drop the dew.   
**3:21** My son, do not let wisdom and understanding out of your sight,<br>preserve sound judgment and discretion;  
**3:22** they will be life for you,<br>an ornament to grace your neck.  
**3:23** Then you will go on your way in safety,<br>and your foot will not stumble.  
**3:24** When you lie down, you will not be afraid;<br>when you lie down, your sleep will be sweet.  
**3:25** Have no fear of sudden disaster<br>or of the ruin that overtakes the wicked,  
**3:26** for the LORD will be at your side<br>and will keep your foot from being snared.   
**3:27** Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due,<br>when it is in your power to act.  
**3:28** Do not say to your neighbor,<br>“Come back tomorrow and I’ll give it to you”—<br>when you already have it with you.  
**3:29** Do not plot harm against your neighbor,<br>who lives trustfully near you.  
**3:30** Do not accuse anyone for no reason—<br>when they have done you no harm.   
**3:31** Do not envy the violent<br>or choose any of their ways.   
**3:32** For the LORD detests the perverse<br>but takes the upright into his confidence.  
**3:33** The LORD’s curse is on the house of the wicked,<br>but he blesses the home of the righteous.  
**3:34** He mocks proud mockers<br>but shows favor to the humble and oppressed.  
**3:35** The wise inherit honor,<br>but fools get only shame.  

## 4장

**4:1** Listen, my sons, to a father’s instruction;<br>pay attention and gain understanding.  
**4:2** I give you sound learning,<br>so do not forsake my teaching.  
**4:3** For I too was a son to my father,<br>still tender, and cherished by my mother.  
**4:4** Then he taught me, and he said to me,<br>“Take hold of my words with all your heart;<br>keep my commands, and you will live.  
**4:5** Get wisdom, get understanding;<br>do not forget my words or turn away from them.  
**4:6** Do not forsake wisdom, and she will protect you;<br>love her, and she will watch over you.  
**4:7** The beginning of wisdom is this: Get<sup>①</sup> wisdom.<br>Though it cost all you have,<sup>②</sup> get understanding.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Wisdom is supreme; therefore get</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>wisdom. / Whatever else you get</i>  
**4:8** Cherish her, and she will exalt you;<br>embrace her, and she will honor you.  
**4:9** She will give you a garland to grace your head<br>and present you with a glorious crown.”   
**4:10** Listen, my son, accept what I say,<br>and the years of your life will be many.  
**4:11** I instruct you in the way of wisdom<br>and lead you along straight paths.  
**4:12** When you walk, your steps will not be hampered;<br>when you run, you will not stumble.  
**4:13** Hold on to instruction, do not let it go;<br>guard it well, for it is your life.  
**4:14** Do not set foot on the path of the wicked<br>or walk in the way of evildoers.  
**4:15** Avoid it, do not travel on it;<br>turn from it and go on your way.  
**4:16** For they cannot rest until they do evil;<br>they are robbed of sleep till they make someone stumble.  
**4:17** They eat the bread of wickedness<br>and drink the wine of violence.   
**4:18** The path of the righteous is like the morning sun,<br>shining ever brighter till the full light of day.  
**4:19** But the way of the wicked is like deep darkness;<br>they do not know what makes them stumble.   
**4:20** My son, pay attention to what I say;<br>turn your ear to my words.  
**4:21** Do not let them out of your sight,<br>keep them within your heart;  
**4:22** for they are life to those who find them<br>and health to one’s whole body.  
**4:23** Above all else, guard your heart,<br>for everything you do flows from it.  
**4:24** Keep your mouth free of perversity;<br>keep corrupt talk far from your lips.  
**4:25** Let your eyes look straight ahead;<br>fix your gaze directly before you.  
**4:26** Give careful thought to the<sup>①</sup> paths for your feet<br>and be steadfast in all your ways.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Make level</i>  
**4:27** Do not turn to the right or the left;<br>keep your foot from evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** My son, pay attention to my wisdom,<br>turn your ear to my words of insight,  
**5:2** that you may maintain discretion<br>and your lips may preserve knowledge.  
**5:3** For the lips of the adulterous woman drip honey,<br>and her speech is smoother than oil;  
**5:4** but in the end she is bitter as gall,<br>sharp as a double-edged sword.  
**5:5** Her feet go down to death;<br>her steps lead straight to the grave.  
**5:6** She gives no thought to the way of life;<br>her paths wander aimlessly, but she does not know it.   
**5:7** Now then, my sons, listen to me;<br>do not turn aside from what I say.  
**5:8** Keep to a path far from her,<br>do not go near the door of her house,  
**5:9** lest you lose your honor to others<br>and your dignity<sup>①</sup> to one who is cruel,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>years</i>  
**5:10** lest strangers feast on your wealth<br>and your toil enrich the house of another.  
**5:11** At the end of your life you will groan,<br>when your flesh and body are spent.  
**5:12** You will say, “How I hated discipline!<br>How my heart spurned correction!  
**5:13** I would not obey my teachers<br>or turn my ear to my instructors.  
**5:14** And I was soon in serious trouble<br>in the assembly of God’s people.”   
**5:15** Drink water from your own cistern,<br>running water from your own well.  
**5:16** Should your springs overflow in the streets,<br>your streams of water in the public squares?  
**5:17** Let them be yours alone,<br>never to be shared with strangers.  
**5:18** May your fountain be blessed,<br>and may you rejoice in the wife of your youth.  
**5:19** A loving doe, a graceful deer—<br>may her breasts satisfy you always,<br>may you ever be intoxicated with her love.  
**5:20** Why, my son, be intoxicated with another man’s wife?<br>Why embrace the bosom of a wayward woman?   
**5:21** For your ways are in full view of the LORD,<br>and he examines all your paths.  
**5:22** The evil deeds of the wicked ensnare them;<br>the cords of their sins hold them fast.  
**5:23** For lack of discipline they will die,<br>led astray by their own great folly.  

## 6장

**6:1** My son, if you have put up security for your neighbor,<br>if you have shaken hands in pledge for a stranger,  
**6:2** you have been trapped by what you said,<br>ensnared by the words of your mouth.  
**6:3** So do this, my son, to free yourself,<br>since you have fallen into your neighbor’s hands:<br>Go—to the point of exhaustion—<sup>①</sup><br>and give your neighbor no rest!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Go and humble yourself,</i>  
**6:4** Allow no sleep to your eyes,<br>no slumber to your eyelids.  
**6:5** Free yourself, like a gazelle from the hand of the hunter,<br>like a bird from the snare of the fowler.   
**6:6** Go to the ant, you sluggard;<br>consider its ways and be wise!  
**6:7** It has no commander,<br>no overseer or ruler,  
**6:8** yet it stores its provisions in summer<br>and gathers its food at harvest.   
**6:9** How long will you lie there, you sluggard?<br>When will you get up from your sleep?  
**6:10** A little sleep, a little slumber,<br>a little folding of the hands to rest—  
**6:11** and poverty will come on you like a thief<br>and scarcity like an armed man.   
**6:12** A troublemaker and a villain,<br>who goes about with a corrupt mouth,  
**6:13** who winks maliciously with his eye,<br>signals with his feet<br>and motions with his fingers,  
**6:14** who plots evil with deceit in his heart—<br>he always stirs up conflict.  
**6:15** Therefore disaster will overtake him in an instant;<br>he will suddenly be destroyed—without remedy.   
**6:16** There are six things the LORD hates,<br>seven that are detestable to him:  
**6:17** haughty eyes,<br>a lying tongue,<br>hands that shed innocent blood,  
**6:18** a heart that devises wicked schemes,<br>feet that are quick to rush into evil,  
**6:19** a false witness who pours out lies<br>and a person who stirs up conflict in the community.  
**6:20** My son, keep your father’s command<br>and do not forsake your mother’s teaching.  
**6:21** Bind them always on your heart;<br>fasten them around your neck.  
**6:22** When you walk, they will guide you;<br>when you sleep, they will watch over you;<br>when you awake, they will speak to you.  
**6:23** For this command is a lamp,<br>this teaching is a light,<br>and correction and instruction<br>are the way to life,  
**6:24** keeping you from your neighbor’s wife,<br>from the smooth talk of a wayward woman.   
**6:25** Do not lust in your heart after her beauty<br>or let her captivate you with her eyes.   
**6:26** For a prostitute can be had for a loaf of bread,<br>but another man’s wife preys on your very life.  
**6:27** Can a man scoop fire into his lap<br>without his clothes being burned?  
**6:28** Can a man walk on hot coals<br>without his feet being scorched?  
**6:29** So is he who sleeps with another man’s wife;<br>no one who touches her will go unpunished.   
**6:30** People do not despise a thief if he steals<br>to satisfy his hunger when he is starving.  
**6:31** Yet if he is caught, he must pay sevenfold,<br>though it costs him all the wealth of his house.  
**6:32** But a man who commits adultery has no sense;<br>whoever does so destroys himself.  
**6:33** Blows and disgrace are his lot,<br>and his shame will never be wiped away.   
**6:34** For jealousy arouses a husband’s fury,<br>and he will show no mercy when he takes revenge.  
**6:35** He will not accept any compensation;<br>he will refuse a bribe, however great it is.  

## 7장

**7:1** My son, keep my words<br>and store up my commands within you.  
**7:2** Keep my commands and you will live;<br>guard my teachings as the apple of your eye.  
**7:3** Bind them on your fingers;<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,”<br>and to insight, “You are my relative.”  
**7:5** They will keep you from the adulterous woman,<br>from the wayward woman with her seductive words.   
**7:6** At the window of my house<br>I looked down through the lattice.  
**7:7** I saw among the simple,<br>I noticed among the young men,<br>a youth who had no sense.  
**7:8** He was going down the street near her corner,<br>walking along in the direction of her house  
**7:9** at twilight, as the day was fading,<br>as the dark of night set in.   
**7:10** Then out came a woman to meet him,<br>dressed like a prostitute and with crafty intent.  
**7:11** (She is unruly and defiant,<br>her feet never stay at home;

**7:13** She took hold of him and kissed him<br>and with a brazen face she said:

**7:24** Now then, my sons, listen to me;<br>pay attention to what I say.  
**7:25** Do not let your heart turn to her ways<br>or stray into her paths.  
**7:26** Many are the victims she has brought down;<br>her slain are a mighty throng.  
**7:27** Her house is a highway to the grave,<br>leading down to the chambers of death.
