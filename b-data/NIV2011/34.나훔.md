# 34.나훔 (NIV2011)



## 1장

**1:1** A prophecy concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.  
**1:2** The LORD is a jealous and avenging God;<br>the LORD takes vengeance and is filled with wrath.<br>The LORD takes vengeance on his foes<br>and vents his wrath against his enemies.  
**1:3** The LORD is slow to anger but great in power;<br>the LORD will not leave the guilty unpunished.<br>His way is in the whirlwind and the storm,<br>and clouds are the dust of his feet.  
**1:4** He rebukes the sea and dries it up;<br>he makes all the rivers run dry.<br>Bashan and Carmel wither<br>and the blossoms of Lebanon fade.  
**1:5** The mountains quake before him<br>and the hills melt away.<br>The earth trembles at his presence,<br>the world and all who live in it.  
**1:6** Who can withstand his indignation?<br>Who can endure his fierce anger?<br>His wrath is poured out like fire;<br>the rocks are shattered before him.   
**1:7** The LORD is good,<br>a refuge in times of trouble.<br>He cares for those who trust in him,  
**1:8** but with an overwhelming flood<br>he will make an end of Nineveh;<br>he will pursue his foes into the realm of darkness.   
**1:9** Whatever they plot against the LORD<br>he will bring<sup>①</sup> to an end;<br>trouble will not come a second time.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>What do you foes plot against the LORD? / He will bring it</i>  
**1:10** They will be entangled among thorns<br>and drunk from their wine;<br>they will be consumed like dry stubble.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew for this verse is uncertain.  
**1:11** From you, Nineveh, has one come forth<br>who plots evil against the LORD<br>and devises wicked plans.  
**1:12** This is what the LORD says:<br>“Although they have allies and are numerous,<br>they will be destroyed and pass away.<br>Although I have afflicted you, Judah,<br>I will afflict you no more.  
**1:13** Now I will break their yoke from your neck<br>and tear your shackles away.”   
**1:14** The LORD has given a command concerning you, Nineveh:<br>“You will have no descendants to bear your name.<br>I will destroy the images and idols<br>that are in the temple of your gods.<br>I will prepare your grave,<br>for you are vile.”   
**1:15** Look, there on the mountains,<br>the feet of one who brings good news,<br>who proclaims peace!<br>Celebrate your festivals, Judah,<br>and fulfill your vows.<br>No more will the wicked invade you;<br>they will be completely destroyed.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Hebrew texts this verse (1:15) is numbered 2:1.  

## 2장

**2:1** <sup>①</sup>An attacker advances against you, Nineveh.<br>Guard the fortress,<br>watch the road,<br>brace yourselves,<br>marshal all your strength!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Hebrew texts 2:1-13 is numbered 2:2-14.  
**2:2** The LORD will restore the splendor of Jacob<br>like the splendor of Israel,<br>though destroyers have laid them waste<br>and have ruined their vines.   
**2:3** The shields of the soldiers are red;<br>the warriors are clad in scarlet.<br>The metal on the chariots flashes<br>on the day they are made ready;<br>the spears of juniper are brandished.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew; Septuagint and Syriac <i>ready; / the horsemen rush to and fro.</i>  
**2:4** The chariots storm through the streets,<br>rushing back and forth through the squares.<br>They look like flaming torches;<br>they dart about like lightning.   
**2:5** Nineveh summons her picked troops,<br>yet they stumble on their way.<br>They dash to the city wall;<br>the protective shield is put in place.  
**2:6** The river gates are thrown open<br>and the palace collapses.  
**2:7** It is decreed<sup>①</sup> that Nineveh<br>be exiled and carried away.<br>Her female slaves moan like doves<br>and beat on their breasts.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew for this word is uncertain.  
**2:8** Nineveh is like a pool<br>whose water is draining away.<br>“Stop! Stop!” they cry,<br>but no one turns back.  
**2:9** Plunder the silver!<br>Plunder the gold!<br>The supply is endless,<br>the wealth from all its treasures!  
**2:10** She is pillaged, plundered, stripped!<br>Hearts melt, knees give way,<br>bodies tremble, every face grows pale.   
**2:11** Where now is the lions’ den,<br>the place where they fed their young,<br>where the lion and lioness went,<br>and the cubs, with nothing to fear?  
**2:12** The lion killed enough for his cubs<br>and strangled the prey for his mate,<br>filling his lairs with the kill<br>and his dens with the prey.   
**2:13** “I am against you,”<br>declares the LORD Almighty.<br>“I will burn up your chariots in smoke,<br>and the sword will devour your young lions.<br>I will leave you no prey on the earth.<br>The voices of your messengers<br>will no longer be heard.”  

## 3장

**3:1** Woe to the city of blood,<br>full of lies,<br>full of plunder,<br>never without victims!  
**3:2** The crack of whips,<br>the clatter of wheels,<br>galloping horses<br>and jolting chariots!  
**3:3** Charging cavalry,<br>flashing swords<br>and glittering spears!<br>Many casualties,<br>piles of dead,<br>bodies without number,<br>people stumbling over the corpses—  
**3:4** all because of the wanton lust of a prostitute,<br>alluring, the mistress of sorceries,<br>who enslaved nations by her prostitution<br>and peoples by her witchcraft.   
**3:5** “I am against you,” declares the LORD Almighty.<br>“I will lift your skirts over your face.<br>I will show the nations your nakedness<br>and the kingdoms your shame.  
**3:6** I will pelt you with filth,<br>I will treat you with contempt<br>and make you a spectacle.  
**3:7** All who see you will flee from you and say,<br>‘Nineveh is in ruins—who will mourn for her?’<br>Where can I find anyone to comfort you?”   
**3:8** Are you better than Thebes,<br>situated on the Nile,<br>with water around her?<br>The river was her defense,<br>the waters her wall.  
**3:9** Cush<sup>①</sup> and Egypt were her boundless strength;<br>Put and Libya were among her allies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>That is, the upper Nile region  
**3:10** Yet she was taken captive<br>and went into exile.<br>Her infants were dashed to pieces<br>at every street corner.<br>Lots were cast for her nobles,<br>and all her great men were put in chains.  
**3:11** You too will become drunk;<br>you will go into hiding<br>and seek refuge from the enemy.   
**3:12** All your fortresses are like fig trees<br>with their first ripe fruit;<br>when they are shaken,<br>the figs fall into the mouth of the eater.  
**3:13** Look at your troops—<br>they are all weaklings.<br>The gates of your land<br>are wide open to your enemies;<br>fire has consumed the bars of your gates.   
**3:14** Draw water for the siege,<br>strengthen your defenses!<br>Work the clay,<br>tread the mortar,<br>repair the brickwork!  
**3:15** There the fire will consume you;<br>the sword will cut you down—<br>they will devour you like a swarm of locusts.<br>Multiply like grasshoppers,<br>multiply like locusts!  
**3:16** You have increased the number of your merchants<br>till they are more numerous than the stars in the sky,<br>but like locusts they strip the land<br>and then fly away.  
**3:17** Your guards are like locusts,<br>your officials like swarms of locusts<br>that settle in the walls on a cold day—<br>but when the sun appears they fly away,<br>and no one knows where.   
**3:18** King of Assyria, your shepherds<sup>①</sup> slumber;<br>your nobles lie down to rest.<br>Your people are scattered on the mountains<br>with no one to gather them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>That is, rulers  
**3:19** Nothing can heal you;<br>your wound is fatal.<br>All who hear the news about you<br>clap their hands at your fall,<br>for who has not felt<br>your endless cruelty?
