# 29.요엘 (NIV2011)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Joel son of Pethuel.  
**1:2** Hear this, you elders;<br>listen, all who live in the land.<br>Has anything like this ever happened in your days<br>or in the days of your ancestors?  
**1:3** Tell it to your children,<br>and let your children tell it to their children,<br>and their children to the next generation.  
**1:4** What the locust swarm has left<br>the great locusts have eaten;<br>what the great locusts have left<br>the young locusts have eaten;<br>what the young locusts have left<br>other locusts<sup>①</sup> have eaten.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The precise meaning of the four Hebrew words used here for locusts is uncertain.  
**1:5** Wake up, you drunkards, and weep!<br>Wail, all you drinkers of wine;<br>wail because of the new wine,<br>for it has been snatched from your lips.  
**1:6** A nation has invaded my land,<br>a mighty army without number;<br>it has the teeth of a lion,<br>the fangs of a lioness.  
**1:7** It has laid waste my vines<br>and ruined my fig trees.<br>It has stripped off their bark<br>and thrown it away,<br>leaving their branches white.   
**1:8** Mourn like a virgin in sackcloth<br>grieving for the betrothed of her youth.  
**1:9** Grain offerings and drink offerings<br>are cut off from the house of the LORD.<br>The priests are in mourning,<br>those who minister before the LORD.  
**1:10** The fields are ruined,<br>the ground is dried up;<br>the grain is destroyed,<br>the new wine is dried up,<br>the olive oil fails.   
**1:11** Despair, you farmers,<br>wail, you vine growers;<br>grieve for the wheat and the barley,<br>because the harvest of the field is destroyed.  
**1:12** The vine is dried up<br>and the fig tree is withered;<br>the pomegranate, the palm and the apple<sup>①</sup> tree—<br>all the trees of the field—are dried up.<br>Surely the people’s joy<br>is withered away.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or possibly <i>apricot</i>  
**1:13** Put on sackcloth, you priests, and mourn;<br>wail, you who minister before the altar.<br>Come, spend the night in sackcloth,<br>you who minister before my God;<br>for the grain offerings and drink offerings<br>are withheld from the house of your God.  
**1:14** Declare a holy fast;<br>call a sacred assembly.<br>Summon the elders<br>and all who live in the land<br>to the house of the LORD your God,<br>and cry out to the LORD.   
**1:15** Alas for that day!<br>For the day of the LORD is near;<br>it will come like destruction from the Almighty.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Shaddai</i>  
**1:16** Has not the food been cut off<br>before our very eyes—<br>joy and gladness<br>from the house of our God?  
**1:17** The seeds are shriveled<br>beneath the clods.<sup>①</sup><br>The storehouses are in ruins,<br>the granaries have been broken down,<br>for the grain has dried up.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew for this word is uncertain.  
**1:18** How the cattle moan!<br>The herds mill about<br>because they have no pasture;<br>even the flocks of sheep are suffering.   
**1:19** To you, LORD, I call,<br>for fire has devoured the pastures in the wilderness<br>and flames have burned up all the trees of the field.  
**1:20** Even the wild animals pant for you;<br>the streams of water have dried up<br>and fire has devoured the pastures in the wilderness.  

## 2장

**2:1** Blow the trumpet in Zion;<br>sound the alarm on my holy hill. Let all who live in the land tremble,<br>for the day of the LORD is coming.<br>It is close at hand—  
**2:2** a day of darkness and gloom,<br>a day of clouds and blackness.<br>Like dawn spreading across the mountains<br>a large and mighty army comes,<br>such as never was in ancient times<br>nor ever will be in ages to come.   
**2:3** Before them fire devours,<br>behind them a flame blazes.<br>Before them the land is like the garden of Eden,<br>behind them, a desert waste—<br>nothing escapes them.  
**2:4** They have the appearance of horses;<br>they gallop along like cavalry.  
**2:5** With a noise like that of chariots<br>they leap over the mountaintops,<br>like a crackling fire consuming stubble,<br>like a mighty army drawn up for battle.   
**2:6** At the sight of them, nations are in anguish;<br>every face turns pale.  
**2:7** They charge like warriors;<br>they scale walls like soldiers.<br>They all march in line,<br>not swerving from their course.  
**2:8** They do not jostle each other;<br>each marches straight ahead.<br>They plunge through defenses<br>without breaking ranks.  
**2:9** They rush upon the city;<br>they run along the wall.<br>They climb into the houses;<br>like thieves they enter through the windows.   
**2:10** Before them the earth shakes,<br>the heavens tremble,<br>the sun and moon are darkened,<br>and the stars no longer shine.  
**2:11** The LORD thunders<br>at the head of his army;<br>his forces are beyond number,<br>and mighty is the army that obeys his command.<br>The day of the LORD is great;<br>it is dreadful.<br>Who can endure it?  
**2:12** “Even now,” declares the LORD,<br>“return to me with all your heart,<br>with fasting and weeping and mourning.”   
**2:13** Rend your heart<br>and not your garments.<br>Return to the LORD your God,<br>for he is gracious and compassionate,<br>slow to anger and abounding in love,<br>and he relents from sending calamity.  
**2:14** Who knows? He may turn and relent<br>and leave behind a blessing—<br>grain offerings and drink offerings<br>for the LORD your God.   
**2:15** Blow the trumpet in Zion,<br>declare a holy fast,<br>call a sacred assembly.  
**2:16** Gather the people,<br>consecrate the assembly;<br>bring together the elders,<br>gather the children,<br>those nursing at the breast.<br>Let the bridegroom leave his room<br>and the bride her chamber.  
**2:17** Let the priests, who minister before the LORD,<br>weep between the portico and the altar.<br>Let them say, “Spare your people, LORD.<br>Do not make your inheritance an object of scorn,<br>a byword among the nations.<br>Why should they say among the peoples,<br>‘Where is their God?’ ”  
**2:18** Then the LORD was jealous for his land<br>and took pity on his people.  
**2:19** The LORD replied<sup>①</sup> to them:<br>“I am sending you grain, new wine and olive oil,<br>enough to satisfy you fully;<br>never again will I make you<br>an object of scorn to the nations.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>LORD will be jealous… / and take pity… / </i>19 <i>The LORD will reply</i>  
**2:20** “I will drive the northern horde far from you,<br>pushing it into a parched and barren land;<br>its eastern ranks will drown in the Dead Sea<br>and its western ranks in the Mediterranean Sea.<br>And its stench will go up;<br>its smell will rise.” Surely he has done great things!  
**2:21** Do not be afraid, land of Judah;<br>be glad and rejoice.<br>Surely the LORD has done great things!  
**2:22** Do not be afraid, you wild animals,<br>for the pastures in the wilderness are becoming green.<br>The trees are bearing their fruit;<br>the fig tree and the vine yield their riches.  
**2:23** Be glad, people of Zion,<br>rejoice in the LORD your God,<br>for he has given you the autumn rains<br>because he is faithful.<br>He sends you abundant showers,<br>both autumn and spring rains, as before.  
**2:24** The threshing floors will be filled with grain;<br>the vats will overflow with new wine and oil.   
**2:25** “I will repay you for the years the locusts have eaten—<br>the great locust and the young locust,<br>the other locusts and the locust swarm<sup>①</sup>—<br>my great army that I sent among you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The precise meaning of the four Hebrew words used here for locusts is uncertain.  
**2:26** You will have plenty to eat, until you are full,<br>and you will praise the name of the LORD your God,<br>who has worked wonders for you;<br>never again will my people be shamed.  
**2:27** Then you will know that I am in Israel,<br>that I am the LORD your God,<br>and that there is no other;<br>never again will my people be shamed.  
**2:28** “And afterward,<br>I will pour out my Spirit on all people.<br>Your sons and daughters will prophesy,<br>your old men will dream dreams,<br>your young men will see visions.  
**2:29** Even on my servants, both men and women,<br>I will pour out my Spirit in those days.  
**2:30** I will show wonders in the heavens<br>and on the earth,<br>blood and fire and billows of smoke.  
**2:31** The sun will be turned to darkness<br>and the moon to blood<br>before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the LORD.  
**2:32** And everyone who calls<br>on the name of the LORD will be saved;<br>for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem<br>there will be deliverance,<br>as the LORD has said,<br>even among the survivors<br>whom the LORD calls.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Hebrew texts 2:28-32 is numbered 3:1-5.  

## 3장

**3:1** <sup>①</sup>“In those days and at that time,<br>when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>In Hebrew texts 3:1-21 is numbered 4:1-21.  
**3:2** I will gather all nations<br>and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat.<sup>①</sup><br>There I will put them on trial<br>for what they did to my inheritance, my people Israel,<br>because they scattered my people among the nations<br>and divided up my land.<br>→ <sup>①</sup><i>Jehoshaphat </i>means <i>the LORD judges;</i> also in verse 12.  
**3:3** They cast lots for my people<br>and traded boys for prostitutes;<br>they sold girls for wine to drink.  
**3:4** “Now what have you against me, Tyre and Sidon and all you regions of Philistia? Are you repaying me for something I have done? If you are paying me back, I will swiftly and speedily return on your own heads what you have done.  
**3:5** For you took my silver and my gold and carried off my finest treasures to your temples.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>palaces</i>  
**3:6** You sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks, that you might send them far from their homeland.  
**3:7** “See, I am going to rouse them out of the places to which you sold them, and I will return on your own heads what you have done.  
**3:8** I will sell your sons and daughters to the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans, a nation far away.” The LORD has spoken.  
**3:9** Proclaim this among the nations:<br>Prepare for war!<br>Rouse the warriors!<br>Let all the fighting men draw near and attack.  
**3:10** Beat your plowshares into swords<br>and your pruning hooks into spears.<br>Let the weakling say,<br>“I am strong!”  
**3:11** Come quickly, all you nations from every side,<br>and assemble there. Bring down your warriors, LORD!   
**3:12** “Let the nations be roused;<br>let them advance into the Valley of Jehoshaphat,<br>for there I will sit<br>to judge all the nations on every side.  
**3:13** Swing the sickle,<br>for the harvest is ripe.<br>Come, trample the grapes,<br>for the winepress is full<br>and the vats overflow—<br>so great is their wickedness!”   
**3:14** Multitudes, multitudes<br>in the valley of decision!<br>For the day of the LORD is near<br>in the valley of decision.  
**3:15** The sun and moon will be darkened,<br>and the stars no longer shine.  
**3:16** The LORD will roar from Zion<br>and thunder from Jerusalem;<br>the earth and the heavens will tremble.<br>But the LORD will be a refuge for his people,<br>a stronghold for the people of Israel.  
**3:17** “Then you will know that I, the LORD your God,<br>dwell in Zion, my holy hill.<br>Jerusalem will be holy;<br>never again will foreigners invade her.   
**3:18** “In that day the mountains will drip new wine,<br>and the hills will flow with milk;<br>all the ravines of Judah will run with water.<br>A fountain will flow out of the LORD’s house<br>and will water the valley of acacias.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Valley of Shittim</i>  
**3:19** But Egypt will be desolate,<br>Edom a desert waste,<br>because of violence done to the people of Judah,<br>in whose land they shed innocent blood.  
**3:20** Judah will be inhabited forever<br>and Jerusalem through all generations.  
**3:21** Shall I leave their innocent blood unavenged?<br>No, I will not.” The LORD dwells in Zion!
