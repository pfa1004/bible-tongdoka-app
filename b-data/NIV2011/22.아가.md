# 22.아가 (NIV2011)



## 1장

**1:1** Solomon’s Song of Songs.  
**1:2** <b>She</b><sup>①</sup><br>Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth—<br>for your love is more delightful than wine.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The main male and female speakers (identified primarily on the basis of the gender of the relevant Hebrew forms) are indicated by the captions <i>He </i>and <i>She </i>respectively. The words of others are marked <i>Friends. </i>In some instances the divisions and their captions are debatable.  
**1:3** Pleasing is the fragrance of your perfumes;<br>your name is like perfume poured out.<br>No wonder the young women love you!  
**1:4** Take me away with you—let us hurry!<br>Let the king bring me into his chambers.<br><b>Friends</b><br>We rejoice and delight in you<sup>①</sup>;<br>we will praise your love more than wine.<br><b>She</b><br>How right they are to adore you!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Hebrew is masculine singular.  
**1:5** Dark am I, yet lovely,<br>daughters of Jerusalem,<br>dark like the tents of Kedar,<br>like the tent curtains of Solomon.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Salma</i>  
**1:6** Do not stare at me because I am dark,<br>because I am darkened by the sun.<br>My mother’s sons were angry with me<br>and made me take care of the vineyards;<br>my own vineyard I had to neglect.  
**1:7** Tell me, you whom I love,<br>where you graze your flock<br>and where you rest your sheep at midday.<br>Why should I be like a veiled woman<br>beside the flocks of your friends?  
**1:8** <b>Friends</b><br>If you do not know, most beautiful of women,<br>follow the tracks of the sheep<br>and graze your young goats<br>by the tents of the shepherds.  
**1:9** <b>He</b><br>I liken you, my darling, to a mare<br>among Pharaoh’s chariot horses.  
**1:10** Your cheeks are beautiful with earrings,<br>your neck with strings of jewels.  
**1:11** We will make you earrings of gold,<br>studded with silver.  
**1:12** <b>She</b><br>While the king was at his table,<br>my perfume spread its fragrance.  
**1:13** My beloved is to me a sachet of myrrh<br>resting between my breasts.  
**1:14** My beloved is to me a cluster of henna blossoms<br>from the vineyards of En Gedi.  
**1:15** <b>He</b><br>How beautiful you are, my darling!<br>Oh, how beautiful!<br>Your eyes are doves.  
**1:16** <b>She</b><br>How handsome you are, my beloved!<br>Oh, how charming!<br>And our bed is verdant.  
**1:17** <b>He</b><br>The beams of our house are cedars;<br>our rafters are firs.  

## 2장

**2:1** <b>She</b><sup>①</sup><br>I am a rose<sup>②</sup> of Sharon,<br>a lily of the valleys.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>He</i> <sup>②</sup>Probably a member of the crocus family  
**2:2** <b>He</b><br>Like a lily among thorns<br>is my darling among the young women.  
**2:3** <b>She</b><br>Like an apple<sup>①</sup> tree among the trees of the forest<br>is my beloved among the young men.<br>I delight to sit in his shade,<br>and his fruit is sweet to my taste.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or possibly <i>apricot;</i> here and elsewhere in Song of Songs  
**2:4** Let him lead me to the banquet hall,<br>and let his banner over me be love.  
**2:5** Strengthen me with raisins,<br>refresh me with apples,<br>for I am faint with love.  
**2:6** His left arm is under my head,<br>and his right arm embraces me.  
**2:7** Daughters of Jerusalem, I charge you<br>by the gazelles and by the does of the field:<br>Do not arouse or awaken love<br>until it so desires.   
**2:8** Listen! My beloved!<br>Look! Here he comes,<br>leaping across the mountains,<br>bounding over the hills.  
**2:9** My beloved is like a gazelle or a young stag.<br>Look! There he stands behind our wall,<br>gazing through the windows,<br>peering through the lattice.  
**2:10** My beloved spoke and said to me,<br>“Arise, my darling,<br>my beautiful one, come with me.  
**2:11** See! The winter is past;<br>the rains are over and gone.  
**2:12** Flowers appear on the earth;<br>the season of singing has come,<br>the cooing of doves<br>is heard in our land.  
**2:13** The fig tree forms its early fruit;<br>the blossoming vines spread their fragrance.<br>Arise, come, my darling;<br>my beautiful one, come with me.”  
**2:14** <b>He</b><br>My dove in the clefts of the rock,<br>in the hiding places on the mountainside,<br>show me your face,<br>let me hear your voice;<br>for your voice is sweet,<br>and your face is lovely.  
**2:15** Catch for us the foxes,<br>the little foxes<br>that ruin the vineyards,<br>our vineyards that are in bloom.  
**2:16** <b>She</b><br>My beloved is mine and I am his;<br>he browses among the lilies.  
**2:17** Until the day breaks<br>and the shadows flee,<br>turn, my beloved,<br>and be like a gazelle<br>or like a young stag<br>on the rugged hills.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the hills of Bether</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** All night long on my bed<br>I looked for the one my heart loves;<br>I looked for him but did not find him.  
**3:2** I will get up now and go about the city,<br>through its streets and squares;<br>I will search for the one my heart loves.<br>So I looked for him but did not find him.  
**3:3** The watchmen found me<br>as they made their rounds in the city.<br>“Have you seen the one my heart loves?”  
**3:4** Scarcely had I passed them<br>when I found the one my heart loves.<br>I held him and would not let him go<br>till I had brought him to my mother’s house,<br>to the room of the one who conceived me.  
**3:5** Daughters of Jerusalem, I charge you<br>by the gazelles and by the does of the field:<br>Do not arouse or awaken love<br>until it so desires.   
**3:6** Who is this coming up from the wilderness<br>like a column of smoke,<br>perfumed with myrrh and incense<br>made from all the spices of the merchant?  
**3:7** Look! It is Solomon’s carriage,<br>escorted by sixty warriors,<br>the noblest of Israel,  
**3:8** all of them wearing the sword,<br>all experienced in battle,<br>each with his sword at his side,<br>prepared for the terrors of the night.  
**3:9** King Solomon made for himself the carriage;<br>he made it of wood from Lebanon.  
**3:10** Its posts he made of silver,<br>its base of gold.<br>Its seat was upholstered with purple,<br>its interior inlaid with love.<br>Daughters of Jerusalem,  
**3:11** come out,<br>and look, you daughters of Zion.<br>Look<sup>①</sup> on King Solomon wearing a crown,<br>the crown with which his mother crowned him<br>on the day of his wedding,<br>the day his heart rejoiced.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>interior lovingly inlaid / by the daughters of Jerusalem. / </i>11 <i>Come out, you daughters of Zion, / and look</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** <b>He</b><br>How beautiful you are, my darling!<br>Oh, how beautiful!<br>Your eyes behind your veil are doves.<br>Your hair is like a flock of goats<br>descending from the hills of Gilead.  
**4:2** Your teeth are like a flock of sheep just shorn,<br>coming up from the washing.<br>Each has its twin;<br>not one of them is alone.  
**4:3** Your lips are like a scarlet ribbon;<br>your mouth is lovely.<br>Your temples behind your veil<br>are like the halves of a pomegranate.  
**4:4** Your neck is like the tower of David,<br>built with courses of stone<sup>①</sup>;<br>on it hang a thousand shields,<br>all of them shields of warriors.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew for this phrase is uncertain.  
**4:5** Your breasts are like two fawns,<br>like twin fawns of a gazelle<br>that browse among the lilies.  
**4:6** Until the day breaks<br>and the shadows flee,<br>I will go to the mountain of myrrh<br>and to the hill of incense.  
**4:7** You are altogether beautiful, my darling;<br>there is no flaw in you.   
**4:8** Come with me from Lebanon, my bride,<br>come with me from Lebanon.<br>Descend from the crest of Amana,<br>from the top of Senir, the summit of Hermon,<br>from the lions’ dens<br>and the mountain haunts of leopards.  
**4:9** You have stolen my heart, my sister, my bride;<br>you have stolen my heart<br>with one glance of your eyes,<br>with one jewel of your necklace.  
**4:10** How delightful is your love, my sister, my bride!<br>How much more pleasing is your love than wine,<br>and the fragrance of your perfume<br>more than any spice!  
**4:11** Your lips drop sweetness as the honeycomb, my bride;<br>milk and honey are under your tongue.<br>The fragrance of your garments<br>is like the fragrance of Lebanon.  
**4:12** You are a garden locked up, my sister, my bride;<br>you are a spring enclosed, a sealed fountain.  
**4:13** Your plants are an orchard of pomegranates<br>with choice fruits,<br>with henna and nard,  
**4:14** nard and saffron,<br>calamus and cinnamon,<br>with every kind of incense tree,<br>with myrrh and aloes<br>and all the finest spices.  
**4:15** You are<sup>①</sup> a garden fountain,<br>a well of flowing water<br>streaming down from Lebanon.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>I am </i>(spoken by <i>She</i>)  
**4:16** <b>She</b><br>Awake, north wind,<br>and come, south wind!<br>Blow on my garden,<br>that its fragrance may spread everywhere.<br>Let my beloved come into his garden<br>and taste its choice fruits.  

## 5장

**5:1** <b>He</b><br>I have come into my garden, my sister, my bride;<br>I have gathered my myrrh with my spice.<br>I have eaten my honeycomb and my honey;<br>I have drunk my wine and my milk.<br><b>Friends</b><br>Eat, friends, and drink;<br>drink your fill of love.  
**5:2** <b>She</b><br>I slept but my heart was awake.<br>Listen! My beloved is knocking:<br>“Open to me, my sister, my darling,<br>my dove, my flawless one.<br>My head is drenched with dew,<br>my hair with the dampness of the night.”  
**5:3** I have taken off my robe—<br>must I put it on again?<br>I have washed my feet—<br>must I soil them again?  
**5:4** My beloved thrust his hand through the latch-opening;<br>my heart began to pound for him.  
**5:5** I arose to open for my beloved,<br>and my hands dripped with myrrh,<br>my fingers with flowing myrrh,<br>on the handles of the bolt.  
**5:6** I opened for my beloved,<br>but my beloved had left; he was gone.<br>My heart sank at his departure.<sup>①</sup><br>I looked for him but did not find him.<br>I called him but he did not answer.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>heart had gone out to him when he spoke</i>  
**5:7** The watchmen found me<br>as they made their rounds in the city.<br>They beat me, they bruised me;<br>they took away my cloak,<br>those watchmen of the walls!  
**5:8** Daughters of Jerusalem, I charge you—<br>if you find my beloved,<br>what will you tell him?<br>Tell him I am faint with love.  
**5:9** <b>Friends</b><br>How is your beloved better than others,<br>most beautiful of women?<br>How is your beloved better than others,<br>that you so charge us?  
**5:10** <b>She</b><br>My beloved is radiant and ruddy,<br>outstanding among ten thousand.  
**5:11** His head is purest gold;<br>his hair is wavy<br>and black as a raven.  
**5:12** His eyes are like doves<br>by the water streams,<br>washed in milk,<br>mounted like jewels.  
**5:13** His cheeks are like beds of spice<br>yielding perfume.<br>His lips are like lilies<br>dripping with myrrh.  
**5:14** His arms are rods of gold<br>set with topaz.<br>His body is like polished ivory<br>decorated with lapis lazuli.  
**5:15** His legs are pillars of marble<br>set on bases of pure gold.<br>His appearance is like Lebanon,<br>choice as its cedars.  
**5:16** His mouth is sweetness itself;<br>he is altogether lovely.<br>This is my beloved, this is my friend,<br>daughters of Jerusalem.

**6:2** <b>She</b><br>My beloved has gone down to his garden,<br>to the beds of spices,<br>to browse in the gardens<br>and to gather lilies.  
**6:3** I am my beloved’s and my beloved is mine;<br>he browses among the lilies.  
**6:4** <b>He</b><br>You are as beautiful as Tirzah, my darling,<br>as lovely as Jerusalem,<br>as majestic as troops with banners.  
**6:5** Turn your eyes from me;<br>they overwhelm me.<br>Your hair is like a flock of goats<br>descending from Gilead.  
**6:6** Your teeth are like a flock of sheep<br>coming up from the washing.<br>Each has its twin,<br>not one of them is missing.  
**6:7** Your temples behind your veil<br>are like the halves of a pomegranate.  
**6:8** Sixty queens there may be,<br>and eighty concubines,<br>and virgins beyond number;

**6:10** <b>Friends</b><br>Who is this that appears like the dawn,<br>fair as the moon, bright as the sun,<br>majestic as the stars in procession?

**6:12** Before I realized it,<br>my desire set me among the royal chariots of my people.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>among the chariots of Amminadab;</i> or <i>among the chariots of the people of the prince</i>

**8:3** His left arm is under my head<br>and his right arm embraces me.  
**8:4** Daughters of Jerusalem, I charge you:<br>Do not arouse or awaken love<br>until it so desires.  
**8:5** <b>Friends</b><br>Who is this coming up from the wilderness<br>leaning on her beloved?<br><b>She</b><br>Under the apple tree I roused you;<br>there your mother conceived you,<br>there she who was in labor gave you birth.
