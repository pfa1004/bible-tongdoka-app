# 35.하박국 (NIV2011)



## 1장

**1:1** The prophecy that Habakkuk the prophet received.  
**1:2** How long, LORD, must I call for help,<br>but you do not listen?<br>Or cry out to you, “Violence!”<br>but you do not save?  
**1:3** Why do you make me look at injustice?<br>Why do you tolerate wrongdoing?<br>Destruction and violence are before me;<br>there is strife, and conflict abounds.  
**1:4** Therefore the law is paralyzed,<br>and justice never prevails.<br>The wicked hem in the righteous,<br>so that justice is perverted.  
**1:5** “Look at the nations and watch—<br>and be utterly amazed.<br>For I am going to do something in your days<br>that you would not believe,<br>even if you were told.  
**1:6** I am raising up the Babylonians,<sup>①</sup><br>that ruthless and impetuous people,<br>who sweep across the whole earth<br>to seize dwellings not their own.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Chaldeans</i>  
**1:7** They are a feared and dreaded people;<br>they are a law to themselves<br>and promote their own honor.  
**1:8** Their horses are swifter than leopards,<br>fiercer than wolves at dusk.<br>Their cavalry gallops headlong;<br>their horsemen come from afar.<br>They fly like an eagle swooping to devour;  
**1:9** they all come intent on violence.<br>Their hordes<sup>①</sup> advance like a desert wind<br>and gather prisoners like sand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew for this word is uncertain.  
**1:10** They mock kings<br>and scoff at rulers.<br>They laugh at all fortified cities;<br>by building earthen ramps they capture them.  
**1:11** Then they sweep past like the wind and go on—<br>guilty people, whose own strength is their god.”  
**1:12** LORD, are you not from everlasting?<br>My God, my Holy One, you<sup>①</sup> will never die.<br>You, LORD, have appointed them to execute judgment;<br>you, my Rock, have ordained them to punish.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>An ancient Hebrew scribal tradition; Masoretic Text <i>we</i>  
**1:13** Your eyes are too pure to look on evil;<br>you cannot tolerate wrongdoing.<br>Why then do you tolerate the treacherous?<br>Why are you silent while the wicked<br>swallow up those more righteous than themselves?  
**1:14** You have made people like the fish in the sea,<br>like the sea creatures that have no ruler.  
**1:15** The wicked foe pulls all of them up with hooks,<br>he catches them in his net,<br>he gathers them up in his dragnet;<br>and so he rejoices and is glad.  
**1:16** Therefore he sacrifices to his net<br>and burns incense to his dragnet,<br>for by his net he lives in luxury<br>and enjoys the choicest food.  
**1:17** Is he to keep on emptying his net,<br>destroying nations without mercy?  

## 2장

**2:1** I will stand at my watch<br>and station myself on the ramparts;<br>I will look to see what he will say to me,<br>and what answer I am to give to this complaint.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>and what to answer when I am rebuked</i>  
**2:2** Then the LORD replied:<br>“Write down the revelation<br>and make it plain on tablets<br>so that a herald<sup>①</sup> may run with it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>so that whoever reads it</i>  
**2:3** For the revelation awaits an appointed time;<br>it speaks of the end<br>and will not prove false.<br>Though it linger, wait for it;<br>it<sup>①</sup> will certainly come<br>and will not delay.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Though he linger, wait for him; / he</i>  
**2:4** “See, the enemy is puffed up;<br>his desires are not upright—<br>but the righteous person will live by his faithfulness<sup>①</sup>—<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>faith</i>  
**2:5** indeed, wine betrays him;<br>he is arrogant and never at rest.<br>Because he is as greedy as the grave<br>and like death is never satisfied,<br>he gathers to himself all the nations<br>and takes captive all the peoples.  
**2:6** “Will not all of them taunt him with ridicule and scorn, saying,<br>“ ‘Woe to him who piles up stolen goods<br>and makes himself wealthy by extortion!<br>How long must this go on?’  
**2:7** Will not your creditors suddenly arise?<br>Will they not wake up and make you tremble?<br>Then you will become their prey.  
**2:8** Because you have plundered many nations,<br>the peoples who are left will plunder you.<br>For you have shed human blood;<br>you have destroyed lands and cities and everyone in them.   
**2:9** “Woe to him who builds his house by unjust gain,<br>setting his nest on high<br>to escape the clutches of ruin!  
**2:10** You have plotted the ruin of many peoples,<br>shaming your own house and forfeiting your life.  
**2:11** The stones of the wall will cry out,<br>and the beams of the woodwork will echo it.   
**2:12** “Woe to him who builds a city with bloodshed<br>and establishes a town by injustice!  
**2:13** Has not the LORD Almighty determined<br>that the people’s labor is only fuel for the fire,<br>that the nations exhaust themselves for nothing?  
**2:14** For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD<br>as the waters cover the sea.   
**2:15** “Woe to him who gives drink to his neighbors,<br>pouring it from the wineskin till they are drunk,<br>so that he can gaze on their naked bodies!  
**2:16** You will be filled with shame instead of glory.<br>Now it is your turn! Drink and let your nakedness be exposed<sup>①</sup>!<br>The cup from the LORD’s right hand is coming around to you,<br>and disgrace will cover your glory.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Masoretic Text; Dead Sea Scrolls, Aquila, Vulgate and Syriac (see also Septuagint) <i>and stagger</i>  
**2:17** The violence you have done to Lebanon will overwhelm you,<br>and your destruction of animals will terrify you.<br>For you have shed human blood;<br>you have destroyed lands and cities and everyone in them.   
**2:18** “Of what value is an idol carved by a craftsman?<br>Or an image that teaches lies?<br>For the one who makes it trusts in his own creation;<br>he makes idols that cannot speak.  
**2:19** Woe to him who says to wood, ‘Come to life!’<br>Or to lifeless stone, ‘Wake up!’<br>Can it give guidance?<br>It is covered with gold and silver;<br>there is no breath in it.”   
**2:20** The LORD is in his holy temple;<br>let all the earth be silent before him.  

## 3장

**3:1** [A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet. On shigionoth].<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Probably a literary or musical term  
**3:2** LORD, I have heard of your fame;<br>I stand in awe of your deeds, LORD.<br>Repeat them in our day,<br>in our time make them known;<br>in wrath remember mercy.   
**3:3** God came from Teman,<br>the Holy One from Mount Paran.<sup>①</sup><br>His glory covered the heavens<br>and his praise filled the earth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Hebrew has <i>Selah </i>(a word of uncertain meaning) here and at the middle of verse 9 and at the end of verse 13.  
**3:4** His splendor was like the sunrise;<br>rays flashed from his hand,<br>where his power was hidden.  
**3:5** Plague went before him;<br>pestilence followed his steps.  
**3:6** He stood, and shook the earth;<br>he looked, and made the nations tremble.<br>The ancient mountains crumbled<br>and the age-old hills collapsed—<br>but he marches on forever.  
**3:7** I saw the tents of Cushan in distress,<br>the dwellings of Midian in anguish.   
**3:8** Were you angry with the rivers, LORD?<br>Was your wrath against the streams?<br>Did you rage against the sea<br>when you rode your horses<br>and your chariots to victory?  
**3:9** You uncovered your bow,<br>you called for many arrows.<br>You split the earth with rivers;  
**3:10** the mountains saw you and writhed.<br>Torrents of water swept by;<br>the deep roared<br>and lifted its waves on high.   
**3:11** Sun and moon stood still in the heavens<br>at the glint of your flying arrows,<br>at the lightning of your flashing spear.  
**3:12** In wrath you strode through the earth<br>and in anger you threshed the nations.  
**3:13** You came out to deliver your people,<br>to save your anointed one.<br>You crushed the leader of the land of wickedness,<br>you stripped him from head to foot.  
**3:14** With his own spear you pierced his head<br>when his warriors stormed out to scatter us,<br>gloating as though about to devour<br>the wretched who were in hiding.  
**3:15** You trampled the sea with your horses,<br>churning the great waters.   
**3:16** I heard and my heart pounded,<br>my lips quivered at the sound;<br>decay crept into my bones,<br>and my legs trembled.<br>Yet I will wait patiently for the day of calamity<br>to come on the nation invading us.  
**3:17** Though the fig tree does not bud<br>and there are no grapes on the vines,<br>though the olive crop fails<br>and the fields produce no food,<br>though there are no sheep in the pen<br>and no cattle in the stalls,  
**3:18** yet I will rejoice in the LORD,<br>I will be joyful in God my Savior.   
**3:19** The Sovereign LORD is my strength;<br>he makes my feet like the feet of a deer,<br>he enables me to tread on the heights. For the director of music. On my stringed instruments.
