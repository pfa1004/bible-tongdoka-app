# 31.오바디야 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision of Obadiah.<br>The LORD God proclaims concerning Edom:<br>We have heard a message from the LORD—<br>a messenger has been sent among the nations:<br>“Rise up! Let us rise against her for battle!”  
**1:2** Look now, I will make you of little importance among the nations;<br>you will be totally despised.  
**1:3** Your proud heart has tricked you—<br>you who live in the cracks of the rock,<br>whose dwelling is high above.<br>You who say in your heart,<br>“Who will bring me down to the ground?”  
**1:4** Though you soar like the eagle,<br>though your nest is set among the stars,<br>I will bring you down from there,<br>—says the LORD.  
**1:5** If thieves approach you,<br>if robbers by night—how you’ve been devastated!—<br>wouldn’t they steal only what they wanted?<br>If those who gather grapes came to you,<br>wouldn’t they leave some grapes?  
**1:6** How Esau has been looted,<br>his treasures taken away!  
**1:7** All those who were your allies<br>have driven you to the border.<br>Those who were on your side tricked you<br>and triumphed over you.<br>They are setting your own bread as a trap under you,<sup>①</sup><br>but you don’t see it coming.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**1:8** Won’t I on that day, says the LORD,<br>destroy the wise from Edom<br>and understanding from Mount Esau?  
**1:9** Your warriors will be shattered, Teman,<br>and everyone from Mount Esau will be eliminated.  
**1:10** Because of the slaughter and violence done to your brother Jacob,<br>shame will cover you,<br>and you will be destroyed forever.  
**1:11** You stood nearby,<br>strangers carried off his wealth,<br>and foreigners entered his gates<br>and cast lots for Jerusalem;<br>you too were like one of them.  
**1:12** But you should have taken no pleasure over your brother<br>on the day of his misery;<br>you shouldn’t have rejoiced over the people of Judah<br>on the day of their devastation;<br>you shouldn’t have bragged<br>on their day of hardship.  
**1:13** You shouldn’t have entered the gate of my people<br>on the day of their defeat;<br>you shouldn’t have even looked on his suffering<br>on the day of his disaster;<br>you shouldn’t have stolen his possessions<br>on the day of his distress.  
**1:14** You shouldn’t have waited on the roads<br>to destroy his escapees;<br>you shouldn’t have handed over his survivors<br>on the day of defeat.  
**1:15** The day of the LORD is near<br>against all the nations.<br>As you have done, so it will be done to you;<br>your actions will make you suffer!  
**1:16** Just as you have drunk on my holy mountain,<br>so will all the nations around you drink;<br>they will drink and swallow quickly,<br>and they will be like they’ve never been before.  
**1:17** But on Mount Zion there will be those who escape,<br>and it will be holy;<br>and the house of Jacob will drive out those who drove them out.  
**1:18** The house of Jacob will be a fire,<br>the house of Joseph a flame,<br>and the house of Esau straw;<br>they will burn them up completely,<br>and there will be no one left of the house of Esau,<br>—for the LORD has spoken.  
**1:19** Those of the arid southern plain will possess Mount Esau,<br>and those of the western foothills, the land of the Philistines;<br>they will possess the land of Ephraim and the land of Samaria,<br>and Benjamin will possess Gilead.  
**1:20** Those who remain of the Israelites<br>will possess the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath;<br>and those left from Jerusalem and who are now living in Sepharad<br>will possess the cities of the arid southern plain.  
**1:21** The deliverers will go up to Mount Zion<br>to rule Mount Esau,<br>and the kingdom will be the LORD’s.
