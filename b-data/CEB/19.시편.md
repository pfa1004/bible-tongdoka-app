# 19.시편 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** The truly happy person<br>doesn’t follow wicked advice,<br>doesn’t stand on the road of sinners,<br>and doesn’t sit with the disrespectful.  
**1:2** Instead of doing those things,<br>these persons love the LORD’s Instruction,<br>and they recite God’s Instruction day and night!  
**1:3** They are like a tree replanted by streams of water,<br>which bears fruit at just the right time<br>and whose leaves don’t fade.<br>Whatever they do succeeds.  
**1:4** That’s not true for the wicked!<br>They are like dust that the wind blows away.  
**1:5** And that’s why the wicked will have no standing in the court of justice—<br>neither will sinners<br>in the assembly of the righteous.  
**1:6** The LORD is intimately acquainted<br>with the way of the righteous,<br>but the way of the wicked is destroyed.  

## 2장

**2:1** Why do the nations rant?<br>Why do the peoples rave uselessly?  
**2:2** The earth’s rulers take their stand;<br>the leaders scheme together<br>against the LORD and<br>against his anointed one.  
**2:3** “Come!” they say.<br>“We will tear off their ropes<br>and throw off their chains!”  
**2:4** The one who rules in heaven laughs;<br>my Lord makes fun of them.  
**2:5** But then God speaks to them angrily;<br>then he terrifies them with his fury:  
**2:6** “I hereby appoint my king on Zion,<br>my holy mountain!”  
**2:7** I will announce the LORD’s decision:<br>He said to me, “You are my son,<br>today I have become your father.  
**2:8** Just ask me,<br>and I will make the nations your possession;<br>the far corners of the earth will be your property.  
**2:9** You will smash them with an iron rod;<br>you will shatter them like a pottery jar.”  
**2:10** So kings, wise up!<br>Be warned, you rulers of the earth!  
**2:11** Serve the LORD reverently—<br>trembling,  
**2:12** kiss his feet<sup>①</sup><br>or else he will become angry,<br>and your way will be destroyed<br>because his anger ignites in an instant.<br>But all who take refuge in the LORD are truly happy!<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Correction; Heb uncertain; MT <i>rejoice with trembling, kiss the son</i> (but with <i>son</i> in Aram, not Heb)
