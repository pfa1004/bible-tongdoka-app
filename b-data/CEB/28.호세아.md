## 1장

**1:1** The LORD’s word that came to Hosea, Beeri’s son, in the days of Judah’s Kings Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, and in the days of Israel’s King Jeroboam, Joash’s son.

**1:5** On that day I will break the bow of Israel in the Jezreel Valley.”  
**1:6** Gomer became pregnant again and gave birth to a daughter. Then the LORD said to Hosea, “Name her No Compassion, because I will no longer have compassion on the house of Israel or forgive them.

**12:2** The LORD has a charge against Judah,<br>and will punish Jacob according to his ways,<br>and respond to him according to his deeds.  
**12:3** From the womb he tried to be the oldest of twin brothers;<br>as an adult he struggled with God.  
**12:4** He struggled with the messenger and survived;<br>he wept and sought his favor;<br>he met him at Bethel,<br>and there he spoke with him.  
**12:5** The LORD God of heavenly forces,<br>the LORD is his name!  
**12:6** But you! Return to your God<br>with faithful love and justice,<br>and wait continually for your God.  
**12:7** He is a merchant; the means to cheat are in his hands;<br>he loves to take advantage of others.  
**12:8** Ephraim has said,<br>“I’m rich,<br>I’ve gained wealth for myself;<br>in all of my gain<br>no offense has been found in me<br>that would be sin.”  
**12:9** I am the LORD your God<br>from the land of Egypt;<br>I will make you live in tents again,<br>as in former days.  
**12:10** I spoke to the prophets;<br>and I multiplied visions,<br>and through them I uttered parables.  
**12:11** In Gilead there is wickedness;<br>they will surely come to nothing.<br>In Gilgal they sacrifice bulls,<br>so their altars will be like piles of stones<br>on the rows of the field.  
**12:12** Jacob fled to the land of Aram;<br>there Israel served for a wife,<br>and for a wife he kept watch over livestock.  
**12:13** By a prophet the LORD brought Israel up from Egypt,<br>and by a prophet he was guarded.  
**12:14** Ephraim has given bitter offense;<br>so the LORD will bring his crimes down on him<br>and pay him back for his wrongdoing.  

## 13장

**13:1** When Ephraim spoke, there was excitement;<br>he was praised in Israel;<br>but he became guilty through Baal and died.  
**13:2** And now they keep on sinning;<br>they have made metal images,<br>idols of silver,<br>as a result of their skill,<br>all of them the work of craftsmen.<br>“Sacrifice to these,” they say.<br>People are kissing calves!  
**13:3** Therefore, they will be like the morning mist,<br>like the dew that passes away early,<br>like husks that swirl from the threshing floor,<br>or like smoke from a window.  
**13:4** Yet I have been the LORD your God<br>ever since the land of Egypt;<br>and you will know no other gods but me;<br>there is no savior besides me.  
**13:5** I knew you in the wilderness,<br>in the land of no rain.  
**13:6** When I fed them, they were satisfied;<br>and their hearts became proud;<br>therefore, they forgot me.  
**13:7** So I will become like a lion to them;<br>like a leopard I will lurk beside the road.  
**13:8** I will fall upon them like a bear robbed of her cubs,<br>and I will tear open the covering of their hearts.<br>I will devour them like a lion,<br>as a wild animal would eat them.  
**13:9** I will destroy you, Israel;<br>for you didn’t realize that I could help you.  
**13:10** Where is your king now,<br>so that he can save you?<br>Where in all your cities are your judges,<br>of whom you said,<br>“Give me a king and rulers”?  
**13:11** I gave you a king in my anger,<br>and I took him away in my wrath.  
**13:12** Ephraim’s wickedness is bound up;<br>his sin is kept in store.  
**13:13** The pangs of a woman in childbirth come for him,<br>but he is not aware of the time to be born;<br>for at the proper time he doesn’t present himself<br>at the mouth of the womb.  
**13:14** Will I ransom them from the power of the grave<sup>①</sup><br>Will I redeem them from death’s hold?<br>Death, where are your diseases?<br>Grave,<sup>②</sup> where is your destruction?<br>Compassion is hidden from my eyes.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>Sheol</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>Sheol</i>  
**13:15** Although he may flourish among rushes,<br>the east wind will come—<br>the breath of God<br>rising from the wilderness;<br>and his spring will dry up;<br>his fountain will be dried up.<br>It will strip his household<br>of every cherished possession.  
**13:16** <sup>①</sup> Samaria will be desolate,<br>because she has rebelled against her God;<br>by the sword they will fall—<br>their babies will be dashed,<br>and their pregnant women ripped open.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:350 14:1'>14:1 in Heb  

## 14장

**14:1** <sup>①</sup> Return, Israel, to the LORD your God;<br>you have stumbled because of your wickedness.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:350 14:2'>14:2 in Heb  
**14:2** Prepare to speak<br>and return to the LORD;<br>say to the LORD,<br>“Forgive all wickedness;<br>and receive the good.<br>Instead of bulls,<br>let us offer what we can say:  
**14:3** Assyria won’t save us;<br>we won’t ride upon horses;<br>we will no longer say, ‘Our God,’<br>to the work of our hands.<br>In you the orphan finds compassion.”  
**14:4** I will heal their faithlessness;<br>I will love them freely,<br>for my anger has turned from them.  
**14:5** I will be like the dew to Israel;<br>he will blossom like the lily;<br>he will cast out his roots like the forests of Lebanon.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>like Lebanon</i>  
**14:6** His branches will spread out;<br>his beauty will be like the olive tree,<br>and his fragrance like that of Lebanon.  
**14:7** They will again live beneath my shadow,<br>they will flourish like a garden;<br>they will blossom like the vine,<br>their fragrance will be like the wine of Lebanon.  
**14:8** Ephraim, what do idols have to do with me?<br>It is I who answer and look after you.<br>I am like a green cypress tree;<br>your fruit comes from me.  
**14:9** Whoever is wise understands these things.<br>Whoever observes carefully knows them.<br>Truly, the LORD’s ways are right,<br>and the righteous will walk in them,<br>but evildoers will stumble in them.
