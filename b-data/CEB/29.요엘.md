# 29.요엘 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** The LORD’s word that came to Joel, Pethuel’s son:  
**1:2** Hear this, elders;<br>pay attention, everyone in the land!<br>Has anything like this ever happened in your days,<br>or in the days of your ancestors?<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>your fathers</i>  
**1:3** Tell it to your children,<br>and have your children tell their children,<br>and their children tell their children.  
**1:4** What the cutting locust left,<br>the swarming locust has eaten.<br>What the swarming locust left,<br>the hopping locust has eaten.<br>And what the hopping locust left,<br>the devouring locust has eaten.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>The Heb uses several different words for <i>locust</i>; none of the meanings are identical.  
**1:5** Wake up, you who drink too much, and weep.<br>Scream over the sweet wine,<br>all you wine drinkers,<br>because it is snatched from your mouth;  
**1:6** because a nation, powerful and beyond number,<br>has invaded my land.<br>Its teeth are like lions’ teeth;<br>its fangs are like those of a lioness.  
**1:7** It has destroyed my vines,<br>splintered my fig trees,<br>stripped off their bark and thrown it down;<br>their branches have turned white.  
**1:8** Lament like a woman dressed in funeral clothing,<br>one who has lost the husband of her youth.  
**1:9** The grain offering and the drink offering are gone from the LORD’s temple.<br>The priests and the LORD’s ministers mourn.  
**1:10** The fields are devastated,<br>the ground mourns;<br>for the grain is destroyed,<br>the new wine dries up,<br>the olive oil fails.  
**1:11** Be shocked, you farmers;<br>howl, you vinedressers,<br>over the wheat and the barley,<br>for the crops of the field are destroyed.  
**1:12** The grapevine is dried up;<br>the fig tree withers.<br>Pomegranate, palm, and apple—<br>all the trees of the field are dried up.<br>Joy fades away from the people.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>from the sons of men</i>; cf <a name='B:360 2:1'>2:1 Heb <i>the people of the land</i>  
**1:13** Dress for a funeral and grieve, you priests;<br>lament, ministers of the altar.<br>Come, spend the night in funeral clothing, servants of my God,<br>because the grain offering and the drink offering<br>have gone from the temple of your God.  
**1:14** Demand a fast,<br>request a special assembly.<br>Gather the elders<br>and all the land’s people<br>to the temple of the LORD your God,<br>and cry out to the LORD.  
**1:15** What a terrible day!<br>The day of the LORD is near;<br>it comes like chaos from the Almighty.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>Shaddai</i>  
**1:16** Isn’t the food cut off<br>right before our eyes?<br>Aren’t joy and gladness also gone from our God’s house?  
**1:17** The grain shrivels under the shovels;<sup>①</sup><br>the barns are empty.<br>The granaries are in ruin<br>because the grain has dried up.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**1:18** How the animals groan!<br>Herds of cattle are in distress<br>because there is no pasture for them;<br>even the flocks of sheep pant.  
**1:19** To you, LORD, I cry,<br>for fire has completely destroyed<br>the pastures of the wilderness;<br>and flames have burned<br>all the trees of the field.  
**1:20** Even the field’s wild animals cry to you<br>because the streams have dried up;<br>the fire has completely destroyed<br>the meadows of the wilderness.  

## 2장

**2:1** Blow the horn in Zion;<br>give a shout on my holy mountain!<br>Let all the people of the land tremble,<br>for the day of the LORD is coming.<br>It is near—  
**2:2** a day of darkness and no light,<br>a day of clouds and thick darkness!<br>Like blackness spread out upon the mountains,<br>a great and powerful army<sup>①</sup> comes,<br>unlike any that has ever come before them,<br>or will come after them in centuries ahead.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>a great and strong people</i>  
**2:3** In front of them a fire consumes;<br>and behind them a flame burns.<br>Land ahead of them is like Eden’s garden,<br>but they leave behind them a barren wasteland;<br>nothing escapes them.  
**2:4** They resemble horses,<br>and like warhorses they charge,  
**2:5** like the rumbling of chariots.<br>They leap on the mountaintops—<br>like the crackling of a fire’s flame,<br>devouring the stubble;<br>like a powerful army<br>ready for battle.  
**2:6** In their presence, peoples shake with fear;<br>all faces turn red with worry.  
**2:7** Like warriors they charge;<br>like soldiers they climb the wall.<br>Each keeps to their own path;<br>they didn’t change their course.  
**2:8** They don’t crowd each other;<br>each keeps to their own path.<br>Even if they fall among the weapons,<br>they won’t stop.  
**2:9** They rush upon the city;<br>they run upon the walls.<br>They climb into the houses;<br>they enter through the windows like thieves.  
**2:10** The earth quakes before them;<br>the heavens shake.<br>The sun and the moon are darkened;<br>the stars have stopped shining,  
**2:11** because the LORD utters his voice<br>at the head of his army.<br>How numerous are his troops!<br>Mighty are those who obey his word.<br>The day of the LORD is great;<br>it stirs up great fear—who can endure it?  
**2:12** Yet even now, says the LORD,<br>return to me with all your hearts,<br>with fasting, with weeping, and with sorrow;  
**2:13** tear your hearts<br>and not your clothing.<br>Return to the LORD your God,<br>for he is merciful and compassionate,<br>very patient, full of faithful love,<br>and ready to forgive.  
**2:14** Who knows whether he will have a change of heart<br>and leave a blessing behind him,<br>a grain offering and a drink offering<br>for the LORD your God?  
**2:15** Blow the horn in Zion;<br>demand a fast;<br>request a special assembly.  
**2:16** Gather the people;<br>prepare a holy meeting;<br>assemble the elders;<br>gather the children,<br>even nursing infants.<br>Let the groom leave his room<br>and the bride her chamber.  
**2:17** Between the porch and the altar<br>let the priests, the LORD’s ministers, weep.<br>Let them say, “Have mercy, LORD, on your people,<br>and don’t make your inheritance a disgrace,<br>an example of failure among the nations.<br>Why should they say among the peoples,<br>‘Where is their God?’”  
**2:18** Then the LORD became passionate about this land,<sup>①</sup> and had pity on his people.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>then the LORD became jealous for his land</i>  
**2:19** The LORD responded to the people:<br>See, I am sending you<br>the corn, new wine, and fresh oil,<br>and you will be fully satisfied by it;<br>and I will no longer make you<br>a disgrace among the nations.  
**2:20** I will remove the northern army far from you<br>and drive it into a dried-up and desolate land,<br>its front into the eastern sea,<br>and its rear into the western sea.<br>Its stench will rise up;<br>its stink will come to the surface.<br>The LORD is about to do great things!  
**2:21** Don’t fear, fertile land;<br>rejoice and be glad,<br>for the LORD is about to do great things!  
**2:22** Don’t be afraid, animals of the field,<br>for the meadows of the wilderness will turn green;<br>the tree will bear its fruit;<br>the fig tree and grapevine will give their full yield.  
**2:23** Children of Zion,<br>rejoice and be glad in the LORD your God,<br>because he will give you the early rain as a sign of righteousness;<br>he will pour down abundant rain for you,<br>the early and the late rain, as before.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>at the first</i>  
**2:24** The threshing floors will be full of grain;<br>the vats will overflow with new wine and fresh oil.  
**2:25** I will repay you for the years<br>that the cutting locust,<br>the swarming locust, the hopping locust, and the devouring locust have eaten—<br>my great army, which I sent against you.  
**2:26** You will eat abundantly and be satisfied,<br>and you will praise the name of the LORD your God,<br>who has done wonders for you;<br>and my people will never again be put to shame.  
**2:27** You will know that I am in the midst of Israel,<br>and that I am the LORD your God—no other exists;<br>never again will my people be put to shame.  
**2:28** After that I will pour out my spirit upon everyone;<br>your sons and your daughters will prophesy,<br>your old men will dream dreams,<br>and your young men will see visions.  
**2:29** In those days, I will also pour out my<br>spirit on the male and female slaves.  
**2:30** I will give signs in the heavens and on the earth—blood and fire and columns of smoke.  
**2:31** The sun will be turned to darkness, and the moon to blood before the great and dreadful day of the LORD comes.  
**2:32** But everyone who calls on the LORD’s name will be saved; for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be security, as the LORD has promised; and in Jerusalem, the LORD will summon those who survive.  

## 3장

**3:1** Truly, in those days and in that time, I will bring back to Judah and Jerusalem those who were sent away.  
**3:2** I will gather all the nations, and I will bring them to the Jehoshaphat Valley. There I will enter into judgment with them in support of my people and my possession, Israel, which they have scattered among the nations. They have divided my land,  
**3:3** and have cast lots for my people. They have traded boys for prostitutes, and sold girls for wine, which they drank down.  
**3:4** What are you to me, Tyre and Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Are you paying me back for something? If you are paying me back, then in a flash I will turn your deeds back upon your own heads.  
**3:5** You have taken my silver and my gold, and have carried my rich treasures into your temples.  
**3:6** You have sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks, removing them far from their own border.  
**3:7** But now I am calling them from the places where you have sold them, and I will repay you for your deeds.  
**3:8** I will sell your sons and your daughters as a possession of the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans, to a nation far away; for the LORD has spoken.  
**3:9** Announce this among the nations:<br>Prepare a holy war,<br>wake up the warriors;<br>let all the soldiers draw near,<br>let them come up!  
**3:10** Beat the iron tips of your plows into swords<br>and your pruning tools into spears;<br>let the weakling say, “I am mighty.”  
**3:11** Come quickly,<br>all you surrounding nations;<br>gather yourselves there;<br>bring your mighty ones, LORD.  
**3:12** Let the nations prepare themselves,<br>and come up to the Jehoshaphat Valley;<br>for there I will sit to judge<br>all the surrounding nations.  
**3:13** Cut with the sickle,<br>for the harvest is ripe.<br>Go and crush grapes,<br>for the winepress is full.<br>The jars overflow with wine,<br>for their wickedness is great.  
**3:14** Crowd after crowd fills the valley of judgment,<br>for the day of the LORD is near<br>in the valley of judgment.  
**3:15** The sun and the moon are darkened;<br>the stars have ceased shining.  
**3:16** The LORD roars from Zion,<br>and utters his voice from Jerusalem;<br>the heavens and the earth quake.<br>But the LORD is a refuge for his people,<br>a shelter for the people of Israel.  
**3:17** So you will know that I am the LORD your God,<br>settle down in Zion, my holy mountain.<br>Jerusalem will be holy,<br>and never again will strangers pass through it.  
**3:18** In that day<br>the mountains will drip sweet wine,<br>the hills will flow with milk,<br>and all the streambeds of Judah<br>will flow with water;<br>a spring will come forth from the LORD’s house<br>and water the Shittim Valley.  
**3:19** Egypt will become desolate<br>and Edom a desolate wilderness.<br>This is because of the violence done to the people of Judah,<br>in whose land they have shed innocent blood.  
**3:20** But Judah will be inhabited forever,<br>and Jerusalem for all generations.  
**3:21** I will forgive their bloodguilt,<br>which I had not forgiven.<br>I will act on their account;<br>I will not pardon the guilty.<br>The LORD dwells in Zion.
