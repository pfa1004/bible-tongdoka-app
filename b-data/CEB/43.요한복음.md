## 1장

**1:1** In the beginning was the Word<br>and the Word was with God<br>and the Word was God.  
**1:2** The Word was with God in the beginning.  
**1:3** Everything came into being through the Word,<br>and without the Word<br>nothing came into being.<br>What came into being  
**1:4** through the Word was life,<sup>①</sup><br>and the life was the light for all people.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Everything came into being through the Word,/and without the Word / nothing came into being that came into being.</i> <i>In the Word was life</i>  
**1:5** The light shines in the darkness,<br>and the darkness doesn’t extinguish the light.  
**1:6** A man named John was sent from God.  
**1:7** He came as a witness to testify concerning the light, so that through him everyone would believe in the light.  
**1:8** He himself wasn’t the light, but his mission was to testify concerning the light.  
**1:9** The true light that shines on all people<br>was coming into the world.  
**1:10** The light was in the world,<br>and the world came into being through the light,<br>but the world didn’t recognize the light.  
**1:11** The light came to his own people,<br>and his own people didn’t welcome him.  
**1:12** But those who did welcome him,<br>those who believed in his name,<br>he authorized to become God’s children,  
**1:13** born not from blood<br>nor from human desire or passion,<br>but born from God.  
**1:14** The Word became flesh<br>and made his home among us.<br>We have seen his glory,<br>glory like that of a father’s only son,<br>full of grace and truth.  
**1:15** John testified about him, crying out, “This is the one of whom I said, ‘He who comes after me is greater than me because he existed before me.’”  
**1:16** From his fullness we have all received grace upon grace;  
**1:17** as the Law was given through Moses,<br>so grace and truth came into being through Jesus Christ.  
**1:18** No one has ever seen God.<br>God the only Son,<br>who is at the Father’s side,<br>has made God known.  
**1:19** This is John’s testimony when the Jewish leaders in Jerusalem sent priests and Levites to ask him, “Who are you?”  
**1:20** John confessed (he didn’t deny but confessed), “I’m not the Christ.”  
**1:21** They asked him, “Then who are you? Are you Elijah?” John said, “I’m not.” “Are you the prophet?” John answered, “No.”  
**1:22** They asked, “Who are you? We need to give an answer to those who sent us. What do you say about yourself?”  
**1:23** John replied,<br>“<i>I am a voice crying out in the wilderness,</i><br><i>Make the Lord’s path straight,</i><sup>①</sup>  just as the prophet Isaiah said.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:290 40:3'>Isa 40:3  
**1:24** Those sent by the Pharisees  
**1:25** asked, “Why do you baptize if you aren’t the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the prophet?”  
**1:26** John answered, “I baptize with water. Someone greater stands among you, whom you don’t recognize.  
**1:27** He comes after me, but I’m not worthy to untie his sandal straps.”  
**1:28** This encounter took place across the Jordan in Bethany where John was baptizing.  
**1:29** The next day John saw Jesus coming toward him and said, “Look! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!  
**1:30** This is the one about whom I said, ‘He who comes after me is really greater than me because he existed before me.’  
**1:31** Even I didn’t recognize him, but I came baptizing with water so that he might be made known to Israel.”  
**1:32** John testified, “I saw the Spirit coming down from heaven like a dove, and it rested on him.  
**1:33** Even I didn’t recognize him, but the one who sent me to baptize with water said to me, ‘The one on whom you see the Spirit coming down and resting is the one who baptizes with the Holy Spirit.’  
**1:34** I have seen and testified that this one is God’s Son.”  
**1:35** The next day John was standing again with two of his disciples.  
**1:36** When he saw Jesus walking along he said, “Look! The Lamb of God!”  
**1:37** The two disciples heard what he said, and they followed Jesus.  
**1:38** When Jesus turned and saw them following, he asked, “What are you looking for?” They said, “Rabbi (which is translated <i>Teacher</i>), where are you staying?”  
**1:39** He replied, “Come and see.” So they went and saw where he was staying, and they remained with him that day. It was about four o’clock in the afternoon.  
**1:40** One of the two disciples who heard what John said and followed Jesus was Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter.  
**1:41** He first found his own brother Simon and said to him, “We have found the Messiah” (which is translated <i>Christ</i><sup>①</sup> ).<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Anointed One</i>  
**1:42** He led him to Jesus. Jesus looked at him and said, “You are Simon, son of John. You will be called Cephas” (which is translated <i>Peter</i>).  
**1:43** The next day Jesus wanted to go into Galilee, and he found Philip. Jesus said to him, “Follow me.”  
**1:44** Philip was from Bethsaida, the hometown of Andrew and Peter.  
**1:45** Philip found Nathanael and said to him, “We have found the one Moses wrote about in the Law and the Prophets: Jesus, Joseph’s son, from Nazareth.”  
**1:46** Nathanael responded, “Can anything from Nazareth be good?” Philip said, “Come and see.”  
**1:47** Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward him and said about him, “Here is a genuine Israelite in whom there is no deceit.”  
**1:48** Nathanael asked him, “How do you know me?” Jesus answered, “Before Philip called you, I saw you under the fig tree.”  
**1:49** Nathanael replied, “Rabbi, you are God’s Son. You are the king of Israel.”  
**1:50** Jesus answered, “Do you believe because I told you that I saw you under the fig tree? You will see greater things than these!  
**1:51** I assure you that you will see heaven open and God’s angels going up to heaven and down to earth on the Human One.”<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Son of Man</i>  

## 2장

**2:1** On the third day there was a wedding in Cana of Galilee. Jesus’ mother was there, and  
**2:2** Jesus and his disciples were also invited to the celebration.  
**2:3** When the wine ran out, Jesus’ mother said to him, “They don’t have any wine.”  
**2:4** Jesus replied, “Woman, what does that have to do with me? My time hasn’t come yet.”  
**2:5** His mother told the servants, “Do whatever he tells you.”  
**2:6** Nearby were six stone water jars used for the Jewish cleansing ritual, each able to hold about twenty or thirty gallons.  
**2:7** Jesus said to the servants, “Fill the jars with water,” and they filled them to the brim.  
**2:8** Then he told them, “Now draw some from them and take it to the headwaiter,” and they did.  
**2:9** The headwaiter tasted the water that had become wine. He didn’t know where it came from, though the servants who had drawn the water knew. The headwaiter called the groom  
**2:10** and said, “Everyone serves the good wine first. They bring out the second-rate wine only when the guests are drinking freely. You kept the good wine until now.”  
**2:11** This was the first miraculous sign that Jesus did in Cana of Galilee. He revealed his glory, and his disciples believed in him.  
**2:12** After this, Jesus and his mother, his brothers, and his disciples went down to Capernaum and stayed there for a few days.  
**2:13** It was nearly time for the Jewish Passover, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.  
**2:14** He found in the temple those who were selling cattle, sheep, and doves, as well as those involved in exchanging currency sitting there.  
**2:15** He made a whip from ropes and chased them all out of the temple, including the cattle and the sheep. He scattered the coins and overturned the tables of those who exchanged currency.  
**2:16** He said to the dove sellers, “Get these things out of here! Don’t make my Father’s house a place of business.”  
**2:17** His disciples remembered that it is written, <i>Passion for your house consumes me.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:230 69:9'>Ps 69:9  
**2:18** Then the Jewish leaders asked him, “By what authority are you doing these things? What miraculous sign will you show us?”  
**2:19** Jesus answered, “Destroy this temple and in three days I’ll raise it up.”  
**2:20** The Jewish leaders replied, “It took forty-six years to build this temple, and you will raise it up in three days?”  
**2:21** But the temple Jesus was talking about was his body.  
**2:22** After he was raised from the dead, his disciples remembered what he had said, and they believed the scripture and the word that Jesus had spoken.  
**2:23** While Jesus was in Jerusalem for the Passover Festival, many believed in his name because they saw the miraculous signs that he did.  
**2:24** But Jesus didn’t trust himself to them because he knew all people.  
**2:25** He didn’t need anyone to tell him about human nature, for he knew what human nature was.  

## 3장

**3:1** There was a Pharisee named Nicodemus, a Jewish leader.  
**3:2** He came to Jesus at night and said to him, “Rabbi, we know that you are a teacher who has come from God, for no one could do these miraculous signs that you do unless God is with him.”  
**3:3** Jesus answered, “I assure you, unless someone is born anew,<sup>①</sup> it’s not possible to see God’s kingdom.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>from above</i>  
**3:4** Nicodemus asked, “How is it possible for an adult to be born? It’s impossible to enter the mother’s womb for a second time and be born, isn’t it?”  
**3:5** Jesus answered, “I assure you, unless someone is born of water and the Spirit, it’s not possible to enter God’s kingdom.  
**3:6** Whatever is born of the flesh is flesh, and whatever is born of the Spirit is spirit.  
**3:7** Don’t be surprised that I said to you, ‘You must be born anew.’  
**3:8** God’s Spirit<sup>①</sup> blows wherever it wishes. You hear its sound, but you don’t know where it comes from or where it is going. It’s the same with everyone who is born of the Spirit.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>wind</i>  
**3:9** Nicodemus said, “How are these things possible?”  
**3:10** “Jesus answered, “You are a teacher of Israel and you don’t know these things?  
**3:11** I assure you that we speak about what we know and testify about what we have seen, but you don’t receive our testimony.  
**3:12** If I have told you about earthly things and you don’t believe, how will you believe if I tell you about heavenly things?  
**3:13** No one has gone up to heaven except the one who came down from heaven, the Human One.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Son of Man</i>  
**3:14** Just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness, so must the Human One<sup>①</sup> be lifted up<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Son of Man</i>  
**3:15** so that everyone who believes in him will have eternal life.  
**3:16** God so loved the world that he gave his only Son, so that everyone who believes in him won’t perish but will have eternal life.  
**3:17** God didn’t send his Son into the world to judge the world, but that the world might be saved through him.  
**3:18** Whoever believes in him isn’t judged; whoever doesn’t believe in him is already judged, because they don’t believe in the name of God’s only Son.  
**3:19** “This is the basis for judgment: The light came into the world, and people loved darkness more than the light, for their actions are evil.  
**3:20** All who do wicked things hate the light and don’t come to the light for fear that their actions will be exposed to the light.  
**3:21** Whoever does the truth comes to the light so that it can be seen that their actions were done in God.”  
**3:22** After this Jesus and his disciples went into Judea, where he spent some time with them and was baptizing.  
**3:23** John was baptizing at Aenon near Salem because there was a lot of water there, and people were coming to him and being baptized.  
**3:24** <n><sup>①</sup></n> (John hadn’t yet been thrown into prison.)  
**3:25** A debate started between John’s disciples and a certain Jew about cleansing rituals.  
**3:26** They came to John and said, “Rabbi, look! The man who was with you across the Jordan, the one about whom you testified, is baptizing and everyone is flocking to him.”  
**3:27** John replied, “No one can receive anything unless it is given from heaven.  
**3:28** You yourselves can testify that I said that I’m not the Christ but that I’m the one sent before him.  
**3:29** The groom is the one who is getting married. The friend of the groom stands close by and, when he hears him, is overjoyed at the groom’s voice. Therefore, my joy is now complete.  
**3:30** He must increase and I must decrease.  
**3:31** The one who comes from above is above all things. The one who is from the earth belongs to the earth and speaks as one from the earth. The one who comes from heaven is above all things.  
**3:32** He testifies to what he has seen and heard, but no one accepts his testimony.  
**3:33** Whoever accepts his testimony confirms that God is true.  
**3:34** The one whom God sent speaks God’s words because God gives the Spirit generously.  
**3:35** The Father loves the Son and gives everything into his hands.  
**3:36** Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life. Whoever doesn’t believe in the Son won’t see life, but the angry judgment of God remains on them.”  

## 4장

**4:1** Jesus learned that the Pharisees had heard that he was making more disciples and baptizing more than John  
**4:2** (although Jesus’ disciples were baptizing, not Jesus himself).  
**4:3** Therefore, he left Judea and went back to Galilee.  
**4:4** Jesus had to go through Samaria.  
**4:5** He came to a Samaritan city called Sychar, which was near the land Jacob had given to his son Joseph.  
**4:6** Jacob’s well was there. Jesus was tired from his journey, so he sat down at the well. It was about noon.  
**4:7** A Samaritan woman came to the well to draw water. Jesus said to her, “Give me some water to drink.”  
**4:8** His disciples had gone into the city to buy him some food.  
**4:9** The Samaritan woman asked, “Why do you, a Jewish man, ask for something to drink from me, a Samaritan woman?” (Jews and Samaritans didn’t associate with each other.)  
**4:10** Jesus responded, “If you recognized God’s gift and who is saying to you, ‘Give me some water to drink,’ you would be asking him and he would give you living water.”  
**4:11** The woman said to him, “Sir, you don’t have a bucket and the well is deep. Where would you get this living water?  
**4:12** You aren’t greater than our father Jacob, are you? He gave this well to us, and he drank from it himself, as did his sons and his livestock.”  
**4:13** Jesus answered, “Everyone who drinks this water will be thirsty again,  
**4:14** but whoever drinks from the water that I will give will never be thirsty again. The water that I give will become in those who drink it a spring of water that bubbles up into eternal life.”  
**4:15** The woman said to him, “Sir, give me this water, so that I will never be thirsty and will never need to come here to draw water!”  
**4:16** Jesus said to her, “Go, get your husband, and come back here.”  
**4:17** The woman replied, “I don’t have a husband.” “You are right to say, ‘I don’t have a husband,’” Jesus answered.  
**4:18** “You’ve had five husbands, and the man you are with now isn’t your husband. You’ve spoken the truth.”  
**4:19** The woman said, “Sir, I see that you are a prophet.  
**4:20** Our ancestors worshipped on this mountain, but you and your people say that it is necessary to worship in Jerusalem.”  
**4:21** Jesus said to her, “Believe me, woman, the time is coming when you and your people will worship the Father neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem.  
**4:22** You and your people worship what you don’t know; we worship what we know because salvation is from the Jews.  
**4:23** But the time is coming—and is here!—when true worshippers will worship in spirit and truth. The Father looks for those who worship him this way.  
**4:24** God is spirit, and it is necessary to worship God in spirit and truth.”  
**4:25** The woman said, “I know that the Messiah is coming, the one who is called the Christ. When he comes, he will teach everything to us.”  
**4:26** Jesus said to her, “I Am—the one who speaks with you.”<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>It is I, the one who speaks with you.</i>  
**4:27** Just then, Jesus’ disciples arrived and were shocked that he was talking with a woman. But no one asked, “What do you want?” or “Why are you talking with her?”  
**4:28** The woman put down her water jar and went into the city. She said to the people,  
**4:29** “Come and see a man who has told me everything I’ve done! Could this man be the Christ?”  
**4:30** They left the city and were on their way to see Jesus.  
**4:31** In the meantime the disciples spoke to Jesus, saying, “Rabbi, eat.”  
**4:32** Jesus said to them, “I have food to eat that you don’t know about.”  
**4:33** The disciples asked each other, “Has someone brought him food?”  
**4:34** Jesus said to them, “I am fed by doing the will of the one who sent me and by completing his work.  
**4:35** Don’t you have a saying, ‘Four more months and then it’s time for harvest’? Look, I tell you: open your eyes and notice that the fields are already ripe for the harvest.  
**4:36** Those who harvest are receiving their pay and gathering fruit for eternal life so that those who sow and those who harvest can celebrate together.  
**4:37** This is a true saying, that one sows and another harvests.  
**4:38** I have sent you to harvest what you didn’t work hard for; others worked hard, and you will share in their hard work.”  
**4:39** Many Samaritans in that city believed in Jesus because of the woman’s word when she testified, “He told me everything I’ve ever done.”  
**4:40** So when the Samaritans came to Jesus, they asked him to stay with them, and he stayed there two days.  
**4:41** Many more believed because of his word,  
**4:42** and they said to the woman, “We no longer believe because of what you said, for we have heard for ourselves and know that this one is truly the savior of the world.”  
**4:43** After two days Jesus left for Galilee.  
**4:44** <n><sup>①</sup></n> (Jesus himself had testified that prophets have no honor in their own country.)  
**4:45** When he came to Galilee, the Galileans welcomed him because they had seen all the things he had done in Jerusalem during the festival, for they also had been at the festival.  
**4:46** He returned to Cana in Galilee where he had turned the water into wine. In Capernaum there was a certain royal official whose son was sick.  
**4:47** When he heard that Jesus was coming from Judea to Galilee, he went out to meet him and asked Jesus if he would come and heal his son, for his son was about to die.  
**4:48** Jesus said to him, “Unless you see miraculous signs and wonders, you won’t believe.”  
**4:49** The royal official said to him, “Lord, come before my son dies.”  
**4:50** Jesus replied, “Go home. Your son lives.” The man believed the word that Jesus spoke to him and set out for his home.  
**4:51** While he was on his way, his servants were already coming to meet him. They said, “Your son lives!”  
**4:52** So he asked them at what time his son had started to get better. And they said, “The fever left him yesterday at about one o’clock in the afternoon.”  
**4:53** Then the father realized that this was the hour when Jesus had said to him, “Your son lives.” And he and his entire household believed in Jesus.  
**4:54** This was the second miraculous sign Jesus did while going from Judea to Galilee.  

## 5장

**5:1** After this there was a Jewish festival, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.  
**5:2** In Jerusalem near the Sheep Gate in the north city wall is a pool with the Aramaic name Bethsaida. It had five covered porches,  
**5:3** and a crowd of people who were sick, blind, lame, and paralyzed sat there.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Critical editions of the Gk New Testament do not include the following addition <i>waiting for the water to move.</i> <sup>4 </sup><i>Sometimes an angel would come down to the pool and stir up the water. Then the first one going into the water after it had been stirred up was cured of any sickness.</i>  
**5:4**   
**5:5** A certain man was there who had been sick for thirty-eight years.  
**5:6** When Jesus saw him lying there, knowing that he had already been there a long time, he asked him, “Do you want to get well?”  
**5:7** The sick man answered him, “Sir,<sup>①</sup> I don’t have anyone who can put me in the water when it is stirred up. When I’m trying to get to it, someone else has gotten in ahead of me.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Lord</i>  
**5:8** Jesus said to him, “Get up! Pick up your mat and walk.”  
**5:9** Immediately the man was well, and he picked up his mat and walked. Now that day was the Sabbath.  
**5:10** The Jewish leaders said to the man who had been healed, “It’s the Sabbath; you aren’t allowed to carry your mat.”  
**5:11** He answered, “The man who made me well said to me, ‘Pick up your mat and walk.’”  
**5:12** They inquired, “Who is this man who said to you, ‘Pick it up and walk’?”  
**5:13** The man who had been cured didn’t know who it was, because Jesus had slipped away from the crowd gathered there.  
**5:14** Later Jesus found him in the temple and said, “See! You have been made well. Don’t sin anymore in case something worse happens to you.”  
**5:15** The man went and proclaimed to the Jewish leaders that Jesus was the man who had made him well.  
**5:16** As a result, the Jewish leaders were harassing Jesus, since he had done these things on the Sabbath.  
**5:17** Jesus replied, “My Father is still working, and I am working too.”  
**5:18** For this reason the Jewish leaders wanted even more to kill him—not only because he was doing away with the Sabbath but also because he called God his own Father, thereby making himself equal with God.  
**5:19** Jesus responded to the Jewish leaders, “I assure you that the Son can’t do anything by himself except what he sees the Father doing. Whatever the Father does, the Son does likewise.  
**5:20** The Father loves the Son and shows him everything that he does. He will show him greater works than these so that you will marvel.  
**5:21** As the Father raises the dead and gives life, so too does the Son give life to whomever he wishes.  
**5:22** The Father doesn’t judge anyone, but he has given all judgment to the Son  
**5:23** so that everyone will honor the Son just as they honor the Father. Whoever doesn’t honor the Son doesn’t honor the Father who sent him.  
**5:24** “I assure you that whoever hears my word and believes in the one who sent me has eternal life and won’t come under judgment but has passed from death into life.  
**5:25** “I assure you that the time is coming—and is here!—when the dead will hear the voice of God’s Son, and those who hear it will live.  
**5:26** Just as the Father has life in himself, so he has granted the Son to have life in himself.  
**5:27** He gives the Son authority to judge, because he is the Human One.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Son of Man</i>  
**5:28** Don’t be surprised by this, because the time is coming when all who are in their graves will hear his voice.  
**5:29** Those who did good things will come out into the resurrection of life, and those who did wicked things into the resurrection of judgment.  
**5:30** I can’t do anything by myself. Whatever I hear, I judge, and my judgment is just. I don’t seek my own will but the will of the one who sent me.  
**5:31** “If I testify about myself, my testimony isn’t true.  
**5:32** There is someone else who testifies about me, and I know his testimony about me is true.  
**5:33** You sent a delegation to John, and he testified to the truth.  
**5:34** Although I don’t accept human testimony, I say these things so that you can be saved.  
**5:35** John was a burning and shining lamp, and, at least for a while, you were willing to celebrate in his light.  
**5:36** “I have a witness greater than John’s testimony. The Father has given me works to do so that I might complete them. These works I do testify about me that the Father sent me.  
**5:37** And the Father who sent me testifies about me. You have never even heard his voice or seen his form,  
**5:38** and you don’t have his word dwelling with you because you don’t believe the one whom he has sent.  
**5:39** Examine the scriptures, since you think that in them you have eternal life. They also testify about me,  
**5:40** yet you don’t want to come to me so that you can have life.  
**5:41** “I don’t accept praise from people,  
**5:42** but I know you, that you don’t have God’s love in you.  
**5:43** I have come in my Father’s name, and you don’t receive me. If others come in their own name, you receive them.  
**5:44** How can you believe when you receive praise from each other but don’t seek the praise that comes from the only God?  
**5:45** “Don’t think that I will accuse you before the Father. Your accuser is Moses, the one in whom your hope rests.  
**5:46** If you believed Moses, you would believe me, because Moses wrote about me.  
**5:47** If you don’t believe the writings of Moses, how will you believe my words?”  

## 6장

**6:1** After this Jesus went across the Galilee Sea (that is, the Tiberias Sea).  
**6:2** A large crowd followed him, because they had seen the miraculous signs he had done among the sick.  
**6:3** Jesus went up a mountain and sat there with his disciples.  
**6:4** It was nearly time for Passover, the Jewish festival.  
**6:5** Jesus looked up and saw the large crowd coming toward him. He asked Philip, “Where will we buy food to feed these people?”  
**6:6** Jesus said this to test him, for he already knew what he was going to do.  
**6:7** Philip replied, “More than a half year’s salary<sup>①</sup> worth of food wouldn’t be enough for each person to have even a little bit.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>two hundred denaria</i>  
**6:8** One of his disciples, Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, said,  
**6:9** “A youth here has five barley loaves and two fish. But what good is that for a crowd like this?”  
**6:10** Jesus said, “Have the people sit down.” There was plenty of grass there. They sat down, about five thousand of them.  
**6:11** Then Jesus took the bread. When he had given thanks, he distributed it to those who were sitting there. He did the same with the fish, each getting as much as they wanted.  
**6:12** When they had plenty to eat, he said to his disciples, “Gather up the leftover pieces, so that nothing will be wasted.”  
**6:13** So they gathered them and filled twelve baskets with the pieces of the five barley loaves that had been left over by those who had eaten.  
**6:14** When the people saw that he had done a miraculous sign, they said, “This is truly the prophet who is coming into the world.”  
**6:15** Jesus understood that they were about to come and force him to be their king, so he took refuge again, alone on a mountain.  
**6:16** When evening came, Jesus’ disciples went down to the lake.

**21:20** Peter turned around and saw the disciple whom Jesus loved following them. This was the one who had leaned against Jesus at the meal and asked him, “Lord, who is going to betray you?”  
**21:21** When Peter saw this disciple, he said to Jesus, “Lord, what about him?”  
**21:22** Jesus replied, “If I want him to remain until I come, what difference does that make to you? You must follow me.”  
**21:23** Therefore, the word spread among the brothers and sisters that this disciple wouldn’t die. However, Jesus didn’t say he wouldn’t die, but only, “If I want him to remain until I come, what difference does that make to you?”  
**21:24** This is the disciple who testifies concerning these things and who wrote them down. We know that his testimony is true.  
**21:25** Jesus did many other things as well. If all of them were recorded, I imagine the world itself wouldn’t have enough room for the scrolls that would be written.
