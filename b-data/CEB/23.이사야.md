# 23.이사야 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision about Judah and Jerusalem that Isaiah, Amoz’s son, saw in the days of Judah’s kings Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah.  
**1:2** Hear you heavens, and listen earth,<br>for the LORD has spoken:<br>I reared children; I raised them,<br>and they turned against me!  
**1:3** An ox knows its owner,<br>and a donkey its master’s feeding trough.<br>But Israel doesn’t know;<br>my people don’t behave intelligently.  
**1:4** Doom! Sinful nation, people weighed down with crimes,<br>evildoing offspring, corrupt children!<br>They have abandoned the LORD,<br>despised the holy one of Israel;<br>they turned their backs on God.  
**1:5** Why do you invite further beatings?<br>Why continue to rebel?<br>Everyone’s head throbs,<br>and everyone’s heart fails.  
**1:6** From head to toe, none are well—<br>only bruises, cuts, and raw wounds,<br>not treated, not bandaged,<br>not soothed with oil.  
**1:7** Your country is deserted,<br>your cities burned with fire;<br>your land—strangers are devouring it in plain sight.<br>It’s a wasteland, as when foreigners raid.  
**1:8** Daughter Zion is left like a small shelter in a vineyard,<br>like a hut in a cucumber field,<br>like a city besieged.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>LXX, Vulg; MT <i>spared</i>  
**1:9** If the LORD of heavenly forces had not spared a few of us,<br>we would be like Sodom; we would resemble Gomorrah.  
**1:10** Hear the LORD’s word, you leaders of Sodom.<br>Listen to our God’s teaching,<br>people of Gomorrah!  
**1:11** What should I think about all your sacrifices?<br>says the LORD.<br>I’m fed up with entirely burned offerings of rams<br>and the fat of well-fed beasts.<br>I don’t want the blood of bulls, lambs, and goats.  
**1:12** When you come to appear before me,<br>who asked this from you,<br>this trampling of my temple’s courts?  
**1:13** Stop bringing worthless offerings.<br>Your incense repulses me.<br>New moon, sabbath, and the calling of an assembly—<br>I can’t stand wickedness with celebration!  
**1:14** I hate your new moons and your festivals.<br>They’ve become a burden that I’m tired of bearing.  
**1:15** When you extend your hands,<br>I’ll hide my eyes from you.<br>Even when you pray for a long time,<br>I won’t listen.<br>Your hands are stained with blood.  
**1:16** Wash! Be clean!<br>Remove your ugly deeds from my sight.<br>Put an end to such evil;  
**1:17** learn to do good.<br>Seek justice:<br>help the oppressed;<sup>①</sup><br>defend the orphan;<br>plead for the widow.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>LXX, Vulg; MT <i>lead the oppressor</i>  
**1:18** Come now, and let’s settle this,<br>says the LORD.<br>Though your sins are like scarlet,<br>they will be white as snow.<br>If they are red as crimson,<br>they will become like wool.  
**1:19** If you agree and obey,<br>you will eat the best food of the land.  
**1:20** But if you refuse and rebel,<br>you will be devoured by the sword.<br>The LORD has said this.  
**1:21** This faithful town has become a prostitute!<br>She was full of justice;<br>righteousness lived in her—<br>but now murderers.  
**1:22** Your silver has become impure;<br>your beer is diluted with water.  
**1:23** Your princes are rebels,<br>companions of thieves.<br>Everyone loves a bribe and pursues gifts.<br>They don’t defend the orphan,<br>and the widow’s cause never reaches them.  
**1:24** Therefore, says the LORD God of heavenly forces,<br>the mighty one of Israel:<br>Doom! I will vent my anger against my foes;<br>I will take it out on my enemies,  
**1:25** and I will turn my hand against you.<br>I will refine your impurities as with lye,<br>and remove all your cinders.  
**1:26** Then I will restore your judges as in earlier times,<br>and your counselors as at the beginning.<br>After this you will be called Righteous City, Faithful Town.  
**1:27** Zion will be redeemed by justice,<br>and those who change their lives by righteousness.  
**1:28** But God will shatter rebels and sinners alike;<br>those who abandon the LORD will be finished.  
**1:29** You will be ashamed of the oaks you once desired,<br>and embarrassed by the gardens you once chose.  
**1:30** You will be like an oak with withering leaves,<br>like a garden without water.  
**1:31** The strong will be like dry twigs,<br>their deeds like sparks;<br>the two will burn together,<br>with no one to extinguish them.

**2:18** the idols will completely pass away.  
**2:19** Go into caves in the rocks<br>and holes in the dust<br>before the terror of the LORD<br>and the splendor of God’s majesty,<br>when he arises to terrify the earth.  
**2:20** On that day, people will toss<br>to the rodents<sup>①</sup> and to the bats<br>their idols of silver and idols of gold,<br>which they made for themselves to worship.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain

**2:22** Quit admiring the human race,<br>who breathe through their nostrils.<br>Why should they be admired?  

## 3장

**3:1** Now the LORD God of heavenly forces is removing from Jerusalem and from Judah every form of support:<br>all rations of food and water;  
**3:2** soldier and warrior;<br>judge and prophet;<br>fortune-teller and elder;  
**3:3** commander and celebrity;<br>counselor, clever craftsman, and cunning charmer.  
**3:4** I will make youths their commanders;<br>mischief makers will rule over them.  
**3:5** The people will oppress each other,<br>each one against the other, neighbor against neighbor.<br>The young will bully the old,<br>the rogue, and the respectable.  
**3:6** Someone will seize a family member, saying, “You have clothing! You be our leader!<br>This mess will be your responsibility!”  
**3:7** Someone else will cry out on that day,<br>“I’m no healer!<br>I have neither food nor clothing in my house!<br>Don’t make me the leader of the people!”  
**3:8** Yes, Jerusalem has stumbled<br>and Judah has fallen,<br>because the way they talk and act in word and deed insults the LORD,<br>defying his brilliant glory.  
**3:9** Their bias in judgment gives them away;<br>like Sodom, they display their sins in public.<br>Doom to them, for they have done themselves in!  
**3:10** Tell the righteous how blessed they are;<br>they will eat the fruit of their labors.  
**3:11** Doom to the wicked; they are evil.<br>What they have done will be done to them.  
**3:12** As for my people—oppressors strip them<br>and swindlers<sup>①</sup> rule them.<br>My people—your leaders mislead you and confuse your paths.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>LXX; MT <i>women</i>  
**3:13** The LORD arises to accuse;<br>he stands to judge the peoples.  
**3:14** The LORD will enter into judgment<br>with the elders and princes of his people:<br>You yourselves have devoured the vineyard;<br>the goods stolen from the poor are in your houses.  
**3:15** How dare you crush my people<br>and grind the faces of the poor?<br>says the LORD God of heavenly forces.  
**3:16** The LORD says:<br>Because Zion’s daughters applaud themselves,<br>walking with their chins in the air,<br>flirting with their eyes,<br>tiptoeing as they walk, feet jingling—  
**3:17** the Lord will shave the heads of Zion’s daughters,<br>and will expose their scalps.  
**3:18** On that day, the LORD will remove:<br>the splendid ankle chains; headbands and moon-shaped pendants;  
**3:19** the earrings, bracelets, and veils;  
**3:20** the hats, bangles, and sashes;<br>the amulets and charms;  
**3:21** the signet rings and nose rings;  
**3:22** the robes and capes;<br>the shawls and handbags;  
**3:23** the mirrors and linen garments;<br>the turbans and the veils.  
**3:24** Instead of perfume there will be a disgusting odor;<br>instead of a sash, a rope;<br>instead of styled hair, shaved heads;<br>instead of expensive clothes, rags as mourning clothes;<br>instead of beauty, shame.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>DSS (1QIsa<sup>a</sup>); MT lacks <i>shame.</i>  
**3:25** Your men will fall by the sword,<br>your warriors in battle!  
**3:26** Her gates will lament and mourn;<br>desolate, she will sit on the ground.  

## 4장

**4:1** Seven women will grab one man on that day, saying, “We will eat our own bread and wear our own clothes—only let us take your name; take away our disgrace.”  
**4:2** On that day, the LORD’s branch will become beautiful and glorious. The earth’s fruit will be the pride and splendor of Israel’s survivors.  
**4:3** Whoever remains in Zion and is left in Jerusalem will be called holy, everyone who is on the list of those living in Jerusalem.  
**4:4** When the Lord washes the filth from Zion’s daughters, and cleanses Jerusalem’s bloodguilt from within it by means of a wind of judgment and a searing wind,  
**4:5** then the LORD will create over the whole site of Mount Zion and over its assembly a cloud by day and smoke and the light of a blazing fire by night. Over all the glory there will be a canopy,  
**4:6** which will be a booth by day for shade from the heat and a hiding place and shelter from a stormy downpour.

## 6장

**6:1** In the year of King Uzziah’s death, I saw the Lord sitting on a high and exalted throne, the edges of his robe filling the temple.  
**6:2** Winged creatures were stationed around him. Each had six wings: with two they veiled their faces, with two their feet, and with two they flew about.  
**6:3** They shouted to each other, saying:<br>“Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of heavenly forces!<br>All the earth is filled with God’s glory!”  
**6:4** The doorframe shook at the sound of their shouting, and the house was filled with smoke.  
**6:5** I said, “Mourn for me; I’m ruined! I’m a man with unclean lips, and I live among a people with unclean lips. Yet I’ve seen the king, the LORD of heavenly forces!”  
**6:6** Then one of the winged creatures flew to me, holding a glowing coal that he had taken from the altar with tongs.  
**6:7** He touched my mouth and said, “See, this has touched your lips. Your guilt has departed, and your sin is removed.”  
**6:8** Then I heard the Lord’s voice saying, “Whom should I send, and who will go for us?” I said, “I’m here; send me.”  
**6:9** God said, “Go and say to this people:<br>Listen intently, but don’t understand;<br>look carefully, but don’t comprehend.  
**6:10** Make the minds of this people dull.<br>Make their ears deaf and their eyes blind,<br>so they can’t see with their eyes<br>or hear with their ears,<br>or understand with their minds,<br>and turn, and be healed.”  
**6:11** I said, “How long, Lord?” And God said, “Until cities lie ruined with no one living in them, until there are houses without people and the land is left devastated.”  
**6:12** The LORD will send the people far away, and the land will be completely abandoned.  
**6:13** Even if one-tenth remain there, they will be burned again, like a terebinth or an oak, which when it is cut down leaves a stump. Its stump is a holy seed.  

## 7장

**7:24** Only those with bows and arrows will go there, because the entire land will become thorns and thistles.  
**7:25** As for the hills that were once farmed with hoes, you won’t go there for fear of the thorns and thistles. They will become places where cattle are turned loose and sheep wander.
