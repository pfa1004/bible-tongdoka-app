## 1장

**1:1** A revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave him to show his servants what must soon take place. Christ made it known by sending it through his angel to his servant John,  
**1:2** who bore witness to the word of God and to the witness of Jesus Christ, including all that John saw.  
**1:3** Favored is the one who reads the words of this prophecy out loud, and favored are those who listen to it being read, and keep what is written in it, for the time is near.  
**1:4** John, to the seven churches that are in Asia: Grace and peace to you from the one who is and was and is coming, and from the seven spirits that are before God’s throne,  
**1:5** and from Jesus Christ—the faithful witness, the firstborn from among the dead, and the ruler of the kings of the earth. To the one who loves us and freed us from our sins by his blood,  
**1:6** who made us a kingdom, priests to his God and Father—to him be glory and power forever and always. Amen.  
**1:7** Look, he is coming with the clouds! Every eye will see him, including those who pierced him, and all the tribes of the earth will mourn because of him. This is so. Amen.  
**1:8** “I am the Alpha and the Omega,” says the Lord God, “the one who is and was and is coming, the Almighty.”  
**1:9** I, John, your brother who shares with you in the hardship, kingdom, and endurance that we have in Jesus, was on the island called Patmos because of the word of God and my witness about Jesus.  
**1:10** I was in a Spirit-inspired trance on the Lord’s day, and I heard behind me a loud voice that sounded like a trumpet.  
**1:11** It said, “Write down on a scroll whatever you see, and send it to the seven churches: to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea.”  
**1:12** I turned to see who was speaking to me, and when I turned, I saw seven oil lamps burning on top of seven gold stands.  
**1:13** In the middle of the lampstands I saw someone who looked like the Human One.<sup>①</sup> He wore a robe that stretched down to his feet, and he had a gold sash around his chest.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Son of Man</i>  
**1:14** His head and hair were white as white wool—like snow—and his eyes were like a fiery flame.  
**1:15** His feet were like fine brass that has been purified in a furnace, and his voice sounded like rushing water.  
**1:16** He held seven stars in his right hand, and from his mouth came a sharp, two-edged sword. His appearance was like the sun shining with all its power.  
**1:17** When I saw him, I fell at his feet like a dead man. But he put his right hand on me and said, “Don’t be afraid. I’m the first and the last,  
**1:18** and the living one. I was dead, but look! Now I’m alive forever and always. I have the keys of Death and the Grave.  
**1:19** So write down what you have seen, both the scene now before you and the things that are about to unfold after this.  
**1:20** As for the mystery of the seven stars that you saw in my right hand and the seven gold lampstands, here is what they mean: the seven stars are the angels of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are the seven churches.  

## 2장

**2:1** “Write this to the angel of the church in Ephesus: These are the words of the one who holds the seven stars in his right hand and walks among the seven gold lampstands:  
**2:2** I know your works, your labor, and your endurance. I also know that you don’t put up with those who are evil. You have tested those who say they are apostles but are not, and you have found them to be liars.  
**2:3** You have shown endurance and put up with a lot for my name’s sake, and you haven’t gotten tired.  
**2:4** But I have this against you: you have let go of the love you had at first.  
**2:5** So remember the high point from which you have fallen. Change your hearts and lives and do the things you did at first. If you don’t, I’m coming to you. I will move your lampstand from its place if you don’t change your hearts and lives.  
**2:6** But you have this in your favor: you hate what the Nicolaitans are doing, which I also hate.  
**2:7** If you can hear, listen to what the Spirit is saying to the churches. I will allow those who emerge victorious to eat from the tree of life, which is in God’s paradise.  
**2:8** “Write this to the angel of the church in Smyrna: These are the words of the one who is the first and the last, who died and came back to life:  
**2:9** I know your hardship and poverty (though you are actually rich). I also know the hurtful things that have been spoken about you by those who say they are Jews (though they are not, but are really Satan’s synagogue).  
**2:10** Don’t be afraid of what you are going to suffer. Look! The devil is going to throw some of you into prison in order to test you. You will suffer hardship for ten days. Be faithful even to the point of death, and I will give you the crown of life.  
**2:11** If you can hear, listen to what the Spirit is saying to the churches. Those who emerge victorious won’t be hurt by the second death.  
**2:12** “Write this to the angel of the church in Pergamum: These are the words of the one who has the sharp, two-edged sword:  
**2:13** I know that you are living right where Satan’s throne is. You are holding on to my name, and you didn’t break faith with me even at the time that Antipas, my faithful witness, was killed among you, where Satan lives.  
**2:14** But I have a few things against you, because you have some there who follow Balaam’s teaching. Balaam had taught Balak to trip up the Israelites so that they would eat food sacrificed to idols and commit sexual immorality.  
**2:15** In the same way, you have some who follow the Nicolaitans’ teaching.  
**2:16** So change your hearts and lives. If you don’t, I am coming to you soon, and I will make war on them with the sword that comes from my mouth.  
**2:17** If you can hear, listen to what the Spirit is saying to the churches. I will give those who emerge victorious some of the hidden manna to eat. I will also give to each of them a white stone with a new name written on it, which no one knows except the one who receives it.  
**2:18** “Write this to the angel of the church in Thyatira: These are the words of God’s Son, whose eyes are like a fiery flame, and whose feet are like fine brass.  
**2:19** I know your works, your love and faithfulness, your service and endurance. I also know that the works you have done most recently are even greater than those you did at first.  
**2:20** But I have this against you: you put up with that woman, Jezebel, who calls herself a prophet. You allow her to teach and to mislead my servants into committing sexual immorality and eating food sacrificed to idols.  
**2:21** I gave her time to change her heart and life, but she refuses to change her life of prostitution.  
**2:22** Look! I’m throwing her onto a sickbed. I am casting those who have committed adultery with her into terrible hardship—if they don’t change their hearts from following her practices—  
**2:23** and I will even put her children to death with disease. Then all the churches will know that I’m the one who examines minds and hearts, and that I will give to each of you what your actions deserve.  
**2:24** As for the rest of you in Thyatira—those of you who don’t follow this teaching and haven’t learned the so-called “deep secrets” of Satan—I won’t burden you with anything else.  
**2:25** Just hold on to what you have until I come.  
**2:26** To those who emerge victorious, keeping my practices until the end, I will give authority over the nations—  
**2:27** to rule the nations with an iron rod and smash them like pottery—  
**2:28** just as I received authority from my Father. I will also give them the morning star.  
**2:29** If you can hear, listen to what the Spirit is saying to the churches.  

## 3장

**3:1** “Write this to the angel of the church in Sardis: These are the words of the one who holds God’s seven spirits and the seven stars: I know your works. You have the reputation of being alive, and you are in fact dead.  
**3:2** Wake up and strengthen whatever you have left, teetering on the brink of death, for I’ve found that your works are far from complete in the eyes of my God.  
**3:3** So remember what you received and heard. Hold on to it and change your hearts and lives. If you don’t wake up, I will come like a thief, and you won’t know what time I will come upon you.  
**3:4** But you do have a few people in Sardis who haven’t stained their clothing. They will walk with me clothed in white because they are worthy.  
**3:5** Those who emerge victorious will wear white clothing like this. I won’t scratch out their names from the scroll of life, but will declare their names in the presence of my Father and his angels.  
**3:6** If you can hear, listen to what the Spirit is saying to the churches.  
**3:7** “Write this to the angel of the church in Philadelphia: These are the words of the one who is holy and true, who has the key of David. Whatever he opens, no one will shut; and whatever he shuts, no one opens.  
**3:8** I know your works. Look! I have set in front of you an open door that no one can shut. You have so little power, and yet you have kept my word and haven’t denied my name.  
**3:9** Because of this I will make the people from Satan’s synagogue (who say they are Jews and really aren’t, but are lying)—I will make them come and bow down at your feet and realize that I have loved you.  
**3:10** Because you kept my command to endure, I will keep you safe through the time of testing that is about to come over the whole world, to test those who live on earth.  
**3:11** I’m coming soon. Hold on to what you have so that no one takes your crown.  
**3:12** As for those who emerge victorious, I will make them pillars in the temple of my God, and they will never leave it. I will write on them the name of my God and the name of the city of my God, the New Jerusalem that comes down out of heaven from my God. I will also write on them my own new name.  
**3:13** If you can hear, listen to what the Spirit is saying to the churches.  
**3:14** “Write this to the angel of the church in Laodicea: These are the words of the Amen, the faithful and true witness, the ruler<sup>①</sup> of God’s creation.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>beginning</i>  
**3:15** I know your works. You are neither cold nor hot. I wish that you were either cold or hot.  
**3:16** So because you are lukewarm, and neither hot nor cold, I’m about to spit you out of my mouth.  
**3:17** After all, you say, ‘I’m rich, and I’ve grown wealthy, and I don’t need a thing.’ You don’t realize that you are miserable, pathetic, poor, blind, and naked.  
**3:18** My advice is that you buy gold from me that has been purified by fire so that you may be rich, and white clothing to wear so that your nakedness won’t be shamefully exposed, and ointment to put on your eyes so that you may see.  
**3:19** I correct and discipline those whom I love. So be earnest and change your hearts and lives.  
**3:20** Look! I’m standing at the door and knocking. If any hear my voice and open the door, I will come in to be with them, and will have dinner with them, and they will have dinner with me.  
**3:21** As for those who emerge victorious, I will allow them to sit with me on my throne, just as I emerged victorious and sat down with my Father on his throne.  
**3:22** If you can hear, listen to what the Spirit is saying to the churches.”  

## 4장

**4:1** After this I looked and there was a door that had been opened in heaven. The first voice that I had heard, which sounded like a trumpet, said to me, “Come up here, and I will show you what must take place after this.”  
**4:2** At once I was in a Spirit-inspired trance and I saw a throne in heaven, and someone was seated on the throne.  
**4:3** The one seated there looked like jasper and carnelian, and surrounding the throne was a rainbow that looked like an emerald.  
**4:4** Twenty-four thrones, with twenty-four elders seated upon them, surrounded the throne. The elders were dressed in white clothing and had gold crowns on their heads.  
**4:5** From the throne came lightning, voices, and thunder. In front of the throne were seven flaming torches, which are the seven spirits of God.  
**4:6** Something like a glass sea, like crystal, was in front of the throne. In the center, by the throne, were four living creatures encircling the throne. These creatures were covered with eyes on the front and on the back.  
**4:7** The first living creature was like a lion. The second living creature was like an ox. The third living creature had a face like a human being. And the fourth living creature was like an eagle in flight.  
**4:8** Each of the four living creatures had six wings, and each was covered all around and on the inside with eyes. They never rest day or night, but keep on saying,<br>“Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty,<br>who was and is and is coming.”  
**4:9** Whenever the living creatures give glory, honor, and thanks to the one seated on the throne, who lives forever and always,  
**4:10** the twenty-four elders fall before the one seated on the throne. They worship the one who lives forever and always. They throw down their crowns before the throne and say,  
**4:11** “You are worthy, our Lord and God,<br>to receive glory and honor and power,<br>because you created all things.<br>It is by your will that they existed and were created.”  

## 5장

**5:1** Then I saw a scroll in the right hand of the one seated on the throne. It had writing on the front and the back, and it was sealed with seven seals.  
**5:2** I saw a powerful angel, who proclaimed in a loud voice, “Who is worthy to open the scroll and break its seals?”  
**5:3** But no one in heaven or on earth or under the earth could open the scroll or look inside it.  
**5:4** So I began to weep and weep, because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or to look inside it.  
**5:5** Then one of the elders said to me, “Don’t weep. Look! The Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has emerged victorious so that he can open the scroll and its seven seals.”  
**5:6** Then, in between the throne and the four living creatures and among the elders, I saw a Lamb, standing as if it had been slain. It had seven horns and seven eyes, which are God’s seven spirits, sent out into the whole earth.  
**5:7** He came forward and took the scroll from the right hand of the one seated on the throne.  
**5:8** When he took the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb. Each held a harp and gold bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints.  
**5:9** They took up a new song, saying,<br>“You are worthy to take the scroll and open its seals,<br>because you were slain,<br>and by your blood you purchased for God<br>persons from every tribe, language, people, and nation.  
**5:10** You made them a kingdom and priests to our God,<br>and they will rule on earth.”  
**5:11** Then I looked, and I heard the sound of many angels surrounding the throne, the living creatures, and the elders. They numbered in the millions—thousands upon thousands.  
**5:12** They said in a loud voice,<br>“Worthy is the slaughtered Lamb<br>to receive power, wealth, wisdom, and might,<br>and honor, glory, and blessing.”  
**5:13** And I heard every creature in heaven and on earth and under the earth and in the sea—I heard everything everywhere say,<br>“Blessing, honor, glory, and power belong<br>to the one seated on the throne<br>and to the Lamb<br>forever and always.”  
**5:14** Then the four living creatures said, “Amen,” and the elders fell down and worshipped.  

## 6장

**6:1** Then I looked on as the Lamb opened one of the seven seals. I heard one of the four living creatures say in a voice like thunder, “Come!”  
**6:2** So I looked, and there was a white horse. Its rider held a bow and was given a crown. And he went forth from victory to victory.  
**6:3** When the Lamb opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature say, “Come!”  
**6:4** Out came another horse, fiery red. Its rider was allowed to take peace from the earth so that people would kill each other. He was given a large sword.  
**6:5** When he opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature say, “Come!” So I looked, and there was a black horse. Its rider held a balance for weighing in his hand.  
**6:6** I heard what sounded like a voice from among the four living creatures. It said, “A quart of wheat for a denarion,<sup>①</sup> and three quarts of barley for a denarion, but don’t damage the olive oil and the wine.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>A denarion was a day’s pay for a laborer.  
**6:7** When he opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature say, “Come!”  
**6:8** So I looked, and there was a pale green horse. Its rider’s name was Death, and the Grave was following right behind. They were given authority over a fourth of the earth, to kill by sword, famine, disease, and the wild animals of the earth.  
**6:9** When he opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar those who had been slaughtered on account of the word of God and the witness they had given.  
**6:10** They cried out with a loud voice, “Holy and true Master, how long will you wait before you pass judgment? How long before you require justice for our blood, which was shed by those who live on earth?”  
**6:11** Each of them was given a white robe, and they were told to rest a little longer, until their fellow servants and brothers and sisters—who were about to be killed as they were—were finished.  
**6:12** I looked on as he opened the sixth seal, and there was a great earthquake. The sun became black as funeral clothing, and the entire moon turned red as blood.  
**6:13** The stars of the sky fell to the earth as a fig tree drops its fruit when shaken by a strong wind.  
**6:14** The sky disappeared like a scroll being rolled up, and every mountain and island was moved from its place.  
**6:15** Then the kings of the earth, the officials and the generals, the rich and the powerful, and everyone, slave and free, hid themselves in caves and in the rocks of the mountains.  
**6:16** They called to the mountains and the rocks, “Fall on us and hide us from the face of the one seated on the throne and from the Lamb’s wrath!  
**6:17** The great day of their wrath has come, and who is able to stand?”

## 8장

**8:1** Then, when the Lamb opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven for about half an hour.  
**8:2** Then I saw the seven angels who stand before God, and seven trumpets were given to them.  
**8:3** Another angel came and stood at the altar, and he held a gold bowl for burning incense. He was given a large amount of incense, in order to offer it on behalf of the prayers of all the saints on the gold altar in front of the throne.  
**8:4** The smoke of the incense offered for the prayers of the saints rose up before God from the angel’s hand.  
**8:5** Then the angel took the incense container and filled it with fire from the altar. He threw it down to the earth, and there were thunder, voices, lightning, and an earthquake.  
**8:6** Then the seven angels who held the seven trumpets got ready to blow them.  
**8:7** The first angel blew his trumpet, and hail and fire mixed with blood appeared, and was thrown down to the earth. A third of the earth was burned up. A third of the trees were burned up. All the green grass was burned up.  
**8:8** Then the second angel blew his trumpet, and something like a huge mountain burning with fire was thrown down into the sea. A third of the sea became blood,  
**8:9** a third of the creatures living in the sea died, and a third of the ships were destroyed.  
**8:10** Then the third angel blew his trumpet, and a great star, burning like a torch, fell from heaven. It fell on a third of the rivers and springs of water.  
**8:11** The star’s name is Wormwood, and a third of the waters became wormwood, and many people died from the water, because it became so bitter.  
**8:12** Then the fourth angel blew his trumpet, and a third of the sun was struck, and a third of the moon, and a third of the stars so that a third of them became dark. The day lost a third of its light, and the night lost a third of its light too.  
**8:13** Then I looked and I heard an eagle flying high overhead. It said with a loud voice, “Horror, horror, oh! The horror for those who live on earth because of the blasts of the remaining trumpets that the three angels are about to blow!”  

## 9장

**9:1** Then the fifth angel blew his trumpet, and I saw a star that had fallen from heaven to earth, and he was given the key to the shaft of the abyss.  
**9:2** He opened the shaft of the abyss; and smoke rose up from the shaft, like smoke from a huge furnace. The sun and air were darkened by the smoke from the shaft.  
**9:3** Then locusts came forth from the smoke and onto the earth. They were given power like the power that scorpions have on the earth.  
**9:4** They were told not to hurt the grass of the earth or any green plant or any tree. They could only hurt the people who didn’t have the seal of God on their foreheads.  
**9:5** The locusts weren’t allowed to kill them, but only to make them suffer for five months—and the suffering they inflict is like that of a scorpion when it strikes a person.  
**9:6** In those days people will seek death, but they won’t find it. They will want to die, but death will run away from them.  
**9:7** The locusts looked like horses ready for battle. On their heads were what seemed to be gold crowns. Their faces were like human faces,  
**9:8** their hair was like women’s hair, and their teeth were like lions’ teeth.  
**9:9** In front they had what seemed to be iron armor upon their chests, and the sound of their wings was like the sound of many chariots and horses racing into battle.  
**9:10** They also have tails with stingers, just like scorpions; and in their tails is their power to hurt people for five months.  
**9:11** Their king is an angel from the abyss, whose Hebrew name is Abaddon,<sup>①</sup> and whose Greek name is Apollyon.<sup>②</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Destruction <sup>②</sup>Destroyer  
**9:12** The first horror has passed. Look! Two horrors are still coming after this.  
**9:13** Then the sixth angel blew his trumpet, and I heard a voice from the four horns of the gold altar that is before God.  
**9:14** It said to the sixth angel, who had the trumpet, “Release the four angels who are bound at the great river Euphrates.”  
**9:15** Then the four angels who had been made ready for that hour, day, month, and year were released to kill a third of humankind.  
**9:16** The number of cavalry troops was two hundred million. I heard their number.  
**9:17** And this is the way I saw the horses and their riders in the vision: they had breastplates that were fiery red, dark blue, and yellow as sulfur. The horses’ heads were like lions’ heads, and out of their mouths came fire, smoke, and sulfur.  
**9:18** By these three plagues a third of humankind was killed: by the fire, smoke, and sulfur coming out of their mouths.  
**9:19** The horses’ power is in their mouths and their tails, for their tails are like snakes with heads that inflict injuries.  
**9:20** The rest of humankind, who weren’t killed by these plagues, didn’t change their hearts and lives and turn from their handiwork. They didn’t stop worshipping demons and idols made of gold, silver, bronze, stone, and wood—idols that can’t see or hear or walk.  
**9:21** They didn’t turn away from their murders, their spells and drugs, their sexual immorality, or their stealing.  

## 10장

**10:1** Then I saw another powerful angel coming down from heaven. He was robed with a cloud, with a rainbow over his head. His face was like the sun, and his feet were like fiery pillars.  
**10:2** He held an open scroll in his hand. He put his right foot on the sea and his left foot on the land.  
**10:3** He called out with a loud voice like a lion roaring, and when he called out, the seven thunders raised their voices.  
**10:4** When the seven thunders spoke, I was about to write, but I heard a voice from heaven say, “Seal up what the seven thunders have said, and don’t write it down.”  
**10:5** Then the angel I saw standing on the sea and on the land raised his right hand to heaven.  
**10:6** He swore by the one who lives forever and always, who created heaven and what is in it, the earth and what is in it, and the sea and what is in it, and said, “The time is up.  
**10:7** In the days when the seventh angel blows his trumpet, God’s mysterious purpose will be accomplished, fulfilling the good news he gave to his servants the prophets.”  
**10:8** Then the voice I heard from heaven spoke to me again and said, “Go, take the opened scroll from the hand of the angel who stands on the sea and on the land.”  
**10:9** So I went to the angel and told him to give me the scroll. He said to me, “Take it and eat it. It will make you sick to your stomach, but sweet as honey in your mouth.”  
**10:10** So I took the scroll from the angel’s hand and ate it. And it was sweet as honey in my mouth, but when I swallowed it, it made my stomach churn.  
**10:11** I was told, “You must prophesy again about many peoples, nations, languages, and kings.”  

## 11장

**11:2** But don’t measure the court outside the temple. Leave that out, because it has been given to the nations, and they will trample the holy city underfoot for forty-two months.  
**11:3** “And I will allow my two witnesses to prophesy for one thousand two hundred sixty days, wearing mourning clothes.  
**11:4** These are the two olive trees and the two lampstands that stand before the Lord of the earth.  
**11:5** If anyone wants to hurt them, fire comes out of their mouth and burns up their enemies. So if anyone wants to hurt them, they have to be killed in this way.  
**11:6** They have the power to close up the sky so that no rain will fall for as long as they prophesy. They also have power over the waters, to turn them into blood, and to strike the earth with any plague, as often as they wish.

**21:4** He will wipe away every tear from their eyes. Death will be no more. There will be no mourning, crying, or pain anymore, for the former things have passed away.”  
**21:5** Then the one seated on the throne said, “Look! I’m making all things new.” He also said, “Write this down, for these words are trustworthy and true.”  
**21:6** Then he said to me, “All is done. I am the Alpha and the Omega, the beginning and the end. To the thirsty I will freely give water from the life-giving spring.  
**21:7** Those who emerge victorious will inherit these things. I will be their God, and they will be my sons and daughters.  
**21:8** But for the cowardly, the faithless, the vile, the murderers, those who commit sexual immorality, those who use drugs and cast spells, the idolaters and all liars—their share will be in the lake that burns with fire and sulfur. This is the second death.”  
**21:9** Then one of the seven angels who had the seven bowls full of the seven last plagues spoke with me. “Come,” he said, “I will show you the bride, the Lamb’s wife.”  
**21:10** He took me in a Spirit-inspired trance to a great, high mountain, and he showed me the holy city, Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God.  
**21:11** The city had God’s glory. Its brilliance was like a priceless jewel, like jasper that was as clear as crystal.  
**21:12** It had a great high wall with twelve gates. By the gates were twelve angels, and on the gates were written the names of the twelve tribes of Israel’s sons.  
**21:13** There were three gates on the east, three gates on the north, three gates on the south, and three gates on the west.  
**21:14** The city wall had twelve foundations, and on them were the twelve names of the Lamb’s twelve apostles.  
**21:15** The angel who spoke to me had a gold measuring rod with which to measure the city, its gates, and its wall.  
**21:16** Now the city was laid out as a square. Its length was the same as its width. He measured the city with the rod, and it was fifteen hundred miles.<sup>①</sup> Its length and width and height were equal.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>twelve thousand stades</i>  
**21:17** He also measured the thickness of its wall. It was two hundred sixteen feet<sup>①</sup> thick, as a person—or rather, an angel—measures things.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>one hundred forty-four pechon</i> (cubits)  
**21:18** The wall was built of jasper, and the city was pure gold, like pure glass.  
**21:19** The city wall’s foundations were decorated with every kind of jewel. The first foundation was jasper, the second was sapphire, the third was chalcedony, and the fourth was emerald.  
**21:20** The fifth was sardonyx, the sixth was carnelian, the seventh was chrysolite, and the eighth was beryl. The ninth was topaz, the tenth was chrysoprase, the eleventh was jacinth, and the twelfth was amethyst.  
**21:21** The twelve gates were twelve pearls; each one of the gates was made from a single pearl. And the city’s main street was pure gold, as transparent as glass.  
**21:22** I didn’t see a temple in the city, because its temple is the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb.  
**21:23** The city doesn’t need the sun or the moon to shine on it, because God’s glory is its light, and its lamp is the Lamb.  
**21:24** The nations will walk by its light, and the kings of the earth will bring their glory into it.  
**21:25** Its gates will never be shut by day, and there will be no night there.  
**21:26** They will bring the glory and honor of the nations into it.  
**21:27** Nothing unclean will ever enter it, nor anyone who does what is vile and deceitful, but only those who are registered in the Lamb’s scroll of life.
