# 20.잠언 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** The proverbs of Solomon, King David’s son, from Israel:  
**1:2** Their purpose is to teach wisdom and discipline,<br>to help one understand wise sayings.  
**1:3** They provide insightful instruction,<br>which is righteous, just, and full of integrity.  
**1:4** They make the naive mature,<br>the young knowledgeable and discreet.  
**1:5** The wise hear them and grow in wisdom;<br>those with understanding gain guidance.  
**1:6** They help one understand proverbs and difficult sayings,<br>the words of the wise, and their puzzles.  
**1:7** Wisdom begins with the fear of the LORD,<br>but fools despise wisdom and instruction.  
**1:8** Listen, my son, to your father’s instruction;<br>don’t neglect your mother’s teaching;  
**1:9** for they are a graceful wreath on your head,<br>and beads for your neck.  
**1:10** My son, don’t let sinners entice you.<br>Don’t go  
**1:11** when they say:<br>“Come with us.<br>Let’s set up a deadly ambush.<br>Let’s secretly wait for the innocent just for fun.  
**1:12** Let’s swallow up the living like the grave<sup>①</sup>—<br>whole, like those who go down into the pit.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>Sheol</i>  
**1:13** We’ll find all sorts of precious wealth;<br>we’ll fill our houses with plunder.  
**1:14** Throw in your lot with us;<br>we’ll share our money.”  
**1:15** My son, don’t go on the path with them;<br>keep your feet from their way,  
**1:16** because their feet run to evil;<br>they hurry to spill blood.  
**1:17** It’s useless to cast a net<br>in the sight of a bird.  
**1:18** But these sinners set up a deadly ambush;<br>they lie in wait for their own lives.  
**1:19** These are the ways of all who seek unjust gain;<br>it costs them their lives.  
**1:20** Wisdom shouts in the street;<br>in the public square she raises her voice.  
**1:21** Above the noisy crowd, she calls out.<br>At the entrances of the city gates, she has her say:  
**1:22** “How long will you clueless people love your naïveté,<br>mockers hold their mocking dear,<br>and fools hate knowledge?  
**1:23** You should respond when I correct you.<br>Look, I’ll pour out my spirit on you.<br>I’ll reveal my words to you.  
**1:24** I invited you, but you rejected me;<br>I stretched out my hand to you,<br>but you paid no attention.  
**1:25** You ignored all my advice,<br>and you didn’t want me to correct you.  
**1:26** So I’ll laugh at your disaster;<br>I’ll make fun of you when dread comes over you,  
**1:27** when terror hits you like a hurricane,<br>and your disaster comes in like a tornado,<br>when distress and oppression overcome you.  
**1:28** Then they will call me, but I won’t answer;<br>they will seek me, but won’t find me  
**1:29** because they hated knowledge<br>and didn’t choose the fear of the LORD.  
**1:30** They didn’t want my advice;<br>they rejected all my corrections.  
**1:31** They will eat from the fruit of their way,<br>and they’ll be full of their own schemes.  
**1:32** The immature will die because they turn away;<br>smugness will destroy fools.  
**1:33** Those who obey me will dwell securely,<br>untroubled by the dread of harm.”  

## 2장

**2:1** My son, accept my words<br>and store up my commands.  
**2:2** Turn your ear toward wisdom,<br>and stretch your mind toward understanding.  
**2:3** Call out for insight,<br>and cry aloud for understanding.  
**2:4** Seek it like silver;<br>search for it like hidden treasure.  
**2:5** Then you will understand the fear of the LORD,<br>and discover the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** The LORD gives wisdom;<br>from his mouth come knowledge and understanding.  
**2:7** He reserves ability for those with integrity.<br>He is a shield for those who live a blameless life.  
**2:8** He protects the paths of justice<br>and guards the way of those who are loyal to him.  
**2:9** Then you will understand righteousness and justice,<br>as well as integrity, every good course.  
**2:10** Wisdom will enter your mind,<br>and knowledge will fill you with delight.  
**2:11** Discretion will guard you;<br>understanding will protect you.  
**2:12** Wisdom will rescue you from the evil path,<br>from people who twist their words.  
**2:13** They forsake the way of integrity<br>and go on obscure paths.  
**2:14** They enjoy doing evil,<br>rejoicing in their twisted evil.  
**2:15** Their paths are confused;<br>they get lost on their way.  
**2:16** Wisdom will rescue you from the mysterious woman,<br>from the foreign woman with her slick words.  
**2:17** She leaves behind the partner of her youth;<br>she even forgets her covenant with God.  
**2:18** Her house sinks down to death,<br>and her paths go down to the shadowy dead.  
**2:19** All those who go to her will never return;<br>they will never again reach the ways of the living.  
**2:20** So you should stay on the path of good people,<br>guarding the road of the righteous.  
**2:21** Those who have integrity will dwell in the land;<br>the innocent will remain in it.  
**2:22** But the wicked will be cut off from the land,<br>and the treacherous will be ripped up.  

## 3장

**3:1** My son, don’t forget my instruction.<br>Let your heart guard my commands,  
**3:2** because they will help you live a long time<br>and provide you with well-being.  
**3:3** Don’t let loyalty and faithfulness leave you.<br>Bind them on your neck;<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**3:4** Then you will find favor and approval<br>in the eyes of God and humanity.  
**3:5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart;<br>don’t rely on your own intelligence.  
**3:6** Know him in all your paths,<br>and he will keep your ways straight.  
**3:7** Don’t consider yourself wise.<br>Fear the LORD and turn away from evil.  
**3:8** Then your body<sup>①</sup> will be healthy<br>and your bones strengthened.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>navel</i>  
**3:9** Honor the LORD with your wealth<br>and with the first of all your crops.  
**3:10** Then your barns will be filled<br>with plenty,<br>and your vats will burst with wine.  
**3:11** Don’t reject the instruction of the LORD, my son;<br>don’t despise his correction.  
**3:12** The LORD loves those he corrects,<br>just like a father who treats his son with favor.  
**3:13** Happy are those who find wisdom<br>and those who gain understanding.  
**3:14** Her profit is better than silver,<br>and her gain better than gold.  
**3:15** Her value exceeds pearls;<br>all you desire can’t compare with her.  
**3:16** In her right hand is a long life;<br>in her left are wealth and honor.  
**3:17** Her ways are pleasant;<br>all her paths are peaceful.  
**3:18** She is a tree of life to those who embrace her;<br>those who hold her tight are happy.  
**3:19** The LORD laid the foundations of the earth with wisdom,<br>establishing the heavens with understanding.  
**3:20** With his knowledge, the watery depths burst open,<br>and the skies drop dew.  
**3:21** My son, don’t let them slip from your eyes;<br>hold on to sound judgment and discretion.  
**3:22** They will be life for your whole being,<br>and an ornament for your neck.  
**3:23** Then you will walk safely on your path,<br>and your foot won’t stumble.  
**3:24** If you lie down, you won’t be terrified.<br>When you lie down, your sleep will be pleasant.  
**3:25** Don’t fear sudden terror<br>or the ruin that comes to the wicked.  
**3:26** The LORD will be your confidence;<br>he will guard your feet from being snared.  
**3:27** Don’t withhold good from someone who deserves it,<br>when it is in your power to do so.  
**3:28** Don’t say to your neighbor, “Go and come back;<br>I’ll give it to you tomorrow,” when you have it.  
**3:29** Don’t plan to harm your neighbor<br>who trusts and lives near you.  
**3:30** Don’t accuse anyone without reason,<br>when they haven’t harmed you.  
**3:31** Don’t envy violent people<br>or choose any of their ways.  
**3:32** Devious people are detestable to the LORD,<br>but the virtuous are his close friends.  
**3:33** The LORD’s curse is on the house of the wicked,<br>but he blesses the home of the righteous.  
**3:34** He mocks mockers,<br>but he shows favor to the humble.  
**3:35** The wise gain respect,<br>but fools receive shame.  

## 4장

**4:1** Hear, children, fatherly instruction;<br>pay attention to gain understanding.  
**4:2** I’ll teach you well.<br>Don’t abandon my instruction.  
**4:3** When I was a son to my father,<br>tender and my mother’s favorite,  
**4:4** he taught me and said to me:<br>“Let your heart hold on to my words:<br>Keep my commands and live.  
**4:5** Get wisdom; get understanding.<br>Don’t forget and don’t turn away from my words.  
**4:6** Don’t abandon her, and she will guard you.<br>Love her, and she will protect you.  
**4:7** The beginning of wisdom:<br>Get wisdom!<br>Get understanding before anything else.  
**4:8** Highly esteem her, and she will exalt you.<br>She will honor you if you embrace her.  
**4:9** She will place a graceful wreath on your head;<br>she will give you a glorious crown.”  
**4:10** Listen, my son, and take in my speech,<br>then the years of your life will be many.  
**4:11** I teach you the path of wisdom.<br>I lead you in straight courses.  
**4:12** When you walk, you won’t be hindered;<br>when you run, you won’t stumble.  
**4:13** Hold on to instruction; don’t slack off;<br>protect it, for it is your life.  
**4:14** Don’t go on the way of the wicked;<br>don’t walk on the path of evil people.  
**4:15** Avoid it! Don’t turn onto it;<br>stay off of it and keep going!  
**4:16** They don’t sleep unless they do evil;<br>they are robbed of sleep unless they make someone stumble.  
**4:17** They eat the bread of evil,<br>and they drink the wine of violence.  
**4:18** The way of the righteous is like morning light<br>that gets brighter and brighter till it is full day.  
**4:19** The path of the wicked is like deep darkness;<br>they don’t know where they will stumble.  
**4:20** My son, pay attention to my words.<br>Bend your ear to my speech.  
**4:21** Don’t let them slip from your sight.<br>Guard them in your mind.  
**4:22** They are life to those who find them,<br>and healing for their entire body.  
**4:23** More than anything you guard, protect your mind, for life flows from it.  
**4:24** Have nothing to do with a corrupt mouth;<br>keep devious lips far from you.  
**4:25** Focus your eyes straight ahead;<br>keep your gaze on what is in front of you.  
**4:26** Watch your feet on the way,<br>and all your paths will be secure.  
**4:27** Don’t deviate a bit to the right or the left;<br>turn your feet away from evil.
