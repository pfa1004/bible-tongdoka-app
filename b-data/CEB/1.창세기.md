# 1.창세기 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** When God began to create<sup>①</sup> the heavens and the earth—<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>In the beginning, God created</i>  
**1:2** the earth was without shape or form, it was dark over the deep sea, and God’s wind swept over the waters—  
**1:3** God said, “Let there be light.” And so light appeared.  
**1:4** God saw how good the light was. God separated the light from the darkness.  
**1:5** God named the light Day and the darkness Night. There was evening and there was morning: the first day.  
**1:6** God said, “Let there be a dome in the middle of the waters to separate the waters from each other.”  
**1:7** God made the dome and separated the waters under the dome from the waters above the dome. And it happened in that way.  
**1:8** God named the dome Sky. There was evening and there was morning: the second day.  
**1:9** God said, “Let the waters under the sky come together into one place so that the dry land can appear.” And that’s what happened.  
**1:10** God named the dry land Earth, and he named the gathered waters Seas. God saw how good it was.  
**1:11** God said, “Let the earth grow plant life: plants yielding seeds and fruit trees bearing fruit with seeds inside it, each according to its kind throughout the earth.” And that’s what happened.  
**1:12** The earth produced plant life: plants yielding seeds, each according to its kind, and trees bearing fruit with seeds inside it, each according to its kind. God saw how good it was.  
**1:13** There was evening and there was morning: the third day.  
**1:14** God said, “Let there be lights in the dome of the sky to separate the day from the night. They will mark events, sacred seasons, days, and years.  
**1:15** They will be lights in the dome of the sky to shine on the earth.” And that’s what happened.  
**1:16** God made the stars and two great lights: the larger light to rule over the day and the smaller light to rule over the night.  
**1:17** God put them in the dome of the sky to shine on the earth,  
**1:18** to rule over the day and over the night, and to separate the light from the darkness. God saw how good it was.  
**1:19** There was evening and there was morning: the fourth day.  
**1:20** God said, “Let the waters swarm with living things, and let birds fly above the earth up in the dome of the sky.”  
**1:21** God created the great sea animals and all the tiny living things that swarm in the waters, each according to its kind, and all the winged birds, each according to its kind. God saw how good it was.  
**1:22** Then God blessed them: “Be fertile and multiply and fill the waters in the seas, and let the birds multiply on the earth.”  
**1:23** There was evening and there was morning: the fifth day.  
**1:24** God said, “Let the earth produce every kind of living thing: livestock, crawling things, and wildlife.” And that’s what happened.  
**1:25** God made every kind of wildlife, every kind of livestock, and every kind of creature that crawls on the ground. God saw how good it was.  
**1:26** Then God said, “Let us make humanity in our image to resemble us so that they may take charge of the fish of the sea, the birds in the sky, the livestock, all the earth, and all the crawling things on earth.”  
**1:27** God created humanity in God’s own image,<br>in the divine image God created them,<sup>①</sup><br>male and female God created them.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb has singular <i>him,</i> referring to <i>humanity.</i>  
**1:28** God blessed them and said to them, “Be fertile and multiply; fill the earth and master it. Take charge of the fish of the sea, the birds in the sky, and everything crawling on the ground.”  
**1:29** Then God said, “I now give to you all the plants on the earth that yield seeds and all the trees whose fruit produces its seeds within it. These will be your food.  
**1:30** To all wildlife, to all the birds in the sky, and to everything crawling on the ground—to everything that breathes—I give all the green grasses for food.” And that’s what happened.  
**1:31** God saw everything he had made: it was supremely good. There was evening and there was morning: the sixth day.  

## 2장

**2:1** The heavens and the earth and all who live in them were completed.  
**2:2** On the sixth<sup>①</sup> day God completed all the work that he had done, and on the seventh day God rested from all the work that he had done.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>LXX, Sam, Syr; MT <i>seventh</i>  
**2:3** God blessed the seventh day and made it holy, because on it God rested from all the work of creation.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>from all his work, which God created to do</i>  
**2:4** This is the account of the heavens and the earth when they were created. <i>World’s creation in the garden</i> </pb> On the day the LORD God made earth and sky—  
**2:5** before any wild plants appeared on the earth, and before any field crops grew, because the LORD God hadn’t yet sent rain on the earth and there was still no human being<sup>①</sup> to farm the fertile land,<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>man</i> (Heb <i>adam</i>)

**2:8** The LORD God planted a garden in Eden in the east and put there the human he had formed.  
**2:9** In the fertile land, the LORD God grew every beautiful tree with edible fruit, and also he grew the tree of life in the middle of the garden and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.  
**2:10** A river flows from Eden to water the garden, and from there it divides into four headwaters.  
**2:11** The name of the first river is the Pishon. It flows around the entire land of Havilah, where there is gold.  
**2:12** That land’s gold is pure, and the land also has sweet-smelling resins and gemstones.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain

**2:14** The name of the third river is the Tigris, flowing east of Assyria; and the name of the fourth river is the Euphrates.  
**2:15** The LORD God took the human and settled him in the garden of Eden to farm it and to take care of it.  
**2:16** The LORD God commanded the human, “Eat your fill from all of the garden’s trees;  
**2:17** but don’t eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, because on the day you eat from it, you will die!”  
**2:18** Then the LORD God said, “It’s not good that the human is alone. I will make him a helper that is perfect for him.”  
**2:19** So the LORD God formed from the fertile land all the wild animals and all the birds in the sky and brought them to the human to see what he would name them. The human gave each living being its name.  
**2:20** The human named all the livestock, all the birds in the sky, and all the wild animals. But a helper perfect for him was nowhere to be found.  
**2:21** So the LORD God put the human into a deep and heavy sleep, and took one of his ribs and closed up the flesh over it.  
**2:22** With the rib taken from the human, the LORD God fashioned a woman and brought her to the human being.  
**2:23** The human<sup>①</sup> said,<br>“This one finally is bone from my bones<br>and flesh from my flesh.<br>She will be called a woman<sup>②</sup><br>because from a man<sup>③</sup> she was taken.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>man</i> (Heb <i>adam</i>) <sup>②</sup>Or <i>wife</i> (Heb <i>ishshah</i>) <sup>③</sup>Or <i>husband</i> (Heb <i>ish</i>)  
**2:24** This is the reason that a man leaves his father and mother and embraces his wife, and they become one flesh.  
**2:25** The two of them were naked, the man and his wife, but they weren’t embarrassed.  

## 3장

**3:3** but not the fruit of the tree in the middle of the garden. God said, ‘Don’t eat from it, and don’t touch it, or you will die.’”  
**3:4** The snake said to the woman, “You won’t die!  
**3:5** God knows that on the day you eat from it, you will see clearly and you will be like God, knowing good and evil.”

**3:7** Then they both saw clearly and knew that they were naked. So they sewed fig leaves together and made garments for themselves.  
**3:8** During that day’s cool evening breeze, they heard the sound of the LORD God walking in the garden; and the man and his wife hid themselves from the LORD God in the middle of the garden’s trees.  
**3:9** The LORD God called to the man and said to him, “Where are you?”  
**3:10** The man<sup>①</sup> replied, “I heard your sound in the garden; I was afraid because I was naked, and I hid myself.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>He</i>  
**3:11** He said, “Who told you that you were naked? Did you eat from the tree, which I commanded you not to eat?”

**3:13** The LORD God said to the woman, “What have you done?!” And the woman said, “The snake tricked me, and I ate.”  
**3:14** The LORD God said to the snake,<br>“Because you did this,<br>you are the one cursed<br>out of all the farm animals,<br>out of all the wild animals.<br>On your belly you will crawl,<br>and dust you will eat<br>every day of your life.

**3:16** To the woman he said,<br>“I will make your pregnancy very painful;<br>in pain you will bear children.<br>You will desire your husband,<br>but he will rule over you.”  
**3:17** To the man he said, “Because you listened to your wife’s voice and you ate from the tree that I commanded, ‘Don’t eat from it,’<br>cursed is the fertile land because of you;<br>in pain you will eat from it<br>every day of your life.  
**3:18** Weeds and thistles will grow for you,<br>even as you eat the field’s plants;  
**3:19** by the sweat of your face you will eat bread—<br>until you return to the fertile land,<br>since from it you were taken;<br>you are soil,<br>to the soil you will return.”  
**3:20** The man named his wife Eve<sup>①</sup> because she is the mother of everyone who lives.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb sounds like <i>live.</i>  
**3:21** The LORD God made the man and his wife leather clothes and dressed them.  
**3:22** The LORD God said, “The human being<sup>①</sup> has now become like one of us, knowing good and evil.” Now, so he doesn’t stretch out his hand and take also from the tree of life and eat and live forever,<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>man</i> (Heb <i>adam</i>)  
**3:23** the LORD God sent him out of the garden of Eden to farm the fertile land from which he was taken.  
**3:24** He drove out the human. To the east of the garden of Eden, he stationed winged creatures wielding flaming swords to guard the way to the tree of life.  

## 4장

**4:3** Some time later, Cain presented an offering to the LORD from the land’s crops  
**4:4** while Abel presented his flock’s oldest offspring with their fat. The LORD looked favorably on Abel and his sacrifice  
**4:5** but didn’t look favorably on Cain and his sacrifice. Cain became very angry and looked resentful.  
**4:6** The LORD said to Cain, “Why are you angry, and why do you look so resentful?

**4:25** Adam knew his wife intimately again, and she gave birth to a son. She named him Seth<sup>①</sup> “because God has given me another child in place of Abel, whom Cain killed.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Sounds like the Heb verb <i>gave</i>  
**4:26** Seth also fathered a son and named him Enosh. At that time, people began to worship in the LORD’s name.  

## 5장

**5:31** In all, Lamech lived 777 years, and he died.  
**5:32** When Noah was 500 years old, Noah became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth.  

## 6장

**6:1** When the number of people started to increase throughout the fertile land, daughters were born to them.  
**6:2** The divine beings saw how beautiful these human women were, so they married the ones they chose.  
**6:3** The LORD said, “My breath<sup>①</sup> will not remain in humans forever, because they are flesh. They will live one hundred twenty years.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>spirit</i>  
**6:4** In those days, giants<sup>①</sup> lived on the earth and also afterward, when divine beings and human daughters had sexual relations and gave birth to children. These were the ancient heroes, famous men.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the Nephilim</i>  
**6:5** The LORD saw that humanity had become thoroughly evil on the earth and that every idea their minds thought up was always completely evil.  
**6:6** The LORD regretted making human beings on the earth, and he was heartbroken.  
**6:7** So the LORD said, “I will wipe off of the land the human race that I’ve created: from human beings to livestock to the crawling things to the birds in the skies, because I regret I ever made them.”  
**6:8** But as for Noah, the LORD approved of him.  
**6:9** These are Noah’s descendants. In his generation, Noah was a moral and exemplary man; he<sup>①</sup> walked with God.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>Noah</i>  
**6:10** Noah had three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.  
**6:11** In God’s sight, the earth had become corrupt and was filled with violence.  
**6:12** God saw that the earth was corrupt, because all creatures behaved corruptly on the earth.  
**6:13** God said to Noah, “The end has come for all creatures, since they have filled the earth with violence. I am now about to destroy them along with the earth,  
**6:14** so make a wooden ark.<sup>①</sup> Make the ark with nesting places and cover it inside and out with tar.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>ark of gopher wood</i>, an unknown species of tree

**6:16** Make a roof<sup>①</sup> for the ark and complete it one foot from the top.<sup>②</sup> Put a door in its side. In the hold below, make the second and third decks.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>window</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb uncertain  
**6:17** “I am now bringing the floodwaters over the earth to destroy everything under the sky that breathes. Everything on earth is about to take its last breath.  
**6:18** But I will set up my covenant with you. You will go into the ark together with your sons, your wife, and your sons’ wives.  
**6:19** From all living things—from all creatures—you are to bring a pair, male and female, into the ark with you to keep them alive.  
**6:20** From each kind of bird, from each kind of livestock, and from each kind of everything that crawls on the ground—a pair from each will go in with you to stay alive.  
**6:21** Take some from every kind of food and stow it as food for you and for the animals.”  
**6:22** Noah did everything exactly as God commanded him.  

## 7장

**7:1** The LORD said to Noah, “Go into the ark with your whole household, because among this generation I’ve seen that you are a moral man.  
**7:2** From every clean animal, take seven pairs, a male and his mate; and from every unclean animal, take one pair, a male and his mate;  
**7:3** and from the birds in the sky as well, take seven pairs, male and female, so that their offspring will survive throughout the earth.  
**7:4** In seven days from now I will send rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights. I will wipe off from the fertile land every living thing that I have made.”  
**7:5** Noah did everything the LORD commanded him.  
**7:6** Noah was 600 years old when the floodwaters arrived on earth.  
**7:7** Noah, his sons, his wife, and his sons’ wives with him entered the ark to escape the floodwaters.  
**7:8** From the clean and unclean animals, from the birds and everything crawling on the ground,  
**7:9** two of each, male and female, went into the ark with Noah, just as God commanded Noah.  
**7:10** After seven days, the floodwaters arrived on the earth.  
**7:11** In the six hundredth year of Noah’s life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day—on that day all the springs of the deep sea erupted, and the windows in the skies opened.  
**7:12** It rained on the earth forty days and forty nights.  
**7:13** That same day Noah, with his sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth, Noah’s wife, and his sons’ three wives, went into the ark.  
**7:14** They and every kind of animal—every kind of livestock, every kind that crawls on the ground, every kind of bird<sup>①</sup>—<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>LXX; MT <i>every bird, every winged thing</i>

**7:17** The flood remained on the earth for forty days. The waters rose, lifted the ark, and it rode high above the earth.  
**7:18** The waters rose and spread out over the earth. The ark floated on the surface of the waters.  
**7:19** The waters rose even higher over the earth; they covered all of the highest mountains under the sky.  
**7:20** The waters rose twenty-three feet high, covering the mountains.  
**7:21** Every creature took its last breath: the things crawling on the ground, birds, livestock, wild animals, everything swarming on the ground, and every human being.  
**7:22** Everything on dry land with life’s breath in its nostrils died.  
**7:23** God wiped away every living thing that was on the fertile land—from human beings to livestock to crawling things to birds in the sky. They were wiped off the earth. Only Noah and those with him in the ark were left.  
**7:24** The waters rose over the earth for one hundred fifty days.  

## 8장

**8:1** God remembered Noah, all those alive, and all the animals with him in the ark. God sent a wind over the earth so that the waters receded.  
**8:2** The springs of the deep sea and the skies<sup>①</sup> closed up. The skies held back the rain.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the windows of the skies</i>  
**8:3** The waters receded gradually from the earth. After one hundred fifty days, the waters decreased;  
**8:4** and in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day, the ark came to rest on the Ararat mountains.  
**8:5** The waters decreased gradually until the tenth month, and on the first day of the tenth month the mountain peaks appeared.  
**8:6** After forty days, Noah opened the window of the ark that he had made.  
**8:7** He sent out a raven, and it flew back and forth until the waters over the entire earth had dried up.  
**8:8** Then he sent out a dove to see if the waters on all of the fertile land had subsided,  
**8:9** but the dove found no place to set its foot. It returned to him in the ark since waters still covered the entire earth. Noah stretched out his hand, took it, and brought it back into the ark.  
**8:10** He waited seven more days and sent the dove out from the ark again.  
**8:11** The dove came back to him in the evening, grasping a torn olive leaf in its beak. Then Noah knew that the waters were subsiding from the earth.  
**8:12** He waited seven more days and sent out the dove, but it didn’t come back to him again.  
**8:13** In Noah’s six hundred first year, on the first day of the first month, the waters dried up from the earth. Noah removed the ark’s hatch and saw that the surface of the fertile land had dried up.  
**8:14** In the second month, on the seventeenth day, the earth was dry.  
**8:15** God spoke to Noah,  
**8:16** “Go out of the ark, you and your wife, your sons, and your sons’ wives with you.  
**8:17** Bring out with you all the animals of every kind—birds, livestock, everything crawling on the ground—so that they may populate the earth, be fertile, and multiply on the earth.”
