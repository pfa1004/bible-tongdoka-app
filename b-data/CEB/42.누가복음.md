## 1장

**1:1** Many people have already applied themselves to the task of compiling an account of the events that have been fulfilled among us.  
**1:2** They used what the original eyewitnesses and servants of the word handed down to us.  
**1:3** Now, after having investigated everything carefully from the beginning, I have also decided to write a carefully ordered account for you, most honorable Theophilus.  
**1:4** I want you to have confidence in the soundness of the instruction you have received.  
**1:5** During the rule of King Herod of Judea there was a priest named Zechariah who belonged to the priestly division of Abijah. His wife Elizabeth was a descendant of Aaron.  
**1:6** They were both righteous before God, blameless in their observance of all the Lord’s commandments and regulations.  
**1:7** They had no children because Elizabeth was unable to become pregnant and they both were very old.  
**1:8** One day Zechariah was serving as a priest before God because his priestly division was on duty.  
**1:9** Following the customs of priestly service, he was chosen by lottery to go into the Lord’s sanctuary and burn incense.  
**1:10** All the people who gathered to worship were praying outside during this hour of incense offering.  
**1:11** An angel from the Lord appeared to him, standing to the right of the altar of incense.  
**1:12** When Zechariah saw the angel, he was startled and overcome with fear.  
**1:13** The angel said, “Don’t be afraid, Zechariah. Your prayers have been heard. Your wife Elizabeth will give birth to your son and you must name him John.  
**1:14** He will be a joy and delight to you, and many people will rejoice at his birth,  
**1:15** for he will be great in the Lord’s eyes. He must not drink wine and liquor. He will be filled with the Holy Spirit even before his birth.  
**1:16** He will bring many Israelites back to the Lord their God.  
**1:17** He will go forth before the Lord, equipped with the spirit and power of Elijah. He will turn the hearts of fathers<sup>①</sup> back to their children, and he will turn the disobedient to righteous patterns of thinking. He will make ready a people prepared for the Lord.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>parents</i>  
**1:18** Zechariah said to the angel, “How can I be sure of this? My wife and I are very old.”  
**1:19** The angel replied, “I am Gabriel. I stand in God’s presence. I was sent to speak to you and to bring this good news to you.  
**1:20** Know this: What I have spoken will come true at the proper time. But because you didn’t believe, you will remain silent, unable to speak until the day when these things happen.”  
**1:21** Meanwhile, the people were waiting for Zechariah, and they wondered why he was in the sanctuary for such a long time.  
**1:22** When he came out, he was unable to speak to them. They realized he had seen a vision in the temple, for he gestured to them and couldn’t speak.  
**1:23** When he completed the days of his priestly service, he returned home.  
**1:24** Afterward, his wife Elizabeth became pregnant. She kept to herself for five months, saying,  
**1:25** “This is the Lord’s doing. He has shown his favor to me by removing my disgrace among other people.”  
**1:26** When Elizabeth was six months pregnant, God sent the angel Gabriel to Nazareth, a city in Galilee,  
**1:27** to a virgin who was engaged to a man named Joseph, a descendant of David’s house. The virgin’s name was Mary.  
**1:28** When the angel came to her, he said, “Rejoice, favored one! The Lord is with you!”  
**1:29** She was confused by these words and wondered what kind of greeting this might be.  
**1:30** The angel said, “Don’t be afraid, Mary. God is honoring you.  
**1:31** Look! You will conceive and give birth to a son, and you will name him Jesus.  
**1:32** He will be great and he will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of David his father.  
**1:33** He will rule over Jacob’s house forever, and there will be no end to his kingdom.”  
**1:34** Then Mary said to the angel, “How will this happen since I haven’t had sexual relations with a man?”  
**1:35** The angel replied, “The Holy Spirit will come over you and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. Therefore, the one who is to be born will be holy. He will be called God’s Son.  
**1:36** Look, even in her old age, your relative Elizabeth has conceived a son. This woman who was labeled ‘unable to conceive’ is now six months pregnant.  
**1:37** Nothing is impossible for God.”  
**1:38** Then Mary said, “I am the Lord’s servant. Let it be with me just as you have said.” Then the angel left her.  
**1:39** Mary got up and hurried to a city in the Judean highlands.  
**1:40** She entered Zechariah’s home and greeted Elizabeth.  
**1:41** When Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, the child leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.  
**1:42** With a loud voice she blurted out, “God has blessed you above all women, and he has blessed the child you carry.  
**1:43** Why do I have this honor, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?  
**1:44** As soon as I heard your greeting, the baby in my womb jumped for joy.  
**1:45** Happy is she who believed that the Lord would fulfill the promises he made to her.”  
**1:46** Mary said,<br>“With all my heart I glorify the Lord!  
**1:47** In the depths of who I am I rejoice in God my savior.  
**1:48** He has looked with favor on the low status of his servant.<br>Look! From now on, everyone will consider me highly favored  
**1:49** because the mighty one has done great things for me.<br>Holy is his name.  
**1:50** He shows mercy to everyone,<br>from one generation to the next,<br>who honors him as God.  
**1:51** He has shown strength with his arm.<br>He has scattered those with arrogant thoughts and proud inclinations.  
**1:52** He has pulled the powerful down from their thrones<br>and lifted up the lowly.  
**1:53** He has filled the hungry with good things<br>and sent the rich away empty-handed.  
**1:54** He has come to the aid of his servant Israel,<br>remembering his mercy,  
**1:55** just as he promised to our ancestors,<br>to Abraham and to Abraham’s descendants forever.”  
**1:56** Mary stayed with Elizabeth about three months, and then returned to her home.  
**1:57** When the time came for Elizabeth to have her child, she gave birth to a boy.  
**1:58** Her neighbors and relatives celebrated with her because they had heard that the Lord had shown her great mercy.  
**1:59** On the eighth day, it came time to circumcise the child. They wanted to name him Zechariah because that was his father’s name.  
**1:60** But his mother replied, “No, his name will be John.”  
**1:61** They said to her, “None of your relatives have that name.”  
**1:62** Then they began gesturing to his father to see what he wanted to call him.  
**1:63** After asking for a tablet, he surprised everyone by writing, “His name is John.”  
**1:64** At that moment, Zechariah was able to speak again, and he began praising God.  
**1:65** All their neighbors were filled with awe, and everyone throughout the Judean highlands talked about what had happened.  
**1:66** All who heard about this considered it carefully. They said, “What then will this child be?” Indeed, the Lord’s power was with him.  
**1:67** John’s father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied,  
**1:68** “Bless the Lord God of Israel<br>because he has come to help and has delivered his people.  
**1:69** He has raised up a mighty savior for us in his servant David’s house,  
**1:70** just as he said through the mouths of his holy prophets long ago.  
**1:71** He has brought salvation from our enemies<br>and from the power of all those who hate us.  
**1:72** He has shown the mercy promised to our ancestors,<br>and remembered his holy covenant,  
**1:73** the solemn pledge he made to our ancestor Abraham.<br>He has granted  
**1:74** that we would be rescued<br>from the power of our enemies<br>so that we could serve him without fear,  
**1:75** in holiness and righteousness in God’s eyes,<br>for as long as we live.  
**1:76** You, child, will be called a prophet of the Most High,<br>for you will go before the Lord to prepare his way.  
**1:77** You will tell his people how to be saved<br>through the forgiveness of their sins.  
**1:78** Because of our God’s deep compassion,<br>the dawn from heaven will break upon us,  
**1:79** to give light to those who are sitting in darkness<br>and in the shadow of death,<br>to guide us on the path of peace.”  
**1:80** The child grew up, becoming strong in character. He was in the wilderness until he began his public ministry to Israel.  

## 2장

**2:1** In those days Caesar Augustus declared that everyone throughout the empire should be enrolled in the tax lists.  
**2:2** This first enrollment occurred when Quirinius governed Syria.  
**2:3** Everyone went to their own cities to be enrolled.  
**2:4** Since Joseph belonged to David’s house and family line, he went up from the city of Nazareth in Galilee to David’s city, called Bethlehem, in Judea.  
**2:5** He went to be enrolled together with Mary, who was promised to him in marriage and who was pregnant.  
**2:6** While they were there, the time came for Mary to have her baby.  
**2:7** She gave birth to her firstborn child, a son, wrapped him snugly, and laid him in a manger, because there was no place for them in the guestroom.  
**2:8** Nearby shepherds were living in the fields, guarding their sheep at night.  
**2:9** The Lord’s angel stood before them, the Lord’s glory shone around them, and they were terrified.  
**2:10** The angel said, “Don’t be afraid! Look! I bring good news to you—wonderful, joyous news for all people.  
**2:11** Your savior is born today in David’s city. He is Christ the Lord.  
**2:12** This is a sign for you: you will find a newborn baby wrapped snugly and lying in a manger.”  
**2:13** Suddenly a great assembly of the heavenly forces was with the angel praising God. They said,  
**2:14** “Glory to God in heaven, and on earth peace among those whom he favors.”  
**2:15** When the angels returned to heaven, the shepherds said to each other, “Let’s go right now to Bethlehem and see what’s happened. Let’s confirm what the Lord has revealed to us.”  
**2:16** They went quickly and found Mary and Joseph, and the baby lying in the manger.  
**2:17** When they saw this, they reported what they had been told about this child.  
**2:18** Everyone who heard it was amazed at what the shepherds told them.  
**2:19** Mary committed these things to memory and considered them carefully.  
**2:20** The shepherds returned home, glorifying and praising God for all they had heard and seen. Everything happened just as they had been told.  
**2:21** When eight days had passed, Jesus’ parents circumcised him and gave him the name Jesus. This was the name given to him by the angel before he was conceived.  
**2:22** When the time came for their ritual cleansing, in accordance with the Law from Moses, they brought Jesus up to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord.  
**2:23** <n><sup>①</sup></n> (It’s written in the Law of the Lord, “Every firstborn male will be dedicated to the Lord.”)  
**2:24** They offered a sacrifice in keeping with what’s stated in the Law of the Lord, <i>A pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:30 12:8'>Lev 12:8; <a name='B:30 5:11'>5:11 LXX  
**2:25** A man named Simeon was in Jerusalem. He was righteous and devout. He eagerly anticipated the restoration of Israel, and the Holy Spirit rested on him.  
**2:26** The Holy Spirit revealed to him that he wouldn’t die before he had seen the Lord’s Christ.  
**2:27** Led by the Spirit, he went into the temple area. Meanwhile, Jesus’ parents brought the child to the temple so that they could do what was customary under the Law.  
**2:28** Simeon took Jesus in his arms and praised God. He said,  
**2:29** “Now, master, let your servant go in peace according to your word,  
**2:30** because my eyes have seen your salvation.  
**2:31** You prepared this salvation in the presence of all peoples.  
**2:32** It’s a light for revelation to the Gentiles<br>and a glory for your people Israel.”  
**2:33** His father and mother were amazed by what was said about him.  
**2:34** Simeon blessed them and said to Mary his mother, “This boy is assigned to be the cause of the falling and rising of many in Israel and to be a sign that generates opposition  
**2:35** so that the inner thoughts of many will be revealed. And a sword will pierce your innermost being too.”  
**2:36** There was also a prophet, Anna the daughter of Phanuel, who belonged to the tribe of Asher. She was very old. After she married, she lived with her husband for seven years.  
**2:37** She was now an 84-year-old widow. She never left the temple area but worshipped God with fasting and prayer night and day.  
**2:38** She approached at that very moment and began to praise God and to speak about Jesus to everyone who was looking forward to the redemption of Jerusalem.  
**2:39** When Mary and Joseph had completed everything required by the Law of the Lord, they returned to their hometown, Nazareth in Galilee.  
**2:40** The child grew up and became strong. He was filled with wisdom, and God’s favor was on him.  
**2:41** Each year his parents went to Jerusalem for the Passover Festival.  
**2:42** When he was 12 years old, they went up to Jerusalem according to their custom.  
**2:43** After the festival was over, they were returning home, but the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem. His parents didn’t know it.  
**2:44** Supposing that he was among their band of travelers, they journeyed on for a full day while looking for him among their family and friends.  
**2:45** When they didn’t find Jesus, they returned to Jerusalem to look for him.  
**2:46** After three days they found him in the temple. He was sitting among the teachers, listening to them and putting questions to them.  
**2:47** Everyone who heard him was amazed by his understanding and his answers.  
**2:48** When his parents saw him, they were shocked. His mother said, “Child, why have you treated us like this? Listen! Your father and I have been worried. We’ve been looking for you!”  
**2:49** Jesus replied, “Why were you looking for me? Didn’t you know that it was necessary for me to be in my Father’s house?”  
**2:50** But they didn’t understand what he said to them.  
**2:51** Jesus went down to Nazareth with them and was obedient to them. His mother cherished every word in her heart.  
**2:52** Jesus matured in wisdom and years, and in favor with God and with people.

**4:2** There he was tempted for forty days by the devil. He ate nothing during those days and afterward Jesus was starving.  
**4:3** The devil said to him, “Since you are God’s Son, command this stone to become a loaf of bread.”  
**4:4** Jesus replied, “It’s written, <i>People won’t live only by bread.”</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:50 8:3'>Deut 8:3  
**4:5** Next the devil led him to a high place and showed him in a single instant all the kingdoms of the world.  
**4:6** The devil said, “I will give you this whole domain and the glory of all these kingdoms. It’s been entrusted to me and I can give it to anyone I want.  
**4:7** Therefore, if you will worship me, it will all be yours.”  
**4:8** Jesus answered, “It’s written, <i>You will worship the Lord your God and serve only him.”</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:50 6:13'>Deut 6:13

**4:43** But he said to them, “I must preach the good news of God’s kingdom in other cities too, for this is why I was sent.”  
**4:44** So he continued preaching in the Judean synagogues.  

## 5장

**5:1** One day Jesus was standing beside Lake Gennesaret when the crowd pressed in around him to hear God’s word.  
**5:2** Jesus saw two boats sitting by the lake. The fishermen had gone ashore and were washing their nets.  
**5:3** Jesus boarded one of the boats, the one that belonged to Simon, then asked him to row out a little distance from the shore. Jesus sat down and taught the crowds from the boat.  
**5:4** When he finished speaking to the crowds, he said to Simon, “Row out farther, into the deep water, and drop your nets for a catch.”  
**5:5** Simon replied, “Master, we’ve worked hard all night and caught nothing. But because you say so, I’ll drop the nets.”  
**5:6** So they dropped the nets and their catch was so huge that their nets were splitting.  
**5:7** They signaled for their partners in the other boat to come and help them. They filled both boats so full that they were about to sink.  
**5:8** When Simon Peter saw the catch, he fell at Jesus’ knees and said, “Leave me, Lord, for I’m a sinner!”  
**5:9** Peter and those with him were overcome with amazement because of the number of fish they caught.  
**5:10** James and John, Zebedee’s sons, were Simon’s partners and they were amazed too. Jesus said to Simon, “Don’t be afraid. From now on, you will be fishing for people.”  
**5:11** As soon as they brought the boats to the shore, they left everything and followed Jesus.  
**5:12** Jesus was in one of the towns where there was also a man covered with a skin disease. When he saw Jesus, he fell on his face and begged, “Lord, if you want, you can make me clean.”  
**5:13** Jesus reached out his hand, touched him, and said, “I do want to. Be clean.” Instantly, the skin disease left him.  
**5:14** Jesus ordered him not to tell anyone. “Instead,” Jesus said, “go and show yourself to the priest and make an offering for your cleansing, as Moses instructed. This will be a testimony to them.”  
**5:15** News of him spread even more and huge crowds gathered to listen and to be healed from their illnesses.  
**5:16** But Jesus would withdraw to deserted places for prayer.  
**5:17** One day when Jesus was teaching, Pharisees and legal experts were sitting nearby. They had come from every village in Galilee and Judea, and from Jerusalem. Now the power of the Lord was with Jesus to heal.

**23:54** It was the Preparation Day for the Sabbath, and the Sabbath was quickly approaching.  
**23:55** The women who had come with Jesus from Galilee followed Joseph. They saw the tomb and how Jesus’ body was laid in it,  
**23:56** then they went away and prepared fragrant spices and perfumed oils. They rested on the Sabbath, in keeping with the commandment.  

## 24장

**24:1** Very early in the morning on the first day of the week, the women went to the tomb, bringing the fragrant spices they had prepared.  
**24:2** They found the stone rolled away from the tomb,  
**24:3** but when they went in, they didn’t find the body of the Lord Jesus.  
**24:4** They didn’t know what to make of this. Suddenly, two men were standing beside them in gleaming bright clothing.  
**24:5** The women were frightened and bowed their faces toward the ground, but the men said to them, “Why do you look for the living among the dead?  
**24:6** He isn’t here, but has been raised. Remember what he told you while he was still in Galilee,  
**24:7** that the Human One<sup>①</sup> must be handed over to sinners, be crucified, and on the third day rise again.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Son of Man</i>  
**24:8** Then they remembered his words.  
**24:9** When they returned from the tomb, they reported all these things to the eleven and all the others.  
**24:10** It was Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and the other women with them who told these things to the apostles.  
**24:11** Their words struck the apostles as nonsense, and they didn’t believe the women.  
**24:12** But Peter ran to the tomb. When he bent over to look inside, he saw only the linen cloth. Then he returned home, wondering what had happened.  
**24:13** On that same day, two disciples were traveling to a village called Emmaus, about seven miles from Jerusalem.  
**24:14** They were talking to each other about everything that had happened.  
**24:15** While they were discussing these things, Jesus himself arrived and joined them on their journey.  
**24:16** They were prevented from recognizing him.  
**24:17** He said to them, “What are you talking about as you walk along?” They stopped, their faces downcast.  
**24:18** The one named Cleopas replied, “Are you the only visitor to Jerusalem who is unaware of the things that have taken place there over the last few days?”  
**24:19** He said to them, “What things?” They said to him, “The things about Jesus of Nazareth. Because of his powerful deeds and words, he was recognized by God and all the people as a prophet.  
**24:20** But our chief priests and our leaders handed him over to be sentenced to death, and they crucified him.  
**24:21** We had hoped he was the one who would redeem Israel. All these things happened three days ago.  
**24:22** But there’s more: Some women from our group have left us stunned. They went to the tomb early this morning  
**24:23** and didn’t find his body. They came to us saying that they had even seen a vision of angels who told them he is alive.  
**24:24** Some of those who were with us went to the tomb and found things just as the women said. They didn’t see him.”  
**24:25** Then Jesus said to them, “You foolish people! Your dull minds keep you from believing all that the prophets talked about.  
**24:26** Wasn’t it necessary for the Christ to suffer these things and then enter into his glory?”  
**24:27** Then he interpreted for them the things written about himself in all the scriptures, starting with Moses and going through all the Prophets.  
**24:28** When they came to Emmaus, he acted as if he was going on ahead.  
**24:29** But they urged him, saying, “Stay with us. It’s nearly evening, and the day is almost over.” So he went in to stay with them.  
**24:30** After he took his seat at the table with them, he took the bread, blessed and broke it, and gave it to them.  
**24:31** Their eyes were opened and they recognized him, but he disappeared from their sight.  
**24:32** They said to each other, “Weren’t our hearts on fire when he spoke to us along the road and when he explained the scriptures for us?”  
**24:33** They got up right then and returned to Jerusalem. They found the eleven and their companions gathered together.  
**24:34** They were saying to each other, “The Lord really has risen! He appeared to Simon!”  
**24:35** Then the two disciples described what had happened along the road and how Jesus was made known to them as he broke the bread.  
**24:36** While they were saying these things, Jesus himself stood among them and said, “Peace be with you!”  
**24:37** They were terrified and afraid. They thought they were seeing a ghost.  
**24:38** He said to them, “Why are you startled? Why are doubts arising in your hearts?  
**24:39** Look at my hands and my feet. It’s really me! Touch me and see, for a ghost doesn’t have flesh and bones like you see I have.”  
**24:40** As he said this, he showed them his hands and feet.  
**24:41** Because they were wondering and questioning in the midst of their happiness, he said to them, “Do you have anything to eat?”  
**24:42** They gave him a piece of baked fish.  
**24:43** Taking it, he ate it in front of them.  
**24:44** Jesus said to them, “These are my words that I spoke to you while I was still with you—that everything written about me in the Law from Moses, the Prophets, and the Psalms must be fulfilled.”  
**24:45** Then he opened their minds to understand the scriptures.  
**24:46** He said to them, “This is what is written: the Christ will suffer and rise from the dead on the third day,  
**24:47** and a change of heart and life for the forgiveness of sins must be preached in his name to all nations, beginning from Jerusalem.  
**24:48** You are witnesses of these things.  
**24:49** Look, I’m sending to you what my Father promised, but you are to stay in the city until you have been furnished with heavenly power.”  
**24:50** He led them out as far as Bethany, where he lifted his hands and blessed them.  
**24:51** As he blessed them, he left them and was taken up to heaven.  
**24:52** They worshipped him and returned to Jerusalem overwhelmed with joy.  
**24:53** And they were continuously in the temple praising God.
