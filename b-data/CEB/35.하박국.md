# 35.하박국 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** The oracle that Habakkuk the prophet saw.  
**1:2** LORD, how long will I call for help and you not listen?<br>I cry out to you, “Violence!”<br>but you don’t deliver us.  
**1:3** Why do you show me injustice and look at anguish<br>so that devastation and violence are before me?<br>There is strife, and conflict abounds.  
**1:4** The Instruction is ineffective.<br>Justice does not endure<br>because the wicked surround the righteous.<br>Justice becomes warped.  
**1:5** Look among the nations and watch!<br>Be astonished and stare<br>because something is happening in your days<br>that you wouldn’t believe even if told.  
**1:6** I am about to rouse the Chaldeans,<br>that bitter and impetuous nation,<br>which travels throughout the earth to possess dwelling places it does not own.  
**1:7** The Chaldean is dreadful and fearful.<br>He makes his own justice and dignity.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>his justice and dignity come from him</i>  
**1:8** His horses are faster than leopards;<br>they are quicker than wolves of the evening.<br>His horsemen charge forward;<br>his horsemen come from far away.<br>They fly in to devour, swiftly, like an eagle.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>vulture</i>  
**1:9** They come for violence,<br>the horde with all their faces set toward the desert.<sup>①</sup><br>He takes captives like sand.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**1:10** He makes fun of kings;<br>rulers are ridiculous to him.<br>He laughs at every fortress,<br>then he piles up dirt and takes it.  
**1:11** He passes through like the wind and invades;<br>but he will be held guilty,<br>the one whose strength is his god.  
**1:12** LORD, aren’t you ancient, my God, my holy one?<br>Don’t let us die.<sup>①</sup><br>LORD, you put the Chaldean here for judgment.<br>Rock, you established him as a rebuke.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**1:13** Your eyes are too pure to look on evil;<br>you are unable to look at disaster.<br>Why would you look at the treacherous<br>or keep silent when the wicked swallows one who is more righteous?  
**1:14** You made humans like the fish of the sea,<br>like creeping things with no one to rule over them.  
**1:15** The Chaldean brings all of them up with a fishhook.<br>He drags them away with a net;<br>he collects them in his fishing net,<br>then he rejoices and celebrates.  
**1:16** Therefore, he sacrifices to his net;<br>he burns incense to his fishing nets,<br>because due to them his portion grows fat<br>and his food becomes luxurious.  
**1:17** Should he continue to empty his net<br>and continue to slay nations without sparing them?  

## 2장

**2:1** I will take my post;<br>I will position myself on the fortress.<br>I will keep watch to see what the Lord says to me<br>and how he<sup>①</sup> will respond to my complaint.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Syr <i>he</i>; MT <i>I</i>  
**2:2** Then the LORD answered me and said,<br>Write a vision, and make it plain upon a tablet<br>so that a runner can read it.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>a reader can run with it</i>  
**2:3** There is still a vision for the appointed time;<br>it testifies to the end;<br>it does not deceive.<sup>①</sup><br>If it delays, wait for it;<br>for it is surely coming; it will not be late.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain; antecedants to pronouns in <a name='B:420 2:3-6'>2:3–6 are uncertain.  
**2:4** Some people’s desires are truly audacious;<sup>①</sup><br>they don’t do the right thing.<br>But the righteous person will live honestly.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**2:5** Moreover, wine betrays an arrogant man.<br>He doesn’t rest.<br>He opens his jaws<sup>①</sup> like the grave;<sup>②</sup><br>like death, he is never satisfied.<br>He gathers all nations to himself<br>and collects all peoples for himself.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>throat</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>Sheol</i>  
**2:6** Won’t everyone tell parables about him<br>or mocking poems concerning him?  They will say:<br>Doom to the one who multiplies what doesn’t belong to him<br>and who increases his own burden.<br>How long?  
**2:7** Won’t they suddenly rise up to bite you?<br>Those who frighten you will awaken;<br>you will become plunder for them.  
**2:8** Since you yourself have plundered many nations,<br>all the rest of the peoples will plunder you<br>because of the human bloodshed<br>and the violence done to the earth,<br>to every village, and to all its inhabitants.  
**2:9** Doom to the one making evil gain for his own house,<br>for putting his own nest up high,<br>for delivering himself from the grasp of calamity.  
**2:10** You plan shame for your own house,<br>cutting off many peoples<br>and sinning against your own life.  
**2:11** A stone will cry out from a village wall,<br>and a tree branch will respond.  
**2:12** Pity the one building a city with bloodshed<br>and founding a village with injustice.  
**2:13** Look, isn’t this from the LORD of heavenly forces?<br>Peoples grow weary from making just enough fire;<br>nations become tired for nothing.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**2:14** But the land will be full of the knowledge of the LORD’s glory,<br>just as water covers the sea.  
**2:15** Doom to the one who makes his companions drunk,<br>pouring out your wrath in order to see them naked.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**2:16** You have drunk your fill of dishonor rather than glory.<br>So drink and stagger.<sup>①</sup><br>The cup of the LORD’s strong hand will come around to you;<br>disgrace will engulf you.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>DSS, LXX; MT <i>uncircumcised</i>  
**2:17** Because of the violence done to Lebanon, he will overwhelm you;<br>the destruction of animals will terrify you,<br>as will human bloodshed and violence throughout the land, the villages,<br>and all their inhabitants.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**2:18** Of what value is an idol, when its potter carves it,<br>or a cast image that has been shaped?<br>It is a teacher of lies,<br>for the potter trusts the pottery, though it is incapable of speaking.  
**2:19** Doom to the one saying to the tree, “Wake up!”<br>or “Get up” to the silent stone.<br>Does it teach?<br>Look, it is overlaid with gold and silver,<br>but there is no breath within it.  
**2:20** But the LORD is in his holy temple.<br>Let all the earth be silent before him.  

## 3장

**3:1** The prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, according to Shigionoth:  
**3:2** LORD, I have heard your reputation.<br>I have seen your work.<br>Over time, revive it.<br>Over time, make it known.<br>Though angry, remember compassion.  
**3:3** God comes from Teman<br>and the holy one from the mountain of Paran. <i>Selah</i><br>His majesty covers the heavens<br>and his praise fills the earth.  
**3:4** His radiance is like the sunlight,<br>with rays flashing from his hand.<br>That is the hiding place of his power.  
**3:5** Pestilence walks in front of him.<br>Plague marches at his feet.  
**3:6** He stops and measures the earth.<br>He looks and sets out against the nations.<br>The everlasting mountains collapse;<br>the eternal hills bow down;<br>the eternal paths belong to him.  
**3:7** I saw the tents of Cushan under duress.<br>The curtains of the land of Midian were quaking.  
**3:8** Was the LORD raging against the rivers?<br>Or was your anger directed against the rivers?<br>Or was your fury directed against the sea<br>when you rode on your horses<br>or rode your chariots to victory?  
**3:9** You raise up your empty bow,<br>uttering curses for the arrows.<sup>①</sup> <i>Selah</i><br>With rivers you split open the earth.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**3:10** The mountains see you and writhe.<br>A flood of water rushes through.<br>The deep utters its voice;<br>it raises its hands aloft.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**3:11** Sun and moon stand still high above.<br>With the light, your arrows shoot,<br>your spear at the flash of lightning.  
**3:12** In fury, you stride the earth;<br>in anger you tread the nations.  
**3:13** You go out to save your people.<br>For the salvation of your anointed<br>you smashed the head of the house of wickedness,<br>laying bare the foundation up to the neck. <i>Selah</i>  
**3:14** You pierce the head of his warrior with his own spear.<br>His warriors are driven off,<br>those who take delight in oppressing us,<sup>①</sup><br>those who take pleasure in secretly devouring the poor.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>me</i>  
**3:15** You make your horses tread on the sea;<br>turbulent waters foam.  
**3:16** I hear and my insides tremble.<br>My lips quiver at the sound.<br>Rottenness enters my bones.<br>I tremble while I stand,<sup>①</sup><br>while I wait for the day of distress to come against the people who attack us.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>I tremble beneath me.</i>  
**3:17** Though the fig tree doesn’t bloom,<br>and there’s no produce on the vine;<br>though the olive crop withers,<br>and the fields don’t provide food;<br>though the sheep are cut off from the pen,<br>and there are no cattle in the stalls;  
**3:18** I will rejoice in the LORD.<br>I will rejoice in the God of my deliverance.  
**3:19** The LORD God is my strength.<br>He will set my feet like the deer.<br>He will let me walk upon the heights.<sup>①</sup>  To the director, with stringed instruments<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>my heights</i>
