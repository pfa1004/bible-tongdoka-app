# 34.나훔 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** An oracle about Nineveh: the scroll containing the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.  
**1:2** The LORD is a jealous and vengeful God;<br>the LORD is vengeful and strong in wrath.<br>The LORD is vengeful against his foes;<br>he rages against his enemies.  
**1:3** The LORD is very patient but great in power;<br>the LORD punishes.<br>His way is in whirlwind and storm;<br>clouds are the dust of his feet.  
**1:4** He can blast the sea and make it dry up;<br>he can dry up all the rivers.<br>Bashan and Carmel wither;<br>the bud of Lebanon withers.  
**1:5** The mountains quake because of him;<br>the hills melt away.<br>The earth heaves before him—<br>the world and all who dwell in it.  
**1:6** Who can stand before his indignation?<br>Who can confront the heat of his fury?<br>His wrath pours out like fire;<br>the rocks are shattered because of him.  
**1:7** The LORD is good,<br>a haven in a day of distress.<br>He acknowledges those who take refuge in him.  
**1:8** With a rushing flood, he will utterly destroy her<sup>①</sup> place<br>and pursue his enemies into darkness.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>her;</i> likely Nineveh  
**1:9** What are you plotting against the LORD?<br>He is one who can annihilate!<br>Distress will not arise twice.  
**1:10** They are tangled up like thorns,<br>like drunkards in their cups.<br>They are consumed like stubble<br>that is entirely dried up.  
**1:11** From you<sup>①</sup> goes out<br>one who plots evil against the LORD—<br>a worthless counselor!<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>you</i> (feminine singular); likely Nineveh  
**1:12** The LORD proclaims:<br>Though once they were a healthy and numerous force,<br>they have been cut off and have disappeared.<br>I have afflicted you;<br>I won’t afflict you further, Zion.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb lacks <i>Zion.</i>  
**1:13** Now I will break off his yoke from you<br>and tear off your chains.  
**1:14** The LORD has commanded concerning you:<br>You will have no children to carry on your name.<br>I will remove carved idol and cast image from the house of your gods;<br>I will make your grave, for you are worthless.  
**1:15** <sup>①</sup> Look, on the mountains: the feet of a messenger who announces peace!<br>Celebrate your festivals, Judah!<br>Fulfill your solemn promises!<br>The worthless one will never again invade you;<br>he has been completely cut off.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:410 2:1'>2:1 in Heb  

## 2장

**2:1** A scatterer has come up against you.<br>Guard the ramparts, watch the road,<br>protect your groin, save your<sup>①</sup> strength!<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb lacks <i>your.</i>  
**2:2** The LORD will restore the pride of Jacob,<br>indeed, the pride of Israel,<br>because ravagers have destroyed<br>them and spoiled their branches.  
**2:3** The shields of his warriors are red;<br>his soldiers are dressed in crimson.<br>The ironwork of the chariots flashes like fire<br>on the day he has prepared;<br>the horses quiver.  
**2:4** The chariots race wildly through the streets;<br>they rush back and forth through the squares.<br>They look like flaming torches;<br>they dart like bolts of lightning.  
**2:5** He musters his officers;<br>they stumble as they press forward.<br>They hurry to the city wall,<br>and the portable shield is set up.  
**2:6** The gates of the rivers are opened;<br>the palace melts.  
**2:7** It is decreed:<sup>①</sup> She is sent into exile, carried away,<br>while her female servants moan like doves, beating their breasts.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**2:8** Nineveh has been like a pool of water.<br>Such are its waters,<sup>①</sup> and others are fleeing.<br>“Stop, stop!”—but no one can turn them back.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**2:9** Plunder silver! Plunder gold!<br>There is no end to the supplies,<br>an abundance of precious objects!  
**2:10** Destruction and devastation;<br>the city is laid waste!<br>The heart grows faint and knees buckle;<br>there is anguish in every groin;<br>all the faces grow pale.  
**2:11** Where is the lions’ den, the meadow of the young lions,<br>where lion, lioness, even lion cub go about with no one to terrify them?  
**2:12** The lion has torn enough prey<br>for his cubs and strangled enough for his lionesses;<br>he has filled his lairs with prey,<br>his dens with torn flesh.  
**2:13** Look! I am against you, proclaims the LORD of heavenly forces.<br>I will burn your<sup>①</sup> chariots in smoke;<br>the sword will devour your young lions;<br>I will cut off your prey from the earth,<br>the voice of your messengers will never again be heard!<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>her</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** Doom, city of bloodshed—all deceit,<br>full of plunder: prey cannot get away.  
**3:2** Cracking whip and rumbling wheel,<br>galloping horse and careening chariot!  
**3:3** Charging cavalry, flashing sword, and glittering spear;<br>countless slain, masses of corpses,<br>endless dead bodies—they stumble over their dead bodies!  
**3:4** Because of the many whorings of the whore,<br>the lovely graces of the mistress of sorceries,<br>the one who sells nations by means of her whorings<br>and peoples by means of her sorceries:  
**3:5** Look! I am against you, proclaims<br>the LORD of heavenly forces.<br>I will lift your skirts over your face;<br>I will show nations your nakedness<br>and kingdoms your dishonor.  
**3:6** I will throw disgusting things at you;<br>I will treat you with contempt and make you a spectacle.  
**3:7** Then all who look at you will recoil from you and say,<br>“Nineveh has been devastated!<br>Who will lament for her?”<br>Where could I possibly seek comforters for you?  
**3:8** Are you better than Thebes,<br>situated by the Nile, waters surrounding her,<br>whose fortress is sea and whose city wall is waters?  
**3:9** Cush<sup>①</sup> and Egypt constituted her strength, without limit;<br>Put and the Libyans were her<sup>②</sup> help.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Traditionally <i>Ethiopia</i> <sup>②</sup>LXX; MT <i>your</i>  
**3:10** Yet even she was destined for exile;<br>she went into captivity.<br>Indeed, her infants were dashed to pieces at the head of every street.<br>They cast lots for her officials;<br>all of her powerful citizens were bound in chains.  
**3:11** Yes, even you will become drunk;<br>you will have to hide!<br>Even you will have to seek refuge from the enemy!  
**3:12** All your fortifications are fig trees with ripe fruit;<br>when the trees are shaken, the fruit falls into the mouth of the eater.  
**3:13** Look, your people are women in your midst.<br>The gates of your land have been flung wide open to your enemies.<br>Fire consumes the bars of your gates.  
**3:14** Draw water for yourself to prepare for siege!<br>Strengthen your fortifications!<br>Tread the clay, trample the mortar, grab the brick mold!  
**3:15** Fire will consume you there;<br>the sword will cut you down;<br>like the locust it will consume you.<br>Multiply like the locust; multiply like the grasshopper!  
**3:16** You boasted more traders than the heavens have stars.<br>The locust sheds its skin and flies away.  
**3:17** Your guards are like grasshoppers,<br>your marshals like swarms of cicadas<br>that encamp on stone fences on a chilly day;<br>when the sun rises, they take flight;<br>no one knows where they have gone.  
**3:18** Your shepherds have fallen asleep, king of Assyria!<br>Your officials are lying down.<br>Your people are scattered across the mountains;<br>there is no one to gather them.  
**3:19** There is no remedy for your injury;<br>your wound is grievous.<br>All who hear the news about you clap their hands over you.<br>Who has not suffered from your continual cruelty?
