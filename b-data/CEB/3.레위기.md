# 3.레위기 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** Then the LORD called to Moses and said to him from the meeting tent,  
**1:2** Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When any of you present a livestock offering to the LORD, you can present it from either the herd or the flock.  
**1:3** If the offering is an entirely burned offering from the herd, you must present a flawless male, bringing it to the meeting tent’s entrance for its acceptance before the LORD.  
**1:4** You must press your hand on the head of the entirely burned offering so that it will be accepted for you, to make reconciliation for you.  
**1:5** Then you will slaughter the bull before the LORD. Aaron’s sons the priests will present the blood and toss it against every side of the altar at the meeting tent’s entrance.  
**1:6** Then the entirely burned offering will be skinned and cut up into pieces.  
**1:7** The sons of Aaron the priest<sup>①</sup> will light the altar and lay wood on the fire.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Some Heb sources, Sam, LXX, Syr, and some Tg sources have <i>Aaron’s sons, the priests,</i> as in <a name='B:30 1:5'>1:5, <a name='B:30 1:8'>8.  
**1:8** Then Aaron’s sons the priests will arrange the pieces, the head, and the fat on the wood that is on the altar fire,  
**1:9** but the animal’s insides and lower legs must be washed with water. The priest will then completely burn all of it on the altar as an entirely burned offering, a food gift<sup>①</sup> of soothing smell to the LORD.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or (here and throughout Leviticus) <i>offering by fire</i> (cf <a name='B:30 3:11'>3:11)  
**1:10** If the offering is an entirely burned offering from the flock—whether sheep or goat—you must present a flawless male.  
**1:11** You must slaughter it on the north side of the altar before the LORD. Aaron’s sons the priests will toss its blood against every side of the altar.  
**1:12** Once it has been cut into pieces, including the head and the fat, the priest will arrange these out on the wood that is on the altar fire,  
**1:13** but its insides and lower legs must be washed with water. Then the priest will present all of it and completely burn it on the altar. It is an entirely burned offering, a food gift of soothing smell to the LORD.  
**1:14** If the offering for the LORD is an entirely burned offering from the birds, you can present your offering from the doves or pigeons.  
**1:15** The priest will bring it to the altar. He will tear off its head and completely burn it on the altar. Its blood will be drained against the side of the altar.  
**1:16** Then the priest will remove its throat along with its contents<sup>①</sup> and throw it by the east side of the altar, into the place for the ashes.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**1:17** He will then tear the bird open by its wings, without splitting it. The priest will completely burn it on the altar, on the wood that is on the altar fire. It is an entirely burned offering, a food gift of soothing smell to the LORD.  

## 2장

**2:1** When anyone presents a grain offering to the LORD, the offering must be of choice flour. They must pour oil on it and put frankincense on it,  
**2:2** then bring it to Aaron’s sons, the priests. A priest will take a handful of its choice flour and oil, along with all of its frankincense, and will completely burn this token portion on the altar as a food gift of soothing smell to the LORD.  
**2:3** The rest of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons as a most holy portion from the LORD’s food gifts.  
**2:4** When you present a grain offering baked in an oven, it must be of choice flour: unleavened flatbread mixed with oil or unleavened wafers spread with oil.  
**2:5** If your offering is grain prepared on a griddle, it must be of choice flour mixed with oil and it must be unleavened.  
**2:6** Crumble it into pieces and pour oil on it; it is a grain offering.  
**2:7** If your offering is grain prepared in a pan, it must be made of choice flour with oil.  
**2:8** You will bring the grain offering made in one of these ways to the LORD, presenting it to the priest, who will then bring it to the altar.  
**2:9** The priest will remove from the grain offering the token portion and completely burn it on the altar as a food gift of soothing smell to the LORD.  
**2:10** The rest of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons as a most holy portion from the LORD’s food gifts.  
**2:11** No grain offering that you give to the LORD can be made with yeast. You must not completely burn any yeast or honey as a food gift for the LORD.  
**2:12** You can present those as first-choice offerings to the LORD, but they must not be entirely burned up on the altar as a soothing smell.  
**2:13** You must season all your grain offerings with salt. Do not omit the salt of your God’s covenant from your grain offering. You must offer salt with all your offerings.  
**2:14** If you present a grain offering to the LORD from the first produce, you must make such an offering from the crushed heads of newly ripe grain, roasted with fire.  
**2:15** You must put oil and frankincense on it; it is a grain offering.  
**2:16** The priest will completely burn the token portion—some of the crushed new grain and oil along with all of the frankincense—as a food gift for the LORD.  

## 3장

**3:1** If the offering is a communal sacrifice of well-being,<sup>①</sup> the one who offers the herd animal—whether it is male or female—must present a flawless specimen before the LORD.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>peace offering</i>  
**3:2** You must press your hand on the head of the offering and slaughter it at the meeting tent’s entrance. Aaron’s sons the priests will toss the blood against every side of the altar.  
**3:3** Then you can offer a food gift to the LORD from the communal sacrifice of well-being: the fat that covers and surrounds the insides;  
**3:4** the two kidneys and the fat around them at the loins; and the lobe on the liver, which should be removed with the kidneys.  
**3:5** Aaron’s sons will completely burn all of this on the altar—along with the entirely burned offering on the wood that is on the altar fire—as a food gift of soothing smell to the LORD.  
**3:6** If the offering for a communal sacrifice of well-being for the LORD is from the flock—whether it is male or female—you must present a flawless specimen.  
**3:7** If you present a sheep as the offering, you must present it before the LORD.  
**3:8** You must press your hand on the head of the offering and slaughter it before the meeting tent. Aaron’s sons will toss the blood against every side of the altar.  
**3:9** Then you may offer the fat from the communal sacrifice of well-being as a food gift for the LORD: the whole fat tail, which should be removed close to the tailbone; the fat that covers and surrounds the insides;  
**3:10** the two kidneys and the fat around them at the loins; and the lobe on the liver, which should be removed with the kidneys.  
**3:11** The priest will then completely burn all of this on the altar as food—as a food gift for the LORD.  
**3:12** If the offering is a goat, you must present it before the LORD.  
**3:13** You must press your hand on its head and slaughter it before the meeting tent. Aaron’s sons will toss its blood against every side of the altar.  
**3:14** Then you may present as your offering—a food gift for the LORD—the fat that covers and surrounds the insides;  
**3:15** the two kidneys and the fat around them at the loins; and the lobe on the liver, which should be removed with the kidneys.  
**3:16** The priest will then completely burn all of this on the altar as food—as a food gift for a soothing smell. All fat belongs to the LORD.  
**3:17** This is a permanent rule for your future generations, wherever you live: you must not eat any fat or blood.  

## 4장

**4:1** The LORD said to Moses,  
**4:2** Say to the Israelites: Do the following whenever someone sins unintentionally against any of the LORD’s commands, doing something that shouldn’t be done:  
**4:3** If it is the anointed priest who has sinned, making the people guilty of sin, he must present to the LORD a flawless bull from the herd as a purification offering<sup>①</sup> for the sin he has committed.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sin offering</i> (Heb <i>hatta’t,</i> which recurs frequently in Leviticus)  
**4:4** He will bring the bull before the LORD at the entrance to the meeting tent and press his hand on the bull’s head. Then he will slaughter the bull before the LORD.  
**4:5** The anointed priest will take some of the bull’s blood and take it into the meeting tent.  
**4:6** The priest will dip his finger into the blood and sprinkle some of it seven times before the LORD, toward the sanctuary’s inner curtain.  
**4:7** Then the priest will put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of perfumed incense, which is in the meeting tent before the LORD. But he will pour out all the rest of the bull’s blood at the base of the altar of entirely burned offerings, which is at the meeting tent’s entrance.  
**4:8** Then he will remove all the fat from the bull for the purification offering: the fat that covers and surrounds the insides;  
**4:9** the two kidneys and the fat around them at the loins; and the lobe on the liver, which he will remove with the kidneys,  
**4:10** just as this is removed from the ox for the communal sacrifice of well-being. Then the priest will completely burn these on the altar of entirely burned offerings.  
**4:11** But the bull’s hide and all of its flesh, along with its head, lower legs, entrails, and dung—  
**4:12** all that remains of the bull—will be taken to a clean location outside the camp, to the ash heap. It should be burned there at the ash heap on a wood fire.  
**4:13** If it is the entire Israelite community that has done something wrong unintentionally and the deed escapes the assembly’s notice—but they’ve done something that shouldn’t be done in violation of the LORD’s commands, becoming guilty of sin—  
**4:14** once the sin that they committed becomes known, the assembly must present a bull from the herd as a purification offering. They will bring it before the meeting tent.  
**4:15** The community elders will press their hands on the bull’s head before the LORD and then slaughter it before the LORD.  
**4:16** The anointed priest will take some of the bull’s blood into the meeting tent.  
**4:17** The priest will dip his finger into the blood and sprinkle it seven times before the LORD toward the inner curtain.  
**4:18** Then he will put some of the blood on the horns of the altar that is before the LORD in the meeting tent. But he will pour all the rest of the blood out at the base of the altar of entirely burned offerings that is at the meeting tent’s entrance.  
**4:19** Then he will remove all the fat from it and completely burn it on the altar.  
**4:20** He will do the same with this bull as he did with the other bull for the purification offering; that is exactly what he must do. In this way, the priest will make reconciliation for them, and they will be forgiven.  
**4:21** Then the priest will take the bull outside the camp and burn it, just as the first bull was burned. It is the purification offering for the assembly.  
**4:22** If a leader sins by unintentionally breaking any of the commands of the LORD his God, doing something that shouldn’t be done, and becomes guilty of sin—  
**4:23** once the sin that he committed is made known to him—he must bring as his offering a flawless male goat.  
**4:24** He will press his hand on the goat’s head. It will be slaughtered<sup>①</sup> at the place where an entirely burned offering would be slaughtered before the LORD. It is a purification offering.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>He will slaughter it … where he would slaughter</i>  
**4:25** The priest will take some of the blood from the purification offering and, using his finger, will put it on the horns of the altar of entirely burned offerings. But he will pour the rest of the blood out at the base of the altar of entirely burned offerings.  
**4:26** He will completely burn all of its fat on the altar just as the fat of the communal sacrifice of well-being is burned. In this way the priest will make reconciliation for the leader to remove his sin, and he will be forgiven.  
**4:27** If any ordinary person<sup>①</sup> sins unintentionally by breaking one of the LORD’s commands, doing something that shouldn’t be done, and becomes guilty of sin—<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>one of the people of the land</i>  
**4:28** once the sin they committed is made known to them—they must bring as their offering a flawless female goat because of the sin that was committed.  
**4:29** They will press their hand on the head of the purification offering. It will be slaughtered<sup>①</sup> at the place for the entirely burned offerings.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>They will slaughter it.</i>  
**4:30** The priest will take some of its blood and, using his finger, will put it on the horns of the altar of entirely burned offerings. But he will pour all the rest of the blood out at the base of the altar.  
**4:31** He will remove all of its fat, just as the fat from a communal sacrifice of well-being is removed. Then the priest will completely burn it on the altar as a soothing smell to the LORD. In this way, the priest will make reconciliation for them, and they will be forgiven.  
**4:32** If you offer a sheep as a purification offering, it must be a flawless female.  
**4:33** You must press your hand on the head of the purification offering. It will be slaughtered<sup>①</sup> as a purification offering in the place where the entirely burned offering is slaughtered.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>You will slaughter it</i>.  
**4:34** Then the priest will take some of the blood from the purification offering and, using his finger, will put it on the horns of the altar of entirely burned offerings. But he will pour all the rest of the blood out at the base of the altar.  
**4:35** He will remove all of its fat, just as the fat of a sheep would be removed from the communal sacrifice of well-being. Then the priest will completely burn it on the altar along with the LORD’s food gifts. In this way, the priest will make reconciliation for you for the sin you committed, and you will be forgiven.  

## 5장

**5:1** If you sin:<br>by not providing information after hearing a public solemn pledge even though you are a witness, knowing something, or having seen something so that you become liable to punishment;  
**5:2** or by touching some unclean thing—the dead body of an unclean wild animal, unclean livestock, or unclean swarming creature—but the fact goes unknown so that you become unclean and guilty of sin;  
**5:3** or by touching human uncleanness—any uncleanness that makes one unclean—and the fact goes unknown, but you later learn of it and become guilty of sin;  
**5:4** or by carelessly swearing to do something, whether bad or good—whatever one might swear carelessly—and the fact goes unknown, but you later learn of it and become guilty of sin concerning one of these things—  
**5:5** at that point, when you have become guilty of sin in one of these ways, you must confess how you have sinned  
**5:6** and bring to the LORD as compensation for the sin that was committed a female from the flock, either a sheep or goat, as a purification offering. The priest will then make reconciliation for you, to remove your sin.  
**5:7** If you can’t afford an animal from the flock, you can bring to the LORD as compensation for your sin two doves or two pigeons, one as a purification offering and the other as an entirely burned offering.  
**5:8** You will bring them to the priest, who will first present the one for the purification offering. He will pinch off its head at the back of its neck without splitting it.  
**5:9** Then he will sprinkle some of the blood of the purification offering on the side of the altar. The rest of the blood will be drained out at the base of the altar. It is a purification offering.  
**5:10** Then, with the second bird, the priest will perform an entirely burned offering according to the regulation. In this way, the priest will make reconciliation for you because of the sin you committed, and you will be forgiven.  
**5:11** If you cannot afford two doves or two pigeons, you can bring as the offering for your sin a tenth of an ephah<sup>①</sup> of choice flour as a purification offering. You must not put any oil on it, nor any frankincense, because it is a purification offering.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Two quarts; one ephah is approximately twenty quarts dry.  
**5:12** You will bring it to the priest, and the priest will take a handful from it—the token portion—and will burn it completely on the altar along with the food gifts for the LORD. It is a purification offering.  
**5:13** In this way, the priest will make reconciliation for you for whichever one of the sins you committed, and you will be forgiven. The rest of the offering will belong to the priest like the grain offering.  
**5:14** The LORD said to Moses,  
**5:15** Whenever you commit wrongdoing, unintentionally sinning against any of the LORD’s holy things, you must bring to the LORD as your compensation a flawless ram from the flock, its value calculated in silver shekels according to the sanctuary’s shekel, as a compensation offering.  
**5:16** You will make amends for the way you have sinned against the holy thing: you will add one-fifth to its value and give it to the priest. Then the priest will make reconciliation for you with the ram for the compensation offering, and you will be forgiven.  
**5:17** If you sin by breaking any of the LORD’s commands, but without realizing it, doing something that shouldn’t be done, and then become guilty and liable to punishment,  
**5:18** you must bring a flawless ram from the flock, at the standard value, as a compensation offering to the priest. The priest will make reconciliation for you for the unintentional fault that you committed, even though you didn’t realize it, and you will be forgiven.  
**5:19** It is a compensation offering. You have definitely become guilty before the LORD.

**6:1** <sup>①</sup> The LORD said to Moses,<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>5:20 in Heb

**6:3** or, though you’ve found lost property, you lie about it; or by swearing falsely about anything that someone might do and so sin,  
**6:4** at that point, once you have sinned and become guilty of sin, you must return the property you took by robbery or fraud, or the deposit that was left with you for safekeeping, or the lost property that you found,  
**6:5** or whatever it was that you swore falsely about. You must make amends for the principal amount and add one-fifth to it. You must give it to the owner on the day you become guilty.  
**6:6** You must bring to the priest as your compensation to the LORD a flawless ram from the flock at the standard value as a compensation offering.  
**6:7** The priest will make reconciliation for you before the LORD, and you will be forgiven for anything you may have done that made you guilty.

## 7장

**7:1** This is the Instruction for the compensation offering: It is most holy.  
**7:2** The compensation offering must be slaughtered at the same place where the entirely burned offering is slaughtered, and its blood must be tossed against all sides of the altar.  
**7:3** All of its fat will be offered: the fat tail; the fat that covers the insides;  
**7:4** the two kidneys and the fat around them at the loins; and the lobe on the liver, which must be removed with the kidneys.  
**7:5** The priest must burn them completely on the altar as a food gift for the LORD; it is a compensation offering.  
**7:6** Any male priest can eat it. It must be eaten in a holy place; it is most holy.  
**7:7** The compensation offering is like the purification offering—they share the same Instruction: It belongs to the priest who makes reconciliation with it.  
**7:8** The hide of the entirely burned offering that a priest has offered belongs to the priest who offered it.  
**7:9** Any grain offering that is baked in an oven or that is prepared in a pan or on a griddle also belongs to the priest who offered it.  
**7:10** But every other grain offering, whether mixed with oil or dry, will belong to all of Aaron’s sons equally.  
**7:11** This is the Instruction for the communal sacrifice of well-being that someone may offer to the LORD:  
**7:12** If you are offering it for thanksgiving, you must offer the following with the communal sacrifice of thanksgiving: unleavened flatbread mixed with oil, unleavened thin loaves spread with oil, and flatbread of choice flour thoroughly mixed with oil.  
**7:13** You must present this offering, plus the leavened flatbread, with the communal thanksgiving sacrifice of well-being.  
**7:14** From this you will present one of each kind of offering as a gift to the LORD. It will belong to the priest who tosses the blood of the well-being offering.  
**7:15** The flesh of your communal thanksgiving sacrifice of well-being must be eaten on the day you offer it; you cannot save any of it until morning.  
**7:16** But if your communal sacrifice of well-being is payment for a solemn promise or if it is a spontaneous gift, it may be eaten on the day you offer it as your communal sacrifice, and whatever is left over can be eaten the next day.  
**7:17** But whatever is left over of the flesh of the communal sacrifice on the third day must be burned with fire.  
**7:18** If any of it is eaten on the third day, it will not be accepted. It will not be credited to the one who offered it. It will be considered foul, and the person who eats of it will be liable to punishment.  
**7:19** Flesh that touches any unclean thing must not be eaten; it must be burned with fire. Any clean person may eat the flesh,  
**7:20** but anyone who eats the flesh of a communal sacrifice of well-being that belongs to the LORD while in an unclean state will be cut off from their people.  
**7:21** Whenever anyone touches any unclean thing—whether it is human uncleanness, an unclean animal, or any unclean and disgusting creature—and then eats the flesh of a communal sacrifice of well-being that belongs to the LORD, that person will be cut off from their people.  
**7:22** The LORD said to Moses:  
**7:23** Tell the Israelites: You must not eat the fat of an ox, sheep, or goat.  
**7:24** The fat of an animal that has died naturally or the fat of an animal that was killed by another animal may be put to any use, but you must definitely not eat it.  
**7:25** If anyone eats the fat of an animal from which a food gift could be offered to the LORD, that person will be cut off from their people.  
**7:26** You must not consume any blood whatsoever—whether bird or animal blood—wherever you may live.  
**7:27** Any person who consumes any blood whatsoever will be cut off from their people.  
**7:28** The LORD said to Moses:  
**7:29** Say to the Israelites: If you wish to offer a communal sacrifice of well-being to the LORD, you are allowed to bring your offering to the LORD as your communal sacrifice of well-being.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**7:30** Your own hands must bring the LORD’s food gifts. You will bring the fat with the breast so that the breast can be lifted as an uplifted offering before the LORD.  
**7:31** The priest will completely burn the fat on the altar, but the breast will go to Aaron and his sons.  
**7:32** You will give the right thigh of your communal sacrifice of well-being to the priest as a gift.  
**7:33** The right thigh will belong to the son of Aaron who offers the blood and fat of the well-being offering.  
**7:34** I have taken the breast of the uplifted offering and the thigh that is given by the Israelites from their communal sacrifices of well-being, and have given them to Aaron the priest and to his sons as a permanent portion from the Israelites.  
**7:35** This is what Aaron and his sons are allotted from the LORD’s food gifts once they have been presented to serve the LORD as priests.  
**7:36** The LORD commanded that these things be given to the priests by the Israelites, following their anointment. It is their permanent portion throughout their future generations.  
**7:37** This concludes the Instructions for the entirely burned offering, the grain offering, the purification offering, the compensation offering, the ordination offering, and the communal sacrifice of well-being,  
**7:38** which the LORD commanded Moses at Mount Sinai on the day when he ordered the Israelites to present their offerings to the LORD, in the Sinai desert.  

## 8장

**8:1** The LORD said to Moses,  
**8:2** Take Aaron and his sons with him, the priestly clothing, the anointing oil, a bull for the purification offering, two rams, and a basket of unleavened bread,  
**8:3** and assemble the whole community at the meeting tent’s entrance.  
**8:4** Moses did as the LORD commanded him, and the community assembled at the meeting tent’s entrance.  
**8:5** Moses said to the community, “This is what the LORD has commanded us to do.”  
**8:6** Then Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them in water.  
**8:7** Moses put the tunic on Aaron, tied the sash around him, and dressed him in the robe. Moses then put the priestly vest on Aaron, tied the woven waistband of the vest around him, and secured the vest to him with it.  
**8:8** Then Moses placed the chest piece on Aaron and set the Urim and Thummim into the chest piece.  
**8:9** Moses placed the turban on Aaron’s head and put the gold flower ornament, the holy crown, on the turban’s front, just as the LORD had commanded him.  
**8:10** Moses then took the anointing oil and anointed the dwelling<sup>①</sup> and everything in it, making them holy by doing so.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>tabernacle</i>  
**8:11** He sprinkled some of the oil on the altar seven times, and anointed the altar and all its equipment, as well as the basin and its base, to make them holy.  
**8:12** He poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron’s head, thereby anointing him to make him holy.  
**8:13** Then Moses brought Aaron’s sons forward, dressed them in tunics, tied sashes around them, and wrapped headbands on them, just as the LORD had commanded him.

## 13장

**13:1** The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,  
**13:2** When a person has a swelling, a scab, or a shiny spot on their skin, and it becomes an infection of skin disease<sup>①</sup> on their skin, they will be brought to the priests, either to Aaron or one of his sons.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>The precise meaning is uncertain; traditionally <i>leprosy</i>—a term used for several different skin diseases.

**13:4** But if the shiny spot on the skin is white and does not appear to be deeper than the skin and the hair has not turned white, the priest will quarantine the infected person for seven days.  
**13:5** On the seventh day the priest will again examine the infection. If he sees that it has remained the same—the infection has not spread on the skin—the priest will quarantine the person for seven more days.  
**13:6** On the seventh day the priest will examine it again. If the infection has faded and has not spread over the skin, the priest will declare the person clean; it is just a rash. The person must wash their clothes, then they will be clean again.  
**13:7** But if the rash continues to spread over the skin after they appeared before the priest for purification, they must again show themselves to the priest.  
**13:8** If the priest sees that the rash has spread over the skin, the priest will declare the person unclean; it is a case of skin disease.  
**13:9** Whenever someone has an infection of skin disease, they will be brought to the priest.  
**13:10** If the priest sees that there is a white swelling on the skin, it has turned the hair white, and there is a patch of raw flesh in the swelling,  
**13:11** then it is a case of chronic skin disease on their skin. The priest will declare the person unclean. The priest will not quarantine such persons, because they are already unclean.  
**13:12** But if the skin disease continues to break out so that the disease covers all of the infected person’s skin from head to toe, as far as the priest can tell—  
**13:13** then the priest will make an examination. If the skin disease has covered the person’s whole body, the priest will declare the infected person clean. The person has turned entirely white; he is clean.  
**13:14** But as soon as raw flesh appears in the swelling, they will be unclean.  
**13:15** When the priest sees the raw flesh, he will declare the person unclean. Raw flesh is unclean; it is a case of skin disease.  
**13:16** But if the raw flesh turns white again, the person will go back to the priest.  
**13:17** The priest will examine it. If the infection has turned white, the priest will declare the infected person clean; at that point, the person is clean.  
**13:18** Whenever someone has a boil on their skin, it heals,  
**13:19** and in place of the boil there is a white swelling or reddish-white shiny spot, it must be shown to the priest.  
**13:20** If the priest sees that it appears to be lower than the skin, and its hair has turned white, the priest will declare the person unclean. It is an infection of skin disease that has broken out in the boil.  
**13:21** But if the priest examines it and there is no white hair in it, it is not lower than the skin, and it is faded, the priest will quarantine the person seven days.  
**13:22** If it continues to spread over the skin, the priest will declare the person unclean; it is an infection.  
**13:23** But if the shiny spot remains where it was and does not spread, it is just a scar from the boil. The priest will declare the person clean.  
**13:24** Whenever there is a burn on someone’s skin, and the raw patch of the burn becomes a reddish-white or white shiny spot,  
**13:25** the priest will examine it. If the hair has turned white in the shiny spot, and it appears to be deeper than the skin, it is a case of skin disease that has broken out in the burn. The priest will declare the person unclean; it is an infection of skin disease.  
**13:26** But if the priest examines it, and there is no white hair in the shiny spot, it is not lower than the skin, and it is faded, the priest will quarantine the person seven days.  
**13:27** On the seventh day the priest will again examine it. If it has continued to spread over the skin, the priest will declare the person unclean; it is an infection of skin disease.  
**13:28** But if the shiny spot remains where it was, has not spread over the skin, and is faded, it is just swelling from the burn. The priest will declare the person clean, because it is just the scar from the burn.  
**13:29** Whenever a man or woman has an infection, whether on the head or in the beard,  
**13:30** the priest will examine it. If it appears to be deeper than the skin, and there is thin yellow hair in it, the priest will declare the person unclean; it is a case of scabies—a skin disease of the head or beard.  
**13:31** When the priest examines the scabies infection, if it does not appear to be deeper than the skin, but there is no black hair in it, the priest will quarantine the person with the scabies infection for seven days.  
**13:32** On the seventh day the priest will examine the infection again. If the scabies has not spread, there is no yellow hair in it, and it does not appear to be deeper than the skin,  
**13:33** the person must shave the area, without shaving the scabies. The priest will then quarantine that person another seven days.  
**13:34** On the seventh day the priest will again examine the scabies. If it has not spread over the skin and does not appear to be deeper than the skin, the priest will declare the person clean. They must wash their clothes; then they will be clean again.  
**13:35** But if the scabies continues to spread over the skin after the person’s purification,  
**13:36** the priest must examine it again. If the scabies has spread over the skin at all, the priest does not need to look for the yellow hair; the person is unclean.  
**13:37** But if the priest sees that the scabies has remained the same, and black hair has grown in it, the scabies has healed. The person is clean, and the priest will declare them to be so.  
**13:38** Whenever a man or woman has many white shiny spots on their skin,  
**13:39** if the priest sees that there are faded white shiny spots on the skin of the body, it is just a rash that has broken out on the skin; the person is clean.  
**13:40** If someone loses their hair, they are bald, but they are clean.  
**13:41** If the hair is lost at the sides of the forehead, the person has a receding hairline, but they are clean.  
**13:42** But whenever there is a reddish-white infection in the bald spot or in the receding hairline, it is a case of skin disease breaking out there.  
**13:43** The priest must examine it. If the swelling of the infection is reddish white in the bald spot or receding hairline and resembles skin disease on the body,  
**13:44** the person is afflicted with skin disease; they are unclean. The priest must declare them unclean on account of the head infection.  
**13:45** Anyone with an infection of skin disease must wear torn clothes, dishevel their hair, cover their upper lip, and shout out, “Unclean! Unclean!”  
**13:46** They will be unclean as long as they are infected. They are unclean. They must live alone outside the camp.  
**13:47** Whenever there is an infection of skin disease on clothing—on wool or linen clothing,  
**13:48** in the weaving of the linen or wool, or on a skin or skin item—  
**13:49** and the infection is greenish or reddish on the clothing, the weaving, or the skin or skin item, it is an infection of skin disease. It must be shown to the priest.  
**13:50** The priest will examine the infection and quarantine the infected item seven days.  
**13:51** On the seventh day he will examine the infection again. If the infection has spread in the clothing, the weaving, or the skin, whatever it is used for, the infection is a case of infectious skin disease; the item is unclean.  
**13:52** The priest will burn the clothing, the weaving of the wool or linen, or whatever skin item in which the infection was found, because it is an infectious skin disease; it must be burned with fire.  
**13:53** But if the priest sees that the infection has not spread in the clothing, the weaving, or on any skin item,  
**13:54** the priest will order that the infected piece be washed, and he will quarantine it for another seven days.  
**13:55** After it has been washed, if the priest sees that the infection has not changed its appearance, even though the infection has not spread, it is unclean. You must burn it with fire. It is a fungus,<sup>①</sup> whether it is on the inside or outside.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain

**13:57** If it appears again in the cloth, the weaving, or any item of skin, it is starting to break out. You must burn the infected item with fire.  
**13:58** But if the infection disappears from the cloth, the weaving, or any item of skin that you washed, it must be washed again. Then it will be clean.  
**13:59** This concludes the Instruction about the infection of skin disease in a woolen or linen cloth, weaving, or any skin item, in order to declare whether it is clean or unclean.

**25:22** You can plant again in the eighth year and eat food from the previous year’s produce until the ninth year. Until its produce comes, you will eat the food from the previous year.  
**25:23** The land must not be permanently sold because the land is mine. You are just immigrants and foreign guests of mine.  
**25:24** Throughout the whole land that you possess, you must allow for the land to be bought back.  
**25:25** When one of your fellow Israelites faces financial difficulty and must sell part of their family property, the closest relative<sup>①</sup> will come and buy back what their fellow Israelite has sold.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>next of kin</i>; traditionally <i>redeemer</i>  
**25:26** If the person doesn’t have someone to buy it back, but then manages to afford buying it back,  
**25:27** they must calculate the years since its sale and refund the balance to the person to whom they sold it. Then it will go back to the family property.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>they will go back to their family property;</i> also in <a name='B:30 25:28'>25:28.  
**25:28** If they cannot afford to make a refund to the buyer, whatever was sold will remain in the possession of the buyer until the Jubilee year. It will be released in the Jubilee year, at which point it will return to the family property.  
**25:29** When a person sells a home in a walled city, it may be bought back until a year after its sale. The period for buying it back will be one year.  
**25:30** If it is not bought back before a full year has passed, the house in the walled city will belong to the buyer permanently and their descendants forever. It will not be released at the Jubilee.  
**25:31** But houses in settlements that are unwalled will be considered as if they were country fields. They can be bought back, and they must be released at the Jubilee.  
**25:32** Levites will always have the right to buy back homes in the levitical cities that are part of their family property.  
**25:33** Levite property that can be bought back—houses sold in a city that is their family property—must be released at the Jubilee, because homes in levitical cities are the Levites’ family property among the Israelites.  
**25:34** But the pastureland around their cities cannot be sold, because that is their permanent family property.  
**25:35** If one of your fellow Israelites faces financial difficulty and is in a shaky situation with you,<sup>①</sup> you must assist them as you would an immigrant or foreign guest so that they can survive among you.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**25:36** Do not take interest from them, or any kind of profit from interest, but fear your God so that your fellow Israelite can survive among you.  
**25:37** Do not lend a poor Israelite money with interest or lend food at a profit.  
**25:38** I am the LORD your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt to give you Canaan’s land and to be your God.  
**25:39** If one of your fellow Israelites faces financial difficulty with you and sells themselves to you, you must not make him work as a slave.  
**25:40** Instead, they will be like a hired laborer or foreign guest to you. They will work for you until the Jubilee year,  
**25:41** at which point the poor Israelite along with their children will be released from you. They can return to their extended family and to their family property.  
**25:42** You must do this because these people are my servants—I brought them out of Egypt’s land. They must not be sold as slaves.  
**25:43** You will not harshly rule over them but must fear your God.  
**25:44** Regarding male or female slaves that you are allowed to have: You can buy a male or a female slave from the nations that are around you.  
**25:45** You can also buy them from the foreign guests who live with you and from their extended families that are with you, who were born in your land. These can belong to you as property.  
**25:46** You can pass them on to your children as inheritance that they can own as permanent property. You can make these people work as slaves, but you must not rule harshly over your own people, the Israelites.  
**25:47** If an immigrant or foreign guest prospers financially among you, but your fellow Israelite faces financial difficulty and so sells themselves to the immigrant or foreign guest, or to a descendant of a foreigner,  
**25:48** the Israelite will have the right to be bought back after they sold themselves. One of their relatives can buy them back:  
**25:49** their uncle or cousin can buy them back; one of their blood relatives from their family can buy them back; or they may be able to afford their own purchase.  
**25:50** The Israelite will calculate with their owner the time from the year they were sold until the Jubilee year. The price of their release will be based on the number of years they were with the owner, as in the case of a hired laborer.  
**25:51** If there are many years left before the Jubilee, the Israelite will pay for their purchase in proportion to their purchase price.  
**25:52** If only a few years are left, they will calculate that and pay for their purchase according to the years of service.  
**25:53** Regardless, the Israelite will be to the buyer like a yearly laborer; the buyer must not harshly rule over them in your sight.  
**25:54** If the Israelite is not bought back in one of these ways, they and their children must be released in the Jubilee year  
**25:55** because the Israelites belong to me as servants. They are my servants—I brought them out of Egypt’s land; I am the LORD your God.  

## 26장

**26:1** You must not make any idols, and do not set up any divine image or sacred pillar. You must not place any carved<sup>①</sup> stone in your land, bowing down to it, because I am the LORD your God.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**26:2** You must keep my sabbaths and respect my sanctuary; I am the LORD.  
**26:3** If you live according to my rules, keep my commands, and do them,  
**26:4** I will give you rain at the proper time, the land will produce its yield, and the trees of the field will produce their fruit.  
**26:5** Your threshing season will last until the grape harvest, and the grape harvest will last until planting time. You will eat your fill of food and live securely in your land.  
**26:6** I will grant peace in the land so that you can lie down without anyone frightening you. I will remove dangerous animals from the land, and no sword will pass through it.  
**26:7** You will chase your enemies, and they will fall before you in battle.  
**26:8** Five of you will chase away a hundred, and a hundred of you will chase away ten thousand, and your enemies will fall before you in battle.  
**26:9** I will turn my face to you, will make you fruitful and numerous, and will keep my covenant with you.  
**26:10** You will still be eating the previous year’s harvest when the time will come to clear it out to make room for the new!  
**26:11** I will place my dwelling<sup>①</sup> among you, and I will not despise you.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>tabernacle</i>  
**26:12** I will walk around among you; I will be your God, and you will be my people.  
**26:13** I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt’s land—who brought you out from being Egypt’s slaves. I broke your bonds and made you stand up straight.  
**26:14** But if you do not obey me and do not carry out all these commands—  
**26:15** if you reject my rules and despise my regulations, not doing all my commands and breaking my covenant—  
**26:16** then I will do the following to you: I will bring horrific things:<sup>①</sup> wasting diseases and fevers that make the eyes fail and drain life away. You will plant seed for no reason because your enemies will eat the food.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Precise nature of the diseases uncertain  
**26:17** I will turn my face against you: you will be defeated by your enemies; those who hate you will rule over you; and you will run away even when no one is chasing you.  
**26:18** If, despite all that, you still do not obey me, I will punish you for your sins seven more times:  
**26:19** I will destroy your prideful power. I will turn your sky to iron and your land to bronze  
**26:20** so that your strength will be spent for no reason: your land will not produce its yield, and the trees of the land won’t produce their fruit.  
**26:21** If you continue to oppose me and are unwilling to obey me, I will strike you for your sins seven more times:  
**26:22** I will send wild animals against you, and they will kill your children and destroy your livestock. They will make you so few in number that your roads will seem deserted.  
**26:23** If, despite these things, you still do not accept my discipline and continue to oppose me,  
**26:24** then I will continue to oppose you. I will strike you for your sins seven more times:  
**26:25** I will bring the sword against you, avenging the breaking of the covenant.<sup>①</sup> If you retreat into your cities, I will send a plague on you, and you will be handed over to the enemy.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>executing covenant vengeance</i>  
**26:26** When I destroy your food supply, ten women will bake bread in a single oven, and they will ration out bread by weight. You will eat but will never get full.  
**26:27** If, despite all this, you still do not obey me and continue to oppose me,  
**26:28** then I will continue to oppose you—with anger! I will punish you for your sins seven more times:  
**26:29** You will eat the flesh of your own sons and daughters.  
**26:30** I will eliminate your shrines, chop down your incense altars, and pile your dead bodies on the dead bodies of your idols. I will despise you.  
**26:31** I will turn your cities into ruins, I will devastate your sanctuaries, and I will not smell the soothing smells of your offerings.  
**26:32** I will personally devastate the land so much that your enemies who resettle it will be astonished by it.  
**26:33** I will scatter you among the nations. I will unsheathe my sword against you. Your land will be devastated and your cities will be ruins.  
**26:34** At that time, while it is devastated and you are in enemy territory, the land will enjoy its sabbaths. At that time, the land will rest and enjoy its sabbaths.  
**26:35** During the whole time it is devastated, it will have the rest it didn’t have during the sabbaths you lived in it.  
**26:36** I will bring despair into the hearts of those of you who survive in enemy territory. Just the sound of a windblown leaf will put them to running, and they will run scared as if running from a sword! They will fall even when no one is chasing them!  
**26:37** They will stumble over each other as they would before a sword, even though no one is chasing them! You will have no power to stand before your enemies.  
**26:38** You will disappear among the nations—the land of your enemies will devour you.  
**26:39** Any of you who do survive will rot in enemy territory on account of their guilty deeds. And they will rot too on account of their ancestors’ guilty deeds.  
**26:40** But if they confess their and their ancestors’ guilt for the wrongdoing they did to me, and for their continued opposition to me—  
**26:41** which made me oppose them, so I took them into enemy territory—or if their uncircumcised hearts are humbled and they make up for their guilt,  
**26:42** then I will remember my covenant with Jacob. I will also remember my covenant with Isaac. And my covenant with Abraham. And I will remember the land.  
**26:43** The land will be absent of them and will be enjoying its sabbaths while it lies devastated, free of them. They will be making up for their guilty deeds for no other reason than the fact that they rejected my regulations and despised my rules.  
**26:44** But despite all that, when they are in enemy territory, I will not reject them or despise them to the point of totally destroying them, breaking my covenant with them by doing so, because I am the LORD their God.  
**26:45** But for their sake I will remember the covenant with the first generation, the ones I brought out of Egypt’s land in the sight of all the nations, in order to be their God; I am the LORD.  
**26:46** These are the rules, regulations, and instructions between the LORD and the Israelites that he gave through Moses on Mount Sinai.  

## 27장

**27:1** The LORD said to Moses,  
**27:2** Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When a person makes a solemn promise to the LORD involving the value of a person,  
**27:3** if it is the value for a male between 20 and 60 years old, his value is fifty silver shekels according to the sanctuary’s shekel.  
**27:4** If the person is a female, her value is thirty shekels.  
**27:5** If the age of the person is between 5 and 20 years, the value for a male is twenty shekels, for a female ten shekels.  
**27:6** If the age of the person is between one month and 5 years, the value for a male is five silver shekels, for a female three silver shekels.  
**27:7** If the age of the person is 60 years or more, the value is fifteen shekels if the person is male, ten shekels for a female.  
**27:8** But if financial difficulty prevents the promise maker from giving the full value, they must set the person before the priest. The priest will assign the person a value according to what the promise maker can afford.  
**27:9** If a solemn promise involves livestock that can be offered to the LORD, any such animal given to the LORD will be considered holy.  
**27:10** The promise maker cannot replace or substitute for it, either good for bad or bad for good. But if one should substitute one animal for another, both it and the substitute will be holy.  
**27:11** If the solemn promise involves any kind of unclean animal that cannot be offered to the LORD, the promise maker must set the animal before the priest.  
**27:12** The priest will assign it a value, whether high or low.<sup>①</sup> Its value will be what the priest says.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>good or bad</i>; also in <a name='B:30 27:14'>27:14  
**27:13** If the promise maker wishes to buy it back, they must add one-fifth to its value.  
**27:14** When someone dedicates their house to the LORD as holy, the priest will assign a value to it, whether high or low. The value is fixed, whatever value the priest assigns to it.  
**27:15** If the one who dedicates the house wishes to buy it back, they must add one-fifth to its valued price, and it will be theirs again.  
**27:16** If a person dedicates part of the land from their family property to the LORD, the value will be set according to the seed needed to plant it: fifty silver shekels per homer of barley seed.  
**27:17** If the person dedicates the piece of land during the Jubilee year, its value will stay fixed.  
**27:18** But if the person dedicates the piece after the Jubilee year, the priest will calculate the price according to the years that are left until the next Jubilee year, and the value will be reduced.  
**27:19** If the one who dedicates the land wishes to buy it back, they must add one-fifth to its valued price, and it will be theirs again.  
**27:20** But if they do not buy it back or if it was sold to someone else, it is no longer able to be bought back.  
**27:21** When the piece of land is released in the Jubilee year, it will be holy to the LORD like a piece of devoted land; it will be the priest’s property.  
**27:22** If the person dedicates land they purchased to the LORD—land that is not part of their family property—  
**27:23** the priest will calculate the amount of its value until the Jubilee year. The person must pay the value on that day as a holy donation to the LORD.  
**27:24** In the Jubilee year the piece of land will return to the seller, to the one who is the original owner of the family property.  
**27:25** Every value will be according to the sanctuary’s shekel. The shekel will be twenty gerahs.  
**27:26** But note that a person cannot dedicate any oldest offspring from livestock, which already belongs to the LORD because it is the oldest. Whether ox or sheep, it belongs to the LORD.  
**27:27** If it is an unclean animal, it may be bought back at its value plus twenty percent. If it is not bought back, it will be sold at its set value.  
**27:28** Also note that everything someone devotes<sup>①</sup> to the LORD from their possessions—whether humans, animals, or pieces of land from their family property—cannot be sold or bought back. Every devoted thing is most holy to the LORD.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>places under the ban</i> (also in <a name='B:30 27:29'>27:29), a technique of holy war, in which all is dedicated to the deity who helps in the battle; it often involved total destruction.  
**27:29** No human beings that have been devoted can be bought back; they must be executed.  
**27:30** All tenth-part gifts<sup>①</sup> from the land, whether of seed from the ground or fruit from the trees, belong to the LORD; they are holy to the LORD.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>tithes</i>  
**27:31** If someone wishes to buy back part of their tenth-part gift, they must add one-fifth to it.  
**27:32** All tenth-part gifts from a herd or flock—every tenth animal that passes under the shepherd’s staff—will be holy to the LORD.  
**27:33** The one bringing the tenth-part gift must not pick out the good from the bad, and cannot substitute any animal. But if one should substitute an animal, both it and the substitute will be holy and cannot be bought back.  
**27:34** These are the commands that the LORD gave Moses on Mount Sinai for the Israelites.
