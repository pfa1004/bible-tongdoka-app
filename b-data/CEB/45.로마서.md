# 45.로마서 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** From Paul, a slave of Christ Jesus, called to be an apostle and set apart for God’s good news.  
**1:2** <n><sup>①</sup></n> God promised this good news about his Son ahead of time through his prophets in the holy scriptures. His Son was descended from David.  
**1:3**   
**1:4** He was publicly identified as God’s Son with power through his resurrection from the dead, which was based on the Spirit of holiness. This Son is Jesus Christ our Lord.  
**1:5** Through him we have received God’s grace and our appointment to be apostles. This was to bring all Gentiles to faithful obedience for his name’s sake.  
**1:6** You who are called by Jesus Christ are also included among these Gentiles.  
**1:7** To those in Rome who are dearly loved by God and called to be God’s people. Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.  
**1:8** First of all, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you, because the news about your faithfulness is being spread throughout the whole world.  
**1:9** I serve God in my spirit by preaching the good news about God’s Son, and God is my witness that I continually mention you  
**1:10** in all my prayers. I’m always asking that somehow, by God’s will, I might succeed in visiting you at last.  
**1:11** I really want to see you to pass along some spiritual gift to you so that you can be strengthened.  
**1:12** What I mean is that we can mutually encourage each other while I am with you. We can be encouraged by the faithfulness we find in each other, both your faithfulness and mine.  
**1:13** I want you to know, brothers and sisters, that I planned to visit you many times, although I have been prevented from coming until now. I want to harvest some fruit among you, just as I have done among the other Gentiles.  
**1:14** I have a responsibility both to Greeks and to those who don’t speak Greek, both to the wise and to the foolish.  
**1:15** That’s why I’m ready to preach the gospel also to you who are in Rome.  
**1:16** I’m not ashamed of the gospel: it is God’s own power for salvation to all who have faith in God, to the Jew first and also to the Greek.  
**1:17** God’s righteousness is being revealed in the gospel, from faithfulness<sup>①</sup> for faith,<sup>②</sup> as it is written, <i>The righteous person will live by faith.</i><sup>③</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>faith</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>faithfulness</i> <sup>③</sup><a name='B:420 2:4'>Hab 2:4  
**1:18** God’s wrath is being revealed from heaven against all the ungodly behavior and the injustice of human beings who silence the truth with injustice.  
**1:19** This is because what is known about God should be plain to them because God made it plain to them.  
**1:20** Ever since the creation of the world, God’s invisible qualities—God’s eternal power and divine nature—have been clearly seen, because they are understood through the things God has made. So humans are without excuse.  
**1:21** Although they knew God, they didn’t honor God as God or thank him. Instead, their reasoning became pointless, and their foolish hearts were darkened.  
**1:22** While they were claiming to be wise, they made fools of themselves.  
**1:23** They exchanged the glory of the immortal God for images that look like mortal humans: birds, animals, and reptiles.  
**1:24** So God abandoned them to their hearts’ desires, which led to the moral corruption of degrading their own bodies with each other.  
**1:25** They traded God’s truth for a lie, and they worshipped and served the creation instead of the creator, who is blessed forever. Amen.  
**1:26** That’s why God abandoned them to degrading lust. Their females traded natural sexual relations for unnatural sexual relations.  
**1:27** Also, in the same way, the males traded natural sexual relations with females, and burned with lust for each other. Males performed shameful actions with males, and they were paid back with the penalty they deserved for their mistake in their own bodies.  
**1:28** Since they didn’t think it was worthwhile to acknowledge God, God abandoned them to a defective mind to do inappropriate things.  
**1:29** So they were filled with all injustice, wicked behavior, greed, and evil behavior. They are full of jealousy, murder, fighting, deception, and malice. They are gossips,  
**1:30** they slander people, and they hate God. They are rude and proud, and they brag. They invent ways to be evil, and they are disobedient to their parents.  
**1:31** They are without understanding, disloyal, without affection, and without mercy.  
**1:32** Though they know God’s decision that those who persist in such practices deserve death, they not only keep doing these things but also approve others who practice them.  

## 2장

**2:1** So every single one of you who judge others is without any excuse. You condemn yourself when you judge another person because the one who is judging is doing the same things.  
**2:2** We know that God’s judgment agrees with the truth, and his judgment is against those who do these kinds of things.  
**2:3** If you judge those who do these kinds of things while you do the same things yourself, think about this: Do you believe that you will escape God’s judgment?  
**2:4** Or do you have contempt for the riches of God’s generosity, tolerance, and patience? Don’t you realize that God’s kindness is supposed to lead you to change your heart and life?  
**2:5** You are storing up wrath for yourself because of your stubbornness and your heart that refuses to change. God’s just judgment will be revealed on the day of wrath.  
**2:6** <i>God will repay everyone based on their works</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:230 62:12'>Ps 62:12; <a name='B:240 24:12'>Prov 24:12  
**2:7** On the one hand, he will give eternal life to those who look for glory, honor, and immortality based on their patient good work.  
**2:8** But on the other hand, there will be wrath and anger for those who obey wickedness instead of the truth because they are acting out of selfishness and disobedience.  
**2:9** There will be trouble and distress for every human being who does evil, for the Jew first and also for the Greek.  
**2:10** But there will be glory, honor, and peace for everyone who does what is good, for the Jew first and also for the Greek.  
**2:11** God does not have favorites.  
**2:12** Those who have sinned outside the Law will also die outside the Law, and those who have sinned under the Law will be judged by the Law.  
**2:13** It isn’t the ones who hear the Law who are righteous in God’s eyes. It is the ones who do what the Law says who will be treated as righteous.  
**2:14** Gentiles don’t have the Law. But when they instinctively do what the Law requires they are a Law in themselves, though they don’t have the Law.  
**2:15** They show the proof of the Law written on their hearts, and their consciences affirm it. Their conflicting thoughts will accuse them, or even make a defense for them,  
**2:16** on the day when, according to my gospel, God will judge the hidden truth about human beings through Christ Jesus.  
**2:17** But,<br>if you call yourself a Jew;<br>if you rely on the Law;<br>if you brag about your relationship to God;  
**2:18** if you know the will of God;<br>if you are taught by the Law so that you can figure out the things that really matter;  
**2:19** if you have persuaded yourself that you are:<br>a guide for the blind;<br>a light to those who are in darkness;  
**2:20** an educator of the foolish;<br>a teacher of infants (since you have the full content of knowledge and truth in the Law);  
**2:21** then why don’t you who are teaching others teach yourself?<br>If you preach, “No stealing,” do you steal?  
**2:22** If you say, “No adultery,” do you commit adultery?<br>If you hate idols, do you rob temples?  
**2:23** If you brag about the Law, do you shame God by breaking the Law?  
**2:24** As it is written: <i>The name of God is discredited by the Gentiles because of you</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:290 52:5'>Isa 52:5 LXX  
**2:25** Circumcision is an advantage if you do what the Law says. But if you are a person who breaks the Law, your status of being circumcised has changed into not being circumcised.  
**2:26** So if the person who isn’t circumcised keeps the Law, won’t his status of not being circumcised be counted as if he were circumcised?  
**2:27** The one who isn’t physically circumcised but keeps the Law will judge you. You became a lawbreaker after you had the written Law and circumcision.  
**2:28** It isn’t the Jew who maintains outward appearances who will receive praise from God, and it isn’t people who are outwardly circumcised on their bodies.  
**2:29** Instead, it is the person who is a Jew inside, who is circumcised in spirit, not literally. That person’s praise doesn’t come from people but from God.  

## 3장

**3:1** So what’s the advantage of being a Jew? Or what’s the benefit of circumcision?  
**3:2** Plenty in every way. First of all, the Jews were trusted with God’s revelations.  
**3:3** What does it matter, then, if some weren’t faithful? Their lack of faith won’t cancel God’s faithfulness, will it?  
**3:4** Absolutely not! God must be true, even if every human being is a liar, as it is written:<br><i>So that it can show that you are right in your words;</i><br><i>and you will triumph when you are judged.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:230 51:4'>Ps 51:4  
**3:5** But if our lack of righteousness confirms God’s justice, what will we say? That God, who brings wrath upon us, isn’t just (I’m speaking rhetorically)?  
**3:6** Absolutely not! If God weren’t just, how could he judge the world?  
**3:7** But if God’s truth is demonstrated by my lie and it increases his glory, why am I still judged as a sinner?  
**3:8** Why not say, “Let’s do evil things so that good things will come out of it”? (Some people who slander us accuse us of saying that, but these people deserve criticism.)  
**3:9** So what are we saying? Are we better off? Not at all. We have already stated the charge: both Jews and Greeks are all under the power of sin.  
**3:10** As it is written,<br><i>There is no righteous person, not even one.</i>  
**3:11** <i>There is no one who understands.</i><br><i>There is no one who looks for God.</i>  
**3:12** <i>They all turned away.</i><br><i>They have become worthless together.</i><br><i>There is no one who shows kindness.</i><br><i>There is not even one.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:230 14:1-3'>Ps 14:1–3  
**3:13** <i>Their throat is a grave that has been opened.</i><br><i>They are deceitful with their tongues,</i><br><i>and the poison of vipers is under their lips.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:230 5:9'>Ps 5:9  
**3:14** <i>Their mouths are full of cursing and bitterness.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:230 10:7'>Ps 10:7  
**3:15** <i>Their feet are quick to shed blood;</i>  
**3:16** <i>destruction and misery are in their ways;</i>  
**3:17** <i>and they don’t know the way of peace.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:290 59:7-8'>Isa 59:7–8  
**3:18** <i>There is no fear of God in their view of the world.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:230 36:1'>Ps 36:1  
**3:19** Now we know that whatever the Law says, it speaks to those who are under the Law, in order to shut every mouth and make it so the whole world has to answer to God.  
**3:20** It follows that no human being will be treated as righteous in his presence by doing what the Law says, because the knowledge of sin comes through the Law.  
**3:21** But now God’s righteousness has been revealed apart from the Law, which is confirmed by the Law and the Prophets.  
**3:22** God’s righteousness comes through the faithfulness of Jesus Christ for all who have faith in him. There’s no distinction.  
**3:23** All have sinned and fall short of God’s glory,  
**3:24** but all are treated as righteous freely by his grace because of a ransom that was paid by Christ Jesus.  
**3:25** Through his faithfulness, God displayed Jesus as the place of sacrifice where mercy is found by means of his blood. He did this to demonstrate his righteousness in passing over sins that happened before,  
**3:26** during the time of God’s patient tolerance. He also did this to demonstrate that he is righteous in the present time, and to treat the one who has faith in Jesus as righteous.  
**3:27** What happens to our bragging? It’s thrown out. With which law? With what we have accomplished under the Law?  
**3:28** No, not at all, but through the law of faith. We consider that a person is treated as righteous by faith, apart from what is accomplished under the Law.  
**3:29** Or is God the God of Jews only? Isn’t God the God of Gentiles also? Yes, God is also the God of Gentiles.  
**3:30** Since God is one, then the one who makes the circumcised righteous by faith will also make the one who isn’t circumcised righteous through faith.  
**3:31** Do we then cancel the Law through this faith? Absolutely not! Instead, we confirm the Law.  

## 4장

**4:1** So what are we going to say? Are we going to find that Abraham is our ancestor on the basis of genealogy?  
**4:2** Because if Abraham was made righteous because of his actions, he would have had a reason to brag, but not in front of God.  
**4:3** What does the scripture say? <i>Abraham had faith in God, and it was credited to him as righteousness</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:10 15:6'>Gen 15:6  
**4:4** Workers’ salaries aren’t credited to them on the basis of an employer’s grace but rather on the basis of what they deserve.  
**4:5** But faith is credited as righteousness to those who don’t work, because they have faith in God who makes the ungodly righteous.  
**4:6** In the same way, David also pronounces a blessing on the person to whom God credits righteousness apart from actions:  
**4:7** <i>Happy are those whose actions outside the Law are forgiven,</i><br><i>and whose sins are covered.</i>  
**4:8** <i>Happy are those whose sin isn’t counted against them by the Lord</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:230 32:1-2'>Ps 32:1–2  
**4:9** Is this state of happiness only for the circumcised or is it also for those who aren’t circumcised? We say, “Faith was credited to Abraham as righteousness.”  
**4:10** So how was it credited? When he was circumcised, or when he wasn’t circumcised? In fact, it was credited while he still wasn’t circumcised, not after he was circumcised.  
**4:11** He received the sign of circumcision as a seal of the righteousness that comes from the faith he had while he still wasn’t circumcised. It happened this way so that Abraham could be the ancestor of all those people who aren’t circumcised, who have faith in God, and so are counted as righteous.  
**4:12** He could also be the ancestor of those circumcised people, who aren’t only circumcised but who also walk in the path of faith, like our ancestor Abraham did while he wasn’t circumcised.  
**4:13** The promise to Abraham and to his descendants, that he would inherit the world, didn’t come through the Law but through the righteousness that comes from faith.  
**4:14** If they inherit because of the Law, then faith has no effect and the promise has been canceled.  
**4:15** The Law brings about wrath. But when there isn’t any law, there isn’t any violation of the law.  
**4:16** That’s why the inheritance comes through faith, so that it will be on the basis of God’s grace. In that way, the promise is secure for all of Abraham’s descendants, not just for those who are related by Law but also for those who are related by the faith of Abraham, who is the father of all of us.  
**4:17** As it is written: <i>I have appointed you to be the father of many nations.</i><sup>①</sup> So Abraham is our father in the eyes of God in whom he had faith, the God who gives life to the dead and calls things that don’t exist into existence.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:10 17:5'>Gen 17:5  
**4:18** When it was beyond hope, he had faith in the hope that he would become the father of many nations, in keeping with the promise God spoke to him: <i>That’s how many descendants you will have.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:10 15:5'>Gen 15:5  
**4:19** Without losing faith, Abraham, who was nearly 100 years old, took into account his own body, which was as good as dead, and Sarah’s womb, which was dead.  
**4:20** He didn’t hesitate with a lack of faith in God’s promise, but he grew strong in faith and gave glory to God.  
**4:21** He was fully convinced that God was able to do what he promised.  
**4:22** Therefore, it was credited to him as righteousness.  
**4:23** But the scripture that says <i>it was credited to him</i><sup>①</sup> wasn’t written only for Abraham’s sake.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:10 15:6'>Gen 15:6  
**4:24** It was written also for our sake, because it is going to be credited to us too. It will be credited to those of us who have faith in the one who raised Jesus our Lord from the dead.  
**4:25** He was handed over because of our mistakes, and he was raised to meet the requirements of righteousness for us.  

## 5장

**5:1** Therefore, since we have been made righteous through his faithfulness,<sup>①</sup> we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>faith</i>  
**5:2** We have access by faith into this grace in which we stand through him, and we boast in the hope of God’s glory.  
**5:3** But not only that! We even take pride in our problems, because we know that trouble produces endurance,  
**5:4** endurance produces character, and character produces hope.  
**5:5** This hope doesn’t put us to shame, because the love of God has been poured out in our hearts through the Holy Spirit, who has been given to us.  
**5:6** While we were still weak, at the right moment, Christ died for ungodly people.  
**5:7** It isn’t often that someone will die for a righteous person, though maybe someone might dare to die for a good person.  
**5:8** But God shows his love for us, because while we were still sinners Christ died for us.  
**5:9** So, now that we have been made righteous by his blood, we can be even more certain that we will be saved from God’s wrath through him.  
**5:10** If we were reconciled to God through the death of his Son while we were still enemies, now that we have been reconciled, how much more certain is it that we will be saved by his life?  
**5:11** And not only that: we even take pride in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, the one through whom we now have a restored relationship with God.  
**5:12** Just as through one human being sin came into the world, and death came through sin, so death has come to everyone, since everyone has sinned.  
**5:13** Although sin was in the world, since there was no Law, it wasn’t taken into account until the Law came.  
**5:14** But death ruled from Adam until Moses, even over those who didn’t sin in the same way Adam did—Adam was a type of the one who was coming.  
**5:15** But the free gift of Christ isn’t like Adam’s failure. If many people died through what one person did wrong, God’s grace is multiplied even more for many people with the gift—of the one person Jesus Christ—that comes through grace.  
**5:16** The gift isn’t like the consequences of one person’s sin. The judgment that came from one person’s sin led to punishment, but the free gift that came out of many failures led to the verdict of acquittal.  
**5:17** If death ruled because of one person’s failure, those who receive the multiplied grace and the gift of righteousness will even more certainly rule in life through the one person Jesus Christ.  
**5:18** So now the righteous requirements necessary for life are met for everyone through the righteous act of one person, just as judgment fell on everyone through the failure of one person.  
**5:19** Many people were made righteous through the obedience of one person, just as many people were made sinners through the disobedience of one person.  
**5:20** The Law stepped in to amplify the failure, but where sin increased, grace multiplied even more.  
**5:21** The result is that grace will rule through God’s righteousness, leading to eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord, just as sin ruled in death.  

## 6장

**6:1** So what are we going to say? Should we continue sinning so grace will multiply?  
**6:2** Absolutely not! All of us died to sin. How can we still live in it?  
**6:3** Or don’t you know that all who were baptized into Christ Jesus were baptized into his death?  
**6:4** Therefore, we were buried together with him through baptism into his death, so that just as Christ was raised from the dead through the glory of the Father, we too can walk in newness of life.  
**6:5** If we were united together in a death like his, we will also be united together in a resurrection like his.  
**6:6** This is what we know: the person that we used to be was crucified with him in order to get rid of the corpse that had been controlled by sin. That way we wouldn’t be slaves to sin anymore,  
**6:7** because a person who has died has been freed from sin’s power.  
**6:8** But if we died with Christ, we have faith that we will also live with him.  
**6:9** We know that Christ has been raised from the dead and he will never die again. Death no longer has power over him.  
**6:10** He died to sin once and for all with his death, but he lives for God with his life.  
**6:11** In the same way, you also should consider yourselves dead to sin but alive for God in Christ Jesus.  
**6:12** So then, don’t let sin rule your body, so that you do what it wants.  
**6:13** Don’t offer parts of your body to sin, to be used as weapons to do wrong. Instead, present yourselves to God as people who have been brought back to life from the dead, and offer all the parts of your body to God to be used as weapons to do right.  
**6:14** Sin will have no power over you, because you aren’t under Law but under grace.  
**6:15** So what? Should we sin because we aren’t under Law but under grace? Absolutely not!  
**6:16** Don’t you know that if you offer yourselves to someone as obedient slaves, that you are slaves of the one whom you obey? That’s true whether you serve as slaves of sin, which leads to death, or as slaves of the kind of obedience that leads to righteousness.  
**6:17** But thank God that although you used to be slaves of sin, you gave wholehearted obedience to the teaching that was handed down to you, which provides a pattern.  
**6:18** Now that you have been set free from sin, you have become slaves of righteousness.  
**6:19** (I’m speaking with ordinary metaphors because of your limitations.) Once, you offered the parts of your body to be used as slaves to impurity and to lawless behavior that leads to still more lawless behavior. Now, you should present the parts of your body as slaves to righteousness, which makes your lives holy.  
**6:20** When you were slaves of sin, you were free from the control of righteousness.  
**6:21** What consequences did you get from doing things that you are now ashamed of? The outcome of those things is death.  
**6:22** But now that you have been set free from sin and become slaves to God, you have the consequence of a holy life, and the outcome is eternal life.  
**6:23** The wages that sin pays are death, but God’s gift is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.  

## 7장

**7:1** Brothers and sisters, I’m talking to you as people who know the Law. Don’t you know that the Law has power over someone only as long as he or she lives?  
**7:2** A married woman is united with her husband under the Law while he is alive. But if her husband dies, she is released from the Law concerning her husband.  
**7:3** So then, if she lives with another man while her husband is alive, she’s committing adultery. But if her husband dies, she’s free from the Law, so she won’t be committing adultery if she marries someone else.  
**7:4** Therefore, my brothers and sisters, you also died with respect to the Law through the body of Christ, so that you could be united with someone else. You are united with the one who was raised from the dead so that we can bear fruit for God.  
**7:5** When we were self-centered, the sinful passions aroused through the Law were at work in all the parts of our body, so that we bore fruit for death.  
**7:6** But now we have been released from the Law. We have died with respect to the thing that controlled us, so that we can be slaves in the new life under the Spirit, not in the old life under the written Law.  
**7:7** So what are we going to say? That the Law is sin? Absolutely not! But I wouldn’t have known sin except through the Law. I wouldn’t have known the desire for what others have if the Law had not said, <i>Don’t desire to take what others have</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:20 20:17'>Exod 20:17; <a name='B:50 5:21'>Deut 5:21  
**7:8** But sin seized the opportunity and used this commandment to produce all kinds of desires in me. Sin is dead without the Law.  
**7:9** I used to be alive without the Law, but when the commandment came, sin sprang to life,  
**7:10** and I died. So the commandment that was intended to give life brought death.  
**7:11** Sin seized the opportunity through the commandment, deceived me, and killed me.  
**7:12** So the Law itself is holy, and the commandment is holy, righteous, and good.  
**7:13** So did something good bring death to me? Absolutely not! But sin caused my death through something good so that sin would be exposed as sin. That way sin would become even more thoroughly sinful through the commandment.  
**7:14** We know that the Law is spiritual, but I’m made of flesh and blood, and I’m sold as a slave to sin.  
**7:15** I don’t know what I’m doing, because I don’t do what I want to do. Instead, I do the thing that I hate.  
**7:16** But if I’m doing the thing that I don’t want to do, I’m agreeing that the Law is right.  
**7:17** But now I’m not the one doing it anymore. Instead, it’s sin that lives in me.  
**7:18** I know that good doesn’t live in me—that is, in my body. The desire to do good is inside of me, but I can’t do it.  
**7:19** I don’t do the good that I want to do, but I do the evil that I don’t want to do.  
**7:20** But if I do the very thing that I don’t want to do, then I’m not the one doing it anymore. Instead, it is sin that lives in me that is doing it.  
**7:21** So I find that, as a rule, when I want to do what is good, evil is right there with me.  
**7:22** I gladly agree with the Law on the inside,  
**7:23** but I see a different law at work in my body. It wages a war against the law of my mind and takes me prisoner with the law of sin that is in my body.  
**7:24** I’m a miserable human being. Who will deliver me from this dead corpse?  
**7:25** Thank God through Jesus Christ our Lord! So then I’m a slave to God’s Law in my mind, but I’m a slave to sin’s law in my body.  

## 8장

**8:1** So now there isn’t any condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.  
**8:2** The law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus has set you free from the law of sin and death.  
**8:3** God has done what was impossible for the Law, since it was weak because of selfishness. God condemned sin in the body by sending his own Son to deal with sin in the same body as humans, who are controlled by sin.  
**8:4** He did this so that the righteous requirement of the Law might be fulfilled in us. Now the way we live is based on the Spirit, not based on selfishness.  
**8:5** People whose lives are based on selfishness think about selfish things, but people whose lives are based on the Spirit think about things that are related to the Spirit.  
**8:6** The attitude that comes from selfishness leads to death, but the attitude that comes from the Spirit leads to life and peace.  
**8:7** So the attitude that comes from selfishness is hostile to God. It doesn’t submit to God’s Law, because it can’t.  
**8:8** People who are self-centered aren’t able to please God.  
**8:9** But you aren’t self-centered. Instead you are in the Spirit, if in fact God’s Spirit lives in you. If anyone doesn’t have the Spirit of Christ, they don’t belong to him.

## 13장

**13:1** Every person should place themselves under the authority of the government. There isn’t any authority unless it comes from God, and the authorities that are there have been put in place by God.  
**13:2** So anyone who opposes the authority is standing against what God has established. People who take this kind of stand will get punished.  
**13:3** The authorities don’t frighten people who are doing the right thing. Rather, they frighten people who are doing wrong. Would you rather not be afraid of authority? Do what’s right, and you will receive its approval.  
**13:4** It is God’s servant given for your benefit. But if you do what’s wrong, be afraid because it doesn’t have weapons to enforce the law for nothing. It is God’s servant put in place to carry out his punishment on those who do what is wrong.  
**13:5** That is why it is necessary to place yourself under the government’s authority, not only to avoid God’s punishment but also for the sake of your conscience.  
**13:6** You should also pay taxes for the same reason, because the authorities are God’s assistants, concerned with this very thing.  
**13:7** So pay everyone what you owe them. Pay the taxes you owe, pay the duties you are charged, give respect to those you should respect, and honor those you should honor.  
**13:8** Don’t be in debt to anyone, except for the obligation to love each other. Whoever loves another person has fulfilled the Law.  
**13:9** The commandments, <i>Don’t commit adultery, don’t murder, don’t steal, don’t desire what others have,</i><sup>①</sup> and any other commandments, are all summed up in one word: <i>You must love your neighbor as yourself.</i><sup>②</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:20 20:13-15'>Exod 20:13–15, <a name='B:20 20:17'>17; <a name='B:50 5:17-19'>Deut 5:17–19, <a name='B:50 5:21'>21 <sup>②</sup><a name='B:30 19:18'>Lev 19:18

**13:11** As you do all this, you know what time it is. The hour has already come for you to wake up from your sleep. Now our salvation is nearer than when we first had faith.  
**13:12** The night is almost over, and the day is near. So let’s get rid of the actions that belong to the darkness and put on the weapons of light.  
**13:13** Let’s behave appropriately as people who live in the day, not in partying and getting drunk, not in sleeping around and obscene behavior, not in fighting and obsession.  
**13:14** Instead, dress yourself with the Lord Jesus Christ, and don’t plan to indulge your selfish desires.

**14:9** This is why Christ died and lived: so that he might be Lord of both the dead and the living.  
**14:10** But why do you judge your brother or sister? Or why do you look down on your brother or sister? We all will stand in front of the judgment seat of God.  
**14:11** Because it is written,<br><i>As I live, says the Lord, every knee will bow to me,</i><br><i>and every tongue will give praise to God.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:290 45:23'>Isa 45:23  
**14:12** So then, each of us will give an account of ourselves to God.  
**14:13** So stop judging each other. Instead, this is what you should decide: never put a stumbling block or obstacle in the way of your brother or sister.  
**14:14** I know and I’m convinced in the Lord Jesus that nothing is wrong to eat in itself. But if someone thinks something is wrong to eat, it becomes wrong for that person.  
**14:15** If your brother or sister is upset by your food, you are no longer walking in love. Don’t let your food destroy someone for whom Christ died.  
**14:16** And don’t let something you consider to be good be criticized as wrong.  
**14:17** God’s kingdom isn’t about eating food and drinking but about righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit.  
**14:18** Whoever serves Christ this way pleases God and gets human approval.  
**14:19** So let’s strive for the things that bring peace and the things that build each other up.  
**14:20** Don’t destroy what God has done because of food. All food is acceptable, but it’s a bad thing if it trips someone else.  
**14:21** It’s a good thing not to eat meat or drink wine or to do anything that trips your brother or sister.  
**14:22** Keep the belief that you have to yourself—it’s between you and God. People are blessed who don’t convict themselves by the things they approve.  
**14:23** But those who have doubts are convicted if they go ahead and eat, because they aren’t acting on the basis of faith. Everything that isn’t based on faith is sin.  

## 15장

**15:1** We who are powerful need to be patient with the weakness of those who don’t have power, and not please ourselves.  
**15:2** Each of us should please our neighbors for their good in order to build them up.  
**15:3** Christ didn’t please himself, but, as it is written, <i>The insults of those who insulted you fell on me.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:230 69:9'>Ps 69:9  
**15:4** Whatever was written in the past was written for our instruction so that we could have hope through endurance and through the encouragement of the scriptures.  
**15:5** May the God of endurance and encouragement give you the same attitude toward each other, similar to Christ Jesus’ attitude.  
**15:6** That way you can glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ together with one voice.  
**15:7** So welcome each other, in the same way that Christ also welcomed you, for God’s glory.  
**15:8** I’m saying that Christ became a servant of those who are circumcised for the sake of God’s truth, in order to confirm the promises given to the ancestors,  
**15:9** and so that the Gentiles could glorify God for his mercy. As it is written,<br><i>Because of this I will confess you among the Gentiles,</i><br><i>and I will sing praises to your name</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:230 18:49'>Ps 18:49  
**15:10** And again, it says,<br><i>Rejoice, Gentiles, with his people</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:50 32:43'>Deut 32:43  
**15:11** And again,<br><i>Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles,</i><br><i>and all the people should sing his praises</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:230 117:1'>Ps 117:1  
**15:12** And again, Isaiah says,<br><i>There will be a root of Jesse,</i><br><i>who will also rise to rule the Gentiles.</i><br><i>The Gentiles will place their hope in him</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:290 11:10'>Isa 11:10  
**15:13** May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in faith so that you overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.  
**15:14** My brothers and sisters, I myself am convinced that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, and are able to teach each other.  
**15:15** But I’ve written to you in a sort of daring way, partly to remind you of what you already know. I’m writing to you in this way because of the grace that was given to me by God.  
**15:16** It helps me to be a minister of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles. I’m working as a priest of God’s gospel so that the offering of the Gentiles can be acceptable and made holy by the Holy Spirit.  
**15:17** So in Christ Jesus I brag about things that have to do with God.  
**15:18** I don’t dare speak about anything except what Christ has done through me to bring about the obedience of the Gentiles. He did it by what I’ve said and what I’ve done,  
**15:19** by the power of signs and wonders, and by the power of God’s Spirit. So I’ve completed the circuit of preaching Christ’s gospel from Jerusalem all the way around to Illyricum.  
**15:20** In this way, I have a goal to preach the gospel where they haven’t heard of Christ yet, so that I won’t be building on someone else’s foundation.  
**15:21** Instead, as it’s written, <i>Those who hadn’t been told about him will see, and those who hadn’t heard will understand</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:290 52:15'>Isa 52:15  
**15:22** That’s why I’ve been stopped so many times from coming to see you.  
**15:23** But now, since I don’t have any place to work in these regions anymore, and since I’ve wanted to come to see you for many years,  
**15:24** I’ll visit you when I go to Spain. I hope to see you while I’m passing through. And I hope you will send me on my way there, after I have first been reenergized by some time in your company.  
**15:25** But now I’m going to Jerusalem, to serve God’s people.  
**15:26** Macedonia and Achaia have been happy to make a contribution for the poor among God’s people in Jerusalem.  
**15:27** They were happy to do this, and they are actually in debt to God’s people in Jerusalem. If the Gentiles got a share of the Jewish people’s spiritual resources, they ought to minister to them with material resources.  
**15:28** So then after I have finished this job and have safely delivered the final amount of the Gentiles’ offering to them, I will leave for Spain, visiting you on the way.  
**15:29** And I know that when I come to you I will come with the fullest blessing of Christ.  
**15:30** Brothers and sisters, I urge you, through our Lord Jesus Christ and through the love of the Spirit, to join me in my struggles in your prayers to God for me.  
**15:31** Pray that I will be rescued from the people in Judea who don’t believe. Also, pray that my service for Jerusalem will be acceptable to God’s people there  
**15:32** so that I can come to you with joy by God’s will and be reenergized with your company.  
**15:33** May the God of peace be with you all. Amen.
