# 25.예레미야애가 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** Oh, no!<br>She sits alone, the city that was once full of people.<br>Once great among nations, she has become like a widow.<br>Once a queen over provinces, she has become a slave.  
**1:2** She weeps bitterly in the night, her tears on her cheek.<br>None of her lovers comfort her. All her friends lied to her;<br>they have become her enemies.  
**1:3** Judah was exiled after suffering and hard service.<br>She lives among the nations; she finds no rest.<br>All who were chasing her caught her—right in the middle of<br>her distress.  
**1:4** Zion’s roads are in mourning; no one comes to the festivals.<br>All her gates are deserted. Her priests are groaning,<br>her young women grieving. She is bitter.  
**1:5** Her adversaries have become rulers; her enemies relax.<br>Certainly the LORD caused her grief because of her many wrong acts.<br>Her children have gone away, captive before the enemy.  
**1:6** Daughter Zion lost all her glory.<br>Her officials are like deer that can’t find pasture.<br>They have gone away, frail, before the hunter.  
**1:7** While suffering and homeless, Jerusalem remembers all her treasures from days long past.<br>When her people fell by the enemy’s hand, there was no one to help her.<br>Enemies saw her, laughed at her defeat.  
**1:8** Jerusalem has sinned greatly; therefore, she’s become a joke.<sup>①</sup><br>All who honored her now detest her, for they’ve seen her naked.<br>Even she groans and turns away.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>she’s become unclean.</i>  
**1:9** Her uncleanness shows on her clothing; she didn’t consider what would happen to her.<br>She’s gone down shockingly; she has no comforter.<br>“LORD, look at my suffering—the enemy has definitely triumphed!”  
**1:10** The enemy grabbed all her treasures.<br>She watched nations enter her sanctuary—<br>nations that you, God,<sup>①</sup> commanded: They must not enter your assembly.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb lacks <i>God</i>.  
**1:11** All her people are groaning, seeking bread.<br>They give up their most precious things for food to survive.<br>“LORD, look and take notice: I am most certainly despised.”  
**1:12** Is this nothing to all you who pass by?<sup>①</sup><br>Look around: Is there any suffering like the suffering inflicted on me,<br>the grief that the LORD caused on the day of his fierce anger?<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**1:13** From above he sent fire into my bones; he trampled them.<br>He spread a net for my feet; he forced me backward.<br>He left me devastated, constantly sick.  
**1:14** My steps<sup>①</sup> are being watched;<sup>②</sup> by his hand they are tripped up.<br>His yoke is on my neck; he makes my strength fail.<br>My Lord has handed me over to people I can’t resist.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Correction; or <i>my wrong acts</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>a yoke is bound to my wrong acts</i>; Heb uncertain  
**1:15** My Lord has despised my mighty warriors.<br>He called a feast for me—in order to crush my young men!<br>My Lord has stomped on the winepress of the young woman Daughter Judah.  
**1:16** Because of all these things I’m crying. My eyes, my own eyes pour water<br>because a comforter who might encourage me is nowhere near.<br>My children are destroyed because the enemy was so strong.  
**1:17** Zion spreads out her hands; she has no comforter.<br>The LORD commanded Jacob’s enemies to surround him.<br>Jerusalem is just a piece of garbage to them.  
**1:18** The LORD is right, because I disobeyed his word.<br>Listen, all you people; look at my suffering.<br>My young women and young men have gone away as prisoners.  
**1:19** I called to my lovers, but they deceived me.<br>My priests and my elders have perished in the city;<br>they were looking for food to survive.  
**1:20** Pay attention, LORD, for I am in trouble. My stomach is churning;<br>my heart is pounding inside me because I am so bitter.<br>In the streets the sword kills; in the house it is like death.  
**1:21** People heard that I was groaning, that I had no comforter.<br>All my enemies heard about my distress; they were thrilled that you had done this.<br>Bring the day you have announced so they become like me!  
**1:22** Let all their evil come before you.<br>Then injure them like you’ve injured me because of all my wrong acts;<br>my groans are many, my heart is sick.  

## 2장

**2:1** Oh, no!<br>In anger, my Lord put Daughter Zion under a cloud;<sup>①</sup><br>he threw Israel’s glory from heaven down to earth.<br>On that day of wrath, he didn’t consider his own footstool.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**2:2** Showing no compassion, my Lord devoured each of Jacob’s meadows;<br>in his wrath he tore down the walled cities of Daughter Judah.<br>The kingdom and its officials, he forced to the ground, shamed.  
**2:3** In his burning rage, he cut off each of Israel’s horns;<br>right in front of the enemy, he withdrew his strong hand;<br>he burned against Jacob like a flaming fire that ate up everything nearby.  
**2:4** He bent his bow as an enemy would; his strong hand was poised like an adversary.<br>He killed every precious thing in sight;<br>he poured out his wrath like fire on Daughter Zion’s tent.  
**2:5** My Lord has become like an enemy. He devoured Israel;<br>he devoured all her palaces; he made ruins of her city walls.<br>In Daughter Judah he multiplied mourning along with more mourning!  
**2:6** He wrecked his own booth like a garden; he destroyed his place for festivals.<br>The LORD made Zion forget both festival and sabbath;<br>in his fierce rage, he scorned both monarch and priest.  
**2:7** My Lord rejected his altar, he abandoned his sanctuary;<br>he handed Zion’s palace walls over to enemies.<br>They shouted in the LORD’s own house as if it were a festival day.  
**2:8** The LORD planned to destroy Daughter Zion’s wall.<br>He stretched out a measuring line, didn’t stop himself from devouring.<br>He made barricades and walls wither—together they wasted away.  
**2:9** Zion’s gates sank into the ground; he broke and shattered her bars;<br>her king and her officials are now among the nations. There is no Instruction!<sup>①</sup><br>Even her prophets couldn’t find a vision from the LORD.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>Torah</i>  
**2:10** Daughter Zion’s elders sit on the ground and mourn.<br>They throw dust on their heads; they put on mourning clothes.<br>Jerusalem’s young women bow their heads all the way to the ground.  
**2:11** My eyes are worn out from weeping; my stomach is churning.<br>My insides are poured on the ground because the daughter of my people is shattered,<br>because children and babies are fainting in the city streets.  
**2:12** They say to their mothers, “Where are grain and wine?”<br>while fainting like the wounded in the city streets,<br>while their lives are draining away at their own mothers’ breasts.  
**2:13** What can I testify about you, Daughter Jerusalem?<sup>①</sup> To what could I compare you?<br>With what could I equate you? How can I comfort you, young woman Daughter Zion?<br>Your hurt is as vast as the sea. Who can heal you?<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>How can I warn you?</i> or <i>To what could I liken you?</i>; Heb uncertain  
**2:14** Your prophets gave you worthless and empty visions.<br>They didn’t reveal your sin so as to prevent your captivity.<br>Instead, they showed you worthless and incorrect prophecies.  
**2:15** All who pass by on the road clap their hands about you;<br>they whistle, shaking their heads at Daughter Jerusalem:<br>“Could this be the city called Perfect Beauty, the Joy of All the Earth?”  
**2:16** All your enemies open wide their mouths against you;<br>they whistle, grinding their teeth. They say, “We have devoured!<br>This is definitely the day we’ve been waiting for. We’ve seen it come to pass.”  
**2:17** The LORD did what he had planned. He accomplished the word<br>that he had commanded long ago. He ripped down, showing no compassion.<br>He made the enemy rejoice over you; he raised up your adversaries’ horn.  
**2:18** Cry out to my Lord from the heart,<sup>①</sup> you wall of Daughter Zion;<br>make your<sup>②</sup> tears run down like a flood all day and night.<br>Don’t relax at all; don’t rest your eyes a moment.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Correction; or <i>their heart cried out to my Lord</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb lacks <i>your</i>.  
**2:19** Get up and cry out at nighttime, at the start of the night shift; pour out your heart before my Lord like water.<br>Lift your hands up to him for the life of your children—<br>the ones who are fainting from hunger on every street corner.  
**2:20** LORD, look and see to whom you have done this!<br>Should women eat their own offspring, their own beautiful babies?<br>Should priest and prophet be killed in my Lord’s own sanctuary?  
**2:21** Young and old alike lie on the ground in the streets;<br>my young women and young men fall dead by the sword.<br>On the day of your anger, you killed; you slaughtered, showing no compassion.  
**2:22** You invited—as if to a festival!—terrors<sup>①</sup> from every side.<br>On the day of the LORD’s anger, no one escaped, not one survived.<br>The children that I nurtured, that I raised myself, my enemy finished them off.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Correction; or <i>my attackers</i>

## 4장

**4:1** Oh, no!<br>Gold is tarnished;<sup>①</sup> even the purest gold is changed.<br>Sacred jewels are scattered on every street corner.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**4:2** Zion’s precious children, once valued as pure gold—<br>oh no!—now they are worth no more than clay pots made by a potter.  
**4:3** Even jackals offer the breast; they nurse their young.<br>But the daughter of my people has become cruel, like desert ostriches.  
**4:4** The baby’s tongue sticks to the roof of its mouth, thirsty.<br>Children ask for bread, beg for it—but there is no bread.  
**4:5** Those who once ate gourmet food now tremble in the streets.<br>Those who wore the finest purple clothes now cling to piles of garbage.  
**4:6** Greater was the punishment<sup>①</sup> of the daughter of my people than Sodom’s penalty,<sup>②</sup><br>which was quickly overthrown without any hand-wringing.<sup>③</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>iniquity</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>sin</i> <sup>③</sup>Heb uncertain  
**4:7** Her nazirites were purer than snow; they were more dazzling than milk.<br>Their limbs were redder than coral; their bodies were sapphire.  
**4:8** But their appearance grew darker than soot; they weren’t recognized in the streets.<br>Their skin shriveled on their bones; it became dry like wood.  
**4:9** Things were better for those stabbed by the sword than for those stabbed by famine—<br>those who bled away, pierced, lacking food from the field.  
**4:10** The hands of loving women boiled their own children<br>to become their food during the destruction of the daughter of my people.  
**4:11** The LORD let loose his fury; he poured out his fierce anger.<br>He started a fire in Zion; it licked up its foundations.  
**4:12** The earth’s rulers didn’t believe it—neither did any who inhabit the world—<br>that either enemy or adversary could enter Jerusalem’s gates.  
**4:13** It was because of her prophets’ sins, her priests’ iniquities,<br>those who shed righteous blood in the middle of the city.  
**4:14** People wandered blindly in the streets, polluted with blood.<br>No one would even touch their clothing.  
**4:15** “Go away! Unclean!” was shouted at them, “Go away! Away! Don’t touch!”<br>So they fled and wandered around. The nations said, “They can’t stay here anymore.”  
**4:16** It was the LORD’s presence that scattered them;<sup>①</sup> he no longer notices them.<br>They didn’t honor the priests’ presence; they didn’t favor the elders.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**4:17** Our eyes continually failed, looking for some help, but for nothing.<br>From our watchtower we watched for a nation that doesn’t save.  
**4:18** Our steps were tracked; we could no longer walk in our streets.<br>Our end had drawn near; our days were done—our end had definitely come.  
**4:19** Our hunters were faster than airborne eagles.<br>They chased us up the mountains; they ambushed us in the wilderness.  
**4:20** The LORD’s chosen one, the very breath in our lungs, was caught in their traps—<br>the one we used to talk about, saying, Under his protection we will live among the nations.”  
**4:21** Rejoice and be happy, Daughter Edom, you who live in the land of Uz.<br>But this cup will pass over to you too. You will get drunk on it. You will be stripped naked.  
**4:22** Your punishment<sup>①</sup> is over, Daughter Zion; God won’t expose you anymore.<br>But he will attend to your punishment, Daughter Edom; he will expose your sins.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>iniquity</i>
