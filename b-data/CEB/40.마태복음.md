# 40.마태복음 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** A record of the ancestors of Jesus Christ, son of David, son of Abraham:  
**1:2** Abraham was the father of Isaac. Isaac was the father of Jacob. Jacob was the father of Judah and his brothers.  
**1:3** Judah was the father of Perez and Zerah, whose mother was Tamar. Perez was the father of Hezron. Hezron was the father of Aram.  
**1:4** Aram was the father of Amminadab. Amminadab was the father of Nahshon. Nahshon was the father of Salmon.  
**1:5** Salmon was the father of Boaz, whose mother was Rahab. Boaz was the father of Obed, whose mother was Ruth. Obed was the father of Jesse.  
**1:6** Jesse was the father of David the king. David was the father of Solomon, whose mother had been the wife of Uriah.  
**1:7** Solomon was the father of Rehoboam. Rehoboam was the father of Abijah. Abijah was the father of Asaph.  
**1:8** Asaph was the father of Jehoshaphat. Jehoshaphat was the father of Joram. Joram was the father of Uzziah.  
**1:9** Uzziah was the father of Jotham. Jotham was the father of Ahaz. Ahaz was the father of Hezekiah.  
**1:10** Hezekiah was the father of Manasseh. Manasseh was the father of Amos. Amos was the father of Josiah.  
**1:11** Josiah was the father of Jechoniah and his brothers. This was at the time of the exile to Babylon.  
**1:12** After the exile to Babylon: Jechoniah was the father of Shealtiel. Shealtiel was the father of Zerubbabel.  
**1:13** Zerubbabel was the father of Abiud. Abiud was the father of Eliakim. Eliakim was the father of Azor.  
**1:14** Azor was the father of Zadok. Zadok was the father of Achim. Achim was the father of Eliud.  
**1:15** Eliud was the father of Eleazar. Eleazar was the father of Matthan. Matthan was the father of Jacob.  
**1:16** Jacob was the father of Joseph, the husband of Mary—of whom Jesus was born, who is called the Christ.  
**1:17** So there were fourteen generations from Abraham to David, fourteen generations from David to the exile to Babylon, and fourteen generations from the exile to Babylon to the Christ.  
**1:18** This is how the birth of Jesus Christ took place. When Mary his mother was engaged to Joseph, before they were married, she became pregnant by the Holy Spirit.  
**1:19** Joseph her husband was a righteous man. Because he didn’t want to humiliate her, he decided to call off their engagement quietly.  
**1:20** As he was thinking about this, an angel from the Lord appeared to him in a dream and said, “Joseph son of David, don’t be afraid to take Mary as your wife, because the child she carries was conceived by the Holy Spirit.  
**1:21** She will give birth to a son, and you will call him Jesus, because he will save his people from their sins.”  
**1:22** Now all of this took place so that what the Lord had spoken through the prophet would be fulfilled:  
**1:23** <i>Look! A virgin will become pregnant and give birth to a son,</i><br><i>And they will call him,</i> Emmanuel.<sup>①</sup>  (<i>Emmanuel</i> means “God with us.”)<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:290 7:14'>Isa 7:14  
**1:24** When Joseph woke up, he did just as an angel from God commanded and took Mary as his wife.  
**1:25** But he didn’t have sexual relations with her until she gave birth to a son. Joseph called him Jesus.  

## 2장

**2:1** After Jesus was born in Bethlehem in the territory of Judea during the rule of King Herod, magi came from the east to Jerusalem.  
**2:2** They asked, “Where is the newborn king of the Jews? We’ve seen his star in the east, and we’ve come to honor him.”  
**2:3** When King Herod heard this, he was troubled, and everyone in Jerusalem was troubled with him.  
**2:4** He gathered all the chief priests and the legal experts and asked them where the Christ was to be born.  
**2:5** They said, “In Bethlehem of Judea, for this is what the prophet wrote:  
**2:6** <i>You, Bethlehem, land of Judah,</i><br><i>by no means are you least among the rulers of Judah,</i><br><i>because from you will come one who governs,</i><br><i>who will shepherd my people Israel.</i>”<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:400 5:2'>Mic 5:2; <a name='B:100 5:2'>2 Sam 5:2  
**2:7** Then Herod secretly called for the magi and found out from them the time when the star had first appeared.  
**2:8** He sent them to Bethlehem, saying, “Go and search carefully for the child. When you’ve found him, report to me so that I too may go and honor him.”  
**2:9** When they heard the king, they went; and look, the star they had seen in the east went ahead of them until it stood over the place where the child was.  
**2:10** When they saw the star, they were filled with joy.  
**2:11** They entered the house and saw the child with Mary his mother. Falling to their knees, they honored him. Then they opened their treasure chests and presented him with gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh.  
**2:12** Because they were warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they went back to their own country by another route.  
**2:13** When the magi had departed, an angel from the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream and said, “Get up. Take the child and his mother and escape to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you, for Herod will soon search for the child in order to kill him.”  
**2:14** Joseph got up and, during the night, took the child and his mother to Egypt.  
**2:15** He stayed there until Herod died. This fulfilled what the Lord had spoken through the prophet: <i>I have called my son out of Egypt</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:350 11:1'>Hos 11:1  
**2:16** When Herod knew the magi had fooled him, he grew very angry. He sent soldiers to kill all the children in Bethlehem and in all the surrounding territory who were two years old and younger, according to the time that he had learned from the magi.  
**2:17** This fulfilled the word spoken through Jeremiah the prophet:  
**2:18** <i>A voice was heard in Ramah,</i><br><i>weeping and much grieving.</i><br><i>Rachel weeping for her children,</i><br><i>and she did not want to be comforted,</i><br><i>because they were no more.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:300 31:15'>Jer 31:15  
**2:19** After King Herod died, an angel from the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt.  
**2:20** “Get up,” the angel said, “and take the child and his mother and go to the land of Israel. Those who were trying to kill the child are dead.”  
**2:21** Joseph got up, took the child and his mother, and went to the land of Israel.  
**2:22** But when he heard that Archelaus ruled over Judea in place of his father Herod, Joseph was afraid to go there. Having been warned in a dream, he went to the area of Galilee.  
**2:23** He settled in a city called Nazareth so that what was spoken through the prophets might be fulfilled: He will be called a Nazarene.  

## 3장

**3:1** In those days John the Baptist appeared in the desert of Judea announcing,  
**3:2** “Change your hearts and lives! Here comes the kingdom of heaven!”  
**3:3** He was the one of whom Isaiah the prophet spoke when he said:<br><i>The voice of one shouting in the wilderness,</i><br>“<i>Prepare the way for the Lord;</i><br><i>make his paths straight.</i>”<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:290 40:3'>Isa 40:3  
**3:4** John wore clothes made of camel’s hair, with a leather belt around his waist. He ate locusts and wild honey.  
**3:5** People from Jerusalem, throughout Judea, and all around the Jordan River came to him.  
**3:6** As they confessed their sins, he baptized them in the Jordan River.  
**3:7** Many Pharisees and Sadducees came to be baptized by John. He said to them, “You children of snakes! Who warned you to escape from the angry judgment that is coming soon?  
**3:8** Produce fruit that shows you have changed your hearts and lives.  
**3:9** And don’t even think about saying to yourselves, Abraham is our father. I tell you that God is able to raise up Abraham’s children from these stones.  
**3:10** The ax is already at the root of the trees. Therefore, every tree that doesn’t produce good fruit will be chopped down and tossed into the fire.  
**3:11** I baptize with water those of you who have changed your hearts and lives. The one who is coming after me is stronger than I am. I’m not worthy to carry his sandals. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire.  
**3:12** The shovel he uses to sift the wheat from the husks is in his hands. He will clean out his threshing area and bring the wheat into his barn. But he will burn the husks with a fire that can’t be put out.”  
**3:13** At that time Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan River so that John would baptize him.  
**3:14** John tried to stop him and said, “I need to be baptized by you, yet you come to me?”  
**3:15** Jesus answered, “Allow me to be baptized now. This is necessary to fulfill all righteousness.” So John agreed to baptize Jesus.  
**3:16** When Jesus was baptized, he immediately came up out of the water. Heaven was opened to him, and he saw the Spirit of God coming down like a dove and resting on him.  
**3:17** A voice from heaven said, “This is my Son whom I dearly love; I find happiness in him.”  

## 4장

**4:1** Then the Spirit led Jesus up into the wilderness so that the devil might tempt him.  
**4:2** After Jesus had fasted for forty days and forty nights, he was starving.  
**4:3** The tempter came to him and said, “Since you are God’s Son, command these stones to become bread.”  
**4:4** Jesus replied, “It’s written, <i>People won’t live only by bread, but by every word spoken by God.”</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:50 8:3'>Deut 8:3  
**4:5** After that the devil brought him into the holy city and stood him at the highest point of the temple. He said to him,  
**4:6** “Since you are God’s Son, throw yourself down; for it is written, <i>I will command my angels concerning you, and they will take you up in their hands so that you won’t hit your foot on a stone.</i>”<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:230 91:11-12'>Ps 91:11–12  
**4:7** Jesus replied, “Again it’s written, <i>Don’t test the Lord your God.”</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:50 6:16'>Deut 6:16  
**4:8** Then the devil brought him to a very high mountain and showed him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory.  
**4:9** He said, “I’ll give you all these if you bow down and worship me.”  
**4:10** Jesus responded, “Go away, Satan, because it’s written, <i>You will worship the Lord your God and serve only him.”</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:50 6:13'>Deut 6:13  
**4:11** The devil left him, and angels came and took care of him.  
**4:12** Now when Jesus heard that John was arrested, he went to Galilee.  
**4:13** He left Nazareth and settled in Capernaum, which lies alongside the sea in the area of Zebulun and Naphtali.  
**4:14** This fulfilled what Isaiah the prophet said:  
**4:15** <i>Land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali,</i><br><i>alongside the sea, across the Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles,</i>  
**4:16** <i>the people who lived in the dark have seen a great light,</i><br><i>and a light has come upon those who lived in the region and in shadow of death.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:290 9:1-2'>Isa 9:1–2  
**4:17** From that time Jesus began to announce, “Change your hearts and lives! Here comes the kingdom of heaven!”  
**4:18** As Jesus walked alongside the Galilee Sea, he saw two brothers, Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew, throwing fishing nets into the sea, because they were fishermen.  
**4:19** “Come, follow me,” he said, “and I’ll show you how to fish for people.”  
**4:20** Right away, they left their nets and followed him.  
**4:21** Continuing on, he saw another set of brothers, James the son of Zebedee and his brother John. They were in a boat with Zebedee their father repairing their nets. Jesus called them and  
**4:22** immediately they left the boat and their father and followed him.  
**4:23** Jesus traveled throughout Galilee, teaching in their synagogues. He announced the good news of the kingdom and healed every disease and sickness among the people.  
**4:24** News about him spread throughout Syria. People brought to him all those who had various kinds of diseases, those in pain, those possessed by demons, those with epilepsy, and those who were paralyzed, and he healed them.  
**4:25** Large crowds followed him from Galilee, the Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and from the areas beyond the Jordan River.  

## 5장

**5:1** Now when Jesus saw the crowds, he went up a mountain. He sat down and his disciples came to him.  
**5:2** He taught them, saying:  
**5:3** “Happy are people who are hopeless, because the kingdom of heaven is theirs.  
**5:4** “Happy are people who grieve, because they will be made glad.  
**5:5** “Happy are people who are humble, because they will inherit the earth.  
**5:6** “Happy are people who are hungry and thirsty for righteousness, because they will be fed until they are full.  
**5:7** “Happy are people who show mercy, because they will receive mercy.  
**5:8** “Happy are people who have pure hearts, because they will see God.  
**5:9** “Happy are people who make peace, because they will be called God’s children.  
**5:10** “Happy are people whose lives are harassed because they are righteous, because the kingdom of heaven is theirs.  
**5:11** “Happy are you when people insult you and harass you and speak all kinds of bad and false things about you, all because of me.  
**5:12** Be full of joy and be glad, because you have a great reward in heaven. In the same way, people harassed the prophets who came before you.  
**5:13** “You are the salt of the earth. But if salt loses its saltiness, how will it become salty again? It’s good for nothing except to be thrown away and trampled under people’s feet.  
**5:14** You are the light of the world. A city on top of a hill can’t be hidden.  
**5:15** Neither do people light a lamp and put it under a basket. Instead, they put it on top of a lampstand, and it shines on all who are in the house.  
**5:16** In the same way, let your light shine before people, so they can see the good things you do and praise your Father who is in heaven.  
**5:17** “Don’t even begin to think that I have come to do away with the Law and the Prophets. I haven’t come to do away with them but to fulfill them.  
**5:18** I say to you very seriously that as long as heaven and earth exist, neither the smallest letter nor even the smallest stroke of a pen will be erased from the Law until everything there becomes a reality.  
**5:19** Therefore, whoever ignores one of the least of these commands and teaches others to do the same will be called the lowest in the kingdom of heaven. But whoever keeps these commands and teaches people to keep them will be called great in the kingdom of heaven.  
**5:20** I say to you that unless your righteousness is greater than the righteousness of the legal experts and the Pharisees, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven.  
**5:21** “You have heard that it was said to those who lived long ago, <i>Don’t commit murder,</i><sup>①</sup> and all who commit murder will be in danger of judgment.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:20 20:13'>Exod 20:13  
**5:22** But I say to you that everyone who is angry with their brother or sister will be in danger of judgment. If they say to their brother or sister, ‘You idiot,’ they will be in danger of being condemned by the governing council. And if they say, ‘You fool,’ they will be in danger of fiery hell.  
**5:23** Therefore, if you bring your gift to the altar and there remember that your brother or sister has something against you,  
**5:24** leave your gift at the altar and go. First make things right with your brother or sister and then come back and offer your gift.  
**5:25** Be sure to make friends quickly with your opponents while you are with them on the way to court. Otherwise, they will haul you before the judge, the judge will turn you over to the officer of the court, and you will be thrown into prison.  
**5:26** I say to you in all seriousness that you won’t get out of there until you’ve paid the very last penny.  
**5:27** “You have heard that it was said, <i>Don’t commit adultery.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:20 20:14'>Exod 20:14; <a name='B:50 5:18'>Deut 5:18  
**5:28** But I say to you that every man who looks at a woman lustfully has already committed adultery in his heart.  
**5:29** And if your right eye causes you to fall into sin, tear it out and throw it away. It’s better that you lose a part of your body than that your whole body be thrown into hell.  
**5:30** And if your right hand causes you to fall into sin, chop it off and throw it away. It’s better that you lose a part of your body than that your whole body go into hell.  
**5:31** “It was said, ‘Whoever divorces his wife must <i>give her a divorce certificate.’</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:50 24:1'>Deut 24:1  
**5:32** But I say to you that whoever divorces his wife except for sexual unfaithfulness forces her to commit adultery. And whoever marries a divorced woman commits adultery.  
**5:33** “Again you have heard that it was said to those who lived long ago: <i>Don’t make a false solemn pledge, but you should follow through on what you have pledged to the Lord.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:30 19:12'>Lev 19:12; <a name='B:40 30:2'>Num 30:2; <a name='B:50 23:21'>Deut 23:21  
**5:34** But I say to you that you must not pledge at all. You must not pledge by heaven, because it’s God’s throne.  
**5:35** You must not pledge by the earth, because it’s God’s footstool. You must not pledge by Jerusalem, because it’s the city of the great king.  
**5:36** And you must not pledge by your head, because you can’t turn one hair white or black.  
**5:37** Let your <i>yes</i> mean yes, and your <i>no</i> mean no. Anything more than this comes from the evil one.  
**5:38** “You have heard that it was said, <i>An eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth</i>.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:20 21:24'>Exod 21:24; <a name='B:30 24:20'>Lev 24:20; <a name='B:50 19:21'>Deut 19:21  
**5:39** But I say to you that you must not oppose those who want to hurt you. If people slap you on your right cheek, you must turn the left cheek to them as well.  
**5:40** When they wish to haul you to court and take your shirt, let them have your coat too.  
**5:41** When they force you to go one mile, go with them two.  
**5:42** Give to those who ask, and don’t refuse those who wish to borrow from you.  
**5:43** “You have heard that it was said, <i>You must love your neighbor</i><sup>①</sup> and hate your enemy.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:30 19:18'>Lev 19:18  
**5:44** But I say to you, love your enemies and pray for those who harass you  
**5:45** so that you will be acting as children of your Father who is in heaven. He makes the sun rise on both the evil and the good and sends rain on both the righteous and the unrighteous.  
**5:46** If you love only those who love you, what reward do you have? Don’t even the tax collectors do the same?  
**5:47** And if you greet only your brothers and sisters, what more are you doing? Don’t even the Gentiles do the same?  
**5:48** Therefore, just as your heavenly Father is complete in showing love to everyone, so also you must be complete.  

## 6장

**6:1** “Be careful that you don’t practice your religion in front of people to draw their attention. If you do, you will have no reward from your Father who is in heaven.  
**6:2** “Whenever you give to the poor, don’t blow your trumpet as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the streets so that they may get praise from people. I assure you, that’s the only reward they’ll get.  
**6:3** But when you give to the poor, don’t let your left hand know what your right hand is doing  
**6:4** so that you may give to the poor in secret. Your Father who sees what you do in secret will reward you.  
**6:5** “When you pray, don’t be like hypocrites. They love to pray standing in the synagogues and on the street corners so that people will see them. I assure you, that’s the only reward they’ll get.  
**6:6** But when you pray, go to your room, shut the door, and pray to your Father who is present in that secret place. Your Father who sees what you do in secret will reward you.  
**6:7** “When you pray, don’t pour out a flood of empty words, as the Gentiles do. They think that by saying many words they’ll be heard.  
**6:8** Don’t be like them, because your Father knows what you need before you ask.  
**6:9** Pray like this:<br>Our Father who is in heaven, uphold the holiness of your name.  
**6:10** Bring in your kingdom so that your will is done on earth as it’s done in heaven.  
**6:11** Give us the bread we need for today.  
**6:12** Forgive us for the ways we have wronged you, just as we also forgive those who have wronged us.  
**6:13** And don’t lead us into temptation, but rescue us from the evil one.  
**6:14** “If you forgive others their sins, your heavenly Father will also forgive you.  
**6:15** But if you don’t forgive others, neither will your Father forgive your sins.  
**6:16** “And when you fast, don’t put on a sad face like the hypocrites. They distort their faces so people will know they are fasting. I assure you that they have their reward.  
**6:17** When you fast, brush your hair and wash your face.  
**6:18** Then you won’t look like you are fasting to people, but only to your Father who is present in that secret place. Your Father who sees in secret will reward you.  
**6:19** “Stop collecting treasures for your own benefit on earth, where moth and rust eat them and where thieves break in and steal them.  
**6:20** Instead, collect treasures for yourselves in heaven, where moth and rust don’t eat them and where thieves don’t break in and steal them.  
**6:21** Where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.  
**6:22** “The eye is the lamp of the body. Therefore, if your eye is healthy, your whole body will be full of light.  
**6:23** But if your eye is bad, your whole body will be full of darkness. If then the light in you is darkness, how terrible that darkness will be!  
**6:24** No one can serve two masters. Either you will hate the one and love the other, or you will be loyal to the one and have contempt for the other. You cannot serve God and wealth.  
**6:25** “Therefore, I say to you, don’t worry about your life, what you’ll eat or what you’ll drink, or about your body, what you’ll wear. Isn’t life more than food and the body more than clothes?  
**6:26** Look at the birds in the sky. They don’t sow seed or harvest grain or gather crops into barns. Yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Aren’t you worth much more than they are?  
**6:27** Who among you by worrying can add a single moment to your life?  
**6:28** And why do you worry about clothes? Notice how the lilies in the field grow. They don’t wear themselves out with work, and they don’t spin cloth.  
**6:29** But I say to you that even Solomon in all of his splendor wasn’t dressed like one of these.  
**6:30** If God dresses grass in the field so beautifully, even though it’s alive today and tomorrow it’s thrown into the furnace, won’t God do much more for you, you people of weak faith?  
**6:31** Therefore, don’t worry and say, ‘What are we going to eat?’ or ‘What are we going to drink?’ or ‘What are we going to wear?’  
**6:32** Gentiles long for all these things. Your heavenly Father knows that you need them.  
**6:33** Instead, desire first and foremost God’s kingdom and God’s righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well.  
**6:34** Therefore, stop worrying about tomorrow, because tomorrow will worry about itself. Each day has enough trouble of its own.  

## 7장

**7:1** “Don’t judge, so that you won’t be judged.  
**7:2** You’ll receive the same judgment you give. Whatever you deal out will be dealt out to you.  
**7:3** Why do you see the splinter that’s in your brother’s or sister’s eye, but don’t notice the log in your own eye?  
**7:4** How can you say to your brother or sister, ‘Let me take the splinter out of your eye,’ when there’s a log in your eye?  
**7:5** You deceive yourself! First take the log out of your eye, and then you’ll see clearly to take the splinter out of your brother’s or sister’s eye.  
**7:6** Don’t give holy things to dogs, and don’t throw your pearls in front of pigs. They will stomp on the pearls, then turn around and attack you.  
**7:7** “Ask, and you will receive. Search, and you will find. Knock, and the door will be opened to you.  
**7:8** For everyone who asks, receives. Whoever seeks, finds. And to everyone who knocks, the door is opened.  
**7:9** Who among you will give your children a stone when they ask for bread?  
**7:10** Or give them a snake when they ask for fish?  
**7:11** If you who are evil know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your heavenly Father give good things to those who ask him.  
**7:12** Therefore, you should treat people in the same way that you want people to treat you; this is the Law and the Prophets.  
**7:13** “Go in through the narrow gate. The gate that leads to destruction is broad and the road wide, so many people enter through it.  
**7:14** But the gate that leads to life is narrow and the road difficult, so few people find it.  
**7:15** “Watch out for false prophets. They come to you dressed like sheep, but inside they are vicious wolves.  
**7:16** You will know them by their fruit. Do people get bunches of grapes from thorny weeds, or do they get figs from thistles?  
**7:17** In the same way, every good tree produces good fruit, and every rotten tree produces bad fruit.  
**7:18** A good tree can’t produce bad fruit. And a rotten tree can’t produce good fruit.  
**7:19** Every tree that doesn’t produce good fruit is chopped down and thrown into the fire.  
**7:20** Therefore, you will know them by their fruit.  
**7:21** “Not everybody who says to me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ will get into the kingdom of heaven. Only those who do the will of my Father who is in heaven will enter.  
**7:22** On the Judgment Day, many people will say to me, ‘Lord, Lord, didn’t we prophesy in your name and expel demons in your name and do lots of miracles in your name?’  
**7:23** Then I’ll tell them, ‘I’ve never known you. Get away from me, you people who do wrong.’  
**7:24** “Everybody who hears these words of mine and puts them into practice is like a wise builder who built a house on bedrock.  
**7:25** The rain fell, the floods came, and the wind blew and beat against that house. It didn’t fall because it was firmly set on bedrock.  
**7:26** But everybody who hears these words of mine and doesn’t put them into practice will be like a fool who built a house on sand.  
**7:27** The rain fell, the floods came, and the wind blew and beat against that house. It fell and was completely destroyed.”  
**7:28** When Jesus finished these words, the crowds were amazed at his teaching  
**7:29** because he was teaching them like someone with authority and not like their legal experts.  

## 8장

**8:1** Now when Jesus had come down from the mountain, large crowds followed him.

**8:4** Jesus said to him, “Don’t say anything to anyone. Instead, go and show yourself to the priest and offer the gift that Moses commanded. This will be a testimony to them.”  
**8:5** When Jesus went to Capernaum, a centurion approached,  
**8:6** pleading with him, “Lord, my servant is flat on his back at home, paralyzed, and his suffering is awful.”  
**8:7** Jesus responded, “I’ll come and heal him.”  
**8:8** But the centurion replied, “Lord, I don’t deserve to have you come under my roof. Just say the word and my servant will be healed.  
**8:9** I’m a man under authority, with soldiers under me. I say to one, ‘Go,’ and he goes, and to another, ‘Come,’ and he comes. I say to my servant, ‘Do this,’ and the servant does it.”  
**8:10** When Jesus heard this, he was impressed and said to the people following him, “I say to you with all seriousness that even in Israel I haven’t found faith like this.  
**8:11** I say to you that there are many who will come from east and west and sit down to eat with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven.  
**8:12** But the children of the kingdom will be thrown outside into the darkness. People there will be weeping and grinding their teeth.”

**8:15** He touched her hand, and the fever left her. Then she got up and served them.  
**8:16** That evening people brought to Jesus many who were demon-possessed. He threw the spirits out with just a word. He healed everyone who was sick.  
**8:17** This happened so that what Isaiah the prophet said would be fulfilled: <i>He is the one who took our illnesses and carried away our diseases.</i><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:290 53:4'>Isa 53:4

**8:24** A huge storm arose on the lake so that waves were sloshing over the boat. But Jesus was asleep.  
**8:25** They came and woke him, saying, “Lord, rescue us! We’re going to drown!”  
**8:26** He said to them, “Why are you afraid, you people of weak faith?” Then he got up and gave orders to the winds and the lake, and there was a great calm.

**8:31** The demons pleaded with him, “If you throw us out, send us into the herd of pigs.”  
**8:32** Then he said to the demons, “Go away,” and they came out and went into the pigs. The whole herd rushed down the cliff into the lake and drowned.

**27:57** That evening a man named Joseph came. He was a rich man from Arimathea who had become a disciple of Jesus.  
**27:58** He came to Pilate and asked for Jesus’ body. Pilate gave him permission to take it.  
**27:59** Joseph took the body, wrapped it in a clean linen cloth,  
**27:60** and laid it in his own new tomb, which he had carved out of the rock. After he rolled a large stone at the door of the tomb, he went away.  
**27:61** Mary Magdalene and the other Mary were there, sitting in front of the tomb.  
**27:62** The next day, which was the day after Preparation Day, the chief priests and the Pharisees gathered before Pilate.  
**27:63** They said, “Sir, we remember that while that deceiver was still alive he said, ‘After three days I will arise.’  
**27:64** Therefore, order the grave to be sealed until the third day. Otherwise, his disciples may come and steal the body and tell the people, ‘He’s been raised from the dead.’ This last deception will be worse than the first.”  
**27:65** Pilate replied, “You have soldiers for guard duty. Go and make it as secure as you know how.”  
**27:66** Then they went and secured the tomb by sealing the stone and posting the guard.  

## 28장

**28:1** After the Sabbath, at dawn on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to look at the tomb.  
**28:2** Look, there was a great earthquake, for an angel from the Lord came down from heaven. Coming to the stone, he rolled it away and sat on it.  
**28:3** Now his face was like lightning and his clothes as white as snow.  
**28:4** The guards were so terrified of him that they shook with fear and became like dead men.  
**28:5** But the angel said to the women, “Don’t be afraid. I know that you are looking for Jesus who was crucified.  
**28:6** He isn’t here, because he’s been raised from the dead, just as he said. Come, see the place where they laid him.  
**28:7** Now hurry, go and tell his disciples, ‘He’s been raised from the dead. He’s going on ahead of you to Galilee. You will see him there.’ I’ve given the message to you.”  
**28:8** With great fear and excitement, they hurried away from the tomb and ran to tell his disciples.  
**28:9** But Jesus met them and greeted them. They came and grabbed his feet and worshipped him.  
**28:10** Then Jesus said to them, “Don’t be afraid. Go and tell my brothers that I am going into Galilee. They will see me there.”  
**28:11** Now as the women were on their way, some of the guards came into the city and told the chief priests everything that had happened.  
**28:12** They met with the elders and decided to give a large sum of money to the soldiers.  
**28:13** They told them, “Say that Jesus’ disciples came at night and stole his body while you were sleeping.  
**28:14** And if the governor hears about this, we will take care of it with him so you will have nothing to worry about.”  
**28:15** So the soldiers took the money and did as they were told. And this report has spread throughout all Judea to this very day.  
**28:16** Now the eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus told them to go.  
**28:17** When they saw him, they worshipped him, but some doubted.  
**28:18** Jesus came near and spoke to them, “I’ve received all authority in heaven and on earth.  
**28:19** Therefore, go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,  
**28:20** teaching them to obey everything that I’ve commanded you. Look, I myself will be with you every day until the end of this present age.”
