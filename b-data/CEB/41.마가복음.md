# 41.마가복음 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** The beginning of the good news about Jesus Christ, God’s Son,  
**1:2** happened just as it was written about in the prophecy of Isaiah:<br><i>Look, I am sending my messenger before you</i>.<br><i>He will prepare your way,</i>  
**1:3** <i>a voice shouting in the wilderness:</i><br>“<i>Prepare the way for the Lord;</i><br><i>make his paths straight</i>.”<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><a name='B:290 40:3'>Isa 40:3; <a name='B:460 3:1'>Mal 3:1; <a name='B:20 23:20'>Exod 23:20  
**1:4** John the Baptist was in the wilderness calling for people to be baptized to show that they were changing their hearts and lives and wanted God to forgive their sins.  
**1:5** Everyone in Judea and all the people of Jerusalem went out to the Jordan River and were being baptized by John as they confessed their sins.  
**1:6** John wore clothes made of camel’s hair, with a leather belt around his waist. He ate locusts and wild honey.  
**1:7** He announced, “One stronger than I am is coming after me. I’m not even worthy to bend over and loosen the strap of his sandals.  
**1:8** I baptize you with water, but he will baptize you with the Holy Spirit.”  
**1:9** About that time, Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee, and John baptized him in the Jordan River.  
**1:10** While he was coming up out of the water, Jesus saw heaven splitting open and the Spirit, like a dove, coming down on him.  
**1:11** And there was a voice from heaven: “You are my Son, whom I dearly love; in you I find happiness.”  
**1:12** At once the Spirit forced Jesus out into the wilderness.  
**1:13** He was in the wilderness for forty days, tempted by Satan. He was among the wild animals, and the angels took care of him.  
**1:14** After John was arrested, Jesus came into Galilee announcing God’s good news,  
**1:15** saying, “Now is the time! Here comes God’s kingdom! Change your hearts and lives, and trust this good news!”  
**1:16** As Jesus passed alongside the Galilee Sea, he saw two brothers, Simon and Andrew, throwing fishing nets into the sea, for they were fishermen.  
**1:17** “Come, follow me,” he said, “and I’ll show you how to fish for people.”  
**1:18** Right away, they left their nets and followed him.  
**1:19** After going a little farther, he saw James and John, Zebedee’s sons, in their boat repairing the fishing nets.  
**1:20** At that very moment he called them. They followed him, leaving their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired workers.  
**1:21** Jesus and his followers went into Capernaum. Immediately on the Sabbath Jesus entered the synagogue and started teaching.  
**1:22** The people were amazed by his teaching, for he was teaching them with authority, not like the legal experts.  
**1:23** Suddenly, there in the synagogue, a person with an evil spirit screamed,  
**1:24** “What have you to do with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are. You are the holy one from God.”  
**1:25** “Silence!” Jesus said, speaking harshly to the demon. “Come out of him!”  
**1:26** The unclean spirit shook him and screamed, then it came out.  
**1:27** Everyone was shaken and questioned among themselves, “What’s this? A new teaching with authority! He even commands unclean spirits and they obey him!”  
**1:28** Right away the news about him spread throughout the entire region of Galilee.  
**1:29** After leaving the synagogue, Jesus, James, and John went home with Simon and Andrew.  
**1:30** Simon’s mother-in-law was in bed, sick with a fever, and they told Jesus about her at once.  
**1:31** He went to her, took her by the hand, and raised her up. The fever left her, and she served them.  
**1:32** That evening, at sunset, people brought to Jesus those who were sick or demon-possessed.  
**1:33** The whole town gathered near the door.  
**1:34** He healed many who were sick with all kinds of diseases, and he threw out many demons. But he didn’t let the demons speak, because they recognized him.  
**1:35** Early in the morning, well before sunrise, Jesus rose and went to a deserted place where he could be alone in prayer.  
**1:36** Simon and those with him tracked him down.  
**1:37** When they found him, they told him, “Everyone’s looking for you!”  
**1:38** He replied, “Let’s head in the other direction, to the nearby villages, so that I can preach there too. That’s why I’ve come.”  
**1:39** He traveled throughout Galilee, preaching in their synagogues and throwing out demons.  
**1:40** A man with a skin disease approached Jesus, fell to his knees, and begged, “If you want, you can make me clean.”  
**1:41** Incensed,<sup>①</sup> Jesus reached out his hand, touched him, and said, “I do want to. Be clean.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Most critical editions of the Gk New Testament read <i>filled with compassion</i>.  
**1:42** Instantly, the skin disease left him, and he was clean.  
**1:43** Sternly, Jesus sent him away,  
**1:44** saying, “Don’t say anything to anyone. Instead, go and show yourself to the priest and offer the sacrifice for your cleansing that Moses commanded. This will be a testimony to them.”  
**1:45** Instead, he went out and started talking freely and spreading the news so that Jesus wasn’t able to enter a town openly. He remained outside in deserted places, but people came to him from everywhere.  

## 2장

**2:1** After a few days, Jesus went back to Capernaum, and people heard that he was at home.  
**2:2** So many gathered that there was no longer space, not even near the door. Jesus was speaking the word to them.  
**2:3** Some people arrived, and four of them were bringing to him a man who was paralyzed.  
**2:4** They couldn’t carry him through the crowd, so they tore off part of the roof above where Jesus was. When they had made an opening, they lowered the mat on which the paralyzed man was lying.  
**2:5** When Jesus saw their faith, he said to the paralytic, “Child, your sins are forgiven!”  
**2:6** Some legal experts were sitting there, muttering among themselves,  
**2:7** “Why does he speak this way? He’s insulting God. Only the one God can forgive sins.”  
**2:8** Jesus immediately recognized what they were discussing, and he said to them, “Why do you fill your minds with these questions?  
**2:9** Which is easier—to say to a paralyzed person, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or to say, ‘Get up, take up your bed, and walk’?  
**2:10** But so you will know that the Human One<sup>①</sup> has authority on the earth to forgive sins”—he said to the man who was paralyzed,<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Son of Man</i>  
**2:11** “Get up, take your mat, and go home.”  
**2:12** Jesus raised him up, and right away he picked up his mat and walked out in front of everybody. They were all amazed and praised God, saying, “We’ve never seen anything like this!”  
**2:13** Jesus went out beside the lake again. The whole crowd came to him, and he began to teach them.  
**2:14** As he continued along, he saw Levi, Alphaeus’ son, sitting at a kiosk for collecting taxes. Jesus said to him, “Follow me.” Levi got up and followed him.  
**2:15** Jesus sat down to eat at Levi’s house. Many tax collectors and sinners were eating with Jesus and his disciples. Indeed, many of them had become his followers.  
**2:16** When some of the legal experts from among the Pharisees saw that he was eating with sinners and tax collectors, they asked his disciples, “Why is he eating with sinners and tax collectors?”  
**2:17** When Jesus heard it, he said to them, “Healthy people don’t need a doctor, but sick people do. I didn’t come to call righteous people, but sinners.”  
**2:18** John’s disciples and the Pharisees had a habit of fasting. Some people asked Jesus, “Why do John’s disciples and the Pharisees’ disciples fast, but yours don’t?”  
**2:19** Jesus said, “The wedding guests can’t fast while the groom is with them, can they? As long as they have the groom with them, they can’t fast.  
**2:20** But the days will come when the groom will be taken away from them, and then they will fast.  
**2:21** “No one sews a piece of new, unshrunk cloth on old clothes; otherwise, the patch tears away from it, the new from the old, and makes a worse tear.  
**2:22** No one pours new wine into old leather wineskins; otherwise, the wine would burst the wineskins and the wine would be lost and the wineskins destroyed. But new wine is for new wineskins.”  
**2:23** Jesus went through the wheat fields on the Sabbath. As the disciples made their way, they were picking the heads of wheat.  
**2:24** The Pharisees said to Jesus, “Look! Why are they breaking the Sabbath law?”  
**2:25** He said to them, “Haven’t you ever read what David did when he was in need, when he and those with him were hungry?  
**2:26** During the time when Abiathar was high priest, David went into God’s house and ate the bread of the presence, which only the priests were allowed to eat. He also gave bread to those who were with him.”  
**2:27** Then he said, “The Sabbath was created for humans; humans weren’t created for the Sabbath.  
**2:28** This is why the Human One<sup>①</sup> is Lord even over the Sabbath.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Son of Man</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** Jesus returned to the synagogue. A man with a withered hand was there.  
**3:2** Wanting to bring charges against Jesus, they were watching Jesus closely to see if he would heal on the Sabbath.  
**3:3** He said to the man with the withered hand, “Step up where people can see you.”  
**3:4** Then he said to them, “Is it legal on the Sabbath to do good or to do evil, to save life or to kill?” But they said nothing.  
**3:5** Looking around at them with anger, deeply grieved at their unyielding hearts, he said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” So he did, and his hand was made healthy.  
**3:6** At that, the Pharisees got together with the supporters of Herod to plan how to destroy Jesus.  
**3:7** Jesus left with his disciples and went to the lake. A large crowd followed him because they had heard what he was doing. They were from Galilee,  
**3:8** Judea, Jerusalem, Idumea, beyond the Jordan, and the area surrounding Tyre and Sidon.  
**3:9** Jesus told his disciples to get a small boat ready for him so the crowd wouldn’t crush him.  
**3:10** He had healed so many people that everyone who was sick pushed forward so that they could touch him.  
**3:11** Whenever the evil spirits saw him, they fell down at his feet and shouted, “You are God’s Son!”  
**3:12** But he strictly ordered them not to reveal who he was.  
**3:13** Jesus went up on a mountain and called those he wanted, and they came to him.  
**3:14** He appointed twelve and called them apostles. He appointed them to be with him, to be sent out to preach,  
**3:15** and to have authority to throw out demons.  
**3:16** He appointed twelve: Peter, a name he gave Simon;  
**3:17** James and John, Zebedee’s sons, whom he nicknamed Boanerges, which means “sons of Thunder”;  
**3:18** and Andrew; Philip; Bartholomew; Matthew; Thomas; James, Alphaeus’ son; Thaddaeus; Simon the Cananaean;<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>zealot</i>  
**3:19** and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed Jesus.  
**3:20** Jesus entered a house. A crowd gathered again so that it was impossible for him and his followers even to eat.  
**3:21** When his family heard what was happening, they came to take control of him. They were saying, “He’s out of his mind!”  
**3:22** The legal experts came down from Jerusalem. Over and over they charged, “He’s possessed by Beelzebul. He throws out demons with the authority of the ruler of demons.”  
**3:23** When Jesus called them together he spoke to them in a parable: “How can Satan throw Satan out?  
**3:24** A kingdom involved in civil war will collapse.  
**3:25** And a house torn apart by divisions will collapse.  
**3:26** If Satan rebels against himself and is divided, then he can’t endure. He’s done for.  
**3:27** No one gets into the house of a strong person and steals anything without first tying up the strong person. Only then can the house be burglarized.  
**3:28** I assure you that human beings will be forgiven for everything, for all sins and insults of every kind.  
**3:29** But whoever insults the Holy Spirit will never be forgiven. That person is guilty of a sin with consequences that last forever.”  
**3:30** He said this because the legal experts were saying, “He’s possessed by an evil spirit.”  
**3:31** His mother and brothers arrived. They stood outside and sent word to him, calling for him.  
**3:32** A crowd was seated around him, and those sent to him said, “Look, your mother, brothers, and sisters are outside looking for you.”  
**3:33** He replied, “Who is my mother? Who are my brothers?”  
**3:34** Looking around at those seated around him in a circle, he said, “Look, here are my mother and my brothers.  
**3:35** Whoever does God’s will is my brother, sister, and mother.”  

## 4장

**4:1** Jesus began to teach beside the lake again. Such a large crowd gathered that he climbed into a boat there on the lake. He sat in the boat while the whole crowd was nearby on the shore.  
**4:2** He said many things to them in parables. While teaching them, he said,  
**4:3** “Listen to this! A farmer went out to scatter seed.  
**4:4** As he was scattering seed, some fell on the path; and the birds came and ate it.  
**4:5** Other seed fell on rocky ground where the soil was shallow. They sprouted immediately because the soil wasn’t deep.  
**4:6** When the sun came up, it scorched the plants; and they dried up because they had no roots.  
**4:7** Other seed fell among thorny plants. The thorny plants grew and choked the seeds, and they produced nothing.  
**4:8** Other seed fell into good soil and bore fruit. Upon growing and increasing, the seed produced in one case a yield of thirty to one, in another case a yield of sixty to one, and in another case a yield of one hundred to one.”  
**4:9** He said, “Whoever has ears to listen should pay attention!”  
**4:10** When they were alone, the people around Jesus, along with the Twelve, asked him about the parables.  
**4:11** He said to them, “The secret of God’s kingdom has been given to you, but to those who are outside everything comes in parables.  
**4:12** This is so that they can look and see but have no insight, and they can hear but not understand. Otherwise, they might turn their lives around and be forgiven.  
**4:13** “Don’t you understand this parable? Then how will you understand all the parables?  
**4:14** The farmer scatters the word.  
**4:15** This is the meaning of the seed that fell on the path: When the word is scattered and people hear it, right away Satan comes and steals the word that was planted in them.  
**4:16** Here’s the meaning of the seed that fell on rocky ground: When people hear the word, they immediately receive it joyfully.  
**4:17** Because they have no roots, they last for only a little while. When they experience distress or abuse because of the word, they immediately fall away.  
**4:18** Others are like the seed scattered among the thorny plants. These are the ones who have heard the word;  
**4:19** but the worries of this life, the false appeal of wealth, and the desire for more things break in and choke the word, and it bears no fruit.  
**4:20** The seed scattered on good soil are those who hear the word and embrace it. They bear fruit, in one case a yield of thirty to one, in another case sixty to one, and in another case one hundred to one.”  
**4:21** Jesus said to them, “Does anyone bring in a lamp in order to put it under a basket or a bed? Shouldn’t it be placed on a lampstand?  
**4:22** Everything hidden will be revealed, and everything secret will come out into the open.  
**4:23** Whoever has ears to listen should pay attention!”  
**4:24** He said to them, “Listen carefully! God will evaluate you with the same standard you use to evaluate others. Indeed, you will receive even more.  
**4:25** Those who have will receive more, but as for those who don’t have, even what they don’t have will be taken away from them.”  
**4:26** Then Jesus said, “This is what God’s kingdom is like. It’s as though someone scatters seed on the ground,  
**4:27** then sleeps and wakes night and day. The seed sprouts and grows, but the farmer doesn’t know how.  
**4:28** The earth produces crops all by itself, first the stalk, then the head, then the full head of grain.  
**4:29** Whenever the crop is ready, the farmer goes out to cut the grain because it’s harvesttime.”  
**4:30** He continued, “What’s a good image for God’s kingdom? What parable can I use to explain it?  
**4:31** Consider a mustard seed. When scattered on the ground, it’s the smallest of all the seeds on the earth;  
**4:32** but when it’s planted, it grows and becomes the largest of all vegetable plants. It produces such large branches that the birds in the sky are able to nest in its shade.”  
**4:33** With many such parables he continued to give them the word, as much as they were able to hear.  
**4:34** He spoke to them only in parables, then explained everything to his disciples when he was alone with them.  
**4:35** Later that day, when evening came, Jesus said to them, “Let’s cross over to the other side of the lake.”  
**4:36** They left the crowd and took him in the boat just as he was. Other boats followed along.  
**4:37** Gale-force winds arose, and waves crashed against the boat so that the boat was swamped.  
**4:38** But Jesus was in the rear of the boat, sleeping on a pillow. They woke him up and said, “Teacher, don’t you care that we’re drowning?”  
**4:39** He got up and gave orders to the wind, and he said to the lake, “Silence! Be still!” The wind settled down and there was a great calm.  
**4:40** Jesus asked them, “Why are you frightened? Don’t you have faith yet?”  
**4:41** Overcome with awe, they said to each other, “Who then is this? Even the wind and the sea obey him!”  

## 5장

**5:1** Jesus and his disciples came to the other side of the lake, to the region of the Gerasenes.  
**5:2** As soon as Jesus got out of the boat, a man possessed by an evil spirit came out of the tombs.  
**5:3** This man lived among the tombs, and no one was ever strong enough to restrain him, even with a chain.  
**5:4** He had been secured many times with leg irons and chains, but he broke the chains and smashed the leg irons. No one was tough enough to control him.  
**5:5** Night and day in the tombs and the hills, he would howl and cut himself with stones.  
**5:6** When he saw Jesus from far away, he ran and knelt before him,  
**5:7** shouting, “What have you to do with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? Swear to God that you won’t torture me!”  
**5:8** He said this because Jesus had already commanded him, “Unclean spirit, come out of the man!”  
**5:9** Jesus asked him, “What is your name?” He responded, “Legion is my name, because we are many.”  
**5:10** They pleaded with Jesus not to send them out of that region.  
**5:11** A large herd of pigs was feeding on the hillside.  
**5:12** “Send us into the pigs!” they begged. “Let us go into the pigs!”  
**5:13** Jesus gave them permission, so the unclean spirits left the man and went into the pigs. Then the herd of about two thousand pigs rushed down the cliff into the lake and drowned.  
**5:14** Those who tended the pigs ran away and told the story in the city and in the countryside. People came to see what had happened.  
**5:15** They came to Jesus and saw the man who used to be demon-possessed. They saw the very man who had been filled with many demons sitting there fully dressed and completely sane, and they were filled with awe.  
**5:16** Those who had actually seen what had happened to the demon-possessed man told the others about the pigs.  
**5:17** Then they pleaded with Jesus to leave their region.  
**5:18** While he was climbing into the boat, the one who had been demon-possessed pleaded with Jesus to let him come along as one of his disciples.  
**5:19** But Jesus wouldn’t allow it. “Go home to your own people,” Jesus said, “and tell them what the Lord has done for you and how he has shown you mercy.”  
**5:20** The man went away and began to proclaim in the Ten Cities all that Jesus had done for him, and everyone was amazed.  
**5:21** Jesus crossed the lake again, and on the other side a large crowd gathered around him on the shore.  
**5:22** Jairus, one of the synagogue leaders, came forward. When he saw Jesus, he fell at his feet  
**5:23** and pleaded with him, “My daughter is about to die. Please, come and place your hands on her so that she can be healed and live.”  
**5:24** So Jesus went with him. A swarm of people were following Jesus, crowding in on him.  
**5:25** A woman was there who had been bleeding for twelve years.  
**5:26** She had suffered a lot under the care of many doctors, and had spent everything she had without getting any better. In fact, she had gotten worse.  
**5:27** Because she had heard about Jesus, she came up behind him in the crowd and touched his clothes.  
**5:28** She was thinking, If I can just touch his clothes, I’ll be healed.  
**5:29** Her bleeding stopped immediately, and she sensed in her body that her illness had been healed.  
**5:30** At that very moment, Jesus recognized that power had gone out from him. He turned around in the crowd and said, “Who touched my clothes?”  
**5:31** His disciples said to him, “Don’t you see the crowd pressing against you? Yet you ask, ‘Who touched me?’”  
**5:32** But Jesus looked around carefully to see who had done it.  
**5:33** The woman, full of fear and trembling, came forward. Knowing what had happened to her, she fell down in front of Jesus and told him the whole truth.  
**5:34** He responded, “Daughter, your faith has healed you; go in peace, healed from your disease.”  
**5:35** While Jesus was still speaking with her, messengers came from the synagogue leader’s house, saying to Jairus, “Your daughter has died. Why bother the teacher any longer?”  
**5:36** But Jesus overheard their report and said to the synagogue leader, “Don’t be afraid; just keep trusting.”  
**5:37** He didn’t allow anyone to follow him except Peter, James, and John, James’ brother.  
**5:38** They came to the synagogue leader’s house, and he saw a commotion, with people crying and wailing loudly.  
**5:39** He went in and said to them, “What’s all this commotion and crying about? The child isn’t dead. She’s only sleeping.”  
**5:40** They laughed at him, but he threw them all out. Then, taking the child’s parents and his disciples with him, he went to the room where the child was.  
**5:41** Taking her hand, he said to her, <i>“Talitha koum,”</i> which means, “Young woman, get up.”  
**5:42** Suddenly the young woman got up and began to walk around. She was 12 years old. They were shocked!  
**5:43** He gave them strict orders that no one should know what had happened. Then he told them to give her something to eat.  

## 6장

**6:1** Jesus left that place and came to his hometown. His disciples followed him.  
**6:2** On the Sabbath, he began to teach in the synagogue. Many who heard him were surprised. “Where did this man get all this? What’s this wisdom he’s been given? What about the powerful acts accomplished through him?  
**6:3** Isn’t this the carpenter? Isn’t he Mary’s son and the brother of James, Joses, Judas, and Simon? Aren’t his sisters here with us?” They were repulsed by him and fell into sin.  
**6:4** Jesus said to them, “Prophets are honored everywhere except in their own hometowns, among their relatives, and in their own households.”  
**6:5** He was unable to do any miracles there, except that he placed his hands on a few sick people and healed them.  
**6:6** He was appalled by their disbelief. <i>Sending out the disciples</i> </pb> Then Jesus traveled through the surrounding villages teaching.  
**6:7** He called for the Twelve and sent them out in pairs. He gave them authority over unclean spirits.  
**6:8** He instructed them to take nothing for the journey except a walking stick—no bread, no bags, and no money in their belts.  
**6:9** He told them to wear sandals but not to put on two shirts.  
**6:10** He said, “Whatever house you enter, remain there until you leave that place.  
**6:11** If a place doesn’t welcome you or listen to you, as you leave, shake the dust off your feet as a witness against them.”  
**6:12** So they went out and proclaimed that people should change their hearts and lives.  
**6:13** They cast out many demons, and they anointed many sick people with olive oil and healed them.  
**6:14** Herod the king heard about these things, because the name of Jesus had become well-known. Some were saying, “John the Baptist has been raised from the dead, and this is why miraculous powers are at work through him.”  
**6:15** Others were saying, “He is Elijah.” Still others were saying, “He is a prophet like one of the ancient prophets.”  
**6:16** But when Herod heard these rumors, he said, “John, whom I beheaded, has been raised to life.”  
**6:17** He said this because Herod himself had arranged to have John arrested and put in prison because of Herodias, the wife of Herod’s brother Philip. Herod had married her,  
**6:18** but John told Herod, “It’s against the law for you to marry your brother’s wife!”  
**6:19** So Herodias had it in for John. She wanted to kill him, but she couldn’t.  
**6:20** This was because Herod respected John. He regarded him as a righteous and holy person, so he protected him. John’s words greatly confused Herod, yet he enjoyed listening to him.  
**6:21** Finally, the time was right. It was on one of Herod’s birthdays, when he had prepared a feast for his high-ranking officials and military officers and Galilee’s leading residents.  
**6:22** Herod’s daughter Herodias<sup>①</sup> came in and danced, thrilling Herod and his dinner guests. The king said to the young woman, “Ask me whatever you wish, and I will give it to you.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the daughter of Herodias herself</i>; Gk uncertain  
**6:23** Then he swore to her, “Whatever you ask I will give to you, even as much as half of my kingdom.”  
**6:24** She left the banquet hall and said to her mother, “What should I ask for?” “John the Baptist’s head,” Herodias replied.  
**6:25** Hurrying back to the ruler, she made her request: “I want you to give me John the Baptist’s head on a plate, right this minute.”  
**6:26** Although the king was upset, because of his solemn pledge and his guests, he didn’t want to refuse her.  
**6:27** So he ordered a guard to bring John’s head. The guard went to the prison, cut off John’s head,  
**6:28** brought his head on a plate, and gave it to the young woman, and she gave it to her mother.  
**6:29** When John’s disciples heard what had happened, they came and took his dead body and laid it in a tomb.  
**6:30** The apostles returned to Jesus and told him everything they had done and taught.  
**6:31** Many people were coming and going, so there was no time to eat. He said to the apostles, “Come by yourselves to a secluded place and rest for a while.”  
**6:32** They departed in a boat by themselves for a deserted place.  
**6:33** Many people saw them leaving and recognized them, so they ran ahead from all the cities and arrived before them.  
**6:34** When Jesus arrived and saw a large crowd, he had compassion on them because they were like sheep without a shepherd. Then he began to teach them many things.  
**6:35** Late in the day, his disciples came to him and said, “This is an isolated place, and it’s already late in the day.  
**6:36** Send them away so that they can go to the surrounding countryside and villages and buy something to eat for themselves.”  
**6:37** He replied, “You give them something to eat.” But they said to him, “Should we go off and buy bread worth almost eight months’ pay<sup>①</sup> and give it to them to eat?”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>two hundred denaria</i>; a denarion was a typical day’s wage.  
**6:38** He said to them, “How much bread do you have? Take a look.” After checking, they said, “Five loaves of bread and two fish.”  
**6:39** He directed the disciples to seat all the people in groups as though they were having a banquet on the green grass.  
**6:40** They sat down in groups of hundreds and fifties.

**15:41** When Jesus was in Galilee, these women had followed and supported him, along with many other women who had come to Jerusalem with him.  
**15:42** Since it was late in the afternoon on Preparation Day, just before the Sabbath,  
**15:43** Joseph from Arimathea dared to approach Pilate and ask for Jesus’ body. (Joseph was a prominent council member who also eagerly anticipated the coming of God’s kingdom.)  
**15:44** Pilate wondered if Jesus was already dead. He called the centurion and asked him whether Jesus had already died.  
**15:45** When he learned from the centurion that Jesus was dead, Pilate gave the dead body to Joseph.  
**15:46** He bought a linen cloth, took Jesus down from the cross, wrapped him in the cloth, and laid him in a tomb that had been carved out of rock. He rolled a stone against the entrance to the tomb.  
**15:47** Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses saw where he was buried.
