# 39.말라기 (CEB)



## 1장

**1:1** A pronouncement. The LORD’s word to Israel through Malachi.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>my messenger</i>  
**1:2** I have loved you, says the LORD;<br>but you say, “How have you loved us?”<br>Wasn’t Esau Jacob’s brother?<br>—says the LORD.<br>I loved Jacob,  
**1:3** but I rejected Esau.<br>I turned Esau’s mountains into desolation,<br>his inheritance into a wilderness for jackals.  
**1:4** Edom may say, “We are beaten down, but we will rebuild the ruins”;<br>but the LORD of heavenly forces proclaims:<br>They may build, but I will tear them down.<br>They will call themselves a wicked territory,<br>the people against whom the LORD rages forever.  
**1:5** Your eyes will see it and you will say,<br>“May the LORD be great beyond the borders of Israel.”  
**1:6** A son honors a father,<br>and a servant honors his master.<br>But if I’m a father, where is my honor?<br>Or if I’m a master, where is my respect?<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces<br>to you priests who despise my name.<br>So you say, “How have we despised your name?”  
**1:7** By approaching my altar with polluted food.<br>But you say, “How have we polluted it<sup>①</sup> ?”<br>When you say, “The table of the LORD can be despised.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Gk; Heb <i>you</i>  
**1:8** If you bring a blind animal to sacrifice, isn’t that evil?<br>If you bring a lame or sick one, isn’t that evil?<br>Would you bring it to your governor?<br>Would he be pleased with it or accept you?<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.  
**1:9** So now ask God to be gracious to us.<br>After what you have done, will he accept you?<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.  
**1:10** Who among you will shut the doors of the temple<sup>①</sup><br>so that you don’t burn something on my altar in vain?<br>I take no delight in you,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.<br>I won’t accept a grain offering from your hand.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb lacks <i>of the temple.</i>  
**1:11** Nevertheless, from sunrise to sunset,<br>my name will be great among the nations.<br>Incense and a pure grain offering will be offered everywhere in my name,<br>because my name is great among the nations,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.  
**1:12** But you make my name impure when you say,<br>“The table of the LORD is polluted.<br>Its fruit, its food, is despised.”  
**1:13** But you say, “How tedious!”<br>and you groan about it,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.<br>You permit what is stolen, lame, or sick to be brought for a sacrifice,<sup>①</sup><br>and you bring the grain offering.<br>Should I accept such from your hands?<br>—says the LORD.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb lacks <i>for a sacrifice.</i>  
**1:14** I will curse the cheater who has a healthy<sup>①</sup> male in his flock,<br>but who promises and sacrifices to the LORD that which is corrupt.<br>I am truly a great king,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces,<br>and my name is feared among the nations.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb lacks <i>healthy.</i>  

## 2장

**2:1** But now, this command is for you, priests:  
**2:2** If you don’t listen,<br>or don’t intend to glorify my name,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces,<br>then I will send a curse among you.<br>I will curse your blessings,<br>and I mean really curse them,<br>because none of you intend to do it.  
**2:3** I am about to denounce your offspring;<br>I will scatter feces on your faces,<br>the feces of your festivals.<br>Then I will lift you up to me,<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain; MT <i>He will carry you to it.</i>  
**2:4** and you will know that I have sent this command to you<br>so that my covenant with Levi can continue to exist,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.  
**2:5** My covenant with him involved life and peace, which I gave him,<br>and also fear so that he honored me.<br>He was in awe of my name.  
**2:6** True Instruction was in his mouth;<br>injustice wasn’t found on his lips.<br>He walked with me in peace and did the right thing;<br>he made many turn from iniquity.  
**2:7** The lips of the priest should guard knowledge;<br>everyone<sup>①</sup> should seek Instruction from his mouth,<br>for he is the messenger from the LORD of heavenly forces.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>they</i>  
**2:8** But you have turned from the path.<br>You have caused many to stumble by your instruction.<br>You have corrupted the covenant of Levi,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.  
**2:9** Moreover, I have made you despised and humiliated in the view of all the people,<br>since none of you keep my ways<br>or show respect for Instruction.  
**2:10** Isn’t there one father for all of us,<br>one God who created us?<br>Why does everyone cheat each other to make the covenant of our ancestors impure?  
**2:11** Judah cheated—<br>a detestable thing was done in Israel and Jerusalem.<br>Judah made the LORD’s holy place impure, which God loved,<br>and married the daughter of a foreign god.  
**2:12** May the LORD eliminate anyone who does so from the tents of Jacob,<br>anyone awaking, testifying,<sup>①</sup><br>and making an offering to the LORD of heavenly forces.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**2:13** You should do this as well:<br>cover the altar of the LORD with tears, weeping, and groaning<br>because there is still no divine favor for your offering or favorable regard<br>for anything from your hand.  
**2:14** But you say, “Why?”<br>Because the LORD testifies about you<br>and the wife of your youth against whom you cheated.<br>She is your partner,<br>the wife of your covenant.  
**2:15** Didn’t he make her<sup>①</sup> the one<sup>②</sup><br>and the remnant of his spirit?<sup>③</sup> What is the one?<br>The one seeking godly offspring.<br>You should guard your own spirit.<br>Don’t cheat on the wife of your youth<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb lacks <i>her.</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb uncertain; <i>her</i> refers to the <i>wife</i> in <a name='B:460 2:14'>Mal 2:14. <sup>③</sup>Heb uncertain  
**2:16** because he hates divorce,<br>—says the LORD God of Israel,<br>and he also hates the one covering his garment with violence,<sup>①</sup><br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.<br>Guard your own life, and don’t cheat.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**2:17** You have made the LORD tired with your words.<br>You say, “How have we made him tired?”<br>When you say:<br>“Anyone doing evil is good in the LORD’s eyes,”<br>or “He delights in those doing evil,”<sup>①</sup><br>or “Where is the God of justice?”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>them</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** Look, I am sending my messenger who will clear the path before me;<br>suddenly the LORD whom you are seeking will come to his temple.<br>The messenger of the covenant in whom you take delight is coming,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.  
**3:2** Who can endure the day of his coming?<br>Who can withstand his appearance?<br>He is like the refiner’s fire or the cleaner’s soap.  
**3:3** He will sit as a refiner and a purifier of silver.<br>He will purify the Levites<br>and refine them like gold and silver.<br>They will belong to the LORD,<br>presenting a righteous offering.  
**3:4** The offering of Judah and Jerusalem will be pleasing to the LORD<br>as in ancient days and in former years.  
**3:5** I will draw near to you for judgment.<br>I will be quick to testify against the sorcerers,<br>the adulterers, those swearing falsely,<br>against those who cheat the day laborers out of their wages<br>as well as oppress the widow and the orphan,<br>and against those who brush aside the foreigner and do not revere me,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.  
**3:6** I am the LORD, and I do not change;<br>and you, children of Jacob, have not perished.  
**3:7** Ever since the time of your ancestors,<br>you have deviated from my laws<br>and have not kept them.<br>Return to me and I will return to you,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.<br>But you say,<br>“How should we return?”  
**3:8** Should a person deceive God?<br>Yet you deceive me.<br>But you say,<br>“How have we deceived you?”<br>With your tenth-part gifts and offerings.  
**3:9** You are being cursed with a curse,<br>and you, the entire nation, are robbing me.  
**3:10** Bring the whole tenth-part to the storage house so there might be food in my house.<br>Please test me in this,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.<br>See whether I do not open all the windows of the heavens for you<br>and empty out a blessing until there is enough.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Until what is required is lacking.</i>  
**3:11** I will threaten the one who wants to devour you<br>so that it doesn’t spoil the fruit of your fertile land,<br>and so that the vine doesn’t abort its fruit in your field,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.  
**3:12** All the nations will consider you fortunate,<br>for you will be a desirable land,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.  
**3:13** You have spoken harshly about me,<br>—says the LORD;<br>but you say,<br>“What have we spoken about you?”  
**3:14** You said,<br>“Serving God is useless.<br>What do we gain by keeping his obligation<br>or by walking around as mourners<br>before the LORD of heavenly forces?  
**3:15** So now we consider the arrogant fortunate.<br>Moreover, those doing evil are built up;<br>they test God and escape.”  
**3:16** Then those revering the LORD,<br>each and every one, spoke among themselves.<br>The LORD paid attention and listened to them.<br>Then a scroll of remembrance was written before the LORD<br>about those revering the LORD,<br>the ones meditating on his name.  
**3:17** On the day that I am preparing,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces,<br>they will be my special possession.<br>I will spare them just as parents spare a child who serves them.  
**3:18** You will again distinguish between the righteous and the wicked,<br>between those serving God and those not serving him.  

## 4장

**4:1** <sup>①</sup> Look, the day is coming,<br>burning like an oven.<br>All the arrogant ones and all those doing evil will become straw.<br>The coming day will burn them,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces,<br>leaving them neither root nor branch.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>3:19 in Heb  
**4:2** But the sun of righteousness will rise on those revering my name;<br>healing will be in its wings<br>so that you will go forth and jump about like calves in the stall.  
**4:3** You will crush the wicked;<br>they will be like dust beneath the soles of your feet<br>on the day that I am preparing,<br>—says the LORD of heavenly forces.  
**4:4** Remember the Instruction from Moses, my servant,<br>to whom I gave Instruction and rules for all Israel at Horeb.  
**4:5** Look, I am sending Elijah the prophet to you,<br>before the great and terrifying day of the LORD arrives.  
**4:6** Turn the hearts of the parents to the children<br>and the hearts of the children to their parents.<br>Otherwise, I will come and strike the land with a curse.
