## 1장

**1:1** In the eighth month in the second year of Darius, the LORD’s word came to Zechariah the prophet, Berechiah’s son and Iddo’s grandson:  
**1:2** The LORD was terribly angry with your ancestors.  
**1:3** But you must say to the people,<br>The LORD of heavenly forces proclaims: Return to me,<br>says the LORD of heavenly forces,<br>and I will return to you, says the LORD of heavenly forces.  
**1:4** Don’t be like your ancestors to whom the former prophets preached:<br>The LORD of heavenly forces proclaims: Turn from your evil ways and your evil deeds.<br>But they didn’t listen;<br>they didn’t draw near to me.  
**1:5** So where are your ancestors?<br>Do the prophets live forever?  
**1:6** In fact, didn’t my words and laws,<br>which I gave to my servants, the prophets,<br>pursue your ancestors?<br>And then the people changed their hearts, and they said,<br>The LORD of heavenly forces has treated us according to<br>what we have done,<sup>①</sup><br>exactly as he planned.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>our ways and our actions</i>  
**1:7** On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month (the month of Shebat<sup>①</sup> ) in the second year of Darius, the LORD’s word came to Zechariah the prophet, Berechiah’s son and Iddo’s grandson:<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>January–February  
**1:8** Tonight I looked and saw a man riding on a red horse,<br>which was standing among the myrtle trees in the valley;<br>and behind him were red, sorrel, and white horses.  
**1:9** I said, “What are these, sir?”<br>The messenger speaking with me said,<br>“I will show you what they are.”  
**1:10** The man standing among the myrtles responded,<br>“These are the ones the LORD sent to patrol the earth.”  
**1:11** Then they responded to the LORD’s messenger,<br>who was standing among the myrtles,<br>“We have patrolled the earth. The whole earth is peaceful and quiet.”  
**1:12** Then the LORD’s messenger, who was speaking with me, said:<br>“LORD of heavenly forces,<br>how long will you withhold compassion from Jerusalem and the cities of Judah,<br>with whom you have been angry these seventy years?”  
**1:13** The LORD responded to the messenger who was speaking with me<br>with kind and compassionate words.  
**1:14** The messenger speaking with me called out,<br>“This is what the LORD of heavenly forces says:<br>I care passionately about Jerusalem and Zion.  
**1:15** And I am exceedingly angry with those carefree nations.<br>Though I was somewhat angry, they added to the violence.”  
**1:16** Therefore, this is what the LORD says:<br>I have returned to Jerusalem with compassion.<br>My house will be built in it, says the LORD of heavenly forces.<br>Let a measuring line be stretched over Jerusalem.  
**1:17** “Call out again,<br>The LORD of heavenly forces proclaims:<br>My cities will again overflow with prosperity.<br>The LORD will again show compassion to Zion<br>and will again choose Jerusalem.”

**1:21** I said,<br>“What are they coming to do?”<br>And he said,<br>“These are the horns that scattered Judah so that no one could raise his head.<br>The metalworkers have come to terrify them and to destroy the horns of the nations, those who were attacking the land of Judah with<sup>①</sup> their horns to scatter it.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>those lifting their horns toward Judah</i>
