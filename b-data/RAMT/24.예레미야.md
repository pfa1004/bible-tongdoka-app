# 24.예레미야 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** The words of Jeremiah son of Hilkiah, of the priests who were in Anathoth in the region of Benjamin,  
**1:2** to whom the word of the LORD came in the days of Josiah son of Amon king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.  
**1:3** And it continued in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah until the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah king of Judah, until Jerusalem went into exile in the fifth month.  
**1:4** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying:  
**1:5** Before I fashioned you in the belly I knew you,<br>and before you came out of the womb, I consecrated you.<br>A prophet to the nations I made you.  
**1:6** And I said, “Alas, O Master, LORD,<br>for, look, I know not how to speak,<br>for I am but a lad.”  
**1:7** And the LORD said to me,<br>Do not say, “I am but a lad,”<br>for wherever I send you, you shall go,<br>and whatever I charge you, you shall speak.  
**1:8** Do not fear them,<br>for I am with you to save you,<br>said the LORD.  
**1:9** And the LORD reached out His hand and touched my mouth, and the LORD said to me, “Look, I have put My words in your mouth.  
**1:10** See, I have appointed you this day over nations and over kingdoms to uproot and to smash and to destroy and to lay waste, to build and to plant.”  
**1:11** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying: “What do you see, Jeremiah?” And I said, “An almond-tree wand do I see.”  
**1:12** And the LORD said to me, “You have seen well, for I am vigilant with My word to do it.”  
**1:13** And the word of the LORD came to me a second time, saying: “What do you see?” And I said, “A seething pot I see, and it is turned to the north.”  
**1:14** And the LORD said to me, “From the north shall the evil be broached on all the dwellers of the land.  
**1:15** For I am about to call forth all the clans of the kingdoms of the north,” said the LORD, “and they shall come and each set his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem and against its walls all round and against all the towns of Judah.  
**1:16** And I will speak out My judgments against them, for all their evil in that they forsook Me and burned incense to other gods and bowed down to the work of their hands.  
**1:17** As for you, you shall gird your loins and rise and speak to them all that I charge you. Do not be broken-spirited before them, lest I break you before them.  
**1:18** As for Me, look, I have made you today a fortress town and an iron pillar and walls of bronze against all the land, against the kings of Judah and its nobles, against its priests and the people of the land.  
**1:19** And they shall battle against you but shall not prevail over you, for I am with you,” said the LORD, “to save you.”  

## 2장

**2:1** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,  
**2:2** “Go and call out in the hearing of Jerusalem, saying, Thus said the LORD:<br>I recalled for you the kindness of your youth,<br>your bridal love,<br>your coming after Me in the wilderness,<br>in an unsown land.  
**2:3** Israel is holy to the LORD,<br>the first fruits of His harvest.<br>All who eat it bear guilt,<br>evil shall come upon them,<br>said the LORD.  
**2:4** Listen to the word of the LORD, House of Jacob,<br>and all the clans of the House of Israel.  
**2:5** Thus said the LORD:<br>What wrong did your fathers find in Me<br>that they grew distant from Me<br>went after mere breath and turned into mere breath?  
**2:6** And they did not say, “Where is the LORD,<br>Who brought us up from the land of Egypt<br>and led us through the wilderness<br>in a land of desert and pits,<br>in a land of parched earth and death’s shadow,<br>in a land where no man had gone,<br>and where no human dwelled?”  
**2:7** And I brought you to a country of farmland<br>to eat its fruit and its bounty,<br>and you came and defiled My land<br>and My estate you made abhorrent.  
**2:8** The priests did not say, “Where is the LORD?”<br>And those skilled in the Teaching did not know Me,<br>and the shepherds rebelled against Me,<br>and the prophets prophesied for Baal,<br>and after what cannot avail they went.  
**2:9** Therefore will I yet dispute with you—<br>said the LORD—and with the sons of your sons will I dispute.  
**2:10** For pass through the isles of the Kittites and see,<br>send out to Kedar and look carefully<br>and see, has there been the like of this?  
**2:11** Has a nation given up its gods<br>though they are ungods?<br>But My people exchange its Glory<br>for what cannot avail.  
**2:12** Be appalled, O heavens, for this,<br>be shocked, altogether, desolate—<br>said the LORD.  
**2:13** For two evils My people has done:<br>Me they forsook, the source of living waters,<br>to hew for themselves cisterns, broken cisterns,<br>that cannot hold the water.  
**2:14** Is Israel a slave, is he home-born chattel?<br>Why has he become plunder?  
**2:15** Lions roar over him,<br>lift up their cry,<br>and they have made his land a desolation,<br>his towns are ravaged with no dweller there.  
**2:16** The men, too, of Noph and Tahpanes<br>shall smash your pate.  
**2:17** Is not this how you fare<br>for forsaking the LORD your God<br>when He led you on the way?  
**2:18** And now, why go in the way of Egypt<br>to drink the waters of the Nile,<br>and why go in the way of Assyria<br>to drink the Euphrates’ waters?  
**2:19** Let your evil chastise you<br>and your rebellion reprove you,<br>and mark and see that it is evil and bitter,<br>your forsaking the LORD your God.<br>The fear of Me is not upon you<br>said the Master, LORD of Armies.  
**2:20** For of old you broke your yoke,<br>you tore apart your bands,<br>and you said, “I will not serve.”<br>For on every high hill<br>and under every lush tree<br>you lean back, a whore.  
**2:21** And I, I planted you as a choice vine,<br>a wholly true seed,<br>and how have you turned against Me<br>into a wayward alien vine?  
**2:22** Though you scrub with natron<br>and use abundant lye,<br>your crime is stained before Me,<br>said the Master, the LORD.  
**2:23** How can you say, “I was not defiled,<br>after the Baalim I have not gone”?<br>See your way in the valley,<br>know what you have done.<br>A swift she-camel threading her way,  
**2:24** a wild ass at home in the wilderness,<br>sniffing wind in the lust of her appetite,<br>her desire cannot be turned back.<br>All who seek her will not tire,<br>in her season they will find her.  
**2:25** Keep your feet from walking bare<br>and your throat from going dry.<br>And you give up hope, saying,<br>“No, I love strangers,<br>and after them I will go.”  
**2:26** Like the shame of a thief when he is caught,<br>thus the house of Israel acted shamefully,<br>they, their kings, and their nobles,<br>and their priests, and their prophets.  
**2:27** They say to a tree, “You are my father,”<br>and to a stone, “You gave birth to me.”<br>For they have turned their backs to Me<br>and not their faces.<br>And when disaster befalls them, they say,<br>“Arise and rescue us.”  
**2:28** And where are your gods that you made for yourself?<br>Let them rise and rescue when disaster befalls you.<br>For as the number of your towns<br>were your gods, O Judah.  
**2:29** Why do you dispute with Me?<br>All of you rebelled against Me, said the LORD.  
**2:30** In vain did I strike your sons,<br>they did not accept reproval.<br>Your sword has consumed your prophets<br>like a ravaging lion.  
**2:31** O you generation, see the word of the LORD!<br>Was I a wilderness to Israel,<br>a land of deep darkness?<br>Why did My people say, “We have broken loose,<br>we will no longer come to you”?  
**2:32** Does a virgin forget her jewels,<br>a bride her knotted sash?<br>Yet My people has forgotten Me,<br>days without number.  
**2:33** How you make your way fair<br>to seek love!<br>Thus, even to wicked women<br>you have taught your ways.  
**2:34** In your skirts, as well, is found<br>the lifeblood of the innocent poor.<br>Not in a hideout did I find them,<br>but upon all of these.  
**2:35** And you said, “I am innocent.<br>Why, His wrath has turned back from me.”<br>I am about to exact judgment from you,<br>for your saying, “I did not offend.”  
**2:36** Why do you cheapen yourself so much<br>to change your way?<br>By Egypt, too, you shall be shamed<br>as you were shamed by Assyria.  
**2:37** For from this you shall come out<br>with your hands upon your head.<br>For the LORD spurns the ones you trust,<br>and you shall not succeed through them.  

## 3장

**3:1** [And the word of the LORD came to me], saying:<br>Look, should a man send away his wife,<br>and she go from him and become another man’s,<br>can he go back to her again?<br>Would not that land be wholly polluted?<br>And you, you have whored with many lovers,<br>and would you come back to Me? said the LORD.  
**3:2** Lift up your eyes to the bare heights and see.<br>Where have they not lain with you?<br>On the roads you sat waiting for them<br>like an Arab in the desert,<br>and you polluted the land<br>through your whoring and through your evil.  
**3:3** And the showers were held back,<br>and the latter rains did not come,<br>and a whore-woman’s brow you had,<br>you refused to be shamed.  
**3:4** Have you now not called Me, “My father,”<br>You are the guide of my youth.  
**3:5** Will He bear a grudge forever,<br>will He keep it for all time?<br>Look, you spoke and did evil things, and will you prevail?  
**3:6** And the LORD said to me in the days of King Josiah, “Have you seen what Rebel Israel has done? She goes on every high mountain and under every lush tree and plays the whore there.  
**3:7** And I thought, after she has done all those, she will come back to Me, but she did not come back, and her sister, Judah the Treacherous, saw.  
**3:8** And she saw that because Rebel Israel had committed adultery I sent her away and gave her a bill of divorce, yet Judah the Treacherous did not fear, and she, too, went and played the whore.  
**3:9** And it happened that from all her whoring the land was polluted, and she committed adultery with stone and with tree.  
**3:10** And yet, despite all this, Judah the Treacherous did not turn back to Me with a whole heart but falsely,” said the LORD.  
**3:11** And the LORD said to me, “Rebel Israel has shown herself more in the right than Judah the Treacherous.  
**3:12** Go and call out these words to the north and say, Turn back, Rebel Israel, said the LORD, and I will not set My face against you, for I am faithful, said the LORD. I will not bear a grudge forever.  
**3:13** But know your crime, for against the LORD your God you have rebelled, and you have scattered your ways among strangers under every lush tree, and My voice you have not heeded, said the LORD.  
**3:14** Turn back, rebellious sons, said the LORD, for I have claimed possession of you and have taken you, one from a town and two from a clan, and brought you to Zion.  
**3:15** And I have given you shepherds after My own heart, and they have shepherded you with knowledge and discernment.  
**3:16** And it shall happen when you multiply and are fruitful in those days, said the LORD, that they no longer shall say ‘the Ark of the LORD’s Covenant’ nor shall they bring it to mind nor shall they recall it nor seek it out, nor shall it again be made.  
**3:17** At that time they shall call Jerusalem ‘Throne of the LORD,’ and all the nations shall gather in it in the name of the LORD, in Jerusalem, and they shall not go after the willfulness of their evil heart.  
**3:18** In those days the house of Judah shall go with the house of Israel, and they shall come together from the land of the north to the land that I gave in estate to your fathers.”  
**3:19** As for Me, I said,<br>How shall I place you among children?<br>I gave you a land of delight,<br>an estate of the greatest splendor of nations,<br>and I said, You shall call Me “my Father,”<br>and you shall not turn back from Me.  
**3:20** Yet, as a woman betrays her companion,<br>you have betrayed Me, O house of Israel,<br>said the LORD.  
**3:21** A voice is heard on the bare heights,<br>the weeping supplications of Israel’s children.<br>For they have made their way crooked,<br>forgotten the LORD their God.  
**3:22** Turn back, rebellious children—<br>I will heal your rebellion.<br>“Here we are, we have come to you,<br>for You are the LORD and God.<br>Indeed, falsehood is from the hills,<br>the clamor of the mountains.<br>Indeed, in the LORD our God<br>is Israel’s rescue.  
**3:23** And the shameful thing consumed<br>the toil of our fathers from our youth,<br>their sheep and their cattle,<br>their sons and their daughters.  
**3:24** Let us lie down in our shame,<br>and our disgrace be our cover,<br>for the LORD our God we have offended,<br>we and our fathers from our youth<br>to this very day,<br>and we did not heed the voice of the LORD our God.”  

## 4장

**4:1** If you turn back, Israel, said the LORD,<br>to Me you shall turn back,<br>and if you remove your foul things<br>from before Me and do not waiver,  
**4:2** I vow, as the LORD lives, in truth,<br>in justice, and in righteousness,<br>that nations shall bless themselves through you,<br>and through you shall they be praised.  
**4:3** For thus said the LORD to the men of Judah and Jerusalem:<br>Till for yourselves tilled ground,<br>and do not sow among thorns.  
**4:4** Be circumcised to the LORD<br>and remove your hearts’ foreskins,<br>men of Judah and dwellers of Jerusalem,<br>lest My wrath come forth like fire<br>and burn with none to quench it<br>because of the evil of your acts.  
**4:5** Tell it in Judah,<br>and in Jerusalem make it heard and say,<br>blow the ram’s horn in the land,<br>call out with full voice and say:<br>Assemble and let us come<br>to the fortified towns.  
**4:6** Raise a banner toward Zion,<br>take refuge, do not stand,<br>for I am about to bring harm from the north<br>and a great disaster.  
**4:7** A lion has sprung from its thicket,<br>and the ravager of nations has journeyed,<br>he has come forth from his place<br>to make your land desolate.<br>Your towns shall be ruined with no dweller.  
**4:8** For this gird sackcloth,<br>keen and howl,<br>for the wrath of the LORD<br>has not turned back from him.  
**4:9** And it shall happen on that day, said the LORD,<br>the heart of the king shall fail<br>and the heart of the nobles,<br>and desolate the priests shall be,<br>and the prophets shall be dumbfounded.  
**4:10** And I said, “Alas, O Master, LORD!<br>Surely You have misled this people and Jerusalem,<br>saying, ‘You shall have peace,’<br>but the sword has touched the throat.”  
**4:11** And at that time it shall be said<br>to this people and to Jerusalem:<br>a parching wind from the bare heights in the desert<br>going through My People’s Daughter—<br>neither to winnow nor to sift.  
**4:12** A full wind from these comes against Me.<br>Now will I speak judgments against them.  
**4:13** Look, he comes up like the clouds<br>and like the whirlwind his chariots.<br>His horses are swifter than eagles—<br>woe to us, for we are destroyed!  
**4:14** Cleanse your heart of evil, Jerusalem,<br>so that you may be rescued.<br>How long will there lodge in your midst<br>your wicked devisings?  
**4:15** For a voice proclaims from Dan<br>and announces disaster from Mount Ephraim.  
**4:16** Make it known to the nations, look!<br>Announce it concerning Jerusalem.<br>Watchers are coming from a faraway land,<br>and they shall raise their voice against Judah’s towns.  
**4:17** Like guards of the field they are against her all round,<br>for she has rebelled against Me.  
**4:18** Your way and your deeds<br>have done these to you,<br>this evil of yours, which is bitter,<br>for it has touched your very heart.  
**4:19** My gut, my gut—I writhe,<br>the walls of my heart,<br>my heart moans in me,<br>I am not still,<br>for the ram’s horn’s sound I have heard,<br>the blare of war.  
**4:20** Disaster upon disaster is called forth,<br>for all the land is destroyed,<br>all at once my tents are destroyed,<br>in a moment, my tent curtains.  
**4:21** How long shall I see the banner,<br>hear the ram’s horn’s sound?  
**4:22** For My people are fools,<br>Me they did not know.<br>Ignorant children are they,<br>and they are not discerning,<br>they are wise to do evil,<br>but they know not how to do good.  
**4:23** I saw the earth, and, look, welter and waste,<br>the heavens, and their light was gone.  
**4:24** I saw the mountains and, look, they quaked,<br>and all the hills broke apart.  
**4:25** I saw, and look, there was no human there,<br>and all the fowl of the heavens had gone away.  
**4:26** I saw, and, look, the farmland was desert,<br>and all its towns were ruined<br>before the LORD and before His blazing wrath.  
**4:27** For thus said the LORD:<br>A desolation shall all the earth be<br>but I will not wreak utter destruction.  
**4:28** For this all the earth shall mourn,<br>and the heavens above shall darken,<br>for I have spoken, I have laid plans,<br>and I did not repent nor turn back from it.  
**4:29** From the sound of the horseman and archer<br>all the town flees.<br>They have entered the crannies,<br>gone up to the cliffs,<br>all the town is abandoned,<br>and no man dwells in it.  
**4:30** As for you, the destroyed one, what have you done,<br>that you dress up in scarlet,<br>that you put on bangles of gold,<br>that you set off your eyes in kohl?<br>For naught you make yourself lovely.<br>Your lovers despise you,<br>your life they seek.  
**4:31** For I have heard a sound like a woman in labor,<br>distress like one giving birth the first time,<br>the voice of Zion’s Daughter panting,<br>she stretches out her hands:<br>“Woe to me, for my being goes faint before the killers!”  

## 5장

**5:1** Roam through the streets of Jerusalem,<br>and see, pray, and mark,<br>and seek in her squares.<br>If you find a man,<br>if there be a man doing justice, seeking faithfulness,<br>I shall forgive her.  
**5:2** But if, “as the LORD lives,” they say,<br>they swear falsely.  
**5:3** O LORD, Your eyes look for faithfulness.<br>You struck them but they did not flinch,<br>You made an end of them, they refused to take reproof.<br>They made their faces harder than rock,<br>they refused to turn back.  
**5:4** As for me, I said, they are but poor people,<br>they are foolish,<br>for they know not the way of the LORD,<br>their God’s justice.  
**5:5** Let me go to the great ones,<br>and let me speak with them,<br>for they know the way of the LORD,<br>the justice of their God.<br>But they together broke the yoke,<br>they tore apart the bonds.  
**5:6** Therefore has the lion from the forest struck them,<br>the wolf of the steppes destroys them,<br>the leopard lies in wait at their towns,<br>all who come forth from there are ripped up.<br>For their crimes are many,<br>numerous their rebellions.  
**5:7** “Why, for this should I pardon you?<br>Your sons have forsaken Me<br>and sworn by ungods.<br>I sated them, yet they were adulterous,<br>and to the whore’s house they trooped.  
**5:8** They were horses in heat rising early,<br>each man for his fellow’s wife neighed.  
**5:9** For these shall I not exact judgment,” said the LORD,<br>“and against a nation such as this not wreak vengeance?  
**5:10** Go up against her vine rows and ruin them,<br>but an utter end do not bring about.<br>Strip off her trailing branches,<br>for they are not the LORD’s.  
**5:11** For the house of Israel and the house of Judah<br>have surely betrayed Me,” said the LORD.  
**5:12** “They have been false to the LORD<br>and said, ‘Not He.<br>And no harm will come upon us<br>and the sword and famine we shall not see.  
**5:13** And the prophets are but wind,<br>and there is no Word in them—<br>thus shall be done to them.’  
**5:14** Therefore thus said the LORD God of Armies:<br>Because you spoke this word,<br>I am about to put My words in your mouth like fire<br>and this people shall be wood it consumes.  
**5:15** I am about to bring against you a nation from afar,<br>O house of Israel,” said the LORD.<br>“It is a nation of unfailing strength,<br>a nation from of old,<br>a nation whose tongue you do not know,<br>nor understand what it speaks.  
**5:16** Its quiver—an open grave,<br>all of them are warriors,  
**5:17** It shall devour your harvest and your bread,<br>devour your sons and your daughters,<br>devour your sheep and your cattle,<br>devour your vines and your fig trees.<br>I shall slash with the sword your fortress towns<br>in which you trusted.”  
**5:18** “And even in those days,” said the LORD, “I will not make an end of you.  
**5:19** And it shall happen when they say, ‘For what has the LORD our God done all these things to us?’ You shall say to them, ‘Because you forsook Me and served alien gods in your own land, thus shall you serve strangers in a land that is not yours.’”  
**5:20** Tell this in the House of Jacob,<br>and let it be heard in Judah, saying:  
**5:21** Hear this, pray,<br>ignorant, mindless people.<br>Eyes they have but they do not see,<br>ears they have but they do not hear.  
**5:22** Is it Me you do not fear, said the LORD,<br>before Me you do not quake?<br>For I set sand a boundary to the sea,<br>an everlasting limit not to be crossed—<br>the waves tossed but could not prevail,<br>they surged but could not cross it.  
**5:23** And this people had a wayward rebellious heart,<br>they swerved away and went off.  
**5:24** And they did not say in their heart,<br>“Let us fear, pray, the LORD our God,<br>Who gives rain, early and late rain in its season,<br>the set weeks of harvest He keeps for us.”  
**5:25** Your crimes turned aside these things,<br>and your offenses withheld the bounty from you.  
**5:26** For among My people wicked men are found,<br>they watch as in a fowler’s blind,<br>they set out an ambush,<br>they capture men.  
**5:27** Like a cage full of fowl,<br>so their homes are filled with deceit,<br>therefore have they prospered, become rich.  
**5:28** They have fattened, have thickened,<br>even passed beyond words of evil.<br>They did not judge a just case—<br>the orphan’s case, that he should do well,<br>and the needy’s judgment they did not judge.  
**5:29** For these shall I not exact judgment, said the LORD,<br>against a nation such as this not wreak vengeance?  
**5:30** A frightful and fearsome thing<br>has come about in the land:  
**5:31** The prophets have prophesied falsely,<br>and the priests held sway alongside them,<br>and My people loved it so.<br>But what will you do for its end?  

## 6장

**6:1** Seek shelter, you Benjaminites, from within Jerusalem,<br>and in Tekoa sound the ram’s horn,<br>and on Beth Hakerem raise a signal fire,<br>for evil is in sight from the north<br>and a great disaster.  
**6:2** Lovely and delicate<br>did I imagine Zion’s Daughter.  
**6:3** Against her came shepherds with their flocks,<br>they pitched their tents all round,<br>each shepherded his charge.  
**6:4** “Ready battle against her,<br>arise and let us go up at noon.<br>Woe to us, for the day declines,<br>for the shadows of evening stretch out!  
**6:5** Arise and let us go up by night,<br>and let us lay waste to her citadels.”  
**6:6** For thus said the LORD of Armies:<br>Cut down the trees<br>and build a siege-ramp against Jerusalem.<br>She is the city singled out,<br>naught but oppression is in her midst.  
**6:7** As a well flows with water,<br>so she has flowed with her evil.<br>“Outrage” and “plunder” are heard within her<br>before Me perpetually sickness and plague.  
**6:8** Accept reproof, O Jerusalem,<br>lest I loathe you,<br>lest I make you a desolation,<br>an uninhabitable land.  
**6:9** Thus said the LORD of Armies:<br>They shall wholly glean as a vine<br>the remnant of Israel.<br>Bring back your hand like a picker of grapes<br>to the baskets.  
**6:10** To whom should I speak,<br>and bear witness that they might heed?<br>Look, their ear is uncircumcised,<br>and they are unable to listen.<br>Look, the word of the LORD has become for them<br>a disgrace in which they do not delight.  
**6:11** And with the wrath of the LORD I am filled,<br>I cannot hold it in.<br>Pour it on the babe in the street,<br>and on the gathering of young men together,<br>for both man and woman shall be caught,<br>the elder with the one full of years.  
**6:12** And their homes shall be turned over to others,<br>fields and wives together,<br>for I will stretch out My hand<br>against the land’s dwellers, said the LORD.  
**6:13** For from the least of them to their greatest<br>all of them chase gain,<br>and from prophet to priest<br>all of them work lies.  
**6:14** And they would heal My people’s wound easily saying,<br>“All is well, all is well,” but it is not well.  
**6:15** They acted shamefully, for they performed abominations.<br>They were not even ashamed,<br>they did not even know how to be disgraced.<br>Therefore shall they fall among those who fall.<br>When I exact judgment from them, they shall stumble—<br>said the LORD.  
**6:16** Thus said the LORD:<br>Stand by the ways and see,<br>ask for the paths of old,<br>what is the good way, and go on it,<br>and find rest for yourselves.<br>And they said: “We will not go.”  
**6:17** And I set up watchmen for you—<br>listen to the ram’s horn’s sound.<br>And they said: “We will not listen.”  
**6:18** Therefore, hear O nations,<br>and note the testimony against them.  
**6:19** Hear, O earth, I am about to bring evil against this people, the fruit of their devisings, for they did not listen to all My words, and My teaching they have spurned.  
**6:20** Why do I need frankincense that comes from Sheba and the goodly fragrant cane from a faraway land? Your burnt offerings are not acceptable, and your sacrifices do not please Me.  
**6:21** Therefore, thus said the LORD:<br>I am about to bring upon this people<br>stumbling blocks, and they shall stumble on them.<br>Fathers and sons together,<br>a neighbor and his fellow shall perish.
