# 25.예레미야애가 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** How she sits alone, ℵ<br>the city once great with people.<br>She has become like a widow.<br>Great among nations,<br>mistress among provinces,<br>reduced to forced labor.  
**1:2** She weeps on through the night, ב<br>and her tears are on her cheek.<br>She has no consoler<br>from all her lovers.<br>All her friends have betrayed her,<br>have become enemies to her.  
**1:3** Judah is exiled in affliction ג<br>and in hard labor.<br>She dwells among the nations,<br>she finds no rest.<br>All her pursuers overtake her<br>in straits.  
**1:4** The roads of Zion mourn ד<br>without festival pilgrims.<br>All her gates are desolate,<br>her priests are groaning,<br>her virgins are sorrow-stricken<br>and she—it is bitter for her.  
**1:5** Her foes are at the head, ה<br>her enemies are tranquil,<br>for the LORD has stricken her with sorrow<br>because of all her trespasses.<br>Her babes have fallen captive<br>before the foe.  
**1:6** And from Zion’s Daughter is departed ו<br>all her glory.<br>Her nobles have become like stags<br>that find no pasture,<br>and they go without strength<br>before the pursuer.  
**1:7** Jerusalem recalls ז<br>in the days of her affliction and her wandering<br>all her treasures<br>that were in days of yore,<br>as when her people fell to the foe’s hand<br>with none to help her.<br>The foes saw her and they laughed<br>over her disasters.  
**1:8** An offense did Jerusalem commit, ח<br>therefore she became despised.<br>All who honored her degrade her,<br>for they have seen her nakedness.<br>She on her part groans<br>and has fallen back.  
**1:9** Her uncleanness is in her skirts, ט<br>she has no mind of her future,<br>and she plunges wondrously,<br>there is none to console her.<br>See, O LORD, my affliction,<br>for the enemy is boasting.  
**1:10** The foe has laid his hand י<br>on all her treasures,<br>for she has seen nations<br>come into her sanctuary<br>of whom I charged they must not come<br>into assembly with you.  
**1:11** All her people groan כ<br>seeking bread.<br>They gave their treasures for food<br>to revive their failing lives.<br>See, O LORD, and look,<br>for she has become cheapened.  
**1:12** Not upon you, all passersby! ל<br>Look and see,<br>is there pain like my pain<br>that He inflicted upon me,<br>that the LORD struck me with sorrow<br>on the day His wrath flared?  
**1:13** From on high He sent out fire, מ<br>into my bones He brought it down,<br>Spread a net for my feet,<br>made me fall back.  
**1:14** The yoke of my trespasses is tight, נ<br>twisted by His hand,<br>climbing round my neck—<br>He made my strength stumble.<br>The LORD has given me into the hand<br>of one I cannot stand against.  
**1:15** The Master has spurned ס<br>all my champions in my midst,<br>has proclaimed an appointed time against me<br>to break my young men.<br>A winepress the Master has trampled<br>for the Virgin Zion’s Daughter.  
**1:16** For these do I weep. ע<br>My eyes, my eyes are flooded,<br>for the consoler is far from me,<br>the reviver of my failing life.<br>My children have become desolate,<br>for the enemy has prevailed.  
**1:17** Zion spread her hands.פ<br>There is none to console her.<br>The LORD has summoned for Jacob<br>round about him his foes.<br>Jerusalem has become<br>despised among them.  
**1:18** Righteous is the LORD, צ<br>for I have rebelled against Him.<br>Hear, pray, all you peoples,<br>and see my pain.<br>My virgins and my young men<br>have gone off captive.  
**1:19** I called out to my lovers—ק<br>they deceived me.<br>My priests and my elders perished in the city<br>when they sought food<br>that would revive their failing lives.  
**1:20** See, O LORD, for I am distressed, ר<br>my innards are roiled,<br>my heart churns within me.<br>For I have surely rebelled.<br>Outside, the sword slays sons,<br>inside, very death.  
**1:21** They have heard that I groan, ש<br>I have none to console me.<br>All my enemies heard of my harm, were gladdened.<br>But it was You who did it.<br>You brought the day You summoned.<br>May they be like me!  
**1:22** Let all their evil come before You ת<br>and do to them as You did to me<br>for all my trespasses.<br>For my groans are many<br>and my heart aches.  

## 2장

**2:1** How has the Master beclouded in His wrath ℵ<br>the daughter of Zion,<br>has flung from the heavens to the ground<br>the splendor of Israel,<br>nor did He recall His footstool<br>on the day of His wrath.  
**2:2** The Master obliterated, had no mercy, ב<br>all of Jacob’s dwellings,<br>brought to the ground, profaned,<br>a kingdom and its nobles.  
**2:3** He hacked down in smoldering wrath ג<br>the whole horn of Israel,<br>pulled back His right hand<br>from before the enemy,<br>and burned in Jacob like a white-hot fire<br>consuming all around.  
**2:4** Like an enemy He bent His bow, ד<br>poised with right hand like a foe<br>and slayed all precious to the eye<br>in the tent of Zion’s Daughter,<br>poured forth like fire His fury.  
**2:5** The Master became like an enemy, ה<br>obliterated Israel,<br>obliterated all her citadels,<br>laid in ruins her fortresses,<br>and made abundant in Judah’s Daughter<br>wailing and woe.  
**2:6** And He stripped bare His shelter like a garden, ו<br>laid in ruins His appointed place.<br>The LORD wiped out the memory in Zion<br>of festival and sabbath,<br>and spurned in His raging wrath<br>king and priest.  
**2:7** The Master abandoned His altar, ז<br>cast aside His sanctuary,<br>handed over to enemies<br>the wall of her citadels.<br>They raised a sound in the house of the LORD<br>as on a festival day.  
**2:8** The LORD contrived to lay in ruins ח<br>the wall of Zion’s Daughter.<br>He stretched out the measuring line,<br>did not pull His hand back from obliteration,<br>and rampart and wall did mourn,<br>together they were bleak.  
**2:9** Her gates sunk into the ground, ט<br>He destroyed and shattered her bolts.<br>Her king and her nobles were among the nations,<br>there was no teaching.<br>Her prophets, too, were not vouchsafed<br>vision from the LORD.  
**2:10** They sat on the ground, were silent, י<br>the elders of Zion’s Daughter.<br>They covered their heads with dust,<br>they girded sackcloth.<br>They lowered their heads to the ground,<br>Jerusalem’s virgins.  
**2:11** My eyes were worn out with tears, כ<br>my innards were roiled.<br>My bile spilled out on the ground<br>for the breaking of my People’s Daughter<br>when the babe and the suckling grew faint<br>in the squares of the city.  
**2:12** To their mothers they say, ל<br>“Where is grain and wine?”<br>as they faint like the slain<br>in the squares of the city.  
**2:13** How can I bear witness for you, what can I liken to you, מ<br>O Daughter of Jerusalem?<br>What can I compare to you and console you,<br>O Virgin, Zion’s Daughter?<br>For great as the sea is your breaking.<br>Who can heal you?  
**2:14** Your prophets saw visions for you—נ<br>empty and insipid,<br>and did not lay bare your crime<br>to restore your fortunes<br>and saw visions for you<br>of emptiness and delusion.  
**2:15** They clapped their hands over you, ס<br>all passersby.<br>They hissed and wagged their heads<br>over Jerusalem’s Daughter:<br>“Is this the city of which was said<br>‘The perfection of beauty,<br>the joy of all the earth’?”  
**2:16** They opened wide their mouths to mock you, פ<br>all your enemies.<br>They hissed, they gnashed their teeth,<br>they said, “We obliterated her.<br>Why, this day for which we hoped<br>we have attained it, we have seen it.”  
**2:17** The LORD has done what He had planned, ע<br>He carried out His utterance<br>that He charged in days of old.<br>He destroyed and had no mercy,<br>and the enemy rejoiced over you—<br>He raised the horn of your foes.  
**2:18** Their heart cried out to the Master. צ<br>O wall of Zion’s Daughter,<br>shed like a brook your tears<br>day and night.<br>Give yourself no respite,<br>let your pupils be not still.  
**2:19** Arise, sing out in the night ק<br>at the beginning of the watches.<br>Pour out your heart like water<br>before the presence of the LORD.<br>Raise your palms to Him<br>over the life of your babes<br>faint with famine<br>at the corner of every street.  
**2:20** See, O LORD, and look: ר<br>To whom have you acted thus?<br>Should women eat their fruit,<br>the dandled babes?<br>Should priest and prophet be slain<br>in the sanctuary of the Master?  
**2:21** They lay on the ground in the streets, ש<br>lad and elder.<br>My virgins and my young men<br>fell by the sword.<br>You slew on the day of Your wrath,<br>You slaughtered, had no mercy.  
**2:22** You called in as on festival days ת<br>my neighbors from all around,<br>and on the day of the LORD’s wrath<br>there was neither refuge nor survivor.<br>Those whom I dandled and raised up<br>my enemy destroyed.  

## 3장

**3:1** I am the man who has seen affliction ℵ<br>by the rod of His wrath.  
**3:2** Me He drove off, led away—<br>darkness and no light!  
**3:3** Just to me He comes back,<br>turns His hand against me all day.  
**3:4** He wasted my flesh and my skin. ב<br>He shattered my bones.  
**3:5** He built up against me, encompassed me<br>with misery and suffering.  
**3:6** He made me dwell in darkness<br>like those long dead.  
**3:7** He walled me in, I could not go out, ג<br>piled heavy bronze fetters upon me.  
**3:8** Even though I cried out and shouted,<br>he blocked my prayer.  
**3:9** He walled in my way with hewn stone,<br>He twisted my paths.  
**3:10** A lurking bear He was to me, ד<br>a lion in hiding.  
**3:11** My ways He led astray and He ripped me apart,<br>He made me desolate.  
**3:12** He bent His bow<br>and stood me up as a target for the arrow.  
**3:13** He drove into my innards ה<br>the shafts of His quiver.  
**3:14** I became a laughingstock to all my people,<br>the theme of their taunts all day.  
**3:15** He fed me full with bitterness,<br>gave me wormwood’s draught.  
**3:16** He made my teeth crunch down on gravel, ו<br>crushed me in the dust.  
**3:17** My life abandoned peace,<br>I forgot what was good.  
**3:18** And I thought, “My strength<br>and my hope are lost before the LORD.”  
**3:19** Recall my affliction and my wandering—ז<br>wormwood and poison.  
**3:20** My very life does recall<br>and bends down upon me.  
**3:21** Thus I answer to my heart,<br>therefore I yet hope:  
**3:22** The LORD’s kindness has not ended, ח<br>for His mercies are not exhausted.  
**3:23** They are renewed every morning.<br>Great is Your faithfulness.  
**3:24** “My portion is the LORD,” I said.<br>therefore I yet hope for Him.  
**3:25** Good is the LORD for those who look to Him, ט<br>for the person who seeks Him out.  
**3:26** Good that he hopes in silence<br>for rescue from the LORD.  
**3:27** Good is it for a man that he bear<br>a yoke in his youth.  
**3:28** Let him sit alone and be silent י<br>when He has set it upon him.  
**3:29** Let him put his mouth into the dust—<br>perhaps there still is hope.  
**3:30** Let him offer his cheek to him who strikes him,<br>let him take his fill of disgrace.  
**3:31** For the LORD will not abandon forever. כ  
**3:32** Though He strikes with sorrow,<br>He will show mercy in His great kindness.  
**3:33** For He does not afflict on purpose<br>and aggrieve the sons of men  
**3:34** to crush beneath His feet ל<br>all the prisoners of the earth,  
**3:35** to sidetrack the case of a man<br>before the presence of the Most High,  
**3:36** to deflect a person in his plea—<br>the Master would not brook this.  
**3:37** Who can speak and it comes about מ<br>if the Master has not decreed it?  
**3:38** From the Most High’s mouth shall not issue<br>the evil and the good?  
**3:39** Of what shall a living person complain,<br>a man for his offenses?  
**3:40** Let us seek out and plumb our ways נ<br>that we come back to the LORD.  
**3:41** Let us lift up our heart on our palms<br>to God in the heavens.  
**3:42** We trespassed and rebelled—<br>You did not forgive.  
**3:43** You enveloped us in anger and pursued us, ס<br>You slew and showed no pity.  
**3:44** You enveloped Yourself in a cloud,<br>that prayer could not pass through.  
**3:45** Filth and disgust You made us<br>in the midst of the peoples.  
**3:46** They gaped with their mouths upon us, פ<br>all our enemies.  
**3:47** Terror and trap they were for us,<br>shearing and shattering.  
**3:48** Rivers of tears my eye does shed<br>for the shattering of my people.  
**3:49** My eye streams and is not still, ע<br>without respite  
**3:50** till the LORD look out<br>and see from the heavens.  
**3:51** My eyes have dealt ill to me<br>for all the daughters of my city.  
**3:52** They have ensnared me like a bird, צ<br>my enemies for no cause.  
**3:53** They have cut off my life in a pit<br>and cast stones at me.  
**3:54** Waters whelmed over my head,<br>I thought, “I am gone.”  
**3:55** I called Your name, O LORD ק<br>from the bottommost pit.  
**3:56** My voice You have heard—<br>do not shut Your ear to my sigh, to my cry.  
**3:57** You drew close on the day I called You,<br>You said, “Do not fear.”  
**3:58** You pleaded, O Master, my cause, ר<br>You redeemed my life.  
**3:59** You have seen, O LORD, the wrong done me.<br>Grant me justice!  
**3:60** You have seen all their vindictiveness,<br>all their schemes against me.  
**3:61** You have heard their insults, O LORD, ש<br>all their schemes against me,  
**3:62** the slurs of my foes and their prattle<br>against me all day.  
**3:63** Look on their sitting and their rising—<br>I am the theme of their taunts.  
**3:64** Pay them back their deserts, O LORD, ת<br>according to their own acts.  
**3:65** Give them anguish of the heart,<br>Your curse upon them.  
**3:66** Pursue in wrath and destroy them<br>from beneath the heavens of the LORD.  

## 4장

**4:1** How has gold turned dull, ℵ<br>how tarnished finest gold!<br>The sacred gems spilled out<br>at the corner of each street.  
**4:2** Zion’s precious children, ב<br>worth their weight in gold,<br>how are they reckoned as earthenware jars,<br>the work of the potter’s hands!  
**4:3** Even jackals offer the breast, ג<br>suckle their own cubs.<br>My People’s Daughter is now ruthless<br>as ostriches in the desert.  
**4:4** The suckling’s tongue has cleaved ד<br>to his palate out of thirst.<br>Babes ask for bread—<br>none offers it to them.  
**4:5** They who ate delicacies ה<br>are desolate in the streets.<br>They who were reared on purple,<br>embrace the refuse heaps.  
**4:6** And the crime of my People’s Daughter ו<br>is greater than the offense of Sodom<br>that was overturned in a moment<br>and no hands were laid on her.  
**4:7** Her elite were purer than snow, ז<br>they were whiter than milk.<br>Their limbs were ruddier than coral,<br>sapphire was their body.  
**4:8** Their mien is darker than black, ח<br>they are not recognized in the streets.<br>Their skin is shriveled on their bones,<br>become as dry as wood.  
**4:9** Better the slain of the sword ט<br>than the slain by famine.<br>For those run through ooze blood<br>more than the crop of the field.  
**4:10** The hands of compassionate women י<br>cooked their own children—<br>they became nourishment for them<br>in the shattering of my People’s Daughter.  
**4:11** The LORD has spent His fury, כ<br>poured out His smoldering wrath,<br>and lit a fire in Zion,<br>and her foundations it consumed.  
**4:12** The kings of the earth did not believe, ל<br>nor all the world’s dwellers,<br>that a foe and enemy would enter<br>the gates of Jerusalem  
**4:13** because of her prophets’ offenses, מ<br>the crimes of her priests,<br>who shed in her midst<br>the blood of the just.  
**4:14** The blind wandered through the streets, נ<br>became so foul with blood<br>that none could touch their garb.  
**4:15** “Turn away, you unclean,” they called to them, ס<br>“Turn away, turn away, do not touch!”<br>So they flew off, they wandered.<br>It was said in the nations, “They shall dwell no more.”  
**4:16** The LORD’s presence has set them apart, פ<br>no more shall one look on them.<br>They showed no favor to the priests,<br>no mercy to the elders.  
**4:17** Our eyes still pine ע<br>for our vain hope<br>in our hope that we harbored<br>from a nation that cannot rescue.  
**4:18** They stalked our steps צ<br>to block walking through the streets.<br>Our end drew near, our days played out,<br>for our end had come.  
**4:19** Swifter were our pursuers ק<br>than the eagles of the heavens.<br>On the mountains they raced after us,<br>in the desert ambushed us.  
**4:20** Our very life-breath, the LORD’s anointed, ר<br>was captured in their traps,<br>of whom we had said,<br>“In his shade we shall live among the nations.”  
**4:21** Exult and rejoice, Edom’s Daughter, ש<br>you who dwell in the land of Uz.<br>To you as well shall the cup pass on,<br>you shall be drunk and naked be.  
**4:22** Your guilt is done with, Zion’s Daughter, ת<br>He no more shall exile you.<br>He makes a reckoning of your guilt, Edom’s Daughter,<br>your offenses He exposed.  

## 5장

**5:1** Recall, O LORD, what befell us,<br>look and see our disgrace—  
**5:2** our estate turned over to strangers,<br>our homes to foreigners.  
**5:3** Orphans we were without fathers,<br>our mothers like widows.  
**5:4** Our water we drank for silver,<br>our wood came at a price.  
**5:5** Up to our necks we were pursued,<br>we wearied, we got no rest.  
**5:6** To Egypt we put out our hand,<br>to Assyria, to be sated with bread.  
**5:7** Our fathers offended and are no more,<br>it was we bore the weight of their crimes.  
**5:8** Slaves ruled over us,<br>none broke us free from their hand.  
**5:9** At the cost of our lives we got bread<br>because of the desert sword.  
**5:10** Our skin burned hot as a kiln<br>from the raging fever of famine.  
**5:11** Women they ravaged in Zion,<br>virgins in Judah’s towns.  
**5:12** Nobles were impaled by them,<br>elders they did not honor.  
**5:13** Young men carried millstones,<br>and lads stumbled with loads of wood.  
**5:14** Elders were no more in the gates,<br>nor young men at their songs.  
**5:15** Our heart’s joy is no more,<br>our dance has turned to mourning.  
**5:16** The crown of our head has fallen.<br>Woe to us, for we have offended.  
**5:17** For this our heart is anguished,<br>for these our eyes go dark.  
**5:18** On Mount Zion that is desolate<br>foxes go about.  
**5:19** But You, O LORD, for all time are enthroned,<br>Your throne for all generations!  
**5:20** Why should You forget us forever,<br>forsake us for endless years?  
**5:21** Bring us back to You, LORD, that we come back,<br>renew our days as of old.  
**5:22** For indeed You have rejected us,<br>You have been grievously furious with us.
