# 23.이사야 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision of Isaiah son of Amoz that he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, Hezekiah, kings of Judah.  
**1:2** Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth,<br>for the LORD has spoken.<br>Sons I have nurtured and raised<br>but they rebelled against Me.  
**1:3** The ox knows its owner<br>and the donkey its master’s stall.<br>Israel did not know,<br>my people did not pay heed.  
**1:4** Woe, offending nation,<br>people weighed down with crime, s<br>eed of evildoers,<br>sons acting ruinously.<br>They have forsaken the LORD,<br>scorned Israel’s Holy One,<br>they have fallen behind.  
**1:5** Why would you be beaten more,<br>still swerving from the way?<br>Every head is sick<br>and every heart in pain.  
**1:6** From footsole to head<br>no place in him intact,<br>wound, bruise,<br>and open sore—<br>not drained, not bandaged,<br>nor soothed with oil.  
**1:7** Your land is desolate,<br>your towns are burned in fire.<br>Your soil, before your eyes<br>strangers devour it,<br>and desolation like an upheaval by strangers.  
**1:8** And the daughter of Zion remains<br>like a hut in a vineyard,<br>like a shed in a patch of greens,<br>like a town besieged.  
**1:9** Had not the LORD of Armies<br>left us a scant remnant,<br>we would be like Sodom.<br>We would resemble Gomorrah.  
**1:10** Listen to the word of the LORD,<br>O leaders of Sodom,<br>give ear to our God’s teaching,<br>O people of Gomorrah.  
**1:11** “Why need I all your sacrifices?”<br>says the LORD.<br>“I am sated with the burnt offerings of rams<br>and the suet of fatted beasts,<br>and the blood of bulls and sheep and he-goats<br>I do not desire.  
**1:12** When you come to see My face,<br>who asked this of you,<br>to trample My courts?  
**1:13** You shall no longer bring false grain offering,<br>it is incense of abomination to me.<br>New moon and sabbath call an assembly—<br>I cannot bear crime and convocation.  
**1:14** Your new moons and your appointed times<br>I utterly despise.<br>They have become a burden to me,<br>I cannot bear them.  
**1:15** And when you spread your palms,<br>I avert My eyes from you.<br>Though you abundantly pray,<br>I do not listen.<br>Your hands are full of blood.  
**1:16** Wash, become pure,<br>Remove your evil acts from My eyes.<br>Cease doing evil.  
**1:17** Learn to do good,<br>seek justice.<br>Make the oppressed happy,<br>defend the orphan,<br>argue the widow’s case.”  
**1:18** “Come, pray, let us come to terms,”<br>the Lord said.<br>“If your offenses be like scarlet,<br>like snow shall they turn white.<br>If they be red as dyed cloth,<br>they shall become like pure wool.  
**1:19** If you assent and listen,<br>the land’s bounty you shall eat.  
**1:20** But if you refuse and rebel,<br>by the sword you shall be eaten,<br>for the Lord’s mouth has spoken.”  
**1:21** How has the faithful town<br>become a whore?<br>Filled with justice,<br>where righteousness did lodge,<br>and now—murderers.  
**1:22** Your silver has turned to dross,<br>your drink is mixed with water.<br>Your nobles are knaves<br>and companions to thieves.  
**1:23** All of them lust for bribes<br>and chase illicit payments.<br>They do not defend the orphan,<br>and the widow’s case does not touch them.  
**1:24** Therefore, says the Master, LORD of Armies, Israel’s Mighty One:<br>Oh, I will settle scores with my foes<br>and take vengeance of my enemies,  
**1:25** and bring My hand back upon you<br>and take away all your dross  
**1:26** and bring back your judges as before<br>and your councillors as long ago.<br>Then shall you be called town of righteousness,<br>faithful city.  
**1:27** Zion shall be redeemed through justice,<br>and those who turn back in her, through righteousness.  
**1:28** But the rebels and offenders together are shattered,<br>and those who forsake the LORD shall perish.  
**1:29** For they shall be shamed of the cult-trees<br>after which you lusted,<br>and be disgraced by the gardens that you have chosen.  
**1:30** For you shall be like an oak<br>whose leaf withers<br>and like a garden where there is no water.  
**1:31** And the strong shall turn into tow<br>and his deeds into a spark,<br>and both shall burn together,<br>with none to put it out.

**2:17** And human haughtiness shall bow low<br>and men’s loftiness be brought down,<br>and the LORD alone shall be exalted<br>on that day.

**2:19** And they shall come into caves in the crags<br>and into hollows in the dust<br>from the fear of the LORD<br>and from His pride’s glory<br>when He rises to wreak havoc on earth.  
**2:20** On that day man shall fling away<br>his silver idols and his golden idols<br>that he made to bow before<br>to the hedgehogs and the bats.  
**2:21** And they shall come into the crevices in the crags<br>and into the clefts in the rocks<br>from the fear of the LORD<br>and from His pride’s glory<br>when He rises to wreak havoc on earth.

**3:25** Your men shall fall by the sword,<br>your valor, in battle.  
**3:26** And her gates shall mourn and lament,<br>and stripped, she shall sit on the ground.

## 5장

**5:2** And he hoed it and took off its stones,<br>and planted it with choice vines.<br>And he built a tower in its midst,<br>and a winepress, too, he hewed in it.<br>And he hoped to get grapes<br>but it put forth rotten fruit.  
**5:3** And, now, O dweller of Jerusalem<br>and man of Judah,<br>judge, pray, between Me<br>and My vineyard.  
**5:4** What more could be done for My vineyard<br>that I did not do?<br>Why did I hope to get grapes<br>and it put forth rotten fruit?  
**5:5** And now, let Me inform you, pray,<br>what I am about to do to My vineyard:<br>take away its hedge, and it shall turn to waste,<br>break down its fence, and it shall be trampled.  
**5:6** And I will make it a wild field,<br>and it shall not be pruned nor raked,<br>and thorn and thistle shall spring up.<br>And I will charge the clouds<br>not to rain on it.  
**5:7** For the house of Israel is the vineyard of the LORD of Armies<br>and the men of Judah are His delightful planting.<br>He hoped for justice, and, look, jaundice,<br>for righteousness, and, look, wretchedness.  
**5:8** Woe, who add house to house,<br>who put field together with field<br>till there is no space left<br>and you alone are settled, in the heart of the land.  
**5:9** In the hearing of the LORD of Armies:<br>I swear, many houses shall turn to ruin,<br>great and good ones with none living in them.  
**5:10** For ten acres of vineyard shall yield a single bat,<br>and a homer of seed shall yield but an ephah.  
**5:11** Woe, who rise in the morning early<br>to chase after strong drink,<br>who linger late in twilight,<br>enflamed by wine.  
**5:12** Whose banquets are lyre and lute, timbrel and flute, and wine,<br>and who do not regard the LORD’s deeds,<br>and the work of His hands do not see.  
**5:13** Therefore is My people exiled<br>for lack of knowledge,<br>its honored men victims of famine<br>and its masses parched with thirst.  
**5:14** Therefore Sheol has widened its gullet<br>and gaped open its mouth beyond measure,<br>and her splendor and her hubbub have gone down,<br>her noise and her revelers.  
**5:15** And humans are bowed low and man brought down,<br>and the eyes of the haughty are brought down.  
**5:16** And the LORD of Armies shall be raised up in justice<br>and the Holy One hallowed in righteousness.  
**5:17** And sheep shall graze as in their meadows<br>and goats shall feed in the ruins of the fatted.  
**5:18** Woe, who haul crime with the cords of falseness<br>and like the ropes of a cart, offense.  
**5:19** Who say: “Let Him hurry, let Him hasten His deed,<br>that we may see,<br>and let the counsel of the Israel’s Holy One<br>draw near and come that we may see.”  
**5:20** Woe, who say “good” to evil<br>and “evil” to good,<br>who turn darkness to light<br>and light into darkness,<br>turn bitter into sweet<br>and sweet into bitter.  
**5:21** Woe, wise in their own eyes<br>and in their own opinion discerning  
**5:22** Woe, mighty to drink wine<br>and men of valor to mix strong drink,  
**5:23** who declare the wicked innocent because of bribes<br>and righteous men’s just cause deny.  
**5:24** Therefore, as a tongue of fire consumes straw<br>and hay falls apart in flame,<br>their root shall be like rot<br>and their flower go up like dust.<br>For they have spurned the teaching of the LORD of Armies,<br>and the utterance of Israel’s Holy One they despised.  
**5:25** Therefore is the LORD incensed with His people,<br>and has stretched out His hand and struck it.<br>And mountains have quaked,<br>and their corpses are become like offal in the streets.<br>Yet His wrath has not abated<br>and His arm is still stretched out.  
**5:26** And He shall raise a banner for nations from afar<br>and whistle to one at the end of the earth,<br>and, look, swiftly, quick, he shall come.  
**5:27** None tires, none stumbles among them,<br>he does not slumber, does not sleep.<br>The belt round his loins does not slip open,<br>and his sandals’ thong does not snap.  
**5:28** His arrows are sharpened,<br>all his bows are drawn.<br>The hooves of his horses are hard as flint<br>and his wheels like the whirlwind.  
**5:29** He has a roar like the lion<br>he roars like the king of beasts.<br>He howls and seizes his prey,<br>whisks it off and none can save.  
**5:30** And he shall howl against him on that day<br>like the howling of the sea,<br>and he shall peer toward the earth<br>and, look, constricting darkness,<br>and the light shall go dark in its clouds.  

## 6장

**6:1** In the year of the death of King Uzziah, I saw the Master seated on a high and lofty throne, and the skirts of his robe filled the Temple.  
**6:2** Seraphim were stationed over him, six wings for each one. With two it would cover its face, and with two it would cover its feet, and with two it would hover.  
**6:3** And each called out to each and said:<br>“Holy, holy, holy, is the LORD of Armies.<br>The fullness of all the earth is His glory.”  
**6:4** And the pillars of the thresholds swayed from the voice calling out and the house was filled with smoke.  
**6:5** And I said,<br>“Woe to me, for I am undone,<br>for I am a man of impure lips,<br>and in a people of impure lips do I dwell.<br>My eyes have seen the King LORD of Armies.”  
**6:6** And one of the seraphim flew down to me, in his hand a glowing coal in tongs that he had taken from the altar.  
**6:7** And he touched my mouth and said,<br>“Look, this has touched your lips,<br>and your crime is gone, your offense shall be atoned.”  
**6:8** And I heard the voice of the Master saying,<br>“Whom shall I send,<br>and who will go for us?”<br>And I said, “Here I am, send me.”  
**6:9** And he said, “Go and say to this people:<br>‘Indeed you must hear but you will not understand,<br>indeed you must see but you will not know.’  
**6:10** Make the heart of this people obtuse<br>and block its ears and seal its eyes.<br>Lest it see with its eyes<br>and with its ears hear<br>and its heart understand<br>and it turn back and be healed.”  
**6:11** And I said, “Till when, O Master?”<br>And he said,<br>“Till towns are laid waste with no dwellers<br>and homes with no man<br>and the land is laid waste, a desolation.  
**6:12** And the LORD shall drive man far away<br>and abandonment grow in the midst of the land.  
**6:13** And yet a tenth part shall be in it and turn back.<br>And it shall be ravaged<br>like a terebinth and an oak<br>which though felled have a stump within them,<br>the holy seed is its stump.

**37:3** And they said to him, “Thus said Hezekiah: ‘A day of distress and chastisement and insult in this day.<br>For children have come to the birth-stool,<br>and there is no strength to give birth.

**37:5** And the servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah.  
**37:6** And Isaiah said to them, “Thus shall you say to your master: Thus said the LORD: ‘Do not fear the words that you have heard, with which the flunkies of the king of Assyria reviled Me.  
**37:7** I am about to send an ill spirit into him, and he shall hear a rumor and go back to his land, and I shall make him fall by the sword in his land.’”  
**37:8** And Rabshakeh went back and found the king of Assyria battling against Libnah, for he had heard that he had journeyed on from Lachish.  
**37:9** And he heard about Tirhakah king of Cush, saying, “Look, he has sallied forth to do battle with you.” And he heard, and he sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying,  
**37:10** “Thus shall you say to Hezekiah king of Judah, ‘Let not your god in whom you trust deceive you, saying, Jerusalem will not be given into the hand of the king of Assyria.  
**37:11** Look, you yourself have heard what the kings of Assyria did to all the lands, annihilating them—and will you be saved?  
**37:12** Did the gods of the nations save them, when my fathers destroyed Gozan and Haram and Rezeph and the Edomites who are in Telassar?  
**37:13** Where is the king of Hammath, and the kings of Arpad and the king of Lair, Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?’”  
**37:14** And Hezekiah took the letters from the hand of the messengers and read them, and he went up to the house of the LORD, and Hezekiah spread them out before the LORD.  
**37:15** And Hezekiah prayed to the LORD, saying:  
**37:16** “LORD of Armies, enthroned on the cherubim, You alone are God of all the kingdoms of earth. You it was made heaven and earth.  
**37:17** Bend Your ear and listen; open, LORD, Your eyes and see; and listen to the words of Sennacherib that he sent to insult the living God.  
**37:18** Indeed, LORD, the king of Assyria destroyed all the nations and their lands  
**37:19** and consigned their gods to fire, for they are not gods but the work of human hands, wood and stone, and they destroyed them.  
**37:20** And now, O LORD our God, rescue us from his hand, that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that You alone are the LORD our God.”  
**37:21** And Isaiah son of Amoz sent to Hezekiah, saying, “Thus said the LORD God of Israel: ‘Of which you prayed to Me about Sennacherib king of Assyria,

**37:23** Whom did you insult and revile,<br>and against whom have you lifted your voice,<br>and raised your eyes up high<br>against Israel’s Holy One?  
**37:24** By your messengers you insulted the Master<br>and thought, “With my many chariots<br>I will go up to the heights of the mountains,<br>the far reaches of Lebanon.<br>I will cut down its lofty cedars,<br>its choicest cypresses,<br>and will come to its uttermost heights,<br>and the woods of its undergrowth.  
**37:25** It is I who have dug and drunk<br>the waters of foreigners<br>and dried up with the soles of my feet<br>all Egypt’s rivers.  
**37:26** Have you not heard from afar<br>that which I did from time of old?<br>I fashioned it, brought it to pass—<br>and fortified towns<br>have turned into heaps of ruins.  
**37:27** Their inhabitants, impotent,<br>are shattered and put to shame,<br>become the grass of the field<br>and green growth,<br>thatch on the roofs<br>by the east wind blasted.”  
**37:28** And your stayings and comings and goings I know<br>and your raging against Me.  
**37:29** Because of your raging against Me<br>and your din that came up in My ears<br>I will put My hook in your nose<br>and My bit between your lips,<br>and will turn you back on the way<br>on which you came.

**38:4** And the word of the LORD came to Isaiah, saying,  
**38:5** “Go and say to Hezekiah, ‘Thus said the LORD God of David your forefather: I have heard your prayer, I have seen your tears. I am about to add to your days fifteen years.

**38:18** For Sheol will not acclaim You,<br>nor will Death praise You.<br>Those who go down to the Pit<br>cannot hope for Your faithfulness.  
**38:19** The living, the living, he will acclaim you,<br>like me on this day.<br>Father will make known to sons<br>Your faithfulness.  
**38:20** The LORD is here to rescue us,<br>and let us play music<br>all the days of our life<br>in the house of the LORD.
