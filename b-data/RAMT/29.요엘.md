# 29.요엘 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Joel son of Pethuel.  
**1:2** Hear this, you elders,<br>and give ear, all dwellers of the land.<br>Has its like happened in your days<br>and in the days of your fathers?  
**1:3** Recount it to your children<br>and to your children’s children<br>and to their children in a generation to come.  
**1:4** What remained from the locust the grasshopper ate,<br>and what remained from the grasshopper the swarmer ate<br>and what remained from the swarmer the grub did eat.  
**1:5** Wake up, you drunkards and weep,<br>and wail, all drinkers of wine,<br>for the fermented juice that is cut off from your mouth.  
**1:6** For a nation has come up against my land,<br>vast and countless.<br>Its teeth are the teeth of a lion,<br>the maned beast’s jaws it has.  
**1:7** It has turned my vine to a desolation<br>and my fig tree into shards.<br>It has stripped it bare,<br>its branches are gone white.  
**1:8** Howl like a virgin girt in sackcloth<br>over the husband of her youth.  
**1:9** Grain offering and libation are cut off<br>from the house of the LORD.<br>The priests mourn,<br>the ministers of the LORD.  
**1:10** The field is ravaged,<br>the soil mourns,<br>for the new grain has been ravaged,<br>the new wine dried up,<br>the oil is bleak.  
**1:11** The farmers are shamed,<br>the vintners wail<br>over wheat and over barley,<br>for the field’s harvest is gone.  
**1:12** The vine has dried up<br>and the fig tree is bleak.<br>Pomegranate, also palm tree and quince,<br>all the trees of the field are sear,<br>for joy has dried up<br>from the sons of man.  
**1:13** Gird and lament, you priests,<br>wail, ministrants of the altar.<br>Come, spend nights in sackcloth,<br>ministrants of my God.  
**1:14** Pronounce a fast,<br>proclaim convocation.<br>Gather the elders,<br>all the land’s dwellers,<br>to the house of the LORD your God<br>and cry out to the LORD.  
**1:15** Alack for the day,<br>for the day of the LORD is near,<br>and like a shattering from Shaddai it comes.  
**1:16** Why, before our eyes<br>food is cut off<br>from the house of our God,<br>rejoicing and exultation.  
**1:17** The seeds have rotted<br>beneath their clods.<br>The storehouses are desolate,<br>the granaries ruined,<br>for the new grain has dried up.  
**1:18** How the livestock groans,<br>the herds of cattle are confounded,<br>for there is no pasture for them,<br>the flocks of sheep are desolate.  
**1:19** To you, O LORD, I call,<br>for fire consumed the wilderness meadows.<br>and a flame burned all the trees of the field.  
**1:20** The beast of the field, too,<br>yearns for You.<br>For the channels of water are dry.<br>Fire consumed the wilderness meadows.  

## 2장

**2:1** Blow the ram’s horn in Zion,<br>sound the alarm on my holy mountain.<br>Let all the land’s dwellers shake,<br>for the day of the LORD comes, yes, nears.  
**2:2** A day of darkness and of gloom,<br>a day of clouds and dense fog.<br>Like dawn spread out on the mountains—<br>a vast and numerous people.<br>Its like has never been and after it will be none like it<br>through years of generations without end.  
**2:3** Before it fire consumes<br>and behind it a flame burns hot.<br>Like the Garden of Eden was the land before it,<br>and behind it desolate desert,<br>nor does anything escape it.  
**2:4** Like the look of horses is its look,<br>like horsemen so they race.  
**2:5** Like the sound of chariots<br>on the mountaintops they bound.<br>Like the sound of fiery flame<br>consuming straw,<br>like a vast troop<br>arrayed for battle.  
**2:6** Before it peoples tremble,<br>all faces lose their luster.  
**2:7** Like warriors they race,<br>like men of war they scale the walls.<br>Each goes ahead on his way<br>and they do not bend their paths,  
**2:8** and each does not press against his fellow,<br>every man goes on his road,<br>and through the outer wall they pounce,<br>they do not suffer wounds.  
**2:9** In the town they raise a clamor,<br>they race upon the walls,<br>they go up in the houses,<br>through the windows they come like a thief.  
**2:10** Before it the earth shudders,<br>the heavens shake,<br>sun and moon go dark<br>and stars their radiance withdraw.  
**2:11** And the LORD sends forth His voice<br>before His force,<br>for very great is His camp,<br>for vast are those who do His command.<br>For great is the day of the LORD,<br>and who can endure it?  
**2:12** And now, too, said the LORD,<br>turn back to Me with all your heart,<br>in fasting and weeping and mourning,  
**2:13** and rend your heart, not your garments,<br>and turn back to the LORD your God,<br>for He is gracious and compassionate,<br>slow to anger and abounding in kindness<br>and relenting over evil.  
**2:14** Who knows? He may once more relent<br>and leave blessing after Him,<br>grain offering and libation to the LORD your God.  
**2:15** Blast the ram’s horn in Zion,<br>pronounce a fast,<br>proclaim convocation.  
**2:16** Gather the people,<br>dedicate an assembly,<br>summon the elders,<br>gather the babes,<br>the sucklings at the breast.<br>Let the bridegroom come out from his chamber<br>and the bride from her wedding canopy.  
**2:17** Between the great hall and the altar<br>let the priests weep,<br>let the LORD’s ministrants say,<br>“Have pity, LORD, on Your people,<br>and do not let Your estate be disgraced<br>for nations to rule over them.<br>Why should they say among the peoples,<br>‘Where is their God?’  
**2:18** And let the LORD be zealous for His land<br>and show mercy for His people.”  
**2:19** And the LORD answered and said to His people,<br>I am about to send to you<br>new grain and new wine and oil<br>and you will be sated with them.<br>And I will no more make you<br>disgraced among the nations.  
**2:20** And the northerner I will put far from you<br>and scatter him to a parched and desolate land,<br>his face to the eastern sea<br>and his end to the western sea.<br>And his stench shall go up<br>and his foul odor go up,<br>for he has done enormities.  
**2:21** Do not fear, O land,<br>exult and rejoice,<br>for the LORD has done enormous things.  
**2:22** Do not fear, O beasts of the field,<br>for the wilderness meadows sprout grass,<br>for the tree yields its fruit,<br>the fig tree and vine put forth their wealth.  
**2:23** And children of Zion,<br>exult and rejoice<br>in the LORD your God.<br>For he has given you the early rain as bounty<br>and brought down the rain for you,<br>early rain and latter rain as before.  
**2:24** And the threshing floors shall fill with grain<br>and the vats overflow with new wine and oil.  
**2:25** And I will pay you back for the years<br>that the grasshopper ate,<br>the swarmer, the grub, and the locust,<br>My great force that I sent against you.  
**2:26** And you shall eat, be continuously sated,<br>and you shall praise the name of the LORD your God<br>Who has done wondrously for you,<br>and My people shall not be shamed evermore.  
**2:27** And you shall know that I am in the midst of Israel,<br>and I am the LORD your God, there is none else,<br>and My people shall not be shamed evermore.  
**2:28** And it shall happen afterward:<br>I will pour My spirit upon all flesh,<br>and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy.<br>Your elders shall dream dreams,<br>your young men see visions.  
**2:29** And even upon male slaves and slavegirls<br>in those days will I pour My spirit.  
**2:30** And I will set portents in the heavens and on earth,<br>blood and fire and columns of smoke.  
**2:31** The sun shall turn into darkness<br>and the moon into blood<br>before the coming of the day of the LORD,<br>great and fearful.  
**2:32** And all who call in the name of the LORD shall escape. For on Mount Zion there shall be a remnant, as the LORD has said, in the survivors whom the LORD calls.  

## 3장

**3:1** For, look, days are coming and that time when I will restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem.  
**3:2** And I will gather all the nations<br>and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat<br>and come to judgment with them there<br>over My people and Israel, My estate,<br>whom they scattered among the nations,<br>and they divided up their land.  
**3:3** And over My people they cast lots<br>and bartered a boy for the price of a whore<br>and sold a girl for wine which they drank.  
**3:4** And also what are you to Me, Tyre and Sidon<br>and all the provinces of Philistia?<br>Are you paying back against Me?<br>If you are paying back against Me,<br>swiftly, quick will I pay it back on your head.  
**3:5** As My silver and My gold you took,<br>and My goodly treasures you brought to your palaces,  
**3:6** and the people of Judah and the people of Jerusalem<br>you sold to the Greeks<br>to put them far from their frontiers.  
**3:7** I am about to raise them from the place<br>where you sold them<br>and pay you back upon your head.  
**3:8** And I will sell your sons and your daughters<br>into the hands of the Judahites,<br>and they shall sell their captives to a distant nation.<br>For the LORD has spoken.  
**3:9** Proclaim this among the nations,<br>assemble for battle.<br>Raise the warriors,<br>let all the men of war<br>come forth, go up.  
**3:10** Grind your plowshares into swords<br>and your pruning hooks into spears.<br>The weak shall say: I am a warrior.  
**3:11** Hasten and come,<br>all nations round about, and gather.<br>There bring down, O LORD, your warriors.  
**3:12** The nations shall be roused and come up<br>to the Valley of Jehoshaphat.<br>For there will I sit to judge<br>all the nations round about.  
**3:13** Wield the sickle,<br>for the harvest is ripe.<br>Come, go down,<br>for the winepress is full.<br>The vats overflow,<br>for their evil is great.  
**3:14** Crowds upon crowds<br>in the Valley of Doom,<br>for near is the day of the LORD<br>in the Valley of Doom.  
**3:15** Sun and moon go dark<br>and the stars withdraw their light.  
**3:16** And the LORD from Zion roars<br>and from Jerusalem sounds His voice,<br>and the heavens and the earth shudder.<br>But the LORD is a refuge to His people.<br>and a stronghold to the Israelites.  
**3:17** And you shall know that I am the LORD your God<br>Who dwells in Zion, My holy nation,<br>and Jerusalem shall be holy,<br>no more shall strangers pass through it.  
**3:18** And it shall happen on that day:<br>the mountains shall drip fermented juice<br>and the hills run with milk<br>and all the channels of Judah<br>run with water.<br>And a spring shall issue from the house of the LORD<br>and water the Wadi of Acacias.  
**3:19** Egypt shall become a desolation<br>and Edom a desolate wilderness<br>for the outrage done to the Judahites<br>in whose land they shed the blood of the innocent.  
**3:20** And Judah shall be settled forever<br>and Jerusalem for all generations.  
**3:21** Shall I acquit for their blood? I will not acquit.<br>And the LORD dwells in Zion.
