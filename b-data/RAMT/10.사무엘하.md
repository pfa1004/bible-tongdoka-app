# 10.사무엘하 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** And it happened after the death of Saul, when David had returned from striking down Amalek, that David stayed in Ziklag two days.  
**1:2** And it happened on the third day that, look, a man was coming from the camp, from Saul, his garments torn and earth on his head. And it happened when he came to David, that he fell to the ground and did obeisance.  
**1:3** And David said to him, “From where do you come?” And he said, “From the camp of Israel I have gotten away.”  
**1:4** And David said to him “What has happened? Pray, tell me.” And he said, “The troops fled from the battle, and also many of the troops have fallen and died, and also Saul and Jonathan his son died.”  
**1:5** And David said to the lad who was telling him, “How do you know that Saul died, and Jonathan his son?”  
**1:6** And the lad who was telling him said, “I just chanced to be on Mount Gilboa, and, look, Saul was leaning on his spear, and, look, chariots and horsemen had overtaken him.  
**1:7** And he turned round behind him and saw me and called to me, and I said, ‘Here I am.’  
**1:8** And he said to me, ‘Who are you?’ And I said to him, ‘I am an Amalekite.’  
**1:9** And he said to me, ‘Pray, stand over me and finish me off, for the fainting spell has seized me, for while life is still within me. . . .’  
**1:10** And I stood over him and finished him off, for I knew that he could not live after having fallen. And I took the diadem that was on his head and the band that was on his arm, and I have brought them here to my lord.”  
**1:11** And David took hold of his garments and tore them, and all the men who were with him did so, too.  
**1:12** And they keened and they wept and they fasted till evening for Saul and for Jonathan his son and for the LORD’S people and for the house of Israel because they had fallen by the sword.  
**1:13** And David said to the lad who had told him, “From where are you?” And he said, “The son of an Amalekite sojourner am I.”  
**1:14** And David said to him, “How were you not afraid to reach out your hand to do violence to the LORD’s anointed?”  
**1:15** And David called to one of the lads and said, “Come forward, stab him.” And he struck him down, and he died.  
**1:16** And David said to him, “Your blood is on your own head, for your mouth bore witness against you, saying, ‘I was the one who finished off the LORD’s anointed.’”  
**1:17** And David sang this lament for Saul and for Jonathan,  
**1:18** and he said to teach hard things to the sons of Judah—look, it is written down in the Book of Jashar:  
**1:19** “The splendor, O Israel, on your heights lies slain,<br>how have the warriors fallen!  
**1:20** Tell it not in Gath,<br>proclaim not in Ashkelon’s streets.<br>Lest the Philistine daughters rejoice,<br>lest the daughters of the uncircumcised gloat.  
**1:21** O hills of Gilboa—no dew!<br>and no rain upon you, O lofty fields.<br>For there the warriors’ shield was besmirched,<br>the shield of Saul unburnished with oil.  
**1:22** From the blood of the slain,<br>from the warriors’ fat—<br>Jonathan’s bow did not retreat,<br>and the sword of Saul never turned away empty.  
**1:23** Saul and Jonathan, beloved and dear,<br>in their life and their death they were not parted.<br>They were swifter than eagles,<br>and stronger than lions.  
**1:24** O daughters of Israel, weep over Saul,<br>who clothed you in scarlet and bangles,<br>who studded your garments with jewelry of gold.  
**1:25** How have the warriors fallen<br>in the midst of the battle.<br>Jonathan, upon your heights slain!  
**1:26** I grieve for you, my brother, Jonathan.<br>Very dear you were to me.<br>More wondrous your love to me<br>than the love of women.  
**1:27** How have the warriors fallen,<br>and the gear of battle is lost.”  

## 2장

**2:1** And it happened afterward that David inquired of the LORD, saying, “Shall I go up into one of the towns of Judah?” And the LORD said to him, “Go up.” And David said, “Where shall I go up?” And He said, “To Hebron.”  
**2:2** And David went up there, and his two wives as well, Ahinoam the Jezreelite and Abigail wife of Nabal the Carmelite.  
**2:3** And his men who were with him David brought up, each man and his household, and they settled in Hebron.  
**2:4** aAnd the men of Judah came and anointed David there as king over the house of Judah. 4bAnd they told David, saying, “It was the men of Jabesh-Gilead who buried Saul.”  
**2:5** And David sent messengers to the men of Jabesh-Gilead and said to them, “Blessed are you to the LORD, that you have done this kindness with your lord, with Saul, and have buried him.  
**2:6** And now, may the LORD show faithful kindness to you, and I on my part as well shall do this bounty for you because you have done this thing.  
**2:7** And now, may your hands be strengthened and be you men of valor, for your lord Saul is dead, and it is I whom the house of Judah has anointed as king over them.”  
**2:8** And Abner son of Ner commander of Saul’s army had taken Ish-Bosheth son of Saul and brought him over to Mahanaim  
**2:9** and made him king over Gilead and over the Asherite and over Jezreel and over Ephraim and over Benjamin and over Israel altogether.  
**2:10** Forty years old was Ish-Bosheth when he became king over Israel, and two years did he reign. But the house of Judah followed David.  
**2:11** And the span of time that David was king in Hebron over the house of Judah was seven years and six months.  
**2:12** And Abner son of Ner with the servants of Ish-Bosheth sallied forth from Mahanaim to Gibeon.  
**2:13** And Joab son of Zeruiah and the servants of David had sallied forth, and they met each other by the pool of Gibeon, and they took up their positions on either side of the pool.  
**2:14** And Abner said to Joab, “Pray, let the lads arise and play before us.” And Joab said, “Let them arise.”  
**2:15** And they arose and crossed over—twelve in number for Benjamin and for Ish-Bosheth son of Saul and twelve from the servants of David.  
**2:16** And each man grasped the head of the other with his sword at the side of the other, and they fell together. And they called that place the Field of Flints, which is in Gibeon.  
**2:17** And the fighting was very fierce on that day, and Abner with the men of Israel were routed by the servants of David.  
**2:18** And the three sons of Zeruiah were there, Joab and Abishai and Asahel. And Asahel was as swift-footed as one of the gazelles of the open field.  
**2:19** And Asahel chased after Abner, and he swerved not to the right or left in going after Abner.  
**2:20** And Abner turned round and said, “Are you Asahel?” And he said, “I am.”  
**2:21** And Abner said to him, “Swerve you to your right or your left and seize for yourself one of the lads, and take you his armor.” But Asahel did not want to turn away from him.  
**2:22** And Abner once more said to Asahel, “Turn you away from me. Why should I strike you to the ground, and how would I show my face to Joab your brother?”  
**2:23** And he refused to turn aside, and Abner struck him in the belly with the butt of the spear and the spear came out behind him, and he fell there and died on the spot. And it happened that whoever came to the place where Asahel fell and died, stood still.  
**2:24** And Joab and Abishai chased after Abner, and as the sun was setting, they had come to the hill of Ammah, which faces Giah on the way to the wilderness of Gibeon.  
**2:25** And the Benjaminites gathered behind Abner and formed a single band, and they took a stance on the top of a certain hill.  
**2:26** And Abner called out to Joab and said, “Must the sword devour forever? You surely know that it will be bitterness in the end. And how long will you not say to the troops to turn back from their brothers?”  
**2:27** And Joab said, “As God lives, had you but spoken, from this morning the troops would have given up pursuit of their brothers.”  
**2:28** And Joab sounded the ram’s horn, and all the troops halted, and they no longer chased after Israel, and they fought no more.  
**2:29** And Abner and his men went all that night through the Arabah, and they crossed the Jordan and went all the way through the ravine and came to Mahanaim.  
**2:30** And Joab had turned back from pursuing Abner, and he gathered together all the troops, and nineteen of David’s servants were missing, and Asahel.  
**2:31** And David’s servants had struck down from Benjamin and from Abner’s men three hundred and sixty men.  
**2:32** And they bore off Asahel and buried him in his father’s grave, which is in Bethlehem, and Joab and his men went all night long, and day brightened for them in Hebron.  

## 3장

**3:1** And the fighting between the house of Saul and the house of David went on a long time, and David grew stronger and the house of Saul weaker and weaker.  
**3:2** And sons were born to David in Hebron. And his firstborn was Amnon, by Ahinoam the Jezreelite.  
**3:3** And the second was Chileab, by Abigail wife of Nabal the Carmelite. And the third was Absalom son of Maachah daughter of Talmai king of Geshur.  
**3:4** And the fourth was Adonijah son of Haggith. And the fifth was Shephatiah son of Abital.  
**3:5** And the sixth was Ithream, by Eglah wife of David. These were born to David in Hebron.  
**3:6** And it happened during the fighting between the house of Saul and the house of David that Abner kept growing in strength in the house of Saul. And Saul had a concubine named Rizpah daughter of Aiah.  
**3:7** And Ish-Bosheth said to Abner, “Why did you come to bed with my father’s concubine?”  
**3:8** And Abner was very incensed over the words of Ish-Bosheth, and he said, “Am I a dog’s head attached to Judah? Today I have kept faith with the house of Saul your father, with his kinsmen and his companions, in not handing you over to David, and you dare reproach me with guilt over the woman today?  
**3:9** Thus may God do to Abner, and even more, for as the LORD has sworn to David, just so will I do for him—  
**3:10** to transfer the kingship from the house of Saul and to set up the throne of David over Israel and over Judah from Dan to Beersheba!”  
**3:11** And he could say back not a word more to Abner in his fear of him.  
**3:12** And Abner sent messengers to David in his stead, saying, “To whom should the land belong? Make a pact with me and, look, my hand will be with you to bring round to you all Israel.”  
**3:13** And he said, “Good. I shall make a pact with you. But one thing do I ask of you, namely, you shall not see my face until you bring Michal daughter of Saul when you come to see my face.”  
**3:14** And David sent messengers to Ish-Bosheth son of Saul, saying, “Give back my wife Michal, whom I betrothed with a hundred Philistine foreskins.”  
**3:15** And Ish-Bosheth sent and took her from her husband, from Paltiel son of Laish.  
**3:16** And her husband went with her, weeping as he went after her, as far as Bahurim. And Abner said to him, “Go back!” And he went back.  
**3:17** And Abner parlayed with the elders of Israel, saying, “Time and again in the past you sought to have David become king over you.  
**3:18** And now, act, for the LORD has said, ‘By the hand of David My servant will I rescue My people Israel from the hand of the Philistines and from the hand of all their enemies.’”  
**3:19** And Abner spoke as well in the hearing of Benjamin, and Abner went as well to speak in David’s hearing in Hebron all that was good in the eyes of Israel and in the eyes of Benjamin.  
**3:20** And Abner came to David in Hebron, and with him were twenty men. And David made a feast for Abner and for the men who were with him.  
**3:21** And Abner said to David, “Let me rise and go and gather to my lord the king all Israel, that they may make a pact with you, and you shall reign over all your heart desires.” And David sent Abner off, and he went in peace.  
**3:22** And look, David’s servants and Joab had come from a raid, and abundant booty they brought with them, and Abner was not with David in Hebron, for he had sent him off, and he went in peace.  
**3:23** And Joab and all the force that was with him had come, and they told Joab, saying, “Abner son of Ner has come to the king, and he sent him off, and he went in peace.”  
**3:24** And Joab came to the king and said, “What have you done? Look, Abner has come to you! Why did you send him off, and he went, going off?  
**3:25** You know that Abner son of Ner to dupe you has come and to learn your comings and goings and to learn all that you do.”  
**3:26** And Joab went out from David’s presence and sent messengers after Abner, and they brought him back from the cistern of Sirah, and David did not know.  
**3:27** And Abner came back to Hebron, and Joab drew him aside into the gate to speak with him deceptively, and he struck him there in the belly, and he died for the blood of Asahel, Joab’s brother.  
**3:28** And David heard afterward and said, “Innocent am I, and my kingship, before the LORD for all time of the blood of Abner son of Ner!  
**3:29** May the bloodguilt come down on the head of Joab and all his father’s house, and may there never lack in the house of Joab a sufferer of discharge from his member and running sores on his skin and a man clutching the woman’s spindle, and one falling by the sword and one wanting for bread!”  
**3:30** And Joab and Abishai his brother had lain in wait for Abner because he put to death Asahel their brother in Gibeon in the fighting.  
**3:31** And David said to Joab and to all the troops who were with him, “Tear your garments and gird on sackcloth and keen for Abner.” And King David was walking behind the bier.  
**3:32** And they buried Abner in Hebron. And the king raised his voice and wept over the grave of Abner, and all the people wept.  
**3:33** And the king lamented over Abner and said,<br>“Like the death of the base<br>should Abner have died?  
**3:34** Your hands—never bound,<br>your feet never placed in fetters!<br>As one falls before scoundrels you fell.”  
**3:35** And all the people continued to weep over him. And all the people came to give David bread to eat while it was still day, and David swore, saying, “Thus and more may God do to me, if before the sun sets I taste bread or anything at all.”  
**3:36** And all the people took note and it was good in their eyes, all that the king had done was good in the eyes of the people.  
**3:37** And all the people and all Israel knew on that day that it had not been from the king to put to death Abner son of Ner.  
**3:38** And the king said to his servants, “You must know that a commander and a great man has fallen this day in Israel.  
**3:39** And I am gentle, and just anointed king, and these sons of Zeruiah are too hard for me. May the LORD pay back the evildoer according to his evil!”  

## 4장

**4:1** And the son of Saul heard that Abner had died in Hebron, and he was utterly shaken, and all Israel was dismayed.  
**4:2** And the son of Saul had two men, commanders of raiding parties, the name of the one was Baanah and of the other Rechab, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, who was of the Benjaminites, for Beeroth, too, was reckoned with Benjamin.  
**4:3** And the Beerothites fled to Gittaim and have been sojourners there till this day.  
**4:4** And Jonathan son of Saul had a lame son, five years old he was when the news of Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel. And his nurse bore him off and fled, and it happened in her haste to flee that he fell and was crippled. And his name was Mephibosheth.  
**4:5** And the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, Rechab and Baanah went and came in the heat of the day to the house of Ish-Bosheth as he was taking his midday rest.  
**4:6** And, look, the woman who kept the gate had been gleaning wheat, and nodded and fell asleep.  
**4:7** Andthey came into the house as he was lying in his bed in his bedchamber, and they struck him and killed him and cut off his head and took his head and went off through the Arabah all night long.  
**4:8** And they brought Ish-Bosheth’s head to David in Hebron and said to the king, “Here is the head of Ish-Bosheth son of Saul your enemy, who sought your life. The LORD has granted my lord the king vengeance this day against Saul and his seed.”  
**4:9** And David answered Rechab and Baanah his brother, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, and he said to them, “As the LORD lives, Who saved my life from every strait,  
**4:10** he who told me, saying ‘Look, Saul is dead,’ and thought he was a bearer of good tidings, I seized him and killed him in Ziklag instead of giving him something for his tidings.  
**4:11** How much more so when wicked men have killed an innocent man in his bed in his house, and so, will I not requite his blood from you and rid the land of you?”  
**4:12** And David commanded the lads, and they killed them and chopped off their hands and feet and hung them by the pool in Hebron. And Ish-Bosheth’s head they took and buried in the grave of Abner in Hebron.  

## 5장

**5:1** And all the tribes of Israel came to David in Hebron, and they said, “Here we are, your bone and your flesh are we.  
**5:2** Time and again in the past when Saul was king over us you were the one who led Israel into the fray, and the LORD said to you,<br>‘It is you who will shepherd My people Israel<br>and it is you who will be prince over Israel.’”  
**5:3** And all the elders of Israel came to the king in Hebron, and King David made a pact with them in Hebron before the LORD and they anointed David as king over Israel.  
**5:4** Thirty years old was David when he became king, forty years he was king.  
**5:5** In Hebron he was king over Judah seven years and six months, and in Jerusalem he was king thirty-three years over all Israel and Judah.  
**5:6** And the king went, and his men with him, to Jerusalem, to the Jebusite, the inhabitant of the land, and he said to David, saying, “You shall not enter here unless you can remove the blind and the lame,” which is to say, “David shall not enter here!”  
**5:7** And David captured the stronghold of Zion, which is the City of David.  
**5:8** And David said on that day, “Whoever strikes down the Jebusite and reaches the conduit and the lame and the blind utterly despised by David . . .” Therefore do they say, “No blind man nor lame shall enter the House.”  
**5:9** And David stayed in the stronghold and called it the City of David, and David built round the rampart and within.  
**5:10** And David grew greater and greater, and the LORD God of Armies was with him.  
**5:11** And Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David with cedarwood and carpenters and stonemasons, and they built a house for David.  
**5:12** And David knew that the LORD had set him up unshaken as king over Israel and had exalted his kingship for the sake of His people Israel.  
**5:13** And David took other concubines and wives from Jerusalem after coming from Hebron, and other sons and daughters were born to David.  
**5:14** And these are the names of those born to him in Jerusalem: Shammua and Shobab and Nathan and Solomon,  
**5:15** and Ibhar and Elishua and Nepheg and Japhia,  
**5:16** and Elishama and Eliada and Eliphelet.  
**5:17** And the Philistines heard that David had been anointed as king over Israel, and all the Philistines came up to seek David. And David heard and went down to the stronghold.  
**5:18** The Philistines had come and deployed in the Valley of Rephaim.  
**5:19** And David inquired of the LORD, saying, “Shall I go up against the Philistines? Will You give them into my hand?” And the LORD said, “Go up, for I will surely give the Philistines into your hand.”  
**5:20** And David came into Baal-Perazim, and David struck them down there, and he said, “The LORD has burst through my enemies before me like a bursting of water!” Therefore did he call the name of that place Baal-Perazim.  
**5:21** And they abandoned their idols there, and David with his men bore them off.  
**5:22** And once more the Philistines came up and deployed in the Valley of Rephaim.  
**5:23** And David inquired of the LORD, and He said, “You shall not go up. Turn around behind them and come at them from opposite the willows.  
**5:24** And as soon as you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the willows, then you must move boldly, for then shall the LORD go out before you to strike down the camp of the Philistines.”  
**5:25** And David did just as the LORD had commanded him, and he struck down the Philistines from Geba till you come to Gezer.  

## 6장

**6:1** And David gathered again all the picked men of Israel, thirty thousand.  
**6:2** And David arose and went, and all the troops who were with him, to Baalah in Judah to bring up from there the Ark of God, over which the name of the LORD of Armies enthroned on the cherubim is called.  
**6:3** And they mounted the Ark of God on a new cart and carried it off from the house of Abinadab, which is on the Hill, and Uzza and Ahio, the sons of Abinadab, were driving the cart,  
**6:4** and Ahio was walking before the Ark.  
**6:5** And David and the whole house of Israel were playing before the LORD with all their might in song on lyres and tambourines and castanets and cymbals.  
**6:6** And they came to the threshing floor of Nacon, and Uzza reached out to the Ark of God and took hold of it, for the oxen had slipped.  
**6:7** And the LORD’s wrath flared up against Uzza, and God struck him down there for reaching out his hand to the Ark, and he died there by the Ark of God.  
**6:8** And David was incensed because the LORD had burst out against Uzza. And that place has been called Perez-Uzza to this day.  
**6:9** And David was afraid of the LORD on that day and he said, “How can the Ark of the LORD come to me?”  
**6:10** And David did not want to remove the Ark of the LORD to himself in the City of David, and David had it turned aside to the house of Obed-Edom the Gittite.  
**6:11** And the Ark of the LORD remained in the house of Obed-Edom three months and the LORD blessed Obed-Edom and all his house.  
**6:12** And it was told to King David, saying, “The LORD has blessed the house of Obed-Edom and all that he has on account of the Ark of God.” And David went and brought up the Ark of God from the house of Obed-Edom to the City of David with rejoicing.  
**6:13** And it happened when the bearers of the Ark of the LORD had taken six steps that he sacrificed a fatted bull.  
**6:14** And David was whirling with all his might before the LORD, girt in a linen ephod.  
**6:15** And David and the whole house of Israel were bringing up the Ark of the LORD in shouts and with the sound of the ram’s horn.  
**6:16** And as the Ark of the LORD came into the City of David, Michal daughter of Saul looked out through the window and saw King David leaping and whirling before the LORD, and she scorned him in her heart.  
**6:17** And they brought the Ark of the LORD and set it up in its place within the tent that David had pitched for it, and David offered up burnt offerings before the LORD and well-being sacrifices.  
**6:18** And David finished offering up the burnt offerings and the well-being sacrifices, and he blessed the people in the name of the LORD of Hosts.  
**6:19** And he shared out to all the people, to all the multitude of Israel, every man and woman, one loaf of bread and one date cake and one raisin cake, and every one of the people went to his home.  
**6:20** And David turned back to bless his house. And Michal daughter of Saul came out to meet David, and she said, “How honored today is the king of Israel who has exposed himself today to the eyes of his servants’ slavegirls as some scurrilous fellow would expose himself!”  
**6:21** And David said to Michal, “Before the LORD, Who chose me instead of your father and instead of all his house, to appoint me prince over the LORD’S people, over Israel, I will play before the LORD!  
**6:22** And I will be dishonored still more than this and will be debased in my own eyes! But with the slavegirls about whom you spoke, with them let me be honored!”  
**6:23** And Michal daughter of Saul had no child till her dying day.  

## 7장

**7:1** And it happened when the king was dwelling in his house and the LORD had granted him respite all around from his enemies,  
**7:2** that the king said to Nathan the prophet, “See, pray, I dwell in a cedarwood house while the Ark of God dwells within curtains.”  
**7:3** And Nathan said to the king, “Whatever is in your heart, go, do, for the LORD is with you.”  
**7:4** And it happened on that night, that the word of the LORD came to Nathan, saying,  
**7:5** “Go, say to My servant, to David, ‘Thus says the LORD: Is it you who would build Me a house for Me to dwell in?  
**7:6** For I have dwelled in no house from the day I brought up the Israelites out of Egypt until this day, but I have gone about in tent and tabernacle.  
**7:7** Wherever I went about among all the Israelites, did I speak a word with any of the tribal chiefs of Israel whom I charged to shepherd My people Israel, saying, Why did you not build me a cedarwood house?  
**7:8** And now, thus shall you say to My servant, to David, Thus says the LORD of Hosts: I Myself took you from the pasture, from following the flocks, to be prince over My people, over Israel.  
**7:9** And I have been with you wherever you have gone, and I have cut down your enemies before you. And I will make you a great name, like the name of the great of the earth.  
**7:10** And I will set aside a place for My people, for Israel, and plant them, and they shall abide there and no longer quake, and the wicked shall no more afflict them as before,  
**7:11** from the day that I appointed judges over My people Israel. And I will grant you respite from all your enemies, and the LORD declares that it is He Who will make you a house.  
**7:12** When your days are full and you lie with your fathers, I will raise up your seed after you, who will issue from your loins, and I will make his kingship unshaken.  
**7:13** He it is who will build a house for My name, and I will make the throne of his kingship unshaken forever.  
**7:14** I will be a father to him and he will be a son to Me, so should he do wrong, I will chastise him with the rod men use and with the afflictions of humankind.  
**7:15** But My loyalty shall not swerve from him as I made it swerve from Saul whom I removed from before you.  
**7:16** And your house and your kingship shall be steadfast forever, your throne unshaken forever.’”  
**7:17** In accordance with all these words and in accordance with all this vision, so did Nathan speak to David.  
**7:18** And King David came and sat before the LORD and said, “Who am I, LORD God, and what is my house, that you have brought me this far?  
**7:19** And even this is too little in Your eyes, LORD God, for You have also spoken of Your servant’s house in distant time, and this is a man’s instruction, LORD God.  
**7:20** And how can David speak more to You, when You know Your servant, LORD God?  
**7:21** For the sake of Your word, and according to Your heart You have done all these great things, to make known to Your servant.  
**7:22** Therefore are You great, O LORD God, for there is none like You and there is no god beside You, in all we have heard with our own ears.  
**7:23** And who is like Your people Israel, a unique nation upon earth, whom a god has gone out to redeem as a people to make Him a name and to do great and awesome things for them, to drive out from before Your people whom You redeemed from Egypt nations with their gods?  
**7:24** And You made Your own people Israel unshaken forever, and You, O LORD, became their God.  
**7:25** And now LORD God, the word that You have spoken to Your servant concerning his house—make it stand forever and do as You have spoken.  
**7:26** And may Your name be great forever, so it be said, ‘The LORD of Armies is God over Israel, and the house of David Your servant shall be unshaken before You.’  
**7:27** For You, O LORD of Armies, God of Israel, have revealed to Your servant, saying, ‘A house will I build you.’ Therefore has Your servant found the heart to pray to you this prayer.

## 17장

## 18장

## 19장
