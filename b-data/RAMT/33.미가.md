# 33.미가 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Micah the Morashtite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw in visions concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.  
**1:2** Listen, all you peoples,<br>hearken, earth and its fullness,<br>that the Master, the LORD, be witness against you forever,<br>the Master from His holy temple.  
**1:3** For the LORD is about to come out from His place,<br>and go down and tread on earth’s high places,  
**1:4** and the mountains shall melt beneath Him<br>and the valleys split open,<br>like wax before the fire,<br>like water pouring down a slope.  
**1:5** For Jacob’s trespass all this has happened<br>and for the house of Israel’s offenses.<br>What is Jacob’s trespass<br>if not Samaria?<br>And what Judah’s high places<br>if not Jerusalem?  
**1:6** And I will make Samaria a ruined heap in the field,<br>a place for the planting of vineyards.<br>And I will pour down her stones to the valley<br>and her foundations I will lay bare.  
**1:7** And all her idols shall be shattered,<br>and all her whore’s pay burned in fire,<br>and all her icons I will make a desolation.<br>For from a whore’s pay she amassed it,<br>and to a whore’s pay it shall revert.  
**1:8** For this would I lament and would wail,<br>I would go naked and bare.<br>I would raise a lament like the jackals<br>and mourning like the ostriches.  
**1:9** For grievous is her wound,<br>for it has come as far as Judah,<br>has reached My people’s gate,<br>as far as Jerusalem.  
**1:10** Tell it not in Gath,<br>surely do not weep.<br>In Beth-Leaphrah<br>wallow in the dust.  
**1:11** Pass on, you who dwell in Shapier<br>Did not Zaanan’s dweller go out in shame?<br>Lament in Beth-Ezel.<br>He shall take his station from you.  
**1:12** Though the dweller of Maroth<br>had hoped for good,<br>evil came down from the LORD<br>to the gate of Jerusalem.  
**1:13** Harness the steed to the chariot,<br>you who dwell in Lachish.<br>It is the source of offense for Zion’s Daughter<br>for in you are found the trespasses of Israel.  
**1:14** Therefore give parting gifts<br>for Moresheth-Gath.<br>Achziv’s houses betray<br>the kings of Israel.  
**1:15** Yet will I bring to you the dispossessor,<br>you who dwell in Mareshah.<br>As far as Adullam he shall come—<br>the glory of Israel.  
**1:16** Shave the pate and shear your hair<br>over your pampered children.<br>Make yourself bald as an eagle,<br>for they are gone from you into exile.  

## 2장

**2:1** Woe, who plot crime<br>and work evil on their couches.<br>In the morn they do it,<br>for they have the power.  
**2:2** They covet fields and rob them,<br>houses, and bear them away.<br>They exploit a man and his home,<br>a person and his estate.  
**2:3** Therefore, Thus said the LORD: I am about to plot evil against this clan, that you shall not be able to pull out your necks from it, and you shall not walk with high heads, for it is an evil time.  
**2:4** On that day this theme shall be sounded about you, and sobbing shall be sobbed. One shall say:<br>Ravaged, we are ravaged.<br>My people’s portion is passed to another.<br>How it is taken away from me,<br>to a miscreant our fields are shared out!  
**2:5** Therefore there shall not be among you any who casts a lot by cord in the LORD’s assembly.  
**2:6** “Do not preach—they preach.<br>They shall not preach these things.<br>Shame shall not overtake us.  
**2:7** What is said of the house of Jacob—<br>will the LORD’s patience be short,<br>are these His acts?<br>Will not His words do well<br>with him who goes upright?”  
**2:8** But against My people<br>an enemy arises.<br>From him without cloak<br>you strip the mantle,<br>from those who pass by thinking themselves safe<br>returning from the war.  
**2:9** My people’s wives you drive out<br>from their luxurious homes.<br>From their infants you take away<br>My glory for all times.  
**2:10** Rise up and go,<br>for this is not a resting place.<br>Because she has defiled herself,<br>dire destruction shall descend.  
**2:11** Were a man to go<br>after wind and cheating lies,<br>“I would preach to you<br>for wine and for strong drink.”<br>He would be this people’s preacher.  
**2:12** I will surely assemble Jacob, all of you.<br>I will surely gather Israel’s remnant.<br>I will make him like sheep in the pen,<br>like a flock within the fold.<br>They shall bustle with people.  
**2:13** He who makes the breach shall go up before them,<br>they shall break out and pass through the gate and go out there.<br>And their king shall pass through before them,<br>the LORD at their head.  

## 3장

**3:1** And I said:<br>Listen, pray, chieftains of Jacob<br>and captains of the house of Israel.<br>Is it not yours<br>to know what is right?  
**3:2** Haters of good and lovers of evil,<br>who flay their skin from them<br>and their flesh from their bones.  
**3:3** Who devour My people’s flesh<br>and strip their skin from them<br>and crack open their bones.<br>And they cut it like flesh in the pot<br>and like meat in the cauldron.  
**3:4** Then shall they cry to the LORD,<br>and He shall not answer them.<br>He shall hide His face from them at that time<br>as they did evil through their acts.  
**3:5** Thus said the LORD concerning the prophets who lead My people astray; who bite with their teeth and proclaim peace, and as to him who gives nothing for their mouths, they declare war against him.  
**3:6** Therefore shall it be night for your vision<br>and darkness for you for divining,<br>and the sun shall set on the prophets,<br>and the day turn to gloom upon them.  
**3:7** And the seers shall be shamed<br>and the diviners disgraced,<br>and all of them cover their moustaches,<br>for there shall be no response from God.  
**3:8** And yet, I have been filled with power,<br>with the spirit of the LORD,<br>with justice and valor,<br>to tell to Jacob his trespass<br>and to Israel his offense.  
**3:9** Hear this, pray, chieftains of the house of Jacob<br>and captains of the house of Israel,<br>who despise justice<br>and everything straight they twist,  
**3:10** Who build Zion with bloodshed<br>and Jerusalem with wickedness.  
**3:11** Her chieftains judge with bribes,<br>and her priests instruct for payment,<br>and her prophets divine for silver<br>and on the LORD they lean, saying,<br>“Is not the LORD in our midst?<br>No harm will come upon us.”  
**3:12** Therefore, because of you,<br>Zion shall be plowed like a field,<br>and Jerusalem become heaps of ruins<br>and the Temple mount a high forest.  

## 4장

**4:1** And it shall happen in future days<br>that the mount of the LORD’s house shall be firm-founded<br>at the top of the mountains and lifted over the hills.<br>And the people shall flow to it,  
**4:2** and many nations shall go and say:<br>Come, let us go up to the mount of the LORD,<br>and to the house of Jacob’s God,<br>that He may teach us of His ways<br>and that we may walk in His paths.<br>For from Zion shall teaching come forth<br>and the LORD’s word from Jerusalem.  
**4:3** And He shall judge among many peoples<br>and be arbiter to vast nations from far away.<br>And they shall grind their swords into plowshares<br>and their spears into pruning hooks.<br>Nation shall not raise sword against nation,<br>nor shall they learn war anymore.  
**4:4** And they shall dwell each man beneath his vine<br>and beneath his fig tree, with none to make him tremble,<br>for the mouth of the LORD of Armies has spoken.  
**4:5** For all the peoples shall walk<br>each in the name of his god.<br>But we shall walk<br>in the name of the LORD our God<br>forevermore.  
**4:6** On that day, said the LORD:<br>I will gather the lame one,<br>and the outcast I will take in<br>and to whom I did harm.  
**4:7** And I will make the lame one a remnant<br>and the failing one a vast nation,<br>and the LORD shall reign over them<br>on Mount Zion, from hence and forever.  
**4:8** And you, watchtower of the flock,<br>rampart of Zion’s Daughter,<br>to you shall come the former kingdom,<br>and the kingship of Jerusalem’s Daughter shall arrive.  
**4:9** Now, why should you scream so loud?<br>Is there no king in you?<br>Is your councillor gone,<br>that pangs seize you like a woman in labor?  
**4:10** Writhe and groan,<br>Zion’s Daughter, like a woman in labor,<br>for now will you go from the city<br>and dwell in the field.<br>And you shall come as far as Babylonia.<br>There you shall be saved.<br>There shall the LORD redeem you<br>from the clutch of your enemies.  
**4:11** And now many nations<br>have gathered against you, saying:<br>“Let her be tainted, that we may gloat over Zion.”  
**4:12** But they do not know<br>the plans of the LORD,<br>and they do not understand His counsel,<br>for He has gathered them like a sheaf on the threshing floor.  
**4:13** Rise and thresh, Zion’s Daughter!<br>For I will make your horn like iron<br>and your hooves I will make like bronze,<br>and you shall grind down many peoples,<br>and I will devote to the LORD their riches<br>and their wealth to all the earth’s Master.  
**4:14** Now gash yourself, gashing’s Daughter<br>they have laid a siege against us.<br>With a rod they strike on the cheek<br>the judge of Israel.  

## 5장

**5:1** And you, Bethlehem of Ephrath,<br>the least of Judah’s clans,<br>from you shall one come forth for Me<br>to be ruler of Israel<br>whose origins are from ancient times,<br>from days of yore.  
**5:2** Therefore shall He give them over<br>till the time the woman in labor bears her child,<br>and the rest of his brothers shall come back<br>with the Israelites.  
**5:3** And he shall stand and shepherd them by the might of the LORD,<br>by the pride of the name of the LORD his God.<br>And they shall dwell secure,<br>for then shall he be great to the ends of the earth.  
**5:4** And thus shall be the peace:<br>Assyria shall not enter our land<br>nor tread in our citadels.<br>And we shall set up against him seven shepherds<br>and eight princes of the peoples,  
**5:5** and they shall smash the land of Assyria with the sword<br>and the land of Nimrod in its gateways.<br>And they shall save us from Assyria<br>should he enter our land<br>and should he tread within our borders.  
**5:6** And the remnant of Jacob shall be<br>in the midst of many peoples<br>like the dew from the LORD,<br>like gentle rain upon the grass,<br>as he shall not place hope in man<br>nor expectation in humankind.  
**5:7** And the remnant of Jacob shall be<br>in the midst of many peoples<br>like a lion among forest beasts,<br>like a young lion among the flocks of sheep<br>that passes through and tramples<br>and tears apart with none to save.  
**5:8** Your hand shall loom over your foes<br>and all your enemies be cut off.  
**5:9** And it shall happen on that day, said the LORD:<br>I will cut off your horses from your midst,<br>and I will destroy your chariots.  
**5:10** And I will cut off the towns of your land<br>and reduce to ruins all your fortresses.  
**5:11** And I will cut off sorcery from your hands,<br>nor soothsayers shall you have.  
**5:12** And I will cut off your idols<br>and your cultic pillars from your midst.<br>And no more shall you bow down<br>to the work of your hands.  
**5:13** And I will uproot your cultic poles from your midst,<br>and I will destroy your icons.  
**5:14** And I will act in anger and in vengeful wrath<br>toward the nations that did not heed.  

## 6장

**6:1** Listen, pray, to what the LORD has said.<br>Rise, plead a case before the mountains,<br>and let the hills hear your voice.  
**6:2** Hear, O mountains, the case of the LORD,<br>and you mighty pillars of the earth.<br>For the LORD has a case against His people,<br>and with Israel He would dispute.  
**6:3** “My people, what have I done to you,<br>and in what did I do you in? Testify against Me!  
**6:4** For I brought you up from the land of Egypt,<br>and from the house of slaves I redeemed you,<br>and sent before you<br>Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.  
**6:5** My people, recall, pray, what Balak king of Moab devised<br>and what Balaam son of Beor answered him,<br>from Shittim to Gilgal,<br>that the LORD’s bounties would be known.”  
**6:6** With what shall I come before the LORD<br>bow to the most high God?<br>Shall I come before Him with burnt offerings,<br>with yearling calves?  
**6:7** Is the LORD pleased with thousands of rams,<br>myriads of streams of oil?<br>Shall I give Him my firstborn for my trespass,<br>the fruit of my loins for my offense?  
**6:8** It was told to you, man, what is good<br>and what the LORD demands of you—<br>only doing justice and loving kindness<br>and walking humbly with your God.  
**6:9** The voice of the LORD calls out to the town,<br>and a man of insight shall see Your name.<br>Heed the rod—and who brought it about?  
**6:10** Can there yet be in the house of the wicked<br>treasures of wickedness<br>and an accursed short ephah?  
**6:11** Could I declare innocent who has wicked scales<br>and in his pouch cheating weight-stones?  
**6:12** Whose rich are filled with outrage<br>and those who dwell in her speak lies<br>and their tongue in their mouth is cheating.  
**6:13** And I on My part have made you ill, have smitten you,<br>made you desolate for your offenses.  
**6:14** As for you, you shall eat and not be sated<br>and your filth shall be within you.<br>She will conceive and not give birth<br>and what she bears, to the sword I will give.  
**6:15** As for you, you shall sow and not reap.<br>You, you shall trample the olives and not use the oil,<br>get fermented juice and not drink the wine.  
**6:16** And the practices of Omri are kept<br>and all the acts of the house of Ahab,<br>and you go by their counsels,<br>so that I turn you into a desolation<br>and those who dwell in her into hissing,<br>and My people’s disgrace you shall bear.  

## 7장

**7:1** Alas for me,<br>as I have become like the leavings of summer fruit,<br>like the last gleanings of the vintage—<br>there is no cluster for eating,<br>no ripe figs that my palate has longed for.  
**7:2** The faithful has vanished from the land,<br>and the upright among men is gone.<br>They all lie in wait to shed blood,<br>each man hunts his brother with a net.  
**7:3** For evil their hands are skilled.<br>The noble and the judge ask payment.<br>The great one speaks the disaster he wants,<br>and they pervert it for him.  
**7:4** Their good is like a thornbush,<br>no straighter than a hedge.<br>The day those who look for You, of Your reckoning, has come.<br>Now shall their confounding take place.  
**7:5** Do not trust in a friend,<br>nor place confidence in a leader.<br>From her who lies in your lap<br>guard your lips.  
**7:6** For the son reviles the father,<br>the daughter rises against her mother,<br>the daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law—<br>a man’s enemies are the people of his household.  
**7:7** But I look for You,<br>I await the God of my rescue<br>May the LORD hear me.  
**7:8** Rejoice not, O my enemy.<br>Though I fall, I will arise.<br>Though I sit in darkness,<br>the LORD is a light for me.  
**7:9** The LORD’s wrath I will bear<br>for I have offended against Him,<br>till He takes up my case<br>and renders Me justice.<br>He shall bring me out to the light,<br>I will see His vindication.  
**7:10** And my enemy shall see<br>and shame shall cover her,<br>who has said to me,<br>“Where is the LORD your God?”<br>I will gloat over her—<br>now she shall be trampled<br>like the mud of the streets.  
**7:11** The day for rebuilding your walls,<br>that day the borders shall widen,  
**7:12** that day—and to you shall it come,<br>from Assyria and the towns of Egypt<br>and from Egypt to the Euphrates<br>and from sea to sea and mountain to mountain.  
**7:13** And the earth shall become a desolation<br>with its dwellers, as the fruit of their acts.  
**7:14** Shepherd Your people with Your staff,<br>the flock of Your estate,<br>that dwells secure in the forest<br>in the midst of the farmland.<br>They shall feed in Bashan and Gilead<br>as in days of yore.  
**7:15** As the days when you came out from the land of Egypt,<br>I will show him wonders.  
**7:16** Nations shall see and be shamed<br>of all their valor.<br>They shall put hand over mouth,<br>their ears shall be deafened.  
**7:17** They shall lick the dust like a snake,<br>like the crawlers on the ground.<br>They shall be shaken out from their enclosures<br>to the LORD our God.<br>They shall dread and fear You.  
**7:18** Who is a God like You dismissing crime<br>and forgiving trespass for the remnant of His estate?<br>He does not cling forever to His wrath,<br>for He desires kindness.  
**7:19** Again He shall have mercy on us.<br>he shall cleanse our crimes.<br>And You shall fling into the depths of the sea<br>all our offenses.  
**7:20** Grant truth to Jacob,<br>kindness to Abraham,<br>as You swore to our fathers<br>in ancient days.
