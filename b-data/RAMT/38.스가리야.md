# 38.스가리야 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** In the seventh month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah the prophet son of Berechiah son of Iddo, saying,  
**1:2** “The LORD was very furious with your fathers.  
**1:3** And you shall say to them, Thus said the LORD of Armies: Turn back to Me, said the LORD of Armies, that I may turn back to you, said the LORD of Armies.  
**1:4** Do not be like your fathers to whom the former prophets called saying, Thus said the LORD of Armies: Turn back, pray, from your evil ways and from your evil acts. But they did not listen to Me, said the LORD.  
**1:5** Your fathers, where are they?” “And the prophets, did they live forever?”  
**1:6** “But My words and My statutes that I charged to My servants the prophets, did they not overtake your fathers?” And they turned back and said, “As the LORD of Armies aimed to do to us according to our ways and according to our acts, so He has done with us.”  
**1:7** On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, which is the month of Shebat, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah the prophet son of Berechiah son of Iddo, saying,  
**1:8** “I saw at night, and, look, there was a man riding on a bay horse, and he was standing among the myrtles that are by the deep, and behind him were bay, sorrel, and white horses.  
**1:9** And I said, ‘What are these my lord?’ And the messenger speaking to me answered, ‘I shall show you what these are.’  
**1:10** And the man standing among the myrtles answered and said, ‘It is these that the LORD has sent to go about the earth.’  
**1:11** And they answered the LORD’s messenger who was standing among the myrtles and they said, ‘We have gone about the earth, and, look, all the earth dwells tranquilly.’  
**1:12** And the LORD’s messenger answered and said, ‘O LORD of Armies, how long will You show no mercy to Jerusalem and to the towns of Judah against which You have been wrathful now seventy years?’  
**1:13** And the LORD answered the messenger who was speaking to me good words, comforting words.  
**1:14** And the messenger who was speaking to me said to me, ‘Call out, saying, Thus said the LORD of Armies: I have been greatly zealous for Jerusalem and for Zion.  
**1:15** And I am very greatly furious with the nations resting quiet; as I was but a little furious yet they piled on the harm.  
**1:16** Therefore, thus said the LORD, I have turned back in mercy to Jerusalem, and a building line shall be stretched over Jerusalem. My house shall be built in her.’”  
**1:17** Call out again, saying, Thus said the LORD of Armies: “Again shall my towns spread out from bounty, and again shall the LORD comfort Zion, and again shall He choose Jerusalem.”  

## 2장

**2:1** And I raised my eyes and saw and look, there were four horns.  
**2:2** And I said to the messenger speaking to me, “What are these?” And he said to me, “These are the horns that scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”  
**2:3** And the LORD showed me four smiths.  
**2:4** And I said, “What have these come to do?” And he said, saying, “These are the horns that scattered Judah, so no man could raise his head, and these have come to rattle them, to hurl down the horns of the nations that bore horns against the land of Judah to scatter it.”  
**2:5** And I raised my eyes and saw, and, look, there was a man and in his hand a measuring cord.  
**2:6** And I said, “Where are you going?” And he said to me, “To measure Jerusalem, to see what is its width and what is its length.”  
**2:7** And, look, the messenger speaking to me was going out and another messenger was coming out to meet him.  
**2:8** And he said to him, “Run, speak to that lad, saying: ‘Jerusalem shall dwell as an unwalled city because of all the people and beasts within her.  
**2:9** And I will be for her, said the LORD, a wall of fire all around, and for glory will I be within her.  
**2:10** Away, away, flee from the land of the north, said the LORD, for like the four corners of the heavens did I spread you out, said the LORD.  
**2:11** Away, Zion, escape, you who dwell with the Daughter of Babylon.’  
**2:12** For thus said the LORD of Armies Whose glory sent me to the nations that despoil you. For he who touches you touches the apple of His eye.  
**2:13** For I am about to swing My hand against them, and they shall be spoil for their slaves, and you shall know that the LORD of Armies sent me.  
**2:14** Sing gladly and rejoice, Daughter of Zion, for I am coming and will abide in your midst, said the LORD.  
**2:15** And many nations shall join the LORD on that day and become My people, and I will abide in your midst, and you shall know that the LORD of Armies sent me to you.  
**2:16** And the LORD shall bestow upon Judah its portion on the holy soil and again choose Jerusalem.  
**2:17** Hush, all flesh before the LORD, for He has stirred from His holy abode!”  

## 3장

**3:1** And he showed me Joshua the high priest standing before a messenger of the LORD, and the Adversary was standing on his right to accuse him.  
**3:2** And the LORD said to the Adversary, “The LORD rebuke you, Adversary, the LORD, Who has chosen Jerusalem, rebuke you. Is not this a brand saved from the fire?  
**3:3** And Joshua was clothed in foul garments and was standing before the messenger.  
**3:4** And he spoke out and said before all those standing before him, saying, “Remove the foul garments from him.” And he said to him, “See, I have taken away your guilt from you and dressed you in fine raiment.”  
**3:5** And he said, “Let them put a pure diadem on his head.” And they put a pure diadem on his head and dressed him in fit garments with the LORD’s messenger standing by.  
**3:6** And the LORD’s messenger warned Joshua, saying,  
**3:7** “Thus said the LORD of Armies: If you walk in My ways and if you keep My watch, and also if you oversee My house and also guard My courts, I will let you come and go among these attendants.  
**3:8** Listen, pray, Joshua, high priest, you and your companions sitting before you, for they are men who have had a portent that I am about to bring My servant, Branch.  
**3:9** For, look, the stone that I set before Joshua, on a single stone are seven eyes. I am about to engrave its engraving, said the LORD of Armies, and I will wipe away the guilt of this land on a single day.  
**3:10** On that day, said the LORD of Armies, you shall invite, each man his fellow, to come under the vine and under the fig tree.”  

## 4장

**4:1** And the messenger speaking to me came back and woke me as a man wakes from his sleep.  
**4:2** And he said to me, “What do you see?” And I said, “I have seen, and, look, a lampstand all of gold and a bowl on its top, and its lamps were on it, seven pipes for the lamps on its top.  
**4:3** And there were two olive trees by it, one to the right of the bowl and one to its left.”  
**4:4** And I spoke out and said to the messenger speaking to me, saying, “What are these, my lord?”  
**4:5** And the messenger speaking to me answered and said, “Why, you know what these are.” And I said, “No, my lord.”  
**4:6** And he answered and said to me, saying, “This is the word of the LORD to Zerubbabel, saying, Not by might and not by power but by My spirit, said the LORD of Armies.  
**4:7** What are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel you become a plain. And he shall bring out the capstone—shouts of ‘lovely, lovely’ for it.”  
**4:8** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,  
**4:9** “Zerubbabel’s hands laid the foundation for this house, and his hands shall complete it, and you shall know that the LORD of Armies sent me to you.  
**4:10** For who has despised the day of small things? They shall rejoice and see the stone of separation in Zerubbabel’s hand. These seven are the eyes of the LORD that roam through all the earth.”  
**4:11** And I spoke out and said to him, “What are these two olive trees on the right of the lampstand and on its left?”  
**4:12** And I spoke out again and said to him, “What are these two branches of the olive trees that are by the golden tubes emptying out the gold?”  
**4:13** And he said to me, saying, “Why, you know what these are.” And I said, “No, my lord.”  
**4:14** And he said, “These are the two men consecrated with oil who attend upon the Master of all the earth.”  

## 5장

**5:1** And again I raised my eyes and saw and, look, a flying scroll.  
**5:2** And he said to me, “What do you see?” And I said, “I see a flying scroll, twenty cubits long and ten cubits wide.”  
**5:3** And he said to me, “This is the imprecation going out to all the land. Whoever steals, as on one side of it, has not been condemned, and whoever swears, as on the other side of it, has not been condemned.  
**5:4** I have brought it out, said the LORD of Armies, and it shall enter the house of the thief and the house of him who swears falsely in My name and lodge within his house and make an utter end of it, with its timbers and with its stones.”  
**5:5** And the messenger speaking to me came out and said to me, “Raise your eyes, pray, and see what is this going out.”  
**5:6** And I said, “What is this?” And he said, “This is a measuring basin going out.” And he said, “This is their crime through all the land.”  
**5:7** And, look, an ingot of lead was being lifted, and there was a certain woman seated in the basin.  
**5:8** And he said, “This is Wickedness,” and he flung her down within the basin and flung the lead stone over its opening.  
**5:9** And I raised my eyes and saw and, look, two women were going out, and there was wind in their wings, and they had wings like the wings of a stork. And they lifted the basin between the earth and the heavens.  
**5:10** And I said to the messenger speaking to me, “Where are they taking the basin?”  
**5:11** And he said to me, “To build a house for it in the land of Shinar, and it shall be founded, and they shall set it down there on its firm place.”  

## 6장

**6:1** And again I raised my eyes and saw and, look, four chariots were going out from between two mountains, and the mountains were mountains of copper.  
**6:2** The first chariot had bay horses and the second chariot black horses.  
**6:3** And the third chariot had white horses and the fourth chariot dappled, spotted horses.  
**6:4** And I spoke out and said to the messenger speaking to me, “What are these, my lord?”  
**6:5** And the messenger answered and said to me, “These are the four winds of the heavens going out from standing in attendance before the Master of all the earth  
**6:6** in which the black horses go out to the land of the north and the white ones have gone out to the western sea, and the dappled ones have gone out to the land of the south,  
**6:7** and the red ones have gone out and sought to go about on the earth. And he said, ‘Go, go about on the earth,’ and they went about on the earth.”  
**6:8** And He summoned me and spoke to me, saying, “See, the ones going out to the north have pleased Me in the land of the north.”  
**6:9** And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,  
**6:10** “Take from the exiles, from Hildi and from Tobiah and from Jediah, who have come from Babylonia, and as for you, on that day you shall come to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah.  
**6:11** And you shall take silver and gold and make diadems and put one on the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak the high priest.  
**6:12** And you shall say to him, saying, “Thus said the LORD of Armies, saying: Here is the man named Branch, and from his place it shall branch out, and he shall build the LORD’s Temple.  
**6:13** And he shall build the LORD’s Temple, and he shall assume majesty, and he shall sit on his throne and rule. And the priest shall be by his throne, and there shall be a counsel of harmony between the two of them.  
**6:14** And the diadems shall be in the LORD’s Temple as a memorial, for Tobiah and Jedaiah and Hen son of Zephaniah. The far-away shall come and build in the LORD’s Temple, and you shall know that the LORD of Armies has sent me to you. And it shall happen, if you heed the voice of the LORD your God.”  

## 7장

**7:1** And it happened in the fourth year of King Darius that the word of the LORD came to Zechariah on the fourth of the ninth month, Kislev.  
**7:2** And Beth-El Sar-Ezer and Regen-Melech and his men had sent to entreat the LORD,  
**7:3** saying to the priests who were in the house of the LORD of Armies and to the prophets, saying, “Shall I weep and practice abstinence in the fifth month as I have done for years?”  
**7:4** And the word of the LORD of Armies came to me, saying,  
**7:5** “Say to the people of the land and to the priests, saying, ‘Though you fasted and mourned in the fifth month and the seventh these seventy years, have you actually fasted for Me?  
**7:6** And if you eat and if you drink, are you not the ones who eat and you the ones who drink?  
**7:7** Are not these the words that the LORD proclaimed through the former prophets when Jerusalem was dwelling tranquil and her towns round about her and the Negeb and the lowland dwelled tranquil?’”  
**7:8** And the word of the LORD came to Zechariah, saying,  
**7:9** “Thus said the LORD of Armies, saying: Judge true justice, and do kindness and mercy each man with his fellow.  
**7:10** And do not exploit widow and orphan, sojourner and poor man, and do not plot evil in your heart, each man against his fellow.”  
**7:11** But they refused to listen and turned their backs in defiance, and their ears they stopped up from hearing.  
**7:12** And they made their heart adamantine against hearing the teaching and the words that the LORD of Armies had sent with His spirit through the former prophets, and there was a great fury from the LORD of Armies.  
**7:13** “And just as He had called and they would not hear, so shall they call and I will not hear, said the LORD of Armies.  
**7:14** And I will whirl them out over all the nations that they did not know, and the land behind them shall be desolate of passersby. They turned a precious land into a desolation.”  

## 8장

**8:1** And the word of the LORD of Armies came, saying,  
**8:2** “Thus said the LORD of Armies: I have been very jealous for Zion, and with great wrath I have been jealous for her.  
**8:3** Thus said the LORD: I have come back to Zion and abided within Jerusalem, and Jerusalem shall be called the City of Truth, and the Mountain of the LORD, the Holy Mountain.  
**8:4** Thus said the LORD of Armies: Again shall old men and old women sit in the squares of Jerusalem, each with his staff in hand because of their many years.  
**8:5** And the squares of the city shall be filled with boys and girls playing in her squares.  
**8:6** Thus said the LORD of Armies: Does it seem beyond the remnant of this people in these days? So it would be beyond Me, said the LORD of Armies.  
**8:7** Thus said the LORD of Armies: I am about to rescue My people from the land of the east and from the land of the sunset, and I will bring them, and they shall abide in Jerusalem and be My people and I will be their God in truth and in righteousness.  
**8:8** Thus said the LORD of Armies: May your hands be strong, you who hear in these days these words from the prophets who are in the time the foundation of the house of the LORD of Armies was laid for the Temple to be rebuilt.  
**8:9** For before these days the wages of man was naught and the wages of beast was nothing, and for him coming and going there was no peace from the foe, and I set each man against his fellow.  
**8:10** And now not as in the former days am I to the remnant of this people, said the LORD of Armies.  
**8:11** For the peaceful sowing—the vine shall give its fruit and the land shall give its yield and the heavens shall give their dew, and I will bestow on the remnant of this people all these.  
**8:12** And it shall be, as you were a curse among the nations, house of Judah and house of Israel, so will I rescue you and you shall become a blessing. Do not fear, and may your hands be strong.  
**8:13** For thus said the LORD of Armies: As I devised to do harm to you when your fathers infuriated me, said the LORD of Armies, and I did not repent,  
**8:14** so I have again devised in these days to do good for Jerusalem and for the house of Judah. Do not fear.  
**8:15** These are the things that you should do: Speak truth, each man to his neighbor, and render truth and justice in your gates.  
**8:16** And do not plot evil in your hearts, each man against his fellow, and do not love a false vow, for all these do I hate, said the LORD.”  
**8:17** And the word of the LORD of Armies came to me, saying,  
**8:18** “Thus said the LORD of Armies: the fast of the fourth month and the fast of the fifth month and the fast of the tenth month shall become a rejoicing for the house of Judah and gladness and good festivals. And love truth and peace.”  
**8:19** Thus said the LORD of Armies: “Peoples and the dwellers of many towns yet shall come, and the dwellers of one town shall go to another, saying, ‘Let us go to entreat the LORD and to seek the LORD of Armies—I, too, shall go.’  
**8:20** And many peoples and vast nations shall come to seek the LORD in Jerusalem and to entreat the LORD.”  
**8:21** Thus said the LORD of Armies: “In those days ten people from all the tongues of the nations shall grasp the border of a Jew’s garment, saying, ‘Let us go up with you, for we have heard that God is with you.’”

**10:2** For the household gods spoke deceit,<br>and the sorcerers envisioned lies,<br>and vain dreams do they speak,<br>with mere breath they console.<br>Therefore they have strayed like sheep,<br>wandered, for there is no shepherd.  
**10:3** Against the shepherds I am incensed<br>and with the he-goats I will make a reckoning.<br>For the LORD of Armies has singled out His flock,<br>the house of Judah,<br>and made them like a stallion, His glory in battle.  
**10:4** From them the cornerstone and from them the tent peg,<br>from them the bow of battle,<br>from them all commanders together.  
**10:5** And they shall be as warriors<br>trampling the mud of the streets in battle,<br>and they shall battle, for the LORD is with them,<br>and they shall put the horsemen to shame.  
**10:6** And I will make the house of Judah mighty,<br>and the house of Joseph I will make victorious.<br>And I will restore them, for I have mercy upon them,<br>and they shall be as though I never forsook them,<br>for I am the LORD their God and will answer them.  
**10:7** And Ephraim shall be like a warrior,<br>and their heart shall rejoice as from wine,<br>and their children shall see and rejoice,<br>their heart shall exult in the LORD.  
**10:8** I will whistle to them and gather them,<br>for I will ransom them, and they shall increase as before.  
**10:9** And I will sow them among the peoples, in far places they shall recall Me,<br>and they shall thrive with their children and return.  
**10:10** And I will bring them back from the land of Egypt<br>and from Assyria I will gather them.<br>And to the land of Gilead and Lebanon I will bring them,<br>and still it shall not suffice for them.  
**10:11** And he shall pass through the sea against the foe<br>and strike waves in the sea,<br>and all the depths of the Nile shall dry up,<br>and the pride of Assyria be brought down,<br>and the scepter of Egypt shall vanish.  
**10:12** And I will make them mighty in the LORD,<br>and in His name they shall go about<br>—said the LORD.  

## 11장

**11:1** Open, O Lebanon, your doors,<br>that fire consume your cedars.  
**11:2** Wail, O cypress,<br>for the cedar has fallen,<br>as the majestic ones are ravaged.<br>Wail, O Bashan oaks,<br>for the dense forest is taken down.  
**11:3** Hark! The wailing of shepherds,<br>for their majesty is ravaged.<br>Hark! The roar of lions,<br>for Jordan’s bends are ravaged.  
**11:4** Thus said the LORD my God: “Look after the sheep to be slaughtered,  
**11:5** whose buyers will slaughter them and bear no guilt and whose sellers will say, ‘Praise the LORD, and I will get rich.’ And their shepherds show no pity for them.  
**11:6** For I will no longer show pity for the dwellers of the land, said the LORD, and I am about to deliver every man into the hand of his fellow and into the hand of his king, and they shall grind up the land, and I will not save it from their hand.”  
**11:7** And I looked after the sheep to be slaughtered for the sheep traders, and I took me two staffs. One I called Pleasantness and the other I called Bruising, and I looked after the sheep.  
**11:8** But I got rid of the three shepherds in a single month and lost patience with them, and they on their part were disgusted with me.  
**11:9** And I said, “I will not look after you.” The sheep dying will die, and the missing will go missing, and those rescuing, each will eat the other’s flesh.  
**11:10** And I took my staff Pleasantness and broke it apart to annul my covenant that I had sealed with all the peoples.  
**11:11** And on that very day it was annulled, and the sheep traders watching me knew that it was the word of the LORD.  
**11:12** And I said to them, “If it is good in your eyes, give me my wages, and if not, don’t.” And they weighed out my wages, thirty silver shekels.  
**11:13** And the LORD said to me, “Fling it into the potter’s kiln,” this majestic sum that I was worth to them. And I took the thirty silver shekels and flung them into the potter’s kiln at the house of the LORD.  
**11:14** And I broke apart my second staff, Bruising, to annul the brotherhood between the house of Judah and the house of Israel.  
**11:15** And the LORD said to me once again, “Take you the gear of a foolish shepherd.  
**11:16** For I am about to raise up a shepherd in the land of the missing sheep. The lad shall not count them nor seek for them, and the injured he shall not heal nor the immobile one sustain, but the fat one he shall eat and break off their hooves.”  
**11:17** Woe, O useless shepherds,<br>forsakers of the sheep.<br>Let a sword be over his arm<br>and over his right eye.<br>Let his arm entirely wither<br>and his right eye go utterly dark.  

## 12장

**12:1** A portent.<br>The word of the LORD concerning Israel, said the LORD, Who stretches out the heavens and founds the earth and fashions the spirit of man within him.  
**12:2** I am about to make Jerusalem a bowl of poison to all the peoples round about, and Judah, too, shall be caught in the siege against Jerusalem.  
**12:3** And it shall happen on that day that I will make Jerusalem a burdensome stone for all the peoples. All who lift her burden shall surely be scraped, while all the nations of the earth gather against her.  
**12:4** On that day, said the LORD, I will strike every horse with confusion and its rider with madness, but upon the house of Judah I will open My eyes, while all the horses of the peoples I will strike with blindness.  
**12:5** And the leaders of Judah shall say in their heart, “The dwellers of Jerusalem are strength for me through the LORD their God.”  
**12:6** On that day I will make the leaders of Judah like a flaming brazier among wood and like a flaming torch among sheaves, and they shall consume on the right and on the left all the peoples round about, and Jerusalem shall again dwell in its place.  
**12:7** And the LORD shall rescue the tents of Judah first so that the glory of the house of David and the glory of Jerusalem’s dwellers shall not surpass Judah.  
**12:8** On that day, the LORD shall defend Jerusalem, and the faltering among them on that day shall be like David and the house of David like a god, like the LORD’s messenger at their head.  
**12:9** And on that day I will set about to destroy all the nations coming against Jerusalem.  
**12:10** And I will pour out upon the house of David and upon Jerusalem’s dwellers a spirit of grace and graciousness, and they shall look upon those who were stabbed and mourn for them like the mourning for an only child, and they shall grieve bitterly for them as one grieves bitterly for a firstborn.  
**12:11** On that day the mourning in Jerusalem shall be as great as the mourning for Hadad-Rimmon in the Valley of Megiddo.  
**12:12** And the land shall mourn clan by clan, the house of Nathan by itself and their women by themselves,  
**12:13** the clan of the house of Levi by itself and their women by themselves, the house of Shimei by itself, and their women by themselves,  
**12:14** all the remaining clans, clan by clan by itself, and their women by themselves.  

## 13장

**13:1** On that day shall a spring be opened for the house of David and for Jerusalem’s dwellers for the cleansing of offense and impurity.  
**13:2** And it shall happen on that day, said the LORD of Armies, that I will cut off the names of the idols from the land, and they no longer shall be recalled, and the prophets, too; and the spirit of uncleanness I will take away from the land.  
**13:3** And it shall happen that should a man still prophesy, his father and his mother, his begetters, shall say, “You shall not live, for you have spoken lies in the name of the LORD.” And his father and his mother shall stab him for his prophesying.  
**13:4** And it shall happen on that day that the prophets shall be shamed, each of his visions when he prophesies, and they shall not wear a hairy mantle in order to deceive.  
**13:5** And he shall say, “I am no prophet. I am a tiller of the soil, for the soil has been consigned to me from my youth.”

## 14장

**14:1** Look, a day is coming for the LORD when what is despoiled from you shall be shared out in your midst,  
**14:2** and I will gather all the nations against Jerusalem for battle. And the city shall be captured and the houses looted and the women ravished. And half the city shall go out in exile, but the rest of the people shall not be cut off from the city.  
**14:3** And the LORD shall sally forth and do battle with those nations as on the day He fought, on the day of battle.  
**14:4** And His feet shall stand on that day on the Mount of Olives, which faces Jerusalem from the east. And the Mount of Olives shall split in half, from east to west, into a very great valley, and half the mountain shall shift to the north and half to the south.  
**14:5** And the valley of My mountains shall be blocked, as the valley of the mountains reaches as far as Azal, and it shall be blocked as it was blocked by the earthquake in the days of Uzziah king of Judah. And the LORD my God shall come, all the holy ones with Him.  
**14:6** And it shall happen that on that day there shall be no daylight nor chill moonlight.  
**14:7** And it shall be a single day—it shall be known to the LORD—neither day nor night, and it shall be, at eventide there shall be light.  
**14:8** And it shall happen on that day, fresh waters shall come out from Jerusalem, half of them toward the eastern sea and half of them toward the western sea, in summer and in winter it shall be.  
**14:9** And the LORD shall be king over all the earth. On that day the LORD shall be one and His name one.  
**14:10** All the land shall become as a plain, from Geba to Rimmon south of Jerusalem, but the city shall rise high and sit in her place, from the Benjamin Gate to the site of the Former Gate as far as the Armor Gate and from Hanamel Tower as far as the king’s winepress.  
**14:11** And they shall dwell within her, and no more shall there be devastation, and Jerusalem shall dwell secure.  
**14:12** And this shall be the plague with which the LORD shall strike all the peoples that marshaled against Jerusalem: their flesh shall rot as they stand on their feet, and their eyes shall rot in their sockets, and their tongues shall rot in their mouths.  
**14:13** And it shall happen on that day, the panic from the LORD among them shall be great, and every man shall seize his fellow, and his hand shall be raised against the hand of his fellow.  
**14:14** And Judah, too, shall do battle in Jerusalem, and the wealth of the nations round about shall be gathered in—gold and silver and very many garments.  
**14:15** And so shall there be a plague against horse and mule and camel and donkey and every beast that will be in those camps, like this plague.  
**14:16** And it shall happen that all who remain of the nations coming up against Jerusalem shall go up year after year to bow to the King, the LORD of Armies, and to celebrate the Festival of Booths.  
**14:17** And it shall happen that he who does not go up from the clans of the earth to Jerusalem to bow to the King, the LORD of Armies, no rain shall fall on him.  
**14:18** And if the clan of Egypt does not go up and does not come, upon them shall be the plague with which the LORD shall strike all the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Festival of Booths.  
**14:19** This shall be the punishment for the offense of Egypt and for the offense of all the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Festival of Booths.  
**14:20** On that day there shall be on the bells of the horses “Holy to the LORD,” and the pails in the house of the LORD shall be like the basins before the altar.  
**14:21** And every pail in Jerusalem and in Judah shall be “Holy to the LORD of Armies.” And all those offering sacrifice shall come and take from them and cook in them, and there shall no longer be a merchant in the house of the LORD of Armies on that day.
