# 20.잠언 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** The proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel.  
**1:2** To know wisdom and reproof,<br>to understand discerning maxims.  
**1:3** To accept the reproof of insight,<br>righteousness, justice, and uprightness.  
**1:4** To give shrewdness to the simple,<br>to a lad, knowledge and cunning.  
**1:5** Let the wise man hear and gain learning,<br>and the discerning acquire designs.  
**1:6** To understand proverbs and adages,<br>the words of the wise and their riddles.  
**1:7** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge.<br>Wisdom and reproof dolts despise.  
**1:8** Hear, my son, your father’s reproof,<br>and do not forsake your mother’s teaching.  
**1:9** For they are a garland of grace on your head<br>and a necklace round your throat.  
**1:10** My son, should offenders seduce you,<br>do not be willing.  
**1:11** Should they say,<br>“Go with us, let us lie in wait for blood,<br>stalk the innocent for no reason.  
**1:12** Let us swallow them live like Sheol,<br>and the blameless like those gone down to the Pit.  
**1:13** All precious treasure we shall find,<br>we shall fill our houses with loot.  
**1:14** Your lot you should throw in with us,<br>one purse we all shall have.”  
**1:15** My son, do not go on a road with them,<br>hold back your foot from their path.  
**1:16** For their feet run to evil,<br>and they hurry to shed blood.  
**1:17** For the net is spread out for no reason<br>in the eyes of each wingèd thing.  
**1:18** Yet they lie in wait for their own blood,<br>they lurk for their own lives.  
**1:19** Thus are the ways of all who chase gain,<br>its possessor’s life it will take.  
**1:20** Wisdom cries out in the streets,<br>in the squares she lifts her voice.  
**1:21** At the bustling crossroads she calls,<br>at the entrance to the town’s gate says her sayings:  
**1:22** How long, dupes, will you love being duped,<br>and scoffers lust scoffing,<br>and fools hate knowledge?  
**1:23** Turn back to my rebuke.<br>Look, I would pour out my spirit to you,<br>I would make my words known with you.  
**1:24** Because I called and you resisted,<br>I reached out my hand and none paid heed,  
**1:25** and you flung aside all my counsel,<br>and you did not want my rebuke.  
**1:26** I, too, shall laugh at your ruin,<br>I shall mock when what you feared comes,  
**1:27** when what you feared comes like disaster,<br>and your ruin like a whirlwind descends,<br>when straits and distress come upon you.  
**1:28** Then they will call me and I shall not answer,<br>they will seek me and they will not find me.  
**1:29** Because they have hated knowledge,<br>and the LORD’s fear they did not choose.  
**1:30** They did not want my counsel,<br>they spurned all my rebuke.  
**1:31** And they ate from the fruit of their way,<br>and from their own counsels they were sated.  
**1:32** For the waywardness of dupes will kill them<br>and the smugness of fools will destroy them.  
**1:33** But who heeds me will dwell secure,<br>and tranquil from the fear of harm.  

## 2장

**2:1** My son, if you take up my sayings,<br>and my commands you store within you,  
**2:2** to make your ear hearken to wisdom,<br>incline your heart to discernment,  
**2:3** for if you call out to understanding,<br>raise your voice to discernment,  
**2:4** if you seek it like silver,<br>search for it like treasure,  
**2:5** then will you understand the LORD’s fear,<br>and you will find the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For the LORD gives wisdom,<br>from His mouth, discerning knowledge.  
**2:7** He stores for the upright prudence,<br>a shield to those who walk blameless,  
**2:8** to keep the paths of justice<br>and He watches the way of His faithful.  
**2:9** Then you will understand righteousness, justice,<br>and uprightness, each pathway of good.  
**2:10** For wisdom will enter your heart,<br>and knowledge be sweet to your palate.  
**2:11** Cunning will watch over you,<br>discernment will keep you,  
**2:12** to save you from a way of evil,<br>from a man who speaks perversely.  
**2:13** They forsake the paths of uprightness<br>to go in the ways of darkness,  
**2:14** they rejoice to do evil,<br>delight in evil’s perverseness,  
**2:15** whose paths are crooked,<br>and twisted in their pathways.  
**2:16** To save you from a stranger-woman,<br>from a smooth-talking alien woman,  
**2:17** who forsakes the guide of her youth<br>and the pact of her God forgets.  
**2:18** For her house leads down to death<br>and to the shades, her pathways.  
**2:19** All who come to her will not return,<br>and will not attain the paths of life.  
**2:20** So that you walk in the way of the good,<br>and the paths of the just you keep.  
**2:21** For the upright will dwell on earth<br>and the blameless survive on it.  
**2:22** But the wicked are cut off from the earth,<br>and traitors torn away from it.  

## 3장

**3:1** My son, do not forget my teaching,<br>and let your heart keep my commands.  
**3:2** For length of days and years of life<br>and peace they will add for you.  
**3:3** Kindness and truth will not forsake you.<br>Bind them round your neck,<br>write them on your heart’s tablet,  
**3:4** and find favor and good regard<br>in the eyes of God and man.  
**3:5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart,<br>and do not lean on your discernment.  
**3:6** Through all your ways know Him,<br>and He will make your paths straight.  
**3:7** Do not be wise in your own eyes,<br>fear the LORD and swerve from evil.  
**3:8** Let it be healing for your flesh<br>and a balm to your bones.  
**3:9** Honor the LORD more than your wealth<br>and than the first fruits of your crop,  
**3:10** and your barns will be filled with abundance,<br>your vats will burst with new wine.  
**3:11** The LORD’s reproof, my son, do not spurn,<br>and do not despise His rebuke.  
**3:12** For whom the LORD loves He rebukes,<br>and like a father his son, regards him kindly.  
**3:13** Happy the man who has found wisdom,<br>and the man who acquires discernment.  
**3:14** For her worth is better than silver’s worth,<br>and her yield better than fine gold.  
**3:15** More precious is she than rubies,<br>and all your cherished things could not equal her.  
**3:16** Length of days are in her right hand,<br>in her left hand wealth and honor.  
**3:17** Her ways are ways of pleasantness,<br>and all her paths are peace.  
**3:18** A tree of life is she to those who grasp her,<br>and those who hold her are deemed happy.  
**3:19** The LORD through wisdom founded earth,<br>set heavens firm through discernment.  
**3:20** Through His knowledge the deeps burst open,<br>and the skies dripped dew.  
**3:21** My son, let these things not slip away from your eyes,<br>keep prudence and cunning,  
**3:22** and they will be life to your neck<br>and grace to your throat.  
**3:23** Then you shall walk secure on your way,<br>and your foot shall not be bruised.  
**3:24** If you lie down, you shall not be afraid.<br>You shall lie down, and your sleep shall be sweet.  
**3:25** You shall not fear any sudden fright,<br>nor the plight of the wicked when it comes.  
**3:26** For the LORD will be your trust,<br>and will guard your foot from the snare.  
**3:27** Don’t hold back bounty from him who earned it<br>when it’s within your hand’s power to perform.  
**3:28** Don’t say to your friend, “Go and come back,<br>and tomorrow I’ll give,” when you have it.  
**3:29** Don’t plot harm against your fellow,<br>when he dwells secure alongside you.  
**3:30** Don’t quarrel with a man for no reason<br>if he has done you no harm.  
**3:31** Don’t envy a man of violence<br>and don’t choose any of his ways.  
**3:32** For a crooked man is the LORD’s loathing,<br>and the upright are His intimates.  
**3:33** The LORD’s blight is on the house of the wicked,<br>and the abode of the righteous He blesses.  
**3:34** As for the scoffers, He scoffs at them,<br>but to the humble He grants favor.  
**3:35** The wise inherit honor,<br>and fools take away disgrace.  

## 4장

**4:1** Hear, O sons, a father’s reproof,<br>and listen to discerning knowledge.  
**4:2** For good learning I have given you,<br>do not forsake my teaching.  
**4:3** For I was a son to my father,<br>a tender only child for my mother.  
**4:4** And he taught me and said to me:<br>“Let your heart hold on to my words.<br>Keep my commands and live.  
**4:5** Get wisdom, get discernment.<br>Do not forget nor swerve from my mouth’s sayings.  
**4:6** Do not forsake her and she will guard you.<br>Love her and she will keep you.  
**4:7** The beginning of wisdom is—get wisdom,<br>and in all that you get, get discernment.  
**4:8** Dandle her and she will exalt you,<br>will honor you when you embrace her.  
**4:9** She will put on your head a garland of grace,<br>a crown of splendor she will hand you.”  
**4:10** Hear, my son, and take my sayings,<br>that the years of your life be many.  
**4:11** In the way of wisdom I teach you,<br>I guide you on pathways of rightness.  
**4:12** When you walk, your step is not straitened.<br>If you run, you will not stumble.  
**4:13** Hold fast to reproof, don’t let go.<br>Keep it, for it is your life.  
**4:14** On the wicked’s path do not enter,<br>and do not stride on the way of the evil.  
**4:15** Shun it, don’t pass upon it,<br>turn away from it and pass on.  
**4:16** For they do not sleep if they’ve done no evil,<br>and they’re robbed of sleep if they trip no one up.  
**4:17** For they break the bread of wickedness,<br>and the wine of outrage they drink.  
**4:18** But the path of the righteous is like light’s radiance,<br>ever brighter till day has come.  
**4:19** The way of the wicked is like darkness.<br>They know not on what they stumble.  
**4:20** My son, listen to my words,<br>to my sayings bend your ear.  
**4:21** Let them not slip away from your eyes,<br>guard them within your heart.  
**4:22** For they are life to those who find them,<br>and healing to all their flesh.  
**4:23** More than anything watched guard your heart,<br>for from it are the ways out to life.  
**4:24** Put away from you twisted speech<br>and lips’ contortion keep far from you.  
**4:25** Let your eyes look in front,<br>and your gaze straight before you.  
**4:26** Level the pathway of your foot,<br>and all your ways will be sound.  
**4:27** Do not veer to the right or the left.<br>Keep your foot away from evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** My son, to my wisdom hearken,<br>to my discernment bend your ear,  
**5:2** to guard cunning<br>so that your lips may keep knowledge.  
**5:3** For the stranger-woman’s lips drip honey,<br>smoother than oil her open mouth.  
**5:4** But in the end she’s as bitter as wormwood,<br>sharp as a double-edged sword.  
**5:5** Her feet go down to Death,<br>in Sheol her steps take hold.  
**5:6** No path of life she traces,<br>her pathways wander, and she does not know.  
**5:7** And now, sons, hear me,<br>and do not swerve from my mouth’s sayings.  
**5:8** Keep your way far from her<br>and do not go near the entrance of her house,  
**5:9** lest you give to others your glory<br>and your years to a ruthless man,  
**5:10** lest strangers sate themselves with your vigor,<br>and your toil—in an alien’s house,  
**5:11** and in the end you roar<br>when your body and flesh waste away.  
**5:12** And you will say, “How I hated reproof,<br>and my heart despised rebuke.  
**5:13** And I did not heed my teachers’ voice,<br>to my instructors I did not bend my ear.  
**5:14** Soon I fell into every harm<br>in the midst of the assembled crowd.”  
**5:15** Drink water from your own well,<br>fresh water from your cistern.  
**5:16** Your springs will spread to the street,<br>in the squares, streams of water.  
**5:17** Let them be yours alone<br>and not for strangers alongside you.  
**5:18** Let your fountain be blessed,<br>and rejoice in the wife of your youth.  
**5:19** Love’s doe, a graceful gazelle,<br>her breasts ever slake your thirst,<br>you will always dote in her love.  
**5:20** And why dote, my son, on a stranger-woman,<br>clasp an alien woman’s lap?  
**5:21** For before the LORD’s eyes are the ways of a man,<br>He traces all his pathways.  
**5:22** The crimes of the wicked ensnare him,<br>in the ropes of his offense he is held.  
**5:23** He will die for want of reproof,<br>in his great doltishness he will dote.  

## 6장

**6:1** My son, if you stood pledge for your fellow man,<br>gave your handshake to a stranger,  
**6:2** you’ve been ensnared by your mouth’s sayings,<br>trapped by your mouth’s sayings.  
**6:3** Do this, then, my son, and escape,<br>for you have fallen into your fellow man’s grasp,<br>go grovel, and pester your fellow man.  
**6:4** Give no sleep to your eyes<br>nor slumber to your eyelids.  
**6:5** Escape like the deer from the hunter,<br>and the bird from the fowler’s hand.  
**6:6** Go to the ant, you sluggard,<br>see its ways and get wisdom.  
**6:7** For she has no foreman,<br>no taskmaster nor ruler.  
**6:8** She readies her bread in summer,<br>stores up her food at the harvest.  
**6:9** How long, O sluggard, will you lie there.<br>When will you rise from your sleep?  
**6:10** A bit more sleep, a bit more slumber,<br>a bit more lying with folded arms,  
**6:11** and your privation will come like a wayfarer,<br>your want like a shield-bearing man.  
**6:12** A worthless fellow, a wrongdoing man,<br>goes about with a crooked mouth,  
**6:13** winking his eyes, shuffling his feet,<br>pointing with his fingers,  
**6:14** perverseness in his heart, plotting evil,<br>ever fomenting strife.  
**6:15** Therefore his ruin will come suddenly,<br>he’ll be broken all at once beyond cure.  
**6:16** Six things are there that the LORD hates,<br>and seven He utterly loathes.  
**6:17** Haughty eyes, a lying tongue,<br>and hands shedding innocent blood,  
**6:18** a heart plotting wicked designs,<br>feet hurrying to run to evil,  
**6:19** a lying deposer, a false witness,<br>fomenting strife among brothers.  
**6:20** Keep, my son, your father’s command,<br>and do not abandon your mother’s teaching.  
**6:21** Bind them on your heart at all times,<br>garland them round your neck.  
**6:22** When you walk about, it will guide you,<br>when you lie down, it will guard you,<br>when you wake, it will converse with you.  
**6:23** For a command is a lamp and teaching a light,<br>and the way of life—stern rebukes.  
**6:24** To keep you from your fellow man’s wife,<br>from the smooth tongue of an alien woman.  
**6:25** Do not covet her beauty in your heart,<br>and let her not take you with her eyelids.  
**6:26** For a whore’s price is no more than a loaf of bread,<br>but a married woman stalks a precious life.  
**6:27** Can a man scoop fire into his lap<br>without his garments burning?  
**6:28** Can a man walk on glowing coals<br>without his feet being scorched?  
**6:29** Thus who comes to bed with his fellow man’s wife,<br>whoever touches her will not go scot-free.  
**6:30** Let one not scorn the thief when he robs<br>to fill his belly when he hungers.  
**6:31** If he is caught, he must pay sevenfold,<br>all the wealth of his house he must give.  
**6:32** Who commits adultery with a woman is senseless,<br>ruining his life, it is he who does it.  
**6:33** Blight and disgrace he will find,<br>and his shame will not be wiped out.  
**6:34** For jealousy turns into a man’s wrath,<br>he will show no pity on the day of vengeance.  
**6:35** He will take no account of ransom,<br>and will not be content, though you offer large bribes.  

## 7장

**7:1** My son, keep my sayings,<br>and store up my commands within you.  
**7:2** Keep my commands and live,<br>my teaching like the apple of your eye.  
**7:3** Bind them on your fingers,<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to Wisdom, “You are my sister,”<br>and call Discernment a friend.  
**7:5** To keep you from a stranger-woman,<br>from a smooth-talking alien woman.  
**7:6** For from the window of my house,<br>through my lattice I looked down,  
**7:7** and I saw among the dupes,<br>discerned among the young men a witless lad,  
**7:8** passing through streets, by the corner,<br>on the way to her house he strides,  
**7:9** at twilight, as evening descends,<br>in pitch-black night and darkness.  
**7:10** And, look, a woman to meet him,<br>whore’s attire and hidden intent.  
**7:11** Bustling she is and wayward,<br>in her house her feet do not stay.  
**7:12** Now outside, now in the square,<br>and by every corner she lurks.  
**7:13** She seizes him and kisses him,<br>impudently says to him:  
**7:14** “I had to make well-being offerings,<br>today I’ve fulfilled my vows.  
**7:15** And so I’ve come out to meet you,<br>to seek you, and I’ve found you.  
**7:16** With coverlets I’ve spread my couch,<br>dyed cloths of Egyptian linen.  
**7:17** I’ve sprinkled my bed with myrrh,<br>with aloes and cinnamon.  
**7:18** Come, let us drink deep of loving till morn,<br>let us revel in love’s delights.  
**7:19** For the man is not in his house,<br>he’s gone on a far-off way.  
**7:20** The purse of silver he took in his hand,<br>at the new moon he’ll return to his house.”  
**7:21** She sways him with all her talk,<br>with her smooth speech she leads him astray.  
**7:22** He goes after her in a trice,<br>as an ox goes off to the slaughter,<br>as a stag prances into a halter.  
**7:23** Till an arrow pierces his liver,<br>as a bird hastens to the snare,<br>not knowing the cost is his life.  
**7:24** And now, sons, listen to me,<br>and attend to my mouth’s sayings.  
**7:25** Let your heart not veer to her ways,<br>and do not go astray on her paths.  
**7:26** For many the victims she has felled,<br>innumerable all whom she has killed.  
**7:27** Through her house are the ways to Sheol,<br>going down to the chambers of Death.  

## 8장

**8:1** Look, Wisdom calls out,<br>and Discernment lifts her voice.  
**8:2** At the top of the heights, on the way,<br>at the crossroads, she takes her stand,  
**8:3** by the gates, at the city’s entrance,<br>at the approach to the portals, she shouts:  
**8:4** To you, men, I call out,<br>and my voice, to humankind.  
**8:5** Understand shrewdness, you dupes,<br>and fools, make your heart understand.  
**8:6** Listen, for I speak noble things,<br>my mouth’s utterance—uprightness.  
**8:7** For my tongue declares truth<br>and my lips loathe wickedness.  
**8:8** In the right are all my mouth’s sayings,<br>nothing in them is twisted or crooked.  
**8:9** They are all plain to the discerning<br>and straightforward for those who find knowledge.  
**8:10** Take my reproof rather than silver,<br>and knowledge is choicer than fine gold.  
**8:11** For wisdom is better than rubies,<br>all precious things can’t match her worth.  
**8:12** I, Wisdom, dwell in shrewdness,<br>and cunning knowledge I find.  
**8:13** Fear of the LORD is hating evil.<br>Pride, haughtiness, an evil way,<br>and perverse speech do I hate.  
**8:14** Mine is counsel and prudence,<br>I am Discernment, mine is might.  
**8:15** Through me kings reign,<br>and rulers decree righteous laws.  
**8:16** Through me princes hold sway,<br>and nobles, all the judges of earth.  
**8:17** I, all my lovers I love,<br>and my seekers do find me.  
**8:18** Riches and honor are with me,<br>long-lasting wealth and righteousness.  
**8:19** My fruit is better than all fine gold,<br>and my yield, than the choicest silver.  
**8:20** On the path of righteousness I walk,<br>within the ways of justice,  
**8:21** to pass substance on to my lovers,<br>and their storehouses to fill.  
**8:22** The LORD created me at the outset of His way,<br>the very first of His works of old.  
**8:23** In remote eons I was shaped,<br>at the start of the first things of earth.  
**8:24** When there were no deeps I was spawned,<br>when there were no wellsprings, water sources.  
**8:25** Before mountains were anchored,<br>before hills I was spawned.  
**8:26** He had yet not made earth and open land,<br>and the world’s first clods of soil.  
**8:27** When He founded the heavens, I was there,<br>when He traced a circle on the face of the deep,  
**8:28** when He propped up the skies above,<br>when He powered the springs of the deep,  
**8:29** when He set to the sea its limit,<br>that the waters not flout His command,<br>when He strengthened the earth’s foundations.  
**8:30** And I was by Him, an intimate,<br>I was His delight day after day,<br>playing before Him at all times,  
**8:31** playing in the world, His earth,<br>and my delight with humankind.  
**8:32** And now, sons, listen to me,<br>happy who keeps my ways.  
**8:33** Listen to reproof and get wisdom,<br>and do not cast it aside.  
**8:34** Happy the man who listens to me,<br>to wait at my doors day after day,<br>to watch the posts of my portals.  
**8:35** For who finds me has found life,<br>and will be favored by the LORD.  
**8:36** And who offends me lays waste his life,<br>all who hate me love death.  

## 9장

**9:1** Wisdom has built her house,<br>she has hewn her pillars, seven.  
**9:2** She has slaughtered her meat,<br>has mixed her wine,<br>also laid out her table.  
**9:3** She has sent out her young women,<br>calls loud from the city’s heights:  
**9:4** Whoever the dupe, let him turn aside here,<br>the senseless—she said to him.  
**9:5** Come, partake of my bread,<br>and drink the wine I have mixed.  
**9:6** Forsake foolishness and live,<br>and stride on the way of discernment.  
**9:7** Who reproves the scoffer takes on disgrace,<br>who rebukes the wicked is maimed.  
**9:8** Rebuke not the scoffer lest he hate you.<br>Rebuke the wise and he will love you.  
**9:9** Give to the wise, he will get more wisdom,<br>inform the righteous, he will increase instruction.  
**9:10** The beginning of wisdom is fear of the LORD,<br>and knowing the Holy One is discernment.  
**9:11** For through Me your days will be many,<br>and the years of your life will increase.  
**9:12** If you get wisdom, you get yourself wisdom,<br>but if you scoff, you bear it alone.  
**9:13** The foolish woman bustles about.<br>Gullibility!—and she knows nothing of it.  
**9:14** And she sits at the entrance of her house<br>in a chair on the city’s heights,  
**9:15** to call out to the wayfarers<br>who go on straight paths:  
**9:16** Whoever the dupe, let him turn aside here,<br>and the senseless—she said to him.  
**9:17** “Stolen waters are sweet,<br>and purloined bread is delicious.”  
**9:18** And he does not know that shades are there,<br>in the depths of Sheol, her guests.  

## 10장

**10:1** The proverbs of Solomon.<br>A wise son gladdens his father,<br>but a foolish son is his mother’s sorrow.  
**10:2** The treasures of wickedness will not avail,<br>but righteousness saves from death.  
**10:3** The LORD will not make the righteous man hunger,<br>but He rebuffs the desire of the wicked.  
**10:4** A deceitful palm brings privation,<br>but the diligent hand enriches.  
**10:5** A clever son stores up in the summer,<br>a disgraceful son slumbers at harvesttime.  
**10:6** Blessings on the righteous man’s head,<br>but outrage will cover the mouth of the wicked.  
**10:7** The memory of the righteous is for a blessing,<br>but the name of the wicked will rot.  
**10:8** The wise of heart takes commands,<br>but who speaks stupidly comes to grief.  
**10:9** Who walks in innocence walks secure,<br>but who walks crooked ways is exposed.  
**10:10** Who winks with his eye gives pain,<br>and who speaks stupidly comes to grief.  
**10:11** The righteous man’s mouth is a wellspring of life,<br>but outrage will cover the mouth of the wicked.  
**10:12** Hatred foments strife,<br>but love covers up all misdeeds.  
**10:13** On the discerning man’s lips wisdom is found,<br>but a rod for the back of the senseless!  
**10:14** Wise men lay up knowledge,<br>but the dolt’s mouth is impending disaster.  
**10:15** The rich man’s wealth is his fortress city,<br>the disaster of the poor, their privation.
