# 18.욥기 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** A man there was in the land of Uz—Job, his name. And the man was blameless and upright and feared God and shunned evil.  
**1:2** , And seven sons were born to him, and three daughters.  
**1:3** And his flocks came to seven thousand sheep and three thousand camels and five hundred yokes of cattle and five hundred she-asses and a great abundance of slaves. And that man was greater than all the Easterners.  
**1:4** And his sons would go and hold a feast, in each one’s house on his set day, and they would call to their sisters to eat and drink with them.  
**1:5** And it happened when the days of the feast came round, that Job would send and consecrate them and rise early in the morning and offer up burnt offerings according to the number of them all. For Job thought, Perhaps my sons have offended and cursed God in their hearts. Thus would Job do at all times.  
**1:6** And one day, the sons of God came to stand in attendance before the LORD, and the Adversary, too, came among them.  
**1:7** And the LORD said to the Adversary, “From where do you come?” And the Adversary answered the LORD and said, “From roaming the earth and walking about in it.”  
**1:8** And the LORD said to the Adversary, “Have you paid heed to My servant Job, for there is none like him on earth, a blameless and upright man, who fears God and shuns evil?”  
**1:9** And the Adversary answered the LORD and said, “Does Job fear God for nothing?  
**1:10** Have You not hedged him about and his household and all that he has all around? The work of his hands You have blessed, and his flocks have spread over the land.  
**1:11** And yet, reach out Your hand, pray, and strike all he has. Will he not curse You to Your face?”  
**1:12** And the LORD said to the Adversary, “Look, all that he has is in your hands. Only against him do not reach out your hand.” And the Adversary went out from before the LORD’s presence.  
**1:13** And one day, his sons and his daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their brother, the firstborn.  
**1:14** And a messenger came to Job and said, “The cattle were plowing and the she-asses grazing by them,  
**1:15** and Sabeans fell upon them and took them, and the lads they struck down by the edge of the sword, and I alone escaped to tell you.”  
**1:16** This one was still speaking when another came and said, “God’s fire fell from the heavens and burned among the sheep and the lads and consumed them, and I alone escaped to tell you.”  
**1:17** This one was still speaking when another came and said, “Chaldaeans set out in three bands and pounced upon the camels and took them, and the lads they struck down by the edge of the sword.”  
**1:18** This one was still speaking when another came and said, “Your sons and your daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their brother, the firstborn.  
**1:19** And, look, a great wind came from beyond the wilderness and struck the four corners of the house, and it fell on the young people, and they died. And I alone escaped to tell you.”  
**1:20** And Job rose and tore his garment and shaved his head and fell to the earth and bowed down.  
**1:21** And he said,<br>“Naked I came out from my mother’s womb,<br>and naked shall I return there.<br>The LORD has given and the LORD has taken.<br>May the LORD’s name be blessed.”  
**1:22** With all this, Job did not offend, nor did he put blame on God.  

## 2장

**2:1** And one day, the sons of God came to stand in attendance before the LORD, and the Adversary, too, came among them to stand in attendance before the LORD.  
**2:2** And the LORD said to the Adversary, “From whence do you come?” And the Adversary answered the LORD and said, “From roaming the earth and walking about in it.”  
**2:3** And the LORD said to the Adversary, “Have you paid heed to My servant Job, for there is none like him on earth, a blameless and upright man, who fears God and shuns evil and still clings to his innocence, and you incited Me against him to destroy him for nothing.”  
**2:4** And the Adversary answered the LORD and said, “Skin for skin! A man will give all he has for his own life.  
**2:5** Yet, reach out, pray, Your hand and strike his bone and his flesh. Will he not curse You to Your face?”  
**2:6** And the LORD said to the Adversary, “Here he is in your hands. Only preserve his life.”  
**2:7** And the Adversary went out from before the LORD’s presence. And he struck Job with a grievous burning rash from the soles of his feet to the crown of his head.  
**2:8** And he took a potsherd to scrape himself with, and he was sitting among the ashes.  
**2:9** And his wife said to him, “Do you still cling to your innocence? Curse God and die.”  
**2:10** And he said to her, “You speak as one of the base women would speak. Shall we accept good from God, too, and evil we shall not accept?” With all this, Job did not offend with his lips.  
**2:11** And Job’s three companions heard of all this harm that had come upon him, and they came, each from his place—Eliphaz the Temanite and Bildad the Shuhite and Zophar the Naamathite, and they agreed to meet to grieve with him and to comfort him.  
**2:12** And they lifted up their eyes from afar and did not recognize him, and they lifted up their voices and wept, and each tore his garment, and they tossed dust on their heads toward the heavens.  
**2:13** And they sat with him on the ground seven days and seven nights, and none spoke a word to him, for they saw that the pain was very great.  

## 3장

**3:1** Afterward, Job opened his mouth and cursed his day.  
**3:2** And Job spoke up and he said:  
**3:3** Annul the day that I was born<br>and the night that said, “A man is conceived.”  
**3:4** That day, let it be darkness.<br>Let God above not seek it out,<br>nor brightness shine upon it.  
**3:5** Let darkness, death’s shadow, foul it,<br>let a cloud-mass rest upon it,<br>let day-gloom dismay it.  
**3:6** That night, let murk overtake it.<br>Let it not join in the days of the year,<br>let it not enter the number of months.  
**3:7** Oh, let that night be barren,<br>let it have no song of joy.  
**3:8** Let the day-cursers hex it,<br>those ready to rouse Leviathan.  
**3:9** Let its twilight stars go dark.<br>Let it hope for day in vain,<br>and let it not see the eyelids of dawn.  
**3:10** For it did not shut the belly’s doors<br>to hide wretchedness from my eyes.  
**3:11** Why did I not die<br>from the womb, from the belly come out, breathe my last?  
**3:12** Why did knees welcome me,<br>and why breasts, that I should suck?  
**3:13** For now I would lie and be still,<br>would sleep and know repose  
**3:14** with kings and the councillors of earth,<br>who build ruins for themselves,  
**3:15** or with princes, possessors of gold,<br>who fill their houses with silver.  
**3:16** Or like a buried stillborn I’d be,<br>like babes who never saw light.  
**3:17** There the wicked cease their troubling,<br>and there the weary repose.  
**3:18** All together the prisoners are tranquil,<br>they hear not the taskmaster’s voice.  
**3:19** The small and the great are there,<br>and the slave is free of his master.  
**3:20** Why give light to the wretched<br>and life to the deeply embittered,  
**3:21** who wait for death in vain,<br>dig for it more than for treasure,  
**3:22** who rejoice at the tomb,<br>are glad when they find the grave?  
**3:23** —To a man whose way is hidden,<br>and God has hedged him about.  
**3:24** For before my bread my moaning comes,<br>and my roar pours out like water.  
**3:25** For I feared a thing—it befell me,<br>what I dreaded came upon me.  
**3:26** I was not quiet, I was not still,<br>I had no repose, and trouble came.  

## 4장

**4:1** And Eliphaz the Temanite spoke out and he said:  
**4:2** If speech were tried against you, could you stand it?<br>Yet who can hold back words?  
**4:3** Look, you reproved many,<br>and slack hands you strengthened.  
**4:4** The stumbler your words lifted up,<br>and bended knees you bolstered.  
**4:5** But now it comes to you and you cannot stand it,<br>it reaches you and you are dismayed.  
**4:6** Is not your reverence your safety,<br>your hope—your blameless ways?  
**4:7** Recall, pray: what innocent man has died,<br>and where were the upright demolished?  
**4:8** As I have seen, those who plow mischief,<br>those who plant wretchedness, reap it.  
**4:9** Through Godov’s breath they die,<br>before his nostrils’ breathing they vanish.  
**4:10** The lion’s roar, the maned beast’s sound—<br>and the young lions’ teeth are smashed.  
**4:11** The king of beasts dies with no prey,<br>the whelps of the lion are scattered.  
**4:12** And to me came a word in secret,<br>and my ear caught a tag end of it,  
**4:13** in musings from nighttime’s visions<br>when slumber falls upon men.  
**4:14** Fear called to me, and trembling,<br>and all my limbs it gripped with fear.  
**4:15** And a spirit passed over my face,<br>made the hair on my flesh stand on end.  
**4:16** It halted, its look unfamiliar,<br>an image before my eyes,<br>stillness, and a sound did I hear:  
**4:17** Can a mortal be cleared before God,<br>can a man be made pure by his Maker?  
**4:18** Why, His servants He does not trust,<br>His agents He charges with blame.  
**4:19** All the more so, the clay-house dwellers,<br>whose foundation is in the dust,<br>who are crushed more quickly than moths.  
**4:20** From morning to eve they are shattered,<br>unawares they are lost forever.  
**4:21** Should their life thread be broken within them,<br>they die, and without any wisdom.  

## 5장

**5:1** Call out, pray: will any answer you,<br>and to whom of the angels will you turn?  
**5:2** For anger kills a fool,<br>and the simple, envy slays.  
**5:3** I have seen a fool striking root—<br>all at once his abode I saw cursed.  
**5:4** His children are distant from rescue<br>and are crushed in the gate—none will save.  
**5:5** Whose harvest the hungry eat<br>and from among thorns they take it away,<br>and the thirsty pant for their wealth.  
**5:6** For crime does not spring from the dust,<br>nor from the soil does wretchedness sprout.  
**5:7** But man is to wretchedness born<br>like sparks flying upward.  
**5:8** Yet I search for El<br>and to God I make my case,  
**5:9** Who does great things without limit<br>wonders beyond all number,  
**5:10** Who brings rain down on the earth<br>and sends water over the fields.  
**5:11** Who raises the lowly on high—<br>the downcast are lifted in rescue.  
**5:12** Thwarts the designs of the cunning,<br>and their hands do not perform wisely.  
**5:13** He entraps the wise in their cunning,<br>and the crooked’s counsel proves hasty.  
**5:14** By day they encounter darkness,<br>as in night they go groping at noon.  
**5:15** He rescues the simple from the sword,<br>and from the hand of the strong, the impoverished,  
**5:16** and the indigent then has hope,<br>and wickedness clamps its mouth shut.  
**5:17** Why, happy the man whom God corrects.<br>Shaddai’s reproof do not spurn!  
**5:18** For He causes pain and binds the wound,<br>He deals blows but His hands will heal.  
**5:19** In six straits He will save you,<br>and in seven harm will not touch you.  
**5:20** In famine He redeems you from death,<br>and in battle from the sword.  
**5:21** From the scourge of the tongue you are hidden,<br>and you shall fear not assault when it comes.  
**5:22** At assault and starvation you laugh,<br>and the beast of the earth you fear not.  
**5:23** With the stones of the field is your pact,<br>the beasts of the field leagued with you.  
**5:24** And you shall know that your tent is peaceful,<br>probe your home and find nothing amiss.  
**5:25** And you shall know that your seed is abundant,<br>your offspring like the grass of the earth.  
**5:26** You shall come to the grave in vigor,<br>as grain-shocks mount in their season.  
**5:27** Look, this we have searched, it is so.<br>Hear it, and you—you should know.  

## 6장

**6:1** And Job spoke out and he said:  
**6:2** Could my anguish but be weighed,<br>and my disaster on the scales be borne,  
**6:3** they would be heavier now than the sand of the sea.<br>Thus my words are choked back.  
**6:4** For Shaddai’s arrows are in me—<br>their venom my spirit drinks.<br>The terrors of God beset me.  
**6:5** Does the wild ass bray over his grass,<br>the ox bellow over his feed?  
**6:6** Is tasteless food eaten unsalted,<br>does the oozing of mallows have savor?  
**6:7** My throat refuses to touch them.<br>They resemble my sickening flesh.  
**6:8** If only my wish were fulfilled,<br>and my hope God might grant.  
**6:9** If God would deign to crush me,<br>loose His hand and tear me apart.  
**6:10** And this still would be my comfort,<br>I shrink back in pangs—He spares not.<br>Yet I withhold not the Holy One’s words.  
**6:11** What is my strength, that I should hope,<br>and what my end that I should endure?  
**6:12** Is my strength the strength of stones,<br>is my flesh made of bronze?  
**6:13** Indeed, there is no help within me,<br>and prudence is driven from me.  
**6:14** The blighted man’s friend owes him kindness,<br>though the fear of Shaddai he forsake.  
**6:15** My brothers betrayed like a wadi,<br>like the channel of brooks that run dry.  
**6:16** They are dark from the ice,<br>snow heaped on them.  
**6:17** When they warm, they are gone,<br>in the heat they melt from their place.  
**6:18** The paths that they go on are winding,<br>they mount in the void and are lost.  
**6:19** The caravans of Tema looked out,<br>the convoys of Sheba awaited.  
**6:20** Disappointed in what they had trusted,<br>they reached it and their hopes were dashed.  
**6:21** For now you are His.<br>You see panic and you fear.  
**6:22** Did I say, Give for me,<br>and with your wealth pay a ransom for me,  
**6:23** and free me from the hands of the foe,<br>from the oppressors’ hands redeem me?  
**6:24** Instruct me—as for me, I’ll keep silent,<br>and let me know where I went wrong.  
**6:25** How forceful are honest words.<br>Yet what rebuke is the rebuke by you?  
**6:26** Do you mean to rebuke with words,<br>treat the speech of the desperate as wind?  
**6:27** Even for the orphan you cast lots,<br>and haggle for your companion.  
**6:28** And now, deign to turn toward me.<br>To your face I will surely not lie.  
**6:29** Relent, pray, let there be no injustice.<br>Relent. I am yet in the right.  
**6:30** Is there injustice on my tongue?<br>Does my palate not taste disasters?  

## 7장

**7:1** Does not man have fixed service on earth,<br>and like a hired worker’s his days?  
**7:2** Like a slave he pants for shade,<br>like a hired worker he waits for his pay.  
**7:3** Thus I was heir to futile moons,<br>and wretched nights were allotted to me.  
**7:4** Lying down, I thought, When shall I rise?—<br>Each evening, I was sated with tossing till dawn.  
**7:5** My flesh was clothed with worms and earth-clods,<br>my skin rippled with running sores.  
**7:6** My days are swifter than the weaver’s shuttle.<br>They snap off without any hope.  
**7:7** Recall that my life is a breath.<br>Not again will my eyes see good.  
**7:8** The eye of who sees me will not make me out.<br>Your eyes are on me—I am gone.  
**7:9** A cloud vanishes and goes off.<br>Thus, who goes down to Sheol will not come up.  
**7:10** He will not return to his home.<br>His place will not know him again.  
**7:11** As for me, I will not restrain my mouth.<br>I would lament with my spirit in straits<br>I would speak when my being is bitter.  
**7:12** Am I Yamm or the Sea Beast,<br>that You should put a watch upon me?  
**7:13** When I thought my couch would console me,<br>that my bed would bear my lament,  
**7:14** You panicked me in dreams<br>and in visions You struck me with terror.  
**7:15** And my throat would have chosen choking,<br>my bones—death.  
**7:16** I am sickened—I won’t live forever.<br>Let me be, for my days are mere breath.  
**7:17** What is man that You make him great<br>and that You pay heed to him?  
**7:18** You single him out every morning,<br>every moment examine him.  
**7:19** How long till You turn away from me?<br>You don’t let me go while I swallow my spit.  
**7:20** What is my offense that I have done to You,<br>O Watcher of man?<br>Why did You make me Your target,<br>and I became a burden to You?  
**7:21** And why do You not pardon my crime<br>and let my sin pass away?<br>For soon I shall lie in the dust.<br>You will seek me, and I shall be gone.  

## 8장

**8:1** And Bildad the Shuhite spoke out and he said,  
**8:2** How long will you jabber such things?—<br>the words of your mouth, one huge wind.  
**8:3** Would God pervert justice,<br>would Shaddai pervert what is right?  
**8:4** If your children offended Him,<br>He dispatched them because of their crime.  
**8:5** If you yourself sought out El,<br>and pleaded to Shaddai,  
**8:6** if you were honest and pure,<br>by now He would rouse Himself for you,<br>and would make your righteous home whole.  
**8:7** Then your beginning would seem a trifle<br>and your latter day very grand.  
**8:8** For ask, pray, generations of old,<br>take in what their fathers found out.  
**8:9** For we are but yesterday, unknowing,<br>for our days are a shadow on earth.  
**8:10** Will they not teach you and say to you,<br>and from their heart bring out words?  
**8:11** Will papyrus sprout with no marsh,<br>reeds grow grand without water?  
**8:12** Still in its blossom, not yet plucked,<br>before any grass it will wither.  
**8:13** Thus is the end of all who forget God,<br>and the hope of the tainted is lost.  
**8:14** Whose faith is mere cobweb,<br>a spider’s house his trust.  
**8:15** He leans on his house and it will not stand,<br>he grasps it and it does not endure.  
**8:16** —He is moist in the sun,<br>and his tendrils push out in his garden.  
**8:17** Round a knoll his roots twist,<br>on a stone house they take hold.  
**8:18** If his place should uproot him<br>and deny him—“I never saw you,”  
**8:19** why, this is his joyous way,<br>from another soil he will spring.  
**8:20** Look, God will not spurn the blameless,<br>nor hold the hand of evildoers.  
**8:21** He will yet fill your mouth with laughter<br>and your lips with a shout of joy.  
**8:22** Your foes will be clothed in disgrace,<br>and the tent of the wicked gone.  

## 9장

**9:1** And Job spoke out and he said:  
**9:2** Of course, I knew it was so:<br>how can man be right before God?  
**9:3** Should a person bring grievance against Him,<br>He will not answer one of a thousand.  
**9:4** Wise in mind, staunch in strength,<br>who can argue with Him and come out whole?  
**9:5** He uproots mountains and they know not,<br>overturns them in His wrath.  
**9:6** He makes earth shake in its setting,<br>and its pillars shudder.  
**9:7** He bids the sun not to rise,<br>and the stars He seals up tight.  
**9:8** He stretches the heavens alone<br>and tramples the crests of the sea.  
**9:9** He makes the Bear and Orion,<br>the Pleiades and the South Wind’s chambers.  
**9:10** He performs great things without limit<br>and wonders without number.  
**9:11** Look, He passes over me and I do not see,<br>slips by me and I cannot grasp Him.  
**9:12** Look, He seizes—who can resist Him?<br>Who can tell him, “What do You do?”  
**9:13** God will not relent His fury.<br>Beneath Him Rahab’s minions stoop.  
**9:14** And yet, as for me, I would answer Him,<br>would choose my words with Him.  
**9:15** Though in the right, I can’t make my plea.<br>I would have to entreat my own judge.  
**9:16** Should I call out and He answer me,<br>I would not trust Him to heed my voice.  
**9:17** Who for a hair would crush me<br>and make my wounds many for naught.  
**9:18** He does not allow me to catch my breath<br>as He sates me with bitterness.  
**9:19** If it’s strength—He is staunch,<br>and if it’s justice—who can arraign Him?  
**9:20** Though in the right, my mouth will convict me,<br>I am blameless, yet He makes me crooked.  
**9:21** I am blameless—I know not myself,<br>I loathe my life.  
**9:22** It’s all the same, and so I thought:<br>the blameless and the wicked He destroys.  
**9:23** If a scourge causes death in an instant,<br>He mocks the innocent’s plight.  
**9:24** The earth is given in the wicked man’s hand,<br>the face of its judges He veils.<br>If not He—then who else?  
**9:25** And my days are swifter than a courier.<br>They have fled and have never seen good,  
**9:26** slipped away like reed ships,<br>like an eagle swooping on prey.  
**9:27** If I said, I would forget my lament.<br>I would leave my grim mood and be gladdened,  
**9:28** I was in terror of all my suffering.<br>I knew You would not acquit me.  
**9:29** I will be guilty.<br>Why should I toil in vain?  
**9:30** Should I bathe in snow,<br>make my palms pure with lye,  
**9:31** You would yet plunge me into a pit,<br>and my robes would defile me.  
**9:32** For He is not a man like me that I might answer Him,<br>that we might come together in court.  
**9:33** Would there were an arbiter between us,<br>who could lay his hand on us both,  
**9:34** who could take from me His rod,<br>and His terror would not confound me.  
**9:35** I would speak, and I will not fear Him,<br>for that is not the way I am.  

## 10장

**10:1** My whole being loathes my life.<br>Let me give vent to my lament.<br>Let me speak when my being is bitter.  
**10:2** I shall say to God: Do not convict me.<br>Inform me why You accuse me.  
**10:3** Is it good for You to oppress,<br>to spurn Your own palms’ labor,<br>and on the council of the wicked to shine?  
**10:4** Do You have the eyes of mortal flesh,<br>do You see as man would see?  
**10:5** Are Your days like a mortal’s days,<br>Your years like the years of a man,  
**10:6** that You should search out my crime<br>and inquire for my offense?  
**10:7** You surely know I am not guilty,<br>but there is none who saves from Your hand.  
**10:8** Your hands fashioned me and made me,<br>and then You turn round and destroy me!  
**10:9** Recall, pray, that like clay You worked me,<br>and to the dust You will make me return.  
**10:10** Why, You poured me out like milk<br>and like cheese You curdled me.  
**10:11** With skin and flesh You clothed me,<br>with bones and sinews entwined me.  
**10:12** Life and kindness you gave me,<br>and Your precept my spirit kept.  
**10:13** Yet these did You hide in Your heart;<br>I knew that this was with You:  
**10:14** If I offended, You kept watch upon me<br>and of my crime would not acquit me.  
**10:15** If I was guilty, alas for me,<br>and though innocent, I could not raise my head,<br>sated with shame and surfeited with disgrace.  
**10:16** Like a triumphant lion You hunt me,<br>over again wondrously smite me.  
**10:17** You summon new witnesses against me<br>and swell up Your anger toward me—<br>vanishings and hard service are mine.  
**10:18** And why from the womb did You take me?<br>I’d breathe my last, no eye would have seen me.  
**10:19** As though I had not been, I would be.<br>From belly to grave I’d be carried.  
**10:20** My days are but few—let me be.<br>Turn away that I may have some gladness  
**10:21** before I go, never more to return,<br>to the land of dark and death’s shadow,  
**10:22** the land of gloom, thickest murk,<br>death’s shadow and disorder,<br>where it shines thickest murk.  

## 11장

**11:1** And Zophar the Naamathite spoke out and he said:  
**11:2** Shall a swarm of words be unanswered,<br>and should a smooth talker be in the right?  
**11:3** Your lies may silence folk,<br>you mock and no one protests.  
**11:4** And you say: my teaching is spotless,<br>and I am pure in your eyes.  
**11:5** Yet, if only God would speak,<br>and would open His lips against you,  
**11:6** would tell you wisdom’s secrets,<br>for prudence is double-edged.<br>And know, God leaves some of your crime forgotten.  
**11:7** Can you find what God has probed,<br>can you find Shaddai’s last end?  
**11:8** Higher than heaven, what can you do,<br>deeper than Sheol, what can you know?  
**11:9** Longer than earth is its measure,<br>and broader than the sea.  
**11:10** Should He slip away or confine or assemble,<br>who can resist Him?  
**11:11** For He knows the empty folk,<br>He sees wrongdoing and surely takes note.  
**11:12** And a hollow man will get a wise heart<br>when a wild ass is born a man.  
**11:13** If you yourself readied your heart<br>and spread out your palms to Him,  
**11:14** if there is wrongdoing in your hand, remove it,<br>let no mischief dwell in your tents.  
**11:15** For then you will raise your face unstained,<br>you will be steadfast and will not fear.  
**11:16** For you will forget wretchedness,<br>like water gone off, recall it.

**42:17** And Job died, aged and sated in years.
