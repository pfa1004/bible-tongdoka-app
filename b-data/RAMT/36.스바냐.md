# 36.스바냐 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi son of Gedaliah son of Amariah son of Hezekiah in the days of Josiah son of Amon king of Judah.  
**1:2** I will surely sweep away everything<br>from the face of the earth, said the LORD.  
**1:3** I will surely sweep away man and beast,<br>sweep away the fowl of the heavens and the fish of the sea—<br>with stumbling blocks for the wicked.<br>And I will cut off humankind<br>from the face of the earth, said the LORD.  
**1:4** And I will reach out My hand against Judah<br>and against all who dwell in Jerusalem.<br>And I will cut off from this place<br>the remnant of Baal,<br>the name of the pagan priests with the priests,  
**1:5** and those bowing on the roofs to the array of the heavens<br>and those bowing and swearing to the LORD<br>and yet swearing by Milcom,  
**1:6** and those falling back from the LORD<br>and who did not seek the LORD<br>and did not search for Him.  
**1:7** Hush before the LORD!<br>—for the day of the LORD is near.<br>For the LORD has readied a slaughter,<br>has invited His guests.  
**1:8** And on that day there shall be a slaughter for the LORD,<br>and I will make a reckoning with the nobles<br>and with the sons of the king<br>and with all who don<br>the garb of the foreigner.  
**1:9** And I will make a reckoning with all who hop<br>over the threshold on that day,<br>who fill the house of their Master<br>with outrage and deceit.  
**1:10** And it shall happen on that day—<br>the sound of outcry from the Fish Gate<br>and wailing from the Mishneh<br>and a great disaster from the hills.  
**1:11** Wail, you who dwell in the Ravine,<br>for the people of traders is destroyed,<br>all weighers of silver are cut off.  
**1:12** And it shall happen at that time—<br>I will search out Jerusalem with lamps<br>and make a reckoning with the men<br>who sit still on their lees<br>saying in their hearts,<br>“The LORD does neither good nor evil.”  
**1:13** And their wealth shall become plunder<br>and their homes a desolation,<br>and they shall build homes and not dwell in them<br>and plant vineyards and not drink their wine.  
**1:14** The great day of the LORD is near,<br>near and very swift.<br>The sound of the day of the LORD is bitter,<br>on it the warrior shrieks.  
**1:15** A day of wrath is that day,<br>a day of distress and discomfort,<br>a day of devastation and desolation,<br>a day of darkness and deep dusk,<br>and a day of cloud and fog.  
**1:16** a day of ram’s horn and trumpet blast<br>against the fortress towns<br>and against the lofty corner-towers.  
**1:17** And I will bring humankind into straits,<br>and they shall walk about like the blind,<br>for they have offended against the LORD.<br>And their blood shall be spilled like dust<br>and their flesh like turds.  
**1:18** Neither their silver nor their gold<br>shall avail to save them.<br>On the day of the wrath of the LORD<br>all the land shall be consumed in His zealous fire,<br>for a ghastly end<br>shall He make of all who dwell in the land.  

## 2장

**2:1** Gather yourselves, O gather,<br>nation undesired,  
**2:2** before the decree is born—<br>the day slips by like chaff—<br>before it comes upon you,<br>the day of the LORD’s fury.  
**2:3** Seek the LORD,<br>all you humble of the land<br>who have fulfilled His law.<br>Seek justice, seek to be humble.<br>Perhaps you will be sheltered on the day of the LORD’s fury.  
**2:4** For Gaza shall be abandoned<br>and Ashkelon a desolation.<br>Ashdod shall be banished at noon,<br>and Ekron be uprooted.  
**2:5** Woe, you who dwell in the coastal region,<br>nation of Cherithites.<br>The word of the LORD is against you.<br>Canaan, land of the Philistines,<br>I will destroy you with none dwelling there.  
**2:6** And the coastal region shall become<br>meadows for shepherd’s feasts and sheep pens.  
**2:7** And it shall become a region<br>for the remnant of the house of Judah.<br>On these they shall graze their flocks,<br>in the houses of Ashkelon at evening they shall bed down,<br>for the LORD their God shall single them out<br>and restore their fortunes.  
**2:8** I have heard the insult of Moab<br>and the jibes of the Ammonites,<br>who insulted My people<br>and gloated over their lands.  
**2:9** Therefore, as I live, said the LORD of Armies, God of Israel:<br>Moab shall be like Sodom<br>and the Ammonites like Gomorrah—<br>nettle growth and salt mine<br>and a desolation for all time.<br>The remnant of My people shall despoil them,<br>and the remains of the nation inherit them.  
**2:10** This comes to them for their pride because they insulted and gloated over the people of the LORD of Armies.  
**2:11** Fearsome is the LORD over you, for He holds sway over all the gods of the earth, and all the coastlands of the nations shall bow to Him, each man in his place.  
**2:12** You, too, Cushites,<br>are slain by My sword.  
**2:13** And He shall reach out His hand in the north<br>and destroy Assyria<br>and make Nineveh a desolation,<br>parched land like the desert.  
**2:14** And flocks shall bed down in her midst,<br>all the beasts of the nations.<br>Both jackdaw and owl<br>on her capitals shall roost.<br>The screech owl shall hoot in the window,<br>the raven on the threshold,<br>for the cedarwood is laid bare.  
**2:15** This the merry city,<br>dwelling secure,<br>saying in her heart,<br>“I and none besides me,”<br>how has she become a desolation,<br>a bedding-down place for beasts!<br>All who pass by her shall hiss,<br>wag a mocking hand.  

## 3장

**3:1** Woe, sullied and besmirched,<br>oppressive city!  
**3:2** She heeded not the voice,<br>did not accept reproof.<br>In the LORD she did not trust,<br>nor drew near to her God.  
**3:3** Her nobles within her<br>are roaring lions<br>Her judges are wolves of the steppes,<br>they gnawed all the bones by morning.  
**3:4** Her prophets are unsteady,<br>men of treachery.<br>Her priests profaned the holy,<br>did outrage to the teaching.  
**3:5** The LORD is righteous in her midst,<br>He does not do wrong.<br>Morning after morning He brings His justice to light—<br>it never lacks.<br>But the wrongdoer knows no shame.  
**3:6** I have cut off nations,<br>their corner-towers are devastated.<br>I have destroyed their streets<br>with none passing by.<br>Their towns are demolished,<br>with no men, without dwellers.  
**3:7** I said, “If you but fear Me,<br>if you accept reproof,<br>her abode will not be cut off,<br>all that I had summoned against her.”<br>Yet they continued acting ruinously<br>in all their deeds.  
**3:8** Therefore, wait for Me, said the LORD,<br>for the day I rise as witness,<br>for My judgment is to gather in nations,<br>to assemble kingdoms,<br>to pour out My wrath upon them,<br>all my smoldering anger.<br>For in My zealous fire<br>all the earth shall be consumed.  
**3:9** For then will I transform peoples<br>with a pure language<br>for them all to call in the name of the LORD,<br>to serve Him with single intent.  
**3:10** From beyond the rivers of Cush<br>they who entreat Me, whom I dispersed,<br>shall bring My tribute.  
**3:11** On that day:<br>You shall not be shamed by all your acts<br>in which you trespassed against Me,<br>for then will I remove from your midst<br>those merry with your pride,<br>and no longer shall you be haughty<br>on My holy mountain.  
**3:12** And I will leave in your midst<br>a poor and lonely people,<br>and they shall shelter in the LORD’s name.  
**3:13** They shall do no wrong<br>nor speak lies,<br>and there shall be in their mouth<br>no tongue of deceit,<br>but they shall graze and bed down,<br>and none shall make them tremble.  
**3:14** Sing gladly, Daughter Zion,<br>shout out, Israel.<br>Rejoice and delight with whole heart,<br>O Daughter Jerusalem.  
**3:15** The LORD has set aside your judgments<br>removed your enemy.<br>The King of Israel, the LORD, is in your midst.<br>You need no longer fear evil.  
**3:16** On that day shall it be said to Jerusalem:<br>Do not fear, O Zion,<br>let not your hands fall slack.<br>The LORD your God is in your midst,<br>a rescuing warrior.<br>He delights over you with rejoicing,<br>He renews His love,<br>He exults over you with glad song.  
**3:17** Those sorrowing from lack of festivals, bearing her disgrace,<br>I will gather from you.<br>I am about to deal with all your tormentors at that time.  
**3:18** And I will rescue the lame,<br>and the scattered I will gather,<br>and I will make them a glorious name<br>through all the land of their shame.  
**3:19** At that time I will bring you,<br>at that time gather you,<br>for I will make you a glorious name<br>among all the peoples of the earth<br>when I restore your fortunes before your eyes.<br>—said the LORD.
