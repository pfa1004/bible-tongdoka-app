# 32.요나 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** And the word of the LORD came to Jonah son of Amittai, saying,  
**1:2** “Get up, go to Nineveh the great city, and call out against it, for their evil has risen before Me.”  
**1:3** And Jonah got up to flee to Tarshish from before the LORD to Jaffa and found a ship coming from Tarshish, and he paid its fare and went down with them to go to Tarshish from before the LORD.  
**1:4** And the LORD cast a great wind upon the sea, and there was a great storm on the sea, and the ship threatened to break up.  
**1:5** And the sailors were afraid, and each man cried out to his god, and they cast the gear that was in the ship into the sea to lighten their load. And Jonah had come down into the far corners of the craft and had laid down and fallen deep asleep.  
**1:6** And the captain approached him and said, “What are you doing deep asleep? Call out to your god. Perhaps the god will give some thought to us, that we may not perish.”  
**1:7** And they said to each other, “Let us cast lots that we may know on whose account this evil is upon us.” And they cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah.  
**1:8** And they said to him, “Tell us, pray, you on whose account this evil is upon us, what is your work and from where do you come? What is your land, and from what people are you?”  
**1:9** And he said to them, “I am a Hebrew and the LORD God of the heavens do I fear, Who made the sea and the dry land.”  
**1:10** And the men feared greatly, and they said to him, “What is this you have done?” For the men knew that he was fleeing from before the LORD, for he had told them.  
**1:11** And they said to him, “What shall we do that the sea calm for us?” For the sea was storming more and more.  
**1:12** And he said to them, “Lift me up and cast me into the sea that the sea calm for you, for I know that on my account this great storm is upon you.”  
**1:13** And the men rowed to get back to the dry land and were not able, for the sea was storming upon them more and more.  
**1:14** And they called out to the LORD and said, “Please, O LORD, pray let us not perish on account of the life of this man, and do not exact from us the blood of the innocent, for You, O LORD, as You desire You do.”  
**1:15** And they lifted up Jonah and cast him into the sea, and the sea ceased from its fury.  
**1:16** And the men feared the LORD greatly and offered sacrifices to the LORD and made vows.  
**1:17** And the LORD set out a great fish to swallow Jonah, and he was three days and three nights in the innards of the fish.  

## 2장

**2:1** And Jonah prayed to the LORD his God from the innards of the fish.  
**2:2** And he said:<br>“I called out from my straits<br>to the LORD, and He answered me.<br>From the belly of Sheol I cried out—<br>You heard my voice.  
**2:3** You flung me into the deep, in the heart of the sea,<br>and the current came round me.<br>All your breakers and waves<br>streamed over me.  
**2:4** And I thought:<br>I am banished from before Your eyes.<br>Yet again will I look<br>on Your holy temple.  
**2:5** Water lapped about me to the neck,<br>the deep came round me,<br>weed was bound round my head.  
**2:6** To the roots of the mountains I went down—<br>the underworld’s bolts against me forever.<br>But You brought up my life from the Pit,<br>O LORD my God.  
**2:7** As my life-breath grew faint within me,<br>the LORD did I recall,<br>and my prayer came unto You,<br>to Your holy Temple.  
**2:8** Those who look to vaporous lies<br>will turn away from their mercy.  
**2:9** And I with a voice of thanksgiving<br>let me sacrifice to You.<br>What I vowed let me pay.<br>Rescue is the LORD’s.”  
**2:10** And the LORD spoke to the fish, and it vomited Jonah onto the dry land.  

## 3장

**3:1** And the word of the LORD came to Jonah a second time, saying,  
**3:2** “Get up, go to Nineveh the great city, and call out to it the call that I speak to you.”  
**3:3** And Jonah got up and went to Nineveh according to the word of the LORD. And Nineveh was a great city of God’s, a three days’ walk across.  
**3:4** And Jonah began to come into the city, one day’s walk, and he called out and said, “Forty days more, and Nineveh is overthrown.”  
**3:5** And the people of Nineveh trusted God, and they called a fast and donned sackcloth, from the greatest of them to the least.  
**3:6** And the word reached the king of Nineveh, and he rose from his throne and took off his mantle and covered himself in sackcloth and sat upon ashes.  
**3:7** And he had it proclaimed and he said in Nineveh: “By the authority of the king and his great men, saying, man and beast, cattle and sheep, shall taste nothing. They shall not graze and they shall not drink water.  
**3:8** And man and beast shall cover themselves with sackcloth, and they shall call out to God with all their might, and every man of them shall turn back from his evil way and from the outrage to which they hold fast.  
**3:9** Who knows? Perhaps God will turn back and relent and turn back from His blazing wrath, and we shall not perish.”  
**3:10** And God saw their acts, that they had turned back from their evil way, and God relented from the evil that He said to do to them, and he did not do it.  

## 4장

**4:1** And the thing was very evil for Jonah, and he was incensed.  
**4:2** And he prayed to the LORD and said, “I beseech You, LORD, was it not my word when I was still in my land? Therefore did I hasten to flee to Tarshish, for I knew that You are a gracious and compassionate God, slow to anger and abundant in kindness and relenting from evil.  
**4:3** And now, LORD, take my life, pray, from me, for better my death than my life.”  
**4:4** And the LORD said, “Are you good and angry?”  
**4:5** And Jonah went out of the city and sat down to the east of the city and made himself a shelter there and sat under it in the shade till he might see what would happen in the city.  
**4:6** And the LORD God set out a qiqayon plant, and it rose up over Jonah to be a shade over his head to save him from his evil plight. And Jonah rejoiced greatly over the qiqayon.  
**4:7** And God set out a worm as dawn came up on the morrow, and it struck the qiqayon and it withered.  
**4:8** And it happened, as the sun rose, that God set out a slashing east wind, and the sun struck Jonah’s head, and he grew faint and wanted to die, and he said, “Better my death than my life.”  
**4:9** And God said to Jonah, “Are you good and angry over the qiqayon?” And he said, “I am good and angry, to the point of death.”  
**4:10** And the LORD said, “You—you had pity over the qiqayon, for which you did not toil and which you did not grow, which overnight came and overnight was gone.  
**4:11** And I, shall I not have pity for Nineveh the great city, in which there are many more than one hundred twenty thousand human beings who do not know between their right hand and their left, and many beasts?”
