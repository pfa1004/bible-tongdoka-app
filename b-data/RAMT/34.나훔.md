# 34.나훔 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** A portent concerning Nineveh, the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.  
**1:2** A jealous and vengeful God is the LORD;<br>vengeful, the LORD and master of wrath.<br>The LORD is avenged of His foes,<br>and bears a grudge against His enemies.  
**1:3** The LORD is slow to anger and great in power,<br>but the LORD surely does not acquit.<br>In tempest and whirlwind His way,<br>and a cloud is the dust at His feet.  
**1:4** He rebukes the sea and it dries up,<br>and all rivers He turns to dry land.<br>Bleak are Bashan and Carmel,<br>and Lebanon’s blossom is bleak.  
**1:5** Mountains shake before Him,<br>and the hills melt.<br>The earth lies in ruins before Him,<br>the world and all who dwell in it.  
**1:6** Before His fury who can stand,<br>and who can arise in His smoldering ire?<br>His wrath pours out like fire,<br>and the rocks are shattered by it.  
**1:7** The LORD is a good stronghold on a day of distress,<br>and He embraces those who trust Him.  
**1:8** And in his sweeping torrent He puts an end to His foes,<br>and His enemies the dark pursues.  
**1:9** What could you plot against the LORD?<br>An utter end He brings about—<br>not twice shall the foe arise.  
**1:10** For like tangled thorns—<br>as they swilled they were besotted—<br>they were consumed like heaps of dry straw.  
**1:11** From you has come forth<br>one who plots evil against the LORD,<br>a worthless councillor.  
**1:12** Thus said the LORD:<br>Though they be joined in a pact and many,<br>yet shall they be done with and gone.<br>I afflicted you but will afflict you no more.  
**1:13** And now I will break his shaft from upon you<br>and snap your cords.  
**1:14** And the LORD has charged concerning you:<br>There shall be no seed of your name anymore.<br>I will cut off from the house of your god<br>idol and molten image.<br>I will lay out your grave,<br>for you are of no account.  

## 2장

**2:1** Look, upon the mountains<br>are the feet of the herald<br>announcing good tidings.<br>Celebrate, Judah, your pilgrim feasts,<br>fulfill your pledges.<br>For no more shall the base man pass through you—<br>he is cut off altogether.  
**2:2** The battering ram has come up against you.<br>Put up a watch. Look out to the road.<br>Brace yourself, flex all your strength.  
**2:3** For the LORD has brought back the pride of Jacob<br>like the pride of Israel.<br>For marauders have blighted them<br>and have ruined their branches.  
**2:4** His warriors’ shields are reddened,<br>the soldiers are stained crimson.<br>Like torch-fire are the chariots<br>on the day they are made ready,<br>and the cypress shafts are poisoned.  
**2:5** Through the streets the chariots run wild,<br>they rumble through the squares.<br>Their look is like torches,<br>like lightning they race.  
**2:6** He calls out his staunch men—<br>they stumble as they go.<br>They rush to the wall,<br>and mantlets are set up.  
**2:7** The gates of the rivers are opened<br>and the palace is swept away.  
**2:8** And the mistress is brought out, exiled,<br>and her slavegirls moan like doves,<br>beating on their chests.  
**2:9** And Nineveh in time past was like a pool of water,<br>but now they flee.<br>“Stop! Stop!” but none turns round.  
**2:10** Loot the silver, loot the gold!<br>and there is no end to the horde,<br>treasure of every precious vessel.  
**2:11** Stripped and distraught and despoiled,<br>fainting heart and buckling knees<br>and shuddering in all loins,<br>and all faces lose their luster.  
**2:12** Where now is the den of the lions,<br>the cave of the king of beasts?<br>There the lion walked, the maned beast there,<br>the lion’s cub, with none to make them tremble.  
**2:13** The lion tearing prey for its cubs<br>and breaking necks for his lioness,<br>he filled his lair with prey<br>and his den with torn-apart flesh.  
**2:14** Here am I against you,<br>said the LORD of Armies.<br>I will burn up your chariots in smoke,<br>and your maned beasts the sword shall devour,<br>and I will cut off from the earth your prey,<br>nor shall the sound of your envoys be heard again.  

## 3장

**3:1** Woe, city of bloodshed,<br>all of it deceit.<br>Filled with plunder,<br>prey never gone from it.  
**3:2** The sound of the whip<br>and the sound of the wheel’s clatter,<br>galloping horse<br>and chariot bounding.  
**3:3** Rearing charger<br>and blade of the sword<br>and flash of the spear,<br>and many the slain<br>and the press of the corpses—<br>there is no end to the bodies,<br>they stumble on the bodies.  
**3:4** Because of all the whoring of the whore,<br>the beguiling sorceress,<br>who ensnares nations with her whoring<br>and clans with her spells,  
**3:5** here am I against you, said the LORD of Armies—<br>I will lay bare your skirts over your face<br>and show nations your nakedness<br>and kingdoms your shame.  
**3:6** And I will fling foul things upon you<br>and make you vile and make a spectacle of you.  
**3:7** And it shall be that all who see you<br>shall shrink from you and say:<br>“Nineveh is ravaged!<br>Who will grieve for her,<br>and where can I seek<br>comforters for her?”  
**3:8** Are you better than No-Amon<br>that sits by the Nile,<br>water all around her,<br>that has a sea as a rampart,<br>water is her wall?  
**3:9** Nubia the vast<br>and Egypt without end,<br>Put and the Lybians<br>were her allies.  
**3:10** Yet she, too, went captive into exile.<br>Her babes, too, were smashed<br>at every street corner,<br>and for her notables they cast lots<br>and all her great men were shackled in chains.  
**3:11** You, too, shall be drunk,<br>you shall be overcome.<br>You, too, shall seek a stronghold against the enemy.  
**3:12** All your fortresses are figs,<br>ripe fruit.<br>If they are shaken, they fall<br>into the mouth of the eater.  
**3:13** Look, your people are women<br>in your midst for your enemies.<br>The gates of your land are wide open.<br>Fire has consumed their bolts.  
**3:14** Siege water draw for yourself,<br>reinforce your fortresses.<br>Come into the mud<br>and trample the clay,<br>grasp the brickwork.  
**3:15** There shall fire consume you,<br>the sword shall cut you off,<br>shall consume you like locusts.<br>Be as many as locusts,<br>be as many as grasshoppers.  
**3:16** You had merchants more numerous<br>than the stars of the heavens—<br>locusts spread out and flew off.  
**3:17** Your commanders were like grasshoppers<br>and your officers like swarms of locusts<br>that settle on stone fences<br>on a cold day.<br>When the sun rises, they go off,<br>and where their place is no one knows.  
**3:18** Your shepherds have slumbered, Assyrian king,<br>your staunch men are asleep,<br>your people scattered over the mountains, none gathering them.  
**3:19** There is no healing for your disaster,<br>your wound is grievous.<br>All who hear the report of you<br>clap hands over you.<br>For over whom has not passed<br>your constant evil?
