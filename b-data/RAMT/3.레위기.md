# 3.레위기 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** And He called to Moses and the LORD spoke to him from the Tent of Meeting, saying,  
**1:2** “Speak to the Israelites and say to them, ‘Should any person from you bring forward to the LORD an offering, of beasts from herd and from flock you shall bring forward your offering.  
**1:3** If his offering is a burnt offering, an unblemished male from the herd he shall bring it forward, at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, to be accepted for him before the LORD.  
**1:4** And he shall lay his hand on the head of the burnt offering, and it will be accepted for him to atone for him.  
**1:5** And he shall slaughter the male of the herd before the LORD, and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall bring forward the blood and cast the blood round the altar which is at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.  
**1:6** And he shall flay the burnt offering and cut it up into its parts.  
**1:7** And the sons of Aaron the priest shall place fire on the altar and lay out the wood on the fire.  
**1:8** And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall lay out the parts, the head and the fat, on the wood that is on the fire which is on the altar.  
**1:9** And its innards and its legs he shall wash in water, and the priest shall turn it all to smoke on the altar, a fire offering, a fragrant odor to the LORD.  
**1:10** And if his offering is from the flock, from the sheep or from the goats, for a burnt offering, an unblemished male he shall bring it forward.  
**1:11** And he shall slaughter it on the north side of the altar before the LORD, and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall cast its blood round the altar.  
**1:12** And he shall cut it up into its parts, its head and its fat, and the priest shall lay them out on the wood that is on the fire which is on the altar.  
**1:13** And the innards and the legs he shall wash in water, and the priest shall bring it all forward and turn it to smoke on the altar. It is a burnt offering, a fire offering, a fragrant odor to the LORD.  
**1:14** And if his burnt offering to the LORD is from the birds, he shall bring forward his offering from the turtledoves or from the young pigeons.  
**1:15** And the priest shall bring it forward on the altar and pinch off its head and turn it to smoke on the altar, and its blood shall be drained on the wall of the altar.  
**1:16** And he shall remove its crop with its feathers and fling it by the altar to the east, to the place of the ashes.  
**1:17** And he shall tear it open by its wings, he shall not divide them, and the priest shall turn it to smoke on the altar, on the wood that is on the fire. It is a burnt offering, a fire offering, a fragrant odor to the LORD.’”  

## 2장

**2:1** “‘Should a person bring forward a grain offering to the LORD, fine semolina his offering shall be, and he shall pour oil over it and place frankincense upon it.  
**2:2** And he shall bring it to the sons of Aaron, the priests, and a handful from there shall be scooped up, from its fine semolina and from its oil, together with all its frankincense, and the priest shall turn its token portion to smoke on the altar, a fire offering, a fragrant odor to the LORD.  
**2:3** And what is left of the grain offering is for Aaron and for his sons, holy of holies from the fire offerings of the LORD.  
**2:4** And when you bring forward a grain offering, baked in an oven, it is to be fine semolina, flatbread cakes mixed in oil or wafers of flatbread coated with oil.  
**2:5** And if your offering is a grain offering on a griddle, mixed in oil, flatbread it shall be.  
**2:6** Break it into bits, and you shall pour oil over it. It is a grain offering.  
**2:7** And if your offering is a grain offering in a pan, with fine semolina in oil it shall be made.  
**2:8** And you shall bring the grain offering that will be made from these to the LORD, and the priest shall bring it forward and put it out on the altar.  
**2:9** And the priest shall set aside from the grain offering its token portion and turn it to smoke on the altar, a fire offering, a fragrant odor to the LORD.  
**2:10** And what is left of the grain offering is for Aaron and for his sons, holy of holies from the fire offerings of the LORD.  
**2:11** Any grain offering that you bring forward to the LORD shall not be made leavened, for you shall not turn to smoke any leaven nor any honey from it as a fire offering to the LORD.  
**2:12** You may bring them forward to the LORD as an offering of first yield, but they shall not go up on the altar as a fragrant odor.  
**2:13** And every offering of your grain you shall season with salt. You shall not leave out the salt of the covenant of your God from your grain offering. With each of your offerings you shall offer salt.  
**2:14** And if you bring forward an offering of first fruits to the LORD, new ears of grain parched in fire, grits of fresh ears you shall bring forward the offering of your first fruits.  
**2:15** And you shall put oil upon it and you shall place frankincense on it. It is a grain offering.  
**2:16** And the priest shall turn its token portion to smoke, from its grits and from its oil together with all its frankincense, a fire offering to the LORD.’”  

## 3장

**3:1** “‘And if his offering is a communion sacrifice, if he brings it forward from the herd, whether male or female, unblemished he shall bring it forward before the LORD.  
**3:2** And he shall lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, and the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall cast the blood round the altar.  
**3:3** And he shall bring forward from the communion sacrifice a fire offering to the LORD, the fat covering the innards and all the fat that is on the innards,  
**3:4** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the sinews, and the lobe on the liver, together with the kidneys, he shall remove it.  
**3:5** And the sons of Aaron shall turn it to smoke on the altar together with the burnt offering that is on the wood which is on the fire, a fire offering, a fragrant odor to the LORD.  
**3:6** And if his offering is from the flock as a communion sacrifice to the LORD, male or female, unblemished he shall bring it forward.  
**3:7** If a sheep he brings forward as his offering, he shall bring it forward before the LORD.  
**3:8** And he shall lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it before the Tent of Meeting, and the sons of Aaron shall cast its blood round the altar.  
**3:9** And he shall bring forward from the communion sacrifice a fire offering to the LORD, its fat, the entire broad tail opposite the backbone, he shall remove it, and the fat covering the innards and all the fat that is on the innards.  
**3:10** And the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the sinews, and the lobe on the liver, together with the kidneys, he shall remove it.  
**3:11** And the priest shall turn it to smoke on the altar, fire-offering bread for the LORD.  
**3:12** And if his offering is a goat, he shall bring it forward before the LORD.  
**3:13** And he shall lay his hand on its head and slaughter it before the Tent of Meeting, and the sons of Aaron shall fling its blood round the altar.  
**3:14** And he shall bring forward his offering from it, a fire offering to the LORD, the fat covering the innards and the fat that is on the innards.  
**3:15** And the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the sinews, and the lobe on the liver, together with the kidneys, he shall remove it.  
**3:16** And the priest shall turn them to smoke on the altar, fire-offering bread for the LORD, as a fragrant odor, all the fat to the LORD.  
**3:17** An everlasting statute for your generations in all your dwelling places, no fat and no blood shall you eat.’”  

## 4장

**4:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**4:2** “Speak to the Israelites, saying, ‘Should a person offend errantly in regard to any of the LORD’s commands that should not be done and he do one of these,  
**4:3** if the anointed priest should offend, incurring guilt for the people, he shall bring forward for his offense that he has committed an unblemished bull from the herd for the LORD as an offense offering.  
**4:4** And he shall bring the bull to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting before the LORD and lay his hand on the head of the bull and slaughter the bull before the LORD.  
**4:5** And the anointed priest shall take from the blood of the bull and bring it to the Tent of Meeting.  
**4:6** And the priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle from the blood seven times before the LORD against the covering of the shrine.  
**4:7** And the priest shall put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of aromatic incense before the LORD, which is in the Tent of Meeting, and all the blood of the bull he shall pour out at the base of the burnt-offering altar which is at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.  
**4:8** And all the fat of the bull of offense offering, he shall set aside from it the fat covering the innards and all the fat that is on the innards.  
**4:9** The two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the sinews, and the lobe on the liver, together with the kidneys, he shall take away,  
**4:10** as it is set aside from the communion-sacrifice bull. And the priest shall turn them to smoke on the burnt-offering altar,  
**4:11** and the hide of the bull together with its head and together with its shanks and its innards and its dung.  
**4:12** And he shall take the whole bull out beyond the camp to a clean place, to the ash heap, and he shall burn it on wood with fire, on the ash heap it shall be burned.  
**4:13** And if all the community of Israel should err, and the thing be hidden from the eyes of the assembly, and they do one of all the LORD’s commands that should not be done, and they bear guilt,  
**4:14** and the offense that they committed become known, the assembly shall bring forward a bull from the herd as an offense offering, and they shall bring it before the Tent of Meeting.  
**4:15** And the elders of the community shall lay their hands on the head of the bull before the LORD, and the bull shall be slaughtered before the LORD.  
**4:16** And the anointed priest shall bring from the blood of the bull to the Tent of Meeting.  
**4:17** And the priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle from the blood seven times before the LORD against the covering of the shrine.  
**4:18** And some of the blood he shall put on the horns of the altar that is before the LORD, which is in the Tent of Meeting, and all the blood he shall pour out at the base of the burnt-offering altar, which is at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.  
**4:19** And he shall set aside all the fat from the bull and turn it to smoke on the altar.  
**4:20** And he shall do to the bull as he did to the offense-offering bull, so shall it be done to it, and the priest shall atone for them and it shall be forgiven them.  
**4:21** And he shall take the bull out beyond the camp and burn it as he burned the first bull. It is an offense offering of the assembly.  
**4:22** When a chieftain offends and does one of all the commands of the LORD his God that should not be done, in errance, and he bears guilt,  
**4:23** or his offense that he committed is made known to him, he shall bring his offering, an unblemished male goat.  
**4:24** And he shall lay his hand on the head of the goat and slaughter it in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered before the LORD. It is an offense offering.  
**4:25** And the priest shall take from the blood of the offense offering on his finger and place it on the horns of the burnt-offering altar, and he shall pour out its blood at the base of the burnt-offering altar.  
**4:26** And all its fat he shall turn to smoke on the altar like the fat of the communion sacrifice, and the priest shall atone for him for his offense, and it shall be forgiven him.  
**4:27** And if a single person from the common people should offend errantly in doing one of the LORD’s commands that should not be done, and bear guilt,  
**4:28** or his offense that he committed is made known to him, he shall bring his offering, an unblemished female goat, for his offense which he committed.  
**4:29** And he shall lay his hand on the head of the offense offering and slaughter the offense offering in the place of the burnt offering.  
**4:30** And the priest shall take from its blood on his finger and place it on the horns of the burnt-offering altar, and he shall pour out all its blood at the base of the altar.  
**4:31** And all its fat he shall take away, as the fat was taken away from the communion sacrifice, and the priest shall turn it to smoke on the altar as a fragrant odor to the LORD, and the priest shall atone for him and it shall be forgiven him.  
**4:32** And if he brings a sheep as his offering for an offense, an unblemished female he shall bring it.  
**4:33** And he shall lay his hand on the head of the offense offering and slaughter it as an offense offering in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered.  
**4:34** And the priest shall take from the blood of the offense offering on his finger and place it on the horns of the altar, and all its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar.  
**4:35** And all its fat he shall take away as the fat of the sheep is taken away from the communion sacrifice, and the priest shall turn it to smoke together with the fire offerings of the LORD, and the priest shall atone for him, for his offense that he committed, and it shall be forgiven him.’”  

## 5장

**5:1** “‘And should a person offend when he has heard a voice in adjuration, he being witness, or has seen or known, if he does not tell, he shall bear his punishment.  
**5:2** Or a person who touches any unclean thing, whether the carcass of an unclean beast or the carcass of an unclean domestic animal, or the carcass of an unclean crawling thing, and it be hidden from him, and he is unclean and guilty,  
**5:3** or should he touch human uncleanness, of any of the uncleannesses with which one may be defiled, and it be hidden from him, and he be guilty,  
**5:4** or should a person swear to utter with the lips, whether for evil or for good, of all that a human utters in a vow, and it be hidden from him, and he know and be guilty of one of these,  
**5:5** it shall be when he is guilty of any of these, he shall confess that concerning which he has offended.  
**5:6** And he shall bring his guilt offering to the LORD for his offense that he has committed, a female from the flock, a ewe or a she-goat, as an offense offering, and the priest shall atone for him for his offense.  
**5:7** And if his hand cannot attain as much as a sheep, he shall bring as his guilt offering for what he has committed two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the LORD, one for an offense offering and one for a burnt offering.  
**5:8** And he shall bring them to the priest, who shall bring forward first the one for the offense offering and pinch its head at its nape but not sever it.  
**5:9** And he shall sprinkle from the blood of the offense offering against the wall of the altar, and what is left of the blood shall be drained at the base of the altar. It is an offense offering.  
**5:10** And the second one he shall make a burnt offering according to regulation, and the priest shall atone for him for his offense that he has committed, and it shall be forgiven him.  
**5:11** And if his hand cannot attain two turtledoves or two young pigeons, he shall bring as his offering for what he has committed a tenth of an ephah of fine semolina as offense offering. He shall not put oil on it nor shall he place frankincense on it, for it is an offense offering.  
**5:12** And he shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall scoop up a fistful from it as its token and turn it to smoke on the altar together with the fire offerings of the LORD. It is an offense offering.  
**5:13** And the priest shall atone for him for his offense that he has committed of any of these, and it shall be forgiven him, and it shall be for the priest like the grain offering.’”  
**5:14** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**5:15** “Should a person betray trust and offend errantly in regard to any of the LORD’s sancta, he shall bring his guilt offering to the LORD, an unblemished ram from the flock, or its equivalent in silver shekels, according to the sanctuary shekel, as a guilt offering.  
**5:16** And that concerning which he has offended from the sanctum he shall pay and a fifth part he shall add to it and give to the priest, and the priest shall atone for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and it shall be forgiven him.  
**5:17** And if a person offends and does any one of all the commands of the LORD that should not be done and does not know and is guilty, he shall bear his punishment.  
**5:18** And he shall bring an unblemished ram from the flock, or its equivalent, as a guilt offering, to the priest, and the priest shall atone for him for his errancy that he has committed without knowing, and it shall be forgiven him.  
**5:19** It is a guilt offering. He has surely incurred guilt to the LORD.”  
**5:20** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**5:21** “Should a person offend and betray the LORD’s trust and dissemble with his fellow about a deposit or pledge, or by theft, or defraud his fellow,  
**5:22** or should he find something lost and dissemble about it and swear falsely about anything of what a person may do to offend,  
**5:23** and it shall be, when he offends and is guilty, he shall return the theft that he stole or the fraud that he committed or the deposit that was placed with him or the lost thing that he found,  
**5:24** or anything that he swore about falsely, and he shall pay back the principal and add a fifth, to him to whom it belongs he shall give it, when his guilt is confirmed.  
**5:25** And his guilt offering he shall bring to the LORD, an unblemished ram from the flock, or its equivalent, as a guilt offering, to the priest.  
**5:26** And the priest shall atone for him before the LORD, and it shall be forgiven him for whatever he may have done to incur guilt thereby.”  

## 6장

**6:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**6:2** “Charge Aaron and his sons, saying, ‘This is the teaching of the burnt offering. It is the very burnt offering over its flame on the altar all night till morning, and the fire of the altar shall keep burning on in it.  
**6:3** And the priest shall wear his linen garb and linen breeches he shall wear on his body, and he shall take away the ashes that the fire consumes from the burnt offering on the altar and put them beside the altar.  
**6:4** And he shall take off his clothes and wear other clothes and take out the ashes beyond the camp to a clean place.  
**6:5** And the fire on the altar shall keep burning on it, it shall not go out, and the priest shall burn wood on it morning after morning and lay out on it the burnt offering and turn the fat part of the communion offerings to smoke.  
**6:6** A perpetual fire shall keep burning on the altar. It shall not go out.  
**6:7** “‘And this is the teaching of the grain offering. The sons of Aaron are to bring it forward before the LORD in front of the altar.  
**6:8** And a handful of it shall be removed, from the semolina of the grain offering and from its oil, with all the frankincense that is on the grain offering, and it shall be turned to smoke on the altar, a fragrant odor, its token to the LORD.  
**6:9** And what is left of it Aaron and his sons shall eat, as flatcakes it shall be eaten, in a holy place, in the court of the Tent of Meeting they shall eat it.  
**6:10** It shall not be baked leavened. As their share I have given it from my fire offerings. It is holy of holies, like the offense offering and like the guilt offering.  
**6:11** Every male among the sons of Aaron shall eat it, a perpetual portion for your generations from the fire offerings of the LORD. Whatever touches them shall become holy.’”  
**6:12** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**6:13** “This is the offering of Aaron and his sons which they shall bring forward to the LORD on the day he is anointed: a tenth of an ephah of semolina as a perpetual grain offering, half of it in the morning and half of it in the evening.  
**6:14** On a griddle in oil it shall be done, soaked through you shall bring it, as a grain offering of baked pieces you shall bring it forward, a fragrant odor to the LORD.  
**6:15** And the anointed priest, successor among his sons, shall do it, a perpetual portion for the LORD, it shall be entirely turned to smoke.  
**6:16** And every priest’s grain offering shall be entire, it shall not be eaten.”  
**6:17** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**6:18** “Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying, ‘This is the teaching of the offense offering. In the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, the offense offering shall be slaughtered before the LORD. It is holy of holies.  
**6:19** The priest performing it as an offense offering shall eat it. In a holy place it shall be eaten, in the court of the Tent of Meeting.  
**6:20** Whatever touches its flesh shall become holy, and when some of its blood is spattered on a garment, that which has been spattered shall be laundered in a holy place.  
**6:21** And an earthen vessel in which it is boiled shall be broken, and if it was boiled in a copper vessel, it shall be scoured and rinsed with water.  
**6:22** Every male among the priests shall eat it. It is holy of holies.  
**6:23** And every offense offering from which blood is brought into the Tent of Meeting to atone in the sanctum shall not be eaten. In fire it shall be burned.’”  

## 7장

**7:1** And this is the teaching of the guilt offering. It is holy of holies.  
**7:2** In the place where they slaughter the burnt offering they shall slaughter the guilt offering and its blood shall be cast on the altar all around.  
**7:3** And all of its fat shall be brought forward, the broad tail and the fat covering the innards,  
**7:4** and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them which is over the loins and the lobe on the liver, together with the kidneys, it shall be removed.  
**7:5** And the priest shall turn them to smoke on the altar, a fire offering to the LORD. It is a guilt offering.  
**7:6** Any male among the priests may eat it; in a holy place it shall be eaten. It is holy of holies.  
**7:7** The offense offering like the sin offering, a single teaching do they have: the priest who atones through it, his shall it be.  
**7:8** And the priest bringing forward a man’s burnt offering, the hide of the burnt offering that the priest brought forward is the priest’s, his it shall be.  
**7:9** And every grain offering that is baked in an oven and everything made in a pan and on a griddle is the priest’s who brings it forward, his it shall be.  
**7:10** And every grain offering, mixed with oil or dry, shall be for all the sons of Aaron, each man of them alike.  
**7:11** And this is the teaching of the communion sacrifice that is brought forward to the LORD.  
**7:12** If in thanksgiving he brings it forward, he shall bring forward with the thanksgiving sacrifice flatcakes mixed in oil and flatcake wafers coated with oil and cakes of semolina soaked through, mixed in oil.  
**7:13** With cakes of leavened bread he shall bring forward his offering with his thanksgiving communion sacrifice.  
**7:14** And he shall bring forward from it one kind of each offering, a levy to the LORD; for the priest casting the blood of the communion sacrifice, his it shall be.  
**7:15** And the flesh of his thanksgiving communion sacrifice shall be eaten on the day of its offering; he shall not leave anything of it till the morning.  
**7:16** And if his offering is a votive or a freewill offering, on the day he brings forward his sacrifice it shall be eaten, and on the morrow what is left of it may be eaten.  
**7:17** And what is left of the flesh of the sacrifice on the third day shall be burned in fire.  
**7:18** And if some of the flesh of his communion sacrifice should indeed be eaten on the third day, it will not be acceptable, he who brings it forth, it will not be reckoned for him, it is desecrated meat; and the person eating of it will bear his guilt.  
**7:19** And the flesh that touches anything unclean shall not be eaten, in fire it shall be burned. And other flesh, whoever is clean may eat the flesh.  
**7:20** And the person who eats flesh from the communion sacrifice which is the LORD’s and his uncleanness is upon him, that person shall be cut off from his kin.  
**7:21** And should a person touch anything unclean through human uncleanness or an unclean beast or any unclean abominable creature and eat of the flesh of the communion sacrifice which is the LORD’s, that person shall be cut off from his kin.  
**7:22** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**7:23** “Speak to the Israelites, saying, ‘No fat of ox or sheep or goat shall you eat,  
**7:24** and the fat of a beast that has died and the fat of a beast torn by predators may be used for any task but it shall by no means be eaten.  
**7:25** For whoever eats fat from the animal from which fire offering is brought forward to the LORD, the person eating shall be cut off from his kin.  
**7:26** And no blood shall you consume in all your dwelling places, whether from fowl or beast.  
**7:27** Any person who consumes any blood shall be cut off from his kin.’”  
**7:28** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**7:29** “Speak to the Israelites, saying, ‘He who brings forward his communion sacrifice to the LORD shall bring his offering to the LORD from his communion sacrifice.  
**7:30** His own hands shall bring the fire offerings of the LORD, the fat together with the breast he shall bring it, the breast to elevate as an elevation offering before the LORD.  
**7:31** And the priest shall turn the breast into smoke on the altar, and the breast shall be Aaron’s and his sons’.  
**7:32** And the right thigh you shall give as a levy to the priest from the communion sacrifices.  
**7:33** He of the sons of Aaron who brings forward the blood of the communion sacrifices and the fat, his shall be the right thigh as a share.  
**7:34** For the breast of the elevation offering and the thigh of the levy I have taken from the Israelites, from their communion sacrifices, and have given them to Aaron the priest and to his sons as a perpetual portion from the Israelites.  
**7:35** This is the allotment of Aaron and the allotment of his sons from the fire offerings of the LORD from the day they were brought forward to be priests to the LORD,  
**7:36** which the LORD charged to give to them from the day they were anointed, from the Israelites, a perpetual statute for their generations.  
**7:37** This is the teaching for the burnt offering, for the grain offering, and for the offense offering and for the guilt offering and for the installation offering and for the communion sacrifice  
**7:38** which the LORD charged Moses on Mount Sinai on the day He charged the Israelites to bring forward their sacrifices to the LORD in the Wilderness of Sinai.’”  

## 8장

**8:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**8:2** “Take Aaron and his sons with him, and the garments and the anointing oil and the offense-offering bull and the two rams and the basket of flatbread.  
**8:3** And assemble all the community at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.”  
**8:4** And Moses did as the LORD had charged him, and the community assembled at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.  
**8:5** And Moses said to the community, “This is the thing that the LORD charged to do.”  
**8:6** And Moses brought forward Aaron and his sons and bathed them in water.  
**8:7** And he placed the tunics on them and girded them with the sash and dressed them in the robe and put the ephod over it and girded it with the ornamental band of the ephod, and tied it to him with it.  
**8:8** And he put the breastplate on him and placed in the breastplate the Urim and the Thummim.  
**8:9** And he put the turban on his head, and he put upon the turban at the front the golden diadem, the holy crown, as the LORD had charged Moses.  
**8:10** And Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the Tabernacle and everything in it and consecrated them.  
**8:11** And he sprinkled from it on the altar seven times and anointed the altar and all its implements and the laver and its stand to consecrate them.  
**8:12** And he poured from the anointing oil on Aaron’s head and anointed him to consecrate him.  
**8:13** And Moses brought forward the sons of Aaron and dressed them in tunics and girded them with sashes and bound headdresses on them as the LORD had charged Moses.  
**8:14** And he brought close the offense-offering bull, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the offense-offering bull,  
**8:15** and it was slaughtered. And Moses took the blood and put it on the horns of the altar all around with his finger and rid the altar of offense, and the blood he poured out at the base of the altar and consecrated it to atone over it.  
**8:16** And he took all the fat that was on the innards and the lobe on the liver and the two kidneys and their fat, and Moses turned them to smoke on the altar.  
**8:17** And the bull and its hide and its flesh and its dung he burned in fire outside the camp, as the LORD had charged Moses.  
**8:18** And he brought forward the burnt-offering ram, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram.  
**8:19** And it was slaughtered, and Moses cast the blood on the altar all around.  
**8:20** And the ram he cut up into its parts, and Moses turned the head and the cut parts and the fat into smoke.  
**8:21** And the innards and the legs he washed in water. And Moses turned the whole ram to smoke on the altar—a burnt offering it was—as a fragrant odor to the LORD, a fire offering to the LORD it was, as the LORD had charged Moses.

**26:14** “‘But if you do not heed Me and do not do all these commands,  
**26:15** and if you reject My statutes and if you loathe My laws, not doing all My commands, voiding My covenant,  
**26:16** I on My part will do this to you: I will direct panic against you, consumption and fever wasting the eyes and making the throat ache, and you shall sow your seed in vain, and your enemies shall eat it.  
**26:17** And I will set My face against you and you shall be routed before your enemies, and your foes shall hold sway over you, and you shall flee with none pursuing you.  
**26:18** And if even with these you do not heed Me, I will go on to chastise you sevenfold more for your offenses.  
**26:19** And I will break the pride of your strength and make your heavens like iron and your earth like bronze.  
**26:20** And your power shall be spent in vain, and your land shall not give its yield, and the tree of the land shall not give its fruit.  
**26:21** And if you come in encounter against Me and do not want to heed Me, I will go on with blows against you sevenfold for your offenses.  
**26:22** And I will unleash the beasts of the field among you and they shall bereave you of your children and cut off your cattle and diminish you, and your roads will be desolate.  
**26:23** And if through these you do not take chastisement by Me, and you come in encounter against Me,  
**26:24** I on My part will come in encounter against you and I Myself will strike you sevenfold for your offenses.  
**26:25** And I will bring against you the avenging sword of the covenant’s vengeance, and you shall gather into your towns, and I will send pestilence in your midst, and you shall be given into an enemy’s hands.  
**26:26** When I break your staff of bread, ten women shall bake your bread in one oven and dole out your bread by weight, and you shall eat and not be sated.  
**26:27** And if despite this you do not heed Me and you come in encounter against Me,  
**26:28** I will come against you in wrathful encounter and I on My part will chastise you sevenfold for your offenses.  
**26:29** And you shall eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters you shall eat.  
**26:30** And I will destroy your cult-places and cut off your incense stands, and I will put your corpses on top of the corpses of your fetishes, and I will loathe you.  
**26:31** And I will turn your towns into ruins and lay waste your sanctuaries, and I will not smell your fragrant odors.  
**26:32** And I Myself will lay waste to the land, and all your enemies who settle in it shall be appalled by it.  
**26:33** And you I will scatter among the nations, and I will unsheath the sword after you, and your land shall be a desolation, and your towns a ruin.  
**26:34** Then shall the land expiate its sabbath years all the days it is desolate while you are in the land of your enemies, then shall the land keep a sabbath and expiate its sabbath years.  
**26:35** All the days of the desolation it shall keep a sabbath for not having kept your sabbath years when you dwelled there.  
**26:36** And those left among you, I will bring faintness into their hearts in the lands of their enemies, and the sound of a driven leaf shall pursue them, and they shall flee as in flight from the sword and fall, with none pursuing.  
**26:37** And each man shall stumble against his brother as before the sword, with none pursuing, and there shall be no standing up for you in the face of your enemies.  
**26:38** And you shall perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies shall consume you.  
**26:39** And those left among you shall rot in their guilt in the lands of your enemies, and also in the guilt of their fathers with them they shall rot.  
**26:40** And they shall confess their guilt and the guilt of their fathers, in their betraying My trust,  
**26:41** and also in their coming in encounter against Me. I on My part will come in encounter against them and bring them into the land of their enemies, and then shall their uncircumcised heart be humbled, and then shall they expiate their guilt.  
**26:42** And I will remember My covenant with Jacob and also My covenant with Isaac and also My covenant with Abraham I will remember, and the land I will remember.  
**26:43** And the land shall be forsaken of them, and it shall expiate its sabbath years when it is desolate of them, and they shall expiate their guilt, by very reason that they have rejected My laws, and My statutes they have loathed.  
**26:44** Yet even this, too—when they are in the land of their enemies I will not reject them and I will not loathe them to put an end to them, to void My covenant with them, for I am the LORD their God.  
**26:45** And I will remember for them the covenant of the first ones whom I brought out from the land of Egypt before the eyes of the nations to be God for them, I am the LORD.’”  
**26:46** These are the statutes and the laws and the teachings that the LORD set out between Himself and the Israelites on Mount Sinai through the hand of Moses.  

## 27장

**27:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**27:2** “Speak to the Israelites, and you shall say to them, ‘Should a man set aside a votive offering in the value fixed for persons to the LORD,  
**27:3** the value for the male from twenty years old up to sixty years old shall be fifty shekels by the sanctuary shekel.  
**27:4** And if it is for a female, the value shall be thirty shekels.  
**27:5** And if it is for someone from five years old up to twenty years old, the value shall be twenty shekels for the male and for the female ten shekels.  
**27:6** And if it is for someone from a month old up to a year, the value of the male shall be five silver shekels and for the female the value three silver shekels.  
**27:7** And if it is for someone from sixty years old and above, if a male, the value shall be fifteen shekels and for the female ten shekels.  
**27:8** And if one should become too impoverished to meet the value, he shall be set before the priest, and the priest shall evaluate him, according to what the hand of the votary can attain shall the priest evaluate him.  
**27:9** And if it is an animal that is brought forward as an offering to the LORD, any of which may be given to the LORD shall be holy.  
**27:10** He shall not exchange it and shall not replace it, whether good for bad or bad for good, and if in fact he replaces one animal for another, both it and its replacement shall be holy.  
**27:11** And if it is any unclean animal that is not to be brought forward as an offering to the LORD, the animal shall be set before the priest.  
**27:12** And the priest shall evaluate it, whether good or bad, according to the priest’s valuation, thus shall it be.  
**27:13** And if he in fact redeems it, he shall add a fifth to its value.  
**27:14** And should a man consecrate his house as holy to the LORD, the priest shall evaluate it, whether good or bad. As the priest evaluates it, thus shall it stand.  
**27:15** And if he who consecrates his house redeems it, he shall add a fifth to the silver of its value, and it shall be his.  
**27:16** And if a man should consecrate anything from a field of his holding to the LORD, its value shall be according to its seed—a homer of barley seed at fifty silver shekels.  
**27:17** If he consecrates his field from the jubilee year, it shall stand according to its value.  
**27:18** And if he consecrates his field after the jubilee, the priest shall reckon the silver for him according to the remaining years until the jubilee, and these shall be deducted from its value.  
**27:19** And if he who consecrates the field shall in fact redeem it, he shall add a fifth to its value, and it shall become his.  
**27:20** And if he does not redeem the field and had sold the field to another man, it may no longer be redeemed.  
**27:21** And the field when it is released in the jubilee shall be holy to the LORD, as a proscribed field. His holding shall become the priest’s.  
**27:22** And if he consecrates a field he has purchased, which is not from the field of his holding, to the LORD,  
**27:23** the priest shall reckon for him the amount of the value until the jubilee year, and he shall give the value on that day, a sacred donation to the LORD.  
**27:24** In the jubilee year the field shall revert to him from whom it was bought to him who has the holding of the land.  
**27:25** And every valuation shall be by the sanctuary shekel, twenty gerahs the shekel shall be.  
**27:26** But the firstborn of the animals, which is marked firstborn to the LORD, no man shall consecrate. Whether ox or sheep, it is the LORD’s.  
**27:27** And if it is of an unclean animal, he shall ransom it by its value and add a fifth, and if it is not redeemed, it shall be sold at its value.  
**27:28** But anything proscribed, that a man may proscribe for the LORD, of anything he has, whether of humans or animals or of the field of his holding, shall not be sold and shall not be redeemed. Anything proscribed is holy of holies to the LORD.  
**27:29** No human who has been proscribed may be ransomed. He is doomed to die.  
**27:30** And all tithes of the land, of the seed of the land, of the fruit of the land, are the Lord’s, holy to the LORD.  
**27:31** And if a man in fact redeems something of his tithe, he shall add a fifth to it.  
**27:32** And all tithes of cattle and sheep, anything that passes under the staff, the tenth shall be holy to the LORD.  
**27:33** He shall not look out for good or bad, and he shall not replace it. And if in fact he replaces it, it or its replacement shall be holy, it shall not be redeemed.’”  
**27:34** These are the commands that the LORD charged Moses for the Israelites on Mount Sinai.
