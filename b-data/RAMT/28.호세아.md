# 28.호세아 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Hosea son of Beeri in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, Hezekiah, kings of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash king of Israel.  
**1:2** When the LORD began to speak to Hosea, the LORD said to Hosea:<br>Go, take you a wife of whoring<br>and children of whoring,<br>for the land has surely whored<br>away from the LORD.  
**1:3** And he went and took Gomer daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore him a son.  
**1:4** And the LORD said to him: “Call his name Jezreel, for soon I will make a reckoning for the blood of Jezreel against the house of Jehu and put an end to the kingdom of the house of Israel.  
**1:5** And it shall be on that day I will break the bow of Israel in the Valley of Jezreel.”  
**1:6** And she conceived again and bore a daughter. And He said to him: “Call her name Lo-Ruhamah, for I will no more show mercy to the house of Israel nor will I forgive them in any way.  
**1:7** But to the house of Judah I will show mercy and rescue them through the LORD their God, but I will not rescue them through bow and sword and in battle with horses and with horsemen.”  
**1:8** And she weaned Lo-Ruhamah and conceived and bore a son.  
**1:9** And He said: “Call his name, Lo-Ami, Not My People, and I will not be yours.”  

## 2장

**2:1** And the number of the Israelites shall be like the sand of the sea that cannot be measured and cannot be counted, and it shall happen, instead of its being said of them, “You Are Not My People,” it shall be said of them, “Children of the Living God.”  
**2:2** And the people of Judah and the people of Israel shall gather together and set over them a single chief, and they shall go up from the lands, for great is the day of Jezreel.  
**2:3** Say to your brothers, “My People,”<br>and to your sisters, “She Is Shown Mercy.”  
**2:4** Bring a case against your mother, bring a case,<br>for she is not My wife,<br>and I am not her husband.<br>Let her take off her whoring from her face<br>and her adultery from between her breasts,  
**2:5** lest I strip her naked<br>and set her out as the day she was born.<br>And I will turn her into a desert<br>and make her like parched land<br>and let her die of thirst.  
**2:6** And to her children I will show no mercy,<br>for they are the children of whoring.  
**2:7** For their mother played the whore,<br>she who conceived them acted shamefully.<br>For she said, “Let me go<br>after my lovers<br>who give me my bread and water,<br>my wool and my flax,<br>my oil and my unguents.”  
**2:8** Therefore I am about to hedge in your way with thorns<br>and raise a wall for her,<br>and she shall not find her paths.  
**2:9** And she shall run after her lovers<br>and shall not catch them,<br>and she shall seek them and not find them.<br>And she shall say, “Let me<br>go back to my first husband,<br>for it was then better for me than now.”  
**2:10** And she did not know<br>that it was I who gave her<br>the new grain and the wine and the oil,<br>and silver I showered upon her<br>and gold that they fashioned for Baal.  
**2:11** Therefore will I turn back and take away<br>My new grain in its time<br>and My wine in its season<br>and reclaim My wool and My flax<br>that would cover her nakedness.  
**2:12** And now, I will lay bare her shame<br>before the eyes of her lovers,<br>and no man shall save her.  
**2:13** And I will put an end to her rejoicing,<br>to her festivals, her new moons, and her sabbaths<br>and all her appointed times.  
**2:14** And I will wither her vines and her fig trees<br>of which she said, “They are a whore’s pay for me<br>that my lovers gave to me.”<br>And I will turn them into scrubland,<br>and the beasts of the field shall devour them.  
**2:15** And I will make a reckoning against her for the days of the Baalim<br>to whom she burned incense,<br>and she put on her nose-ring and her jewelry<br>and went after her lovers,<br>but Me she forgot, said the LORD.  
**2:16** Therefore, I am about to beguile her<br>and will lead her to the wilderness<br>and speak to her very heart.  
**2:17** And I will give her from there her vineyards<br>and the Valley of Achor an opening to hope,<br>and she shall sing out there as in the days of her youth,<br>as on the day she came up from the land of Egypt.  
**2:18** And it shall be, on that day, said the LORD,<br>she shall call Me “my Husband”<br>and no longer call Me “my Baal.”  
**2:19** And I will take away the names of the Baalim from your mouth,<br>and they shall no more be recalled by their name.  
**2:20** And I will seal a pact with them on that day,<br>with the beasts of the field and with the fowl of the heavens<br>and the creeping things of the earth.<br>And bow and sword and battle<br>will I break from the earth,<br>and I will make them lie down secure.  
**2:21** And I will betroth you to Me forever,<br>I will betroth you in right and in justice<br>and in kindness and in mercy.  
**2:22** And I will betroth you in faithfulness,<br>and you shall know that I am the LORD.  
**2:23** And it shall be on that day,<br>I will answer, said the LORD,<br>I will answer for the heavens,<br>and they shall answer for the earth.  
**2:24** And the earth shall answer for the new grain<br>and for the wine and for the oil,<br>and they shall answer for Jezreel.  
**2:25** And I will sow her for Me in the land<br>and show mercy to Lo-Ruhamah,<br>and I will say to Lo-Ami, “You are My people,”<br>and he shall say, “You are my God.”  

## 3장

**3:1** And the LORD said to me, “Again, go love a woman beloved by a companion yet an adulteress, like the love of God for the Israelites when they turn to other gods and to lovers of cups of the grape.” And I loved a lusting woman.  
**3:2** And I hired her for myself with fifteen weights of silver and a homer of barley and a letekh of barley.  
**3:3** And I said to her, “Many days you shall dwell with me. You shall not play the whore and you shall be no man’s, and I, too, shall not come to bed with you.  
**3:4** For many days shall the Israelites dwell without king and without commander and without sacrifice and without sacred pillar and without ephod and without teraphim.  
**3:5** Afterward, the Israelites shall turn back and seek the LORD their God and David their king, and they shall revere the LORD and His bounty in the latter days.”  

## 4장

**4:1** Hear the word of the LORD, O Israelites,<br>for the LORD has a brief<br>against the dwellers of the land.<br>For there is no truth and there is no trust<br>and there is no knowledge of the LORD in the land.  
**4:2** Falsely swear and murder<br>and steal and commit adultery.<br>They burst bonds—and blood spills upon blood.  
**4:3** Therefore the land does languish,<br>and all those dwelling within it are bleak.<br>With the beasts of the field and the fowl of the heavens<br>and with the fish of the sea, too—they shall perish.  
**4:4** But let no man inveigh<br>and let no man rebuke<br>when your people inveighs against priest.  
**4:5** And you stumbled by day,<br>and the prophet, too, stumbled by night,<br>and I will destroy your people.  
**4:6** My people is destroyed without knowledge,<br>for you—you rejected knowledge,<br>and I rejected you from being priest to Me.<br>And you forgot your God’s teaching—<br>I will forget your sons on My part.  
**4:7** As they increased, they offended against Me,<br>I will exchange their honor for disgrace.  
**4:8** My people’s offense offerings they eat,<br>and they long for its crimes.  
**4:9** And it shall be, people and priest alike,<br>I will make a reckoning with them for their ways,<br>and for their acts I will pay them back.  
**4:10** And they shall eat yet not be sated,<br>play the whore yet not burst bonds.<br>For they have forsaken the LORD  
**4:11** to keep on whoring and drinking<br>and new wine that takes away the mind.  
**4:12** My people asks oracles of a tree,<br>and his rod tells him what to do.<br>For a spirit of whoring made them stray,<br>and they played the whore against their God.  
**4:13** On the mountaintops they sacrificed,<br>on the hills they offered incense,<br>beneath the oak and the poplar,<br>and the terebinth of goodly shade.<br>Therefore their daughters go whoring,<br>and their daughters-in-law are adulterous.  
**4:14** I will make no reckoning with your daughters for whoring<br>and with your daughters-in-law who are adulterous.<br>For they themselves go off with the whores<br>and with the cult-harlots they sacrifice,<br>and a people undiscerning comes to grief.  
**4:15** If you go whoring, Israel,<br>let Judah not be held guilty<br>And do not come to Gilgal<br>nor go up to Beth-Aven,<br>and do not swear, “As the LORD lives.”  
**4:16** For like a wayward cow<br>Israel was wayward.<br>Now shall the LORD herd him<br>like a sheep in an open field?  
**4:17** Ephraim is stuck fast to idols.<br>Let him be!  
**4:18** When their swilling is over,<br>they go on to whore.<br>They love the disgrace of their defenders.  
**4:19** The wind bundles them in its wings,<br>and their altars are shamed.  

## 5장

**5:1** Hear this, you priests,<br>and listen, house of Israel,<br>and the house of the king, bend your ear,<br>for against you is the judgment.<br>For a trap you have been to Mizpah,<br>and an outspread net against Tabor,  
**5:2** and have dug a deep pit at Shittim,<br>but I am chastisement for them all.  
**5:3** I know Ephraim,<br>and Israel is not hidden from Me.<br>For now you have whored, Ephraim,<br>Israel is defiled.  
**5:4** Their acts do not let them<br>turn back to their God.<br>For the spirit of whoring is within them,<br>and the LORD they do not know.  
**5:5** And Israel’s pride bore witness against him,<br>and Israel and Ephraim stumble in their crime.<br>Judah, too, has stumbled with them.  
**5:6** With their sheep and their cattle they go<br>to seek out the LORD.<br>But they do not find Him.<br>He slipped away from them.  
**5:7** The LORD they betrayed,<br>for alien sons they bore.<br>Now the new moon shall consume them,<br>together with their fields.  
**5:8** Sound the ram’s horn at Gibeah,<br>the trumpet at Ramah.<br>Shout out at Beth-Aven:<br>“After you, Benjamin!”  
**5:9** Ephraim shall be a desolation<br>on the day of stern rebuke.<br>Among the tribes of Israel<br>I have faithfully made it known.  
**5:10** The nobles of Judah have become<br>like those who move boundaries.<br>Upon them I will pour<br>My wrath like water.  
**5:11** Exploited is Ephraim,<br>and crushed in justice,<br>For he undertook to go<br>after an empty thing.  
**5:12** And I am like the moth to Ephraim<br>and like rot to the house of Judah.  
**5:13** When Ephraim saw his sickness<br>and Judah his running sores,<br>Ephraim went off to Assyria<br>and sent out to King Jareb.<br>But he cannot cure you<br>and will not give you healing.  
**5:14** For I am like a lion to Ephraim<br>and like the king of beasts to the house of Judah.<br>I, I will maul the prey and go off,<br>bear it away and no one will save it.  
**5:15** I will go, return to My place,<br>until they sense their guilt and seek Me,<br>when in straits they shall search for Me.

## 7장

**7:1** and I would heal Israel,<br>Ephraim’s crime is laid bare<br>and the evils of Samaria,<br>for they have acted in lies;<br>the thief comes within<br>and the gang raids outside.  
**7:2** And let them not say in their heart<br>that I have recalled all their evil.<br>Now their deeds have turned them around—<br>before My face they are.  
**7:3** In their evil they gladden the king,<br>and with their deceits, the nobles.  
**7:4** All of them are adulterers,<br>like a burning oven.<br>The baker ceases from stirring,<br>from kneading the dough till it rises.  
**7:5** On the day of our king<br>the nobles made him sick with poisoned wine.<br>He had set his hand with the scoffers.  
**7:6** For they drew near in their ambush,<br>their hearts like an oven.<br>All night their leader sleeps,<br>in the morning he burns like a tongue of flame.  
**7:7** They all grow hot like an oven<br>and devour their judges<br>All of their kings have fallen,<br>none among them calls to Me.  
**7:8** Ephraim among the nations—<br>it is he who mingles.<br>Ephraim is like a loaf<br>not turned over.  
**7:9** Strangers consumed his strength,<br>but he did not know.<br>His hair turned suddenly gray,<br>but he did not know.  
**7:10** And Israel’s pride bore witness against it,<br>yet for all that they did not seek him.  
**7:11** And Ephraim became like a<br>foolish senseless dove.<br>To Egypt they called,<br>to Assyria went.  
**7:12** Where they go<br>I will spread My net upon them.<br>Like the fowl of the heavens I will bring them down.<br>I will bind them as their kinfolk listen.  
**7:13** Woe to them for they have wandered from Me.<br>Disaster for them, as they rebelled against Me!<br>Shall I redeem them<br>when they have spoken against Me lies?  
**7:14** And they did not cry out to Me from their heart,<br>but they wailed upon their couch,<br>over grain and new wine they gashed themselves,<br>they swerved away from Me.  
**7:15** I braced, I strengthened their arm,<br>but against Me they plotted evil.  
**7:16** They go back to what is worthless,<br>they are like a faulty bow.<br>Their nobles shall fall by the sword<br>because of their angry tongue,<br>which is their mockery in the land of Egypt.  

## 8장

**8:1** A ram’s horn to your lips!<br>—he is like an eagle against the LORD’s house.<br>For they have breached My covenant<br>and rebelled against My teaching.  
**8:2** To Me they cry out—<br>“We know you, O God of Israel!”  
**8:3** Israel rejects what is good—<br>an enemy shall pursue him.  
**8:4** They set up kings but not through Me,<br>installed nobles but without My knowledge.<br>From their silver and their gold<br>they made themselves idols,<br>that they might be cut off.  
**8:5** Your calf rejects you, Samaria,<br>My wrath against them flares.<br>How long will they fail to be clean?  
**8:6** And it—a craftsman made it,<br>and it is not a god.<br>For shards it shall become,<br>the calf of Samaria.  
**8:7** For they sow the wind<br>and harvest a storm.<br>Standing grain that has no sprouts,<br>it will not make flour.<br>If perhaps it should make some,<br>strangers shall swallow it up.  
**8:8** Israel has been swallowed,<br>now they become among the nations<br>like a vessel no one wants.  
**8:9** For they have gone up to Assyria,<br>a wild ass on its own is Ephraim,<br>they have made courtship’s plea.  
**8:10** Though they give gifts among the nations,<br>now will I gather them up,<br>and they will soon tremble<br>from the burden of kings and nobles.  
**8:11** For Ephraim made many altars<br>an offense they were for him,<br>altars to offend.  
**8:12** I wrote for him My many teachings—<br>like something strange they were viewed.  
**8:13** The sacrifices I gave they slaughtered,<br>it was flesh, and they ate.<br>The LORD did not accept them favorably.<br>Now will their crime be recalled,<br>and a reckoning made for their offense.<br>They shall go back to Egypt.  
**8:14** And Israel forgot his Maker<br>and he built palaces.<br>And Judah made many fortified towns,<br>but I set fire to his towns,<br>and it consumed his citadels.  

## 9장

**9:1** Do not rejoice, O Israel,<br>no exulting like the peoples.<br>For you went whoring from your God,<br>you loved a whore’s pay<br>on every new grain threshing floor.  
**9:2** Threshing floor and winepress know them not,<br>and new wine shall deny them.  
**9:3** They shall not dwell in the land of the LORD,<br>and Ephraim shall go back to Egypt<br>and in Assyria eat unclean things.  
**9:4** They shall pour no wine libation to the LORD,<br>and their sacrifices shall not please Him.<br>Like mourners’ bread it shall be to them,<br>all who eat it become unclean.<br>For their food is for their gullet,<br>it shall not enter the house of the LORD.  
**9:5** What will you do for the appointed day,<br>for the day of the LORD’s festival?  
**9:6** For, look, they go off from destruction.<br>Egypt gathers them in,<br>Memphis buries them.<br>The treasure house for their silver<br>the thistle shall inherit,<br>the thorn is in their tents.  
**9:7** The days of reckoning have arrived,<br>the days of retribution have arrived.<br>Israel shall know it.<br>The prophet is witless,<br>the man of spirit crazed<br>by all your crimes,<br>all your hate.  
**9:8** The lookout of Ephraim,<br>the prophet with my God—<br>a snare is laid in all his ways,<br>hate in the house of his God.  
**9:9** They have acted most ruinously<br>as in the days of Gibeah.<br>He shall recall their crime,<br>make a reckoning for their offense.  
**9:10** Like grapes in the wilderness<br>I found Israel.<br>Like the first fruit on the fig tree when it appears<br>I saw your fathers<br>Yet they came to Baal Peor<br>and devoted themselves to a shameful god<br>and became vile things like what they loved.  
**9:11** Ephraim—their glory shall fly off like a bird,<br>from birth and from the womb and from conception.  
**9:12** For should they raise their sons,<br>I would bereave them of humankind,<br>for woe to them indeed<br>when I swerve away from them.  
**9:13** Ephraim as I saw him—<br>a palm frond planted in a meadow.<br>But Ephraim brings out his sons to the slayer.  
**9:14** Give them, LORD,<br>what should you give?<br>Give them a miscarrying womb<br>and shriveled breasts.  
**9:15** All their evil at Gilgal,<br>for there did I hate them.<br>For the evil of their deeds<br>I will banish them from My house.<br>I will no longer love them.<br>All their nobles are knaves.  
**9:16** Ephraim is stricken,<br>their root is dry,<br>they cannot make fruit.<br>Even when they give birth,<br>I will put to death the precious ones of their womb.  
**9:17** My God shall reject them,<br>for they did not heed Him,<br>and they shall be wanderers among the nations.  

## 10장

**10:1** A blighted vine is Israel,<br>his fruit is just the same.<br>When his fruit was abundant,<br>he made abundant altars.<br>When it was good in his land,<br>they made goodly cult-pillars.  
**10:2** Their heart is divided—<br>now they bear guilt.<br>He shall break the back of their altars,<br>ravage their cultic pillars.  
**10:3** For now they say,<br>“We have no king,<br>for we have not feared the LORD,<br>and a king—what can he do to us?”  
**10:4** They have spoken words,<br>empty oaths,<br>sealed a pact,<br>and justice blooms like poison weeds<br>in the furrows of the field.  
**10:5** The calf of Beth-Aven they fear,<br>the dwellers of Samaria.<br>For his people mourns for it,<br>and his priests for it.<br>They wail over their glory,<br>for it has departed from them.  
**10:6** It, too, shall be brought to Assyria,<br>a tribute to King Jareb.<br>Ephraim shall be disgraced<br>and Israel shamed by its counsel.  
**10:7** Samaria shall be destroyed and her king,<br>like foam upon the water.  
**10:8** And the high places of Aven are ravaged,<br>the offense of Israel.<br>Thorn and thistle shall spring up<br>upon their altars.<br>And they shall say to the mountains, “Cover us,”<br>and to the hills, “Fall upon us.”

**10:15** Thus is it done to you, Bethel,<br>because of your utter evil.<br>At daybreak the king of Israel<br>will indeed be destroyed.

## 13장

**13:1** When Ephraim spoke in trembling,<br>he was a prince in Israel,<br>but he was guilty with Baal and died.  
**13:2** And now they continue to offend<br>and make themselves molten images<br>from their silver in their form as idols,<br>all of it craftsmen’s work.<br>To them they say:<br>sacrifices of man, calves to be kissed.  
**13:3** Therefore shall they be like a morning cloud<br>and like early dew that melts away,<br>like chaff whirled out from the threshing floor<br>and like smoke from a chimney.  
**13:4** Yet I am the LORD your God<br>ever since the land of Egypt,<br>and no God save Me shall you know<br>and no rescuer except for Me.  
**13:5** I knew you in the wilderness<br>in a parched land.  
**13:6** When they grazed and they were sated,<br>they were sated and grew proud.<br>Therefore they forgot Me.  
**13:7** And I will become to them like a lion,<br>like a leopard I spy on the way.  
**13:8** I will meet them like a bear robbed of her cubs<br>and rip the sinews round their heart.<br>And I will devour them there like a lion,<br>the beasts of the field shall tear them apart.  
**13:9** You are ruined, O Israel,<br>for who will come to your aid?  
**13:10** Where is your king, then?<br>Let him rescue you in all your towns.<br>And your leaders to whom you said,<br>“Give me a king and nobles.”  
**13:11** But I will give you a king in My wrath<br>and take him away in My anger.  
**13:12** Ephraim’s crime is bundled up,<br>hidden, his offense.  
**13:13** Birth pangs come upon him,<br>and the child is not wise.<br>For now he shall not last<br>on the birth-stool for children.  
**13:14** From Sheol shall I ransom them,<br>from death shall I redeem them?<br>Where are your words, O Death,<br>where your scourge, O Sheol?<br>Regret is hidden from My eyes.  
**13:15** Though he put forth fruit in meadows,<br>the east wind shall come, the LORD’s wind.<br>from the desert rising up.<br>And his fountain shall dry up<br>and his spring shall arid be.<br>It shall ravage treasure,<br>every precious vessel.  
**13:16** Samaria is guilty,<br>for it rebelled against its God.<br>They shall fall by the sword,<br>their infants shall be smashed,<br>and their pregnant women split apart.  

## 14장

**14:1** Turn back, O Israel, to the LORD your God,<br>for you have stumbled in your crime.  
**14:2** Take words with you<br>and turn back to the LORD.<br>Say to Him, “All crime You shall forgive.<br>And take what is good,<br>and we shall offer our speech instead of bulls.  
**14:3** Assyria will not rescue us,<br>on horses we shall not ride.<br>And we shall say no more ‘our God’<br>to our handiwork,<br>as in You alone the orphan is shown pity.”  
**14:4** “I will heal their rebellion,<br>I will love them freely,<br>for My wrath has turned back from them.  
**14:5** I will be like dew to Israel.<br>He shall blossom like the lily<br>and strike root like Lebanon.  
**14:6** His branches shall go forth<br>and his glory be like the olive tree,<br>and his fragrance like Lebanon.  
**14:7** Those who dwell in his shade shall come back,<br>they shall give life to new grain,<br>and like the vine they shall blossom.<br>His fame is like Lebanon wine.  
**14:8** Ephraim—‘Why more should I deal with idols?<br>I have answered and I espy Him<br>I am like the lush cypress.<br>from me your fruit is found.’”  
**14:9** Who is wise and can grasp these things,<br>discerning, and can know them?<br>For straight are the ways of the LORD,<br>and the righteous shall walk on them,<br>but rebels shall stumble on them.
