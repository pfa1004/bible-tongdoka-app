# 31.오바디야 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision of Obadiah. Thus said the LORD concerning Edom:<br>A report we have heard from the LORD,<br>and an envoy among the nations was sent.<br>Rise and let us rise against her for battle.  
**1:2** Look, I have made you last among the nations,<br>you are utterly spurned.  
**1:3** Your heart’s arrogance deceived you<br>who dwell in the clefts of the rock,<br>in your abode on high,<br>who say in your heart,<br>“Who can bring me down to the ground?”  
**1:4** Should you go high as the eagle<br>and should you nest among the stars,<br>from there I would bring you down, said the LORD.  
**1:5** Should thieves come to you,<br>plunderers in the night,<br>how you would be destroyed!<br>They would take but what they needed.<br>Should grape harvesters come to you,<br>would they not leave gleanings?  
**1:6** How Esau has been stripped,<br>his hidden places laid bare!  
**1:7** To the border they have sent you off,<br>all the men allied with you.<br>They deceived you, prevailed against you,<br>all the men in league with you.<br>Who ate bread at your table<br>have laid a trap beneath you.<br>There is no discerning in him.  
**1:8** Why, on that day, said the LORD:<br>I shall destroy the sages from Edom<br>and discerning from Esau’s mountain.  
**1:9** And your survivors shall be terror-stricken, Teman,<br>that every man be cut off from Esau’s mountain.  
**1:10** For the slaughter, for the outrage against your brother Jacob,<br>shame shall cover you, and you shall be cut off forever.  
**1:11** On the day you stood aloof,<br>on the day strangers seized his wealth<br>and aliens entered his gates,<br>and for Jerusalem they cast lots,<br>and you were as one of them.  
**1:12** And do not gloat on your brother’s day,<br>on the day of his downfall.<br>And do not rejoice over the Judahites<br>on the day of their destruction.<br>And do not boast<br>on the day of distress.  
**1:13** Do not enter My people’s gate<br>on the day of their disaster.<br>Do not gloat on your part<br>over the evil that befalls him on the day of his disaster.  
**1:14** And do not stand at the crossroads<br>to cut off his fugitives<br>and do not hand over his survivors<br>on the day of distress.  
**1:15** For near is the day of the LORD<br>against all the nations.<br>As you have done, it shall be done to you.<br>Your requital shall come back on your head.  
**1:16** For as you drank on My holy mountain,<br>all the nations shall drink ever more.<br>They shall drink and babble<br>and be as though they never had been.  
**1:17** But on Mount Zion there shall be a remnant,<br>and it shall be sacrosanct,<br>and the house of Jacob shall dispossess their dispossessors.  
**1:18** And the house of Jacob shall be a fire<br>and the house of Joseph a flame,<br>and the house of Esau shall be straw,<br>and they shall ignite them and consume them.<br>And the house of Esau shall have no survivor,<br>for the LORD has spoken.  
**1:19** And they shall take hold of Esau’s mountain and the lowland of the Philistines and the field of Ephraim and the field of Samaria, and Benjamin—Gilead.  
**1:20** And this force of exiles of the Israelites that is among the Canaanites as far as Zarephath and the exiles of Jerusalem who are at Sepharad—they shall take hold of the Negeb towns.  
**1:21** And rescuers shall go up Mount Zion to exact judgment against Esau’s mountain, and the kingdom shall be the LORD’s.
