## 1장

**78:2** Let me open my mouth in a rhapsody,<br>let me voice the verses of old,  
**78:3** that we have heard and we have known,<br>and that our fathers recounted to us.  
**78:4** We shall not conceal from their sons,<br>to the last generation recounting<br>the praise of the LORD<br>and His might and His wonders that He did.  
**78:5** He established a precept in Jacob<br>and His teaching put forth in Israel,<br>this He charged to our fathers<br>to make them known to their sons,  
**78:6** so that the last generation might know,<br>sons yet to be born<br>might arise and recount to their sons,  
**78:7** and place their trust in God<br>and forget not the acts of God,<br>and observe His commands.  
**78:8** That they be not like their fathers,<br>a wayward, rebellious generation,<br>a generation that was not firm of heart,<br>and its spirit not faithful to God.  
**78:9** The Ephraimites, deft wielders of bows,<br>turned tail on the day of battle,  
**78:10** and did not keep God’s pact,<br>and His teaching refused to follow.  
**78:11** And they forgot His acts<br>and His wonders that He showed them.  
**78:12** Before their fathers He did wonders,<br>in the land of Egypt, in Zoan’s field.  
**78:13** He split open the sea and let them pass through,<br>He made water stand up like a heap.  
**78:14** And He led them with the cloud by day<br>and all night long with the light of fire.  
**78:15** He split apart rocks in the wilderness<br>and gave drink as from the great deep.  
**78:16** He brought forth streams from stone,<br>and poured down water like rivers.  
**78:17** And still they offended him more,<br>to rebel against the High One in parched land.  
**78:18** And they tried God in their heart<br>to ask food for their gullet.  
**78:19** And they spoke against God.<br>They said: “Can God set a table in the wilderness?  
**78:20** Look, He struck the rock and water flowed<br>and currents streamed.<br>Can He also give bread?<br>Will He ready flesh for His people?”  
**78:21** Then the LORD heard and was angered,<br>and a fire was lit against Jacob<br>and wrath, too, went up against Israel.  
**78:22** For they had no faith in God<br>and did not trust in His rescue.  
**78:23** And He charged the skies above,<br>and the doors of the heavens He opened,  
**78:24** and rained on them manna to eat<br>and the grain of the heavens He gave to them.  
**78:25** Princely bread a man did eat,<br>provisions He sent them to sate them.  
**78:26** He moved the east wind across the heavens<br>and drove the south wind with His might,  
**78:27** and rained flesh upon them like dust<br>and like sand of the seas wingèd fowl,  
**78:28** brought it down in the midst of His camp,<br>round about His dwelling place.  
**78:29** And they ate and were fully sated,<br>what they craved He brought to them.  
**78:30** They were not revolted by their craving,<br>their food was still in their mouths,  
**78:31** when God’s wrath went up against them,<br>and He killed their stoutest fellows.<br>Israel’s young men He brought to their knees.  
**78:32** Even so, they offended still<br>and had no faith in His wonders.  
**78:33** And they wasted their days in mere vapor<br>and their years in dismay.  
**78:34** When He killed them, they sought Him out,<br>and came back and looked for God.  
**78:35** And they recalled that God was their rock<br>and the Most High God their redeemer.  
**78:36** Yet they beguiled Him with their lips,<br>and with their tongue they lied to Him  
**78:37** and their heart was not firm with Him,<br>and they were not faithful to His pact.  
**78:38** Yet He is compassionate, He atones for crime and does not destroy,<br>and abundantly takes back His wrath<br>and does not arouse all His fury.  
**78:39** And He recalls that they are flesh,<br>a spirit that goes off and does not come back.  
**78:40** How much they rebelled against Him in the wilderness,<br>caused Him pain in the wasteland!  
**78:41** And again did they try God,<br>and Israel’s Holy One they provoked.  
**78:42** They did not recall His great hand,<br>the day He ransomed them from the foe,  
**78:43** when He set out His signs in Egypt<br>and His portents in Zoan’s field,  
**78:44** and He turned their rivers to blood,<br>their currents they could not drink.  
**78:45** He sent against them the horde to consume them<br>and the frogs to destroy them.  
**78:46** And He gave to the cicada their produce<br>and their labor to the locust.  
**78:47** He blighted their vines with the hail,<br>and their sycamores with the frost.  
**78:48** He gave over to the pestilence their beasts,<br>and their cattle to the murrain.  
**78:49** He sent against them His smoldering fury,<br>anger, indignation, and distress,<br>a cohort of evil messengers.  
**78:50** He blazed a path for His fury,<br>He did not keep them from death,<br>and to the pestilence He gave their life.  
**78:51** And He struck down each firstborn in Egypt,<br>first fruit of manhood in the tents of Ham.  
**78:52** And He led His people forward like sheep,<br>drove them like sheep in the wilderness.  
**78:53** And He guided them safely—they feared not,<br>and their enemies the sea covered.  
**78:54** And He brought them to His holy realm,<br>the mount His right hand had acquired.  
**78:55** And He drove out nations before them<br>and set them down in a plot of estate,<br>and made Israel’s tribes dwell in their tents.  
**78:56** Yet they tried God the Most High and rebelled,<br>and His precepts they did not keep.  
**78:57** They fell back and betrayed like their fathers,<br>whipped around like an untrusty bow.  
**78:58** They vexed Him with their high places,<br>incensed Him with their idols.  
**78:59** God heard and was angry,<br>wholly rejected Israel.  
**78:60** He abandoned the sanctuary of Shiloh,<br>the tent where He dwelled among men.  
**78:61** And He let His might become captive,<br>gave His splendor to the hand of the foe.  
**78:62** He gave over his people to the sword,<br>against His estate He was enraged.  
**78:63** His young men the fire consumed<br>and His virgins no wedding song knew.  
**78:64** His priests fell to the sword,<br>and His widows did not keen.  
**78:65** And the Master awoke as one sleeping,<br>like a warrior shaking off wine.  
**78:66** And He beat back His foes,<br>everlasting disgrace He gave them.  
**78:67** Yet He rejected the tent of Joseph,<br>and the tribe of Ephraim He did not choose.  
**78:68** And He chose the tribe of Judah,<br>Mount Zion that He loves.  
**78:69** And He built on the heights His sanctuary,<br>like the earth He had founded forever.  
**78:70** And He chose David His servant<br>and took him from the sheepfolds.  
**78:71** From the nursing ewes He brought him<br>to shepherd Jacob His people<br>and Israel His estate.  
**78:72** And with his heart’s innocence he shepherded them,<br>with skilled hands he guided them.
