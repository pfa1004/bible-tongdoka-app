# 35.하박국 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** The portent that Habakkuk the prophet saw in a vision.  
**1:2** How long, O LORD, shall I cry out,<br>yet You do not listen?<br>I scream “outrage” to You,<br>and You do not rescue!  
**1:3** Why do You show me mischief,<br>and You look upon wretchedness?<br>Plunder and outrage are before me,<br>quarrel and contention I bear.  
**1:4** Therefore teaching fails,<br>and justice never comes forth.<br>For the wicked surrounds the righteous.<br>Therefore perverted justice comes forth.  
**1:5** See among the nations and look,<br>and be altogether astonished.<br>For a deed is being done in your time,<br>you would not believe it were it told.  
**1:6** For I am about to raise up the Chaldeans,<br>the harsh and headlong nation<br>that goes to the wide reaches of the earth<br>to take hold of dwellings not theirs.  
**1:7** Fearsome he is and frightful,<br>from him his rule and his majesty come forth.  
**1:8** And his horses are swifter than leopards<br>and quicker than the wolves of the steppes.<br>And his horsemen spread out,<br>his horsemen come, they fly from afar,<br>like a vulture pouncing on prey.  
**1:9** All of them come for outrage,<br>devastation is before them,<br>and they gather captives like the sand.  
**1:10** And he is scornful of kings,<br>and rulers are a mockery for him.<br>He mocks every fortress,<br>piles up earth and captures it,  
**1:11** then passes on like the wind<br>and attributes his might to his god.  
**1:12** Are you not of old, O LORD,<br>my holy God? You shall not die!<br>LORD, You have arraigned him for justice<br>and Rock, You set him aside for censure.  
**1:13** Too pure of eyes to see evil,<br>and You cannot look on wretchedness,<br>Why do You look upon traitors and stay silent,<br>when the wicked destroys one more righteous than he?  
**1:14** And You make humankind like the fish of the sea,<br>like creeping things that have no ruler.  
**1:15** They are all brought up with a line,<br>swept up in a net,<br>and he gathers them in his trawl,<br>therefore he rejoices and exalts.  
**1:16** Therefore he sacrifices to his net<br>and burns incense to his trawl,<br>for through them his share is rich,<br>and his food is fat.  
**1:17** Thus does he ever unsheathe his sword<br>to slay nations with no pity.  

## 2장

**2:1** On my watch let me stand,<br>and let me take my station at the watchtower and look out<br>to see what He will speak to me<br>and what He will respond to my complaint.  
**2:2** And the LORD answered me and said:<br>Write the vision, make it clear on the tablets,<br>so that one may read it readily.  
**2:3** For there is yet a vision for the appointed time<br>and a witness for the end who is not false.<br>Though it tarries, wait for it,<br>for it shall surely come, it shall not delay.  
**2:4** Look, the spirit within him is callous, not upright,<br>but the righteous man lives through his faithfulness.  
**2:5** How much more so the arrogant treacherous one who presumes<br>and who does not prosper,<br>who gapes open his maw like Sheol<br>and like death he is never sated.<br>And he rounds up for himself all the nations,<br>gathers in to him all the peoples.  
**2:6** Will not all these an adage pronounce against him,<br>verses and maxims against him, and say:<br>Woe, who amasses what is not his—for how long?—<br>and weighs himself down with debt.  
**2:7** Will not your creditors suddenly rise<br>and those who will shake you will awake?  
**2:8** For as you despoiled many nations,<br>the remnant of nations shall despoil you<br>for the bloodshed of people and the outrage of lands,<br>the town and all who dwell in it.  
**2:9** Woe, who takes illicit gain,<br>through evil, to his house<br>to set his nest on high<br>to be saved from the clutch of harm.  
**2:10** You have counseled shame for your house<br>when you maimed many peoples<br>and gravely offended.  
**2:11** For a stone from the wall shall shriek<br>and a wooden beam answer it:  
**2:12** Woe, who builds a town with bloodshed<br>founds a city with wrongdoing.  
**2:13** Is it not from the LORD of Armies?<br>And peoples shall strive for the fire<br>and nations for naught shall be wearied.  
**2:14** For the earth shall be filled<br>with knowing the LORD’s glory<br>as water covers the sea.  
**2:15** Woe, who gives drink to his friend,<br>adding venom and making him drunk<br>so as to look on his nakedness.  
**2:16** You shall be sated with shame instead of glory.<br>You, too, drink and expose yourself.<br>The cup of the LORD’s right hand shall come round to you,<br>and noxious shame instead of your glory.  
**2:17** For the outrage of Lebanon shall cover you,<br>and the plunder of beasts shall dismay you<br>for the bloodshed of people and the outrage of lands,<br>the town and all who dwell in it.  
**2:18** What will the idol avail when its fashioner carves it,<br>the molten image that gives false oracles,<br>Though its fashioner puts his trust in it,<br>making speechless ungods.  
**2:19** Woe, who says to wood, “Awake,”<br>“Bestir” to lifeless stone.<br>“It will give oracles.” Look, it is inlaid with gold and silver<br>and no spirit is there within it.  
**2:20** But the LORD is in His holy palace.<br>Hush before Him all the earth!  

## 3장

**3:1** A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, on the <i>shigyonot</i>.  
**3:2** LORD, I have heard the report of You,<br>am in awe, O LORD, of your acts.<br>In these very years revive them,<br>in these very years make them known.<br>In anger, remember to show mercy.  
**3:3** God shall come from Teman<br>and the Holy One from Mount Paran.​ selah.<br>His majesty covers the heavens,<br>and His splendor fills the earth.  
**3:4** And the radiance is like light.<br>Beams from His hand He has,<br>and there His might is hidden.  
**3:5** Before Him pestilence goes,<br>and plague comes forth at His feet.  
**3:6** He halts, and He makes the earth rock,<br>looks, and makes nations leap,<br>and the age-old mountains crumble,<br>the ancient hills collapse.<br>The ancient marches are His.  
**3:7** The tents of Cushan are shattered,<br>shaken the tent curtains of Midian’s land.  
**3:8** With Neharim is the LORD incensed,<br>against Neharim Your wrath, against Yamm Your fury,<br>when You ride Your horses,<br>Your chariots of victory.  
**3:9** Laid bare is Your bow,<br>and the seven Rods of Eimar. ​​selah.<br>You split the earth with rivers.  
**3:10** The mountains see you, shudder.<br>A stream of water surges.<br>The deep sends forth its voice.<br>The sky swears solemnly.  
**3:11** Sun and moon stand still at the zenith<br>by the light of Your arrows they go,<br>by the radiance of the gleam of Your spear.  
**3:12** In wrath You stride across earth,<br>in fury You trample nations.  
**3:13** You sally forth to Your people’s rescue,<br>to the rescue of Your anointed.<br>You smash the top of the wicked’s house,<br>You raze the foundation down to bedrock.​ selah.  
**3:14** You pierce the head with a rod.<br>His troops storm in their glee to scatter me<br>as one devours the poor in ambush.  
**3:15** You made Your horses tread in the sea—<br>the great waters were roiled.  
**3:16** I heard and I quaked within,<br>at the sound my lips quivered.<br>Rot comes into my bones<br>and I quake where I stand.<br>Will You rest on the day of distress<br>when a people comes up to assault us?  
**3:17** For the fig tree does not bud,<br>and there is no yield from the vines.<br>The olive tree’s crop is shriveled<br>and the fields do not grow grain.<br>The sheep are gone from the fold,<br>and no cattle are in the barns.  
**3:18** But I in the LORD will exult,<br>will rejoice in the God of my rescue.  
**3:19** The LORD Master is my strength,<br>He makes my feet like the gazelle’s<br>and has me tread upon the heights.<br>For the lead player, on stringed instruments.
