# 30.아모스 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** The words of Amos, who was among the sheep-breeders of Tekoa, who saw visions concerning Israel in the days of Uzziah king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.  
**1:2** And he said:<br>The LORD from Zion roars<br>and from Jerusalem puts forth His voice,<br>and the shepherds’ meadows wither,<br>and the peak of Carmel dries up.  
**1:3** Thus said the LORD:<br>For three trespasses of Damascus<br>and for four I will not turn it back—<br>for their threshing Gilead<br>with iron threshing boards.  
**1:4** And I will set fire to the house of Hazael,<br>and it shall consume the citadels of Ben-Hadad,  
**1:5** and I will break the bolt of Damascus<br>and cut off dwellers from Aven Vale<br>and him who holds the scepter from Beth-Eden.<br>And the peoples of Aram shall be exiled to Kir, said the LORD.  
**1:6** Thus said the LORD:<br>For three trespasses of Gaza<br>and for four I will not turn it back—<br>for their inflicting a total exile<br>to hand over to Edom.  
**1:7** And I will set fire to Gaza’s wall,<br>and it shall consume her citadels.  
**1:8** And I will cut off dwellers from Ashdod<br>and him who holds the scepter from Ashkelon,<br>and I will bring My hand against Ekron,<br>and the Philistines’ remnant shall perish.  
**1:9** Thus said the LORD:<br>For three trespasses of Tyre<br>and for four I will not turn it back—<br>for their inflicting a total exile,<br>handing over to Edom,<br>and they did not recall the pact of brothers.  
**1:10** And I will set fire to Tyre’s wall<br>and it shall consume her citadels.  
**1:11** Thus said the LORD:<br>For three trespasses of Edom,<br>and for four I will not turn it back—<br>for his pursuing his brothers with the sword<br>and stifling his compassion,<br>and he bore a grudge ceaselessly<br>and kept up his wrath unending.  
**1:12** And I will set a fire in Teman,<br>and it shall consume the citadels of Bosra.  
**1:13** Thus said the LORD:<br>For three trespasses of the Ammonites<br>and for four I will not turn it back—<br>for their splitting open the pregnant women of Gilead<br>so as to expand their borders.  
**1:14** And I will light a fire on the wall of Rabbah,<br>and it shall consume her citadels<br>with trumpet blast on the day of battle<br>and with a storm on the tempest day.  
**1:15** And their king shall go into exile,<br>he and his nobles together<br>—said the LORD.  

## 2장

**2:1** Thus said the LORD:<br>For three trespasses of Moab,<br>and for four, I will not turn it back—<br>for his burning the bones of Edom’s king into lime.  
**2:2** And I will set fire to Moab,<br>and it shall consume the citadels of Kerioth,<br>and Moab shall die in an uproar,<br>in shouts and the sound of the ram’s horn.  
**2:3** And I will cut off the leader from her midst<br>and all her nobles I will slay with him<br>—said the LORD.  
**2:4** For three trespasses of Judah,<br>and for four, I will not turn it back—<br>for their spurning the LORD’s teaching,<br>and His statutes they did not keep,<br>and their false things led them astray,<br>after which their fathers had gone.  
**2:5** And I will set a fire in Judah,<br>and it shall consume Jerusalem’s citadels.  
**2:6** Thus said the LORD:<br>For three trespasses of Israel,<br>and for four, I will not turn it back—<br>for their selling the just man for silver<br>and the needy for sandals.  
**2:7** Who trample the head of the needy<br>in the dust of the ground<br>and pervert the way of the poor.<br>And a man and his father go to the same girl<br>to profane My holy name.  
**2:8** And on pawned garments they stretch out,<br>alongside every altar,<br>and wine bought with funds from fines<br>they drink in the house of their God.  
**2:9** Yet I had destroyed the Amorite before them,<br>who is tall as the height of cedars<br>and sturdy as the oaks.<br>And I destroyed his fruit above<br>and his roots below.  
**2:10** And I brought you up from the land of Egypt<br>and led you in the wilderness forty years.<br>to take hold of the Amorite’s land.  
**2:11** And I raised up from your sons to be prophets<br>and from your men to be nazirites.<br>Is it not so, O Israelites? said the LORD.  
**2:12** But you gave the nazirites wine to drink<br>and to the prophets you charged, “Prophesy not!”  
**2:13** I am about to halt you where you are<br>as the wagon overflowing with grain halts.  
**2:14** And flight shall elude the swift,<br>and the strong shall not summon his power,<br>and the warrior shall not escape with his life.  
**2:15** And the bowman shall not stand,<br>and the fleet-footed shall not escape,<br>and the horseman shall not escape with his life.  
**2:16** And whose heart is staunch among the warriors<br>naked shall flee on that day—said the LORD.  

## 3장

**3:1** Hear this word that the LORD has spoken concerning you, O Israelites, concerning the entire clan that I brought up from Egypt, saying:  
**3:2** Only with you was I intimate<br>of all the clans of the earth.<br>Therefore will I make a reckoning with you<br>for all your crimes.  
**3:3** Do two walk together<br>if they have not first agreed?  
**3:4** Does the lion roar in the forest<br>unless it has taken prey?<br>Does the maned beast put forth its voice from its lair<br>if it has not made a catch?  
**3:5** Does a bird fall into a trap on the ground<br>when there is no snare for it?<br>Does a trap spring up from the soil<br>and fail to make a catch?  
**3:6** Is the ram’s horn sounded in the town<br>and the people do not tremble?<br>Is there harm within the town<br>and the LORD has not done it?  
**3:7** For the Master, the LORD, does nothing without revealing His secret to His servants, the prophets.  
**3:8** A lion roars.<br>Who does not fear?<br>The Master, the LORD, speaks.<br>Who cannot prophesy?  
**3:9** Let it be heard in the citadels of Ashdod and in the citadels of the land of Egypt, and say: Gather on the mountains of Samaria and see the many upheavals within it and the oppressed in its midst.  
**3:10** And they do not know how to do what is right, said the LORD, who store up outrage and plunder in their citadels.  
**3:11** Therefore, thus said the Master, the LORD:<br>A foe goes round the land.<br>He shall take down from you your strength,<br>and your citadels shall be despoiled.  
**3:12** Thus said the LORD:<br>As the shepherd may save<br>from the lion’s mouth<br>two shank bones or an earlobe,<br>so shall Israel be saved.<br>You who sit in Samaria<br>by the head of a bed or at the end of a couch,  
**3:13** listen and bear witness against the house of Jacob,<br>said the Master, the LORD God of Israel.  
**3:14** For on the day I make a reckoning<br>for Israel’s trespasses<br>I will make a reckoning with the altars of Bethel<br>and the horns of the altar shall be hacked off,<br>and they shall fall to the ground.  
**3:15** And I will strike down the winter house<br>together with the summer house,<br>and the houses of ivory shall be destroyed<br>and the great houses swept away<br>—said the LORD.  

## 4장

**4:1** Listen to this word,<br>you cows of Bashan who are on Mount Samaria,<br>who exploit the poor,<br>who crush the needy,<br>who say to their husbands,<br>“Bring, that we may drink.”  
**4:2** The Master, the LORD, has sworn by His holiness<br>that days are coming upon you<br>when you shall be borne off in baskets<br>and the last of you with fishhooks,  
**4:3** and through the breaches each woman shall go out straight ahead,<br>and you shall be flung on the refuse heap—said the LORD.  
**4:4** Come to Bethel and trespass,<br>to Gilgal and continue to trespass,<br>and bring the next morning your sacrifices<br>and on the third day your tithes.  
**4:5** And burn a thanksgiving offering of leavened bread<br>and proclaim freewill offerings, make them heard.<br>For so have you loved,<br>O Israelites—said the Master, the LORD.  
**4:6** And I on My part have given you<br>cleanness of teeth in all your towns<br>and want of bread in all your places,<br>but you did not come back to Me,<br>said the LORD.  
**4:7** And I on My part withheld from you the rain<br>with still three months till the harvest,<br>and I rained on one town<br>but on another town did not rain.<br>One field would have rain,<br>but the field without rain would dry up.  
**4:8** And two or three towns would wander<br>to a single town to drink water,<br>and would not slake their thirst.<br>But you did not turn back to Me, said the LORD.  
**4:9** I struck you with blight and with mildew.<br>Ruins were your gardens and your vineyards,<br>and your fig trees and your olive trees the locusts devoured.<br>But you did not turn back to Me, said the LORD.  
**4:10** I let loose the pestilence against you as in Egypt.<br>I slew your young men by the sword with your captured horses<br>and made your camps’ stench rise in your very nostrils.<br>But you did not turn back to Me, said the LORD.  
**4:11** I overthrew you<br>as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah,<br>and you became like a brand saved from burning.<br>But you did not turn back to Me, said the LORD.  
**4:12** Therefore, this will I do to you, Israel,<br>because this will I do to you,<br>prepare to meet your God, O Israel.  
**4:13** For, look, He fashions the mountains and creates the wind<br>and tells man what is his thought,<br>turns dawn into darkness<br>and treads on the earth’s high places.<br>The LORD God of Armies is His name.  

## 5장

**5:1** Listen to this word that I bear about you as a dirge, O house of Israel.  
**5:2** She has fallen, no more shall rise,<br>the Virgin of Israel.<br>She is abandoned on her soil,<br>none lifts her up.  
**5:3** For thus said the Master the LORD:<br>The town that goes out a thousand<br>shall be left a hundred<br>and that goes out a hundred<br>shall be left ten—for the house of Israel.  
**5:4** For thus said the LORD to the house of Israel:<br>Seek Me and live  
**5:5** and seek not Bethel<br>and to Gilgal do not come<br>and to Beersheba do not pass on.<br>For Gilgal shall go in exile<br>and Bethel shall turn into evil.  
**5:6** Seek the LORD and live,<br>lest the house of Joseph flare like fire<br>and consume Bethel with none to quench it.  
**5:7** You who turn justice into wormwood<br>and righteousness bring to the ground—  
**5:8** He makes the Pleiades and Orion<br>and turns death’s darkness into morning<br>and day He darkens to night,<br>calls to the waters of the sea<br>and pours them over the land.<br>The LORD is His name.  
**5:9** He flashes destruction on the strong,<br>and destruction comes down on the fortress.  
**5:10** They hated the reprover in the gate,<br>and the truth speaker they despised.  
**5:11** Therefore, as you trampled upon the poor man,<br>and a payment of grain you exacted from him,<br>hewn-stone houses you have built,<br>but you shall not dwell in them.<br>Lovely vineyards you have planted,<br>but you shall not drink their wine.  
**5:12** For I know that your trespasses are many<br>and numerous your offenses—<br>foes of the righteous, takers of bribes,<br>you pervert the needy’s case in the gate.  
**5:13** Therefore the prudent on that day shall fall silent,<br>for it is an evil time.  
**5:14** Seek good and not evil,<br>that you may live.<br>And so may the LORD God of Armies be with you,<br>as you have said.  
**5:15** Hate evil and love good<br>and set out justice in the gate.<br>Perhaps the LORD God of Israel<br>may grant grace to Joseph’s remnant.  
**5:16** Therefore thus said the LORD,<br>the God of Israel, the Master:<br>In all the squares—lament,<br>and in all the streets they say “alas.”<br>And they shall call the farmer to mourning,<br>and to lament, those expert in weeping.  
**5:17** And in all the vineyards there is wailing<br>as I pass in your midst, said the LORD.  
**5:18** Woe, who long for the day of the LORD!<br>Why should you need the day of the LORD?<br>It is darkness and not light.  
**5:19** As a man flees from a lion<br>and a bear blocks his way,<br>and he enters the house<br>and leans his hand on the wall,<br>and a snake bites him.  
**5:20** Why, the day of the LORD is darkness and not light,<br>pitch black, and no radiance in it.  
**5:21** I hate, I spurn your festivals<br>and smell no fragrance in your convocations.  
**5:22** Should you offer up to Me burnt offerings<br>or grain offerings, I will not accept them;<br>nor will I look on the well-being sacrifice of your fatted calves.  
**5:23** Take away from me the noise of your singers,<br>nor will I listen to the melody of your lutes.  
**5:24** But let justice well up like water<br>and righteousness like a steady stream.  
**5:25** Did you bring Me sacrifices and grain offering<br>in the wilderness forty years, house of Israel?  
**5:26** And you shall bear away Sikkuth your king and Kiyyun,<br>your icons, your star gods that you made for yourselves,  
**5:27** and I will exile you beyond Damascus,<br>said the LORD, God of Israel in His name.  

## 6장

**6:1** Woe, you complacent in Zion<br>and you trusting ones on Mount Samaria,<br>eminent among the first of nations,<br>and the house of Israel came to them.  
**6:2** Pass on to Calneh and see,<br>and go from there to great Hammath,<br>and go down to the Philistines’ Gath.<br>Are you better than these kingdoms,<br>is your territory larger than theirs?  
**6:3** You who dismiss the evil day<br>but bring on disaster and outrage;  
**6:4** who lie on ivory-inlaid beds<br>and lounge on their couches<br>and eat the lambs from the flock<br>and calves from the stall,  
**6:5** who pluck on the lute,<br>like David they devise song’s instruments.  
**6:6** Who drink from bowls of wine<br>and with the finest oils anoint themselves<br>and are not distressed by Joseph’s disaster.  
**6:7** Therefore now shall they be exiled at the head of exiles,<br>and the lounging feasts shall be no more.  
**6:8** The Master, the LORD, has sworn by His life,<br>said the LORD, God of Armies:<br>I loathe the pride of Jacob,<br>and his citadels I hate,<br>and I will hand over the town and its fullness.  
**6:9** And it shall happen that if ten men are left in a house, they shall die.  
**6:10** And a handful of men shall remain to carry out the bones from the house. And one shall say to him who is in the far corners of the house, “Is anyone still with you?” And he shall say, “None.” And he shall say, “Hush!” so as not to mention the name of the LORD.  
**6:11** For, look, the LORD commands,<br>and He shall strike the great house into splinters<br>and the small house into shards.  
**6:12** Can horses race on rock,<br>can one plow with an ox in the sea?<br>For you have turned justice into poison<br>and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood.  
**6:13** You who rejoice over Lo-Dabar,<br>who say, Why, with our strength<br>we have captured Karnaim.  
**6:14** But I am about to raise against you, house of Israel,<br>said the LORD, God of Armies, a nation.<br>And they shall harry you<br>from Lebo-Hammath<br>to the Wadi of Arabah.  

## 7장

**7:1** Thus did the Master, the LORD show me: And, look, He was creating locusts at the beginning of the sprouting of the late grain, and, look, it was the late grain after the king’s reaping.  
**7:2** And so, after they had finished devouring the green growth of the land, I said, “O Master, LORD, pray forgive! How will Jacob stand, for he is small?”  
**7:3** The LORD repented concerning this. “It shall not be,” said the LORD.  
**7:4** Thus did the LORD show me: And look, the Master, the LORD was calling forth to contend with fire, and it consumed the great deep and consumed the fields.  
**7:5** And I said, “O Master, LORD, pray cease. How will Jacob stand, for he is small?”  
**7:6** The LORD relented concerning this. “This, too, shall not be,” said the Master, the LORD.  
**7:7** Thus did the LORD show me: And, look, the Master was stationed by a wall built with a plumb line, and in His hand was a plumb line.  
**7:8** And the LORD said to me; “What do you see, Amos?” And I said, “A plumb line.” And the Master said to me, “I am about to place a plumb line in the midst of My people Israel. I will no longer forgive them.”  
**7:9** And Isaac’s high places shall be desolate,<br>and Israel’s sanctuaries shall become ruins,<br>and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with a sword.  
**7:10** And Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, “Amos has plotted against you in the midst of the house of Israel. The land cannot bear all his words.”  
**7:11** For thus has Amos said:<br>By the sword Jeroboam shall die,<br>and Israel shall surely be exiled from its soil.”  
**7:12** And Amaziah said to Amos, “Seer, go, flee to the land of Judah and there eat bread and prophesy there.  
**7:13** But in Bethel you shall no longer prophesy, for it is the king’s sanctuary and a royal house.”  
**7:14** And Amos answered and said to Amaziah, “No prophet am I, nor the son of a prophet am I, but a cattle herder am I and a tender of sycamore fruit.  
**7:15** And the LORD took me from going after the flock, and the LORD said to me ‘Go, prophesy to My people Israel.’  
**7:16** And, now, hear the word of the LORD, you who say, ‘You shall not prophesy to Israel and shall not preach to the house of Isaac.’  
**7:17** Therefore thus said the LORD:<br>Your wife shall play the whore in the town,<br>and your sons and your daughters shall fall by the sword,<br>and you shall be shared out with a measuring line,<br>and you shall die on unclean soil,<br>and Israel shall surely be exiled from its soil.”  

## 8장

**8:1** Thus did the Master, the LORD, show me: And, look, a basket of summer’s-end fruit.  
**8:2** And He said to me, “What do you see, Amos?” And I said, “A basket of summer’s-end fruit.” And the LORD said to me:<br>The end has come upon My people Israel.<br>I will no longer forgive them.  
**8:3** And the palace’s songstresses shall howl<br>on that day, said the LORD:<br>“Many the corpses flung everywhere. Hush!”  
**8:4** Hear this, who trample the needy,<br>destroying the poor of the land,  
**8:5** saying, “When will the new moon be over, that we may sell grain,<br>and the sabbath, that we may trade in wheat?<br>to use a short ephah measure and an oversize shekel-weight<br>and to tilt cheating scales,  
**8:6** to buy the indigent with silver<br>and the needy for the price of sandals,<br>and we may sell chaff as grain.”  
**8:7** The LORD has sworn by the Pride of Jacob:<br>I will never forget their acts.  
**8:8** For this should not the earth shudder<br>and all its dwellers mourn?<br>It shall rise, altogether, like the Nile,<br>heave and sink like the Nile of Egypt.  
**8:9** And it shall happen on that day, said the LORD,<br>I will make the sun set at noon<br>and darken the earth on a day of light.<br>And I will turn your festivals into mourning<br>and all your songs into lament,<br>And lay sackcloth on all loins<br>and every head a shaved pate.<br>And I will make her as the mourning for an only child<br>and her end as a bitter day.  
**8:10** Look, days are coming, said the LORD, when I will let loose famine in the land,<br>not famine for bread<br>and not thirst for water<br>but for hearing the words of the LORD.  
**8:11** And they shall wander from sea to sea,<br>and from the north to the east they shall roam<br>to seek the LORD’s word,<br>but they shall not find it.  
**8:12** On that day the lovely virgins shall faint<br>and the young men, too, with thirst.  
**8:13** Who swear by the Guilt of Samaria<br>and say, “As your God lives, Dan,”<br>and “As the way to Beersheba lives.”<br>They shall fall and rise no more.  

## 9장

**9:1** I saw the Master stationed by the altar, and He said:<br>Strike the capitals that the thresholds shake.<br>I will split them on the heads of them all,<br>and who is left of them I will slay with the sword.<br>None from them shall be able to flee,<br>and no survivor from them shall escape.  
**9:2** Were they to dig down to Sheol,<br>from there My hand would take them,<br>and were they to ascend to the heavens,<br>from there I would bring them down.  
**9:3** And were they to hide on the peak of Carmel,<br>I would search them out there and take them.<br>And were they to take cover from My eyes on the floor of the sea,<br>from there I would summon the Serpent and it would bite them.  
**9:4** And should they go in captivity before their enemies,<br>from there I will summon the sword and it would slay them.<br>And I will put My eye on them<br>for evil and not for good.  
**9:5** And the Master, LORD of Armies,<br>Who but touches the earth and it melts<br>and all dwellers upon it mourn,<br>and it all goes up like the Nile,<br>and sinks like the Nile of Egypt.  
**9:6** Who builds in the heavens His lofts<br>and His vault upon earth He founds.<br>Who calls forth the waters of the sea<br>and pours them over the earth—the LORD is His name.  
**9:7** Are you not like the Cushites to Me,<br>O Israelites? said the LORD.<br>Did I not bring up Israel from the land of Egypt<br>and the Philistines from Crete<br>and Aram from Kir?  
**9:8** Look, the eyes of the Master, the LORD,<br>are upon the offending kingdom,<br>and I will destroy it<br>from the face of the earth.<br>But I will surely not destroy the house of Jacob, said the LORD.  
**9:9** For I am about to command,<br>and I will shake up the house of Israel in all the nations<br>as one shakes in a sieve<br>and no pebble shall fall to the ground.  
**9:10** By the sword shall die<br>all the offenders among My people<br>who say “The evil shall not<br>approach and come close to us.”  
**9:11** On that day I will raise up<br>the fallen shelter of David<br>and I will stop up its breaches<br>and its ruins will I raise<br>and rebuild it as in days of yore,  
**9:12** so that they take hold of the remnant of Edom<br>and all the nations on which My name has been called,<br>said the LORD, Who does this.  
**9:13** Look, days are coming, said the LORD,<br>when the plowman shall overtake the reaper<br>and the treader of grapes the sower of seed.<br>And the mountains shall drip fermented juice,<br>and all the hills shall melt.  
**9:14** And I will restore the fortunes of My people Israel,<br>and they shall rebuild desolate towns and dwell there<br>and plant vineyards and drink their wine.<br>And they shall make gardens<br>and eat their fruit.  
**9:15** And I will plant them on their soil,<br>and they shall no more be uprooted from their soil<br>that I have given them, said the LORD your God.
