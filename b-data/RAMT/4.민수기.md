# 4.민수기 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses in the Wilderness of Sinai in the Tent of Meeting on the first of the second month in the second year of their going out from Egypt, saying,  
**1:2** “Count the heads of all the community of the Israelites by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of names, every male by their heads.  
**1:3** From twenty years old and up, everyone who goes out in the army in Israel, you shall reckon them by their battalions, you and Aaron.  
**1:4** And with you let there be a man from each tribe, each man the head of his father’s house.  
**1:5** And these are the names of the men who will stand with you: for Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur;  
**1:6** for Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;  
**1:7** for Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab;  
**1:8** for Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar;  
**1:9** for Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon;  
**1:10** for the sons of Joseph—for Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud, for Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur;  
**1:11** for Benjamim, Abidan son of Gideoni;  
**1:12** for Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;  
**1:13** for Asher, Pagiel son of Ochran;  
**1:14** for Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel;  
**1:15** for Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan.  
**1:16** These are the ones called from the community, chieftains of their fathers’ tribes, they are the heads of the kin-groups of Israel.”  
**1:17** And Moses, and Aaron with him, took these men who had been marked out by name.  
**1:18** And all the community they assembled on the first of the second month, and they were affiliated by their clans, by their fathers’ houses according to the number of names, from twenty years old and up, by their heads,  
**1:19** as the LORD had charged Moses, and he made a reckoning of them in the Wilderness of Sinai.  
**1:20** And the sons of Reuben, Israel’s firstborn, those born to them by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of names, by their heads, every male from twenty years old and up, everyone who went out in the army,  
**1:21** their reckoning for the tribe of Reuben, forty-six thousand five hundred.  
**1:22** For the sons of Simeon, those born to them by their clans, by their fathers’ houses according to the number of names, by their heads, every male from twenty years old and up, everyone who went out in the army,  
**1:23** their reckoning for the tribe of Simeon, fifty-nine thousand three hundred.  
**1:24** For the sons of Gad, those born to them by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and up, everyone who went out in the army,  
**1:25** their reckoning for the tribe of Gad, forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.  
**1:26** For the sons of Judah, those born to them by their clans, by their fathers’ houses according to the number of names, from twenty years old and up, everyone who went out in the army,  
**1:27** those reckoned for the tribe of Judah, seventy-four thousand six hundred.  
**1:28** For the sons of Issachar, those born to them by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years and up, everyone who went out in the army,  
**1:29** their reckoning for the tribe of Issachar, fifty-four thousand four hundred.  
**1:30** For the sons of Zebulun those born to them, by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and up, everyone who went out in the army,  
**1:31** their reckoning for the tribe of Zebulun, fifty-seven thousand four hundred.  
**1:32** For the sons of Joseph—for the sons of Ephraim, those born to them by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and up, everyone who went out in the army,  
**1:33** their reckoning for the tribe of Ephraim, forty thousand five hundred.  
**1:34** For the sons of Manasseh, those born to them by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and up, everyone who went out in the army,  
**1:35** their reckoning, for the tribe of Manasseh, thirty-two thousand two hundred.  
**1:36** For the sons of Benjamin, those born to them, by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and up, everyone who went out in the army,  
**1:37** their reckoning, for the tribe of Benjamin thirty-five thousand four hundred.  
**1:38** For the sons of Dan, those born to them by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and up, everyone who went out in the army,  
**1:39** their reckoning for the tribe of Dan sixty-two thousand seven hundred.  
**1:40** For the sons of Asher, those born to them, by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and up, everyone who went out in the army,  
**1:41** their reckoning for the tribe of Asher forty-one thousand five hundred.  
**1:42** The sons of Naphtali, those born to them by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old up, everyone who went out in the army,  
**1:43** their reckoning for the tribe of Naphtali, fifty-three thousand four hundred.  
**1:44** These are the reckonings that Moses, and Aaron and the chieftains of Israel, made, twelve men, one man for each father’s house they were.  
**1:45** And all the Israelites who were reckoned by their fathers’ houses, from twenty years old and up, everyone who went out in the army in Israel,  
**1:46** all the reckonings came to six hundred three thousand five hundred and fifty.  
**1:47** And the Levites by the tribe of their fathers were not reckoned in their midst.  
**1:48** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**1:49** “But the tribe of Levi you shall not reckon, and their heads you shall not count in the midst of the Israelites.  
**1:50** And you, make the Levites reckon with the Tabernacle of the Covenant and with all its furnishings and with all that belongs to it. They it is who shall bear the Tabernacle and all its furnishings, and they shall serve it, and around the Tabernacle they shall camp.  
**1:51** And when the Tabernacle journeys onward, the Levites shall take it down, and when the Tabernacle camps, the Levites shall set it up, and the stranger who draws near shall be put to death.  
**1:52** And the Israelites shall camp each man with his camp and each man with his banner by their battalions.  
**1:53** And the Levites shall camp around the Tabernacle of the Covenant, that there be no fury against the community of Israelites, and the Levites shall keep watch over the Tabernacle of the Covenant.  
**1:54** And the Israelites did as all that the LORD had charged Moses, thus did they do.

**2:4** And his division and those reckoned with them, seventy-four thousand six hundred.  
**2:5** And those camped by him, the tribe of Issachar, and the chieftain of the Issacharites, Nethanel son of Zuar.  
**2:6** And his division and those reckoned with it, fifty-four thousand four hundred.  
**2:7** The tribe of Zebulun, and the chieftain of the Zebulunites Eliab son of Helon.

**2:9** All the reckoning for the camp of Judah one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred by their divisions. They shall journey first.

**2:11** And his division and those reckoned with it, forty-six thousand five hundred.

**2:19** And his division and their reckoning, forty thousand five hundred.

**2:27** And those who camped by him, the tribe of Asher, and the chieftain of the Asherites Pagiel son of Ochran.

**2:31** All the reckoning for the camp of Dan one hundred fifty-seven thousand six hundred. They shall journey last by their banners.  
**2:32** These are the reckonings of the Israelites by their fathers’ houses, all the reckonings of the camps by their divisions, six hundred three thousand five hundred and fifty.  
**2:33** But the Levites were not reckoned in the midst of the Israelites, as the LORD had charged Moses.  
**2:34** And the Israelites did as all that the LORD had charged Moses, thus they camped by their banners and thus they journeyed, each man by his clans, together with his father’s house.  

## 3장

**3:1** And these are the generations of Aaron and Moses on the day the LORD spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai.  
**3:2** And these are the names of the sons of Aaron: the firstborn Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.  
**3:3** These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests whom he installed to serve as priests.  
**3:4** And Nadab and Abihu died before the LORD when they brought forward unfit fire before the LORD in the Wilderness of Sinai, and sons they did not have, and Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests in the lifetime of Aaron their father.  
**3:5** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**3:6** “Bring forward the tribe of Levi, and set them before Aaron the priest, that they may serve him.  
**3:7** And they shall keep his watch and the watch of all the community before the Tent of Meeting to do the work of the Tabernacle.  
**3:8** And they shall keep watch over all the furnishings of the Tent of Meeting and the watch of the Israelites to do the work of the Tabernacle.  
**3:9** And you shall give the Levites to Aaron and to his sons, wholly given shall they be from the Israelites.  
**3:10** And Aaron and his sons you shall single out, that they keep their priesthood, and the stranger who draws near shall be put to death.”  
**3:11** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**3:12** “And as for Me, look, I have taken the Levites from the midst of the Israelites in place of every firstborn womb-breach of the Israelites, that the Levites be Mine.  
**3:13** For Mine is every firstborn. On the day I struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt I consecrated to Me every firstborn in Israel from man to beast—Mine they shall be. I am the LORD.”  
**3:14** And the LORD spoke to Moses in the Wilderness of Sinai, saying,  
**3:15** “Reckon the Levites by their fathers’ houses, by their clans, every male from a month old and up you shall reckon them.”  
**3:16** And Moses reckoned them according to the word of the LORD as he had been charged.  
**3:17** And these were the Levites by their names: Gershon and Kohath and Merari.  
**3:18** And these are the names of the sons of Gershon by their clans: Libni and Shimei.  
**3:19** And the sons of Kohath by their clans: Amram and Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.  
**3:20** And the sons of Merari by their clans: Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of Levi by their fathers’ houses.  
**3:21** To Gershon, the Libnite clan and the Shimeite clan, these are the Gershonite clans.  
**3:22** Their reckoning, in the number of every male from a month old and up, their reckoning is seven thousand five hundred.  
**3:23** The Gershonite clans shall camp behind the Tabernacle to the west.  
**3:24** And the chieftain of the father’s house for the Gershonite, Eliasaph son of Lael.  
**3:25** And the watch of the Gershonites in the Tent of Meeting, the Tabernacle and the Tent, its cover and the screen of the entrance to the Tent of Meeting,  
**3:26** and the hangings of the court and the screen of the entrance to the court which is around the Tabernacle and the altar and its cords for all its service.  
**3:27** And to Kohath, the Amramite clan and the Izharite clan and the Hebronite clan and the Uzzielite clan, these are the Kohathite clans.  
**3:28** In the number of every male from one month old and up, eight thousand six hundred, keepers of the watch of the sanctuary.  
**3:29** The clans of the Kohathites shall camp on the side of the Tabernacle to the south.  
**3:30** And the chieftain of the father’s house for the clans of the Kohathite, Elizaphan son of Uzziel.  
**3:31** And their watch is the ark and the table and the lamp stand and the altars and the sacred vessels with which they shall serve and the screen and all its work.  
**3:32** And the chieftain of the chieftains of the Levites is Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, the charge of those who keep the watch of the sanctuary.  
**3:33** To the Merarite, the Mahlite clan and the Mushite clan, these are the Merarite clans.  
**3:34** And their reckoning, in the number of every male from one month old and up, six thousand two hundred.  
**3:35** And the chieftain of the father’s house for the clans of the Merarite, Zuriel son of Abihail. On the side of the Tabernacle they shall camp to the north.  
**3:36** And the charge of the Merarites, the boards of the Tabernacle and its bars and its posts and its sockets and all its furnishings and all its work,  
**3:37** and the posts of the court all around and their sockets and their pegs and their cords.  
**3:38** And those who camped before the Tabernacle to the east before the Tent of Meeting to the east, Moses and Aaron and his sons, keeping the watch of the Tabernacle, for the watch of the Israelites, and the stranger who draws near shall be put to death.  
**3:39** All the reckonings of the Levites that Moses and Aaron reckoned according to the word of the LORD by their clans, every male from a month old and up—twenty-two thousand.  
**3:40** And the LORD said to Moses, “Reckon every firstborn male of the Israelites from a month old and up and count the number of their names.  
**3:41** And you shall take the Levites for Me—I am the LORD—instead of every firstborn among the Israelites, and the cattle of the Levites instead of every firstborn among the cattle of the Israelites.”  
**3:42** And Moses reckoned as the LORD had charged him every firstborn among the Israelites.  
**3:43** And all the firstborn males in the number of names from a month old and up in their reckoning came to twenty-two thousand two hundred and seventy-three.  
**3:44** And the LORD said to Moses, saying,  
**3:45** “Take the Levites instead of every firstborn among the Israelites and the cattle of the Levites instead of their cattle, and the Levites shall be Mine. I am the LORD.  
**3:46** And for the redemption of the two hundred seventy-three of the firstborn of the Israelites who exceed the number of the Levites,  
**3:47** you shall take five shekels for each head, by the sanctuary shekel, you shall take, twenty gerahs to the shekel,  
**3:48** and you shall give the silver to Aaron and his sons as redemption for those among them who exceed the number.”  
**3:49** And Moses took the redemption silver from those who exceeded the number of those redeemed by the Levites.  
**3:50** From the firstborn of the Israelites he took the silver, one thousand three hundred and sixty-five shekels, by the sanctuary shekel.  
**3:51** And Moses gave the redemption silver to Aaron and to his sons according to the word of the LORD, as the LORD had charged Moses.  

## 4장

**4:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,  
**4:2** “Count the heads of the Kohathites from the midst of the Levites by their clans, by their fathers’ houses.  
**4:3** From thirty years old up till fifty years old, all who come to the army to do the task in the Tent of Meeting.  
**4:4** This is the work of the Kohathites in the Tent of Meeting, the holy of holies.  
**4:5** And Aaron shall come, and his sons, when the camp journeys onward, and take down the covering of the screen and cover with it the Ark of the Covenant.  
**4:6** And they shall place over it a cover of ocher-dyed skin and spread a garment of pure indigo above it and put in its poles.  
**4:7** And on the table of the Presence they shall spread an indigo garment and place on it the bowls and the ladles and the jars and the libation flasks, and the perpetual bread shall be upon it.  
**4:8** And they shall spread over them a crimson garment and cover it in a covering of ocher-dyed skin and put in its poles.  
**4:9** And they shall take an indigo garment and cover the lamp stand of the light and its lamps and its tongs and its fire-pans and all its oil vessels with which they serve.  
**4:10** And they shall place it and all its vessels in a covering of ocher-dyed skin and place it on a beam.  
**4:11** And over the golden altar they shall spread an indigo garment and cover it with a covering of ocher-dyed skin and put in its poles.  
**4:12** And they shall take all the vessels of service with which they serve in the sanctuary and place them in an indigo garment and cover them with a covering of ocher-dyed skin and place on a beam.  
**4:13** And they shall clear the ashes from the altar and spread over it a purple garment.  
**4:14** And they shall place on it all its vessels with which they serve upon it, the fire-pans and the flesh-hooks and the scrapers and the basins, all the vessels of the altar, and they shall spread over it a covering of ocher-dyed skin and put in its poles.  
**4:15** And Aaron, and his sons, shall finish covering the sanctuary and all the vessels of the sanctuary when the camp journeys onward, and afterward the Kohathites shall come to carry, so that they do not touch the holy and die. These make up the carriage of the Kohathites in the Tent of Meeting.  
**4:16** And what is appointed for Eleazar son of Aaron the priest—the oil for the lamp and the aromatic incense and the perpetual grain offering and the anointing oil, what is appointed for all the Tabernacle and all that is in it, in the sanctuary and in its vessels.”  
**4:17** And the LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,  
**4:18** “Do not let the tribe of the clans of the Kohathites be cut off from the midst of the Levites.  
**4:19** And this shall you do for them, that they live and not die when they draw near the holy of holies: Aaron and his sons shall come and set every man of them at his work and at his carriage.  
**4:20** And they shall not come in to see the sanctuary for even a moment and die.”  
**4:21** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**4:22** “Count the heads of the Gershonites, too, by their fathers’ houses, by their clans.  
**4:23** From thirty years old up till fifty years old you shall reckon them, all who do army service to do the work in the Tent of Meeting.  
**4:24** This is the work of the Gershonite clans, for work and for carriage:  
**4:25** They shall carry the curtains of the Tabernacle and the Tent of Meeting, its covering and the covering of ocher-dyed skin that is over it above and the screen of the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.  
**4:26** And the panels of the court and the screen for the entrance to the gate of the court which is all around the Tabernacle and the altar, and their cords, and all the vessels for their service and all that is done for them, and they shall do the work.  
**4:27** By the word of Aaron and his sons shall be all the work of the Gershonites, for all their carriage and for all their work, and you shall oversee them watchfully in all their carriage.  
**4:28** This is the work of the Gershonite clans in the Tent of Meeting, and their watch is in the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.  
**4:29** The Merarites by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, you shall reckon them.  
**4:30** From thirty years old up till fifty years old you shall reckon them, all who come to the army to do the work of the Tent of Meeting.

**4:32** and the posts of the court all around and their sockets and their pegs and their cords, for all their vessels and for all their work. And by name you shall reckon the vessels of their carriage watch.

**4:35** from thirty years old up till fifty years old, all who came to the army, to the work in the Tent of Meeting.  
**4:36** And their reckoning by their clans was two thousand seven hundred and fifty.  
**4:37** This is the reckoning of the Kohathite clans, all who worked in the Tent of Meeting, which Moses, and Aaron with him, reckoned, by the LORD’s word, through the hand of Moses.

**4:39** from thirty years old up till fifty years, all who came to the army, to the work in the Tent of Meeting.  
**4:40** And their reckoning by their clans, by their fathers’ houses, came to two thousand six hundred and thirty.  
**4:41** This is the reckoning of the Gershonites, all who worked in the Tent of Meeting, which Moses, and Aaron with him, reckoned by the LORD’s word.  
**4:42** And the reckoning of the Merarite clans, by their clans, by their fathers’ houses,  
**4:43** from thirty years old up till fifty years old, all who came to the army, to the work in the Tent of Meeting.  
**4:44** And their reckoning by their clans came to three thousand two hundred.

**4:48** And their reckoning came to eight thousand five hundred and eighty.

## 5장

**5:1** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**5:2** “Charge the Israelites, that they send out from the camp everyone infected with skin blanch and everyone suffering from genital flux and everyone defiled by a corpse.  
**5:3** Whether male or female, you shall send them off, outside the camp you shall send them, that they do not defile their camps in whose midst I abide.”  
**5:4** And thus did the Israelites do, and they sent them outside the camp. As the LORD had spoken to Moses, thus the Israelites did.  
**5:5** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**5:6** “Speak to the Israelites: Should man or woman commit any of the human offenses, to betray the trust of the LORD, that person shall bear guilt.  
**5:7** And they shall confess their offenses which they committed, and he shall render back for his guilt the sum of its principal, and a fifth part of it he shall add to it, and give it to him whom he wronged.  
**5:8** And if the man should have no redeemer to render back to him for his guilt, what is rendered back shall be the LORD’s, the priest’s, besides the ram of atonement with which he will atone for himself.  
**5:9** And every donation for all the sacred offerings of the Israelites that they bring forward to the priest, his shall it be.  
**5:10** And a man’s sacred offerings shall be his. That which he gives to the priest shall be his.”  
**5:11** And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,  
**5:12** “Speak to the Israelites and say to them: ‘Any man whose wife may stray and betray his trust,  
**5:13** and a man lie with her in seed-coupling and it be concealed from her husband’s eyes and she hide and be defiled, with no witness against her, and she herself be not apprehended—  
**5:14** then a spirit of jealousy may come over him and he will be jealous about his wife, with her being defiled, or a spirit of jealousy may come over him and he will be jealous about his wife, she not being defiled.  
**5:15** The man shall bring his wife to the priest and bring her sacrifice for her, a tenth of an ephah of barley flour; he shall pour no oil over it nor put frankincense upon it, for it is a grain offering of jealousy, a grain offering of remembrance, a remembering of guilt,  
**5:16** and the priest shall bring her forward and stand her before the LORD.  
**5:17** And the priest shall take holy water in an earthen vessel, and from the earth that is on the floor of the sanctuary the priest shall take and put into the water.  
**5:18** And the priest shall stand the woman before the LORD and undo her hair and place on her palms the grain offering of remembrance, a grain offering of jealousy it is, and in the hand of the priest shall be the bitter besetting water.  
**5:19** And the priest shall make her swear and shall say to the woman: If no man has lain with you and if you have not strayed in defilement from your husband, be cleared by this bitter besetting water.  
**5:20** And you, if you have strayed from your husband and if you have been defiled and a man other than your husband has put his semen in you. . . .  
**5:21** And the priest shall make the woman swear this oath of imprecation, and the priest shall say to the woman: May the LORD make you an imprecation and oath in the midst of your people through the LORD’s making your thigh sag and your belly swell.  
**5:22** And this besetting water shall enter your innards to swell the belly and to sag the thigh. And the woman shall say: Amen, amen.  
**5:23** And the priest shall write these imprecations in a record and wipe them out in the bitter water.  
**5:24** And he shall make the woman drink the bitter besetting water and the bitter besetting water shall enter her.  
**5:25** And the priest shall take from the woman’s hand the grain offering of jealousy and elevate the grain offering before the LORD and bring it forward to the altar.  
**5:26** And the priest shall take a handful from the grain offering, its token, and turn it to smoke on the altar and, after, shall make the woman drink the water.  
**5:27** Once he has made her drink the water, it shall come about that if she was defiled and betrayed her husband’s trust, the besetting water will enter her as bitter and her belly will swell and her thigh sag, and the woman shall become an imprecation in the midst of her people.  
**5:28** And if the woman has not been defiled and she is pure, she will be cleared and sown with seed.  
**5:29** This is the teaching of jealousy, should a woman stray from her husband and be defiled.

**35:11** you shall set out for yourselves towns, towns of asylum you shall have, and a murderer may flee there, one who strikes down a person in errance.  
**35:12** And the towns will be an asylum for you from the avenger, and the murderer will not die until he stands before the community for judgment.  
**35:13** And the towns that you give, six towns of asylum you shall have.  
**35:14** Three towns you shall give beyond the Jordan the avenger, and three towns in the land of Canaan, towns of asylum they shall be.  
**35:15** For the Israelites and for the sojourner and for the settler in their midst these six towns shall be an asylum for everyone who strikes down a person in errance, to flee there.  
**35:16** But if he struck him with an iron tool and he died, he is a murderer, the murderer is doomed to die.  
**35:17** And if he struck him with a hand stone by which one may die and he died, he is a murderer, the murderer is doomed to die.  
**35:18** Or with a wooden hand tool by which one may die he struck him and he died, he is a murderer, the murderer is doomed to die.  
**35:19** The blood avenger shall put the murderer to death, when he comes upon him he shall put him to death.  
**35:20** And if in hatred he should knock him down or fling something on him by design and he die,  
**35:21** or in enmity he strike him with his hand and he die, he who struck is doomed to die, he is a murderer. The blood avenger shall put the murderer to death when he comes upon him.  
**35:22** But if on an impulse, without enmity, he knocked him down or flung upon him any tool, without design,  
**35:23** or with any stone by which one may die, without seeing he dropped it on him and he died, he not being his enemy nor seeking his harm,  
**35:24** the community shall judge between him who struck and the blood avenger on these matters of judgment.  
**35:25** And the community shall rescue the murderer from the hands of the blood avenger and the community shall take him back to the town of asylum where he fled, and he shall stay there until the death of the high priest who was anointed with the holy oil.  
**35:26** But if the murderer should indeed go out beyond the border of his town of asylum where he has fled,  
**35:27** and the blood avenger finds him outside the border of his town of asylum and the blood avenger murders the murderer, he has no bloodguilt.  
**35:28** But he shall stay in his town of asylum until the death of the high priest, and after the death of the high priest the murderer shall go back to the land of his holding.  
**35:29** And these shall be for you a statute of judgment for your generations in all your dwelling places.  
**35:30** Whoever strikes down a person, by witnesses shall the murderer be murdered, and a single witness shall not testify against a person to die.  
**35:31** And you shall not take ransom for the life of a murderer who is guilty to die, for he is doomed to die.  
**35:32** And you shall not take ransom in lieu of flight to his town of asylum to let him go back to dwell in the land, until the death of the high priest.  
**35:33** And you shall not pollute the land in which you are, for blood will pollute the land, and for the land there will be no ransoming for the blood that has been shed in it except through the blood of him who shed it.  
**35:34** And you shall not defile the land in which you dwell, in the midst of which I abide, for I am the LORD, abiding in the midst of the Israelites.’”

**36:4** And though the jubilee comes for the Israelites, their estate would be added to the estate of the tribe to which they would belong, and from the estate of our fathers’ tribe it would be withdrawn.”

**36:6** This is the thing that the LORD charged concerning the daughters of Zelophehad, saying, ‘To whomever is good in their eyes they may become wives, only within the clan of their father’s tribe must they become wives.  
**36:7** And an estate of the Israelites shall not turn round from tribe to tribe, but the Israelites shall cling each man to the estate of the tribe of his fathers.  
**36:8** And every daughter inheriting an estate from the tribes of the Israelites shall become wife to someone from the clan of her father’s tribe, so that the Israelites may inherit each man the estate of his fathers.  
**36:9** And an estate shall not turn round from a tribe to another tribe, but each man shall cling to his estate in the tribes of the Israelites.’”
