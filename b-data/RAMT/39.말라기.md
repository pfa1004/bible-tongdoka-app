# 39.말라기 (RAMT)



## 1장

**1:1** A portent: the word of the LORD through Malachi.  
**1:2** I have loved you, said the LORD,<br>and you said, “How have you loved us?”<br>Is not Esau Jacob’s brother? said the LORD.<br>And I loved Jacob,  
**1:3** but Esau I hated,<br>and I made his mountains a desolation<br>and his estate—for the desert jackals.  
**1:4** Should Edom say, “We are beggared,<br>but once more we will build the ruins,”<br>thus said the LORD of Armies:<br>They shall build but I will destroy,<br>and they shall be called the region of wickedness<br>and the people the LORD cursed for all time.  
**1:5** And your own eyes shall see and you shall say,<br>“May the LORD be great beyond the region of Israel.”  
**1:6** A son honors his father,<br>and a slave his master,<br>but if I am a father,<br>where is My honor,<br>and if I am a master,<br>where is the fear of Me?<br>said the LORD of Armies to you,<br>priests who despise My name.<br>And you said, “How have we despised Your name?”  
**1:7** Bringing on My altar<br>defiled food,<br>and you say, “How have we defiled You?”<br>When you say the LORD’s table<br>is despised,  
**1:8** and when you bring a blind beast to sacrifice—<br>“there is nothing wrong.”<br>And when you bring a lame and sickly beast—<br>“there is nothing wrong.”<br>Offer it, pray, to your prefect.<br>Will he be pleased with you or favor you?<br>said the LORD of Armies.  
**1:9** And now, entreat, pray,<br>God, that He be gracious to us<br>From your hand this was.<br>Will He show favor to you?<br>said the LORD of Armies.  
**1:10** Who then among you would close double doors<br>and not light fire on My altar in vain?<br>I have no desire of you,<br>said the LORD of Armies,<br>and with grain offering from your land I will not be pleased.  
**1:11** For from the sun’s rising to its setting<br>great is My name among the nations,<br>and in every place incense is offered to My name<br>and pure grain offering,<br>for great is My name among the nations,<br>said the LORD of Armies.  
**1:12** But you profane it<br>when you say, “The Master’s table is defiled,<br>and the food on it despised.”  
**1:13** And you say, “How tiresome,”<br>and you insult Me, said the LORD of Armies.<br>And you bring the stolen and the lame and the sickly,<br>and you bring the grain offering.<br>Will I be pleased with it from your hand?<br>said the LORD.  
**1:14** And cursed be the schemer who has in his flock a sound male but pledges and sacrifices a damaged one to the Master. For I am a great king, said the LORD of Armies, and My name is fearsome among the nations.  

## 2장

**2:1** And now, for you is this command, O priests.  
**2:2** If you do not heed and do not pay mind to give honor to My name, said the LORD of Armies, I will let loose the curse among you and make your blessings curses, yes, make them curses, for you do not pay mind.  
**2:3** I am about to rebuke your seed and scatter dung on your faces, the dung of your festival offerings, and it shall carry you off to where it is.  
**2:4** And you shall know that I have sent you this command, for My covenant to be with Levi, said the LORD of Armies.  
**2:5** My covenant has been with him, life and peace, and I have given them to him—fear, and he did fear Me, and before My name he was awestruck.  
**2:6** A teaching of truth was in his mouth, and no wrong was found on his lips. In peace and in uprightness he walked with Me, and many did he bring back from crime.  
**2:7** For the lips of the priest preserved knowledge, and teachings they sought from his mouth, for he was the messenger of the LORD of Armies.  
**2:8** Yet you yourselves swerved from the way; you made many stumble through rulings. You made a ruin of the covenant of the Levite, said the LORD of Armies.  
**2:9** And I on My part made you despised and lowly to every people, as you have not been keeping My ways, and you have been showing favoritism in rulings.  
**2:10** Do we not all have one father,<br>did not one God create us?<br>Why should we each betray our brothers<br>to profane the covenant of our fathers?  
**2:11** Judah has betrayed, and an abomination was done in Israel and in Jerusalem. For Judah profaned the LORD’s sanctity that He had loved and coupled with the daughter of an alien god.  
**2:12** May the LORD cut off for the man who does this, a witness and answerer from the tents of Jacob and bringer of grain offering to the LORD of Armies.  
**2:13** And this, besides, did you do: cause to cover with tears the LORD’s altar, with weeping and groans, because there is no more turning to the grain offering or accepting with favor from your hand.  
**2:14** And you say, “Why?” Because the LORD bore witness between you and the wife of your youth. It was you who betrayed her when she was your friend and your covenanted wife.  
**2:15** And did not one do [right], who has exceeding spirit? And what does the one seek?—seed of God. And you should guard yourselves with your spirit and not betray the wife of your youth.  
**2:16** For I hate divorce, said the LORD God of Israel, and one who covers his garb with outrage, said the LORD of Armies. And you shall guard yourselves with your spirit and not betray.  
**2:17** You have wearied the LORD with your words, and you say, “How have we wearied Him?” In your saying, “All evildoers are good in the eyes of the LORD, and them He desires,” or, “Where is the God of justice?”  

## 3장

**3:1** I am about to send My messenger,<br>and he shall clear the way before Me.<br>In a trice He shall enter His Temple,<br>the Master Whom you seek,<br>and the covenant’s messenger whom you desire,<br>look, he comes, said the LORD of Armies.  
**3:2** And who can bear the day of His coming<br>and who can stand when He appears?<br>For He is like the smelter’s fire<br>and like the launderers’ lye.  
**3:3** And the smelter shall sit and purify silver,<br>and purify the sons of Levi,<br>and refine them like gold and silver,<br>and they shall become grain offerings to the LORD in righteousness.  
**3:4** And the grain offering of Judah and Jerusalem<br>shall be sweet to the LORD<br>as in days of yore and in former years.  
**3:5** But I will approach you for judgment,<br>and I will be a swift witness<br>against sorcerers and against adulterers<br>and against those who swear falsely,<br>and against those who extort the hired man’s wages,<br>who wrong widow and orphan and sojourner<br>and do not fear Me,<br>said the LORD of Armies.  
**3:6** For I am the LORD, I have not changed,<br>and you, sons of Jacob, have not come to an end.  
**3:7** From your fathers’ days you swerved from My statutes<br>and you did not keep them.<br>Turn back to Me, that I may turn back to you,<br>said the LORD of Armies.<br>But you said, “How shall we turn back?”  
**3:8** Can a human cheat God?<br>For you are cheating Me.<br>And you said, “How did we cheat You?”<br>—in tithes and in donations.  
**3:9** Despite the curse with which you are cursed,<br>Me you cheat, the whole nation.  
**3:10** Bring the whole tithe<br>to the treasure house<br>that there be provision in My house.<br>and test Me, pray, in this,<br>said the LORD of Armies.<br>I will surely open for you<br>the casements of the heavens<br>and shower upon you<br>blessings without end.  
**3:11** And I will rebuke for you the devourer,<br>and it shall not ruin for you the fruit of the soil,<br>nor shall the vine of the field lose its yield,<br>said the LORD of Armies.  
**3:12** And all the nations shall call you happy,<br>for you shall be a land desired,<br>said the LORD of Armies.  
**3:13** Your words against Me have been harsh,<br>said the LORD.<br>And you said, “How did we speak against You?”  
**3:14** You said, “It is for naught to serve God,<br>and what profit if we keep His watch<br>and if we walk downcast because of the LORD of Armies?  
**3:15** And now we see the arrogant happy,<br>evildoers actually flourish,<br>actually test God and escape.”  
**3:16** Then did the LORD-fearers speak together,<br>each man to his neighbor,<br>and the LORD hearkened and He heard,<br>and a book of remembrance was written before Him<br>for the LORD-fearers who value His name.  
**3:17** And they shall become for Me, said the LORD of Armies,<br>a treasure on the day that I prepare.<br>And I will have pity on them<br>as a man has pity<br>on his son who serves him.  
**3:18** And you shall turn back and see<br>between righteous and wicked,<br>between him who serves God<br>and him who does not serve Him.  

## 4장

**4:1** For, look, the day is coming,<br>burning like a kiln,<br>when all the arrogant and all the evildoers shall be straw.<br>And the day that is coming shall set them ablaze,<br>said the LORD of Armies,<br>shall not leave them root or branch.  
**4:2** But to you who fear My name shall dawn<br>a sun of righteousness with healing in its wings,<br>and you shall go forth and become plump<br>like stall-fed calves.  
**4:3** And you shall trample the wicked, for they shall be ashes<br>beneath the soles of your feet<br>on the day that I prepare,<br>said the LORD of Armies.  
**4:4** Recall the teaching of Moses My servant<br>that I charged him on Horeb,<br>for all Israel, statutes and laws.  
**4:5** Look, I am about to send to you<br>Elijah the prophet<br>before the coming of the day of the LORD,<br>great and fearsome.  
**4:6** And he shall bring fathers’ hearts back to sons<br>and the sons’ hearts to their fathers—<br>lest I come and strike the land with utter destruction.
