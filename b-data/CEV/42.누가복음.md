# 42.누가복음 (CEV)



## 1장

**1:1** Many people have tried to tell the story of what God has done among us.  
**1:2** They wrote what we had been told by the ones who were there in the beginning and saw what happened.  
**1:3** So I made a careful study<sup>①</sup> of everything and then decided to write and tell you exactly what took place. Honorable Theophilus,<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>a careful study: </i>Or “a study from the beginning.”  
**1:4** I have done this to let you know the truth about what you have heard.  
**1:5**  When Herod was king of Judea, there was a priest by the name of Zechariah from the priestly group of Abijah. His wife Elizabeth was from the family of Aaron.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Aaron: </i>The brother of Moses and the first priest.  
**1:6** Both of them were good people and pleased the Lord God by obeying all that he had commanded.  
**1:7** But they did not have children. Elizabeth could not have any, and both Zechariah and Elizabeth were already old.  
**1:8** One day Zechariah's group of priests were on duty, and he was serving God as a priest.  
**1:9** According to the custom of the priests, he had been chosen to go into the Lord's temple that day and to burn incense,<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>burn incense: </i>This was done twice a day, once in the morning and again in the late afternoon.  
**1:10** while the people stood outside praying.  
**1:11** All at once an angel from the Lord appeared to Zechariah at the right side of the altar.  
**1:12** Zechariah was confused and afraid when he saw the angel.  
**1:13** But the angel told him: Don't be afraid, Zechariah! God has heard your prayers. Your wife Elizabeth will have a son, and you must name him John.  
**1:14** His birth will make you very happy, and many people will be glad.  
**1:15**  Your son will be a great servant of the Lord. He must never drink wine or beer, and the power of the Holy Spirit will be with him from the time he is born.  
**1:16** John will lead many people in Israel to turn back to the Lord their God.  
**1:17**  He will go ahead of the Lord with the same power and spirit that Elijah<sup>①</sup> had. And because of John, parents will be more thoughtful of their children. And people who now disobey God will begin to think as they ought to. This is how John will get people ready for the Lord.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Elijah: </i>The prophet Elijah was known for his power to work miracles.  
**1:18** Zechariah said to the angel, “How will I know this is going to happen? My wife and I are both very old.”  
**1:19**  The angel answered, “I am Gabriel, God's servant, and I was sent to tell you this good news.  
**1:20** You have not believed what I have said. So you will not be able to say a thing until all this happens. But everything will take place when it is supposed to.”  
**1:21** The crowd was waiting for Zechariah and kept wondering why he was staying in the temple so long.  
**1:22** When he did come out, he could not speak, and they knew he had seen a vision. He motioned to them with his hands, but did not say a thing.  
**1:23** When Zechariah's time of service in the temple was over, he went home.  
**1:24** Soon after this, his wife was expecting a baby, and for five months she did not leave the house. She said to herself,  
**1:25** “What the Lord has done for me will keep people from looking down on me.”<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>keep people from looking down on me: </i>When a married woman could not have children, it was thought that the Lord was punishing her.  
**1:26** One month later God sent the angel Gabriel to the town of Nazareth in Galilee  
**1:27**  with a message for a virgin named Mary. She was engaged to Joseph from the family of King David.  
**1:28** The angel greeted Mary and said, “You are truly blessed! The Lord is with you.”  
**1:29** Mary was confused by the angel's words and wondered what they meant.  
**1:30** Then the angel told Mary, “Don't be afraid! God is pleased with you,  
**1:31**  and you will have a son. His name will be Jesus.  
**1:32**  He will be great and will be called the Son of God Most High. The Lord God will make him king, as his ancestor David was.  
**1:33** He will rule the people of Israel forever, and his kingdom will never end.”  
**1:34** Mary asked the angel, “How can this happen? I am not even married!”  
**1:35** The angel answered, “The Holy Spirit will come down to you, and God's power will come over you. So your child will be called the holy Son of God.  
**1:36** Your relative Elizabeth is also going to have a son, even though she is old. No one thought she could ever have a baby, but in three months she will have a son.  
**1:37**  Nothing is impossible for God!”  
**1:38** Mary said, “I am the Lord's servant! Let it happen as you have said.” And the angel left her.  
**1:39** A short time later Mary hurried to a town in the hill country of Judea.  
**1:40** She went into Zechariah's home, where she greeted Elizabeth.  
**1:41** When Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, her baby moved within her. The Holy Spirit came upon Elizabeth.  
**1:42**  Then in a loud voice she said to Mary: God has blessed you more than any other woman! He has also blessed the child you will have.  
**1:43** Why should the mother of my Lord come to me?  
**1:44** As soon as I heard your greeting, my baby became happy and moved within me.  
**1:45** The Lord has blessed you because you believed that he will keep his promise.  
**1:46**  Mary said:<br>With all my heart<br>I praise the Lord,  
**1:47** and I am glad<br>because of God my Savior.  
**1:48** God cares for me,<br>his humble servant.<br>From now on,<br>all people will say<br>God has blessed me.  
**1:49** God All-Powerful has done<br>great things for me,<br>and his name is holy.  
**1:50** He always shows mercy<br>to everyone<br>who worships him.  
**1:51** The Lord has used<br>his powerful arm<br>to scatter those<br>who are proud.  
**1:52**  God drags strong rulers<br>from their thrones<br>and puts humble people<br>in places of power.  
**1:53** God gives the hungry<br>good things to eat,<br>and sends the rich away<br>with nothing.  
**1:54** God helps his servant Israel<br>and is always merciful<br>to his people.  
**1:55**  The Lord made this promise<br>to our ancestors,<br>to Abraham and his family<br>forever!  
**1:56** Mary stayed with Elizabeth about three months. Then she went back home.  
**1:57** When Elizabeth's son was born,  
**1:58** her neighbors and relatives heard how kind the Lord had been to her, and they too were glad.  
**1:59**  Eight days later they did for the child what the Law of Moses commands.<sup>①</sup> They were going to name him Zechariah, after his father.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>what the Law of Moses commands: </i>This refers to circumcision. It is the cutting off of skin from the private part of Jewish boys eight days after birth to show that they belong to the Lord.  
**1:60** But Elizabeth said, “No! His name is John.”  
**1:61** The people argued, “No one in your family has ever been named John.”  
**1:62** So they motioned to Zechariah to find out what he wanted to name his son.  
**1:63** Zechariah asked for a writing tablet. Then he wrote, “His name is John.” Everyone was amazed.  
**1:64** At once, Zechariah started speaking and praising God.  
**1:65** All the neighbors were frightened because of what had happened, and everywhere in the hill country people kept talking about these things.  
**1:66** Everyone who heard about this wondered what this child would grow up to be. They knew the Lord was with him.  
**1:67** The Holy Spirit came upon Zechariah, and he began to speak:  
**1:68** Praise the Lord,<br>the God of Israel!<br>He has come<br>to save his people.  
**1:69** Our God has given us<br>a mighty Savior<sup>①</sup><br>from the family<br>of David his servant.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>a mighty Savior: </i>The Greek text has “a horn of salvation.” In the Scriptures animal horns are often a symbol of great strength.  
**1:70** Long ago the Lord promised<br>by the words<br>of his holy prophets  
**1:71** to save us from our enemies<br>and from everyone<br>who hates us.  
**1:72** God said he would be kind<br>to our people and keep<br>his sacred promise.  
**1:73** He told our ancestor Abraham  
**1:74** that he would rescue us<br>from our enemies.<br>Then we could serve him<br>without fear,  
**1:75** by being holy and good<br>as long as we live.   
**1:76**  You, my son, will be called<br>the prophet of God Most High.<br>You will go ahead of the Lord<br>to get everything ready<br>for him.  
**1:77** You will tell his people<br>that they can be saved<br>when their sins<br>are forgiven.  
**1:78** God's love and kindness<br>will shine upon us<br>like the sun that rises<br>in the sky.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>like the sun that rises in the sky: </i>Or “like the Messiah coming from heaven.”  
**1:79**  On us who live<br>in the dark shadow<br>of death<br>this light will shine<br>to guide us<br>into a life of peace.  
**1:80** As John grew up, God's Spirit gave him great power. John lived in the desert until the time he was sent to the people of Israel.  

## 2장

**2:1** About that time Emperor Augustus gave orders for the names of all the people to be listed in record books.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>names … listed in record books: </i>This was done so that everyone could be made to pay taxes to the Emperor.  
**2:2** These first records were made when Quirinius was governor of Syria.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Quirinius was governor of Syria: </i>It is known that Quirinius made a record of the people in A.D. 6 or 7. But the exact date of the record taking that Luke mentions is not known.  
**2:3** Everyone had to go to their own hometown to be listed.  
**2:4** So Joseph had to leave Nazareth in Galilee and go to Bethlehem in Judea. Long ago Bethlehem had been King David's hometown, and Joseph went there because he was from David's family.  
**2:5** Mary was engaged to Joseph and traveled with him to Bethlehem. She was soon going to have a baby,  
**2:6** and while they were there,  
**2:7** she gave birth to her first-born<sup>①</sup> son. She dressed him in baby clothes<sup>②</sup> and laid him on a bed of hay, because there was no room for them in the inn.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>first-born: </i>The Jewish people said that the first-born son in each of their families belonged to the Lord. <sup>②</sup><i>dressed him in baby clothes: </i>The Greek text has “wrapped him in wide strips of cloth,” which was how young babies were dressed.  
**2:8** That night in the fields near Bethlehem some shepherds were guarding their sheep.  
**2:9**  All at once an angel came down to them from the Lord, and the brightness of the Lord's glory flashed around them. The shepherds were frightened.  
**2:10** But the angel said, “Don't be afraid! I have good news for you, which will make everyone happy.  
**2:11** This very day in King David's hometown a Savior was born for you. He is Christ the Lord.  
**2:12** You will know who he is, because you will find him dressed in baby clothes and lying on a bed of hay.”  
**2:13** Suddenly many other angels came down from heaven and joined in praising God. They said:  
**2:14** “Praise God in heaven!<br>Peace on earth to everyone<br>who pleases God.”  
**2:15** After the angels had left and gone back to heaven, the shepherds said to each other, “Let's go to Bethlehem and see what the Lord has told us about.”  
**2:16** They hurried off and found Mary and Joseph, and they saw the baby lying on a bed of hay.  
**2:17** When the shepherds saw Jesus, they told his parents what the angel had said about him.  
**2:18** Everyone listened and was surprised.  
**2:19** But Mary kept thinking about all this and wondering what it meant.  
**2:20** As the shepherds returned to their sheep, they were praising God and saying wonderful things about him. Everything they had seen and heard was just as the angel had said.  
**2:21**  Eight days later Jesus' parents did for him what the Law of Moses commands.<sup>①</sup> And they named him Jesus, just as the angel had told Mary when he promised she would have a baby.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>what the Law of Moses commands: </i>See the <a name='C490 1:59'>note at <a name='B:490 1:59'>1.59.  
**2:22**  The time came for Mary and Joseph to do what the Law of Moses says a mother is supposed to do after her baby is born.<sup>①</sup> They took Jesus to the temple in Jerusalem and presented him to the Lord,<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>after her baby is born: </i>After a Jewish mother gave birth to a son, she was considered “unclean” and had to stay home until he was circumcised (see the <a name='C490 1:59'>note at <a name='B:490 1:59'>1.59). Then she had to stay home for another 33 days, before offering a sacrifice to the Lord.  
**2:23**  just as the Law of the Lord says, “Each first-born<sup>①</sup> baby boy belongs to the Lord.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>first-born: </i>See the <a name='C490 2:7'>note at <a name='B:490 2:7'>2.7.  
**2:24** The Law of the Lord also says parents have to offer a sacrifice, giving at least a pair of doves or two young pigeons. So that is what Mary and Joseph did.  
**2:25** At this time a man named Simeon was living in Jerusalem. Simeon was a good man. He loved God and was waiting for him to save the people of Israel. God's Spirit came to him  
**2:26** and told him that he would not die until he had seen Christ the Lord.  
**2:27** When Mary and Joseph brought Jesus to the temple to do what the Law of Moses says should be done for a new baby, the Spirit told Simeon to go into the temple.  
**2:28** Simeon took the baby Jesus in his arms and praised God,  
**2:29** “Lord, I am your servant,<br>and now I can die in peace,<br>because you have kept<br>your promise to me.  
**2:30** With my own eyes I have seen<br>what you have done<br>to save your people,  
**2:31** and foreign nations<br>will also see this.  
**2:32**  Your mighty power is a light<br>for all nations,<br>and it will bring honor<br>to your people Israel.”  
**2:33** Jesus' parents were surprised at what Simeon had said.  
**2:34** Then he blessed them and told Mary, “This child of yours will cause many people in Israel to fall and others to stand. The child will be like a warning sign. Many people will reject him,  
**2:35** and you, Mary, will suffer as though you had been stabbed by a dagger. But all this will show what people are really thinking.”  
**2:36**  The prophet Anna was also there in the temple. She was the daughter of Phanuel from the tribe of Asher, and she was very old. In her youth she had been married for seven years, but her husband died.  
**2:37** And now she was 84 years old.<sup>①</sup> Night and day she served God in the temple by praying and often going without eating.<sup>②</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>And now she was 84 years old: </i>Or “And now she had been a widow for 84 years.” <sup>②</sup><i>without eating: </i>The Jewish people sometimes went without eating (also called “fasting”) to show their love for God or to show sorrow for their sins.  
**2:38** At this time Anna came in and praised God. She spoke about the child Jesus to everyone who hoped for Jerusalem to be set free.  
**2:39**  After Joseph and Mary had done everything that the Law of the Lord commands, they returned home to Nazareth in Galilee.  
**2:40** The child Jesus grew. He became strong and wise, and God blessed him.  
**2:41**  Every year Jesus' parents went to Jerusalem for Passover.  
**2:42** And when Jesus was twelve years old, they all went there as usual for the celebration.  
**2:43** After Passover his parents left, but they did not know that Jesus had stayed on in the city.  
**2:44** They thought he was traveling with some other people, and they went a whole day before they started looking for him.  
**2:45** When they could not find him with their relatives and friends, they went back to Jerusalem and started looking for him there.  
**2:46** Three days later they found Jesus sitting in the temple, listening to the teachers and asking them questions.  
**2:47** Everyone who heard him was surprised at how much he knew and at the answers he gave.  
**2:48** When his parents found him, they were amazed. His mother said, “Son, why have you done this to us? Your father and I have been very worried, and we have been searching for you!”  
**2:49** Jesus answered, <J>“Why did you have to look for me? Didn't you know that I would be in my Father's house?”</J><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>in my Father's house: </i>Or “doing my Father's work.”  
**2:50** But they did not understand what he meant.  
**2:51** Jesus went back to Nazareth with his parents and obeyed them. His mother kept on thinking about all that had happened.  
**2:52**  Jesus became wise, and he grew strong. God was pleased with him and so were the people.  

## 3장

**3:1** For 15 years<sup>①</sup> Emperor Tiberius had ruled that part of the world. Pontius Pilate was governor of Judea, and Herod<sup>②</sup> was the ruler of Galilee. Herod's brother, Philip, was the ruler in the countries of Iturea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias was the ruler of Abilene.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>For 15 years: </i>This was either A.D. 28 or 29, and Jesus was about 30 years old (see 3.23). <sup>②</sup><i>Herod: </i>Herod Antipas, the son of Herod the Great.  
**3:2** Annas and Caiaphas were the Jewish high priests.<sup>①</sup> At that time God spoke to Zechariah's son John, who was living in the desert.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Annas and Caiaphas … high priests: </i>Annas was high priest from A.D. 6 until 15. His son-in-law Caiaphas was high priest from A.D. 18 until 37.  
**3:3** So John went along the Jordan Valley, telling the people, “Turn back to God and be baptized! Then your sins will be forgiven.”  
**3:4**  Isaiah the prophet wrote about John when he said,<br>“In the desert<br>someone is shouting,<br>‘Get the road ready<br>for the Lord!<br>Make a straight path<br>for him.  
**3:5** Fill up every valley<br>and level every mountain<br>and hill.<br>Straighten the crooked paths<br>and smooth out<br>the rough roads.  
**3:6** Then everyone will see<br>the saving power of God.’ ”  
**3:7**  Crowds of people came out to be baptized, but John said to them, “You bunch of snakes! Who warned you to run from the coming judgment?  
**3:8**  Do something to show that you really have given up your sins. Don't start saying you belong to Abraham's family. God can turn these stones into children for Abraham.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>children for Abraham: </i>The Jewish people thought they were God's chosen people because of God's promises to their ancestor Abraham.  
**3:9**  An ax is ready to cut the trees down at their roots. Any tree that doesn't produce good fruit will be cut down and thrown into a fire.”  
**3:10** The crowds asked John, “What should we do?”  
**3:11** John told them, “If you have two coats, give one to someone who doesn't have any. If you have food, share it with someone else.”  
**3:12**  When tax collectors<sup>①</sup> came to be baptized, they asked John, “Teacher, what should we do?”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>tax collectors: </i>These were usually Jewish people who paid the Romans for the right to collect taxes. They were hated by other Jews who thought of them as traitors to their country and to their religion.  
**3:13** John told them, “Don't make people pay more than they owe.”  
**3:14** Some soldiers asked him, “And what about us? What do we have to do?” John told them, “Don't force people to pay money to make you leave them alone. Be satisfied with your pay.”  
**3:15** Everyone became excited and wondered, “Could John be the Messiah?”  
**3:16** John said, “I am just baptizing with water. But someone more powerful is going to come, and I am not good enough even to untie his sandals.<sup>①</sup> He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>untie his sandals: </i>This was the duty of a slave.  
**3:17** His threshing fork<sup>①</sup> is in his hand, and he is ready to separate the wheat from the husks. He will store the wheat in his barn and burn the husks with a fire that never goes out.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>threshing fork: </i>After Jewish farmers had trampled out the grain, they used a large fork to pitch the grain and the husks into the air. Wind would blow away the light husks, and the grain would fall back to the ground, where it could be gathered up.  
**3:18** In many different ways John preached the good news to the people.  
**3:19**  But to Herod the ruler, he said, “It was wrong for you to take Herodias, your brother's wife.” John also said Herod had done many other bad things.  
**3:20** Finally, Herod put John in jail, and this was the worst thing he had done.  
**3:21** While everyone else was being baptized, Jesus himself was baptized. Then as he prayed, the sky opened up,  
**3:22**  and the Holy Spirit came down upon him in the form of a dove. A voice from heaven said, “You are my own dear Son, and I am pleased with you.”  
**3:23** When Jesus began to preach, he was about 30 years old. Everyone thought he was the son of Joseph. But his family went back through Heli,  
**3:24** Matthat, Levi, Melchi, Jannai, Joseph,  
**3:25** Mattathias, Amos, Nahum, Esli, Naggai,  
**3:26** Maath, Mattathias, Semein, Josech, Joda;  
**3:27** Joanan, Rhesa, Zerubbabel, Shealtiel, Neri,  
**3:28** Melchi, Addi, Cosam, Elmadam, Er,  
**3:29** Joshua, Eliezer, Jorim, Matthat, Levi;  
**3:30** Simeon, Judah, Joseph, Jonam, Eliakim,  
**3:31** Melea, Menna, Mattatha, Nathan, David,  
**3:32** Jesse, Obed, Boaz, Salmon, Nahshon;  
**3:33** Amminadab, Admin, Arni, Hezron, Perez, Judah,  
**3:34** Jacob, Isaac, Abraham, Terah, Nahor,  
**3:35** Serug, Reu, Peleg, Eber, Shelah;  
**3:36** Cainan, Arphaxad, Shem, Noah, Lamech,  
**3:37** Methuselah, Enoch, Jared, Mahalaleel, Kenan,  
**3:38** Enosh, and Seth. The family of Jesus went all the way back to Adam and then to God.  

## 4장

**4:1** When Jesus returned from the Jordan River, the power of the Holy Spirit was with him, and the Spirit led him into the desert.  
**4:2** For 40 days Jesus was tested by the devil, and during that time he went without eating.<sup>①</sup> When it was all over, he was hungry.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>went without eating: </i>See the <a name='C490 2:37'>note at <a name='B:490 2:37'>2.37.  
**4:3** The devil said to Jesus, “If you are God's Son, tell this stone to turn into bread.”  
**4:4**  Jesus answered, <J>“The Scriptures say, ‘No one can live only on food.’ ”</J>  
**4:5** Then the devil led Jesus up to a high place and quickly showed him all the nations on earth.  
**4:6** The devil said, “I will give all this power and glory to you. It has been given to me, and I can give it to anyone I want to.  
**4:7** Just worship me, and you can have it all.”  
**4:8**  Jesus answered, <J>“The Scriptures say:<br>‘Worship the Lord your God<br>and serve only him!’ ”</J>  
**4:9** Finally, the devil took Jesus to Jerusalem and had him stand on top of the temple. The devil said, “If you are God's Son, jump off.  
**4:10**  The Scriptures say:<br>‘God will tell his angels<br>to take care of you.<br>They will catch you<br>in their arms,<br>and you will not even hurt<br>your feet on the stones.’ ”  
**4:11**   
**4:12**  Jesus answered, <J>“The Scriptures also say, ‘Don't try to test the Lord your God!’ ”</J>  
**4:13** After the devil had finished testing Jesus in every way possible, he left him for a while.  
**4:14** Jesus returned to Galilee with the power of the Spirit. News about him spread everywhere.  
**4:15** He taught in the Jewish synagogues, and everyone praised him.  
**4:16** Jesus went back to Nazareth, where he had been brought up, and as usual he went to the synagogue on the Sabbath. When he stood up to read from the Scriptures,  
**4:17** he was given the book of Isaiah the prophet. He opened it and read,  
**4:18**  <J>“The Lord's Spirit<br>has come to me,<br>because he has chosen me<br>to tell the good news<br>to the poor.<br>The Lord has sent me<br>to announce freedom<br>for prisoners,<br>to give sight to the blind,<br>to free everyone<br>who suffers,</J>  
**4:19** <J>and to say, ‘This is the year<br>the Lord has chosen.’ ”</J>  
**4:20** Jesus closed the book, then handed it back to the man in charge and sat down. Everyone in the synagogue looked straight at Jesus.  
**4:21** Then Jesus said to them, <J>“What you have just heard me read has come true today.”</J>  
**4:22** All the people started talking about Jesus and were amazed at the wonderful things he said. They kept on asking, “Isn't he Joseph's son?”  
**4:23** Jesus answered: <J>You will certainly want to tell me this saying, “Doctor, first make yourself well.” You will tell me to do the same things here in my own hometown that you heard I did in Capernaum.</J>  
**4:24**  <J>But you can be sure that no prophets are liked by the people of their own hometown.</J>  
**4:25**  <J>Once during the time of Elijah there was no rain for three and a half years, and people everywhere were starving. There were many widows in Israel,</J>  
**4:26**  <J>but Elijah was sent only to a widow in the town of Zarephath near the city of Sidon.</J>  
**4:27**  <J>During the time of the prophet Elisha, many men in Israel had leprosy.<sup>①</sup> But no one was healed, except Naaman who lived in Syria.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>leprosy: </i>In biblical times the word “leprosy” was used for many different kinds of skin diseases.  
**4:28** When the people in the synagogue heard Jesus say this, they became so angry  
**4:29** that they got up and threw him out of town. They dragged him to the edge of the cliff on which the town was built, because they wanted to throw him down from there.  
**4:30** But Jesus slipped through the crowd and got away.  
**4:31** Jesus went to the town of Capernaum in Galilee and taught the people on the Sabbath.  
**4:32**  His teaching amazed them because he spoke with power.  
**4:33** There in the synagogue was a man with an evil spirit. He yelled out,

## 14장

**14:1** One Sabbath, Jesus was having dinner in the home of an important Pharisee, and everyone was carefully watching Jesus.  
**14:2** All of a sudden a man with swollen legs stood up in front of him.  
**14:3** Jesus turned and asked the Pharisees and the teachers of the Law of Moses, <J>“Is it right to heal on the Sabbath?”</J>  
**14:4** But they did not say a word. Jesus took hold of the man. Then he healed him and sent him away.  
**14:5**  Afterwards, Jesus asked the people, <J>“If your son or ox falls into a well, wouldn't you pull him out at once, even on the Sabbath?”</J>  
**14:6** There was nothing they could say.

**14:9** <J>Then the one who invited you will come and say, “Give your place to this other guest!” You will be embarrassed and will have to sit in the worst place.</J>  
**14:10** <J>When you are invited to be a guest, go and sit in the worst place. Then the one who invited you may come and say, “My friend, take a better seat!” You will then be honored in front of all the other guests.</J>  
**14:11**  <J>If you put yourself above others, you will be put down. But if you humble yourself, you will be honored.</J>

**14:17** <J>When the banquet was ready, he sent a servant to tell the guests, “Everything is ready! Please come.”</J>

**14:19** <J>Another guest said, “I bought five teams of oxen, and I need to try them out. Please excuse me.”</J>  
**14:20** <J>Still another guest said, “I've just now married, and I can't be there.”</J>  
**14:21** <J>The servant told his master what happened, and the master became so angry he said, “Go as fast as you can to every street and alley in town! Bring in everyone who is poor or paralyzed or blind or lame.”</J>  
**14:22** <J>When the servant returned, he said, “Master, I've done what you told me, and there is still plenty of room for more people.”</J>  
**14:23** <J>His master then told him, “Go out along the back roads and make people come in, so my house will be full.</J>

**14:25** Large crowds were walking along with Jesus, when he turned and said:  
**14:26**  <J>You cannot be my disciple, unless you love me more than you love your father and mother, your wife and children, and your brothers and sisters. You cannot follow me unless you love me more than you love your own life.</J>  
**14:27**  <J>You cannot be my disciple unless you carry your own cross and follow me.</J>  
**14:28** <J>Suppose one of you wants to build a tower. What is the first thing you will do? Won't you sit down and figure out how much it will cost and if you have enough money to pay for it?</J>  
**14:29** <J>Otherwise, you will start building the tower, but not be able to finish. Then everyone who sees what is happening will laugh at you.</J>  
**14:30** <J>They will say, “You started building, but could not finish the job.”</J>  
**14:31** <J>What will a king do if he has only 10,000 soldiers to defend himself against a king who is about to attack him with 20,000 soldiers? Before he goes out to battle, won't he first sit down and decide if he can win?</J>  
**14:32** <J>If he thinks he won't be able to defend himself, he will send messengers and ask for peace while the other king is still a long way off.</J>  
**14:33** <J>So then, you cannot be my disciple unless you give away everything you own.</J>  
**14:34** <J>Salt is good, but if it no longer tastes like salt, how can it be made to taste salty again?</J>  
**14:35** <J>It is no longer good for the soil or even for the manure pile. People simply throw it out. If you have ears, pay attention!</J>

## 20장

**20:5** They talked this over and said to each other, “We can't say God gave John this right. Jesus will ask us why we didn't believe John.  
**20:6** And we can't say it was merely some human who gave John the right to baptize. The crowd will stone us to death, because they think John was a prophet.”  
**20:7** So they told Jesus, “We don't know who gave John the right to baptize.”  
**20:8** Jesus replied, <J>“Then I won't tell you who gave me the right to do what I do.”</J>  
**20:9**  Jesus told the people this story: <J>A man once planted a vineyard and rented it out. Then he left the country for a long time.</J>

**20:11** <J>So the owner sent another servant. The renters also beat him up. They insulted him terribly and sent him away without a thing.</J>  
**20:12** <J>The owner sent a third servant. He was also beaten terribly and thrown out of the vineyard.</J>

**20:14** <J>When the renters saw the owner's son, they said to one another, “Someday he will own the vineyard. Let's kill him! Then we can have it all for ourselves.”</J>  
**20:15** <J>So they threw him out of the vineyard and killed him. Jesus asked, “What do you think the owner of the vineyard will do?</J>

**20:21** The spies said to him, “Teacher, we know you teach the truth about what God wants people to do. And you treat everyone with the same respect, no matter who they are.  
**20:22** Tell us, should we pay taxes to the Emperor or not?”  
**20:23** Jesus knew they were trying to trick him. So he told them,  
**20:24** <J>“Show me a coin.”</J> Then he asked, <J>“Whose picture and name are on it?”</J> “The Emperor's,” they answered.  
**20:25** Then he told them, <J>“Give the Emperor what belongs to him and give God what belongs to God.”</J>  
**20:26** Jesus' enemies could not catch him saying anything wrong there in front of the people. They were amazed at his answer and kept quiet.

**20:30** The second one married his brother's widow, and he also died without having any children.

**20:40** From then on, no one dared to ask Jesus any questions.  
**20:41** Jesus asked, <J>“Why do people say that the Messiah will be the son of King David?</J><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>the son of King David: </i>See the <a name='C490 18:38'>note at <a name='B:490 18:38'>18.38.

**20:45** While everyone was listening to Jesus, he said to his disciples:  
**20:46** <J>Guard against the teachers of the Law of Moses! They love to walk around in long robes, and they like to be greeted in the market. They want the front seats in the synagogues and the best seats at banquets.</J>  
**20:47** <J>But they cheat widows out of their homes and then pray long prayers just to show off. These teachers will be punished most of all.</J>  

## 21장

**21:22**  <J>This time of punishment is what is written about in the Scriptures.</J>

**21:38** Everyone got up early and came to the temple to hear him teach.

**22:28** <J>You have stayed with me in all my troubles.</J>

## 23장

**23:4** Pilate told the chief priests and the crowd, “I don't find him guilty of anything.”  
**23:5** But they all kept on saying, “He has been teaching and causing trouble all over Judea. He started in Galilee and has now come all the way here.”  
**23:6** When Pilate heard this, he asked, “Is this man from Galilee?”

**23:10** Then the chief priests and the teachers of the Law of Moses stood up and accused him of all kinds of bad things.  
**23:11** Herod and his soldiers made fun of Jesus and insulted him. They put a fine robe on him and sent him back to Pilate.

**23:17**

**23:20** Pilate wanted to set Jesus free, so he spoke again to the crowds.  
**23:21** But they kept shouting, “Nail him to a cross! Nail him to a cross!”
