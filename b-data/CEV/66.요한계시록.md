# 66.요한계시록 (CEV)



## 1장

**1:1** This is what God showed to Jesus Christ, so that he could tell his servants what must happen soon. Christ then sent his angel with the message to his servant John.  
**1:2** And John told everything he had seen about God's message and about what Jesus Christ had said and done.  
**1:3** God will bless everyone who reads this prophecy to others,<sup>①</sup> and he will bless everyone who hears and obeys it. The time is almost here.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>who reads this prophecy to others: </i>A public reading, in a worship service.  
**1:4**  From John to the seven churches in Asia.<sup>①</sup><br>I pray that you<br>will be blessed<br>with kindness and peace<br>from God, who is and was<br>and is coming.<br>May you receive<br>kindness and peace<br>from the seven spirits<br>before the throne of God.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Asia: </i>The section <a name='B:730 1:4-3:22'>1.4-3.22 is in the form of a letter. Asia was in the eastern part of the Roman Empire and is present day Turkey.  
**1:5**  May kindness and peace<br>be yours<br>from Jesus Christ,<br>the faithful witness. Jesus was the first<br>to conquer death,<br>and he is the ruler<br>of all earthly kings.<br>Christ loves us,<br>and by his blood<br>he set us free<br>from our sins.  
**1:6**  He lets us rule as kings<br>and serve God his Father<br>as priests.<br>To him be glory and power<br>forever and ever! Amen.  
**1:7**  Look! He is coming<br>with the clouds.<br>Everyone will see him,<br>even the ones who stuck<br>a sword through him.<br>All people on earth<br>will weep because of him.<br>Yes, it will happen! Amen.  
**1:8**  The Lord God says, <J>“I am Alpha and Omega,<sup>①</sup> the one who is and was and is coming. I am God All-Powerful!”</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Alpha and Omega: </i>The first and last letters of the Greek alphabet, which sometimes mean “first” and “last.”  
**1:9** I am John, a follower together with all of you. We suffer because Jesus is our king, but he gives us the strength to endure. I was sent to Patmos Island,<sup>①</sup> because I had preached God's message and had told about Jesus.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Patmos Island: </i>A small island where prisoners were sometimes kept by the Romans.  
**1:10** On the Lord's day the Spirit took control of me, and behind me I heard a loud voice that sounded like a trumpet.  
**1:11** The voice said, <J>“Write in a book what you see. Then send it to the seven churches in Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea.”</J><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Ephesus … Laodicea: </i>Ephesus was in the center with the six other cities forming a half-circle around it.  
**1:12** When I turned to see who was speaking to me, I saw seven gold lampstands.  
**1:13**  There with the lampstands was someone who seemed to be the Son of Man.<sup>①</sup> He was wearing a robe that reached down to his feet, and a gold cloth was wrapped around his chest.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Son of Man: </i>That is, Jesus.  
**1:14**   His head and his hair were white as wool or snow, and his eyes looked like flames of fire.  
**1:15**  His feet were glowing like bronze being heated in a furnace, and his voice sounded like the roar of a waterfall.  
**1:16** He held seven stars in his right hand, and a sharp double-edged sword was coming from his mouth. His face was shining as bright as the sun at noon.  
**1:17**  When I saw him, I fell at his feet like a dead person. But he put his right hand on me and said: <J>Don't be afraid! I am the first, the last,</J>  
**1:18** <J>and the living one. I died, but now I am alive forever, and I have the keys to death and the world of the dead.</J><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>keys to death and the world of the dead: </i>That is, power over death and the world of the dead.  
**1:19** <J>Write what you have seen and what is and what will happen after these things.</J>  
**1:20** <J>I will explain the mystery of the seven stars you saw at my right side and the seven gold lampstands. The seven stars are the angels<sup>①</sup> of the seven churches, and the lampstands are the seven churches.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>angels: </i>Perhaps guardian angels that represent the churches, or they may be church leaders or messengers sent to the churches.  

## 2장

**2:1** <J>This is what you must write to the angel of the church in Ephesus: I am the one who holds the seven stars in my right hand, and I walk among the seven gold lampstands. Listen to what I say.</J>  
**2:2** <J>I know everything you have done, including your hard work and how you have endured. I know you won't put up with anyone who is evil. When some people pretended to be apostles, you tested them and found out they were liars.</J>  
**2:3** <J>You have endured and gone through hard times because of me, yet you have not given up.</J>  
**2:4** <J>But I do have something against you! And it is this: You don't have as much love as you used to.</J>  
**2:5** <J>Think about where you have fallen from, and then turn back and do as you did at first. If you don't turn back, I will come and take away your lampstand.</J>  
**2:6** <J>But there is one thing you are doing right. You hate what the Nicolaitans<sup>①</sup> are doing, and so do I.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Nicolaitans: </i>Nothing else is known about these people, though it is possible that they claimed to be followers of Nicolaus from Antioch (see <a name='B:510 6:5'>Acts 6.5).  
**2:7**  <J>If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit says to the churches. I will let everyone who wins the victory eat from the life-giving tree in God's wonderful garden.</J>  
**2:8**  <J>This is what you must write to the angel of the church in Smyrna: I am the first and the last. I died, but now I am alive! Listen to what I say.</J>  
**2:9** <J>I know how much you suffer and how poor you are, but you are rich. I also know the cruel things being said about you by people who claim to be God's people. But they are not really God's people. They are a group that belongs to Satan.</J>  
**2:10** <J>Don't worry about what you will suffer. The devil will throw some of you into jail, and you will be tested and made to suffer for ten days. But if you are faithful until you die, I will reward you with a glorious life.</J><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>a glorious life: </i>The Greek text has “a crown of life.” In ancient times an athlete who had won a contest was rewarded with a crown of flowers as a sign of victory.  
**2:11**  <J>If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit says to the churches. Whoever wins the victory will not be hurt by the second death.</J><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>second death: </i>The first death is physical death, and the “second death” is eternal death.  
**2:12** <J>This is what you must write to the angel of the church in Pergamum: I am the one who has the sharp double-edged sword! Listen to what I say.</J>  
**2:13** <J>I know you live where Satan has his throne.<sup>①</sup> But you have kept true to my name. Right there where Satan lives, my faithful witness Antipas<sup>②</sup> was taken from you and put to death. Even then you did not give up your faith in me.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>where Satan has his throne: </i>The meaning is uncertain, but it may refer to the city as a center of pagan worship or of Emperor worship. <sup>②</sup><i>Antipas: </i>Nothing else is known about this man, who is mentioned only here in the New Testament.  
**2:14**  <J>I do have a few things against you. Some of you are following the teaching of Balaam.<sup>①</sup> Long ago he told Balak to teach the people of Israel to eat food that had been offered to idols and to be immoral.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Balaam: </i>According to <a name='B:40 22:1-24:1'>Numbers 22-24, Balaam refused to disobey the Lord. But in other books of the Old Testament, he is spoken of as evil (see <a name='B:50 23:4'>Deuteronomy 23.4,<a name='B:50 23:5'>5; <a name='B:60 13:22'>Joshua 13.22; <a name='B:60 24:9'>24.9,<a name='B:60 24:10'>10; <a name='B:160 13:2'>Nehemiah 13.2).  
**2:15** <J>Now some of you are following the teaching of the Nicolaitans.</J><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Nicolaitans: </i>See the <a name='C730 2:6'>note at <a name='B:730 2:6'>2.6.  
**2:16** <J>Turn back! If you don't, I will come quickly and fight against these people. And my words will cut like a sword.</J>  
**2:17**  <J>If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit says to the churches. To everyone who wins the victory, I will give some of the hidden food.<sup>①</sup> I will also give each one a white stone<sup>②</sup> with a new name<sup>③</sup> written on it. No one will know that name except the one who is given the stone.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>hidden food: </i>When the people of Israel were going through the desert, the Lord provided a special food for them. Some of this was placed in a jar and stored in the sacred chest (see <a name='B:20 16:1'>Exodus 16). According to later Jewish teaching, the prophet Jeremiah rescued the sacred chest when the temple was destroyed by the Babylonians. He hid the chest in a cave, where it would stay until God came to save his people. <sup>②</sup><i>white stone: </i>The meaning of this is uncertain, though it may be the same as a ticket that lets a person into God's banquet where the “hidden food” is eaten. Or it may be a symbol of victory. <sup>③</sup><i>a new name: </i>Either the name of Christ or God or the name of the follower who is given the stone.  
**2:18** <J>This is what you must write to the angel of the church in Thyatira: I am the Son of God! My eyes are like flames of fire, and my feet are like bronze. Listen to what I say.</J>  
**2:19** <J>I know everything about you, including your love, your faith, your service, and how you have endured. I know you are doing more now than you have ever done before.</J>  
**2:20**  <J>But I still have something against you because of that woman Jezebel.<sup>①</sup> She calls herself a prophet, and you let her teach and mislead my servants to do immoral things and to eat food offered to idols.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Jezebel: </i>Nothing else is known about her. This may have been her real name or a name that was given to her because she was like Queen Jezebel, who opposed the Lord (see <a name='B:110 19:1'>1 Kings 19.1,<a name='B:110 19:2'>2; <a name='B:110 21:1-26'>21.1-26).  
**2:21** <J>I gave her a chance to turn from her sins, but she did not want to stop doing these immoral things.</J>  
**2:22** <J>I am going to strike down Jezebel. Everyone who does these immoral things with her will also be punished, if they don't stop.</J>  
**2:23**  <J>I will even kill her followers.<sup>①</sup> Then all the churches will see that I know everyone's thoughts and feelings. I will treat each of you as you deserve.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>her followers: </i>Or “her children.”  
**2:24** <J>Some of you in Thyatira don't follow Jezebel's teaching. You don't know anything about what her followers call the “deep secrets of Satan.” So I won't burden you down with any other commands.</J>  
**2:25** <J>But until I come, you must hold firmly to the teaching you have.</J>  
**2:26**  <J>I will give power over the nations to everyone who wins the victory and keeps on obeying me until the end.</J>  
**2:27** <J>I will give each of them the same power my Father has given me. They will rule the nations with an iron rod and smash those nations to pieces like clay pots. I will also give them the morning star.<sup>①</sup> </J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>the morning star: </i>Probably thought of as the star that signals the end of night and the beginning of day. In <a name='B:730 22:16'>22.16 Christ is called the “morning star.”

## 4장

**4:1** After this, I looked and saw a door that opened into heaven. Then the voice that had spoken to me at first and that sounded like a trumpet said, <J>“Come up here! I will show you what must happen next.”</J>  
**4:2**  Right then the Spirit took control of me, and there in heaven I saw a throne and someone sitting on it.  
**4:3** The one who was sitting there sparkled like precious stones of jasper<sup>①</sup> and carnelian.<sup>②</sup> A rainbow that looked like an emerald<sup>③</sup> surrounded the throne.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>jasper: </i>Usually green or clear. <sup>②</sup><i>carnelian: </i>Usually deep-red or reddish-white. <sup>③</sup><i>emerald: </i>A precious stone, usually green.  
**4:4** Twenty-four other thrones were in a circle around that throne. And on each of these thrones there was an elder dressed in white clothes and wearing a gold crown.  
**4:5**  Flashes of lightning and roars of thunder came out from the throne in the center of the circle. Seven torches, which are the seven spirits of God, were burning in front of the throne.  
**4:6**   Also in front of the throne was something that looked like a glass sea, clear as crystal. Around the throne in the center were four living creatures covered front and back with eyes.  
**4:7** The first creature was like a lion, the second one was like a bull, the third one had the face of a human, and the fourth was like a flying eagle.  
**4:8**  Each of the four living creatures had six wings, and their bodies were covered with eyes. Day and night they never stopped singing,<br>“Holy, holy, holy is the Lord,<br>the all-powerful God,<br>who was and is<br>and is coming!”  
**4:9** The living creatures kept praising, honoring, and thanking the one who sits on the throne and who lives forever and ever.  
**4:10** At the same time the 24 elders knelt down before the one sitting on the throne. And as they worshiped the one who lives forever, they placed their crowns in front of the throne and said,  
**4:11**  “Our Lord and God,<br>you are worthy<br>to receive glory,<br>honor, and power.<br>You created all things,<br>and by your decision they are<br>and were created.”  

## 5장

**5:1**  In the right hand of the one sitting on the throne I saw a scroll<sup>①</sup> that had writing on the inside and on the outside. And it was sealed in seven places.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>scroll: </i>A roll of paper or special leather used for writing on. Sometimes a scroll would be sealed on the outside with one or more pieces of wax.  
**5:2** I saw a mighty angel ask with a loud voice, “Who is worthy to open the scroll and break its seals?”  
**5:3** No one in heaven or on earth or under the earth was able to open the scroll or look inside it.  
**5:4** I cried hard because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or look inside it.  
**5:5**  Then one of the elders said to me, “Stop crying and look! The one who is called both the ‘Lion from the Tribe of Judah’<sup>①</sup> and ‘King David's Great Descendant’<sup>②</sup> has won the victory. He will open the scroll and its seven seals.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>‘Lion from the Tribe of Judah’: </i>In <a name='B:10 49:9'>Genesis 49.9 the tribe of Judah is called a young lion, and King David was from Judah. <sup>②</sup><i>‘King David's Great Descendant’: </i>The Greek text has “the root of David” which is a title for the Messiah based on <a name='B:290 11:1'>Isaiah 11.1,<a name='B:290 11:10'>10.  
**5:6**  Then I looked and saw a Lamb standing in the center of the throne surrounded by the four living creatures and the elders. The Lamb looked as if it had once been killed. It had seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven spirits<sup>①</sup> of God, sent out to all the earth.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>the seven spirits: </i>Some manuscripts have “the spirits.”  
**5:7** The Lamb went over and took the scroll from the right hand of the one who sat on the throne.  
**5:8**  After he had taken it, the four living creatures and the 24 elders knelt down before him. Each of them had a harp and a gold bowl full of incense,<sup>①</sup> which are the prayers of God's people.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>incense: </i>A material that produces a sweet smell when burned. Sometimes it is a symbol for the prayers of God's people.  
**5:9**  Then they sang a new song,<br>“You are worthy<br>to receive the scroll<br>and open its seals,<br>because you were killed.<br>And with your own blood<br>you bought for God<br>people from every tribe,<br>language, nation, and race.  
**5:10**  You let them become kings<br>and serve God as priests,<br>and they will rule on earth.”  
**5:11**  As I looked, I heard the voices of a large number of angels around the throne and the voices of the living creatures and of the elders. There were millions and millions of them,  
**5:12** and they were saying in a loud voice,<br>“The Lamb who was killed<br>is worthy to receive power,<br>riches, wisdom, strength,<br>honor, glory, and praise.”  
**5:13** Then I heard all beings in heaven and on the earth and under the earth and in the sea offer praise. Together, all of them were saying,<br>“Praise, honor, glory,<br>and strength<br>forever and ever<br>to the one who sits<br>on the throne<br>and to the Lamb!”  
**5:14** The four living creatures said “Amen,” while the elders knelt down and worshiped.  

## 6장

**6:1** At the same time I saw the Lamb open the first of the seven seals, I heard one of the four living creatures shout with a voice like thunder. It said, “Come out!”  
**6:2**  Then I saw a white horse. Its rider carried a bow and was given a crown. He had already won some victories, and he went out to win more.  
**6:3** When the Lamb opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature say, “Come out!”  
**6:4**  Then another horse came out. It was fiery red. And its rider was given the power to take away all peace from the earth, so people would slaughter one another. He was also given a big sword.  
**6:5**  When the Lamb opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature say, “Come out!” Then I saw a black horse, and its rider had a balance scale in one hand.  
**6:6** I heard what sounded like a voice from somewhere among the four living creatures. It said, “A liter of wheat will cost you a whole day's wages! Three liters of barley will cost you a day's wages too. But don't ruin the olive oil or the wine.”  
**6:7** When the Lamb opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature say, “Come out!”  
**6:8**  Then I saw a pale green horse. Its rider was named Death, and Death's Kingdom followed close behind. They were given power over one fourth of the earth, and they could kill its people with swords, famines, diseases, and wild animals.  
**6:9** When the Lamb opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of everyone who had been killed for speaking God's message and telling about their faith.  
**6:10** They shouted, “Master, you are holy and faithful! How long will it be before you judge and punish the people of this earth who killed us?”  
**6:11**  Then each of those who had been killed was given a white robe and told to rest for a little while. They had to wait until the complete number of the Lord's other servants and followers would be killed.  
**6:12**  When I saw the Lamb open the sixth seal, I looked and saw a great earthquake. The sun turned as dark as sackcloth,<sup>①</sup> and the moon became red as blood.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>sackcloth: </i>A rough, dark-colored cloth made from goat or camel hair and used to make grain sacks. It was worn in times of trouble or sorrow.  
**6:13**  The stars in the sky fell to earth, just like figs shaken loose by a windstorm.  
**6:14**  Then the sky was rolled up like a scroll,<sup>①</sup> and all mountains and islands were moved from their places.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>scroll: </i>See the <a name='C730 5:1'>note at <a name='B:730 5:1'>5.1.  
**6:15**  The kings of the earth, its famous people, and its military leaders hid in caves or behind rocks on the mountains. They hid there together with the rich and the powerful and with all the slaves and free people.  
**6:16**  Then they shouted to the mountains and the rocks, “Fall on us! Hide us from the one who sits on the throne and from the anger of the Lamb.  
**6:17**  That terrible day has come! God and the Lamb will show their anger, and who can face it?”

**7:2**

**7:4** Then I heard how many people had been marked on the forehead. There were 144,000, and they came from every tribe of Israel:  
**7:5** 12,000 from Judah,<br>12,000 from Reuben,<br>12,000 from Gad,  
**7:6** 12,000 from Asher,<br>12,000 from Naphtali,<br>12,000 from Manasseh,
