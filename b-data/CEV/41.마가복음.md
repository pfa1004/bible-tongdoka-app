# 41.마가복음 (CEV)



## 1장

**1:1** This is the good news about Jesus Christ, the Son of God.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>the Son of God: </i>These words are not in some manuscripts.  
**1:2**  It began just as God had said in the book written by Isaiah the prophet,<br>“I am sending my messenger<br>to get the way ready<br>for you.  
**1:3**  In the desert<br>someone is shouting,<br>‘Get the road ready<br>for the Lord!<br>Make a straight path<br>for him.’ ”  
**1:4** So John the Baptist showed up in the desert and told everyone, “Turn back to God and be baptized! Then your sins will be forgiven.”  
**1:5** From all Judea and Jerusalem crowds of people went to John. They told how sorry they were for their sins, and he baptized them in the Jordan River.  
**1:6**  John wore clothes made of camel's hair. He had a leather strap around his waist and ate grasshoppers and wild honey.  
**1:7** John also told the people, “Someone more powerful is going to come. And I am not good enough even to stoop down and untie his sandals.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>untie his sandals: </i>This was the duty of a slave.  
**1:8** I baptize you with water, but he will baptize you with the Holy Spirit!”  
**1:9** About that time Jesus came from Nazareth in Galilee, and John baptized him in the Jordan River.  
**1:10** As soon as Jesus came out of the water, he saw the sky open and the Holy Spirit coming down to him like a dove.  
**1:11**  A voice from heaven said, “You are my own dear Son, and I am pleased with you.”  
**1:12** At once God's Spirit made Jesus go into the desert.  
**1:13** He stayed there for 40 days while Satan tested him. Jesus was with the wild animals, but angels took care of him.  
**1:14** After John was arrested, Jesus went to Galilee and told the good news that comes from God.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>that comes from God: </i>Or “that is about God.”  
**1:15**  He said, <J>“The time has come! God's kingdom will soon be here.<sup>①</sup> Turn back to God and believe the good news!”</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>will soon be here: </i>Or “is already here.”  
**1:16** As Jesus was walking along the shore of Lake Galilee, he saw Simon and his brother Andrew. They were fishermen and were casting their nets into the lake.  
**1:17** Jesus said to them, <J>“Follow me! I will teach you how to bring in people instead of fish.”</J>  
**1:18** Right then the two brothers dropped their nets and went with him.  
**1:19** Jesus walked on and soon saw James and John, the sons of Zebedee. They were in a boat, mending their nets.  
**1:20** At once Jesus asked them to come with him. They left their father in the boat with the hired workers and went with him.  
**1:21** Jesus and his disciples went to the town of Capernaum. Then on the next Sabbath he went into the synagogue and started teaching.  
**1:22**  Everyone was amazed at his teaching. He taught with authority, and not like the teachers of the Law of Moses.  
**1:23** Suddenly a man with an evil spirit<sup>①</sup> in him entered the synagogue and yelled,<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>evil spirit: </i>A Jewish person who had an evil spirit was considered “unclean” and was not allowed to eat or worship with other Jewish people.  
**1:24** “Jesus from Nazareth, what do you want with us? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are! You are God's Holy One.”  
**1:25** Jesus told the evil spirit, <J>“Be quiet and come out of the man!”</J>  
**1:26** The spirit shook him. Then it gave a loud shout and left.  
**1:27** Everyone was completely surprised and kept saying to each other, “What is this? It must be some new kind of powerful teaching! Even the evil spirits obey him.”  
**1:28** News about Jesus quickly spread all over Galilee.  
**1:29** As soon as Jesus left the synagogue with James and John, they went home with Simon and Andrew.  
**1:30** When they got there, Jesus was told that Simon's mother-in-law was sick in bed with fever.  
**1:31** Jesus went to her. He took hold of her hand and helped her up. The fever left her, and she served them a meal.  
**1:32** That evening after sunset,<sup>①</sup> all who were sick or had demons in them were brought to Jesus.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>after sunset: </i>The Sabbath was over, and a new day began at sunset.  
**1:33** In fact, the whole town gathered around the door of the house.  
**1:34** Jesus healed all kinds of terrible diseases and forced out a lot of demons. But the demons knew who he was, and he did not let them speak.  
**1:35** Very early the next morning before daylight, Jesus got up and went to a place where he could be alone and pray.  
**1:36** Simon and the others started looking for him.  
**1:37** And when they found him, they said, “Everyone is looking for you!”  
**1:38** Jesus replied, <J>“We must go to the nearby towns, so that I can tell the good news to those people. This is why I have come.”</J>  
**1:39**  Then Jesus went to their synagogues everywhere in Galilee, where he preached and forced out demons.  
**1:40** A man with leprosy<sup>①</sup> came to Jesus and knelt down.<sup>②</sup> He begged, “You have the power to make me well, if only you wanted to.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>leprosy: </i>In biblical times the word “leprosy” was used for many different kinds of skin diseases. <sup>②</sup><i>and knelt down: </i>These words are not in some manuscripts.  
**1:41** Jesus felt sorry for<sup>①</sup> the man. So he put his hand on him and said, <J>“I want to! Now you are well.”</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>felt sorry for: </i>Some manuscripts have “was angry with.”  
**1:42** At once the man's leprosy disappeared, and he was well.  
**1:43** After Jesus strictly warned the man, he sent him on his way.  
**1:44**  He said, <J>“Don't tell anyone about this. Just go and show the priest that you are well. Then take a gift to the temple as Moses commanded, and everyone will know that you have been healed.”</J><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>everyone will know that you have been healed: </i>People with leprosy had to be examined by a priest and told that they were well (that is, “clean”) before they could once again live a normal life in the Jewish community. The gift that Moses commanded was the sacrifice of some lambs together with flour mixed with olive oil.  
**1:45** The man talked about it so much and told so many people, that Jesus could no longer go openly into a town. He had to stay away from the towns, but people still came to him from everywhere.  

## 2장

**2:1** Jesus went back to Capernaum, and a few days later people heard that he was at home.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>at home: </i>Or “in the house” (perhaps Simon Peter's home).  
**2:2** Then so many of them came to the house that there wasn't even standing room left in front of the door. Jesus was still teaching  
**2:3** when four people came up, carrying a man on a mat because he could not walk.  
**2:4** But because of the crowd, they could not get him to Jesus. So they made a hole in the roof<sup>①</sup> above him and let the man down in front of everyone.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>roof: </i>In Palestine the houses usually had a flat roof. Stairs on the outside led up to the roof that was made of beams and boards covered with packed earth.  
**2:5** When Jesus saw how much faith they had, he said to the man, <J>“My friend, your sins are forgiven.”</J>  
**2:6** Some of the teachers of the Law of Moses were sitting there. They started wondering,  
**2:7** “Why would he say such a thing? He must think he is God! Only God can forgive sins.”  
**2:8** At once, Jesus knew what they were thinking, and he said, <J>“Why are you thinking such things?</J>  
**2:9** <J>Is it easier for me to tell this man his sins are forgiven or to tell him to get up and pick up his mat and go on home?</J>  
**2:10** <J>I will show you that the Son of Man has the right to forgive sins here on earth.”</J> So Jesus said to the man,  
**2:11** <J>“Get up! Pick up your mat and go on home.”</J>  
**2:12** The man got right up. He picked up his mat and went out while everyone watched in amazement. They praised God and said, “We have never seen anything like this!”  
**2:13** Once again, Jesus went to the shore of Lake Galilee. A large crowd gathered around him, and he taught them.  
**2:14** As he walked along, he saw Levi, the son of Alphaeus. Levi was sitting at the place for paying taxes, and Jesus said to him, <J>“Follow me!”</J> So he got up and went with Jesus.  
**2:15** Later, Jesus and his disciples were having dinner at Levi's house.<sup>①</sup> Many tax collectors<sup>②</sup> and other sinners had become followers of Jesus, and they were also guests at the dinner.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Levi's house: </i>Or “Jesus' house.” <sup>②</sup><i>tax collectors: </i>These were usually Jewish people who paid the Romans for the right to collect taxes. They were hated by other Jews who thought of them as traitors to their country and to their religion.  
**2:16** Some of the teachers of the Law of Moses were Pharisees, and they saw Jesus eating with sinners and tax collectors. So they asked his disciples, “Why does he eat with tax collectors and sinners?”  
**2:17** Jesus heard them and answered, <J>“Healthy people don't need a doctor, but sick people do. I didn't come to invite good people to be my followers. I came to invite sinners.”</J>  
**2:18** The followers of John the Baptist and the Pharisees often went without eating.<sup>①</sup> Some people came and asked Jesus, “Why do the followers of John and those of the Pharisees often go without eating, while your disciples never do?”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>without eating: </i>The Jewish people sometimes went without eating (also called “fasting”) to show their love for God or to show sorrow for their sins.  
**2:19** Jesus answered: <J>The friends of a bridegroom don't go without eating while he is still with them.</J>  
**2:20** <J>But the time will come when he will be taken from them. Then they will go without eating.</J>  
**2:21** <J>No one patches old clothes by sewing on a piece of new cloth. The new piece would shrink and tear a bigger hole.</J>  
**2:22** <J>No one pours new wine into old wineskins. The wine would swell and burst the old skins.<sup>①</sup> Then the wine would be lost, and the skins would be ruined. New wine must be put into new wineskins.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>swell and burst the old skins: </i>While the juice from grapes was becoming wine, it would swell and stretch the skins in which it had been stored. If the skins were old and stiff, they would burst.  
**2:23**  One Sabbath Jesus and his disciples were walking through some wheat fields. His disciples were picking grains of wheat as they went along.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>went along: </i>It was the custom to let hungry travelers pick grains of wheat.  
**2:24** Some Pharisees asked Jesus, “Why are your disciples picking grain on the Sabbath? They are not supposed to do that!”  
**2:25**  Jesus answered, <J>“Haven't you read what David did when he and his followers were hungry and in need?</J>  
**2:26**  <J>It was during the time of Abiathar the high priest. David went into the house of God and ate the sacred loaves of bread that only priests are allowed to eat. He also gave some to his followers.”</J>  
**2:27** Jesus finished by saying, <J>“People were not made for the good of the Sabbath. The Sabbath was made for the good of people.</J>  
**2:28** <J>So the Son of Man is Lord over the Sabbath.”</J>  

## 3장

**3:1** The next time Jesus went into the synagogue, a man with a paralyzed hand was there.  
**3:2** The Pharisees<sup>①</sup> wanted to accuse Jesus of doing something wrong, and they kept watching to see if Jesus would heal him on the Sabbath.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Pharisees: </i>The Greek text has “they” (but see verse 6).  
**3:3** Jesus told the man to stand up where everyone could see him.  
**3:4** Then he asked, <J>“On the Sabbath should we do good deeds or evil deeds? Should we save someone's life or destroy it?”</J> But no one said a word.  
**3:5** Jesus was angry as he looked around at the people. Yet he felt sorry for them because they were so stubborn. Then he told the man, <J>“Stretch out your hand.”</J> He did, and his bad hand was healed.  
**3:6** The Pharisees left. And at once they started making plans with Herod's followers<sup>①</sup> to kill Jesus.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Herod's followers: </i>People who were political followers of the family of Herod the Great and his son Herod Antipas.  
**3:7** Jesus led his disciples down to the shore of the lake. Large crowds followed him from Galilee, Judea,  
**3:8** and Jerusalem. People came from Idumea, as well as other places east of the Jordan River. They also came from the region around the towns of Tyre and Sidon. All of these crowds came because they had heard what Jesus was doing.  
**3:9**  He even had to tell his disciples to get a boat ready to keep him from being crushed by the crowds.  
**3:10** After Jesus had healed many people, the other sick people begged him to let them touch him.  
**3:11** And whenever any evil spirits saw Jesus, they would fall to the ground and shout, “You are the Son of God!”  
**3:12** But Jesus warned the spirits not to tell who he was.  
**3:13** Jesus decided to ask some of his disciples to go up on a mountain with him, and they went.  
**3:14** Then he chose twelve of them to be his apostles,<sup>①</sup> so they could be with him. He also wanted to send them out to preach<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>to be his apostles: </i>These words are not in some manuscripts.  
**3:15** and to force out demons.  
**3:16** Simon was one of the twelve, and Jesus named him Peter.  
**3:17** There were also James and John, the two sons of Zebedee. Jesus called them Boanerges, which means “Thunderbolts.”  
**3:18** Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus were also apostles. The others were Simon, known as the Eager One,<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>known as the Eager One: </i>The Greek text has “Cananaean,” which probably comes from a Hebrew word meaning “zealous” (see <a name='B:490 6:15'>Luke 6.15). “Zealot” was the name later given to the members of a Jewish group that resisted and fought against the Romans.  
**3:19** and Judas Iscariot,<sup>①</sup> who later betrayed Jesus.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Iscariot: </i>This may mean “a man from Kerioth” (a place in Judea). But more probably it means “a man who was a liar” or “a man who was a betrayer.”  
**3:20** Jesus went back home,<sup>①</sup> and once again such a large crowd gathered that there was no chance even to eat.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>went back home: </i>Or “entered a house” (perhaps the home of Simon Peter).  
**3:21** When Jesus' family heard what he was doing, they thought he was crazy and went to get him under control.  
**3:22**  Some teachers of the Law of Moses came from Jerusalem and said, “This man is under the power of Beelzebul, the ruler of demons! He is even forcing out demons with the help of Beelzebul.”  
**3:23** Jesus told the people to gather around him. Then he spoke to them in riddles and said: <J>How can Satan force himself out?</J>  
**3:24** <J>A nation whose people fight each other won't last very long.</J>  
**3:25** <J>And a family that fights won't last long either.</J>  
**3:26** <J>So if Satan fights against himself, that will be the end of him.</J>  
**3:27** <J>How can anyone break into the house of a strong man and steal his things, unless he first ties up the strong man? Then he can take everything.</J>  
**3:28** <J>I promise you that any of the sinful things you say or do can be forgiven, no matter how terrible those things are.</J>  
**3:29**  <J>But if you speak against the Holy Spirit, you can never be forgiven. That sin will be held against you forever.</J>  
**3:30** Jesus said this because the people were saying that he had an evil spirit in him.  
**3:31** Jesus' mother and brothers came and stood outside. Then they sent someone with a message for him to come out to them.  
**3:32** The crowd sitting around Jesus told him, “Your mother and your brothers and sisters<sup>①</sup> are outside and want to see you.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>and sisters: </i>These words are not in some manuscripts.  
**3:33** Jesus asked, <J>“Who is my mother and who are my brothers?”</J>  
**3:34** Then he looked at the people sitting around him and said, <J>“Here are my mother and my brothers.</J>  
**3:35** <J>Anyone who obeys God is my brother or sister or mother.”</J>  

## 4장

**4:1**  The next time Jesus taught beside Lake Galilee, a big crowd gathered. It was so large that he had to sit in a boat out on the lake, while the people stood on the shore.  
**4:2** He used stories to teach them many things, and this is part of what he taught:  
**4:3** <J>Now listen! A farmer went out to scatter seed in a field.</J>  
**4:4** <J>While the farmer was scattering the seed, some of it fell along the road and was eaten by birds.</J>  
**4:5** <J>Other seeds fell on thin, rocky ground and quickly started growing because the soil wasn't very deep.</J>  
**4:6** <J>But when the sun came up, the plants were scorched and dried up, because they did not have deep roots.</J>  
**4:7** <J>Some other seeds fell where thornbushes grew up and choked out the plants. So they did not produce any grain.</J>  
**4:8** <J>But a few seeds did fall on good ground where the plants grew and produced 30 or 60 or even 100 times as much as was scattered.</J>  
**4:9** Then Jesus said, <J>“If you have ears, pay attention.”</J>  
**4:10** When Jesus was alone with the twelve apostles and some others, they asked him about these stories.  
**4:11** He answered: <J>I have explained the secret about God's kingdom to you, but for others I can use only stories.</J>  
**4:12**  <J>The reason is,<br>“These people will look<br>and look, but never see.<br>They will listen and listen,<br>but never understand.<br>If they did,<br>they would turn to God<br>and be forgiven.”</J>  
**4:13** Jesus then told them: <J>If you don't understand this story, you won't understand any others.</J>  
**4:14** <J>What the farmer is spreading is really the message about the kingdom.</J>  
**4:15** <J>The seeds that fell along the road are the people who hear the message. But Satan soon comes and snatches it away from them.</J>  
**4:16** <J>The seeds that fell on rocky ground are the people who gladly hear the message and accept it at once.</J>  
**4:17** <J>But they don't have roots, and they don't last very long. As soon as life gets hard or the message gets them in trouble, they give up.</J>  
**4:18** <J>The seeds that fell among the thornbushes are also people who hear the message.</J>  
**4:19** <J>But they start worrying about the needs of this life. They are fooled by the desire to get rich and to have all kinds of other things. So the message gets choked out, and they never produce anything.</J>  
**4:20** <J>The seeds that fell on good ground are the people who hear and welcome the message. They produce 30 or 60 or even 100 times as much as was planted.</J>  
**4:21**  Jesus also said: <J>You don't light a lamp and put it under a clay pot or under a bed. Don't you put a lamp on a lampstand?</J>  
**4:22**  <J>There is nothing hidden that will not be made public. There is no secret that will not be well known.</J>  
**4:23** <J>If you have ears, pay attention!</J>  
**4:24**  <J>Listen carefully to what you hear! The way you treat others will be the way you will be treated—and even worse.</J>  
**4:25**  <J>Everyone who has something will be given more. But people who don't have anything will lose what little they have.</J>  
**4:26** Again Jesus said: <J>God's kingdom is like what happens when a farmer scatters seed in a field.</J>  
**4:27** <J>The farmer sleeps at night and is up and around during the day. Yet the seeds keep sprouting and growing, and he doesn't understand how.</J>  
**4:28** <J>It is the ground that makes the seeds sprout and grow into plants that produce grain.</J>  
**4:29**  <J>Then when harvest season comes and the grain is ripe, the farmer cuts it with a sickle.</J><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>sickle: </i>A knife with a long curved blade, used to cut grain and other crops.  
**4:30** Finally, Jesus said: <J>What is God's kingdom like? What story can I use to explain it?</J>  
**4:31** <J>It is like what happens when a mustard seed is planted in the ground. It is the smallest seed in all the world.</J>  
**4:32** <J>But once it is planted, it grows larger than any garden plant. It even puts out branches that are big enough for birds to nest in its shade.</J>  
**4:33** Jesus used many other stories when he spoke to the people, and he taught them as much as they could understand.  
**4:34** He did not tell them anything without using stories. But when he was alone with his disciples, he explained everything to them.  
**4:35** That evening, Jesus said to his disciples, <J>“Let's cross to the east side.”</J>  
**4:36** So they left the crowd, and his disciples started across the lake with him in the boat. Some other boats followed along.  
**4:37** Suddenly a storm struck the lake. Waves started splashing into the boat, and it was about to sink.  
**4:38** Jesus was in the back of the boat with his head on a pillow, and he was asleep. His disciples woke him and said, “Teacher, don't you care that we're about to drown?”  
**4:39** Jesus got up and ordered the wind and the waves to be quiet. The wind stopped, and everything was calm.  
**4:40** Jesus asked his disciples, <J>“Why were you afraid? Don't you have any faith?”</J>  
**4:41** Now they were more afraid than ever and said to each other, “Who is this? Even the wind and the waves obey him!”  

## 5장

**5:1** Jesus and his disciples crossed Lake Galilee and came to shore near the town of Gerasa.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Gerasa: </i>Some manuscripts have “Gadara,” and others have “Gergesa.”  
**5:2** When he was getting out of the boat, a man with an evil spirit quickly ran to him  
**5:3** from the graveyard<sup>①</sup> where he had been living. No one was able to tie the man up anymore, not even with a chain.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>graveyard: </i>It was thought that demons and evil spirits lived in graveyards.  
**5:4** He had often been put in chains and leg irons, but he broke the chains and smashed the leg irons. No one could control him.  
**5:5** Night and day he was in the graveyard or on the hills, yelling and cutting himself with stones.  
**5:6** When the man saw Jesus in the distance, he ran up to him and knelt down.  
**5:7** He shouted, “Jesus, Son of God in heaven, what do you want with me? Promise me in God's name that you won't torture me!”  
**5:8** The man said this because Jesus had already told the evil spirit to come out of him.  
**5:9** Jesus asked, <J>“What is your name?”</J> The man answered, “My name is Lots, because I have ‘lots’ of evil spirits.”  
**5:10** He then begged Jesus not to send them away.  
**5:11** Over on the hillside a large herd of pigs was feeding.  
**5:12** So the evil spirits begged Jesus, “Send us into those pigs! Let us go into them.”  
**5:13** Jesus let them go, and they went out of the man and into the pigs. The whole herd of about 2,000 pigs rushed down the steep bank into the lake and drowned.  
**5:14** The men taking care of the pigs ran to the town and the farms to spread the news. Then the people came out to see what had happened.  
**5:15** When they came to Jesus, they saw the man who had once been full of demons. He was sitting there with his clothes on and in his right mind, and they were terrified.  
**5:16** Everyone who had seen what had happened told about the man and the pigs.  
**5:17** Then the people started begging Jesus to leave their part of the country.  
**5:18** When Jesus was getting into the boat, the man begged to go with him.  
**5:19** But Jesus would not let him. Instead, he said, <J>“Go home to your family and tell them how much the Lord has done for you and how good he has been to you.”</J>  
**5:20** The man went away into the region near the ten cities known as Decapolis<sup>①</sup> and began telling everyone how much Jesus had done for him. Everyone who heard what had happened was amazed.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>the ten cities known as Decapolis: </i>A group of ten cities east of Samaria and Galilee, where the people followed the Greek way of life.  
**5:21** Once again Jesus got into the boat and crossed Lake Galilee.<sup>①</sup> Then as he stood on the shore, a large crowd gathered around him.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>crossed Lake Galilee: </i>To the west side.  
**5:22** The person in charge of the synagogue was also there. His name was Jairus, and when he saw Jesus, he went over to him. He knelt at Jesus' feet  
**5:23** and started begging him for help. He said, “My little daughter is about to die! Please come and touch her, so she will get well and live.”  
**5:24** Jesus went with Jairus. Many people followed along and kept crowding around.  
**5:25** In the crowd was a woman who had been bleeding for twelve years.  
**5:26**  She had gone to many doctors, and they had not done anything except cause her a lot of pain. She had paid them all the money she had. But instead of getting better, she only got worse.  
**5:27** The woman had heard about Jesus, so she came up behind him in the crowd and barely touched his clothes.  
**5:28** She had said to herself, “If I can just touch his clothes, I will be healed.”  
**5:29** As soon as she touched them, her bleeding stopped, and she knew she was healed.  
**5:30** At that moment Jesus felt power go out from him. He turned to the crowd and asked, <J>“Who touched my clothes?”</J>  
**5:31** His disciples said to him, “Look at all these people crowding around you! How can you ask who touched you?”  
**5:32** But Jesus turned to see who had touched him.  
**5:33** The woman knew what had happened to her. So she came trembling with fear and knelt down in front of Jesus. Then she told him the whole story.  
**5:34** Jesus said to the woman, <J>“You are now well because of your faith. May God give you peace! You are healed, and you will no longer be in pain.”</J>  
**5:35** While Jesus was still speaking, some people came from Jairus' home and said, “Your daughter has died! Why bother the teacher anymore?”  
**5:36** Jesus heard<sup>①</sup> what they said, and he said to Jairus, <J>“Don't worry. Just have faith!”</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>heard: </i>Or “ignored.”  
**5:37** Jesus did not let anyone go with him except Peter and the two brothers, James and John.

## 14장

**14:1**  It was now two days before Passover and the Festival of Thin Bread. The chief priests and the teachers of the Law of Moses were planning how they could sneak around and have Jesus arrested and put to death.  
**14:2** They were saying, “We must not do it during the festival, because the people will riot.”  
**14:3**  Jesus was eating in Bethany at the home of Simon, who once had leprosy,<sup>①</sup> when a woman came in with a very expensive bottle of sweet-smelling perfume.<sup>②</sup> After breaking it open, she poured the perfume on Jesus' head.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>leprosy: </i>In biblical times the word “leprosy” was used for many different skin diseases. <sup>②</sup><i>sweet-smelling perfume: </i>The Greek text has “perfume made of pure spikenard,” a plant used to make perfume.  
**14:4** This made some of the guests angry, and they complained, “Why such a waste?  
**14:5** We could have sold this perfume for more than 300 silver coins and given the money to the poor!” So they started saying cruel things to the woman.  
**14:6** But Jesus said: <J>Leave her alone! Why are you bothering her? She has done a beautiful thing for me.</J>  
**14:7**  <J>You will always have the poor with you. And whenever you want to, you can give to them. But you won't always have me here with you.</J>  
**14:8** <J>She has done all she could by pouring perfume on my body to prepare it for burial.</J>  
**14:9** <J>You may be sure that wherever the good news is told all over the world, people will remember what she has done. And they will tell others.</J>  
**14:10** Judas Iscariot<sup>①</sup> was one of the twelve disciples. He went to the chief priests and offered to help them arrest Jesus.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Iscariot: </i>See the <a name='C480 3:19'>note at <a name='B:480 3:19'>3.19.

**14:13** Jesus said to two of the disciples, <J>“Go into the city, where you will meet a man carrying a jar of water.<sup>①</sup> Follow him,</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>a man carrying a jar of water: </i>A male slave carrying water could mean that the family was rich.

**14:15** <J>The owner will take you upstairs and show you a large room furnished and ready for you to use. Prepare the meal there.”</J>  
**14:16** The two disciples went into the city and found everything just as Jesus had told them. So they prepared the Passover meal.  
**14:17**  While Jesus and the twelve disciples were eating together that evening, he said, “The one who will betray me is now eating with me.”  
**14:18**   
**14:19** This made the disciples sad, and one after another they said to Jesus, “You surely don't mean me!”  
**14:20** He answered, <J>“It is one of you twelve men who is eating from this dish with me.</J>  
**14:21** <J>The Son of Man will die, just as the Scriptures say. But it is going to be terrible for the one who betrays me. That man would be better off if he had never been born.”</J>  
**14:22** During the meal Jesus took some bread in his hands. He blessed the bread and broke it. Then he gave it to his disciples and said, <J>“Take this. It is my body.”</J>  
**14:23** Jesus picked up a cup of wine and gave thanks to God. He gave it to his disciples, and they all drank some.  
**14:24**  Then he said, <J>“This is my blood, which is poured out for many people, and with it God makes his agreement.</J>  
**14:25** <J>From now on I will not drink any wine, until I drink new wine in God's kingdom.”</J>  
**14:26** Then they sang a hymn and went out to the Mount of Olives.

**14:29** Peter spoke up, “Even if all the others reject you, I never will!”  
**14:30** Jesus replied, <J>“This very night before a rooster crows twice, you will say three times that you don't know me.”</J>  
**14:31** But Peter was so sure of himself that he said, “Even if I have to die with you, I will never say I don't know you!” All the others said the same thing.  
**14:32** Jesus went with his disciples to a place called Gethsemane, and he told them, <J>“Sit here while I pray.”</J>  
**14:33** Jesus took along Peter, James, and John. He was sad and troubled and

**14:35** Jesus walked on a little way. Then he knelt down on the ground and prayed, “Father,<sup>①</sup> if it is possible, don't let this happen to me! Father, you can do anything. **14:36**   
**14:37** When Jesus came back and found the disciples sleeping, he said to Simon Peter, <J>“Are you asleep? Can't you stay awake for just one hour?</J>  
**14:38** <J>Stay awake and pray that you won't be tested. You want to do what is right, but you are weak.”</J>  
**14:39** Jesus went back and prayed the same prayer.  
**14:40** But when he returned to the disciples, he found them sleeping again. They simply could not keep their eyes open, and they did not know what to say.  
**14:41** When Jesus returned to the disciples the third time, he said, <J>“Are you still sleeping and resting?<sup>①</sup> Enough of this! **14:43** Jesus was still speaking, when Judas the betrayer came up. He was one of the twelve disciples, and a mob of men armed with swords and clubs were with him. They had been sent by the chief priests, the nation's leaders, and the teachers of the Law of Moses.  
**14:44** Judas had told them ahead of time, “Arrest the man I greet with a kiss.<sup>①</sup> Tie him up tight and lead him away.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>greet with a kiss: </i>It was the custom for people to greet each other with a kiss on the cheek.  
**14:45** Judas walked right up to Jesus and said, “Teacher!” Then Judas kissed him,  
**14:46** and the men grabbed Jesus and arrested him.  
**14:47** Someone standing there pulled out a sword. He struck the servant of the high priest and cut off his ear.  
**14:48** Jesus said to the mob, <J>“Why do you come with swords and clubs to arrest me like a criminal?</J>  
**14:49**  <J>Day after day I was with you and taught in the temple, and you didn't arrest me. But what the Scriptures say must come true.”</J>  
**14:50** All of Jesus' disciples ran off and left him.  
**14:51** One of them was a young man who was wearing only a linen cloth. And when the men grabbed him,  
**14:52** he left the cloth behind and ran away naked.  
**14:53** Jesus was led off to the high priest. Then the chief priests, the nation's leaders, and the teachers of the Law of Moses all met together.  
**14:54** Peter had followed at a distance, and when he reached the courtyard of the high priest's house, he sat down with the guards to warm himself beside a fire.  
**14:55** The chief priests and the whole council tried to find someone to accuse Jesus of a crime, so they could put him to death. But they could not find anyone to accuse him.  
**14:56** Many people did tell lies against Jesus, but they did not agree on what they said.  
**14:57** Finally, some men stood up and lied about him. They said,  
**14:58**  “We heard him say he would <J>tear down this temple that we built. He also claimed that in three days he would build another one without any help</J>.”  
**14:59** But even then they did not agree on what they said.  
**14:60** The high priest stood up in the council and asked Jesus, “Why don't you say something in your own defense? Don't you hear the charges they are making against you?”  
**14:61** But Jesus kept quiet and did not say a word. The high priest asked him another question, “Are you the Messiah, the Son of the glorious God?”<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Son of the glorious God: </i>“Son of God” was one of the titles used for the kings of Israel.  
**14:62**  <J>“Yes, I am!”</J> Jesus answered.<br><J>“Soon you will see<br>the Son of Man<br>sitting at the right side<sup>①</sup><br>of God All-Powerful,<br>and coming with the clouds<br>of heaven.”</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>right side: </i>See the <a name='C480 12:36'>note at <a name='B:480 12:36'>12.36.  
**14:63** At once the high priest ripped his robe apart and shouted, “Why do we need more witnesses?  
**14:64**  You heard him claim to be God! What is your decision?” They all agreed he should be put to death.  
**14:65** Some of the people started spitting on Jesus. They blindfolded him, hit him with their fists, and said, “Tell us who hit you!” Then the guards took charge of Jesus and beat him.  
**14:66** While Peter was still in the courtyard, a servant girl of the high priest came up  
**14:67** and saw Peter warming himself by the fire. She stared at him and said, “You were with Jesus from Nazareth!”  
**14:68** Peter replied, “That isn't true! I don't know what you're talking about. I don't have any idea what you mean.” He went out to the gate, and a rooster crowed.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>a rooster crowed: </i>These words are not in some manuscripts.  
**14:69** The servant girl saw Peter again and said to the people standing there, “This man is one of them!”  
**14:70** “No, I'm not!” Peter replied. A little while later some of the people said to Peter, “You certainly are one of them. You're a Galilean!”  
**14:71** This time Peter began to curse and swear, “I don't even know the man you're talking about!”  
**14:72** At once the rooster crowed a second time. Then Peter remembered that Jesus had told him, <J>“Before a rooster crows twice, you will say three times that you don't know me.”</J> So Peter started crying.  

## 15장

**15:1** Early the next morning the chief priests, the nation's leaders, and the teachers of the Law of Moses met together with the whole Jewish council. They tied up Jesus and led him off to Pilate.  
**15:2** He asked Jesus, “Are you the king of the Jews?” <J>“Those are your words,”</J> Jesus answered.  
**15:3** The chief priests brought many charges against Jesus.  
**15:4** Then Pilate questioned him again, “Don't you have anything to say? Don't you hear what crimes they say you have done?”  
**15:5** But Jesus did not answer, and Pilate was amazed.  
**15:6** During Passover, Pilate always freed one prisoner chosen by the people.  
**15:7** And at that time there was a prisoner named Barabbas. He and some others had been arrested for murder during a riot.  
**15:8** The crowd now came and asked Pilate to set a prisoner free, just as he usually did.  
**15:9** Pilate asked them, “Do you want me to free the king of the Jews?”  
**15:10** Pilate knew that the chief priests had brought Jesus to him because they were jealous.  
**15:11** But the chief priests told the crowd to ask Pilate to free Barabbas.  
**15:12** Then Pilate asked the crowd, “What do you want me to do with this man you say is<sup>①</sup> the king of the Jews?”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>this man you say is: </i>These words are not in some manuscripts.  
**15:13** They yelled, “Nail him to a cross!”  
**15:14** Pilate asked, “But what crime has he done?” “Nail him to a cross!” they yelled even louder.  
**15:15** Pilate wanted to please the crowd, so he set Barabbas free. Then he ordered his soldiers to beat Jesus with a whip and nail him to a cross.  
**15:16** The soldiers led Jesus inside the courtyard of the fortress<sup>①</sup> and called together the rest of the troops.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>fortress: </i>The place where the Roman governor stayed. **15:17** They put a purple robe<sup>①</sup> on him, and on his head they placed a crown they had made out of thorn branches.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>purple robe: </i>This was probably a Roman soldier's robe.  
**15:18** They made fun of Jesus and shouted, “Hey, you king of the Jews!”  
**15:19** Then they beat him on the head with a stick. They spit on him and knelt down and pretended to worship him.  
**15:20** When the soldiers had finished making fun of Jesus, they took off the purple robe. They put his own clothes back on him and led him off to be nailed to a cross.  
**15:21**  Simon from Cyrene happened to be coming in from a farm, and they forced him to carry Jesus' cross. Simon was the father of Alexander and Rufus.  
**15:22** The soldiers took Jesus to Golgotha, which means “Place of a Skull.”<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Place of a Skull: </i>The place was probably given this name because it was near a large rock in the shape of a human skull.

**15:24**  They nailed Jesus to a cross and gambled to see who would get his clothes.  
**15:25** It was about nine o'clock in the morning when they nailed him to the cross.  
**15:26** On it was a sign that told why he was nailed there. It read, “This is the King of the Jews.”  
**15:27**  The soldiers also nailed two criminals on crosses, one to the right of Jesus and the other to his left.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>left: </i>Some manuscripts add, “So the Scriptures came true which say, ‘He was accused of being a criminal.’ ”  
**15:28**   
**15:29**  People who passed by said terrible things about Jesus. They shook their heads and shouted, “Ha! So you're the one who claimed you could tear down the temple and build it again in three days.  
**15:30** Save yourself and come down from the cross!”  
**15:31** The chief priests and the teachers of the Law of Moses also made fun of Jesus. They said to each other, “He saved others, but he can't save himself.  
**15:32** If he is the Messiah, the king of Israel, let him come down from the cross! Then we will see and believe.” The two criminals also said cruel things to Jesus.  
**15:33** About noon the sky turned dark and stayed that way until around three o'clock.

**15:40**  Some women were looking on from a distance. They and many others had come with Jesus to Jerusalem. Salome was also one of them.  
**15:41**   
**15:42** It was now the evening before the Sabbath, and the Jewish people were getting ready for that sacred day.  
**15:43** A man named Joseph from Arimathea was brave enough to ask Pilate for the body of Jesus. Joseph was a highly respected member of the Jewish council, and he was also waiting for God's kingdom to come.  
**15:44** Pilate was surprised to hear that Jesus was already dead, and he called in the army officer to find out if Jesus had been dead very long.  
**15:45** After the officer told him, Pilate let Joseph have Jesus' body.

**15:47** Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joseph were watching and saw where the body was placed.
