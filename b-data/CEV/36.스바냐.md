# 36.스바냐 (CEV)



## 1장

**1:1**  I am Zephaniah, the son of Cushi, the grandson of Gedaliah, the great-grandson of Amariah, and the great-great-grandson of Hezekiah.<sup>①</sup> When Josiah son of Amon was king of Judah,<sup>②</sup> the LORD gave me this message.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Hezekiah: </i>Ruled 716–687 B.C. <sup>②</sup><i>Josiah … king of Judah: </i>Ruled 640–609 B.C.  
**1:2** I, the LORD, now promise<br>to destroy everything<br>on this earth—  
**1:3** people and animals,<br>birds and fish.<br>Everyone who is evil<br>will crash to the ground,<sup>①</sup><br>and I will wipe out<br>the entire human race.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Everyone … ground: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  
**1:4** I will reach out to punish<br>Judah and Jerusalem—<br>nothing will remain<br>of the god Baal;<sup>①</sup><br>nothing will be remembered<br>of his pagan priests.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Baal: </i>A Canaanite fertility god.  
**1:5** Not a trace will be found<br>of those who worship stars<br>from their rooftops,<br>or bow down to the god Milcom,<sup>①</sup><br>while claiming loyalty<br>to me, the LORD.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Milcom: </i>An Ammonite fertility god.  
**1:6** Nothing will remain of anyone<br>who has turned away<br>and rejected me.   
**1:7** Be silent! I am the LORD God,<br>and the time is near.<br>I am preparing<br>to sacrifice my people<br>and to invite my guests.  
**1:8** On that day I will punish<br>national leaders<br>and sons of the king,<br>along with all who follow<br>foreign customs.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>follow foreign customs: </i>Hebrew “wear foreign clothes.”  
**1:9** I will punish worshipers<br>of pagan gods<sup>①</sup><br>and cruel palace officials<br>who abuse their power.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>worshipers … gods: </i>The Hebrew text has “all who jump over the threshold,” which was a Philistine religious practice (see <a name='B:90 5:5'>1 Samuel 5.5).  
**1:10** I, the LORD, promise<br>that on that day<br>noisy crying will be heard<br>from Fish Gate, New Town,<br>and Upper Hills.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Fish Gate, New Town, and Upper Hills … Lower Hollow: </i>Names for different sections of Jerusalem: Fish Gate was probably the main gate on the north side of the city; New Town was a newer section; Upper Hills may have been a suburb north of the city; Lower Hollow was probably on the southern edge of town.  
**1:11** Everyone in Lower Hollow<sup>①</sup><br>will mourn loudly,<br>because merchants<br>and money changers<br>will be wiped out.  
**1:12** I'll search Jerusalem with lamps<br>and punish those people<br>who sit there unworried<br>while thinking,<br>“The LORD won't do anything,<br>good or bad.”  
**1:13** Their possessions will be taken,<br>their homes left in ruins.<br>They won't get to live<br>in the houses they build,<br>or drink wine from the grapes<br>in their own vineyards.  
**1:14** The great day of the LORD<br>is coming soon, very soon.<br>On that terrible day,<br>fearsome shouts of warriors<br>will be heard everywhere.  
**1:15** It will be a time of anger—<br>of trouble and torment,<br>of disaster and destruction,<br>of darkness and despair,<br>of storm clouds and shadows,  
**1:16** of trumpet calls<br>and battle cries<br>against fortified cities<br>and mighty fortresses.   
**1:17** The LORD warns everyone<br>who has sinned against him,<br>“I'll strike you blind!<br>Then your blood and your insides<br>will gush out like vomit.  
**1:18** Not even your silver or gold<br>can save you on that day<br>when I, the LORD, am angry.<br>My anger will flare up<br>like a furious fire<br>scorching the earth<br>and everyone on it.”  

## 2장

**2:1** You disgraceful nation,<br>gather around,  
**2:2** before it's too late.<br>The LORD has set a time<br>when his fierce anger<br>will strike like a storm<br>and sweep you away.  
**2:3** If you humbly obey the LORD,<br>then come and worship him.<br>If you do right and are humble,<br>perhaps you will be safe<br>on that day when the LORD<br>turns loose his anger.  
**2:4**  Gaza and Ashkelon<br>will be deserted<br>and left in ruins.<br>Ashdod will be emptied<br>in broad daylight,<br>and Ekron<sup>①</sup> uprooted.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Gaza … Ekron: </i>Gaza, Ashkelon, Ashdod, Ekron, and Gath (not mentioned because it was already destroyed) were the five major Philistine towns.  
**2:5** To you people of Philistia<sup>①</sup><br>who live along the coast,<br>the LORD has this to say:<br>“I am now your enemy,<br>and I'll wipe you out!”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>people of Philistia: </i>The Hebrew text also mentions “Canaan” and “Cherethites,” which are other ways of referring to the Philistines.  
**2:6** Your seacoast will be changed<br>into pastureland<br>and sheep pens.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>pens: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text of verse <a name='B:430 2:6'>6.  
**2:7** The LORD God hasn't forgotten<br>those survivors in Judah,<br>and he will help them—<br>his people will take your land<br>to use for pasture.<br>And when evening comes,<br>they will rest<br>in houses at Ashkelon.<sup>①</sup><br>*<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Ashkelon: </i>A Philistine town; see the <a name='C430 2:4'>note at <a name='B:430 2:4'>2.4.  
**2:8**  The LORD All-Powerful,<br>the God of Israel, said:<br>I've heard Moab and Ammon<br>insult my people<br>and threaten their nation.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>threaten their nation: </i>Or “boast about their own nation.”  
**2:9**  And so, I swear by my very life<br>that Moab and Ammon will end up<br>like Sodom and Gomorrah—<br>covered with thornbushes<br>and salt pits forever.<br>Then my people who survive<br>will take their land.  
**2:10** This is how Moab and Ammon<br>will at last be repaid<br>for their pride—<br>and for sneering at the nation<br>that belongs to me,<br>the LORD All-Powerful.  
**2:11** I will fiercely attack.<br>Then every god on this earth<br>will shrink to nothing,<br>and everyone of every nation<br>will bow down to me,<br>right where they are.  
**2:12**  People of Ethiopia,<sup>①</sup><br>the sword of the LORD<br>will slaughter you!<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Ethiopia: </i>The Hebrew text has “Cush,” which was a region south of Egypt that included parts of the present countries of Ethiopia and Sudan.  
**2:13**  The LORD will reach to the north<br>to crush Assyria<br>and overthrow Nineveh.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Nineveh: </i>The capital of Assyria; Nineveh was protected by a moat filled with water from the nearby Tigris River.  
**2:14** Herds of wild animals<br>will live in its rubble;<br>all kinds of desert owls<br>will perch on its stones<br>and hoot in the windows.<br>Noisy ravens will be heard<br>inside its buildings,<br>stripped bare of cedar.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>stripped … cedar: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  
**2:15** This is the glorious city<br>that felt secure and said,<br>“I am the only one!”<br>Now it's merely ruins,<br>a home for wild animals.<br>Every passerby simply sneers<br>and makes vulgar signs.  

## 3장

**3:1** Too bad for that disgusting,<br>corrupt, and lawless city!  
**3:2** Forever rebellious<br>and rejecting correction,<br>Jerusalem refuses to trust<br>or obey the LORD God.  
**3:3** Its officials are roaring lions,<br>its judges are wolves;<br>in the evening they attack,<br>by morning nothing is left.  
**3:4** Jerusalem's prophets are proud<br>and not to be trusted.<br>The priests have disgraced<br>the place of worship<br>and abused God's Law.  
**3:5** All who do evil are shameless,<br>but the LORD does right<br>and is always fair.<br>With the dawn of each day,<br>God brings about justice.   
**3:6** The LORD wiped out nations<br>and left fortresses<br>crumbling in the dirt.<br>Their streets and towns<br>were reduced to ruins<br>and emptied of people.  
**3:7** God felt certain that Jerusalem<br>would learn to respect<br>and obey him.<br>Then he would hold back<br>from punishing the city<br>and not wipe it out.<br>But everyone there was eager<br>to start sinning again.  
**3:8** The LORD said:<br>Just wait for the day<br>when I accuse you nations.<br>I have decided on a day,<br>when I will bring together<br>every nation and kingdom<br>and punish them all<br>in my fiery anger.<br>I will become furious<br>and destroy the earth.   
**3:9** I will purify each language<br>and make those languages<br>acceptable for praising me.<sup>①</sup><br>Then, with hearts united,<br>everyone will serve<br>only me, the LORD.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>I will … praising me: </i>Or “I will change the hearts of all people and make them fit for praising me.”  
**3:10** From across the rivers<br>of Ethiopia,<sup>①</sup><br>my scattered people,<br>my true worshipers,<br>will bring offerings to me.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Ethiopia: </i>See the <a name='C430 2:12'>note at <a name='B:430 2:12'>2.12.  
**3:11** When that time comes,<br>you won't rebel against me<br>and be put to shame.<br>I'll do away with those<br>who are proud and arrogant.<br>Never will any of them<br>strut around<br>on my holy mountain.  
**3:12** But I, the LORD, won't destroy<br>any of your people<br>who are truly humble<br>and turn to me for safety.  
**3:13**  The people of Israel who survive<br>will live right<br>and refuse to tell lies.<br>They will eat and rest<br>with nothing to fear.  
**3:14** Everyone in Jerusalem and Judah,<br>celebrate and shout<br>with all your heart!  
**3:15** Zion, your punishment is over.<br>The LORD has forced your enemies<br>to turn and retreat.<br>Your LORD is King of Israel<br>and stands at your side;<br>you don't have to worry<br>about any more troubles.   
**3:16** Jerusalem, the time is coming,<br>when it will be said to you:<br>“Don't be discouraged<br>or grow weak from fear!  
**3:17** The LORD your God<br>wins victory after victory<br>and is always with you.<br>He celebrates and sings<br>because of you,<br>and he will refresh your life<br>with his love.”<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>refresh … love: </i>Two ancient translations; Hebrew “silently show you his love.”  
**3:18** The LORD has promised:<br>Your sorrow has ended,<br>and you can celebrate.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>celebrate: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text of verse <a name='B:430 3:18'>18.  
**3:19** I will punish those<br>who mistreat you.<br>I will bring together the lame<br>and the outcasts,<br>then they will be praised,<br>instead of despised,<br>in every country on earth.  
**3:20** I will lead you home,<br>and with your own eyes<br>you will see me bless you<br>with all you once owned.<br>Then you will be famous<br>everywhere on this earth.<br>I, the LORD, have spoken!
