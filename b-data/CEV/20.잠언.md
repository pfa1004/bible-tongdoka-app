# 20.잠언 (CEV)



## 1장

**1:1**  These are the proverbs<br>of King Solomon of Israel,<br>the son of David.  
**1:2** Proverbs will teach you<br>wisdom and self-control<br>and how to understand<br>sayings with deep meanings.  
**1:3** You will learn what is right<br>and honest and fair.  
**1:4** From these, an ordinary person<br>can learn to be smart,<br>and young people can gain<br>knowledge and good sense.   
**1:5** If you are already wise,<br>you will become even wiser.<br>And if you are smart,<br>you will learn to understand  
**1:6** proverbs and sayings,<br>as well as words of wisdom,<br>and all kinds of riddles.  
**1:7**  Respect and obey the LORD!<br>This is the beginning<br>of knowledge.<sup>①</sup><br>Only a fool rejects wisdom<br>and good advice.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>the beginning of knowledge: </i>Or “what knowledge is all about.”  
**1:8** My child, obey the teachings<br>of your parents,  
**1:9** and wear their teachings<br>as you would a pretty hat<br>or a lovely necklace.  
**1:10** Don't be tempted by sinners<br>or listen  
**1:11** when they say,<br>“Come on! Let's gang up<br>and kill somebody,<br>just for the fun of it!  
**1:12** They're well and healthy now,<br>but we'll finish them off<br>once and for all.  
**1:13** We'll take their valuables<br>and fill our homes<br>with stolen goods.  
**1:14** If you join our gang,<br>you'll get your share.”   
**1:15** Don't follow anyone like that<br>or do what they do.  
**1:16** They are in a big hurry<br>to commit some crime,<br>perhaps even murder.  
**1:17** They are like a bird<br>that sees the bait,<br>but ignores the trap.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>They are … trap: </i>Or “Be like a bird that won't go for the bait, if it sees the trap.”

**1:19** The wealth you get from crime<br>robs you of your life.  
**1:20**  Wisdom<sup>①</sup> shouts in the streets<br>wherever crowds gather.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Wisdom: </i>In the book of Proverbs the word “wisdom” is sometimes used as though wisdom were a supernatural being who was with God at the time of creation.

**1:22** “How much longer<br>will you enjoy<br>being stupid fools?<br>Won't you ever stop sneering<br>and laughing at knowledge?  
**1:23** Listen as I correct you<br>and tell you what I think.  
**1:24** You completely ignored me<br>and refused to listen;  
**1:25** you rejected my advice<br>and paid no attention<br>when I warned you.   
**1:26** “So when you are struck<br>by some terrible disaster,  
**1:27** or when trouble and distress<br>surround you like a whirlwind,<br>I will laugh and make fun.  
**1:28** You will ask for my help,<br>but I won't listen;<br>you will search,<br>but you won't find me.  
**1:29** No, you would not learn,<br>and you refused<br>to respect the LORD.  
**1:30** You rejected my advice<br>and paid no attention<br>when I warned you.   
**1:31** “Now you will eat the fruit<br>of what you have done,<br>until you are stuffed full<br>with your own schemes.  
**1:32** Sin and self-satisfaction<br>bring destruction and death<br>to stupid fools.  
**1:33** But if you listen to me,<br>you will be safe and secure<br>without fear of disaster.”  

## 2장

**2:1** My child, you must follow<br>and treasure my teachings<br>and my instructions.  
**2:2** Keep in tune with wisdom<br>and think what it means<br>to have common sense.  
**2:3** Beg as loud as you can<br>for good common sense.  
**2:4** Search for wisdom<br>as you would search for silver<br>or hidden treasure.  
**2:5** Then you will understand<br>what it means to respect<br>and to know the LORD God.   
**2:6**  All wisdom comes from the LORD,<br>and so do common sense<br>and understanding.  
**2:7** God gives helpful advice<sup>①</sup><br>to everyone who obeys him<br>and protects those<br>who live as they should.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>helpful advice: </i>Or “wisdom.”

**2:9** With wisdom you will learn<br>what is right<br>and honest and fair.   
**2:10** Wisdom will control your mind,<br>and you will be pleased<br>with knowledge.  
**2:11** Sound judgment and good sense<br>will watch over you.  
**2:12** Wisdom will protect you<br>from evil schemes<br>and from those liars  
**2:13** who turned from doing good<br>to live in the darkness.  
**2:14** Most of all they enjoy<br>being mean and deceitful.  
**2:15** They are dishonest themselves,<br>and so are all their deeds.  
**2:16** Wisdom will protect you<br>from the smooth talk<br>of a sinful woman,

**2:20** Follow the example<br>of good people<br>and live an honest life.  
**2:21** If you are honest and innocent,<br>you will keep your land;
