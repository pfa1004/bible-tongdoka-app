# 28.호세아 (CEV)



## 1장

**1:1**  I am Hosea son of Beeri. When Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah were the kings of Judah, and when Jeroboam son of Jehoash<sup>①</sup> was king of Israel,<sup>②</sup> the LORD spoke this message to me.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Jehoash: </i>The Hebrew text has “Joash,” another spelling of the name. <sup>②</sup><i>kings of Judah … king of Israel: </i>Uzziah (781–740 B.C.), Jotham (740-736), Ahaz (736–716), Hezekiah (716–687), and Jeroboam II (783–743).  
**1:2** The LORD said, “Hosea, Israel has betrayed me like an unfaithful wife.<sup>①</sup> Marry such a woman and have children by her.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>unfaithful wife: </i>In some Canaanite religions of Old Testament times, young women were expected to have sex with the worshipers of their god before marriage. Such women were called “temple prostitutes.” Many of the Israelite women did this same thing, and Hosea is told to marry one of them to show that the nation has turned from the LORD to worship idols.  
**1:3** So I married Gomer the daughter of Diblaim, and we had a son.  
**1:4**  Then the LORD said, “Hosea, name your son Jezreel,<sup>①</sup> because I will soon punish the descendants of King Jehu of Israel for the murders he committed in Jezreel Valley.<sup>②</sup> I will destroy his kingdom,<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Jezreel: </i>In Hebrew “Jezreel” means “God scatters (seed).” Here the name is used as a threat (meaning the LORD will punish Israel by scattering its people), while in verse 11 it is used as a promise (meaning the LORD will bless Israel by giving their nation many people, just as a big harvest comes when many seeds are scattered in a field). <sup>②</sup><i>murders … Valley: </i>Jehu murdered the wife and relatives of King Ahab (see <a name='B:120 9:15-10:14'>2 Kings 9.15-10.14).  
**1:5** and in Jezreel Valley I will break the power of Israel.”  
**1:6** Later, Gomer had a daughter, and the LORD said, “Name her Lo-Ruhamah,<sup>①</sup> because I will no longer have mercy and forgive Israel.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Lo-Ruhamah: </i>In Hebrew “Lo-Ruhamah” means “No Mercy.”  
**1:7** But I am the LORD God of Judah, and I will have mercy and save Judah by my own power—not by wars and arrows or swords and cavalry.”  
**1:8** After Gomer had stopped nursing Lo-Ruhamah, she had another son.  
**1:9** Then the LORD said, “Name him Lo-Ammi,<sup>①</sup> because these people are not mine, and I am not their God.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Lo-Ammi: </i>In Hebrew “Lo-Ammi” means “Not My People.”  
**1:10**  Someday it will be impossible to count the people of Israel, because there will be as many of them as there are grains of sand along the seashore. They are now called “Not My People,” but in the future they will be called “Children of the Living God.”  
**1:11** Israel and Judah will unite and choose one leader. Then they will take back their land, and this will be a great day for Jezreel.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Jezreel: </i>See the <a name='C350 1:4'>note at <a name='B:350 1:4'>1.4.

**2:16** I promise from that day on, you will call me your husband instead of your master.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>husband … master: </i>In Hebrew the word “master” is the same as the name of the god Baal. **2:17** I will no longer even let you mention the names of those pagan gods that you called “Master.”  
**2:18** And I will agree to let you live in peace—you will no longer be attacked by wild animals and birds or by weapons of war.  
**2:19** I will accept you as my wife forever, and instead of a bride price<sup>①</sup> I will give you justice, fairness, love, kindness,<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>bride price: </i>It was the custom for the husband to pay his wife's parents a bride price. **2:20** and faithfulness. Then you will truly know who I am.  
**2:21** I will command the sky to send rain on the earth,  
**2:22** and it will produce grain, grapes, and olives in Jezreel Valley.  
**2:23**  I will scatter the seeds and show mercy to Lo-Ruhamah.<sup>①</sup> I will say to Lo-Ammi,<sup>②</sup> “You are my people,” and they will answer, “You are our God.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Lo-Ruhamah: </i>See the <a name='C350 1:6'>note at <a name='B:350 1:6'>1.6. <sup>②</sup><i>Lo-Ammi: </i>See the <a name='C350 1:9'>note at <a name='B:350 1:9'>1.9.
