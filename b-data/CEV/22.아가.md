# 22.아가 (CEV)



## 1장

**1:1**  This is Solomon's<br>most beautiful song.   
**1:2** Kiss me tenderly!<br>Your love is better than wine,  
**1:3** and you smell so sweet.<br>All the young women adore you;<br>the very mention of your name<br>is like spreading perfume.<br>*  
**1:4** Hurry, my king! Let's hurry.<br>Take me to your home. We are happy for you!<br>And we praise your love<br>even more than wine. Young women of Jerusalem,<br>it is only right<br>that you should adore him.  
**1:5** My skin is dark and beautiful,<br>like a tent in the desert<br>or like Solomon's curtains.  
**1:6** Don't stare at me<br>just because the sun<br>has darkened my skin.<br>My brothers were angry with me;<br>they made me work in the vineyard,<br>and so I neglected<br>my complexion.   
**1:7** My darling, I love you!<br>Where do you feed your sheep<br>and let them rest at noon?<br>Don't let the other shepherds<br>think badly of me.<br>I'm not one of those women<br>who shamelessly follow<br>after shepherds.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Don't let … after shepherds: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  
**1:8** My dearest, if you don't know,<br>just follow the path<br>of the sheep.<br>Then feed your young goats<br>near the shepherds' tents.  
**1:9** You move as gracefully<br>as the pony that leads<br>the chariot of the king.  
**1:10** Earrings add to your beauty,<br>and you wear a necklace<br>of precious stones.  
**1:11** Let's make you some jewelry<br>of gold, woven with silver.   
**1:12** My king, while you<br>were on your couch,<br>my aroma was a magic charm.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>magic charm: </i>The Hebrew text has “spikenard” (or “nard”), a sweet-smelling ointment made from a plant that comes from India. The ointment was sometimes used as a love charm.  
**1:13** My darling, you are perfume<br>between my breasts;  
**1:14** you are flower blossoms<br>from the gardens of En-Gedi.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>En-Gedi: </i>An oasis west of the Dead Sea.  
**1:15** My darling, you are lovely,<br>so very lovely—<br>your eyes are those of a dove.   
**1:16** My love, you are handsome,<br>truly handsome—<br>the fresh green grass<br>will be our wedding bed  
**1:17** in the shade of cedar<br>and cypress trees.  

## 2장

**2:1** I am merely a rose<sup>①</sup><br>from the land of Sharon,<br>a lily from the valley.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>rose: </i>The traditional translation. The exact variety of the flower is not known, though it may have been a crocus.  
**2:2** My darling, when compared<br>with other young women,<br>you are a lily among thorns.   
**2:3** And you, my love,<br>are an apple tree<br>among trees of the forest.<br>Your shade brought me pleasure;<br>your fruit was sweet.  
**2:4** You led me to your banquet room<br>and showered me with love.  
**2:5** Refresh and strengthen me<br>with raisins and apples.<br>I am hungry for love!  
**2:6** Put your left hand under my head<br>and embrace me<br>with your right arm.   
**2:7** Young women of Jerusalem,<br>promise me by the power<br>of deer and gazelles<sup>①</sup><br>never to awaken love<br>before it is ready.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>deer and gazelles: </i>Deer and gazelles were sacred animals in some religions of Old Testament times, and they were thought to have special powers.  
**2:8** I hear the voice<br>of the one I love,<br>as he comes leaping<br>over mountains and hills  
**2:9** like a deer or a gazelle.<br>Now he stands outside our wall,<br>looking through the window  
**2:10** and speaking to me. My darling, I love you!<br>Let's go away together.  
**2:11** Winter is past,<br>the rain has stopped;  
**2:12** flowers cover the earth,<br>it's time to sing.<sup>①</sup><br>The cooing of doves<br>is heard in our land.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>sing: </i>Or “trim the vines.”  
**2:13** Fig trees are bearing fruit,<br>while blossoms on grapevines<br>fill the air with perfume.<br>My darling, I love you!<br>Let's go away together.  
**2:14** You are my dove<br>hiding among the rocks<br>on the side of a cliff.<br>Let me see how lovely you are!<br>Let me hear the sound<br>of your melodious voice.  
**2:15** Our vineyards are in blossom;<br>we must catch the little foxes<br>that destroy the vineyards.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>vineyards: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text of verse <a name='B:260 2:15'>15.  
**2:16** My darling, I am yours,<br>and you are mine,<br>as you feed your sheep<br>among the lilies.  
**2:17** Pretend to be a young deer<br>dancing on mountain slopes<sup>①</sup><br>until daylight comes<br>and shadows fade away.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>mountain slopes: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  

## 3장

**3:1** While in bed at night,<br>I reached for the one I love<br>with heart and soul.<br>I looked for him,<br>but he wasn't there.  
**3:2** So I searched through the town<br>for the one I love.<br>I looked on every street,<br>but he wasn't there.  
**3:3** I even asked the guards<br>patrolling the town,<br>“Have you seen the one<br>I love so much?”  
**3:4** Right after that, I found him.<br>I held him and would not let go<br>until I had taken him<br>to the home of my mother.  
**3:5** Young women of Jerusalem,<br>promise me by the power<br>of deer and gazelles,<sup>①</sup><br>never to awaken love<br>before it is ready.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>deer and gazelles: </i>See the <a name='C260 2:7'>note at <a name='B:260 2:7'>2.7.  
**3:6** What do we see approaching<br>from the desert<br>like a cloud of smoke?<br>With it comes the sweet smell<br>of spices, including myrrh<br>and frankincense.  
**3:7** It is King Solomon<br>carried on a throne,<br>surrounded by sixty<br>of Israel's best soldiers.  
**3:8** Each of them wears a sword.<br>They are experts at fighting,<br>even in the dark.  
**3:9** The throne is made of trees<br>from Lebanon.  
**3:10** Its posts are silver,<br>the back is gold,<br>and the seat is covered<br>with purple cloth.<br>You women of Jerusalem<br>have taken great care<br>to furnish the inside.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>inside: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  
**3:11** Now come and see the crown<br>given to Solomon by his mother<br>on his happy wedding day.  

## 4장

**4:1** My darling, you are lovely,<br>so very lovely—<br>as you look through your veil,<br>your eyes are those of a dove.<br>Your hair tosses about<br>as gracefully as goats<br>coming down from Gilead.  
**4:2** Your teeth are whiter<br>than sheep freshly washed;<br>they match perfectly,<br>not one is missing.  
**4:3** Your lips are crimson cords,<br>your mouth is shapely;<br>behind your veil are hidden<br>beautiful rosy cheeks.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>beautiful rosy cheeks: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  
**4:4** Your neck is more graceful<br>than the tower of David,<br>decorated with thousands<br>of warriors' shields.   
**4:5** Your breasts are perfect;<br>they are twin deer<br>feeding among lilies.  
**4:6** I will hasten to those hills<br>sprinkled with sweet perfume<br>and stay there till sunrise.  
**4:7** My darling, you are lovely<br>in every way.  
**4:8** My bride, together<br>we will leave Lebanon!<br>We will say goodbye<br>to the peaks of Mount Amana,<br>Senir, and Hermon,<br>where lions and leopards<br>live in the caves.   
**4:9** My bride, my very own,<br>you have stolen my heart!<br>With one glance from your eyes<br>and the glow of your necklace,<br>you have stolen my heart.  
**4:10** Your love is sweeter than wine;<br>the smell of your perfume<br>is more fragrant than spices.  
**4:11** Your lips are a honeycomb;<br>milk and honey<br>flow from your tongue.<br>Your dress has the aroma<br>of cedar trees from Lebanon.   
**4:12** My bride, my very own,<br>you are a garden, a fountain<br>closed off to all others.  
**4:13** Your arms<sup>①</sup> are vines,<br>covered with delicious fruits<br>and all sorts of spices—<br>henna, nard,<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Your arms: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  
**4:14** saffron,<br>calamus, cinnamon,<br>frankincense, myrrh, and aloes<br>—all the finest spices.  
**4:15** You are a spring in the garden,<br>a fountain of pure water,<br>and a refreshing stream<br>from Mount Lebanon.   
**4:16** Let the north wind blow,<br>the south wind too!<br>Let them spread the aroma<br>of my garden,<br>so the one I love<br>may enter and taste<br>its delicious fruits.  

## 5장

**5:1** My bride, my very own,<br>I come to my garden<br>and enjoy its spices.<br>I eat my honeycomb and honey;<br>I drink my wine and milk. Eat and drink until<br>you are drunk with love.   
**5:2** I was asleep, but dreaming:<br>The one I love was at the door,<br>knocking and saying,<br>“My darling, my very own,<br>my flawless dove,<br>open the door for me!<br>My head is drenched<br>with evening dew.”   
**5:3** But I had already undressed<br>and bathed my feet.<br>Should I dress again<br>and get my feet dirty?  
**5:4** Then my darling's hand<br>reached to open the latch,<br>and my heart stood still.  
**5:5** When I rose to open the door,<br>my hands and my fingers<br>dripped with perfume.   
**5:6** And I yearned for him<br>while he spoke to me,<br>but when I opened the door,<br>my darling had disappeared.<br>I searched and shouted,<br>but I could not find him—<br>there was no answer.  
**5:7** Then I was found by the guards<br>patrolling the town<br>and guarding the wall.<br>They beat me up<br>and stripped off my robe.   
**5:8** Young women of Jerusalem,<br>if you find the one I love,<br>please say to him,<br>“She is weak with desire.”   
**5:9** Most beautiful of women,<br>why is the one you love<br>more special than others?<br>Why do you ask us<br>to tell him how you feel?   
**5:10** He is handsome and healthy,<br>the most outstanding<br>among ten thousand.  
**5:11** His head is purest gold;<br>his hair is wavy,<br>black as a raven.  
**5:12** His eyes are a pair of doves<br>bathing in a stream<br>flowing with milk.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>milk: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text of verse <a name='B:260 5:12'>12.  
**5:13** His face is a garden<br>of sweet-smelling spices;<br>his lips are lilies<br>dripping with perfume.   
**5:14** His arms are branches of gold<br>covered with jewels;<br>his body is ivory<sup>①</sup><br>decorated with sapphires.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>his … ivory: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  
**5:15** His legs are columns of marble<br>on feet of gold.<br>He stands there majestic<br>like Mount Lebanon<br>and its choice cedar trees.  
**5:16** His kisses are sweet.<br>I desire him so much!<br>Young women of Jerusalem,<br>he is my lover and friend.

## 8장

**8:3** Put your left hand under my head<br>and embrace me<br>with your right arm.   
**8:4** Young women of Jerusalem,<br>promise me never to awaken love<br>before it is ready.   
**8:5** Who is this young woman<br>coming in from the desert<br>and leaning on the shoulder<br>of the one she loves? I stirred up your passions<br>under the apple tree<br>where you were born.

**8:13** You are in the garden<br>with friends all around.<br>Let me hear your voice!   
**8:14** Hurry to me, my darling!<br>Run faster than a deer<br>to mountains of spices.
