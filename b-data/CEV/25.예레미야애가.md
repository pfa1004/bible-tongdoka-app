**2:11** My eyes are red from crying,<br>my stomach is in knots,<br>and I feel sick all over.<br>My people are being wiped out,<br>and children lie helpless<br>in the streets of the city.  
**2:12** A child begs its mother<br>for food and drink,<br>then blacks out<br>like a wounded soldier<br>lying in the street.<br>The child slowly dies<br>in its mother's arms.   
**2:13** Zion, how can I comfort you?<br>How great is your pain?<sup>①</sup><br>Lovely city of Jerusalem,<br>how can I heal your wounds,<br>gaping as wide as the sea?<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>How great … pain: </i>Or “What are you really like?” or “What can I say about you?”

**2:15** Those who pass by<br>shake their heads and sneer<br>as they make fun and shout,<br>“What a lovely city you were,<br>the happiest on earth,<br>but look at you now!”   
**2:16** Zion, your enemies curse you<br>and snarl like wild animals,<br>while shouting,<br>“This is the day<br>we've waited for!<br>At last, we've got you!”   
**2:17** The LORD has done everything<br>that he had planned<br>and threatened long ago.<br>He destroyed you without mercy<br>and let your enemies boast<br>about their powerful forces.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>powerful forces: </i>The Hebrew text has “horn,” which refers to the horn of a bull, one of the most powerful animals in ancient Palestine.

**2:19** Get up and pray for help<br>all through the night.<br>Pour out your feelings<br>to the Lord,<br>as you would pour water<br>out of a jug.<br>Beg him to save your people,<br>who are starving to death<br>at every street crossing.

**2:21** My people, both young and old,<br>lie dead in the streets.<br>Because you were angry,<br>my young men and women<br>were brutally slaughtered.  
**2:22** When you were angry, LORD,<br>you invited my enemies<br>like guests for a party.<br>No one survived that day;<br>enemies killed my children,<br>my own little ones.
