# 29.요엘 (CEV)



## 1장

**1:1** I am Joel the son of Pethuel.<br>And this is the message<br>the LORD gave to me.  
**1:2** Listen, you leaders<br>and everyone else<br>in the land.<br>Has anything like this<br>ever happened before?  
**1:3** Tell our children!<br>Let it be told<br>to our grandchildren<br>and their children too.   
**1:4** Swarm after swarm of locusts<sup>①</sup><br>has attacked our crops,<br>eating everything in sight.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Swarm … locusts: </i>The Hebrew text lists either four kinds of locusts or locusts in four stages of their development. Locusts are a type of grasshopper that comes in swarms and causes great damage to plant life.  
**1:5** Sober up, you drunkards!<br>Cry long and loud;<br>your wine supply is gone.  
**1:6**  A powerful nation<sup>①</sup><br>with countless troops<br>has invaded our land.<br>They have the teeth and jaws<br>of powerful lions.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>A powerful nation: </i>The swarms of locusts.  
**1:7** Our grapevines and fig trees<br>are stripped bare;<br>only naked branches remain.   
**1:8** Grieve like a young woman<br>mourning for the man<br>she was to marry.  
**1:9** Offerings of grain and wine<br>are no longer brought<br>to the LORD's temple.<br>His servants, the priests,<br>are deep in sorrow.  
**1:10** Barren fields mourn;<br>grain, grapes, and olives<br>are scorched and shriveled.   
**1:11** Mourn for our farms<br>and our vineyards!<br>There's no wheat or barley<br>growing in our fields.  
**1:12** Grapevines have dried up<br>and so has every tree—<br>figs and pomegranates,<sup>①</sup><br>date palms and apples.<br>All happiness has faded away.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>pomegranates: </i>A bright red fruit that looks like an apple.  
**1:13** Mourn, you priests who serve<br>at the altar of my God.<br>Spend your days and nights<br>wearing sackcloth.<sup>①</sup><br>Offerings of grain and wine<br>are no longer brought<br>to the LORD's temple.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>sackcloth: </i>A rough, dark-colored cloth made from goat or camel hair and used to make grain sacks. It was worn in times of trouble or sorrow.  
**1:14** Tell the leaders and people<br>to come together<br>at the temple.<br>Order them to go without eating<sup>①</sup><br>and to pray sincerely.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>go without eating: </i>As a way of showing sorrow for their sins.  
**1:15**  We are in for trouble!<br>Soon the LORD All-Powerful<br>will bring disaster.  
**1:16** Our food is already gone;<br>there's no more celebrating<br>at the temple of our God.   
**1:17** Seeds dry up in the ground;<sup>①</sup><br>no harvest is possible.<br>Our barns are in bad shape,<br>with no grain<br>to store in them.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Seeds … ground: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  
**1:18** Our cattle wander aimlessly,<br>moaning for lack of pasture,<br>and sheep are suffering.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>sheep are suffering: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  
**1:19** I cry out to you, LORD.<br>Grasslands and forests are eaten<br>by the scorching heat.  
**1:20** Wild animals have no water<br>because of you;<br>rivers and streams are dry,<br>and pastures are parched.  

## 2장

**2:1** Sound the trumpet on Zion,<br>the LORD's sacred hill.<br>Warn everyone to tremble!<br>The judgment day of the LORD<br>is coming soon.  
**2:2** It will be dark and gloomy<br>with storm clouds overhead.<br>Troops will cover the mountains<br>like thunderclouds.<br>No army this powerful<br>has ever been gathered before<br>or will be gathered again.  
**2:3** Fiery flames surround them;<br>no one escapes.<br>Before they invaded,<br>the land was like Eden;<br>now only a desert remains.   
**2:4**  They look like horses<br>and charge like cavalry.  
**2:5** They roar over mountains<br>like noisy chariots,<br>or a mighty army<br>ready for battle.<br>They are a forest fire<br>that feasts on straw.  
**2:6** The very sight of them<br>is frightening.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>The very … frightening: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  
**2:7** They climb over walls<br>like warriors;<br>they march in columns<br>and never turn aside.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>and never turn aside: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  
**2:8** They charge straight ahead,<br>without pushing each other;<br>even arrows and spears<br>cannot make them retreat.  
**2:9** They swarm over city walls<br>and enter our homes;<br>they crawl in through windows,<br>just like thieves.   
**2:10**  They make the earth tremble<br>and the heavens shake;<br>the sun and moon turn dark,<br>and stars stop shining.  
**2:11**  The LORD God leads this army<br>of countless troops,<br>and they obey his commands.<br>The day of his judgment<br>is so terrible<br>that no one can stand it.  
**2:12** The LORD said:<br>It isn't too late.<br>You can still return to me<br>with all your heart.<br>Start crying and mourning!<br>Go without eating.  
**2:13** Don't rip your clothes<br>to show your sorrow.<br>Instead, turn back to me<br>with broken hearts.<br>I am merciful, kind, and caring.<br>I don't easily lose my temper,<br>and I don't like to punish.   
**2:14** I am the LORD your God.<br>Perhaps I will change my mind<br>and treat you with mercy.<br>Then you will be blessed<br>with enough grain and wine<br>for offering sacrifices to me.   
**2:15** Sound the trumpet on Zion!<br>Call the people together.<br>Show your sorrow<br>by going without food.  
**2:16**  Make sure that everyone<br>is fit to worship me.<sup>①</sup><br>Bring adults, children, babies,<br>and even bring newlyweds<br>from their festivities.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>fit to worship me: </i>This required going through certain kinds of ceremonies.  
**2:17**  Tell my servants, the priests,<br>to cry inside the temple<br>and to offer this prayer<br>near the altar:<sup>①</sup><br>“Save your people, LORD God!<br>Don't let foreign nations<br>make jokes about us.<br>Don't let them laugh and ask,<br>‘Where is your God?’ ”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>inside … altar: </i>The Hebrew text has “between the porch and the altar,” which is the place where the priests usually prayed for the people.  
**2:18** The LORD was deeply concerned<br>about his land<br>and had pity on his people.  
**2:19** In answer to their prayers<br>he said,<br>“I will give you enough grain,<br>wine, and olive oil<br>to satisfy your needs.<br>No longer will I let you<br>be insulted by the nations.  
**2:20** An army attacked from the north,<br>but I will chase it<br>into a scorching desert.<br>There it will rot and stink<br>from the Dead Sea<br>to the Mediterranean.” The LORD works wonders  
**2:21** and does great things.<br>So tell the soil to celebrate  
**2:22** and wild animals<br>to stop being afraid.<br>Grasslands are green again;<br>fruit trees and fig trees<br>are loaded with fruit.<br>Grapevines are covered<br>with grapes.   
**2:23** People of Zion,<sup>①</sup><br>celebrate in honor<br>of the LORD your God!<br>He is generous and has sent<br>the autumn and spring rains<br>in the proper seasons.<sup>②</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Zion: </i>Jerusalem. <sup>②</sup><i>in … seasons: </i>Or “as he used to do.”  
**2:24** Grain will cover<br>your threshing places;<br>jars will overflow<br>with wine and olive oil.  
**2:25** I, the LORD your God,<br>will make up for the losses<br>caused by those swarms<br>and swarms of locusts<sup>①</sup><br>I sent to attack you.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>swarms … locusts: </i>See the <a name='C360 1:4'>note at <a name='B:360 1:4'>1.4.  
**2:26** My people, you will eat<br>until you are satisfied.<br>Then you will praise me<br>for the wonderful things<br>I have done.<br>Never again will you<br>be put to shame.   
**2:27** Israel, you will know<br>that I stand at your side.<br>I am the LORD your God—<br>there are no other gods.<br>Never again will you<br>be put to shame.   
**2:28**  Later, I will give my Spirit<br>to everyone.<br>Your sons and daughters<br>will prophesy.<br>Your old men<br>will have dreams,<br>and your young men<br>will see visions.  
**2:29** In those days I will even give<br>my Spirit to my servants,<br>both men and women.   
**2:30** I will work wonders<br>in the sky above<br>and on the earth below.<br>There will be blood and fire<br>and clouds of smoke.  
**2:31**  The sun will turn dark,<br>and the moon<br>will be as red as blood<br>before that great<br>and terrible day<br>when I appear.  
**2:32**  Then I, the LORD will save everyone who faithfully worships me. I have promised there will be survivors on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, and among them will be my chosen ones.  

## 3장

**3:1** At that time I, the LORD, will make Judah and Jerusalem prosperous again.  
**3:2** Then in Judgment Valley<sup>①</sup> I will bring together the nations that scattered my people Israel everywhere in the world, and I will bring charges against those nations. They divided up my land<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Judgment Valley: </i>The Hebrew text has “Jehoshaphat Valley,” which means “Valley of the LORD's Judgment.” This valley is mentioned here and in verse 12, but nowhere else in the Bible.  
**3:3** and gambled to see who would get my people; they sold boys and girls to pay for prostitutes and wine.  
**3:4**  You people of Tyre and Sidon<sup>①</sup> and you Philistines, why are you doing this? Are you trying to get even with me? I'll strike back before you know what's happened.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Tyre and Sidon: </i>Two Phoenician coastal cities.  
**3:5** You've taken my prized possessions, including my silver and gold, and carried them off to your temples.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>temples: </i>Or “palaces.”  
**3:6** You have dragged the people of Judah and Jerusalem from their land and sold them to the Greeks.  
**3:7** But I'll make the people of Judah determined to come home, and what happened to them will happen to you.  
**3:8** I'll hand over your sons and daughters to the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans,<sup>①</sup> who live far away. I, the LORD, have spoken!<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Sabeans: </i>The people of Seba, a region in southwest Arabia.  
**3:9** Say to the nations:<br>“Get ready for war!<br>Be eager to fight.<br>Line up for battle<br>and prepare to attack.  
**3:10**  Make swords out of plows<br>and spears out of garden tools.<br>Strengthen every weakling.”   
**3:11** Hurry, all you nations!<br>Come quickly.<br>Ask the LORD to bring<br>his warriors along.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Ask … along: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.  
**3:12** You must come now<br>to Judgment Valley,<sup>①</sup><br>where the LORD will judge<br>the surrounding nations.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Judgment Valley: </i>See the <a name='C360 3:2'>note at <a name='B:360 3:2'>3.2.  
**3:13**  They are a field of ripe crops.<br>Bring in the harvest!<br>They are grapes piled high.<br>Start trampling them now!<sup>①</sup><br>If our enemy's sins were wine,<br>every jar would overflow.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>grapes … now: </i>People trampled grapes with their bare feet to squeeze out the juice.  
**3:14** Crowds fill Decision Valley.<br>The judgment day of the LORD<br>will soon be here—  
**3:15** no light from the sun or moon,<br>and stars no longer shine.  
**3:16**  From the heart of Jerusalem<br>the LORD roars like a lion,<br>shaking the earth and sky.<br>But the LORD is a fortress,<br>a place of safety<br>for his people Israel.  
**3:17** I am the LORD your God.<br>And you will know I live on Zion,<br>my sacred hill,<br>because Jerusalem will be sacred,<br>untouched by foreign troops.  
**3:18** On that day, fruitful vineyards<br>will cover the mountains.<br>And your cattle and goats<br>that graze on the hills<br>will produce a lot of milk.<br>Streams in Judah<br>will never run dry;<br>a stream from my house<br>will flow in Acacia Valley.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Acacia Valley: </i>In the plains of Moab, northeast of the Dead Sea.  
**3:19** Egypt and Edom were cruel<br>and brutal to Judah,<br>without a reason.<br>Now their countries will become<br>a barren desert,  
**3:20** but Judah and Jerusalem<br>will always have people.  
**3:21** I, the LORD, live on Mount Zion.<br>I will punish the guilty<br>and defend the innocent.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>I will … innocent: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text.
