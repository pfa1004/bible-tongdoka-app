## 2장

**2:1** When Jesus was born in the village of Bethlehem in Judea, Herod was king. During this time some wise men<sup>①</sup> from the east came to Jerusalem<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>wise men: </i>People famous for studying the stars.  
**2:2** and said, “Where is the child born to be king of the Jews? We saw his star in the east<sup>①</sup> and have come to worship him.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>his star in the east: </i>Or “his star rise.”  
**2:3** When King Herod heard about this, he was worried, and so was everyone else in Jerusalem.  
**2:4** Herod brought together the chief priests and the teachers of the Law of Moses and asked them, “Where will the Messiah be born?”  
**2:5** They told him, “He will be born in Bethlehem, just as the prophet wrote,  
**2:6**  ‘Bethlehem in the land<br>of Judea,<br>you are very important<br>among the towns of Judea.<br>From your town<br>will come a leader,<br>who will be like a shepherd<br>for my people Israel.’ ”  
**2:7** Herod secretly called in the wise men and asked them when they had first seen the star.  
**2:8** He told them, “Go to Bethlehem and search carefully for the child. As soon as you find him, let me know. I also want to go and worship him.”  
**2:9** The wise men listened to what the king said and then left. And the star they had seen in the east went on ahead of them until it stopped over the place where the child was.  
**2:10** They were thrilled and excited to see the star.  
**2:11** When the men went into the house and saw the child with Mary, his mother, they knelt down and worshiped him. They took out their gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh<sup>①</sup> and gave them to him.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>frankincense, and myrrh: </i>Frankincense was a valuable powder that was burned to make a sweet smell. Myrrh was a valuable sweet-smelling powder often used in perfume.  
**2:12** Later they were warned in a dream not to return to Herod, and they went back home by another road.  
**2:13** After the wise men had gone, an angel from the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream and said, “Get up! Hurry and take the child and his mother to Egypt! Stay there until I tell you to return, because Herod is looking for the child and wants to kill him.”  
**2:14** That night, Joseph got up and took his wife and the child to Egypt,  
**2:15**  where they stayed until Herod died. So the Lord's promise came true, just as the prophet had said, “I called my son out of Egypt.”  
**2:16** When Herod found out that the wise men from the east had tricked him, he was very angry. He gave orders for his men to kill all the boys who lived in or near Bethlehem and were two years old and younger. This was based on what he had learned from the wise men.  
**2:17** So the Lord's promise came true, just as the prophet Jeremiah had said,  
**2:18**  “In Ramah a voice was heard<br>crying and weeping loudly.<br>Rachel was mourning<br>for her children,<br>and she refused<br>to be comforted,<br>because they were dead.”  
**2:19** After King Herod died, an angel from the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph while he was still in Egypt.  
**2:20** The angel said, “Get up and take the child and his mother back to Israel. The people who wanted to kill him are now dead.”  
**2:21** Joseph got up and left with them for Israel.  
**2:22** But when he heard that Herod's son Archelaus was now ruler of Judea, he was afraid to go there. Then in a dream he was told to go to Galilee,  
**2:23**  and they went to live there in the town of Nazareth. So the Lord's promise came true, just as the prophet had said, “He will be called a Nazarene.”<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>He will be called a Nazarene: </i>The prophet who said this is not known.  

## 3장

**3:1** Years later, John the Baptist started preaching in the desert of Judea.  
**3:2**  He said, “Turn back to God! The kingdom of heaven<sup>①</sup> will soon be here.”<sup>②</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>kingdom of heaven: </i>In the Gospel of Matthew “kingdom of heaven” is used with the same meaning as “God's kingdom” in Mark and Luke. <sup>②</sup><i>will soon be here: </i>Or “is already here.”  
**3:3**  John was the one the prophet Isaiah was talking about, when he said,<br>“In the desert someone<br>is shouting,<br>‘Get the road ready<br>for the Lord!<br>Make a straight path<br>for him.’ ”  
**3:4**  John wore clothes made of camel's hair. He had a leather strap around his waist and ate grasshoppers and wild honey.  
**3:5** From Jerusalem and all Judea and from the Jordan River Valley crowds of people went to John.  
**3:6** They told how sorry they were for their sins, and he baptized them in the river.  
**3:7**  Many Pharisees and Sadducees also came to be baptized. But John said to them: You bunch of snakes! Who warned you to run from the coming judgment?  
**3:8** Do something to show you have really given up your sins.  
**3:9**  And don't start telling yourselves that you belong to Abraham's family. I tell you that God can turn these stones into children for Abraham.  
**3:10**  An ax is ready to cut the trees down at their roots. Any tree that doesn't produce good fruit will be chopped down and thrown into a fire.  
**3:11** I baptize you with water so you will give up your sins.<sup>①</sup> But someone more powerful is going to come, and I am not good enough even to carry his sandals.<sup>②</sup> He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>carry his sandals: </i>This was one of the duties of a slave. <sup>②</sup><i>so you will give up your sins: </i>Or “because you have given up your sins.”  
**3:12**  His threshing fork is in his hand, and he is ready to separate the wheat from the husks.<sup>①</sup> He will store the wheat in a barn and burn the husks in a fire that never goes out.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>His threshing fork is in his hand, and he is ready to separate the wheat from the husks: </i>After Jewish farmers had trampled out the grain, they used a large fork to pitch the grain and the husks into the air. Wind would blow away the light husks, and the grain would fall back to the ground, where it could be gathered up.  
**3:13** Jesus left Galilee and went to the Jordan River to be baptized by John.  
**3:14** But John kept objecting and said, “I ought to be baptized by you. Why have you come to me?”  
**3:15** Jesus answered, <J>“For now this is how it should be, because we must do all God wants us to do.”</J> Then John agreed.  
**3:16** So Jesus was baptized. And as soon as he came out of the water, the sky opened, and he saw the Spirit of God coming down on him like a dove.  
**3:17**  Then a voice from heaven said, “This is my own dear Son, and I am pleased with him.”  

## 4장

**4:1**  The Holy Spirit led Jesus into the desert, so that the devil could test him.  
**4:2** After Jesus had gone without eating<sup>①</sup> for 40 days and nights, he was very hungry.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>without eating: </i>The Jewish people sometimes went without eating (also called “fasting”) to show their love for God or to show sorrow for their sins.  
**4:3** Then the devil came to him and said, “If you are God's Son, tell these stones to turn into bread.”  
**4:4**  Jesus answered, <J>“The Scriptures say:<br>‘No one can live only on food.<br>People need every word<br>that God has spoken.’ ”</J>  
**4:5** Next, the devil took Jesus into the holy city to the highest part of the temple.  
**4:6**  The devil said, “If you are God's Son, jump off. The Scriptures say:<br>‘God will give his angels<br>orders about you.<br>They will catch you<br>in their arms,<br>and you won't hurt<br>your feet on the stones.’ ”  
**4:7**  Jesus answered, <J>“The Scriptures also say, ‘Don't try to test the Lord your God!’ ”</J>  
**4:8** Finally, the devil took Jesus up on a very high mountain and showed him all the kingdoms on earth and their power.  
**4:9** The devil said to him, “I will give all this to you, if you will bow down and worship me.”  
**4:10**  Jesus answered, <J>“Go away Satan! The Scriptures say:<br>‘Worship the Lord your God<br>and serve only him.’ ”</J>  
**4:11** Then the devil left Jesus, and angels came to help him.  
**4:12**  When Jesus heard that John had been put in prison, he went to Galilee.  
**4:13**  But instead of staying in Nazareth, Jesus moved to Capernaum. This town was beside Lake Galilee in the territory of Zebulun and Naphtali.<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Zebulun and Naphtali: </i>In Old Testament times these tribes were in northern Palestine, and in New Testament times many Gentiles lived where these tribes had once been.  
**4:14** So God's promise came true, just as the prophet Isaiah had said,  
**4:15**  “Listen, lands of Zebulun<br>and Naphtali,<br>lands along the road<br>to the sea<br>and across the Jordan.<br>Listen Galilee,<br>land of the Gentiles!  
**4:16** Although your people<br>live in darkness,<br>they will see<br>a bright light.<br>Although they live<br>in the shadow of death,<br>a light will shine<br>on them.”  
**4:17**  Then Jesus started preaching, <J>“Turn back to God! The kingdom of heaven will soon be here.”</J><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>The kingdom of heaven will soon be here: </i>See the two notes at <a name='B:470 3:2'>3.2.  
**4:18** While Jesus was walking along the shore of Lake Galilee, he saw two brothers. One was Simon, also known as Peter, and the other was Andrew. They were fishermen, and they were casting their net into the lake.  
**4:19** Jesus said to them, <J>“Follow me! I will teach you how to bring in people instead of fish.”</J>  
**4:20** Right then the two brothers dropped their nets and went with him.  
**4:21** Jesus walked on until he saw James and John, the sons of Zebedee. They were in a boat with their father, mending their nets. Jesus asked them to come with him.  
**4:22** At once they left the boat and their father and went with Jesus.  
**4:23**  Jesus went all over Galilee, teaching in their synagogues and preaching the good news about God's kingdom. He also healed every kind of disease and sickness.  
**4:24** News about him spread all over Syria, and people with every kind of sickness or disease were brought to him. Some of them had a lot of demons in them, others were thought to be crazy,<sup>①</sup> and still others could not walk. But Jesus healed them all.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>thought to be crazy: </i>In ancient times people with epilepsy were thought to be crazy.  
**4:25** Large crowds followed Jesus from Galilee and the region around the ten cities known as Decapolis.<sup>①</sup> They also came from Jerusalem, Judea, and from across the Jordan River.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>the ten cities known as Decapolis: </i>A group of ten cities east of Samaria and Galilee, where the people followed the Greek way of life.

**5:17** <J>Don't suppose I came to do away with the Law and the Prophets.<sup>①</sup> I did not come to do away with them, but to give them their full meaning.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>the Law and the Prophets: </i>The Jewish Scriptures, that is, the Old Testament.  
**5:18**  <J>Heaven and earth may disappear. But I promise you not even a period or comma will ever disappear from the Law. Everything written in it must happen.</J>  
**5:19** <J>If you reject even the least important command in the Law and teach others to do the same, you will be the least important person in the kingdom of heaven. But if you obey and teach others its commands, you will have an important place in the kingdom.</J>  
**5:20** <J>You must obey God's commands better than the Pharisees and the teachers of the Law obey them. If you don't, I promise you will never get into the kingdom of heaven.</J>  
**5:21**  <J>You know our ancestors were told, “Do not murder” and “A murderer must be brought to trial.”</J>  
**5:22** <J>But I promise you if you are angry with someone,<sup>①</sup> you will have to stand trial. If you call someone a fool, you will be taken to court. And if you say that someone is worthless, you will be in danger of the fires of hell.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>someone: </i>In verses 22-24 the Greek text has “brother,” which may refer to people in general or to other followers.  
**5:23** <J>So if you are about to place your gift on the altar and remember that someone is angry with you,</J>  
**5:24** <J>leave your gift there in front of the altar. Make peace with that person, then come back and offer your gift to God.</J>  
**5:25** <J>Before you are dragged into court, make friends with the person who has accused you of doing wrong. If you don't, you will be handed over to the judge and then to the officer who will put you in jail.</J>  
**5:26** <J>I promise you will not get out until you have paid the last cent you owe.</J>  
**5:27**  <J>You know the commandment which says, “Be faithful in marriage.”</J>  
**5:28** <J>But I tell you if you look at another woman and want her, you are already unfaithful in your thoughts.</J>  
**5:29**  <J>If your right eye causes you to sin, poke it out and throw it away. It is better to lose one part of your body, than for your whole body to end up in hell.</J>  
**5:30**  <J>If your right hand causes you to sin, chop it off and throw it away! It is better to lose one part of your body, than for your whole body to be thrown into hell.</J>

**5:32**  <J>But I tell you not to divorce your wife unless she has committed some terrible sexual sin.<sup>①</sup> If you divorce her, you will cause her to be unfaithful, just as any man who marries her is guilty of taking another man's wife.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>some terrible sexual sin: </i>This probably refers to the laws about the wrong kinds of marriages that are forbidden in <a name='B:30 18:6-18'>Leviticus 18.6-18 or to some serious sexual sin.

**5:35**  <J>The earth is God's footstool, so don't swear by the earth. Jerusalem is the city of the great king, so don't swear by it.</J>  
**5:36** <J>Don't swear by your own head. You cannot make one hair white or black.</J>  
**5:37** <J>When you make a promise, say only “Yes” or “No.” Anything else comes from the devil.</J>  
**5:38**  <J>You know you have been taught, “An eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth.”</J>

**5:40** <J>If someone sues you for your shirt, give up your coat as well.</J>  
**5:41** <J>If a soldier forces you to carry his pack one kilometer, carry it two kilometers.<sup>①</sup> </J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>two kilometers: </i>A Roman soldier had the right to force a person to carry his pack as far as one kilometer.  
**5:42** <J>When people ask you for something, give it to them. When they want to borrow money, lend it to them.</J>  
**5:43**  <J>You have heard people say, “Love your neighbors and hate your enemies.”</J>  
**5:44** <J>But I tell you to love your enemies and pray for anyone who mistreats you.</J>

**5:47** <J>If you greet only your friends, what's so great about this? Don't even unbelievers do that?</J>  
**5:48**  <J>But you must always act like your Father in heaven.</J>  

## 6장

**6:1**  <J>When you do good deeds, don't try to show off. If you do, you won't get a reward from your Father in heaven.</J>  
**6:2** <J>When you give to the poor, don't blow a loud horn. That's what show-offs do in the synagogues and on the street corners, because they are always looking for praise. I can assure you that they already have their reward.</J>  
**6:3** <J>When you give to the poor, don't let anyone know about it.</J><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>don't let anyone know about it: </i>The Greek text has, “Don't let your left hand know what your right hand is doing.”  
**6:4** <J>Then your gift will be given in secret. Your Father knows what is done in secret and will reward you.</J>  
**6:5**  <J>When you pray, don't be like those show-offs who love to stand up and pray in the synagogues and on the street corners. They do this just to look good. I can assure you that they already have their reward.</J>  
**6:6** <J>When you pray, go into a room alone and close the door. Pray to your Father in private. He knows what is done in private and will reward you.</J>  
**6:7**  <J>When you pray, don't talk on and on as people do who don't know God. They think God likes to hear long prayers.</J>  
**6:8** <J>Don't be like them. Your Father knows what you need even before you ask.</J>  
**6:9** <J>You should pray like this:<br>Our Father in heaven,<br>help us to honor<br>your name.</J>  
**6:10** <J>Come and set up<br>your kingdom,<br>so that everyone on earth<br>will obey you,<br>as you are obeyed<br>in heaven.</J>  
**6:11** <J>Give us our food for today.</J><sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>our food for today: </i>Or “the food that we need” or “our food for the coming day.”  
**6:12** <J>Forgive us for doing wrong,<br>as we forgive others.</J>  
**6:13** <J>Keep us from being tempted<sup>①</sup><br>and protect us from evil.</J><sup>②</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>tempted: </i>Or “tested.” <sup>②</sup><i>evil: </i>Or “the evil one,” that is, the devil. Some manuscripts add, “The kingdom, the power, and the glory are yours forever. Amen.”  
**6:14**   <J>If you forgive others for the wrongs they do to you, your Father in heaven will forgive you.</J>  
**6:15** <J>But if you don't forgive others, your Father will not forgive your sins.</J>  
**6:16** <J>When you go without eating,<sup>①</sup> don't try to look gloomy as those show-offs do when they go without eating. I can assure you that they already have their reward.</J><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>without eating: </i>See the <a name='C470 4:2'>note at <a name='B:470 4:2'>4.2.  
**6:17**  <J>Instead, comb your hair and wash your face.</J>  
**6:18** <J>Then others won't know you are going without eating. But your Father sees what is done in private, and he will reward you.</J>  
**6:19**  <J>Don't store up treasures on earth! Moths and rust can destroy them, and thieves can break in and steal them.</J>  
**6:20**  <J>Instead, store up your treasures in heaven, where moths and rust cannot destroy them, and thieves cannot break in and steal them.</J>  
**6:21** <J>Your heart will always be where your treasure is.</J>  
**6:22** <J>Your eyes are a window for your body. When they are good, you have all the light you need.</J>  
**6:23** <J>But when your eyes are bad, everything is dark. If the light inside you is dark, you surely are in the dark.</J>  
**6:24** <J>You cannot be the slave of two masters! You will like one more than the other or be more loyal to one than the other. You cannot serve both God and money.</J>

## 28장

**28:1** The Sabbath was over, and it was almost daybreak on Sunday when Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to see the tomb.  
**28:2** Suddenly a strong earthquake struck, and the Lord's angel came down from heaven. He rolled away the stone and sat on it.  
**28:3** The angel looked as bright as lightning, and his clothes were white as snow.  
**28:4** The guards shook from fear and fell down, as though they were dead.  
**28:5** The angel said to the women, “Don't be afraid! I know you are looking for Jesus, who was nailed to a cross.  
**28:6** He isn't here! God has raised him to life, just as Jesus said he would. Come, see the place where his body was lying.  
**28:7** Now hurry! Tell his disciples he has been raised to life and is on his way to Galilee. Go there, and you will see him. This is what I came to tell you.”  
**28:8** The women were frightened and yet very happy, as they hurried from the tomb and ran to tell his disciples.  
**28:9** Suddenly Jesus met them and greeted them. They went near him, held on to his feet, and worshiped him.  
**28:10** Then Jesus said, <J>“Don't be afraid! Tell my followers to go to Galilee. They will see me there.”</J>  
**28:11** While the women were on their way, some soldiers who had been guarding the tomb went into the city. They told the chief priests everything that had happened.  
**28:12** So the chief priests met with the leaders and decided to bribe the soldiers with a lot of money.  
**28:13** They said to the soldiers, “Tell everyone that Jesus' disciples came during the night and stole his body while you were asleep.  
**28:14** If the governor<sup>①</sup> hears about this, we will talk to him. You won't have anything to worry about.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>governor: </i>Pontius Pilate.  
**28:15** The soldiers took the money and did what they were told. The people of Judea still tell each other this story.  
**28:16**  Jesus' eleven disciples went to a mountain in Galilee, where Jesus had told them to meet him.  
**28:17** They saw him and worshiped him, but some of them doubted.  
**28:18** Jesus came to them and said: <J>I have been given all authority in heaven and on earth!</J>  
**28:19**  <J>Go to the people of all nations and make them my disciples. Baptize them in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit,</J>  
**28:20** <J>and teach them to do everything I have told you. I will be with you always, even until the end of the world.</J>
