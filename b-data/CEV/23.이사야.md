## 1장

**1:1**  I am Isaiah, the son of Amoz. **1:3** Oxen and donkeys know<br>who owns and feeds them,<br>but my people won't ever learn.”

**1:5** Why be punished more?<br>Why not give up your sin?<br>Your head is badly bruised,<br>and you are weak all over.

**1:7** Your country lies in ruins;<br>your towns are in ashes.<br>Foreigners and strangers<br>take and destroy your land<br>while you watch.

**1:14** I hate your New Moon Festivals<br>and all others as well.<br>They are a heavy burden<br>I am tired of carrying.   
**1:15** “No matter how much you pray,<br>I won't listen.<br>You are too violent.  
**1:16** Wash yourselves clean!<br>I hate your filthy deeds.<br>Stop doing wrong  
**1:17** and learn to live right.<br>See that justice is done.<br>Defend widows and orphans<br>and help the oppressed.”<sup>①</sup><br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>and help the oppressed: </i>Or “and punish cruel people.”  
**1:18** I, the LORD, invite you<br>to come and talk it over.<br>Your sins are scarlet red,<br>but they will be whiter<br>than snow or wool.  
**1:19** If you willingly obey me,<br>the best crops in the land<br>will be yours.  
**1:20** But if you turn against me,<br>your enemies will kill you.<br>I, the LORD, have spoken.  
**1:21** Jerusalem, you are like<br>an unfaithful wife.<br>Once your judges were honest<br>and your people lived right;<br>now you are a city<br>full of murderers.  
**1:22** Your silver is fake,<br>and your wine<br>is watered down.  
**1:23** Your leaders have rejected me<br>to become friends of crooks;<br>your rulers are looking<br>for gifts and bribes.<br>Widows and orphans<br>never get a fair trial.   
**1:24** I am the LORD All-Powerful,<br>the mighty ruler of Israel,<br>and I make you a promise:<br>You are now my enemy,<br>and I will show my anger<br>by taking revenge on you.  
**1:25** I will punish you terribly<br>and burn away everything<br>that makes you unfit<br>to worship me.  
**1:26** Jerusalem, I will choose<br>judges and advisors<br>like those you had before.<br>Your new name will be<br>“Justice and Faithfulness.”  
**1:27** Jerusalem, you will be saved<br>by showing justice;<sup>①</sup><br>Zion's people who turn to me<br>will be saved<br>by doing right.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>by showing justice: </i>Or “by my saving power.”

**1:29** You will be made ashamed<br>of those groves of trees<br>where you worshiped idols.  
**1:30** You will be like a grove of trees<br>dying in a drought.  
**1:31** Your strongest leaders<br>will be like dry wood<br>set on fire by their idols.<sup>①</sup><br>No one will be able to help,<br>as they all go up in flames.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Your … idols: </i>Or “Your wealth will be like dry wood, set on fire by its owners.”
