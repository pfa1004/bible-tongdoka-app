# 31.오바디야 (CEV)



## 1장

**1:1**  The LORD God gave Obadiah<br>a message<sup>①</sup> about Edom,<br>and this is what we heard:<br>“I, the LORD, have sent<br>a messenger<br>with orders for the nations<br>to attack Edom.”<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>message: </i>Or “vision.”  
**1:2** The LORD said to Edom:<br>I will make you the weakest<br>and most despised nation.  
**1:3** You live in a mountain fortress,<sup>①</sup><br>because your pride<br>makes you feel safe from attack,<br>but you are mistaken.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>mountain fortress: </i>The Hebrew text has “rocky cliff,” which sounds like “Sela,” the capital of Edom, a fortress city built on a mountain.  
**1:4** I will still bring you down,<br>even if you fly higher<br>than an eagle<br>or nest among the stars.<br>I, the LORD, have spoken!   
**1:5** If thieves break in at night,<br>they steal<br>only what they want.<br>And people who harvest grapes<br>always leave some unpicked.<br>But, Edom, you are doomed!  
**1:6** Everything you treasure most<br>will be taken from you.  
**1:7** Your allies can't be trusted.<br>They will force you out<br>of your own country.<br>And your best friends<br>will trick and trap you,<br>even before you know it.   
**1:8** Edom, when this happens,<br>I, the LORD, will destroy<br>all your marvelous wisdom.  
**1:9** Warriors from the city of Teman<sup>①</sup><br>will be terrified,<br>and you descendants of Esau<sup>②</sup><br>will be wiped out.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Teman: </i>A famous city in Edom. <sup>②</sup><i>descendants of Esau: </i>The people of Edom were descendants of Esau, the brother of Jacob (Israel).  
**1:10** You were cruel to your relatives,<br>the descendants of Jacob.<sup>①</sup><br>Now you will be destroyed,<br>disgraced forever.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>descendants of Jacob: </i>Jacob and Esau were brothers (see the <a name='C380 1:9'>note on Esau at verse <a name='B:380 1:9'>9).  
**1:11** You stood there and watched<br>as foreigners entered Jerusalem<br>and took what they wanted.<br>In fact, you were no better<br>than those foreigners.   
**1:12** Why did you celebrate<br>when such a dreadful disaster<br>struck your relatives?<br>Why were you so pleased<br>when everyone in Judah<br>was suffering?  
**1:13** They are my people,<br>and you were cruel to them.<br>You went through their towns,<br>sneering and stealing<br>whatever was left.  
**1:14** In their time of torment,<br>you ambushed refugees<br>and handed them over<br>to their attackers.  
**1:15** The day is coming<br>when I, the LORD,<br>will judge the nations.<br>And, Edom, you will pay in full<br>for what you have done.   
**1:16** I forced the people of Judah<sup>①</sup><br>to drink the wine of my anger<br>on my sacred mountain.<br>Soon the neighboring nations<br>must drink their fill—<br>then vanish without a trace.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>I forced … Judah: </i>Or “I will force the people of Edom.”  
**1:17** The LORD's people who escape<br>will go to Mount Zion,<br>and it will be holy.<br>Then Jacob's descendants<br>will capture the land of those<br>who took their land.  
**1:18** Israel<sup>①</sup> will be a fire,<br>and Edom will be straw<br>going up in flames.<br>The LORD has spoken!<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Israel: </i>Hebrew “The descendants of Jacob and of Joseph.”  
**1:19** The people of Israel<br>who live in the Southern Desert<br>will take the land of Edom.<br>Those who live in the hills<br>will capture Philistia,<br>Ephraim, and Samaria.<br>And the tribe of Benjamin<br>will conquer Gilead.   
**1:20** Those who return from captivity<br>will control Phoenicia<br>as far as Zarephath.<sup>①</sup><br>Captives from Jerusalem<br>who were taken to Sepharad<sup>②</sup><br>will capture the towns<br>of the Southern Desert.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Those who return … Zarephath: </i>One possible meaning for the difficult Hebrew text. <sup>②</sup><i>Sepharad: </i>Possibly the city of Sardis, the capital of Lydia, a country north and west of Media. This would refer to those captives from Judah who had been taken beyond the kingdom of Babylonia.  
**1:21** Those the LORD has saved<br>will live on Mount Zion<br>and rule over Edom.<sup>①</sup><br>Then the kingdom will belong<br>to the LORD.<br>▷ <sup>①</sup><i>Those the LORD … Edom: </i>Or “Leaders on (from) Mount Zion will save the people and rule over Edom.”
