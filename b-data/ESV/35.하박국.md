# 35.하박국 (ESV)



## 1장

**1:1** The oracle that Habakkuk the prophet saw.  
**1:2** O LORD, how long shall I cry for help,<br>and you will not hear?<br>Or cry to you “Violence!”<br>and you will not save?  
**1:3** Why do you make me see iniquity,<br>and why do you idly look at wrong?<br>Destruction and violence are before me;<br>strife and contention arise.  
**1:4** So the law is paralyzed,<br>and justice never goes forth.<br>For the wicked surround the righteous;<br>so justice goes forth perverted.   
**1:5** “Look among the nations, and see;<br>wonder and be astounded.<br>For I am doing a work in your days<br>that you would not believe if told.  
**1:6** For behold, I am raising up the Chaldeans,<br>that bitter and hasty nation,<br>who march through the breadth of the earth,<br>to seize dwellings not their own.  
**1:7** They are dreaded and fearsome;<br>their justice and dignity go forth from themselves.  
**1:8** Their horses are swifter than leopards,<br>more fierce than the evening wolves;<br>their horsemen press proudly on.<br>Their horsemen come from afar;<br>they fly like an eagle swift to devour.  
**1:9** They all come for violence,<br>all their faces forward.<br>They gather captives like sand.  
**1:10** At kings they scoff,<br>and at rulers they laugh.<br>They laugh at every fortress,<br>for they pile up earth and take it.  
**1:11** Then they sweep by like the wind and go on,<br>guilty men, whose own might is their god!”   
**1:12** Are you not from everlasting,<br>O LORD my God, my Holy One?<br>We shall not die.<br>O LORD, you have ordained them as a judgment,<br>and you, O Rock, have established them for reproof.  
**1:13** You who are of purer eyes than to see evil<br>and cannot look at wrong,<br>why do you idly look at traitors<br>and remain silent when the wicked swallows up<br>the man more righteous than he?  
**1:14** You make mankind like the fish of the sea,<br>like crawling things that have no ruler.  
**1:15** He<sup>①</sup> brings all of them up with a hook;<br>he drags them out with his net;<br>he gathers them in his dragnet;<br>so he rejoices and is glad.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>That is, the wicked foe  
**1:16** Therefore he sacrifices to his net<br>and makes offerings to his dragnet;<br>for by them he lives in luxury,<sup>①</sup><br>and his food is rich.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>his portion is fat</i>  
**1:17** Is he then to keep on emptying his net<br>and mercilessly killing nations forever?   

## 2장

**2:1** I will take my stand at my watchpost<br>and station myself on the tower,<br>and look out to see what he will say to me,<br>and what I will answer concerning my complaint.   
**2:2** And the LORD answered me:<br>“Write the vision;<br>make it plain on tablets,<br>so he may run who reads it.  
**2:3** For still the vision awaits its appointed time;<br>it hastens to the end—it will not lie.<br>If it seems slow, wait for it;<br>it will surely come; it will not delay.   
**2:4** “Behold, his soul is puffed up; it is not upright within him,<br>but the righteous shall live by his faith.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>faithfulness</i>  
**2:5** “Moreover, wine<sup>①</sup> is a traitor,<br>an arrogant man who is never at rest.<sup>②</sup><br>His greed is as wide as Sheol;<br>like death he has never enough.<br>He gathers for himself all nations<br>and collects as his own all peoples.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Masoretic Text; Dead Sea Scroll <i>wealth</i> <sup>②</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew of these two lines is uncertain  
**2:6** Shall not all these take up their taunt against him, with scoffing and riddles for him, and say,<br>“Woe to him who heaps up what is not his own—<br>for how long?—<br>and loads himself with pledges!”  
**2:7** Will not your debtors suddenly arise,<br>and those awake who will make you tremble?<br>Then you will be spoil for them.  
**2:8** Because you have plundered many nations,<br>all the remnant of the peoples shall plunder you,<br>for the blood of man and violence to the earth,<br>to cities and all who dwell in them.   
**2:9** “Woe to him who gets evil gain for his house,<br>to set his nest on high,<br>to be safe from the reach of harm!  
**2:10** You have devised shame for your house<br>by cutting off many peoples;<br>you have forfeited your life.  
**2:11** For the stone will cry out from the wall,<br>and the beam from the woodwork respond.   
**2:12** “Woe to him who builds a town with blood<br>and founds a city on iniquity!  
**2:13** Behold, is it not from the LORD of hosts<br>that peoples labor merely for fire,<br>and nations weary themselves for nothing?  
**2:14** For the earth will be filled<br>with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD<br>as the waters cover the sea.   
**2:15** “Woe to him who makes his neighbors drink—<br>you pour out your wrath and make them drunk,<br>in order to gaze at their nakedness!  
**2:16** You will have your fill of shame instead of glory.<br>Drink, yourself, and show your uncircumcision!<br>The cup in the LORD's right hand<br>will come around to you,<br>and utter shame will come upon your glory!  
**2:17** The violence done to Lebanon will overwhelm you,<br>as will the destruction of the beasts that terrified them,<br>for the blood of man and violence to the earth,<br>to cities and all who dwell in them.   
**2:18** “What profit is an idol<br>when its maker has shaped it,<br>a metal image, a teacher of lies?<br>For its maker trusts in his own creation<br>when he makes speechless idols!  
**2:19** Woe to him who says to a wooden thing, Awake;<br>to a silent stone, Arise!<br>Can this teach?<br>Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver,<br>and there is no breath at all in it.  
**2:20** But the LORD is in his holy temple;<br>let all the earth keep silence before him.”   

## 3장

**3:1** A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, according to Shigionoth.  
**3:2** O LORD, I have heard the report of you,<br>and your work, O LORD, do I fear.<br>In the midst of the years revive it;<br>in the midst of the years make it known;<br>in wrath remember mercy.  
**3:3** God came from Teman,<br>and the Holy One from Mount Paran. <i>Selah</i><br>His splendor covered the heavens,<br>and the earth was full of his praise.  
**3:4** His brightness was like the light;<br>rays flashed from his hand;<br>and there he veiled his power.  
**3:5** Before him went pestilence,<br>and plague followed at his heels.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>feet</i>  
**3:6** He stood and measured the earth;<br>he looked and shook the nations;<br>then the eternal mountains were scattered;<br>the everlasting hills sank low.<br>His were the everlasting ways.  
**3:7** I saw the tents of Cushan in affliction;<br>the curtains of the land of Midian did tremble.  
**3:8** Was your wrath against the rivers, O LORD?<br>Was your anger against the rivers,<br>or your indignation against the sea,<br>when you rode on your horses,<br>on your chariot of salvation?  
**3:9** You stripped the sheath from your bow,<br>calling for many arrows.<sup>①</sup> <i>Selah</i><br>You split the earth with rivers.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew line is uncertain  
**3:10** The mountains saw you and writhed;<br>the raging waters swept on;<br>the deep gave forth its voice;<br>it lifted its hands on high.  
**3:11** The sun and moon stood still in their place<br>at the light of your arrows as they sped,<br>at the flash of your glittering spear.  
**3:12** You marched through the earth in fury;<br>you threshed the nations in anger.  
**3:13** You went out for the salvation of your people,<br>for the salvation of your anointed.<br>You crushed the head of the house of the wicked,<br>laying him bare from thigh to neck.<sup>①</sup> <i>Selah</i><br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew line is uncertain  
**3:14** You pierced with his own arrows the heads of his warriors,<br>who came like a whirlwind to scatter me,<br>rejoicing as if to devour the poor in secret.  
**3:15** You trampled the sea with your horses,<br>the surging of mighty waters.   
**3:16** I hear, and my body trembles;<br>my lips quiver at the sound;<br>rottenness enters into my bones;<br>my legs tremble beneath me.<br>Yet I will quietly wait for the day of trouble<br>to come upon people who invade us.   
**3:17** Though the fig tree should not blossom,<br>nor fruit be on the vines,<br>the produce of the olive fail<br>and the fields yield no food,<br>the flock be cut off from the fold<br>and there be no herd in the stalls,  
**3:18** yet I will rejoice in the LORD;<br>I will take joy in the God of my salvation.  
**3:19** GOD, the Lord, is my strength;<br>he makes my feet like the deer's;<br>he makes me tread on my high places.<br>To the choirmaster: with stringed<sup>①</sup> instruments.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>my stringed</i>
