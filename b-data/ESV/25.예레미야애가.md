# 25.예레미야애가 (ESV)



## 1장

**1:1** How lonely sits the city<br>that was full of people!<br>How like a widow has she become,<br>she who was great among the nations!<br>She who was a princess among the provinces<br>has become a slave.   
**1:2** She weeps bitterly in the night,<br>with tears on her cheeks;<br>among all her lovers<br>she has none to comfort her;<br>all her friends have dealt treacherously with her;<br>they have become her enemies.   
**1:3** Judah has gone into exile because of affliction<sup>①</sup><br>and hard servitude;<br>she dwells now among the nations,<br>but finds no resting place;<br>her pursuers have all overtaken her<br>in the midst of her distress.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>under affliction</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>in the narrow passes</i>  
**1:4** The roads to Zion mourn,<br>for none come to the festival;<br>all her gates are desolate;<br>her priests groan;<br>her virgins have been afflicted,<sup>①</sup><br>and she herself suffers bitterly.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Septuagint, Old Latin <i>dragged away</i>  
**1:5** Her foes have become the head;<br>her enemies prosper,<br>because the LORD has afflicted her<br>for the multitude of her transgressions;<br>her children have gone away,<br>captives before the foe.   
**1:6** From the daughter of Zion<br>all her majesty has departed.<br>Her princes have become like deer<br>that find no pasture;<br>they fled without strength<br>before the pursuer.   
**1:7** Jerusalem remembers<br>in the days of her affliction and wandering<br>all the precious things<br>that were hers from days of old.<br>When her people fell into the hand of the foe,<br>and there was none to help her,<br>her foes gloated over her;<br>they mocked at her downfall.   
**1:8** Jerusalem sinned grievously;<br>therefore she became filthy;<br>all who honored her despise her,<br>for they have seen her nakedness;<br>she herself groans<br>and turns her face away.   
**1:9** Her uncleanness was in her skirts;<br>she took no thought of her future;<sup>①</sup><br>therefore her fall is terrible;<br>she has no comforter.<br>“O LORD, behold my affliction,<br>for the enemy has triumphed!”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>end</i>  
**1:10** The enemy has stretched out his hands<br>over all her precious things;<br>for she has seen the nations<br>enter her sanctuary,<br>those whom you forbade<br>to enter your congregation.   
**1:11** All her people groan<br>as they search for bread;<br>they trade their treasures for food<br>to revive their strength.<br>“Look, O LORD, and see,<br>for I am despised.”   
**1:12** “Is it nothing to you, all you who pass by?<br>Look and see<br>if there is any sorrow like my sorrow,<br>which was brought upon me,<br>which the LORD inflicted<br>on the day of his fierce anger.   
**1:13** “From on high he sent fire;<br>into my bones<sup>①</sup> he made it descend;<br>he spread a net for my feet;<br>he turned me back;<br>he has left me stunned,<br>faint all the day long.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Septuagint; Hebrew <i>bones and</i>  
**1:14** “My transgressions were bound<sup>①</sup> into a yoke;<br>by his hand they were fastened together;<br>they were set upon my neck;<br>he caused my strength to fail;<br>the Lord gave me into the hands<br>of those whom I cannot withstand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain  
**1:15** “The Lord rejected<br>all my mighty men in my midst;<br>he summoned an assembly against me<br>to crush my young men;<br>the Lord has trodden as in a winepress<br>the virgin daughter of Judah.   
**1:16** “For these things I weep;<br>my eyes flow with tears;<br>for a comforter is far from me,<br>one to revive my spirit;<br>my children are desolate,<br>for the enemy has prevailed.”   
**1:17** Zion stretches out her hands,<br>but there is none to comfort her;<br>the LORD has commanded against Jacob<br>that his neighbors should be his foes;<br>Jerusalem has become<br>a filthy thing among them.   
**1:18** “The LORD is in the right,<br>for I have rebelled against his word;<br>but hear, all you peoples,<br>and see my suffering;<br>my young women and my young men<br>have gone into captivity.   
**1:19** “I called to my lovers,<br>but they deceived me;<br>my priests and elders<br>perished in the city,<br>while they sought food<br>to revive their strength.   
**1:20** “Look, O LORD, for I am in distress;<br>my stomach churns;<br>my heart is wrung within me,<br>because I have been very rebellious.<br>In the street the sword bereaves;<br>in the house it is like death.   
**1:21** “They heard<sup>①</sup> my groaning,<br>yet there is no one to comfort me.<br>All my enemies have heard of my trouble;<br>they are glad that you have done it.<br>You have brought<sup>②</sup> the day you announced;<br>now let them be as I am.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Septuagint, Syriac <i>Hear</i> <sup>②</sup>Syriac <i>Bring</i>  
**1:22** “Let all their evildoing come before you,<br>and deal with them<br>as you have dealt with me<br>because of all my transgressions;<br>for my groans are many,<br>and my heart is faint.”   

## 2장

**2:1** How the Lord in his anger<br>has set the daughter of Zion under a cloud!<br>He has cast down from heaven to earth<br>the splendor of Israel;<br>he has not remembered his footstool<br>in the day of his anger.   
**2:2** The Lord has swallowed up without mercy<br>all the habitations of Jacob;<br>in his wrath he has broken down<br>the strongholds of the daughter of Judah;<br>he has brought down to the ground in dishonor<br>the kingdom and its rulers.   
**2:3** He has cut down in fierce anger<br>all the might of Israel;<br>he has withdrawn from them his right hand<br>in the face of the enemy;<br>he has burned like a flaming fire in Jacob,<br>consuming all around.   
**2:4** He has bent his bow like an enemy,<br>with his right hand set like a foe;<br>and he has killed all who were delightful in our eyes;<br>in the tent of the daughter of Zion,<br>he has poured out his fury like fire.   
**2:5** The Lord has become like an enemy;<br>he has swallowed up Israel;<br>he has swallowed up all its palaces;<br>he has laid in ruins its strongholds,<br>and he has multiplied in the daughter of Judah<br>mourning and lamentation.   
**2:6** He has laid waste his booth like a garden,<br>laid in ruins his meeting place;<br>the LORD has made Zion forget<br>festival and Sabbath,<br>and in his fierce indignation has spurned king and priest.   
**2:7** The Lord has scorned his altar,<br>disowned his sanctuary;<br>he has delivered into the hand of the enemy<br>the walls of her palaces;<br>they raised a clamor in the house of the LORD<br>as on the day of festival.   
**2:8** The LORD determined to lay in ruins<br>the wall of the daughter of Zion;<br>he stretched out the measuring line;<br>he did not restrain his hand from destroying;<br>he caused rampart and wall to lament;<br>they languished together.   
**2:9** Her gates have sunk into the ground;<br>he has ruined and broken her bars;<br>her king and princes are among the nations;<br>the law is no more,<br>and her prophets find<br>no vision from the LORD.   
**2:10** The elders of the daughter of Zion<br>sit on the ground in silence;<br>they have thrown dust on their heads<br>and put on sackcloth;<br>the young women of Jerusalem<br>have bowed their heads to the ground.   
**2:11** My eyes are spent with weeping;<br>my stomach churns;<br>my bile is poured out to the ground<br>because of the destruction of the daughter of my people,<br>because infants and babies faint<br>in the streets of the city.   
**2:12** They cry to their mothers,<br>“Where is bread and wine?”<br>as they faint like a wounded man<br>in the streets of the city,<br>as their life is poured out<br>on their mothers' bosom.   
**2:13** What can I say for you, to what compare you,<br>O daughter of Jerusalem?<br>What can I liken to you, that I may comfort you,<br>O virgin daughter of Zion?<br>For your ruin is vast as the sea;<br>who can heal you?   
**2:14** Your prophets have seen for you<br>false and deceptive visions;<br>they have not exposed your iniquity<br>to restore your fortunes,<br>but have seen for you oracles<br>that are false and misleading.   
**2:15** All who pass along the way<br>clap their hands at you;<br>they hiss and wag their heads<br>at the daughter of Jerusalem:<br>“Is this the city that was called<br>the perfection of beauty,<br>the joy of all the earth?”   
**2:16** All your enemies<br>rail against you;<br>they hiss, they gnash their teeth,<br>they cry: “We have swallowed her!<br>Ah, this is the day we longed for;<br>now we have it; we see it!”   
**2:17** The LORD has done what he purposed;<br>he has carried out his word,<br>which he commanded long ago;<br>he has thrown down without pity;<br>he has made the enemy rejoice over you<br>and exalted the might of your foes.   
**2:18** Their heart cried to the Lord.<br>O wall of the daughter of Zion,<br>let tears stream down like a torrent<br>day and night!<br>Give yourself no rest,<br>your eyes no respite!   
**2:19** “Arise, cry out in the night,<br>at the beginning of the night watches!<br>Pour out your heart like water<br>before the presence of the Lord!<br>Lift your hands to him<br>for the lives of your children,<br>who faint for hunger<br>at the head of every street.”   
**2:20** Look, O LORD, and see!<br>With whom have you dealt thus?<br>Should women eat the fruit of their womb,<br>the children of their tender care?<br>Should priest and prophet be killed<br>in the sanctuary of the Lord?   
**2:21** In the dust of the streets<br>lie the young and the old;<br>my young women and my young men<br>have fallen by the sword;<br>you have killed them in the day of your anger,<br>slaughtering without pity.   
**2:22** You summoned as if to a festival day<br>my terrors on every side,<br>and on the day of the anger of the LORD<br>no one escaped or survived;<br>those whom I held and raised<br>my enemy destroyed.   

## 3장

**3:1** I am the man who has seen affliction<br>under the rod of his wrath;  
**3:2** he has driven and brought me<br>into darkness without any light;  
**3:3** surely against me he turns his hand<br>again and again the whole day long.   
**3:4** He has made my flesh and my skin waste away;<br>he has broken my bones;  
**3:5** he has besieged and enveloped me<br>with bitterness and tribulation;  
**3:6** he has made me dwell in darkness<br>like the dead of long ago.   
**3:7** He has walled me about so that I cannot escape;<br>he has made my chains heavy;  
**3:8** though I call and cry for help,<br>he shuts out my prayer;  
**3:9** he has blocked my ways with blocks of stones;<br>he has made my paths crooked.   
**3:10** He is a bear lying in wait for me,<br>a lion in hiding;  
**3:11** he turned aside my steps and tore me to pieces;<br>he has made me desolate;  
**3:12** he bent his bow and set me<br>as a target for his arrow.   
**3:13** He drove into my kidneys<br>the arrows of his quiver;  
**3:14** I have become the laughingstock of all my peoples,<sup>①</sup><br>the object of their taunts all day long.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Some manuscripts <i>all peoples</i>  
**3:15** He has filled me with bitterness;<br>he has sated me with wormwood.   
**3:16** He has made my teeth grind on gravel,<br>and made me cower in ashes;  
**3:17** my soul is bereft of peace;<br>I have forgotten what happiness<sup>①</sup> is;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>good</i>  
**3:18** so I say, “My endurance has perished;<br>so has my hope from the LORD.”   
**3:19** Remember my affliction and my wanderings,<br>the wormwood and the gall!  
**3:20** My soul continually remembers it<br>and is bowed down within me.  
**3:21** But this I call to mind,<br>and therefore I have hope:   
**3:22** The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases;<sup>①</sup><br>his mercies never come to an end;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Syriac, Targum; Hebrew <i>Because of the steadfast love of the LORD, we are not cut off</i>  
**3:23** they are new every morning;<br>great is your faithfulness.  
**3:24** “The LORD is my portion,” says my soul,<br>“therefore I will hope in him.”   
**3:25** The LORD is good to those who wait for him,<br>to the soul who seeks him.  
**3:26** It is good that one should wait quietly<br>for the salvation of the LORD.  
**3:27** It is good for a man that he bear<br>the yoke in his youth.   
**3:28** Let him sit alone in silence<br>when it is laid on him;  
**3:29** let him put his mouth in the dust—<br>there may yet be hope;  
**3:30** let him give his cheek to the one who strikes,<br>and let him be filled with insults.   
**3:31** For the Lord will not<br>cast off forever,  
**3:32** for, though he cause grief, he will have compassion<br>according to the abundance of his steadfast love;  
**3:33** for he does not afflict from his heart<br>or grieve the children of men.   
**3:34** To crush underfoot<br>all the prisoners of the earth,  
**3:35** to deny a man justice<br>in the presence of the Most High,  
**3:36** to subvert a man in his lawsuit,<br>the Lord does not approve.   
**3:37** Who has spoken and it came to pass,<br>unless the Lord has commanded it?  
**3:38** Is it not from the mouth of the Most High<br>that good and bad come?  
**3:39** Why should a living man complain,<br>a man, about the punishment of his sins?   
**3:40** Let us test and examine our ways,<br>and return to the LORD!  
**3:41** Let us lift up our hearts and hands<br>to God in heaven:  
**3:42** “We have transgressed and rebelled,<br>and you have not forgiven.   
**3:43** “You have wrapped yourself with anger and pursued us,<br>killing without pity;  
**3:44** you have wrapped yourself with a cloud<br>so that no prayer can pass through.  
**3:45** You have made us scum and garbage<br>among the peoples.   
**3:46** “All our enemies<br>open their mouths against us;  
**3:47** panic and pitfall have come upon us,<br>devastation and destruction;  
**3:48** my eyes flow with rivers of tears<br>because of the destruction of the daughter of my people.   
**3:49** “My eyes will flow without ceasing,<br>without respite,  
**3:50** until the LORD from heaven<br>looks down and sees;  
**3:51** my eyes cause me grief<br>at the fate of all the daughters of my city.   
**3:52** “I have been hunted like a bird<br>by those who were my enemies without cause;  
**3:53** they flung me alive into the pit<sup>①</sup><br>and cast stones on me;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>they end my life in the pit</i>  
**3:54** water closed over my head;<br>I said, ‘I am lost.’   
**3:55** “I called on your name, O LORD,<br>from the depths of the pit;  
**3:56** you heard my plea, ‘Do not close<br>your ear to my cry for help!’  
**3:57** You came near when I called on you;<br>you said, ‘Do not fear!’   
**3:58** “You have taken up my cause, O Lord;<br>you have redeemed my life.  
**3:59** You have seen the wrong done to me, O LORD;<br>judge my cause.  
**3:60** You have seen all their vengeance,<br>all their plots against me.   
**3:61** “You have heard their taunts, O LORD,<br>all their plots against me.  
**3:62** The lips and thoughts of my assailants<br>are against me all the day long.  
**3:63** Behold their sitting and their rising;<br>I am the object of their taunts.   
**3:64** “You will repay them,<sup>①</sup> O LORD,<br>according to the work of their hands.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Repay them</i>  
**3:65** You will give them<sup>①</sup> dullness of heart;<br>your curse will be<sup>②</sup> on them.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Give them</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>place your curse</i>  
**3:66** You will pursue them<sup>①</sup> in anger and destroy them<br>from under your heavens, O LORD.”<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Pursue them</i> <sup>②</sup>Syriac (compare Septuagint, Vulgate); Hebrew <i>the heavens of the LORD</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** How the gold has grown dim,<br>how the pure gold is changed!<br>The holy stones lie scattered<br>at the head of every street.   
**4:2** The precious sons of Zion,<br>worth their weight in fine gold,<br>how they are regarded as earthen pots,<br>the work of a potter's hands!   
**4:3** Even jackals offer the breast;<br>they nurse their young;<br>but the daughter of my people has become cruel,<br>like the ostriches in the wilderness.   
**4:4** The tongue of the nursing infant sticks<br>to the roof of its mouth for thirst;<br>the children beg for food,<br>but no one gives to them.   
**4:5** Those who once feasted on delicacies<br>perish in the streets;<br>those who were brought up in purple<br>embrace ash heaps.   
**4:6** For the chastisement<sup>①</sup> of the daughter of my people has been greater<br>than the punishment<sup>②</sup> of Sodom,<br>which was overthrown in a moment,<br>and no hands were wrung for her.<sup>③</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>iniquity</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>sin</i> <sup>③</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain  
**4:7** Her princes were purer than snow,<br>whiter than milk;<br>their bodies were more ruddy than coral,<br>the beauty of their form<sup>①</sup> was like sapphire.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain <sup>②</sup>Hebrew <i>lapis lazuli</i>  
**4:8** Now their face is blacker than soot;<br>they are not recognized in the streets;<br>their skin has shriveled on their bones;<br>it has become as dry as wood.   
**4:9** Happier were the victims of the sword<br>than the victims of hunger,<br>who wasted away, pierced<br>by lack of the fruits of the field.   
**4:10** The hands of compassionate women<br>have boiled their own children;<br>they became their food<br>during the destruction of the daughter of my people.   
**4:11** The LORD gave full vent to his wrath;<br>he poured out his hot anger,<br>and he kindled a fire in Zion<br>that consumed its foundations.   
**4:12** The kings of the earth did not believe,<br>nor any of the inhabitants of the world,<br>that foe or enemy could enter<br>the gates of Jerusalem.   
**4:13** This was for the sins of her prophets<br>and the iniquities of her priests,<br>who shed in the midst of her<br>the blood of the righteous.   
**4:14** They wandered, blind, through the streets;<br>they were so defiled with blood<br>that no one was able to touch<br>their garments.   
**4:15** “Away! Unclean!” people cried at them.<br>“Away! Away! Do not touch!”<br>So they became fugitives and wanderers;<br>people said among the nations,<br>“They shall stay with us no longer.”   
**4:16** The LORD himself<sup>①</sup> has scattered them;<br>he will regard them no more;<br>no honor was shown to the priests,<br>no favor to the elders.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>The face of the LORD</i>  
**4:17** Our eyes failed, ever watching<br>vainly for help;<br>in our watching we watched<br>for a nation which could not save.   
**4:18** They dogged our steps<br>so that we could not walk in our streets;<br>our end drew near; our days were numbered,<br>for our end had come.   
**4:19** Our pursuers were swifter<br>than the eagles in the heavens;<br>they chased us on the mountains;<br>they lay in wait for us in the wilderness.   
**4:20** The breath of our nostrils, the LORD's anointed,<br>was captured in their pits,<br>of whom we said, “Under his shadow<br>we shall live among the nations.”   
**4:21** Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom,<br>you who dwell in the land of Uz;<br>but to you also the cup shall pass;<br>you shall become drunk and strip yourself bare.   
**4:22** The punishment of your iniquity, O daughter of Zion, is accomplished;<br>he will keep you in exile no longer;<sup>①</sup><br>but your iniquity, O daughter of Edom, he will punish;<br>he will uncover your sins.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>he will not exile you again</i>  

## 5장

**5:1** Remember, O LORD, what has befallen us;<br>look, and see our disgrace!  
**5:2** Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers,<br>our homes to foreigners.  
**5:3** We have become orphans, fatherless;<br>our mothers are like widows.  
**5:4** We must pay for the water we drink;<br>the wood we get must be bought.  
**5:5** Our pursuers are at our necks;<br>we are weary; we are given no rest.  
**5:6** We have given the hand to Egypt, and to Assyria,<br>to get bread enough.  
**5:7** Our fathers sinned, and are no more;<br>and we bear their iniquities.  
**5:8** Slaves rule over us;<br>there is none to deliver us from their hand.  
**5:9** We get our bread at the peril of our lives,<br>because of the sword in the wilderness.  
**5:10** Our skin is hot as an oven<br>with the burning heat of famine.  
**5:11** Women are raped in Zion,<br>young women in the towns of Judah.  
**5:12** Princes are hung up by their hands;<br>no respect is shown to the elders.  
**5:13** Young men are compelled to grind at the mill,<br>and boys stagger under loads of wood.  
**5:14** The old men have left the city gate,<br>the young men their music.  
**5:15** The joy of our hearts has ceased;<br>our dancing has been turned to mourning.  
**5:16** The crown has fallen from our head;<br>woe to us, for we have sinned!  
**5:17** For this our heart has become sick,<br>for these things our eyes have grown dim,  
**5:18** for Mount Zion which lies desolate;<br>jackals prowl over it.  
**5:19** But you, O LORD, reign forever;<br>your throne endures to all generations.  
**5:20** Why do you forget us forever,<br>why do you forsake us for so many days?  
**5:21** Restore us to yourself, O LORD, that we may be restored!<br>Renew our days as of old—  
**5:22** unless you have utterly rejected us,<br>and you remain exceedingly angry with us.
