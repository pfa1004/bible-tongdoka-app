# 31.오바디야 (ESV)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision of Obadiah.<br>Thus says the Lord GOD concerning Edom:<br>We have heard a report from the LORD,<br>and a messenger has been sent among the nations:<br>“Rise up! Let us rise against her for battle!”  
**1:2** Behold, I will make you small among the nations;<br>you shall be utterly despised.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Behold, I have made you small among the nations; you are utterly despised</i>  
**1:3** The pride of your heart has deceived you,<br>you who live in the clefts of the rock,<sup>①</sup><br>in your lofty dwelling,<br>who say in your heart,<br>“Who will bring me down to the ground?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>of Sela</i>  
**1:4** Though you soar aloft like the eagle,<br>though your nest is set among the stars,<br>from there I will bring you down, declares the LORD.   
**1:5** If thieves came to you,<br>if plunderers came by night—<br>how you have been destroyed!—<br>would they not steal only enough for themselves?<br>If grape gatherers came to you,<br>would they not leave gleanings?  
**1:6** How Esau has been pillaged,<br>his treasures sought out!  
**1:7** All your allies have driven you to your border;<br>those at peace with you have deceived you;<br>they have prevailed against you;<br>those who eat your bread<sup>①</sup> have set a trap beneath you—<br>you have<sup>②</sup> no understanding.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew lacks <i>those who eat</i> <sup>②</sup>Hebrew <i>he has</i>  
**1:8** Will I not on that day, declares the LORD,<br>destroy the wise men out of Edom,<br>and understanding out of Mount Esau?  
**1:9** And your mighty men shall be dismayed, O Teman,<br>so that every man from Mount Esau will be cut off by slaughter.   
**1:10** Because of the violence done to your brother Jacob,<br>shame shall cover you,<br>and you shall be cut off forever.  
**1:11** On the day that you stood aloof,<br>on the day that strangers carried off his wealth<br>and foreigners entered his gates<br>and cast lots for Jerusalem,<br>you were like one of them.  
**1:12** But do not gloat over the day of your brother<br>in the day of his misfortune;<br>do not rejoice over the people of Judah<br>in the day of their ruin;<br>do not boast<sup>①</sup><br>in the day of distress.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>do not enlarge your mouth</i>  
**1:13** Do not enter the gate of my people<br>in the day of their calamity;<br>do not gloat over his disaster<br>in the day of his calamity;<br>do not loot his wealth<br>in the day of his calamity.  
**1:14** Do not stand at the crossroads<br>to cut off his fugitives;<br>do not hand over his survivors<br>in the day of distress.   
**1:15** For the day of the LORD is near upon all the nations.<br>As you have done, it shall be done to you;<br>your deeds shall return on your own head.  
**1:16** For as you have drunk on my holy mountain,<br>so all the nations shall drink continually;<br>they shall drink and swallow,<br>and shall be as though they had never been.  
**1:17** But in Mount Zion there shall be those who escape,<br>and it shall be holy,<br>and the house of Jacob shall possess their own possessions.  
**1:18** The house of Jacob shall be a fire,<br>and the house of Joseph a flame,<br>and the house of Esau stubble;<br>they shall burn them and consume them,<br>and there shall be no survivor for the house of Esau, for the LORD has spoken.   
**1:19** Those of the Negeb shall possess Mount Esau,<br>and those of the Shephelah shall possess the land of the Philistines;<br>they shall possess the land of Ephraim and the land of Samaria,<br>and Benjamin shall possess Gilead.  
**1:20** The exiles of this host of the people of Israel<br>shall possess the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath,<br>and the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad<br>shall possess the cities of the Negeb.  
**1:21** Saviors shall go up to Mount Zion<br>to rule Mount Esau,<br>and the kingdom shall be the LORD's.
