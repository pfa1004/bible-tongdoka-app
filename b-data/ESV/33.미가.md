# 33.미가 (ESV)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Micah of Moresheth in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.  
**1:2** Hear, you peoples, all of you;<sup>①</sup><br>pay attention, O earth, and all that is in it,<br>and let the Lord GOD be a witness against you,<br>the Lord from his holy temple.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>all of them</i>  
**1:3** For behold, the LORD is coming out of his place,<br>and will come down and tread upon the high places of the earth.  
**1:4** And the mountains will melt under him,<br>and the valleys will split open,<br>like wax before the fire,<br>like waters poured down a steep place.  
**1:5** All this is for the transgression of Jacob<br>and for the sins of the house of Israel.<br>What is the transgression of Jacob?<br>Is it not Samaria?<br>And what is the high place of Judah?<br>Is it not Jerusalem?  
**1:6** Therefore I will make Samaria a heap in the open country,<br>a place for planting vineyards,<br>and I will pour down her stones into the valley<br>and uncover her foundations.  
**1:7** All her carved images shall be beaten to pieces,<br>all her wages shall be burned with fire,<br>and all her idols I will lay waste,<br>for from the fee of a prostitute she gathered them,<br>and to the fee of a prostitute they shall return.   
**1:8** For this I will lament and wail;<br>I will go stripped and naked;<br>I will make lamentation like the jackals,<br>and mourning like the ostriches.  
**1:9** For her wound is incurable,<br>and it has come to Judah;<br>it has reached to the gate of my people,<br>to Jerusalem.   
**1:10** Tell it not in Gath;<br>weep not at all;<br>in Beth-le-aphrah<br>roll yourselves in the dust.  
**1:11** Pass on your way,<br>inhabitants of Shaphir,<br>in nakedness and shame;<br>the inhabitants of Zaanan<br>do not come out;<br>the lamentation of Beth-ezel<br>shall take away from you its standing place.  
**1:12** For the inhabitants of Maroth<br>wait anxiously for good,<br>because disaster has come down from the LORD<br>to the gate of Jerusalem.  
**1:13** Harness the steeds to the chariots,<br>inhabitants of Lachish;<br>it was the beginning of sin<br>to the daughter of Zion,<br>for in you were found<br>the transgressions of Israel.  
**1:14** Therefore you shall give parting gifts<sup>①</sup><br>to Moresheth-gath;<br>the houses of Achzib shall be a deceitful thing<br>to the kings of Israel.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>give dowry</i>  
**1:15** I will again bring a conqueror to you,<br>inhabitants of Mareshah;<br>the glory of Israel<br>shall come to Adullam.  
**1:16** Make yourselves bald and cut off your hair,<br>for the children of your delight;<br>make yourselves as bald as the eagle,<br>for they shall go from you into exile.   

## 2장

**2:1** Woe to those who devise wickedness<br>and work evil on their beds!<br>When the morning dawns, they perform it,<br>because it is in the power of their hand.  
**2:2** They covet fields and seize them,<br>and houses, and take them away;<br>they oppress a man and his house,<br>a man and his inheritance.  
**2:3** Therefore thus says the LORD:<br>behold, against this family I am devising disaster,<sup>①</sup><br>from which you cannot remove your necks,<br>and you shall not walk haughtily,<br>for it will be a time of disaster.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The same Hebrew word can mean <i>evil</i> or <i>disaster</i>, depending on the context  
**2:4** In that day they shall take up a taunt song against you<br>and moan bitterly,<br>and say, “We are utterly ruined;<br>he changes the portion of my people;<br>how he removes it from me!<br>To an apostate he allots our fields.”  
**2:5** Therefore you will have none to cast the line by lot<br>in the assembly of the LORD.   
**2:6** “Do not preach”—thus they preach—<br>“one should not preach of such things;<br>disgrace will not overtake us.”  
**2:7** Should this be said, O house of Jacob?<br>Has the LORD grown impatient?<sup>①</sup><br>Are these his deeds?<br>Do not my words do good<br>to him who walks uprightly?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>Has the spirit of the LORD grown short?</i>  
**2:8** But lately my people have risen up as an enemy;<br>you strip the rich robe from those who pass by trustingly<br>with no thought of war.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>returning from war</i>  
**2:9** The women of my people you drive out<br>from their delightful houses;<br>from their young children you take away<br>my splendor forever.  
**2:10** Arise and go,<br>for this is no place to rest,<br>because of uncleanness that destroys<br>with a grievous destruction.  
**2:11** If a man should go about and utter wind and lies,<br>saying, “I will preach to you of wine and strong drink,”<br>he would be the preacher for this people!  
**2:12** I will surely assemble all of you, O Jacob;<br>I will gather the remnant of Israel;<br>I will set them together<br>like sheep in a fold,<br>like a flock in its pasture,<br>a noisy multitude of men.  
**2:13** He who opens the breach goes up before them;<br>they break through and pass the gate,<br>going out by it.<br>Their king passes on before them,<br>the LORD at their head.   

## 3장

**3:1** And I said:<br>Hear, you heads of Jacob<br>and rulers of the house of Israel!<br>Is it not for you to know justice?—  
**3:2** you who hate the good and love the evil,<br>who tear the skin from off my people<sup>①</sup><br>and their flesh from off their bones,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>from off them</i>  
**3:3** who eat the flesh of my people,<br>and flay their skin from off them,<br>and break their bones in pieces<br>and chop them up like meat in a pot,<br>like flesh in a cauldron.   
**3:4** Then they will cry to the LORD,<br>but he will not answer them;<br>he will hide his face from them at that time,<br>because they have made their deeds evil.   
**3:5** Thus says the LORD concerning the prophets<br>who lead my people astray,<br>who cry “Peace”<br>when they have something to eat,<br>but declare war against him<br>who puts nothing into their mouths.  
**3:6** Therefore it shall be night to you, without vision,<br>and darkness to you, without divination.<br>The sun shall go down on the prophets,<br>and the day shall be black over them;  
**3:7** the seers shall be disgraced,<br>and the diviners put to shame;<br>they shall all cover their lips,<br>for there is no answer from God.  
**3:8** But as for me, I am filled with power,<br>with the Spirit of the LORD,<br>and with justice and might,<br>to declare to Jacob his transgression<br>and to Israel his sin.   
**3:9** Hear this, you heads of the house of Jacob<br>and rulers of the house of Israel,<br>who detest justice<br>and make crooked all that is straight,  
**3:10** who build Zion with blood<br>and Jerusalem with iniquity.  
**3:11** Its heads give judgment for a bribe;<br>its priests teach for a price;<br>its prophets practice divination for money;<br>yet they lean on the LORD and say,<br>“Is not the LORD in the midst of us?<br>No disaster shall come upon us.”  
**3:12** Therefore because of you<br>Zion shall be plowed as a field;<br>Jerusalem shall become a heap of ruins,<br>and the mountain of the house a wooded height.   

## 4장

**4:1** It shall come to pass in the latter days<br>that the mountain of the house of the LORD<br>shall be established as the highest of the mountains,<br>and it shall be lifted up above the hills;<br>and peoples shall flow to it,  
**4:2** and many nations shall come, and say:<br>“Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD,<br>to the house of the God of Jacob,<br>that he may teach us his ways<br>and that we may walk in his paths.”<br>For out of Zion shall go forth the law,<sup>①</sup><br>and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>teaching</i>  
**4:3** He shall judge between many peoples,<br>and shall decide disputes for strong nations far away;<br>and they shall beat their swords into plowshares,<br>and their spears into pruning hooks;<br>nation shall not lift up sword against nation,<br>neither shall they learn war anymore;  
**4:4** but they shall sit every man under his vine and under his fig tree,<br>and no one shall make them afraid,<br>for the mouth of the LORD of hosts has spoken.  
**4:5** For all the peoples walk<br>each in the name of its god,<br>but we will walk in the name of the LORD our God<br>forever and ever.   
**4:6** In that day, declares the LORD,<br>I will assemble the lame<br>and gather those who have been driven away<br>and those whom I have afflicted;  
**4:7** and the lame I will make the remnant,<br>and those who were cast off, a strong nation;<br>and the LORD will reign over them in Mount Zion<br>from this time forth and forevermore.   
**4:8** And you, O tower of the flock,<br>hill of the daughter of Zion,<br>to you shall it come,<br>the former dominion shall come,<br>kingship for the daughter of Jerusalem.   
**4:9** Now why do you cry aloud?<br>Is there no king in you?<br>Has your counselor perished,<br>that pain seized you like a woman in labor?  
**4:10** Writhe and groan,<sup>①</sup> O daughter of Zion,<br>like a woman in labor,<br>for now you shall go out from the city<br>and dwell in the open country;<br>you shall go to Babylon.<br>There you shall be rescued;<br>there the LORD will redeem you<br>from the hand of your enemies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>push</i>  
**4:11** Now many nations<br>are assembled against you,<br>saying, “Let her be defiled,<br>and let our eyes gaze upon Zion.”  
**4:12** But they do not know<br>the thoughts of the LORD;<br>they do not understand his plan,<br>that he has gathered them as sheaves to the threshing floor.  
**4:13** Arise and thresh,<br>O daughter of Zion,<br>for I will make your horn iron,<br>and I will make your hoofs bronze;<br>you shall beat in pieces many peoples;<br>and shall devote<sup>①</sup> their gain to the LORD,<br>their wealth to the Lord of the whole earth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>devote to destruction</i>  

## 5장

**5:1** <sup>①</sup> Now muster your troops, O daughter<sup>②</sup> of troops;<br>siege is laid against us;<br>with a rod they strike the judge of Israel<br>on the cheek.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch 4:14 in Hebrew <sup>②</sup>That is, city

## 6장

**6:1** Hear what the LORD says:<br>Arise, plead your case before the mountains,<br>and let the hills hear your voice.  
**6:2** Hear, you mountains, the indictment of the LORD,<br>and you enduring foundations of the earth,<br>for the LORD has an indictment against his people,<br>and he will contend with Israel.   
**6:3** “O my people, what have I done to you?<br>How have I wearied you? Answer me!  
**6:4** For I brought you up from the land of Egypt<br>and redeemed you from the house of slavery,<br>and I sent before you Moses,<br>Aaron, and Miriam.  
**6:5** O my people, remember what Balak king of Moab devised,<br>and what Balaam the son of Beor answered him,<br>and what happened from Shittim to Gilgal,<br>that you may know the righteous acts of the LORD.”   
**6:6** “With what shall I come before the LORD,<br>and bow myself before God on high?<br>Shall I come before him with burnt offerings,<br>with calves a year old?  
**6:7** Will the LORD be pleased with<sup>①</sup> thousands of rams,<br>with ten thousands of rivers of oil?<br>Shall I give my firstborn for my transgression,<br>the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Will the LORD accept</i>  
**6:8** He has told you, O man, what is good;<br>and what does the LORD require of you<br>but to do justice, and to love kindness,<sup>①</sup><br>and to walk humbly with your God?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>steadfast love</i>  
**6:9** The voice of the LORD cries to the city—<br>and it is sound wisdom to fear your name:<br>“Hear of the rod and of him who appointed it!<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain  
**6:10** Can I forget any longer the treasures<sup>①</sup> of wickedness in the house of the wicked,<br>and the scant measure that is accursed?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Are there still treasures</i>  
**6:11** Shall I acquit the man with wicked scales<br>and with a bag of deceitful weights?  
**6:12** Your<sup>①</sup> rich men are full of violence;<br>your inhabitants speak lies,<br>and their tongue is deceitful in their mouth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>whose</i>  
**6:13** Therefore I strike you with a grievous blow,<br>making you desolate because of your sins.  
**6:14** You shall eat, but not be satisfied,<br>and there shall be hunger within you;<br>you shall put away, but not preserve,<br>and what you preserve I will give to the sword.  
**6:15** You shall sow, but not reap;<br>you shall tread olives, but not anoint yourselves with oil;<br>you shall tread grapes, but not drink wine.  
**6:16** For you have kept the statutes of Omri,<sup>①</sup><br>and all the works of the house of Ahab;<br>and you have walked in their counsels,<br>that I may make you a desolation, and your<sup>②</sup> inhabitants a hissing;<br>so you shall bear the scorn of my people.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>For the statutes of Omri are kept</i> <sup>②</sup>Hebrew <i>its</i>  

## 7장

**7:1** Woe is me! For I have become<br>as when the summer fruit has been gathered,<br>as when the grapes have been gleaned:<br>there is no cluster to eat,<br>no first-ripe fig that my soul desires.  
**7:2** The godly has perished from the earth,<br>and there is no one upright among mankind;<br>they all lie in wait for blood,<br>and each hunts the other with a net.  
**7:3** Their hands are on what is evil, to do it well;<br>the prince and the judge ask for a bribe,<br>and the great man utters the evil desire of his soul;<br>thus they weave it together.  
**7:4** The best of them is like a brier,<br>the most upright of them a thorn hedge.<br>The day of your watchmen, of your punishment, has come;<br>now their confusion is at hand.  
**7:5** Put no trust in a neighbor;<br>have no confidence in a friend;<br>guard the doors of your mouth<br>from her who lies in your arms;<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>bosom</i>  
**7:6** for the son treats the father with contempt,<br>the daughter rises up against her mother,<br>the daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;<br>a man's enemies are the men of his own house.  
**7:7** But as for me, I will look to the LORD;<br>I will wait for the God of my salvation;<br>my God will hear me.   
**7:8** Rejoice not over me, O my enemy;<br>when I fall, I shall rise;<br>when I sit in darkness,<br>the LORD will be a light to me.  
**7:9** I will bear the indignation of the LORD<br>because I have sinned against him,<br>until he pleads my cause<br>and executes judgment for me.<br>He will bring me out to the light;<br>I shall look upon his vindication.  
**7:10** Then my enemy will see,<br>and shame will cover her who said to me,<br>“Where is the LORD your God?”<br>My eyes will look upon her;<br>now she will be trampled down<br>like the mire of the streets.   
**7:11** A day for the building of your walls!<br>In that day the boundary shall be far extended.  
**7:12** In that day they<sup>①</sup> will come to you,<br>from Assyria and the cities of Egypt,<br>and from Egypt to the River,<sup>②</sup><br>from sea to sea and from mountain to mountain.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>he</i> <sup>②</sup>That is, the Euphrates  
**7:13** But the earth will be desolate<br>because of its inhabitants,<br>for the fruit of their deeds.   
**7:14** Shepherd your people with your staff,<br>the flock of your inheritance,<br>who dwell alone in a forest<br>in the midst of a garden land;<sup>①</sup><br>let them graze in Bashan and Gilead<br>as in the days of old.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>of Carmel</i>  
**7:15** As in the days when you came out of the land of Egypt,<br>I will show them<sup>①</sup> marvelous things.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>him</i>  
**7:16** The nations shall see and be ashamed of all their might;<br>they shall lay their hands on their mouths;<br>their ears shall be deaf;  
**7:17** they shall lick the dust like a serpent,<br>like the crawling things of the earth;<br>they shall come trembling out of their strongholds;<br>they shall turn in dread to the LORD our God,<br>and they shall be in fear of you.   
**7:18** Who is a God like you, pardoning iniquity<br>and passing over transgression<br>for the remnant of his inheritance?<br>He does not retain his anger forever,<br>because he delights in steadfast love.  
**7:19** He will again have compassion on us;<br>he will tread our iniquities underfoot.<br>You will cast all our<sup>①</sup> sins<br>into the depths of the sea.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>their</i>  
**7:20** You will show faithfulness to Jacob<br>and steadfast love to Abraham,<br>as you have sworn to our fathers<br>from the days of old.
