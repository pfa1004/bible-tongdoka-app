# 34.나훔 (ESV)



## 1장

**1:1** An oracle concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum of Elkosh.  
**1:2** The LORD is a jealous and avenging God;<br>the LORD is avenging and wrathful;<br>the LORD takes vengeance on his adversaries<br>and keeps wrath for his enemies.  
**1:3** The LORD is slow to anger and great in power,<br>and the LORD will by no means clear the guilty.<br>His way is in whirlwind and storm,<br>and the clouds are the dust of his feet.  
**1:4** He rebukes the sea and makes it dry;<br>he dries up all the rivers;<br>Bashan and Carmel wither;<br>the bloom of Lebanon withers.  
**1:5** The mountains quake before him;<br>the hills melt;<br>the earth heaves before him,<br>the world and all who dwell in it.   
**1:6** Who can stand before his indignation?<br>Who can endure the heat of his anger?<br>His wrath is poured out like fire,<br>and the rocks are broken into pieces by him.  
**1:7** The LORD is good,<br>a stronghold in the day of trouble;<br>he knows those who take refuge in him.  
**1:8** But with an overflowing flood<br>he will make a complete end of the adversaries,<sup>①</sup><br>and will pursue his enemies into darkness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>of her place</i>  
**1:9** What do you plot against the LORD?<br>He will make a complete end;<br>trouble will not rise up a second time.  
**1:10** For they are like entangled thorns,<br>like drunkards as they drink;<br>they are consumed like stubble fully dried.  
**1:11** From you came one<br>who plotted evil against the LORD,<br>a worthless counselor.   
**1:12** Thus says the LORD,<br>“Though they are at full strength and many,<br>they will be cut down and pass away.<br>Though I have afflicted you,<br>I will afflict you no more.  
**1:13** And now I will break his yoke from off you<br>and will burst your bonds apart.”   
**1:14** The LORD has given commandment about you:<br>“No more shall your name be perpetuated;<br>from the house of your gods I will cut off<br>the carved image and the metal image.<br>I will make your grave, for you are vile.”   
**1:15** <sup>①</sup> Behold, upon the mountains, the feet of him<br>who brings good news,<br>who publishes peace!<br>Keep your feasts, O Judah;<br>fulfill your vows,<br>for never again shall the worthless pass through you;<br>he is utterly cut off.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch <a name='B:410 2:1'>2:1 in Hebrew  

## 2장

**2:1** The scatterer has come up against you.<br>Man the ramparts;<br>watch the road;<br>dress for battle;<sup>①</sup><br>collect all your strength.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>gird your loins</i>  
**2:2** For the LORD is restoring the majesty of Jacob<br>as the majesty of Israel,<br>for plunderers have plundered them<br>and ruined their branches.   
**2:3** The shield of his mighty men is red;<br>his soldiers are clothed in scarlet.<br>The chariots come with flashing metal<br>on the day he musters them;<br>the cypress spears are brandished.  
**2:4** The chariots race madly through the streets;<br>they rush to and fro through the squares;<br>they gleam like torches;<br>they dart like lightning.  
**2:5** He remembers his officers;<br>they stumble as they go,<br>they hasten to the wall;<br>the siege tower<sup>①</sup> is set up.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>the mantelet</i>  
**2:6** The river gates are opened;<br>the palace melts away;  
**2:7** its mistress<sup>①</sup> is stripped;<sup>②</sup> she is carried off,<br>her slave girls lamenting,<br>moaning like doves<br>and beating their breasts.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew word rendered <i>its mistress</i> is uncertain <sup>②</sup>Or <i>exiled</i>  
**2:8** Nineveh is like a pool<br>whose waters run away.<sup>①</sup><br>“Halt! Halt!” they cry,<br>but none turns back.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Compare Septuagint; the meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain  
**2:9** Plunder the silver,<br>plunder the gold!<br>There is no end of the treasure<br>or of the wealth of all precious things.   
**2:10** Desolate! Desolation and ruin!<br>Hearts melt and knees tremble;<br>anguish is in all loins;<br>all faces grow pale!  
**2:11** Where is the lions' den,<br>the feeding place of the young lions,<br>where the lion and lioness went,<br>where his cubs were, with none to disturb?  
**2:12** The lion tore enough for his cubs<br>and strangled prey for his lionesses;<br>he filled his caves with prey<br>and his dens with torn flesh.   
**2:13** Behold, I am against you, declares the LORD of hosts, and I will burn your<sup>①</sup> chariots in smoke, and the sword shall devour your young lions. I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers shall no longer be heard.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>her</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** Woe to the bloody city,<br>all full of lies and plunder—<br>no end to the prey!  
**3:2** The crack of the whip, and rumble of the wheel,<br>galloping horse and bounding chariot!  
**3:3** Horsemen charging,<br>flashing sword and glittering spear,<br>hosts of slain,<br>heaps of corpses,<br>dead bodies without end—<br>they stumble over the bodies!  
**3:4** And all for the countless whorings of the prostitute,<br>graceful and of deadly charms,<br>who betrays nations with her whorings,<br>and peoples with her charms.   
**3:5** Behold, I am against you,<br>declares the LORD of hosts,<br>and will lift up your skirts over your face;<br>and I will make nations look at your nakedness<br>and kingdoms at your shame.  
**3:6** I will throw filth at you<br>and treat you with contempt<br>and make you a spectacle.  
**3:7** And all who look at you will shrink from you and say,<br>“Wasted is Nineveh; who will grieve for her?”<br>Where shall I seek comforters for you?   
**3:8** Are you better than Thebes<sup>①</sup><br>that sat by the Nile,<br>with water around her,<br>her rampart a sea,<br>and water her wall?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>No-amon</i>  
**3:9** Cush was her strength;<br>Egypt too, and that without limit;<br>Put and the Libyans were her<sup>①</sup> helpers.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Hebrew <i>your</i>  
**3:10** Yet she became an exile;<br>she went into captivity;<br>her infants were dashed in pieces<br>at the head of every street;<br>for her honored men lots were cast,<br>and all her great men were bound in chains.  
**3:11** You also will be drunken;<br>you will go into hiding;<br>you will seek a refuge from the enemy.  
**3:12** All your fortresses are like fig trees<br>with first-ripe figs—<br>if shaken they fall<br>into the mouth of the eater.  
**3:13** Behold, your troops<br>are women in your midst.<br>The gates of your land<br>are wide open to your enemies;<br>fire has devoured your bars.   
**3:14** Draw water for the siege;<br>strengthen your forts;<br>go into the clay;<br>tread the mortar;<br>take hold of the brick mold!  
**3:15** There will the fire devour you;<br>the sword will cut you off.<br>It will devour you like the locust.<br>Multiply yourselves like the locust;<br>multiply like the grasshopper!  
**3:16** You increased your merchants<br>more than the stars of the heavens.<br>The locust spreads its wings and flies away.   
**3:17** Your princes<sup>①</sup> are like grasshoppers,<br>your scribes<sup>②</sup> like clouds of locusts<br>settling on the fences<br>in a day of cold—<br>when the sun rises, they fly away;<br>no one knows where they are.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>guards</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>marshals</i>  
**3:18** Your shepherds are asleep,<br>O king of Assyria;<br>your nobles slumber.<br>Your people are scattered on the mountains<br>with none to gather them.  
**3:19** There is no easing your hurt;<br>your wound is grievous.<br>All who hear the news about you<br>clap their hands over you.<br>For upon whom has not come<br>your unceasing evil?
