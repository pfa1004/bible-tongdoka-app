## 1장

**1:1** The Song of Songs, which is Solomon's.  
**1:2** <b>She</b><sup>①</sup><br>Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth!<br>For your love is better than wine;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The translators have added speaker identifications based on the gender and number of the Hebrew words

**1:4** Draw me after you; let us run.<br>The king has brought me into his chambers.<br><b>Others</b><br>We will exult and rejoice in you;<br>we will extol your love more than wine;<br>rightly do they love you.   
**1:5** <b>She</b><br>I am very dark, but lovely,<br>O daughters of Jerusalem,<br>like the tents of Kedar,<br>like the curtains of Solomon.  
**1:6** Do not gaze at me because I am dark,<br>because the sun has looked upon me.<br>My mother's sons were angry with me;<br>they made me keeper of the vineyards,<br>but my own vineyard I have not kept!  
**1:7** Tell me, you whom my soul loves,<br>where you pasture your flock,<br>where you make it lie down at noon;<br>for why should I be like one who veils herself<br>beside the flocks of your companions?   
**1:8** <b>He</b><br>If you do not know,<br>O most beautiful among women,<br>follow in the tracks of the flock,<br>and pasture your young goats<br>beside the shepherds' tents.   
**1:9** I compare you, my love,<br>to a mare among Pharaoh's chariots.  
**1:10** Your cheeks are lovely with ornaments,<br>your neck with strings of jewels.   
**1:11** <b>Others</b><br>We will make for you<sup>①</sup> ornaments of gold,<br>studded with silver.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Hebrew for <i>you</i> is feminine singular  
**1:12** <b>She</b><br>While the king was on his couch,<br>my nard gave forth its fragrance.  
**1:13** My beloved is to me a sachet of myrrh<br>that lies between my breasts.  
**1:14** My beloved is to me a cluster of henna blossoms<br>in the vineyards of Engedi.   
**1:15** <b>He</b><br>Behold, you are beautiful, my love;<br>behold, you are beautiful;<br>your eyes are doves.

**1:17** the beams of our house are cedar;<br>our rafters are pine.

## 3장

**3:1** On my bed by night<br>I sought him whom my soul loves;<br>I sought him, but found him not.  
**3:2** I will rise now and go about the city,<br>in the streets and in the squares;<br>I will seek him whom my soul loves.<br>I sought him, but found him not.  
**3:3** The watchmen found me<br>as they went about in the city.<br>“Have you seen him whom my soul loves?”  
**3:4** Scarcely had I passed them<br>when I found him whom my soul loves.<br>I held him, and would not let him go<br>until I had brought him into my mother's house,<br>and into the chamber of her who conceived me.  
**3:5** I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem,<br>by the gazelles or the does of the field,<br>that you not stir up or awaken love<br>until it pleases.   
**3:6** What is that coming up from the wilderness<br>like columns of smoke,<br>perfumed with myrrh and frankincense,<br>with all the fragrant powders of a merchant?  
**3:7** Behold, it is the litter<sup>①</sup> of Solomon!<br>Around it are sixty mighty men,<br>some of the mighty men of Israel,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>That is, the couch on which servants carry a king  
**3:8** all of them wearing swords<br>and expert in war,<br>each with his sword at his thigh,<br>against terror by night.  
**3:9** King Solomon made himself a carriage<sup>①</sup><br>from the wood of Lebanon.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>sedan chair</i>  
**3:10** He made its posts of silver,<br>its back of gold, its seat of purple;<br>its interior was inlaid with love<br>by the daughters of Jerusalem.  
**3:11** Go out, O daughters of Zion,<br>and look upon King Solomon,<br>with the crown with which his mother crowned him<br>on the day of his wedding,<br>on the day of the gladness of his heart.
