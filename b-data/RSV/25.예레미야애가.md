# 25.예레미야애가 (RSV)



## 1장

**1:1** How lonely sits the city<br>that was full of people!<br>How like a widow has she become,<br>she that was great among the nations!<br>She that was a princess among the cities<br>has become a vassal.  
**1:2** She weeps bitterly in the night,<br>tears on her cheeks;<br>among all her lovers<br>she has none to comfort her;<br>all her friends have dealt treacherously with her,<br>they have become her enemies.  
**1:3** Judah has gone into exile because of affliction<br>and hard servitude;<br>she dwells now among the nations,<br>but finds no resting place;<br>her pursuers have all overtaken her<br>in the midst of her distress.  
**1:4** The roads to Zion mourn,<br>for none come to the appointed feasts;<br>all her gates are desolate,<br>her priests groan;<br>her maidens have been dragged away,<sup>①</sup><br>and she herself suffers bitterly.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Old Latin: Heb <i>afflicted</i>  
**1:5** Her foes have become the head,<br>her enemies prosper,<br>because the LORD has made her suffer<br>for the multitude of her transgressions;<br>her children have gone away,<br>captives before the foe.  
**1:6** From the daughter of Zion has departed<br>all her majesty.<br>Her princes have become like harts<br>that find no pasture;<br>they fled without strength<br>before the pursuer.  
**1:7** Jerusalem remembers<br>in the days of her affliction and bitterness<sup>①</sup><br>all the precious things<br>that were hers from days of old.<br>When her people fell into the hand of the foe,<br>and there was none to help her,<br>the foe gloated over her,<br>mocking at her downfall.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>wandering</i>  
**1:8** Jerusalem sinned grievously,<br>therefore she became filthy;<br>all who honored her despise her,<br>for they have seen her nakedness;<br>yea, she herself groans,<br>and turns her face away.  
**1:9** Her uncleanness was in her skirts;<br>she took no thought of her doom;<br>therefore her fall is terrible,<br>she has no comforter.<br>“O LORD, behold my affliction,<br>for the enemy has triumphed!”  
**1:10** The enemy has stretched out his hands<br>over all her precious things;<br>yea, she has seen the nations<br>invade her sanctuary,<br>those whom thou didst forbid<br>to enter thy congregation.  
**1:11** All her people groan<br>as they search for bread;<br>they trade their treasures for food<br>to revive their strength.<br>“Look, O LORD, and behold,<br>for I am despised.”  
**1:12** “Is it nothing to you,<sup>①</sup> all you who pass by?<br>Look and see<br>if there is any sorrow like my sorrow<br>which was brought upon me,<br>which the LORD inflicted<br>on the day of his fierce anger.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**1:13** “From on high he sent fire;<br>into my bones<sup>①</sup> he made it descend;<br>he spread a net for my feet;<br>he turned me back;<br>he has left me stunned,<br>faint all the day long.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk: Heb <i>bones and</i>  
**1:14** “My transgressions were bound<sup>①</sup> into a yoke;<br>by his hand they were fastened together;<br>they were set upon my neck;<br>he caused my strength to fail;<br>the Lord gave me into the hands<br>of those whom I cannot withstand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb uncertain  
**1:15** “The Lord flouted all my mighty men<br>in the midst of me;<br>he summoned an assembly against me<br>to crush my young men;<br>the Lord has trodden as in a wine press<br>the virgin daughter of Judah.  
**1:16** “For these things I weep;<br>my eyes flow with tears;<br>for a comforter is far from me,<br>one to revive my courage;<br>my children are desolate,<br>for the enemy has prevailed.”  
**1:17** Zion stretches out her hands,<br>but there is none to comfort her;<br>the LORD has commanded against Jacob<br>that his neighbors should be his foes;<br>Jerusalem has become<br>a filthy thing among them.  
**1:18** “The LORD is in the right,<br>for I have rebelled against his word;<br>but hear, all you peoples,<br>and behold my suffering;<br>my maidens and my young men<br>have gone into captivity.  
**1:19** “I called to my lovers<br>but they deceived me;<br>my priests and elders<br>perished in the city,<br>while they sought food<br>to revive their strength.  
**1:20** “Behold, O LORD, for I am in distress,<br>my soul is in tumult,<br>my heart is wrung within me,<br>because I have been very rebellious.<br>In the street the sword bereaves;<br>in the house it is like death.  
**1:21** “Hear<sup>①</sup> how I groan;<br>there is none to comfort me.<br>All my enemies have heard of my trouble;<br>they are glad that thou hast done it.<br>Bring thou<sup>②</sup> the day thou hast announced,<br>and let them be as I am.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Syr: Heb <i>they heard</i> <sup>②</sup>Syr: Heb <i>thou hast brought</i>  
**1:22** “Let all their evil-doing come before thee;<br>and deal with them<br>as thou hast dealt with me<br>because of all my transgressions;<br>for my groans are many<br>and my heart is faint.”  

## 2장

**2:1** How the Lord in his anger<br>has set the daughter of Zion under a cloud!<br>He has cast down from heaven to earth<br>the splendor of Israel;<br>he has not remembered his footstool<br>in the day of his anger.  
**2:2** The Lord has destroyed without mercy<br>all the habitations of Jacob;<br>in his wrath he has broken down<br>the strongholds of the daughter of Judah;<br>he has brought down to the ground in dishonor<br>the kingdom and its rulers.  
**2:3** He has cut down in fierce anger<br>all the might of Israel;<br>he has withdrawn from them his right hand<br>in the face of the enemy;<br>he has burned like a flaming fire in Jacob,<br>consuming all around.  
**2:4** He has bent his bow like an enemy,<br>with his right hand set like a foe;<br>and he has slain all the pride of our eyes<br>in the tent of the daughter of Zion;<br>he has poured out his fury like fire.  
**2:5** The Lord has become like an enemy,<br>he has destroyed Israel;<br>he has destroyed all its palaces,<br>laid in ruins its strongholds;<br>and he has multiplied in the daughter of Judah<br>mourning and lamentation.  
**2:6** He has broken down his booth like that of a garden,<br>laid in ruins the place of his appointed feasts;<br>the LORD has brought to an end in Zion<br>appointed feast and sabbath,<br>and in his fierce indignation has spurned<br>king and priest.  
**2:7** The Lord has scorned his altar,<br>disowned his sanctuary;<br>he has delivered into the hand of the enemy<br>the walls of her palaces;<br>a clamor was raised in the house of the LORD<br>as on the day of an appointed feast.  
**2:8** The LORD determined to lay in ruins<br>the wall of the daughter of Zion;<br>he marked it off by the line;<br>he restrained not his hand from destroying;<br>he caused rampart and wall to lament,<br>they languish together.  
**2:9** Her gates have sunk into the ground;<br>he has ruined and broken her bars;<br>her king and princes are among the nations;<br>the law is no more,<br>and her prophets obtain<br>no vision from the LORD.  
**2:10** The elders of the daughter of Zion<br>sit on the ground in silence;<br>they have cast dust on their heads<br>and put on sackcloth;<br>the maidens of Jerusalem<br>have bowed their heads to the ground.  
**2:11** My eyes are spent with weeping;<br>my soul is in tumult;<br>my heart is poured out in grief<sup>①</sup><br>because of the destruction of the daughter of my people,<br>because infants and babes faint<br>in the streets of the city.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>to the ground</i>  
**2:12** They cry to their mothers,<br>“Where is bread and wine?”<br>as they faint like wounded men<br>in the streets of the city,<br>as their life is poured out<br>on their mothers’ bosom.  
**2:13** What can I say for you, to what compare you,<br>O daughter of Jerusalem?<br>What can I liken to you, that I may comfort you,<br>O virgin daughter of Zion?<br>For vast as the sea is your ruin;<br>who can restore you?  
**2:14** Your prophets have seen for you<br>false and deceptive visions;<br>they have not exposed your iniquity<br>to restore your fortunes,<br>but have seen for you oracles<br>false and misleading.  
**2:15** All who pass along the way<br>clap their hands at you;<br>they hiss and wag their heads<br>at the daughter of Jerusalem;<br>“Is this the city which was called<br>the perfection of beauty,<br>the joy of all the earth?”  
**2:16** All your enemies<br>rail against you;<br>they hiss, they gnash their teeth,<br>they cry: “We have destroyed her!<br>Ah, this is the day we longed for;<br>now we have it; we see it!”  
**2:17** The LORD has done what he purposed,<br>has carried out his threat;<br>as he ordained long ago,<br>he has demolished without pity;<br>he has made the enemy rejoice over you,<br>and exalted the might of your foes.  
**2:18** Cry aloud<sup>①</sup> to the Lord!<br>O<sup>②</sup> daughter of Zion!<br>Let tears stream down like a torrent<br>day and night!<br>Give yourself no rest,<br>your eyes no respite!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>Their heart cried</i> <sup>②</sup>Cn: Heb <i>O wall of</i>  
**2:19** Arise, cry out in the night,<br>at the beginning of the watches!<br>Pour out your heart like water<br>before the presence of the Lord!<br>Lift your hands to him<br>for the lives of your children,<br>who faint for hunger<br>at the head of every street.  
**2:20** Look, O LORD, and see!<br>With whom hast thou dealt thus?<br>Should women eat their offspring,<br>the children of their tender care?<br>Should priest and prophet be slain<br>in the sanctuary of the Lord?  
**2:21** In the dust of the streets<br>lie the young and the old;<br>my maidens and my young men<br>have fallen by the sword;<br>in the day of thy anger thou hast slain them,<br>slaughtering without mercy.  
**2:22** Thou didst invite as to the day of an appointed feast<br>my terrors on every side;<br>and on the day of the anger of the LORD<br>none escaped or survived;<br>those whom I dandled and reared<br>my enemy destroyed.  

## 3장

**3:1** I am the man who has seen affliction<br>under the rod of his wrath;  
**3:2** he has driven and brought me<br>into darkness without any light;  
**3:3** surely against me he turns his hand<br>again and again the whole day long.  
**3:4** He has made my flesh and my skin waste away,<br>and broken my bones;  
**3:5** he has besieged and enveloped me<br>with bitterness and tribulation;  
**3:6** he has made me dwell in darkness<br>like the dead of long ago.  
**3:7** He has walled me about so that I cannot escape;<br>he has put heavy chains on me;  
**3:8** though I call and cry for help,<br>he shuts out my prayer;  
**3:9** he has blocked my ways with hewn stones,<br>he has made my paths crooked.  
**3:10** He is to me like a bear lying in wait,<br>like a lion in hiding;  
**3:11** he led me off my way and tore me to pieces;<br>he has made me desolate;  
**3:12** he bent his bow and set me<br>as a mark for his arrow.  
**3:13** He drove into my heart<br>the arrows of his quiver;  
**3:14** I have become the laughingstock of all peoples,<br>the burden of their songs all day long.  
**3:15** He has filled me with bitterness,<br>he has sated me with wormwood.  
**3:16** He has made my teeth grind on gravel,<br>and made me cower in ashes;  
**3:17** my soul is bereft of peace,<br>I have forgotten what happiness is;  
**3:18** so I say, “Gone is my glory,<br>and my expectation from the LORD.”  
**3:19** Remember my affliction and my bitterness,<sup>①</sup><br>the wormwood and the gall!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>wandering</i>  
**3:20** My soul continually thinks of it<br>and is bowed down within me.  
**3:21** But this I call to mind,<br>and therefore I have hope:  
**3:22** The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases,<sup>①</sup><br>his mercies never come to an end;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Syr Tg: Heb <i>we are not cut off</i>  
**3:23** they are new every morning;<br>great is thy faithfulness.  
**3:24** “The LORD is my portion,” says my soul,<br>“therefore I will hope in him.”  
**3:25** The LORD is good to those who wait for him,<br>to the soul that seeks him.  
**3:26** It is good that one should wait quietly<br>for the salvation of the LORD.  
**3:27** It is good for a man that he bear<br>the yoke in his youth.  
**3:28** Let him sit alone in silence<br>when he has laid it on him;  
**3:29** let him put his mouth in the dust —<br>there may yet be hope;  
**3:30** let him give his cheek to the smiter,<br>and be filled with insults.  
**3:31** For the Lord will not<br>cast off for ever,  
**3:32** but, though he cause grief, he will have compassion<br>according to the abundance of his steadfast love;  
**3:33** for he does not willingly afflict<br>or grieve the sons of men.  
**3:34** To crush under foot<br>all the prisoners of the earth,  
**3:35** to turn aside the right of a man<br>in the presence of the Most High,  
**3:36** to subvert a man in his cause,<br>the Lord does not approve.  
**3:37** Who has commanded and it came to pass,<br>unless the Lord has ordained it?  
**3:38** Is it not from the mouth of the Most High<br>that good and evil come?  
**3:39** Why should a living man complain,<br>a man, about the punishment of his sins?  
**3:40** Let us test and examine our ways,<br>and return to the LORD!  
**3:41** Let us lift up our hearts and hands<br>to God in heaven:  
**3:42** “We have transgressed and rebelled,<br>and thou hast not forgiven.  
**3:43** “Thou hast wrapped thyself with anger and pursued us,<br>slaying without pity;  
**3:44** thou hast wrapped thyself with a cloud<br>so that no prayer can pass through.  
**3:45** Thou hast made us offscouring and refuse<br>among the peoples.  
**3:46** “All our enemies<br>rail against us;  
**3:47** panic and pitfall have come upon us,<br>devastation and destruction;  
**3:48** my eyes flow with rivers of tears<br>because of the destruction of the daughter of my people.  
**3:49** “My eyes will flow without ceasing,<br>without respite,  
**3:50** until the LORD from heaven<br>looks down and sees;  
**3:51** my eyes cause me grief<br>at the fate of all the maidens of my city.  
**3:52** “I have been hunted like a bird<br>by those who were my enemies without cause;  
**3:53** they flung me alive into the pit<br>and cast stones on me;  
**3:54** water closed over my head;<br>I said, ‘I am lost.’  
**3:55** “I called on thy name, O LORD,<br>from the depths of the pit;  
**3:56** thou didst hear my plea, ‘Do not close<br>thine ear to my cry for help!’<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**3:57** Thou didst come near when I called on thee;<br>thou didst say, ‘Do not fear!’  
**3:58** “Thou hast taken up my cause, O Lord,<br>thou hast redeemed my life.  
**3:59** Thou hast seen the wrong done to me, O LORD;<br>judge thou my cause.  
**3:60** Thou hast seen all their vengeance, all their devices against me.  
**3:61** “Thou hast heard their taunts, O LORD,<br>all their devices against me.  
**3:62** The lips and thoughts of my assailants<br>are against me all the day long.  
**3:63** Behold their sitting and their rising;<br>I am the burden of their songs.  
**3:64** “Thou wilt requite them, O LORD,<br>according to the work of their hands.  
**3:65** Thou wilt give them dullness of heart;<br>thy curse will be on them.

## 4장

**4:1** How the gold has grown dim,<br>how the pure gold is changed!<br>The holy stones lie scattered<br>at the head of every street.  
**4:2** The precious sons of Zion,<br>worth their weight in fine gold,<br>how they are reckoned as earthen pots,<br>the work of a potter’s hands!  
**4:3** Even the jackals give the breast<br>and suckle their young,<br>but the daughter of my people has become cruel,<br>like the ostriches in the wilderness.  
**4:4** The tongue of the nursling cleaves<br>to the roof of its mouth for thirst;<br>the children beg for food,<br>but no one gives to them.  
**4:5** Those who feasted on dainties<br>perish in the streets;<br>those who were brought up in purple<br>lie on ash heaps.  
**4:6** For the chastisement<sup>①</sup> of the daughter of my people has been greater<br>than the punishment<sup>②</sup> of Sodom,<br>which was overthrown in a moment,<br>no hand being laid on it.<sup>③</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>iniquity</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>sin</i> <sup>③</sup>Heb uncertain  
**4:7** Her princes were purer than snow,<br>whiter than milk;<br>their bodies were more ruddy than coral,<br>the beauty of their form<sup>①</sup> was like sapphire.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>lapis lazuli</i>  
**4:8** Now their visage is blacker than soot,<br>they are not recognized in the streets;<br>their skin has shriveled upon their bones,<br>it has become as dry as wood.  
**4:9** Happier were the victims of the sword<br>than the victims of hunger,<br>who pined away, stricken<br>by want of the fruits of the field.  
**4:10** The hands of compassionate women<br>have boiled their own children;<br>they became their food<br>in the destruction of the daughter of my people.  
**4:11** The LORD gave full vent to his wrath,<br>he poured out his hot anger;<br>and he kindled a fire in Zion,<br>which consumed its foundations.  
**4:12** The kings of the earth did not believe,<br>or any of the inhabitants of the world,<br>that foe or enemy could enter<br>the gates of Jerusalem.  
**4:13** This was for the sins of her prophets<br>and the iniquities of her priests,<br>who shed in the midst of her<br>the blood of the righteous.  
**4:14** They wandered, blind, through the streets,<br>so defiled with blood<br>that none could touch<br>their garments.  
**4:15** “Away! Unclean!” men cried at them;<br>“Away! Away! Touch not!”<br>So they became fugitives and wanderers;<br>men said among the nations,<br>“They shall stay with us no longer.”  
**4:16** The LORD himself has scattered them,<br>he will regard them no more;<br>no honor was shown to the priests,<br>no favor to the elders.  
**4:17** Our eyes failed, ever watching<br>vainly for help;<br>in our watching<sup>①</sup> we watched<br>for a nation which could not save.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**4:18** Men dogged our steps<br>so that we could not walk in our streets;<br>our end drew near; our days were numbered;<br>for our end had come.  
**4:19** Our pursuers were swifter<br>than the vultures in the heavens;<br>they chased us on the mountains,<br>they lay in wait for us in the wilderness.  
**4:20** The breath of our nostrils, the LORD’s anointed,<br>was taken in their pits,<br>he of whom we said, “Under his shadow<br>we shall live among the nations.”  
**4:21** Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom,<br>dweller in the land of Uz;<br>but to you also the cup shall pass;<br>you shall become drunk and strip yourself bare.  
**4:22** The punishment of your iniquity, O daughter of Zion, is accomplished,<br>he will keep you in exile no longer;<br>but your iniquity, O daughter of Edom, he will punish,<br>he will uncover your sins.

**5:1** Remember, O LORD, what has befallen us;<br>behold, and see our disgrace!  
**5:2** Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers,<br>our homes to aliens.  
**5:3** We have become orphans, fatherless;<br>our mothers are like widows.  
**5:4** We must pay for the water we drink,<br>the wood we get must be bought.  
**5:5** With a yoke<sup>①</sup> on our necks we are hard driven;<br>we are weary, we are given no rest.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Symmachus: Heb lacks <i>with a yoke</i>

**5:8** Slaves rule over us;<br>there is none to deliver us from their hand.  
**5:9** We get our bread at the peril of our lives,<br>because of the sword in the wilderness.  
**5:10** Our skin is hot as an oven<br>with the burning heat of famine.

**5:15** The joy of our hearts has ceased;<br>our dancing has been turned to mourning.

**5:19** But thou, O LORD, dost reign for ever;<br>thy throne endures to all generations.  
**5:20** Why dost thou forget us for ever,<br>why dost thou so long forsake us?  
**5:21** Restore us to thyself, O LORD, that we may be restored!<br>Renew our days as of old!
