# 34.나훔 (RSV)



## 1장

**1:1** An oracle concerning Nin′eveh. The book of the vision of Nahum of Elkosh.  
**1:2** The LORD is a jealous God and avenging,<br>the LORD is avenging and wrathful;<br>the LORD takes vengeance on his adversaries<br>and keeps wrath for his enemies.  
**1:3** The LORD is slow to anger and of great might,<br>and the LORD will by no means clear the guilty.<br>His way is in whirlwind and storm,<br>and the clouds are the dust of his feet.  
**1:4** He rebukes the sea and makes it dry,<br>he dries up all the rivers;<br>Bashan and Carmel wither,<br>the bloom of Lebanon fades.  
**1:5** The mountains quake before him,<br>the hills melt;<br>the earth is laid waste before him,<br>the world and all that dwell therein.  
**1:6** Who can stand before his indignation?<br>Who can endure the heat of his anger?<br>His wrath is poured out like fire,<br>and the rocks are broken asunder by him.  
**1:7** The LORD is good,<br>a stronghold in the day of trouble;<br>he knows those who take refuge in him.  
**1:8** But with an overflowing flood<br>he will make a full end of his adversaries,<sup>①</sup><br>and will pursue his enemies into darkness.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk: Heb <i>her place</i>  
**1:9** What do you plot against the LORD?<br>He will make a full end;<br>he will not take vengeance<sup>①</sup> twice on his foes.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk: Heb <i>rise up</i> <sup>②</sup>Cn: Heb <i>distress</i>  
**1:10** Like entangled thorns they are consumed,<sup>①</sup><br>like dry stubble.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>are consumed, drunken as with their drink</i>  
**1:11** Did one not<sup>①</sup> come out from you,<br>who plotted evil against the LORD,<br>and counseled villainy?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>fully</i>  
**1:12** Thus says the LORD,<br>“Though they be strong and many,<sup>①</sup><br>they will be cut off and pass away.<br>Though I have afflicted you,<br>I will afflict you no more.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**1:13** And now I will break his yoke from off you<br>and will burst your bonds asunder.”  
**1:14** The LORD has given commandment about you:<br>“No more shall your name be perpetuated;<br>from the house of your gods I will cut off<br>the graven image and the molten image.<br>I will make your grave, for you are vile.”  
**1:15** <sup>①</sup> Behold, on the mountains the feet of him<br>who brings good tidings,<br>who proclaims peace!<br>Keep your feasts, O Judah,<br>fulfil your vows,<br>for never again shall the wicked come against you,<br>he is utterly cut off.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch 2.1 in Heb  

## 2장

**2:1** The shatterer has come up against you.<br>Man the ramparts;<br>watch the road;<br>gird your loins;<br>collect all your strength.  
**2:2** (For the LORD is restoring the majesty of Jacob<br>as the majesty of Israel,<br>for plunderers have stripped them<br>and ruined their branches.)  
**2:3** The shield of his mighty men is red,<br>his soldiers are clothed in scarlet.<br>The chariots flash like flame<sup>①</sup><br>when mustered in array;<br>the chargers<sup>②</sup> prance.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: The meaning of the Hebrew word is uncertain <sup>②</sup>Cn Compare Gk Syr: Heb <i>cypresses</i>  
**2:4** The chariots rage in the streets,<br>they rush to and fro through the squares;<br>they gleam like torches,<br>they dart like lightning.  
**2:5** The officers are summoned,<br>they stumble as they go,<br>they hasten to the wall,<br>the mantelet is set up.  
**2:6** The river gates are opened,<br>the palace is in dismay;  
**2:7** its mistress<sup>①</sup> is stripped, she is carried off,<br>her maidens lamenting,<br>moaning like doves,<br>and beating their breasts.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain  
**2:8** Nin′eveh is like a pool<br>whose waters<sup>①</sup> run away.<br>“Halt! Halt!” they cry;<br>but none turns back.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn Compare Gk: Heb <i>from the days that she has become, and they</i>  
**2:9** Plunder the silver,<br>plunder the gold!<br>There is no end of treasure,<br>or wealth of every precious thing.  
**2:10** Desolate! Desolation and ruin!<br>Hearts faint and knees tremble,<br>anguish is on all loins,<br>all faces grow pale!  
**2:11** Where is the lions’ den,<br>the cave<sup>①</sup> of the young lions,<br>where the lion brought his prey,<br>where his cubs were, with none to disturb?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>pasture</i>  
**2:12** The lion tore enough for his whelps<br>and strangled prey for his lionesses;<br>he filled his caves with prey<br>and his dens with torn flesh.  
**2:13** Behold, I am against you, says the LORD of hosts, and I will burn your<sup>①</sup> chariots in smoke, and the sword shall devour your young lions; I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers shall no more be heard.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>her</i>  

## 3장

**3:1** Woe to the bloody city,<br>all full of lies and booty —<br>no end to the plunder!  
**3:2** The crack of whip, and rumble of wheel,<br>galloping horse and bounding chariot!  
**3:3** Horsemen charging,<br>flashing sword and glittering spear,<br>hosts of slain,<br>heaps of corpses,<br>dead bodies without end —<br>they stumble over the bodies!  
**3:4** And all for the countless harlotries of the harlot,<br>graceful and of deadly charms,<br>who betrays nations with her harlotries,<br>and peoples with her charms.  
**3:5** Behold, I am against you,<br>says the LORD of hosts,<br>and will lift up your skirts over your face;<br>and I will let nations look on your nakedness<br>and kingdoms on your shame.  
**3:6** I will throw filth at you<br>and treat you with contempt,<br>and make you a gazingstock.  
**3:7** And all who look on you will shrink from you and say,<br>Wasted is Nin′eveh; who will bemoan her?<br>whence shall I seek comforters for her?<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk: Heb <i>you</i>  
**3:8** Are you better than Thebes<sup>①</sup><br>that sat by the Nile,<br>with water around her,<br>her rampart a sea,<br>and water her wall?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>No-amon</i>  
**3:9** Ethiopia was her strength,<br>Egypt too, and that without limit;<br>Put and the Libyans were her<sup>①</sup> helpers.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk: Heb <i>your</i>  
**3:10** Yet she was carried away,<br>she went into captivity;<br>her little ones were dashed in pieces<br>at the head of every street;<br>for her honored men lots were cast,<br>and all her great men were bound in chains.  
**3:11** You also will be drunken,<br>you will be dazed;<br>you will seek<br>a refuge from the enemy.  
**3:12** All your fortresses are like fig trees<br>with first-ripe figs —<br>if shaken they fall<br>into the mouth of the eater.  
**3:13** Behold, your troops<br>are women in your midst.<br>The gates of your land<br>are wide open to your foes;<br>fire has devoured your bars.  
**3:14** Draw water for the siege,<br>strengthen your forts;<br>go into the clay,<br>tread the mortar,<br>take hold of the brick mold!  
**3:15** There will the fire devour you,<br>the sword will cut you off.<br>It will devour you like the locust.<br>Multiply yourselves like the locust,<br>multiply like the grasshopper!  
**3:16** You increased your merchants<br>more than the stars of the heavens.<br>The locust spreads its wings and flies away.  
**3:17** Your princes are like grasshoppers,<br>your scribes<sup>①</sup> like clouds of locusts<br>settling on the fences<br>in a day of cold —<br>when the sun rises, they fly away;<br>no one knows where they are.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>marshals</i>  
**3:18** Your shepherds are asleep,<br>O king of Assyria;<br>your nobles slumber.<br>Your people are scattered on the mountains<br>with none to gather them.  
**3:19** There is no assuaging your hurt,<br>your wound is grievous.<br>All who hear the news of you<br>clap their hands over you.<br>For upon whom has not come<br>your unceasing evil?
