# 31.오바디야 (RSV)



## 1장

**1:1** The vision of Obadi′ah.<br>Thus says the Lord GOD concerning Edom:<br>We have heard tidings from the LORD,<br>and a messenger has been sent among the nations:<br>“Rise up! let us rise against her for battle!”  
**1:2** Behold, I will make you small among the nations,<br>you shall be utterly despised.  
**1:3** The pride of your heart has deceived you,<br>you who live in the clefts of the rock,<sup>①</sup><br>whose dwelling is high,<br>who say in your heart,<br>“Who will bring me down to the ground?”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>Sela</i>  
**1:4** Though you soar aloft like the eagle,<br>though your nest is set among the stars,<br>thence I will bring you down,<br>says the LORD.  
**1:5** If thieves came to you,<br>if plunderers by night —<br>how you have been destroyed! —<br>would they not steal only enough for themselves?<br>If grape gatherers came to you,<br>would they not leave gleanings?  
**1:6** How Esau has been pillaged,<br>his treasures sought out!  
**1:7** All your allies have deceived you,<br>they have driven you to the border;<br>your confederates have prevailed against you;<br>your trusted friends have set a trap under you —<br>there is no understanding of it.  
**1:8** Will I not on that day, says the LORD,<br>destroy the wise men out of Edom,<br>and understanding out of Mount Esau?  
**1:9** And your mighty men shall be dismayed, O Teman,<br>so that every man from Mount Esau will be cut off by slaughter.  
**1:10** For the violence done to your brother Jacob,<br>shame shall cover you,<br>and you shall be cut off for ever.  
**1:11** On the day that you stood aloof,<br>on the day that strangers carried off his wealth,<br>and foreigners entered his gates<br>and cast lots for Jerusalem,<br>you were like one of them.  
**1:12** But you should not have gloated over the day of your brother<br>in the day of his misfortune;<br>you should not have rejoiced over the people of Judah<br>in the day of their ruin;<br>you should not have boasted<br>in the day of distress.  
**1:13** You should not have entered the gate of my people<br>in the day of his calamity;<br>you should not have gloated over his disaster<br>in the day of his calamity;<br>you should not have looted his goods<br>in the day of his calamity.  
**1:14** You should not have stood at the parting of the ways<br>to cut off his fugitives;<br>you should not have delivered up his survivors<br>in the day of distress.  
**1:15** For the day of the LORD is near upon all the nations.<br>As you have done, it shall be done to you,<br>your deeds shall return on your own head.  
**1:16** For as you have drunk upon my holy mountain,<br>all the nations round about shall drink;<br>they shall drink, and stagger,<sup>①</sup><br>and shall be as though they had not been.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>swallow</i>  
**1:17** But in Mount Zion there shall be those that escape,<br>and it shall be holy;<br>and the house of Jacob shall possess their own possessions.  
**1:18** The house of Jacob shall be a fire,<br>and the house of Joseph a flame,<br>and the house of Esau stubble;<br>they shall burn them and consume them,<br>and there shall be no survivor to the house of Esau;<br>for the LORD has spoken.  
**1:19** Those of the Negeb shall possess Mount Esau,<br>and those of the Shephe′lah the land of the Philistines;<br>they shall possess the land of E′phraim and the land of Samar′ia<br>and Benjamin shall possess Gilead.  
**1:20** The exiles in Halah<sup>①</sup> who are of the people of Israel<br>shall possess<sup>②</sup> Phoenicia as far as Zar′ephath;<br>and the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sephar′ad<br>shall possess the cities of the Negeb.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>this army</i> <sup>②</sup>Cn: Heb <i>which</i>  
**1:21** Saviors shall go up to Mount Zion<br>to rule Mount Esau;<br>and the kingdom shall be the LORD’s.
