# 20.잠언 (RSV)



## 1장

**1:1** The proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel:  
**1:2** That men may know wisdom and instruction,<br>understand words of insight,  
**1:3** receive instruction in wise dealing,<br>righteousness, justice, and equity;  
**1:4** that prudence may be given to the simple,<br>knowledge and discretion to the youth —  
**1:5** the wise man also may hear and increase in learning,<br>and the man of understanding acquire skill,  
**1:6** to understand a proverb and a figure,<br>the words of the wise and their riddles.  
**1:7** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge;<br>fools despise wisdom and instruction.  
**1:8** Hear, my son, your father’s instruction,<br>and reject not your mother’s teaching;  
**1:9** for they are a fair garland for your head,<br>and pendants for your neck.  
**1:10** My son, if sinners entice you,<br>do not consent.  
**1:11** If they say, “Come with us, let us lie in wait for blood,<br>let us wantonly ambush the innocent;  
**1:12** like Sheol let us swallow them alive<br>and whole, like those who go down to the Pit:  
**1:13** we shall find all precious goods,<br>we shall fill our houses with spoil;  
**1:14** throw in your lot among us,<br>we will all have one purse” —  
**1:15** my son, do not walk in the way with them,<br>hold back your foot from their paths;  
**1:16** for their feet run to evil,<br>and they make haste to shed blood.  
**1:17** For in vain is a net spread<br>in the sight of any bird;  
**1:18** but these men lie in wait for their own blood,<br>they set an ambush for their own lives.  
**1:19** Such are the ways of all who get gain by violence;<br>it takes away the life of its possessors.  
**1:20** Wisdom cries aloud in the street;<br>in the markets she raises her voice;  
**1:21** on the top of the walls<sup>①</sup> she cries out;<br>at the entrance of the city gates she speaks:<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**1:22** “How long, O simple ones, will you love being simple?<br>How long will scoffers delight in their scoffing<br>and fools hate knowledge?  
**1:23** Give heed<sup>①</sup> to my reproof;<br>behold, I will pour out my thoughts<sup>②</sup> to you;<br>I will make my words known to you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>Turn</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>spirit</i>  
**1:24** Because I have called and you refused to listen,<br>have stretched out my hand and no one has heeded,  
**1:25** and you have ignored all my counsel<br>and would have none of my reproof,  
**1:26** I also will laugh at your calamity;<br>I will mock when panic strikes you,  
**1:27** when panic strikes you like a storm,<br>and your calamity comes like a whirlwind,<br>when distress and anguish come upon you.  
**1:28** Then they will call upon me, but I will not answer;<br>they will seek me diligently but will not find me.  
**1:29** Because they hated knowledge<br>and did not choose the fear of the LORD,  
**1:30** would have none of my counsel,<br>and despised all my reproof,  
**1:31** therefore they shall eat the fruit of their way<br>and be sated with their own devices.  
**1:32** For the simple are killed by their turning away,<br>and the complacence of fools destroys them;  
**1:33** but he who listens to me will dwell secure<br>and will be at ease, without dread of evil.”  

## 2장

**2:1** My son, if you receive my words<br>and treasure up my commandments with you,  
**2:2** making your ear attentive to wisdom<br>and inclining your heart to understanding;  
**2:3** yes, if you cry out for insight<br>and raise your voice for understanding,  
**2:4** if you seek it like silver<br>and search for it as for hidden treasures;  
**2:5** then you will understand the fear of the LORD<br>and find the knowledge of God.  
**2:6** For the LORD gives wisdom;<br>from his mouth come knowledge and understanding;  
**2:7** he stores up sound wisdom for the upright;<br>he is a shield to those who walk in integrity,  
**2:8** guarding the paths of justice<br>and preserving the way of his saints.  
**2:9** Then you will understand righteousness and justice<br>and equity, every good path;  
**2:10** for wisdom will come into your heart,<br>and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul;  
**2:11** discretion will watch over you;<br>understanding will guard you;  
**2:12** delivering you from the way of evil,<br>from men of perverted speech,  
**2:13** who forsake the paths of uprightness<br>to walk in the ways of darkness,  
**2:14** who rejoice in doing evil<br>and delight in the perverseness of evil;  
**2:15** men whose paths are crooked,<br>and who are devious in their ways.  
**2:16** You will be saved from the loose<sup>①</sup> woman,<br>from the adventuress<sup>②</sup> with her smooth words,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>strange</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>foreign woman</i>  
**2:17** who forsakes the companion of her youth<br>and forgets the covenant of her God;  
**2:18** for her house sinks down to death,<br>and her paths to the shades;  
**2:19** none who go to her come back<br>nor do they regain the paths of life.  
**2:20** So you will walk in the way of good men<br>and keep to the paths of the righteous.  
**2:21** For the upright will inhabit the land,<br>and men of integrity will remain in it;  
**2:22** but the wicked will be cut off from the land,<br>and the treacherous will be rooted out of it.  

## 3장

**3:1** My son, do not forget my teaching,<br>but let your heart keep my commandments;  
**3:2** for length of days and years of life<br>and abundant welfare will they give you.  
**3:3** Let not loyalty and faithfulness forsake you;<br>bind them about your neck,<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**3:4** So you will find favor and good repute<sup>①</sup><br>in the sight of God and man.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>understanding</i>  
**3:5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart,<br>and do not rely on your own insight.  
**3:6** In all your ways acknowledge him,<br>and he will make straight your paths.  
**3:7** Be not wise in your own eyes;<br>fear the LORD, and turn away from evil.  
**3:8** It will be healing to your flesh<sup>①</sup><br>and refreshment<sup>②</sup> to your bones.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>navel</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>medicine</i>  
**3:9** Honor the LORD with your substance<br>and with the first fruits of all your produce;  
**3:10** then your barns will be filled with plenty,<br>and your vats will be bursting with wine.  
**3:11** My son, do not despise the LORD’s discipline<br>or be weary of his reproof,  
**3:12** for the LORD reproves him whom he loves,<br>as a father the son in whom he delights.  
**3:13** Happy is the man who finds wisdom,<br>and the man who gets understanding,  
**3:14** for the gain from it is better than gain from silver<br>and its profit better than gold.  
**3:15** She is more precious than jewels,<br>and nothing you desire can compare with her.  
**3:16** Long life is in her right hand;<br>in her left hand are riches and honor.  
**3:17** Her ways are ways of pleasantness,<br>and all her paths are peace.  
**3:18** She is a tree of life to those who lay hold of her;<br>those who hold her fast are called happy.  
**3:19** The LORD by wisdom founded the earth;<br>by understanding he established the heavens;  
**3:20** by his knowledge the deeps broke forth,<br>and the clouds drop down the dew.  
**3:21** My son, keep sound wisdom and discretion;<br>let them not escape from your sight,<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Reversing the order of the clauses  
**3:22** and they will be life for your soul<br>and adornment for your neck.  
**3:23** Then you will walk on your way securely<br>and your foot will not stumble.  
**3:24** If you sit down,<sup>①</sup> you will not be afraid;<br>when you lie down, your sleep will be sweet.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk: Heb <i>lie down</i>  
**3:25** Do not be afraid of sudden panic,<br>or of the ruin<sup>①</sup> of the wicked, when it comes;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>storm</i>  
**3:26** for the LORD will be your confidence<br>and will keep your foot from being caught.  
**3:27** Do not withhold good from those to whom it<sup>①</sup> is due,<br>when it is in your power to do it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>Do not withhold good from its owners</i>  
**3:28** Do not say to your neighbor, “Go, and come again,<br>tomorrow I will give it” — when you have it with you.  
**3:29** Do not plan evil against your neighbor<br>who dwells trustingly beside you.  
**3:30** Do not contend with a man for no reason,<br>when he has done you no harm.  
**3:31** Do not envy a man of violence<br>and do not choose any of his ways;  
**3:32** for the perverse man is an abomination to the LORD,<br>but the upright are in his confidence.  
**3:33** The LORD’s curse is on the house of the wicked,<br>but he blesses the abode of the righteous.  
**3:34** Toward the scorners he is scornful,<br>but to the humble he shows favor.  
**3:35** The wise will inherit honor,<br>but fools get<sup>①</sup> disgrace.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>exalt</i>  

## 4장

**4:1** Hear, O sons, a father’s instruction,<br>and be attentive, that you may gain<sup>①</sup> insight;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>know</i>  
**4:2** for I give you good precepts:<br>do not forsake my teaching.  
**4:3** When I was a son with my father,<br>tender, the only one in the sight of my mother,  
**4:4** he taught me, and said to me,<br>“Let your heart hold fast my words;<br>keep my commandments, and live;  
**4:5** do not forget, and do not turn away from the words of my mouth.<br>Get wisdom; get insight.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Reversing the order of the lines  
**4:6** Do not forsake her, and she will keep you;<br>love her, and she will guard you.  
**4:7** The beginning of wisdom is this: Get wisdom,<br>and whatever you get, get insight.  
**4:8** Prize her highly,<sup>①</sup> and she will exalt you;<br>she will honor you if you embrace her.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain  
**4:9** She will place on your head a fair garland;<br>she will bestow on you a beautiful crown.”  
**4:10** Hear, my son, and accept my words,<br>that the years of your life may be many.  
**4:11** I have taught you the way of wisdom;<br>I have led you in the paths of uprightness.  
**4:12** When you walk, your step will not be hampered;<br>and if you run, you will not stumble.  
**4:13** Keep hold of instruction, do not let go;<br>guard her, for she is your life.  
**4:14** Do not enter the path of the wicked,<br>and do not walk in the way of evil men.  
**4:15** Avoid it; do not go on it;<br>turn away from it and pass on.  
**4:16** For they cannot sleep unless they have done wrong;<br>they are robbed of sleep unless they have made some one stumble.  
**4:17** For they eat the bread of wickedness<br>and drink the wine of violence.  
**4:18** But the path of the righteous is like the light of dawn,<br>which shines brighter and brighter until full day.  
**4:19** The way of the wicked is like deep darkness;<br>they do not know over what they stumble.  
**4:20** My son, be attentive to my words;<br>incline your ear to my sayings.  
**4:21** Let them not escape from your sight;<br>keep them within your heart.  
**4:22** For they are life to him who finds them,<br>and healing to all his flesh.  
**4:23** Keep your heart with all vigilance;<br>for from it flow the springs of life.  
**4:24** Put away from you crooked speech,<br>and put devious talk far from you.  
**4:25** Let your eyes look directly forward,<br>and your gaze be straight before you.  
**4:26** Take heed to<sup>①</sup> the path of your feet,<br>then all your ways will be sure.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew word is uncertain  
**4:27** Do not swerve to the right or to the left;<br>turn your foot away from evil.  

## 5장

**5:1** My son, be attentive to my wisdom,<br>incline your ear to my understanding;  
**5:2** that you may keep discretion,<br>and your lips may guard knowledge.  
**5:3** For the lips of a loose woman drip honey,<br>and her speech<sup>①</sup> is smoother than oil;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>palate</i>

**5:5** Her feet go down to death;<br>her steps follow the path to<sup>①</sup> Sheol;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>lay hold of</i>  
**5:6** she does not take heed to<sup>①</sup> the path of life;<br>her ways wander, and she does not know it.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew word is uncertain  
**5:7** And now, O sons, listen to me,<br>and do not depart from the words of my mouth.  
**5:8** Keep your way far from her,<br>and do not go near the door of her house;  
**5:9** lest you give your honor to others<br>and your years to the merciless;  
**5:10** lest strangers take their fill of your strength,<sup>①</sup><br>and your labors go to the house of an alien;<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>wealth</i>

**5:14** I was at the point of utter ruin<br>in the assembled congregation.”  
**5:15** Drink water from your own cistern,<br>flowing water from your own well.  
**5:16** Should your springs be scattered abroad,<br>streams of water in the streets?  
**5:17** Let them be for yourself alone,<br>and not for strangers with you.  
**5:18** Let your fountain be blessed,<br>and rejoice in the wife of your youth,  
**5:19** a lovely hind, a graceful doe.<br>Let her affection fill you at all times with delight,<br>be infatuated always with her love.  
**5:20** Why should you be infatuated, my son, with a loose woman<br>and embrace the bosom of an adventuress?  
**5:21** For a man’s ways are before the eyes of the LORD,<br>and he watches<sup>①</sup> all his paths.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The meaning of the Hebrew word is uncertain  
**5:22** The iniquities of the wicked ensnare him,<br>and he is caught in the toils of his sin.  
**5:23** He dies for lack of discipline,<br>and because of his great folly he is lost.  

## 6장

**6:20** My son, keep your father’s commandment,<br>and forsake not your mother’s teaching.

**6:22** When you walk, they<sup>①</sup> will lead you;<br>when you lie down, they<sup>②</sup> will watch over you;<br>and when you awake, they<sup>③</sup> will talk with you.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>it</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>it</i> <sup>③</sup>Heb <i>it</i>

**6:24** to preserve you from the evil woman,<br>from the smooth tongue of the adventuress.

## 7장

**7:1** My son, keep my words<br>and treasure up my commandments with you;  
**7:2** keep my commandments and live,<br>keep my teachings as the apple of your eye;  
**7:3** bind them on your fingers,<br>write them on the tablet of your heart.  
**7:4** Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,”<br>and call insight your intimate friend;  
**7:5** to preserve you from the loose woman,<br>from the adventuress with her smooth words.  
**7:6** For at the window of my house<br>I have looked out through my lattice,  
**7:7** and I have seen among the simple,<br>I have perceived among the youths,<br>a young man without sense,
