# 35.하박국 (RSV)



## 1장

**1:1** The oracle of God which Habak′kuk the prophet saw.  
**1:2** O LORD, how long shall I cry for help,<br>and thou wilt not hear?<br>Or cry to thee “Violence!”<br>and thou wilt not save?  
**1:3** Why dost thou make me see wrongs<br>and look upon trouble?<br>Destruction and violence are before me;<br>strife and contention arise.  
**1:4** So the law is slacked<br>and justice never goes forth.<br>For the wicked surround the righteous,<br>so justice goes forth perverted.  
**1:5** Look among the nations, and see;<br>wonder and be astounded.<br>For I am doing a work in your days<br>that you would not believe if told.  
**1:6** For lo, I am rousing the Chalde′ans,<br>that bitter and hasty nation,<br>who march through the breadth of the earth,<br>to seize habitations not their own.  
**1:7** Dread and terrible are they;<br>their justice and dignity proceed from themselves.  
**1:8** Their horses are swifter than leopards,<br>more fierce than the evening wolves;<br>their horsemen press proudly on.<br>Yea, their horsemen come from afar;<br>they fly like an eagle swift to devour.  
**1:9** They all come for violence;<br>terror<sup>①</sup> of them goes before them.<br>They gather captives like sand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb uncertain  
**1:10** At kings they scoff,<br>and of rulers they make sport.<br>They laugh at every fortress,<br>for they heap up earth and take it.  
**1:11** Then they sweep by like the wind and go on,<br>guilty men, whose own might is their god!  
**1:12** Art thou not from everlasting,<br>O LORD my God, my Holy One?<br>We shall not die.<br>O LORD, thou hast ordained them as a judgment;<br>and thou, O Rock, hast established them for chastisement.  
**1:13** Thou who art of purer eyes than to behold evil<br>and canst not look on wrong,<br>why dost thou look on faithless men,<br>and art silent when the wicked swallows up<br>the man more righteous than he?  
**1:14** For thou makest men like the fish of the sea,<br>like crawling things that have no ruler.  
**1:15** He brings all of them up with a hook,<br>he drags them out with his net,<br>he gathers them in his seine;<br>so he rejoices and exults.  
**1:16** Therefore he sacrifices to his net<br>and burns incense to his seine;<br>for by them he lives in luxury,<sup>①</sup><br>and his food is rich.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>his portion is fat</i>  
**1:17** Is he then to keep on emptying his net,<br>and mercilessly slaying nations for ever?  

## 2장

**2:1** I will take my stand to watch,<br>and station myself on the tower,<br>and look forth to see what he will say to me,<br>and what I will answer concerning my complaint.  
**2:2** And the LORD answered me:<br>“Write the vision;<br>make it plain upon tablets,<br>so he may run who reads it.  
**2:3** For still the vision awaits its time;<br>it hastens to the end — it will not lie.<br>If it seem slow, wait for it;<br>it will surely come, it will not delay.  
**2:4** Behold, he whose soul is not upright in him shall fail,<sup>①</sup><br>but the righteous shall live by his faith.<sup>②</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>is puffed up</i> <sup>②</sup>Or <i>faithfulness</i>  
**2:5** Moreover, wine is treacherous;<br>the arrogant man shall not abide.<sup>①</sup><br>His greed is as wide as Sheol;<br>like death he has never enough.<br>He gathers for himself all nations,<br>and collects as his own all peoples.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>The Hebrew of these two lines is obscure  
**2:6** Shall not all these take up their taunt against him, in scoffing derision of him, and say,<br>“Woe to him who heaps up what is not his own —<br>for how long? —<br>and loads himself with pledges!”  
**2:7** Will not your debtors suddenly arise,<br>and those awake who will make you tremble?<br>Then you will be booty for them.  
**2:8** Because you have plundered many nations,<br>all the remnant of the peoples shall plunder you,<br>for the blood of men and violence to the earth,<br>to cities and all who dwell therein.  
**2:9** Woe to him who gets evil gain for his house,<br>to set his nest on high,<br>to be safe from the reach of harm!  
**2:10** You have devised shame to your house<br>by cutting off many peoples;<br>you have forfeited your life.  
**2:11** For the stone will cry out from the wall,<br>and the beam from the woodwork respond.  
**2:12** Woe to him who builds a town with blood,<br>and founds a city on iniquity!  
**2:13** Behold, is it not from the LORD of hosts<br>that peoples labor only for fire,<br>and nations weary themselves for nought?  
**2:14** For the earth will be filled<br>with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD,<br>as the waters cover the sea.  
**2:15** Woe to him who makes his neighbors drink<br>of the cup of his wrath,<sup>①</sup> and makes them drunk,<br>to gaze on their shame!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>joining to your wrath</i>  
**2:16** You will be sated with contempt instead of glory.<br>Drink, yourself, and stagger!<sup>①</sup><br>The cup in the LORD’S right hand<br>will come around to you,<br>and shame will come upon your glory!<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn Compare Gk Syr: Heb <i>be uncircumcised</i>  
**2:17** The violence done to Lebanon will overwhelm you;<br>the destruction of the beasts will terrify you,<sup>①</sup><br>for the blood of men and violence to the earth,<br>to cities and all who dwell therein.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Syr: Heb <i>them</i>  
**2:18** What profit is an idol<br>when its maker has shaped it,<br>a metal image, a teacher of lies?<br>For the workman trusts in his own creation<br>when he makes dumb idols!  
**2:19** Woe to him who says to a wooden thing, Awake;<br>to a dumb stone, Arise!<br>Can this give revelation?<br>Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver,<br>and there is no breath at all in it.  
**2:20** But the LORD is in his holy temple;<br>let all the earth keep silence before him.  

## 3장

**3:1** A prayer of Habak′kuk the prophet, according to Shigion′oth.  
**3:2** O LORD, I have heard the report of thee,<br>and thy work, O LORD, do I fear.<br>In the midst of the years renew it;<br>in the midst of the years make it known;<br>in wrath remember mercy.  
**3:3** God came from Teman,<br>and the Holy One from Mount Paran.<br>His glory covered the heavens,<br>and the earth was full of his praise.<br>Selah  
**3:4** His brightness was like the light,<br>rays flashed from his hand;<br>and there he veiled his power.  
**3:5** Before him went pestilence,<br>and plague followed close behind.  
**3:6** He stood and measured the earth;<br>he looked and shook the nations;<br>then the eternal mountains were scattered,<br>the everlasting hills sank low.<br>His ways were as of old.  
**3:7** I saw the tents of Cushan in affliction;<br>the curtains of the land of Mid′ian did tremble.  
**3:8** Was thy wrath against the rivers, O LORD?<br>Was thy anger against the rivers,<br>or thy indignation against the sea,<br>when thou didst ride upon thy horses,<br>upon thy chariot of victory?  
**3:9** Thou didst strip the sheath from thy bow,<br>and put the arrows to the string.<sup>①</sup><br>Selah<br>Thou didst cleave the earth with rivers.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb obscure  
**3:10** The mountains saw thee, and writhed;<br>the raging waters swept on;<br>the deep gave forth its voice,<br>it lifted its hands on high.  
**3:11** The sun and moon stood still in their habitation<sup>①</sup><br>at the light of thine arrows as they sped,<br>at the flash of thy glittering spear.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**3:12** Thou didst bestride the earth in fury,<br>thou didst trample the nations in anger.  
**3:13** Thou wentest forth for the salvation of thy people,<br>for the salvation of thy anointed.<br>Thou didst crush the head of the wicked,<sup>①</sup><br>laying him bare from thigh to neck.<sup>②</sup><br>Selah<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>head from the house of the wicked</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb obscure  
**3:14** Thou didst pierce with thy<sup>①</sup> shafts the head of his warriors,<sup>②</sup><br>who came like a whirlwind to scatter me,<br>rejoicing as if to devour the poor in secret.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>his</i> <sup>②</sup>Vg Compare Gk Syr: Heb uncertain  
**3:15** Thou didst trample the sea with thy horses,<br>the surging of mighty waters.  
**3:16** I hear, and my body trembles,<br>my lips quiver at the sound;<br>rottenness enters into my bones,<br>my steps totter<sup>①</sup> beneath me.<br>I will quietly wait for the day of trouble<br>to come upon people who invade us.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn Compare Gk: Heb <i>I tremble because</i>  
**3:17** Though the fig tree do not blossom,<br>nor fruit be on the vines,<br>the produce of the olive fail<br>and the fields yield no food,<br>the flock be cut off from the fold<br>and there be no herd in the stalls,  
**3:18** yet I will rejoice in the LORD,<br>I will joy in the God of my salvation.  
**3:19** GOD, the Lord, is my strength;<br>he makes my feet like hinds’ feet,<br>he makes me tread upon my high places.<br>To the choirmaster: with stringed<sup>①</sup> instruments.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>my stringed</i>
