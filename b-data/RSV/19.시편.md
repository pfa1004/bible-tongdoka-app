# 19.시편 (RSV)



## 1장

**1:1** Blessed is the man<br>who walks not in the counsel of the wicked,<br>nor stands in the way of sinners,<br>nor sits in the seat of scoffers;  
**1:2** but his delight is in the law of the LORD,<br>and on his law he meditates day and night.  
**1:3** He is like a tree<br>planted by streams of water,<br>that yields its fruit in its season,<br>and its leaf does not wither.<br>In all that he does, he prospers.  
**1:4** The wicked are not so,<br>but are like chaff which the wind drives away.  
**1:5** Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment,<br>nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous;  
**1:6** for the LORD knows the way of the righteous,<br>but the way of the wicked will perish.  

## 2장

**2:1** Why do the nations conspire,<br>and the peoples plot in vain?  
**2:2** The kings of the earth set themselves,<br>and the rulers take counsel together,<br>against the LORD and his anointed, saying,  
**2:3** “Let us burst their bonds asunder,<br>and cast their cords from us.”  
**2:4** He who sits in the heavens laughs;<br>the LORD has them in derision.  
**2:5** Then he will speak to them in his wrath,<br>and terrify them in his fury, saying,  
**2:6** “I have set my king<br>on Zion, my holy hill.”  
**2:7** I will tell of the decree of the LORD:<br>He said to me, “You are my son,<br>today I have begotten you.  
**2:8** Ask of me, and I will make the nations your heritage,<br>and the ends of the earth your possession.  
**2:9** You shall break them with a rod of iron,<br>and dash them in pieces like a potter’s vessel.”  
**2:10** Now therefore, O kings, be wise;<br>be warned, O rulers of the earth.  
**2:11** Serve the LORD with fear,<br>with trembling  
**2:12** kiss his feet,<sup>①</sup><br>lest he be angry, and you perish in the way;<br>for his wrath is quickly kindled.<br>Blessed are all who take refuge in him.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: The Hebrew of 11b and 12a is uncertain  

## 3장

## 4장

**4:2** O men, how long shall my honor suffer shame?<br>How long will you love vain words, and seek after lies? <i>Selah</i>

**4:4** Be angry, but sin not;<br>commune with your own hearts on your beds, and be silent. <i>Selah</i>

**4:7** Thou hast put more joy in my heart<br>than they have when their grain and wine abound.  
**4:8** In peace I will both lie down and sleep;<br>for thou alone, O LORD, makest me dwell in safety.
