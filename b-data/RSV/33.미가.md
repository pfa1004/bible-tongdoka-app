# 33.미가 (RSV)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Micah of Mo′resheth in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezeki′ah, kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samar′ia and Jerusalem.  
**1:2** Hear, you peoples, all of you;<br>hearken, O earth, and all that is in it;<br>and let the Lord GOD be a witness against you,<br>the Lord from his holy temple.  
**1:3** For behold, the LORD is coming forth out of his place,<br>and will come down and tread upon the high places of the earth.  
**1:4** And the mountains will melt under him<br>and the valleys will be cleft,<br>like wax before the fire,<br>like waters poured down a steep place.  
**1:5** All this is for the transgression of Jacob<br>and for the sins of the house of Israel.<br>What is the transgression of Jacob?<br>Is it not Samar′ia?<br>And what is the sin of the house<sup>①</sup> of Judah?<br>Is it not Jerusalem?<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Tg Compare Syr: Heb <i>what are the high places</i>  
**1:6** Therefore I will make Samar′ia a heap in the open country,<br>a place for planting vineyards;<br>and I will pour down her stones into the valley,<br>and uncover her foundations.  
**1:7** All her images shall be beaten to pieces,<br>all her hires shall be burned with fire,<br>and all her idols I will lay waste;<br>for from the hire of a harlot she gathered them,<br>and to the hire of a harlot they shall return.  
**1:8** For this I will lament and wail;<br>I will go stripped and naked;<br>I will make lamentation like the jackals,<br>and mourning like the ostriches.  
**1:9** For her wound<sup>①</sup> is incurable;<br>and it has come to Judah,<br>it has reached to the gate of my people,<br>to Jerusalem.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Syr Vg: Heb <i>wounds</i>  
**1:10** Tell it not in Gath,<br>weep not at all;<br>in Beth-le-aph′rah<br>roll yourselves in the dust.  
**1:11** Pass on your way,<br>inhabitants of Shaphir,<br>in nakedness and shame;<br>the inhabitants of Za′anan<br>do not come forth;<br>the wailing of Beth-e′zel<br>shall take away from you its standing place.  
**1:12** For the inhabitants of Maroth<br>wait anxiously for good,<br>because evil has come down from the LORD<br>to the gate of Jerusalem.  
**1:13** Harness the steeds to the chariots,<br>inhabitants of Lachish;<br>you were<sup>①</sup> the beginning of sin<br>to the daughter of Zion,<br>for in you were found<br>the transgressions of Israel.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>it was</i>  
**1:14** Therefore you shall give parting gifts<br>to Mo′resheth-gath;<br>the houses of Achzib shall be a deceitful thing<br>to the kings of Israel.  
**1:15** I will again bring a conqueror upon you,<br>inhabitants of Mare′shah;<br>the glory of Israel<br>shall come to Adullam.  
**1:16** Make yourselves bald and cut off your hair,<br>for the children of your delight;<br>make yourselves as bald as the eagle,<br>for they shall go from you into exile.  

## 2장

**2:1** Woe to those who devise wickedness<br>and work evil upon their beds!<br>When the morning dawns, they perform it,<br>because it is in the power of their hand.  
**2:2** They covet fields, and seize them;<br>and houses, and take them away;<br>they oppress a man and his house,<br>a man and his inheritance.  
**2:3** Therefore thus says the LORD:<br>Behold, against this family I am devising evil,<br>from which you cannot remove your necks;<br>and you shall not walk haughtily,<br>for it will be an evil time.  
**2:4** In that day they shall take up a taunt song against you,<br>and wail with bitter lamentation,<br>and say, “We are utterly ruined;<br>he changes the portion of my people;<br>how he removes it from me!<br>Among our captors<sup>①</sup> he divides our fields.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>the rebellious</i>  
**2:5** Therefore you will have none to cast the line by lot<br>in the assembly of the LORD.  
**2:6** “Do not preach” — thus they preach —<br>“one should not preach of such things;<br>disgrace will not overtake us.”  
**2:7** Should this be said, O house of Jacob?<br>Is the Spirit of the LORD impatient?<br>Are these his doings?<br>Do not my words do good<br>to him who walks uprightly?  
**2:8** But you rise against my people<sup>①</sup> as an enemy;<br>you strip the robe from the peaceful,<sup>②</sup><br>from those who pass by trustingly<br>with no thought of war.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Cn: Heb <i>yesterday my people rose</i> <sup>②</sup>Cn: Heb <i>from before a garment</i>  
**2:9** The women of my people you drive out<br>from their pleasant houses;<br>from their young children you take away<br>my glory for ever.  
**2:10** Arise and go,<br>for this is no place to rest;<br>because of uncleanness that destroys<br>with a grievous destruction.  
**2:11** If a man should go about and utter wind and lies,<br>saying, “I will preach to you of wine and strong drink,”<br>he would be the preacher for this people!  
**2:12** I will surely gather all of you, O Jacob,<br>I will gather the remnant of Israel;<br>I will set them together<br>like sheep in a fold,<br>like a flock in its pasture,<br>a noisy multitude of men.  
**2:13** He who opens the breach will go up before them;<br>they will break through and pass the gate,<br>going out by it.<br>Their king will pass on before them,<br>the LORD at their head.  

## 3장

**3:1** And I said:<br>Hear, you heads of Jacob<br>and rulers of the house of Israel!<br>Is it not for you to know justice? —  
**3:2** you who hate the good and love the evil,<br>who tear the skin from off my people,<br>and their flesh from off their bones;  
**3:3** who eat the flesh of my people,<br>and flay their skin from off them,<br>and break their bones in pieces,<br>and chop them up like meat<sup>①</sup> in a kettle,<br>like flesh in a caldron.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk: Heb <i>as</i>  
**3:4** Then they will cry to the LORD,<br>but he will not answer them;<br>he will hide his face from them at that time,<br>because they have made their deeds evil.  
**3:5** Thus says the LORD concerning the prophets<br>who lead my people astray,<br>who cry “Peace”<br>when they have something to eat,<br>but declare war against him<br>who puts nothing into their mouths.  
**3:6** Therefore it shall be night to you, without vision,<br>and darkness to you, without divination.<br>The sun shall go down upon the prophets,<br>and the day shall be black over them;  
**3:7** the seers shall be disgraced,<br>and the diviners put to shame;<br>they shall all cover their lips,<br>for there is no answer from God.  
**3:8** But as for me, I am filled with power,<br>with the Spirit of the LORD,<br>and with justice and might,<br>to declare to Jacob his transgression<br>and to Israel his sin.  
**3:9** Hear this, you heads of the house of Jacob<br>and rulers of the house of Israel,<br>who abhor justice<br>and pervert all equity,  
**3:10** who build Zion with blood<br>and Jerusalem with wrong.  
**3:11** Its heads give judgment for a bribe,<br>its priests teach for hire,<br>its prophets divine for money;<br>yet they lean upon the LORD and say,<br>“Is not the LORD in the midst of us?<br>No evil shall come upon us.”  
**3:12** Therefore because of you<br>Zion shall be plowed as a field;<br>Jerusalem shall become a heap of ruins,<br>and the mountain of the house a wooded height.  

## 4장

**4:1** It shall come to pass in the latter days<br>that the mountain of the house of the LORD<br>shall be established as the highest of the mountains,<br>and shall be raised up above the hills;<br>and peoples shall flow to it,  
**4:2** and many nations shall come, and say:<br>“Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD,<br>to the house of the God of Jacob;<br>that he may teach us his ways<br>and we may walk in his paths.”<br>For out of Zion shall go forth the law,<br>and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.  
**4:3** He shall judge between many peoples,<br>and shall decide for strong nations afar off;<br>and they shall beat their swords into plowshares,<br>and their spears into pruning hooks;<br>nation shall not lift up sword against nation,<br>neither shall they learn war any more;  
**4:4** but they shall sit every man under his vine and under his fig tree,<br>and none shall make them afraid;<br>for the mouth of the LORD of hosts has spoken.  
**4:5** For all the peoples walk<br>each in the name of its god,<br>but we will walk in the name of the LORD our God<br>for ever and ever.  
**4:6** In that day, says the LORD,<br>I will assemble the lame<br>and gather those who have been driven away,<br>and those whom I have afflicted;  
**4:7** and the lame I will make the remnant;<br>and those who were cast off, a strong nation;<br>and the LORD will reign over them in Mount Zion<br>from this time forth and for evermore.  
**4:8** And you, O tower of the flock,<br>hill of the daughter of Zion,<br>to you shall it come,<br>the former dominion shall come,<br>the kingdom of the daughter of Jerusalem.  
**4:9** Now why do you cry aloud?<br>Is there no king in you?<br>Has your counselor perished,<br>that pangs have seized you like a woman in travail?  
**4:10** Writhe and groan,<sup>①</sup> O daughter of Zion,<br>like a woman in travail;<br>for now you shall go forth from the city<br>and dwell in the open country;<br>you shall go to Babylon.<br>There you shall be rescued,<br>there the LORD will redeem you<br>from the hand of your enemies.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**4:11** Now many nations<br>are assembled against you,<br>saying, “Let her be profaned,<br>and let our eyes gaze upon Zion.”  
**4:12** But they do not know<br>the thoughts of the LORD,<br>they do not understand his plan,<br>that he has gathered them as sheaves to the threshing floor.  
**4:13** Arise and thresh,<br>O daughter of Zion,<br>for I will make your horn iron<br>and your hoofs bronze;<br>you shall beat in pieces many peoples,<br>and shall<sup>①</sup> devote their gain to the LORD,<br>their wealth to the Lord of the whole earth.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Syr Tg: Heb <i>I will</i>

## 7장

**7:3** Their hands are upon what is evil, to do it diligently;<br>the prince and the judge ask for a bribe,<br>and the great man utters the evil desire of his soul;<br>thus they weave it together.  
**7:4** The best of them is like a brier,<br>the most upright of them a thorn hedge.<br>The day of their<sup>①</sup> watchmen, of their<sup>②</sup> punishment, has come;<br>now their confusion is at hand.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb <i>your</i> <sup>②</sup>Heb <i>your</i>
