# 29.요엘 (RSV)



## 1장

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Joel, the son of Pethu′el:  
**1:2** Hear this, you aged men,<br>give ear, all inhabitants of the land!<br>Has such a thing happened in your days,<br>or in the days of your fathers?  
**1:3** Tell your children of it,<br>and let your children tell their children,<br>and their children another generation.  
**1:4** What the cutting locust left,<br>the swarming locust has eaten.<br>What the swarming locust left,<br>the hopping locust has eaten,<br>and what the hopping locust left,<br>the destroying locust has eaten.  
**1:5** Awake, you drunkards, and weep;<br>and wail, all you drinkers of wine,<br>because of the sweet wine,<br>for it is cut off from your mouth.  
**1:6** For a nation has come up against my land,<br>powerful and without number;<br>its teeth are lions’ teeth,<br>and it has the fangs of a lioness.  
**1:7** It has laid waste my vines,<br>and splintered my fig trees;<br>it has stripped off their bark and thrown it down;<br>their branches are made white.  
**1:8** Lament like a virgin girded with sackcloth<br>for the bridegroom of her youth.  
**1:9** The cereal offering and the drink offering are cut off<br>from the house of the LORD.<br>The priests mourn,<br>the ministers of the LORD.  
**1:10** The fields are laid waste,<br>the ground mourns;<br>because the grain is destroyed,<br>the wine fails,<br>the oil languishes.  
**1:11** Be confounded, O tillers of the soil,<br>wail, O vinedressers,<br>for the wheat and the barley;<br>because the harvest of the field has perished.  
**1:12** The vine withers,<br>the fig tree languishes.<br>Pomegranate, palm, and apple,<br>all the trees of the field are withered;<br>and gladness fails<br>from the sons of men.  
**1:13** Gird on sackcloth and lament, O priests,<br>wail, O ministers of the altar.<br>Go in, pass the night in sackcloth,<br>O ministers of my God!<br>Because cereal offering and drink offering<br>are withheld from the house of your God.  
**1:14** Sanctify a fast,<br>call a solemn assembly.<br>Gather the elders<br>and all the inhabitants of the land<br>to the house of the LORD your God;<br>and cry to the LORD.  
**1:15** Alas for the day!<br>For the day of the LORD is near,<br>and as destruction from the Almighty it comes.  
**1:16** Is not the food cut off<br>before our eyes,<br>joy and gladness<br>from the house of our God?  
**1:17** The seed shrivels under the clods,<sup>①</sup><br>the storehouses are desolate;<br>the granaries are ruined<br>because the grain has failed.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Heb uncertain  
**1:18** How the beasts groan!<br>The herds of cattle are perplexed<br>because there is no pasture for them;<br>even the flocks of sheep are dismayed.  
**1:19** Unto thee, O LORD, I cry.<br>For fire has devoured<br>the pastures of the wilderness,<br>and flame has burned<br>all the trees of the field.  
**1:20** Even the wild beasts cry to thee<br>because the water brooks are dried up,<br>and fire has devoured<br>the pastures of the wilderness.  

## 2장

**2:1** Blow the trumpet in Zion;<br>sound the alarm on my holy mountain!<br>Let all the inhabitants of the land tremble,<br>for the day of the LORD is coming, it is near,  
**2:2** a day of darkness and gloom,<br>a day of clouds and thick darkness!<br>Like blackness there is spread upon the mountains<br>a great and powerful people;<br>their like has never been from of old,<br>nor will be again after them<br>through the years of all generations.  
**2:3** Fire devours before them,<br>and behind them a flame burns.<br>The land is like the garden of Eden before them,<br>but after them a desolate wilderness,<br>and nothing escapes them.  
**2:4** Their appearance is like the appearance of horses,<br>and like war horses they run.  
**2:5** As with the rumbling of chariots,<br>they leap on the tops of the mountains,<br>like the crackling of a flame of fire<br>devouring the stubble,<br>like a powerful army<br>drawn up for battle.  
**2:6** Before them peoples are in anguish,<br>all faces grow pale.  
**2:7** Like warriors they charge,<br>like soldiers they scale the wall.<br>They march each on his way,<br>they do not swerve<sup>①</sup> from their paths.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Syr Vg: Heb <i>take a pledge</i>  
**2:8** They do not jostle one another,<br>each marches in his path;<br>they burst through the weapons<br>and are not halted.  
**2:9** They leap upon the city,<br>they run upon the walls;<br>they climb up into the houses,<br>they enter through the windows like a thief.  
**2:10** The earth quakes before them,<br>the heavens tremble.<br>The sun and the moon are darkened,<br>and the stars withdraw their shining.  
**2:11** The LORD utters his voice<br>before his army,<br>for his host is exceedingly great;<br>he that executes his word is powerful.<br>For the day of the LORD is great and very terrible;<br>who can endure it?  
**2:12** “Yet even now,” says the LORD,<br>“return to me with all your heart,<br>with fasting, with weeping, and with mourning;  
**2:13** and rend your hearts and not your garments.”<br>Return to the LORD, your God,<br>for he is gracious and merciful,<br>slow to anger, and abounding in steadfast love,<br>and repents of evil.  
**2:14** Who knows whether he will not turn and repent,<br>and leave a blessing behind him,<br>a cereal offering and a drink offering<br>for the LORD, your God?  
**2:15** Blow the trumpet in Zion;<br>sanctify a fast;<br>call a solemn assembly;  
**2:16** gather the people.<br>Sanctify the congregation;<br>assemble the elders;<br>gather the children,<br>even nursing infants.<br>Let the bridegroom leave his room,<br>and the bride her chamber.  
**2:17** Between the vestibule and the altar<br>let the priests, the ministers of the LORD, weep<br>and say, “Spare thy people, O LORD,<br>and make not thy heritage a reproach,<br>a byword among the nations.<br>Why should they say among the peoples,<br>‘Where is their God?’”  
**2:18** Then the LORD became jealous for his land,<br>and had pity on his people.  
**2:19** The LORD answered and said to his people,<br>“Behold, I am sending to you<br>grain, wine, and oil,<br>and you will be satisfied;<br>and I will no more make you<br>a reproach among the nations.  
**2:20** “I will remove the northerner far from you,<br>and drive him into a parched and desolate land,<br>his front into the eastern sea,<br>and his rear into the western sea;<br>the stench and foul smell of him will rise,<br>for he has done great things.  
**2:21** “Fear not, O land;<br>be glad and rejoice,<br>for the LORD has done great things!  
**2:22** Fear not, you beasts of the field,<br>for the pastures of the wilderness are green;<br>the tree bears its fruit,<br>the fig tree and vine give their full yield.  
**2:23** “Be glad, O sons of Zion,<br>and rejoice in the LORD, your God;<br>for he has given the early rain for your vindication,<br>he has poured down for you abundant rain,<br>the early and the latter rain, as before.  
**2:24** “The threshing floors shall be full of grain,<br>the vats shall overflow with wine and oil.  
**2:25** I will restore to you the years<br>which the swarming locust has eaten,<br>the hopper, the destroyer, and the cutter,<br>my great army, which I sent among you.  
**2:26** “You shall eat in plenty and be satisfied,<br>and praise the name of the LORD your God,<br>who has dealt wondrously with you.<br>And my people shall never again be put to shame.  
**2:27** You shall know that I am in the midst of Israel,<br>and that I, the LORD, am your God and there is none else.<br>And my people shall never again<br>be put to shame.  
**2:28** <sup>①</sup> “And it shall come to pass afterward,<br>that I will pour out my spirit on all flesh;<br>your sons and your daughters shall prophesy,<br>your old men shall dream dreams,<br>and your young men shall see visions.<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch 3.1 in Heb  
**2:29** Even upon the menservants and maidservants<br>in those days, I will pour out my spirit.  
**2:30** “And I will give portents in the heavens and on the earth, blood and fire and columns of smoke.  
**2:31** The sun shall be turned to darkness, and the moon to blood, before the great and terrible day of the LORD comes.  
**2:32** And it shall come to pass that all who call upon the name of the LORD shall be delivered; for in Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there shall be those who escape, as the LORD has said, and among the survivors shall be those whom the LORD calls.  

## 3장

**3:1** <sup>①</sup> “For behold, in those days and at that time, when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem,<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Ch 4.1 in Heb  
**3:2** I will gather all the nations and bring them down to the valley of Jehosh′aphat, and I will enter into judgment with them there, on account of my people and my heritage Israel, because they have scattered them among the nations, and have divided up my land,  
**3:3** and have cast lots for my people, and have given a boy for a harlot, and have sold a girl for wine, and have drunk it.  
**3:4** “What are you to me, O Tyre and Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Are you paying me back for something? If you are paying me back, I will requite your deed upon your own head swiftly and speedily.  
**3:5** For you have taken my silver and my gold, and have carried my rich treasures into your temples.<sup>①</sup><br>→ <sup>①</sup>Or <i>palaces</i>  
**3:6** You have sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks, removing them far from their own border.  
**3:7** But now I will stir them up from the place to which you have sold them, and I will requite your deed upon your own head.  
**3:8** I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hand of the sons of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabe′ans, to a nation far off; for the LORD has spoken.”  
**3:9** Proclaim this among the nations:<br>Prepare war,<br>stir up the mighty men.<br>Let all the men of war draw near,<br>let them come up.  
**3:10** Beat your plowshares into swords,<br>and your pruning hooks into spears;<br>let the weak say, “I am a warrior.”  
**3:11** Hasten and come,<br>all you nations round about,<br>gather yourselves there.<br>Bring down thy warriors, O LORD.  
**3:12** Let the nations bestir themselves,<br>and come up to the valley of Jehosh′aphat;<br>for there I will sit to judge<br>all the nations round about.  
**3:13** Put in the sickle,<br>for the harvest is ripe.<br>Go in, tread,<br>for the wine press is full.<br>The vats overflow,<br>for their wickedness is great.  
**3:14** Multitudes, multitudes,<br>in the valley of decision!<br>For the day of the LORD is near<br>in the valley of decision.  
**3:15** The sun and the moon are darkened,<br>and the stars withdraw their shining.  
**3:16** And the LORD roars from Zion,<br>and utters his voice from Jerusalem,<br>and the heavens and the earth shake.<br>But the LORD is a refuge to his people,<br>a stronghold to the people of Israel.  
**3:17** “So you shall know that I am the LORD your God,<br>who dwell in Zion, my holy mountain.<br>And Jerusalem shall be holy<br>and strangers shall never again pass through it.  
**3:18** “And in that day<br>the mountains shall drip sweet wine,<br>and the hills shall flow with milk,<br>and all the stream beds of Judah<br>shall flow with water;<br>and a fountain shall come forth from the house of the LORD<br>and water the valley of Shittim.  
**3:19** “Egypt shall become a desolation<br>and Edom a desolate wilderness,<br>for the violence done to the people of Judah,<br>because they have shed innocent blood in their land.  
**3:20** But Judah shall be inhabited for ever,<br>and Jerusalem to all generations.  
**3:21** I will avenge their blood, and I will not clear the guilty,<sup>①</sup><br>for the LORD dwells in Zion.”<br>→ <sup>①</sup>Gk Syr: Heb <i>I will hold innocent their blood which I have not held innocent</i>
